# Elemental Alchemy Illustration Generation Guide

Complete instructions for generating the mythopoetic illustrations in Midjourney.

---

## Overview

**Total Images to Generate:**
- 12 Chapter Thresholds (hero images)
- 24 Vignettes (Preface + Introduction)
- 36 total for Phase 1

**Style:** Mythopoetic Minimalism — ancient wisdom in modern form

**Primary Reference:** Hilma af Klint + alchemical manuscript aesthetic

---

## Step 1: Midjourney Setup

### Access Midjourney
1. Go to [midjourney.com](https://midjourney.com) or use Discord
2. Subscribe if needed (Basic plan works, Standard recommended for volume)
3. Use `/imagine` command to generate

### Recommended Settings
```
/settings
```
- Model: V6.1 (or latest)
- Style: Raw (for more control)
- Variability: High (for more options)

---

## Step 4: Selection Process

For each prompt:

1. **Generate 4 variations** — Midjourney creates a grid of 4
2. **Upscale favorites** — Click U1, U2, U3, or U4
3. **Vary if needed** — Use V buttons or "Make Variations" for refinements
4. **Download final** — Right-click > Save Image

### Selection Criteria

**Keep images that feel:**
- Luminous (light from within)
- Spacious (generous negative space)
- Timeless (could be ancient or modern)
- Contemplative (invites pause)
- Clean (not busy or cluttered)

**Reject images that feel:**
- Busy or cluttered
- Too literal or photographic
- Culturally specific
- Dark or heavy
- Fantasy/gaming aesthetic

## Troubleshooting

### Images too literal/photographic
Add: `abstract, symbolic, not photorealistic`

### Images too busy
Add: `minimal, sparse, lots of negative space`

### Wrong color palette
Specify: `amber and charcoal palette` or `blue-violet palette`

### Figures too detailed
Add: `figure as simple silhouette only, no facial features, no details`

### Style not matching Hilma af Klint
Add: `geometric abstraction, theosophical art influence, spiritual modernism`
