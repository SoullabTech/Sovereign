# Elemental Alchemy — Illustration Style Bible

## Core Aesthetic: Mythopoetic Minimalism

**Philosophy:** Ancient wisdom in modern form. Images that feel like they've always existed — recovered rather than created. The timeless made visible through clean geometry and archetypal presence. Hilma af Klint meets Apple spatial design.

---

## Visual Principles

### 1. Light as Primordial Force
- Images should feel **luminous**, not busy
- Prefer gradient glows over hard edges
- Light emanates from within, not external sources
- Think: the first light of creation, not modern illumination
- Evoke cave paintings lit by firelight, temple ceilings at dawn

### 2. Ancient Geometry + Organic Flow
- Sacred geometry (circles, spirals, toroids) as underlying structure
- Organic elements (water, flames, leaves, breath) as surface texture
- The geometry holds; the organic flows within it
- Reference: alchemical diagrams, Pythagorean forms, mandalic structures
- Feeling: these patterns predate language

### 3. Archetypal Human Form
- When figures appear: **silhouettes or abstracted forms**
- No specific ethnicity, age, or gender markers
- Figures are archetypes, not individuals — the Seeker, the Witness, the Transformed
- Postures convey state: reaching, resting, opening, grounding
- Reference: prehistoric cave figures, Egyptian profiles, Greek black-figure pottery

### 4. Elemental Color Language

| Element | Primary | Accent | Feeling |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Fire | Warm amber/gold | White-hot core | Radiant, rising |
| Water | Deep blue-violet | Silver reflections | Depths, stillness |
| Earth | Rich ochre/umber | Forest green | Grounded, fertile |
| Air | Soft sky blue | White/silver | Expansive, clear |
| Aether | Indigo to white | Iridescent | Infinite, unified |
| Neutral | Warm charcoal | Soft cream | Contemplative |

### 5. Composition Rules
- **Center-weighted** for threshold art (portal feeling)
- **Asymmetric balance** for vignettes (movement feeling)
- Generous negative space (40-60% of frame)
- No cluttered backgrounds

---

## What TO Include

- Spirals, toroids, concentric circles
- Natural elements in abstract form (flame wisps, water ripples, root patterns, wind currents)
- Human silhouettes in contemplative postures
- Soft particle effects (embers, droplets, dust motes, light rays)
- Subtle sacred geometry (flower of life, vesica piscis) as background texture
- Horizon lines suggesting dawn/dusk

## What NOT to Include

- Specific cultural/religious iconography (no crosses, om symbols, mandalas with specific tradition)
- Literal faces or recognizable individuals
- Fantasy tropes (wizards, dragons, crystals, pentagrams)
- Busy patterns or maximalist decoration
- Harsh shadows or dramatic contrast
- Text or typography in images
- Photorealistic humans

---

## Format Specifications

### Threshold Art (Chapter Covers)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape) or 1:1 (square)
- Resolution: 2048x2048 minimum
- Use: Chapter header, shareable card, reader UI
- Feeling: Portal, invitation, elemental essence

### Vignettes (Inline Illustrations)
- Aspect ratio: 3:2 or 4:3 (landscape preferred)
- Resolution: 1024x768 minimum
- Use: Between segments, reflection prompts
- Feeling: Moment, symbol, contemplation anchor

### Elemental Icons (UI Elements)
- Aspect ratio: 1:1
- Resolution: 512x512
- Use: Badges, ritual mode indicators, navigation
- Feeling: Clean, recognizable, scalable

## Reference Touchstones

**Ancient Sources:**
- Alchemical manuscript illuminations (15th-17th century)
- Lascaux and Chauvet cave paintings
- Egyptian Book of the Dead vignettes
- Byzantine icon luminosity

**Modern Artists:**
- Hilma af Klint (sacred abstraction, primary reference)
- Olafur Eliasson (light + geometry)
- James Turrell (luminous space)
- Agnes Martin (meditative minimalism)

**Design Language:**
- Apple's spatial computing aesthetic (for clean execution)
- Monument Valley game art (geometric mysticism)

**Feeling:**
- Ancient manuscript discovered in an archive
- The moment before sunrise
- The space between breaths
- Recognition without words — like remembering something you never learned
