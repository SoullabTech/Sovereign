GOVERNING FINDING FROM DISCOVER

  The unfinished Studio is not thirteen grey buttons.
  It is disagreement about what the Studio already is,
  what MAIA is allowed to become,
  and which proposed rooms deserve to exist at all.

  "Unbuilt Studio" is not one problem. Some things are already built
  but misdeclared. Some are constitutionally premature. Some should
  disappear.

PURPOSE
Discover, constitute, sequence, and eventually build the unfinished
functional territory of Writer's Studio.

This is NOT:
- onboarding
- contextual help
- Canvas appearance
- a rail-cleanup exercise
- permission to turn every destination marked "later" into a feature

The rail already exposes parts of the Studio that are not yet available.
FR-C established that intentional incompleteness may be visible when its
state is stated honestly.

This lane asks the next question:

  What should these destinations actually become?

1 · WHY THIS IS A SEPARATE LANE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

These destinations are not one class of feature.

They divide by DIRECTION OF AUTHORITY.

PACKET A · MEMBER-AUTHORED

  Notes
  Goals

  plus any unfinished destination discovered by census whose primary
  object is authored, declared, organized, or owned by the writer.

Authority direction:

  MEMBER → SYSTEM

The member originates the substantive object.
The system may store, organize, retrieve, and assist with it.
MAIA does not acquire authorship merely by helping.


PACKET B · MAIA-DERIVED

  Discover
  Insights
  Suggestions

Authority direction:

  MAIA → MEMBER

These are potentially generated, inferred, proposed, noticed,
or synthesized by MAIA.

They require explicit standing rules.

A generated observation must not silently become:
- member truth
- manuscript fact
- durable memory
- accepted advice
- project state
- authorial intention

No Packet B capability may be built until its authority,
standing, provenance, durability, and adoption semantics are ruled.


PACKET C · MECHANICAL INSTRUMENTS

  Find / Replace
  Statistics
  Timeline
  Word Web

Authority direction:

  WORK → INSTRUMENT → MEMBER

These tools inspect, calculate, navigate, visualize, or transform
according to explicit member action.

They must not acquire interpretive or authorial standing merely
because they operate on the manuscript.


IMPORTANT

Navigation geography does NOT establish authority class.

Items appearing beside one another in a rail may belong to completely
different constitutional relationships.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2 · STRUCTURAL RULE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE THREE PACKETS MAY NOT BE AUTHORIZED AS ONE BUILD.

A ruling in one packet grants no authority to another.

Specifically:

  A member-authored object may not be used as precedent
  for a MAIA-derived object.

  A mechanical transformation may not be used as precedent
  for interpretive authority.

  A MAIA suggestion may not inherit durability because Notes are durable.

  Shared rail placement is not shared ontology.


A finding may span packets.
A remedy may not.

If one product problem genuinely affects two authority classes,
record the finding in both and adjudicate the remedies separately.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5 · PACKET A — MEMBER-AUTHORED OBJECTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For Notes, Goals, and any other Packet A candidate, answer before build:

  What exactly does the member create?

  Is it:
    - attached to a Work?
    - attached to a section?
    - global to the writer?
    - ephemeral?
    - durable?

  Who may edit it?

  Can MAIA propose content into it?

  If MAIA proposes something, does it remain a proposal until the member
  explicitly adopts it?

  Does deleting the Work delete the object?

  Does it enter versions?

  Does it enter export?

  Does it enter memory?

  What provenance must survive?

DEFAULT AUTHORITY

  Member-authored means MEMBER OWNS THE SUBSTANTIVE CONTENT.

MAIA assistance must not erase the distinction between:
  authored
  proposed
  adopted
  transformed


STOP FOR FOUNDER RULING before implementation.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6 · PACKET B — MAIA-DERIVED OBJECTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This packet receives the highest scrutiny.

For Discover, Insights, Suggestions, establish separately:

  WHAT IS IT?

  What act produces it?

  Is it:
    observation
    possibility
    question
    suggestion
    synthesis
    interpretation
    recommendation
    something else?

  WHAT STANDING DOES IT HAVE?

  Does it exist only in-turn?
  Does it persist?
  If persisted, why?
  Who may promote it?
  What constitutes adoption?

  WHAT PROVENANCE DOES IT CARRY?

  MAIA-derived material must remain distinguishable from:
    member-authored material
    manuscript fact
    accepted project state

  WHAT DOES IT NEVER BECOME AUTOMATICALLY?

  No derived object may silently become:
    truth
    memory
    intention
    decision
    manuscript content
    psychological fact

  WHAT IS THE DIRECTION OF AUTHORITY?

  MAIA may offer.
  The member decides what obtains standing.

If any proposed capability requires new interpretive,
memory, or authorial power:

  STOP.

That is not implementation.
That is constitutional inquiry.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7 · PACKET C — MECHANICAL TOOLS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For Find / Replace, Statistics, Timeline, Word Web:

Determine whether each is genuinely mechanical.

Ask:

  What inputs does it inspect?
  What output does it produce?
  Does it alter the manuscript?
  Is alteration previewable?
  Is it reversible?
  Does the member explicitly invoke the act?
  Does it infer meaning or merely compute structure?
  Does it persist derived state?

Mechanical tools must remain instruments.

Examples:

  Find
    locates text

  Replace
    changes text only under explicit member authority

  Statistics
    computes measurable properties

  Timeline
    may visualize chronology only if chronology is actually represented

  Word Web
    must be examined carefully:
    if it begins inferring concepts, themes, psychological meaning,
    or semantic importance, it may no longer belong in Packet C

Reclassify rather than stretch the category.


STOP FOR FOUNDER RULING before implementation.

10 · PRODUCT QUALITY STANDARD
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The purpose is not feature count.

Writer's Studio should increasingly feel like:

  a coherent place where a writer can live with a Work,
  understand it,
  shape it,
  develop it,
  and bring it toward completion

without surrendering authorship to the system.

Every capability must answer:

  Why does this belong in a Writer's Studio?

not merely:

  Can we build it?

Acceptance asks:

  Is the capability reachable?
  Is its purpose intelligible?
  Is its authority correctly bounded?
  Does it preserve authorship?
  Does it belong naturally in the Studio?
  Does it improve the writer's relationship with the Work?

Passing tests without those answers is not product acceptance.
