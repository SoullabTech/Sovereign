Soullab operational SMS alerts: recipient consent form

This is the blank form Soullab uses to record consent before a mobile number is added to its operational alerts.

Soullab sends short operational text messages to its owner and a small number of staff: alerts when the platform's email or uptime fails, a notice when a member submits in-app feedback (it includes the member's first name and a short preview of what they wrote), and content-free safety alerts to a designated person.

Message frequency varies with events on the platform. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Soullab does not share mobile numbers with third parties for marketing. These messages are never marketing and are not sent to the general public. See the privacy policy and terms.
