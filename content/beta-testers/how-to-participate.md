---
title: How to Participate
---

## 1. Bring Real Questions

The most valuable interactions begin with real questions.

- A decision you are facing
- A relationship challenge
- A recurring pattern in your life
- A moment of uncertainty
- A personal or professional transition
- Something you cannot quite make sense of

*Less useful: testing random prompts, trying to trick the system, asking what features exist.*

The quality of the question matters more than the number of interactions.

## 2. Pay Attention to What You Notice

After an interaction, pause for a moment and ask:

- What stood out?
- What surprised me?
- What felt relevant?
- What felt incomplete?
- What felt inaccurate?
- What did MAIA miss entirely?

There are no right answers. Observation is more valuable than evaluation.

## 3. Return Later

Some insights only become clear with time. When you revisit a conversation, ask:

- What do I see now that I didn’t see then?
- What became clearer?
- What turned out to be important?
- What no longer feels relevant?
- What was missing?

Your later observations are often more valuable than your immediate reactions.
