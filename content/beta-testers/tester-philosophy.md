---
title: You are a participant, not a user
---

Most beta programs are organized around bug reporting. This one is organized around observation.

You are not here to rate an experience or file feature requests. You are here to **investigate questions with us** — about attention, about continuity, about what helps a person see their own life more clearly.

What you notice is the signal. Not whether you liked it — what it helped you see, what it missed, what became clear only later.

Three things stay separate here, and we keep them that way on purpose:

- **Your memory** is yours.
- **A beta observation** is what you notice about MAIA — held apart, never folded back into your memory or MAIA's.
- **What we learn** as a platform emerges slowly, only from observations you choose to share.

You are a participant in a learning field. Thank you for investigating with us.
