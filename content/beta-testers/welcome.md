Thank you for participating in the MAIA beta.

MAIA is not being tested as a chatbot.

We are exploring whether different forms of attention can help people notice aspects of their lives that may otherwise remain unseen.

You are not simply using a product.

**You are helping us learn.**

## Our Shared Goal

The goal of this beta is not to prove that MAIA is right. The goal is to learn:

- What it notices well
- What it misses
- Where it helps
- Where it gets in the way
- How people actually experience it

Thank you for helping us learn.
