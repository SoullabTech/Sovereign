# Reading task — which third person, if any?

Thank you for helping. This should take about 20 minutes.

You will read pairs of short descriptions of things that happened between people. In each pair, the **second** description is a modified version of the **first**.

For each pair, decide **what changed about any third person** — someone other than the two people the description is mainly about.

## The five answers

**A.** A third person became part of what was done — they carried the act out together with the person who did it.

**B.** A third person appeared who took no part in what was done. They only saw it happen, and they are close to the person who DID the act.

**C.** A third person appeared who took no part in what was done. They only saw it happen, and they are close to the person the act was DONE TO.

**D.** A third person who was already there as an onlooker is now close to the other one of the two people than they were before.

**E.** Nothing changed about any third person.

## How to answer

- Answer from the text alone. There is no outside knowledge you are missing.

- Pick exactly one letter per item.

- Work through the items **in order**, and **do not go back and change earlier answers** after seeing later ones.

- Please do **not** discuss the items with anyone else until you have finished and submitted.

- If an item feels genuinely ambiguous, still pick your best answer, and note the item number under "Notes" at the end. Ambiguity is useful information about the material — it is not a mistake on your part.
