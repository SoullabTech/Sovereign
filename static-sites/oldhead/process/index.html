High-end clients don't want more options — they want confidence that the finish will be right, the process will be clean, and nothing will surprise them.

01

Discovery

Understanding Light, Architecture & Intent

Every project begins with a conversation — either on-site or at our Material Library. We walk through your space, noting light conditions, substrate realities, and your vision.

  • 45–90 minute consultation
  • Lighting and substrate assessment
  • Style and finish preferences
  • Timeline and scope alignment
Deliverable: Project Direction Brief
02

Concept

From Inspiration to Direction

We distill your inspiration into 2–3 clear directions. No Pinterest chaos — just curated concepts with reference images, finish recommendations, and palette suggestions.

  • 2–3 curated design directions
  • Reference imagery and mood
  • Recommended finishes and sheens
  • Suggested color palette
Deliverable: Concept Board
03

Sampling

The Luxury of Certainty

This is where we separate from the rest. Custom sample boards — each named, documented, and QR-coded — tested in your actual space, under your actual light.

  • Full-scale sample boards
  • QR codes linking to specifications
  • Multiple lighting condition photos
  • Recommended pairings and applications
Deliverable: Sample Kit & QR Library
04

Specification

Locking the Decision

Your approved finish is documented with precision: color codes, sheen levels, square footage, substrate prep requirements. No ambiguity. No surprises.

  • Final finish documentation
  • Room-by-room specifications
  • Substrate preparation plan
  • Protection and ventilation notes
Deliverable: Finish Specification Sheet
05

Application

The Craft

Impeccable execution with a calm client experience. Daily progress updates, photo documentation, and the patience each coat demands. We never rush.

  • Site prep and substrate readiness
  • Daily progress rhythm
  • Photo updates for off-site clients
  • Detail finishing and edge perfection
Deliverable: Progress Documentation
06

Reveal

The Unveiling

Final walkthrough in correct lighting. Touch-ups if needed. Care instructions for long-term beauty. Your finish is presented like the work of art it is.

  • Final inspection walkthrough
  • Touch-ups and refinements
  • Care and maintenance guide
  • Warranty documentation
Deliverable: Care Card & Guarantee
