Venetian Plaster

Guilford Estate

Featuring Louisburgh Silk — Venetian Satin

Project Details

Location
Guilford, CT
Scope
5,600 sq ft
Type
Private Residence
Duration
5 weeks
Architect

01

The Challenge

A new-construction estate required decorative plaster throughout the principal rooms. The architectural vision called for understated luxury — walls that would read as expensive without competing with the furnishings.

02

The Approach

We specified Louisburgh Silk — our satin-finish Venetian — in a custom warm neutral developed through six sample iterations. The soft glow provides depth without the mirror-like reflection of polished Venetian.

Technical Notes

Rooms:
Entry, living room, dining room, library, primary suite, three guest rooms
Substrate:
New construction, level 5 drywall
03

The Result

The plaster provides a consistent, refined backdrop throughout the home. The architects selected this project for their portfolio, citing the finish quality as instrumental to the overall effect.
