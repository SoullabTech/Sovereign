The Difference

More Than a Showroom

Full-Scale Boards

Every sample is a minimum 12"×12" — large enough to evaluate texture, depth, and how light plays across the surface. No tiny swatches.

QR-Coded Specifications

Each sample board is tagged with a QR code linking to complete specifications: material name, application method, sheen level, and substrate requirements.

Controlled Lighting

View samples under warm, cool, and natural lighting conditions. See exactly how a finish will behave in different environments.

Custom Sample Creation

Can't find exactly what you need? We create custom sample boards for your specific project — available within 5 business days.
