Heritage

The Name

Old Head is a promontory near Louisburgh in County Mayo, Ireland — a rocky headland where the Atlantic meets ancient stone. My family has lived in its shadow for generations.

The plasterers there learned their craft in conditions that demanded durability: wet winters, salt air, structures built to outlast centuries. Nothing survived that was built to fail.

I brought those standards to Connecticut. The climate is different, but the principles are the same: proper materials, proper technique, proper time. No shortcuts, no compromises.
