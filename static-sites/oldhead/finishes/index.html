The Sampling Promise

"You don't choose plaster
from a thumbnail."

That's why we create large-format sample boards — 12" x 12" minimum — and show them in controlled lighting conditions: morning, midday, and evening.

Each sample has a QR code linking to its specification page: photos in multiple lighting conditions, application video, substrate requirements, and care notes.

You see exactly what you're getting. No surprises, no guesswork.
