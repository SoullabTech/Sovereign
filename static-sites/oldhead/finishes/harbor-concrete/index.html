For clients who want the industrial poetry of concrete without the weight or cold. Harbor Concrete is a polymer-modified cementitious coating that can be applied over almost any substrate — creating seamless floors, walls, countertops, and wet areas.

Technical Specifications

Composition

Portland cement, polymers, fine aggregates, pigments

Texture

Smooth, uniform, industrial

Thickness

2-4mm

Curing Time

7 days before use, 28 days full cure

Available Colorways

Harbor (Original)

Medium gray with concrete character

Slate

Dark charcoal, architectural

Bone

Warm off-white

Custom

Any RAL color available

Best For

  • Kitchens
  • Bathrooms
  • Floors
  • Showers
  • Feature walls
  • Commercial spaces

Not Recommended

  • Exterior (without specific formulation)
  • Historic restoration

Pairs Beautifully With

  • Wood
  • Black steel
  • White fixtures
  • Minimalist design

Care & Maintenance

  • Sealed surface — wipe clean with mild soap and water
  • Reseal every 2-3 years in wet areas
  • Avoid abrasive cleaners
  • Highly durable — designed for daily use
