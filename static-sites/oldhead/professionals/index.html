The Challenge

Decorative Plaster Is Hard to Specify

You've seen the Pinterest boards. Your client has a vision. But translating that vision into a reliable specification — and finding someone who can execute it — is where projects go wrong.

Paint contractors who "also do Venetian" deliver inconsistent results. High-end importers send a crew you've never met. Lead times stretch. Samples don't match final work.

We solve this by treating every project like a specification exercise — documented, sampled, and agreed upon before any plaster touches the wall.
