The Crown Jewel of Decorative Finishes

Venetian Plaster

Multiple layers of lime and marble dust, applied by hand and burnished to a subtle, luminous depth that catches light in ways no paint ever could. This is the finish that made Italian palazzos famous — and it demands the same patience today.

Technical Expertise

  • Traditional lime-based formulations with natural marble dust
  • Multi-coat application with proper cure times between layers
  • Hand burnishing for controlled sheen and depth
  • Custom color development and on-site matching
  • Restoration of existing Venetian plaster surfaces

Best Applications

Feature walls, dining rooms, foyers, and any space where light quality matters.

We maintain relationships with Italian material suppliers for projects requiring authentic imported plasters.

Ancient Technique, Living Walls

Lime Wash

A breathable, mineral finish with soft, cloudy variations that shift with the light. Lime washing creates walls that feel alive — naturally antibacterial, humidity-regulating, and developing character as they age.

Technical Expertise

  • Pure lime and natural mineral pigments
  • Proper substrate preparation for adhesion and longevity
  • Controlled application for even color distribution
  • Multi-coat techniques for depth and saturation
  • Touch-up protocols that blend seamlessly

Best Applications

Beach houses, Mediterranean-style architecture, bedrooms, and anywhere organic warmth is desired.

Lime wash is forgiving to live with — touch-ups blend naturally, and the finish improves with age.

Seamless Contemporary Surfaces

Microcement

A thin, polymer-modified cement coating that creates monolithic, seamless surfaces. Applied in layers just millimeters thick, microcement transforms walls, floors, and wet areas into sculptural statements without the demolition of traditional concrete work.

Technical Expertise

  • Waterproof systems for showers and wet areas
  • Overlay applications on existing tile and surfaces
  • Floor-to-wall transitions without seams
  • Precise color matching and custom tinting
  • Sealer selection for specific use conditions

Best Applications

Modern kitchens, bathrooms, showers, floors, and minimalist spaces seeking clean, continuous surfaces.

We use commercial-grade systems rated for heavy traffic and daily wet exposure.

Preserving Original Craftsmanship

Historic Plaster Restoration

Restoring historic plasterwork requires more than skill — it requires understanding of period materials and techniques. We repair, stabilize, and restore original plaster using lime-based materials that are compatible with historic construction.

Technical Expertise

  • Condition assessment and documentation
  • Stabilization of delaminating and failing plaster
  • Matching of original textures, profiles, and finishes
  • Traditional lime-based repair materials
  • Integration with existing ornamental schemes

Best Applications

Pre-war homes, historic properties, landmark buildings, and renovation projects preserving original character.

EPA Lead-Safe Certified for work in pre-1978 structures. We work regularly with historic commissions.

Classical Detail, Period Accuracy

Ornamental Mouldings

Crown mouldings, ceiling medallions, corbels, and decorative elements — either restored to their original condition or created new using traditional running and casting techniques. This is plasterwork as sculpture.

Technical Expertise

  • Traditional running mould technique for linear elements
  • Custom profile creation matched to existing architecture
  • Casting and installation of decorative elements
  • Repair and replication of damaged ornament
  • Hand-finished details and final fitting

Best Applications

Historic homes, classical architecture, ceiling restorations, and projects requiring period-appropriate detail.

We can match existing profiles or work from architectural drawings to create new classical details.
