# Welcome to Your MAIA Spiral Archive

This vault contains your MAIA journey data exported from Soullab.

## How to Use

### 1) Open the Spiral Map
Open the `Spiralogic Map.canvas` file to see your spiral journey visualized.

### 2) Explore Your Data
Use the Bases views to analyze your patterns:
- **Journal Base**: View your daily reflections
- **Checkins Base**: Track your elemental states
- **Reflections Base**: See your emerging insights

### 3) Ask MAIA
Continue your journey in Soullab while maintaining this personal archive.

## About This Data

All data is exported from your MAIA experience and stored locally in this vault. No data leaves your device without your explicit consent.
