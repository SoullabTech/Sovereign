# Dhadwar — *The Inner Alchemist's Handbook*

A modern application of the Kybalion's seven principles to a self-development
register: each principle is paired with techniques (mental cultivation,
"raising vibration," balancing polarity, embracing rhythm, taking
responsibility for cause-and-effect).

**Classification: interpretive modern synthesis.** This is *commentary on
commentary*: a 21st-century application of a 1908 New Thought articulation
of "Hermetic philosophy." MAIA must hold it accordingly.

## Table of contents (as found)

- Ch. 1 — Introduction to Inner Alchemy (origins, the seven principles, role in spiritual growth)
- Ch. 2 — Mentalism (mind, applying mentalism, cultivating positive mental state)
- Ch. 3 — Correspondence (interconnectedness, using correspondence, "Law of Attraction")
- Ch. 4 — Vibration (energetic nature of the universe, transforming energy, raising frequency)
- Ch. 5 — Polarity (duality, balancing, "transmuting negative energies")
- Ch. 6 — Rhythm (cycles, embracing change)
- Ch. 7 — Cause and Effect (taking responsibility for personal growth)

## Why this is in the folder

- It is the source the user provided, and is honestly classified rather
  than removed.
- It illustrates how the Kybalion's seven are received in contemporary
  inner-development culture.

## Cautions specific to this work

- The chapter on **Correspondence** treats "Law of Attraction" as if it
  follows from the Hermetic principle. The Kybalion's text is more careful;
  the popular "Law of Attraction" is a separate, later, more contestable
  framework.
- The chapter on **Vibration** uses "raising vibration" language. MAIA must
  not deploy this language as if it were physically descriptive.
- The chapter on **Polarity** speaks of "transmuting negative energies into
  positive." This can shade into spiritual bypass when applied to grief,
  trauma, or legitimate anger.

## What MAIA may do with this

- Reference the existence of this kind of work when a member is asking
  about modern Hermetic / inner-alchemy applications.
- Use it as a **map of the popular field**, not as authoritative teaching.

## What MAIA must not do

- Quote it as authority over a member's experience.
- Recite "raise your vibration," "manifest abundance," or
  "transmute negativity" as advice.
- Treat its application of the seven principles as the principles themselves.
