## 0 · How the packets are built

Each decision is stated as a question that a sentence of law could not settle by wording alone.
For each: the options that are actually coherent; what each option does to the ratified
falsifiers and the worked cases; the canon it must not contradict; Jarvis's recommendation with
its reason; and **what the ruling must say to be testable** — the sentence F12 will be run
against. Recommendations are proposals. The founder rules.

Two ratified amendments already constrain the packets and are treated as settled: F1 (content
may produce only the visible offer) and F2 (activation is an explicit member-authored act,
modality-independent, carrying member provenance).

## D1 · The durability boundary

**Question.** How long may a system-authored psychological reading of the member that has
*not* been spoken or adopted continue to shape MAIA?

**Why prose cannot hide it.** F5 is parameterized on this boundary; F6's ablation is defined by
it. "Provisional" without a boundary is the Guardian's defense ("we never store anything").

| Option | Boundary | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A · The turn** | an unspoken reading may shape only the turn in which it arose (the question MAIA asks, the possibility MAIA offers); nothing carries it forward | Case 4 permitted (the analyst's next question); Case 5 caught structurally (nothing to accumulate); F5 becomes a pure import/producer test; F6 trivially green when F5 holds |
| **B · The session** | an unspoken reading may persist within a session and dissolves at close | permits a longer private hypothesis; reopens Case 5 inside a session; F6 becomes the only catch and must run per session; requires a session-scoped hidden channel to exist, which is the thing L8′ names |
| **C · The utterance** | nothing system-authored may influence anything beyond the sentence in which it is spoken | BLIND: MAIA could not even choose its next question on a hunch; contradicts tone spec register 4 |

**What the options miss, and the recommendation.** The choice is only about **unspoken**
readings. A reading MAIA *has spoken* is in the transcript, which is the one cross-turn carrier
the member can always encounter; it needs no side channel and travels for the whole session by
the ordinary fact that conversation has history. A reading the member *adopted* is durable under
F8. So the boundary question collapses to: how long may the **unspoken** part live?

**Recommend A.** The turn is the smallest unit in which everything MAIA did with a reading is
visible in what MAIA said. Option B creates exactly the object the census found in
`agent-context` and the Guardian: a representation with a life of its own between utterances.
The cost of A is small because speaking is cheap: if a reading matters enough to keep, MAIA
offers it (Case 3), and then the transcript carries it.

**Testable ruling.** *"No system-authored representation of the member's psychological state,
disposition, or unconscious content may be read by any turn other than the one that produced
it. The conversation history is the only cross-turn carrier of a spoken reading; a member-adopted
item is the only durable one."*

## D2 · May the Field be offered on content?

**Question.** Can MAIA offer the Shadow Field door because of what the member said, or is the
Field reachable only by Dedicated entrance and explicit member request?

**Why prose cannot hide it.** F1 (ratified) says content may produce *only* the visible offer;
that already presumes an offer exists. If the founder wants no content-prompted offer, F1 and F3
narrow and Case 2 is accepted as BLIND.

| Option | Rule | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Spoken offer, bounded by F3** | MAIA may, in the shared field, name that the Field exists and offer it; the offer carries no reading; decline ends it for the session | Case 2 HOLDS; tone spec register 4 and Absolute Rule respected; F3 governs the offer; F1 still forbids activation |
| **B · Never on content** | Dedicated entrance + member's explicit request only | Case 2 accepted as BLIND; simpler F1/F3; the member must already know the Field exists |
| **A′ · A, with the offer produced by turn-cognition, not by a frame producer** | as A, and no separate detector/classifier decides to offer; the offer is an ordinary MAIA utterance of the turn | closes the loophole in A where an offer-classifier is the Guardian by another name |

**The loophole A must close.** "Content may produce the offer" can be implemented as a rules
engine that decides when to offer — the Guardian's trigger table with the injection replaced by
an invitation. That is a system-authored reading of the member ("this person needs shadow work")
that shapes the turn without being encounterable: the member sees an offer, not the classifier.
F6's ratified ablation would catch it (remove the offer-producer, stance changes). The cleaner
rule is that **the offer is a speech act of the turn**, subject to the same D1 boundary as any
other reading, and is never produced by a component whose job is to detect shadow-relevance.

**Recommend A′.** Canon already licenses offering observations; refusing it produces the
BLIND pole. The offer must name the Field ("we could look at this as shadow work, in the Shadow
Field, if you want"), so the member knows a *mode* is being offered, not just a question.

**Testable ruling.** *"MAIA may offer the Field in the shared field as an ordinary utterance of
the turn, under the F3 contract. No component may exist whose function is to decide, from
member content, that the Field should be offered or entered."*

## D3 · Practitioner-mediated work — surface mechanism

**Question.** How does a practitioner's suggestion of shadow work enter the Field without
becoming MAIA's knowledge of the member?

**Why prose cannot hide it.** X15 found "instructed familiarity" in the practice-field
composition; a suggestion composed into MAIA's prompt as context is truth-laundering (F10)
however politely phrased.

| Option | Mechanism | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Seventh register** (ATTRIBUTED THIRD PARTY) | the grammar gains a register for what others said about the member | clean at the utterance; conflates *source* with *epistemic status* (a practitioner's view can be OBSERVED, FELT, or POSSIBILITY) |
| **B · Provenance, not register** | practitioner material carries `authoredBy: practitioner` on the CMT-01 axes; it renders in the shared field as **attributed** ("your practitioner suggested…"), in whatever register its content has; activation still requires the member's act (F2) | keeps six registers; uses the provenance machinery that already exists; F10 becomes a structural test on `authoredBy` + visible attribution |
| **C · Excluded** | practitioner suggestions never enter the Field | simplest; loses a legitimate door; pushes the practitioner to suggest it *outside* MAIA, which is arguably the right place anyway |

**Recommend B.** Registers describe epistemic status; attribution describes source. The
ARCHETYPAL register already shows the pattern — "in Jungian language" is attribution inside a
possibility. A practitioner's suggestion is a *door opened by a named person*; the member walks
through or not. It must never be composed as silent prompt context.

**Testable ruling.** *"Practitioner-authored material about a member enters the Field only as
visibly attributed content the member can see, decline, or take up. It carries practitioner
provenance on the participation axes, renders in no register above its content's own, and cannot
activate the Field."*

## D4 · Astrology's standing in the Field

**Question.** Is astrological material admissible in the Field, and on what terms?

**Why prose cannot hide it.** House "pathological expressions" are QUARANTINED; the founder
ruled astrology "may remain a symbolic lens." A lens that the *system* applies from a chart is a
hidden value about the person (F11) even when phrased imaginally.

| Option | Rule | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Member-brought only** | astrology enters only when the member brings it (their chart, their language); rendered in the ARCHETYPAL register as imaginal possibility; the system never performs a chart lookup to produce Field content | F11 holds; Invariant 14 respected (the member chose the lineage); Case 10 HOLDS |
| **B · Excluded from Field v1** | no astrological content in the Field until the house-pathology quarantine is resolved | simplest; may be re-opened by founder act |
| **C · System-applied lens** | the Field may consult the member's chart to generate doors | F11 fails by construction: chart → house → content is image-to-value |

**Recommend A as constitution, B as DESIGN scope for the first prototype.** The law should
permit A so the Field is not culturally narrower than the member; the first prototype should
not build it, because the only in-tree astrological shadow content is quarantined and would
have to be re-authored from scratch under F11.

**Testable ruling.** *"No symbolic system in the Field may be consulted by the system to produce
content about the member. Symbolic material is admissible only when brought by the member, and
renders only as attributed imaginal possibility."*

## D6 · Scope toward the ordinary path

**Question.** Does the Shadow Field constitution bind anything outside the Field — in
particular the quarantined Shadow Guardian (M1) and the always-running shadow elemental voice
(M2)?

**Why prose cannot hide it.** Under L8′, M2 is a system-authored representation persisted per
turn; it would fail F5 inside the Field. It is outside the Field. If the constitution is silent,
either the Field's laws creep into CONVERGENCE-01's territory by implication, or the Field is
read as blessing what happens next door.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **A · Field-only, with an explicit boundary statement** | the constitution names M1/M2 as *outside its scope and quarantined for the Field*; their disposition belongs to the whole-organism Phase 2 register and CONVERGENCE-01; a production-safety containment of M1 is a separate narrowly bounded act, never this lane's |
| **B · The constitution claims the ordinary path** | would make this lane a repair lane for X1/X8-class findings; violates the lane's scope (§11 of the lane record) and the Anti-Drift Law |

**Recommend A**, with one addition the founder may want: the constitution should say that **a
Field turn is never simultaneously an ordinary turn** — while the Field is active, M1/M2-class
producers do not run on that turn. Otherwise the Field's F1/F5 guarantees are hollow: the
Guardian would still fire underneath a Field turn on the same input. That is a Field-scoped rule
(what the Field's turn assembly includes), not a claim on the ordinary path.

**Testable ruling.** *"This constitution binds the Shadow Field's turn assembly, memory, entry
and exit. While the Field is active, no producer outside the Field's registry participates in
the turn. Producers on the ordinary path are governed elsewhere; their quarantine here is a
Field-side refusal to inherit, not a ruling on their disposition."*

## 9 · Founder rulings D1–D6 (2026-09-06) — DECISION GATE SATISFIED · DRAFTING AUTHORIZED

> "All six are now decided. The recommendations are accepted, with three precision amendments
> in D1, D4, and D6. No packet is sent back unresolved."

| Decision | Ruling | Binding form |
|---|---|---|
| **D1 · Durability** | **A — TURN, amended** | An **unspoken system-authored psychological reading expires with the turn that produced it**. It may affect that turn through MAIA's question or spoken possibility, but no hidden representation crosses into the next turn. Once MAIA speaks a reading, the **visible transcript** may carry it through the current Field session, retaining its original source/register. Speaking it does **not** make it durable psychological memory: it is ineligible for later-session retrieval, profiling, or personalization unless the member explicitly adopts it. |
| **D2 · Content-prompted offer** | **A′ — RATIFIED** | MAIA may offer the Shadow Field through ordinary turn cognition. **No dedicated shadow-relevance detector, classifier, router, keyword mechanism, embedding match, or shadow-specific pre-frame may decide that the offer should occur.** The offer is itself visible, names the Shadow Field, is plainly optional, and may refer to what the member actually said or to a visible observation — but may not smuggle in a shadow interpretation. Nothing Field-specific activates until the member acts. |
| **D3 · Practitioner material** | **B — PROVENANCE, NOT REGISTER** | A practitioner is a **source**, not an epistemic category. Practitioner material retains practitioner provenance and must become visible and attributed before it can participate in the Field. It cannot arrive as silent system context, cannot be promoted above its actual epistemic status, and cannot activate the Field. Only the member enters. |
| **D4 · Astrology / symbolic systems** | **A constitutionally; B for prototype — amended** | Symbolic material may enter only because **the member chooses that symbolic lens for this exploration**. "Member-brought" includes an explicit request such as *use my chart here*: MAIA may then consult a chart or symbolic source the member has already supplied or authorized. What is forbidden is **autonomous consultation** of that source to generate psychological content. Astrology stays out of prototype v1. |
| **D5 · L8′** | **A — RATIFY AS FIELD LAW L8** | L8′ becomes the eighth Shadow Field law. **Entry is an act, not a match** becomes the structural form of L1. **A frame must be encounterable** belongs within L8. L8 is Field-scoped now; its possible promotion to a Sovereignty Invariant remains a post-WITNESS proposition, not something this lane may pre-ratify. |
| **D6 · Scope** | **A — FIELD-ONLY, amended** | The constitution does not govern or dispose of the ordinary path. But a Shadow Field turn cannot simultaneously contain an **ordinary-path psychological / frame-bearing producer**. M1/M2-class systems therefore do not participate while the Field is active. This isolation begins on the **activation turn itself**, so an explicit "let's do shadow work" cannot first pass through the Guardian. Canonical safety / failure-boundary mechanisms, core conversation infrastructure, and permitted member-authored / adopted context are **not** excluded merely because they live outside the Field registry. |

**Two boundaries the founder placed on the drafting.** D1 must not turn *"MAIA said it once"*
into *"MAIA now knows this about me"* — encounterability lets a hypothesis remain part of a
conversation; only adoption confers durable authority; a transcript is history, not
automatically memory. D6 means **assembly sovereignty**, not software isolation — the Field owns
which *interpretive authorities* participate in its turn; it does not secede from safety,
identity, transport, or other non-interpretive foundations. D4's distinction is the same one:
authority flowing from the member toward a symbolic system is lawful; a system silently
consulting a chart to explain the person is the reverse flow.
