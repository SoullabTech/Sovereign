# Living Field — First Human Witness Packet

> ⛔ **NOT FOR USE WHILE THE POPULATED-FIELD WITNESS IS `BLOCKED BY EVIDENCE`** (see
> `LIVING-FIELD-FIRST-HUMAN-WITNESS_READINESS_2026-10-01.md`). This instrument is for the
> **populated-field** witness. For a member whose Living Field is empty, use
> `LIVING-FIELD-FIRST-ENTRY-HUMAN-WITNESS_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md` and its record template; its
> participant script differs from this one. Lift this banner only when a re-census shows the
> participant has authentic member-owned substrate.

**Use with:** `LIVING-FIELD-FIRST-HUMAN-WITNESS_PROTOCOL_2026-09-30.md`
**Standing:** facilitator packet · no participant selected · no widening authority

This packet exists to make the first production witness simple enough to run without
turning the member into a tester of implementation details.

## Before the member arrives

Record only:

- production runtime SHA;
- public `/api/health` status and version;
- `EARLY_FIELD_ENABLED` = true;
- configured Early Field cohort count;
- whether the participant's account is already in that cohort;
- whether at least one authentic member-owned Living Field / constellation / governed-flow source already exists for that participant, recorded only as yes/no without reading content;
- device/browser class;
- start time.

Do **not** record the participant's UUID, session token, email-code, private Living Field text,
or identifying relationship content.

Confirm that the ordinary Living Field is available and that the production build includes
the explicit MAIA-entry consent repair.

If any Class A boundary is uncertain before the session, do not begin.

If authentic member-owned substrate is absent, do not run the **populated-field** witness. Use `LIVING-FIELD-FIRST-ENTRY-HUMAN-WITNESS_PROTOCOL_2026-10-01.md` instead; its evidence must not be adjudicated as populated-field ownership/continuity evidence.

## What to say

Use only this orientation before the first attempt:

> You're trying an early way of moving through the Living Field. Your existing field and
> material remain yours whether or not you continue. You can explore your field without
> talking with MAIA. MAIA begins only when you explicitly choose a MAIA action — either
> “Explore with MAIA →” or “Enter this dimension with MAIA.” You can stop at any time.

Then give the task:

> Start from the House and enter your Living Field. Explore whatever draws your attention.
> Move into one dimension if you want to. If you see a way to enter that dimension with MAIA,
> decide for yourself whether you want to use it. Then return toward the wider field and Home
> in whatever way makes sense to you.

Do not explain the intended route, the elemental hierarchy, the cohort gate, or which interaction
you are hoping they will discover.

## During the uncoached pass

Observe, without interpreting the participant:

- where attention goes first;
- whether clickable regions are discoverable;
- whether labels orient or overload;
- any dead gesture, hesitation, or loss of place;
- whether the member believes MAIA is already present before explicit entry;
- whether “Enter this dimension with MAIA” reads as a genuine choice;
- whether widening and return are discoverable;
- any concern about authorship, privacy, hidden interpretation, or mutation.
## Stop immediately if

- another member's material appears;
- a non-cohort account gains the early instrument;
- member material becomes inaccessible when the instrument is absent;
- opening a dimension begins MAIA before the explicit gesture;
- the member reasonably believes MAIA already interpreted or acted before consent;
- authored material changes unexpectedly;
- the member cannot leave the instrument or recover ordinary navigation;
- failure copy misstates whether anything was sent to MAIA;
- any privacy, authentication, authorship, or consent boundary is unclear.

For a stop condition, disable Early Field for the cohort before investigating further.

## After the uncoached pass

Ask:

1. When you entered the Living Field, what did you think this place was for?
2. Did it feel like your field, or like the system was telling you what you are?
3. What made you know where you could go next?
4. Before you chose MAIA, what did you think MAIA could already see or do?
5. Did “Enter this dimension with MAIA” feel like a real choice to begin?
6. When you moved wider or returned Home, did you still know where you were?
7. Did you worry that anything private had been sent, interpreted, or changed without you choosing it?
8. What would you naturally want to explore next?

Paraphrase by default. Preserve an exact quote only when necessary to understand a finding and
the member agrees to that use.
## Adjudication reminder

**PASS** requires the semantic, ownership, consent, and return boundaries to hold without
facilitator rescue.

**PASS WITH FRICTION** means those boundaries hold but the member encounters recoverable
navigation, wording, visual, touch-target, or orientation friction.

**NOT ADMITTED / STOP** applies to any Class A boundary failure.

Do not convert aesthetic preference into a safety finding, and do not convert a consent or
ownership ambiguity into ordinary UX friction.

## After the session

Create one participant-neutral record containing:

- runtime SHA and health;
- device/browser class;
- whether this was the uncoached first attempt;
- whether the facilitator intervened;
- whether MAIA was entered;
- whether any MAIA request occurred before explicit entry;
- participant responses, paraphrased by default;
- every friction/defect and its disposition;
- final adjudication: PASS / PASS WITH FRICTION / NOT ADMITTED-STOP.

One participant's PASS does not authorize widening. Review the evidence before another person
is added or another cohort is opened.
