⚠️ **Instrument limitation, attached per C-00's lesson:** this is a pattern-match scan. **A matched-definition

⭐ **The asymmetry is nonetheless stark and internal: the corpus's central words do enormous work and are almost
nowhere pinned.** That is the precise condition under which equivocation is invisible — a word can shift referent
between documents with nothing in the corpus registering that it moved.

# RC-02b — EXTERNAL DENOTATION CENSUS · ⛔ BLOCKED, AND BLOCKED HARDER THAN RC-01 WAS

⭐ **The governing rule and V-00 collide, and the collision must be stated rather than worked around.**
Establishing *what an author permits a word to denote* **cannot be done from a search-result summary at all.**
Denotation lives in an author's definitions, hedges, scope conditions and explicit refusals — precisely the
material summaries compress away. RC-01 could at least record *existence* at search level. **RC-02b cannot record
anything at search level without committing C-00's error at a level where it would be worse**: a misattributed
quote corrupts one card; **a misattributed denotation corrupts every comparison built on it.**

⛔ **Therefore RC-02b is not attempted in this pass.** No external denotation entries are written. Populating it
requires a network permitting primary-source retrieval, or a founder ruling on what evidentiary standard a
DISCOVER-stage denotation register may run at.
