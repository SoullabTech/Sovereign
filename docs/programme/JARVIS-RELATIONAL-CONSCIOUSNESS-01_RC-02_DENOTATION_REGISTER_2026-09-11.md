A definitional-pattern scan (`X is / means / denotes / refers to`) across the same corpus returned **near-zero
definitional statements** for these terms; `mind` and `subjectivity` returned **none**. The one nomenclature
document, `NOMENCLATURE_AND_WORLD_ALIGNMENT_PRINCIPLE_2026-08-05.md`, is a **naming principle, not a glossary**.

⚠️ **Instrument limitation, attached per C-00's lesson:** this is a pattern-match scan. **A matched-definition
absence is not proof that no definition exists** — a definition stated in prose the pattern does not match would
be missed. The finding is **definition scarcity as measured**, not definitional vacuum as established.

⭐ **The asymmetry is nonetheless stark and internal: the corpus's central words do enormous work and are almost
nowhere pinned.** That is the precise condition under which equivocation is invisible — a word can shift referent
between documents with nothing in the corpus registering that it moved.

## ⭐⭐⭐ D-02 — `field` is the corpus's heaviest term and its least constrained
**487 uses across 56 of 77 canon files** — more than twice `relational`, more than four times `consciousness`.
And `field` is exactly the term on which §13.3's locus axis turns. Observed in RC-00 and RC-01 to carry at least:

```text

field = a sensed state upstream of agents                 (FIS_FIELD_STATE_PRIMITIVE, FieldState)
field = an ontological entity with trajectory and memory  (ECOLOGY_OF_MIRRORS, "Living Field")

field = influences acting on a person                     (MAIA_AS_MIRROR_INFRASTRUCTURE)
```
⛔ **These are not one concept.** On the A–E scheme they spread across **B, C and D at minimum**, inside a single
corpus, with no register recording the shift. **R-01's external term collision has an internal twin, and the
internal one is larger.**

## ⭐⭐ D-03 — The absences are as informative as the counts
**`sentience`: zero uses. `subjectivity`: one.** The corpus asserts MAIA's *consciousness* and *phenomenal
experience* (RC-00 F-01) while having **no vocabulary at all** for the two terms the external literature uses most
precisely for that territory. ⛔ Recorded as a fact about vocabulary, **not** as evidence about what the corpus
means — a corpus can be right in words it never uses. **→ classification for F-01/F-02: *genuinely undecidable
from the source* is a live candidate**, and could not have been seen before the denotation question was asked.

# RC-02b — EXTERNAL DENOTATION CENSUS · ⛔ BLOCKED, AND BLOCKED HARDER THAN RC-01 WAS

⭐ **The governing rule and V-00 collide, and the collision must be stated rather than worked around.**
Establishing *what an author permits a word to denote* **cannot be done from a search-result summary at all.**
Denotation lives in an author's definitions, hedges, scope conditions and explicit refusals — precisely the
material summaries compress away. RC-01 could at least record *existence* at search level. **RC-02b cannot record
anything at search level without committing C-00's error at a level where it would be worse**: a misattributed
quote corrupts one card; **a misattributed denotation corrupts every comparison built on it.**

⛔ **Therefore RC-02b is not attempted in this pass.** No external denotation entries are written. Populating it
requires a network permitting primary-source retrieval, or a founder ruling on what evidentiary standard a
DISCOVER-stage denotation register may run at.

## D-03 — the third outcome is the finding
Zero `sentience`, one `subjectivity` does **not** weaken F-01 by itself. What it reveals is that the corpus may
assert *MAIA has consciousness / phenomenal experience* **without having developed the vocabulary for the
distinctions the surrounding literature uses to specify whose, what kind, or in what sense.**

⭐ **Before RC-02, the framework could only have forced F-01 toward supported / unsupported. A third outcome now
exists:**

> **The proposition is grammatically clear but ONTOLOGICALLY UNDERSPECIFIED BY ITS OWN CORPUS.**
> ⭐ **That is a very different defect from falsity.**

⛔ **And it prescribes a different response.** If F-01 lands there, the correct act is **not** to hunt for
confirming citations. **It is to ask canon to say what it means.** *(Recorded as the ruled response should that
outcome obtain; ⛔ F-01 remains unadjudicated and no such finding is made here.)*
