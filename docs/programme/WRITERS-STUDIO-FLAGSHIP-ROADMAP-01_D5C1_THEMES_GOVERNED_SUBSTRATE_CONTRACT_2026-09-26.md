# WRITERS-STUDIO-FLAGSHIP-ROADMAP-01 / D5C1 — THEMES GOVERNED SUBSTRATE CONTRACT

**Standing:** GOVERNING BUILD CONTRACT · NEW WRITER'S STUDIO ONLY · NO LEGACY VISUAL AUTHORITY

## Purpose

Themes belongs in the new Writer's Studio. A missing substrate is a build obligation when the capability is coherent and useful; it is not a reason to delete the designed experience.

The Themes lens asks:

> **What recurs, where it recurs, and how its presence changes across the Work?**

## Constitutional amendment

The canonical developmental lens vocabulary becomes:

`structure · development · continuity · arc · themes · voice · coherence · reader`

Themes is now a governed eighth lens. This is an explicit amendment of the earlier seven-lens contract.

## Two objects that must never collapse

A **Themes reading observation** is MAIA-authored and immutable as-read.

A **Work Theme identity** is the governable object the member encounters in Develop Themes.

A Themes observation may propose a short `themeLabel`, but that label begins as:

> **MAIA observation / candidate**

It does not become member-declared merely because it was rendered.

## Required provenance

Every Work Theme carries exactly one provenance kind:

- `member-declared`
- `template-selected`
- `textual-entity`
- `maia-observation`

The UI must distinguish them.

## Required evidence

A MAIA-observed or textual theme must carry recoverable evidence and a route back into the Work.

For every occurrence that is presented, the member must be able to answer:

> **Where?**

No theme card may exist only as a label plus prose.

## Required coverage

Any statement about recurrence or presence across the Work must name what was examined.

Partial coverage may never pose as whole-Work presence.

## Presence law

Presence is descriptive, not evaluative.

Allowed:
- occurrence count;
- section/chapter presence;
- manuscript-order trajectory;
- once or twice / sometimes / often, where derived by an explicit deterministic rule.

Forbidden:
- importance;
- strength as quality;
- significance;
- "key" theme unless the member explicitly named it that way;
- prediction about an unwritten trajectory.

A presence visualization must be derivable from durable occurrences, never hand-authored fixture numbers.

## Member governance

For a MAIA-observed candidate, the member can:

- **Accept**
- **Rename**
- **Not a theme in my book**

Rename changes the governable theme identity, not MAIA's frozen observation text.

Reject does not delete the historical reading. It changes the candidate's standing in the member-facing Themes layer.

Member-declared themes may be renamed or retired by the member.

## Theme identity

Theme identity is opaque and durable.

It is not derived from:
- the label text;
- semantic similarity;
- evidence count;
- a reading observation's wording.

Two independently admitted themes may share the same wording without becoming the same identity.

## Occurrences

A theme occurrence retains:
- Work/manuscript identity;
- theme identity;
- section identity;
- optional code-point range where exact passage evidence exists;
- source reading / observation identity when MAIA-authored;
- source revision when needed for historical recovery;
- provenance;
- creation timestamp.

## Reload law

Everything required to render accepted/renamed/rejected theme standing after reload must be durable.

A pure UI state transition is not Themes substrate.

## Visual contract

The accepted Themes family may render:
- theme rows/cards;
- chapter/section ranges;
- presence bars;
- trajectory across manuscript order;
- exact moments/passages;
- coverage;
- provenance;
- Rename / Not a theme in my book.

It may not render those objects until their values are backed by the contracts above.

## Relationship to existing Soullab theme machinery

`member_theme_signals` is person-scoped and may contain system-inferred participatory-reality signals. It is not Writer's Studio Work-theme authority.

Its recurrence/storage patterns may be reused, but its rows may not be silently imported as manuscript themes.

## D5C build sequence

1. **D5C1** — this contract + eight-lens vocabulary amendment.
2. **D5C2** — durable Work Theme identity + member-governance event store.
3. **D5C3** — Themes reader `themeLabel` admission and evidence-to-occurrence adapter.
4. **D5C4** — deterministic presence / trajectory projection.
5. **D5C5** — live flagship Themes state + Rename / Reject.
6. **D5C6** — Founder visual witness against the accepted Themes references.

## Stop law

A missing implementation step triggers the next build step.

It does not authorize:
- fabricated data;
- silent inference;
- deleting a coherent approved capability;
- substituting the legacy Writer's Studio composition.
