**Goals Support needs no new relational permission.** It needs an occasion rule
that does not quietly violate the ones above.

## 2 · Q1 · INITIATION — who opens it

> **Proposed: the writer, always, at both grant levels.**

The grants differ in **what MAIA may do once the writer is there**, never in who
starts. This is not caution — it is the only reading consistent with the fact
that no MAIA surface in this Studio has ever spoken first, and with FR-14's
*permission to support is not permission to interrupt*.

## 3 · Q2 · LOCATION — where support may appear

```text
MAY       the Goals panel, which the writer opened
MAY       a Goal-scoped MAIA conversation the writer opened   (work_with only)

MAY NOT   the lower band's Goals region
MAY NOT   the rail
MAY NOT   the writing surface
MAY NOT   anywhere else in Studio
```

⚠️ **The lower band is the trap, and it is not obvious.** It is a legitimate
*door* (Q-D), and it shows goals **without the writer having chosen to engage
them** — it is ambient furniture in the room where they write. Encouragement
there would reach a writer who never entered Goals at all. **A door is not an
invitation to speak through it.**

## 4 · Q3 · OCCASION — what makes support relevant *now*

> ### ⚠️ REPETITION CREATES CADENCE, EVEN WHEN EACH INSTANCE IS OCCASIONED.

This is the finding this document exists for. The obvious rule — *"support may
appear when the writer enters the Goal surface"* — fails, because entering
recurs. Support on every open is not a response to anything; it is **furniture
with a heartbeat**, and it would deliver by accumulation exactly the pursuit
FR-14 forbids, while every single instance looked lawful.

**Proposed: the occasion is a member ACT ON A GOAL in the present turn**, not
the presence of a surface.

```text
LAWFUL OCCASIONS (encourage)
  the writer declares a goal
  the writer marks one met            ← FR-13's "notice and honor completion"
  the writer sets one aside or reopens it
  the writer asks, in so many words

NOT AN OCCASION
  opening the Goals panel
  the panel being open
  a count changing
  a date arriving
  time passing
  returning to the Studio
```

Every item in the second list is a **state**, not an act. A state recurs on its
own; only an act is something the writer did.

## 5 · Q4 · FORM — what MAIA may actually do

### `track_only` — silent

Numbers and state only. No MAIA path reads this goal at all.

### `encourage` — brief, occasioned, about what the writer did

```text
MAY    acknowledge the act just performed
MAY    reflect what has moved, in the writer's own terms
MAY    honor a completion the writer declared

MAY NOT  evaluate the writer ("you're doing well" is about a person)
MAY NOT  compare — to other goals, other writers, or an earlier self
MAY NOT  reference the calendar in any form (FR-10, unchanged)
MAY NOT  ask a follow-up that requires a reply to close
MAY NOT  repeat itself if the same act happens again
```

The last two matter most: a follow-up question converts encouragement into an
exchange the writer did not open, and repetition is cadence.

### `help_me_work_with_it` — a door, not a speech

The writer opens a Goal-scoped conversation. Then MAIA may reflect, explore
obstacles, help reconnect with why the goal matters, and consider next
possibilities — **in a conversation, on the writer's turns.**

> **Under Invariant 3, this is the grant that most needs the sovereignty-return
> behaviour.** A writer returning repeatedly for motivation about a goal they
> are not moving on is the exact pattern *"comfort is permitted, reliance is
> interrupted"* was written for. Goals Support must not become a place to feel
> better about not writing.

### Declining — and this is a rule, not a courtesy

FR-13 forbids *"keep pushing after the writer declines support."* So a decline
must be **available in the turn**, must be **honored without asking again**, and
must not require the writer to go and change their grant. Declining an instance
is not withdrawing an invitation.

## 6 · WHAT PERCEPTION THIS DOES **NOT** OPEN

Goal support speaks about **the goal, and what the writer brings to the
conversation.** It does not read the manuscript.

⚠️ Stated explicitly because this is the likeliest quiet expansion: "help me
work with this goal" reads like a reason to let MAIA see the prose. It is not.
FR-06 holds the perception boundary and the 60,000-code-point ceiling remains
untouched — **a support grant is not a reading grant**, and companionship must
not become the side door through which perception widens.
