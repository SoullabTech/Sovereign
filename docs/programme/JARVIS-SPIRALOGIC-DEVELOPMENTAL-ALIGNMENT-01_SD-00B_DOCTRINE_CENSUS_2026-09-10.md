```

Canon gives **three aspects per element** — and they are **kinds, not steps**:

```

        Artificial     discipline, directed will, practice

        Supernatural   grace, awakening, insight that descends

                       consciousness perceives it"
        Artificial     structures created through habit, environment, repetition
        Supernatural   embodiment of spiritual reality in material form

> ⭐⭐⭐ **This is the 1/2/3 of "Fire 1 / Fire 2 / Fire 3". The twelve is `4
> elements × 3 modes`.**
>

> is *cultivated*, Supernatural *cannot be forced and descends when conditions are
> right*. **Nobody "progresses" from Natural Fire to Supernatural Fire**; all
> three are available and distinguishable.

⭐⭐ **Independent corroboration of two founder rulings:** SD-Q2 (*a phase number
is an address, not a measure of attainment*) and *developmental differentiation is
not necessarily ordinal progression.*

### ⚠️ The representational collision this exposes

`phase INTEGER CHECK (phase BETWEEN 1 AND 12)` flattens a **two-axis coordinate
(element × mode)** into **one ordinal scalar**. The matrix structure — which is
what makes the twelve non-hierarchical — **is not recoverable from the stored
value** unless the mapping is fixed and documented somewhere this census did not
find.

```
CANON          (element, mode)        2 axes, non-ordinal
SUBSTRATE      phase ∈ 1..12          1 axis, ordinal by type
```

⛔ Classified **REPRESENTATIONAL COLLISION — axis collapse.** Not a rank
collision (SD-00A found no hierarchy logic), and not member-facing. But *an
integer invites the vertical reading the matrix forbids*, which is exactly the
pressure SD-Q2 named.
