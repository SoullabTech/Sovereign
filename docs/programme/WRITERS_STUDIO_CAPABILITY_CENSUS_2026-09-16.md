## Status law

- ✅ **LIVE** — present, member-reachable, and covered by current tests/witness evidence.
- 🔧 **REPAIRING** — real capability with an active usability/reliability defect in this lane.
- 🟡 **PARTIAL** — meaningful substrate or member surface exists, but the whole promised capability is not yet dependable.
- 📦 **SUBSTRATE** — code/data exists but there is no honest member-facing destination yet.
- ⏳ **LATER** — intentionally not built/reachable yet. It must not render as a live affordance.

A capability is not LIVE merely because a component, route, schema, or specification exists.

## Governing completion rule

> **No capability counts as present until the writer can reach it, use it, recover from failure, and return to the Work without losing place.**

This census supersedes screenshots or mockups as evidence of capability presence. Mockups may guide design; only the live surface + tests/witness establish availability.
