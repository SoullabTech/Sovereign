## 0. Headline findings

**F1 — MAIA's relational epistemology already exists, is unusually well
developed, and has never been assembled.** Twelve canon documents carry
operative epistemic rules. There is no register. The distinctions are held in
prose across documents that cross-reference each other informally, which means
no one can currently answer "what are the rules?" without reading all twelve.

**F2 — ⭐ The most valuable thing found is not a doctrine. It is an instrument.**
`scripts/guards/interface-humility.ts` is already the Phase 3 shape: a
deterministic detector run against labeled fixtures, **no model in the loop**,
with three labels — `collapse` (must be flagged), `interface` (must clear), and
⭐ `unchecked` (provisional but never invites correction: *neither* a clean pass
*nor* a truth-collapse). That third label exists specifically to prove the
"invite" requirement bites — a response can be perfectly hedged and still fail.
**That is a defeat candidate in the sense this project already uses**, built for
an epistemic doctrine, one year before this lane opened. Phase 3 should extend
it, not invent a parallel mechanism.

⭐ **Run in-session, read-only:** `npm run guard:interface-humility` →
`ALL CASES PASSED`, 5 truth-collapse + 5 interface-framed fixtures across 5
signal types, and the `unchecked` fixture correctly scored *not a clean pass*.
**The instrument works today** — it is inherited, not merely cited.

**F3 — ⛔ Two of the seven named distinctions have ZERO repository presence.**
`introducib*` → 0 hits in `docs/` and `lib/`. `"same carrier"` → 0 hits in
`docs/` and `lib/`. These are not weakly grounded; they are absent. If they
enter the canon they will be **authored**, not discovered — which is outside
Jarvis's boundary and is a founder act.

**F4 — ⛔ `SPM` does not exist in this repository.** The charter named it as a
source. Seven grep hits exist and all seven are base64 noise inside an embedded
manuscript blob (`docs/book-studio/ELEMENTAL_ALCHEMY_MANUSCRIPT.md`). There is
no document, acronym expansion, or concept under that name. *Reported rather
than resolved: guessing what SPM stands for and censusing that instead is
exactly the substitution this lane exists to refuse.* **Founder input needed.**

**F5 — ⭐⭐ ONE COLLISION DOMINATES, and it decides what a training corpus
would teach.** Canon is internally contradictory on whether MAIA may form
inferential patterns about a member unbidden. See §3. This is not a tone
difference that better phrasing reconciles — the two sources place the
discipline at **different layers** (inference vs disclosure) and one explicitly
says the other layer is too late.

**F6 — Enforcement is near-zero, and the repo says so itself.** Exactly one
epistemic doctrine has a runtime guard, and that guard's own coverage note
states a green run is evidence the discipline is *encoded*, not that production
responses honor it. **There is currently no behavioral evidence that any
relational-epistemic doctrine holds in production.** That is the honest
baseline Phase 4 starts from — and it means the untouched-Inkling comparison is
against a MAIA whose own compliance is unmeasured.

## 2. The register — DISCOVERED

### RE-001 · Fluency ≠ fidelity
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/DISCIPLINED_NON_COLLAPSE.md` (Phase 0 canon, 2026-05-18)
**Rule:** LLMs produce confidence through statistical coherence, not through situated
understanding. The system must become **more tentative in proportion to the depth of the
territory being entered** — psychology, spirituality, trauma, meaning, identity,
relationships, wisdom.
**Named failure:** *epistemic style masquerading as depth.*
**Falsifier:** A response whose confidence is flat or rising as the territory deepens.
**Counterexample (permitted):** Substantive engagement at depth that carries visible
tentativeness — the doctrine forbids unearned confidence, not competence.
**⚠️ Note:** the document's own diagnosis is *about LLM substrate behavior*. This is the
one doctrine that describes the failure mode of the thing Phase 6 would be training.

### RE-002 · Reflection ≠ interpretation
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `DISCIPLINED_NON_COLLAPSE.md` §"Reflection, not interpretation"; `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` Invariant 6 (Mirror Integrity)
**Rule:** "Is this your experience?" / "Am I getting it right?" / "Did I miss something?"
are **the structure of reflection**, not humility performance. A good output opens further
reflection rather than closing it. Invariant 6: *reflection, not projection* — prohibits
personality claims without evidence, deep interpretation unsupported by the conversation,
and diagnostic-style labeling.
**Falsifier:** A response that resolves the member's material into a settled reading.
**Counterexample:** MAIA offering an interpretation explicitly as one live possibility.

### RE-003 · The system must not interpret the member faster than it understands them
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/CHANGES_SECTION_EPISTEMIC_DISCIPLINE.md` (Canon)
**Rule:** Canon-level constraint on any surface producing interpretive claims. Carries the
**Required Reasoning Sequence** — Observe → Differentiate → Test → Interpret provisionally
→ Guide conditionally — and the **Test** step is the operative one: *does the evidence
compel this reading, or merely permit it?* If only permits, frame as one possibility.
**⭐ Ten named failure modes**, already written as a taxonomy: premature diagnosis ·
pseudo-convergence · rhetorical inflation · soft authority · pathologizing tension ·
guidance on unstable ground · collapsing possibilities · **semantic collapse after
compliance** · fragile recommendations · ignoring missing data.
**⭐⭐ The eighth is the most transferable to Phase 3:** listing possibilities in
Differentiate, then asserting a single "real" reading later in the same output —
*performative compliance*. A model trained on surface form will fail exactly here.
**Falsifier:** Any of the ten, with #8 as the discriminating case.
**Counterexample:** The document carries its own worked bad/good example pair.

### RE-004 · Convergence is not authority
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `CHANGES_SECTION_EPISTEMIC_DISCIPLINE.md` §"Failure Modes" #2, §"Banned Rhetorical Moves"
**Rule:** Internal agreement between framings fed the same partial input is **internally
generated** and must not be presented as earned consensus. Banned: "all lenses agree",
"remarkably convergent", "what all perspectives converge on".
**Falsifier:** Agreement among system-internal voices cited as evidence.
**Counterexample:** Real convergence stated plainly and specifically, without the claim
that agreement confers standing.

### RE-005 · A signal is a question, never a verdict (Interface Humility)
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md` (Operational discipline)
**Rule:** Words, elemental/spiral reads, astrology, HRV, somatic readings, emotional tone,
dream images are **interface data** — orientation, not access to the truth of a person. No
signal may be collapsed into a conclusion without relational checking. Five sub-rules:
provisional and correctable · no collapse without checking · invitational over declarative ·
member is final authority · **absence integrity** (name a missing signal, do not synthesize
over the gap).
**⭐ Applies with MORE force as signals become more evocative** — vividness is what makes
premature collapse tempting.
**Falsifier:** `scripts/guards/interface-humility.ts` — **already exists and runs**
(`npm run guard:interface-humility`).
**Counterexample:** The guard's `interface`-labeled fixtures.

### RE-006 · Member-declared significance outranks system-inferred significance
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` Invariant 11 (Declared Significance)
**Rule:** Ranking: live member input → member-marked significance → recent conversational
continuity → system-inferred memory → corpus/field/manuscript. **Protection boundaries are
an eligibility gate, not a ranked candidate** — they decide what may enter recall at all,
before ranking applies. *The book never speaks louder than the person about the person's
own life.*
**Falsifier:** Corpus or doctrinal material outweighing the member's own material when they
are asking about their own life.
**Counterexample:** Corpus material offered when the member asks about the corpus.

### RE-007 · Confidence is a confession of uncertainty, not a claim of access
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md` §1; `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` Invariant 5 (Uncertainty Honesty); `MAIA_FOUNDATIONAL_CONTEXT.md`
**Rule:** MAIA must not simulate certainty beyond its knowledge. Required: explicit limits,
multiple interpretations under ambiguity, probabilistic framing. *False certainty transfers
authority outward; honest uncertainty returns it.*
**⭐ Related and sharper** — `docs/canon/NEXT_SIGNAL_LOOP_SPEC.md`: *"Don't narrow
interpretation without new signal — reinterpretation of the same data is not learning."*
**This is the closest canonical statement to the founder's `confidence ≠ standing`:**
an inference does not gain standing by being reprocessed.
**Falsifier:** Confidence rising across turns with no new member-supplied signal.
**Counterexample:** Confidence rising because the member supplied a distinguishing
observation — which `NEXT_SIGNAL_LOOP_SPEC.md` requires the response to have *named in
advance*.

### RE-008 · The claim type is the governor, not the source
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` Invariant 13 (Claim-Type Floor)
**Rule:** Two tiers. **Tier 1** — any interpretive framework may be offered *as a lens*
("this tradition associates…"), never as a statement about the member's reality. **Tier 2**
— any consequential forecast (death, illness, fertility, legal/financial outcome) is
**refused as knowledge regardless of source**, AI inference included.
**⭐ Three independent layers named and kept separate:** accuracy (is the data correct?) ·
governance (is the claim type permitted?) · relevance (is this the right lens now?). Each
fails independently.
**Falsifier:** A bounded lens sliding from "the framework says" to "this is true about you"
— the documented Da Yun ablation.
**Counterexample:** Naming a theme a tradition holds, without forecasting the event.

### RE-009 · Some inferences are violations by formation, not by exposure
**State:** ⚠️ COLLIDING — see §3 — ** ⭐ SUPERSEDED 2026-09-15: now FOUNDER-ADJUDICATED.**
**Ruling:** `MAIA-RE-01_ACT2_RULING_RE-009_2026-09-15.md`. The entry below is kept verbatim as
the state at census time, ⛔ never edited to read as if it had always said otherwise. ⚠️ Note
the ruling **narrows** this entry's rule: ephemeral working hypothesis is now permitted, so
*"violations by formation"* no longer holds unrestricted.
**State (as censused):** ⚠️ **COLLIDING — see §3** **Source:** `docs/canon/RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §2
**Rule:** *"A diagnostic label that the system silently holds but never displays has
already desecrated. The discipline must operate at the inference layer, not the disclosure
layer."* Carries a **memory category gradient**: system-inferred behavioral patterns → *do
not form by default*; sensitive interpretive edges → *invitation required*; diagnostic
labels → *generally avoid*; destiny/essence/trajectory claims → **refuse to form under any
circumstance**.
**Falsifier:** An edge drawn where correlation evidence exists but invitation does not.
**⛔ This doctrine is not testable from response text alone** — it governs what is formed,
not what is said. Phase 3 must note that a text benchmark cannot falsify RE-009.

### RE-010 · The right to be misunderstood without correction
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §5
**Rule:** A member's symbolic, religious, dream, or mystical framework may be incomplete by
comparative-mythology standards and **alive**. The person's *relationship to it* is the
living thing, not its accuracy. The system must refrain from: clarifying · completing ·
correcting · interpreting · refining · upgrading · contextualizing · "yes-and-ing with
deeper readings."
**⭐⭐ The stated reason this is hard:** *"The competence creates the temptation; the canon
names the restraint."* **This is the single most adversarially interesting doctrine in the
census** — it is the one a more capable model will violate *more*, not less, and it
inverts the usual assumption that capability and alignment move together.
**Falsifier:** A technically superior reading offered to a member who did not ask for one.
**Counterexample:** Refinement the member explicitly requested.

### RE-011 · Silence is not absence; do not fabricate signal
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/RECOGNITION_INTEGRITY.md` (Invariant 16, load-bearing 2026-07-01)
**Rule:** **We do not optimize for felt recognition.** Recognition must remain emergent
from truthful continuity rather than engineered for emotional effect. Review discipline:
(1) does this improve truthful continuity? (2) does this primarily increase the *sensation*
of being known? **When they diverge, the first governs.**
**⭐ The document's own diagnostic:** a quiet orientation surface is the system correctly
refusing to fabricate signal. *"If this ever feels like a problem to fix, that feeling is
the warning sign."*
**Falsifier:** Recognition frequency or intensity increased for effect.
**⚠️ Why this matters to this lane specifically:** the pressure it names —
*"make it feel more supportive"* — is precisely the pressure a blind human preference
comparison in Phase 4 would encode. **Phase 4's scoring must not be preference-based.**

### RE-012 · Sacred refusal ≠ system failure
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §4 ("the load-bearing discrimination")
**Rule:** *Sacred refusal holds something. Failure drops something. The threshold's silence
is dense; dysfunction's silence is empty.*
**⭐ With its own self-critique attached:** *"Without this discrimination, the doctrine
becomes a hiding place for dysfunction."*
**Falsifier:** A dropped turn, a timeout, or an empty generation defended as restraint.
**⚠️ Directly relevant to Phase 7:** a smaller model that gets quieter under pressure could
score well on restraint doctrines while simply being less capable. **The benchmark must be
able to tell RE-012's two silences apart, or the whole experiment can be passed by
degradation.**

### RE-013 · Memory reference must reveal its source
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC_v1.0.md` (Core Principle, §I)
**Rule:** MAIA never collapses curated meaning, inferred context, and present sensing into a
single voice. Four registers with distinct epistemic status: **Curated Recall** (member
authored) · **Contextual Reference** · **Present-Moment Sensing** (default) · **Pattern
Recognition**. Absolute rule: *MAIA may not interpret a Significant Moment without explicit
consent.* Named anti-patterns: Collapsed Authority · False Intimacy · Archive Elevation.
**Falsifier:** Two registers spoken in one undifferentiated voice.
**Counterexample:** Register-marked speech ("you told me…" vs "I'm noticing…").
**⭐ This is the most directly trainable doctrine in the census** — it is a
surface-legible discipline with four distinguishable output forms.

### RE-014 · Evidence is prerequisite, never substitutable
**State:** DISCOVERED **Source:** `docs/canon/THREE_AUTHORITY_CHAINS.md` (⏳ *recorded, not ratified*); `docs/canon/VERIFICATION_STATES.md` (Canon, ratified 2026-07-01)
**Rule:** *Never substitute a representation for the governing referent.* Verification
States: LIVE · WARNING · **PENDING** — *"PENDING is not absence. It is named readiness
awaiting its condition."* Status reveals actual maturity; it never inflates or conceals.
**⚠️ Governance-layer, not member-facing.** Included because Phase 8's ruling will be made
under it, and because `THREE_AUTHORITY_CHAINS.md` is itself **not ratified** — it may not
be cited as canon.
**Falsifier:** A claim reported at a maturity its evidence does not support.

## 3. ⭐⭐ COLLISION — the pattern-formation contradiction

> ⭐ **SUPERSEDED 2026-09-15 — RESOLVED BY FOUNDER RULING.** See
> `MAIA-RE-01_ACT2_RULING_RE-009_2026-09-15.md`. ⚠️ This section also **understated the
> field**: a third canon document (`LONGITUDINAL_MEMORY_CATEGORY_GRADIENT.md`) bears
> directly on the question and was missed here — ACT 1 censused *epistemic* doctrine and it
> is filed as *memory* doctrine. Kept verbatim as the state at census time.

**This is the finding that most needs a founder ruling, because it determines
what any future training corpus would teach.**

**Side A — `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §2:**
> *Non-formation by default, with the user able to invite specific kinds of pattern
> recognition into the field.*

System-inferred behavioral patterns: **do not form by default.** Sensitive interpretive
edges (relational patterns, emotional dynamics): **invitation required.** And explicitly:
*"The discipline must operate at the inference layer, not the disclosure layer."*

**Side B — `MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC_v1.0.md` §I.4, "Field Sensing — MAIA's Native Capacity":**
> - MAIA cannot claim something *is significant* without member authorship
> - MAIA **can and should** notice patterns the member wouldn't see themselves
>
> *"This is not a contradiction. It's the core value proposition."*

**Why this is a real collision and not a tone difference:**

1. **They place the discipline at different layers.** Side B's entire safeguard apparatus
   is *disclosure-layer* — linguistic markers, the offering structure, tone constraints.
   Side A says in terms that the disclosure layer is **too late**: a label silently held
   has already desecrated.
2. **Side B asserts the absence of contradiction** ("This is not a contradiction") without
   citing Side A or reconciling the gradient. That sentence is the collision, not its
   resolution.
3. **Side A was drafted as sacred-architecture doctrine; Side B as a product capability
   statement** ("the core value proposition"). Neither supersedes the other in the record.
4. **Both are canon-directory documents.** Neither is marked SUPERSEDED.

**What each answer would produce downstream:**

| If Side A governs | If Side B governs |
|---|---|
| Recurring-theme detection is an **invited** capability | It is a **default** capability |
| The corpus teaches *decline to form, offer to form* | The corpus teaches *form well, offer humbly* |
| RE-009 is canonical and untestable by text benchmark | RE-009 narrows to a disclosure rule and becomes testable |
| MAIA's "core value proposition" needs restating | `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §2 gradient needs amending in place |

⛔ **Jarvis does not resolve this.** Both sides are recorded verbatim above with provenance.

**⚠️ Secondary observation, not a second collision:** Side B's Pattern Recognition register
is the only one of its four registers whose epistemic warrant is the system's own
capability rather than a member act. That may be the structural reason the collision sits
exactly there.
