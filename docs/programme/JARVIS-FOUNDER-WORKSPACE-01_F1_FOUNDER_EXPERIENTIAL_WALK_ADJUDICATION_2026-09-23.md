Five materially different Founder intents were entered into the exact Work surface:

1. continue a governed JARVIS programme from its exact next lawful boundary;
2. fix a Writer's Studio product defect without choosing tools/models first;
3. create a rigorous paper from existing governed research;

## IV. Surface rulings

### Today — KEEP · NEW CAPABILITY REQUIRED

Today succeeds as orientation. It makes visible:

- where the Founder is;
- what needs Founder attention;
- what is in motion;
- what changed recently;
- what is outside the workspace's authority.

During the walk, Today exposed no action buttons and no openable links. It therefore stops at awareness.

**Binding B5 revision:** Today must let the Founder enter the relevant work context directly from an attention item, programme, change, or external boundary where authority permits. The transition must preserve the selected subject as workspace context.

### Work — REVISE · NEW CAPABILITY REQUIRED

Work's governing posture is accepted:

> ordinary-language intent first; machinery hidden underneath; plan and authority shown before execution.

The present interaction is not accepted as the final experience because materially different Founder intents receive the same generic explanation.

**Binding B5/B6 requirement:** Work must become a contextual Founder conversation that:

- understands whether an intent is programme continuation, product repair, research/artifact work, operational investigation, or another governed work class;
- retrieves relevant existing programme/work context before proposing a next act;
- identifies the exact lawful boundary where one exists;
- proposes specific work rather than repeating a generic three-step template;
- names what JARVIS knows, what remains uncertain, what authority is required, and what happens next;
- carries the work context across all five surfaces;
- preserves typed and spoken entry as the same operator-intent path when voice later opens.

The accepted target experience is not a form explaining JARVIS. It is JARVIS meeting the Founder inside the work.

### Graph — KEEP · REVISE

The evidence-backed relationship model is accepted. In the walk, selecting Founder Workspace exposed five real connections, each with an evidence reference. Filtering by node kind also behaved coherently.

However, the promise that relationships are backed by something the Founder can open is not yet realized: evidence references are rendered as text, with no openable evidence destination.

**Binding B5 revision:** Graph must support direct traversal:

`relationship → underlying evidence/record → return to same graph/work context`

Graph should normally open already scoped to the current work context, with relevant nodes emphasized, rather than requiring the Founder to enter a cold network and reconstruct relevance.

### Monitor — KEEP · NEW CAPABILITY REQUIRED

Monitor's epistemic model is accepted and should be protected. It clearly distinguishes:

- observed now and fine;
- observed earlier, present state unknown;
- observed and needs care;
- failed;
- not observed;
- not permitted from here.

This was the strongest truth model in the prototype.

The missing capability is action after observation.

**Binding B5 revision:** where lawful, an observation must offer an obvious path such as:

`Refresh` · `Investigate` · `Take me to the relevant work`

Those actions must preserve the observation and its provenance as context. “Not observed” may open a lawful observation act; it may never silently become green.

### System — KEEP · REVISE

System succeeds as the advanced accountability layer. It answered:

- which JARVIS artifact/checkout is being discussed;
- which authority axes belong to each programme;
- what internal vocabulary means in ordinary language.

The authority separation and vocabulary lookup are accepted.

As with Graph, provenance sources are not openable.

**Binding B5 revision:** provenance and vocabulary sources must be traversable where locally available, with a reliable return path into the same work context. System remains the deep accountability layer, not the ordinary starting point for work.

## V. Cross-surface law for B5

The five surfaces are retained:

`Today · Work · Graph · Monitor · System`

They are not five independent dashboards.

B5 must establish one **contextual Founder work session** that all five presentations share.

If the Founder enters Writer's Studio work, for example:

- Today shows the relevant outstanding attention in that context;
- Work holds the current conversation, programme boundary, plan and next act;
- Graph emphasizes the relationships relevant to that work;
- Monitor surfaces the instruments relevant to that work first;
- System exposes the corresponding authority and provenance;
- changing surfaces does not make the Founder reconstruct what “this” means.

The selected work/programme/subject must therefore travel with the Founder across surfaces.

## VI. Evidence traversal law

A displayed source reference that the environment claims the Founder can inspect must become directly traversable in the live Desktop where the source is locally available.

The minimum interaction is:

`Open evidence` → inspect exact source → `Back to work/context`

No evidence link may imply reachability that the Desktop does not actually possess.

## VII. Investigation law

Monitor and Today may become entry points into Work, but they do not independently execute repairs.

An observation such as storage pressure may open an observe-first work context. A failed instrument may open investigation. A programme needing a Founder ruling may open the relevant decision context once a governed decision surface exists.

The originating observation/evidence must remain visible as the reason the work was opened.

## VIII. Voice implication

and `this` resolves from the same work context visible across Today, Work, Graph, Monitor and System.
