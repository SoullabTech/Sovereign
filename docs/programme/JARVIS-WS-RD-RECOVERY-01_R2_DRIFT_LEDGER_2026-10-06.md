## Drift diagnosis

The dominant pattern is **capability accretion without experience re-adjudication**.

A feature could pass because:
- route works;
- model returns;
- evidence is bound;
- UI renders;
- test is green.

But the R&D acceptance question was often missing:

> Does this preserve the Writer–MAIA relationship and the human pacing we designed?

## What should not be thrown away

The last several days produced substantial usable substrate:

- governed local structured inference;
- paragraph/folio presentation repair;
- truthful section-vs-passage handoff;
- persistent Studio-level Working with MAIA control;
- Work conversation continuity;
- relationship-first prompt law;
- exact Write locus custody;
- scorecard completeness guard;
- hierarchical book-reading strategy;
- crash repair and return-place coalescing.

The recovery should **recompose these under the relationship**, not remove them.

## R2 terminal finding

The product is not back at zero.

The substrate has advanced farther than the experience.

The next act is to make the relationship architecture executable enough that new substrate cannot outrun the product again.
