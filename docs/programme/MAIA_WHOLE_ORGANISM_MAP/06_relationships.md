## 1 · The founder's question for this subsystem

**Does MAIA increase human relational capacity or merely deepen the MAIA bond?**

**Answer (READ; class E for structure, D for doctrine; no class C):** The *designed* surfaces point outward; the *silently-written* substrate points inward, and the outward-pointing surfaces have no measurement of "beyond the AI."

Where the system points a member toward other humans:
- R-e prompt `lib/maia/relationalNavigation/prompts.ts:47-49`: "You do not issue directives. You widen options … The final beat is always: this is yours to choose. Do not end with a follow-up question that bait-loops the conversation … Do not promise to remember"; `:41` "You may want to check this directly with them"; integrate output carries `nextStepOptions` of kind `communication | boundary | repair | pause` (`:88-90`). The room is built to send the member back to the absent human.
- R-d handoff block `formatRelationalContextForPrompt.ts:82-85`: "do not diagnose the relationship, and do not characterise the other person … If the member has moved on to something else, follow them."
- R-f relationship spaces and R-g Co-Labs / encounters are human-to-human containers: MAIA is a bot member that reflects only when asked (`maia-reflect/route.ts:20`, `messageBody` required), DMs carry no MAIA path (grep `lib/team/DMService.ts` → none).
- Spec falsifier `docs/specs/RELATIONAL_NAVIGATION_ROOM.md:200`: "The flow gets used and then nothing changes in the member's external life. Signal … the flow is producing reflection without capacity."

Where the system keeps the member in dialogue with MAIA:
- R-a empty state `components/relationships/EmptyRelationalField.tsx:45-46`: "Let patterns emerge — As you talk with MAIA, relational threads will surface naturally." The invitation to *talk with MAIA* is the mechanism by which the Relationships room fills; the fill is performed by R-b without a member act.
- R-b writes on every non-Sanctuary turn (`list/route.ts:1823-1826`); the audit records MAIA "wrote relational content on every live turn … and never once read it back" (`list/route.ts:897-898`) until R-d; 98.5 % of what it wrote is un-attributed.
- R-c the card lead phrases ("Something may be active…", `:99-110`) speak about the member's relationships to the member, sourced from keyword reading of the MAIA conversation, with `source_turn_id` 0/440 (rupture record §UNRESOLVED).

Whether any surface measures "beyond the AI": **NOT FOUND.** No table, log marker, or copy asks whether a conversation with the other human happened, was attempted, or changed. The rel-nav spec names the falsifier but no instrument (`RELATIONAL_NAVIGATION_ROOM.md:192-202`). `MEASUREMENT_VOCABULARY_v0.1.md` row 1 states "no instrument anywhere in the field."
