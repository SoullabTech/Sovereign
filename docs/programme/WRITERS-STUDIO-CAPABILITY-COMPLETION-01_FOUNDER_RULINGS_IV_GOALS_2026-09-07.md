## FR-09 · TWO GOAL KINDS — ACCEPTED

The meaningful distinction is **who can truthfully observe progress.**

```text
MEASURABLE
writer declares:  target + countable quantity
system may:       measure current quantity
                  compare it to the writer's target
                  report the result

INTENTION
writer declares:  a qualitative aim
system may:       preserve it · show it
                  let the writer mark / change / complete it
system may NOT:   manufacture a numerical proxy for it
```

> **"Finish Chapter 7" is an INTENTION unless the writer explicitly defines what
> completion means. Word count does not become "finished" merely because
> software can count it.**

### Structural, not conventional

**Do not model this as "target nullable."** Make the kind explicit and invalid
states unrepresentable as far as practical:

```text
MEASURABLE   metric REQUIRED   target REQUIRED
INTENTION    metric FORBIDDEN  target FORBIDDEN
```

> *Otherwise six months from now someone will add a target to an intention
> because the column happens to accept it.*

## FR-10 · TIME MAY BOUND A GOAL, NEVER JUDGE THE WRITER — ACCEPTED, with a precision

The executable rule stands:

> **No progress figure may be generated as a function of the clock.**

An absolute date is preserved as **writer-authored data**:

```text
Target:    30 September
Progress:  2,140 / 3,000 words
```

Derived temporal judgement is prohibited:

```text
NO   73% on track · 860 words behind · 215 words/day required
     4 days remaining at current pace · projected completion
     streaks · pace score
```

> **The calendar may be displayed because the writer declared it. It may not
> become an evaluator of the writer.**

Stronger than "don't be judgemental" because **the architecture cannot generate
the judgement family.**

## FR-11 · SCOPE AND ANCHOR LOSS — ACCEPTED

Reuses FR-07/FR-08's ontology — `PRIMARY manuscript · OPTIONAL section anchor ·
CONTEXT Work when unambiguous` — with **one additional semantic**:

> **Loss of an anchor may destroy MEASURABILITY without destroying the GOAL.**

```text
Goal: 3,000 words in "The Torus"

section deleted
→ goal survives
→ section_id = NULL
→ historical anchor retained
→ measurable status becomes UNAVAILABLE
→ target is NOT silently re-scoped to the whole manuscript
```

> **Changing "3,000 words in this chapter" into "3,000 words in this book" would
> be the system rewriting the writer's intention.**

## FR-12 · MAIA'S AUTHORITY — BOUNDED BY DECLARATION

```text
MAIA MAY
  reflect the goal back
  report mechanically observed progress
  ask what the goal means to the writer
  ask whether the writer still wants it
  notice an apparent relationship between a writer-declared goal and
  something the writer brings into conversation, AS A QUESTION

MAIA MAY NOT
  create a goal
  alter its target
  add a deadline
  convert an intention into a metric
  call the writer ahead or behind
  infer motivation or commitment from progress
  automatically mark a qualitative intention complete
```

> **The writer declares the aim. MAIA may accompany the relationship to it;
> MAIA does not become the manager of it.**

## Q-D · EXPLORE OWNS GOALS, AND EXPLORE DOES NOT EXIST — RULED

> ### Ownership is not reachability.

`FUNCTION-PLACEMENT` remains true and unamended:

```text
CONCEPTUAL HOME   EXPLORE owns Goals
WORK HOME         shows / reaches active Goals
WRITE             may show / reach Goals where the ratified reference calls for it
EXPLORE           eventual primary place for sustained Goals work
```

**The ruling:** ship **one Goals object and one Goals capability now**. Existing
ratified viewing surfaces may provide **doors** into it before EXPLORE ships.
**Those surfaces are hosts, not owners.** When EXPLORE arrives it becomes the
primary room **without requiring a data or semantic migration.**

> **The mistake would be building a temporary second Goals system in WRITE or
> Work Home.**

## THE GOALS v1 PRODUCT CONTRACT

```text
AUTHOR       writer
ANCHOR       manuscript · optional section · optional Work context
KINDS        measurable | intention
TIME         writer-authored boundary only · never pace/judgment
PROGRESS     only what the system can directly measure
MAIA         reflect / ask / report · never invent or manage
ANCHOR LOSS  goal survives · measurement may cease
OWNER        EXPLORE
DOORS        may exist in already-ratified surfaces before EXPLORE ships
```

> Enough to build Goals **without either inventing a productivity tracker or
> waiting for the whole future Studio to exist.**
