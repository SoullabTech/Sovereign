# AIN-MASTER-FIELD-01 — Kelly's World Projection Contract

Date: 2026-10-01
Status: design contract
Source programme: AIN-MASTER-FIELD-01 Full JARVIS Flow

## Core law

Kelly's World is the human-readable operational surface of the field.

Today, Work, Monitor, System, and Graph are projections of governed objects. They may emphasize different aspects, but they may not create competing copies of truth or independently change standing.

One object, multiple views.

## Projection invariants

1. Today answers: what requires Kelly now?
2. Work answers: what is being carried?
3. Monitor answers: what is happening now?
4. System answers: what mechanism makes this possible?
5. Graph answers: how did this become what it is?

A surface may compress representation. It may not silently strengthen or weaken certainty, scope, persistence, authority, consequence, or uncertainty.

## Today

Only foreground objects with current founder relevance.

Eligible reasons:
- founder authority required
- unresolved consequential state
- boundary conflict requiring adjudication
- repair requiring founder participation
- explicit decision due

Do not surface raw telemetry merely because it exists.

Every item should be able to answer:
- why Kelly is seeing it
- what requires Kelly
- what happens if nothing is done
- what evidence supports the claim

## Work

A work object is a continuity vessel for unresolved consequence.

Required fields:
- origin
- current standing
- owner
- authority
- evidence
- next lawful act
- resolution condition
- lineage

Work must preserve ruling scope. Implementation may not silently broaden authority.

## Monitor

Monitor represents effective current state, not historical existence.

Primary classes:
- healthy
- degraded
- action required
- automatic control active
- unconfirmed
- failed
- recovering

Detection, delivery, acknowledgement, and resolution remain separate.

## System

System exposes mechanism without claiming meaning beyond mechanism.

Examples:
- transport
- dependency
- failure domain
- runtime health
- policy
- permission source
- current implementation state

System is technical truth, not founder-action truth.

## Graph

Graph is relational and temporal.

Initial relation set:
- DERIVED_FROM
- AUTHORIZED_BY
- SUPPORTED_BY
- CONTRADICTED_BY
- TRANSFORMED_INTO
- GAVE_RISE_TO
- WITNESSED_BY
- ACTED_ON_BY
- SUPERSEDED_BY
- CORRECTED_BY

Graph must distinguish observed history, current standing, and possible future configurations.

Projected future relations must never render as observed facts.

## Constitutional projection

The following programme domains must find lawful expression where relevant:
- consequence truth
- standing conservation
- transformation lineage
- temporal validity
- personhood irreducibility
- relational permission
- rupture and repair
- attention and salience
- whole-field orientation

This does not mean every law appears in every room.

Projection follows purpose and minimum necessary disclosure.

## Attention hierarchy

Kelly's World must not confuse available information with information deserving foreground attention.

Default hierarchy:
- Needs Kelly
- In motion
- Watching

Everything else remains discoverable through Work, Monitor, System, or Graph without crowding Today.

## Sensitive-content boundary

Operational projection should prefer state, provenance, and obligation over sensitive member payload.

For example:
- "urgent safety delivery unconfirmed"
is preferred on an overview surface to reproducing the underlying member content.

Authorization governs drill-down.

## Temporal fidelity

Current state and historical truth must remain distinct.

A past outage can remain historically true while current transport is healthy.

A superseded ruling remains historical evidence without remaining effective authority.

A corrected claim remains visible in lineage without remaining current truth.

## Personhood fidelity

A person node is a governed representation, never the person.

Kelly's World must preserve distinctions among:
- member self-description
- practitioner observation
- system observation
- system inference
- symbolic interpretation
- future possibility

No person page may imply exhaustive capture.

## Relational fidelity

Consent and permission are not inferred from familiarity.

Where displayed, relational permission must preserve:
- grantor
- grantee
- act
- object scope
- context
- purpose
- current validity

Revoked permission governs future action while historical lawful acts remain historically true.

## Rupture and repair

Consequential ruptures may surface in Today or Work only when action is required.

Monitor may show aggregate fidelity state without creating a trust score.

Graph preserves rupture lineage, correction, downstream impact, and repair.

## Attention and Master field

The Master field does not become a dashboard ranking engine.

Its projection duty is proportion:
- do not let one high-volume faculty dominate
- do not let risk erase beauty or ordinary life
- do not let memory erase novelty
- do not let future possibility erase the present
- do not let symbolic force erase material fact

Kelly should be able to ask of any high-impact foreground item: "Why am I seeing this?"

The answer must be grounded in standing, authority, consequence, or explicit attention policy.

## Implementation boundary

This contract does not authorize UI mutation.

Before implementation:
1. census existing Kelly's World viewmodel/projector mechanisms
2. identify healthy substrate
3. map each required projection onto existing seams
4. design falsifiers
5. open bounded implementation work only for true gaps

No second source of truth is permitted.
