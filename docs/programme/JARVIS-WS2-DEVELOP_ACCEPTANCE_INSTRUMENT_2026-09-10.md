## Why it lives outside the codebase

⭐ **A throwaway acceptance instrument should be structurally incapable of becoming the implementation.** A prototype inside the repo will be tempting to grow. This one cannot become Writer's Studio by accident — **production, schema, routes and migrations are untouched by construction rather than by restraint**, which is exactly the founder's *"the prototype does not become canonical merely because it passes."*

## What it is


⛔ **No labelled machinery.** There is no `Recall`, `History`, `North Star`, `Deferred`, `Saved`, `Current Inquiry`. **One unlabelled input.** If the instrument had needed those controls to be usable, that would itself have been the answer: criterion 11 failed.

**Fixture provenance, stated honestly**: the observations are real against the real text — *the fifth element* is introduced at sections **0, 1, 2 and 10**, then arrives at **15** as *"the fifth element represents the integration…"*, phrased as a first arrival. Verified before building, not asserted.
