## STOP check

Stop the witness and close H1 exposure if any of these occur:

- wrong-member or wrong-Work material;
- cohort authority bypass or unexpected exposure;
- silent manuscript selection where member choice is required;
- unexpected mutation or credible fear the original writing was lost;
- inability to return safely;
- privacy, consent, or authentication boundary failure.

Ordinary hesitation, aesthetic dislike, or recoverable UI friction is not a constitutional STOP condition.

## Post-walk questions

Ask only after the uncoached pass.

1. When you moved from the House into Writer's Studio, did it feel like the same Work continued with you?
2. At any point did you wonder whether the system had chosen or interpreted something on your behalf?
3. When you moved among Write, Develop and Review, did you still know what Work you were in?
4. When you returned Home, did it feel like returning from somewhere in the same larger place?
5. Was there any moment you worried your writing could be lost or changed without you?
6. What would you naturally want to do next?

Paraphrase answers unless an exact quote is necessary and separately consented.
