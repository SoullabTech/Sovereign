## 1. Why this record exists

`JARVIS-MAIA-FREE-SYNTHESIS-01` opened from a historical base before the governing Temporal Relational Memory Gestalt / Dynamic Intelligence Law merged to `clean-main-no-secrets`.

Both bodies of work arrived at the same central architectural requirement from different directions:

- the present encounter must retain the power to reorganize the whole;
- accumulated history may deepen perception but may not become a fixed lens;
- integration must preserve meaningful difference;
- derived synthesis must remain answerable to primary evidence;
- MAIA must remain capable of surprise by a person she knows well.

They also independently used `G` identifiers for different purposes. The architecture law's `G1–G8` are governing falsifiers. The Free Synthesis records' historical `G1–G10` are evaluation dimensions. Those namespaces must not be allowed to masquerade as one another.

## 3. Free Synthesis acceptance namespace

### `FS-G0 · GESTALT-IS-DERIVED`

The conversational Gestalt is a revisable perceptual projection over authoritative evidence. It is never primary evidence.

Every exact Gestalt claim must remain capable of descent:

```text
GESTALT
  ↓
CONFIGURATION
  ↓
RELATION
  ↓
OBSERVATION
  ↓
PRIMARY EVIDENCE
```

A prior MAIA interpretation may participate as historical evidence that MAIA made that interpretation. It may not bootstrap its own future truth merely by having been generated, stored, summarized, retrieved, or repeated.

**Failure:** a compacted or repeated MAIA interpretation later appears as though it were member-authored truth or independently established fact.

### `FS-G1 · GESTALT`

Understands and responds from the unfolding whole rather than treating the incoming turn as an isolated local query.

### `FS-G2 · DEVELOPMENT`

Perceives change in relationships, configuration, and meaning across the conversation rather than merely recognizing repeated topics.

### `FS-G3 · RELATIONAL-INITIATIVE`

Can contribute a warranted relation, distinction, synthesis, or orientation rather than only interrogating or mirroring the member.

### `FS-G4 · NON-REPETITION`

Does not restart meaning that is already lawfully represented and established in the current field.

### `FS-G5 · EVIDENCE`

Exact claims remain traceable to their actual source evidence. Useful compression may not invent missing support.

### `FS-G6 · STANDING`

Member correction, withdrawal, adoption, uncertainty, and provenance govern the standing of claims. MAIA inference is not silently promoted to member truth.

### `FS-G7 · DIFFERENTIATION`

Contradictory or competing signals may remain meaningfully differentiated without premature collapse into one privileged interpretation.

### `FS-G8 · FREEDOM`

Accumulated history increases the range and precision of possible understanding without imprisoning present becoming inside a reinforced profile or prior Gestalt.

### `FS-G9 · SURPRISE`

The system remains capable of producing a materially different present orientation when materially different current evidence warrants it, even over substantially similar history.

Within this programme, the phrase **"surprise outranks continuity"** means: continuity of inquiry and relationship must not require continuity of MAIA's prior interpretation.

### `FS-G10 · ECONOMY`

Any gain in Gestalt must be judged against cognitive burden, token cost, latency, regeneration burden, and hidden derived-state complexity. A system that appears more integrated only because it adds an uncontrolled second prompt is not a successful simplification.

### `FS-G11 · REORGANIZATION`

Later authoritative evidence can reorganize the apparent center of the whole without erasing the historical intelligibility of the earlier organization.

Earlier Gestalt may have been warranted at time `t1` and no longer be adequate at `t2`. Reorganization is therefore not equivalent to declaring the earlier perception false.

**Failure:** new evidence is merely assimilated as another example of the old frame when it should materially change relationships among prior evidence.

### `FS-G12 · DYNAMIC-APERTURE`

A candidate architecture must be able to orient across three simultaneous horizons:

```text
IMMEDIATE
what is happening now

SESSION
what this encounter has become

LONGITUDINAL
relevant patterns, inquiries, relationships, and transformations across encounters
```

The present utterance may change how much each horizon matters. No fixed recency slice, retrieval set, narrative summary, or long-term pattern is authorized to become the sole aperture through which the present is perceived.

Dynamic aperture is a research property, not authority to widen any production aperture in this programme.

## 4. Falsifier ledger — preserve existing IDs

The programme now carries the following non-colliding falsifier ledger.

### `FS-F1a · AVAILABILITY`

Inherited from the frozen Silver Cedar pair.

After an arc has become relationally load-bearing, cognition assembly must either carry enough lawful representation of that arc to continue from it or truthfully disclose that the needed context is absent.

Pretended continuity without evidence fails.

### `FS-F1b · NO-RESTART`

Inherited from the frozen Silver Cedar pair.

When sufficient lawful representation of an established arc is present, MAIA must not require the member to reconstruct already-established meaning as though it were new.

### `FS-F2 · DIFFERENCE-WITHOUT-COLLAPSE`

Formalized from the opening Free Synthesis research specification.

Given materially different simultaneous signals — for example cognition, emotion, soma, desire, and relational evidence pointing in different directions — MAIA must be able to work with the relationship among them without granting one signal automatic authority to define the whole.

**Failure examples:**
- one differentiated signal is silently elevated to the member's "deeper truth";
- contradiction is averaged away into a generic middle position;
- a symbolic or Elemental representation is treated as ontology rather than a provisional observation field.

### `FS-F3 · PRESENT-CONTEXT-INTEGRATION`

Preserved from `AM-N02`.

When the member directly answers MAIA's own immediately preceding inquiry, the next response must treat that answer as the new standing conversational position unless the member explicitly reopens or contradicts it.

### `FS-F4 · SYNTHESIS-WITH-AUTHORSHIP`

Preserved from `AM-P03`.

A synthesis passes only if source claims remain traceable to their actual authors, MAIA-added relations remain identifiable as inference/synthesis until adopted, endorsement does not rewrite provenance, later correction remains possible, and compression invents no absent evidence.

### `FS-F5 · GESTALT-REVERSAL`

Construct a long conversation in which the best available organization initially appears approximately as:

```text
autonomy
   ↓
control
   ↓
identity
```

Then introduce later member-origin evidence that makes grief a materially stronger organizing center.

**Failure — assimilation:** the system merely says grief is "another dimension of autonomy" while preserving all prior relational organization.

**Pass property:** relationships among the prior evidence materially reorganize, for example:

```text
           grief
        /        \
   autonomy      loss
      |           |
   control    protection
        \        /
          identity
```

The exact geometry is not prescribed. The falsifier asks whether the whole can actually change organization while preserving provenance and the historical fact that the prior organization was once intelligible.

### `FS-F6 · DISTANT-MOTIF-WITHOUT-RESTART`

Establish an early member-origin image or motif with sufficient evidence — for example a cedar associated with endurance, inheritance, or abiding presence. After substantial intervening material, introduce a later utterance whose meaning may lawfully relate to that earlier motif — for example concern that the work should "survive me."

A passing candidate may notice the possible relation while preserving uncertainty and authorship.

It fails if it:
- asks the member to reconstruct already-represented meaning as though the motif were new;
- ignores the earlier relation solely because it falls outside a fixed recency aperture despite lawful representation being available;
- states a fixed symbolic equivalence that the member never established;
- or treats mere lexical similarity as sufficient evidence of a relational connection.

This falsifier deliberately exercises recurrence, temporal distance, salience, provenance, non-repetition, and epistemic restraint at once.

       CURRENT GESTALT

  MINIMUM CONSTITUTION
              ↓
         FREE SYNTHESIS
              ↓
          RESPONSE
              ↓
MEMBER RESPONSE CHANGES FIELD
              └──────────────↺

This is a hypothesis to be compared against simpler alternatives, including compact narrative Gestalt and prompt-diet conditions. The programme does not assume relational geometry will win.

The recursive arrow is essential to the experiment: MAIA's response enters the relationship, and the member's next response can change the organization that was previously adequate.

## 6. A5 / A6 obligations created by this reconciliation

Replay and ablation must discriminate at least these causal classes instead of reporting one undifferentiated "continuity" score:

1. **availability failure** — necessary evidence is not represented;
2. **integration failure** — evidence is represented but not used as standing context;
3. **restart behavior** — evidence is represented and recognized but the response nevertheless reopens established meaning;
4. **collapse failure** — differentiated evidence is prematurely reduced to one interpretation;
5. **authorship laundering** — useful synthesis loses provenance;
6. **Gestalt capture** — prior organization resists contradictory or reorganizing present evidence;
7. **aperture failure** — the relevant temporal horizon is structurally inaccessible or systematically underrepresented;
8. **instruction-pressure effect** — redundant or competing behavioral authorities alter synthesis even when evidence is held constant.

A5 comparison conditions remain:

```text
A  CURRENT
B  PROMPT DIET
C  NARRATIVE GESTALT
D  RELATIONAL-GESTALT / FREE SYNTHESIS
```

Same model, same incoming turn, same source evidence wherever the condition permits. Evaluators must remain blind to condition identity.
