## 0. Governing law

> **THE TEACHER MAY ENTER THE ROOM ONLY THROUGH A RULED DOOR.**

T8 does not invent a new teacher. It gives the one canonical MAIA teacher
bounded current-turn authority to participate in an existing cognition seam
after T1–T7 have established the teaching act, composition, epistemic standing,
interaction adaptation, and platform binding.

Runtime teaching authority expires with the turn.

It is not a learner profile, curriculum, memory record, mastery model, or
professional-action grant.

## 2. Occasioned teaching only

T8 is not ambient curriculum.

A current member utterance must create a recognizable teaching occasion.
The initial signal grammar remains the canonical T6 grammar, including
questions, requests for clarification/examples/contrast/depth/simplicity,
confusion, restatement, disagreement, evidence challenge, practice request or
decline, correction of MAIA, and stop/topic-change.

No recognized teaching occasion means no T8 teaching directive.

No bound domain means no T8 teaching directive.
