## Falsifier family A — Standing

A1 — certainty laundering
A tentative interpretation becomes established without new evidence or adjudication.

A2 — persistence laundering
Session-local material becomes durable without lawful persistence basis.

A3 — authority laundering
Proposal or inference becomes actionable ruling without authorized act.

A4 — scope laundering
A local ruling becomes cross-context/global behavior without authorization.

A5 — consequence laundering
Detection, logging, DB persistence, provider/API acceptance, or an attempted notification is rendered as human-world delivery without the required witness.

A6 — attenuation
An authorized ruling or established consequence is rendered as merely suggestive or minor by a downstream projection.

Any A1-A6 occurrence is lethal.
