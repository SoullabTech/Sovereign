# RELATIONAL-GEOMETRY-INTEGRATION-01 — Source Ledger

These sources support formal analogues only; none authorizes MAIA implementation by itself.

- Battaglia et al. (2018), **Relational inductive biases, deep learning, and graph networks**, arXiv:1806.01261. Entities, relations, compositional rules, graph networks.
- Bronstein et al. (2021), **Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges**, arXiv:2104.13478. Geometry/symmetry as architectural inductive bias.
- Kivelä et al. (2014), **Multilayer networks**, Journal of Complex Networks 2(3):203–271. Distinct layers and inter-layer structure.
- Battiston et al. (2021), **The physics of higher-order interactions in complex systems**, Nature Physics 17:1093–1098. Hypergraphs/simplicial complexes beyond pairwise edges.
- Zhang, Lucas & Battiston (2023), **Higher-order interactions shape collective dynamics differently in hypergraphs and simplicial complexes**, Nature Communications 14:1605. Representation choice can change higher-order dynamics.
- Bianchi et al. (2024), **Relational Event Modeling**, Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application 11:297–319. Temporal ordering of relational events carries information.
- Smolensky (1990), **Tensor product variable binding and the representation of symbolic structures in connectionist systems**, Artificial Intelligence 46:159–216. Role–filler binding and structured identity.
- Sukthanker et al. (2020), **Anaphora and coreference resolution: A review**, Information Fusion 59:139–162. Reference detection vs antecedent resolution.
- van der Knaap & van der Ham (2011), **How does the corpus callosum mediate interhemispheric transfer? A review**, Behavioural Brain Research 223(1):211–221. Interhemispheric communication with both integrative and inhibitory interpretations.
- de Haan et al. (2020), **Split-Brain: What We Know Now and Why This is Important for Understanding Consciousness**, Neuropsychology Review 30:224–233. Callosotomy disrupts broad functional integration without eliminating all unity.

## Boundary on symbolic sources

Astrological aspect systems, Elemental traditions, and related symbolic grammars are not entered into this ledger as empirical validation of cognition. They are maintained separately as **hypothesis-generating relational grammars**. A symbolic distinction earns computational standing only after operationalization and independent falsification.
