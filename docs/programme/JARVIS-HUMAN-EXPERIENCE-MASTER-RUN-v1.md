## 1 · Standing law — applies in every phase

These are not phase tasks. They bind every task in every phase, and a phase cannot exit while
violating one.

1. **The Human Question governs.** Every phase, task and artifact answers: *does this serve the human question — Self, Relationship, World — or does it make the human–AI relationship the destination?* AI is a participant inside the inquiry, never its object or center (charter §13–§14; *Why Soul Lab*).
2. **Direction of authority.** Authority moves upward only through authored experience (CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY, Invariant 16). Collective learning may inform design; it may never tell a member who they are. The system may not manufacture higher-order meaning.
3. **MAIA shapes; legitimacy is not self-certified (P4′).** No phase may certify Soullab's influence as legitimate by intent, transparency, or member satisfaction. Every phase that touches influence names the constraint, the disclosure, the inspectability, the corrective friction, the dispensability, and the accountable party (P13).
4. **External-witness gate.** A principle marked REQUIRED in v0.2 §8 may not move from CANDIDATE to canon in any phase without the named witness. REQUIRED is constitutionally blocking; no founder act or internal review waives it. RECOMMENDED may be absent only with the three-part record (why not obtained · what uncertainty remains · what reopens it).
5. **Prohibitions AP1–AP17 are in force as design constraints now**, CANDIDATE status notwithstanding — including AP15 Approval-Optimized Relationship, AP16 Memory as Leverage, AP17 Recurrence as Identity. No optimization toward approval, attachment, disclosure or continued use, anywhere in the run.
6. **Claim discipline.** Nothing is claimed above its rung (CLAIM_LADDER); state is a finding, never a decision (CLAIM_STATE_AUTHORITY); no figure goes public before paraphrase check; *we do not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's.*
7. **Consent and Sanctuary.** No member conversation serves research until the Phase 4 consent act exists and its boundary verifier passes in production. Sanctuary is excluded absolutely from every phase. No covert emotional A/B testing (Anti-Features).
8. **One mind, registered producers.** Any architecture work respects the Deep-Intelligence Gate (convergence before cognition) and CMT-01 (registered producers, one canonical turn, one renderer).
9. **Capacity, not engagement.** The measure is what the person becomes capable of beyond MAIA (Self capacity · World capacity, kept separate). The brain-training rule governs every capacity claim: *a gain on the practiced task is never, by itself, evidence of a gain in life.* Engagement is diagnostic, not success.
10. **The fork** (§8) is asked of every output before it is retained.

**Admissibility is not correctness (founder).** Passing the census gate establishes that a page is
admissible, not that its proposed interventions are correct. The R11 matrix and the scoring pass
are where surviving claims are compared rather than inherited; checkpointed-but-ungated pages
carry no ranked-map authority and their findings are evidence candidates only.

**Scoring rule (founder):** composite = impact × leverage ÷ risk with **risk on a bounded nonzero
scale 1–5** — never 0, so no "zero-risk" candidate becomes infinite by artifact. Every gap row also
carries **evidence state** (observed | inferred | unknown) and **confidence** (high | medium |
low). A high-impact unknown may rank highly *as an inquiry*; it must not quietly become a finding.

**Two classification rules for compilation (founder, 2026-09-06).** (1) A finding that touches an
existing sovereignty invariant or an open ADR (e.g. the reported OpenAI TTS egress outside the
allow-list) is classed **GOVERNANCE DECISION**, not an experimental gap: the map may establish and
rank its significance, but its endpoint is a founder/governance decision, never "run an experiment
and see." (2) A contradiction survives as a **contradiction** — a question about where interpretive
authority currently resides (e.g. inferred-profile depth shaping; undisclosed pattern
interruption/reframing; regeneration when the response conflicts with the system's elemental
frame) — and is never silently normalized into a feature gap. Both classes carry through the R11
matrix and the top ten with their class named.

**Remaining sequence for this phase:** agents complete → 13-page acceptance gate → cross-subsystem
deduplication → R11 matrix → score gaps → top ten → candidate answer to the governing question →
commit + push → **FOUNDER RANKING.** The ranked map authorizes no repair; its output is epistemic
and prioritizing. The founder ranking is the boundary where inquiry may later become intervention.

## 12 · The permanent Jarvis loop, and the fork

Once Phase 7 opens, the run becomes a loop. Each turn of the loop is a Stage 0–16 pass at
whatever scale the observation warrants:

```text
OBSERVE → QUESTION → RESEARCH → EVIDENCE → INTERPRET → HYPOTHESIS → PROTOTYPE → WITNESS
       → MEASURE → ADJUDICATE → CODIFY → MAIA → LIFE / WORLD → OBSERVE
```

With one crucial fork, asked at ADJUDICATE of every output, before CODIFY:

```text
DOES THIS INCREASE THE HUMAN'S CAPACITY BEYOND MAIA?

YES   → learn / retain      (CODIFY; principle may ascend the ladder via the witness gate)
NO    → refine / reject     (REFINE or REJECT; nothing enters MAIA)
HARM  → prohibit            (new AP row; design constraint immediately, canon by ruling)
```

"Beyond MAIA" is measured, not asserted: Self capacity and World capacity, separately; transfer
distance named; the brain-training rule applied; engagement read as diagnostic. Where the answer
cannot be known yet, the output is PARKED with the instrument that would answer it.

## 13 · Advance protocol — how the cockpit state changes

1. A phase EXIT GATE is checked by Jarvis against its own criteria, in writing, in the charter.
2. The FOUNDER STOP that closes the phase is recorded (a charter section quoting the act).
3. Only then does Jarvis edit `state` in §0: `current_phase`, `current_act`, `next_phase_on_exit`, `last_state_change`, and any authorization line the act changed. Nothing else in `state` moves without a founder act; the four NOT AUTHORIZED lines move only by explicit founder wording.
4. Phase 1 is the one automatic advance: on Phase 0.5 exit, the read-only census may begin without a separate opening act, because it changes nothing.
5. If an act reopens an earlier phase (a source check that changes v0.2; a finding that reopens a ruling), `state` records the reopening and the reason; the run does not pretend to be further along than it is.
6. Every state change is mirrored in one line in the session anchor (`CLAUDE.md` priority thread) so a fresh session lands on the right phase.
