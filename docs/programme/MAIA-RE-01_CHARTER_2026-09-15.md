## 1. What this lane is

A research lane to establish what MAIA already knows about *knowing* — the
distinctions that govern how the system relates to a member's material — and
only then to ask whether that discipline is transferable to a smaller model.

Jarvis is the **custodian** of this lane, not the teacher of MAIA.

---

## 2. Role boundary (binding)

```
JARVIS MAY
  discover
  trace provenance
  normalize
  compare
  generate falsifiers
  assemble datasets
  execute experiments
  score against adjudicated doctrine
  report collisions

JARVIS MAY NOT
  author relational doctrine
  resolve doctrinal collisions
  promote a principle to canon
  use member material
  declare a model "MAIA"
  change production routing
  train from unadjudicated repository material
```

The load-bearing clause is the last one. A statement enters the relational
epistemology canon **only** through explicit founder adjudication. Repetition
across the repository is not adjudication; frequency is not standing.

---

## 3. Doctrine states

```
DISCOVERED          found in the repository, provenance traced, NOT authority
COLLIDING           two or more sources give incompatible rules
FOUNDER-ADJUDICATED ruled on, not yet placed
CANONICAL           load-bearing
SUPERSEDED          replaced in place, never deleted
```

**No training example may be generated from `DISCOVERED` or `COLLIDING`
material.** This is the structural protection against Jarvis quietly
converting an architectural observation into MAIA doctrine because it
appeared often enough.

## 5. Acceptance criterion

> **A better MAIA imitation is not a PASS.
> A demonstrable increase in relational-epistemic discipline is.**

Stated as a falsifier: if the trained adapter reproduces MAIA's characteristic
sentences while failing adversarial cases that were never in training, the
result is a **FAIL**, regardless of how much it sounds like MAIA. The
experiment is designed to be able to return that answer.

## 7. Why the census comes first

The alternative is to write the doctrine from what a capable model believes
MAIA's epistemology *should* be. That would produce a coherent, plausible,
well-argued relational epistemology that no one in this project ever ratified
— and it would be indistinguishable in tone from the real one.

The census exists so that the difference between *discovered* and *authored*
stays visible at the moment it is cheapest to preserve.
