# RELATIONAL-GEOMETRY-INTEGRATION-01 — R7 / H2b Differentiation Under Pressure

**Status:** COMPLETE · RESEARCH ONLY
**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Question

Under equal compression pressure, does preserving differentiated AIR / WATER / EARTH / FIRE channels reduce signal loss and forced synthesis compared with a flat integrated representation?

## Design

Five synthetic multi-signal fixtures were represented twice under two conditions using production-equivalent `claude-sonnet-4-6`:

- **A_FLAT:** one integrated representation
- **B_DIFFERENTIATED:** four explicit channels, no final synthesis

Both conditions had a hard 70-word maximum. A blind zero-temperature decoder scored recoverable atomic signals, tension-pair preservation, missing-channel handling, and forced resolution.

## Result

| Measure | A flat | B differentiated |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Atom retention | 75/76 (98.68%) | **76/76 (100%)** |
| Tension pairs preserved | 33/34 (97.06%) | **34/34 (100%)** |
| Forced resolution | 1 | **0** |
| Missing-channel handling | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| Budget violations | 0 | 0 |

Under the fixed 70-word budget, differentiation produced a small but clean advantage: one additional atomic signal retained, one additional tension pair preserved, and zero forced resolutions.
## Adjudication

**H2 is narrowly supported under compression/contradiction pressure.** This does not establish that Elemental differentiation is globally superior to flat narrative representation. The earlier H2/H3 pilot showed that flat representation can preserve more immediately recoverable content on an easy small benchmark.

What H2b supports is the narrower operational claim: when multiple simultaneous signals must survive constrained compression, preserving differentiated channels can reduce overwrite and forced synthesis.

This is exactly the use case relevant to MAIA's Elemental architecture: differentiation is justified as a protection against premature collapse, not as an ontology of the person.

**H3 remains unconfirmed.** Explicit cross-layer mediation has not yet earned architectural standing; the pilot increased false relational structure. A separate mediated-integration experiment is required.
