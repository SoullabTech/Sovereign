### 0.2 · ⚠️ Consequence for Strand G — a precondition, recorded now

⭐ **Architecture and model quality are confoundable, and the confound runs in a direction that would mislead.**

Running the bake-off against an architecture that cannot represent non-local observations would measure **how well each model produces locally-representable findings** — systematically favouring the *weaker* developmental reader, and returning a result that looks like a model finding while actually being an architecture artifact.

⛔ **Worse, the §12 acceptance floor is frozen.** *"Did she help me see something genuinely useful?"* would be answered against a room that cannot show what the better model saw — **locking a misattributed result against the wrong thing.**

```text
RECORDED AS AN INPUT TO STRAND G's ARMING PROTOCOL (research lane §13):
the registered protocol should state the representational capability of the
architecture the models are evaluated through — or the bake-off measures
the room and reports it as the reader.
```

**Not a reason to change models now. A reason not to run the bake-off early.** The right question, later: *given an architecture capable of representing developmental thought, which model is the best developmental partner for this writer?*

Suppose the Work carries: *a psychologically and spiritually grounded book beginning in lived experience and gradually revealing a coherent system* · qualities to protect — *intimacy, depth, mystery, intellectual clarity without over-explanation, my natural spoken cadence* · influences — *Jung for staying with symbolic ambiguity, Campbell for making large patterns intelligible* · and *not a textbook, not a spiritual instruction manual, not polished generic self-help.*


### ⭐⭐ Finding A/M4-2 — and yet A may *honour* the North Star better than any architecture that displays it

The interesting result. **The North Star need not be an object at all.**

```text
AS AN OBJECT       it must live somewhere, be returned to, be consulted
AS A DISPOSITION   it shapes how MAIA speaks AT EVERY LOCUS,
                   and is displayed nowhere
```

In A, the second is the only option available — and it may be the **right** answer generally. The D8 question was: *can I tell MAIA what I'm trying to make without that becoming another form, prompt, or set of rules hanging over the writing?*

⭐ **A North Star that is never shown cannot hang over the writing.** A's inability to display it is, for this one requirement, indistinguishable from the correct design.

```text
A FAILS to REPRESENT the North Star
A may SUCCEED at HONOURING it — precisely because it cannot display it
```

**Carried as a cross-candidate question, not an A finding**: when B and C are walked, ask whether their ability to *show* the North Star is a strength or the beginning of a manifesto.
