## Mathematical provenance and distinctions

- **Conceptual spaces:** Gärdenfors (2000), Gärdenfors & Osta-Vélez (2026), RGR-01 source corpus. They motivate regions and similarity but do not specify this experiment's semantics.
- **Structure mapping:** Gentner (1983) and later relational analogical work; a serious non-geometric baseline.
- **Grassmannians / subspace semantics:** candidate model family for finite collections of representations; requires fixed rank/ambient geometry. Subspaces are not necessarily experiences.
- **Sheaves / local-to-global methods:** mathematical inspiration for heterogeneous perspectives; the toy solver in this packet is **plain finite CSP enumeration**, not cohomology or a deployed sheaf ML layer. A synthetic inconsistent cycle is not a diagnosis of anyone's worldview.
- **Temporal networks / transport:** FLOW_D architectural proposal, typed and provenance-bound; October RGR-05B's sampler failed before neural evaluation.
- **Positive geometry / amplituhedron:** *source of bounded inspiration* for admissible regions, boundaries and multiple decompositions; no bridge from planar N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills physics to consciousness or human meaning is asserted.
- **Neuroscientific abstract/social maps:** empirical support for specific relational mapping in studied neural tasks, not proof of RGR or panentheism.
- **Sophontic / Julian:** its publicly described Geometric Reasoning is an internal-model reasoning and perturbation programme. Neither its proprietary implementation nor its promotional scaling claims have been independently validated in this packet.
- **Zhou et al., “The Geometry of Reasoning: Flowing Logics in Representation Space”:** separate academic research about reasoning trajectories, not Sophontic's method; no genealogical identity.
- **Burger/Stone and Elemental Alchemy:** source-attributed phenomenological and philosophical origins; not a validated cellular energy mechanism, a clinical diagnostic key or ground-truth dataset.

The companion team paper *Relational Geometry of Meaning: RGR/AIN and Sophontic's Geometric Reasoning* (October 15; ChatGPT Library, `Soullab Knowledge/Relational Flow and Embodied Consciousness/Papers`) supplies comparative exposition. It is a **working paper**, not an automatically admitted source corpus in MAIA. The research laws above remain authoritative.
