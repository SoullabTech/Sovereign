## Finding A3-F1 — the current floor contains mixed-class objects

`MAIA_RUNTIME_PROMPT` is classified wholesale as `floor.runtime_prompt` in the canonical-turn shadow floor. Source inspection shows it is not one kind of thing. It contains identity/capability law, memory posture, relational orientation, response-shape coaching, rupture scripts and few-shots, micro-calibration rules, conversational-style conventions, and two interpolated prompt systems (`AIN_INTEGRATIVE_ALCHEMY_PROMPT` and `MEMORY_CANON_GUARD_PROMPT`).

Therefore **"runtime prompt is constitutional" is too coarse a classification for minimum-floor work.** The unit that A3 must classify is the obligation, not the string or file that currently carries it.

The same, at smaller scale, is true of `MAIA_RELATIONAL_SPEC`: identity language, relational values, response templates and repair behavior coexist in one block.

## Finding A3-F2 — structural enforcement exists, but only for bounded properties

Repository truth supplies several deterministic precedents:
- `scrubMemoryAmnesia()` refuses a defined family of false self-amnesia claims;
- `scrubIdentityDisclaimers()` catches provider/identity and memory-disclaimer shapes on the `maiaService` tail;
- `enforceIdentityPredicateConstraint()` constrains a bounded family of system-authored member-identity assertions at final egress;
- Presence Mode can deterministically remove some question/advancement shapes when that mode triggers;
- the Selflet delivery guard structurally ensures a required acknowledgment is delivered exactly once.

The constitutional checks for floor invariance and identity-predicate egress both pass at this source. The memory-canon unit suite passes `51 / 51`.

This proves that **structural enforcement is an existing architectural technique**, not that every semantic obligation can leave cognition.

## Finding A3-F3 — the canonical floor is precedent, not current serving substitution

`lib/maia/canonical-turn/floor.ts` and `render.ts` already model a typed, provenance-bearing constitutional floor. The renderer's own header states: `M1 standing: zero live callers. Becomes the tier prompt seam at M3.`

Therefore the canonical-turn apparatus is valuable evidence that obligations can be named and typed outside prose, but it is not evidence that live serving paths already enforce the floor through that renderer.

No present prompt block may be declared redundant merely because a shadow producer exists.

## Finding A3-F4 — semantic law and emission law are different constitutional species

Some invariants concern whether an outgoing sentence may be emitted. Those can sometimes be evaluated after generation.

Other invariants govern **how evidence is perceived and related before generation**. Examples include:
- a signal is provisional rather than a verdict;
- member statement and MAIA inference have different standing;
- contradictory signals may remain differentiated;
- new authoritative evidence may reorganize the whole;
- a relation must remain traceable to its sources.

An egress regex cannot reconstruct a distinction the model was never given or undo a synthesis that collapsed provenance upstream. These remain cognition/substrate questions even if some downstream language can also be guarded.

## Finding A3-F5 — House Knowledge is evidence, not constitution

The canonical producer registry itself classifies `house.platform_knowledge` as `authored` with `authority: situate`, while the four `floor.*` producers are `constitutional`.

That distinction should govern A3. House Knowledge can be mandatory standing evidence today without becoming constitutional law merely because it is always present.

## Provisional minimum invariant set — not minimum prose

Source plus A2 currently supports these obligations as load-bearing invariants:

1. **Evidence status:** derived synthesis is not primary evidence.
2. **Source standing:** member, practitioner/collaborator, system inference and MAIA interpretation remain distinguishable.
3. **Member authority and correction:** later member self-report can revise present standing without erasing history.
4. **Epistemic humility:** symbolic/computed/inferred signals remain provisional and corrigible.
5. **Difference without collapse:** disagreement among signals can remain meaningful.
6. **Truthful absence:** MAIA cannot pretend access to evidence that is not represented.
7. **Capability/speech-act honesty:** MAIA cannot claim a Keep/account capability outcome the substrate has not established.
8. **Dynamic revisability:** present authoritative evidence can reorganize prior Gestalt.
9. **Speaker/product identity boundary:** serving responses retain MAIA's governed product identity without provider leakage.

A3 does **not** yet establish the minimum token representation of these invariants. Some may become typed substrate properties, some deterministic egress properties, and some may still require concise model-visible semantic law.
