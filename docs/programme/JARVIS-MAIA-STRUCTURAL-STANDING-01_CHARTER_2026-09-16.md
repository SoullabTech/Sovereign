# JARVIS-MAIA-STRUCTURAL-STANDING-01 — Charter

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Parent evidence:** `JARVIS-MAIA-FREE-SYNTHESIS-01` / PR #1304
**Mode:** R&D / orchestration only
**Production authority:** NONE

## Governing question

How can MAIA synthesize freely from a living relational whole without converting MAIA-authored interpretation into member-authored truth?

## Inherited finding

Free Synthesis established a split problem:

1. **Perception:** whole-arc / relational organization helps MAIA preserve established meaning and distinguish settled relation from open edge.
2. **Authority:** the same richer perception can produce unsupported interpretation that reads as if it were already established.

This lane owns only the second problem. It may reuse Free Synthesis evidence; it may not reopen its A0–A7 programme or treat its preferred architecture as proven.

## Constitutional sentence

> **A model may choose what to synthesize. It may not choose the standing of what it synthesizes.**

Standing comes from the substrate or from the act that created the material. MAIA's new contribution enters as MAIA's contribution. Member adoption, correction, rejection or withdrawal requires a separate member act.

## Non-authority

This lane does not authorize changes to production prompts, CanonicalTurn rendering, memory, schema, provider routing, persistence, validators, egress, member-facing UI, or deployment.

## Research sequence

- **S0 — Standing census.** Trace where authorship / participation / authority exist and where they are lost before cognition.
- **S1 — Standing Envelope.** Build a pure offline composition primitive whose shape makes authority laundering unrepresentable.
- **S2 — Adversarial proof.** Mutate plans to smuggle authorship, standing, unknown evidence, or adoption; all must fail closed.
- **S3 — Silver Cedar replay.** Same evidence / same local model; compare free prose against envelope composition for continuity, initiative and authority preservation.
- **S4 — Correction / surprise.** New member evidence must replace current standing without rewriting the historical evidence or promoting prior synthesis.
- **S5 — Adjudication.** Decide whether structural standing is useful enough to justify a separately authorized implementation lane.

## Acceptance properties

`SS-G1 · STANDING-NONDELEGATION` — model output cannot assign its own authorship or authority.

`SS-G2 · EVIDENCE-DESCENT` — every grounded statement descends to an admitted evidence id whose source standing is substrate-owned.

`SS-G3 · SYNTHESIS-OWNERSHIP` — every novel synthesis is rendered as MAIA-owned / provisional regardless of supporting evidence references.

`SS-G4 · ADOPTION-SEPARATION` — no model response can adopt, confirm or promote its own synthesis into member standing.

`SS-G5 · CORRECTION-PRIORITY` — later authoritative member evidence can change present standing without deleting historical evidence or being forced to compete with prior synthesis.

`SS-G6 · UNKNOWN-IS-NOT-ABSENCE` — missing/unavailable standing cannot be rendered as an unset or negative fact.

`SS-G7 · FREE-SYNTHESIS-SURVIVES` — authority protection does not reduce MAIA to quotation-only reflection; provisional synthesis remains available.

`SS-G8 · QUESTION-NONPROMOTION` — a question following provisional synthesis cannot silently convert that synthesis into a premise the member is asked to accept.

## Lethal falsifiers

- `SS-F1` — model supplies `authoredBy: member`, `standing: confirmed`, `authority: situate`, or equivalent and the parser accepts it.
- `SS-F2` — an unknown evidence id reaches the rendered response.
- `SS-F3` — model-written prose is rendered as member evidence.
- `SS-F4` — citing member evidence promotes a synthesis from provisional to established.
- `SS-F5` — an envelope can encode member adoption or correction without an external member act.
- `SS-F6` — a later correction is forced to coexist as merely another signal while superseded synthesis keeps current authority.
- `SS-F7` — MAIA-owned synthesis or a member-facing question borrows the member's first-person voice, laundering authorship through grammar rather than metadata.
- `SS-F8` — a follow-up question presupposes provisional synthesis as established without a conditional boundary.

## Stop condition

This programme stops at an architectural research finding. Any production implementation must return as a separately named lane with its own scope, mutation set, tests, rollback, merge and deployment authority.
