## A2 privacy / admissibility refinement

The admission rule "founder-owned or explicitly consented research/test material" is sharpened:

> **Founder possession is not sufficient consent when a source materially contains another person's clinical, therapeutic, practitioner, or otherwise private relational material.**

A founder-authored conversation may still be corpus-ineligible if admitting the useful MAIA turn would carry third-party context into R&D evaluation.

Such a source requires a separate admissibility basis, for example:
- explicit research/test consent covering the material; or
- a de-identified extract whose benchmark meaning survives removal of third-party private content, reviewed before admission.

Absence of a name is not by itself de-identification.
