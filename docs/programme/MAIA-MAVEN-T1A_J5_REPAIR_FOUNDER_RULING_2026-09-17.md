## R9 — KEEP / EXACT REFERENT

**RULING:** Keep remains a family concept. A Reflection Capsule is an interpretive artifact and is
not the default referent of the member saying **“Keep this.”**

In `/maia`, a Keep request must first resolve the exact material the member intends to preserve.
If the referent is ambiguous, MAIA asks. MAIA may not substitute a recent-turn window, summary,
gold-line selection, inferred pattern, or other derived meaning for that referent.

Interpretive distillation may occur only as a separately named act when the member explicitly
asks for that assistance.

**Immediate repair consequence:** the conversational Keep recognizer may FACILITATE exact
selection, but it may not route `keep_material` or `open_keep` into the Reflection Capsule
distiller. No new referent resolver is invented in this lane.

## R10 — KEEP / REOPEN SEPARATION

**RULING:** KEEP never grants REOPEN.

New member Memory objects are private to ambient MAIA retrieval by default. Ambient return
requires a separate, explicit member authorization with durable provenance.

A `return_preference` value that may have arisen from a database default is not evidence of that
authorization. Existing rows whose return authority cannot be proved are authority-ambiguous and
may not be treated as explicitly authorized merely because they contain `contextual_doorway` or
`ritual_review_opt_in`.

**Immediate repair consequence:** new keeps default private; the existing member gesture that
sets return preference is the authority-bearing act; legacy rows fail closed until such a gesture
occurs.

## R11 — T1-A SCOPE

**RULING:** T1-A is **Press Keep READ** for this tranche: the already-governed verbatim manuscript
Keep read path. Generic “Keep” vocabulary does not silently unify Press passages, conversational
member-marked moments, Field atoms, or Reflection Capsules.

Every other Keep implementation retains a qualified identity and requires its own governed
crossing before T1-A may read it.
