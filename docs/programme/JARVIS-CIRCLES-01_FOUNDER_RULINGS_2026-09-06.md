## FR-01 · Ecosystem consent law — **RATIFIED**

> **Connection may increase reach, but may never lower the consent level of what is being
> connected.**
>
> A more connected field never acquires greater rights to source material merely because it is
> larger or more connected.

Crossing remains **explicit · representational · destination-specific · revocable where revocation
is possible · opaque by default.** **The source remains untouched.**

## FR-02 · Field ontology — **RATIFIED**

```text
PERSONAL        the individual human field
DYADIC          a two-person relational field
CIRCLE          a bounded sovereign relational field among three or more persons,
                without any ratified numerical maximum
CONSTELLATION   a field whose primary participating units are sovereign Circles
COMMONS         a larger shared-interest ecology of persons and Circles
CO-LAB          an intentional field organized around shared work
```

Fields are defined by **unit, purpose, authority and membrane** — never by arbitrary headcount.
⛔ **No numerical maximum is ratified for Circle.**

## FR-03 · Dyadic Field is distinct — **RATIFIED**

A dyad is a **first-class relational field distinct from a Circle.**

> This is not a statement that dyadic relationship is lesser. It is an ontological distinction:
> two-person relational dynamics have a different geometry from a plural Circle.

**Circle begins with plurality, not merely connection.** The ontology is therefore **six fields.**
⛔ **No maximum Circle size may be inferred from this ruling.**

## FR-04 · Witnessing vs structured inquiry — **RATIFIED (research correction)**

`Feel → Contribute → Browse` applies **specifically to structured inquiry** where preservation of
independent perception matters. **It does not govern ordinary presence in a living Circle.**

```text
STRUCTURED INQUIRY                        LIVING CIRCLE FIELD
Feel                                      Arrive
→ Contribute independently                → Witness / Offer / Respond / Rest
→ Encounter others                        → Participate when and how one chooses
→ Deliberate / witness
→ Integrate
```

> **A member may witness without being compelled to contribute. Quiet presence is a legitimate mode
> of participation.**

⛔ The existing doctrine wording returns for **explicit amendment later**. Doctrine is not silently
rewritten while implementing the verifier.

## FR-05 · Facilitator and removal authority — **RATIFIED NARROWLY**

**Facilitator authority is procedural, not interpretive.**

| A facilitator **may** | A facilitator **may not** |
|---|---|
| hold the container | determine what the Circle means |
| orient members | declare the correct interpretation of a member |
| open and close structured inquiries | define consensus members have not given |
| facilitate participation | **remove someone merely for disagreement, difference or discomfort** |
| invite attention to boundaries | |
| initiate procedural boundary actions | |

**Removal** is a **boundary or safety action, never an interpretive judgment.** Constitutional
minimum:

1. An ordinary member **cannot** remove another member.
2. An authorized facilitator **may** enact removal where an explicit Circle boundary or safety
   condition has been breached.
3. The grounds **must be recorded.**
4. Removal **must cut access.**
5. Removal **must revoke that member's Circle shares exactly as leaving does.**
6. Removal **must never alter or delete the member's original source material.**
7. The removed member **must have a route to request review by someone other than the person who
   enacted the removal.**

The review institution and richer governance remain open (**CA-10**) beyond VERIFY.
*The verifier proves the authority boundary and the technical consequences. It does not adjudicate
human conflict.*

## FR-06 · Discovery privacy — **RATIFIED**

Circle discovery may use **declared interests only.** It may **never** derive recommendations from:

```text
MAIA conversation · memory atoms · semantic memory · anchors · inferred themes
psychological interpretation · private Circle membership · behavioral surveillance
```

**Interest declarations are contextual** — defaulted to the relevant **Commons**, never silently
creating a global interest profile. A member may later explicitly choose to reuse a declaration
elsewhere; **reuse is another member act** (**CA-11**).

> **This deliberate limitation is part of Soullab's sovereignty model** — not a capability gap.

## FR-07 · Release authority — **RATIFIED**

Across Circle → Constellation → Commons:

> **No facilitator, majority, system or larger field acquires the right to re-release a member's
> authored material.**

**Individually authored material:** release authority remains with the **author**, who explicitly
chooses the destination representation.

**Jointly or collectively authored material:**
- crosses only through a **new representation explicitly created or approved for that crossing**;
- **every contributor whose authored material is represented retains consent authority over their
  contribution**;
- a facilitator may **propose or facilitate** a release but **may not consent for other members.**

> ⛔ **Until a collective-release mechanism satisfying this rule exists, collective Circle material
> does not cross.** (**CA-12**)

A Constellation or Commons sees **the representation released to it, never the Circle interior that
produced it.**

## FR-08 · Never-crosses — **RATIFIED WITH CORRECTION**

**The draft hard list was not ratified verbatim.** Its MAIA clause — *"private MAIA conversation
content into any shared field, by any path, including summary"* — was **overbroad**, and would have
prohibited the very thing the Personal → Circle membrane was designed for: **a member choosing a
piece of their own experience and offering a representation of it.**

> **Privacy forbids ambient appropriation. It does not forbid a person's sovereign act of sharing
> their own material.**

This was Jarvis's drafting error. Recorded, corrected, not repeated.

### ABSOLUTE
1. **Sanctuary content never crosses**, by any mechanism, **including member request within
   Sanctuary.**

### NEVER AUTOMATICALLY OR BY INFERENCE
2. **Inferred material** never enters a shared field without the already-required explicit member
   ratification and collective eligibility.
3. **Private MAIA conversation content never crosses automatically** — not through inference,
   system summarization, recommendation, memory mining, or ambient context.
   ✅ **A member outside Sanctuary may explicitly select or author a representation of their own
   MAIA exchange and intentionally Offer to Circle**, subject to the normal consent and revocation
   rules.
4. **A Circle's interior** never becomes visible to a Constellation, Commons, other Circle, or
   Co-Lab. **Only an explicitly released representation crosses.**
5. **Membership** never transfers as an implicit consequence of any crossing.
6. **Identity** never crosses a membrane without the member's explicit act, where identity
   disclosure is not already inherent to the destination relationship.
7. **Counts, scores, ranks, streaks, badges, leaderboards and trust levels** do not become Circle
   social-status mechanisms.

### REPRESENTATIONAL INVARIANT
8. **No membrane crossing is a live pointer giving the destination continuing access to the source
   field.**

## FR-10 · Multiplication law — **RATIFIED**

> **Circles scale by multiplication, not enlargement.**

**Rationale, as ratified:** enlargement increasingly makes recognizability, participation,
facilitation, differentiation and repair difficult to preserve. Multiplication and nesting allow
collective scale without requiring one relational field to absorb unlimited complexity.

⛔ **No numerical threshold attaches to this law.**

## FR-11 · FR-03 lifecycle clarification — **RATIFIED** *(added after the first VERIFY run)*

> **FR-03 describes an ACTIVE Circle, not every instant of Circle formation.**

```text
FORMING   Circle intentionally initiated but not yet a plural relational field   (1–2 persons)
ACTIVE    at least three active members; may exercise active-Circle semantics    (3+ persons)
```

- A **one-person Circle record is not intrinsically unconstitutional.**
- A **two-person forming Circle is not yet an active Circle**; its geometry is dyadic (FR-03).
- The Constitution distinguishes **administrative existence** from **relational constitution**.
- ⛔ **Never** encode as `CHECK(member_count >= 3)`. ⛔ No maximum is ratified.

> **Creation is not constitution. You can create a Circle alone; you cannot constitute one alone.**

**Consequence:** CA-04 is no longer wholly optional — the minimum `FORMING | ACTIVE` distinction is
required before REPAIR can close S4. The rest of the lifecycle stays experimental.

## FR-12 · Facilitator authority is not widened — **RATIFIED** *(after the R2 run)*

- ⛔ **Do not widen FR-05 removal authority from `facilitator` to `helper`.**
- ⛔ **Do not automatically promote a Circle creator from `helper` to `facilitator`.**
- `helper`, `facilitator` and `member` remain **distinct roles.**

The substrate already draws this line intentionally: structured inquiries permit **helper or
facilitator**; **removal is a materially stronger power.**

> **Helper is not facilitator. Facilitation must be conferred by an explicit later governance act.**

**The absence of any facilitator-assignment pathway is an INVOKE / governance gap — not a reason to
corrupt R2.** The removal contract can be correctly built before anyone holds that authority in a
live Circle. **Keep the HTTP route** (a service with no reachable contract is the built≠wired
ambiguity); it stays founder-gated with every other Circle route, so its existence opens nothing.
**Facilitator appointment is not built.**

## FR-13 · Constitution state is derived, never stored — **RATIFIED**

FR-03/FR-11 are implemented as a **derived constitutional state**, not a stored lifecycle status.

```text
CircleConstitutionState = 'forming' | 'active'

active membership count  < 3   →  FORMING
active membership count >= 3   →  ACTIVE
```

- **FORMING** — the Circle has been intentionally initiated but is **not presently constituted as a
  plural relational field.** Covers **both** initial formation **and re-formation** after an active
  Circle falls below plurality.
- **ACTIVE** — three or more active members are presently participating, so the field satisfies the
  minimum plurality condition. **No maximum attaches.**

⛔ **Do not add a `circles.lifecycle_status` column merely to make S4 pass.** It would duplicate
membership truth and create a drift the system could not adjudicate — *stored = ACTIVE while
membership count = 2.* Membership rows already hold the authoritative fact. If a persistent
**historical** fact proves genuinely necessary, **return it for founder adjudication** rather than
adding a mutable lifecycle column.

**Transitions are facts, not discretionary system acts.** No timer, no administrator act, no
subjective inference. ⛔ On falling below three: **do not delete the Circle · do not recruit · do
not split · do not interpret the cause · do not describe it as failure.** It simply does not
presently satisfy active-Circle plurality.

**One canonical derivation path** — `getCircleConstitutionState()`. ⛔ No `COUNT(*) >= 3` reproduced
around the codebase · no database `CHECK(member_count >= 3)` · no cached member count as
constitutional authority.

> **FORMING and ACTIVE are not stages the software declares. They are constitutional facts derived
> from whether plurality is presently there.** The richer lifecycle — maturation · rest ·
> completion · birth · separation — is where human interpretation and member acts return, and stays
> separate (CA-04).

## Principle · Fact derivation vs meaning declaration *(founder, cross-lane)*

⚠️ **Not ratified as Circle law.** Articulated by the founder as a general architectural principle
while ruling on FR-13, and recorded here so it is not lost.

> **Prefer derived facts over declared system state when the authoritative fact already exists and
> the derivation is deterministic.**

Deliberately narrower than *all state should be derived*:

| | |
|---|---|
| **FACT DERIVATION** | the system reads what is already true — a Circle's **plurality** |
| **MEANING DECLARATION** | a person authors what it means — whether a Circle is **complete**, whether a relationship is **repaired**, whether something new is **being born** |

The second class requires **human acts of meaning and authority** and must never be derived. This
names precisely why FR-13 works, and why CA-04's richer lifecycle (maturation · rest · completion ·
birth · separation) has to stay separate from it.
