## 3. The three contract questions

### 3.1 What member act legitimately invokes Keep?

Distinguish, and do not let them collapse:

- the act of **keeping** (a formation gesture — creates the object);
- the act of **invoking** kept material (a retrieval request — reads existing objects);
- an **ambient surfacing** of kept material (⛔ neither of the above, and not chartered).

⭐ Only the second is in scope. ⛔ A design in which conversation *implies* invocation is the
failure mode to refuse: it converts every mention into a retrieval and makes the member's act
unnecessary.

**Owed:** the positive intents that invoke, the negative intents that must NOT, and a stated
answer to whether invocation requires the member's words to name keeping at all.

### 3.2 What exactly is being kept?

Under **R8**, `KEEP` is the family concept and every implementation carries a **qualified
identity**:

```text
KEEP
│
├── [Press-qualified Keep] ......... app/api/sovereign/keeps/route.ts — verbatim marked
│                                    manuscript passages
├── [Psyche/portfolio-qualified Keep] app/api/psyche/portfolio/keep/route.ts — a formation
│                                    event typed to MemoryAtomSourceType
└── [Capture implementation] ....... House destination `keeps` → /maia/keep-capture
```

**Owed:** the qualified names themselves (this lane proposes; the founder rules), and a decision
on **which qualified Keep or Keeps T1-A's invocation may reach.**

⛔⛔ **Reaching all three because the member said one word "keeps" is forbidden.** That is a
capability arriving through vocabulary ambiguity rather than a governed act — the defect class
`JARVIS-CIRCLES-01` already named against "Commons".

### 3.3 How does MAIA assist without becoming the selector?

The governing doctrine is already earned and is **not reopened**:

> **Keeps may order what the member has chosen; they never select on the member's behalf.**

`app/api/sovereign/keeps/route.ts` states it structurally: *"⛔ This route ORDERS but never
SELECTS… It does not choose which of a member's lines is most beautiful, relevant, or timely —
that would be the system curating the member's book, which no gesture underneath it
authorizes."*

⭐⭐ **The hard part is that MAIA is a conversational surface, and conversation is intrinsically
selective.** Returning twenty-four kept lines into a spoken turn is not possible; *something*
narrows them. The contract must say what legitimately narrows, and by whose authority:

- **member-supplied constraint** (*"the ones from the third chapter"*) — the member selects;
- **the route's own ordering** (recency of the member's act) — already doctrinal;
- ⛔ **relevance to the current conversation** — this is the system selecting, and is exactly
  what the doctrine forbids;
- ⛔ **affinity, score, or salience** of any kind.

**Owed:** a rule for what MAIA does when the eligible set exceeds what a turn can carry, that is
not covert selection. ⭐ *Refusing to narrow, and saying so, is an admissible answer.*
