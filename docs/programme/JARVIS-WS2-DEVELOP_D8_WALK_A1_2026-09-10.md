```text

what the architecture added  "...HERE"               (free, unrequested)
```

⭐ **"Here" is information, and it cost the writer nothing.** They were not asked to name the problem, choose a lens, or select a scope. **The Margin passes Moment 1 without ever asking the writer to explain themselves** — which is the specific thing Moment 1 was built to test.
