## 0 · The rule being applied

> **The programme does not manufacture a next stage merely to satisfy its own sequence.**

U1 completing does not open U2. Every proposed observation must carry five fields, and:

> ⛔ **If no proposed observation passes all five, U2 closes unrun.** That is a legitimate outcome.
> There is no obligation to manufacture `[O]` merely because the taxonomy contains `[O]`.

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Unresolved proposition** | What precisely do we still not know? |
| **Evidence gap** | Why can `[D]`, `[E]`, existing research or inference not settle it? |
| **Discriminating observation** | What would we observe that separates the competing possibilities? |
| **Competitive consequence** | What conclusion *in UARE-01* changes depending on the answer? |
| **Jurisdiction check** | Can the result stay competitive evidence rather than becoming an internal MAIA requirement? |

**Invalid justifications, named so they cannot be smuggled in:** *we haven't looked at the product
yet* · *it would complete the study* · *we might notice something useful* · *we need firsthand
experience* · *it could inspire MAIA* · *we should see how polished it feels.* Each may be personally
interesting; none establishes research necessity.

### ⛔ C-3 · Source chips — **FAILS: competitive consequence**

### ⛔ C-4 · Practitioner disclosure at point of sharing — **FAILS: competitive consequence**

### ⛔ C-5 · What an actual export contains — **FAILS: competitive consequence (narrowly)**

### ⛔ C-6 · AI-to-AI authority mechanism — **FAILS: discriminating observation**
Proposition: whether Uare solves multi-producer authority (§7). **No observation discriminates.**
Internal orchestration is not visible from a product surface; a walkthrough cannot separate *solved

## 2 · ⭐ C-7 · The one candidate that passes

**Does a `MODEL-OF-ME` system, in practice, ever decline to answer as the person, hedge, or mark its
own uncertainty — or does it fabricate in their voice?**

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| **Unresolved proposition** | Whether the fork in charter §3 is as sharp in practice as it is in their language. *"Always you"* is a marketing register; it is not known whether the product has any refusal, hedge, or uncertainty-marking behaviour when asked something its corpus cannot ground. |
| **Evidence gap** | `[M]` asserts the opposite of hedging by construction. `[D]` is silent — terms and pricing say nothing about epistemic behaviour. No `[E]` located covers it. **Inference cannot settle it**: a system can market as total identity and still refuse in practice. |
| **Discriminating observation** | With a wholly fabricated persona whose corpus we authored, ask the Individual AI something **provably outside** that corpus. Three outcomes separate cleanly: **(a)** fabricates confidently in the persona's voice · **(b)** hedges or marks the limit · **(c)** declines. ⭐ The synthetic-only constraint of charter §8 is what makes this measurable — *we would know exactly what the corpus does and does not contain.* |
| **Competitive consequence** | **§4 and the primary discriminator are currently pure `[I]`.** Outcome (a) confirms the fork as stated. **Outcomes (b) or (c) mean the fork is softer than we have written it, and §4 must be weakened.** ⭐ This is the field the other six fail: the observation can **falsify our own interpretation**, not only decorate it. |
| **Jurisdiction check** | ⚠️ **The real risk in the set.** Observing a well-made refusal is exactly the sort of thing that becomes *"MAIA should do X."* It is containable: the deliverable is a **correction to §4's wording**, never a MAIA behaviour. ⛔ If the finding cannot be written as an amendment to our reading of *them*, it does not leave U2. |

**Open questions attached to C-7, not resolved here:** whether creating an Individual AI requires a
paid tier (⛔ a contract with a competitor is a founder decision, §8.5) · whether their onboarding can
be completed without a voice sample (⛔ §8.4 forbids cloning a real person's voice; a synthesized
sample or an unobserved-and-recorded skip) · how much fabricated corpus is needed before the system
will answer at all.
