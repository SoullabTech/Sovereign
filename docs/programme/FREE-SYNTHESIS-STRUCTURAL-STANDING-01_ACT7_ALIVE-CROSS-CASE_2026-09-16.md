## Main finding

ACT 7 separates architecture from synthesis capacity:

> **The standing substrate can preserve authorship, present-turn authority, reversible lineage, non-restart, and free interpretation across a non-correction relational-field case. The richness of the resulting synthesis still depends materially on the model doing the synthesizing.**

Structural standing is therefore not a replacement for intelligence. Its purpose is to stop intelligence from acquiring authority it does not possess.

The 8B / 32B difference also argues against solving flattened synthesis by adding more behavioral prompt prose. With the same standing architecture and same 2,017-character research prompt, synthesis breadth changed materially with model capacity.
