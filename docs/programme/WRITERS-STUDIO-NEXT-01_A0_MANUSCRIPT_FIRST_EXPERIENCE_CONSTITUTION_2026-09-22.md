## I. Why this programme exists

Writer's Studio has accumulated real governed capability — observation identity, evidence binding, coverage records, revision custody, editorial scope law, support facets, convergence law. Each was earned against its own falsifiers.

What it does not yet have is **a governing law for the experience those capabilities compose into**.

The consequence is visible and structural: capability lanes have each been constituted at their own altitude, and the writer's actual encounter — open the manuscript, notice something, talk about it, understand it, change it, undo it — has never been the object any constitution governs. It has been an emergent residue of lanes that were each individually disciplined.

This programme makes the experience itself a governed object.

It does so **without reopening any lane that has already earned its law.**

## II. What "above" means here

This constitution sits **above** `WRITERS-STUDIO-EDITORIAL-READING-01` and its sibling programmes in **constitutional hierarchy only**.

> **"Above" means: this document establishes the larger product within which those programmes have a defined place.
> It does not mean: this document absorbs their rules, restates their law, or gates their implementation.**

Three explicit consequences, binding:

1. **No absorption.** `EDITORIAL-READING-01 / A0` is preserved **unchanged**. Its §I–XV remain the authority on Editorial Reading. Nothing in this document amends, weakens, widens, or re-ratifies any clause of it.
2. **No reopening.** Subordination is not an occasion to revisit a ratified sibling. A lane beneath this constitution does not require re-adjudication merely because a constitution now names its place.
3. **No implementation dependency.** A subordinate programme is not blocked on this one. `EDITORIAL-READING-01` may proceed through its own A1–A6 under its own authority. Hierarchy orders **meaning**, not **build order**.

The inverse also binds: a subordinate programme may not amend this constitution from below. Where a subordinate lane's law and this constitution genuinely conflict, that is a founder question, not a precedence rule either lane may apply to itself.

## VI. The experience law

Each law is marked **NEW** (constituted here, at experience altitude) or **APPLICATION** (an existing ratified law applied to the experience object, creating no new authority).

### E1 — Manuscript primacy is structural, not decorative · NEW

The Work occupies the primary surface. Conversation, analysis, and revision are arranged **around** it and return **into** it.

A layout in which the manuscript is a narrow rail beside a dominant assistant surface contradicts this constitution, whatever the assistant surface contains.

### E2 — One conversation, many scopes · NEW

Whole Work, chapter, passage, and sentence are **scopes of one editorial conversation**, not separate products with separate memories.

Changing scope does not begin a new relationship, discard what was said, or require the writer to re-establish context.

### E3 — Nothing in the Work changes without an authored act · APPLICATION (L5, Convergence spine)

MAIA proposes. The writer decides.

There is no path — no shortcut, no confirmation-by-default, no convenience affordance — by which manuscript text changes without an act the writer performed knowing what it would do.

### E4 — Every change is legible, reversible, and attributed · APPLICATION (L5) + NEW (attribution clause)

For any change to the Work the writer can see **what changed**, **where**, **why it was proposed**, and **what it was before** — and can undo it.

**NEW:** provenance distinguishes **writer-authored** from **MAIA-proposed-and-adopted** text, durably. This is the operative form of the FACETS-01 §9.3 rider: authorship authority is guaranteed by nothing unless the record can answer *how much of this prose did MAIA write*.

*No writer should ever wonder: what did MAIA just change?*

### E5 — No dead end, no loss of place · APPLICATION (Convergence spine acceptance conditions)

From any state the writer can return to the Work at the location they came from.

Reading, discussing, comparing, applying, and undoing are all recoverable positions. The spine `WORK → OBSERVATION → PASSAGE → CONVERSATION → REVISION → WORK` closes.

### E6 — MAIA carries complexity; the writer carries the Work · APPLICATION (L3)

The writer is never asked to operate the machinery. Scope selection, range construction, pass orchestration, coverage accounting and lens configuration are MAIA's burden.

**A step that exists only because the system has exposed its own operational machinery is a defect, not a choice to be delegated to the writer. Genuine questions of authorship, meaning, ownership, consent, or member authority remain the writer's to decide and must never be silently inferred.**

### E7 — A facet changes how much MAIA explains, never how much MAIA writes · APPLICATION (FACETS-01 §9.3)

Cited because it is the rider most likely to be lost at this altitude. "MAIA holds more of the process" is the exact phrase under which authorship drift arrives sounding like care, in the population where dependency risk is highest.

### E8 — The map is honest about the Work, not only about navigation · APPLICATION (`assertStudioMapHonest()`)

A capability offered about the writer's book binds at least as hard as a navigation item. An intent the system cannot serve is not listed, previewed, or implied.

### E9 — Teaching describes technique; it does not prescribe quality · APPLICATION (FACETS-01 failure condition 6; `voice` lens)

The manuscript is the reference. An imported external standard of good writing breaches Invariant 14.

**Held:** whether a given teaching act describes or prescribes is **not mechanically testable** and remains `UNKNOWN — REQUIRES HUMAN WITNESS`. It is recorded here as law, not as a proved property.

### E10 — The Studio reduces its own centrality over time · APPLICATION (Sovereignty Invariants)

A writer who works here should become more capable of working without MAIA — not more dependent on the instrument for access to their own Work.

## XI. Falsifiers for this constitution

A future implementation contradicts this constitution if any of the following is true:

1. **Manuscript displacement** — the writing surface is subordinate to an assistant surface, or the Studio becomes a place to be managed rather than a place to write.
2. **Conversation fragmentation** — changing scope loses the conversation, the place, or the context the writer already established.
3. **Unauthored mutation** — manuscript text changes by any path other than an act the writer performed knowing its effect.
4. **Opaque change** — a writer cannot determine what changed, where, why, what it was before, or cannot undo it.
5. **Authorship laundering** — the record cannot distinguish writer-authored prose from MAIA-proposed prose the writer adopted.
6. **Dead end** — a state exists from which the writer cannot return to their place in the Work.
7. **Machinery exposure** — the writer is required to operate scope, range, pass, or coverage mechanics to obtain an ordinary editorial act.
8. **Facet authorship drift** — a support facet changes how much of the writer's prose MAIA produces.
9. **Empty promise** — a capability is offered about the Work with no substrate behind it.
10. **Imported standard** — teaching evaluates the Work against an external ideal rather than describing technique against the manuscript itself.
11. **Silent supersession** — a subordinate programme's ratified law is amended, weakened, or re-ratified by this constitution or by a lane beneath it, without a founder act.

Falsifier 11 is the one this document is most able to violate by its own existence, and is stated for that reason.
