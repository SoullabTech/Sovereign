## 0 · The provenance finding, first, because it explains the instability

The eight phenomena were frozen by the founder's 07C opening act, which took `UNDERSTAND §4`
**verbatim, no new taxonomy**. That act is sound. What it inherited is not what it looks like:

> **UNDERSTAND §4, "What is NOT settled here":** *"The phenomenon list is **not** frozen — the left
> column above is illustrative, drawn from the two existing documents, and **DECIDE decides which
> phenomena are actually detectable and worth naming**."*

**DECIDE never did.** Searched: `WS2-07-DECIDE_DEVELOPMENTAL_READING_OBJECT.md` names no
phenomenon except one passing reference to the `recurrence × Structure` / `recurrence × Development`
crossing, and defines none. So an **illustrative** list passed through a document that expected to
adjudicate it, was not adjudicated, and arrived at the classifier as a **closed** family of eight
bare labels. No document in the corpus defines them.

That is the condition, and it is nobody's error — it is a seam between two documents, each correct
about its own scope.

## 1 · The seven lenses — GROUNDED, all seven

`DEVELOPMENTAL_EDITOR_CAPABILITY.md` §"The lenses" is authoritative and complete. Quoted, not
paraphrased. This is what should reach the 07B reader as the meaning of the commissioned lens.

| Lens | Asks (verbatim from the capability spec) |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Does this belong here? Does the sequence work? What is missing? What repeats? |
| **Development** | Which ideas are underdeveloped · sufficiently developed · overexplained · introduced too late · **abandoned** · repeated without advancing? |
| **Continuity** | Prospective language where *later* has already happened. Requires chronology across the Work, not phrase search. |
| **Arc** | What journey does this chapter take the reader through, and what journey has the whole book taken? |
| **Voice** | Where does this depart from the established voice *of this Work* — the manuscript itself is the reference, never an external standard. |
| **Coherence** | Internally consistent? Has a term changed meaning? Does this contradict an earlier chapter? |
| **Reader** | What does the reader already know here? Where might they lose orientation? |

**Two riders the sources attach, which belong in the rendered meaning:**

- **Development, at the observation layer.** The spec's word *abandoned* is an **interpretation**,
  not an observation. UNDERSTAND §4 is explicit: *"`unresolved` is the phenomenon name — it is what
  the evidence supports — while `abandoned` remains valid at the interpretation and judgment
  layers."* Under 07C's observation-only v1 the reader may notice that a thread is not taken up
  again; it may not say it was abandoned.
- **Arc is scope-sensitive** (UNDERSTAND §4): local movement is structure-independent; a
  division-level arc requires authored division identity; a whole-Work arc requires authoritative
  Work Structure. This already matches the 07B non-conclusions.

**Proposed for ratification:** the seven above, verbatim, with the two riders. No lens is added,
renamed or re-scoped.

## 2 · The claim boundary that ruling C turns on — GROUNDED

`UNDERSTAND §1` fixes six layers and the test that assigns a claim to one:

```text
WORK                       the authored text and the member's declared structure
MECHANICAL EVIDENCE        recoverable, re-derivable, NO READER REQUIRED
DEVELOPMENTAL OBSERVATION  what the evidence shows, stated without consequence
INTERPRETATION · POSSIBILITY · AUTHOR JUDGMENT
```

> **The test:** *"Not the wording. **What would have to be true for the claim to be false.**"*
> Evidence is **mechanically falsifiable** — re-run the measurement. Observation is **falsifiable
> against evidence** — point at the record and show it does not say that.

The worked example in UNDERSTAND makes the same cut this ruling makes:

```text
EVIDENCE     X is introduced in sections 18-21 and does not reappear until 47
             ── re-derivable from the Work; NO READER NEEDED
OBSERVATION  the thread disappears for a substantial stretch
             ── "substantial" is the only added judgment, and it is scalar, not evaluative
```

**Proposed reader-boundary rule (requirement 6, regression-proofing Lantern Road):**

> **A claim whose whole content is re-derivable from the Work and the member's declared structure
> by measurement alone is MECHANICAL EVIDENCE, not a developmental observation, and is not a
> claim.** Heading format, section lengths, how many sections a division holds, section counts and
> ordering, and the evenness or unevenness of those quantities are properties of the container. A
> claim becomes an observation only where reading the Work is required to make it.

Applied to the recovered claim — *"all six sections open with a spelled-out numeral, all fall in a
196–267 code-point band, each authored part holds exactly three"* — every clause is a measurement.
It is evidence. It should never have become a `ReaderClaimDraft` under any lens, and under
**Development** it also answers none of the six questions the lens asks.

This rule belongs to **07B**, rendered as part of what a claim is. It is not a classifier rule and
does not touch refuse-whole.

## 3 · The eight phenomena — inclusion and exclusion

Grounding is marked honestly per entry. This is what should reach the 07C classifier, and **only**
the 07C classifier: the 07B reader is never given this taxonomy (requirement 5).

### recurrence — GROUNDED (UNDERSTAND §4)
> *"Is Repetition its own lens…? **A phenomenon, not a lens**"*; crossings: *recurrence × Structure →
> does this repetition belong here? · recurrence × Development → does this repetition advance the idea?*

**Is:** an element of the Work — a word, phrase, image, figure, named entity or attribute —
appears at two or more **separated** points in what was read.
**Is not:** a property holding uniformly across every unit read (that is regularity, and §2 makes
it evidence); a whole-Work pattern asserted from partial coverage.
**Accepted example** (F1 act 1 [4], act 2 [3], act 3 [2] — all three acts agreed): *"the counted
'Eleven' at position 1 recurs at position 3 as 'the council's eleven silences'."*

### unresolved-thread — GROUNDED (UNDERSTAND §4, verbatim)
> *"the thread is introduced and not subsequently developed"* — the OBSERVABLE layer name.

**Is:** something introduced in what was read and not taken up again within it; or a withheld item
the text itself marks as still withheld.
**Is not:** *abandoned* — UNDERSTAND places that at the interpretation layer, and observation-only
v1 excludes it. Not a claim about why.
**Accepted example** (F1 acts 1, 2, 3 — all agreed): *Tomas's plan introduced at position 2, marked
unstated at 3, disclosed at 5.*

### register-shift — GROUNDED (crossings + CAPABILITY Voice)
> *register shift × Voice → has the Work's voice changed here? · register shift × Arc → is this a
> movement, or a lapse?* · Voice lens: *"where does this depart from the established voice of this
> Work — the manuscript itself is the reference, never an external standard."*

**Is:** the manner of telling changes between points read — tense, person, narrative distance,
diction, or mode of presentation (scene versus summary).
**Is not:** a departure from any standard outside this Work.
**Accepted example** (F1 acts 1, 2, 3): *past-tense narration at positions 0–2, present tense and
self-describing summary at 3–4, past tense again at 5.*

⚠ **Overlap requiring a ruling.** UNDERSTAND defines `movement` as *"where intensity, orientation
**or register** shifts"* — which makes register-shift a subset of movement, while both are in the
family. **Proposed rule:** where the claim's subject is **the telling itself**, it is
`register-shift`; where a change in telling is cited as one step in a **tracked trajectory**, it is
`movement`. Founder ruling requested.

### prospective-reference — GROUNDED (CAPABILITY Continuity)
> *"Prospective language where later has already happened. Requires chronology across the Work, not
> phrase search."*

**Is:** the text points forward — *later*, *we will see*, *as I will explain*, *for now* — to
something it defers.
**Is not:** a verdict on whether the forward reference is satisfied, where the span it points into
was not read (the `across-unread-span` and `chronology` non-conclusions already say so).

### re-explanation-first-mention — GROUNDED (CAPABILITY Reader)
> *"What does the reader already know here? Where might they lose orientation?"*

**Is:** something is introduced as new after it has already been established, or explained again
after it has been explained; or, conversely, is used as already known without having been
introduced in what was read.
**Is not:** what a reader actually experiences (the `reader-effect` non-conclusion).

### movement — GROUNDED (UNDERSTAND §4, verbatim)
> *"Arc is the lens; **movement is the phenomenon**. Movement is the observable: where intensity,
> orientation or register shifts."* Scope: local movement is structure-independent.

**Is:** a tracked element changes state across the span read — intensity, orientation, placement,
or degree of disclosure — with the change stated in the text at its endpoints.
**Is not:** the journey's meaning or value; that is Arc reasoning at the interpretation layer.
**Accepted example** (F1 acts 1, 2, 3 — all agreed, the strongest in the fixture): *the lantern
tracked from found-and-told-to-no-one, to withheld, to under the bed, to carried to the water, to
"on the windowsill where anyone could see it" — concealment to display, stated at both endpoints.*

### term-drift — GROUNDED (CAPABILITY Coherence)
> *"Internally consistent? **Has a term changed meaning?** Does this contradict an earlier chapter?"*

**Is:** one specific term or phrase carries a different sense at different points read.
**Is not:** the same term recurring unchanged (that is `recurrence`); different words for one thing
without a change of sense.

### positional-asymmetry — **UNGROUNDED. No source defines it.**

Searched: it appears in `UNDERSTAND §4`'s illustrative list and **nowhere else** — not in
UNDERSTAND's prose, not in DECIDE, not in the capability spec. It is the only one of the eight with
no basis anywhere in the corpus.

**And it is the label at the centre of the failure.** That is not a coincidence: the classifier was
handed a bare word with no meaning in any document it or we could consult, and resolved it
inconsistently — twice stretching it onto a uniformity claim, once refusing per its own
instruction.

**The fixture does, however, contain uses that look sound**, and they are the only evidence
available for what the label could honestly mean:

```text
F1 act 2 [6]  "Mara's stated interiority is given directly in the three sections of 'Before the
               water' … while in the three sections of 'After' her motive is either unstated by the
               narration or explicitly disclaimed by her"
F1 act 2 [5]  the river's state-changes placed at each division boundary and at the close
F1 act 3 [6]  "the week the river changed course" sits inside the part titled 'Before the water',
               while "The river rose" opens the part titled 'After'
```

Each says the same shape of thing: **something is distributed unevenly across comparable positions
or authored divisions — present, direct or dense in one, and absent, disclaimed or thin in another.**
That is a developmental observation, it requires reading, and it excludes the Lantern Road claim by
construction, because evenness is the opposite of asymmetry.

**Two options, and this is the founder's to rule:**

```text
(i)  DEFINE IT, from the sound uses above:
     IS      an element, quality or kind of material is distributed unevenly across comparable
             positions or authored divisions in what was read — present, direct or dense in one and
             absent, disclaimed or thin in another
     IS NOT  uniformity or regularity (the opposite shape)
     IS NOT  mechanical properties of the container — heading format, section length, how many
             sections a division holds (§2: that is evidence, not observation)

(ii) RETIRE IT from the v1 family, leaving seven.
     This is NOT the ninth-phenomenon move the ruling forbids — it removes rather than adds — but
     it IS a vocabulary change and needs its own explicit act. Note the cost: the three sound uses
     above would then have no home, and would themselves become unclassifiable.
```

**Recommendation: (i).** The label has honest work to do in this very fixture, and defining it
resolves the failure without touching the family's size in either direction.
