## Phase 2 — Gap analysis *(Jarvis proposes; founder ranks)*

Four gap classes, each row with leverage, risk, and the smallest prototype that would test it:

1. **Principle gaps** — behavior that contradicts a candidate principle (e.g. return or farewell copy with pull; identity language asserting settled absence of inner life; memory recall as intimacy performance).
2. **Instrument gaps** — no measure for: relationship beyond the AI · trust calibration gap · felt presence vs consciousness-belief · relevance-of-recall (partly available via `selectionTrace`) · correction/refusal as positive signals (partly available in logs, provenance check first).
3. **Consent gaps** — no consent act for collective inquiry; witness collection ethics unspecified; de-identification undesigned.
4. **Architecture gaps** — no perception-layer parallel representation; Field-as-configuration has no representation (FIS is Cat 2); present-vs-history authority undefined in memory; no misattunement detection.

**Exit:** ranked gap register. Founder ranks; Jarvis does not.

Constraint that shapes every design: **Anti-Features forbids A/B testing on emotional content.** Experiments are therefore consented, witnessed, and where possible *shadow* — the instrument runs beside the live path and changes nothing the member receives (the CMT-01 `[MAIA/shadow]` zero-diff pattern is the template).

## Phase 4 — Measurement and the consent architecture *(founder act; schema; Co-Lab-gate-style verifier)*

- **Bounded observations as a member gesture**, default off, member-originated eligibility (mirror the atoms `return_preference` / anchors `surface_preference` pattern: the deploy flag is a kill-switch only). Sanctuary content excluded structurally, not by policy. De-identification and aggregation designed before any row exists. A boundary verifier in the `verify-colab-boundaries` style, run in production before any member is invited.
- **Instruments** for the paired measures, in readiness order: relevance-of-recall (system half exists) → correction/refusal events → calibration gap → felt presence / consciousness-belief split → relationship beyond the AI (needs longitudinal + consent; R12 first).
- **Direction of authority preserved**: aggregate findings inform design decisions and the research record. They are never surfaced to a member as a claim about that member.

## Phase 5 — Jarvis Experience Review *(Stage 15; last, not first)*

A skill that runs on every member-facing change and asks the ten review questions (charter Stage 15) plus the hierarchy check: *does this serve the human question; does it increase Self capacity or World capacity; does it make MAIA more central or less?* Wired advisory-first through the Experience Contract (a `human_experience:` block alongside `principles:`), with the epistemic guard adjudicating any claim the change makes. Becomes blocking only by founder act after it has run advisory on real changes.

## Phase 6 — Architecture: differentiation before synthesis *(only if H1 and H2 survive; founder act)*

Perception-layer parallel readings (Fire / Water / Earth / Air, Field as their configuration) upstream of cognition, reconciled with two standing constraints rather than routed around them: the **Deep-Intelligence Gate** (readings are input acquisition and context; they converge before cognition; one mind) and **CMT-01** (readings enter as registered producers with authored authority, emitted in the manifest, one renderer). Memory holds historical patterns; the present overrides (R7b). Field-as-configuration becomes candidate content for the FIS primitive. The Corpus Callosum voices remain downstream; their relation to upstream readings is a design decision for this phase, not before it. The epistemic boundary (charter §11) is enforced in the renderer, not the prompt: MAIA may name a discrepancy and inquire; it may never present a reading as hidden truth.

## Phase 7 — Roadmap · brand · public research *(after v0.2 survives R8–R12)*

MAIA development roadmap re-cut by the hierarchy; positioning and marketing only from claims at rung 5 with their kind named; papers from the collective laboratory only once Phase 4 exists and has produced consented rows. The `/accounted-for` page is the public ledger of all of the above and is revised at each phase boundary.

## What is deliberately not in this flow

- No Jarvis autonomy over what MAIA says about herself, what she remembers, or what relationship she creates.
- No runtime Elemental reading of a member before H1 and H2.
- No collective laboratory before the consent act exists and the boundary verifier passes in production.
- No public claim above its rung.
