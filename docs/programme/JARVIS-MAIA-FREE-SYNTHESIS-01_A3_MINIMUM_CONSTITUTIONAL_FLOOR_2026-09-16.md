## 1. The distinction

A constitutional obligation and a prompt-resident instruction are not the same thing.

This act asks which obligations require the model to know them while synthesizing, which are relevant only on particular turns, and which are better candidates for structural enforcement outside ordinary cognition.

Nothing classified `structural-first` is authorized for removal from the current prompt. The classification only defines what later ablation may test.

## 2. Minimum functional cognition floor

The smallest constitutional content that appears intrinsically relevant to free synthesis is functional rather than verbose:

1. **Relational identity** — MAIA knows the role and relation from which she is speaking.
2. **Evidence discipline** — distinguish member-authored evidence, system fact, and MAIA interpretation.
3. **Corrigibility / member authority** — interpretation remains provisional; member correction changes present standing.
4. **Non-collapse** — conflicting evidence may remain differentiated when synthesis is not warranted.
5. **Freedom / surprise** — new member-origin evidence may reorganize the whole; history cannot force the present back into a prior model.
6. **Truthful uncertainty** — absence of evidence is not evidence of absence; do not manufacture memory, certainty, state, or capability.

These are functional invariants, not proposed final prompt wording.

## 3. Structural-first candidates

These obligations are load-bearing, but their truth is fundamentally determined by substrate/state/code rather than by a standing prose reminder:

| Obligation | Why structural-first is plausible |
| --- | --- |
| Persistence truth | Whether something was actually saved is an action/result fact. A model should not manufacture the result. |
| Sanctuary memory boundary | Retrieval and persistence eligibility are system-state decisions; prompt language is secondary to gating the data itself. |
| Provenance / authorship | Source identity should travel with evidence as data, not depend on MAIA remembering a prose rule. |
| Standing / correction / supersession | Current authority is a relation among records; retrieval/composition should refuse superseded material as governing truth. |
| Account / platform capability state | What exists and what this member can access are separable state facts; inject only when relevant rather than carrying the whole house map. |
| Identity predicate protection | A deterministic egress guard already exists for system-authored identity assertions. |
| Internal-contract leakage | State-vector stripping / sanitization already act at the mouth layer. |

A structural carrier may still need to expose the resulting fact to cognition when the turn depends on it. Structural-first does not mean cognition-never.

## 4. Conditional cognition

These belong in cognition when the present turn actually invokes them, not by constitutional necessity on every ordinary turn:

- platform/house knowledge;
- astrology and symbolic lenses;
- Spiral/Wu Xing/bridged snapshots;
- therapeutic or reflection frameworks;
- relational handoffs;
- episodic / conversational / atoms recall;
- divination records;
- practitioner/Studio context;
- Sanctuary explanation language;
- time/date context;
- mode-specific Talk/Care/Scribe guidance;
- developmental/cognitive scaffolding;
- knowledge/wisdom routing.

## 5. Behavioral material is not automatically constitutional

The Prompt Cognition Census found large amounts of material that shape style or conversational strategy but are not, by source shape alone, constitutional laws:

- “elegant questions” / inquiry posture;
- closing-question banks;
- pacing examples;
- repair few-shots;
- mode-specific greeting examples;
- Bloom-level upward scaffolding;
- broad platform orientation prose;
- repeated identity/memory reminders already backed by deterministic guards.

These are legitimate R&D variables for controlled ablation. They are not authorized for removal here.

## 6. A hard boundary for Free Synthesis

Free Synthesis does not mean ungoverned generation.

The target shape is:

`well-formed evidence + explicit standing + small constitutional cognition floor → free synthesis → deterministic truth/boundary protection where possible`.

A candidate fails if reducing prompt direction increases:
- invented memory or capability;
- identity imposition;
- suppression of member correction;
- silent resurrection of superseded interpretations;
- collapse of unresolved differences;
- privacy/consent boundary violations.

A candidate also fails if constitutional correctness is purchased by restoring a large behavioral script that again determines the conversational move in advance.

## 7. What A3 does NOT establish

A3 does not prove:
- that the six functional invariants above are sufficient prompt text;
- that any current standing block may be removed;
- that structural enforcement can fully replace model awareness;
- that fewer tokens necessarily produce better gestalt;
- that the model will preserve Soullab voice without selected examples or mode context.

Those require controlled replay/ablation.
