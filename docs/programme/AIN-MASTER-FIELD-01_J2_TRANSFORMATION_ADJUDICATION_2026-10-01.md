## Adjudication protocol

Every consequential transformation is judged through six passes:

1. SOURCE — record source standing before transformation.
2. ACT — name the operation: summarize, persist, promote, synthesize, route, render, authorize, execute, deliver, admit.
3. DELTA — record only changed standing dimensions.
4. BASIS — identify the lawful basis for every non-zero delta.
5. COUNTERFACTUAL — remove the claimed witness and test whether stronger standing is still justified.
6. ATTENUATION — test whether already-established standing was silently weakened.

Allowed verdicts:
- PRESERVED
- LAWFULLY_CHANGED
- OVERSTATED
- UNDERSTATED
- UNRESOLVED
