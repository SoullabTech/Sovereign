## 1. The ruling

**`WRITERS-STUDIO-SEMANTIC-FIELD-01` owns the direction "MAIA as the interactive field"**, and
absorbs the learning scope that `WRITERS-STUDIO-ACADEMY-01` (draft, 2026-10-31) left for a founder
ruling.

Founder direction carried in, same day:

1. The key role of Writer's Studio is to train MAIA to respond to **natural-language conversation about
   a member's own work**, supporting it through **edits, navigation and learning**.
2. The endeavour is to **shrink the Studio's complexity into an interactive field held within MAIA**,
   rather than across panels, menus, modes and gates the writer must operate.
3. Writers should be **encouraged to ask MAIA questions** — about their work, and about **best methods
   within the Studio**. An unanswered "how do I use this?" is a gap in MAIA, not a case for another help
   panel.
4. Founder framing, recorded as **VISION, not a live claim** (Marketing Claim Discipline): *"the
   renaissance — the ability to dynamically engage wisdom to support creativity in real time."*

This is continuous with the lane's existing law: *Manuscript first. Conversation first. Meaning before
mechanics.* and *meaning should ordinarily be discovered through conversation before it is formalized
through controls* (charter, 2026-10-01).

## 3. Gates stay; MAIA holds them

Privacy posture, the MAIA relationship for a Work, body-read permission, and Apply as the writer's act
remain constitutional. The direction changes **how they are met** — MAIA names and explains them in
conversation — ⛔ never whether they apply. The writer remains the author; MAIA offers, teaches and
navigates.
