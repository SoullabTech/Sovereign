## 1 · Why this is its own lane

The acceptance lane (`JARVIS-WS2-07-DEVELOPMENTAL-INTELLIGENCE-01`) proves that a
capability *is what it says it is*. It does not answer whether a writer can find
it, name it, or understand what they are looking at. Those are different
questions with different evidence, and folding them into an acceptance unit
would let a walked surface substitute for a understood one.

**This lane's object is the member's comprehension, not the system's behaviour.**

## 2 · Mission

Not "teach them where the buttons are." Help a writer understand:

- what Writer's Studio **is**,
- where they **are** in the work,
- what each **mode** is for,
- how to move through **Development without surrendering authorship**.

## 4 · The Development interaction sequence — binding

```text
MAIA observation → evidence → conversation → writer recognition →
writer decision → optional change → writer adoption
```

⛔ **Every arrow may stop.** This is the elaboration of the programme invariant
already ratified in `WRITERS_STUDIO_ROADMAP_STAGE_6_TO_15.md`:

```text
SOURCE / MATERIAL → MAIA MAY NOTICE → WRITER MAY RECOGNIZE
                  → WRITER MAY DECIDE → WORK MAY CHANGE
```

It is what prevents the Studio from becoming *"AI critiques your book and tells
you what to change."* Any onboarding artifact that presents the sequence as a
pipeline rather than as a chain of stoppable permissions is out of compliance
with this charter, regardless of how clear it reads.
