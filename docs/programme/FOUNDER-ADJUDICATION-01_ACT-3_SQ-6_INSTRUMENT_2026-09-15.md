> **By what act does a capability become live in this organism — and by what act does a declared
> hold lift?**

## 1 · ⭐⭐ The chain the ruling must not collapse

```text
DECLARING A HOLD
  ≠ EVALUATING ITS CONDITIONS
      ≠ DETERMINING THOSE CONDITIONS ARE MET
          ≠ AUTHORIZING THE HOLD TO LIFT
              ≠ ACTUALLY ACTIVATING THE CAPABILITY
```

> ⛔⛔ **If those acts collapse, `SQ-6` recreates exactly the ambiguity it exists to resolve.**

⭐ Five acts, ⛔ not one act described five ways. ⚠️ A founder may locate them in **different
authorities**, require **different evidence** for each, and permit some **without** the others.

### 1a · ⭐ Derived from `ACT 1 §1`, ⛔ not newly invented

`ACT 1 §1` requires **WHO · WHAT · WHERE · WHEN · HOW** of any authority-bearing act. Applied to the
chain, the ruling must therefore also answer, **for each of the five**:

```text
Is this act AUTHORITY-BEARING, or is it an OBSERVATION / a MEASUREMENT / an OPERATION?
If authority-bearing — WHO holds it, and may it be delegated (ACT 1 §3)?
```

⚠️ **An evaluation that is not authority-bearing cannot lift a hold; ⭐ an evaluation that IS
authority-bearing is a governed act and needs its own WHO.** ⛔ Which it is, is for the ruling.

## 2 · Questions the ruling must hold apart

```text
1  ACTIVATION      By what act does a capability become LIVE?
                   ⚠️ and is "live" one state, or several? (reachable · invoked · member-facing)

2  HOLD DECLARATION By what act does a capability or territory enter a declared HOLD or freeze?
                   ⚠️ ACT 1 §4 and ACT 2 §9 already declare holds by founder act — ⛔ whether that
                   is the ONLY route is unruled.

3  CONDITIONS      Must a declared hold carry DEFINED release conditions AT DECLARATION?
                   ⚠️ or may a hold lawfully exist with none?

4  EVALUATION      Who or what may EVALUATE whether conditions are met, and what evidence counts?
                   ⛔ D-P1-07's ruled limb constrains this — see §4.

5  LIFT            By what act does a hold LIFT?
                   ⚠️ Distinguish: lift because CONDITIONS WERE MET · lift by a FRESH FOUNDER ACT
                   that supersedes the hold · lift by AMENDING the conditions themselves.
                   ⛔ Whether all three are lawful is for the ruling.

6  EXPIRY          May a hold lapse by TIME, DISUSE, or SILENCE?
                   ⚠️ the census found holds that persist without any evaluating act.

7  DRIFT           What prevents a capability becoming live WITHOUT any act at all?
                   ⚠️ the census's findings in §3 are all of this shape.
```
