## 0 · Purpose

> Converge Writer's Studio around **one simple relationship between the writer, the Work, and
> MAIA**, while preserving the governed editorial intelligence already built.

⛔ This lane **subtracts and composes**. It does not add editorial capability. A change that
increases what MAIA can *do* is out of scope; a change that reduces what the writer must *operate*
is in scope.

## 1 · Frozen product laws

Ratified 2026-09-21. Reopening requires a formal freeze reopening with evidence a **law** was
wrong — ⛔ never implementation difficulty, ⛔ never "this would be simpler if L4 meant X."

| # | Law |
|---|---|
| **L1** | **The Work is primary.** The manuscript is one expression of the Work. |
| **L2** | **The Elemental opening remains foundational.** Inspiration, meaning, form, function and deeper coherence orient MAIA to the Work before editorial intervention. |
| **L3** | **MAIA carries the complexity. The writer carries the Work.** |
| **L4** | **Default Writer-mode editorial language is relational, not technical:** *Here is what I am noticing. Here is what I think may help. What do you think?* |
| **L5** | **The writer may:** agree and explore an edit · disagree and discuss the observation · revise MAIA's proposed edit · keep the original · apply an authorized change · **undo**. |
| **L6** | **Writer mode and Pro mode address the same Commission layer.** Pro mode may expose technical editorial vocabulary; ⛔ it may not become a separate editorial behaviour or a separate product. |

### 1a · Notes binding on implementation

- **L6 is testable, and must be tested.** ⭐ *Writer mode and Pro mode, given the same intent, must
  produce the same reading.* If they can diverge, the lane has failed and produced two products.
- **L8 is not a debug affordance.** Coverage is the ground of trust, not technical depth. A writer
  must never have to ask whether MAIA read the whole book. ⛔ It does not hide behind "Show
  editorial detail."
- **L4 governs the default.** ⛔ It does not license MAIA to make a claim the evidence does not
  support merely because the phrasing is gentle. Relational language is a presentation law, never a
  licence.

## 4 · ⭐ THE RANKING QUESTION IS DISSOLVED, NOT ANSWERED

Founder ruling, 2026-09-21:

> Ranking and severity classification are **NOT a prerequisite** for the core manuscript-review
> experience. The initial whole-work review may present **unranked patterns/observations, all
> reachable by the writer.** Prioritization may later become a **separate, explicitly commissioned
> act**.

⭐ This is the most consequential ruling in the direction, and it is worth recording *why* it
works rather than merely *that* it was made.

The study named ranking as a second constitutional act blocking three surfaces
(priority map · severity buckets · "help me finish"), because ordering by consequence is
`editorial-consequence`, a closed non-conclusion. ⭐ **The ruling does not weaken that law. It
removes the dependency** — a review that presents its observations unranked asserts no
consequence judgment and needs no such licence.

Three binding consequences:

- ⛔ **No implicit ranking.** Presentation order is not a ranking, and must not become one —
  no `suggested`, no `mostRecent`, no `primary`, no visual weight standing in for importance.
  ⭐ The existing `relationships` route already holds exactly this line (*"Whole, and unranked.
  ⛔ No `mostRecent`, no `suggested`, no `default`"*) and is the precedent to follow.
- ⭐ **All reachable.** The study asked whether suppressed observations are recoverable or
  discarded. ⛔ *Discarded* is now refused: every observation the reading produced is reachable by
  the writer.
- ⭐ **Prioritization, when it comes, is a commissioned act** — the writer asks for it. It is
  therefore member-invoked, which is the same structure that makes the Elemental opening lawful
  under L2 and work state lawful under L7. ⛔ MAIA never volunteers a ranking.

## 6 · Elemental Alchemy acceptance instrument

### 6.1 Precondition — ⛔ BINDING

> **EA-as-governed-knowledge must remain unavailable to the reader while EA-as-manuscript is under
> review.**

⚠️ Two Elemental Alchemys exist: `lib/corpus/eaIngestContract.ts` (frozen — 1,238 chunks,
SHA-pinned source and chunk set, `nomic-embed-text` 768-dim) and EA-as-manuscript (174 sections).
⭐ If the reader can reach the first while reviewing the second, **no whole-work claim is
falsifiable** — a reading becomes indistinguishable from a recollection. This precondition must be
**proved, not assumed**, and its proof recorded in the walk.

### 6.2 The walk

```
open Work → request whole-manuscript review → complete recorded reading passes
→ receive coverage-licensed observations → choose an observation → open exact passage
→ converse → create/modify proposal → authorize/apply → return to Work
```

### 6.3 Acceptance conditions — all five, or the walk fails

1. ⛔ **No dead end**
2. ⛔ **No loss of manuscript location**
3. ⛔ **No requirement that the writer understand lenses**
4. ⛔ **No unlicensed whole-work claim**
5. ⛔ **No unauthorized prose mutation**

### 6.4 Defeat candidates — named in advance

Per this codebase's standing discipline, each condition carries the plausible, competent, **wrong**
implementation that passes the others and fails this one. ⭐ A walk that cannot fail is not an
acceptance instrument.

| Condition | Defeat candidate |
|---|---|
| No dead end | An observation whose only action is "Show me", with no route back to the review |
| No loss of location | "Show me" opens the **chapter** but not the **passage**; or the place is not linkable |
| No lens requirement | The intent menu silently requires the writer to pick a discipline, renamed |
| No unlicensed claim | A whole-work sentence emitted after partial coverage — ⭐ **or after complete coverage that was never recorded**, which reads identically and is not licensed |
| No unauthorized mutation | Prose changes without an authorization row naming **one exact version** |
| Unranked (§4) | Presentation order doubles as ranking — a `suggested` or `primary` in any form |
| L6 parity | Writer mode and Pro mode produce different readings from the same intent |
| §6.1 precondition | The walk passes without proving EA-as-knowledge was unreachable |

⛔ A walk that hits a stop condition yields **NO EVIDENCE** — never a partial pass.

# 8 · AMENDMENT 1 — L6 becomes three facets (founder, 2026-09-21)

⭐ **Founder amendment to a frozen law**, taken as a named act rather than an edit. The original L6
text is preserved in §1 and is **SUPERSEDED AS CURRENT LAW**, ⛔ not deleted.

## 8.1 What changed

L6 as frozen named **two** modes — Writer and Pro. The founder names **three facets**:

1. a writer who needs the most help and is not a professional writer
2. a writer who wants to **learn**
3. full professional level

⭐ The amendment is right, and the reason is worth stating: two modes assumed the only axis was
*vocabulary*. There are two axes, and the second one is **pedagogy** — a writer may want ordinary
language *and* want to understand the craft behind what MAIA notices. That is a real and distinct
need, and it was not expressible in a two-mode split.

## 8.2 L6, amended

> **L6 (AMENDED).** Three facets address **one Commission layer**. A facet governs **register and
> pedagogy** — how much MAIA explains, and in whose vocabulary.
>
> ⛔ A facet **never** governs what MAIA reads, what she concludes, what is reachable, or how much
> of the Work she writes.

| Facet | What MAIA does |
|---|---|
| **F1 · Plain** | Ordinary language. MAIA says what she notices and what may help, and explains what she means without craft vocabulary. |
| **F2 · Teaching** | F1, plus the craft reasoning behind the observation — offered, ⛔ never imposed. |
| **F3 · Professional** | Editorial vocabulary, lenses, evidence, coverage, scope, rationale, provenance. |

### The riders, each load-bearing

**L6a · Facet parity — ⭐ testable, and the release test for this amendment.**
All three facets, same intent, same Work → **the same reading, the same observations, the same
reachability.** Only register and explanation differ. ⛔ If a facet can change what MAIA finds,
three products have been built and the amendment has failed exactly where the original law
succeeded.

**L6b · ⛔ Authorship is facet-invariant.**
Facets differ in how much MAIA **explains**, ⛔ never in how much MAIA **writes**. ⭐ This is the
sharpest hazard in the amendment: a facet built for *"needs the most help"* drifts naturally toward
*MAIA does more of it*, and that is precisely the population for whom dependency risk is highest.
The Sovereignty Invariant check applies directly — *does this increase user agency? does it reduce
the system's psychological centrality over time?* ⛔ No facet grants MAIA more authorship of the
member's prose. The proposal chain, the authorization, and `Use it · Change it · Talk about it ·
Undo` are **identical in all three**.

**L6c · ⛔ A facet is never assigned.**
Member-declared under **L7** (observe → ask → **member declares** → record). ⛔ MAIA never infers,
assigns, or silently adjusts a facet. ⭐ A facet is a judgment about *the writer*, not about the
work — MAIA assigning one would be **MAIA grading the writer**, which is more invasive than
anything this lane has otherwise contemplated. Same family as FR-06 and D-003.

**L6d · Changeable without ceremony, and per-observation.**
⛔ Nobody is one kind of writer permanently. A professional novelist writing a first memoir may want
F2. A writer may be F1 about structure and F3 about voice. ⛔ A facet is a **dial, not an identity**,
and it is never sticky in a way the writer cannot reverse in one gesture.

**L6e · ⛔ No self-classification at the door.**
⛔ No threshold question asks a writer to rank their own competence. *"I need the most help"* is a
sentence almost nobody will click, and asking for it is the questionnaire-at-the-door failure the
founder already refused for the Elemental opening. ⭐ The facet names **what MAIA does**, never
**who the writer is** — which is also why the table above is written in MAIA's verbs.

## 8.3 ⭐ Recommended implementation — facets as depth, not as a selector

⭐ **Recommended, ⛔ not ruled.** The three facets can be delivered **without a mode selector at
all**, which satisfies L6e structurally rather than by careful wording.

Every observation already has one shape under L4:

```
   What I'm noticing.              ← all facets
   What I think may help.          ← all facets
   What do you think?              ← all facets
   ▸ Why this matters              ← F2 expands this by default
   ▸ Editorial detail              ← F3 expands this by default
```

⭐ One answer. One reading. The facet is simply **which disclosures are open by default** — so:

- ⛔ nobody self-classifies; the writer pulls depth and MAIA remembers the pull
- ⭐ **L6a is satisfied by construction** — there is only one reading to present, so no facet can
  change what was found
- ⭐ L6d is free — the dial moves per observation, because each disclosure is per observation
- ⭐ L7 is honoured in its natural form: MAIA **observes** the pull, **asks** (*"would you like me
  to explain the craft as we go?"*), the **member declares**, the system **records**
- ⛔ and F1 is not a reduced product: it is the full observation with the machinery closed

⚠️ The alternative — three named modes chosen up front — is buildable and is the founder's call.
⛔ If taken, L6e still binds: the labels must name MAIA's behaviour, never the writer's level.
