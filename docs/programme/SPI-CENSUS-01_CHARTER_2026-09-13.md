## 0 · Compass

> **The objective is not to create an AI copy of a human. It is to understand whether Soullab already
> contains the foundations for a sovereign intelligence capable of knowing a human deeply while
> preserving the reality and authority of the human as other.**

## 1 · Governing question

> **How much of a sovereign personal intelligence system already exists across Soullab, where does it
> exist, what is its actual evidentiary state, and what — if anything — is genuinely missing?**

The brief is deliberately **not** *"let's build our version of Uare."* It is:

> *Determine how much of the useful capability already exists within Soullab, identify only the
> genuine missing capabilities, and do not redesign settled architecture.*

## 2 · What this lane may not do

- ⛔ **No architecture. No implementation. No migrations. No repairs.**
- ⛔ **No new canon.** The census may discover that constitutional custody is *missing*; **it cannot
  constitute it.** Finding a gap is not authority to fill it — that is the same crossing rule the
  whole programme runs on.
- ⛔ **No product requirements** and no roadmap as first output.
- ⛔ **No UARE-derived doctrine.** Uare is a reference specimen that shows a user need exists. It is
  never a reason to build, and its taxonomy, prompts, branding and implementation are out of bounds.
- ⛔ **No gap-filling**, however small and however obvious it looks while the map is being drawn.

## 4 · Evidence law — the Anchor's own rule

The census uses the **six-category typology** already recorded in the Anchor, and its existing rule:

> **Declaration is not liveness. Built ≠ wired. Wired ≠ surfacing. Surfacing ≠ verified.**

Pinned:

```text
LIVE  =  production runtime evidence, at the Cat 6 standard

code · migration · service · test   ≠  LIVE by themselves
```

A service plus a migration with zero live callers is **Cat 3 — built substrate**, and the Anchor
already names that trap. ⛔ Do not import `UARE-01`'s ladder; this rule predates it and says the same
thing under its own authority.

## 5 · Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **LIVE** | Production runtime evidence, Cat 6 standard. |
| **PARTIAL** | Real substrate, reaching some but not all of the claimed capability. |
| **DESIGNED** | Specified or directed, not implemented. |
| **ABSENT** | **Not found in the declared search scope** (§6). |
| **REFUSED** | Refused by **competent canon or authority**, with the ruling named. |
| **UNDECIDED** | Not yet adjudicated by anyone. |

⛔ **`REFUSED` and `UNDECIDED` are not interchangeable.** A lane-local refusal must never become a
Soullab-wide prohibition by appearing in a census row. If no competent authority refused it, it is
`UNDECIDED`.

## 6 · Branch-scope law

> **Every census finding declares its search scope. `ABSENT` means "not found in the declared scope",
> never "does not exist in Soullab."**

Read-only inspection of named refs is **authorized without merging them** — the canonical base, other
programme branches, and any ref discovered as relevant. **Every finding records the ref it came from.**

⭐ **This prevents branch topology from becoming ontology.** Run naively from a single branch, the
census would classify live capabilities as `ABSENT`, and the failure would be invisible because
everything it reported would be true of what it could see.

## 7 · Output

**One map, not another architecture.** The first deliverable is not a roadmap. It is:

> *Here is what Soullab already possesses. Here is what the specimen demonstrates. Here are the few
> things, if any, that remain genuinely absent.*

Instrument, dimensions and finding format: `SPI-CENSUS-01_INSTRUMENT_2026-09-13.md`.
