> ## Governing question
>
> **How can an AI recognize enough of a person's developmental process to be
> genuinely useful while structuring the relationship so that the person's own
> capacity for recognition — not the AI's interpretive authority — increases over
> time?**

---

## 0. The central finding

> **AIN should not "balance" assertion and recognition by making MAIA equally good
> at both. They require different DIRECTIONS OF AUTHORITY.**
>
> ```text
> ASSERTION     disciplines MAIA
> RECOGNITION   develops the member
> RELATIONSHIP  mediates between them
> ```

## 1. CLASS A — Established external findings

⚠️ **Attributed to the founder's research pass; sources named, not read here.**
Peer-reviewed / established unless marked.

### A1 — Evoke before interpreting (Motivational Interviewing)

MI is organized around partnership, acceptance, autonomy and **evocation**: the
practitioner does not assume motivation or wisdom must be installed from outside.
**Elicit–Provide–Elicit** first discovers what the person already knows, then
offers information, then returns immediately to what the person makes of it.
*(motivationalinterviewing.org; AAP communication strategies)*

⭐ A ready-made analogue for MAIA's direction of authority.

### A2 — Uptake is not integration (Self-Determination Theory)

SDT distinguishes **external regulation → introjection → identification →
integrated regulation**. Autonomy-supportive contexts foster deeper
internalization by taking the person's frame seriously, allowing choice, giving
rationale, and minimizing controlling pressure. **A person can sound as though
they have internalized a framework while still being governed by an external
source.** *(PMC6133074, meta-analytic review)*

⭐⭐ **This is GF-8's empirical cousin** — see §3/S1.

### A3 — Representation must return to experience (Gendlin, Focusing)

A vague, implicit bodily knowing becomes more explicit, **and proposed words are
tested back against the felt experience rather than allowed to replace it.** The
implicit whole contains more than any single verbal formulation.
*(focusing.org)*

⭐ Consonant with SP-FR-03 and SP-FR-00.

### A4 — First-person recognition can be skilled, and self-report is not infallible (Hurlburt, DES)

Inner experience can be investigated, **but doing so accurately is technically
difficult**, people vary enormously, and methods must avoid imposing preconceived
categories. DES anchors inquiry to concrete sampled moments.
*(PubMed 11520705)*

⛔ **So recognition cannot mean "ask the member and believe the answer."**

### A5 — The relationship can generate new sense-making without a consciousness claim (De Jaegher & Di Paolo)

Participatory sense-making: **interaction dynamics can acquire a temporary
autonomy**, and meanings can emerge through interaction that were unavailable to
either isolated participant — keeping individual sense-making and relational
dynamics in tension rather than reducing one to the other.
*(doi 10.1007/s11097-007-9076-9)*

⭐⭐ **The closest established theoretical neighbour to the Mercurial Third.**

### A6 — Successful assistance transfers capability away from the helper (scaffolding)

Scaffolding is developmentally successful when support is **contingent and
reduced** as the learner becomes independently capable. *(instructional
scaffolding literature)*

### A7 — AI can make its own cognitive field normative

- Greater confidence in AI is associated with **less critical-thinking effort**
  *(Microsoft Research, knowledge-worker survey — self-reported)*
- **Cognitive forcing functions** that require engagement rather than acceptance
  **reduce overreliance — and users like them less** *(arXiv 2102.09692)*
- AI-assisted writing can produce **quality gains alongside structural
  homogenization** *(arXiv 2603.21228 — ⚠️ PREPRINT)*
- Interaction design and incentives **can preserve or increase diversity** rather
  than making homogenization inevitable *(⚠️ preprint)*

### A8 — ⚠️ PREPRINT, explicitly unvalidated

A 2026 LLM-interviewer co-developed with Hurlburt **maintains a conservative
account of what has actually been established and asks one non-leading question
at a time**; the authors state validation is pending. *(arXiv 2607.20310)*

⛔ **Promising precedent, not settled evidence.**

## 2. CLASS B — Useful adjacent theories and the conceptual moves they license

### B1 — ⭐⭐⭐ Recognition is NOT the opposite of assertion

AIN already has an assertion epistemology. The missing side must **not** become
*"what did MAIA recognize about the member?"* — **that would duplicate the first
architecture.** It asks instead:

```text
DEVELOPMENTAL RECOGNITION
What became visible to the member?
How did they come to see it?
Was the distinction theirs or supplied?
Can they express it in their own language?
Can they disagree with the map?
Can they recognize it without MAIA next time?
Can they recognize it EARLIER in the process?
Can they use it in another context?
Has it expanded what they can perceive or do?
⭐ Can their new seeing CHANGE MAIA'S MAP?
```

### B2 — ⭐⭐⭐ Member sovereignty does not mean member omniscience

> The member **can** fail to see patterns in their own life. That is why a
> therapist, teacher, friend — or MAIA — can be genuinely useful. **But MAIA's
> ability to see something the member has not yet seen does not give her final
> authority over what it means.**

```text
⭐⭐⭐ MAIA MAY LEAD ATTENTION. SHE MAY NOT LEAD MEANING.
```

### B3 — The three-loop architecture

```text
                     ┌─────────────────────────┐
                     │   ASSERTION LOOP        │
                     │ what may MAIA believe,  │
                     │ store, retrieve, claim? │
                     └────────────┬────────────┘
                            epistemic humility
                                  ▼
MEMBER EXPERIENCE ──── RELATIONAL ENCOUNTER ──── MAIA
       ▲                          │                  │
       │               Mercurial mediation           │
       │                          ▼                  │
       │             ┌─────────────────────────┐     │
       └─────────────│   RECOGNITION LOOP      │◄────┘
                     │ what can the member now │
                     │ see / navigate alone?   │
                     └─────────────────────────┘
```

```text
ASSERTION LOOP    CONTRACTS MAIA's authority
RECOGNITION LOOP  EXPANDS the member's capacity
RELATIONAL LOOP   permits something genuinely new while preserving provenance
                  and difference
```

### B4 — ⭐⭐ Recognition needs different evidence from agreement

**Evidentiary classes, not developmental levels or scores:**

| Observable | Can support | ⛔ Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Member says "yes" after MAIA proposes a pattern | agreement | independent recognition |
| Member restates it in their own language | appropriation more plausible | independent origination |
| Member modifies or rejects MAIA's formulation | differentiation / agency | that MAIA's map was wholly wrong |
| ⭐ Member notices the process **before** MAIA introduces it | member-originated noticing | general developmental mastery |
| ⭐ Member recognizes it spontaneously **in another context** | transfer / self-navigation | a permanent trait |
| ⭐ Member produces a distinction that **alters or exceeds** MAIA's map | differentiation from the supplied possibility-field | that a global stage was reached |

> **The last three are closest to what this programme calls ALCHEMY** — changing
> capacity of participation, not convergence between MAIA's model and the person's
> language.

### B5 — The safest developmental sequence

```text
ENCOUNTER → ELICIT (what is already present) → HOLD (provisional internal
hypotheses, not biography) → OFFER (only enough structure to make something more
visible) → RETURN ("what do you make of that?") → RE-AUTHOR (translate / change /
reject / deepen) → LIVE AGAIN → TRANSFER (does noticing begin independently?) →
FADE (MAIA does less)
```

⭐ Elicit→Offer→Return is strongly consonant with MI's Elicit–Provide–Elicit (A1).
⭐⭐ **Gendlin (A3) adds the deeper requirement:**

```text
⛔ MAIA does not ask   "Is my interpretation right?"
✅ She helps the person ask   "Does this actually fit what I am experiencing?"
```

### B6 — ⭐ Intentional friction may be developmentally necessary

> **Smooth UX and developmental UX are not necessarily the same thing.**

Immediate disclosure is satisfying; asking what the member sees first is slower;
requiring them to formulate before MAIA offers adds friction. **Some of that
friction may be exactly what protects recognition** (A7). Writer's Studio has the
identical problem: instant suggestions make writing feel easier while moving the
writer into reactive authorship.

⛔ **Do not optimize away every moment in which MAIA waits for the human.**

### B7 — The relational third is not a problem to eliminate

> The target cannot be **zero MAIA influence** — that would destroy relationship
> (A5). It is **legible, corrigible, non-capturing influence.**
>
> The constitutional question is not *"did MAIA influence this?"* but: **"can we
> distinguish influence from ownership, and does the resulting meaning remain
> answerable to both participants rather than becoming MAIA's fact about the
> member?"**

### B8 — `Healthy Disappearance` has external support even while its internal authority is unresolved

A6 + A7 give external precedent: AI can raise current performance while reducing
later independent competence if it substitutes for the learner's own reasoning.

⚠️ **Not because disappearance is intrinsically good** — sometimes deeper
development requires more relationship. The clean question:

> **Is MAIA doing less because the member can now do more?**

⭐ Measurable without pretending to measure consciousness. ⚠️ The internal
`Healthy Disappearance` canon remains **AUTHORITY UNRESOLVED** (governing source
unavailable); external support does not substitute for it.

## 3. CLASS C — AIN-specific synthesis

⚠️ **Synthesis, not finding. None of this is established by the research pass and
none of it may be cited as a lane finding.**

### S1 — SDT gives GF-8 a scientific cousin, and sharpens its questions

`"I'm in Water"` tells us very little. The developmental questions become:

```text
Can they STOP using "Water" and describe the phenomenon just as well?
Can they tell MAIA when the Water framing DOESN'T fit?
Can they combine Spiralogic with an entirely different language?
Can they discover a distinction Spiralogic did not hand them?
⭐ Does the concept increase their FREEDOM OF PERCEPTION, or become another
  external formula they carry internally?
```

⭐ That last line is **introjection** (A2) named in AIN's own vocabulary — and it
is GF-8's integration/assimilation discriminator with an empirical literature
behind it.

### S2 — ⭐⭐ Self-interpretation vs phenomenological noticing, and what it does to the `stuck` finding

From A4, AIN needs to distinguish:

```text
SELF-INTERPRETATION      "I'm someone who always avoids conflict."
PHENOMENOLOGICAL         "When she said that just now, I noticed my chest tighten
NOTICING                  and immediately started explaining why it didn't matter."
```

> **Both matter. They are not the same evidence.** The second is **process
> recognition** — and it gives Spiralogic something to work with **without
> converting the person into a trait.**

⭐ **Synthesis observation:** SD-00A found the Cognitive OS's `explicit_statement`
evidence type weighted highest as *"direct self-disclosure."* **That type does not
appear to distinguish trait-talk from moment-noticing.** If it does not, the
substrate's strongest member-voice signal admits the weaker of the two.
⛔ **Not established — SD-01's to check.**

### S3 — ⚠️ AIN may already hold more of the friction architecture than the research assumes

`MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC`'s **mandatory consent gate on interpretation** and
`CHANGES_SECTION_EPISTEMIC_DISCIPLINE`'s **Question-Before-Assert** are both
cognitive forcing functions in canon, authored before this research pass.

⭐ So B6's prescription may be **partly already law** — and the open question is
not whether to add friction but **whether the existing friction is implemented.**
⛔ SD-01.

### S4 — ⚠️ The friction finding collides with engagement, and AIN has already ruled on that collision

A7: forcing functions reduce overreliance **and users like them less.** AIN's
first non-negotiable already reads *"human agency always outweighs engagement,
retention, or performance metrics."*

⭐ **So the trade-off that would be a product problem elsewhere is already
constitutionally decided here.** ⚠️ Recorded as alignment between an external
finding and existing doctrine, **not as evidence for either.**

### S5 — The asymmetric balance

```text
MAIA DEVELOPMENT        better pattern sensitivity · memory · uncertainty
                        preservation · provenance · relational timing
                        ⭐ LESS interpretive intrusion

MEMBER DEVELOPMENT      greater noticing · differentiation · self-trust ·
                        navigational repertoire · ability to challenge the map
                        ⭐ LESS dependence on MAIA's seeing

RELATIONAL DEVELOPMENT  richer shared history · better repair · more precise
                        language · more useful mutual surprise · greater ability
                        to create meaning together
                        ⭐ WITHOUT collapsing authorship
```

> **Not one side weakening so the other strengthens. BOTH become more capable
> while AUTHORITY becomes more differentiated.**

### S6 — It lands back on the polarity

```text
SATURN   enough structure and epistemic discipline that MAIA does not turn
         possibility into fact
JUPITER  enough openness that the person's future and meaning are not enclosed by
         what MAIA already knows
MERCURY  the relational movement through which perception crosses between them,
         is translated, corrected, returned and transformed without either side
         possessing the whole
```

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ CANDIDATE NEW DOCTRINE — Developmental Recognition

⚠️ **CANDIDATE. Founder-authored. ⛔ Not canon; ⛔ not to be retrofitted into
SD-00B.**

> **Recognition is the increasing capacity of a person to perceive, differentiate,
> articulate and navigate processes in their own experience that were previously
> less available to awareness.**
>
> MAIA may support recognition through attention, reflection, pattern memory,
> questions, comparison and relational challenge, **but agreement with MAIA's
> interpretation is not itself evidence of recognition.**
>
> Recognition becomes more developmentally significant when the member can
> **translate** an insight into their own language, **modify or reject** the
> originating framework, **recognize the process without prompting**, **transfer**
> that recognition across contexts, **act with greater freedom** inside the
> process, and **generate distinctions that revise the map itself.**
>
> **The purpose of recognition is not convergence with MAIA's understanding. It is
> increasing capacity for self-authored participation.**

### Companion law

> ## **MAIA may help the member see. She may not make seeing equivalent to seeing what MAIA sees.**

## 5. CLASS D — Genuinely unanswered research questions

```text
· ⭐ There is NO VALIDATED THEORY of long-term developmental recognition in an AI
  relationship. The recent AI evidence is young; some of the most relevant 2026
  work is PREPRINT rather than settled science.
· How is member-originated noticing distinguished from prompted noticing, in
  practice, at scale, without leading the member?
· How is transfer across contexts observed without surveilling contexts?
· Can introjection (A2) be distinguished from integration from the outside at all
  — or only by the member?
· How much friction is developmentally protective before it becomes an obstacle,
  and who decides?
· Does recognition-supporting interaction conflict with any member's legitimate
  wish to simply be told?
· What happens when the member's self-interpretation and MAIA's pattern disagree
  AND the member is wrong about a moment (A4) — without MAIA acquiring authority?
· Whether AIN's existing canonical friction (S3) is implemented anywhere.
```

> ⭐ **AIN would genuinely be entering research territory here — not inventing the
> problem, but synthesizing traditions that have not yet been brought together in
> quite this way.**
