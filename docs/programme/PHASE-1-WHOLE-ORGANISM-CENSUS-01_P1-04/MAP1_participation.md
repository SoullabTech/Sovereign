## 1 · What a node carries · what an edge carries

```text
NODE  row · named object · PARTICIPATION (as the slice recovered it) · SYN · COVERAGE (the cognition
      family the register named, or the register's own words for why it named none)
EDGE  source → target · relation · SYN · the quoted text that establishes it

SYN-1 OBSERVED       a quoted sentence establishes it
SYN-2 DERIVED        a relation BETWEEN two quoted observations (INF-7) — ⛔ never a merge of the
                     axes, ⛔ never a verdict
SYN-3 CONTRADICTION  two records state incompatible things; the slice preserved both
SYN-4 UNKNOWN        the evidence does not establish it
```

⭐ **An edge exists only where a slice's or register's own quoted text establishes the relation.**
⛔ Two well-evidenced endpoints do not license one. ⛔ `SYN-4` edges are **omitted** — never dotted,
never *probable*. What was declined is named in §6 so the sparseness is auditable.
