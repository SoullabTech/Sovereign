# WRITER'S STUDIO — LIVING VOICE · 01
## LV-H · MEMBER-OFFERED PASSAGE — RATIFIED

**Founder act** 2026-09-08 **Standing** OPERATIVE · constitutional

> ## THE ONTOLOGY, AND IT IS LOAD-BEARING
>
> **This is not MAIA gaining permission to read the Work.**
> **It is the writer offering MAIA one passage for one creative encounter.**

---

## THE CONSTITUTION

> A writer may explicitly offer a bounded passage of their manuscript to MAIA
> for a Living Voice encounter. The offer is **occasion-bound, passage-scoped,
> non-durable**, and grants **no wider manuscript perception**. MAIA may not
> enlarge, accumulate, persist, or reinterpret that permission as authority to
> read the Work.

### Corollary

> **When the available passage is insufficient, insufficiency is spoken; scope
> is never silently widened.**

## THE SEQUENCE

```text
MEMBER SELECTS TEXT
→ subject exists client-side
→ NOTHING HAS BEEN SENT

MEMBER INVOKES "Explore this passage"
→ explicit occasion
→ selected passage may travel to MAIA

MAIA RESPONDS
→ only within the authority of Living Voice

AFTERWARD
→ the passage acquires NO DURABLE STANDING
```

**Selection remains only the subject (LV-A).** The explicit Living Voice act is
**both the occasion and the authority to transmit that subject** — one gesture
carrying two things, because they arise together and neither exists without the
writer performing it.

## WHAT LV-H AUTHORIZES

```text
the exact passage the writer selected
minimal anchor information needed to situate it — e.g. section identity
the explicitly chosen Living Voice lens / request
```

**Nothing more is implied.**

## WHAT IT DOES NOT AUTHORIZE

```text
NO automatic surrounding paragraphs
NO whole section
NO chapter expansion
NO whole manuscript
NO accumulation of previously selected passages
NO inferred voice profile
NO MAIA memory from the passage
NO developmental-reading coverage claim
NO persistence as evidence / readings
NO reuse in another Work
```

> ### A support or craft grant is not a reading grant. FR-06 remains intact.

## ⚠️ THE 500,000 CEILING IS IRRELEVANT HERE — stated because someone will reuse the constant

```text
DEVELOPMENTAL READER    "What may MAIA read of the Work?"
LIVING VOICE            "What text has the writer handed MAIA for this
                         creative exchange?"
```

Two different acts. `DEVELOPMENTAL_READ_CEILING_CODE_POINTS` governs **bounded
manuscript reading**. **Living Voice is intentionally passage-scale** and takes
its own, much smaller, hard cap.

**The number is an implementation constant, not constitutional law.** The
constitutional requirement is that **the limit preserve the meaning of
"passage"** rather than quietly turning this into another manuscript-reading
route.

## NON-DURABILITY, PHRASED SO IT CAN ACTUALLY BE ENFORCED

The selected prose is **not written to**:

```text
Studio persistence
MAIA memory
developmental-reading artifacts
member attributes
analytics content
a cumulative Living Voice record
```

⚠️ **And an honesty limit on the promise itself.** Inference transport and
provider handling remain subject to the platform's existing privacy contract.

> **We should not promise something at infrastructure level that this module
> cannot itself prove.**

The guarantee this module makes is application-level and testable. It does not
claim more than it can demonstrate.

## SOVEREIGNTY EXPRESSED AS PERCEPTION

> **If MAIA needs more text to answer honestly, she may ask the writer to
> choose more. She may not expand her own scope.**

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE VOICE PRINCIPLE IN v1

The existing contract stands: *the manuscript itself is the reference, never an
external standard.* But **LV-H does not give MAIA enough of the manuscript to
compare one sentence against the whole Work.** So, in v1:

```text
MAIA CAN     notice what is happening inside the selected passage
MAIA MAY     use Work context already lawfully available through a
             separately authorized path
MAIA CANNOT  claim "this departs from the established voice of this Work"
             when she lacks the evidence to establish it
```

**Later, Voice Anchors solve this properly** — the writer explicitly chooses
passages: *"These sound like this Work."* Living Voice could then compare
against **writer-selected references without constructing a personal voice
profile** (LV-C). ⛔ Not v1.

## WRITER-FACING DISCLOSURE

> **This should not feel like a privacy contract every time.**

The button carries the act: **`Explore this passage`**

On first use, something quiet:

```text
Only the passage you selected is shared with MAIA for this exploration.
It isn't added to memory.
```

⛔ **No modal every time. No bureaucracy.**
