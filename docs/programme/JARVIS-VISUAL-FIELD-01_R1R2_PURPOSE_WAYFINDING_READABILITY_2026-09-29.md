## Repair law

The field must answer, in ordinary language and on the surface itself:

1. **Why am I here?**  
   To bring a live question into view from more than one meaningful direction.

2. **What does this region offer?**  
   Each region is a way of seeing the same question through a different human process.

3. **What can I do here?**  
   Choose among visible openings, linger, return to nearby territory, or view the Journey.

4. **What happens at a leaf?**  
   A leaf is a place of focused inquiry, not a dead end.

5. **How does this relate to the beginning?**  
   The original question remains visible and every deeper field explicitly states what angle it brings to that question.
