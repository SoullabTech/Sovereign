**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Branch:** `build/ws-home-redesign-slice-a`
**Evidence class:** `L` (laboratory) · nothing here is deployed or production-witnessed

---

## 0. Why this record exists

The convergence ruling withdrew the Studio-atmosphere axis, which was **one of the four
capabilities the frozen v0.2 release rule named**. Withdrawing it is correct. Letting the
release rule quietly re-form around what happens to be built is not.

> *ATMOSPHERE → replaced by CANVAS MATERIAL*

is the sentence this document exists to prevent. **Canvas Material is not a substitute for
Atmosphere. It answers a different question** — what the page is made of, not what light the
room is in — and it repaints a strictly smaller surface.

## 2. Standing

⛔ **Home v0.2 remains OPEN under its original contract.** Three of its four capabilities are
built; the fourth is withdrawn rather than delivered. The release rule said *"do not call or
deploy this as Home v0.2 until all four defining capabilities are present together."* Three
present and one withdrawn does not satisfy that sentence, and no gate answer has been recorded.

⛔ **Supersession has NOT been performed.** Only the founder may replace the release rule.
This document records the divergence; it does not resolve it. Until an explicit act says
otherwise, "v0.2" names the four-capability contract and nothing else may borrow the name.

## 3. What an eventual supersession would have to say

Not for adoption here — written down so the act, when it happens, is a decision rather than a
drift:

1. whether v0.2 ships as **three capabilities plus Canvas Material**, under a name that is not
   the old one;
2. or whether v0.2 **waits** for Atmosphere to be ruled through the design-contract path;
3. and either way, that Canvas Material entered the release **as its own capability**, never as
   Atmosphere's replacement.
