## One Foundry, five familiar Kelly's World rooms

The Foundry should be legible to a one-person founder without creating another MAIA voice, additional canonical standing or unnecessary decisions. This prototype keeps an extra local "Discover" view strictly within a research preview, with the five existing operational-room projections beneath it.

- Discover: 26 research-only public-source candidates, plain benefits, cautions, priority and possible product lanes, with links followed only by deliberate user action.
- Today: Watching; never Needs you or In motion merely because a source was discovered.
- Work: research questions and source triage, not an execution queue.
- Graph: catalogue-derived, source-backed relationships, 59 nodes and 123 edges. These are possible technology-to-product relationships, not independently demonstrated outcomes or models of human meaning.
- Monitor: separately names verified source custody, documentary founder research acceptance, pending licenses/revisions/security, and denied execution.
- System: nine standing NOT AUTHORIZED classes — install, download, experiment, member/clinical data, provider spending, Work Units, MAIA, merge/deploy and live product wiring.

## Exact custody and failure behavior

The existing readFoundryCatalogue validates the fixed 26-entry research catalogue against its pinned SHA-256. A pure world-projection function checks the incoming catalogue identity, source digest, standing, candidates, licenses and forbidden authorities before showing any candidates or Graph edges.

Founder research acceptance is recognized **only when the separately saved acceptance document matches a hard-pinned SHA-256 and exact candidate commit**. If it is missing or changed, accepted standing is NOT asserted. A hashed documentary receipt is not a cryptographically signed founder identity.

The source catalogue and founder acceptance have NOT been modified. This proposed projection has NOT been connected to the installed JARVIS Electron app or MAIA runtime, nor has a new Work Unit or grant been created.
