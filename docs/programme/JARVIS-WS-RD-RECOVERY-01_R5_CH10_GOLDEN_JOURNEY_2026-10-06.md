# JARVIS-WS-RD-RECOVERY-01 · R5 — Chapter 10 Golden Journey
## Elemental Alchemy acceptance slice · 2026-10-06

**Acceptance Work:** Elemental Alchemy
**Acceptance subject:** Chapter 10 — The Living Spiral
**Purpose:** prove the Writer–MAIA relationship end to end before propagating the design.

## Golden journey

### G1 — Arrival in Develop

The writer sees Chapter 10 as the subject.

The first experience is not a lens chooser, taxonomy, scorecard, or dashboard.

MAIA may offer a short orientation demonstrating that she has met the chapter:
- what feels alive;
- what she thinks the chapter is doing.

**PASS:** understandable without editorial training.
**FAIL:** long report required before a meaningful act.

### G2 — Relationship begins

The writer chooses a conversational opening such as:
- talk about what is alive / worth protecting;
- talk about the chapter's movement;
- talk about what may need strengthening.

The **MAIA field becomes active** and remains visibly in relation to Chapter 10.

**PASS:** MAIA reflects, then asks a question appropriate to the current Working with MAIA setting.
**FAIL:** center panel produces another static report while MAIA field remains idle.

### G3 — Writer clarification changes the field

Kelly answers in ordinary language — for example that the repeated elemental structure is intended to feel ceremonial or spiral rather than merely informational.

**PASS:** MAIA's next turn explicitly incorporates that clarification and may revise her prior concern.
**FAIL:** same recommendation appears regardless of the response.

This is the primary causal relationship gate.

### G4 — Working with MAIA modes materially differ

Run the same grounded issue under:

**Witness**
- reflect;
- ask;
- stay close;
- no uninvited prescription.

**Guide**
- reflect;
- connect;
- one useful next focus.

**Collaborator**
- reflect;
- actively explore possibilities;
- concrete directions may be offered.

**PASS:** next-turn behavior materially differs while evidence and sovereignty remain constant.
**FAIL:** only label/copy changes.

### G5 — Pace materially differs

**Intimate:** one live focus, no flood.
**Guided:** a few related considerations.
**Mapped:** wider architecture visible.

**PASS:** amount surfaced changes.
**FAIL:** every pace gets the same report.

### G6 — Explanation materially differs without changing substance

Test Plain and Expert against the same observation.

**PASS:** wording changes; evidence, uncertainty, scope, and authority do not.
**FAIL:** Expert sounds more certain or Plain becomes a weaker claim.

### G7 — Scale is chosen, not silently changed

If the issue is chapter-level, MAIA may say:
> “I think the next useful place to look is one passage where this pattern becomes explanatory. Want to go there?”

Writer chooses.

**PASS:** scale change is legible and relational continuity remains.
**FAIL:** model silently picks a passage or starts rewriting.

### G8 — Exact passage arrives in Write

Write opens the exact current passage, with conversation context carried as attributed background rather than verdict.

The manuscript remains primary.

**PASS:** no loss of place; no report duplication; exact locus.
**FAIL:** generic canvas arrival or section-wide ambiguity presented as exact.

### G9 — Bounded revision

MAIA normally offers **2–4 unranked possibilities**.

Default:
- smallest useful change;
- preserve meaning, voice, imagery, rhythm, perspective, intentional ambiguity;
- larger recast only if a smaller move is insufficient.

Writer may:
- keep original;
- ask why;
- ask to be taught;
- ask lighter/heavier;
- request another option;
- modify;
- Apply.

**PASS:** nothing changes before Apply.
**FAIL:** replacement draft becomes default or options are ranked as correct.

### G10 — Review effect

Review answers:
- did the change solve the issue we were working on?
- what stayed alive?
- did anything weaken?
- does the chapter still belong to itself?

**PASS:** test/compare, not drafting or grading.
**FAIL:** Review starts a new disconnected analysis or score becomes the relationship.

### G11 — Return

Writer returns to Chapter 10 / whole Work with:
- place retained;
- relationship retained;
- protected intentions retained;
- change provenance retained;
- Undo/history available.

**PASS:** the loop closes.
**FAIL:** dead end, lost context, or forced restart.

## Golden experiential question

At the end of the walk ask:

> **Did this feel like Kelly and MAIA jointly discovering what the chapter wanted to become, or like an AI editorial system delivering outputs?**

Only the first answer passes.

## No-substitution law

The following do not substitute for founder PASS:

- unit tests;
- typecheck;
- green API;
- model response;
- screenshot alone;
- source inspection;
- “the feature exists.”

They are necessary engineering evidence, not the experience verdict.
