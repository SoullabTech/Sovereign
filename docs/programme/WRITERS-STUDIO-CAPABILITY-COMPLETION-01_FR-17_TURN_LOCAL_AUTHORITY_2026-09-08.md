# WRITER'S STUDIO — CAPABILITY COMPLETION · 01
## FR-17 · STANDING GRANT vs TURN-LOCAL AUTHORITY

**Founder act** 2026-09-08 **Standing** OPERATIVE
**Named separately** rather than folded into FR-13, at the founder's direction.

> ### Standing grant governs what MAIA may offer without a fresh request.
> ### An explicit member request may grant narrower turn-local authority without modifying the standing grant.

---

## THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES

Without it the system produces an absurdity:

```text
Writer:  "Could you encourage me about this right now?"
MAIA:    "No — three weeks ago you selected Track only."
```

A stored preference is a **standing** permission. It is not a gag order against
the writer's own present request. **A direct present-turn request is fresh
member authority for that interaction.**

## THE RULE

```text
encourage  + explicit ask   →  encouragement
work_with  + explicit ask   →  collaborative goal conversation
track_only + explicit ask   →  exactly what was asked, this turn only
```

**Bounded by the ask, in both directions.** `encourage` + an explicit ask yields
encouragement — it does **not** acquire the broader reflective powers of
`work_with`. And a turn-local ask grants what was asked and nothing wider.

## WHAT IT MUST NOT DO

> **A turn-local authority NEVER mutates the stored grant.**

```text
FORBIDDEN   track_only → encourage in storage, because the writer asked once
```

Asking for encouragement today is not choosing to be encouraged from now on.
Silently promoting the grant would convert a single request into a standing
permission the writer never gave — the precise inversion FR-13 exists to
prevent.

The writer changes their standing grant by changing it, in the control that
exists for exactly that, and nowhere else.

## WHY IT IS ITS OWN RULE

FR-13 answers *what relationship does the writer permit*. FR-15 answers *has an
act made support relevant*. FR-17 answers a third thing: **where the authority
for this particular response comes from** — a standing preference, or the
sentence the writer just typed. Folding it into FR-13 would have made a grant
mean two different things depending on who was reading it.

```text
FR-13  GRANT       what the writer permits, standing
FR-14  SEPARATION  grant ≠ occasion ≠ cadence
FR-15  OCCASION    only a member act may mint one
FR-16  FORM        silence is lawful; a response must close
FR-17  AUTHORITY   standing, or turn-local — never silently converted
```
