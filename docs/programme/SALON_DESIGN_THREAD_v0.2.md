## 1 · Interests are routing signals (FR-19). D-J2 and D-J3 CLOSED.

> **An interest declaration grants routing eligibility. It does not publish an identity claim about the
> member.**

Nobody sees *Kelly · Maria · David are interested in Jung*. Kelly's declaration lets the system show
**Kelly** the Commons — its Salons, its Circles accepting invitation, its Commons-level material.
**Participation then creates human visibility.** Expressive text is private to the author with **no
discovery authority**; to use that language in a Salon invitation, the member authors that crossing
separately. A future *"show this on my profile"* act is **not the declaration** and is not built.

⭐ This is the resolution v0.1 could only gesture at: the tension between privacy and the north-star verb
dissolves because **the meeting no longer depends on mutual visibility of declarations at all.**

## 2 · Attendance visibility (FR-20). D-L2 CLOSED.

**before** — no attendee directory to attendees; the **host** sees registrations (needed to tend the
gathering), and that access **creates no reusable contact list**.
**during** — those actually present see each other under the identities they chose. Ephemeral room
roster: *who is here right now*.
**after** — ⛔ **no attendee directory remains.** No "people you met" · "people you've gathered with" ·
"others interested in the same things" · automatic network · follower relationship · persistent
co-attendance graph.

> ⭐ **A roster is a consequence of co-presence, not a directory. Co-presence ends; the inferred
> relationship ends with it.**

⚠️ **Honest limit:** the enforceable half is that **the system builds no list, offers no export, and
performs no cross-Salon aggregation.** It cannot stop a person writing names down. The ruling governs
what Soullab constructs.

## 3 · Connect (FR-21)

Not friendship. **"I am open to continuing relationship with you beyond this gathering."** Working
direction, ⚠️ **seam not ratified**: `request → accept → a Dyadic threshold becomes available`, rather
than acceptance instantiating a whole Dyadic environment.

## 4 · Afterlife and completion (FR-25, FR-26)

**Default afterlife: nothing.** ⛔ No automatic transcript · recording · MAIA memory · summary · "key
insights" · attendee graph · relationship graph · sentiment profile; online transports must not record
by default. Anything remaining needs a **new, independently consented act**.

**A gathering is allowed to complete.** The closing threshold: *Nothing — this gathering was enough* ·
Connect with someone · Propose another Salon · Continue this inquiry in a Circle · Bring something into
my Personal Field. ⛔ Absent: mailing list · follow host · add all attendees · subscribe.

## 5 · Circle-hosted Salons (FR-27) — D-L4 constrained, still open

A Circle hosting is **making an outward representation of itself**, not a facilitator making an event.
It requires **explicit Circle-side authorization**; the mechanism is undecided while Circle governance
is incomplete. ⛔ **`created_by` must not become that authority by accident.**

```
member-hosted    designable
Soullab-hosted   designable
Circle-hosted    concept valid, AUTHORITY OPEN
```

## 6 · Discovery surfaces places, never people (FR-28)

⛔ **No people-discovery surface in the first release.** *People discover places of encounter. Then they
discover people by encountering them.*
