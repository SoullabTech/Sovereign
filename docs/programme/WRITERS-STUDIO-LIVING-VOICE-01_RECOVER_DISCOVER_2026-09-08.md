## E · CANDIDATE MINIMUM LIVING VOICE EXPERIENCE

Smallest thing that could answer the success test:

```text
The writer selects a passage of their own.
They choose ONE invitation — see it · feel it · hear it · risk it · simplify it.
MAIA responds with a noticing and an invitation. No rewrite.
The writer writes.
Nothing is persisted about the writer.
```

Reuses: Ask's anchor · the adoption-gesture law · the no-score boundary · the
silence rule. **Adds one thing only: the invitation.**

## F · CONSTITUTIONAL QUESTIONS FOR THE FOUNDER

```text
LV-A  Is selecting a passage the OCCASION (FR-15), or does an invitation need
      its own act? Living Voice is writer-initiated by construction, which may
      make FR-15 satisfied at entry — or may not.

LV-B  Does Living Voice need a GRANT (FR-13-shaped), or is choosing to enter
      it the grant? §1 says "the writer chooses Living Voice", which reads
      like the latter.

LV-C  May any lens observation PERSIST? §4 says never persist them as
      attributes of the writer. But a writer may want to return to what MAIA
      noticed last week. Where is the line between a kept noticing and a
      voice profile?

LV-D  The ELEMENTAL LENS: does using it require the writer to have declared
      anything? §5 says they should not need to learn Elemental Alchemy first.

LV-E  "MAKE IT MORE ALIVE" names a direction. Does an invitation that says
      "more alive" already imply the current passage is less alive — an
      evaluation smuggled into a verb?
```
