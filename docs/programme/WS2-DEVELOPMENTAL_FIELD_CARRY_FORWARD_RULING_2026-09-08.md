## The ruling

> **Developmental context is written only by an explicit member carry-forward act.** Never by
> default, never merely because MAIA observed something, and never merely because a discussion
> occurred.
>
> The manuscript is already durable because the writer is authoring it. **But editing a passage is
> not consent to store an interpretation of why the writer edited it.** Those are different
> objects.

```
ENCOUNTER              ephemeral · grounded noticing · no persistence · no carry-forward
WRITE DISCUSSION       commissioned local engagement · temporary working context
        ↓  explicit "Carry into Development?" — shown exactly what would be kept
DEVELOPMENTAL FIELD    persistent · Work-scoped · provenance-preserving · member-authorized
```

**Persistent context ≠ persistent invitation.**

## Provenance survives persistence — non-negotiable

```
writer_said                 "I want the experience to come first."
maia_observed               This section repeatedly moves from scene into explanation.
maia_proposed_question      Does that relationship reverse later in the manuscript?
writer_carried_question     origin maia_proposed · adopted_by writer · adopted_at …
writer_decided              Keep the current ordering.
system_observed_act         The writer moved passage X after passage Y.
```

⭐ **The `open question` problem is solved without a fictional joint author.** A question MAIA
invents stays `maia_proposed_question` even after discussion. It becomes a durable thread only
when the writer says *yes, I'm carrying that* — and then both truths are preserved: MAIA
originated it, the writer chose to carry it.

⛔ **And the system-observed act needs the same care**: *moved paragraph* is a fact; *moved
paragraph because the reader needed more emotional space* is true only if the writer said so.
The doctrine at another scale — **the system may record the act; it may not infer the reason.**

## Develop returns to the Work

⭐ **Correcting the architectural conclusion in the prior record.** It resolved the synthesis fork
onto the developmental field and left the field carrying too much weight. The founder's version is
epistemically cleaner and is adopted:

```
actual manuscript + section architecture + developmental field
        ↓
whole-work question
        ↓
MAIA identifies CANDIDATE cross-work patterns
        ↓
retrieves / RECHECKS the relevant actual passages
        ↓
synthesis grounded back in manuscript evidence
```

> **The field is a hypothesis generator. The manuscript is the evidence.**

Not *summarize the summaries*. Not *386,031 characters into one prompt and trust the answer*.
*Closer to how a very good editor works: they remember what they have learned about the book, but
when making a serious claim about the book, they go back and look.*

✅ **The recheck primitive already exists.** A carried item's anchor points at real text, and
`anchorsAreCurrent()` reports whether the manuscript moved under it. Going back to look is the
anchor discipline used for the purpose it was built for.

## Three things this ruling opens

### 1 · ⭐⭐ A standing can be contradicted by the writer's own later writing

The ruling gives the good case: *"That standing still holds. I'm noticing that the surrounding
chapters have become increasingly instructional, which changes how this section now sits inside
the whole."* Standing intact, tension surfaced, nothing overruled.

⛔ **The harder case is not yet addressed.** The writer carries *"keep this section experiential
rather than instructional"* — and then, months later, **makes it instructional themselves.** Field
and Work now disagree, and the disagreement is authored by the same person.

| | consequence |
|---|---|
| the standing wins | ⛔ MAIA enforces a decision the writer already superseded **by writing** |
| the Work silently wins | ⛔ the carried decision quietly evaporates; carrying it meant nothing |
| **MAIA surfaces the divergence and asks** | ✅ |

> **A standing is not a promise. A writer may change their mind by writing.**

⭐ **The governing law already exists in another lane and applies directly** — the Temporal Memory
direction (`docs/architecture/TEMPORAL_MEMORY_DIRECTION_2026-09-06.md`): *member statement
overrides as present self-report and does not rewrite history; MAIA impression **asks***, and
*implicit contradiction → question, not transition.* The system never sets `valid_to` from a
timer. ⛔ Do not re-derive this; inherit it.

### 2 · Succession, not deletion

A carried item about a chapter that no longer exists is not garbage to be collected. Same lane's
model applies: **succession is carried by the successor via `supersedes`; `superseded_by` is
derived, never stored.** A standing that was later revised should remain readable as *what the
writer thought then*, with the newer standing pointing back at it.

⛔ *"Keep the fact that an act occurred when integrity needs it; do not keep the person's
surrendered meaning merely because storage makes it easy"* (FR-15) governs what may be dropped.

### 3 · ⛔ I was wrong that DISCOVER is already done — it is done for step 6, not step 1

The calibration charter said the 226 notices are the corpus and *"the lane can start at
CALIBRATE."* Against this sequence that is **half right and misleading**:

```
1  calibrate MAIA beside the writer in one section     ← corpus DOES NOT EXIST
2  constitute the explicit carry-forward act
3  build the Work-scoped developmental field
4  let Write populate it lawfully
5  build whole-Work Develop over Work + field
6  calibrate the larger readings                       ← the 226 serve HERE
```

Step 1 calibrates **conversation** — *"I'm trying to move from personal experience into explaining
Fire; is that coming through?"* and what MAIA says back. The 226 notices are one-way observations
with no writer turn in them. They cannot teach the local register.

⭐ **The step-1 corpus has to be made, and it is cheap to make**: one real section, one real
working conversation, judged as it happens. That is also the smallest honest test of the whole
thesis — *if that local relationship is bad, no amount of whole-work intelligence will rescue it.*
