## 1 · ⭐ The failure, precisely

The instrument says every node **and every edge** carries a `SYN` class. The gate's check 1 says
every **drawn edge** carries an **explicit SYN standing**.

⭐ **The map substituted BASIS for STANDING.** Its own edge-list header reads:

> *"Every edge carries its own quoted basis. ⛔ An edge whose relation is not quoted in the corpus
> is OMITTED — endpoint quality is not edge evidence."*

⭐⭐ **That is the sparsity rule, correctly applied — and it is a different requirement.** A
**basis** is the evidence for the edge. A **`SYN` class** is the standing of the edge as a claim.
An edge can carry an impeccable quote and still be `SYN-2 DERIVED` rather than `SYN-1 OBSERVED`,
and a reader cannot tell which without the class.

⚠️ The item blocks **do** carry `SYN`. ⛔ The edges do not. The failure is bounded and specific.

⛔ **This is NOT a judgement that the edges are wrong.** It is that their standing is unstated —
which is exactly the condition check 1 exists to detect.
