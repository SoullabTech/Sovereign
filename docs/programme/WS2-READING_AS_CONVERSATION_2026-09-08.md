## The move

> A one-shot analysis forces MAIA to infer everything at once: what you are trying to do, what
> matters to you, whether something is intentional, whether you already know about it, whether you
> want challenge or reassurance, whether an apparent inconsistency is part of the design. **That
> is a bad setup for the kind of intelligence you want.**

```
READ → NOTICE → DISCUSS → UNDERSTAND → WRITE → DEVELOP → DISCUSS AGAIN
```

What comes back is **a doorway, not a report**. *"I've read the work. A few things are standing
out. Want to walk through them with me?"* — three strong starting observations, not thirty
findings. Even Develop does not terminate in a document; a written reading may be **composed
after** the conversation, *when she actually understands what mattered*.

> ⭐ **The ability to change her understanding because of what you said may matter more than
> having the perfect first interpretation.**

---

## 1 · ⭐ This explains the G8 failure, and points at a better repair than a word ban

The falsifier was *"A distinction … is **not developed further** within this stretch."*

⭐ **A one-shot statement must carry its own completeness.** With no one to answer it, a perception
about non-return has nowhere to go but into implication — and the implication is that development
was owed. **The same perception, as a turn, is lawful and better:**

> *"You touch this distinction here and then move on. Is that deliberate?"*

The developmental question is **discharged by the writer's answer** instead of asserted by MAIA.

⛔ **This does not dissolve F-7.** Encounter is one-shot by constitution, and the absence-shaped
statement is unlawful there regardless. But it reframes the repair: **the fix is likely not a
lexical ban but a form change** — perceptions of non-return may belong in question form, or may
belong only in the conversational act and not in Encounter at all. ⛔ Ruling owed; not assumed.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ "Three strong observations, not thirty" is a NEW authority nobody has constituted

The fourth witness produced **226** survivors. Opening with three means **223 are not raised**.

Everything this lane has governed concerns what MAIA may **say**: the vocabulary screen, the
authorship boundary, exact excerpts, server-owned scope. **Nothing governs what she may choose
not to say.** Selecting the three that matter most in someone's Work is an act of judgment about
their book — with **no screen, no provenance, no falsifier, and no visibility**.

⛔ **And the existing law points the wrong way if applied naively here.** E2's screen rule — *the
writer is never told that something was withheld; the system's lack of an observation is not
information about their book* — was written for **dropping unlawful notices**, where silence
protects the writer. Applied to **prioritization**, the same rule would hide the fact that MAIA
had 226 things and chose 3.

| | |
|---|---|
| **screening** (constituted) | drops what MAIA may not lawfully say · silence protects |
| **selection** (unconstituted) | chooses what is worth the writer's attention · **silence shapes** |

⭐ **These are different powers and must not inherit each other's rules.** At minimum a selection
act needs: a stated basis, and the writer able to ask *"what else did you see?"* — because with
selection, unlike screening, the remainder is theirs.

## 3 · ⭐⭐ Trajectory evaluation has one failure mode, and it is the same shape as the virtue

*Jarvis must evaluate dialogue trajectories, not isolated outputs* — right, and the reason given
is right: a mediocre observation followed by a good question is valuable; an impressive
observation that ignores the answer is not.

⛔ **But "she changed her understanding because of what you said" and "she agreed with you" are the
same trace.** A MAIA who updates on every writer statement scores perfectly on *did she hear me*
and is worthless — and pleasant, which is worse. That is attachment capture wearing
responsiveness, and the Sovereignty Invariants already forbid it.

⭐ **The founder's own example draws the line exactly, and should be the calibration exemplar:**

> *"Then I wouldn't treat the shift itself as a problem. The question becomes whether the reader
> experiences it as an **earned arrival** at the system rather than a departure from the journey
> that brought them there."*

She **accepted the intent and kept the observation alive in a reframed form.** Intent changed what
the observation *means*; it did not delete it.

> **Update ≠ capitulate.** The measurable version: after a writer's clarification, did the
> observation *survive in altered form*, or did it *disappear*? Both are "she listened." Only the
> first is developmental intelligence.

## 4 · ⛔ A reading composed AFTER conversation is the hardest place to keep provenance

> *"When we worked on the later Fire material, you said you wanted the reader to feel that the
> framework had finally become explicit… Reading the whole work now, I think that intention
> largely comes through, although there is one transition where the change feels abrupt to me."*

Excellent — and note what it contains: `writer_said` (the intent), `maia_observed` (the framework
becomes explicit), and `maia_observed` again (one transition feels abrupt). **Three authorship
classes in one paragraph**, correctly attributed *because the founder wrote it carefully.*

⛔ **Prose is where the provenance discipline is most likely to die**, because good prose wants to
blend. A composed reading must never let *what the writer told her* become *what she found*:

| lawful | unlawful |
|---|---|
| *"You said you wanted the framework to arrive as an earned destination."* | *"The framework arrives as an earned destination."* |

The second states as observation something MAIA was **told**. ⭐ The typed field survives into
prose only if the composition act treats attribution as content, not as style.

## 5 · The one thing that exists today

The current copy — *"MAIA will look at how this work is developing and bring back what she sees"*
— promises a report. Everything else in this chain is unbuilt; **that sentence is live.** It is a
one-line change and would be the first concrete artifact of this doctrine.

⛔ Not authorized here. Noted because it is the cheapest possible first act, and because leaving
it as-is keeps promising the product we have just decided not to build.
