## S7 — longitudinal witness finding

Across the two production snapshots, the shadow substrate successfully preserved the distinction among:
- member-origin evidence;
- MAIA-origin interpretation;
- explicit member standing gestures;
- unresolved antecedent identity.

The live corpus therefore falsifies a tempting shortcut: **the immediately preceding turn is not a lawful standing target merely because a member says “exactly” or “I already told you.”**

The next missing primitive is claim-level identity / antecedent resolution inside multi-unit MAIA turns. Until that exists, natural-language standing changes must remain unresolved when their exact object cannot be established.

This is a capability limitation, not a reason to relax the standing law.
