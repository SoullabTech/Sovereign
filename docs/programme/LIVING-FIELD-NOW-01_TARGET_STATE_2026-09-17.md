## Foundational image

The Living Field is a **multifaceted crystal held around a still human center**.
Each facet is a complete but partial view. No facet is the person. No collection
of facets exhausts the person. The center preserves wholeness while allowing
many true perspectives to coexist.

The field breathes toroidally:

`life / encounter → attention → recognition → center → expression → world → return`

The motion is reciprocal, not linear. Experience comes inward, is attended to
and metabolized, then returns outward as way of being, relationship, practice,
choice, creation, and offering. What returns from the world becomes new
experience and may alter the field again.

> **Facets invite attention; they do not assign identity.**
> **The center belongs to the member. MAIA never occupies it.**

## Constitutional structure

The screen carries two distinct epistemic regions without making the experience
feel split:

### 1. The Living Field — **what I have named**
Member-authored or member-confirmed recognitions. These may be revised, renamed,
retired, or contradicted later. They are not permanent identity claims.

### 2. The Mirror — **what may be becoming visible**
Evidence-backed gatherings, recurrences, tensions, questions, and movements that
have not yet become member-owned meaning. MAIA may surface these; she may not
promote them into the Living Field.

Recognition is the crossing between them. The system never performs that
crossing on the member's behalf.

The governing sequence is:

`Life → Evidence → Gathering → Mirror → Recognition → Living Field → Offering`

Movement may be shown across the whole sequence, but every item retains its
source and authority class.

## Opening screen — `Your Field Now`

The first viewport is not a grid, report, scorecard, or feed. It is a calm
field of attention with a crystalline center and restrained toroidal motion.

### Center
A still central form represents the member in presence. It may evoke the dream's
meditating figure through posture / luminosity rather than requiring literal
religious iconography. The center is visually stable while the field breathes.

Primary copy:

**Your Field Now**  
*What you have named, what may be becoming visible, and how it is moving.*

Quiet epistemic key:

- **Named by you** — part of your authored Living Field
- **Becoming visible** — evidence-backed, still provisional

Nothing else needs explanation before interaction.

### Facets
Only a small number of facets are foregrounded — ordinarily 3–7. A facet appears
because something has earned attention, not because a taxonomy requires a slot.

A facet may hold one of five member-legible objects:

1. **Recognition** — something the member has named as true enough to carry.
2. **Emergence** — something new or newly salient that may deserve attention.
3. **Movement** — a thread whose quality has changed through time.
4. **Tension** — two or more legitimate movements held without forced resolution.
5. **Question** — an inquiry the member is living rather than an answer to reach.

The old thirteen dimensions become secondary lenses / retrieval coordinates.
They may help MAIA gather evidence, but they do not automatically become visible
facets and do not define the member's field.

A facet can disappear from the foreground when it quiets. Its history remains.
A quieting facet is not deleted or scored down; salience simply changes.

## Spatial grammar of the crystal

The visual position of a facet may encode **authority**, never psychological
rank, health, maturity, or worth.

- **Closer / clearer to center:** member-authored recognition.
- **At the crystalline perimeter:** evidence-backed material becoming visible.
- **Between two facets:** a living tension or relationship between recognitions.
- **Open / unfinished edge:** a question intentionally left unresolved.
- **Softening / receding:** a once-active thread that has quieted.

Position must never imply "better," "more evolved," or "more conscious."
There is no top, bottom, ascent score, or completion percentage.

The crystal may rotate or breathe very slightly to reveal that no single facet
is the privileged view. Motion must remain slow enough to support attention.
`prefers-reduced-motion` removes all nonessential motion without losing meaning.

Color may support elemental / source resonance, but color never carries authority
alone and never turns an elemental lens into an identity claim.

## The torus is the interaction model

The torus is not a decorative halo. It expresses the Field's metabolism.

### Inward breath — receiving
What has happened in life moves inward as evidence:

- encounters and conversations;
- journal / reflection;
- relationship experience;
- body / soma reports;
- dreams and images;
- decisions and changes;
- creative work and practice;
- member-kept moments.

The interface may quietly show these sources entering a facet when the member
opens its evidence, but the opening screen does not animate every ingest event.

### Still point — attending
At the center, the member may notice, question, rename, decline, or recognize.
MAIA supports attention; she does not occupy the still point or decide meaning.

### Outward breath — living
A recognition may move outward as practice, conversation, boundary, decision,
creation, offering, or changed way of being. This is member-initiated.

## Opening a facet

Selecting a facet does not navigate away from the Field. The crystal gently
reorients so the selected facet faces the member and a secondary pane / sheet
opens beside or beneath it.

The detail view is always ordered:

### A. What this is
A short label such as **Named by you**, **Becoming visible**, **Living tension**,
**Something changing**, or **Question you're carrying**.

### B. What is here
The member-authored wording, or a plainly provisional candidate statement.
No diagnostic or identity language.

### C. Why it is here
A compact evidence summary: number of moments, time span, contexts, and source
classes. Example: `4 moments · 3 contexts · Aug 12–Sep 14`.

### D. Show me
The actual evidence, in the member's words where possible, with dates and source
provenance. MAIA's wording is never substituted for the source record.

### E. What complicates this
Relevant counter-evidence, exceptions, reversals, or contexts where the apparent
pattern does not hold. Absence of counter-evidence is not proof of universality.

### F. Member authority
For provisional material, the member may respond:

- **Yes — this feels true**
- **Partly**
- **I'd name it differently**
- **These don't belong together**
- **Not anymore**
- **I don't know yet**
- **Keep watching**
- **Don't use this connection**

A recognition is not created by tapping "yes" alone if the proposition contains
system-authored identity language. The accepted wording must be member-owned:
either written by the member or explicitly accepted / revised as their current
expression under the existing authority boundary.

For member-authored material, controls become:

- **Revise what I mean**
- **See how this has changed**
- **Let this rest**
- **Explore with MAIA**
- **Live this outward →**

Nothing is scored for agreement or disagreement.

## Movement without a progress meter

Movement is shown as changes in relationship among moments, not as advancement.

A movement view may say:

> **Something has shifted.** Earlier material repeatedly asks how to make this
> situation work. More recent material increasingly asks whether you want it in
> its present form.

It must then show the actual earlier and later evidence.

Movement claims require longitudinal evidence. A single moment cannot establish
"deepening," "integration," "quieting," or "turning" by itself.

The system may describe measurable presence carefully:
`less present in your recent material` is permissible when true.
`resolved` is never inferred from absence.

No upward arrow means "better." No phase is a destination. No movement is a
score. Spiral return is represented as return-with-difference when evidence
supports that difference, not as regression or failure.

The member may always answer: **That is not the movement I see.**

## Facet admission rules

A visible facet must earn foreground attention.

### Recognition facet
Admissible when the wording is member-authored or member-confirmed under a
provable authorship gesture. System inference alone is never sufficient.

### Emergence facet
May arise from one explicitly member-marked novelty / turn, or from multiple
independent pieces of evidence. It is labeled provisional and cannot use identity
language.

### Recurrence / movement facet
Requires evidence from more than one point in time. The view must expose both
the earlier and later material rather than only MAIA's comparison.

### Tension facet
Requires evidence for each side. The system may not invent the second pole to
create a compelling dialectic.

### Question facet
May be member-authored directly. MAIA may propose a question only as a question,
never as a disguised interpretation.

A facet is excluded from `Now` when its evidence is too thin, stale without
present relevance, inaccessible by consent, or substantially contradicted in a
way the current presentation cannot faithfully show.

## Quiet / early field states

The Living Field must be allowed to be quiet.

If little has been recognized, the center remains present with only one or two
facets and the message:

> **Nothing needs to be named yet.**
> Your field will take shape from what you live, notice, and choose to keep.

Available gestures:

- **Talk with MAIA**
- **Bring something here**
- **See what you've kept**

The system must not manufacture candidate facets to avoid an empty-looking UI.
A sparse field can be truthful and beautiful.

If evidence has gathered but is too thin for a pattern, it may appear only as:

> **Something is here, but not enough has gathered to name it.**

with an optional `See the moments →` action.

## `Explore your field` — secondary depth

The old canonical dimensions move here as **lenses**, not primary objects.
A lens asks the field a question; it does not define the field.

Examples:

- Relationships
- Body / Soma
- Work / Vocation
- Creativity
- Meaning / Spiritual Life
- Dreams / Symbols
- Practices
- Questions / Thresholds
- Elemental perspective
- Time / movement

Selecting a lens reorients the same field and highlights relevant facets,
recognitions, evidence, and unanswered questions. It does not create a separate
profile or duplicate truth.

Advanced detail may reveal the original internal retrieval coordinates and
selection warrant when useful. Raw affinity scores remain developer / audit
instrumentation, not member-facing measures of significance.

### Outer / attendable moments

## Acceptance criteria for `Your Field Now`

The opening experience passes only if all are true:

1. The member, not MAIA, is visually and constitutionally at the center.
2. No fixed taxonomy occupies the first viewport.
3. Every visible facet has a legible authority class.
4. A provisional facet can expose the evidence that earned its place.
5. Evidence can show complications / counter-evidence, not only confirmation.
6. Member-authored recognition is visually distinct from system-proposed noticing.
7. No score, percentage, dominant type, or phase is presented as identity.
8. Contradiction can remain unresolved without being treated as failure.
9. The interface can be sparse without manufacturing content.
10. The field visibly supports return through time, not only current-state display.
11. A member can enter conversation with MAIA without leaving the selected facet.
12. A member can revise / rest / reject meaning without penalty.
13. Outward action is invited only from member-owned meaning.
14. Reduced-motion and linear-accessibility modes preserve all semantic content.
15. At no point does beautiful geometry obscure epistemic status.

## Wisdom Architecture integration

The Living Field inherits the Wisdom Architecture's central discipline: a map is
not trustworthy because its relationships are aesthetically convincing. Every
node and edge must preserve what kind of knowing it represents, where it came
from, and who has authority to say it means what it says.

The three architectures now divide their work cleanly:

| Architecture | Gives the Living Field |
|---|---|
| Crystal / torus | phenomenology: wholeness, facets, presence, circulation |
| Knowledge Field / JARVIS graph | spatial grammar: nodes, edges, zoom, clusters, temporal spiral |
| Wisdom Architecture | epistemic grammar: provenance, standing, authority, evidence, contradiction, correction |

The result is not a mind map of everything the system knows about the member.
It is a **governed cartography of what may be attended to**.

### Every visible node carries an epistemic envelope

At minimum:

- **kind** — encounter, evidence, recognition, question, tension, practice, person, dream, symbol, threshold, creation;
- **provenance** — exact source / source set;
- **authorship** — member, MAIA candidate, practitioner / other attributed witness;
- **standing** — source record, reflection, candidate, member-recognized, authored field expression;
- **temporal standing** — when it was true / active / last revised, not merely when stored;
- **consent scope** — whether it may participate in this view or cross into another context;
- **correction lineage** — renamed, revised, rejected, superseded, rested;
- **evidence links** — what supports the node and what materially complicates it.

The envelope is normally quiet. It becomes legible on inspection rather than
turning the first viewport into an epistemology report.

### Relationships / edges have authority classes

A line between two nodes is itself a claim. It therefore requires governance.

**Class A — structural / directly witnessed**  
Examples: `came from`, `occurred in`, `authored as`, `revised from`, `member marked during`.
These may render as ordinary solid relationships when directly supported.

**Class B — longitudinal / evidence-derived**  
Examples: `recurred across`, `became less present after`, `returned differently`,
`preceded`, `followed`. These require inspectable evidence across time and remain
descriptive rather than causal unless causality was explicitly stated by the member.

**Class C — interpretive / resonant**  
Examples: `may echo`, `may be in tension with`, `may constellate with`, `may be
connected to`. These are provisional. They render differently (e.g. faint / dashed)
and never become authoritative because MAIA drew the line.

**Class D — member-owned relationship**  
When the member explicitly confirms or authors the relationship, the edge may
become part of the authored Living Field while remaining revisable.

No invisible similarity score may silently create a visually authoritative edge.

## One field, three depths + lenses

The Living Field is not divided into separate dashboard pages. The same objects
recompose according to the member's attentional stance.

### PRESENCE — `Now`
The crystal / torus view. 3–7 foreground facets. Answers: **What deserves my
attention now?** The whole remains more important than any node.

### CONSTELLATION — `Field`
Zoom outward into the wider governed graph. Encounters, recognitions, questions,
people, practices, dreams, symbols, thresholds, creations, and authored threads
may become visible with their warranted relations. Selecting one makes it a
temporary center; the rest of the field recedes but remains perceptible.

### SPIRAL — `Through time`
The same objects reorganize temporally. Earlier material is not "lower" and
recent material is not "higher." The spiral shows recurrence, return,
crystallization, interruption, and change without implying linear progress.

### LENSES — `Attend through…`
Relationships · Body · Work · Creativity · Meaning · Dream / Symbol · Practice ·
Elemental · Questions / Thresholds. A lens changes illumination, never ontology.
