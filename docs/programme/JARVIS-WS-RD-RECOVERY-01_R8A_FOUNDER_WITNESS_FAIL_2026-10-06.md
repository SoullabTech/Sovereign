## Correct recovery direction

Do not “fix” this by putting a larger prompt around the same observation component.

The golden slice needs a **Chapter Conversation Context** whose layers remain epistemically distinct:

1. **Current chapter reading**
   - the already-authorized frozen Chapter 10 reading and its observations;
   - exact evidence where available.

2. **Book position and structure**
   - current authored structural facts;
   - Chapter 10's actual position in the Work;
   - what precedes and follows it.

3. **Whole-book orientation already lawfully read**
   - current hierarchical chapter-scale Overview readings / book-level synthesis;
   - never presented as fresh prose rereading;
   - provenance remains visible to the system.

4. **Writer-established understanding**
   - Work purpose;
   - protected intentions / ontology / voice / terms;
   - writer corrections and decisions.

5. **Conversation history**
   - prior writer clarifications and MAIA responses in the same relationship.

6. **Current locus**
   - chapter / section / passage where the writer is presently working;
   - locus changes scale, not relationship identity.

MAIA must be able to distinguish:

```text
I know this from the current chapter reading.
I know this from the book's authored structure.
I know this from prior whole-book reading.
I know this because the writer established it.
I do not currently know this.
```

That is the epistemic shape required for a humane, personalized relationship without pretending MAIA has read material she has not.
