The sovereignty model. Membrane, consent, revocation, and contribute-before-see are invariant at
every scale. **Scale changes the geometry, never the sovereignty model.**

## 3b. ⭐⭐⭐ Amendment B — ontological correction (founder, 2026-09-06, same day)

Amendment A corrected the *number*. Amendment B corrects the *category*, and supersedes A's
numeric framing of Constellation.

> **Circle is not the name of the container. Circle is the name of a particular quality of
> relational field.**

Consequence stated by the founder: 180 people sharing an interest in Jung may belong to one
ecology, but they are **not therefore one Circle**. `"Commons Circle"` (the 61–200 band in the
opening mandate) is now flagged as a **likely category error**.

### The five fields

| Field | Human reality | Provisional scale | Defining unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | self / MAIA / inner process | 1 | the person |
| **Circle** | mutual relational field | ~3–24 | **people** |
| **Constellation** | relationship *among sovereign Circles* | several Circles | **Circles, not individuals** |
| **Commons** | larger shared-interest ecology | dozens → thousands | Circles + people |
| **Co-Lab** | people intentionally working together | determined by the work | the work |

**Constellation is deliberately not defined by a headcount.** Amendment A's `~20–75` band for
Constellation is **withdrawn** — Amendment B supersedes it. Defining Constellation by Circles
rather than individuals is what keeps the architecture fractal.

### Open ontological question — held open on purpose

> Are two people a Circle, or a **dyadic relational field** that is a different object?

Research must be allowed to discover this. **Do not force the ontology.** (Note: Amendment B's
band reads `~3–24`, where Amendment A read `2–24`. That one-person difference *is* the open
question, not a typo.)

### The fractal claim

```text
                    JUNG COMMONS
                         │
          ┌──────────────┼───────────────┐
       Circle          Circle          Circle
      11 people       14 people        9 people
          │              │               │
          └──────── Constellation ───────┘
                temporary / shared inquiry
```

> The individual does not surrender sovereignty to participate in a Circle. The Circle does not
> surrender sovereignty to participate in a Constellation. The Constellation does not flatten
> itself to contribute to a Commons.

**person → circle → constellation → commons.** Soullab's distinctive claim is that collective
intelligence can be made **fractal** — and MAIA's role becomes *helping intelligence move across
scales without collapsing the distinctions between them.*

### The question this lane now asks

**Retired:** *How can one Circle successfully scale from 2 to 200 members?*

**Current:**
> 1. At what point does enlargement change the relational phenomenon enough that the appropriate
>    response is the **birth or federation of additional fields** rather than enlargement of the
>    original Circle?
> 2. How can Soullab connect many small sovereign relational fields into larger collective
>    intelligence **without destroying the intimacy, consent, plurality, or sovereignty** that made
>    those fields valuable?

### Provisional hypotheses — to be falsified, not justified

| Scale | Working hypothesis |
|---|---|
| 2–5 | intimate or possibly dyadic/micro-field; **determine whether all of these are properly Circles** |
| 6–15 | likely Circle sweet spot |
| 16–24 | large but potentially viable Circle |
| 25+ | investigate whether relational integrity degrades enough that fission / sub-Circle formation / another geometry becomes preferable |
| 50–200+ | likely Commons/Constellation territory, **not one conversational Circle** |

**These are not design requirements.** Research must actively attempt to falsify them, and must not
search only for support. Variables to track across scale: mutual recognizability · participation
equality · psychological safety · cohesion · social influence · facilitation burden · rupture and
repair · consent · quieter-member participation · capacity for whole-field inquiry · MAIA's
ability to perceive a field without reducing it to crowd summarization.

### Circle morphology — a life-course, not a growth curve

```text
formation → orientation → relationship → inquiry → maturation →
    continuation │ completion │ fission │ birth │ constellation
```

**A mature Circle must not be assumed to grow indefinitely.** Growth is not synonymous with
membership accumulation.
