## 1. Governing primitive

> **REOPEN is the member's present act authorizing bounded prior persistent material to cross into present generative context.**

The prior material may already exist, be valid, be member-owned, be eligible, be consented for later use, and be correctly ordered.

None of those facts performs REOPEN.

The default state of prior continuity in a new ordinary encounter is therefore:

> **available if legitimately retained; closed to generative crossing until the member deliberately reopens it.**

This is the read-side counterpart to J4's law: **Keep is authorship of persistence; Reopen is authorship of retrieval.**
## 2. The authority sequence

A lawful prior→present path has these stages, in this order:

```text
prior material exists
→ item/source remains legitimately eligible
→ member authors REOPEN now
→ member-authored constraint is resolved
→ authorized candidate set is formed
→ declared mechanical ordering / transparent narrowing operates inside that set
→ bounded material crosses into prompt context
→ MAIA presents or uses only what crossed
```

No later stage may manufacture an earlier one.

Therefore:

- eligibility does not create REOPEN authority;
- recency does not create REOPEN authority;
- relevance does not create REOPEN authority;
- identity does not create REOPEN authority;
- account preference does not create REOPEN authority;
- `memoryMode = continuity` does not create REOPEN authority;
- a producer's technical availability does not create REOPEN authority.

If the REOPEN stage is absent, the prior-content contribution to generative context is **zero**.

### 2.1 Representation Authority application

The member utterance or explicit gesture is the **governed source act**. Any software result that says *"this was REOPEN"* is a **representation of that act**, descriptive by default.

If that representation is used to admit or refuse prior material into generative context, it is exercising **AUTHORITATIVE** effect under `REPRESENTATION_AUTHORITY_LAW.md` because it determines a crossing. It therefore needs both:

1. a governed grant tying that exact representation to the member-authored REOPEN act and its constraint; and
2. an attestation that becomes red when the act is absent, stale, revoked, mismatched, substituted, or used beyond its authorized span.

A phrase match, intent classifier, UI flag, session id, account preference, or handoff id cannot acquire this authority merely because the implementation consults it. A bounded handoff identifier may become authoritative only if its member gesture is explicitly adjudicated to grant that crossing and the binding is attested.

Similarity, salience, recency, and relevance may participate only **after** crossing authority is established and only within whatever SELECTIVE authority has separately been granted. They may never manufacture REOPEN.
## 3. What counts as a REOPEN act

A REOPEN act must be attributable to the member and must deliberately request prior material into the present interaction.

Positive examples include:

- *"Show me what I've kept about this."*
- *"Bring back my recent Keeps."*
- *"Use the Keeps I made about the book."*
- *"What did we talk about last time about this chapter?"*
- *"Pull up what I said earlier about the relocation."*
- an explicit member gesture whose label and consequence are **Take this to MAIA**, **Discuss this with MAIA**, or equivalent bounded handoff of a named prior object.

The member's words or gesture supply the initial constraint. A source, topic, object, thread, time range, or explicitly requested bounded collection may serve as that constraint.

A request may be broad yet still be a REOPEN act. If the resulting set is too large to cross without hidden selection, MAIA must narrow transparently with the member, apply only already-declared mechanical ordering, or refuse to narrow.

> **Refusing to select remains a valid outcome.**
## 4. What does NOT count as REOPEN

The following are insufficient by themselves:

- returning as a recognized member;
- being outside Sanctuary;
- `conversational_recall_enabled = true` or any equivalent preference;
- an eligible Keep, atom, marked moment, journal, summary, prior session, theme, or reading existing in storage;
- topic overlap with earlier material;
- MAIA judging something relevant, important, salient, recent, similar, or useful;
- *"Keep this"* — KEEP only;
- *"Keep this open"* / *"Leave this open"* — CONTINUE only;
- ordinary statements such as *"I've been thinking about this again"*;
- opening a historical surface merely to read one's own material.

Member viewing authority is not generative-use authority. A member may inspect their own records without thereby authorizing MAIA to ingest them into the current turn.

## 5. Compound acts

One utterance may author more than one governed act when its language genuinely does so.

- *"Keep this and show me my older Keeps about it"* = **KEEP + REOPEN**.
- *"Let's continue the book thread from last time"* may be **CONTINUE + REOPEN** when the present instruction clearly asks prior thread material to enter now.
- *"Keep this open"* remains **CONTINUE only** and must never degrade to KEEP or REOPEN.

The system must preserve every authored act without allowing one to substitute for another.
## 6. Constraint and narrowing law

The member-authored REOPEN instruction defines the outer boundary of the retrievable set.

Inside that boundary:

1. source eligibility and consent rules still apply;
2. declared mechanical ordering such as recency may operate;
3. a declared deterministic cap may operate only after the authorized set is formed;
4. transparent member narrowing may reduce the set further.

The system may not silently broaden the constraint because adjacent material appears useful.

The system may not use semantic similarity, conversational relevance, affinity, salience, importance, engagement, emotional intensity, or model judgment as hidden permission to include an item outside the member's constraint.

If the constraint yields no eligible material, the truthful result is none.

If the constraint yields more material than can fit and declared ordering does not lawfully resolve the set, MAIA asks the member to narrow or declines to choose.

> **Selection power does not arise from context-window scarcity.**
## 7. Candidate authority span — founder ruling required

The crossing needs a bounded lifetime. This contract proposes the narrow default:

> **A REOPEN act authorizes the current response only unless the member explicitly grants a broader present-encounter span.**

Examples of a broader explicit span:

- *"Use my book Keeps while we work on this today."*
- *"Keep that prior thread with us for this conversation."*

Even a broader span ends with the current encounter. It does not survive into a future encounter without a new present member act.

A stored receipt proving that REOPEN happened is historical evidence, not reusable crossing authority.

**Founder ruling:** RATIFIED — current response by default; explicit member language may broaden to the present encounter only.

## 8. START_FRESH

`START_FRESH` is the negative counterpart to REOPEN:

> **No prior continuity may contribute to generative context until the member later authors a new REOPEN act.**

START_FRESH does not delete prior material. It closes the crossing.
A non-Sanctuary member may still KEEP newly selected present material while START_FRESH is active. That act authorizes future persistence only; it does not reopen old continuity.

The existing **New Conversation — Clear history and start fresh** gesture is therefore semantically incomplete on current canonical: clearing visible/local history without closing server-side prior-context producers does not establish START_FRESH.

A UI reset, new session id, new day, or blank transcript is not sufficient evidence of this negative authority state.

If the member later deliberately asks for prior material, that new REOPEN act may authorize a bounded crossing from that point forward.

## 9. Sanctuary override

Sanctuary remains stronger than both REOPEN and START_FRESH mechanics:

> **SANCTUARY = ENCOUNTER ONLY.**

While Sanctuary is active:

- prior persistent material does not enter generative context;
- a REOPEN request is refused as a crossing request;
- KEEP cannot originate;
- CONTINUE cannot create a persistence or retrieval bypass.

Material originating in Sanctuary cannot later be reopened from persistence because it must never have crossed into persistence in the first place.
## 10. One crossing, or none

Every producer of prior continuity that can influence MAIA's current generative context must resolve the same REOPEN authority before contributing content.

The gate belongs **before** producer-specific retrieval, ranking, formatting, and prompt assembly.

A system in which MemoryBundle checks REOPEN but Member Live Context, atoms, episodic recall, prior exchanges, developmental memory, or another continuity producer can bypass it is non-conforming even if each individual producer has good item-level consent rules.

> **One surviving bypass makes the REOPEN gate optional in practice.**

The explicit Relationship Context Bridge is the relevant positive precedent: a member-authored handoff carries a bounded identifier and ambient fallback is disabled. If founder-adjudicated as equivalent REOPEN evidence for that object, it should remain object-bounded rather than opening general continuity.

## 11. Evidence and attestation requirements

A later implementation must make the crossing auditable without logging member content.

At minimum, evidence must be able to show:

- that a present member act existed;
- the kind of constraint it supplied;
- the authorized span/lifetime;
- which producer attempted to cross;
- whether the producer was admitted or refused;
- that START_FRESH or Sanctuary can revoke/refuse the crossing;
- that a stale, absent, mismatched, or out-of-scope authority makes the evidence turn red.

A receipt of a completed crossing is evidence of what happened. It is not reusable authority for another crossing.

## 13. Founder adjudications — CLOSED

The J4 law already determines that ambient retrieval is unauthorized. The three implementation-shaping points raised by this contract are now founder-ratified:

1. **Authority span:** current response by default; explicit member language may broaden to the present encounter.
2. **Compound CONTINUE + REOPEN:** YES when the present utterance clearly asks a prior thread to enter now; prior CONTINUE standing alone remains insufficient.
3. **Explicit object handoff:** YES as object-bounded REOPEN evidence when the gesture truthfully communicates generative use with MAIA, subject to the authority-span ruling above.

`START_FRESH` is **not** an owed ruling here: J4 already requires refusal of prior memory→generative-context crossing. The current UI mismatch is therefore an implementation defect against inherited law, not a new semantic choice.

The bounded J5 implementation tranche may now implement these three rulings. No broader authority is conferred.
