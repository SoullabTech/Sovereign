## 1 · Purpose

I4 converges JARVIS Desktop onto the already-proven canonical Work Unit v2 architecture.

It does not redesign the constitutional substrate and does not connect live provider execution.

The governing distinction is:

```text
native Desktop experience
        ↓
canonical Work Unit v2 substrate
        ↓
authority / lifecycle / routing / transport / provenance / adjudication law
```

The Work Unit is the Desktop's constitutional nervous system. It is not the final visible experience.

Future native Desktop environments may become substantially richer than the present operator cockpit, including research, teaching, mentoring, personal-development, long-lived project, evidence-canvas, multi-pane local-file, media, and other native experiences that a browser/PWA cannot fully express.

Those experiences must consume the canonical substrate rather than reimplement authority or lifecycle.
