# LIVING-FIELD-NOW-01 — Constitution

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** CANDIDATE — reconciled design authority; does not supersede ratified canon  
**Base:** `8b80ec21060a102ee2007d12f3182049c7c6ad43`

## Purpose

The Living Field helps a member perceive and author the changing patterns of
their own life without reducing those patterns to an identity.

It is not a personality profile, assessment, diagnostic surface, consciousness
score, spiritual reading, or AI portrait.

> **The Living Field is the member's evolving authorship of what they have come
> to recognize about their life, held beside an inspectable mirror of what may
> currently be gathering, changing, or emerging.**

MAIA may help make movement visible. She may not decide what it means.

## Relation to ratified authority canon

This candidate remains subordinate to `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`:
Encounter → Reflection → Recognition → Living Field. Human development may loop;
system authority may not skip upward. The Living Field proper therefore contains
member-owned meaning, not raw system inference.

## Laws

1. **The member is the authority on meaning.** MAIA may notice, compare, remember,
   and propose; recognition is the member's act.
2. **Evidence precedes interpretation.** Every meaningful candidate can answer
   `Why am I seeing this?` with inspectable source material.
3. **Movement, not identity.** Prefer appearing, returning, changing, quieting,
   integrating, and becoming over static `you are` claims.
4. **Time is constitutive.** Recurrence and development require evidence from
   more than one point in time.
5. **Contradiction remains information.** Legitimate opposing movements may be
   held together without forced synthesis.
6. **Interpretation remains provisional.** Observation, inference, recognition,
   and authored expression never share an unlabeled visual state.
7. **Restraint is intelligence.** The interface surfaces what deserves attention,
   not everything the system can calculate.
8. **Frameworks illuminate; they do not define.** Spiralogic, elemental,
   archetypal, and psychological maps are optional lenses over lived evidence.
9. **Cross-field intelligence preserves jurisdiction and consent.** Integration
   is never permission to collapse boundaries.
10. **The Field belongs to the member.** Meaning can be revised, renamed, rested,
    rejected, or kept unresolved.
11. **The mirror returns the member to life.** Insight is incomplete until it can
    support freer participation in relationship, work, practice, creation, or world.

## Epistemic classes

A visible proposition must preserve its standing:

- **DIRECT** — something the member said, wrote, chose, marked, or did.
- **RECURRENT** — substantially related evidence appears at multiple times.
- **CONNECTED** — a possible relation is being surfaced for inspection.
- **PROPOSED** — MAIA offers language or interpretation the member has not owned.
- **MEMBER-RECOGNIZED** — the member explicitly accepts or meaningfully renames it.
- **AUTHORED FIELD** — the member's current expression carried in the Living Field.

No class silently becomes another.

## The counter-evidence rule

A candidate pattern must not become persuasive merely because retrieval collected
supporting examples. When material materially contradicts, limits, reverses, or
contextualizes the candidate, that material must be available under **What
complicates this**.

The absence of counter-evidence does not prove universality. A retrieval system's
failure to find a contradiction is not evidence that no contradiction exists.

This rule applies to both nodes and relationships in the field map.

## Geometry law

The spatial model is a multifaceted crystal within a toroidal field.

- The **center** represents the member in presence, never MAIA.
- **Facets** are partial views deserving attention, never compartments of identity.
- The **torus** represents reciprocal circulation: life received inward, attended
  to, recognized where appropriate, and returned outward as living participation.
- No facet is hierarchically superior to another.
- Nearness, brightness, size, and motion may encode salience or authority only
  when that encoding is explicit; they may not imply worth or developmental rank.

The geometry is an attentional instrument, not a metaphysical claim about the
member.

## Wisdom Architecture law

Every visible node and edge carries provenance and epistemic standing. A visually
convincing graph must never outrun the evidence that warrants its structure.

For every node / edge the system should be able to recover: source, authorship,
authority class, temporal standing, evidence, consent scope, and correction or
supersession lineage.

A connection inferred from similarity or resonance is visually provisional until
the member owns that relationship. A hidden model score never becomes a solid line.

## Interface test

Every foreground object must answer at least one of:

1. **Reality** — does this reveal something that actually happened?
2. **Movement** — does this reveal meaningful change, recurrence, return, or tension through time?
3. **Sovereignty** — does this increase the member's ability to understand and author meaning rather than MAIA's authority to explain them?
4. **Aliveness** — does this leave room for unfinishedness, contradiction, surprise, and future becoming?

An object that answers none of these does not deserve primary real estate.

## Foundational proposition

> **The Living Field witnesses becoming without converting becoming into identity.**
>
> **It preserves evidence without reducing experience to data.**
>
> **It recognizes pattern without claiming possession of meaning.**
>
> **It remembers the past without making the past destiny.**
>
> **Its intelligence exists to increase the member's capacity to encounter and
> participate in their own life.**

This candidate earns promotion only through reconciliation and a witnessed member
experience. Beauty, conceptual elegance, and founder resonance do not themselves
establish liveness.
