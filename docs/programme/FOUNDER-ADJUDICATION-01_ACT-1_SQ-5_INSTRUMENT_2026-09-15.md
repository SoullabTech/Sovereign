## 1 · Four distinct things the ruling must answer

### 1a · ⭐⭐ By what ACT does language acquire authority?

⛔ **Not** *"which documents sound constitutional?"* Sources of force must be **distinguished rather
than bundled**:

```text
founder ruling · ratified constitution / canon · amendment · operational instruction
accepted ADR · programme ruling · candidate / draft · descriptive architecture · code behavior
```

⚠️ This is the problem the census found where **documents self-declare `Canon` with no located
ratification act**.

### 1b · ⭐ A hierarchy, or a jurisdiction — or is there one at all?

The census found something subtler than *"three constitutions conflict"*: each claims broad scope
while often **partitioning territory by subject**.

```text
A UNIVERSAL STACK            founder > canon > ADR > CLAUDE.md > code
A JURISDICTIONAL MODEL       doc A governs prohibited acts
                             doc B governs relational sovereignty
                             doc C governs direction of authority
                             ⭐ precedence invoked ONLY where jurisdictions truly OVERLAP
```

⛔ A single stack is **not** to be imposed by default. ⭐ These are materially different
constitutions and the choice is the founder's.

### 1c · ⭐ The tie-breaker for a TRUE same-territory conflict

```text
Text A binding · Text B binding · same subject · same altitude · incompatible requirements
```

⚠️ This is exactly where the census located **no rule**. Candidate resolutions, ⛔ **none chosen
here**:

```text
☐ later explicit founder ruling controls
☐ narrower / specific controls over general
☐ explicit amendment controls over inherited text
☐ neither controls until founder adjudication
☐ an ordered combination of the above
```

### 1d · ⭐⭐ How far does a binding rule EXTEND?

A binding text may clearly rule `X` while an adjacent question concerns `Y`. The constitutional
treatment of **adjacency** must be determined:

```text
explicitly ruled · necessarily entailed · analogically related · merely adjacent · unruled
```

⛔ Otherwise **canon spreads by interpretive resemblance.**
