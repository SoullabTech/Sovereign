## H4.1 gate question

Can Cabin Arrival have a concrete visual language that preserves the semantic H4.0 law without turning the mounted field into a dashboard?

## Ruling

Yes, as a visual candidate.

The candidate authority establishes:

- one living full-height field;
- House family resemblance without copying House literally;
- central negative space and member-centered orientation;
- three quiet domain doorways: Work, Relationships, Memory;
- a separate MAIA threshold;
- presence expressed spatially rather than numerically;
- no default symbolic imagery;
- no domain color coding;
- no technical custody/runtime language;
- no automatic activation;
- desktop/mobile parity;
- reduced-motion and keyboard-accessible behavior.

## Visual relationship

House contributes:

- editorial serif hierarchy;
- warm dark materiality;
- restrained bronze/gold wayfinding;
- quiet thresholds;
- holoflower geometry;
- readable human-language copy.

Cabin deliberately does not inherit:

- House architectural photography;
- House public navigation rail;
- world-card dashboard grammar;
- public-platform chrome.

Cabin should feel like the sovereign interior behind the House threshold.

## Implementation boundary
