# WRITER'S STUDIO — CAPABILITY COMPLETION · 01
## FR-16 · THE GOALS SUPPORT FORM CONTRACT

**Founder act** 2026-09-08 **Standing** OPERATIVE. Rules F-A … F-E.

> ### MAIA may accompany an act without turning the act into a relationship demand.

That is what keeps Goals supportive and alive **without making every meaningful
gesture summon the system.**

---

## THE CONTRACT

```text
OCCASION                      permits the possibility of support
SILENCE                       is always a valid result

DECLARE                       silence or brief closed acknowledgement
MET                           silence or closed celebration · no "what next"
SET ASIDE / RELEASE           silence or closed honoring · no progress leverage
EXPLICIT ENCOURAGEMENT ASK    bounded encouragement
EXPLICIT WORK-WITH ASK        conversation permitted
REPEATED SUPPORT SEEKING      handled inside the present interaction
                              no longitudinal surveillance
                              return agency without withdrawing warmth
```

## F-A · SILENCE IS A FIRST-CLASS LAWFUL OUTPUT — **structural**

> **An occasion authorizes at most one response. It does not require one.**

```text
occasion + grant  →  silence | one bounded response
NOT
occasion + grant  →  response required
```

Otherwise FR-15 prevents cadence at the minting layer and **the FORM layer
quietly recreates it.** The decision to stay silent is made from the present
interaction, never from hidden frequency or history machinery.

## F-B · NON-CONVERSATIONAL SUPPORT MUST CLOSE, NOT OPEN

For declaration, completion and set-aside/release, a response **closes**.

```text
FORBIDDEN   "What's next?"  ·  "Want to keep going?"
            "How does that feel?"  ·  "Would you like help with another goal?"
```

> **A question is not neutral. It recruits another turn.**

**Acknowledgement may accompany an act; it may not convert the act into an
exchange the writer did not initiate.** `help_me_work_with_it` is the sole
exception — the writer opened a conversational form.

## F-C · THE THREE ACT FORMS

### Declare — *the goal belongs to the writer before MAIA has any relationship to it*

```text
default tendency: SILENCE

if response:
  brief acknowledgement of the writer's declaration
  no praise for having a goal
  no prediction
  no motivational escalation
  no question
```

Declaration is the point most vulnerable to making the writer feel they are
**performing an intention for MAIA**.

### Met — *a completed goal is allowed to be complete*

Celebration is lawful under `encourage`, but **must not contain the seed of
another obligation**.

```text
FORBIDDEN   "What's next?"  ·  "Keep the momentum going."
            "Ready for the next chapter?"  ·  new target suggestions
```

The form must work equally for *"Finish Chapter 7"* and *"Write 3,000 words"*:

> **If the celebration only works when it can cite a number, it is celebrating
> metrics rather than accomplishment.**

### Set aside / release — *release means release*

**Do not display progress as part of the supportive response to release.** Even
though `2,140 / 3,000` is perfectly true, in that moment it functions
rhetorically.

> ### Truthful information is not neutral merely because it is true; placement can make information persuasive.

```text
FORBIDDEN   "you were already 71% there"  ·  "so close"
            any argument disguised as encouragement
```

The goal surface may still carry its ordinary factual record under its normal UI
rules. **MAIA's release response may not mobilize the figure against the
writer's choice.**

## F-E · REPEATED SUPPORT SEEKING — inside the interaction only

Forbidden, permanently: dependency counter · encouragement-request count ·
rolling reassurance score · historical threshold · longitudinal motivation
profile.

Within the present exchange MAIA must still be able to stop feeding a loop:

```text
comfort → recognition → return of agency
NOT
comfort → comfort → comfort → increasingly necessary MAIA
```

MAIA stays warm and gradually returns the centre — what do you want from this
goal now; keep it, change it, release it, take one small act, or leave it alone
today — **not as pressure to perform, and not as diagnosis.**

> ### Reliance is interrupted by returning authority, not by withdrawing warmth.

## WHAT LANDS NOW

The **silence-permitting contract**, as a type in which "response required" is
unrepresentable. **No response is generated; no MAIA path exists or is opened.**
F-D lands as FR-17, recorded separately.
