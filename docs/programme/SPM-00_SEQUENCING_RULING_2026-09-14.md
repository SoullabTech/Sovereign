# SPM-00 — SEQUENCING RULING · THE FALSIFICATION CONTRACT SPLIT

**Date:** 2026-09-14 · **Authority:** founder ruling · **Kind:** ⭐ **RECORD-ONLY.**
⛔ **NO IMPLEMENTATION ACT IS IMPLIED BY THIS SEQUENCE.** Recording an order is not authorizing its
steps (roadmap, charter §5) — each still requires its own act.

---

## 1 · The order the programme is held at

```text
SPM STABLE  →  CENSUS + D9 (parallel)  →  FALSIFICATION CONTRACT
            →  IMPLEMENTATION  →  MEMBER WITNESS  →  only then reconsider CLAIM STATE
```

⭐ **Claim state is the LAST station, not a parallel track.** The stable specification **earns the
right to build**; ⛔ **it does not earn the right to claim it has been built.**

## 2 · Step 1 — substrate mapping is a PURE CENSUS

It answers exactly one question:

> *Where does each SPM claim currently live, what object carries it, and what provenance/origin
> semantics actually exist?*

⛔ **No normalization · no proposed schema · ⛔ no "closest equivalent" silently treated as
compliance.**

**Four-state output, ⭐ and the fourth is the load-bearing one:**

```text
PRESENT                    the semantics exist
PARTIAL                    some of them exist
⭐ ANALOGOUS-BUT-DIFFERENT  a legitimate object that is CATEGORICALLY NOT THIS ONE
ABSENT                     not found in the declared scope
```

⭐ **Why `ANALOGOUS-BUT-DIFFERENT` gets its own state:** the interpretive-ledger episode already
showed how easily **two legitimate but categorically different objects** get compared as if one were
an implementation of the other. *An analogy recorded as a match is how a census silently becomes a
design.*

⛔ **Carried in from SPI-CENSUS-01, still binding:** `ABSENT` may not rest on a single lexical
search — `grep vocabulary absent ≠ capability absent` — and every finding declares its scope.

## 3 · Step 3 — D9 is a GOVERNANCE act, in parallel, ⛔ not SPM's

**The principle is stronger than *"AI shouldn't impersonate people"*:**

> ⭐⭐ **Knowledge of a person does not confer authority to represent that person.**

What is needed is an **executable refusal boundary** around the crossing from *speaking about its
understanding of the member* into **speaking AS the member, or making the member's identity
actionable toward another person.**

⭐ **The distinction preserves Writer's Studio: INVITED VOICE ASSISTANCE ≠ AUTONOMOUS
REPRESENTATION** — which is the same shape as G2 (invitation permits drafting; adoption creates
representation), now stated as a refusal the system can fail rather than a stance it asserts.

⛔ **SPM may record that the sequence depends on this; SPM may NOT author it** (charter §3 — *a
vacuum next door is not an invitation*).

## 4 · ⭐⭐ Step 2 — THE FALSIFICATION CONTRACT SPLITS IN TWO

**The refinement, and its reason:** an undivided step-2 would make the whole contract wait on the
census, and ⛔ **would let census findings quietly redesign SPM** — *the specification would end up
described by the substrate it was written to judge.* Splitting keeps the specification able to become
executable law immediately, while everything that depends on real storage waits for real findings.

### 4.1 · INVARIANT falsifiers — writable **immediately from SPM**, ⛔ census-independent

```text
· ORIGIN cannot mutate
· member adopted + unreviewed is IMPOSSIBLE
· adoption does not rewrite AUTHORSHIP
· predecessor closure cannot occur through PROHIBITED MUTATION
· inference REQUIRES derivation / provenance
· refusal / non-formation cannot MASQUERADE AS AN AFFIRMATIVE CLAIM
```

### 4.2 · SUBSTRATE falsifiers — writable **only after the census**

```text
· every mapped storage path PRESERVES the invariant
· EXPORT preserves the distinctions
· ERASURE behaves correctly across mapped substrates
· no existing ADAPTER collapses origins
· LEGACY records cannot manufacture provenance they never possessed
```

## 5 · Step 4 — the member surface, and its architectural test

⭐ **The test is NOT whether the UI can display *"I inferred this."*** It is **whether MAIA can prove
why that sentence is true FOR THIS EXACT OBJECT.**

> ⭐⭐ **The member-facing surface should be almost BORING technically — a faithful projection of
> already-enforced custody.**
> ⛔ **If it needs special logic to determine ORIGIN at display time, the architecture beneath it is
> incomplete.**

⭐ Recorded as an **architectural acceptance criterion**, not prose: it is a real test, and it is a
**substrate falsifier** (§4.2) — ⛔ unwritable until there is a substrate to run it against.

## 6 · The proposition this makes tangible — ⛔ and its claim state

> ⭐⭐ **MAIA may know increasingly much about you without acquiring ownership of who you are.**
> *Increasing intimacy without increasing possession.*

⛔ **NOT a Live product claim.** Under `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE` it is **VISION**, resting on a
stable specification with **zero runtime behind it.** It moves only at the **member witness** station,
and only by the ordinary claim-state act.
