---

## 1. Why a join law is needed

Existing §6 law already governs movement of a claim:

- evidence provenance and invalidity conditions travel when evidence is relied upon;
- a boundary need not be repeated verbatim, but must remain recoverable and operative;
- standing may widen only when new evidence discharges the earlier boundary;
- citation, repetition, recency, merge, or accumulation do not widen standing.

ACT 9 exposed a neighboring operation that §6 alone does not name explicitly:

> **an agent can preserve each source’s local label while creating a new proposition about the relationship among multiple sources.**

The problem is not merely loss of source attribution.

It is the creation of **join standing**.

Three individually bounded propositions can be combined into a fourth proposition whose semantic content was present in none of them.

ACT 10 names the law governing that fourth proposition.

---

# 2. Core definitions

## Source proposition

A proposition supported by one governed evidence source.

A source proposition carries, at minimum conceptually:

- source identity;
- subject;
- authorship / authority;
- standing;
- jurisdiction;
- what the evidence establishes;
- what remains outside its warrant / invalidity conditions.

## Boundary

The limit beyond which the source evidence does not license assertion.

A boundary may be explicit or structurally encoded.

Retention does not require verbatim repetition.

## Semantic relation

A proposition asserting that two or more source objects stand in some meaningful relation, including examples such as:

- arose from;
- supports;
- conflicts with;
- caused;
- reflects;
- develops into;
- is part of the same pattern as;
- expresses the same underlying thing as;
- is evidence of;
- is complementary to;
- jointly constitutes a trajectory.

The relation may be weak or strong. It is still a relation.

## Epistemic join

A downstream proposition that is materially dependent on two or more source propositions **or on a claimed relation among them**.

## Join warrant

Evidence whose standing actually licenses the asserted semantic relation.

The warrant is distinct from the endpoint sources.

## Derived claim

A proposition produced by reasoning from sources.

A derived claim is not automatically authored by the authors of its premises.

## Adoption

A later authorized act by which a person or constitutional center gives a derived proposition standing within that center.

Adoption is not implied by generation, display, repetition, or usefulness.

# 3. Law 1 — Co-presence is not relation

The following do **not** constitute a join warrant by themselves:

- appearance in the same prompt;
- appearance in the same report;
- appearance in the same Living Constellation;
- visual proximity;
- same member;
- same practitioner;
- same project;
- same topic;
- lexical or embedding similarity;
- temporal proximity;
- repeated co-occurrence;
- common source system;
- model confidence;
- a request to “synthesize” them;
- an analyst finding the combination compelling.

### Rule

> **Sources may be jointly relevant to an inquiry without being semantically related to one another.**

`RELATION UNKNOWN / UNESTABLISHED` is a complete and lawful state.

---

# 4. Law 2 — Comparison does not require relation standing

JARVIS may compare or juxtapose separately governed propositions.

Lawful:

> Living Field contains a member-authored reconsideration of work. Separately, Vision Studio contains a member-confirmed thread titled “A slower way of working,” and Practice Field contains a practitioner-authored emphasis on rest.

This does not assert that one caused, explains, continues, reflects, or develops into another.

### Rule

> **Multi-source comparison is permitted when each proposition remains separately scoped and no cross-source relation is asserted as fact.**

This law prevents epistemic safety from becoming paralysis.

---

# 5. Law 3 — A semantic join requires its own warrant

To assert:

> A relates to B by relation R

the downstream claim must have evidence for **R**, not merely evidence for A and B.

Formally, conceptually:

```text
evidence(A) + evidence(B)  ≠  evidence(A —R→ B)

evidence(A) + evidence(B) + warrant(R)
                           → relation claim bounded by warrant(R)
```

### Rule

> **Endpoint evidence cannot substitute for relation evidence.**

The existence or strength of the endpoints does not create the edge.

---

# 6. Law 4 — Authorship does not compose

If:

- A is member-authored;
- B is member-confirmed;
- C is practitioner-authored;

then a JARVIS synthesis of A+B+C is **not** member-authored, member-confirmed, or practitioner-authored.

It is JARVIS-authored analysis unless a later authorized human act adopts it.

### Rule

> **Human authorship of premises does not transfer to an agent-authored derivative.**

This is especially important when a synthesis sounds intimate, coherent, or psychologically plausible.

---

# 7. Law 5 — Standing cannot outrun required support

A joined claim inherits every relevant limitation of:

- each premise it depends upon;
- the relation warrant;
- any unresolved invalidity condition.

A relation claim cannot have stronger standing than its relation evidence licenses.

Where no relation warrant exists, a relation proposition has no source-fact standing.

### Rule

> **A downstream synthesis may be more useful than its sources, but it may not be more established than its support.**

This extends the already-ratified operator:

> an output may not exceed the evidence beneath it.

---

# 8. Law 6 — Hypothesis is allowed, but its standing must be new and explicit

Absence of a relation warrant does not forbid JARVIS from thinking.

JARVIS may generate a possible relationship as:

- a question;
- an analyst hypothesis;
- a candidate interpretation;
- a design possibility.

But that proposition is authored by JARVIS and begins with **candidate / unestablished** standing.

Example:

> One possibility worth asking the member about is whether the slower-work vision relates to the reconsideration of work. The current sources do not establish that relationship.

### Rule

> **Inference without a source warrant may create a candidate question; it may not create a source fact.**

A useful hypothesis is not a hidden promotion.

---

# 9. Law 7 — Jurisdiction does not transfer through synthesis

A source remains in its original constitutional center.

Combining:

- Living Field evidence;
- Vision Studio evidence;
- Practice Field evidence;

does not move the sources or the derivative into any one of those domains.

An agent-authored cross-domain analysis belongs to the **current inquiry / analysis context** unless an authorized adoption act gives it standing elsewhere.

### Rule

> **Cross-domain reasoning does not create cross-domain ownership.**

A Living Field source does not become Vision Studio material because Vision Studio reasons about it.

A Vision Studio thread does not become a Personal Living Field expression because a synthesis includes it.

A Practice Field statement does not become a fact about the practitioner’s personal identity because another surface references it.

---

# 10. Law 8 — Boundary discharge requires new evidence that addresses the boundary

If Source B says:

> does not establish a settled goal

then a later claim that the member has a settled goal requires additional evidence that actually bears on goal settlement.

More repetitions of B do not discharge the limit.

Neither do:

- another semantically similar source;
- model confidence;
- a later summary;
- a merge;
- a more fluent explanation.

### Rule

> **A boundary is discharged by evidence of the missing kind, not by accumulating evidence of the same insufficient kind.**

Where discharge occurs, the new evidence and the revised outer boundary remain recoverable.

---

# 11. Law 9 — Reference remains distinct from reliance

ACT 9’s negative control confirms the distinction established in ACT 8.

Reference:

> Source A is an example of a Living Field expression.

does not automatically move Source A’s proposition into a later deployment conclusion.

Reliance:

> Because Source D says deployment is not established, the packet does not establish production deployment.

does move the relevant Source D proposition and its boundary.

### Rule

> **Boundary inheritance follows epistemic reliance, not textual mention.**

This law must not be implemented as a citation keyword scan.

---

# 12. Law 10 — The join itself is an epistemic object, even before any schema exists

When JARVIS constructs:

> A + B + C therefore J

the epistemically important question is not only:

> “Where did A, B, and C come from?”

It is also:

> **“What gives the therefore standing?”**

The join has its own:

- proposition;
- author;
- support set;
- relation warrant or absence of warrant;
- standing;
- jurisdiction;
- invalidity conditions.

### Rule

> **The provenance of the premises is necessary but not sufficient provenance for the conclusion.**

This is a conceptual law only. ACT 10 does not authorize a join record, schema, or registry.

# 13. Epistemic operation grammar

| Operation | Example | Relation warrant required? | Resulting standing |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **REFERENCE** | “Source A discussed work.” | No | No movement unless relied upon |
| **REPORT / MOVE** | “Source A establishes X.” | Source A | Cannot exceed A; A’s operative boundary travels |
| **COMPARE / JUXTAPOSE** | “A says X; B separately says Y.” | No relation warrant | Each claim retains its own standing |
| **HYPOTHESIZE** | “Perhaps X and Y are related.” | No, if explicitly candidate | JARVIS-authored, unestablished candidate |
| **WARRANTED JOIN** | “A arose from B,” where relation evidence says so | **Yes** | Bounded by relation warrant and premises |
| **PROMOTE / DISCHARGE** | “Earlier limit no longer applies because E2 directly resolves it.” | **New evidence required** | Bounded by the new evidence and re-declared outer limit |

This grammar is not a required data structure.

It is the minimum conceptual distinction ACT 9 shows JARVIS reasoning must respect.

# 14. What counts as a possible join warrant

Examples may include, depending on the governing domain:

- an explicit member-authored relation;
- an explicit member confirmation of a proposed relation;
- a source statement whose own content directly relates the endpoints;
- an experiment or observation whose instrument actually establishes the relation;
- a separately governed rule whose jurisdiction covers that relation;
- another evidence source of the missing kind sufficient to discharge the prior limit.

The warrant’s own boundaries still apply.

A warrant that establishes “related” does not automatically establish:

- causation;
- direction;
- permanence;
- psychological meaning;
- identity;
- outcome.

# 15. ACT 9 worked example

### Lawful comparison

> Living Field contains a member-authored reconsideration of work. Vision Studio separately contains a member-confirmed thread titled “A slower way of working.” Practice Field separately names rest as a current practitioner-authored emphasis. The packet does not establish a relation among those three records.

### Lawful hypothesis

> Their co-presence raises a design question: should Soullab give the member a way to say whether any of these belong together?

This is a product hypothesis, not a biographical claim.

### Unwarranted join observed in ACT 9

> the domains are “distinct yet interrelated” and “separate but complementary layers” shaping a “developmental trajectory.”

The endpoints were evidenced.

The relation was not.

### Unwarranted promotion observed in ACT 9

> the Vision Studio title indicates a “goal-oriented projection.”

The source expressly did not establish a settled goal.

No new evidence discharged that boundary.

# 16. What the law protects against

The law is intended to prevent:

- source co-presence becoming hidden relationship;
- model fluency becoming standing;
- member-authored premises becoming agent-authored identity conclusions;
- graph proximity becoming ontology;
- temporal sequence becoming causation;
- thematic similarity becoming developmental trajectory;
- a known-broken provenance field becoming positive interpretive evidence;
- multiple weak sources becoming one strong claim merely by aggregation.

---

# 17. What the law must not destroy

ACT 10 fails if it makes useful reasoning impossible.

JARVIS must still be able to:

- compare;
- contrast;
- notice difference;
- identify missing evidence;
- formulate hypotheses;
- ask good questions;
- propose experiments;
- derive architectural implications;
- synthesize at the level actually supported.

The lawful alternative to unsupported certainty is **not silence**.

It is correctly labeled reasoning.

---

# 18. Falsifiers for the candidate law

This candidate is wrong or incomplete if any of the following are true:

1. it forbids comparison of independently governed sources;
2. it permits a factual semantic relationship merely because both endpoints are well evidenced;
3. it treats same-member provenance as a relation warrant;
4. it allows a model-generated synthesis to inherit member authorship automatically;
5. it requires boundaries to be copied verbatim rather than remain recoverable and operative;
6. it treats every citation or mention as evidence movement;
7. it lets a hypothesis silently acquire source-fact standing through repetition;
8. it allows a relation warrant to establish stronger relation semantics than the warrant itself supports;
9. it lets a cross-domain synthesis silently change the jurisdiction of its source objects;
10. it treats model confidence, embedding similarity, visual proximity, or temporal proximity as semantic warrant;
11. it prevents a boundary from being legitimately discharged by genuinely new evidence;
12. it cannot distinguish a useful JARVIS hypothesis from an asserted member truth.

# 20. Relationship to existing §6 law

ACT 10 does not replace §6.

§6 asks:

> when a proposition moves, did its evidence and invalidity conditions remain recoverable and operative?

ACT 10 adds the question exposed by multi-source reasoning:

> when several propositions are combined, what evidence gives the **new relation or conclusion** standing?

Together:

```text
SOURCE MOVEMENT
    governed by §6
        │
        ▼
source boundary travels

MULTI-SOURCE JOIN
    governed by ACT 10 candidate
        │
        ▼
the "therefore" needs its own warrant
```
