## 1 · `FD-4` · `DECIDES` — FOUNDER DECISION · CLOSED

```text
DEFINITION
  An act DECIDES when it resolves an ARCHITECTURALLY OPEN QUESTION among MATERIALLY
  DIFFERENT OUTCOMES, and the governing rule plus already-established inputs DO NOT
  UNIQUELY DETERMINE which outcome must obtain.
```

**⛔ NOT `DECIDES` — pure execution.** Executing a rule authored elsewhere is not deciding when

```text
rule + established inputs  →  exactly ONE required outcome
```

**and** the executor has no discretion to *alter the criterion · interpret an unresolved
condition · choose among permitted outcomes · supply a missing premise*.

```text
numeric threshold check with a fixed threshold            → ⛔ not DECIDES
route lookup from a complete deterministic table          → ⛔ not DECIDES
mechanical enforcement of an already-set permission       → ⛔ not DECIDES
```

**⭐ IS `DECIDES` — constitutionally meaningful resolution.** An act decides when it must

```text
interpret an ambiguous criterion
determine which of several PERMISSIBLE outcomes applies
weigh competing considerations
resolve conflicting rules
supply a criterion the rule did not supply
classify an unresolved fact where that classification determines the outcome
exercise DELEGATED DISCRETION
```

⭐ **A "policy evaluation" is not classified by its name.** The same phrase may denote pure
execution (⛔ not `DECIDES`) or the resolution of an open evaluative question (`DECIDES`),
according to the actual architectural act.

```text
⛔ GUARD — BRANCHING IS NOT DECIDING
     two code paths exist   ≠   the actor DECIDES
   The question is whether the outcome remained ARCHITECTURALLY OPEN at the point the act
   occurred.
```

⭐⭐ **The distinction is OPEN QUESTION versus EXECUTION — ⛔ not *human judgment versus
software*.** A deterministic software act can merely execute; a software act can genuinely decide
**if authority has been delegated to it to resolve something the governing rule left open.**

⚠️ **Charter cross-reference, carried prospectively.** §3.7's `DOES NOT MEAN` entry —
*"executing a rule someone else authored (⚠️ FD-4: drafted, not decided)"* — is now **decided**,
with the qualification above: rule-execution is outside `DECIDES` **only** where exactly one
outcome is required and no discretion is exercised. ⛔ §3.7 is not rewritten; this record governs.

## 2 · `FD-11a` · `GOVERNING SOURCE` termination — FOUNDER DECISION · CLOSED

```text
A chain of GOVERNING SOURCE is COMPETENT only if it TERMINATES FINITELY in a legitimate root.
```

**Two legitimate terminal roots, and only two:**

```text
1 · THE CONSTITUTION

2 · THE PERSON — but ONLY for authority the person is competent to grant, withhold or
    revoke concerning THAT PERSON'S OWN agency · participation · material · representation ·
    permissions · or other constitutionally recognized domain
```

⭐⭐ **The constitution does not manufacture the person's sovereignty. It RECOGNIZES where
personal authority is terminal, and establishes how the organism must honour it.**

**Conversely, a person's grant cannot:**

```text
⛔ grant authority over ANOTHER person merely by assertion
⛔ override a constitutional prohibition
⛔ expand an authority beyond the person's competent domain
⛔ create organism-wide authority where the constitution does not permit it
```

⚠️ **For shared or multi-person matters, one person's authority does ⛔ NOT silently stand in for
another person's.**

### Validity test — a chain is valid only when all five hold

```text
G-1  the IMMEDIATE grant is identifiable
G-2  the GRANTOR WAS COMPETENT to grant that authority
G-3  every DELEGATED grant stays WITHIN THE SCOPE granted to its source
G-4  the chain TERMINATES FINITELY in the CONSTITUTION or in competent PERSONAL authority
G-5  the required REVOCATION or SUPERSESSION path EXISTS
```

```text
competence or termination cannot be established   →   HAS AUTHORITY: NOT ESTABLISHED
```

⭐⭐ **No source may bootstrap its own competence merely by asserting that it is a governing
source.** *That is the closure `FD-11a` existed to supply: the graph now has somewhere to stop,
and a self-declared source is not a stop.*

**The asymmetry stands, unchanged:**

```text
uncertain CONSEQUENTIALITY   →  governance INCREASES
uncertain GOVERNING SOURCE   →  authority DOES NOT INCREASE
```

⚠️ **Charter cross-reference, carried prospectively.** §3.9's clause *"terminating in the
constitution or in the person, per FD-11"* is now **discharged** by §2 of this record. ⛔ §3.9 is
not rewritten.

**`FD-11b`** — the eventual taxonomy of governing-source forms — **remains downstream.**
