## 1 · `ACTING PERSON` — FOUNDER DECISION · FD-17 · DECIDED

⛔ **Not a seventh semantic kind.** It is a referent under `PERSON` (Amendment 2 §1), serving the
third-party predicate only.

```text
DEFINITION        the PERSON whose intentional action DIRECTLY ORIGINATES the act under
                  examination.
CARDINALITY       one acting person · multiple acting persons · ⭐ NO acting person
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ a model · service · scheduler · daemon or software process
                  ⛔ a person who authored a STANDING INSTRUCTION — they are not
                     automatically the acting person for every later execution of it
                  ⛔ an administrator capable of intervening AFTER THE FACT
GRANTED BY        n/a — ⭐ it is an observation about origination, ⛔ not a grant.
                  IT CONFERS NO AUTHORITY.
REVOKED BY        n/a
EVIDENCE          the intentional action, and its directness to the act examined
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  ⛔ PERSON (the affected class) · ⛔ the requester · ⛔ the account holder
                  · ⛔ the authorizing authority
```

⭐ **Predicate `C-g`, amended:**

```text
BEFORE   Does it affect a person other than the person, if any, who performed the act and
         the person whose material it concerns?
AFTER    Does the act MATERIALLY AFFECT a PERSON other than the ACTING PERSON, if any?
```

⭐⭐ **And the guard the ruling makes explicit: where there is NO acting person, that absence
does ⛔ NOT cause `C-g` to resolve `NO`.** An unattended scheduled act that materially affects a
person answers `C-g` YES. *An act without a human origin is not thereby an act without a human
consequence.*

## 2 · `EXISTS` — the `SUBJECT` collision removed prospectively · FD-18 · DECIDED

Constitutional **`SUBJECT`** is reserved for *what an act, claim, record, permission,
representation or relationship is **about***. ⛔ That term is no longer used inside `EXISTS`.

```text
EXISTS · AMENDED DEFINITION
                  an architectural entity or artifact is PRESENT IN A NAMED SYSTEM STATE OR
                  EVIDENTIARY LOCUS.
```

```text
EXISTS    → entity / artifact PRESENCE
SUBJECT   → what something is ABOUT
```

⛔ **Historical Phase 1 language remains untouched. No retrospective renaming.** Amendment 2 §2's
`EVIDENTIARY SUBJECT` / `REFERENTIAL SUBJECT` pairing is superseded for `EXISTS` by this
definition; it remains the correct reading of Phase 1's own records, which ⛔ are not edited.

Everything else in `EXISTS` stands as accepted: it grants no participation, no capability, no
authority and **no future entitlement**; `EXISTS yes · CAPABILITY no · PARTICIPATES no` is
constitutionally coherent.

## 3 · Selection and `THE ORGANISM'S OWN WORD` · FD-19 · DECIDED

Both tests preserved — `S-1` presented as originating from the organism · `S-2` the organism
supplied the semantic substance. Added:

```text
⛔ Mechanical or explicitly requested selection of ATTRIBUTED source material does not become
   THE ORGANISM'S OWN WORD merely because the organism selected it.

⭐ Selection BECOMES organism speech when the act of selection itself contributes a SEMANTIC
   ASSERTION — including:
     ranking presented as importance
     ordering presented as significance
     selection presented as diagnostic relevance
     selection presented as recommendation
     OMISSION used to imply a conclusion

⭐ Source material also becomes organism speech when ATTRIBUTION COLLAPSES, such that the
   resulting expression is presented as the organism's own substance.
```

```text
selection + intact attribution + no added semantic assertion   → ⛔ NOT organism speech
selection that itself asserts                                  → ⭐ organism speech
collapsed attribution                                          → ⭐ organism speech
```

⭐⭐ **The carrier does not determine authorship. The semantic act does.**

⚠️ Note the fifth trigger is **omission** — the only one that operates by what is *absent*. A
selection that asserts by leaving something out is organism speech, which means `C-e` can be
answered YES by an act that added no words at all.

## 4 · Derived person-authored material · FD-20 · DECIDED — provenance preserved, ⛔ authorship not manufactured

The governance intuition is accepted; the wording is corrected. ⛔ **Do not state that the person
literally authored every derived artifact** — that would collapse source authorship and
transformation authorship.

```text
ORIGINAL            person-authored material
DERIVED ARTIFACT    DERIVED FROM person-authored material
```

```text
GOVERNANCE TEST (the removal test, restated as what it is)
  If removing the person's expression would REMOVE OR MATERIALLY CHANGE the semantic substance
  of the derived artifact, the artifact remains GOVERNED AS DERIVED FROM person-authored
  material.

MIXED PROVENANCE
  ⭐ A derived artifact may ALSO contain organism-supplied semantic substance. Where it does,
  provenance is MIXED — ⛔ never transferred. Neither contribution erases the other.
```

`C-a` therefore reaches **summaries · extracts · semantic representations · embeddings ·
digests · other derived forms** wherever the person's semantic substance survives.

```text
⛔ IT DOES NOT MEAN   the person authored every token
⛔ IT DOES NOT MEAN   the person legally owns every transformation
⛔ IT DOES NOT MEAN   the organism supplied no semantic substance
```

⭐ **P2-00 defines constitutional provenance, ⛔ not copyright law.**

⚠️ **Cross-reference correction to Amendment 2 §3.** Its consequence paragraph read that derived
artifacts *"remain person-authored material."* Amended: they remain **governed as derived from**
person-authored material. ⭐ *Transformation does not erase the originating person, and origin does
not erase the transforming act* — the same authorship discipline this project already enforces on
its own editorial surfaces.

## 5 · `FD-5` · DECIDED — ⛔ NO SINGLE ORDERED AXIS

The epistemic states do ⛔ **not** form one confidence ladder. `CONTESTED` · `SEARCHED / NONE
FOUND` · `NOT SEARCHED` · `NOT AUTHORIZED TO DETERMINE` · `UNKNOWN` **answer different
questions**; none is inherently higher or lower than another.

```text
EPISTEMIC STANDING = a TYPED FAMILY of states, able to retain the relevant distinctions and,
                     where necessary, MORE THAN ONE DIMENSION.

⛔ No ordinal scale may be created for storage, UI, sorting or diagrammatic convenience.
⛔ No architecture may infer  UNKNOWN < CONTESTED < KNOWN  or any other total order,
   unless a later charter explicitly defines one FOR A SPECIFIC PURPOSE.
```

⭐ KIND 4 is therefore a **typed family**, not KIND 1's sibling. ⛔ It has no rungs.

⭐⭐ **Open to amendment, closed to ad hoc extension** — which avoids both failures named in the

lets the governed boundary expand without authority.

## 7 · Durability and the FD-15 guard — restated, unchanged

```text
physically persisted  ≠  architecturally durable

D-1 survives the originating act + its execution context
AND
D-2 remains available to alter a later organism act, relationship, permission,
    representation or decision

D-2 = NO              → constitutionally TRANSIENT, even if bytes remain somewhere
D-2 = UNDETERMINABLE  → PROVISIONALLY DURABLE, under FD-15
```

```text
⭐⭐ THE ASYMMETRY IS CONSTITUTIONAL
    uncertainty MAY increase governance
    uncertainty MAY NEVER increase authority

    undeterminable consequential predicate  → PROVISIONALLY CONSEQUENTIAL
    undeterminable authority                → AUTHORITY NOT ESTABLISHED
```

⛔ **The two directions may never become symmetrical.**
