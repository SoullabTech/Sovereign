## 1 · The four questions, kept separate

```text
CAPABILITY      What can this part do?
PARTICIPATION   Where may it enter the organism's processes?
AUTHORITY       What consequential acts may it legitimately perform?
GOVERNANCE      What grants, constrains, reviews, or revokes that authority?
```

⛔ Not collapsed into one diagram. ⛔ And no fifth question is manufactured here — see **FD-5**,
which asks whether one is *needed* rather than assuming one is.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ A structural finding about the term list itself

The twelve terms are **not one ladder with two additions.** They are **four different kinds of
thing**, and treating them as a single ordered list would silently impose an ordering on things
that have none:

```text
ORDERED RUNGS            PARTICIPATES · KNOWS · CONSIDERS · CONTRIBUTES · CONCLUDES(?)
(a part's position)      · DECIDES · HAS AUTHORITY
                         ⚠️ CONCLUDES is NEW — it was not a Phase 1 rung (FD-3)

UNORDERED CAPACITY       CAPABILITY   — what a part can do, at any rung or none
                         REFUSES      — ⛔ NOT the bottom of the ladder; a governed act

A RELATION               GOVERNING SOURCE — holds BETWEEN an authority and its origin;
                         ⛔ it is not a property a part has

EPISTEMIC STATES         CONTESTED · UNKNOWN — properties of KNOWLEDGE, ⛔ not of parts.
                         A part is not "in" contested; a claim is.
```

⚠️ **Two terms are also missing from the list and are load-bearing in the ruling itself:**
**`CONSEQUENTIAL`** (clauses 2 and 4 scope their entire requirement by it — see **FD-1**) and
**`EXISTS`** (Phase 1's bottom rung, which the twelve-term list drops — see **FD-2**). ⛔ Neither
is invented here.

## 3 · The twelve primitives — DRAFTED

Each carries the six mandated fields. `MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO` is the field that does the work.

---

### 3.1 · CAPABILITY

```text
DEFINITION        what a part CAN technically do, irrespective of any permission.
                  The `can` of the architecture, always and only.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it may do it · ⛔ that it is reached · ⛔ that its output is used
                  · ⛔ that anything authorized it
GRANTED BY        implementation that is reachable. Nothing else. ⛔ Not a design document,
                  ⛔ not an interface promising a result
REVOKED BY        removal, or the loss of reachability
EVIDENCE          a traced path to the capability, at a named subject
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  PARTICIPATES (entry is a permission) · any `may` term.
                  ⭐ The whole point of this primitive is to hold `can` apart from `may`.
```

### 3.2 · PARTICIPATES

```text
DEFINITION        is admitted into a named process of the organism at a named point.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it is callable (that is CAPABILITY) · ⛔ that it knows anything
                  · ⛔ that its output reaches an artifact · ⛔ any authority whatsoever
GRANTED BY        an explicit admission naming the process and the entry point
REVOKED BY        withdrawal of that admission
EVIDENCE          the admission record AND a traced entry
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  CAPABILITY below it · CONTRIBUTES above it.
                  ⭐ Phase 1's own sentence is the law here: "authentication establishes
                  PARTICIPATES, not HAS AUTHORITY" — a gate on WHO MAY INVOKE is not a gate
                  on WHAT MAY BE CLAIMED.
```

### 3.3 · KNOWS

```text
DEFINITION        holds, or may retrieve, information about a subject.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it may use it for anything · ⛔ that it has considered it
                  · ⛔ that the subject consented to its use, as distinct from its holding
GRANTED BY        a read grant, plus the information existing
REVOKED BY        revoking the read grant — including by the member, where the subject is
                  the member and the governing source is member-granted
EVIDENCE          the retrieval path AND the grant that permits retrieval
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  CONSIDERS. ⭐ Phase 1 recorded a KNOWS-not-CONSIDERS case "in its purest
                  form" (P3-B-15); the distinction is already load-bearing in evidence.
```

### 3.4 · CONSIDERS

```text
DEFINITION        the information enters the part's reasoning for a NAMED act.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it affected the outcome (that is CONTRIBUTES) · ⛔ a standing
                  permission for other acts · ⛔ that the member authorized this use because
                  they authorized the holding
GRANTED BY        a use grant scoped to the act
REVOKED BY        the grant's source, or expiry of the act's scope
EVIDENCE          the information reaching the reasoning input for that act
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  KNOWS below · CONTRIBUTES above. ⛔ Considering is not contributing:
                  a considered input may change nothing.
```

### 3.5 · CONTRIBUTES

```text
DEFINITION        its output demonstrably affects an artifact the organism produces.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it asserted anything about a person (that is CONCLUDES)
                  · ⛔ that it selected among options (DECIDES) · ⛔ that it binds anyone
GRANTED BY        a contribution grant naming the artifact and the boundary crossed
REVOKED BY        that grant's source
EVIDENCE          the contribution traceably present in the produced artifact
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  CONSIDERS below · CONCLUDES and DECIDES above.
                  ⚠️ Phase 1's C-01 is exactly the case where the corpus cannot say whether
                  a CONTRIBUTES node contributes — so ⭐ the target architecture must make
                  contribution OBSERVABLE, not merely assertable. (FD-10)
```

### 3.6 · CONCLUDES

```text
DEFINITION        MAY produce a statement ABOUT a subject that asserts something the subject
                  did not author — a characterization, a trajectory, a forecast, a
                  diagnosis-shaped claim.
                  ⭐⭐ This term is a `may`. The `can` of the same act is CAPABILITY.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ technical ability to emit such a sentence · ⛔ that the statement is
                  true · ⛔ that it may be persisted · ⛔ that it may be surfaced to the
                  subject · ⛔ that it may be surfaced to a third party
GRANTED BY        a governing source that names the subject class, the statement class, and
                  whether persistence and surfacing are included
REVOKED BY        that source — and, where the subject is the member, by the member if the
                  source is member-granted
EVIDENCE          the grant; and for its absence, a structural mechanism that prevents the
                  statement, ⛔ never an instruction with nothing measuring obedience
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  CAPABILITY (can ≠ may) · CONTRIBUTES (contributing text is not asserting
                  about a person) · DECIDES · HAS AUTHORITY
```

⭐⭐ **This is the primitive that gives C-04 its shape** — a system with the technical capacity
and a constitutional prohibition is `CAPABILITY: yes · CONCLUDES: no`, and is ⛔ **not**
architecturally equivalent to one authorized to conclude. ⚠️ **Whether that fully settles C-04 is
FD-9, and settling it is a `FOUNDER DECISION`, ⛔ never a Phase 1 finding.** Phase 1's standing
(`concluding-capable count = NOT SETTLED`) remains the evidentiary record either way.

### 3.7 · DECIDES

```text
DEFINITION        selects among options such that the organism acts differently as a result,
                  with no further authority intervening between the selection and the act.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ producing a recommendation · ⛔ producing a conclusion · ⛔ executing a
                  rule someone else authored (⚠️ FD-4: the deciding happened in the
                  authorship, not the execution — drafted, not decided)
GRANTED BY        a governing source naming the option space and the act
REVOKED BY        that source, or a superior one
EVIDENCE          the selection, the act, and the absence of an intervening authority
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  CONTRIBUTES and CONCLUDES below · HAS AUTHORITY above.
                  ⭐ Phase 1 found DECIDES EMPTY across 221 capabilities. ⛔ That is not a
                  finding that nothing decides; it is a finding that nothing was ESTABLISHED
                  as deciding — and Phase 2 may not fill the rung by inference.
```

### 3.8 · HAS AUTHORITY

```text
DEFINITION        its decisions BIND — the organism, or a person — and stand until revoked or
                  superseded according to its governing source.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ that it is trusted · ⛔ that it is correct · ⛔ that it is sovereign over
                  a person (§5 below)
GRANTED BY        ⭐ a GOVERNING SOURCE, and nothing else.
                  ⛔ NEVER: implementation · proximity · technical capability · model
                  intelligence · historical behaviour · naming · routing · persistence ·
                  frequency of use
REVOKED BY        that source, a superior source, or the person where the authority is
                  member-granted
EVIDENCE          a positive, traceable grant naming the source
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  DECIDES below. ⭐ Deciding is an act; authority is that the act binds.
                  ⚠️ Phase 1's one established HAS AUTHORITY node had a PARTIAL gate and NO
                  located governing source — in the TARGET architecture that combination is
                  ⛔ not admissible (§4 requirement in clause 4 of the ruling).
```

### 3.9 · GOVERNING SOURCE

```text
DEFINITION        the identified origin of a grant of authority, which can be CITED and which
                  can REVOKE. A relation, ⛔ not a property of a part.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ a document that mentions the capability · ⛔ a comment · ⛔ a test
                  · ⛔ a dated witness · ⛔ a design note · ⛔ a name in an invariant list
                  with no implementing binding
GRANTED BY        n/a — it is what grants. Its own legitimacy comes from a superior source,
                  terminating in the constitution or in the person, per FD-11
REVOKED BY        a superior source
EVIDENCE          a citable grant AND a working revocation path.
                  ⭐ Both halves: a source that cannot revoke is a description, not a source.
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  ⭐⭐ Phase 1's NAMES-WITHOUT-IMPLEMENTING class is exactly this trap — SEVEN
                  of its 27 located sources say, in the record's own words, that they
                  implement nothing or carry no authority. The target architecture must be
                  able to tell *a rule was found* from *a rule was found that binds nothing*.
REQUIREMENT       ⛔ CONSEQUENTIAL AUTHORITY MAY NOT REMAIN SOURCE-LESS in the target
                  architecture. The taxonomy of source kinds (constitutional ·
                  founder-delegated · member-granted · role-derived · protocol-derived ·
                  situational · other) is ⛔ NOT decided here — FD-11.
```

### 3.10 · REFUSES

```text
DEFINITION        declines to act, where the declining is itself the governed behaviour.
                  ⭐ A first-class capability, ⛔ not the absence of one.
                  TWO KINDS, kept apart: PERMITTED REFUSAL (may decline) and OBLIGATORY
                  REFUSAL (must decline).
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ failure · ⛔ error · ⛔ silence · ⛔ an empty result · ⛔ a timeout
                  · ⛔ an instruction to a model with nothing measuring obedience
GRANTED BY        a governing source (permitted) or imposed by one (obligatory)
REVOKED BY        that source. ⭐ An obligatory refusal revoked without its source acting is
                  the failure mode this primitive exists to make visible
EVIDENCE          a structural mechanism that produces the refusal, AND a refusal that is
                  RECOGNIZABLE AS A REFUSAL by its caller and, where relevant, the member
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  ⭐⭐ FAILURE. Phase 1 recorded a degraded output "indistinguishable in type
                  from a generated answer" — the exact shape this must not take. A refusal
                  the caller cannot tell from an answer is not a refusal.
                  ⭐ And ⛔ not into an unmeasured instruction: Phase 1 found a boundary that
                  was "a model-compliance instruction, not a structural refusal. Nothing
                  measures obedience."
```

### 3.11 · CONTESTED

```text
DEFINITION        two or more LEGITIMATE sources disagree about one claim, under the same
                  scope and altitude, and no competent authority has resolved it.
                  A property of a CLAIM, ⛔ not of a part.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ FALSE · ⛔ UNKNOWN (contested has too many supported accounts; unknown
                  has none) · ⛔ that one side is probably right · ⛔ that resolution is owed
ENTERED BY        detection of mutually incompatible accounts from legitimate sources
EXITED BY         a resolution from an authority competent to resolve it, or a ruling that
                  resolution is unnecessary — ⛔ never by time, ⛔ never by one account
                  becoming more convenient
EVIDENCE          both accounts, each with its own provenance, carried together
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  a single account · NO · UNKNOWN · a silent selection by rendering
BEHAVIOUR         ⚠️ How contested knowledge behaves in the organism is ⛔ NOT decided here:
                  what may still participate · what may be surfaced · what may not be
                  concluded from it · who can resolve it · what evidence resolves it ·
                  whether resolution is necessary at all. That is FD-6, six decisions.
                  ⭐ Do not design contradiction away.
```

### 3.12 · UNKNOWN — and the eight representable states

```text
DEFINITION        the family head: the organism does not hold an answer.
DOES NOT MEAN     ⛔ NO · ⛔ zero · ⛔ absent · ⛔ refused · ⛔ false
REQUIREMENT       ⭐⭐ The target organism must be able to REPRESENT all eight, structurally
                  and not merely conversationally, ⛔ without collapsing any into NO:

  unknown                     no answer held, cause unstated
  not determined              the question was carried and not answered
  not observed                the thing exists and has not been watched
  not searched                ⭐ NEW — nobody looked
  searched / none found       a bounded search returned nothing
  contested                   §3.11
  withheld                    ⭐ a source declined to state it
  not authorized to determine ⭐ NEW — the organism may not find out

MAPPING FROM PHASE 1, exact where exact:
  NONE FOUND · NONE LOCATED  → searched / none found
  NOT DETERMINED             → not determined
  NO STATUS WORD             → withheld
  CONTESTED                  → contested
  ⭐ THREE ARE NEW: not observed · not searched · not authorized to determine.
  ⚠️ Phase 1 could not distinguish "searched and found nothing" from "nobody looked" in
  every case, because its instrument recorded results rather than search scopes. The
  target architecture must not inherit that limit.
GRANTED / REVOKED BY
                  n/a — these are states of knowledge, not grants. ⛔ Which is why FD-5 asks
                  whether they belong on an axis at all rather than assuming they do.
MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO
                  each other, or into NO. ⭐ Epistemic humility represented STRUCTURALLY.
```

## 5 · Human sovereignty is upstream — carried as law, not as a definition

The architecture distinguishes **the authority of the human participant** from **the capabilities
of the computational organism**. The system may perceive, remember, model, infer, reflect,
propose, challenge or refuse **within explicitly granted bounds**. ⛔ Those capacities do not make
it sovereign over the person.

Where the architecture assigns MAIA or any subsystem genuine authority, that authority is
**named · bounded · justified · revocable** according to its governing source. ⚠️ Whether any such
assignment is made at all is **FD-12**.
