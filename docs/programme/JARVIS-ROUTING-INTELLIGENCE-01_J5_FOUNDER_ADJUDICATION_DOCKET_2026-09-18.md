## 2 · Proposed routing constitution

### Article I — routing is capability selection, never authority selection

JARVIS may select:

- whether a model is needed;
- which evidence class is admissible;
- a task shape;
- an evidence-backed model family;
- a review topology;
- an authorized transport.

JARVIS may not infer or widen:

- Work Unit authority;
- external disclosure;
- provider spend;
- merge/deploy/production authority;
- canonical or constitutional standing.

> **Routing chooses a bounded intelligence attempt. It never chooses authority.**

## 3 · Article II — order of decision

Every intelligent routing act must preserve this order:

```text
1. deterministic capability?
2. evidence class / custody
3. task shape
4. eligible model family
5. independent review topology
6. authorized transport
7. explicit external authority if required
8. attempt
9. host-derived reconciliation
10. governing human / founder authority
```

No later layer may answer an unresolved earlier layer.

## 4 · Article III — evidence classes

### E0 — TASK_TEXT

Synthetic or non-confidential task text.

May be local. May be external only when external-network authority already exists.

### E1 — REPOSITORY_LOCAL

Repository evidence inside the local governed worktree.

Local model paths only by default.

### E2 — CONTINUITY_LOCAL

JARVIS / Claude continuity and other LOCAL_ONLY development memory.

**Local only.**

No model capability, cost, urgency or disagreement may promote E2 to external eligibility.

### E3 — EXTERNAL_REPO_BUNDLE

Exact bounded repository evidence admitted for external review.

Requires a distinct, load-bearing repository-disclosure grant in addition to external-network authority and spend when applicable.

The current lower provider seam does not yet carry that grant mechanically; J6 must repair this before E3 automatic routing can exist.

### E4 — SENSITIVE_OR_PRODUCTION

Member/client/PHI/secrets/production/confidential material.

No external authority is created by this programme.

## 8 · Article VII — independent review

> **A retry is not a second opinion.**

An independent second review requires at least one of:

- a distinct model family; or
- an independent deterministic verifier capable of falsifying the material claim.

Two calls to the same model/checkpoint with the same evidence posture are retries, regardless of prompt variation or attempt count.

J6 must repair current reconciliation, which presently counts two same-model clean attempts as multiple evidence.

## 9 · Article VIII — external escalation

JARVIS may recommend, but not automatically execute, an external review when:

1. local independent reviews materially disagree;
2. both local reviewers preserve the same consequential uncertainty;
3. the task is `FRONTIER_UNKNOWN`;
4. a high-risk/Class A claim would materially benefit from an evidence-backed independent external challenge;
5. the founder explicitly requests external review.

Recommendation sends nothing externally.

External execution requires the Work Unit to already carry every required authority:

- `network.external`;
- separate repository-disclosure authority for E3;
- `provider.spend` when metered;
- exact model/transport allowlisting;
- credential readiness.

No authority may be inferred from the routing recommendation.
## 10 · Article IX — model family before transport

The cognitive selection is the model family.

Transport is resolved afterward.

Example:

```text
Nemotron selected for eligible task
        ↓
resolve authorized transport
        ↓
Tinker / NVIDIA / interactive Zen as actually admissible
```

If no authorized transport exists:

> **HOLD / escalate. Do not silently substitute another model family.**

Changing transport within one model family may occur only when the transport carries equivalent evidence membrane and authority properties defined by the implementation contract.

## 11 · Article X — lifecycle state is host-owned

Model output may contain:

- findings;
- counterexamples;
- unresolved questions;
- proposed next actions.

Model output may not author the Work Unit's authoritative lifecycle state.

The host derives state from:

- durable attempt/result contracts;
- deterministic verification;
- review independence;
- governing authority.

Model text claiming `MERGED`, `DEPLOYED`, `COMPLETE`, `RATIFIED`, or equivalent has no state-changing effect.

## 12 · Article XI — consensus is evidence, not authority

Two or more agreeing model families may increase evidence density.

Their strongest automatic standing is:

`EVIDENCE_PRESENTED`

unless another existing non-model rule yields a stricter stop such as:

- `REPAIR_BEFORE_WITNESS`
- `REVIEW_DISAGREEMENT`
- `NEEDS_KELLY`.

Model agreement cannot create:

- merge authority;
- deployment authority;
- production authority;
- constitutional standing;
- founder ruling.

## 14 · Article XIII — response budget is a model/adapter profile

One raw `maxTokens` value is not assumed to create equivalent reasoning opportunity across model families.

J6 may define bounded evidence-backed response-budget profiles by model/adapter/task role.

Constraints:

- profiles are explicit and reviewable;
- no unlimited budget;
- no model may silently increase its own budget;
- budget exhaustion is a distinct execution outcome, not cognitive failure;
- budget changes do not widen evidence or authority.

Current evidence:

- Inkling B4 required more than 1,024 and completed at 2,001 output tokens under a 4,096 ceiling.
- Nemotron B2–B5 required roughly 2,200–3,000 output tokens to produce final text.
- Nemotron B1 remained non-adjudicable at 4,096 and gains no CODE_GROUNDED standing.
