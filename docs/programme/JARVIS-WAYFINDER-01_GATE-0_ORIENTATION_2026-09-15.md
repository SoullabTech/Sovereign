**Status vocabulary — these four only:**
`Established` (attributable evidence against predeclared criteria) · `Plausible hypothesis` (reasonable, untested) · `Contradicted` (evidence points the other way) · `Unexamined`.

## 5. Exit condition

Gate 0 closes when:
- every census field is answered or explicitly `UNKNOWN`
- every standing belief carries its evidence and one of the four statuses
- current members are qualified against the six criteria
- ≥ 5 candidates are named on the bench

On close, Gate 1 opens. Nothing else opens.
