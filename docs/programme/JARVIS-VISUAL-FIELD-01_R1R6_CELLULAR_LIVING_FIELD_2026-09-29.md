# JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01 · R1R6 — Cellular Living Field Prototype

**Parent flow:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Founder decision:** HYBRID direction authorized  
**Physics basis:** R1R5 A/B comparison  
**Geometry basis:** R1R3 recursive Grokker field  
**Standing:** local prototype only; merge, deploy, persistence, MAIA, and autonomous relation generation remain closed.

## Governing proposition

The Living Field should behave like a nested biological ecology:
- membranes hold without imprisoning;
- attention reveals and locally reshapes the field;
- cross-world relationships form legible bridges;
- semantic richness increases with depth;
- recent attention leaves a short-lived trace;
- the wider world remains recognizable.

## Law 1 — LIVING-MEMBRANE-LAW-01

Containment is relational rather than rigid.

A membrane:
- preserves region identity;
- allows local flex;
- resists escape progressively rather than clipping;
- transmits relational influence across boundaries;
- may brighten or expand slightly under attention;
- retains a fading trace of recent crossings.

Shortest form:

> **A membrane holds without imprisoning.**

## Law 2 — ATTENTION-FIELD-LAW-01

Attention reveals and locally reshapes the field without silently rewriting its structure.

### GLANCE
Trigger: brief hover / first touch focus.

Effects:
- focused node clarifies;
- label enlarges;
- direct edges begin to appear;
- unrelated material softens slightly;
- no substantial geometry change.

### ATTEND
Trigger: sustained attention (~350ms) or explicit focus.

Effects:
- direct relational neighborhood brightens;
- unrelated field recedes;
- cross-world bridge corridors activate;
- containing membrane becomes more legible;
- immediate related nodes may move slightly nearer.

### DWELL
Trigger: sustained attention (~1200ms) or explicit "stay with this."

Effects:
- local relation rationale appears;
- second-order semantic detail may become available;
- active bridge remains visible;
- ambient motion cools rather than intensifies;
- recent attention trace is written to session-only visual state.

### Attention memory

Attention may remember:
- current focus node;
- current attention phase;
- most recent 3–5 attended nodes;
- the bridge corridors awakened by those nodes;
- the most recent local focus path;
- the timestamp of each recent trace.

Attention may not infer or persist:
- importance;
- preference;
- trait;
- diagnosis;
- emotional significance;
- durable memory.

Default visual trace decay: ~8–12 seconds.

> **Attention memory is orientation residue, not personal memory.**

## Law 3 — CROSS-WORLD-LINK-LAW-01

Relations may cross worlds freely enough to become perceptible while membership and containment remain stable until explicitly changed.

### Bridge formation

A bridge is a bundled world-to-world corridor derived from explicit cross-world semantic relations.

A bridge may form when:
1. at least one explicit semantic relation crosses two parent worlds; and
2. that relation is currently active through attention, focus, traversal, or an explicit relational view.

At wide scale:
- multiple cross-world relations bundle into one soft corridor;
- bridge intensity reflects the currently active relation set;
- individual edges remain mostly latent.

At deeper attention:
- the corridor differentiates into specific node-to-node links;
- relation verbs and rationale become inspectable.

### Bridge anatomy

A bridge has:
- **origin membrane point**;
- **transit corridor**;
- **arrival membrane point**;
- **relation bundle** inside the corridor.

The membrane crossing points may brighten locally.

### Bridge memory

Recently active bridges may leave a faint fading trace for several seconds after attention moves elsewhere.

This trace is session-only visual continuity.

### Anti-hairball law

Default wide view:
- only currently active or materially strong bridge corridors appear;
- individual cross-world edges sleep.

Focused view:
- direct links become visible;
- unrelated edges stay latent;
- second-degree relations require deeper attention.

## Cellular physics

The hybrid prototype uses:

```text
pack / anchor geography
+ sibling repulsion
+ collision
+ semantic-link springs
+ parent-center cohesion
+ circular membrane resistance
+ local drag response
+ short-lived attention attraction
```

Global topology remains stable.

Local neighborhoods may respond.

The field settles after disturbance.

## Atmospheric elemental ecology

Color is environmental rather than categorical fill.

- Fire — ember / amber atmosphere
- Water — deep blue-teal atmosphere
- Earth — ochre / moss atmosphere
- Air — cool violet / sky atmosphere
- Aether — iridescent plum / gold atmosphere

Each region uses:
- low-opacity interior tint;
- luminous membrane edge;
- attention-responsive local glow;
- relation threads that borrow the participating worlds' tones.

2. constrained living node physics inside worlds;
3. cross-world bridge corridors;
4. glance → attend → dwell behavior;
5. fading attention traces;
6. local membrane response to attention;
7. relation visibility increasing with attention;
8. click-to-zoom world focus and widening outward;

## Falsifiers

R1R6 fails if:
- bridges look like ordinary graph spaghetti;
- attention makes the whole field jump;
- color reads as categorical decoration rather than atmosphere;
- hover becomes tooltip-only behavior;
- membranes behave like hard clipping masks;
- zoom merely enlarges the same information;
- recent attention persists long enough to feel like ranking or surveillance;
- the field never settles.
