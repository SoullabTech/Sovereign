## 12. JARVIS recommendation — proposal only

**Recommend: TAKE AS A CANONICAL COROLLARY OF THE REPRESENTATION AUTHORITY LAW.**

Not as a new implementation layer.

Not as a claim that every domain uses the same mechanism.

Not by widening Interface Humility or Temporal Law.

The proposed canonical corollary is:

> **GRANT–EFFECT BOUNDARY**
>
> **Authority existence ≠ authority consumption ≠ authority sufficiency.**
>
> A governed effect may exercise only authority it actually consumes from a governed grant sufficient for that effect, and it may not exceed the scope of that grant.

### Why corollary rather than separate universal law

The invariant sharpens the existing Representation Authority Law's operative machinery:

`SUBSTITUTION → GRANT → ATTESTATION`

It explains what must be true **after a grant exists**:

- the effect must actually consume it;
- the consumed authority must be sufficient for that effect;
- the effect must remain bounded by scope.

Treating this as a corollary preserves one constitutional home and avoids minting a second overlapping law.

### Retained defeater

Even if taken, the semantic-drift defeater remains live:
