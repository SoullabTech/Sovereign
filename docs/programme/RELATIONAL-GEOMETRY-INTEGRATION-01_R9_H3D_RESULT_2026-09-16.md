## Question

Can binding-evidence classes be acquired from raw member language without an answer key?

Production-equivalent `claude-sonnet-4-6`, two runs across six frozen fixtures.

## Result

10/12 classifications matched the frozen labels.
The only mismatch was the fixture "When I think about the new path, I feel hope rise immediately." The frozen label called this `EXPLICIT_LINK`; Sonnet returned `EXPLICIT_WHEN_THEN` in both runs.

That reveals a category error in the experiment: `EXPLICIT_WHEN_THEN` describes the **binding form**, while `EVOKES` describes the **relation predicate**. They are not competing labels.

## Architectural correction

Relational mediation must preserve two independent coordinates:

1. **Binding form** — how source and target are explicitly connected in evidence.
2. **Predicate semantics** — what kind of relation that connection expresses.

Constitutional sentence:

> **Evidence form may authorize that two nodes are bound; it does not by itself determine the meaning of the edge.**
