## Purpose

R2D lets one locus become perceptually dominant without causing loss of the larger ecology.

The field should support:
- deep node focus;
- relation focus;
- held tension;
- peripheral whole-field orientation;
- immediate whole recovery.
## Node focus

Node focus should preserve:
- the node;
- its containing world;
- its direct neighborhood;
- directional awareness of other worlds.

The camera may move closer.
Other regions may quiet.
The member should still know where the focus belongs.

## Relation focus

Selecting a relation must reframe:
- source;
- target;
- between-space.

The camera should not remain centered on the first-selected node.
## Held tension

For a tension relation:
- both poles receive equal endpoint emphasis;
- the camera frames both simultaneously;
- the relation remains taut;
- the midpoint remains open;
- no synthesis node appears;
- the between-space may be perceptible as held relational space.

Founder question:

> **Does the tension feel held by the field rather than solved by the system?**
## Peripheral context

At deep semantic scale, worlds outside the camera frame may appear as quiet directional context echoes.

Context echoes:
- are not buttons;
- are not app navigation;
- indicate continued presence and direction;
- remain subordinate to the focused locus;
- disappear when the actual world is comfortably visible.

## Mechanical acceptance

Require:
1. selecting a cross-world relation changes camera framing to include both endpoints;
2. tension endpoints are simultaneously visible after relation focus;
3. tension midpoint has no synthetic node;
4. endpoint emphasis is equal;
5. deep node focus yields peripheral context echoes for offscreen worlds;
6. visible worlds do not receive redundant echoes;
7. Whole removes focus/echo state and restores 1.0 scale;
8. mobile/touch provides equivalent orientation;
9. browser errors = 0.

STOP before R2E Semantic Condensation.
