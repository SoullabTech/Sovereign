# 3 · Phase-0 falsification pass — five licensed qualifications found

These are **qualifications**, not defeats. Each is based on a primary-read source and names what would have to remain true for the Phase-0 assumption to survive.

## FQ-1 — `Reading opens conversation, not report`

**Contrary / qualifying evidence**: Sommers' primary text shows durable written feedback can play a leading developmental role when embedded in an ongoing writer-reader partnership; specificity and the writer's ability to use the response matter. The medium is not reducible to synchronous talk.

**Threat**: `not report` could harden into a ban on useful durable/asynchronous readings.

**Survival condition**: preserve the ruling as **conversation is the governing relationship and default doorway**, not `written reading is forbidden`. A written reading may be useful as an artifact of/for dialogue, especially after discussion.

**Disposition**: **QUALIFIED — assumption survives narrowed.**

## FQ-2 — `Writer chooses developmental act`

**Contrary / qualifying evidence**: Singh et al.'s review reports that adaptive, context-sensitive proactive systems can enhance collaboration, while high user control supports satisfaction, trust, and ownership.

**Threat**: interpreting writer choice as `MAIA must never initiate or suggest an act` may make the collaboration unnecessarily inert.

**Survival condition**: MAIA may **offer** a developmental act, question, or change of stance; only the writer can commission/accept it. Proactivity proposes authority; it does not acquire it.

**Disposition**: **QUALIFIED — assumption survives as decision authority, not initiative monopoly.**

## FQ-3 — `Write feeds Develop`

**Contrary / qualifying evidence**: Bhat et al. (2026), *Reactive Writers* (`arXiv:2603.10374`), a falsification source added during this pass, analyzed 1,291 AI co-writing sessions plus 19 retrospective interviews. AI suggestions seeded directions writers later elaborated; writers often did not notice that influence while still feeling in control.

**Threat**: if Write history feeds Develop indiscriminately, MAIA's own earlier suggestions can become recursive developmental context and later look like evidence of what the writer wanted or what the Work was becoming.

**Survival condition**: provenance separation is mandatory across the feed. MAIA-originated suggestions/observations never become writer standing merely through uptake or warm discussion; Develop re-grounds in the current manuscript and current writer commission before making higher-scale claims.

**Disposition**: **QUALIFIED — feed may orient; it may not launder authority.**

## FQ-4 — `Session is the primary quality unit`

**Contrary / qualifying evidence**: MindCopilot shows interaction-aware measures reveal important behavior that output-only evaluation misses, but its metric suite explicitly measures both alignment and editing effort around actual output. It establishes that interaction matters **in addition to** output; it does not establish that session experience is sufficient.

**Threat**: a pleasant/helpful-feeling session could still degrade the artifact, voice, factuality, or authorship.

**Survival condition**: define the session as the **primary collaboration-quality unit**, paired with independent Work outcomes: voice preservation, scope truth, provenance, artifact quality, and writer authority. Session quality cannot waive those gates.

**Disposition**: **QUALIFIED — `primary` narrowed to collaboration quality.**

## FQ-5 — `Complexity stays below the writer experience`

**Contrary / qualifying evidence**: Singh et al.'s review associates high user control with satisfaction/trust/ownership and highlights transparency/social presence; the corroborated B5 lead points in the same direction but is not needed to license this qualification.

**Threat**: hiding complexity can accidentally hide consequential choice, system initiative, memory, or authority that the writer should be able to see and control.

**Survival condition**: hide **machinery**, not **meaningful authority or choice**. Provenance types, retrieval, anchors and internal ontology remain below the experience; controls that determine what MAIA may do, remember, or change remain legible and usable.

**Disposition**: **QUALIFIED — assumption survives with `complexity` distinguished from `choice/authority`.**

## Row C — `sovereignty decides eligibility; quality decides readiness`

**Kind of claim**: **constitutional / normative. Not empirical.**

This is a rule about *how decisions are made*, not a claim about the world. No finding about model performance can falsify it, because it does not assert that sovereign systems perform better — it asserts what may ship and on whose authority.

The row is **self-insulating by design**, and deliberately so: the ruled Strand G disposition already routes contrary evidence to `persistent capability gap → RECORDED FINDING`, explicitly not to a provider exception. Evidence of a capability deficit is an anticipated output of the rule, not a defeat of it.

**Proposed disposition**: **NOT AN EMPIRICAL CLAIM. Exempt from the falsification quota. Survives unamended. Amendable only by separate constitutional act**, never by a benchmark result — per the standing ruling that a result may not amend the constitution that authorized the experiment.

## Row A — `writer chooses scope`

**Kind of claim**: **mixed — normative core, empirical periphery.**

- *Normative*: the writer holds authority over what is examined in their own Work. This follows from the sovereignty invariants and is not decidable by outcome data.
- *Empirical*: whether system-chosen scope produces better developmental insight than writer-chosen scope. Genuinely testable.

The two do not compete. Even if system-controlled scope measurably outperformed, the normative half would stand and the finding would surface as a tension to be designed around — it would change *how* scope is offered, never *who decides*.

**No new literature is required, because FQ-2 already governs this row.** Choosing the scope of examination *is* a developmental act, and FQ-2 ruled that MAIA may offer a developmental act while only the writer commissions it — *proactivity proposes authority; it does not acquire it.* Row A is an instance of that ruling, not an independent question.

**Proposed disposition**: **QUALIFIED by extension of FQ-2 — MAIA may propose a scope or a change of scale; the writer holds the decision.** Empirical evidence may inform how scope is offered; it cannot relocate the decision. No separate falsification finding is owed.
