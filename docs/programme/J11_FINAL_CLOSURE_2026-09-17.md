## 4. Constitutional standing now

The Representation Authority Law remains the constitutional home.

The Grant–Effect Boundary is now its canonical corollary.

Operationally, an authority-bearing effect must distinguish:

1. **authority existence** — can the exact governed grant be named?
2. **authority consumption** — does this exact effect actually consume that authority?
3. **authority sufficiency** — is the consumed grant plus required attestation sufficient for this exact effect?
4. **scope** — does the effect remain within the grant rather than silently widening it?
