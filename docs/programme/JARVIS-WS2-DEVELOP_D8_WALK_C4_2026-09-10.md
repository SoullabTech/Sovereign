## Why the prediction failed — C was never as monist as its name

D7's specification of C says two things with **different persistence laws**:

```text
THE WORK            primary field, held PERMANENTLY
SECOND SURFACE      only MAIA's current contribution, EPHEMERAL, clears
```

```text
A   "there is no room but the Work"   -> ONE primitive: place
B   "inquiry organizes"               -> ONE primitive: the question
C   durable Work / ephemeral turn     -> TWO classes, already
```

⭐ **C was named for its ephemeral half and judged as though that half governed everything. It does not.** The prediction mistook the label for the architecture.

## Stress 1 · Does the North Star belong to the Work without a third room? — **PASS**

The North Star is **writer-authored, Work-level, durable** — the same class as the Work side already holds. Not manuscript prose, but the writer's material *about* their Work.

It differs from the manuscript only in **visibility**, not in **persistence** — and visibility-on-demand is a property, not an organizing principle. **No third primitive is required.**

## Stress 2 · ⭐⭐⭐ Reachable without visible — **and C enforces the B/M4 law STRUCTURALLY**

```text
writing normally              North Star not displayed
writer asks for it            reachable
writer asks MAIA to read
  the Work against it         active FOR THAT EXCHANGE
exchange ends                 the reading clears; the orientation remains
```

⭐⭐ **The aspiration lives on the durable side. Its application lives on the side that cannot persist.**

> **B/M4 ruled: *persistence of an aspiration must not become persistence of its application.*** In B that had to be asserted against its own grammar. **In C the split is the architecture.**

```text
A   neither aspiration nor application could exist above the line
B   both were inquiry-shaped, so both persisted   -> the rubric problem
C   they fall on OPPOSITE SIDES of a split C already has
```

⭐ **C's two-sidedness maps exactly onto the distinction B could not make.** No rubric can form, because the thing that would become a rubric — the standing comparison — is turn-side and clears.

## Stress 3 · Persistence separate from application — **PASS, by stress 2**

*"I don't want this to become a textbook"* persists as orientation. It authorizes no spontaneous *"this section is becoming too textbook-like"* — and in C the comparison has nowhere to live between commissions.

## Stress 4 · Lineage — **PASS, and C makes D5's atomicity non-optional**

If MAIA helped word a North Star sentence, that sentence moved **turn-side → Work-side by a writer act** — which is precisely D5's adoption pattern: proposal, then a writer act, then canonical.

⚠️ **But the turn-side clears.** The proposal that seeded the sentence is *gone* afterward.

⭐ **So in C, provenance not captured at the moment of adoption is gone forever** — no reconstruction is possible. **C turns D5 §6A's atomicity requirement from a discipline into a structural necessity**: record revision and provenance together, or lose it irrecoverably.

*(Consistent with C/M3-4: content ancestry is recordable, process context is not. Here the ancestry survives **only if** written at adoption.)*

## ⭐⭐⭐ The largest conclusion of the walk

All three candidates independently arrived at the same result from different directions:

```text
A   needed to stop being purely place-organized to hold Work-level orientation
B   needed to stop being purely inquiry-organized for the same reason
C   ALREADY had two classes — and succeeded exactly where it was not monist
```

> ### The failure is monism itself.
> **Not "place is wrong", "questions are wrong", or "turns are wrong". Each primitive is excellent for one class of object and damaging when asked to organize every class.**

**And C is the proof by the positive case**: the one cell where a candidate performed well is the one cell where it had two organizing classes available. **The walk did not merely eliminate architectures; it identified what an architecture must have.**

Writer's Studio contains at least these, and they are **not one kind of thing**:

```text
DURABLE CREATIVE ORIENTATION     writer-authored, Work-level, dormant by default
PRESENT INQUIRY                  what the writer is working on now
CURRENT MAIA TURN                ephemeral, clears
HISTORICAL PROCESS CONTEXT       append-only, reachable, never resurfacing
MANUSCRIPT / REVISION STATE      canonical, append-only, provenance-bearing
```
