## 1 · A — RETRIEVAL VOCABULARY AS EVIDENCE OF RETROSPECTIVE CLASS MEMBERSHIP

### ✅ **RATIFIED, NARROWLY.**

> Retrieval vocabulary **may** be used as evidence that a prefix turn is itself a
> retrospective / reaching-back gesture.

⭐ **That is all.** In the two measured runs `HOP 1` admitted the retrospective asks and
excluded the non-retrospective turn, so it has demonstrated usefulness for **class membership
in this specific mechanism**.

⛔ It has **not** demonstrated: what object is being remembered · which retrospective ask is the
intended antecedent · which earlier turn contains the remembered material.

⭐ **A carries no authority beyond the class it actually discriminates.**

---

## 2 · B — CLASS MEMBERSHIP AS ANTECEDENT IDENTITY

### ❌ **REFUSED.**

The mechanism makes an inference the evidence does not support:

```text
"this prefix turn is also retrospective"  →  "this prefix turn is a legitimate bridge
                                              to what the member means now"
```

⛔ **Those are not equivalent.** Both P1 and W1 produced **multiple** retrospective prefix
turns; `HOP 1` established only that they belonged to the same broad class. It never asked
*which of these retrospective turns is the member referring to?* Every admitted hop was
flattened into the same downstream candidate map and `HOP 2` carrier counts took over.

⭐⭐ **The antecedent-selection problem was not answered weakly. It was never represented as a
distinct question. That is the decisive defect.**

---

## 3 · P1 STANDING

⭐ P1's observed output remains a **historical fact**: the bridge returned the marker-bearing
result under the P1 wording. ⛔ **P1 no longer stands as evidence that the bridge lawfully
identified the member's intended antecedent.** Its green depended on this chain:

```text
current retrospective ask
        ↓ shared "remember"
earlier FAILED retrospective ask
        ↓ contains the restated marker
marker-bearing candidates
```

⛔ The bridge did **not** recover the original telling. It recovered a **later failed question
in which the member happened to repeat the remembered object.**

> ⭐⭐ **P1 demonstrated successful recovery by LEXICAL COINCIDENCE, not successful ANTECEDENT
> RESOLUTION.** That distinction is durable.

---

## 4 · ⭐⭐ THE GOVERNING EVIDENTIARY LAW — RATIFIED

> **Evidence that two turns belong to the same retrospective class may establish CLASS
> MEMBERSHIP, but may not establish that one is the ANTECEDENT of the other. Antecedent
> identity requires evidence of REFERENCE, not merely evidence of shared retrospective
> intent.**

Operational form:

> ⭐ **Reaching back is not the same relation as reaching back TO THIS.**

⛔ That is the law `HOP 1` presently violates.

---

## 5 · THE OPACITY RULE — NO CONFLICT

⛔ The conflict claim **remains withdrawn**; it was never entailed. All three stand together:

```text
retrieval vocabulary → may evidence retrospective intent      ✅
retrieval vocabulary → does not identify remembered object    ✅
shared retrospective vocabulary → identifies antecedent       ❌
```

⛔ The defect is **not** that `remember` is inherently forbidden. It is **AUTHORITY INFLATION**:
a signal competent to establish retrospective class membership was allowed to acquire authority
to establish antecedent identity.

---

## 6 · REPAIR STANDING

⛔ This adjudication **does not choose a replacement instrument.** It establishes what a lawful
repair must accomplish. Any future repair must keep these **distinct**:

```text
JOB 1  Is this a retrospective request?
JOB 2  Which prior turn is being referred to?
JOB 3  What remembered object / material does that turn lead to?
```

⭐ The current bridge partially answers **JOB 1**, conflates that answer with **JOB 2**, and
`HOP 2` proceeds as though JOB 2 had already been settled. ⛔ **That is the architectural
break.**

## 7 · ⚠️ TWO CONSEQUENCES OF THIS RULING — ⛔ recorded, not acted on

### 7.1 ⭐⭐ The frozen oracle inherits P1's withdrawal

`tests/constitutional/lane1/c1-bridge-acceptance.ts` asserts P1 as `g => g.includes(MARKER)` —
*must recover 22*. Under §3 that assertion now encodes **recovery by lexical coincidence** as a
pass condition.

⛔ **So P1-as-TEST is in the same position as P1-as-EVIDENCE.** The current implementation
passes P1 **while violating §4** — measured, not inferred — therefore **P1 cannot discriminate
a lawful antecedent resolver from an unlawful one.** ⚠️ A future candidate that passes P1 has
demonstrated it reproduces the coincidence, ⛔ not that it resolves antecedents.

⭐ **Scope, stated precisely so the next lane does not over-withdraw: N1 and N2 are
UNDISTURBED.** N1 tests **origin** (only member words carry a bridge); N2 tests **terminal
abstention**. ⛔ Neither law is touched by this ruling. ⛔ The freeze is not reopened here and no
oracle is amended.

#### 7.1a ⭐ FOUNDER REFINEMENT (ratified 2026-09-15) — P1 is not false, it is non-discriminating

⛔ P1 has **not** become false. It has become **non-discriminating for the newly ratified law**.
`g => g.includes(MARKER)` can still tell you **whether** the marker was recovered; ⛔ it cannot
tell you **whether that recovery was lawful antecedent resolution or the lexical coincidence
now measured in P1**.

```text
P1 observed outcome ............ preserved
P1 as recovery regression ...... ⭐ STILL DESCRIBES OLD BEHAVIOUR — a legitimate residual use
P1 as lawfulness oracle ........ ❌ WITHDRAWN
N1 origin oracle ............... ✅ undisturbed
N2 abstention oracle ........... ✅ undisturbed
frozen acceptance file ......... ⛔ NOT AMENDED
```

⭐⭐ **We do not rewrite history by making the old test disappear. We stop treating its green as
evidence for a property it never tested.** ⚠️ This refines §8's coarser
*"P1 as a discriminating ORACLE — WITHDRAWN BY CONSEQUENCE"*: the withdrawal is of its
**lawfulness** standing only, ⛔ not of its use as a regression on prior behaviour.

### 7.2 ⚠️ The forward question may be thinly evidenced in this corpus

> *What evidence is sufficient to establish antecedent identity once retrospective intent has
> already been established?*

⭐ **In both measured runs, the ONLY referential evidence present anywhere was the member's own
RESTATEMENT of the object.** The two carriers that reached the marker are the restated marker
tokens; at W1 no admitted hop restated anything, and nothing referential existed to find.

⚠️ So this corpus can exhibit antecedent identity **only in the ask-twice case**. ⛔ For a member
who asks once, it contains no referential evidence at all — which is precisely
`FIRST-ASK-OPAQUE-MEMORY-01`, preserved and unopened.

#### 7.2a ⭐⭐ FOUNDER REFINEMENT (ratified 2026-09-15) — a missing CONDITION, not a missing algorithm

The corpus **can** show: *when the member restates the object, referential evidence exists.*
⛔ It **cannot** show: *how antecedent identity should be established when the member does not
restate the object.*

> ⭐⭐ **That is not a missing clever algorithm. It is a missing EXPERIMENTAL CONDITION.**

⭐ Which places `FIRST-ASK-OPAQUE-MEMORY-01` precisely: ⛔ it is **not merely another continuity
case.** It is **the condition required to discover whether antecedent identity can be
established WITHOUT lexical restatement of the remembered object.**

⛔ **The next lane may not let the existing corpus pretend to adjudicate that.**

⛔ **This is not an instrument, not a proposal, and not an answer.** It is recorded so the next
lane learns at the start, rather than late, that **its question may be partly unanswerable from
the evidence base it inherits.**
