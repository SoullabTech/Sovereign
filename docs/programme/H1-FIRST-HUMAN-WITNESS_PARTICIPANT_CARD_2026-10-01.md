# H1 — First Human Witness · Participant Card

**Use:** hand or read this card to the participant only after the facilitator has completed the private preflight.

## Before we begin

You are trying an early House → Writer's Studio continuity experience using your ordinary Soullab account and your existing Work.

- Your normal product data policy still applies.
- I may take brief usability notes about where the experience is clear or confusing.
- I do not need to copy any of your manuscript text into those notes.
- Audio/video recording is off unless we separately agree to it.
- You can stop at any time. Stopping does not affect your account, Work, or access.

If that is okay with you, begin when you are ready.

## Task

> **Start from the House. Continue one of the Works that is alive for you into Writer's Studio.**
>
> **Open the writing, move through Write, Develop and Review in whatever way makes sense to you, then return Home.**
>
> **Say what you are noticing as you go if you are comfortable doing that.**

There is no trick and no required way to move through the rooms. Use the experience as you naturally would.
