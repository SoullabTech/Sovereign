## D-02 · What is the constitutional floor of AIN?

**Finding:** some law is already platform-wide, but some of the strongest protections are still MAIA-named.

**Evidence:** Constitutional Direction of Authority, Encounter as Primitive, Right to Remain Unpossessed, Recognition Integrity, and Federated Relational Architecture already carry broad scope. MAIA Sovereignty Invariants and MAIA Oath carry MAIA identity in name and/or scope.

**Why a ruling is required:** moving MAIA law into a generic runtime by code refactor would silently change jurisdiction.

### Options

**A. Direct promotion.**
Treat MAIA constitutional law wholesale as AIN law and rename/re-home it.

**B. Derivation with non-collapse.**
AIN's constitutional floor is derived only from already platform-wide law plus explicit clause-by-clause founder promotion. MAIA remains a conforming implementation with additional MAIA-specific identity/oath law.

**C. Technical-only runtime.**
AIN supplies neutral infrastructure; constitutional identity remains entirely inside MAIA/each application.

**JARVIS recommendation:** **B.**
**Reason:** B preserves authority direction in both senses: platform-wide law can govern platform-wide contracts, while MAIA-specific meaning does not become universal through proximity to shared code. It also lets another field remain genuinely itself while inheriting the constitutional protections that were actually constituted to be general.

**If A:** a separate ratification/scope reconciliation is required before implementation.
**If B:** J3 may build a clause → contract map beginning with already-platform-wide law and a visible “MAIA-only / candidate-for-promotion” boundary.
**If C:** the present constitutional-platform thesis is materially narrowed and must be reconciled before J3.

**Explicitly not decided:** the text of any new AIN constitution, whether every current MAIA invariant should eventually be promoted, or how certification will work.
