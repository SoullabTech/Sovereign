# JARVIS-MAIA-STANDING-SHADOW-01 — S5 Human Adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** SEALED · CONDITION KEY REVEALED AFTER REVIEW  
**Authority:** R&D evidence only · S6 implementation remains unopened

## Result

The blind human review is complete. Ten admissible A/B cases were judged before the condition key was opened. Two pairs excluded before review remained excluded and were never repaired post hoc.

After the review was sealed, the precommitted condition key was revealed. **The reviewer preferred the existing/current response in all 10 of 10 admissible cases.** No preferred response was marked with a lethal failure.

This is a strong directional result about the **member-facing surface in this bounded experiment**. It is not a production-model, population, or universal-quality claim.

## What the blind review actually selected for

Across the ten judgments, the same qualities recurred:

- centered on the person rather than MAIA's process;
- more personal, soulful, human, and relatable;
- relational synthesis as a form of *coming to know*, rather than mechanical accounting;
- witnessing first, then possible meaning, curiosity, and a clean question;
- directness and relational economy rather than repeated caveats;
- accepting a member correction and moving with it instead of re-provisionalizing settled meaning;
- respecting a rejected interpretation without making the person argue against it again;
- allowing developmental change without trapping the person in an older identity;
- allowing difference or ambivalence to remain difference without forcing resolution.

## Adjudication

The experiment does **not** support shipping the current Standing Envelope rendering as MAIA's voice. The deterministic phrases `One possibility I see — provisionally` and `If that possibility is worth testing rather than assuming` repeatedly made the response feel more procedural, mechanical, confusing, or system-centered even when the underlying standing discipline was desirable.

The correct architectural inference is narrower:

> **Standing should govern cognition without becoming the foreground of conversation.**

Structural Standing survives as an internal epistemic architecture candidate. The member-facing Standing Envelope voice does not.

A useful inversion follows:

> **MAIA's uncertainty should be structurally represented before generation; it should be verbally expressed only when the actual conversational uncertainty requires expression.**

Likewise, member-authored settled meaning should not be linguistically downgraded into a MAIA possibility merely because the substrate tracks epistemic standing.

## What remains unresolved

Two blockers found before human review remain blockers after it:

1. **Support lineage.** A valid evidence id is not proof that the cited evidence semantically entails the model's claim. In the final replay, 0/10 rendered shadow plans used the present-turn evidence id even when the present utterance was load-bearing. Model-selected support ids cannot be promoted into substrate truth.
2. **Voice ownership.** A blanket first-person refusal conflates dangerous member-voice borrowing (`my life`, `my work`) with legitimate MAIA self-positioning (`I hear`, `I wonder`, `I want to respect`). Production law must distinguish these.

Human preference cannot waive either blocker.

## Successor design implication

A successor should no longer ask, "How do we make the Standing Envelope sound nicer?" That would preserve the wrong boundary.

It should ask:

> **Can Structural Standing operate as an invisible control plane beneath MAIA's existing free relational synthesis, constraining authority and stale lineage without templating the response voice?**

The target shape becomes:

```text
source evidence + present encounter
        ↓
substrate-owned standing / correction / provenance
        ↓
relational-temporal perception
        ↓
free MAIA synthesis
        ↓
post-generation structural audit / refusal only where necessary
        ↓
natural relational voice
```

This differs materially from the tested envelope-first rendering. The standing system constrains what may be asserted as established; it does not require every lawful response to narrate the constraint.

## Evidence ceiling

The S5 result supports only these bounded claims:

- in these 10 synthetic, production-shaped Writer's Studio comparisons, one human reviewer preferred the current response 10/10;
- explicit standing-language scaffolding degraded the desired relational quality in the reviewed cases;
- the structural standing problem remains real and unsolved by simply removing the scaffolding;
- no production mutation is justified by this result alone.

**S6 remains UNOPENED.** No production shadow, renderer change, provider call, deployment, or member-facing experiment is authorized by this adjudication.
