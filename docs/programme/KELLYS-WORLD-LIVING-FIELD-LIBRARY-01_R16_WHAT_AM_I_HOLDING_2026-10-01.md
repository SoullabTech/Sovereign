## Purpose

Give Kelly one concise orientation surface across the things currently being held in view without converting visibility into priority, authority, or programme standing.

## Orientation law

`ORIENTATION_DOES_NOT_CREATE_PRIORITY_OR_AUTHORITY`

The view composes six existing sources:

1. **Keep in sight** — Kelly's explicit local attention preferences.
2. **Needs Kelly** — governed Work Units whose canonical next action is explicit human adjudication.
3. **In motion** — open canonical Work Units without a current adjudication hold or observed failure/challenge.
4. **Watching** — governed work carrying unreadable custody, failed attempt, verifier challenge/disagreement/insufficiency, or route blocker/refusal.
5. **Unfinished threads** — older programme lineages with structurally warranted unfinished evidence.
6. **Recently touched** — recent Git activity for re-entry only.

The section order is interface grammar, not an evaluative ranking.

## Standing preservation

R16 is a pure composition layer.

It:
- does not persist a new summary store;
- does not mutate pins;
- does not mutate Work Units;
- does not change recovery-candidate standing;
- does not call a model;
- does not create authority;
- does not infer importance from recency.

Every row retains the reason it is present.
