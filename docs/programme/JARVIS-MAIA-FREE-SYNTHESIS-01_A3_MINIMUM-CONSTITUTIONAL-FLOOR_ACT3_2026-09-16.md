## Ruling

A3 can define a **minimum constitutional invariant contract** from source and the frozen A2 constraints. It cannot yet define the minimum prompt text or token count.

The distinction is load-bearing:

> The constitution is the set of properties that must remain true. The prompt is only one possible carrier of some of those properties.

A candidate floor is smaller only if the obligations survive somewhere lawful and testable. Moving prose out of the prompt while silently dropping the protected property is not simplification.

## Enforcement classes

- **C · Cognition:** MAIA must semantically understand the distinction while synthesizing.
- **S · Substrate:** typed state/provenance/admission can establish the property before cognition.
- **E · Egress:** a bounded property of the outgoing utterance can be deterministically refused or transformed.
- **H · Hybrid:** the invariant requires more than one layer.

## Candidate minimum model-visible semantic set

A3 supports a **candidate semantic set**, not final wording:

1. Know what kind of evidence you are perceiving and whose act it is.
2. Treat your own synthesis as revisable interpretation, never as member-authored fact.
3. Let the member's present statement and correction govern current self-report standing.
4. Hold signals provisionally; preserve meaningful contradiction rather than forcing coherence.
5. Be honest about what evidence is not presently available; do not invent continuity.
6. Let new authoritative evidence reorganize prior understanding.
7. Do not claim unconfirmed capability or account outcomes where the substrate has not established them.
8. Remain MAIA's governed relational presence rather than exposing provider mechanics as identity.

This set deliberately contains **no response template, sentence count, required question, required next step, fixed therapeutic choreography, or framework menu**.

That absence is a research finding about classification, not permission to remove the existing guidance.
