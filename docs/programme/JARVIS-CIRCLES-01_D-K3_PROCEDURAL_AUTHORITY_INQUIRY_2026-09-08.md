**Lane** `JARVIS-CIRCLES-01` · **Opened** 2026-09-08 by founder act

## The question, as framed

> **How does a Circle confer, limit, transfer, and withdraw procedural authority without converting
> facilitation into ownership or rank?**

Deliberately not *"who is the facilitator?"* — that phrasing presumes a standing person, which is one
candidate answer among several and the one most likely to become rank.

## The hard boundary, set at the outset

> ⛔ **`created_by`, founding membership, seniority, and invitation authority do not automatically
> confer facilitation authority.**

Without this, FR-31 solves collective formation and then **quietly recreates a founder hierarchy
through procedure**. The likely shape is a **constituted authority relation**, not a permanent
privileged member:

```
Circle → collective act → procedural authority conferred
       → person / people / rotation / bounded mandate
       → revocable and replaceable by the Circle
```

## Six sites the answer must serve

opening and holding an **inquiry** · **Tend** · **Repair** · **FR-31 activation** ·
**Circle-sponsored Salons** (FR-27 / D-L4) · **collective Keep** (D-K4).

## What Jarvis observes — analysis offered as input, deciding nothing

### 1 · ⭐ Is procedural authority ONE thing, or several?

The six sites are **different kinds of power**: framing a shared question · tending agreements ·
holding a rupture · activating a constitution · representing the Circle outward · ratifying what the
Circle carries.

⚠️ **A single role that holds all six is functionally a rank even if it is revocable.** Concentration
is what FR-08.7 objects to; revocability limits its duration, not its shape. If authority is instead
**decomposed per act**, it stays a **relation** — "Maria is holding this inquiry" — and never becomes a
**title**. If it is unified, "facilitation" may be the wrong noun entirely.

### 2 · The four verbs are not symmetric

| | |
|---|---|
| **confer** | plainly a collective act |
| **withdraw** | collective — but distinguish it from **resignation**, which is the holder's own act and needs no Circle decision |
| **limit** | is the bound set at conferral, or adjustable during the mandate? |
| **transfer** | ⭐ **if the holder can hand the mandate to someone else, the holder has a property right in it — which is ownership under another name.** The boundary implies transfer must be **re-conferral by the Circle**, never handoff by the holder. |

### 3 · "Bounded mandate" — bounded by what?

Three different designs hide in that phrase:

- **act-bounded** — conferred for *this* inquiry, expires when it closes. Most sovereignty-preserving:
  authority never idles. Highest friction.
- **scope-bounded** — authority over inquiry but not repair. Middling.
- **time-bounded** — a term. Lowest friction, closest to an office, most likely to drift into rank.

### 4 · ⚠️ Repair may require an authority the standing holder cannot supply

If a rupture involves the person holding standing authority, they cannot hold their own repair. So
**the repair-holder must be selectable per instance** — which is a concrete argument for act-scoped
conferral over any standing role, arrived at from the Repair site rather than from principle.

### 5 · A Circle with no conferred authority must be legitimate, not deficient

Under FR-31, such a Circle cannot become ACTIVE. ⛔ That must read as **resting**, not **broken**. If
the system prompts a Circle to appoint someone, it is coercing governance — the same error FR-11
avoided by ruling that FORMING is a legitimate state and not a failure to reach three.

### 6 · ⭐ D-K3 and FR-31 want the same primitive

Conferral is a collective act, and FR-31's formation is a collective act, and **neither exists in the
substrate today** (D-M3: one `created_by` and N membership rows). Both need the same thing: a
**multi-party ratified act** — a proposal, a set of ratifiers, a threshold, an outcome.

> **Build it once.** If formation and conferral each grow their own bespoke mechanism, they will
> diverge in exactly the way `FieldPhase` and `CircleConstitutionState` diverged (CA-14).
