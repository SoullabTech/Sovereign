## Pressure 1 · Non-local recovery — ⭐ **STRONG PASS, and it is B's clearest win**

**A could not recover this**, because the observation never had a truthful place (A/M3-3). **B can**, because the observation has identity independent of any locus.

⭐ **And note *how* it is recovered: by description.** The writer need not remember *where*, *when*, or what they called the inquiry. They remember **what the observation was about** — and in B the retrieval key is the content itself.

```text
A   addressed by PLACE    -> you must remember where
B   addressed by MEANING  -> you must remember what
```

**For a developmental observation, "what" is far more likely to survive in a writer's memory than "where."**

## Pressure 2 · ⭐⭐ Selective recovery — **FAILS, and it is M2's defect at a new moment**

Can the writer reach that one observation without reopening its siblings?

**B as specified gives an observation no identity outside its inquiry.** Findings appear *under* the question; nothing states that one is independently addressable. **So the observation's address is its position in the container — and to reach it you open the container.**

```text

```

Recovery has become **resurfacing by navigation.** Nothing was pushed; the writer deliberately returned — **and the architecture still reinstated all four findings, the prior conversation, and the revisions.**
