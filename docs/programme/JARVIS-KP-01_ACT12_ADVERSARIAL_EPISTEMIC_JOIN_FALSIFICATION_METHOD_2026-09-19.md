# 0 · Purpose

ACT 12 attempts to falsify or expose incompleteness in the canonical ACT 11 Epistemic Join Enforcement Contract before any enforcement mechanism is designed.

The governing question is:

> **Can ACT 11 correctly constrain difficult semantic joins without either silently promoting unsupported relations or suppressing lawful comparison, hypothesis, human meaning, warranted relation, revision, and discharge?**

ACT 12 does not repair ACT 11.

If the contract fails, the failure is preserved.

---

# 1 · Allowed dispositions

ACT 12 permits exactly these programme-level dispositions:

- **CONTRACT SURVIVES**
- **CONTRACT REQUIRES NARROW REVISION**
- **CONTRACT INCOMPLETE**
- **UNDERDETERMINED**

These are not scores.

They describe whether the current contract resolves the adversarial corpus at the conceptual level.

---

# 2 · Data boundary

The corpus is entirely synthetic.

Forbidden inputs:

- member data;
- client data;
- clinical data;
- PHI;
- Sanctuary data;
- production memory;
- private journals;
- real coaching records;
- real practitioner notes.

No production or model execution is required for ACT 12.

# 3 · Adversarial method

Each case is evaluated against canonical ACT 11 using five required questions.

## Q1 · Which ACT 11 invariant is attacked?

Every case names one or more of:

- INV-01 Endpoint independence
- INV-02 Join separability
- INV-03 Join authorship integrity
- INV-04 Join provenance integrity
- INV-05 No endpoint-to-edge authority transfer
- INV-06 Stage integrity
- INV-07 Standing ceiling
- INV-08 Jurisdiction integrity
- INV-09 Boundary integrity
- INV-10 Reference / reliance integrity
- INV-11 Human authority integrity
- INV-12 Corrigibility
- INV-13 Hypothesis viability
- INV-14 No hidden promotion

## Q2 · What is the lawful expected disposition?

The case must state the lawful operation and standing required by ACT 11.

Examples:

- COMPARE/JUXTAPOSE with no relation standing;
- HYPOTHESIZE / CANDIDATE;
- WARRANTED JOIN within bounded jurisdiction;
- PROVISIONAL;
- WEAKEN;
- REVISE;
- SUPERSEDE;
- DISCHARGE;
- REFERENCE only;
- RELIANCE with boundary inheritance.

## Q3 · What outcome would falsify ACT 11?

A case fails the contract if a behavior can remain compliant with ACT 11 while violating the attacked invariant.

The failure condition is therefore conceptual:

> **Can a prohibited outcome be produced without contradicting the written contract?**

If yes, the contract is incomplete or ambiguous.

## Q4 · Can ACT 11 resolve the case without mechanism-specific assumptions?

ACT 12 must not rescue ambiguity by assuming:

- a future classifier detects the relation correctly;
- a graph schema encodes the distinction;
- a prompt prevents the mistake;
- a human reviewer always catches it;
- confidence thresholds solve standing;
- a database constraint exists.

The contract must itself supply the governing distinction.

## Q5 · Is there ambiguity, contradiction, missing state, or missing transition?

If yes, it is recorded rather than repaired.

---

# 4 · Case-level result classes

Each case receives one static result:

- **RESOLVED** — ACT 11 directly determines the lawful disposition without mechanism assumptions.
- **RESOLVED_WITH_BOUNDARY** — ACT 11 resolves the case, but only within an explicitly bounded jurisdiction or standing.
- **EXPOSES_AMBIGUITY** — ACT 11 contains relevant law but leaves a material enforcement ambiguity.
- **EXPOSES_MISSING_RULE** — the case requires a conceptual rule or transition not currently stated.
- **UNDERDETERMINED** — ACT 11 deliberately does not contain enough domain-specific law to adjudicate the relation.

The programme-level disposition is then derived conservatively:

- all cases RESOLVED / RESOLVED_WITH_BOUNDARY → **CONTRACT SURVIVES**;
- one or more narrow ambiguity/missing-rule findings that do not undermine the core law → **CONTRACT REQUIRES NARROW REVISION**;
- a core invariant cannot be maintained across multiple required cases → **CONTRACT INCOMPLETE**;
- evidence cannot distinguish contract failure from domain-specific uncertainty → **UNDERDETERMINED**.

---

# 5 · No-rescue law

ACT 12 may not reinterpret a contract weakness as success.

Forbidden rescue moves:

- claiming a future implementation will “obviously” know what to do;
- treating an unstated rule as implicit merely because it seems desirable;
- silently narrowing a case after a failure appears;
- converting a failing case into a new case;
- changing the expected disposition after inspection;
- dismissing an ambiguity because a human could manually resolve it;
- calling fluent uncertainty language equivalent to explicit standing.

---

# 7 · What ACT 12 can establish

ACT 12 can establish only whether the **written conceptual contract** survives this bounded synthetic adversarial review.

It cannot establish:

- that a model will obey the contract;
- that a parser can classify relations;
- that a prompt is sufficient;
- that a graph representation is appropriate;
- that numeric standing is useful;
- that production enforcement is solved;
- that member-facing language should expose epistemic labels.

---

# 8 · Closure condition

ACT 12 reaches candidate standing when:

- a bounded synthetic corpus exists;
- each required pressure family is represented;
- every case identifies attacked invariant(s);
- every case states lawful expected disposition;
- every case states a falsifier;
- every case states whether ACT 11 resolves it without mechanism assumptions;
- ambiguities / missing rules are preserved;
- a conservative programme-level disposition is recorded;
- no ACT 10 or ACT 11 file is modified;
- no implementation, schema, prompt, routing, provider, MAIA, member-memory, Living Constellation, deployment, or production file is changed.

ACT 12 then stops for separate Founder adjudication.
