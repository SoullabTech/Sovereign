## B · WRITING HIERARCHY — RULED · CHANGE REQUIRED

```text

becomes ground.

- rails and bottom dock compete perceptually
- manuscript and surrounding chrome are nearly the same material/tonal field
- strongest tonal emphasis currently occurs in rail selection, not the manuscript
- typography/measure are not the main problem

Dynamic witness:
DEFERRED / BLOCKED by the separate viewport-displacement defect.
Not required to hold B open.
```

⛔ The viewport glitch gets its own lane later. **It does not get to stop Canvas
work.**

## C · MATERIAL CONTRACT — RULED · ADMIT A DISTINCT MANUSCRIPT MATERIAL ROLE

```text
KEEP
existing sampled dark ground ramp for Writer's Studio chrome/environment

ADD
a separate Canvas/manuscript material system

PURPOSE
make the work materially distinct from the Studio holding it

INITIAL DIRECTIONS
- warm paper / parchment
- clean paper / soft off-white
- dark manuscript option retained for writers who prefer it

NOT
- global light mode
- mutation of the sampled dark ground tokens
- one mandatory parchment aesthetic
```

⛔ **No exact colour is ruled here.** Whether `#F2E7D0` is the right cream belongs
to implementation and prototyping. The architectural decision is only:

> **Studio is the dark room. Canvas may be the page within it.**

---

## BUILD SCOPE — one Canvas, not fifteen increments

```text
DO
- visibly name Canvas
- give the manuscript stronger spatial primacy
- reduce visual competition from rails / dock
- introduce the new manuscript material role
- prototype at least Paper/Parchment + Dark writing surfaces
- preserve FR-C / FR-D improvements

DO NOT
- touch capabilities, memory, authorship, or behavioral powers
- repair the viewport-jump defect (separate lane)
- mutate the sampled dark ground tokens
- build a global light mode
```

> The next implementation should make a **substantial perceptual change**, not
> another 11-pixel affordance. Then you look at **one meaningful redesigned
> Canvas**, not fifteen tiny incremental changes.
