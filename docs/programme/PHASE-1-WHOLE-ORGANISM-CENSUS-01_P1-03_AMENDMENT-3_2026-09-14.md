## 1 · ⭐⭐ The inventory is renamed, and the distinction is the reason

```text
⛔ NOT      EFFECT-WITHOUT-AUTHORIZATION
✅ IS       EFFECT-WITHOUT-LOCATED-AUTHORIZATION
```

The earlier name quietly asserted a negative the census cannot prove. The corrected name asserts
only what was searched for and not found:

```text
DECIDES
+ governance gate NONE FOUND
+ no quoted authorization located
        ≠
  PROVED UNAUTHORIZED
```

What the inventory establishes, exactly:

```text
effect                    OBSERVED
authorization located     NO
authorization status      UNKNOWN / NONE LOCATED
```

⭐ This is the same discipline the lane applied to the two-lane receipts law and to `interrupted`:
**a system may not store a confident negative it cannot know.** *"Unauthorized"* is a verdict;
*"no authorization located"* is a reading.

⛔ **`AUTH-EXPOSURE-01` is where some of these may become PROVED unauthorized**, through
actor / resource / relationship witnesses. Keeping the name honest is what stops the two lanes
contaminating one another — a census that pre-announced the security lane's verdict would have
decided its finding for it.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ Participation and authority are ORTHOGONAL AXES from here forward

⛔ **Not one hierarchy.** Two independent axes, recorded separately, on every row.

```text
PARTICIPATION    EXISTS · PARTICIPATES · KNOWS · CONSIDERS · CONTRIBUTES · DECIDES · UNKNOWN
AUTHORITY        GOVERNED · NONE LOCATED · UNKNOWN
```

All of these are **coherent states**, and none is a defect of the schema:

```text
PARTICIPATION  DECIDES        AUTHORITY  NONE LOCATED
PARTICIPATION  CONTRIBUTES    AUTHORITY  GOVERNED
PARTICIPATION  UNKNOWN        AUTHORITY  GOVERNED
```

⭐ **That third one matters as much as the first**: a governed thing whose participation is
unknown is a real and reportable state, not a hole to be filled.

⛔ `HAS AUTHORITY` therefore **leaves the ladder**. It was never a rung; treating it as the top
one is exactly what `INF-6` forbids. Authority is the second axis, and it is read from
authorization text or not at all.

> ⭐⭐ **This gives P1-04 something more valuable than a map of the organism: a map of where
> CAUSAL POWER and LEGITIMATE AUTHORITY DIVERGE.**
