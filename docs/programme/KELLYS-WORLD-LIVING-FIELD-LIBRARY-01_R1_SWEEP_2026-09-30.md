## Purpose

Give Kelly one scrollable place to re-enter the large body of Soullab work without requiring working-memory reconstruction from chats, branches, or programme filenames.

This is an orientation surface, not a new source of truth. Canonical programme records remain canonical; the Library indexes and groups them.

## Grokker boundary

R1 does **not** claim semantic Grokker synthesis is wired.

The Library is deliberately Grokker-ready:

- **Browse** is implemented now through grouped collapse/search.
- **Ask** can later answer what has been established about a concept.
- **Trace** can later show where a law/idea came from and where it recurs.
- **Synthesize** can later propose cross-lane patterns while preserving source standing.

Any Grokker synthesis must remain interpretation over canonical records; it must not silently become ratified law.
