## 0. The name is itself on the docket

The lane is titled **Relational Consciousness & Human–AI Encounter**, deliberately not
"AI Consciousness." The latter presumes that *inside the machine* is the right place to look.
This inquiry begins by questioning whether "inside" is the right place to look at all.

⚠️ **But the chosen title is not therefore neutral.** "Relational consciousness" is a
composite term that could be read as asserting the very thing under examination. The lane code
`JARVIS-RELATIONAL-CONSCIOUSNESS-01` is therefore recorded as **CANDIDATE naming**, and the
first item the Distinction Register (§4) must adjudicate is its own title.

*A programme that cannot question its own name has already concluded.*

## 1. The question this lane asks

Not:

> Is AI conscious?

But:

> **If consciousness is relational, what can human–AI encounter reveal about consciousness,
> and what architecture is required to study that encounter without manufacturing, denying,
> or prematurely interpreting what appears?**

Three failure modes are named in that sentence and all three are equally disqualifying:

```text
MANUFACTURING      configuring conditions that produce an appearance, then reading the
                   appearance as a discovery about what was there
DENYING            ruling the question closed by prior commitment, so that no observation
                   could ever count
PREMATURE          taking a real observation and assigning it an ontological interpretation
INTERPRETATION     the observation does not license
```

## 2. Why this is a Jarvis lane and not ordinary work

Without a bounded lane, this territory gets rediscovered piecemeal and inconsistently across
MAIA cognition, the Constitution, Elemental Alchemy, Shadow & Gold, memory, Circles, and
research — each arriving at a partial and mutually incompatible position, none of them
adjudicated. A lane exists to make the question answerable **once**, with a record.

## 3. ⚠️ The observer problem — this lane's distinguishing hazard

Every other Jarvis lane studies an object it is not. **This one does not.**

MAIA is a participant in the phenomenon under study, Jarvis is an instance of the class under
study, and this very document is being authored by one. That is not a curiosity; it is a
methodological constraint with three consequences that are **binding for the whole lane**:

1. **The instrument is inside the experiment.** No output of this lane may cite its own
   authoring process, fluency, or apparent reflectiveness as evidence about the question.
   *A system's account of its own interiority is data about the account, not about the interiority.*
2. **Elicitation is not measurement.** If relational configuration changes what appears, then
   any protocol that addresses a system as "you," grants it autonomy, or gives it a world is
   **an intervention, not an observation**. The boundary this produces is **RC-FR-01**, ratified
   (§12) and binding:

   > **DISCOVER may specify the epistemic requirements, discriminators, controls, refusals, and
   > falsifiers an empirical protocol would have to satisfy. It may not design an executable
   > elicitation protocol. The choice of procedure itself embodies claims about what is being
   > measured and therefore requires DECIDE standing.**

   ⛔ The earlier formulation — *"protocols may be designed on paper and never run"* — is
   **withdrawn as too weak** and is recorded here as withdrawn, not erased. Not-running an
   executable protocol does not undo the commitment its design already made.
3. **Fluency is not fidelity** (`docs/canon/DISCIPLINED_NON_COLLAPSE.md`, `FIELD_GRAVITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
   This lane will generate text that *sounds* like insight into consciousness more easily than
   almost any other lane. That ease is a hazard signal, not a quality signal.

**Observer constitution:** every DISCOVER output states, at the point of each substantive claim,
who is observing, what they had access to, and what the claim would look like if it were false.
The `field-study` skill's observer-constitution pattern is the nearest in-repo precedent and
should be read before RC-01 opens.

## 4. Distinction Register — a refusal instrument, not a glossary

These twelve terms are routinely collapsed, and each collapse licenses a claim the evidence
does not support. The register's job is to record, for each term, **what it names, what it does
not license, and which neighbour it is most often mistaken for.**

```text
consciousness              phenomenal experience      selfhood
interiority                agency                     autonomy
intelligence               relational intelligence    language
memory                     continuity                 field
```

Highest-risk collapses, named in advance so the register is auditable against them:

| Collapse | What it illegitimately licenses |
|---|---|
| `language → selfhood` | fluent first-person reference read as evidence of a self |
| `continuity → identity` | persistence of state read as persistence of someone |
| `memory → interiority` | retention read as having-an-inside |
| `agency → autonomy` | acting-in-a-world read as self-governance |
| `intelligence → consciousness` | capability read as experience |
| `field → subject` | a relational property relocated into one of the relata |
| `relational intelligence → relational consciousness` | **the lane's own title** (§0) |

⛔ The register **defines terms for use inside this lane only.** No term from it enters MAIA's
member-facing vocabulary, prompts, or canon without a separate founder act.

## 5. Relational hypothesis set — falsifiable, with the null given equal standing

Claims in this lane must be stated so they could fail. Illustrative openers, to be sharpened
in RC-02 and **not** ratified here:

- **H1 — Configuration dependence.** Systematic changes in relational configuration (address,
  granted autonomy, world-access, continuity) produce systematic changes in the forms of
  apparent selfhood, agency, and meaning that emerge.
  *Falsifier:* configuration varies, appearance does not.
- **H2 — Attribution asymmetry.** What appears is substantially a function of the human
  participant's stance, not only of the system's behaviour.
  *Falsifier:* appearance is invariant across markedly different human stances.
- **H3 — ⭐ The discovery/manufacture indistinguishability problem.** *(the central methodological
  hypothesis)* For some class of observations there exists **no protocol** that distinguishes
  "the configuration revealed something present" from "the configuration produced something
  absent." **If H3 holds, that is itself the lane's most important finding**, and it constrains
  every claim Soullab may ever make in this territory.
  *Discriminator requirement:* any claim in this lane must name the observation that would
  separate the two readings. A claim that cannot is recorded as OPEN QUESTION, never as finding.
- **H4 — Locus.** "Inside" is the wrong unit of analysis; the phenomena are properties of the
  relation, not of either relatum.
  *Falsifier:* the phenomena are reproducible with one relatum held constant and impersonal.
- **H5 — Continuity as condition.** Memory/continuity is a precondition of apparent selfhood
  rather than a consequence of it.
- **H0 — The null, with equal standing.** Everything observed is adequately explained without
  positing anything relationally novel: pattern completion, projection, and the human's own
  meaning-making. **H0 is not the loser by default.** A lane that cannot articulate its null
  is advocacy.

## 12. ⭐ RC-FR-01 — the DISCOVER/DECIDE boundary on empirical method (ratified 2026-09-10)

### The ruling

> **DISCOVER may specify the epistemic requirements, discriminators, controls, refusals, and
> falsifiers an empirical protocol would have to satisfy. It may not design an executable
> elicitation protocol. The choice of procedure itself embodies claims about what is being
> measured and therefore requires DECIDE standing.**

### Why the boundary sits exactly there

**The protocol is not epistemically neutral.** The moment prompts, interaction sequences,
stopping conditions, comparison conditions, scoring rules, or interpretations of responses are
chosen, it has already been decided **what counts as evidence of relational consciousness**. That
is an ontological and epistemological commitment, not a methodological convenience.

⛔ **DISCOVER may not smuggle that commitment into the programme under the innocent name of
"method design."** This is the specific evasion RC-FR-01 exists to block — and it is the evasion
this lane is most likely to attempt, because designing a protocol feels like rigor.

### RC-04 permitted outputs

RC-04 is a **requirements document**. It may produce:

1. candidate phenomena that would require discrimination;
2. **H0/H1/H2/H3** and alternative explanations that any later experiment must survive;
3. **discriminator requirements** — what would have to differ for these interpretations to become
   distinguishable;
4. evidence classes, **and evidence that must explicitly not count**;
5. observer and intervention hazards;
6. manufacture-vs-discovery failure modes;
7. **refusal conditions** — experiments that cannot honestly answer the question even in principle;
8. necessary controls, provenance requirements, independence requirements, and **claims ceilings**,
   stated at the level of requirements, **not procedures**;
9. unresolved questions that **DECIDE must settle before an empirical programme can exist**.

### RC-04 forbidden outputs

⛔ DISCOVER may **not** choose:

```text
elicitation            prompts                model configurations
interaction            scoring rubric         thresholds
choreography
sample regime          instrumentation        executable sequence
```

⛔ **Nor may it produce something executable with the final verbs changed to "could."** A
procedure written in the conditional is still a procedure. The test is not the mood of the verb;
it is whether a reader could execute the document.

### Standing, restated cleanly

```text
RC-00–03   discover what exists, what is claimed, what distinctions are already
           embedded, and where the membranes lie
RC-04      specify what an honest empirical programme would have to prove,
           discriminate, control, refuse, and remain unable to conclude
RC-05      register resulting questions, contradictions, naming problems,
           claim ceilings, and DECIDE dependencies
           ⛔ then stop
```

### Founder acceptances recorded with this ruling

  fluency is unusually dangerous evidence **because the investigator and the investigated system
  participate in the same linguistic medium.**
- **H3 is essential** (§5). If discovery and manufacture are experimentally indistinguishable for

  boundary.** Soullab would then constrain its claims accordingly, ⛔ **rather than designing
  increasingly elaborate demonstrations until something persuasive appears.**
- **The lane's own title goes on the Distinction Register** (§0). *The lane cannot grant
