**Supersedes the mandate item "proposed 2–200 scale geometries"** (founder Amendment B).
**Status:** CANDIDATE / VISION. Nothing here is ratified, and nothing here is built.
**Grounding:** `CIRCLE_SCALE_FALSIFICATION.md`. Read that first — it contradicts several numbers below.

## 1. The morphology

```text
PERSONAL FIELD          1 person          MAIA + the member
   │                                      "What is happening in me?"
   │  ── explicit offer only ──
   ▼
DYADIC FIELD            2 persons         proposed distinct object (§3)
   │
   ▼
CIRCLE                  ~3–24             mutual relational field
   │                                      "What are we bringing into the space between us?"
   │  ── explicit release only ──
   ▼
CONSTELLATION           several CIRCLES   relationship among sovereign Circles
   │                    (unit = Circles)  "What exceeds any one of us?"
   │  ── explicit contribution only ──
   ▼
COMMONS                 dozens→thousands  shared-interest ecology
                                          "What may travel beyond us?"

CO-LAB                  sized by the work intentional working collaboration
                                          "What are we doing together?"
```

**Co-Lab sits beside, not above.** It is entered from any field by an explicit act and is governed
by the existing `studio_teams` scoping and the Co-Lab release gate. It is not a rung.

## 2. What defines each field — unit and membrane, never headcount

See `CIRCLE_SCALE_FALSIFICATION.md` §8 for the definition table. The load-bearing move: **each
field is defined by its unit of membership and its membrane rule.** Numbers are diagnostics.

## 3. The dyad question — a recommendation, not a ruling

Evidence (Simmel; falsification report §5) supports treating a two-person field as a **Dyadic
Field**, not a small Circle: a dyad cannot survive one member's withdrawal, cannot anonymize,
cannot hold plurality, and degenerates contribute-before-you-see. **Founder call: Docket D-04.**

## 4. Circle life-course (founder-authored, Amendment B)

```text
formation → orientation → relationship → inquiry → maturation →
      ┌──────────┬───────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────────┐
 continuation  completion  fission    birth   constellation   (dormancy?)
```

| Terminus | Meaning | Substrate today |
|---|---|---|
| **continuation** | stays at 14 indefinitely | ✅ implicit (no growth pressure exists) |
| **completion** | closes when its inquiry completes | ⬜ **no Circle-close path exists**; only inquiries close |
| **fission** | divides consensually around two emerging inquiries | ⬜ **0% built. Likely un-precedented as a designed mechanic** (falsification §OQ-3) |
| **birth** | spawns a new Circle | ⬜ 0% built |
| **constellation** | temporarily joins other Circles | ⬜ 0% built |
| **dormancy** | quiet without dissolution | ⚠️ partially expressible — `FieldPhase = 'quiet'` exists |

**Growth is not synonymous with membership accumulation.** A Circle that stays at 11 for two years
and closes cleanly is a **success case**, and the system must be able to represent it as one.

## 5. What the numbers are for

Per falsification §6, **size ranks fifth** behind facilitation, purpose, interaction structure, and
duration. Therefore the bands are proposed **not as limits but as prompts** — the point at which
the system *asks* rather than *enforces*:

| Band | Proposed system behavior | Never |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | ask whether this is a Dyadic Field | auto-convert |
| 3–5 | nothing | warn about smallness |
| 6–15 | nothing | congratulate, score, or display "optimal" |
| 16–24 | offer facilitation support | cap membership |
| 25+ | **ask the Circle** whether it wishes to divide, birth, or remain | split automatically, or block joining |

**⭐ Fission must be a member act, never a system act.** A system that divides a Circle on a
headcount trigger has taken authority over a relational field — a direct violation of the
Constitutional Direction of Authority (authority moves upward through authored experience only).
The system may **notice and ask**; only the Circle may **decide**. This mirrors the temporal-memory
rule already ratified elsewhere in the project: **detect → ask → record**, never act on a timer.

## 6. The Jung Commons reference case

```text
JUNG COMMONS — 180 interested members
  ├─ Dreams & Individuation Circle     11
  ├─ Active Imagination Circle         14
  ├─ Shadow Work Circle                 9
  ├─ Jungian Parenting Circle           8
  ├─ Red Book Study Circle             15
  └─ Archetypes & Creativity Circle    12
        └── occasional Constellation around a shared inquiry
```

Members may belong to more than one Circle. **No Circle automatically receives another Circle's
material. No Commons automatically receives Circle material.**

External corroboration: this is Ostrom's 8th design principle (nested enterprises + subsidiarity)
and is the structure by which 100+ person citizens' assemblies actually achieve deliberative
quality — nested facilitated small groups, not one large room (falsification §3).

## 7. Anti-goals — named explicitly

1. Making one Circle carry 200 people.
2. A "Commons Circle." *(Likely category error — founder-flagged.)*
3. Solving scale with pagination, feeds, or notification volume.
4. Automatic federation, ambient cross-Circle visibility, or a Constellation that can see Circle
   interiors.
5. Ratifying a size number while leaving facilitation, purpose, and interaction structure
   unspecified — **this would optimize the weakest variable in the evidence.**
