## 1 · Why this programme exists

The census found that existing programmes own ingestion, creation, memory mechanics, practitioner
knowledge, action, and representation governance — and that **nobody owned** what MAIA knows about a
person, where that knowledge came from, what the member can inspect, export, or erase.

⭐ **Six of thirteen census dimensions had no owning lane, and the gaps clustered exactly there.** An
unowned capability is one nobody was accountable for advancing.

> **We did not discover another product to build. We discovered one missing jurisdiction.**

## 2 · Jurisdiction

> **SPM owns the member-facing and machine-readable model of what Soullab knows, infers, retains,
> exposes, exports, corrects, supersedes, and forgets about a person.**

**SPM owns:**

```text
D3   human-model provenance
D4   inferred human model
D11  export completeness
D12  member-scoped forgetting

```

**These are not five features.** They are five properties of one object, which is why they get one
owner rather than five lanes.

**SPM does NOT own:**

⛔ **SPM may require interfaces from those systems. It does not absorb them.**

## 3 · ⭐ The governing separation — the load-bearing section

```text
SPM DETERMINES                        GOVERNANCE DETERMINES
what is in the model?                 what may the system DO with that model?
where did it come from?               when may it represent the person?
what is its status?                   what is forbidden?
what can the member see /
  correct / export / erase?
```

> ⛔ **Owning the personal model does not confer authority to decide how MAIA may represent,
> impersonate, speak for, or act for the person. Those remain governance questions.**

**Why this is written here rather than assumed.** The natural failure is that *the programme owning
the representation quietly acquires authority over its use* — and it would not feel like a seizure.
It would feel like completing the work. The programme that knows most about the model is the one best
placed to say what may be done with it, and that is exactly why it must not.

**Design test for every SPM output:** *does this describe the object and the member's rights over it,
or does it decide what the system may do with it?* The second belongs to governance (D9), which the
census recorded as having **weak canonical custody** — ⛔ and a vacuum next door is not an invitation.

## 3.1 · The four-way separation (founder, 2026-09-13)

The two-way separation above sits inside a larger one:

```text
SPM           knows what the system believes about you
MAIA          relates to you using that knowledge
JARVIS        manages the work — and later, performs governed actions
GOVERNANCE    decides what MAIA and JARVIS are allowed to do with the model
```

**The collapse this prevents, stated as it would actually occur:**

> *"MAIA knows this about Kelly, therefore MAIA may act or speak as Kelly."*

⛔ **No. Knowing, relating, representing and acting remain separate authorities.** Each step in that
sentence is a different permission, and the sentence works by never pausing between them.

### ⚠️ JARVIS names two different things — do not let them inherit each other's standing

JARVIS appears **twice** in the separation above, and they are not the same authority:

| | What it is | Standing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **JARVIS as method** | How this programme is developed: *census → specification → jurisdiction → reuse map → falsifiers → implementation → witness* | A work discipline. ⛔ Confers no runtime authority over anything. |
| **JARVIS as actor** | The governed-action layer — roadmap step 7, D10, JOP | A runtime authority. ⛔ Confers no standing over how SPM is specified. |

⭐ **The first is building SPM. The second will one day be a consumer of SPM.** A method that
acquired the actor's standing could specify the model to suit what it wants to execute; an actor that
acquired the method's standing could authorize its own consumption by calling it process. **Same
name, two roles, opposite directions of drift.**

⛔ **This charter's JARVIS is the method.** SPM's relationship to JARVIS-as-actor is the same as its
relationship to MAIA: **a consumer, governed by governance, not by SPM.**

## 3.2 · Not from scratch

⭐ SPM is **not a new subsystem to invent.** The census found substantial pieces already scattered
across memory, manuscript provenance, export, erasure, member data and MAIA. **SPM-00 is the
programme that brings those pieces into one coherent sovereign model** — which is why roadmap step 3
is substrate mapping and step 4 is *the smallest* canonical model, reuse-first, in that order.

## 4 · D6 is adjacent, not annexed

```text
SPM owns the model.
MAIA relates through it.
```

Relational intelligence **consumes** the model; that is not ownership of it. D6 stays with MAIA and is
**blocked behind D3/D4 sufficiently resolving what the human-model object is and how its claims
acquire provenance** — a dependency, not a transfer.
