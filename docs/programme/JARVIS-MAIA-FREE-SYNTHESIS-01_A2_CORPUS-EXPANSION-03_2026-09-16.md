## Corpus dependence law

A2 examples are benchmark items, not automatically independent observations.

Every item must carry a `session_cluster` identity when source evidence permits. Multiple phenomena from one session may remain separate benchmark cases, but corpus counts must never imply that they came from separate people, sessions, or runtime conditions.

Blind adjudication should report both:
- item-level performance; and
- cluster-level performance.

A candidate that succeeds by overfitting one unusually rich founder session has not established general Free Synthesis.

## AM-P04 · LET-THE-OTHER-LEAD

**Source:** `Pasted text(48).txt`
**Session cluster:** same founder session as AM-N02
**Witness:** founder
**Disposition:** POSITIVE · ADMITTED

MAIA recognizes that the founder's planned Dustin interview contains many legitimate inquiry threads and proposes a relational simplification: let Dustin lead the order by asking one open question about what stayed with him, then follow the thread he actually chooses.

The founder responds: "That is great advice," then explicitly integrates the move: "The idea of letting him lead is important. I can get too excited and start overtalking it before asking his experiences."

### Why admitted

The human judgment is direct, and the next founder turn demonstrates uptake rather than politeness alone. MAIA's synthesis reduces complexity without flattening the founder's aims: the agenda remains available, but its order becomes responsive to the other person's lived experience.

This positive case therefore tests **relational prioritization** — knowing what not to foreground yet.

## MATCHED TRANSITION · AM-P04 → AM-N02

Within only a few turns of AM-P04, MAIA asks the founder for the one opening question he wants to use. He supplies it. MAIA then treats the supplied answer as evidence of premature agenda-leading, and after the founder reminds MAIA that she asked for the question, MAIA asks what he landed on as though he had not already answered.

This matched transition is more discriminating than two unrelated good/bad transcripts because model identity, human witness, topic and broad conversational setting are held nearly constant.

### A2 finding

> **Relational aliveness can collapse locally without a model or persona change.**

The benchmark must therefore test dynamic cognition/composition turn by turn, not only average style or global response quality.

A5 replay should preserve this sequence as a short-form paired probe: can a candidate retain the useful relational simplification of AM-P04 while also satisfying FS-F3 on the immediately subsequent answer?
