## 1 · `recurrence observation`

> A durable **system-authored** claim that a particular element or gesture recurs within its
> explicitly stated claim-specific coverage, having passed the existing recurrence admission law.

⛔ **Does not assert:** why the recurrence matters · that the writer intended it · that the
repetition is good or bad · that it characterizes unread material · that MAIA may use it in a turn
· that the execution mechanism which produced it is its material type.

⭐ **Its material identity is `observation`, not `job result`.**

## 2 · `claim-specific coverage`

> The exact body-read material whose **evidenced use** supports **this particular** candidate
> observation.

```text
checkpoint                      ≠ coverage
lineage                         ≠ coverage
ALL lineage for an execution    ≠ coverage
lineage selected because THIS claim actually rests on it  → candidate claim-specific coverage
```

⭐ **The selection relation must itself be explicit.** A lineage row cannot become coverage merely
by existing.

## 3 · `evidentiary use`

> A relation stating that a particular frozen input lineage item is **offered as evidence for this
> particular candidate observation.**

⭐ This is the missing relation that makes claim-specific coverage lawful — and the reason coverage
is earned only now: there is finally a specific claim for the coverage to qualify.

⛔ **Does not assert** causal effect · importance · model attention · semantic weight. It says only:
*this candidate claim rests on this frozen material.*

## 4 · `material classification`

> Classification according to what a durable output **is**, independent of which worker, job,
> model, queue or execution produced it.

```text
material type = recurrence observation
⛔ NOT   recurrence-sweep result · bounded-cognition output · worker result · job output
```

**F-J2.3 invariance test:** *if an entirely different lawful execution mechanism produced the
identical durable claim with identical evidence, would its material classification remain the
same?* ⭐ Required answer **yes**. If changing machinery changes classification, actor/material
collapse has occurred.

## 5 · `output`

Use only where a generic sentence genuinely means *a durable artifact produced by an execution*.
⛔ **No runtime or schema type named `BoundedCognitionOutput`.** The artifact carries its truthful
material-specific name, keeping `machinery → artifact` without `machinery = artifact identity`.

## 6 · `offer to participation boundary`

> An explicit attempt to present **already-classified** material to the canonical participation
> boundary for adjudication.

⛔ An offer is **not** admission · participation · MAIA use · served-response contribution. The
boundary decides what happens next, and **P12 requires that no alternative direct path exists.**

> Existing, truthfully registered material used **only** to prove the participation instrument can
> admit a lawful neighbouring case.

⛔ **No producer may be created or widened for the control.**

nothing.

## 10 · `observation evidence`

> A durable recurrence observation must be **unable to exist** without recoverable frozen evidence.

The relation must answer: *which frozen sections support this claim · which exact
revision/ranges/digests · what claim-specific coverage was asserted.* ⛔ No prose duplication.
