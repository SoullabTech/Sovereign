## 1. Ratified rulings

### R-A — Keep is a family; T1-A owns one qualified object

**RATIFIED.**

> **Keep is the family concept. T1-A Personal Keeps READ means Personal Field / Portfolio Keeps, not Press Keeps, Marked Moments, Reflection Capsules, or an automatic union of every Keep-labelled artifact.**

The T1-A identifier remains unchanged. No parallel T1-A lane is created.

### R-B — Exact act law

**RATIFIED.**

> **A Keep becomes durable only through a member act bound to a knowable object. An already exact UI gesture may itself be the confirming act. System recognition of relational language is not the confirming act.**

### R-C — Deictic referent law

**RATIFIED.**

> **“This,” “that,” and similar references may authorize persistence only after the referenced object is mechanically resolved without guessing. If resolution is ambiguous, the system must clarify or show the proposed object for confirmation; it must not choose the referent.**

### R-D — Provenance law

**RATIFIED.**

> **`generated_by='member-gesture'` is necessary to call an atom a generic Keep, but the field is not sufficient evidence that the member act satisfied current Keep authority. A truthful reader must not upgrade uncertain historical origin into confirmed member authorship.**

### R-E — Reader truth law

**RATIFIED.**

> **Any surface or prompt producer that says “Keep,” “kept by the member,” or “member-placed” must mechanically prove the origin it names. A broader atom reader may remain broad only if it labels each origin truthfully instead of collapsing them into member authorship.**

### R-F — Qualified Keeps remain qualified

**RATIFIED.**

> **Press Keeps, Marked Moments and Reflection Capsule Keeps retain their existing domain identity and consent semantics. T1-A does not absorb them merely because they share the family verb.**

### R-G — Sanctuary

**INHERITED, not re-ruled.**

> **Sanctuary cannot create a durable Keep, even on explicit in-session request.**

This remains governed by the pre-existing absolute Sanctuary boundary.
