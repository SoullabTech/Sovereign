## Purpose

R2B turns camera depth into perceptual depth without yet creating new semantic claims.

The field should become richer as scale changes while preserving:
- orientation;
- relation;
- ambiguity;
- room identity;
- epistemic standing;
- the recoverable whole.

## Primary founder direction

> The Living Field should be a magical way to explore the full Soullab platform — a true Soul Lab.

> Each node should have the option of asking MAIA for insight into the node, its interconnections, and more.

R2B therefore establishes the visual/perceptual slots for future portals and MAIA inquiry without activating contextless or premature navigation.
## LOD grammar

### LOD0 — Whole ecology
Perceive:
- major worlds;
- atmosphere;
- broad field balance;
- only the strongest currently relevant bridge corridors.

Do not expose a product menu.

### LOD1 — Worlds
Perceive:
- world identity;
- major contained regions;
- relevant neighboring worlds;
- locally meaningful bridge directions.

### LOD2 — Regional cells
Perceive:
- named semantic cells;
- local membrane structure;
- strongest immediate relations;
- subtle room relevance only where earned.

### LOD3 — Local neighborhood
Perceive:
- direct relational neighborhood;
- typed relation hints;
- tension / resonance;
- possible **Ask MAIA** locus affordance.

### LOD4 — Relational meaning
Perceive:
- specific relation verbs;
- shared meaning;
- competing meanings;
- context;
- portal rationale where a crossing is relevant.

### LOD5 — Evidence / provenance
Perceive:
- source standing;
- authorship;
- temporal lineage;
- counterevidence;
- revision history;
- why a relation / portal / meaning is present.
## Whole-platform coherence requirements

R2B must make the whole platform feel continuous without flattening rooms.

### Shared across Soullab
- semantic IDs;
- return-address model;
- attention grammar;
- relation grammar;
- epistemic standing;
- visual family;
- path continuity.

### Preserved per room
- primary activity;
- privacy boundary;
- interaction grammar;
- content density;
- tools;
- local atmosphere.

Writer's Studio remains manuscript-first.
Relationships remains relationship-first.
Dreams may be imaginal.
Practice may be enactment-first.

> **The Living Field connects rooms; it does not homogenize them.**
## Earned portal requirements

A portal cue may appear only when:
1. the current semantic locus is explicit;
2. the destination room has a meaningful continuation role;
3. the relationship can be explained;
4. member choice remains intact;
5. a return address can eventually be supported.

R2B may visually prototype:
- latent portal cue;
- available portal cue;
- invited portal cue.

R2B may not execute:
- cross-room navigation;
- persistence across rooms;
- inferred destination selection.

Founder question:

> Does a portal feel like the field opening somewhere meaningful, rather than an app shortcut appearing?
## MAIA locus requirements

Every inspectable locus should be architecturally capable of supporting **Ask MAIA**.

Eligible loci:
- node;
- relation;
- bridge;
- tension;
- shared meaning;
- question;
- current field configuration.

R2B may define the affordance and context boundary.

It must not create a false live control if no authorized MAIA execution path exists.

Future MAIA inquiry should be able to offer:
- Insight;
- Connections;
- Counterview;
- History;
- Teach me;
- Why here?;
- Where might this lead?;
- Reflect with me.

The field remains primary.
## Whole-field engagement requirements

The member should be able to move fluidly among:
- sensing the whole;
- attending to one locus;
- exploring its relations;
- understanding context;
- considering possibility;
- choosing action;
- widening again.

R2B should favor:
- whole-before-fragment;
- relation-before-list;
- context-before abstraction;
- ambiguity where ambiguity is real;
- member choice over automatic recommendation.

The field may reveal what is:
- here;
- recurring;
- fading;
- emerging;
- possible;
- chosen.

Possibility must not be rendered as destiny.

## Exit criteria

R2B may pass only when founder witness confirms:
1. deeper zoom feels richer, not merely busier;
2. labels / relations emerge without clutter;
3. the larger ecology remains perceptible;
4. portal cues feel earned rather than promotional;
5. the future Ask MAIA locus is obvious without becoming chatbot-first;
6. widening restores a coherent whole.

STOP before R2C Progressive Relational Resolution.
