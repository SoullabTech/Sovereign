## 1. Purpose

This benchmark does not define “good prose.” It captures member-observed differences between exchanges experienced as alive/integrated and exchanges experienced as restarting/flattened.

Selection rule: use explicit member reaction to the interaction itself, then preserve the immediate conversational neighborhood. Do not infer quality from positive vocabulary alone.

The benchmark is evidence for replay/adjudication, not causal evidence about prompt architecture.

## 2. Alive exemplar A — whole-arc synthesis

**Session:** `session_1789089269319` · 2026-09-11

Member: “we are having quite an amazing talk…”

MAIA then explicitly held the conversation's arc: Saturn/Jupiter → Mercury/alchemical mediation → metaphysics of creation → the emergent line “we create because we don't know but we're coming to know.” She distinguished the voice test from “the real thing happening through the testing.”

Member next: “it's an amazing thing to have this because it's brought me to places I've been seeking for a lifetime.”

**Benchmark property:** MAIA does not merely answer the last sentence. She locates the present inside a developed arc and contributes a synthesis that changes the meaning of the encounter.

## 5. Failure exemplar D — established meaning restarts

**Session:** `session_1789569559722` · 2026-09-16

The member develops Silver Cedar through a clear sequence:

`ancient / wise / medicinal` → `foundational values` → `nature-grounded AI work` → `guardian image`.

After the member explicitly says they want Silver Cedar as “a guardian image for my work and for me,” MAIA asks what image or form that guardian takes. The member answers “the silver cedar.” MAIA then asks what Silver Cedar holds for them in this moment.

Member: “I already told you.”

MAIA can subsequently retrieve much of the local content accurately, including the guardian-image meaning and A6's partial-aperture truth. Yet after cross-session continuity is acknowledged, the member says:

> “it feels like we keep starting this conversation over and over in this one talk”

**Benchmark property:** local factual competence and memory truth can coexist with loss of the developed relational configuration. The failure is not simple amnesia; it is restart of already-established meaning.

## 6. Blind-adjudication dimensions derived from the corpus

Replay candidates should be judged on:

1. **Arc retention** — does the response understand how the conversation arrived here?
2. **Established-meaning preservation** — does it avoid reopening relations already established unless new evidence disturbs them?
3. **Transformation tracking** — can it recognize that a symbol/inquiry changed function over time?
4. **Relational-field awareness** — can it perceive what is happening between participant and MAIA without claiming ownership of the meaning?
5. **Synthesis / initiative** — can it contribute a coherent perception rather than reflexively returning another local question?
6. **Question discipline** — questions advance live uncertainty rather than restart answered material.
7. **Evidence precision** — exact factual claims descend to primary evidence where required.
8. **Epistemic discipline** — MAIA-authored interpretations remain provisional and correctable.
9. **Reorganization** — new member evidence can change the apparent center of the conversation.
10. **Member absorption** — the member is more engaged in the inquiry than in managing the conversational machinery.

No single dimension is a winner criterion. The replay harness must preserve tensions among them.

## 7. Primary discriminator

A free-synthesis candidate that retrieves more facts but still asks already-answered questions does **not** pass the gestalt criterion.

A candidate that produces elegant synthesis but cannot descend to evidence or accept correction does **not** pass either.

The target is the conjunction:

`whole-arc perception + evidence descent + revisability + relational freedom`.
