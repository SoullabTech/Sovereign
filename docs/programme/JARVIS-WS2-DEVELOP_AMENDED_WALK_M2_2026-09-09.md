## Deferral

> *"That matters. I want to come back to it after I finish this section."*

**That sentence is enough.** No `Keep · Save · Pin · Defer · Add to later · Mark important`.

### The substrate event — standings, not types

```text
UNLAWFUL   TYPE = deferred_item · STATUS = important

LAWFUL     UTTERANCE   "that matters, but later"
           REFERENCE   -> the thing being discussed
           STANDING    the writer gave it durable significance at this moment
```

**The writer expressed a relationship to something. The system preserves that fact. It does not convert the relationship into a task.** The constitutional ruling doing real work.

### ⭐ And MAIA must not acknowledge the filing

> ⛔ *"Got it, I'll save that for later."*

⭐ **A promise to manage creates the queue in the writer's mind even where none exists in the system.** The writer now believes something is being held on their behalf — **behavioural leakage in its purest form, produced by a single helpful sentence.**

**But silence raises a fair question: how does the writer trust it was heard?** The answer follows from the constitutional rule — *the member encounters the phenomenon; MAIA encounters the architecture*:

```text
LEAK          acknowledging the STORAGE      "saved" · "noted" · "I'll remember"
LAWFUL        continuing the RELATIONSHIP     respond to what was said, or to
                                              the writing — or say nothing and
                                              keep working
```

**MAIA may answer the person. She may not report on the machinery.**

## ⚠️ A cost that must be stated plainly, not glossed

If deferral is an utterance with a reference and a standing, and **nothing surfaces it**, then **the writer must remember to ask.**

```text
C/M2's deferral gap is RELOCATED, not eliminated
  recoverable by meaning   ✓ (unlike C, where it was destroyed)
  reminded                 ✗ (nothing will bring it back to you)
```

⭐ **This is deliberate, not an oversight: being reminded is precisely what D6 forbids.** The writer's own memory of having said *"that matters"* is what brings them back — and the thing is then reachable by meaning, which C could not offer.

**A founder might reasonably expect "deferral" to mean it comes back. It does not.** The lawful mitigation is the one C/M2 already surfaced: **the writer may put a note in their own material.** The system stays quiet; the writer's own draft can carry the reminder. That note is unambiguously theirs, carries no MAIA authority, and creates no system obligation.
