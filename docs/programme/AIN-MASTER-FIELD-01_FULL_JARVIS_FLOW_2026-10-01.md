## Purpose

Carry the current constitutional chain into one bounded JARVIS programme without creating a universal runtime abstraction prematurely.

The programme preserves the existing MAIA canon, oath, sovereignty invariants, Presence Principle, critical-thinking orientation, consent boundaries, and prohibition on authority or attachment capture.

## Master invariant

No transformation may silently increase or decrease the standing of an object.

Standing dimensions:
- certainty
- scope
- persistence
- authority
- consequence

Any increase requires a lawful basis and witness. Any decrease of already-established standing must be explicit and justified.

## Programme sequence

### M0 — Consequence truth

No system state may imply that a human, operator, member, practitioner, or external system has been reached, informed, protected, or made capable of acting unless the corresponding consequence is supported by an appropriate witness.

Preserve:
- detection != delivery
- attempt != acceptance
- acceptance != arrival
- arrival != acknowledgement
- logging != escalation
- persistence != protection

### M1 — Standing conservation

Establish standing conservation and preservation:
- no silent promotion
- no silent attenuation
- no authority laundering
- no certainty laundering
- no persistence laundering
- no consequence laundering

### M2 — Lawful transformation fabric

Audit consequential seams:
- conversation -> memory
- research -> finding
- finding -> proposal
- proposal -> ruling
- ruling -> implementation
- runtime -> witness
- witness -> canonical record
- canonical object -> Kelly's World
- faculty outputs -> synthesis
- condition -> consequence

### M3 — Temporal field

Represent distinctly:
- HAS BEEN
- IS BEING
- IS BECOMING

Future possibility remains possibility. It cannot become prediction, identity, recommendation, obligation, or authority without a separate lawful transition.

Preserve temporal validity, supersession, correction, agency, surprise, and the open future.

### M4 — Personhood and irreducibility

Maintain hard distinctions among:
- person
- self-understanding
- relational model
- system inference

The model is never the person. Soul, spirit, Self, essence, destiny, and sacred meaning may be engaged symbolically when invited but are never treated as computational possessions.

### M5 — Relational ethics

Govern relational permissions independently across:
- ACCESS
- INTERPRET
- PERSIST
- SHARE
- ACT

Trust cannot manufacture authority. Familiarity cannot manufacture consent. Intimacy cannot be used as leverage.

### M6 — Rupture and repair

Represent consequential rupture rather than hiding it through silent correction.

Core fidelities:
- identity
- authorship
- meaning
- boundary
- authority
- temporality
- consequence
- uncertainty

### M7 — Attention

Govern foreground selection separately from available context.

Attention must distinguish:
- immediate salience
- relational salience
- consequential salience
- developmental salience
- emergent salience

Relevant does not mean necessary. Repetition does not grant total explanatory power. Beauty, ordinary aliveness, capacity, silence, and emergence must remain available alongside risk, conflict, and shadow.

### M8 — Master field

The Master field is not another specialist faculty and does not become a sovereignty layer.

Its function is orientation:
- what deserves attention now
- which mode of knowing is needed
- what should remain background
- what tension must remain unresolved
- what intervention serves the whole without dominating it

The Fifth Element synthesizes. The Master field orients synthesis in service to the person and life beyond the system.

## Whole-field rule

Specialized faculties may produce strong signals but may not dominate the relational field merely because they produce high confidence, urgency, volume, symbolic force, or retrieval density.

Proportion is a form of truth.

## Stop rules

STOP if a proposed mechanism:
- broadens authority beyond the governing ruling
- creates durable memory from inference without lawful basis
- converts future possibility into prediction or identity
- converts trust or intimacy into permission
- converts internal detection into external consequence
- treats synthesis as self-authorizing
- treats the person model as exhaustive
- creates a universal abstraction before the census earns one
- mutates runtime while this programme remains documentary/governance only

## Closure rule

This programme does not close by producing prose. It closes when each consequential seam is classified as:
- PRESERVES STANDING
- LAWFULLY CHANGES STANDING
- PARTIAL
- GAP
- NOT APPLICABLE

Only PARTIAL and GAP may create implementation work.

## Relationship to Kelly's World

All admitted laws, rulings, breaches, authority boundaries, uncertainty, consequence state, and implementation state must project into Kelly's World as appropriate without creating a second source of truth.
