## Founder-decision set exposed by J1

J1 narrows the immediate founder work to three decisions.

### D-01 · What does AIN name?

Repository evidence establishes semantic collision but cannot resolve it.

**Decision needed:** whether AIN becomes the name of the constitutional runtime/platform standard and the old Agentic Intelligence Networks object is renamed/reclassified; or whether another explicit naming structure is preferred.

### D-02 · What is the constitutional floor of AIN?

Platform-wide law already exists, but some of the strongest protections remain MAIA-named.

**Decision needed:** whether those MAIA-named invariants are:

- MAIA-only implementation law;
- expressions of a deeper AIN law that must be separately constituted;
- or explicitly promoted into AIN law by founder act.

No implementation may answer this by moving files or renaming symbols.
### D-03 · Should a new field-instantiation specification be constituted?

The historical Flourishing manifest is evidence of an experiment, but the FIELD_MANIFEST.md it claims to test is not recoverable from repository history.

**Decision needed:** whether JARVIS should construct a new **candidate** field-instantiation specification from current ratified canon + MAIA + Now What + a third-field falsifier, rather than treating the historical draft as missing canon.

## JARVIS recommendation — proposal only

1. Reserve **AIN** for the constitutional runtime/platform standard; preserve the old Agentic Intelligence Networks engine as historical/subsystem provenance under a different explicit name rather than pretending it was always the new runtime.
2. Derive AIN-generic constitutional contracts only from law whose scope is already platform-wide or from explicit founder promotion; never generalize MAIA-specific identity law by refactor.
3. Author a new candidate field-instantiation specification from current evidence. Treat the June Flourishing manifest as a falsification specimen, not as authority.
4. Keep AIN Studio closed until MAIA and Now What can both be expressed against the same implementation-independent field/runtime contracts.

These recommendations have **no authority** until ruled.
