**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Class:** read-only reconciliation / no runtime authority

**Purpose:** establish what Grokker already has in Soullab before further implementation.

## 0 · Executive finding

Grokker is not an unbuilt graph product. Soullab already contains most of the substrate required for a mature Grokker: a member-scoped Living Field runtime, a 12-domain Knowledge Field with typed cross-domain mappings, governed local retrieval, canonical RGR research law, mature unmerged Source Fabric experiments, mature unmerged Living Field/Soul-Service laws, and a current-canonical Grokker R1 spatial candidate.

The main engineering problem is **convergence, not invention**.

> Do not build a second knowledge engine, a second member-field model, or an ungoverned graph beside what already exists.

## 1 · Canonical Living Field runtime — BUILT

Canonical runtime already carries 13 stable life dimensions, member-authored expressions, version history, source records, participant consent, personal spirals/state, recent states, gathered Keeps with visible denominators, per-Keep selection warrants and affinity scores, member-memory eligibility guards, and sacred/protected exclusions.

`lib/maia/living-field/affinityMapper.ts` is pure and structural. It maps declared registers, elemental lenses, source type, and breakthrough standing. It does not inspect content or ask a model to infer where a Keep belongs.

`LivingFieldDetailPanel` already supports conversation-first entry with MAIA, direct member writing, gathered-Keep inspectability before refinement, MAIA candidate refinement, voice/upload capture, source display, developmental history, and participant consent controls.

**Consequence:** the personal Living Field is already a real member-scoped data ecology. Grokker must project/traverse it rather than replace it.

## 2 · Canonical Living Constellation — BUILT, deliberately non-relational by default

The constellation is governed by tests that distinguish visible projection, explicit member-authored facet flows, and mere spatial proximity. The interface explicitly states that placement lines do not claim that parts are psychologically or semantically connected.

**Consequence:** proximity, animation, scale, and color cannot become edge creation. A Grokker relation needs declared standing.

## 3 · Canonical Knowledge Field — BUILT

`lib/maia/knowledge/knowledgeField.ts` contains a 12-domain registry spanning Islamic psychology, Jungian/depth psychology, relational intelligence, mystical/contemplative traditions, neuroscience/cognitive science, Spiralogic, somatics, attachment/trauma, systems theory, philosophy of mind, ritual/symbolic traditions, and ethics/discernment.

It already defines explicit cross-domain relationship types: `rough_equivalent`, `partial_overlap`, `contrast`, `developmental_parallel`, `functional_parallel`, `supports`, and `differs`.

It also carries a `queryable: false` containment seam for concepts that are registered canonically but must not surface through retrieval/activation. Current activation is still comparatively simple: keyword/domain detection and concept matching.

**Consequence:** a substantial typed semantic relation vocabulary already exists. R2+ should adapt this standing rather than invent an unrelated edge taxonomy.

## 4 · Canonical retrieval and provenance — BUILT, convergence incomplete

Canonical has governed corpus, embedding, selection-contract, selection-grant and source-allowlist controls, local embedding applicability, fail-closed retrieval, and provenance-bearing prompt formatting.

ADR 004 remains authoritative: **one knowledge-ingestion/retrieval engine, many scopes**. It declares the `library_*` pipeline canonical and `lib/ain/knowledge/RetrievalService.ts` legacy. Current `GovernedRetrievalService` still delegates chunk retrieval to that legacy service after governance gates.

**Consequence:** Grokker must not create another retrieval stack. Source descent should converge on the canonical library substrate as the existing migration completes.

## 5 · RGR — CANONICAL RESEARCH LAW, NOT MEMBER-FACING COGNITION

RGR-00 through RGR-06 are in canonical. The programme establishes relation as a first-class research object; strict separation of analogy, hypothesis, formal model, empirical support, engineering usefulness, and metaphysical truth; H-RT1 and falsification law; synthetic-only early research boundaries; formalism neutrality; and an RGR-06 confirmatory implementation lock with independent verifier PASS.

RGR-06 records zero benchmark examples/pairs, zero training, zero test execution, and zero replication execution. RGR-00 explicitly protects MAIA from premature integration.

**Consequence:** RGR may inform research vocabulary and future experiments. It does not authorize member-facing geometric claims or MAIA interpretation.

## 9 · What is actually missing

The largest gaps are connective:

- **Canonical convergence:** Source Fabric and Living Field/Soul-Service law is stranded on branches more than 100 commits behind canonical.
- **Spatial projection binding:** R1 is a session-local traversal grammar, not yet bound to canonical Living Field dimensions, Knowledge Field concepts, source receipts, or member-authorized relations.
- **Typed relation projection:** mappings and explicit facet flows exist, but no single governed adapter turns authorized relations into Grokker-visible edges.
- **Illumination/source descent:** Living Field sources/gathering exist; Source Fabric R10 adds stronger receipt/ablation semantics; they have not converged into the Grokker illumination panel.
- **Retrieval convergence:** ADR 004's one-engine decision is not fully embodied.
- **Cross-surface continuity:** House → Writer continuity is settling in a separate governed lane; Grokker should consume that law, not create a parallel context spine.

## 10 · Non-negotiable implementation consequences

1. No new knowledge engine.
2. No graph edge from embedding similarity, proximity, frequency, or visual convenience.
3. No member-memory write from Grokker traversal unless separately authorized.
4. No RGR output enters MAIA merely because the vocabulary is useful.
5. No wholesale merge of old experimental branches; reconcile bounded laws/artifacts onto current canonical.
6. Semantic DOM remains truth; SVG/Canvas/GPU may render but never own epistemic standing.
7. Every deep movement needs truthful return.
8. Source/relationship standing must remain inspectable.
9. Member-authored/confirmed meaning outranks model suggestion.
10. Awe is preserved through restraint and depth, not unsupported visual mystification.
