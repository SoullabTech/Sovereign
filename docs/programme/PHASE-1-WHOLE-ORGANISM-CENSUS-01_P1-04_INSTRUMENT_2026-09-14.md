## 2 · ⭐⭐ `GOVERNED SCOPE` — the new requirement

> ### Governance of a thing is not necessarily authorization of what that thing does.

⛔ `GOVERNED` may **not** be a single undifferentiated visual state. Where the **quoted source
explicitly says what is governed**, the scope is displayed:

```text
GOVERNED OBJECT      the capability/object itself is constitutionally defined
GOVERNED SHAPE       the form, schema, boundaries or record structure is governed
GOVERNED ACT         the actual operation is governed
GOVERNED DECISION    the authority to determine an outcome is governed
```

⛔ **Do not invent these labels where the P1-03 record does not distinguish them.**

```text
AUTHORITY            GOVERNED
SCOPE OF GOVERNANCE  UNKNOWN          ← when the evidence says GOVERNED and no more
```

⛔ **Never infer `GOVERNED ACT`.** A legitimate node reads:

```text
PARTICIPATION        UNKNOWN
AUTHORITY            GOVERNED
GOVERNED SCOPE       record shape only — ⛔ does not authorize the act
```

⭐ *That is what stops `GOVERNED` from visually laundering an ungoverned action.*
