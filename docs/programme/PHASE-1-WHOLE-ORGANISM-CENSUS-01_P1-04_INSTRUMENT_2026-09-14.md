
## 4 · Ladder separations — enforced, never bridged

```text

PARTICIPATES ≠ DECIDES             CONSIDERS   ≠ CONTRIBUTES

INFERENCE    ≠ RELATIONAL AUTHORITY
```

⛔ **No edge is promoted because a neighbouring rung is established.** Execution, persistence,
cognition, output, routing, and a decision-producing capability each, alone and together,
establish **nothing** about authority. A rung appears on an edge only where the source record
already established that rung; otherwise the rung layer is absent for that node and the node is
listed under unknowns.
