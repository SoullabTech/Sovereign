## 4. Central research question

*Under what conditions does a human–AI relationship produce an insight, capacity or possibility that neither participant would have developed alone?*

Hazards named in advance (not resolved here):
- **Attribution.** "Neither alone" needs a comparator; absent one, it is a story, not a finding.
- **Dependency.** The population where this effect looks strongest is the population where attachment risk is highest. A positive result must not become a reason to increase MAIA's centrality (Invariants; no attachment capture).
- **Measurement.** Studying real members' relationships touches memory consent and Sanctuary. Sanctuary content is never usable, including for research.

## 5. Claim discipline

**Charter §9 evidence classes (binding, verified):** hypothesis · founder articulation · accepted design · source presence · isolated prototype · synthetic test · integrated behavior · human acceptance · production release. "None may be represented as another." Every finding, candidate discovery and note in this lane carries exactly one of these labels; the §6 census output is hypothesis-class at most.

- Mechanism claims ("this is how it works") require evidence and a stated disconfirmation.
- Experiential/phenomenological exploration need not be falsifiable, but must be *labelled* as such and never worded as established mechanism.
- Mechanism first, metaphor after measurement. Borrowed vocabulary (attractor, phase transition, coupling) is admitted only with what it predicts and what would refute it.
- Every outward statement follows `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` (Live / Designed / Vision). Nothing here is Live.
