## 0 · What makes these materially different

Not three skins on one layout. **Each is organized by a different thing.**

```text
A · THE MARGIN     PLACE organizes.     Everything is anchored to a locus in the text.
B · THE QUESTION   INQUIRY organizes.   Everything is subordinate to what the writer
                                        is currently working on, in their words.
C · TWO HANDS      THE TURN organizes.  MAIA's contribution is ephemeral — it exists
                                        for this exchange and then clears.
```

Under Option C all three are **one environment, one Work, one MAIA**, with Writing and Develop as postures. They differ in **what holds the process together** when the writer is not acting.

## A · The Margin — *place organizes*

**There is no room but the Work.** The manuscript fills the field. MAIA exists at the edge of the text, addressable anywhere. Every process object — question, noticing, conversation, proposal, revision — **is anchored at a place in the prose and has no existence apart from it.**

```text
ARRIVAL        the Work at the last locus. Nothing else. No panel, no list.
INTENTION      an unlabelled line at the top edge. May be blank; blank is complete.
WORK RELATION  total — the Work IS the surface.
OBSERVATIONS   enter as faint marks in the margin AT THEIR LOCUS. The mark says
               only "something was noticed here". Nothing opens unbidden.
CONVERSATION   opens in the margin beside the passage, inline, at that place.
REVISION       the writer edits in place. Commissioned proposals appear as margin
               alternatives, adjacent to the line, never replacing it.
REFLECTION     after an edit the margin is silent. The writer may ask; MAIA
               rereads that passage.
QUIET EXIT     close it. Marks stay where they are, unnumbered and uncounted.
```

**Feels like**: a manuscript with an editor's pencil in the margin.
⚠️ **Its risk is PF-1 / PF-12**: dense margin marks could become an observation list rotated ninety degrees. **Density is the whole design problem** — the mark must read as *someone was here*, never as *here are your six issues*.

---

## B · The Question — *inquiry organizes*

The space is organized around **what the writer is currently working on, in their own words.** The Work is present and scoped to that question's locus. Observations, evidence and analysis are all subordinate to it.

```text
ARRIVAL        the Work, and a question line in the writer's own words —
               EMPTY IF THEY HAVE NONE, and empty is a complete state.
INTENTION      is the organizing object, not a field to satisfy. "Something
               feels wrong here" is a legitimate entire question.
WORK RELATION  present, scoped to where the question lives; the whole Work
               remains reachable.
OBSERVATIONS   never shown unprompted. Reached by asking — "what do you see
               here?" — and answered against the writer's question, in their terms.
CONVERSATION   the main surface once a question exists. Runs in the writer's
               language; MAIA works with "muddy".
REVISION       the passage under the question is directly editable in place.
REFLECTION     the question persists across the edit. The writer restates it,
               keeps it, or drops it. MAIA rereads on request.
QUIET EXIT     leave. The question becomes historically available, not live.
```

**Feels like**: sitting with an editor who begins by asking *what are we working on?*
⚠️ **Its risk is a soft PF-2 inversion**: if the room reads as *requiring* a question, it excludes the writer who arrived with only a felt difficulty. **The blank must be genuinely complete, never a prompt** — this candidate lives or dies on that.

---

## C · Two Hands — *the turn organizes*

The Work holds the primary field permanently. A second surface holds **only what MAIA is contributing to this exchange** — and it is **empty by default and clears after use.** It is not a panel of findings; it is where the current turn lives.

```text
ARRIVAL        the Work. The second surface is EMPTY. It has no default content
               and no resting state to fill.
INTENTION      spoken into the exchange rather than stored — the writer says what
               they want; it is a turn, not a field.
WORK RELATION  permanent primary field, never displaced by anything MAIA does.
OBSERVATIONS   materialise in the second surface ONLY in answer to the writer,
               and clear when the exchange moves on.
CONVERSATION   is the second surface. There is no separate conversation place.
REVISION       in the Work, always. Proposals appear as alternatives in the second
               surface; adoption moves language into the Work by a writer act.
REFLECTION     the writer edits; the surface is empty; if asked, MAIA rereads the
               changed passage and answers there; then it clears again.
QUIET EXIT     both surfaces at rest. Nothing is held open.
```

**Feels like**: a desk where the editor's notes are on a sheet that gets cleared each time.
⚠️ **Its risk is PF-12**: if the second surface ever accretes — keeps a history, stacks turns, grows a scroll — **it becomes the reading report again with better manners.** Clearing is not a nicety here; it is the architecture.

## 1A · ⚠️ Structural failure condition ≠ known tradeoff weakness

**These are different categories and §1/§2 nearly collapse them.** Corrected here before the walk.

```text
STRUCTURAL FAILURE CONDITION   the architecture stops being itself
                               C: the second surface stops clearing
                               A: the margin becomes a list
                               B: the blank becomes a prompt

KNOWN TRADEOFF WEAKNESS        the architecture works as designed and
                               that design costs something
                               C: clearing may discard useful continuity
                               A: density pressure — everything wants a mark
                               B: the wordless arrival needs deliberate welcome
```

⭐ **Operational consequence — this maps onto the three verdicts:**

```text
A TRADEOFF may be met with AMEND.
  The architecture holds; the cost is designed against.

A STRUCTURAL FAILURE cannot be met with AMEND.
  Amending it produces a DIFFERENT CANDIDATE, not a repaired one —
  a Two Hands whose surface persists is no longer Two Hands.
```

⛔ **D8 must not reject C because the continuity tradeoff exists.** The question is whether, in an actual writing process, that tradeoff makes the experience worse enough to require amendment. Same for A's density pressure and B's wordless arrival.

## 2A · D8 walk protocol — **frozen before the walk**

Recorded now, before any candidate is walked, for the same reason the SEL-0 threshold is frozen before its run: **a protocol written afterward can be fitted to whichever candidate won.**

### Constants

```text
same real chapter · same writer · same underlying writing difficulty
```

**A process walk, not a feature comparison.** The flow is explicit: judge process, not styling or button success.

### The three stress moments

Each targets one candidate at its actual seam:

```text
1. ARRIVE WITH ONLY "something feels off"           -> B's real test
2. LET SEVERAL THINGS BECOME NOTICEABLE             -> A's real test
3. MOVE AWAY, EDIT, THEN NEED SOMETHING
   FROM THE PRIOR EXCHANGE                          -> C's real test
```

⚠️ **Run every moment through every candidate — nine cells, not three.** If each moment is walked only in the candidate it targets, the result is three unrelated observations rather than a comparison, and no candidate is ever seen handling a difficulty it was not designed for. **The diagonal is the stress test; the off-diagonal is the evidence.**

### Judgment questions

```text
where did I naturally begin?
did MAIA help without taking over?
did I stay in the Work?
did anything accumulate into obligation?
did context survive when I needed it?
did the architecture make me explain myself unnecessarily?
did I want to keep writing?
```

### ⚠️ Order effect — cheap to control, expensive to discover afterward

Walking A → B → C gives the **last** candidate a writer already warmed to the chapter and the **first** a cold read. That difference will look like an architectural difference.

```text
Either vary which candidate meets the fresh difficulty,
or record the order walked and read the results against it.
```

Not a reason to delay the walk — a reason to write the order down before starting.
