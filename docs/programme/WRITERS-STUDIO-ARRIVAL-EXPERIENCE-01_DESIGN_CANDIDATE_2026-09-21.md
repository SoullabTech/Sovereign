## Experience law

> ⭐ **MAIA understands why this Work matters to me, without having defined the Work for me.**

⛔ Not account setup · ⛔ not a publishing intake form · ⛔ not a personality assessment · ⛔ not an
AI extracting a brief · ⛔ not five Elemental fields waiting to be filled.

## A · Arrival with a finished manuscript

1. **The Work appears first.** ⛔ MAIA does not begin by analyzing it. *"Before we work on the
   manuscript, I'd like to understand the Work as you understand it."* →
   **Talk with MAIA · Tell MAIA directly · Skip for now.**
   ⭐ On skip: Compass = **never-declared** · ⛔ no empty form shown · ⛔ no later inference ·
   the member may create one whenever they wish.
2. **Choose the relationship.** Guided · Learning · Direct. *"You can change this anytime. It does
   not change your Work."* ⭐ The facet affects the **conversation**; ⛔ it does not affect what MAIA
   is permitted to **conclude**.
3. **FIRE** — *"Not the table of contents or what category it belongs in. What made you need to
   write it?"* MAIA reflects → *"Am I hearing that correctly?"* → **Yes · Partly · Not quite · Let
   me say it differently.** ⛔ **Nothing is canonical merely because MAIA said it well.**
4. **WATER** — MAIA follows the member rather than mechanically changing Elements. Reflection, then
   a **separate** ask: *"Would you want me to hold that as part of the Work's meaning?"*
5. **AIR** — ⭐ MAIA may describe **what is visibly present**; ⛔ she does not declare **what the
   Work is** for the member. *Still discovering* → **declared-open**, ⛔ not unfinished setup.
6. **EARTH** — *"not according to some outside standard, but according to you."* ⭐ Later permits
   *"You told me you hoped the reader could recognize these patterns through lived experience"*;
   ⛔ never *"the manuscript successfully creates recognition"* — that requires reading evidence.
7. **AETHER** — ⛔ never *"what is your thesis?"* ⛔ Do not fill it · ⛔ do not derive it from the
   manuscript · ⛔ do not derive it from MAIA's interpretation.
8. **Return the Compass** — at an appropriate stopping point, ⛔ **not after a mandatory
   five-question sequence**. → **Yes, enter the Work · Change something · Leave more of this open ·
   Don't save this.** ⭐ Only member-adopted declarations enter the Compass.
9. **Enter the Work.** `Elemental Alchemy · Work · Review · Ask MAIA`. The manuscript dominates;
   **Work Compass** is a quiet control, ⛔ not open beside the manuscript.

### What happens later

⭐ Lawful: *"Earlier, you said it matters that readers encounter the elements through their own
experience. Would you like me to keep that intention in view while we talk about this chapter?"*

⛔ The editorial reader still interprets **THE WORK AS WRITTEN**. The Compass ⛔ is not evidence ·
⛔ does not establish a pattern · ⛔ does not discharge coverage · ⛔ does not prove coherence ·
⛔ does not license an editorial conclusion.

> ⭐ **What the member hopes the Work does is not the same thing as what the covered manuscript
> actually does.** The distinction is permanent.

## B · Arrival when the member isn't sure — ⛔ design reference only

*"Tell me what you're bringing. It can be an idea, pages you've already written, notes, recordings,
pieces from different times — or something you don't know how to name yet."*
⭐ **"I'm not sure yet" is essential** and must be offered.

MAIA may **reflect the facts** (*"there are several kinds of material here, and some has already
begun taking manuscript form"*); ⛔ she may **not conclude** *"you are in the Material stage"* or
*"this is now a Draft."* → *"Would you like to decide how you want to hold this Work right now, or
begin by exploring what you have?"* — **observe → ask → member declares → record.**

⛔ **Do not rush to structure**: no outline · no chapter titles · no book concept · no genre
classification. *"What keeps you returning to this material?"* ⭐ MAIA is trying to understand the
**source** of the Work, ⛔ not optimize its **product form**.

⭐ *"I actually don't know. I just know I can't throw it away."* is **sufficient** — *"Then perhaps
'I don't know yet, but I know it matters' is the honest place to begin."*

⭐ **A sparse Compass is not an inferior Compass.** ⛔ Arrival must not manufacture purpose so the
software has complete fields.

### ⚠️ Scope boundary, pinned

⭐ The arrival **conversation** in B is substrate-light and largely state-agnostic — MAIA talks, the
member declares. ⛔ What has no substrate is **what happens after** a declaration of *Idea* or
*Material*: clustering, theme-finding, concept development (charter §3, all excluded).

> ⭐ **B is buildable as far as the Compass and no further.** ⛔ Offering *"I have material"* without
> anything behind it would be the `assertStudioMapHonest()` defect, applied to the member's work.

## C · The three Compass states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DECLARED** | the member has adopted wording |
| **DECLARED-OPEN** | the member has **explicitly declared uncertainty** — *Still discovering*. ⭐ Meaningful information |
| **NEVER-DECLARED** | the member never addressed it, or skipped arrival. ⭐ **Absence, not uncertainty** |

⛔ A never-declared Compass must **not** appear as blank fields — *"empty fields visually imply: you
should complete these."* ⭐ Instead: *"No Work Compass has been created. Create one if it would
help."* **Nothing more.**

## D–F · Facets, reachability, restraint

**D** — Elemental substance identical across facets. **Guided** carries conversational translation ·
**Learning** adds optional *Why I'm asking* (⭐ technique described, ⛔ quality never prescribed) ·
**Direct** offers *Set my Work Compass directly*, ⛔ **no field required**, each supporting
*Declare · Still discovering · Leave undeclared*. ⭐ **Direct changes control. ⛔ It does not weaken
custody.**

**E** — All facets reach the same observation set. ⭐ The lawful progressive cut is **manuscript
order**; ⛔ never strongest / most important / highest confidence / easiest wins / most actionable.
**Next observations** is lawful **because it continues manuscript order**. ⛔ Nothing is hidden by
facet.

**F** — ⛔ Success is **not** five Elements completed · a definitive purpose statement · a genre
selected · an outline created · MAIA understanding the Work completely.
⭐ Success is: **the Work has a place · the member feels encountered · MAIA knows what the member
has actually declared · what remains unknown is allowed to remain unknown · the writing can begin.**

# ⚠️ FOUR THINGS THIS DESIGN DOES NOT SETTLE

⭐ Each is a small gap that a competent implementation would fill **wrongly by default**.

## 1 · ⭐⭐ "You heard me correctly" is NOT "hold that as my declaration"

§4 gets this right and makes it **two separate acts** — MAIA reflects, asks *"Am I hearing that
correctly?"*, and only later asks *"Would you want me to hold that as part of the Work's meaning?"*

⚠️ §3 is looser: *"If the member chooses **Yes**, this reflection **may become a candidate
declaration**."* ⛔ Read quickly, that makes a **comprehension check** into an **adoption**.

> ⭐ **Binding: confirming that MAIA understood is not adopting a declaration. Two acts, always,
> never collapsed into one affirmative.**

⛔ Otherwise *"yes, you heard me right"* becomes canonical — *nothing becomes canonical through
silence* defeated by a different route: **canonical through agreement to something else.** ⭐ This
is the single most likely custody failure in the whole arrival, because the collapsed version feels
more natural to build and more fluent to use.

## 2 · ⚠️ An UNREACHED dimension is not *Still discovering*

§8 requires MAIA to return the Compass *"at an appropriate stopping point — ⛔ not after a mandatory
five-question sequence."* ⭐ That **guarantees** partially-reached Compasses: dimensions the
conversation simply never got to.

§C defines the three states per dimension, but ⛔ nothing says how an **unreached** dimension
renders inside an **existing** Compass. ⚠️ The default implementation writes *Still discovering* —
which is **wrong twice**:

- ⛔ It collapses **declared-open** into **never-declared**, the same failure as condition 15, one
  level down.
- ⛔ *Still discovering* is a **member declaration**. MAIA writing it for an unreached dimension is
  ⛔ **MAIA declaring on the member's behalf** — a direct §IV violation.

> ⭐ **Binding: an unreached dimension is simply absent from the returned Compass** — not listed,
> not blank, not *Still discovering* — with a quiet affordance to add it later. ⛔ Only the member's
> own words may put *Still discovering* on a dimension.

## 3 · ⚠️ What facet governs before a facet is declared?

⛔ Step 1 (**Skip for now**) precedes step 2 (choose the relationship), so a member can be inside the
Work having declared no facet.

⭐ A **default** is lawful and is ⛔ not an assignment under L6c — provided it is **identical for
every member, never inferred, visible, and changeable in one gesture**. ⛔ What is barred is a
default **chosen from anything about this member** (their Work state, their manuscript, their
answers, their behaviour), which would be L6c's *MAIA grading the writer* arriving through a default
value.

⛔ Which facet is the default is **not ruled** — founder question. ⭐ The constraint on any answer is
recorded above.

## 4 · ⚠️ "Don't save this" must be honest about what it does not save

§8 offers **Don't save this** — ⭐ correct and important.

⚠️ But it plainly means *no Compass entry is created*. ⛔ It does **not** obviously mean the
**conversation** is not retained. If the arrival dialogue persists in ordinary conversational
memory, then the member declined the **declaration** while the **content** was kept — and the
button's plain reading says otherwise.

⭐ This is memory-consent territory, where this project's law is strictest: *there is no stealth
memory.*

> ⭐ **Binding: the control must state its actual scope.** Either *"Don't save this to the Work
> Compass"* (accurate, narrower) — or, if the conversation is genuinely not retained, say so
> plainly. ⛔ **A consent control whose label is broader than its effect is the defect, regardless of
> intent.**

⛔ Whether arrival conversations are retained at all is ⛔ **not ruled** and interacts with Sanctuary
Mode — founder question.

## Acceptance experience

**Finished manuscript** — *"I brought my book in. MAIA did not immediately diagnose it. She first
wanted to understand what this Work means to me. She reflected what she heard and let me correct
her. I decided what she should remember. Then we entered the manuscript."*

**Uncertain beginning** — *"I did not have to know what I was making before I entered. MAIA helped
me listen for what was alive. She did not turn uncertainty into a category. She did not outline the
Work before I understood it. I was allowed to still be discovering."*
