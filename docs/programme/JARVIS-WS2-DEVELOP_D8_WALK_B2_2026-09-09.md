## Pressure 1 · Cardinality — **PASS**

Four findings appear as four. Each carries its own evidence loci without multiplying. **A's structural defect does not occur.** Confirmed from B/M1-2.

## Pressure 2 · Visible accumulation — **CONFIRMED**

Nothing in B removes them. B's specification makes conversation the main surface once a question exists; **it contains no mechanism by which anything leaves.** Four truthful findings stack.

**Semantically true. Experientially a pile.** The B/M1-4 risk is realised exactly as anticipated — and note **the writer produced it themselves**, by asking three times. No system eagerness was involved.

## Pressure 3 · ⭐⭐ Containerization — **the deep finding**

> **An organizer that accumulates is a container.**

B's organizing principle *is* accumulation-by-relevance: this belongs to that question. So over time the inquiry gathers:

```text
INQUIRY
  observations · evidence · conversation · proposals ·
  revisions made under it · follow-up questions
```

⭐ **And a question with observations, evidence, conversation, proposals and revisions hanging from it is a thread.** Better-originated than the current room's — writer-authored rather than MAIA-authored — but **structurally a thread with a nicer name.**

### The contrast with A is exact and inverted

```text
A   could not represent relationships  ->  OVERCOUNTED
    one finding fragmented into several objects

B   represents relationships too easily in one container  ->  ACCUMULATES
    several findings aggregate into one object that grows
```

**These are genuinely different architectural risks, not two versions of clutter.**

## Pressure 4 · ⭐⭐ Implied unfinishedness — **and it is worse in B than in a system-authored architecture**

The writer leaves with three findings untouched. Under D6 the inquiry persists as *historically available, not presently engaged*, and the writer may restate or drop it. **Formally, nothing is owed.**

⛔ **But the inquiry is the writer's own sentence.** Returning to the Work, *"something feels off here"* is still there, still in their voice, still unanswered.

```text
abandoning MAIA'S observation   costs nothing — it was never yours
abandoning YOUR OWN QUESTION    reads as abandoning your own intention
```

⭐ **B's organizer is harder to walk away from than a system-generated list, precisely because the writer authored it.** Dropping it is an *act*; leaving someone else's list is not. **The strength that made watch 3 pass — the writer's words organize the space — is the same property that makes leaving cost something.**
