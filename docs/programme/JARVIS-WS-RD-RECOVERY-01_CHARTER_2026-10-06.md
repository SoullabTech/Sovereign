# JARVIS — Writer's Studio R&D Recovery 01
## Founder-opened experience-recovery programme · 2026-10-06

**Programme ID:** `JARVIS-WS-RD-RECOVERY-01`
**Founder act:** “let's do so” — 2026-10-06
**Trigger:** founder witness that several days of implementation materially improved infrastructure while the lived Writer–MAIA experience remained too close to the pre-R&D product shape.

## 0. Why this programme exists

Writer's Studio has accumulated strong machinery: manuscript custody, frozen developmental readings, exact passage addressing, reversible proposals, local structured inference, durable conversation, Work directives, typesetting, Review proofing, and case-study evidence.

The founder witness on 2026-10-06 established a different failure:

> the machinery has begun to become the experience.

Develop could still behave like a report generator with MAIA beside it. Relationship controls could be visible without materially governing the next lived interaction. “Work on this” could degrade into navigation. Structured findings could dominate before the writer had a chance to converse, clarify intention, disagree, or let MAIA's understanding change.

This is **R&D drift**.

The recovery target is not another redesign. It is to restore the already-developed product laws as the governing acceptance authority for the live experience.

## 1. Immediate stop rule

Until this recovery packet is founder-ratified:

- no broad Writer's Studio feature expansion;
- no new Develop dashboard/report surface;
- no new interaction whose acceptance basis is merely “the current implementation already does X”;
- no new MAIA setting that collapses distinct relational dimensions into one control;
- no propagation of the current Chapter 10 interaction pattern to the rest of the Studio.

Allowed while the recovery gate is open:

- safety, data-loss, security, and crash repair;
- defects that prevent the recovery witness itself;
- read-only R&D census and reconciliation;
- bounded prototype work explicitly required to witness the golden journey after founder ratification.

The Elemental Alchemy completion lane is **paused as execution priority**, not cancelled. Its valid substrate remains evidence and will be reused.

## 3. Governing recovered principles

The recovery begins with these already-supported laws:

- **The Work is primary.**
- **Manuscript first. Conversation first. Meaning before mechanics.**
- **MAIA carries the sophistication; the writer carries the Work.**
- **Relationship precedes recommendation.**
- **Conversation ordinarily precedes intervention when meaning or intention is uncertain.**
- **The author decides meaning, belonging, structure, adoption, and completion.**
- **MAIA notices → writer encounters → conversation → writer may recognize → writer may decide → Work may change.**
- **Surface what belongs now, not everything that can be seen.**
- **Use the smallest useful intervention.**
- **Develop sees the Work. Write changes the Work. Review tests the Work.**
- **A writer should not need editorial training to receive excellent editorial help.**
- **A professional or technical writer must not be forced into simplified language they did not ask for.**
- **Changing explanatory register never changes evidence, confidence, permission, or editorial authority.**
- **One relationship may move across Work → chapter → section → passage → sentence without starting over.**
- **No manuscript mutation without a writer act that knows its effect.**
- **No technical PASS substitutes for founder experiential PASS.**

## 5. R&D recovery question

Every implementation decision in this programme must answer:

> **Which ratified product law requires this, and what human experience will prove it?**

“No current component exists” is not sufficient reason to build.
“The current component already works this way” is not sufficient reason to preserve it.

## 6. Terminal acceptance claim

This programme closes only when:

1. the recovered R&D is source-custodied;
2. product laws are reconciled rather than merely accumulated;
3. the Writer–MAIA state model is explicit;
4. scenario coverage spans non-writer through technical/professional writer;

10. Review tests the change without becoming another drafting or grading machine;
