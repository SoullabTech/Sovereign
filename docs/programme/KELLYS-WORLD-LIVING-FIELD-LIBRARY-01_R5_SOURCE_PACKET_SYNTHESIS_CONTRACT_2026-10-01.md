# KELLY'S WORLD — LIVING FIELD LIBRARY 01 · R5 Source Packet + Synthesis Contract · 2026-10-01

## Purpose

Create the minimum lawful envelope between Grokker Trace and any future semantic synthesis.

R5 does not invoke a model and does not generate synthesis. It defines what a future synthesis may receive and what it may claim.

## Source packet

`GROKKER_SOURCE_PACKET_V1` carries:
- the member/founder query;
- bounded retrieved sources;
- exact source path where a programme record exists;
- excerpt and headings;
- retrieval matches and rank;
- retrieval standing;
- jurisdiction;
- explicit absence of a relation warrant.

The packet law is:

> **RETRIEVAL DOES NOT CREATE RELATION STANDING.**

## Synthesis proposal

Any future Grokker synthesis begins as:
- authorship: `GROKKER_AUTHORED`;
- standing: `CANDIDATE_UNESTABLISHED`;
- jurisdiction: `KELLYS_WORLD_ORIENTATION`;
- explicit relied-upon source IDs;
- explicit relation warrant, if one actually exists;
- explicit unresolved state.

The validator refuses:
1. source-standing upgrades caused by retrieval;
2. invented source references;
3. synthesis authorship laundering;
4. silent promotion to warranted standing;
5. erasure of relied-upon contested sources;
6. closure without a relation warrant.
