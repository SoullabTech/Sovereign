## Zero behavioral influence

Accepted. The live-corpus computation is not deployed into MAIA. It runs outside the serving request, after exchanges have occurred, over read-only snapshots. Serving-code isolation is enforced by test.

Therefore these witnesses can establish properties of the shadow substrate without creating a circular “the shadow changed the behavior it measured” problem.
## Constitutional result

The resolver / standing laws survive implementation pressure. The first live adapter did not.

V0 demonstrated a dangerous shortcut:

`standing gesture + previous MAIA turn → standing relation`

That is invalid when the previous turn contains multiple claims. It repeats the original continuity error at a new layer: **reaching back is not the same relation as reaching back to this claim.**

V1 correctly refuses that shortcut and records ambiguity.

- reliable automatic extraction of semantic/meaning relations from arbitrary live conversation;
- exact claim identity inside multi-sentence MAIA turns;
- natural-language antecedent resolution for confirmation/correction gestures;
- safe live Gestalt quality across arbitrary conversations;
- any basis for exposing shadow Gestalt to MAIA cognition.
