⚠️ So the arc's step 2 — *clean / correctable / drift / ambiguous / instrument-limited* — is a

precisely the Phase 0 reopening the closure forbids. If behavioral evidence is wanted, it needs
its own witness with its own acceptance — ⛔ never a channel bolted onto a frozen instrument.
