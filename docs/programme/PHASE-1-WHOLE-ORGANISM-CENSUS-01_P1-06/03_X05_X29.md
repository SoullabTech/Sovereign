### Which limb of the schema §7a test was applied, and why

The founder test requires **both** conjuncts for `ALIGNED`:

```text
ALIGNED
  the frozen exclusion is itself a PROHIBITION                            ← limb 1
  AND the census established that the prohibited IN-SCOPE mechanism /
  state is not instantiated                                              ← limb 2
```

- **Limb 1 — SATISFIED.** `X-05` does contain a prohibition: *not enlargement*, which FR-10 makes
  precise as *one relational field absorbing unlimited complexity*.
- **Limb 2 — ⛔ NOT SATISFIED, and this is the decisive finding.** The absent mechanism the census
  established is **member-to-member matching, recommendation and similarity ranking**. FR-10 does
  not govern matching. FR-10 governs **how a collective field grows, and what happens to
  recognizability, participation, facilitation, differentiation and repair when it does.** Matching
  is a mechanism for *gathering people toward a field*; enlargement is a property of *the field
  once they are in it*. A system with no matching can still enlarge a single field without limit,
  and a system full of matching can still multiply and nest. ⭐⭐ **The absence the census
  established is not the absence the exclusion prohibits.**

To read *"no matching exists"* as *"enlargement is not instantiated"* would require asserting that
matching is the route by which a field enlarges — a bridge **no record in P1-01…P1-05 provides**.
That is precisely the second NOT COMPARABLE clause: *treating non-existence as compliance would
require INVENTING a scope or bridge the records do not provide.*

The first NOT COMPARABLE clause is met as well: the chosen statement concerns **field-to-field
structure** (multiplication, nesting, `CONSTELLATION`, `COMMONS`), and every located Circle
artifact concerns **entry to a field** (`requireCircleAccess`), **role inside a field**
(`CircleRole`), or **what may cross out of a field** (`P3-C-09`'s suspension). Those are
non-equivalent objects, and the records do not bridge them.

### Why not `ALIGNED`

Because limb 2 fails. ⛔ And because the one further fact that might tempt an alignment reading —
that Circles are reachable only by founders, the cohort variable being *"not yet constituted"*
(domain I §1.3) — would amount to *"a field cannot enlarge because nobody is in it."* That is a
statement about the gate, not about the scale architecture, and treating a closed door as
conformance with a law about what happens behind it is the same invented bridge in a different
direction.

### Why not `UNKNOWN`, and what changes if a reader prefers it

`UNKNOWN` (*"census evidence cannot support the comparison"*) is a defensible reading, and it is
recorded here rather than argued away: a reader who holds that *Circles-as-scale* and
*Circles-as-located-artifacts* are the **same** object, merely under-evidenced, lands on `UNKNOWN`.
This row chooses `NOT COMPARABLE` because the evidence that exists is not thin evidence *about the
right object* — it is evidence about **different objects**, and one of those (matching) sits close
enough to invite exactly the substitution the schema §7a precision was issued to prevent. ⭐ The
two readings differ in diagnosis and **agree completely in effect**: neither scores alignment,
neither scores divergence, and neither permits a number.

### Why not `DIVERGENT` or `MIXED`

`DIVERGENT` requires the actual evidence to **establish the opposite or excluded state** — here,
that a relational field absorbs unlimited complexity. No record establishes that; ⛔ nor could this
row establish it without a threshold, which FR-10 forbids and schema §4 restates. `MIXED` requires
*different NAMED objects or paths legitimately landing differently*; there is no second named
scale-path to land differently, so `MIXED` here would be a container for unresolved evidence,
which schema §3 forbids.

member experience is not primary or that distribution was not practitioner-led. ⛔ Nor would this
row be entitled to say so if it did: that is a verdict, and P1-04/P1-05 handle these as findings.

`C5`s and two `C6`s and an UNKNOWN runtime mode. Schema §3: *"`MIXED` must never be a euphemism for
unresolved contradiction. Where the evidence conflicts → `UNKNOWN`."*
