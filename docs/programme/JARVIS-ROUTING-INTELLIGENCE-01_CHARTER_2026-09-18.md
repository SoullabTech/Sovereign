## J1 questions

1. What routing already exists, and what does it actually decide?
2. Which provider/model facts are mechanically established versus merely labeled in UI?
3. What evidence can each current execution path actually receive?
4. What existing authority must be satisfied before an external path is even admissible?
5. What does “independent second review” mean mechanically?
6. What standing may one or multiple model outputs acquire?
7. Which model-role assumptions require empirical benchmarking before they may become automatic?

## J1 exit gate

J1 may PASS only when:

- current routing, provider, Work Unit, continuity, and reconciliation mechanisms are enumerated;
- model-family selection is separated from provider-transport selection;
- evidence visibility is stated per current transport;
- live provider connectivity evidence is distinguished from capability-quality evidence;
- at least one falsifier shows why provider registration cannot serve as routing intelligence;
- current second-review and disagreement behavior is stated exactly;
- unresolved Class A disclosure/authority gaps are recorded rather than repaired;
- no external model is called and no repository/runtime code changes.

## Programme target

A routing decision must eventually be explainable as:

```text
work-unit intent + risk + evidence class
        ↓
admissible model families
        ↓
primary cognitive role
        ↓
independent review topology
        ↓
authorized transport
        ↓
attempt evidence
        ↓
mechanical / human adjudication
```

Provider availability may constrain execution. It may never determine epistemic standing.
