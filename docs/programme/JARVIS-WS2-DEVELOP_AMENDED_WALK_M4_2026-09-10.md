## 1 · Dormancy must be computational, not visual

```text
VISIBLE RUBRIC     "protect mystery" shown above the manuscript
                   -> obvious failure
INVISIBLE RUBRIC   "protect mystery" silently conditions every MAIA response
                   -> SAME FAILURE, harder to detect
LAWFUL             orientation exists durably · does not participate in
                   interpretation · until the writer reaches for it
```

### ⭐ A falsifiable test for dormancy

"Computationally dormant" is the right requirement, but a system could claim it and still condition. **Checkable form:**

> **The same request, in a Work with a North Star and in a Work without one, must produce the same answer.**

If *"what do you see here?"* differs depending on whether an orientation exists, **the orientation is being applied silently** — and the rubric has moved below the interface rather than disappearing.

## 2 · The writer reaches — and the M2 grammar holds

> *"Read this against what I said I wanted to protect."*

> *"The cadence still sounds like you. The place that strains what you said you wanted is the explanation after the yogi story: it tells the reader what the image means before the image has had much room to work. If you want to keep the mystery you named, that is the passage I'd question first."*

```text
LAWFUL   one developmental judgment, supported by the writer's stated aspiration
LEAK     MYSTERY ✓ · LIVED EXPERIENCE ✓ · CADENCE ✓ · NOT TEXTBOOK ⚠️ · ...
```

**The second is a rubric whether or not comparison was requested.** Aspirations are **reasons subordinate to a judgment**, never peer criteria.

At the writer's next turn the **application** ceases to be current; the **aspiration** remains. **C/M4's positive result preserved.**

## 3 · ⭐⭐ A specification defect — and its correction

```text
CLASS 1   DURABLE CREATIVE ORIENTATION · "writer-authored"
```

MAIA once proposed *"intellectual clarity without over-explanation"*; the writer said *"yes, that's exactly what I mean."* **D5 says the MAIA origin survives.** So the language carries **writer authority** and **not** writer origin.

> **Adoption can confer authority without conferring authorship.**

```text
MAIA proposed -> writer adopted -> DURABLE WRITER-AUTHORIZED ORIENTATION
   origin      MAIA-seeded        (recorded, never erased)
   authority   writer-granted     (the standing that matters)
```

### ⭐ Proposed correction to the specification

```text
WAS    Class 1 · writer-authored        Class 2 · writer-authored
NOW    Class 1 · writer-AUTHORIZED      Class 2 · writer-AUTHORIZED
```

⭐ **Authorization is a standing the writer grants. Authorship is an origin the record keeps.** As written, `writer-authored` reads as content origin and **contradicts the ratified lineage law** — *a location cannot launder an origin.* The same applies to Class 2: if MAIA helped articulate the writer's question, its origin is likewise mixed. **An architectural truthfulness fix, not a naming preference.**

**Adoption stays atomic**: at *"yes, that's exactly it"* the system captures together the adopted language, its new orientation standing, the MAIA proposal it came from, and the writer act that adopted it. **M4 confirms why C made atomicity necessary** — if the initiating turn releases and origin was not captured then, it cannot be safely reconstructed.

## 4 · Revocability — durable is not immutable

> *"Actually, I think this chapter needs to be more explicit. I'm less concerned about preserving mystery here."*

**That sentence is sufficient.** No form, no criterion removal, no *update orientation*. **The new utterance changes the standing; the prior aspiration remains historically true** — *at that time, the writer wanted to protect mystery here* — **and stops operating as current orientation.**

> **M3's law in another register: historical truth persists after present authority expires.**

⛔ And MAIA may not later volunteer *"you used to say you wanted more mystery."* **True. Forbidden unless reached for.**

### ⚠️ Scope ambiguity — and the adopted constraint that governs it

*"…preserving mystery **here**"* — local to this chapter, or a change of mind about the Work? **Over-reading it globally rewrites the North Star from a local remark; under-reading it ignores a real change of mind.**

⭐ **R-L3 governs exactly this** — one of only three adopted research constraints: *when developmental meaning materially depends on unstated or uncertain author intent, MAIA must ask or mark uncertainty before hardening the inference into a conclusion.* **The utterance means what it says; its scope is the writer's to state; where ambiguous, MAIA asks.**

## 5 · ⭐⭐⭐ The paired guards, and the law beneath them

```text
HISTORY       rich enough to retrieve   -> NEVER VOLUNTEERED       (M3)
ORIENTATION   rich enough to guide      -> NEVER SILENTLY APPLIED  (M4)
```

**Two symmetric temptations produced by the architecture's own power.** And they are instances of one law, now three-times demonstrated:

```text
craft knowledge        does not confer authority over meaning   (founding principle §4)
historical knowledge   does not confer license to surface       (M3)
orientation knowledge  does not confer license to apply         (M4)
```

> ### What MAIA knows never determines what MAIA may do. Only the writer's act does.

> **The system can know vastly more than it is permitted to make present.**
