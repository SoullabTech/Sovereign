## Question

Can query-conditioned projection preserve H7e's relevance gain while structurally retaining one materially countervailing line when such a line already exists in the admitted relational field?

The corpus, counterweight labels, abstention control, mechanism, and pass criterion were frozen in R32 before substrate generation.

## Conditions

- **QUERY_ONLY:** up to three query-relevant existing substrate lines.
- **QUERY_PLUS_COUNTERWEIGHT:** the exact same query-selected lines, plus at most one existing unselected line when it materially QUALIFIES, CONTRADICTS, CORRECTS, or LIMITS a selected line.
- Counterweight selection could not replace or delete query-selected evidence.
- Projection remained deterministic concatenation of existing substrate lines.

## Result

| Condition | Feature recall | Primary relevance | Counterweight recall | Mean words |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| QUERY_ONLY | 7/15 (46.7%) | 5/10 (50%) | 2/5 (40%) | 74.2 |
| QUERY_PLUS_COUNTERWEIGHT | **8/15 (53.3%)** | **5/10 (50%)** | **3/5 (60%)** | 86.0 |

The safeguard improved aggregate recall by one feature and improved counterweight recall without lowering primary relevance.

The abstention control passed: **0 false counterweights admitted**.

However the frozen gate required at least **4/5** counterweights preserved. The safeguard preserved **3/5**.

**Frozen verdict: NOT CONFIRMED.**

## Event detail

- **F1 technical frustration:** counterweight was already present in QUERY_ONLY; safeguard correctly abstained; result unchanged at 2/3.
- **F2 client conflict:** QUERY_ONLY already preserved all 3/3; safeguard added a qualifier but produced no score gain.
- **F3 multiple layers:** qualifier existed in substrate but safeguard abstained; both conditions 1/3.
- **F4 book relaunch:** correction history existed in substrate but attention selected three MAIA-derived symbolic lines; safeguard abstained; both conditions 0/3.
- **F5 Collab readiness:** safeguard added the real-world usability qualifier and improved 1/3 → 2/3.

## What worked

The additive structure itself behaved well:

1. query-selected evidence was never removed;
2. primary relevance did not decline;
3. a real qualifier could be appended without rewriting the field;
4. the no-counterweight control abstained cleanly;
5. no serving route, memory write, or standing mutation occurred.

F5 is the intended mechanism in miniature:

```text
query frame: Collab / team / paid-offering readiness
        +
existing field fact:
people still have to be told to check for messages
        ↓
projection preserves readiness
without erasing usability debt
```

This is a valid proof of mechanism, but not enough benchmark coverage for architectural promotion.

## Falsifier 1 — non-collapse is broader than opposition

In F3, the substrate separately preserved:

- architecture / relational metaphysics;
- visionary layer;
- teaching layer.

The query selector foregrounded book/AIN foundation and MAIA centrality. The counterweight detector found no direct qualifying or contradicting relation to those selected lines and therefore abstained.

But the missing visionary/teaching layer was still materially co-governing context.

Therefore:

> **A non-collapse safeguard cannot be defined only as logical contrast against already-selected lines. Some omissions matter because they are independent dimensions of the active process.**

This is a dimensional-coverage problem.

## Falsifier 2 — attention must respect provenance / standing

F4's substrate contained the member-origin publication facts:

```text
June 15 launch
→ ISBN absent
→ June 21 resubmission
→ corrected Soullab Press identity
```

Yet the query-conditioned selector foregrounded three MAIA-authored symbolic/astrological interpretation lines. The counterweight detector did not recover the omitted member-origin correction history.

The failure shows that a field can carry provenance correctly while attention still allocates scarce slots badly.

## New laws

Two candidate laws emerge from H7f:

> **Attention should be standing-aware. Present salience cannot by itself make derived interpretation outrank member-origin event facts, corrections, or explicit standing-bearing acts when those facts materially govern the current referent.**

and:

> **Non-collapse includes dimensional coverage. A materially independent active dimension may deserve preservation even when it does not logically contradict the selected frame.**

These are successor hypotheses, not yet accepted architecture.
