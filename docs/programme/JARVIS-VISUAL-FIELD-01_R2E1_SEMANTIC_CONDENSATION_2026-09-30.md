- synthesis.

## Visual grammar

At wide / bridge scale:
- individual cross-world edges are withheld;
- one quiet connective bridge represents each world pair;
- unresolved tension remains explicitly marked;
- single-relation bridges do not carry redundant text.

At bundle scale:
- multi-relation bridges may show a minimal multiplicity marker such as `×2`;
- unresolved bridges retain `tension held`;
- single-relation bridges remain visually quiet.

At specific scale:
- condensed bridges disappear;
- the exact cross-world relations return.

Internal / intra-world relations remain specific in R2E1.
