## 5 · Phase 1.25 — Immutable voice baseline · RULED

> **Measurement begins before collaboration; enforcement can mature later.**

Capture the published *Elemental Alchemy* corpus as the immutable pre-MAIA linguistic baseline **before the first working session**.

**Why this cannot wait for Phase 9.** Phase 2 already judges *"Did she preserve my voice?"* — but if instrumentation waits until Phase 9, MAIA will have worked beside the author through six phases and there is no uncontaminated baseline left to measure drift against. The comparison would be post-MAIA prose against post-MAIA prose. The control is lost the first time she edits, and it cannot be reconstructed afterward.

The **gate** may still land at Phase 9, once we know which measures actually discriminate preserved voice from homogenization. The **measurement** cannot.

```text
OPEN — must be predeclared before Phase 9 enforcement:
  exact linguistic baseline measures
  drift thresholds
```

Capture is a read of already-published material. It is not an editing act and requires no provider call.

## 6 · Phase 1.5 — SEL-0 selection falsifier · GATE

Full instrument: research record §14. Specified, **not run**.

> Among observations MAIA is actually permitted to bring into conversation, does she already know which ones are useful to raise first?

**Why it gates Phase 2.** Detection and selection are distinct capabilities. A single-section working session is small enough that selection barely bites — so Phase 2 can pass with selection entirely absent, and the failure surfaces at Develop scope, four phases later, at maximum accumulated cost.

**Candidate set is the lawful surviving corpus only.** Observations already known to be constitutionally unlawful under F-7 are excluded **because they are ineligible, not because they ranked poorly.** A test of selection quality must not be contaminated by material that was never permitted to be raised at all. Rendered in plain member-facing language, instrumentation stripped: the test is selection, not tolerance for instrumentation.

Zero provider cost. No exception required. Threshold frozen before the run.

## 8 · Phase 2 — One excellent section-level working session

The highest-priority build, and the point at which the whole direction is falsifiable.

```text
OPEN SECTION -> READ WITH MAIA -> DISCUSS -> EXPLORE
  -> OPTIONALLY EDIT -> KEEP WRITING
```

No technical findings screen. No 200 observations. No developmental ontology exposed to the writer.

**Acceptance question:** was writing with MAIA easier and more useful than writing without her? Judged on the seven session questions (research record §12).

**If this fails, stop and repair before building anything bigger.** Phases 3–10 are not a fallback path.

## 9 · Phase 3 — Scope control + competence boundary

Scope becomes first-class: current selection · section · chapter · part · whole Work · custom range, addressable in natural language.

This is **architectural, not copy polish.** Three distinct quantities exist underneath:

```text
requested_scope   what the writer asked about
read_scope        what MAIA has actually examined
claim_scope       the scale MAIA's current statement asserts
```

**Law:**

> **`claim_scope` may not exceed `read_scope` without explicit qualification.**

Asked *"how does this chapter sit in the whole book?"* while having read the chapter and two neighbours, MAIA cannot quietly impersonate whole-book knowledge. She says so plainly and offers to zoom out. No technical labels reach the writer; the type distinction exists beneath.

Without this, Phase 3 either forces a whole-manuscript read per turn — Phase 7's problem arriving four stages early — or answers from partial knowledge without saying so.

## 10 · Phases 4–7 — Work understanding through whole-Work cognition

**Phase 4 — Work understanding.** One Work-level permission: *let MAIA carry what she learns while helping me with this Work.* Strict provenance types underneath (writer said / intended / decided · MAIA observed / proposed · system observed an edit · current manuscript state) with a simple surface. Required legibility: *"what do you understand about this Work?"* answered plainly, correctable in one gesture. This is what prevents Work memory from becoming stealth memory.

**Phase 5 — Write → Develop continuity.** Local work accumulates into larger pattern; every larger claim returns to *"show me"* → chapter / section / passage → back into Write.

**Phase 6 — Develop conversation.** Seven lenses (developing · shape · continuity · arc · voice · coherence · reader experience) as conversation openers, never downloadable reports. MAIA opens with a manageable set of grounded observations and never pretends those are all she saw. *"What else did you notice?"* and *"show me where"* are the safeguards against invisible prioritization.

**Phase 7 — Whole-Work cognition.** Hybrid: current manuscript + authored hierarchy + accumulated Work understanding → broad read → candidate pattern → re-ground against actual passages → conversation. Not "226 old notices, summarized"; not "100k tokens, one magic prompt." Whole-Work synthesis must always be able to return to the manuscript.

## 12 · Phase 9 — Voice-preservation gate

The Phase 1.25 baseline becomes an acceptance gate once we know which measures discriminate.

```text
"better" is not smoother
"better" is not more conventional
"better" is not more AI-like

better = serves the writer's stated intention
         while remaining recognizably theirs
```

Load-bearing for practitioners whose distinctive language is part of what they are offering — where flattening the voice destroys the substance.
