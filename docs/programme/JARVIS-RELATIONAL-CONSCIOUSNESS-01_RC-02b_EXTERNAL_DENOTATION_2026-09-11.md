N-03's taxonomy, read at source, is **stronger and more precise than the lane's shorthand**. Relative to a target
phenomenon **X**:


## OQ-01 — OPEN QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER (a rule-interpretation question, ⛔ not mine to settle)
Charter §14 admits *"an author manuscript or repository copy … when bibliographic identity can be verified."*
**Does a third-party full scan hosted on a document-sharing site (e.g. Scribd) fall inside that?**
**The narrow reading:** §14 names *author manuscript* and *repository copy*; a third-party scan is neither, even
when the text matches — provenance of the **copy**, not merely fidelity of the **text**, is what §14 verifies.
**The permissive reading:** §14's operative test is *verifiable bibliographic identity plus substantive text*,
which such a scan can satisfy. ⛔ **Not ruled here.** N-09's body findings remain gated until it is.
