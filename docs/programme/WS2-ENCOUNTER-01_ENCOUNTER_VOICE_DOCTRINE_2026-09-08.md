## 1 · ⭐ There is no translation layer, and that is the load-bearing fact

Today the model's `assertion` becomes `CandidateNotice.text`, is screened, and becomes
`MaiaNotice.text` unchanged. **The words the cognition produces are the words the member would
read.** So this doctrine does not land on a future presentation layer — there isn't one. It has
to land in one of two places, and they are not equivalent:

**(a) In the cognition contract** (`render.ts`). MAIA is asked, from the start, to speak the way
a helper speaks. One act, one voice, and the sentence that is screened is the sentence the writer
sees.

**(b) In a constituted translation step.** A second pass rewrites the analytic observation into
member-facing language.

⛔ **(b) carries a defect (a) does not.** A translation step is *a second cognition rewriting the
first*, and the rewritten sentence is no longer the sentence that was screened, nor the sentence
whose evidence was bound. Everything this lane built — exact excerpts, server-owned scope, the
authorship boundary, the vocabulary screen — attaches to a specific string. Rewriting it after
those instruments have run puts unexamined words in front of the writer wearing the authority of
words that were examined. **A translator would have to be constituted, screened and grounded in
its own right, or it silently voids the chain.**

This is a design question with a real answer either way and it is **the founder's to rule**. It
is stated here rather than assumed.

## 2 · ⭐ It reconciles with the E3 scope constraint — by expressing the bound, not the number

The standing E3 constraint says: *a future surface may not discard the server-owned scope and
present the sentence as an unqualified whole-Work claim.* This doctrine says: the member must not
have to read `visible_window 35600..48000 of 386031`.

Both hold, and the reconciliation is exact:

> **The scope is carried as provenance and EXPRESSED as English.** *"in this part of the book"*,
> *"here, and then the writing moves on"*, *"in this section"* — the boundary is spoken, the
> coordinate is not.

⛔ What is forbidden is dropping the bound, not hiding the integer. A sentence that says *"you
never come back to this"* has discarded the scope no matter how warm it sounds.

## 3 · ⛔ This does not subsume F-7. They are independent.

The G8 falsifier — *"is not developed further"* — fails **both** laws at once, which makes it
tempting to treat one repair as covering both. It does not:

| | analytic voice | human voice |
|---|---|---|
| **developmental shape** | *"This distinction is not developed further."* | *"You never really land this idea."* |
| **observational shape** | *"Introduced at 19,038; no subsequent instance in window."* | *"You make this distinction here, and it appears only briefly in this part of the book."* |

**The bottom-left cell is warm and still a verdict.** Fluent, kind, member-facing language is
fully capable of carrying an unfinished-obligation implication — arguably better capable, because
it disarms the reader. F-7 is about **shape**; this doctrine is about **register**. A repair that
fixes register alone would produce a friendlier developmental case.

Recorded as **F-8 · instrument voice**, distinct from **F-7 · absence-shaped openness**, and both
required before the witness is rerun.

## 4 · What this changes about the G8 lesson

Founder, verbatim: *the problem is not merely "block the phrase not developed further."*

A lexical patch on those six clauses would produce a screen that rejects two spellings of a move
the model can make in fifty. The G8 result is evidence about **how MAIA speaks about someone's
Work**, and the repair belongs at that level — in what she is asked to be when she speaks, not in
a list of banned strings.
