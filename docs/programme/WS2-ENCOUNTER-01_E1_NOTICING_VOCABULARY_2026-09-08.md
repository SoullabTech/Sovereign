# WS2-ENCOUNTER-01 · E1 — The Encounter Noticing Vocabulary

**Status: DESIGN, FOR FOUNDER RATIFICATION. No code. E2 does not open on this document.**
Date: 2026-09-08 · Branch: `claude/studio-bring-work-back-icvfaa`
Authorizing act: founder ruling 2026-09-08 (WS2-ENCOUNTER-01), E1 design authorized.
Doctrine: **PT-1** *Encounter precedes intervention* · E-01 ephemeral by default ·
E-04 no latent agenda · E-05 the vocabulary is constitutional semantics.

**The one sentence this vocabulary must satisfy:**

> An Encounter observation helps the writer recognize the Work without telling the writer
> what ought to happen to it.

**The falsification test:** if an observation naturally invites *"so what should I fix?"*,
it is already developmental.

The prohibition is not against intelligence. MAIA may be perceptive, surprising, even
moving. *Ephemeral does not mean shallow.* What is forbidden is converting presence into
prescription before the writer has authorized that relationship.

---

## 1 — The six families of lawful noticing

Named as **acts of attention**, not as dimensions. They are not scored, not ranked, not
required to be populated, and never reported as coverage.

### 1.1 · PREOCCUPATION — what the Work keeps returning to

*What does this Work appear to care about?*

Belongs: *"This book returns again and again to what happens after someone stops
believing something."* · *"Three of the five parts open with someone leaving a room."*

Does **not** belong: *"The central theme is underexplored."* — that is `development`.
The line: naming what the Work is preoccupied with is a description of the text; naming
what its preoccupation still owes is a claim about what it should be.

### 1.2 · MOVEMENT — its shape across its length

*Where does the Work turn? What is its shape?*

Belongs: *"The first third is almost entirely in winter, and after chapter nine the
seasons stop being mentioned."* · *"The voice moves from plural to singular around the
midpoint and stays there."*

Does **not** belong: anything about **the reader's** journey. The DEVELOP lens `arc` asks
*"what journey does this take the reader through"* — a reader-effect claim, and therefore a
standard the Work can fail. Encounter describes the Work's own shape, never its performance.

### 1.3 · RECURRENCE — what comes back

*What images, phrases, figures, questions recur?*

Belongs: *"Water appears at every threshold — the door, the hospital, the last page."* ·
*"The same question is asked in chapters 2, 7 and 14, in almost the same words."*

Does **not** belong: *"repeated without advancing"* — that is `development`, and the word
*advancing* imports a requirement the Work never agreed to. **Recurrence is a fact of the
text; repetition-as-failure is a verdict.** In a memoir or a liturgy, return is the form.

### 1.4 · HEAT — where the writing intensifies

*Where does the language change register, quicken, slow, or press harder?*

Belongs: *"The sentences get very short for two pages here, and nowhere else."* ·
*"This passage is the only place the narrator addresses someone directly."*

Does **not** belong: **anything comparative about quality.** *"This is the strongest
section"* implies the rest is weaker and is a ranking. HEAT is **per-place and
non-comparative**: it says *something happens here*, never *more than there*.

⚠ This is the family most likely to drift, because praise feels safe. It is not. Praise
establishes a scale, and a scale has a bottom.

### 1.5 · OPENNESS — what the Work leaves open

*What does the Work name and not close?*

This is the family the ruling asks for by name: **how "unfinished" is noticed without
becoming "underdeveloped."**

Belongs: *"The Work names a practice in chapter 4 — 'the evening inventory' — and does not
return to it."* · *"The question the prologue asks is never asked again."*

Does **not** belong: *"underdeveloped"* · *"abandoned"* · *"a loose end"* · *"needs
resolution."* Every one carries failure. **The distinction is verifiability:** *the Work
names X and does not return to it* is a fact anyone can check against the text. *X is
underdeveloped* is a judgment about what the Work should have been.

A thread left open may be the most deliberate thing in the book.

### 1.6 · RECOLLECTION — the writer's own return, held as theirs

*What comes back to the writer, now that they are seeing it again?*

This family is **not MAIA's observation at all.** MAIA may invite it and hold it. MAIA may
not author it, and may not restate it as something she noticed.

Belongs: MAIA asks; the writer answers; the answer is attributed to the writer and stays
that way. If it is ever echoed back, it is echoed **as theirs** — *"you said, when you saw
chapter 3 again, that you had forgotten it was written after the funeral."*

Does **not** belong — **the laundering move, and it is the subtlest failure in this
document**:

```text
writer:  "I'd forgotten this was written right after my father died."
MAIA:    "The Work carries grief throughout its first half."     ⛔
```

MAIA has converted the writer's disclosure into her own reading of the book, and it will
come back later wearing MAIA's authority. **Attribution must survive the whole act.** What
the writer supplies remains the writer's; MAIA does not become its narrator.

## 2 — The anti-vocabulary (falsifier vocabulary)

Sufficient to distinguish Encounter from DEVELOP reproducibly. A loose instruction to *"be
gentle and non-judgmental"* is not.

**2.1 · The structural rule — no consequence clause.**
An Encounter observation states what is there **and stops**. A sentence whose second half
says what follows from it has crossed over.

```text
"Water appears at every threshold."                              ✅
"Water appears at every threshold — you could make more of it."  ⛔
```

**2.2 · Forbidden lexical families.**

| Family | Examples |
|---|---|
| deficit nouns | missing · gap · absence · loose end · weakness · problem · issue · opportunity |
| deficit adjectives | underdeveloped · overexplained · unclear · thin · weak · unresolved · abandoned |
| obligation modals *about the Work* | should · needs · ought · must · has to · could be · would benefit |
| comparative quality | strongest · weakest · better · worse · more effective · the best part |
| prescription verbs | fix · improve · strengthen · tighten · cut · expand · resolve · develop |
| invitation-to-edit | consider · why not · have you thought about · one option |
| standard-importing | doesn't work · loses the reader · fails to · departs from · contradicts |

**2.3 · Forbidden shapes, independent of wording.**
Second-person imperative about the text · any ranking or ordering by quality · any count
offered as a verdict (*"only three of seven chapters…"*) · any claim about reader effect ·
any claim about what a later act would do.

**2.4 · The seven DEVELOP lenses are the boundary itself.** Each already asks its evaluative
question (`LENS_MEANING`, ratified 2026-09-04). If an Encounter observation could be filed
under `structure`, `development`, `continuity`, `arc`, `voice`, `coherence` or `reader` as an
*answer to that lens's question*, it belongs to DEVELOP and must not be said here.

## 3 — Lawful silence, and what absence may not become

**3.1 · MAIA may notice nothing worth saying.** A short Work, a fragment, a book whose
patterns are already obvious to its author — silence is a lawful and complete Encounter.
Saying little is not a failed Encounter, and nothing may be manufactured to fill a family.

**3.2 · Absence is never a finding.** *"I notice the Work has no recurring images"* is a
deficit statement in descriptive clothing, and it is exactly the move that turns the six
families into a checklist. **Only what is present may be noticed.**

**3.3 · Not scoring dimensions.** No Encounter must populate every family; no coverage,
count, ratio or completeness is ever computed or shown. A one-family Encounter is whole.

**3.4 · No latent agenda (E-04).** Nothing noticed here is retained as system input to
Restore, Redevelop or Continue. A later authority act begins from its own boundary. Even an
item the member explicitly keeps stays **inert** with respect to intervention until the
member reintroduces it themselves.

**3.5 · Ephemeral by default (E-01).** Encounter creates no product-level memory merely by
occurring. Persistence begins with an explicit member act, on a named thing the member can
see — never the whole Encounter behind one kept sentence.

---

## 4 — Anchoring

Every Encounter observation should be **locatable in the text** — a place the writer can
turn to. An observation with no anchor is an impression about the book rather than a
noticing of it, and impressions are where theories start.

Anchoring is also the practical guard on §1.6: the writer's recollection has no anchor of
MAIA's, because it was never MAIA's to anchor.

## 5 — E2 falsifier inputs (not authorized, recorded)

The reproducibility this vocabulary exists to create should later be falsifiable, at least:
an Encounter emitting a §2.2 lexical family fails · an observation carrying a consequence
clause fails · an absence statement fails · a comparative-quality statement fails · a
recollection restated as MAIA's own observation fails · an Encounter that persists anything
without a member act fails · an Encounter that populates a family with nothing to say fails ·
and silence passes.
