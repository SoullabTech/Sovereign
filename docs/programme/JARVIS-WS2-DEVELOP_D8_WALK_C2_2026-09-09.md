### The synthesis question, carefully

Not all synthesis is a container:

```text
CONTAINER    "Across what we've seen, there are four things: 1... 2... 3... 4..."
             -> an enumeration inside one turn. B returning through synthesis.
LAWFUL TURN  "These may be one thing — the chapter systematizes before
             it grounds."
             -> a single higher-order observation. Just finding 5, and it
                replaces finding 4 on the surface.
```

C can do the second natively. ⛔ **But the writer cannot check it.** The four supporting findings are not present, so the synthesis must be **trusted or re-elicited one at a time.**

> ⭐ **C can state a relationship but cannot show one.** The writer must hold the terms in their own memory to evaluate any claim about them — **which makes the highest-value developmental insight the least verifiable thing C produces.**

## Pressure 4 · ⭐⭐ Retention — the key, and C's answer is *use-or-lose*

*"That one matters. Keep that."*

```text
keeping creates a collection   -> C recreates B through the writer's own acts
keeping is disallowed          -> useful intelligence is disposable
```

⭐ **C has a third answer already implicit in the walk: the writer keeps a finding by ACTING on it.** Edit, ask further, write it into their own words. **Retention is *use*, not storage** — which is exactly E.3's *taking up is an act of the writer's attention*, and it means **nothing is kept that is not used.**

### ⛔ But writing requires deferral, and C has no mechanism for it

*"That one matters — but after I finish this section."* Deferred relevance is ordinary writing behaviour, and in C it has nowhere to go without becoming a list.

**Two readings, both honest, for the founder to weigh:**

```text
DEFECT     C forces immediate action or loss. A writer mid-passage cannot
           hold something for later without the system holding it.

STRENGTH   deferral requires the writer to EXTERNALIZE it themselves — a
           note, a line in their own draft. That note is unambiguously
           theirs, carries no MAIA authority, creates no system obligation,
           and is how writers already work.
```

⚠️ **Either way the burden of retention moves to the writer.** Whether that is respect or abdication is a judgment about the relationship, not about the architecture.
