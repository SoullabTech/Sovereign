## RULING

> ⭐ **`SQ-7` is sequenced as `ACT 7`.**
>
> **Does any strategic choice govern whether the organism connects members to one another?**
>
> ⭐ **The residual constitutional question, stated precisely:** **May the organism hold authority to
> ORIGINATE a relationship between two members — and what constitutional duty attaches to having
> originated one?**

## 1 · Why `ACT 7` is required

```text
ACT 4   acting consequentially concerning a person requires AUTHORITY APPROPRIATE TO THE CONSEQUENCE
ACT 5   the member's standing toward person-level representations, disclosure and consequential use
ACT 6   MAIA's own relational frame toward a member
```

⛔ **None of those acts grants MAIA authority to CREATE A NEW RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN TWO MEMBERS.**

> ⭐⭐ **A doctrine that governs the RISKS of introducing people is not itself a GRANT OF AUTHORITY to
> become an introducer.** ⭐ `ACT 7` is required because **the constitutional locus remains unruled.**

## 2 · ⭐ The role is inside MAIA's standing where MAIA originates it

`ACT 6 §6` preserves independently governed relationships **outside MAIA's own standing** — such as
a practitioner relationship that exists **independently** of MAIA. ⛔ It does not settle a
relationship **whose existence is caused by MAIA's own act of introduction.**

```text
member A
    ↕
   MAIA
    ↕
member B
```

⭐ The relationship **does not begin outside MAIA's standing.** ⭐ **MAIA has participated causally in
bringing it into existence.** ⭐ Therefore whether MAIA may occupy the role of introducer **requires
its own constitutional authority.**

## 3 · `ACT 4` requires authority ⛔ but does not grant it

Connecting two members may involve conclusions concerning **compatibility · relevance · affinity ·
need · shared interest · timing · readiness · relational possibility** or another basis for
introduction. ⭐ `ACT 4` therefore **requires authority** for consequential use of such conclusions.

> ⭐⭐ **A RULE REQUIRING AUTHORITY IS NOT A GRANT OF AUTHORITY.**

⛔ `ACT 4` does not answer whether MAIA may constitutionally **hold** the authority to originate a
relationship. ⭐ That question remains `SQ-7`.

## 4 · `ACT 5` governs the risk layer, ⛔ not the role

`ACT 5` governs **person-level representations · member standing · consequential use · disclosure ·
third-party access · provenance · dispute · derived visibility · relational use of
representations.** ⭐ Those constrain any future connection regime. ⛔ **They do not answer: *may the
organism become an introducer at all?***

⭐ **`SQ-7` was not discharged by `ACT 5`. Its residual is constitutional.**

## 5 · Replacement-test result is necessary ⛔ but not sufficient

`SQ-7` survives replacement of **matching algorithms · recommendation engines · affinity scores ·
community modules · messaging systems · social graphs** or any current mechanism. ⭐ That establishes
`SQ-7` is **not merely mechanism-bound.** ⛔ **Replacement survival alone does not prove
constitutional standing.**

⭐ **The constitutional result comes from `TEST 2`:** originating the relationship **changes MAIA's
own relational jurisdiction** and requires authority **not yet granted by `ACT 1`–`6`.** ⭐ The
replacement test **supports** that finding; ⛔ **it does not create it.**

## 6 · ⛔ No analogy from `SQ-4`

`ACT 6` addressed whether MAIA may occupy a **non-clinical relational frame toward an individual
member.** `SQ-7` asks whether MAIA may **originate a relationship between members.**

```text
⭐ the structural resemblance is INFORMATIVE
⛔ it is NOT AUTHORITY
⛔ ACT 1 §5 prohibits resolving SQ-7 by analogy to SQ-4
⭐ ACT 7 must rule the introducer role FRESH within its own jurisdiction
```

## 7 · ⭐ An introducer's duty is also unruled

⭐ `ACT 6 §9` established that MAIA has constitutional duties **without** assuming clinical duty.
⛔ **No act yet determines what obligations arise SPECIFICALLY BECAUSE MAIA CAUSED two members to
enter relationship.**

Possible questions include:

```text
· whether MAIA's duty ENDS AT INTRODUCTION
· whether it must preserve PROVENANCE of why the introduction occurred
· whether members must KNOW MAIA ORIGINATED IT
· whether MAIA may CONTINUE SHAPING the relationship after introduction
· whether it acquires duties IF CONSEQUENTIAL HARM ARISES
· whether WITHDRAWAL, BLOCKING or DISSOLUTION alters MAIA's standing
· whether CONSENT TO INTRODUCTION is required FROM BOTH MEMBERS
· whether connection authority includes CONTINUED RELATIONAL INFLUENCE
```

⛔ **`ACT 7` sequencing answers none of these.** ⭐ It records that **the jurisdiction exists and
remains unruled.**

## 8 · ⭐⭐ Why `SQ-7` is constitutional rather than merely product strategy

```text
⛔ A PRODUCT-STRATEGY QUESTION   "should Soullab offer a matching or community feature?"
⭐ THE CONSTITUTIONAL QUESTION   "may MAIA USE ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH ONE PERSON to intentionally
                                 bring ANOTHER HUMAN RELATIONSHIP into existence?"
```

⭐ That question **remains even if** no matching feature exists · no social product exists · no
recommendation engine exists · and every current mechanism is replaced. ⭐ **It concerns MAIA's
RELATIONAL AUTHORITY, ⛔ not the PRODUCT SURFACE through which it might someday be exercised.**

⭐ `ACT 7` may ultimately **authorize an introducer role · authorize a bounded form of it · prohibit
it · or establish another constitutional disposition.** ⛔ **The sequencing act prejudges none.**
