## 1. The three objects

```
READING   what MAIA established        immutable
FIELD     what becomes thinkable /
          understandable in relationship  living · decomposable
WORK      what the author makes        authoritative
```

## 2. Q-B — ratified (carried)

> The field may remain present without MAIA remaining active. MAIA's
> developmental attention is **commissioned, not ambient**. Persistent context
> does not constitute persistent invitation.

## 3. Q-A — ratified

> **Relation does not require merger. What becomes knowable between writer and
> MAIA must remain decomposable according to the authority by which each part
> became knowable.**

⛔ **There is no lawful object called a "joint conclusion" whose authority is
writer+MAIA.** That would invent the third author the whole design exists to
avoid.

### The lawful shape

The field object is a **Developmental Thread** — a relational container, **not
a conclusion, and not a speaker.**

```
DEVELOPMENTAL THREAD
  ├─ frozen observations
  ├─ propositions
  ├─ relations among propositions
  ├─ evidence
  ├─ writer standing
  ├─ unresolved questions
  └─ Work actions taken by the writer
```

A thread can be jointly inhabited. Its propositions are **not** jointly
authored merely because they coexist there.

### Q-A1 — propositions remain atomic by authority

```
P1  "The recurrence is intentional."
    kind       author_intent
    source     author disclosure
    authority  author

P2  "The second appearance doesn't deepen the first."
    kind       developmental_inference
    source     textual evidence + MAIA reasoning + dialogue
    authority  developmental judgment
    status     contestable
```

They may belong to one thread, illuminate one another, and lead to one
decision. ⛔ They do not acquire one provenance.

### Q-A2 — assent does not transfer authority

MAIA proposes P2; the writer says *"Yes. That's exactly it."*

```
proposition source   developmental inference   ← unchanged
writer relation      endorsed / accepted-for-working
```

⛔ Assent does not convert a developmental inference into an author
disclosure. Symmetrically, MAIA understanding *"I intended this repetition"*
does not convert it into a MAIA-established fact. **Authority and assent are
separate axes.**

### Q-A3 — a relation may be stored without becoming a speaker

Lawful relations: `P1 bears on P2` · `P2 explains O7` · `O7 relates to O19` ·
`P2 remains unresolved` · `P2 accepted as working hypothesis`.

⛔ If a relation makes an additional substantive claim, that claim becomes
**another proposition with its own authority** — it may not hide inside an
edge. This is what prevents *writer claim + MAIA claim → mysterious "field
truth."*

### Q-A4 — "jointly reached" is relational status, not joint authorship

Something genuine does emerge between writer and MAIA. Its lawful
representation is *the participants have converged around a proposition for
developmental purposes* — never *a third speaker authored it.* A proposition
may be **developmentally shared · writer-endorsed · a working understanding**
while still preserving who could actually establish each underlying claim.
Relational richness without epistemic laundering.

### Q-A5 — the reader's four non-conclusions are a handoff map

> **The reader contract constrains what enters the field from the reading; it
> does not exhaust what may emerge through the field.**

```
author intent           → the author can establish
editorial consequence   → developmental judgment can PROPOSE
                          → the writer alone DECIDES whether the Work changes
whole-work pattern      → may become supportable through synthesis across
                          sufficient evidence
reader effect           → requires actual reader evidence;
                          ⛔ writer + MAIA cannot manufacture it
```

⚠️ Note the distinction that an earlier phrasing in session flattened: on
editorial consequence the **decision** is the writer's; the **judgment** is the
third territory. Both halves are load-bearing.

### Q-A6 — the field never acquires Work authority

```
observation → dialogue → developmental proposition → writer endorses
            → writer decides → writer edits
```

⛔ No field object — observation, proposition, relation, standing, or thread —
constitutes manuscript-write authority. The three-object separation in §1 holds
unconditionally.

## 4. Ontology and laws, as recorded

```
ONTOLOGY
Developmental Thread    relational container · NO VOICE
Field Proposition       atomic claim · explicit source/authority
Field Relation          connection among propositions/observations
Writer Standing         writer's relation to an observation/proposition
Work Act                separately authorized member act

LAWS
- no "joint conclusion" authority
- relation does not require merger
- assent does not transfer provenance
- shared understanding ≠ joint authorship
- reader non-conclusions are a handoff map, not an exhaustive ontology
- the field carries no manuscript-write authority
```

## 5. Relation to the frozen reader

The reader's four non-conclusions are pinned by the reader contract and are
**upstream** of this constitution: they say which questions the frozen reading
deliberately hands forward. Nothing here alters the reader, its version, or its
prompt-contract hash. ⛔ A change to what the field may contain is not a reason
to change what the reading may conclude.
