**Constraint**: room identity, **not UI design.** No routes, schema, components, implementation. No separate editors, drafts, revision systems or authorship regimes.

> **The finding D3 must not lose**: *the room currently has one entrance and it belongs to MAIA.* The new architecture reverses this. **The writer enters the process; MAIA becomes available within it.**

---

## 1 · Writing — what the writer is entering

**Writing is where the Work is under your hands.** Attention is **in** the text.

The writer entering Writing is entering *the making*. What they came for is the prose at the place they are working. Composition is continuous, and anything that interrupts the line is a cost paid against the reason they came.

## 2 · Develop — what the writer is entering

**Develop is where the Work is in front of you.** Attention is **on** the text.

The writer entering Develop is entering *the standing-back* — a relationship to a Work they have already made. ⛔ **They are not entering MAIA's findings.** The present room says otherwise in its own header (*"What MAIA noticed when she read this work"*), which is the one-entrance defect stated as a purpose.

```text
WRITING   the Work under your hands     attention IN the text
DEVELOP   the Work in front of you      attention ON the text
```

Both are process environments around one living Work. **The pair is a posture, not a partition.**

## 3 · The primary field in each

```text
WRITING   the prose at the working locus. Nothing else.
          Everything about the text is summonable, never resident.

DEVELOP   the Work, AND the writer's live question.
          Readings are available. They are not the field.
```

⭐ **The concrete defect this exposes.** D1: the room *"calls `fetchWriteState` for a section list; it renders no prose."* **The Work is absent from the room whose entire purpose is a relationship to the Work.** You cannot stand in front of something that is not there. Today the primary field in Develop is MAIA's reading — the largest area of the screen — and the manuscript is a list of section names.

### The identity test — falsifiable, both directions

```text
Remove every MAIA reading. Is Develop still a place?
  today   NO — the room is empty
  target  YES — your Work and your question are still there

Remove MAIA entirely. Is Writing still a writing room?
  today   YES
  target  YES  (must not regress)
```

**Develop passes this test when a writer with no readings at all still has somewhere to work.**

## 4 · How the Work stays present and addressable

One canonical Working Draft · one section identity · one revision lineage · one authorship authority (D1: already lawful, structurally defended).

```text
WRITING   present as material — editable at the cursor
DEVELOP   present as object — readable, navigable to exact locus,
          and editable IN PLACE when the writer moves to authored action,
          WITHOUT LEAVING DEVELOP
```

That last clause is D2 §E.2 and is the load-bearing requirement: **the stance changes, the thread does not.**

## 5 · Where intention and felt difficulty live

At the **head** of the process: what the writer is working on, in their words.

⚠️ **It must be able to be empty.** Entry 2 (felt difficulty, nothing nameable yet) is a legitimate way to arrive, so this is *a blank that is allowed to stay blank* — never a required field, never a gate on entry. Demanding articulation before admission would exclude the writer who came precisely because they cannot yet say what is wrong.

> Bears on **F-D2-2**. Proposed, not ratified: the unnamed question is a valid state of the field, and helping name it is a movement the writer may request — not a precondition MAIA supplies.

## 6 · How readings appear without owning Develop

**Available, not resident.** A reading is something the writer can open — never the room's identity.

Today: readings occupy the primary field, the header names them as the room's purpose, and `MAIA · Conversations / Discover / Insights / Suggestions` makes MAIA four *destinations in a rail*. **Nobody sitting beside you is a menu item.**

Target: readings sit **beside** the Work rather than in place of it, and the room does not open onto them.

## 7 · How writing assistance appears without owning Writing

Reachable **from inside composition** without displacing the writer's line — at the writer's call, in the margin of the act. ⛔ **Never a stream.** Unrequested suggestion during composition is the single most ownership-costly form of assistance in the registered evidence (**B2**: drafting assistance reduces ownership more than any other stage).

## 8 · Movement between stances

The thread that must survive the move: **the live question · the locus · the conversation, if any · what the writer already tried.**

⚠️ **Rooms cost context; postures do not.** If Writing and Develop are two destinations, every movement between them is a departure, and the developmental thread is exactly what departures drop. D2 §E.2 requires movement into authored action and back **without ending the thread** — which is a claim about continuity, not about tabs.

## 9 · ⛔ FIRST REQUIRED FOUNDER RULING — D3 STOPS HERE

**F-D2-1 · Is stance declared or inferred?** D2 left this open. D3 cannot proceed past it, because room identity depends on the answer and the current product has already answered it implicitly.

The live nav is `WRITE · DEVELOP · EXPLORE · REVIEW · PUBLISH`. **Tabs declare stance by navigation.** So today stance is *declared*, and declared stance means:

```text
the writer must know which stance they are in BEFORE they act
```

But D2 §A.1 defines stance by **the writer's relationship to their own certainty** — which they often discover *during* the act, not before it. The vignette *"I find the issue and start rewriting at once"* is precisely a case where the stance was Develop and the act was composition, and no navigation decision preceded it.

```text
OPTION A — DECLARED
  the writer chooses the stance; the room follows
  honest, legible, no inference about inner state
  cost: the writer must classify themselves before working,
        and the misclassified case is the most common real one

OPTION B — INFERRED
  the room follows what the writer is doing
  cost: MAIA deciding what stance the writer is in — an inference
        about an inner state, against Interface Humility

OPTION C — NEITHER: stance is not a mode at all
  one place, in which the Work is always present and both the
  hands and the standing-back are continuously available
  cost: the largest departure from the shipped product,
        and EXPLORE / REVIEW / PUBLISH need re-examining too
```

⛔ **Not chosen here.** This is a founder ruling: it decides whether Writing and Develop are rooms, modes, or postures — and §§1–8 above describe an identity that **Option C fits most naturally and Option A fits least**, which is a reason to rule deliberately rather than by inheritance from the existing nav.

**Questions 9 and 10 of the D3 brief** — how correction help becomes available, and how a room permits quiet without manufacturing a next task — **are not answered here**, because both are properties of a room whose modal identity is unresolved. *(Note for whichever is ruled: `MAIA · Suggestions` in the rail is a backlog by name, and a backlog manufactures a next task by existing.)*

## 10 · Write before-state — capture, 2026-09-09 · **evidence bearing on §9, not a resolution of it**

Founder-supplied capture of the live **Write** room, *Elemental Alchemy* Ch. 4, §4 *The Story of the Four Yogis* selected.

### 10.1 · The asymmetry is confirmed visually

**Write renders the prose in the primary field.** Full text, readable, the Work under the writer's hands — exactly what §3 requires and exactly what Develop does not have. D1's *"renders no prose"* is not a subtlety of the code; **the two rooms are the same shell with opposite centres.**

```text
WRITE     centre = the manuscript text        Work PRESENT
DEVELOP   centre = MAIA's reading             Work = a list of section names
```

### 10.2 · ⭐ The rail is identical in both — the product already half-implements Option C

`Work Space` (Home · Manuscript · Materials · Structure · Notes · Versions · Goals), `MAIA` (Conversations · Discover · Insights · Suggestions), `Tools` (Find/Replace · Statistics · Timeline · Word Web · Export) — **byte-for-byte the same rail in Write and in Develop.**

So Writing and Develop are **not two environments.** They are one environment with a swapped middle column, and the tab bar asserts a separation the shell does not implement.

> **This bears directly on §9.** The shipped product already has **Option C's substrate** — one place, one shell, one Work — **under Option A's navigation.** The declared-stance answer was inherited from a tab bar, not designed. That does not decide the ruling, but it means Option C is *less* of a departure than §9 estimated: the shell is already one room.

### 10.3 · MAIA is present in Write and structurally unavailable

The MAIA column reads:

> *"MAIA can speak with you about a declared Work. Declare one in 'This work' and Conversations opens."*
> *"IN RELATION TO — No work is declared for what is on the table, so there is no Work context to carry."*

⚠️ **The writer is looking at Chapter 4 of *Elemental Alchemy*. The manuscript is on screen, the section is selected, and MAIA reports no Work context.** Availability is gated on a *declaration* that duplicates what the room already displays.

Against the north star — *a developmental editor sitting next to you* — this is an editor who is in the room, facing the same page, and cannot discuss it until the writer formally announces which book they are writing.

### 10.4 · Both rooms promise a restraint neither needs to make

```text
WRITE     "MAIA reads; she does not hold your material.
           Nothing in this column is written by her into your manuscript."
DEVELOP   "Nothing changes unless you change it."
```

Per D1, **no write path exists from either column.** The promise is honest and it is also unfalsifiable: *restraint is indistinguishable from absence of reach.* When the reach is built, these sentences must be re-earned rather than retained — a promise that cost nothing to keep has not yet been kept.

### 10.5 · The identity test, re-run against real evidence

```text
Remove MAIA entirely. Is Writing still a writing room?     YES
```

⚠️ **But it passes trivially, and the reason is the finding.** Write is already a writing room without MAIA *because MAIA is gated out of it.* The test is satisfied by absence rather than by proportion — which meets §3's floor and **fails the north star at the same time.** A room MAIA cannot participate in is not a room she is proportionate in.

⛔ **§9 remains open. This capture is evidence, not a ruling.**
