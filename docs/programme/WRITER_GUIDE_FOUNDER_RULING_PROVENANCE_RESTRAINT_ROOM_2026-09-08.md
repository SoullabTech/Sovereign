# FOUNDER RULING — PROVENANCE, RESTRAINT, AND THE OPEN CREATIVE ROOM

**Founder act, 2026-09-08.** Governs `JARVIS-WRITER-GUIDE-APPRENTICESHIP-01` and the
Writer's Studio R&D A3 problem. **No generation implementation is authorized by this ruling.**

---

## 1. North star — ADOPTED

> **Not more MAIA. More them.**
>
> Not a member assessment and not a numerical optimization objective. Where measurable it is
> evidence about **the composition and provenance of the Work** — never the worth, skill,
> progress, or creativity of the writer.
>
> **A lower proportion of MAIA-authored language is never by itself evidence of a better
> system.** The system must still accompany, teach, challenge, clarify, research, reflect and
> support when those acts serve the writer. **The ratio is evidence, never a target.**

## 2. Authorship provenance precedes generation — RULED

> **No new Writer Guide capability may introduce MAIA-authored prose into a Work until
> prose-level authorship provenance can make that act legible.**
>
> The architecture must distinguish at minimum:
> - member-authored language
> - MAIA-authored language
> - member revision of MAIA-authored language **where provenance remains materially relevant**
> - MAIA transformation of member-authored language where applicable
>
> ⛔ **Do not infer provenance retrospectively from style or model behaviour. It must arise
> from the act itself.**
>
> Required not merely for safety but **to make WG-LAW-1 and its falsifiers observable.**

## 3. Restraint is structural, not performed — RATIFIED

> **MAIA should not narrate her restraint in moments that belong to the writer.**
>
> ⛔ No behaviours such as *"I could write this for you, but I want you to find your own
> words"* when architecture can simply return the creative act to the writer. **A system that
> announces its restraint recenters MAIA and makes the writer witness MAIA's virtue.**
>
> The boundary belongs in **permissions · commissioning grammar · route capability · stance
> contracts · provenance · module structure** — **not in model self-presentation.**
>
> **MAIA's restraint should ordinarily be experienced as room.**

## 4. Participatory agency — BOUNDED

> Existing machinery for recurrence, `initiatedBy` and related developmental phenomena is
> extended **only within its ratified contract.**
>
> ⛔ **Do not infer that MAIA may autonomously initiate a developmental intervention merely
> because the system can recognize recurrence.** The existing refusal to make the writer a
> respondent to MAIA's agenda remains governing. Any extension in which MAIA may actively
> bring something forward belongs to the **WS2-07 v2 founder act.**

## 5. The literary jury — BOUNDED

> The jury judges **the Studio, never the member.** The question is: *would this design protect
> the conditions under which an unmistakably individual voice could emerge?* — never *does this
> member's writing meet the standard of these writers?*
>
> **A design ethic, not a literary-quality rubric. Any implementation encouraging stylistic
> convergence toward admired writers fails the ethic.**

## 6. A3 is load-bearing — RULED

> Writer's Studio must provide an **inhabitable creative condition** in which: *there is a Work
> here, but its form is not yet known.* **That condition must be a positive capability, not
> merely a nullable database field.**
>
> The member must be able to — **without first declaring the Work a manuscript** — remain with
> a Work before genre or form is known · gather material · explore · write fragments ·
> encounter Reflections · converse with Writer Guide · notice emerging patterns · experiment ·
> learn craft where relevant · allow possible forms to appear.
>
> Provisionally **the Open Creative Room**, **for design purposes only. ⛔ Not ratified as
> product naming.** A3 must determine what this actually is within existing Studio
> architecture.

## 7. Sequencing — RULED

> **A1** (authorship / provenance boundary) and **A3** (form-undecided creative room) proceed
> **in parallel**. Then **A2** (low door) can meet *"I'm not really a writer, but there is
> something I want to say"* **without forcing either MAIA-generated language or a premature
> form declaration.** The three together establish the foundation for Creative Apprenticeship.

## 8. Consequences recorded *(this session; not founder-authored)*

### ⭐ C-1 — Clause 3 makes A3 a dependency of the AUTHORSHIP LAW, not only of breadth.

*"MAIA's restraint should ordinarily be experienced as room."* Restraint may not be narrated
(clause 3) and must therefore be **encountered somewhere**. Today the Studio has no such
somewhere: the only inhabitable creative condition is a manuscript (A3). So a member who is
not yet writing a book meets MAIA's restraint as **absence** — or MAIA narrates it, which
clause 3 forbids.

> **The Open Creative Room is where restraint becomes room.** Without it, structural restraint
> has no medium and the only remaining way to express it is the performance clause 3 rules out.

A3 was scoped as apprenticeship breadth (poetry, documentary, &c.), then raised to
constitutional prerequisite. **C-1 is the third and strongest reading: A3 is load-bearing for
WG-LAW-1 itself.** A1 and A3 are parallel in sequence and coupled in substance — which is
exactly why clause 7 runs them together rather than in series.

### ✅ C-2 — Clause 3 ratifies the method the Studio already uses.

*"Permissions · route capability · module structure — not model self-presentation"* is the
built practice: the Ask route cannot write the Work **as a property of its module graph**,
asserted by `askRuntimeCannotWrite.test.ts`, rather than as an instruction the model is asked
to honour. Clause 3 names the existing discipline and extends it to restraint. **Nothing new is
required to comply; something existing must not be abandoned.**

### 🔴 WG-Q1 — The revision threshold is open, and both failure modes are real.

Clause 2's third distinction — *member revision of MAIA-authored language **where provenance
remains materially relevant*** — leaves the threshold deliberately unfixed. Two opposite
failures:

- **Silent laundering.** Provenance decays through incremental edits until MAIA-authored prose
  reads as member-authored. WG-F6 becomes unfalsifiable; the north star reports "more them"
  falsely.
- **Permanent stain.** A passage the member has wholly rewritten is still marked MAIA's. The
  member's actual authorship is denied by the record, and the ratio understates them — which,
  under clause 1, would be read as system virtue.

**Owned by A1**, to be answered before any generative path. ⛔ Clause 2 forbids resolving it by
inference from style or model behaviour, so the answer must come from **acts** — which means
the threshold is a rule about *edit provenance*, not about *text similarity*.

### ⚠️ C-3 — Clause 2 constrains WHEN provenance may be built, not only that it must be.

*"Do not infer provenance retrospectively"* plus *"it must arise from the act itself"* means the
substrate must exist **before** the acts it records. Provenance built after a generative path
ships can only ever attest to prose whose origin was already ambiguous — the WG-A1-01 ordering,
now ruled rather than recommended.

**One consequence worth stating**: today's corpus is all member-authored by construction, and
that is a **fact with an expiry date**. It is the only corpus against which the substrate can
be proven with the answer known in advance. That opportunity closes permanently the first time
MAIA writes a word into a Work.
