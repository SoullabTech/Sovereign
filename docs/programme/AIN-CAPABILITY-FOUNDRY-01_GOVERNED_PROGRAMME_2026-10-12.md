### D. Local inference, model exploration and data management

The Mac Studio observed in this work has 48 GiB physical memory, macOS/Apple silicon and locally available GPT-OSS 20B, Qwen coder and other Ollama models. ## 6. Research fields to hold in active comparison

Keep an explicitly plural research map rather than collapsing everything into a single formal theory:

- Relational reasoning and cognitive science: analogy, structure mapping, compositionality, semantic generalization.
- Causal inference and scientific methodology: interventions, counterfactuals, identifiability, preregistration, negative controls and replication.
- Mathematics: graph theory, manifolds, information geometry, dynamical systems, topology, subspace similarity and invariance.
- Computational neuroscience and mechanistic interpretability: neural representations, trajectory/circuit methods, computational modeling; no overclaim about actual human neural states.
- Developmental psychology, attachment and narrative identity: possible human expressions, correction/repair and consented continuity, with appropriate professional limits.
- Enactive cognition, phenomenology, participatory sense-making and contemplative traditions: theoretical/dialogical perspectives, clearly distinguished from measured computational findings.
- Depth psychology and symbolic scholarship: interpretive traditions alongside, not validated by, mechanistic probes.
- HCI, accessibility and pedagogy: ordinary-first language, thoughtful interfaces, ADHD-compatible disclosure, human oversight and safe failure.
- Privacy/security: least privilege, model supply chain, data minimization, signed custody, attack surfaces, rollback.
- Professional and scientific ethics: practitioner confidentiality, research consent, proportional authority, auditability and non-extraction.

The research lab may compare these traditions respectfully. It must never turn a human ontological or spiritual claim into a supposed result of an embeddings metric.
