## Governing question

> Of everything presently injected into MAIA's prompt, what genuinely requires token-resident cognition every turn, and what can instead operate structurally without occupying the cognitive aperture?

This lane is independent of `TEMPORAL-MEMORY-CUT1-TRACEABILITY-01`, `JARVIS-MEMORY-ORGANISM-PASS1-DIVINATION-01`, and `JARVIS-MAIA-FREE-SYNTHESIS-01`.

Prior records may be cited as observations only. They do not extend this lane's authority.

## Scope

Trace current canonical source and inventory all prompt-resident material and all model-cognition re-entry paths, including:
- registered `ADDENDA_SPECS`;
- base/system/spec material interpolated into CORE;
- mode-specific material including Talk;
- scaffolding/instruction blocks;
- history aperture and recovery/context material;
- validators or regeneration paths that cause additional model cognition;
- material duplication and substantial semantic overlap.

## Per-object record

For every prompt-resident object, record:
1. exact source/location;
2. injection condition;
3. standing vs conditional presence;
4. approximate character/token burden where determinable;
5. law/behavior preserved;
6. whether model cognition is genuinely required;
7. observable failure if absent from prompt cognition;
8. dependencies and ordering interactions;
9. duplicate or overlapping obligations.

## Classification

Use only evidence-supported classes:
- `cognition-required`
- `conditional cognition`
- `structural candidate`
- `validator/post-generation`
- `duplicative/overlapping`
- `uncertain`

A structural candidate is a census finding, never removal authority.

## Constitutional boundary

This lane authorizes no prompt edits, addenda removal/consolidation, aperture widening, L1/A6 changes, memory changes, gestalt generation, conversation summarization, MAIA-authored impression injection, implementation experiments, or production acts.

In particular, the lane must not solve the gestalt problem.

Preserve the hazard:

> A derived conversational gestalt would be MAIA-authored interpretation unless and until a separate law establishes its provenance, corrigibility, authority, and relationship to member self-report.

R7b remains load-bearing: member statement may stand as present self-report; MAIA interpretation does not silently become background fact.

## Deliverable and stop

Produce one source-grounded census record containing:
- total standing prompt burden;
- full classification table;
- significant duplication and interaction findings;
- smallest source-supported set genuinely requiring every-turn cognition;
- unresolved questions and falsifiers.

Then STOP.

Sequence: `census → architectural finding → governance of gestalt → only then implementation`.
