## 1 · What survives a reload

```text
SURVIVES
  Work identity                 YES
  Focus Set membership          YES
  active member                 YES
  Focus label / intent          YES
  revision proposal state       YES — ⭐ if explicitly STAGED
  conversation / thread         YES

DOES NOT
  panel open/closed geometry    NO
  temporary selection           NO
  hover / transient UI          NO
```

⭐ **The reason it is coherent rather than a compromise:** if MAIA and the writer were working on
*"Campfire recurrence,"* that is **meaningful creative state**. Whether the MAIA panel happened to be
open at 412px is not.

⚠️ **This AMENDS prototype §20.2** (*"encounter continuity only; persistence across a fresh arrival
is NOT authorized"*) ⭐ **without breaking the §11 arrival ruling it came from** — §11 resets
*inherited tool state* and preserves *standing authority*, and a live Focus Set with its conversation
is the writer's own act still in force, not a panel someone left open.

## 2 · ⭐ THE FOUR DURABILITY CLASSES

```text
Focus Set      DURABLE working state
selection      TRANSIENT gesture
proposal       DURABLE once STAGED
conversation   DURABLE relationship context
```

⭐ **`proposal: durable once staged` is the load-bearing qualifier.** A proposal in `discussing`
does not survive arrival; **staging is the act that makes it durable.** *The writer has not yet asked
for anything to be kept while they are still arguing about it.*

## 4 · ⭐⭐ CANON-WORTHY — the phrase to keep

> **MEMBERSHIP ≠ EDIT TARGET.**

⭐ *"That one distinction prevents an enormous amount of ambiguity once MAIA starts working across an
entire book."*

## 5 · The nine questions a proposal must answer

```text
what Work?
which revision?
which Focus members?
which member was the edit target?
what did MAIA propose?
what did the writer change in the proposal?
what did the writer approve?
what exact manuscript mutation resulted?
what superseded it later?
```

⛔ **Not bureaucracy.** ⭐ It is what lets MAIA later say *"we changed this because you wanted the
campfire to function as a ritual threshold"* **instead of merely remembering that a sentence
changed.**

### 5.1 ⚠️ WHAT THIS LIST DOES NOT SAY — and the silence is informative

**There is no question of the form "what did the writer REJECT?"** The list asks what MAIA proposed,
what the writer *changed*, what the writer *approved* — ⭐ **all of it the ancestry of the line that
was KEPT.**

⭐ **That is consistent with the candidate shape recorded in the abundance ruling §2.2** — *keep the
reasoning that led to what was kept; do not keep an enumeration of what was refused* — and with
*disagreement must be cheap*.

⛔ **But the founder still lists §5.3 as owed in §6 below, so this is NOT a ruling on refused
proposals.** ⚠️ **A silence is evidence, not a decision, and recording it as one would be the
inference this programme keeps refusing.** *The requirement on a proposal and the disposition of
refusals are two questions, and only the first is answered.*
