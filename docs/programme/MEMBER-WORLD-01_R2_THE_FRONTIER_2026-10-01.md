# MEMBER-WORLD-01 · R2 The Frontier · 2026-10-01

## Founder direction

Member World should not only help people orient across what already exists.

It should also help members encounter the creative frontier of AI:

- inspire;
- instruct;
- inform;
- enchant;
- support real making;
- open questions of consciousness, sovereignty, ethics and relationship;
- allow deeper work with Kelly to emerge voluntarily rather than through a sales funnel.

The economic possibility is legitimate but secondary to the educational and creative purpose.

## R2 product expression

The member World now contains **The Frontier** between Living Work and the complete House field map.

The Frontier frames AI as:

> **a medium for imagination, inquiry and creation**
## Admitted paths

Every actionable path in R2 points at an already-existing Soullab place:

1. **Imagine** → Vision Studio
2. **Make** → Writer’s Studio
3. **Understand** → Library
4. **See the larger whole** → Living Field
5. **Consciousness & AI** → Wisdom
6. **Build with Kelly** → local invitation anchor only

R2 deliberately does not invent:

- a booking route;
- a consultation API;
- a checkout flow;
- a paid tier gate;
- a member-to-founder message channel;
- a new AI model or agent surface.

Build with Kelly says only that a direct way to bring an idea to Kelly is being designed.
## Member / founder membrane

The Frontier is available inside the general member World.

It does **not** expose:

- docs/programme;
- JARVIS Work Units;
- deployment state;
- founder dockets;
- repository evidence;
- cross-member context.

The shared capability is creative orientation, not founder operational visibility.

## From the edges

R2 introduces **From the edges** as an experiential promise, not yet a feed implementation.

It names a future living stream of experiments, questions, failures and discoveries from Kelly and, later, consenting community creators.

No community publishing or contribution mechanism is created in R2.
