## The constraint that retired corpus 01, stated as a rule

⛔ **SEL-0B must not reuse previously discussed developmental observations** — nor observations
equivalent to them. Independence is a property of the founder, not of a row id: re-reading the same
material under a new identity produces a corpus that *looks* clean and is not.

Practically, this means the corpus-construction rule has to answer, before anyone looks:

```text
which Work, or which span of a Work, the founder has NOT already met MAIA inside
how prior exposure is established rather than assumed — the ask_threads / ask_turns
  record is the evidence, and it is checkable
what happens if exposure is discovered later — the answer must be decided in advance,
  because deciding it afterwards is how a caveated result gets argued into a finding
```

⭐ **Freeze the rule before exposure, not the corpus before exposure.** Corpus 01's failure was not
that the wrong items were frozen; it was that nobody asked whether the founder had already been in
the room with them. That question belongs in the construction rule.
