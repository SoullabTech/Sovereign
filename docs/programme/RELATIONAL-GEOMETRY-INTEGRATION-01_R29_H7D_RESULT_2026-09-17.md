## Question

Does a compact whole-field Gestalt preserve later-validated process structure better than pairwise or temporal geometry specifically when meaning belongs to the exchange as a whole?

The corpus, cutoffs, event classes, later member-authored outcomes, feature definitions, and pass criterion were frozen in R28 before any representation generation.

## Conditions

- **B_PAIRWISE** — pairwise relation field only.
- **D_TEMPORAL** — multidimensional relation geometry with temporal operators.
- **E_GESTALT** — compact whole-field projection with ACTIVE PROCESS, FIELD QUALITY, ESTABLISHED, TENSIONS/POLARITIES, MOVEMENT, and OPEN EDGE.

All conditions had a 120-word cap. A blind decoder saw one representation plus later-validated feature descriptions, never later raw turns.

## Interpretation

H7d falsifies the stronger proposition that a fixed whole-field Gestalt should become a third durable representation class alongside pairwise and temporal geometry.

The result does **not** falsify whole-field perception. In W1, Gestalt preserved one more later-validated feature than either baseline. But W2 shows the opposite failure: organizing the field globally displaced a specific local relation that both pairwise and temporal representations preserved. W3 shows that when the field was strongly coherent, simpler relational forms already retained the same downstream-relevant structure.

The cost signal is substantial: Gestalt used about **114 words** per whole-field event versus roughly **45–49 words** for pairwise/temporal while preserving the same 7/9 features.

## Architectural consequence

The evidence now supports the earlier constitutional framing more strongly:

> **Gestalt should remain a disposable, task-conditioned projection over admitted relational evidence—not a third memory or standing-bearing representation layer.**

Pairwise relations preserve local structure efficiently. Temporal geometry earns itself when ordered change matters. A Gestalt may still be useful at the moment of synthesis, but H7d does not support persisting or routinely precomputing it as an independent representation.

A fixed Gestalt can spend scarce cognitive budget restating the whole and thereby omit the exact relation needed later.

## Successor hypothesis

The next ecological question should not be "store a better Gestalt." It should test **query-conditioned projection**:

```text
admitted relational field
        ↓
current-turn demand
        ↓
small disposable projection
        ↓
free synthesis
```

A successor H7e must use a new held-out corpus. It should compare fixed representation against a turn-conditioned projection assembled from the same pairwise/temporal substrate, with no new standing and no writeback.

H7d authorizes no live cognition exposure or production integration.
