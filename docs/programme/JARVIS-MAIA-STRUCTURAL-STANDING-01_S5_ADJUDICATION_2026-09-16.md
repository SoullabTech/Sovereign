## Question adjudicated

Can MAIA preserve generative freedom while the system—not the model—owns the epistemic standing of evidence, synthesis and correction?

## Source-grounded finding

Within the bounded fixtures tested here: **yes, structurally**.

This is not a claim that every response becomes accurate or elegant. The model still generates hypotheses that may be wrong, overdrawn or stylistically awkward. The difference is that those hypotheses no longer need to masquerade as settled member truth in order to participate in cognition.

## What the evidence supports

### 1. Standing should not be ordinary prompt prose

CanonicalTurn already carries authorship / participation / authority structurally, but its current cognition renderer flattens admitted participants to `p.text`. That identifies a real seam where substrate standing can disappear before synthesis.

The R&D result does **not** support simply prepending `[member]` / `[infer]` labels and trusting the model. S1 instead removes standing fields from the model's output vocabulary.

### 2. Evidence and synthesis can have different composition rights

Evidence references are resolved deterministically to source material and source standing.

Novel synthesis remains available, but its standing is fixed by the envelope as MAIA/provisional. Supporting member evidence does not promote the synthesis.

### 3. Adoption is a separate act

The envelope has no adoption field. A model response cannot confirm itself into member standing. Any future promotion requires a distinct member-authored act outside cognition.

### 4. Correction can reorganize without erasure

Append-only claim succession lets an earlier member statement remain historically true while a later member statement becomes present standing. Derived MAIA inferences inherit the stale-source constraint through evidence lineage.

This satisfies the Free Synthesis requirement that surprise can reorganize the whole without rewriting the path by which the earlier Gestalt arose.

### 5. Authority protection need not eliminate intelligence

Across four fixed-seed Silver Cedar runs, the envelope still allowed forward synthesis and an open practical question. Across four correction runs, it allowed genuine reorganization rather than quotation-only reflection.
