## Research question

Can the hard Free Synthesis laws be represented with a smaller, reversible typed structure **before** adopting a complex graph/hypergraph/sheaf architecture?

ACT 1 deliberately chooses the smallest candidate that can fail:

```text
PRIMARY EVIDENCE
      ↓
PROVISIONAL OBSERVATION
      ↓
TYPED RELATION
      ↓
CONFIGURATION
      ↓
DERIVED GESTALT PROJECTION
```

A multi-node relation is permitted when the relation itself belongs to a configuration; no higher-order mathematical object is adopted merely because one can be constructed.

## Laws encoded in the prototype

1. Primary evidence is append-only and carries actor/source identity.
2. Observation, relation, configuration and Gestalt remain MAIA-derived objects.
3. Every derived Gestalt must have a transitive descent to primary evidence.
4. A relation may not cite a prior Gestalt as truth; a prior MAIA interpretation must re-enter only as evidence that MAIA previously made that interpretation.
5. Member correction/withdrawal is a new primary act that changes current standing without deleting the prior historical object.
6. Member adoption would likewise be a new primary act; it would not rewrite the provenance of the MAIA synthesis being adopted.
7. Reorganization creates a new configuration/projection and leaves the earlier one historically intact.
8. Distant recurrence is typed as `possible_recurrence`, not symbolic equivalence.
9. A configuration can preserve simultaneous contradictory signals without selecting one as privileged truth.

## ACT 1 falsifiers represented

- `FS-F2 · DIFFERENCE-WITHOUT-COLLAPSE` — four simultaneous member signals remain represented in one configuration.
- `FS-F5 · SOURCE-STANDING` — collaborator evidence cannot be asserted as member-authored evidence.
- `FS-F6 · GESTALT-REVERSAL` / `FS-G11` — later member correction can reorganize the current center while preserving the historical prior Gestalt.
- `FS-F7 · DISTANT-MOTIF-WITHOUT-RESTART` — a distant recurrence can be represented provisionally with descent to both source moments.
- `FS-G0 · GESTALT-IS-DERIVED` — a relation that attempts to use a prior Gestalt as truth is refused.

## What ACT 1 does not prove

- that this representation improves model responses;
- that relational geometry outperforms a compact narrative;
- that all meaningful human relations fit these relation types;
- that a graph is sufficient for every higher-order configuration;
- that a current Gestalt can be generated reliably from raw conversation;
- that this representation reduces tokens or latency;
- that any production memory or prompt should change.

Those are later A4/A5 questions.

## Complexity ruling

The simple typed representation already supports the first constitutional requirements: provenance, differentiation, correction, temporal reorganization, recurrence uncertainty and full evidence descent. More complex mathematics earns entry only if a later falsifier demonstrates a relation/configuration that this representation cannot express without distortion.
