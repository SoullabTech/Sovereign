## The premise

> What is needed is not better code. It is **calibration**: what does genuinely useful, human,
> non-technical, non-editorial MAIA language sound like to a therapist, healer, teacher, coach,
> facilitator or practitioner trying to offer something meaningful?

And the standard:

> **MAIA may think like a sophisticated reader. She speaks like an intelligent human companion
> standing beside someone who cares about what they are making.**
>
> **Never make the member decode the intelligence in order to benefit from it.**

**The diagnosis this lane answers, and it is correct:** the founder has been reverse-engineering
MAIA's voice by reading technical traces. This session is the evidence — four witnesses, hundreds
of anchor lines, and the decisive judgment still had to come from a human reading raw output.
That does not scale, and it wastes the one instrument that cannot be replaced.

> **Kelly provides the ear. Jarvis converts the ear into engineering evidence.**

## The flow, as proposed

```
DISCOVER    sample real Encounter notices
```

Judgment vocabulary — no family names, no coordinates, no screen terms:
**✅ Yes — MAIA · 🤔 too technical · ⚠️ too editorial · 🧊 too clinical · 🌫 too vague · ❌ not useful**

Three levels, in order, and **fine-tuning only if the first three fail**:
**L1 calibration** (judgments → principles) → **L2 exemplar guidance** (few-shot) →
**L3 evaluation** (hold-out).

## Five things to settle before the lane opens

### 1 · ⭐ DISCOVER is already done — the corpus exists and cost nothing extra

The fourth witness produced **226 surviving notices** from a real 386,031-code-point Work by a
practitioner writing for practitioners. Every one is grounded to exact excerpts, scoped, and
screened. **That is the DISCOVER input, already collected.** The lane can start at CALIBRATE.

### 2 · ⛔ …and it is ONE Work, by ONE author, who is ALSO the judge

Kelly is judging observations about **his own book**. That cuts both ways and both must be said:

- It is the **ideal** judging position for the actual question — *does this help a human being
  understand their own work more clearly?* Only the author can answer that.
- It is a **confound** for generality. Preferences learned entirely from one voice, one genre and
  one relationship-to-the-material risk encoding *"what helps Kelly with Elemental Alchemy"* as
  *"what helps a practitioner."*

⭐ **His own Work is the right starting corpus and the wrong only corpus.** The derived laws
should not be treated as general until the set spans several Works and, ideally, at least one
where the judge is not the author. Naming this now costs nothing; discovering it after L2 would
mean re-deriving the laws.

### 3 · ⛔ The L3 evaluator is a second cognition and needs its own constitution

*"A calibrated language evaluator checks human usefulness"* introduces a model judging MAIA's
output. This lane has already refused exactly that once — the G8 record: *"not a second judge
model, which would need its own constitution and its own negative controls first."*

The proposal reintroduces it legitimately, as **triage** — 226 down to 10 — which is a different
job from **verdict**. The distinction has to be built in, not assumed:

> ⛔ **The evaluator may reduce what reaches the ear. It may never pass what the ear would
> reject, invisibly.** A triage instrument that silently filters is a screen with no constitution
> — the exact defect F-4 turned out to be, one layer further out.

Minimum: the evaluator's own false-negative rate is measured against founder judgments on a
sample it did *not* filter. Otherwise its errors are unobservable by construction.

### 4 · ⛔ Exemplars carry a member-confidentiality hazard specific to this system

C8 says the Encounter request carries **the Work and the contract, nothing else**. Few-shot
exemplars quoting approved sentences about *Elemental Alchemy* would put **one member's material
into every other member's Encounter** — permanently, in the system prompt.

⭐ **Exemplars must be synthetic or content-neutral.** *"You come back to this idea several
times"* is portable. *"You return to the image of the crystal"* is one writer's book leaking into
another's. Easy to violate by accident, and it would be a sovereignty breach rather than a style
mistake.

### 5 · ⛔ F-7 is not a calibration question and must not be routed through this lane

The G8 falsifier — *"is not developed further"* — is unlawful under **ratified** E1 §3.2,
independent of how it sounds. Calibration cannot ratify it, and the ear should not be asked to
adjudicate a question already decided.

| defect | nature | resolved by |
|---|---|---|
| **F-7** absence-shaped openness | **constitutional** — violates ratified law | repair, on its own footing |
| **F-8** instrument voice | **calibration** — no law exists yet to state it | this lane |

⭐ **Sending F-7 into calibration would relitigate ratified canon as a matter of taste.** The two
travel together into the rerun; they do not travel together into the judgments.
