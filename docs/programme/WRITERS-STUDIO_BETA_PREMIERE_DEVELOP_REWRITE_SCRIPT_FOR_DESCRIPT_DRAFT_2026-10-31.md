## B. Develop room (replaces 9:59–11:08), about 75 s
*Screen: the real Focused screen, then the no-Work panel, then Guided and Full.*
"Develop is where you step back and ask what the writing is doing.

[no-Work screen] Sometimes your writing is not part of a Work yet. MAIA talks with you inside a Work, so she knows which one she is in. So the Studio says so plainly, and offers two choices: make this a Work, or add it to one you already have. Nothing is read or changed while you decide.

[Focused screen] Once it belongs to a Work, Develop opens in its simplest form: Explore your Work, one thing at a time. You can stay with a chapter and talk with MAIA about it in your own words. Or you can start from a worry a writer really has: I'm not sure what this chapter is doing. Something feels off. This doesn't sound like me. Nothing is read or changed until you choose it.

[Guided, then Full] If you want more, ask for it. Guided adds a few more starting places, like where a reader might lose you, or what keeps appearing. Full opens the whole map. The depth is there when you want it, and not before."
