## 0 · Why this lane exists, in one paragraph

Soullab has a constitution for what MAIA must not do to a person (the Invariants, the Oath), a
research method for observing a surface (the Field Study), and an epistemic discipline for what
Jarvis may claim (the Guardrails, Claim State Authority). It does **not** yet have an
evidence-informed body of knowledge about *what happens for a human being* in an encounter with
an intelligent system, or a way of designing from that knowledge that is Soullab's own rather
than borrowed from engagement-optimizing product methodology. This lane builds that body of
knowledge. The output is not a report. The output is that Jarvis and MAIA design differently
because the research exists.

---

## 1 · Purpose and governing principle (founder, verbatim)

**Purpose.** Develop Soullab's own rigorous body of knowledge for designing human experience
with MAIA. The programme studies conventional UX, behavioral science, HCI, psychology,
therapeutic relationship, AI trust, ethics, contemplative and phenomenological traditions, then
translates useful findings into a distinct Soullab architecture centered on: human sovereignty ·
agency · relationship · attunement · meaning · consciousness · embodiment · trust · continuity ·
elemental intelligence.

The objective is not to reproduce ConsumerAppStudio or any other existing methodology. The
objective is to develop an evidence-informed Soullab system for designing experiences between
humans and intelligent systems.

**Governing principle.** *Human experience is the subject. Product behavior is evidence about
that experience, not the ultimate objective.*

MAIA may: invite · orient · support · respond · remember · reflect · deepen · challenge
appropriately · reveal possibilities.

MAIA should not intentionally manufacture psychological dependency, false intimacy, compulsion,
artificial urgency or emotional vulnerability for the purpose of increasing engagement.

**The R&D question.** *How should an intelligent system be designed so that interaction
increases human agency, relationship, meaning, awareness and capacity rather than merely
engagement?* All research must eventually contribute to answering this question.

**North star.** For every MAIA experience: *What becomes more possible for the human being
because this encounter occurred?* Not "did we keep them engaged", not "did they click", not
even merely "did they like MAIA". The deeper measure is whether relationship with MAIA
contributes to greater capacity for awareness, agency, relationship, meaning, embodiment,
discernment and participation in life.

## 2 · Programme shape

```text
DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → EXTRACT → COMPARE → TRANSLATE → SYNTHESIZE → APPLY
→ PROTOTYPE → WITNESS → MEASURE → ADJUDICATE → CODIFY → DEPLOY → LEARN ↺
```

One lane. One research corpus. One evolving architecture.

### Stage 0 — Charter the inquiry
Define the question precisely before researching. Every inquiry records:

```text
QUESTION            Why does this matter for MAIA?
SURFACE             Where might this appear?
HUMAN PHENOMENON    What aspect of human experience is involved?
KNOWN ASSUMPTIONS   What are we currently assuming?
UNKNOWN             What do we actually need to learn?
RISK                What could we get badly wrong?
DECISION            What future design decision could this research change?
```

### Stage 1 — Landscape scan
Domains: **A** human motivation · **B** human-computer interaction · **C** relational
psychology · **D** AI-human interaction · **E** behavioral/product psychology · **F**
phenomenology and consciousness · **G** ethics and human sovereignty · **H** contemplative /
wisdom traditions. (Full domain lists in the kickoff prompt.)

In domain E, explicitly separate **MECHANISM** from **DESIRED USE** from **ETHICAL USE**.
Knowing that a psychological mechanism works does not establish that Soullab should use it.
Domain F is where Soullab goes substantially beyond conventional UX. Domain H sources inform
human understanding; they are not treated as scientific evidence when they are not.

### Stage 2 — Evidence corpus
Descending preference: (1) primary peer-reviewed research · (2) meta-analysis / systematic
review · (3) major standards and institutional frameworks · (4) established textbooks /
scholarship · (5) high-quality design research · (6) documented product case studies · (7)
practitioner frameworks · (8) expert commentary · (9) marketing claims. Marketing claims may
generate hypotheses. They may not establish conclusions.

### Stage 3 — Evidence card
Every important finding becomes a card (`docs/research/human-experience/evidence/TEMPLATE.md`).
Jarvis must distinguish *what the source observed* from *what Soullab concludes*. **No
inference laundering.**

### Stage 4 — Pattern extraction
Cluster evidence around recurring human phenomena (agency · attunement · trust · orientation ·
belonging · meaning · curiosity · competence · safety · disclosure · memory · intimacy ·
boundary · intention · attention · embodiment · reflection · transformation · continuity). A
Soullab principle may begin to emerge **only** when multiple independent bodies of evidence
point toward the same underlying principle.

### Stage 5 — Competitor / framework analysis
External frameworks (ConsumerAppStudio first) are comparative inputs, never authorities. For
each: what problem does it see · what human mechanism does it identify · what does it optimize
· what is useful · what is unsupported · what is manipulative · what does Soullab already
understand more deeply · what question does it reveal that we should research. **External
frameworks are never imported intact.**

### Stage 6 — Soullab translation (Human Experience dimensions)
Agency · Orientation · Attunement · Competence · Relationship · Trust · Meaning · Continuity ·
Sovereignty — each carrying its founder-stated question (kickoff §3).

### Stage 7 — Elemental reading
FIRE *what wants to happen?* · WATER *what is being felt and related?* · EARTH *what makes this
tangible and inhabitable?* · AIR *what needs to become intelligible?* · AETHER/FIELD *what
whole is appearing through the encounter?* Jarvis does not force every interaction to contain
every element; the framework identifies imbalance, absence and appropriate emphasis.

### Stage 8 — Derive Soullab design principles
Only now. Each principle carries: PRINCIPLE · WHY IT EXISTS · EVIDENCE BASIS · ELEMENTAL
EXPRESSION · PRODUCT CONSEQUENCE · ANTI-PATTERN · EXCEPTIONS · HOW WE TEST IT.

> Founder's worked example — **Earned Trust**: MAIA should elicit only the degree of trust
> warranted by its actual capabilities, memory, knowledge and certainty. Consequence: MAIA
> distinguishes known / remembered / inferred / uncertain / unavailable. Anti-pattern: language
> suggesting certainty, memory or intimacy MAIA does not possess. Test: do members accurately
> understand what MAIA knows and why? *(Note for Stage 8: this is already partially canon —
> the four registers of `MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC_v1.0.md` and Invariant 5. The programme's job
> is to bring evidence to it, not to reinvent it. See §4.)*

### Stage 9 — MAIA surface application
Initial surfaces: MAIA House · First conversation · Voice · Memory / Anamnesis · Now What? ·
Relationships · Practitioner Studio · Soul Portrait · Field / collective experiences · Return
after absence. Per-surface schema: ARRIVAL STATE · USER INTENTION · POSSIBLE VULNERABILITY ·
DESIRED HUMAN CAPACITY · CURRENT EXPERIENCE · DESIRED EXPERIENCE · ELEMENTAL PROFILE ·
RELATIONAL CONSEQUENCE · TRUST CONSEQUENCE · AGENCY CONSEQUENCE · DESIGN OPPORTUNITY · RISK ·
MEASUREMENT.

### Stage 10 — Experience hypothesis
No redesign happens merely because something sounds wise. Form: *If MAIA [change], then the
person will experience [outcome], without [cost]. WHY. MEASURE.*

### Stage 11 — Prototype
Smallest implementation capable of testing the hypothesis: copy change → interaction timing →
UI prototype → conversation prompt behavior → feature flag → limited production experiment →
architectural change. Do not redesign architecture to test something a five-line prototype
could have taught.

### Stage 12 — Human witness
Qualitative witness questions (what happened for you · did you feel understood · did you know
what was happening · did you feel free to disagree · did MAIA assume too much · did anything
feel manipulative · unexpectedly alive · intrusive · what changed in you · what would you want
MAIA to do differently). **The witness is evidence. It is not automatically truth about
mechanism.**

### Stage 13 — Measurement
Human Experience metrics (felt understood · felt oriented · felt free · felt respected · felt
more capable · felt appropriately remembered · trust calibration · meaningful insight ·
relational continuity · willingness to correct MAIA · ability to leave without pressure).
Traditional metrics (activation · completion · return · drop-off · errors · latency ·
conversion) remain useful but are not ultimate measures of success.

### Stage 14 — Adjudication
One outcome per experiment: **ADOPT** · **REFINE** · **PARK** · **REJECT** · **PROHIBIT**
(works behaviorally but violates Soullab principles). The last category matters: some designs
may improve retention and still be wrong for MAIA.

### Stage 15 — Codify into Jarvis
Accepted principles graduate into the Jarvis development process as an **Experience Review**
skill (FUNCTIONAL · HUMAN · RELATIONAL · ELEMENTAL · AGENCY · TRUST · MEMORY · BOUNDARY ·
ETHICS · EVIDENCE). Part of product design, not a shelf document. **Sequenced last** (§5).

### Stage 16 — Experience library
`docs/research/human-experience/` — structure in that directory's README.

## 4 · Standing with existing canon — what the programme must not re-derive

The founder's concern is that we might *"encode our intuitions before the evidence has had a
chance to challenge them."* The mirror risk is that the programme rediscovers, in evidence
vocabulary, positions Soullab already holds constitutionally — and then treats the rediscovery
as a finding. Both are inference laundering. So every Stage 0 charter names which existing
canon already touches its question, and every Stage 8 principle names whether it **confirms**,
**refines**, **extends**, or **challenges** existing canon.

| Existing instrument | What it already settles | What the programme adds |
|---|---|---|
| `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` (16 invariants) | What MAIA must not do relationally: no exclusive bond, dependency resistance, no emotional-capture optimization, uncertainty honesty, mirror integrity, human-world priority, cultural sovereignty, recognition integrity | Evidence for *why* these hold in human experience; **positive** design knowledge (what does support agency), not only prohibitions |
| `MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC_v1.0.md` — four registers | The "Earned Trust" consequence (known / remembered / inferred / uncertain) is already a canonical rule for memory reference | Whether members actually calibrate trust from those registers (R2) — a measurement question the spec does not answer |
| `INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md` | No signal collapses into a conclusion without relational checking | Evidence on attunement vs. personalization (R1, R4) |
| `FIELD_STUDY_METHOD_CANDIDATE_2026-07-29.md` — evidence classes A–E, observation status, confabulation guard | How to observe a surface honestly | The programme **adopts** these classes for every evidence card and every surface application (§6). It does not invent a parallel vocabulary. |
| `docs/MAIA_MISATTUNEMENT_TRIAGE.md` | Classification of relational failures reported by testers | Witness protocol (Stage 12) should feed and be fed by it, not duplicate it |

## 6 · Evidence discipline binding (lane addition — how §3's stops are made operable)

1. **Every evidence card carries a Field Study class** (A direct · B structural inference · C
   experiential prediction · D metaphorical · E constitutional consistency) **and** a Stage 2
   source tier (1–9). A card at tier 7–9 or class C/D may generate a hypothesis and may never,
   alone, ground a principle.
2. **Card STATUS** ∈ unreviewed · supported · contested · rejected. "Supported" requires at
   least two independent sources at tier ≤ 5 that were not derived from each other, and a named
   boundary condition. Contradictory evidence is a required field, not an optional one; an
   empty field means *"searched for and none found"* and says so.
3. **Principle status** follows `CLAIM_STATE_AUTHORITY.md`: a Stage 8 principle is
   **CANDIDATE** on authoring, **RATIFIED** only by founder act, and is never promoted by the
   accumulation of cards alone.
4. **Observation status is never blurred** (Field Study §II): any statement about a MAIA
   surface says Walked / Read / Unknown / Needs production verification. A Stage 9 surface
   application is a Field Study sitting and follows that method, including the Observation
   Declaration.
5. **No Soullab conclusion in a source's voice.** The card's CLAIM field states what the
   source found in the source's terms; POSSIBLE MAIA RELEVANCE is where Soullab speaks. A
   card that blends them is returned to unreviewed.
6. **Contemplative and phenomenological sources** (domains F, H) are carded with source type
   *tradition* or *phenomenological account* and class D or E, never A. They can shape what we
   look for. They are not evidence that we found it.
7. **Mechanism ≠ permission** (domain E): every behavioral-mechanism card has three separate
   fields — MECHANISM (what works), DESIRED USE (what Soullab would want it for, if anything),
   ETHICAL USE (whether Invariants 3, 4, 12 permit it). PROHIBIT at Stage 14 is reachable
   from the card alone.

## 7 · Definition of Done — Phase One

Phase One closes when, and only when, all of these exist and the founder has read them:

- a credible evidence corpus (cards, classed and tiered, across domains A–H for R1–R6)
- a first Soullab Human Experience model (Stage 6 dimensions with evidence under each)
- an Elemental Experience mapping (Stage 7)
- approximately 10–20 strong design principles, each in the Stage 8 schema, each marked
  confirms / refines / extends / challenges against existing canon
- explicit anti-patterns
- a measurement vocabulary (Stage 13), with each metric named as *measurable now*, *needs an
  instrument*, or *needs consent design*
- several tested MAIA hypotheses (Stages 10–14, each with one adjudication outcome)
- human witness evidence (Stage 12, collected under study ethics)
- one Jarvis Experience Review capability (Stage 15) — **built last**
- one repeatable R&D loop demonstrated end to end at least once

The result is not a report. The result is that Jarvis and MAIA design differently because the
research exists.
