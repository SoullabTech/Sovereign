# JARVIS-MAIA-FREE-SYNTHESIS-01 · A4 Apparatus Reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Mode:** R&D only
**Production authority:** NONE

## Why reconciliation was required

Two independent A4 implementations landed concurrently after the A3 handoff.

One emphasized a compact reversible field with explicit member-correction standing and real Silver Cedar pressure. The other implemented a stricter stage-ordered validator, explicit temporal-change nodes, tension support, fail-closed cycle/stage-inversion checks, non-evidentiary Gestalt supersession, and a separate mechanical proof.

Keeping both as competing research engines would make later A5 results apparatus-dependent.

## Ruling

The canonical A4 apparatus for this programme is now:

- `scripts/research/free-synthesis/a4-gestalt-prototype.ts` — representation + validator;
- `scripts/research/free-synthesis/a4-gestalt-proof.ts` — synthetic reversibility proof;
- `scripts/research/free-synthesis/a4-silver-cedar-proof.ts` — founder-owned developmental/source-standing/availability proof.

The earlier root-level `scripts/research/free-synthesis-a4-prototype.ts` is retired after its useful discriminants are ported. Its historical commit remains part of the research record.

## What was preserved from each line

From the stricter validator line:
- primary evidence as a distinct stage;
- observation → relation → configuration → temporal change → Gestalt stage order;
- fail-closed invalid dependency/cycle behavior;
- prior Gestalt may be superseded but never used as evidence;
- explicit evidence-root comparison across Gestalt revision.
- correction/adoption/withdrawal represented as new primary speech acts with explicit standing targets, never mutations of prior evidence.
- explicit collaborator authorship and provisional `possible_recurrence` / `reorganizes` relation types.

From the Silver Cedar line:
- real co-created developmental pressure rather than synthetic-only proof;
- explicit separation of member and MAIA primary evidence;
- initial not-knowing remains inside the final evidence descent;
- MAIA's useful formulations remain MAIA-authored;
- later continuity rupture is represented as an availability change, not as deletion/revision of the earlier content Gestalt.

## Higher-order geometry standing

The canonical representation uses pairwise typed relations plus a `configuration` node whose meaning can belong to the organization of multiple observations/relations. That is already enough to express higher-order configuration without adopting a hypergraph library or sheaf formalism.

Selective n-ary/hyperedge machinery remains a candidate only if a later defeat case cannot be represented without distortion through configuration membership and reversible evidence descent.

## Constitutional result

The reconciled apparatus preserves the A3 contract:
- derived never becomes primary;
- source standing survives synthesis;
- correction/reorganization can change the present without erasing history;
- contradiction can remain explicit;
- absence/availability is a separate fact from historical meaning;
- every Gestalt must descend to primary evidence.

No production prompt, memory, schema, model, route, validator or egress path is changed by this reconciliation.
