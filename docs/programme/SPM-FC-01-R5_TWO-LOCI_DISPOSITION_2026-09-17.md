### R5 replacement for I-8

#### I-8 · Required crossing authority is not supplied by epistemic standing

1. **Law.** Where a governed crossing requires an authority independent of the material's standing,
   no quantity or quality of epistemic standing substitutes for that required authority. This law
   does not assert that every crossing has such an authority, nor that epistemic standing cannot
   govern how strongly MAIA expresses a claim.
2. **Evidence.** D9-B B4 establishes one live purpose-bound authorization whose effect cannot be
   reduced to the material's state without recreating the disputed purpose-indexed capability under
   another name. D9-C §7–§8 independently establishes one genuine human-audience representation
   seam where material, provenance, wording and present standing remain fixed while a member ×
   audience authorization changes whether representation may continue. D9-C expressly preserves
   `epistemicFraming()` as evidence that standing may govern **how strongly MAIA speaks**.
3. **Prohibited.** `material has sufficiently high standing → a separately required crossing
   authority may be omitted`.
4. **Adversarial case.** Hold material provenance and present standing constant at their strongest
   supported values, then vary only an independently required purpose/audience authority at a seam
   where such authority is evidenced.
5. **PASS.** The crossing follows the required authority while epistemic standing remains unchanged.
6. **FAIL.** High standing substitutes for an authority that the governed seam independently
   requires.
