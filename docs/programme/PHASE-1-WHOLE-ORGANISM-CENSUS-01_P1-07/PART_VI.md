⭐⭐ **Consequence: the `DIVERGENT` limb of the outcome vocabulary was structurally unavailable on

> ⭐⭐ **This survives into the final record as a COMPARISON-INSTRUMENT LIMITATION, ⛔ NEVER as
> evidence in favour of the strategic exclusion.**

⭐ And this is precisely where the second principle bites: **a comparison that cannot fail in one
direction has not passed in the other.** The row's own `MIXED` is therefore not a softened

### 3b · ⭐ `X-05` — THE REFUSED INFERENCE

```text
matching absent    ↛    enlargement prohibition satisfied
```

Matching is a mechanism for **gathering people toward a field**; enlargement is a property of
**the field once they are in it**. ⭐ Neighbouring concepts, ⛔ not the same object. A system with no
matching can still enlarge a single field without limit; a system full of matching can still
multiply and nest.

### 3c · ⭐ `X-21B` — REFUSING COMPARISON IS THE STRONGER METHODOLOGY

The exclusion's object is a **claim state**. The census traced **code objects, paths,
participation, persistence and decision effects**, and **no P1 record evaluates any outward
statement of the organism against the governing CAN/CANNOT table.** ⭐ `NOT COMPARABLE` is therefore
**stronger methodology, ⛔ not a gap**: manufacturing a proxy would have been weaker.
