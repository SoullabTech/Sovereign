# PART IV — AUTHORITY MAP

## IV.0 · How to read Part IV — four distinctions that must survive

These are what P1-03 and P1-04 spent their passes establishing, and every line below keeps them:

```text
causal power    ≠  located authority
                   an object can shape or decide an outcome while no authorizing text
                   was found for it. Both facts are recorded; neither implies the other.

GOVERNED SHAPE  ≠  GOVERNED ACT
                   governing the FORM of a record is not authorizing the ACT the object performs.
                   ⭐⭐ "Governance of a thing is not necessarily authorization of what that
                   thing does." — P1-04/MAP3_authority.md, header

DECIDES         ≠  HAS AUTHORITY
                   DECIDES is an observed effect. HAS AUTHORITY is a located grant.
                   INF-6 forbids deriving the second from the first. Amendment 3 §2 removed
                   HAS AUTHORITY from the ladder entirely: "It was never a rung."

NONE LOCATED    ≠  ABSENT
                   NONE LOCATED = the census looked in its permitted evidence and did not find.
                   ⛔ It does NOT mean authority does not exist. ⛔ UNKNOWN is a third, weaker
                   state and is never upgraded to NONE LOCATED to tidy a table.
```

**The two binding sentence-pairs**, quoted from the P1-07 schema and from
`P1-05/02_corpus_register.md §0d`:

```text
✅ MAY SAY   "62 rows showed effect without located authorization."
⛔ MAY NOT   "62 unauthorized capabilities."

✅ MAY SAY   "No row in this census established GOVERNED ACT."
⛔ MAY NOT   "No acts are governed."
```

⛔ Nothing in Part IV is a verdict of legitimacy, a severity, a ranking or a repair order. ⛔ The
separately-owned authorization/exposure lane is neither cited, awaited nor answered.
