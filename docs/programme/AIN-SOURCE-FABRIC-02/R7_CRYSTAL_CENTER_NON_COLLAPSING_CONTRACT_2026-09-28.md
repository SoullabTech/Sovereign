# AIN-SOURCE-FABRIC-02R7 — Crystal Center Non-Collapsing Integration Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `de6d5bb5e39391fb1f7df342f3a2e9c49d7c6fb1`

## Purpose

R7 tests whether the Crystal Center can coordinate differentiated modes of knowing without collapsing them into one epistemic register.

The center is not a truth engine. It is a coordination field.

## Required modes

A full R7 constellation may hold:

- analytic;
- associative;
- temporal;
- contradiction;
- absence;
- uncertainty;
- imaginal.

Each mode remains explicitly typed.

## Non-collapse law

> **Integration does not mean homogenization.**

The analytic may remain analytic.

The imaginal may remain imaginal.

The historical may remain historical.

The contradictory may remain contradictory.

The absent may remain absent.

The uncertain may remain uncertain.

No mode receives authority to silently rewrite another.

## Corpus Callosum law

The Corpus Callosum function is coordination across differentiated processing, not conversion of all processing into a common substance.

Cross-mode relation is permitted.

Mode collapse is not.

## Contradiction law

Contradiction is first-class information.

The center may hold a contradiction, contextualize it, or relate it to other material.

It may not erase the contradiction merely because a coherent synthesis is available.

## Absence law

Absence is first-class information.

The center may name what is missing.

It may not fill the missing place with an invented source, imagined member meaning, or inferred certainty.

## Imaginal law

Imaginal material may participate in synthesis only as `imaginal_provisional`.

Resonance does not promote imaginal material into empirical fact, revelation, causation, diagnosis, or identity.

## Mode-ablation law

Each epistemic mode must survive as a genuinely distinct register.

To test this, R7 removes one mode at a time.

A valid ablation requires:

1. the removed mode is genuinely absent afterward;
2. another mode does not inherit its epistemic standing;
3. the remaining modes continue as themselves.

This detects silent substitution.

## Center authority boundary

The Crystal Center may coordinate.

It may not:

- collapse epistemic modes;
- erase contradiction;
- fill absence;
- promote imaginal material;
- issue whole-person conclusions;
- persist an integrated person-model.

## Soul-Service law

> **The center serves differentiated wholeness; it does not replace the living whole.**

The person remains the locus of lived meaning.

## No-build boundary

R7 is a sandbox only.

No live MAIA runtime binding.
No member persistence.
No production deployment.
No spiritual authority.
No whole-person synthesis object.
