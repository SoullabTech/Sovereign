## Purpose

R8 tests whether two differentiated processing pathways can exchange bounded signals and produce a provisional relation that neither pathway contains independently.

This is a test of **emergence rather than aggregation**.

## Parallel pathway law

The sandbox separates two functional pathways:

### Analytic pathway

Carries:

- admitted evidence;
- temporal standing;
- contradiction;
- absence.

### Associative pathway

Carries:

- explicit relation;
- uncertainty;
- imaginal possibility.

The distinction is functional, not ontological or neurological proof.

## Corpus Callosum law

The two pathways may exchange bounded signals.

The exchange object may carry signal references and their governed provenance.

It may not pass unrestricted raw context merely because both pathways participate in one synthesis.

## Fifth-Element candidate law

A Fifth-Element candidate is admitted only when:

1. both pathways contribute;
2. neither pathway independently contains the candidate;
3. the combined mode signature exceeds either independent pathway signature;
4. removing either pathway removes the candidate;
5. contradiction survives;
6. absence survives;
7. provenance remains visible;
8. the candidate remains provisional;
9. persistence authority remains false;
10. whole-person authority remains false.

## Novelty law

A new identifier is not sufficient evidence of emergence.

R8 therefore compares pathway signatures.

The emergent combination must contain a cross-pathway configuration that is absent from either pathway considered alone.

> **Novelty is relational configuration, not merely renamed output.**

## Bilateral ablation law

The analytic pathway is removed independently.

The associative pathway is removed independently.

A genuine R8 candidate must disappear in both counterfactuals.

If one pathway can reproduce the candidate alone, the claim of cross-pathway emergence fails.

## Preservation law

Emergence may not achieve elegance by deleting resistance.

Contradiction remains contradiction.

Absence remains absence.

Uncertainty remains uncertainty.

Imaginal material remains provisional.

## Fifth-Element authority boundary

The Fifth Element is a synthesis function, not a superior truth class.

It receives no authority for:

- causation;
- prediction;
- diagnosis;
- identity;
- third-party interiority;
- whole-person conclusion;
- persistence.

## Soul-Service law

> **What emerges from relation remains in service to the living field that made relation possible.**

The candidate can be offered back to the person.

It does not become the person.

## No-build boundary

R8 remains a benchmark sandbox.

No live MAIA binding.
No member data.
No persistence.
No production deployment.
No autonomous spiritual interpretation.
