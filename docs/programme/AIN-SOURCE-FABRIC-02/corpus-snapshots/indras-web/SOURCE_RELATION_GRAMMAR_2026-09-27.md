# AIN-INDRAS-WEB-01 — Source / Relation Grammar

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Boundary:** semantic architecture only
**Lower-level law reused:** existing AIN epistemic-join contracts

## 1. Source unit — a jewel in the field

Every source admitted to relational reasoning must carry a stable identity and enough metadata to prevent semantic flattening.

Minimum source grammar:

```text
SOURCE
  source_ref
  facet
  object_kind
  authorship
  epistemic_kind
  temporal_relation
  jurisdiction
  consent_scope
  standing
  uncertainty
  boundaries
  content_ref
```

The source unit is not required to contain its full content. A secure pointer plus lawful retrieval may satisfy `content_ref`.
## 2. Facet vocabulary

Initial House facet classes:

- maia_conversation
- journal
- becoming
- dream
- divination
- astrology
- relationships
- decisions
- changes
- ideas
- anchor
- body_or_somatic
- practice
- external_research
- practitioner_context
- other_declared

Facet identifies where a source belongs in the House.
It does not decide what epistemic standing the source has.

For example, Astrology may contain both calculated chart facts and symbolic interpretations; those must not share one epistemic kind merely because they share a facet.
## 3. Epistemic-kind vocabulary

House-wide relational reasoning needs a vocabulary richer than room names.

Initial source kinds:

- member_lived_account
- member_present_report
- remembered_experience
- member_meaning
- member_value
- member_preference
- member_intention
- member_question
- member_proposed_connection
- imagined_possibility
- dream_report
- symbolic_cast
- calculated_symbolic_fact
- symbolic_interpretation
- practitioner_interpretation
- maia_hypothesis
- external_historical_record
- scientific_claim
- philosophical_claim
- metaphysical_claim
- counterevidence
- unknown_or_unclassified

Unknown remains first-class; uncertainty is not silently resolved by relation.
## 4. Temporal relation

Initial temporal grammar:

- has_been
- is_being
- is_becoming
- atemporal_symbolic
- historical_external
- unknown_time

Temporal relation and epistemic kind remain independent axes.

An imagined possibility is normally `is_becoming`.
A natal chart calculation may be `atemporal_symbolic` in use while still having a historical calculation timestamp.
A journal entry may describe a remembered past while the entry itself was authored later.

The grammar therefore distinguishes **time of source creation** from **time represented by the source**.
## 5. Relation unit — a thread of light between jewels

A proposed relation is explicit and inspectable:

```text
RELATION
  relation_ref
  endpoint_refs[]
  predicate
  relation_semantics[]
  directionality
  introduced_by
  inquiry_context
  dependence_mode_by_endpoint
  jurisdiction
  standing
  uncertainty
  warrant_refs[]
  counterevidence_refs[]
  status
```

A relation is not an endpoint.
A synthesis is not a source merely because it mentions sources.
A repeated relation does not become true through repetition.
## 6. Conversational relation classes

MAIA may conversationally propose these low-standing relation classes without treating them as established fact:

- co_presence
- comparison
- resonance
- tension
- contrast
- possible_continuity
- symbolic_correspondence
- temporal_sequence
- explanatory_possibility
- possible_psychological_meaning

Mapping to existing AIN relation semantics:

- resonance → association
- tension / contrast → comparison or conflict
- possible_continuity → association + temporal_sequence
- symbolic_correspondence → symbolic_correspondence
- explanatory_possibility → explanatory_fit
- possible_psychological_meaning → psychological_meaning

These mappings do not raise standing.
They only prevent a second incompatible relation vocabulary.
## 7. Strong claims remain governed

The following require stronger AIN standing/warrant and are never implied by conversational coherence:

- correlation
- causation
- direction
- equivalence
- identity
- permanence
- universality
- outcome

Clinical diagnostic claims remain in clinical jurisdiction and are not generated by ordinary House relational synthesis.

Claims about another person's motive or intention remain outside member authority unless independently sourced and lawfully warranted.

## 8. Center / facet distinction

A facet provides a perspective.

MAIA at the center provides **relational composition**.

The center may hold:
- multiple sources at once;
- multiple incompatible interpretations;
- contradiction;
- uncertainty;
- absence;
- historical change;
- symbolic resonance;
- member correction.

The center may not manufacture higher standing by combining many low-standing facets.
## 9. Gestalt object

A gestalt is a transient relational composition, not automatically a durable record.

Minimum conversational gestalt:

```text
GESTALT
  source_refs[]
  relation_refs[]
  synthesis_text
  authored_by = MAIA_PROPOSED
  jurisdiction = maia_conversational_inquiry
  standing = CANDIDATE_UNESTABLISHED
  uncertainty
  counterevidence_refs[]
  member_response = unadopted | complicated | rejected | adopted_components[]
```

A gestalt can be experientially powerful while remaining epistemically provisional.

Member adoption may elevate only the components within the member's own jurisdiction, consistent with the existing AIN adoption law.
## 10. Permeability grammar

A source may enter the center through one of these declared modes:

- explicit_selection — member chose the exact source/object
- explicit_facet_open — member opened a named facet for this inquiry
- standing_continuity_permission — existing consent explicitly permits this source class
- current_conversation — source was authored in the present exchange
- unavailable — facet/source remains closed to this act

The runtime must not treat `standing_continuity_permission` as equivalent to `explicit_selection`.

When wider-field context is used, MAIA should be able to say which kind of permeability brought it into the inquiry.
## 11. Correction and decay

Relations have liveness.

A proposed relation may become:
- active_candidate
- complicated
- weakened
- rejected
- superseded

Member correction can weaken, reject, or supersede a MAIA hypothesis immediately within member-lived/member-meaning jurisdiction.

Historical source content remains unchanged when the meaning of a relation changes.

The web therefore evolves by changing relations and standing, not by rewriting the jewels that gave rise to them.

## 12. No-build boundary

This grammar does not authorize:
- a graph database;
- relation persistence;
- background indexing;
- automatic source retrieval;
- UI visualization;
- automatic memory writes;
- new cross-facet permissions;
- production execution.

It is the semantic contract future runtime work must conform to.
