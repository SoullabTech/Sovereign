# AIN-SOURCE-FABRIC-02R6 — Aether Synthesis Falsification Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `e33b6b6638c290049487bfdb8710f0eaae7da06b`

## Purpose

R6 tests whether Aether can propose a new cross-source relation without receiving authority to interpret the living whole.

The object under test is not a person-model and not a free-form answer. It is a **provisional relational proposition** whose source dependence can be falsified.

## Candidate law

Every admitted Aether synthesis candidate must carry a stable candidate reference, originating inquiry and relation, explicit supporting and critical source refs, counterevidence refs, temporal need, uncertainty, `provisional: true`, and `persistenceAuthority: false`.

A candidate relation is therefore not an enduring fact. It is an inspectable proposal inside a governed field.

## Cross-source law

An Aether-originated candidate may not masquerade as synthesis when it depends on only one source. At least two admitted source refs must support the relation.

Source adjacency, community membership, semantic similarity, or graph proximity do not themselves establish support.

## Ablation law

For every source named as critical support:

> remove the jewel and regenerate the relation field.

If the candidate remains unchanged, that source was not actually critical and the dependency claim is false. A legitimate critical ablation must cause the candidate relation to disappear.

Removing an unrelated source must not falsely destroy a relation that does not depend on it.

## Refusal inheritance

A relation is not synthesis-eligible when creative relation permission is false, the relation has been rejected or superseded, required support is not admitted or is revoked, or a current claim relies on superseded support.

Member correction therefore has precedence over candidate generation. A rejected relation cannot be resurrected by Aether.

## Authority boundary

R6 grants no authority for whole-person conclusions, persistence, causation, prediction, diagnostic identity, fixed identity claims, or third-party interiority.

The candidate proposition language is deliberately relational: may be explored; may clarify; may be held; possible continuity; symbolic correspondence; explanatory possibility.

It does not convert relation into revelation.

## Temporal law

A candidate inherits the temporal need of the relation that gave rise to it. Historical evidence may remain visible as historical evidence. Superseded material may not silently support a current-tense proposition.

R6 does not collapse `has been`, `is being`, and `may become` into one undifferentiated present.

## Soul-Service law

> The system serves the dance; it does not become the dancer.

Aether may offer a relation for human consideration. It may not declare the living person's essence, destiny, diagnosis, hidden truth, or final meaning.

## No-build boundary

R6 does not connect candidate synthesis to live MAIA, persist Aether candidates into member memory, mutate member data, create a whole-person model, authorize Crystal Center runtime synthesis, or deploy anything to production.
