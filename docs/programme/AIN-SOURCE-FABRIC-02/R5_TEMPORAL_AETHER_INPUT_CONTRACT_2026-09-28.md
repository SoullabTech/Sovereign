# AIN-SOURCE-FABRIC-02R5 — Temporal / Supersession Graph + Aether Input Contract

**Date:** 28 September 2026
**Parent:** R4G at `b7c1982dc67c004afef8c21ca09d647c207172a2`
**Class:** offline contract + benchmark evidence only

## Purpose

R5 adds time to the governed source constellation and defines what Aether may receive before any creative synthesis occurs.

The contract inherits:

> **time of source creation is not the same as time represented by the source**

and:

> **the field evolves by changing relation standing, not by rewriting the jewels that gave rise to it.**
## Temporal grammar

A source may represent:

- **has_been**
- **is_being**
- **is_becoming**
- **atemporal_symbolic**
- **historical_external**
- **unknown_time**

Temporal relation remains independent of epistemic kind.

A symbolic source can be temporally current while its interpretive use remains symbolic.

A historical record can remain visible while no longer governing a present-tense claim.
## Temporal edge grammar

R5 admits explicit benchmark temporal edges:

- **precedes**
- **evidence_successor**
- **corrects**
- **supersedes**
- **revokes**

with scope:

- current claim;
- relation;
- permission;
- all.

The direction is explicit.

A correction or supersession does not delete the older source.

It changes what the older source may support **now**.
## Present-standing law

For a current-state claim:

> a superseded or revoked source may remain visible as history, but may not silently support the current proposition.

For a historical or mixed inquiry:

> the earlier source may remain active as historical evidence when its historical role is disclosed.

An **evidence successor** is not automatically a supersession.

Later evidence may extend or test an earlier contract without invalidating it.

## Aether is not a new authority class

Aether may be permitted to propose a **creative relation**.

That permission does not authorize:

- causation;
- prediction;
- identity;
- diagnosis;
- third-party interiority;
- persistence.

These remain explicitly false in the R5 input contract.

Aether therefore receives **permission to relate**, not permission to pronounce.
## Cross-source law

An Aether-proposed relation requires multiple distinct supporting sources.

A single jewel cannot be re-described as “Aether synthesis” merely because the language is elegant.

This is structural:

> **creative synthesis must actually be relational.**

The relation's support set is inspectable before any prose is rendered.
## Ablation law

Every Aether-proposed relation names its **critical support refs**.

For each critical jewel:

1. remove the jewel;
2. recompute the structural relation field;
3. require the claimed dependent relation to become invalid.

If removing a supposedly critical source leaves the relation structurally untouched:

> that source was not actually critical and may not be cited as though it were.

Ablation is therefore a dependency test, not a rhetorical provenance list.
## Correction law

Member correction applies to relations and dependent gestalt claims.

It does not rewrite historical source content.

A relation may become:

- weakened;
- rejected;
- superseded.

The jewels remain what they were.

This preserves the distinction between:

> **what happened / was written**

and:

> **what relation the living field currently grants it.**
## Contradiction and absence

Contradiction is a first-class input object.

Removing a source participating in a contradiction must remove or alter that contradiction record.

Absence is also first-class.

A source that is unavailable, not admitted, revoked, or unsupported cannot be represented as silent agreement.

Aether may compose around an absence.

It may not fill it in and call the invention evidence.
## Soul-Service boundary

R5 inherits the Soul-Service / Non-Substitution Law.

The purpose of a temporally rich field is not to produce a more totalizing model of the person.

It is to let the person encounter:

- continuity;
- discontinuity;
- history;
- correction;
- possibility;
- contradiction;
- emergence.

The temporal map remains an instrument in service to the living process.

It is not the living process.
## No-build boundary

R5 authorizes no:

- free-form Aether synthesis;
- MAIA prompt change;
- member-memory retrieval;
- durable relation storage;
- temporal graph database;
- automatic correction inference;
- background supersession;
- production ranking change;
- Indra UI mutation;
- persistence of a gestalt.

It defines the lawful **input** for a later synthesis experiment.
