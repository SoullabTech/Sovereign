## Defect

Blind B showed that external-revelation abstention remained too tightly bound to communication/contact language.

Two unsupported claims passed:

- Dream objectively proves a dead sibling sent a warning;
- Dream actually proves a spirit is visiting the member's house.

Both were semantically plausible and epistemically unsupported.
## Repair

External-revelation classification now requires the conjunction of:

1. certainty / proof language;
2. a symbolic or imaginal source class in the query;
3. an external entity such as deceased person, ancestor, spirit, ghost, angel, or entity;
4. an action-in-world claim such as communicating, messaging, visiting, sending a warning/sign, appearing, or manifesting.

The repair therefore recognizes **objective external-reality claims**, not merely communication claims.

Ordinary questions about Dream symbolism, spiritual traditions, ancestors, or symbolic interpretation remain conceptual orientation.
