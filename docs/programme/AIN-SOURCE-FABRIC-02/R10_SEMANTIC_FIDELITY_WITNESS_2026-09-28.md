## Human review

Human semantic review is mandatory.

This is deliberate.

A machine can establish that:

- the cited source exists;
- the proposition contains traceable source content;
- its dependencies behave correctly under ablation;
- temporal and epistemic boundaries are represented.

But those facts do not prove that the synthesis is the best, deepest, or most faithful human interpretation of the material.

The reviewer therefore asks whether the proposition is:

- accurate;
- genuinely relational;
- temporally honest;
- contradiction-preserving;
- appropriately uncertain;
- free of unsupported expansion.
