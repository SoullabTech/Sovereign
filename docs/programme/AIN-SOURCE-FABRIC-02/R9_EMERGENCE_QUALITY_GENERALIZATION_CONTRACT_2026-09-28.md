# AIN-SOURCE-FABRIC-02R9 — Emergence Quality / Counterfactual Novelty Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `bb3f99aad04660b64f261136e89a6cdd5abc5b95`

## Purpose

R9 tests whether Fifth-Element emergence generalizes beyond one carefully composed constellation and whether the system can refuse trivial or cosmetic novelty.

The target is not merely candidate production.

The target is **nontrivial, counterfactually dependent emergence**.

## Effective-support law

Participating context is not the same as effective support.

A source may be present in the constellation without contributing to the emergent relation.

R9 therefore distinguishes:

- **effective support** — present in both evidential and relational participation;
- **contextual-only support** — present in the field but not constitutive of the emergence.

Distractors must remain contextual-only.

## Nontriviality law

A candidate is not nontrivial merely because:

- two pathways exist;
- two sources co-occur;
- the output has a new identifier;
- the center restates that integration happened.

A valid quality signature must contain actual relational or constraining structure such as:

- explicit relation refs;
- contradiction;
- absence;
- temporal constraint.

Pathway labels themselves never count as novelty.

## Multi-constellation law

R9 requires the same emergence criteria to hold across multiple semantically distinct constellations.

A mechanism that passes only one hand-shaped case is not yet generalized.

## Sparse-field refusal law

A disconnected or relationally empty field must refuse emergence.

The presence of analytic material plus associative capacity is not enough.

There must be an actual bridge structure.

## Distractor law

Adding an isolated source must not make that source effective support merely because it is nearby in the constellation.

Removing the distractor must leave the valid emergence intact.

## Bridge-ablation law

When the sole relational bridge is removed, the emergence must disappear.

Derivative uncertainty, imaginal, or other downstream signals may not survive as orphaned triggers.

> **No derivative signal may outlive the source/relation structure that gave rise to it.**

This is a cascade-ablation requirement.

## Orphan prevention law

When a source ablation removes a relation, R9 also removes:

- uncertainty facets derived from that relation;
- imaginal facets derived from that relation;
- candidate records derived from that relation.

A shadow of a deleted relation cannot continue to generate synthesis.

## Authority boundary

R9 grants no additional authority beyond R8.

No causation.
No prediction.
No diagnosis.
No identity authority.
No persistence.
No whole-person conclusion.

## Soul-Service law

> **A meaningful emergence must depend on the real relational field, not on the system's desire to produce one.**

Refusal is a valid result.

## No-build boundary

R9 remains benchmark-only.

No live MAIA binding.
No member persistence.
No production deployment.
No autonomous interpretation of a person.
