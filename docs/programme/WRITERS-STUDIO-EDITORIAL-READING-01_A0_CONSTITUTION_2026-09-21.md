# WRITERS-STUDIO-EDITORIAL-READING-01 · A0 — EDITORIAL READING CONSTITUTION · 2026-09-21

**Status:** CONSTITUTION CANDIDATE · DOCUMENTARY ONLY · NO RUNTIME / SCHEMA / UI CHANGE

## I. Why this programme exists

Writer's Studio now has bounded editorial authority: MAIA may discuss, propose, apply, and undo within writer-declared limits.

That does **not** yet make MAIA a developmental editor.

The remaining capability gap is one level above wording:

> A developmental editor first forms a governed understanding of the Work, names concerns and patterns that the evidence supports, and only later — if the writer wants — enters revision.

The present system has strong `DevelopmentalObservation` objects, evidence references, coverage records, revision binding, non-conclusions, and an editorial conversation substrate. What is missing is the governed object that stands **above individual readings** and can lawfully say what the Work appears to be doing across its covered structure.

This programme opens that object.

## II. The primitive

The new primitive is:

> **Editorial Reading**

An Editorial Reading is a revision-bound, coverage-licensed synthesis of admitted developmental observations about a Work.

It is **not**:
- a proposal;
- a revision;
- a rewrite;
- a score;
- a ranking;
- a style verdict;
- a source-fact checker;
- a prediction of reader response;
- an inference of author intent;
- a summary generated from raw manuscript prose without governed reading evidence.

Its first job is **to help the writer see the Work**.

## III. Relation to existing objects

### Developmental Reading

A Developmental Reading remains:

`one commissioned lens × one governed scope × one frozen revision × recorded coverage`

It produces admitted `DevelopmentalObservation` objects.

### Editorial Reading

An Editorial Reading is above those readings:

`many admitted observations + explicit coverage + revision identity → governed synthesis`

It does not erase or replace the source readings.

Every material claim in an Editorial Reading must remain traceable to the observations and coverage that license it.

## IV. Coverage is authority

Whole-Work language is not licensed by fluency.

An Editorial Reading may make a whole-Work structural claim only when its recorded coverage is sufficient for that claim.

### Complete whole-Work warrant

A whole-Work structural claim requires:

- the authored structure for the target revision;
- body-depth coverage across every in-scope authored section;
- no unread span material to the claim;
- traceable admitted observations supporting the synthesis.

If coverage is incomplete, the Editorial Reading must speak from the actual coverage:

> "Across the chapters read so far…"

not:

> "The book…"

Coverage limits remain part of the reading. They are not hidden UX machinery.

## V. Synthesis happens over observations, not prose

The synthesis layer consumes governed observations and their evidence.

It does **not** re-read arbitrary raw manuscript text as an ungoverned second reader.

This preserves a single chain of epistemic custody:

`manuscript revision → governed reading → admitted observation → editorial synthesis`

not:

`manuscript → hidden second prompt → confident whole-book verdict`

## VI. Non-conclusions remain binding

An Editorial Reading may synthesize across observations; it does not silently dissolve what those observations declared they do not establish.

The following remain unavailable as direct factual conclusions unless separately constituted:

- **author intent** — do not tell the writer what they meant;
- **reader effect** — do not claim what readers will feel or understand as fact;
- **editorial consequence as command** — do not turn a noticing into "therefore you should revise";
- **external truth** — the manuscript is not source-verified by this capability;
- **quality ranking** — no best/worst chapter, strongest/weakest passage, score, grade, or tier.

Lawful language describes the encounter with the text:

> "The book reads as organizing this question around…"

not:

> "You intended the book to…"

## VII. Observation has priority over intervention

An Editorial Reading may lawfully conclude:

> "I noticed this, and I would leave it."

A concern does not require a revision.

No synthesis step may manufacture a proposal merely because an observation exists.

The default order is:

`perceive → understand → discuss → consider → revise only if invited`

A later revision act must enter the existing governed editorial/revision substrate. This programme does not create a second mutation path.

## VIII. No ranking under A0

A0 does **not** authorize a Priority Map, top-six list, severity score, or consequentiality ranking.

Coverage licenses whether a claim may be made. It does not by itself license saying which concern matters most.

Grouping observations into non-evaluative patterns may be considered later, but ranking those patterns requires its own founder ruling.

## IX. No suppression disguised as simplicity

If a future surface presents a small number of patterns over many observations, the underlying observations must remain reachable.

Compression may organize evidence.

It may not silently discard it.

## X. Writer mode and professional vocabulary

A future writer-facing experience may accept ordinary language such as:

- "Read my book."
- "Something is not working here."
- "Help me understand the arc."
- "Reread this chapter for continuity."

A professional surface may name lens and scope directly.

Those are two ways of commissioning the same governed reading, not two intelligence systems.

Equivalent intent must resolve to equivalent governed work.

## XI. Manuscript primacy

The Work remains primary.

The Editorial Reading is an instrument for returning attention to the manuscript, not a destination that replaces it.

Any future interface must preserve:

- section-precise return to the Work;
- visible relation between concern and evidence;
- conversation without losing place;
- revision as an optional later act;
- safe return from revision to the same manuscript location.

## XII. Explicitly out of scope

A0 authorizes no implementation.

It does not authorize:

- database migrations;
- a new table;
- a new API route;
- a new model call;
- a new prompt;
- multi-pass orchestration;
- ranking or prioritization;
- repetition/similarity search;
- quotation or citation verification;
- external research;
- manuscript mutation;
- UI redesign;
- deployment.

Those require later bounded acts.

## XIII. Falsifiers for the constitution

A future implementation contradicts this constitution if any of the following is true:

1. **Partial-as-whole** — a whole-Work claim is emitted without sufficient recorded coverage.
2. **Observation laundering** — synthesis states as fact something its source observations marked as not established.
3. **Raw-prose bypass** — the synthesis rereads arbitrary manuscript prose outside the governed observation/coverage chain.
4. **Automatic intervention** — every concern becomes a proposal or rewrite.
5. **Ranking smuggled in** — concerns are ordered by importance without separate authority.
6. **Evidence suppression** — summarized patterns make their constituent observations unreachable.
7. **Second mutation substrate** — Editorial Reading gains its own apply/edit mechanism instead of handing off to the existing governed revision system.
8. **Manuscript displacement** — the reading becomes the place the writer must manage instead of a way back into the Work.
9. **Writer/Pro divergence** — equivalent commissions produce materially different governed readings solely because one used ordinary language and one used professional labels.

## XIV. Programme sequence

If A0 is ratified, the bounded sequence is:

1. **A1 — Existing-substrate census**
   - identify the exact observations, coverage records, revision identities, and persistence seams already available;
   - prove what can be reused;
   - identify what does not exist.

2. **A2 — Editorial Reading contract**
   - define the object shape, provenance, revision binding, coverage warrant, and non-conclusion inheritance;
   - still non-executing.

3. **A3 — Multi-pass / whole-Work coverage contract**
   - preserve the existing per-read ceiling;
   - define how multiple bounded readings earn whole-Work warrant;
   - synthesis over observations, not raw prose.

4. **A4 — Composition contract**
   - define lawful synthesis language and defeat conditions;
   - no ranking.

5. **A5 — Runtime implementation + falsification**
   - only after A1–A4 are ratified.

6. **A6 — Writer-facing experience**
   - manuscript-first presentation and return-to-passage behavior.
