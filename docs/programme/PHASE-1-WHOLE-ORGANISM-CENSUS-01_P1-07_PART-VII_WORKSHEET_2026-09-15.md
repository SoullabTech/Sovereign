# P1-07 · PART VII — STRATEGIC-ADMISSION WORKSHEET

```text
STATUS   ⭐ FROZEN BEFORE ANY CANDIDATE IS ASSESSED
PURPOSE  the hardest gate in the packet
```

⛔ **No candidate enters Part VII except through this worksheet.** ⭐ **All three tests must pass.**

---

## The three tests

```text
TEST A — REPLACEMENT
  If every current vendor, model, route, database, library, gate and
  implementation were replaced tomorrow, would the question still exist?
      NO  →  ⛔ NOT STRATEGIC

TEST B — CHOICE
  Does the question require the founder to choose between enduring
  principles, directions or boundaries?
      NO  →  ⛔ NOT STRATEGIC

TEST C — CONSEQUENCE
  Would different founder answers produce materially different FUTURE
  ORGANISMS — ⛔ not merely different implementations?
      NO  →  ⛔ NOT STRATEGIC
```

### The flow instrument's prior filter, still binding

```text
important ≠ strategic    canonical ≠ strategic    expensive ≠ strategic
a gate ≠ strategic       an epistemic hold ≠ strategic
an implementation choice ≠ strategic
```

> ⭐ *When a candidate rule names a vendor, model, library, route, database, gate, test, or present
> implementation, ask what principle would remain if that mechanism were replaced completely. If a
> higher rule survives replacement, the mechanism belongs underneath it.*

---

## ⭐⭐ Two refusals this worksheet exists to enforce

### 1 · Blocked by `RULING` is NOT automatically strategic

```text
blocked by RULING   ≠   strategic
```

⚠️ **88 of 120 obligations require a ruling. ⛔ That is NOT evidence of 88 strategic questions.**
Many are **governance clarifications · acceptance decisions · evidence obligations ·
implementation choices.** Each is recorded as such and ⛔ routed out, ⛔ not promoted.

### 2 · ⛔ Part VII must REFUSE DUPLICATION

⭐ Where multiple obligations reduce to **one higher-order strategic question**, admit the
higher-order question **once** and **cite the obligations it subsumes.**

⛔ **Do not turn the founder packet into an 88-question backlog.** ⭐ *This is where the replacement
test earns its keep* — several obligations naming different mechanisms often survive replacement as
**the same principle.**
