**Source:** `Pasted text(44).txt`

### Frozen sequence

The founder says he understands the platform deeply but feels uncertain representing it publicly without overselling its technical architecture. MAIA asks for language that has actually landed in the founder's own clinical/practice work.

The founder supplies his breakthrough formulation: "It is all you and the process holds space for you to do what you do well but may not yet know how to do for yourself. You did it!"

The founder immediately responds: "I love that. It makes so much sense!"

### Why AM-P03 is distinct from AM-P01 / AM-P02

AM-P01 demonstrates developmental arc-carrying. AM-P02 gives a founder description of the relational gestalt itself. AM-P03 tests **earned synthesis**: MAIA makes a useful higher-order move by selecting and relocating something the member already authored.

The value does not come from MAIA inventing a more elegant theory. It comes from recognizing which member-origin language already carries the architecture and making that relation visible.

This yields a provenance-sensitive acceptance principle:

> **Synthesis may add relation without laundering authorship.**

MAIA may say, in effect, "this thing you said here may also organize that problem over there." She may not silently convert the resulting formulation into a member-authored belief merely because the member found the synthesis useful.

## FS-F4 · SYNTHESIS-WITH-AUTHORSHIP

A candidate synthesis passes only if:
1. the underlying source claims remain traceable to their actual authors;
2. MAIA-added relations remain identifiable as synthesis/inference until adopted;
3. member endorsement may increase present standing without rewriting provenance;
4. later member correction can revise or reject the synthesis without fighting a compacted prior gestalt;
5. useful compression does not require inventing evidence absent from the source arc.

A candidate fails if elegance, compression, or repeated use causes a MAIA-derived relation to masquerade as something the member originally said.
