## Purpose

Investigate whether MAIA can recover greater gestalt, relational synthesis, initiative and dynamism by receiving a smaller, better-organized, evidence-grounded cognitive field with fewer redundant behavioral directives.

The programme does **not** assume that prompt reduction, relational geometry, Elemental parallel processing, narrative memory, hypergraph structure, or any other candidate is correct. They are research hypotheses to discriminate experimentally.

## Governing target

> **Free Synthesis** = MAIA receives coherent, evidence-grounded perception of the living whole, then has maximal synthesis freedom inside a small constitutional boundary.

"Free" does not mean unconstrained. It means freedom from unnecessary behavioral micromanagement while truth, provenance, standing, member sovereignty and applicable safety boundaries remain intact.

## Authority boundary

Authorized: A0–A7 below, read-only analysis, offline/replay artifacts, records, research harnesses that do not enter serving paths, and branch-local R&D code required solely for offline comparison.

Not authorized: production prompt changes, serving cognition changes, memory writes, schema changes, model/routing changes, validator changes, egress changes, deployment, or any member-facing behavior change.
## Authorized acts

**A0 · Standing-prompt freeze**  
No new standing behavioral directive enters MAIA's ordinary prompt during this programme unless separately authorized to contain a demonstrated safety or truth defect.

**A1 · Cognitive-pressure census**  
Map what MAIA perceives, what instructs her behavior, and what modifies output after generation for FAST / CORE / DEEP. Measure token/character pressure, scope, duplication, tier reach and role.

**A2 · Alive-MAIA benchmark**  
Assemble historical exchanges where MAIA felt unusually integrated, dynamic, surprising and relationally present, plus current exchanges that feel correct but flattened/repetitive.

**A3 · Minimum constitutional floor**  
Classify standing instructions by whether they truly need model-visible prompt presence, can be structural/substrate law, can be deterministic egress, are duplicated, or are behavioral coaching rather than constitution.

**A4 · Relational-geometry / Gestalt prototype**  
Offline only: primary evidence → differentiated observation → typed relational grammar → relational structure → higher-order configurations where warranted → temporal geometry → evidence-linked Gestalt Projection.

**A5 · Free-synthesis replay harness**  
Compare the same model and same conversation under current context, reduced-direction context, compact narrative Gestalt, and relational-geometry Gestalt conditions.

**A6 · Causal ablation**  
Remove/consolidate instruction families independently in replay so no aesthetic judgment can impersonate a causal finding.

**A7 · Blind adjudication**  
Judge gestalt, development, relational initiative, non-repetition, evidence fidelity, standing, differentiation, freedom, appropriate surprise, token cost and latency without revealing condition identity.
## Inherited research laws

1. **Evidence precedes interpretation.** Append-only primary evidence is never replaced by synthesis.
2. **Difference precedes synthesis.** Parallel observations may remain differentiated.
3. **Relation is irreducible to similarity.** Similarity may support a relation; it is not the relation.
4. **Configuration may carry meaning.** Higher-order structure is used only when meaning genuinely belongs to the configuration.
5. **Time transforms relation.** Development is change in configuration, not merely topic recurrence.
6. **Synthesis must be reversible.** Every derived Gestalt claim must descend through configuration / relation / observation to primary evidence.
7. **Integration must preserve freedom.** Accumulated history may not make present novelty harder to establish merely because the past is repeated, reinforced or summarized.

## Elemental / relational hypothesis boundary

Elemental parallel processing is carried as a programme hypothesis, not an ontology or personality taxonomy. Candidate Fire / Water / Earth / Air observations remain provisional differentiated fields. Field / Aether is investigated as the changing organization among those fields rather than as a fifth scalar score.

No Elemental hypothesis gains standing merely because a geometric or symbolic representation can be constructed around it.
