## Required behaviors

Both candidates must demonstrate:

1. **breathing / settling**  
   The field visibly finds a relational arrangement and can settle.

2. **meaningful attraction**  
   Explicitly related nodes influence one another physically.

3. **hover intelligence**  
   Hover reveals immediate neighbors, edges, and a concise insight.

4. **drag response**  
   Moving a node affects its local relational neighborhood.

5. **mental-map preservation**  
   The wider field remains recognizable after local movement.

6. **containment**  
   Regions remain meaningful rather than dissolving into one global graph.

7. **edge inspection**  
   Hovering a connection reveals its verb and concise rationale.

## Anti-hairball rule

The full relation graph may remain faintly present, but hover/focus must:
- highlight direct relations;
- fade unrelated material;
- reveal relation text on demand.
