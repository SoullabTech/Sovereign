# 1 · Finding 1 — Composite warrant formation

ACT 12 found that ACT 11 successfully prohibited source-count / accumulation laundering but did not explicitly distinguish that from a legitimate separately governed multi-source inference.

ACT 11A resolves the ambiguity by introducing one narrow conceptual distinction:

`SUPPORT SET ≠ COMPOSITE WARRANT`

Multiple partial sources may form the support set for a new composite inference.

They do not automatically create relation standing.

A lawful composite warrant must itself remain inspectable as an epistemic object with:

- proposition;
- author / authority;
- support set;
- provenance;
- composition method;
- dependence / independence assumptions;
- jurisdiction;
- uncertainty;
- boundaries / invalidity conditions;
- licensed relation semantics;
- standing.

### Required anti-laundering result

`partial(A) + partial(B) + partial(C)`

does **not** automatically yield:

`warranted(R)`.

**PASS**

### Required triangulation viability result

A separately authored and governed composite inference may be evaluated and may earn bounded standing if its method and support justify that standing.

ACT 11A does not prohibit legitimate multi-source inference.

**PASS**

# 2 · Finding 2 — Jurisdiction-bounded adoption

ACT 12 found that ACT 11 preserved member authority and jurisdiction in principle but did not state explicitly enough how adoption applies to a proposition containing semantic components from different jurisdictions.

ACT 11A resolves the ambiguity with the governing rule:

> **Adoption may change authorship or standing only within the adopter's epistemic jurisdiction.**

Strong member authority remains intact for:

- lived experience;
- personal meaning;
- values;
- preferences;
- interpretations of one's own experience;
- one's own intentions;
- memories as reported;
- relationships as personally experienced.

Adoption alone does not establish external claims about:

- another person's motive;
- another person's intention;
- diagnosis;
- hidden causal mechanisms;
- scientific fact;
- historical fact;
- metaphysical fact;
- universal psychological law.

### Required member-authority result

A member may adopt personal meaning proposed by MAIA.

**PASS**

### Required external-fact boundary result

That adoption does not promote another person's hidden motive or external causal fact.

**PASS**

### Required mixed-proposition result

Different semantic components may retain different standing.

**PASS**
