## The architecture floor — five classes

```text
DURABLE CREATIVE ORIENTATION
  what kind of Work I am making
  writer-authored · Work-level · dormant unless invoked

PRESENT INQUIRY
  what I am attending to now
  writer-authored · present authority · may cease without inferred unfinishedness

CURRENT MAIA TURN
  what MAIA is contributing now
  ephemeral · structurally non-accumulating

HISTORICAL PROCESS CONTEXT
  what actually happened between writer and MAIA
  append-only · selectively reachable · never self-resurfacing

MANUSCRIPT / REVISION STATE
  what the Work is and how it changed
  canonical · append-only · provenance-bearing
```

**The three primitives are not competing organizers. Each is excellent in its place:**

```text
PLACE     the natural EVIDENCE / ADDRESS layer for local material —
          encounter, navigation, staleness at changed prose          (from A)
INQUIRY   excellent for PRESENT WRITER ATTENTION                     (from B)
TURN      excellent for CURRENT MAIA CONTRIBUTION                    (from C)
```

> ### Different kinds of things require different persistence, visibility, authority and retrieval laws.

**The portable law, now stronger:**
> **Container properties do not confer content properties.** Where something lives cannot determine who authored it, whether it is presently authoritative, whether it remains relevant, or whether it should be visible.

## Acceptance set — inherited falsifiers, each traced to the cell that produced it

| # | The amended candidate must prove | From |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One developmental observation remains **one** observation even when its evidence spans many loci | A/M2 |
| 2 | Current MAIA contribution cannot accumulate into a pile — **structurally, not by etiquette** | B/M2 · C/M1 w4 |
| 3 | Historical process survives without resurfacing itself; one past thing is retrievable **by meaning** without reopening its siblings | B/M3 |
| 4 | Recall and fresh re-reading are **epistemically distinct**; MAIA never reconstructs the past and presents it as memory | C/M3 |
| 5 | Comparison is possible **on request** without becoming a standing container — the evidence for a synthesis can be seen, then leave | C/M2 |
| 6 | Deferral is possible **through a writer act**, without the system inventing `unfinished`, attention or obligation | C/M2 |
| 7 | Creative orientation persists while its **application** does not — dormant for years without becoming a rubric | B/M4 · C/M4 |
| 8 | D5 provenance stays **atomic and two-axis** — content ancestry and process context captured together at adoption | C/M3 · C/M4 |
| 9 | Local place remains useful **without owning identity** — navigation, evidence and staleness spatial, observations independently addressable | A/M1 · A/M3 |
| 10 | Nothing infers writer attention from silence, elapsed time, topic classification or inactivity | D6 · C/M1 w5 |

### ⚠️ 11 · Added — not from the walk, but from ratified law

**Phase 0 statement 7**: *hide machinery, not meaningful authority or choice.* **Five classes is an ontology, and the walk's conclusion runs directly at that statement.**

> **11. The writer must never need to know there are five classes.**

⭐ **This is the amended architecture's own first hazard**: its correctness is achieved by structure, and structure the writer has to learn is complexity that has escaped the machinery. **A multi-class architecture that the writer can feel has failed Phase 0 even if it satisfies 1–10.**
