than reproducing the attractive lines of a mock.

## HOME TRUTH LAW (founder, 2026-09-07)

> Every piece of process-bearing information shown about a Work must declare one
> of three provenances:
>
> ```text
> FACT     mechanically observed
> WRITER   explicitly declared by the writer
> MAIA     offered interpretation, clearly identified as such
> ```
>
> **The Home may never render MAIA interpretation as Work state.**
>
> Facts may orient. Writer declarations may carry meaning. MAIA may invite
> reflection. **None may silently become another.**

This is the same seam the vocabulary work found (meaning / register /
consequence) and the guidance contract's §1 rule (availability derived, never
authored), arriving at the first surface a writer sees.

## Preserving the jobs without inventing the answers

The walk established these needs. They survive — the substrate for them changes
from inferred to declared.

**Process state → a small writer act.**

```text
THIS WORK IS CURRENTLY   drafting · gathering · restructuring · developing ·
                         revising · resting · other ___
or freer                 "Where are you with this work?"
```

Authored state, not algorithmic state.

**Next act → the writer's own note to themselves.**

```text
WHEN I RETURN   ______________________________

then Home may truthfully say:
  You left yourself: "Re-read Chapter 6 and decide whether Fire belongs
  before Water."
```

Continuity of authorship. The inferred version — *"Next: revise Chapter 6"* — is
covert direction wearing the same clothes.

**Inspiration → reclassified, not removed.** The job is real; the founder reached
for it unprompted. What changes is its class:

```text
FROM YOU        ideas kept · fragments · marked passages · questions left
FROM THE WORK   factual return points · last passages · recent kept versions ·
                Development observations the writer chose to retain
OPTIONAL SPARK  non-directive invitations, clearly offered, never presented as
                what the Work needs
```

Creativity preserved without MAIA quietly becoming the muse-author.

**Visual identity → honest now, writer-chosen later.**

```text
NOW      neutral work-type icon / typographic treatment
LATER    writer uploads or selects, if authorized
NEVER    auto-generated cover masquerading as the Work's identity
```

## What this input does not do

It draws no wireframe, defines no card schema, and authorizes no build or
migration. It establishes only which lines a design may truthfully write.
