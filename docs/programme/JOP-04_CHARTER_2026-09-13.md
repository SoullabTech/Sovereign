### The crossing chain, and its dangerous shortcut

```text
JARVIS MAY OBSERVE
        ↓
JARVIS MAY PROPOSE
        ↓
AUTHORITY MAY BE GRANTED
        ↓
JARVIS MAY EFFECT
        ↓
THE WORLD MAY CHANGE
```

⛔ **The shortcut:**

```text
JARVIS OBSERVES ─────────────► WORLD CHANGES
```

⭐ **This is structurally identical to AI ghostwriting** — the action-side twin of the arrow the
Writer's Studio board forbids:

```text
WRITER'S STUDIO                    JOP-04

SOURCE / MATERIAL                  JARVIS MAY OBSERVE
MAIA MAY NOTICE                    JARVIS MAY PROPOSE
WRITER MAY RECOGNIZE               AUTHORITY MAY BE GRANTED
WRITER MAY DECIDE                  JARVIS MAY EFFECT
WORK MAY CHANGE                    THE WORLD MAY CHANGE

⛔ SOURCE ──► WORK CHANGES          ⛔ OBSERVES ──► WORLD CHANGES
```

Both collapse the same middle: **the point where a human grants permission for a specific thing.**
Neither is refused on taste. Both are refused because the granting step is not in the path.

⭐ **The symmetry is load-bearing, not decorative.** JWS-01's arrow test is already ratified and
mechanical. JOP-04 inherits the *form* of that test — a crossing is legitimate only if every arrow
is separately evidenced — while the crossings themselves are its own.

⛔ Not authorized in this lane, under any finding:

- **No effect-bearing capability implemented, registered, wired, or shipped.** Including the
  disposable-branch specimen in the census instrument — it is a *design subject*, not a build task.
- **No GitHub write implementation.** No `create_branch`, `apply_patch`, `commit`, `open_pr`,
  `merge_pr`.
- **No distribution of any new mutating authority** (JOP-01, above).
- **No re-authoring** of the Living Work Model, operator jurisdiction, Desktop custody, or the read
  capability substrate.
- **No final effect vocabulary ruled.** The census searches for **dimensions**; it does not invent
  their final names (instrument §3).

  `ConsciousnessIntegration` does not become an `OperatorIntegration` by vocabulary replacement
  while preserving hidden psychological assumptions. Such code is **foreign prior art, not inherited
  JOP substrate** (instrument §6).
- **No member in the operator graph** — R2 holds without exception.
