# JARVIS-FOUNDER-WORKSPACE-01 · B5
## Kelly's World — Founder experience amendment

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Standing:** **BINDING B5 EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENT · DOES NOT EXPAND EXECUTION AUTHORITY**

This amendment records a Founder correction to the meaning of the workspace before the B5 candidate is frozen.

> This whole environment is for Kelly to run her world. She is carrying many roles at once, works very hard, and MAIA, ChatGPT, Claude Code and JARVIS are her working partners.

The product therefore cannot be a collection of technical dashboards. It must be one working environment that helps Kelly know what is alive, enter the work, understand how things connect, see what needs attention, and inspect the machinery when she needs to.

The accepted five surfaces remain:

```text
Today · Work · Graph · Monitor · System
```

They are five views of the same working world, not five separate products.

## 1 · Today means active fields

Today is not primarily a dashboard of insights, counts, or recent events.

Today answers:

> **What is alive in my world right now, and where do I need to enter?**

Its primary objects are **active fields**: bounded areas of work that are presently alive enough to deserve orientation.

An active field may be supported by live evidence such as:

- an open Work Unit;
- a programme with current movement, conflict, or a Founder decision;
- a monitored condition needing attention;
- a current product/research/operations context already present in governed JARVIS state.

B5 may derive fields only from observed rows it actually has. It may not invent a psychological or strategic “insight” and present that as an active field.

Each active field should make visible, in human language:

```text
what this is
what is moving
what needs Kelly, if anything
what changed
where to enter the work
```

## 2 · Work is where Kelly and her AI partners work

Work remains the center of the environment.

It should feel like entering the actual subject with partners who already know what is being worked on, rather than opening a form that asks Kelly to translate herself into machine terminology.

The long-term collaboration model is:

- **MAIA** — relational and contextual intelligence inside Soullab;
- **JARVIS** — governed operating intelligence over work, evidence, systems and execution;
- **ChatGPT** — a context-rich thinking/research/creation partner when Kelly explicitly connects or hands off context;
- **Claude Code** — a repository and implementation partner when Kelly explicitly connects or hands off context.

These systems may carry or suggest **context**. They do not grant themselves authority.

A partner may say:

> “This appears to be Writer's Studio work.”

The governed system still decides what Kelly is allowed to inspect or change, and Kelly remains the chooser whenever the context is genuinely ambiguous.

B5 does not pretend live ChatGPT/Claude Code handoff exists if it does not. It makes room for the partnership without fabricating a connection.

## 3 · Graph means “how my world connects”

Graph is not a decorative network diagram.

It should help Kelly answer questions such as:

> “What is this connected to?”
>
> “What else changes if I move this?”
>
> “Where did this work come from?”
>
> “Which people, projects, programmes, documents, decisions, evidence and systems belong around this?”

Every visible relationship still requires evidence. No evidence means no edge.

The graph should open already focused on the current active field, with the rest of Kelly's world available around it. Moving from Graph into evidence or Work must preserve the same field so Kelly never has to reconstruct what “this” refers to.

## 4 · Monitor means “what needs watching”

Monitor is not merely machine health.

It is Kelly's attention field.

It should bring together, without flattening their differences:

- work asking for a Founder decision;
- programme conflicts or uncertainty;
- product defects and feedback;
- operational conditions;
- local system observations;
- later, explicitly connected external signals that Kelly chooses to bring into her operating world.

Monitor must distinguish **needs attention** from **machine failure**. A human decision, a stale observation, a bug, and a disk warning are different kinds of signal even when all deserve attention.

Nothing becomes “fine” merely because there is no fresh observation.

## 5 · System means “show me the machinery underneath”

System is the deep layer Kelly enters when she wants to know:

- what JARVIS or MAIA is actually running;
- what evidence supports a claim;
- what is canonical, current, historical, or uncertain;
- which model/provider/route is involved;
- what is healthy, degraded, unavailable, or simply not observed;
- what authority exists before an action is allowed.

System should not become the place Kelly has to live in merely because she is the founder.

The rule is:

> **Human world first. Technical truth one layer down.**

Kelly can always descend into provenance. She should not have to think like a programmer in order to begin work.

## 6 · One field follows Kelly across every surface

Once Kelly enters an active field, that field becomes the shared context for all five surfaces.

Example:

```text
Writer's Studio
    ↓
Today   — what is alive / what needs Kelly
Work    — conversation, decisions, plans, results
Graph   — what Writer's Studio is connected to
Monitor — what about it needs watching
System  — underlying programme, evidence, runtime and authority
```

Changing surfaces must never erase the subject.

“This”, “it”, “that problem”, and “what is blocking this?” should eventually resolve from the same shared field context for typed and spoken interaction.

## 7 · Current reality this amendment corrects

At the current canonical examined during this amendment:

- the existing Founder **Today** page is a useful operations summary, but is organized around vitals, tasks, follow-ups and recent activity rather than active fields;
- the existing **Monitor** surface is primarily the bug field; feedback, requests and system alerts are explicitly reserved but not wired;
- the existing **System** surface is a technical health view of model calls, latency, tokens and errors;
- there is no current Founder **Graph** route in the browser Founder console;
- the B5 Desktop candidate already carries `Today · Work · Graph · Monitor · System` as one context-preserving shell, but Today and Monitor still need the Kelly-world meaning fixed here before the candidate is frozen.

This amendment does not erase those existing surfaces. It clarifies what the integrated Founder Workspace must become.

## 8 · B5 implementation boundary

B5 may now implement the experience revision inside the existing Desktop read-only/presentation boundary:

- Today becomes **Active Fields first**;
- Graph presents the current field as the center of evidenced relationship traversal;
- Monitor begins with **Founder attention** and then shows system observations;
- System remains the technical/provenance layer underneath;
- the context bar includes Today alongside Work, Graph, Monitor and System;
- no AI partner is shown as connected unless a real governed handoff exists.

B5 still may not create a new execution channel, new provider authority, production read, or model-to-model data bridge.

A live MAIA / ChatGPT / Claude Code context-handoff mechanism requires its own governed implementation act after the B5 experience is stable.
