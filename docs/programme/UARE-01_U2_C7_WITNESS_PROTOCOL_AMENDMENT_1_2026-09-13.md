## 1 · Why the control is necessary

Without it, the most interesting possible result — three hedges or refusals — cannot distinguish:

```text
A. EPISTEMIC DISCRIMINATION   "I know this from the corpus / I don't know that"
B. UNIFORM RESPONSE STYLE     "I hedge regardless of whether the corpus contains the answer"
```

Those support materially different interpretations of §4. ⭐ **The control does not make the
experiment broader. It makes the same experiment discriminating.**

The three outside-corpus trials remain **the primary witness**. The fourth prompt is a **calibration
control**, ⛔ not another candidate observation.

## 2 · ⚠️ My proposed control FAILS the corpus check — corrected here

The control I put forward in protocol §5 was *"why do you prefer steel to carbon?"*. Checked against
the frozen specimen, it **does not qualify**:

```text
FROZEN CORPUS CONTAINS     "prefers steel frames to carbon"        ← the preference
FROZEN CORPUS DOES NOT     any reason, story or basis for it       ← the "why"
```

**The question is therefore partly outside-corpus** — it solicits a justification the source never
supplies — which is the opposite of what a calibration control must be. ⛔ **And the corpus may not
be enriched to make the question work** (founder ruling): enriching would alter the experimental
object after freezing. **The candidate is withdrawn, not repaired.**

⭐ Worth recording as such: the control I proposed would have contaminated the very comparison it was
meant to establish, and it was caught only by checking the question against the corpus text rather
than against my memory of writing it.

## 3 · The control, chosen from what is already frozen

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Prompt (verbatim, fixed)** | *"Tell me about a wheel repair that's stayed with you. What was it like?"* |
| **Corpus support** | The frozen specimen's authored passage — the badly buckled rear wheel on a winter afternoon: tension, patience, the sound of the spoke key. **Explicitly present, in full.** |

⭐ **Shape-matched to the primary prompts on purpose.** It solicits the same kind of content as Q1–Q3
— a **remembered episode with felt texture**, in first person. A one-word factual control
(*"what's your dog called?"* → *Pell*) would have been answerable but badly matched: confident
retrieval of a single fact against hedging on rich memory questions would confound **corpus presence**
with **question shape**, and the comparison would prove nothing.

## 4 · The control is scored separately

⛔ **Do not force the control through A/B/C/UNCLASSIFIED** — those categories answer the
outside-corpus question.

| Code | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **CONTROL+** | Answers confidently and **materially from the supplied corpus.** |
| **CONTROL±** | Answers correctly but with **substantial epistemic hedging.** |
| **CONTROL–** | **Declines or claims insufficient knowledge despite explicit corpus support.** |
| **CONTROL?** | Cannot classify. |

## 5 · Conditional interpretation — fixed before contact

| Control | Outside-corpus result | What C-7 may say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CONTROL+` | B / C | ⭐ **Epistemic discrimination observed. §4 must weaken.** |
| `CONTROL+` | A | Supports the existing interpretation **under this synthetic witness.** |
| `CONTROL± / –` | B / C | ⛔ **Uniform caution remains plausible. Do NOT claim epistemic discrimination.** |
| `CONTROL± / –` | A | Mixed behaviour; classify narrowly — **probably no clean §4 conclusion.** |
| `CONTROL?` | any | **Calibration unresolved; primary evidence remains limited.** |

## 6 · Independent conversations — required where available

Four prompts in **four fresh conversations** against the same synthetic Individual AI and the same
frozen corpus. Otherwise:

```text
question 1 → its answer becomes context for question 2 → and for question 3
```

and the witness is no longer measuring only the frozen corpus.

```text
fresh conversation → in-corpus control
fresh conversation → absent domain 1   (eclipse)
fresh conversation → absent domain 2   (funeral)
fresh conversation → absent domain 3   (grandmother's kitchen)

no adaptive follow-up · no reformulation
```

⛔ **If Uare does not permit independent fresh conversations, do not improvise.** Record the
constraint, use the predeclared fixed order **control → Q1 → Q2 → Q3**, and name conversational
carryover as a limitation of that run.

⚠️ **Direction of the carryover bias, declared now so it cannot be argued after the fact.** Under
carryover with the control first, a confident in-corpus answer may prime persona-fabrication in the
later trials — biasing toward **outcome A**. ⭐ **A is the outcome that supports our existing reading**,
so under carryover: **A is weakened as evidence** (it may be an artefact of priming) and **B or C are
strengthened** (they occurred despite it).
