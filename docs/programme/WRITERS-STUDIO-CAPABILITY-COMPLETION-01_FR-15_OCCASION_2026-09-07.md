# WRITER'S STUDIO — CAPABILITY COMPLETION · 01
## FR-15 · GOALS SUPPORT IS EVENT-RESPONSIVE, NOT STATE-REACTIVE

**Founder act** 2026-09-07, on `…_GOALS_SUPPORT_DECIDE_2026-09-07.md`
**Standing** OPERATIVE. Rules S-A … S-E.

> ### A state may make support relevant; only a member act may occasion it.

> **The writer can invite warmth, encouragement, even real companionship around
> a goal. But the Studio never turns their goal into a standing permission to
> pursue them.**

---

## RULE 1 · OCCASIONED BY ACTS, NEVER BY STATES  *(rules S-A, S-B)*

```text
MAY OCCASION SUPPORT
  declare the goal
  mark it met
  set it aside / release it
  explicitly ask MAIA for help with it

NEVER AN OCCASION
  opening Goals
  seeing the goal in the lower band
  page load / hydration
  progress changing
  a date arriving
  time passing
  merely having an `encourage` grant
```

**A door is not an invitation to speak through it.**

And **not every edit is an act**: changing `3,000 → 3,500` must not make MAIA
chirp at the writer.

## RULE 2 · REPETITION MADE STRUCTURALLY IMPOSSIBLE — for the same act  *(rule S-E)*

Answered **yes** — and deliberately **not** with `lastSupportedAt`, a cadence, or
a motivational history, each of which would build the surveillance the rule
exists to avoid.

> **An eligible member act may produce at most ONE support opportunity in the
> handling of that act. Rendering the resulting state can never reproduce it.**

```text
POST / act      writer marks goal met      → may yield one occasion-bound response
GET  / render   goal remains met           → cannot yield support
```

> ### Support may come from an EVENT. Never reconstruct support from STATE.

That prevents *furniture with a heartbeat* by construction. An explicit later
Ask is **a new member act**, so the writer stays free to seek support again.

### The boundary, pinned hard

```text
GOAL GET / RENDER PATH
  must be INCAPABLE of generating support.

GOAL MEMBER-ACT PATH
  may create ONE occasion for support.

EXPLICIT MAIA ASK
  is itself a new member act.
```

> **That one boundary makes the architecture much safer than trying to teach
> the model "don't nag."**

## RULE 3 · GRANT · OCCASION · PERCEPTION STAY THREE  *(rule S-D)*

```text
GRANT       may MAIA support this goal in this way?
OCCASION    has the writer done something that makes support relevant now?
PERCEPTION  what material is MAIA separately authorized to perceive?
```

**No arrow may collapse those three.** `help_me_work_with_it` gives MAIA **zero
additional manuscript access.** It may work from the writer-declared goal, its
truthful measurable state, and the member's words in the present interaction.
Anything beyond that goes through FR-06's perception architecture.

## RULE 4 · THE THREE GRANTS ARE GENUINELY DIFFERENT RELATIONSHIPS

```text
track_only              completely quiet

encourage               on an eligible act, brief bounded encouragement
                          declaration    → acknowledge the intention
                          met            → celebrate what the writer accomplished
                          set aside      → RESPECT THE CHOICE, never persuade back
                        no conversation is forced open

help_me_work_with_it    still writer-initiated. The writer asks; MAIA may help
                        reconnect with why the goal matters, think through what
                        feels stuck, reconsider whether the goal still fits,
                        identify possibilities for a next act, and revise or
                        release it ONLY through the writer's choice
```

> **Companionship around authorship, not project management.**

## RULE 5 · INVARIANT 3 OPERATES RELATIONALLY, NOT BY SURVEILLANCE  *(rule S-C)*

A writer may ask repeatedly. **No dependency counter, motivation score,
longitudinal reassurance tracker, or "three asks means reliance" rule.**

Inside the present interaction only:

> MAIA may comfort, but must not compound reassurance indefinitely when the
> conversation itself is becoming a request for MAIA to supply the writer's
> agency.

The response then moves toward returning choice — continue, change the goal, set
it aside, talk about what they actually want, or stop for now — **without
diagnosing** (*"you are dependent on me"*) and **without demanding
productivity** (*"prove you've written something first"*).

```text
GOVERNING MOVEMENT      support → agency
NOT                     support → more support → more support
```

That is how *Comfort is permitted. Reliance is interrupted* operates **without
surveillance**.

## WHAT LANDS NOW

The **boundary**, not the behaviours — the same order as FR-13's grant and
FR-14's gate: an occasion becomes a thing only a member-act path can mint and a
render path structurally cannot. **No support prose, no MAIA call, and no path
to Goals is opened by this.**

```text
FR-15            OPERATIVE
occasion type    minted by acts only
render path      proven incapable
BUILD (support)  NOT AUTHORIZED
CEILING          untouched
```
