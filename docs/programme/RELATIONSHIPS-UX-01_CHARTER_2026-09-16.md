## 0. Authority and lane boundary

The current Relationships implementation contains meaningful relational intelligence but presents it through a taxonomy-forward, instrument-panel UI. The defect is not primarily color, spacing, or component polish. The defect is **choreographic and epistemic**: the system often asks the member to understand Soullab's ontology before Soullab has adequately attended to what brought the member there.

**This lane is a recomposition, not a reskin.**

Section I below is the load-bearing authority for all later UX, interaction, copy, visual, motion, information-architecture, and implementation decisions in `RELATIONSHIPS-UX-01`.

> **The visual design must follow from how Soullab believes one ought to attend, rather than deciding first how the page should look and retrofitting a philosophy afterward.**

A component may be elegant, beautiful, novel, or technically efficient and still fail this charter if it narrows attention prematurely, substitutes taxonomy for encounter, objectifies a living relationship, manufactures certainty, or mistakes a representation for the reality it represents.

No later section may override Section I without an explicit founder ruling and charter amendment.

# SECTION I — PHILOSOPHICAL & RESEARCH FOUNDATION

## I.1 — Telos: what Soullab is trying to cultivate

Soullab is not merely a software service for solving problems. It is a relational intelligence and consciousness platform oriented toward **presence, awareness, attending, participation, development, and discovery**.

Its purpose includes helping human beings become more able to:

- be present to what is actually here;
- notice before prematurely concluding;
- encounter persons, bodies, nature, meaning, and world as living relations rather than only as objects to manipulate;
- tolerate ambiguity and remain in contact with what is not yet known;
- perceive pattern without reducing a person to the pattern;
- differentiate without severing;
- articulate without mistaking language for reality;
- participate more consciously in what is unfolding;
- recognize development as transformation through time rather than accumulation of static traits;
- return understanding to lived experience and discover whether it actually fits.

Relationships is therefore not a side utility within Soullab. It is one of the clearest places where the platform's central thesis can become experiential:

> **Relationship is a primary medium through which consciousness becomes visible and development unfolds.**

The purpose of the Relationships surface is not simply to help a member manage relationships. It is to create conditions in which relationship can become more perceptible as a living process.

---

## I.2 — Attention comes before representation

The constitutional ordering is:

> **Attention comes before representation.  
> Relationship comes before object.  
> Process comes before category.  
> Presence comes before explanation.**

This does not reject representation, categories, analysis, models, language, or explicit reasoning. Soullab requires them in order to build, test, communicate, remember, distinguish, and act.

The ordering matters.

Analytic articulation is most generative when it is brought into service of contact with reality rather than allowed to replace that contact.

The platform must therefore be able to move between two complementary modes:

### Open attending

- whole before isolated part;
- context before abstraction;
- relationship before detached object;
- body and felt sense;
- atmosphere;
- image and metaphor;
- ambiguity;
- novelty;
- implicit meaning;
- change and movement;
- what is not yet nameable;
- what surprises us.

### Focused articulation

- distinctions;
- explicit language;
- categories;
- timelines;
- recurrence;
- hypotheses;
- pattern candidates;
- maps;
- elemental or geometric form;
- self/other differentiation;
- options and decisions;
- records and continuity;
- falsifiable propositions where possible.

Neither mode is sufficient by itself.

The governing relationship is:

> **Open attending → differentiation → articulation → testing → return to the living whole.**

---

## I.3 — McGilchrist: attending, not pop lateralization

Soullab's use of Iain McGilchrist is centered on **attending**.

It explicitly rejects the popular simplification that the right hemisphere is merely creative and the left hemisphere merely logical.

The relevant hypothesis is that different modes of attention disclose the world differently: one more broad, contextual, embodied, relational, receptive, implicit, and open to novelty; another more narrow, explicit, abstracting, categorizing, grasping, and oriented toward what has already been selected as relevant.

Soullab is intentionally **open-attending-led**. Focused analytic capacity follows and serves what has been encountered.

This is a design and research orientation, not an instruction to claim literal hemispheric activation from interface behavior.

The operative question is not:

> Which hemisphere is the member using?

It is:

> **What mode of attending does this moment require, and what becomes perceptible under that mode of attention?**

McGilchrist remains a generative research lens, not dogma. Hemispheric asymmetries and attentional differences are empirically real areas of neuroscience; broader phenomenological, cultural, philosophical, and metaphysical conclusions remain debated. Soullab must preserve those epistemic distinctions.

**Rule:** philosophical fertility never licenses empirical overclaim.

---

## I.4 — Relationship as a primary context of consciousness

Soullab begins from a relational hypothesis:

> **A person is not an isolated unit who subsequently enters relationships. Consciousness is continually formed, disclosed, challenged, and transformed through relation.**

Relevant relations include, but are not limited to:

- person ↔ person;
- self ↔ inner figure / part / complex;
- person ↔ family / group / community;
- person ↔ body;
- person ↔ place / nature;
- person ↔ vocation / work / creation;
- person ↔ image / symbol / inherited story;
- person ↔ absent or deceased other;
- person ↔ future possibility;
- person ↔ sacred / divine / mystery;
- human ↔ artificial intelligence;
- part ↔ whole;
- individual ↔ culture / world.

A relationship may therefore have qualities and dynamics that are not adequately described by assigning traits to either participant alone.

Soullab may investigate what it has called **the field**: the lived and changing organization of relation, including the member's experience, the counterpart as encountered or imagined, context, history, reciprocal movement, temporal sequence, and emergent interaction dynamics.

The word `field` remains a phenomenological and research term. It must not be used to imply that Soullab has scientifically measured an autonomous metaphysical entity merely because a member experiences a relational field.

---

## I.5 — Development is process, not classification

Development is not primarily a score, level, type, diagnosis, or inventory of acquired capacities.

It is **becoming through time**.

Relationships should therefore privilege phenomena such as:

- recurrence;
- variation within recurrence;
- threshold;
- rupture;
- repair;
- differentiation;
- boundary formation;
- increased or decreased capacity for contact;
- new perspective;
- release;
- integration;
- dissolution of an old organization;
- emergence of a new relational form;
- shifts in what can be perceived, tolerated, expressed, or chosen.

The timeline is therefore not administrative history. It is potentially one of the most important representations in the entire Relationships experience.

**Constitutional rule:** Relationships should show becoming, not score people.

No relational pattern is an identity. No developmental description is destiny.

---

## I.6 — Participation, presence, and a panentheistic horizon

Soullab is philosophically hospitable to a panentheistic and participatory understanding of reality: human beings do not stand outside an inert world merely observing and manipulating it; they participate in a reality that is living, relational, unfolding, and larger than the concepts through which it is represented.

For Soullab, this orientation supports:

- presence rather than constant instrumentalization;
- participation rather than detached mastery alone;
- wonder rather than premature closure;
- reverence for what exceeds conceptual capture;
- nature as relation rather than merely resource;
- openness to sacred meaning;
- a recognition that the whole can exceed the sum of explicit parts;
- humility about what consciousness and reality ultimately are.

This is a **philosophical, contemplative, and research horizon**, not a belief requirement for members.

A member may understand an experience psychologically, naturally, spiritually, theologically, symbolically, metaphysically, or in another way. Soullab may help them inquire into meaning; it must not manufacture spiritual certainty or present a metaphysical interpretation as an empirically verified fact.

The unknown is therefore not merely missing data. It may be the living edge from which new perception and form emerge.

---

## I.7 — Soullab's original process hypothesis

Soullab is not simply an interface that aggregates McGilchrist, Jung, enactivism, systems theory, attachment, somatics, relationship science, contemplative traditions, and process philosophy.

Its distinctive research architecture is a:

> **relational · geometric · elemental · alchemical process model of consciousness**

### Relational

Consciousness is investigated through relations, not only through isolated internal states.

### Geometric

Relational experience may display forms such as:

- distance / proximity;
- toward / away;
- center / edge;
- boundary / permeability;
- polarity;
- overlap / separation;
- reciprocity / asymmetry;
- triangulation;
- convergence / divergence;
- recursion / loop;
- nesting;
- changing orientation through time.

Geometry is a hypothesis that form can sometimes reveal organization that prose alone obscures. It is not a claim of mathematical precision where none exists.

### Elemental

Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Field / Weather function as a dynamic phenomenological grammar rather than personality buckets.

Possible relational meanings include:

- **Fire** — energy, desire, will, initiation, anger, eros, animation, transformation;
- **Water** — emotion, resonance, grief, longing, receptivity, attachment, flow;
- **Earth** — embodiment, actuality, behavior, boundary, consequence, reliability, form;
- **Air** — thought, distinction, language, narrative, interpretation, perspective;
- **Field / Weather** — the changing relational atmosphere and larger context in which elemental movements co-arise.

The primary question is not:

> Which element is this person?

It is:

> **What is happening in the field, how is it moving, what is seeking expression, and what configuration of elemental processes is emerging?**

### Alchemical

Alchemy names transformation and integration: the way tensions, wounds, oppositions, potentials, contradictions, encounters, and meanings may reorganize into new forms of consciousness and participation.

It does not license magical causation presented as established science.

### Process

No element, geometry, pattern, or identity is primary as a frozen object. The model tracks movement, recurrence, threshold, dissolution, emergence, integration, and return.

**Research question over the entire architecture:**

> **Does this model reveal dimensions of consciousness and development that become less visible when experience is treated primarily as isolated traits, symptoms, categories, or textual propositions?**

That question remains genuinely open.

## I.8 — MAIA's role: relational intelligence without becoming the authority

MAIA must not default only to:

`input → analyze → explain → recommend`

She must also be capable of:

`notice → receive → wonder → remain with → differentiate gently → invite participation → notice what changes`

Sometimes the most intelligent intervention is not another answer. It is an increase in perception.

MAIA may:

- reflect what the member has actually said;
- notice recurrence;
- offer provisional pattern language;
- distinguish observation from inference;
- invite body, context, image, relationship, and time back into awareness;
- help articulate what has become clearer;
- offer options without converting them into prescriptions;
- preserve continuity across time;
- return interpretations to the member for confirmation, revision, rejection, or suspension.

MAIA must not:

- define the member's relationship for them;
- claim access to the absent person's motives or interior state;
- turn a hypothesis into a diagnosis or verdict;
- use framework language to overrule lived experience;
- fill ambiguity merely because the model can generate text;
- equate confidence of expression with truth.

The crucial return question is:

> **Does this fit what you experience?**

Disagreement with MAIA is not evidence of resistance. It is information about the hypothesis and, where governed as research, information about the model.

---

## I.9 — Soullab is here to serve and to research

Soullab does not claim to already know what consciousness, relationship, development, attention, or the sacred ultimately are.

It is deliberately positioned at the edge between established knowledge and disciplined inquiry.

Its research posture is:

- phenomenologically open;
- theoretically pluralistic;
- longitudinal;
- process-oriented;
- idiographic where appropriate;
- willing to operationalize hypotheses;
- willing to preserve contradiction;
- interested in disconfirmation;
- careful about the level of claim evidence can support;
- unwilling to convert a fertile metaphor into a measurement simply by storing it in a database.

The platform should preserve:

- `none of these fit`;
- `not yet`;
- member rejection of a pattern;
- contradictory observations across time;
- cases where no recurrence emerges;
- cases where analysis makes experience less clear;
- cases where elemental or geometric lenses add nothing;
- cases where a favored framework fails;
- relationships that do not conform to expected arcs;
- unexpected phenomena the current architecture cannot yet name.

**What does not fit is not waste. It may be where the research begins.**

---

## I.10 — Service, product learning, and formal research must remain distinct

Three layers must not be silently collapsed.

### A. Personal reflection

Data and interaction used to help a member perceive continuity and possibility in their own life.

### B. Product learning

Evidence used to understand whether a Soullab experience is usable, comprehensible, accessible, safe, and useful as a product.

### C. Formal research

Systematic investigation intended to produce knowledge beyond the immediate member or product context.

These may inform one another, but they are not ethically or epistemically interchangeable.

Formal research may require additional consent, governance, privacy protection, methodological design, human-subjects review or other safeguards depending on the protocol and jurisdiction.

**The Relationships UX redesign does not itself authorize formal research enrollment, new research consent, or new data use.**

The interface must never pressure a member to produce data for Soullab's benefit.

No covert experimentation in emotionally vulnerable moments.

No research consent hidden inside general product consent.

No claim should outrun the method that produced it.

---

## I.11 — The Soullab rhythm

The working developmental and epistemic rhythm is:

1. **Attend** — encounter the whole situation before reducing it.
2. **Sense** — notice body, affect, atmosphere, energy, image, movement, ambiguity.
3. **Encounter** — allow what is other, surprising, resistant, or not-yet-known to remain present.
4. **Participate** — notice how one's own stance, attention, action, and presence are already part of the relational field.
5. **Differentiate** — distinguish self/other, past/present, observation/inference, signal/story, need/fear, known/imagined.
6. **Give form** — use temporal, linguistic, elemental, geometric, relational, or symbolic representation where it genuinely helps.
7. **Articulate** — name patterns, possibilities, distinctions, and questions.
8. **Test** — return the articulation to lived experience: does it fit, distort, omit, or reveal?
9. **Move** — experiment with conversation, boundary, repair, space, contact, creation, ending, or non-action.
10. **Integrate** — notice what is being reorganized.
11. **Return** — re-enter the living whole and attend again.

This is not a fixed therapeutic protocol or mandatory linear flow. It is the design grammar by which Soullab avoids making analysis the terminal state.

The return is crucial: the member, relationship, and perceivable world may no longer be exactly what they were before the articulation.

## I.12 — Constitutional UX laws derived from the philosophy

### LAW 1 — Meet the situation before the category
The threshold must let the member begin with what is actually happening.

**Forbidden at primary entry:** requiring `outer / inner / transpersonal`, bond type, theoretical framework, elemental category, geometric form, attachment style, diagnosis, or tool selection before the member can express the situation.

### LAW 2 — Ambiguity is a valid state
`I don't know`, `something feels off`, `something is moving`, and `I can't name it yet` are complete entry states.

Successful self-classification is never the price of access.

### LAW 3 — Perception before explanation
The interface should first help the member notice what is present before supplying an interpretation.

### LAW 4 — Patterns are invitations, never identities
A pattern is provisional and revisable. The member retains authority over fit.

### LAW 5 — Development is temporal
Recurrence, threshold, rupture, repair, transformation, and change across time are primary structures, not secondary history.

### LAW 6 — Representation remains subordinate to reality
Every map, pattern, element, geometry, framework, and AI reflection remains a representation.

### LAW 7 — Return every articulation to experience
Important interpretation should create a path back to questions such as:

- Does this fit?
- What changes when you see it this way?
- What is present now?
- What does this leave out?
- What becomes possible next?

### LAW 8 — Preserve the unknown
`None of these`, `not yet`, `something else`, and free member language are first-class states.

### LAW 9 — Member agency outranks data completeness
The UX must never become more interrogative merely because structured data would be useful to Soullab.

### LAW 10 — MAIA remains a living doorway
The member can move into dialogue with MAIA at every meaningful depth without losing relational context.

### LAW 11 — The whole includes body, context, time, and world
The interface should not reduce relationship to cognitive content or conversational text alone.

### LAW 12 — No visual device may seize attention merely to prove it exists
Motion, color, cards, alerts, scores, progress indicators, gamification, and hierarchy must serve orientation and perception rather than capture, urgency, compulsion, or artificial reward.

---

## I.13 — Section I acceptance questions

Before any wireframe, component, copy system, motion treatment, or visual concept is accepted, it must answer:

1. Does this widen attention or prematurely narrow it?
2. Does it meet lived experience before taxonomy?
3. Does it preserve uncertainty where uncertainty is authentic?
4. Does it treat the member as participant rather than object of interpretation?
5. Does it distinguish member-authored experience from MAIA's inference?
6. Can the member reject, revise, or suspend the interpretation?
7. Does analytic structure serve renewed contact with the whole?
8. Does it leave room for body, relation, context, time, image, and meaning?
9. Does it avoid implying knowledge of an absent person's interior state?
10. Does it permit a genuinely new or anomalous phenomenon to emerge?
11. Does it preserve the distinction between service, product learning, and formal research?
12. Does it avoid turning a process into a score or identity?
13. Does the interaction increase presence rather than merely increase activity?
14. Does it help the member notice their participation in the field without blaming them for the whole field?
15. Is the representation clearly less than the reality it represents?

Failure on these questions overrides visual preference.

---

## I.14 — What Section I forbids

No later Relationships work may introduce, without a separate explicit ruling:

- compatibility scoring;
- attachment-type scoring as identity;
- hidden psychological diagnosis;
- autonomous profiling of an absent person;
- confident motive inference about someone who is not present;
- mandatory elemental typing;
- fixed relational geometry presented as fact;
- metaphysical claims disguised as measurement;
- McGilchrist language presented as settled whole-brain neuroscience;
- gamification of vulnerability, repair, intimacy, grief, or developmental progress;
- research enrollment concealed within product use;
- UI pressure toward reconciliation;
- a framework that outranks member experience;
- a dashboard that makes the member feel evaluated rather than accompanied;
- data collection that exists primarily because the research team wants a complete row.

---

## I.15 — Research questions carried by the Relationships surface

Relationships may eventually help Soullab investigate questions such as:

- What becomes available when people are invited to attend before categorizing?
- Which relational phenomena are better understood longitudinally than through one-time assessment?
- Can AI help someone recognize recurrence without turning recurrence into identity?
- What sequences precede rupture, attempted repair, reorganization, boundary-setting, or ending?
- Can developmental thresholds be recognized as changes in relational organization rather than simple improvement/decline scores?
- What supports self/other differentiation without severing relationship?
- Can modes of attending be operationalized behaviorally without overclaiming hemispheric mechanism?
- Can MAIA participate in sense-making without becoming the authority that defines the member's relationship?
- Are there interaction-level patterns that cannot be adequately represented by aggregating individual traits?
- What aspects of relational process are generalizable, and what remains irreducibly singular?
- How does the experienced world change when a person shifts from instrumental control toward reciprocal attending and participation?
- Do elemental distinctions generate useful, non-reductive perception beyond ordinary affective, cognitive, somatic, and motivational labels?
- Can spatial or geometric representation reveal organization that text alone misses?
- Can those representations be falsified or revised by later experience?
- What constitutes transformation when the unit of interest is a living relational process?
- What modes of interface increase presence, perceptual richness, agency, and capacity for participation?

These are research questions, not product claims.

It differentiates without severing, gives form without mistaking the form for the living whole, names without reducing, tests what it thinks it sees, and returns every articulation to the unfolding reality from which it came. What does not fit remains an opening for discovery.**

# SECTION II — MEMBER EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

Section II translates Section I into the first Relationships encounter. It may not reverse the philosophical ordering above.

## II.1 — The threshold

### Page title

**Relationships**

Do not lead with **Relational Field** as the primary product title. `Field` may remain part of the deeper conceptual vocabulary.

### Opening invitation

> **What's happening between you?**
>
> Bring a person, a moment, a pattern, or something you cannot quite name. We can stay with what is here, notice what keeps returning, and explore what may be asking for understanding, expression, repair, a boundary, change—or simply attention.

Primary actions:

- **Start with what's happening**
- **Choose a relationship**

Persistent low-pressure path:

- **I'm not sure. I just want to talk with MAIA.**

That path opens MAIA with relational context but does not require creation of a relationship record.

---

## II.2 — Human intentions before tool names

Possible doorways:

### Something happened
A moment, conflict, encounter, message, discovery, attraction, loss, rupture, or event.

### We keep repeating this
A familiar relational movement or recurring loop.

### I feel distant, confused, or unsure
Something has changed or is difficult to read.

### Something needs repair
A rupture, injury, broken trust, misattunement, failed conversation, or disconnection.

### I need to have a difficult conversation
A need for timing, language, differentiation, clarity, or boundary.

### I want to understand this relationship more deeply
No crisis is required. Love, friendship, family, collaboration, mentorship, intimacy, vocation, nature, inner life, and sacred relationship belong here.

### I can't name it yet
Begin with sensing rather than classification.

These are **doorways, not diagnostic categories**.

---

## II.3 — Quiet routing

The existing tools remain, but the member should not have to select a technical module before ordinary-language orientation.

| Member intention | Quiet internal route |
| --- | --- |
| Something happened | Check-in / Relational Field |
| We keep repeating this | Dynamics Map |
| Distant / confused / unsure | Relational Field |
| Something needs repair | Repair Path |
| Difficult conversation | Repair-oriented path / MAIA |
| Deeper understanding | Relationship home + history + MAIA |
| Can't name it yet | Open sensing flow / MAIA |

Tool names may appear later as optional named practices.

---

## II.4 — Relationship creation becomes secondary

The member may begin with experience first and identify the relationship later.

When identity is needed, use ordinary human language:

- Who is this with?
- What do you call this relationship?
- Is this about a person, a group, something within you, or something larger?

`outer / inner / transpersonal` may remain internal persistence categories but should not normally be required threshold vocabulary.

Bond type remains optional metadata unless later evidence shows a member-facing reason to ask for it.

---

## II.5 — Landing architecture

The landing surface should have four perceptual layers.

### Layer A — Arrival
A large, quiet invitation focused on what is happening now.

### Layer B — Recent living relationships
Two or three recent relationships with human hierarchy:

- name;
- ordinary bond language where useful;
- one or two alive signals;
- last meaningful exploration / movement;
- simple continuation action.

Avoid administrative-card presentation.

### Layer C — What is asking for attention
Only where member-grounded evidence supports it:

- Something remains open with Jordan.
- This pattern has appeared more than once.
- You marked a turning point here last week.

No autonomous diagnosis. No unsupported inference.

### Layer D — Explore relationships
A quieter doorway to the fuller saved field: people, groups, inner figures, symbolic/transpersonal relations, and history.

Taxonomy belongs here if useful, not at the threshold.

# SECTION III — RELATIONSHIP HOME & DEVELOPMENT THROUGH TIME

## III.1 — Presence header

- relationship name;
- ordinary relationship label where useful;
- clear return to Relationships;
- first-class **Talk with MAIA** action;
- first-class **Check in** action;
- no dashboard metadata dump.

## III.2 — What is happening now

One coherent current-state surface may draw from:

- tone;
- signals;
- unresolved thread;
- member-authored words;
- member-endorsed pattern hypothesis;
- uncertainty;
- relevant recent movement.

It should feel like a relationship being sensed, not a status widget.

## III.3 — What would be useful?

Human description first; tool name second.

**Understand what's happening**  
Sense the tone and movement between you.  
*Relational Field*

**See what keeps repeating**  
Explore recurrence without turning it into a label.  
*Dynamics Map*

**Work with something that has broken**  
Explore repair, space, conversation, boundaries, or ending.  
*Repair Path*

## III.4 — Your story together

Timeline becomes a major visual structure rather than secondary history.

Possible entries include:

- check-in;
- note;
- reflection;
- rupture;
- repair;
- threshold / turning point;
- member-marked insight;
- accepted / rejected / revised pattern hypothesis;
- meaningful MAIA handoff where consent and provenance permit.

The timeline should make transformation perceptible without forcing every change into a neat developmental narrative.

## III.5 — Return to the living relationship

After any map, tool, reflection, elemental lens, or geometric representation, the experience should create a return:

- What feels different now?
- Does this still fit?
- What is alive between you now?
- What did this representation miss?
- What seems possible now that was not visible before?

# SECTION IV — REPRESENTATION, PROVENANCE & OPTIONAL LENSES

## IV.1 — Elemental Alchemy

Elemental language becomes available **after contact with lived experience**, not before it.

It must support:

- more than one element;
- changing elemental configuration;
- `none of these`;
- member-generated language;
- ability to reject the lens;
- temporal movement rather than fixed typing.

Possible invitation:

> If you look through an elemental lens, what becomes easier to notice?

## IV.2 — Relational geometry

Geometry is discovered from process, not imposed as ornament.

Possible forms include toward/away, near/far, boundary/permeability, center/edge, polarity, triangle, overlap/separation, convergence/divergence, loop, nesting, and changing orientation over time.

A geometry is useful only when it increases perception.

**Never imply mathematical precision where none exists.**

The member may modify or reject the representation.

## IV.3 — The third thing

The existing sentence remains a major design concept:

> **A relationship is not only between two people. It is the third thing that forms when two lives meet.**

This can inform interaction, imagery, motion, and information architecture.

It must not be presented as proof that Soullab has measured an independent metaphysical entity. It is a phenomenological and relational hypothesis open to investigation.

## IV.4 — Framework transparency

Do not greet a distressed or uncertain member with lineage names such as Polyvagal, Bowen, IFS, Gottman, NVC, EFT, Imago, Jung, McGilchrist, or Whitehead.

Provide progressive disclosure instead:

- **Why this approach?**
- **Sources & traditions**
- **How MAIA is thinking about this**
- **Research status**

Where a framework materially shapes a suggestion, provenance should be inspectable.

Soullab's own framework requires the same discipline. The system should preserve whether content is:

- member-authored;
- directly observed system state;
- derived pattern;
- MAIA inference;
- framework-based interpretation;
- research hypothesis;
- spiritual or metaphysical interpretation.

# SECTION V — VISUAL LANGUAGE: RELATIONAL SANCTUARY

Visual design begins here, after the attentional and relational constitution has been established.

The question is not first `what aesthetic looks premium?`

It is:

> **What visual environment supports the kind of attending Section I requires?**

## V.1 — Desired quality

Relationships should feel like a **relational sanctuary** rather than a dashboard, lab console, dating product, or therapy intake form.

Preserve:

- Soullab darkness and depth;
- continuity with MAIA;
- restrained jade lineage;
- seriousness and dignity;
- absence of saccharine romance imagery.

Introduce:

- deep charcoal / ink as field;
- warm bone / ivory reading text;
- restrained jade as living accent rather than universal border;
- muted copper / rose warmth where appropriate;
- atmosphere and depth without decorative mysticism;
- larger typography;
- generous vertical breathing room;
- fewer boxes and visible borders;
- layered/translucent surfaces used sparingly;
- motion only where it helps reveal approach, distance, emergence, continuity, or change;
- visual possibility of two presences and the third field arising between them.

Avoid:

- heart icons as primary symbol;
- dating-app aesthetics;
- clinical intake aesthetics;
- quantified compatibility dashboards;
- low-contrast tiny explanatory copy;
- walls of `text-xs` metadata;
- three-column action grids on narrow screens;
- every concept in a bordered card;
- attention-seizing animation;
- progress mechanics that imply intimacy or development is a game to complete.

## V.2 — Visual acceptance law

**Quiet is not emptiness.** The interface should create perceptual room.

Hierarchy should tell the member what can be attended to without trying to maximize clicks or expose every capability at once.

# SECTION VI — SAFETY, RESEARCH BOUNDARY & CAPABILITY PRESERVATION

## VI.1 — Safety and dignity

Relationships may include coercion, abuse, violence, stalking, exploitation, self-harm risk, child-safety issues, severe power imbalance, or acute instability.

The UX must not assume:

- mutual goodwill;
- equal power;
- repair is always desirable;
- continued contact is always safe;
- both people share intention or reality;
- conflict is merely a communication problem.

Repair Path's current allowance for waiting, boundary-setting, space, and ending is constitutionally important.

The redesign must not become a prettier pressure toward reconciliation.

## VI.2 — Existing capability preservation boundary

Unless separately ruled otherwise, preserve:

- relationship records;
- current API contracts;
- realm persistence;
- bond type persistence;
- relationship notes;
- field state;
- field tone;

# SECTION VII — ACCEPTANCE GATES

The recomposition is not accepted until all of the following are true.

### A1 — Section I compliance
Every primary design decision can be traced to the attending/relational laws in Section I rather than only to aesthetic preference.

### A2 — Five-second orientation
A new member can tell within five seconds:

- this is about relationships;
- they may begin with what is happening;
- they do not need to understand a framework;
- MAIA is available.

### A3 — No ontology toll
A member can begin meaningful exploration without selecting realm, bond type, tool, framework, element, geometry, diagnosis, or type.

### A4 — Ambiguity survives
There is a first-class path for `I don't know / I can't name it yet`.

### A5 — Human intention precedes tool name
No primary threshold requires the member to select Relational Field, Dynamics Map, or Repair Path by technical name before ordinary-language orientation.

### A6 — Existing intelligence preserved
All preserved capabilities in §VI.2 remain reachable and functional.

### A7 — MAIA bridge
The member can enter MAIA from the landing surface, relationship home, and post-exploration state with appropriate relational context.

### A8 — Timeline centrality
Longitudinal change is perceptible on the relationship home without requiring an administrative history screen.

### A9 — Representation humility
Pattern, elemental, and geometric representations support reject / revise / none-of-these / not-yet semantics.

### A10 — Provenance
Member-authored content, derived state, MAIA inference, framework interpretation, and research hypothesis are not silently collapsed.

### A11 — Mobile dignity
No essential explanatory text below 14px rendered size without an explicit accessibility ruling. Primary controls meet practical tap-target requirements. No dense three-column grids on narrow screens.

### A12 — Visual sanctuary
The primary surface no longer reads as repeated equal-weight jade-bordered cards or an instrument dashboard.

### A13 — Attention integrity
Motion, hierarchy, color, prompts, and notifications support orientation and presence rather than capture or compulsion.

### A14 — Research boundary
No formal-research consent, data-use change, or publication-level claim rides inside the UX implementation PR.

### A15 — Safety
Repair and reconciliation are never represented as the only healthy movement.

### A16 — Return-to-whole
Every significant structured exploration has an explicit or implicit path back to lived experience and renewed attending.

### A17 — Discovery remains possible
The member can produce an experience the current taxonomy did not anticipate without being forced into a nearest available category.
