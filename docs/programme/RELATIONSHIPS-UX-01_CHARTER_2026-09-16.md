## 0. Why this lane exists

The current Relationships implementation contains meaningful relational intelligence but presents it through a taxonomy-forward, instrument-panel UI.

The defect is not primarily color, spacing, or component polish. The defect is **choreographic**:

- the system asks members to understand Soullab's ontology before Soullab has understood what brought them here;
- internal categories (`outer`, `inner`, `transpersonal`, bond types, tools, field-state labels) are surfaced too early;
- useful relational capabilities are presented as similarly weighted modules rather than as a coherent human journey;
- the visual language feels like a dashboard rather than a living relational environment;
- analytic naming arrives before sufficient attending;
- the experience emphasizes management of relationship records more than participation in relational process.

**This lane is a recomposition, not a reskin.**

---

## 1. Constitutional product premise

> **Relationship is a primary medium through which consciousness becomes visible and development unfolds.**

Relationships is therefore not a side utility inside Soullab. It is a central expression of the platform's thesis that consciousness is relational, reality is encountered through participation, and development is process.

The interface must help a member encounter a living situation, sense what is present, discover possible pattern and movement, make distinctions, give form where useful, and return to the whole with increased capacity to participate.

---

## 2. The attentional law

The UX must embody an ordering:

### OPEN ATTENDING → PARTICIPATION → ARTICULATION → RETURN TO WHOLE

The platform may use focused analytic structure, but it must not lead with reduction.

### Open attending includes

- the whole situation;
- body and felt sense;
- image and metaphor;
- atmosphere;
- relationship and context;
- ambiguity;
- what is changing;
- what is not yet nameable;
- what surprises the member.

### Participation includes

- noticing that the member is already part of the field;
- noticing stance, distance, reaching, withdrawal, grasping, protection, receptivity;
- recognizing that attention and action change what becomes possible next;
- allowing relationship with people, self, nature, vocation, symbol, community, and sacred meaning without forcing a single metaphysical frame.

### Articulation includes

- distinctions;
- language;
- timeline;
- pattern candidates;
- recurrence;
- self/other differentiation;
- explicit options;
- elemental or geometric representation where useful;
- structured next movements;
- saved continuity.

### Return to whole means

After a map, label, insight, elemental reading, geometry, or suggested movement, the member is invited back to lived reality:

- Does this fit?
- What changes when you see it this way?
- What is present now?
- What does this representation leave out?
- What becomes possible next?

**Analytic structure serves renewed perception. It does not replace it.**

---

## 3. Seven constitutional UX laws

### LAW 1 — Meet the situation before the category
A member may arrive with pain, longing, joy, confusion, conflict, grief, curiosity, repetition, attraction, distance, uncertainty, repair, sacredness, or an experience they cannot yet name.

The threshold must let them begin there.

**Forbidden at primary entry:** requiring `outer / inner / transpersonal`, bond type, theoretical framework, elemental category, geometric form, or tool selection before the member can express what is happening.

### LAW 2 — Ambiguity is a valid state
"I don't know," "something feels off," "something is moving," and "I can't name it yet" are complete, legitimate entry states.

Successful self-classification is never the price of access to MAIA.

### LAW 3 — Patterns are invitations, never identities
The system may say:

> This resembles a pursue/withdraw movement. Does that fit?

It may not say:

> You are the pursuer.

> This relationship is avoidant.

> Your partner is emotionally unavailable.

The member retains authority over fit.

### LAW 4 — Development is temporal
The relationship home must make change across time visible. Timeline, recurrence, threshold, rupture, repair, emergence, and transformation are primary windows into development—not secondary history.

### LAW 5 — MAIA remains a living doorway
At every significant point, the member must be able to bring what is happening into dialogue with MAIA without losing relational context.

### LAW 6 — Representation must remain subordinate to reality
Elemental, geometric, psychological, systems, attachment, Jungian, somatic, or spiritual lenses may illuminate. None may become the relationship itself.

Every representation must preserve an implicit or explicit **"does this fit?"**

### LAW 7 — The unknown is productive
The interface must preserve room for surprise, contradiction, anomaly, and emergence.

"None of these" and "not yet" are first-class responses.

## 4. The threshold experience

### 4.1 Page title

**Relationships**

Do not lead with **Relational Field** as the primary product title. "Field" may remain part of the deeper conceptual language.

### 4.2 Opening invitation

Primary invitation:

> **What's happening between you?**
>
> Bring a person, a moment, a pattern, or something you cannot quite name. We can stay with what is here, notice what keeps returning, and explore what may be asking for understanding, expression, repair, a boundary, change—or simply attention.

Primary actions:

- **Start with what's happening**
- **Choose a relationship**

Persistent low-pressure escape hatch:

- **I'm not sure. I just want to talk with MAIA.**

That path opens MAIA with relational context but without forcing a relationship record.

## 5. Entry intentions

The UI routes from human experience rather than product modules.

### Something happened
A specific moment, conflict, encounter, message, rupture, discovery, attraction, loss, or event.

### We keep repeating this
A recurring relational movement or familiar loop.

### I feel distant, confused, or unsure
The member senses a change but cannot yet organize it.

### Something needs repair
A rupture, injury, broken trust, misattunement, failed conversation, or disconnection.

### I need to have a difficult conversation
The member needs clarity, differentiation, language, timing, or boundaries.

### I want to understand this relationship more deeply
No crisis is required. Curiosity, love, intimacy, friendship, family, collaboration, mentorship, vocation, and sacred relationship belong here too.

### I can't name it yet
The system begins with sensing rather than classification.

These are **doorways**, not diagnostic categories.

## 6. Quiet routing beneath the threshold

The existing tools remain, but the member should not have to select them by technical name at first contact.

| Member intention | Quiet internal route |
| --- | --- |
| Something happened | Check-in / Relational Field |
| We keep repeating this | Dynamics Map |
| Distant / confused / unsure | Relational Field |
| Something needs repair | Repair Path |
| Difficult conversation | Repair-oriented path / MAIA |
| Deeper understanding | Relationship home + history + MAIA |
| Can't name it yet | Open sensing flow / MAIA |

Tool names may appear later as optional named practices.

## 7. Relationship creation must become secondary

The current creation flow begins by selecting a realm and then a bond type. That is backend ontology exposed as threshold choreography.

New law:

**The member may begin with experience first and identify the relationship later.**

When a relationship identity is needed, use ordinary human language:

- Who is this with?
- What do you call this relationship?
- Is this about a person, a group, something within you, or something larger?

`outer / inner / transpersonal` may remain internal storage categories but should not normally appear as required first-step vocabulary.

Bond type should be optional metadata surfaced only when useful.

## 8. Relationships landing architecture

The page should have four perceptual layers, not one long list.

### Layer A — Arrival
A large, quiet opening invitation centered on what is happening now.

### Layer B — Recent living relationships
Two or three recent relationship surfaces with strong human hierarchy:

- name;
- ordinary bond language where relevant;
- one or two currently alive signals;
- meaningful last movement / last exploration;
- clear continuation action.

Avoid rendering relationship records as administrative cards.

### Layer C — What is asking for attention
Where data supports it, surface one or two member-grounded invitations:

- Something remains open with Jordan.
- This pattern has appeared more than once.
- You marked a turning point here last week.

No autonomous diagnosis. No unsupported inference.

### Layer D — Explore relationships
A quieter doorway to all saved relationships, inner figures, groups, symbolic/transpersonal relationships, and historical records.

Taxonomy belongs here, not at the threshold.

## 9. Relationship home architecture

A relationship detail page must no longer read as:

`Current Field → unresolved threads → Timeline → Next Movement → Open with a tool`

It should read as a living relational place.

### 9.1 Presence header

- relationship name;
- simple ordinary relationship label when useful;
- no dashboard metadata dump;
- clear return to Relationships;
- direct **Talk with MAIA** action;
- direct **Check in** action.

### 9.2 What is happening now

One coherent current-state surface using member-grounded material:

- tone;
- signals;
- unresolved thread;
- member-authored words;
- last accepted pattern hypothesis;
- uncertainty if present.

The design should feel like a field being sensed, not a status widget.

### 9.3 What would be useful?

Human descriptions first:

**Understand what's happening**  
Sense the tone and movement between you.

**See what keeps repeating**  
Explore recurrence without turning it into a label.

**Work with something that has broken**  
Explore repair, space, conversation, boundaries, or ending.

Named tools may appear as secondary subtitles:

- Relational Field
- Dynamics Map
- Repair Path

### 9.4 Your story together

Timeline becomes a major visual structure.

Events may include:

- check-in;
- note;
- reflection;
- rupture;
- repair;
- threshold / turning point;
- member-marked insight;
- accepted/rejected pattern hypothesis;
- meaningful MAIA conversation handoff where consent and provenance permit.

The timeline should make transformation perceptible without pretending every change has a neat narrative.

### 9.5 Return to the living relationship

After any tool or map, return with:

- What feels different now?
- Does this still fit?
- What is alive between you now?
- Is there anything this map missed?

## 10. Elemental Alchemy inside Relationships

The elemental model should become available **after contact with lived experience**, not before it.

Possible use:

- **Fire** — energy, desire, will, initiation, anger, eros, spirit, transformation;
- **Water** — feeling, attachment, grief, longing, receptivity, flow;
- **Earth** — body, behavior, boundary, material reality, reliability, consequence;
- **Air** — thought, language, interpretation, narrative, perspective;
- **Field / weather / atmosphere** — changing relational context in which these co-arise.

These are not scores and should not be presented as five fixed buckets into which experience must fit.

Possible prompt:

> If you look through an elemental lens, what becomes easier to notice?

Always preserve:

- none of these;
- more than one;
- changing over time;
- member-generated language;
- ability to reject the lens entirely.

---

## 11. Relational geometry inside Relationships

Geometry should be discovered from process, not imposed as ornament.

Possible forms include:

- toward / away;
- near / far;
- center / edge;
- boundary / permeability;
- triangle;
- polarity;
- overlap / separation;
- pursuit / withdrawal;
- convergence / divergence;
- recursion / loop;
- nesting / systems-within-systems;
- changing orientation through time.

A geometry is useful only if it makes lived organization more perceptible.

**Never imply mathematical precision where none exists.**

The member should be able to modify or reject the representation.

## 12. Visual language: Relational Sanctuary

This surface should feel qualitatively different from a dashboard or lab console.

### Preserve

- Soullab darkness and depth;
- restrained jade lineage;
- continuity with MAIA;
- seriousness;
- absence of saccharine romance imagery.

### Introduce

- deep charcoal / ink rather than repeated green panels;
- warm bone / ivory reading text;
- restrained jade as living accent rather than universal border;
- muted copper / rose warmth where relationally appropriate;
- subtle depth and atmosphere;
- larger type;
- more vertical breathing room;
- fewer boxes;
- fewer visible borders;
- layered translucent fields rather than card grids;
- gentle motion where it conveys approach, distance, change, emergence, or continuity;
- visual symbolism of **two presences and the third field arising between them**.

### Avoid

- heart icons as the primary symbol;
- dating-app aesthetics;
- therapy-clinic aesthetics;
- quantified compatibility dashboards;
- decorative mysticism disconnected from function;
- walls of tiny `text-xs` labels;
- low-contrast 11px explanatory copy;
- three-column action grids on mobile;
- every concept living inside a bordered card.

---

## 13. The third thing

The current empty-state sentence should become a major design concept:

> **A relationship is not only between two people. It is the third thing that forms when two lives meet.**

This should inform interaction, motion, imagery, and information architecture.

But the phrase must not be used to imply that the platform has scientifically measured an independent metaphysical entity. It names a phenomenological and relational hypothesis whose forms may be investigated.

## 14. Framework and research transparency

Do not greet distressed members with:

- Polyvagal
- Bowen
- IFS
- Gottman
- NVC
- EFT
- Imago
- Jung
- McGilchrist
- Whitehead

Those lineages matter, but they should sit behind progressive disclosure:

- **Why this approach?**
- **Sources & traditions**
- **How MAIA is thinking about this**
- **Research status**

Where a framework materially shapes a suggestion, provenance should be inspectable.

Soullab's own Elemental Alchemy / relational-geometric model should be equally transparent about whether a statement is:

- member-authored;
- observed system state;
- derived pattern;
- framework-based interpretation;
- research hypothesis;
- metaphysical or spiritual interpretation.

## 15. Research architecture in the UX

The UI should support research **without making the member feel studied**.

### Member-facing priorities

- presence;
- usefulness;
- consent;
- agency;
- privacy;
- self-recognition;
- ability to disagree;
- continuity.

### Research-legible phenomena underneath

Where consent and governance permit:

- tone;
- movement;
- felt signals;
- recurrence;
- uncertainty;
- rupture;
- repair;
- threshold;
- selected/rejected hypotheses;
- self/other differentiation;
- attention shifts;
- elemental observations;
- geometric relations;
- developmental transitions;
- temporal sequence.

**Never optimize the member experience for data completeness.**

## 16. Safety and dignity

Relationships can include coercion, abuse, violence, stalking, exploitation, self-harm risk, child safety issues, or acute instability.

The UX must not assume:

- mutual goodwill;
- equal power;
- repair is always desirable;
- continued contact is always safe;
- both parties share reality or intention;
- conflict is merely a communication problem.

Repair Path's existing allowance for boundaries, waiting, and ending is constitutionally important and must be preserved.

The redesign must not become a prettier pressure toward reconciliation.

## 18. Non-goals

RELATIONSHIPS-UX-01 does **not** authorize:

- new psychological diagnosis;
- attachment-type scoring;
- relationship compatibility scores;
- autonomous partner profiling;
- inference about an absent person's motives;
- research enrollment changes;
- new data-sharing policy;
- new production AI prompts merely to create more "insight";
- new schemas unless a later implementation census proves they are required;
- metaphysical claims presented as empirically verified findings.

## 19. Acceptance gates

The recomposition is not accepted until all of the following are true.

### A1 — Five-second orientation
A new member can tell within five seconds:

- this is about relationships;
- they may begin with what is happening;
- they do not need to know a framework;
- MAIA is available.

### A2 — No ontology toll
A member can begin meaningful exploration without selecting realm, bond type, tool, framework, element, geometry, or diagnosis.

### A3 — Ambiguity survives
There is a first-class route for "I don't know / I can't name it yet."

### A4 — Human intention precedes tool name
No primary threshold asks the member to choose Relational Field, Dynamics Map, or Repair Path by name before ordinary-language orientation.

### A5 — Existing intelligence preserved
All preserved capabilities in §17 remain reachable and functional.

### A6 — MAIA bridge
The member can enter MAIA from the landing surface, relationship home, and post-exploration state with appropriate relational context.

### A7 — Timeline centrality
Longitudinal change is perceptible on the relationship home without opening a secondary admin/history screen.

### A8 — Representation humility
Pattern, elemental, and geometric representations support reject / revise / none-of-these semantics.

### A9 — Mobile dignity
No essential explanatory text below 14px rendered size without a specific accessibility ruling. Primary controls meet practical tap-target requirements. No dense three-column grids on narrow screens.

### A10 — Visual sanctuary
The primary page no longer reads as a repeated collection of equal-weight jade-bordered cards.

### A11 — Research boundary
No formal-research consent, claim, or data-use change rides inside a UI redesign PR.

### A12 — Safety
Repair and reconciliation are never treated as the only healthy movement.

---

## 20. Sentence over implementation

> **Do not redesign the database. Redesign the encounter. Meet the living relationship before naming it; let form emerge from attention; let analysis serve participation; and return every map to the reality that exceeds the map.**
