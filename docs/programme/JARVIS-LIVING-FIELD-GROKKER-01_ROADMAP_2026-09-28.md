## The inheritance

Grokker supplied a spatial grammar: recursive containment, circles within circles, focus + context, semantic zoom, progressive disclosure, and spatial continuity while moving deeper.

Soullab extends that grammar beyond information hierarchy. The field can eventually contain or relate questions, meanings, processes, life threads, memories, practices, sources, and personal places while preserving the distinction between representation and personhood.

**Working formulation:** Grokker gives the geometry of entering knowledge. Soullab turns that geometry into a way of moving through a living field of meaning.

## Core semantic primitives

- **NODE** — a place of meaning.
- **RELATION** — why two places matter together.
- **TRANSITION** — what changes in understanding when moving between them.
- **PATH** — an unfolding sequence of distinctions through which something becomes clearer.

Later governed objects include Field Configuration, Aetheric Event, Field Memory, Life Thread, Thread Ecology, Process Instance, and Fractal Homology.
