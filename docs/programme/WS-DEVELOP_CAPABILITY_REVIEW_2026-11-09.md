## 1 · What CURRENT Develop is
It is a **reading-and-observation workbench**. A writer *commissions* a reading through a lens; MAIA returns a frozen reading made of observations, each with evidence and stated limits; the writer takes a standing on each (Keep · Dismiss · Unresolved) and may talk about it.

## 3 · The two Develops are different relationships — and the review's main finding
CURRENT is organised around **what MAIA reads and reports** (a reading, then a standing, then maybe a conversation). The founder's direction is organised around **what the writer and MAIA do with the work** (the text, a conversation, a plan, a change). Neither replaces the other:
- A commissioned reading is a *deliberate, coverage-licensed act* (law: whole-work reading only on explicit commission). It should become something the writer can **bring into the stage** ("read this chapter for continuity") and see as notes anchored to the text — not a separate room.
- Standing (Keep / Dismiss / Unresolved) is already the writer's decision record; Dismiss now reaches MAIA (narrow D5 exception). Keep and Unresolved intentionally do not.
- Themes and lineage are *derived artifacts* and belong beside the text as optional layers, not as destinations.
**Verdict:** do not delete CURRENT's capabilities; **absorb them into the stage as layers on the text**, one at a time, each walked. Until then they stay reachable through "More ways to explore" (the Full view).

## 4 · The vital gap: change
The writer's core ask — *discuss Chapter 7's relationship to 2 and 5, receive a grounded proposal, revise it, apply it, leave and return* — breaks at the proposal. What exists: a **passage** desk that can propose and apply an edit to one passage, with authorization and Undo; and **structure proposals** over units. What does not exist: a **development plan** across chapters, and a way for a plan step to become an authorized edit.
Smallest honest design (needs your ruling, §6): a plan step is *MAIA's suggested wording for a named passage*; "Apply" opens that passage's existing desk with the wording as a proposal the writer can Use / Change / Talk about / Keep mine / Undo — so every change goes through the authorization that already exists, one passage at a time, and nothing applies silently.

## 5 · Risks and holds
- The stage is flagged; the default Develop is untouched and its draft-protection guards still pass. Those guards must be re-expressed for the stage before it can become the default.
- Nonfiction source/quote verification and a bibliography check have no substrate; MAIA must say "no support shown", never "true".
- Reading permission, receipts, Sanctuary and the revocation race remain the open security holds from the independent review.
- A helper's book handles other people's lives: the gentle once-only offer exists; identifying details are never asked for or invented.

## 6 · Decisions for the founder (each short)
1. **Plan steps become passage proposals through the existing desk?** (recommended: yes; no new change path.)
2. **Observations and readings as notes on the text inside the stage?** (recommended: yes, as an optional layer, commissioned explicitly.)
3. **When does the stage become the default Develop?** (recommended: after the draft-protection guards are re-expressed, the walk on both books is accepted, and the independent review passes.)
4. **Where does a "move chapter" command live, and who may authorize it?** (separate lane; not required for M4.)
