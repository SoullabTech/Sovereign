### 3e — MAIA as the guidance layer

**INTENT** — *"I want to test MAIA to see if she can help me understand how to use the studio"*

**QUESTION (writer's own words)** — *"HOw can I use this studio. I am new to it and have my book elemental alchemy here"*

**MAIA'S ANSWER (verbatim)**

> Good afternoon, Kelly. Studio is a workspace for practitioners — a place to
> develop your practice, build programs, and work on projects. Since you have
> your book, Elemental Alchemy, there, it sounds like you're in the right place.
> The honest answer is that my map of Studio's specific working surfaces is
> limited — I can tell you what it's \*for\* more reliably than I can walk you
> through every tool inside it. What I'd suggest: the Help area has a User Guide
> and short videos that will show you the actual interface clearly. You can
> reach that from the Help control on the main screen. That said — what are you
> hoping to do with Elemental Alchemy in there? If you tell me more about what
> you're trying to build or develop, I can help you think through the shape of
> it, even if the navigation itself is better learned from the guide.

**INTERPRETATION** — *"First, there is no Help area. Second, I want her to know more than she does"*

⭐ Two distinct failures in one answer, one of which the writer caught and one
of which they did not:
1. **Fabricated affordance** — a Help area, a User Guide, short videos, and a
   "Help control on the main screen". None exist. *Caught by the writer.*
2. **Wrong product model** — *"Studio is a workspace for practitioners — a place
   to develop your practice, build programs, and work on projects"* describes a
   different room. *Not caught by the writer.* It is the more likely root cause:
   she answered confidently about a Studio she was not in.
