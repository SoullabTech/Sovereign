## M1 — **OBJECT-SCOPED**

> **For an assertion about a member whose truth has temporal standing, succession is carried forward
> by the successor. The authoritative predecessor is not rewritten to manufacture its closure.**

The successor carries `supersedes`; **`superseded_by`, `valid_to` and currentness are derived** from
the succession/transition record rather than becoming authoritative backward mutations.

⭐ **The principle is adopted independently.** ⛔ This does **not** promote
`TEMPORAL_MEMORY_DIRECTION` into canon — that document remains Cat 1, held. The ruling arrives at the
same architecture by its own authority.

**Scope, and what it does not reach:**

```text
ASSERTION SUCCESSION        ⭐ OBJECT-SCOPED LAW — ruled
HYPOTHESIS INFLUENCE        separate lifecycle
UNIVERSAL MEMORY LAW        ⛔ NOT CREATED
interpretiveLedger repair   ⛔ NOT IMPLIED
```

A hypothesis may change **influence, surfacing eligibility, contestation state or weight** under its
own lifecycle, provided **history and provenance survive** and ⛔ **those changes are never laundered
into a member-authored fact.**

## M2 — closes on the **prohibition**

> **The system may not set a member assertion to `stale` merely because time passed.**
> **Age may generate uncertainty or a review candidate; it may not decide truth.**

⛔ `detect → ask → record` remains a **candidate mechanism, not newly ratified canon.** The
prohibition is ruled; the replacement shape is not.
