## Governing outcome

Make the successful Elemental Alchemy editorial journey native to Writer's Studio.

The completion test is not “does Studio contain many editing features?” It is:

> Can a writer bring a substantial, mature Work into Writer's Studio; preserve what is uniquely theirs; recover meaningful material lost across prior revisions; understand the whole Work; make precise changes under writer authority; verify lineage and quotations; inspect the Work as rendered pages; distinguish review-ready from publication-ready; and leave with a trustworthy final manuscript without needing a parallel editorial process outside Studio?

### Stop rule

If Kelly must leave Writer's Studio to perform an editorially important act that MAIA reasonably should be able to perform inside the Work, that act is a product gap until explicitly ruled out of scope.

No step may silently redefine authorship, Work identity, provenance, publication authority, or consent.

# 3 — Admit the case-study completion contract

Status: OPEN / IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED by the founder's 1–10 act.

Binding laws:
1. Understand before changing.
2. Protect before improving.
3. Recover before inventing.
4. Move before rewriting; cut repetition before replacing voice.
5. Earlier authorial knowing may outrank later polish.
6. Story / encounter may carry teaching better than explanation.
7. Proposal is never adoption.
8. Text is semantic authority; rendered pages are reader-experience evidence.
9. Review-ready and publication-ready are distinct states.
10. A writer can see what changed, why, what was protected, and how to undo it.

Primary member modes remain Write · Develop · Review.
Do not add a fourth primary Produce mode. Publication preparation remains a contextual completion state.

# 5 — Build Recovery / Lost Gold

Objective: compare prior authorial versions with the current Work without treating newer as automatically superior.

Required:
- attach/import a prior version as a versioned source
- state which source the writer regards as voice/root authority
- compare by structural unit and passage
- surface meaningful absent material as recovery candidates
- explain why a candidate may matter
- distinguish accidental loss, deliberate cut, duplication, and genuine supersession where evidence permits
- never restore automatically

Required actions: Keep current · Discuss · Recover · Adapt · Leave out
