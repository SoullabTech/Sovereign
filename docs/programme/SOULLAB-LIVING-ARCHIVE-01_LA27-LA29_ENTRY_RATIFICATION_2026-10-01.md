# SOULLAB-LIVING-ARCHIVE-01 · LA-27 / LA-28 / LA-29 + Entry Ratification

**Status:** RATIFIED · founder act · 2026-10-01

## Provenance of the act

Immediately after the Source Vault substrate census boundary, the founder was presented with four explicit standing decisions and told that if those were the rulings, the continuation could proceed into the Living Archive Source Vault schema and executable F1–F11 falsifier lane. The founder replied: **“continue.”**

This record preserves that continuation act as ratification of the four rulings **as presented, without expansion**.

## LA-27 — Scale is evidence

**The scale of the archive may be revealed, never performed.** Every visible Dark Field point corresponds to one catalogued artifact at the fixed cataloguing unit. Generated or decorative points are counterfeit evidence.

The fixed point-counting unit remains the **provenance-bearing object as it was made**. Pages, excerpts, clips, transcript spans, frames and sections are sub-artifacts and do not inflate Dark Field point count merely because they are separately addressable.

## LA-28 — Doors, not diagnosis

**The archive offers doors; it does not decide which door fits the visitor.** Navigation never becomes a reading of the person.

Standing consequences:

- no visitor-behavior-derived thread ordering, recommendation, highlighting or ranking;
- no inference from dwell time or path choice;
- choosing a thread is not memory unless the member explicitly saves something;
- no navigation telemetry, including aggregate thread-choice counts;
- thread choice must not be encoded in request paths/query parameters when doing so would make infrastructure logs a covert navigation record;
- `Stay with the field` is a lawful choice, not abandonment or incomplete progress.

## LA-29 — Another person's life is not the founder's to signal

Material carrying another person's intimate or identifying life is **WITHHELD (THIRD PARTY)**.

WITHHELD means:

- no Dark Field point;
- no coordinate;
- no thread adjacency;
- no localized count;
- no member-facing withdrawal trace;
- no founder-facing Dark Field exception.

It may exist as protected Source Vault metadata only where lawful and necessary. If classification between founder-owned private material and third-party material is uncertain, classification fails closed to **WITHHELD**.

Founder-owned private material remains **SEALED (SELF)** and is a different class.

## Layered entry model — RATIFIED

### Anonymous

- PUBLIC artifacts only;
- SEALED points are not rendered;
- bookmarks remain browser-local unless the visitor later authenticates and explicitly saves them.

### Authenticated member

- PUBLIC artifacts;
- member-eligible artifacts;
- founder-owned SEALED (SELF) points may appear as sealed points while their content remains closed;
- account bookmarks exist only by explicit save act.

### WITHHELD

- invisible at every member-facing level;
- not rendered even in founder-facing Dark Field views;
- never becomes discoverable through geometry, thread shape, counts or withdrawal traces.

## What this ratification does not authorize

This does not admit any historical artifact, publish any private material, ingest any source, or authorize an immersive museum surface. It authorizes the **minimum substrate and executable falsifiers** necessary to make these laws testable before the first artifact journey is built.
