## Why begin with methods, not a larger model

The first useful improvement to RGR is a reproducible evaluation and falsification architecture that cannot change its scientific claims after seeing results. The purpose here is to compare possible tool interfaces, preserve original research constraints and define precise future acceptance tests. No library is imported or executed by this design.

RGR-01 finds plausible *plural* geometric languages, not a unified theory of meaning. RGR-02 preserves vector/relational, graph/structural, subspace/Grassmann and local-to-global/sheaf alternatives rather than privileging one ontology. RGR-03 H-RT1 requires both relation-preserving transfer under surface variation and relation-breaking sensitivity under surface matching. Its generator notation is x = G(s,r,η), where s is surface, r is relational structure, and η covers nuisance factors.

RGR-04 freezes benchmark **RGR-RT1-D2C-01** using **DIRECTED_TWO_STEP_COMPOSITION**: six nodes, eight directed edges, no loops or direct source-target shortcut; exactly one positive two-step intermediate versus zero negative intermediates; balanced 16-bit node-surface codes and a frozen counterfactual 2-switch. Its original splits, seeds, comparators, metrics, meaningful-effect thresholds, leakage tests, stopping conditions and replication law must not be altered by an adapter.

**Two RGR-05 facts must stay separate.** RGR-05 pre-materialization for Directed Two-Step Composition reported FEASIBILITY_PASS, but created no benchmark pair files or model results. The later, separate RGR-05B Flow investigation recorded design infeasibility under its own fixed sampler budget. One result does not rescue or contradict the other. RGR-06 independently verified its confirmatory implementation lock with **zero benchmark pairs materialized, zero training, zero TEST and zero REPLICATION**. RGR-CF01 remains a separate exploratory intake, not a replacement or amendment of any existing lock.

## Conceptual future interface, not executable code

A later, separately authorized evaluation adapter must declare:
- **Authority:** exact accepted work unit, scope, consent/data restrictions and independent execution grant.
- **Frozen sources:** immutable RGR-03/04/06 document digests, generator version, relation family, seeds, split allocation, sample IDs, pair atomics and source provenance.
- **Comparators:** preregistered surface-only and relation-aware methods, plus a separately labeled structural/symbolic non-geometric rival.
- **Evidence:** full prompt/model provenance, deterministic scoring implementation, inference configuration, raw outputs, negative controls, confidence rule, stopping/replication trace and source hashes.
- **Failure discipline:** FAIL and UNDERDETERMINED remain possible outcomes. No metric/threshold/sampler rescue and no reclassification of negative results based on a new tool's defaults.
- **Meaning boundary:** hidden-state geometry, causal estimates and neural-feature decodability do not prove relational consciousness, psychological development, lived meaning or spiritual experiences.

No adapter may silently turn a library's default metric, evaluator, dataset split or data collection method into the new constitutional standard.

## Explicit minimum falsification and negative controls

The preserved RGR-03 contract requires two independent comparisons: **same relation, changed surface** and **changed relation, matched surface**. Their paired outcomes cannot be split across train/validation/TEST/REPLICATION; pre-existing RGR-04 sample-generation and uncertainty laws govern any later authorized materialization.

Also retain: symbolic graph/structure-mapping rival; surface-only baseline; nuisance and label leakage tests; frozen codebook and split separation; a relation-breaking counterfactual that preserves admissible pair invariants; scorer implementation fingerprint; and a no-rescue rule when any preregistered effect threshold or control fails.

DoWhy's identification assumptions must be written before interpreting any estimate. Synthetic model interventions may make some causal assumptions testable, but they do not establish analogous human encounter mechanisms. pyvene's internal intervention tasks would be an *additional experimental programme*, not a modification of H-RT1's frozen confirmatory model tests.
