## RULING

> ⭐ **`SQ-3` is sequenced as `ACT 5`.**
>
> **May the organism hold — or let another person hold — a representation of a member, or take an
> act concerning a member, that the member cannot see, dispute, or erase?**

## 1 · Why `SQ-3` is next

Selected primarily on **`TEST B` — ACT-4 DEPENDENCY** and **`TEST E` — JURISDICTIONAL
DISTINCTNESS.**

`ACT 4` established:

```text
⭐ MEMBER-ADOPTED CLAIM as a possible standing
⭐ member adoption as an ACT DISTINCT from system inference
⭐ silence as NOT equivalent to confirmation
⭐ consequential USE as constitutionally relevant EVEN WITHOUT PERSISTENCE
```

⛔ But `ACT 4` did **not** determine whether the member **can SEE the representation · can DISPUTE
it · can REVISE it · can ERASE it · or must have ACCESS to it before adoption can occur.**

> ⭐⭐ **`ACT 4` therefore created a standing whose LAWFUL ENTRY CONDITIONS cannot be fully stated
> until `SQ-3` is adjudicated.**

```text
⛔ NOT THE BASIS   "SQ-3 appears philosophically prior."
⛔ NOT THE BASIS   "member sovereignty sounds foundational."

⭐ THE BASIS       ACT 4 ITSELF names a member-dependent standing and assigns the member's
                   relationship to person-level representations to SQ-3.
```

⭐ An **actual doctrinal gap created by the existing rulings.** `SQ-3` is therefore ⛔ not merely
thematically related to `ACT 4` — ⭐ **it governs territory `ACT 4` intentionally stopped before
entering.**

## 5 · Relation to `SQ-7`

⭐ The re-examination found `SQ-7`'s member-standing dependency **survives the replacement test.**
⛔ The relevant question is not whether the organism uses affinity scores · matching algorithms ·
recommendation routes · community modules · or any present mechanism. ⭐ **The structural question
is:**

> **What standing does a member have toward a representation of them that the organism may use or
> expose in connecting them to another member?**

⭐ That question survives replacement of **every current mechanism.** `SQ-3` can therefore supply a
constitutional premise later relevant to `SQ-7` — ⛔ **but `ACT 5` does not decide whether members
should be connected.**

## 6 · Why `SQ-4` is not `ACT 5`

⭐ `SQ-4` remains lawful and important. ⚠️ But **some of its substantive outcomes would operate in
territory whose member-standing consequences are not yet ruled.** Sequencing it first would require
either:

```text
⛔ silently ASSUMING the member-standing doctrine; or
⛔ deciding only PART of SQ-4 and leaving its consequences constitutionally UNDER-SPECIFIED
```

⭐ `SQ-3` can be adjudicated independently. ⛔ `SQ-4` cannot yet be adjudicated with the same
independence **across all its available outcomes.**

## 7 · Why `SQ-7` is not `ACT 5`

⭐ Independently strategic, ⛔ **not constitutionally upstream of `SQ-3`** — its structural

competing doctrinal pressure requiring `SQ-7` to precede. ⭐ It remains **unsequenced.**
