## 1. Governing idea

> *We do not compose sound to determine someone's experience. We compose environments in which experience can reveal itself.*

Sound as a living environment, not a soundtrack. Music supports a relationship with experience **without telling the listener what that experience must be**.

## 3. What the Atlas is

A **study and quality framework** that feeds the existing Sound Studio. It is not a new app, player, or release train.

1. **Reference works** — ten annotated, chosen jointly (founder + ChatGPT + reviewers). Each analysed on seven dimensions: harmony, timbre, time, space, nature, emotion, form.
2. **Original sound-palette library** — Soullab-owned recordings and syntheses only.
3. **Three composition proofs**, reviewed by ear before any long-form extension:
   - A — *Between the Tides* (Water + Aether, 20–25 min)
   - B — *Deep Stillness* (Earth + Aether, ~20 min)
   - C — *Ember & Sky* (Fire + Air, 15–20 min)
4. **Elemental families** as artistic directions, never prescribed moods (Water may be turbulent, Fire tender, Earth restless).

## 4. Rules the study must obey

1. **Principles, not copies.** Study harmonic rhythm, texture, spatial design, duration. Do not reproduce melodies, recordings, stems, or distinctive arrangements. Reference recordings are never embedded in Soullab, never used as training or conditioning material, and never imitated closely enough to be recognisable.
2. **No therapeutic or physiological claims.** Binaural beats, tuned frequencies, entrainment, "healing" or "anxiety relief" are not claimed by Soullab. Where a tradition makes such claims (Goldman, Source Vibrations), the Atlas records them **as that tradition's account, attributed**, separate from acoustic description. Ties to `docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` (Live / Designed / Vision).
3. **Member sovereignty over the experience.** No outcome promised or implied; some pieces stay in the background, some invite attention. Nothing tells the listener what they are feeling or should feel.
4. **Cultures, generations, genres, systems — with Invariant 14.** "Across cultures" means traditions are studied and credited **in their practitioners' own terms**; Soullab does not translate them into Elemental vocabulary or treat them as raw material for a palette. Sacred or lineage-held music is not sampled, imitated, or re-framed without the consent and participation of its keepers. Any culturally rooted piece needs a named human collaborator and a rights/attribution record before it enters the library.
5. **Rights first.** Every Atlas item and every palette element enters the LISTEN-01 rights registry (#1979 item 4) before use. Field recordings carry location, recordist, and permission. Streaming-provider metadata is never a license.
6. **One audio authority.** All Atlas listening and playback goes through the AUDIO-PLATFORM-01 contract: member-initiated start/stop, no auto-restart after interruption, no overlap with speech or recording, no microphone use without consent.
7. **Human judgment closes every piece.** Methods may include human performance, field recording, procedural synthesis, and AI-assisted experiments; the final artistic decision is a named human's. Provenance of each element (human / procedural / AI-assisted) is recorded.
8. **Ear before metrics.** A measurement of difference never stands in for a listening verdict. Proofs are judged on headphones, speakers, and phone speaker, by named listeners, with the result recorded as an observation, not an acceptance.

## 5. How it joins the existing Studio

- Participation model stays as built: Receive · Shape · Make · Study/Offer. The Atlas informs **Make** and **Study/Offer**; it adds no new surface.
- Seven Focus musical worlds are re-listened to against Atlas findings. Any synthesis change is a separate, reviewed change under the single LISTEN-01 source owner.
- Member-saved soundscape recipes remain **not built**; this charter does not start them.
