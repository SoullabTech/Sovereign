## 1. REOPEN authority span — RATIFIED

A REOPEN act authorizes prior continuity for the **current response by default**.

The member may explicitly broaden that authority to the **present encounter** through language such as:

- “Use my book Keeps while we work on this today.”
- “Keep that prior thread with us for this conversation.”

Encounter-scoped authority ends with the encounter. It never survives into a future encounter without a new member act.

A receipt that a prior REOPEN occurred is historical evidence, never reusable authority by itself.
## 2. Compound CONTINUE + REOPEN — RATIFIED YES

A single natural instruction may author both acts when it clearly asks a prior thread to enter now.

Example:

> “Let’s continue the book thread from last time.”

may resolve to **CONTINUE + REOPEN**.

Prior CONTINUE standing alone remains insufficient. A thread being available for resumption does not authorize its later retrieval.

`Keep this open` remains CONTINUE only.

## 3. Explicit object handoff — RATIFIED YES

A member gesture that truthfully communicates **Take this to MAIA**, **Discuss this with MAIA**, or equivalent may serve as **object-bounded REOPEN authority**.

The grant is bounded to the handed-off object/context. It does not open adjacent memories, related items, or general continuity.

The handoff representation must satisfy Representation Authority: explicit governed grant plus an attestation that fails when the binding is absent, stale, mismatched, revoked, or used beyond its authorized span.
## 4. START_FRESH — inherited law, implementation consequence affirmed

START_FRESH closes **every** prior-memory-to-generative-context crossing until a later member REOPEN act.

It does not delete prior material.

A non-Sanctuary member may still KEEP newly selected present material while START_FRESH is active; that future-persistence act does not reopen anything that came before.

Clearing only the visible transcript is not START_FRESH.
