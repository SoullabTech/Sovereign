## 2 · The re-frozen eight

### 1 · Reading opens a conversation, not a report — **QUALIFIED (FQ-1)**

Conversation is the **governing relationship and default doorway**, not a prohibition on durable readings. A written reading may be useful as an artifact of or for dialogue, especially after discussion. `not a report` may not harden into a ban on asynchronous or durable response.

### 2 · Writer chooses both question and scope — **QUALIFIED (FQ-2 + Row A)**

The writer holds **decision authority** over both the developmental act and its scale. MAIA may offer an act, a question, a change of stance, or a broader or narrower scope, and may explain why one could be useful. **Proactivity proposes authority; it does not acquire it.** Evidence that system-selected scope performs better may change *how* scope is offered; it can never relocate *who decides*.

### 3 · Multi-scale scope — **DESIGN COMMITMENT · QUALIFIED · amended**

```text
PRE-RECONCILIATION
Scope can be selection -> section -> chapter -> part -> whole Work.

RE-FROZEN
Scope may range from a selection, through member-declared structural
units at whatever scale the Work actually has, to the whole Work.
```

Amended because Writer's Studio already holds that structure is **member-declared** and explicitly rejects a universal `Part → Chapter → Section → Passage` enum. The original wording preserved multi-scale developmental reading at the cost of quietly constitutionalizing one book topology.

**Kind**: design commitment — a declaration of what the system offers. Not an empirical prediction; not a constitutional rule. **Status**: evidence-informed, **not empirically validated for book-length developmental reading.** E3 (hierarchical / discourse-aware retrieval over flat chunking) is architectural support only — the Pass B ledger qualifies it because the tested tasks were long-document QA, not developmental manuscript cognition.

### 4 · Write feeds Develop — **QUALIFIED (FQ-3)**

The feed **may orient; it may not launder authority.** Provenance separation is mandatory across it. MAIA-originated suggestions and observations never acquire writer standing merely through uptake or warm discussion, and Develop re-grounds in the current manuscript and the current writer commission before making higher-scale claims.

### 5 · MAIA can learn the Work over time, within Work-level permission — **QUALIFIED (FQ-3 + item 4 Row B evidence)**

> Work understanding may persist with permission. **Persistence confers neither present authority nor presumptive relevance.** Persisted understanding must remain revisable and supersedable, and its influence on a present reading must be responsive to the writer's present intention and to later evidence.

> **Consent to remember is not consent to be interpreted through the memory every time.**

The evidence separates two virtues previously bundled:

```text
PERSISTENCE   preserving continuity across the life of a Work
      is not
RELIANCE      how strongly a prior understanding shapes this reading
```

Freshness must **not** be encoded merely as deleting or masking memory — binary masking discards constraints that remain important. The constitutional shape is:

```text
writer authorizes persistence
        |
understanding may remain available
        |
later Work evidence may revise / supersede it
        |
present intention governs how much it influences this encounter
```

### 6 · The session is the primary quality unit — **QUALIFIED (FQ-4)**

Narrowed to the primary **collaboration-quality** unit, paired with independent Work outcomes: **voice preservation, scope truth, provenance, artifact quality, and writer authority.** Session quality cannot waive those gates — a session that feels helpful can still degrade the artifact, the voice, or authorship.

> **Open dependency**: this narrowing is what places the frozen acceptance floor (research record §12, stated on the seven session questions alone) in tension. **Step 1 close item 8 is not disposed by this re-freeze** and remains amendable only while no blind model result exists.

### 7 · Complexity stays below the writer experience — **QUALIFIED (FQ-5)**

Hide **machinery**, not **meaningful authority or choice.** Provenance types, retrieval, anchors and internal ontology remain below the experience. Controls that determine what MAIA may do, remember, or change remain legible and usable. Hiding complexity must not accidentally hide consequential choice, system initiative, memory, or authority.

### 8 · Sovereignty governs what may ship; quality governs whether we ship — **CONSTITUTIONAL · EXEMPT · survives unamended**

A rule about how decisions are made, not a claim about the world. No finding can falsify it: it does not assert that sovereign systems perform better, only what may ship and on whose authority. **Self-insulating by design** — the ruled Strand G disposition already routes contrary evidence to `persistent capability gap → RECORDED FINDING`, never to a provider exception. **Amendable only by separate constitutional act**, never by a benchmark result.
