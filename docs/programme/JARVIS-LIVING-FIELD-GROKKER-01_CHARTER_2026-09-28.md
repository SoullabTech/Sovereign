- **Focus + Context:** entering feels like moving deeper into the same world; widening restores context.
- **Meaningful Transition:** a crossing matters when the question, distinction, resolution, perspective, or possibility changes.
- **Enriched Return:** widening preserves what became visible without silently rewriting canon or personal meaning.
- **Agency:** paths are invitations; dwelling, widening, disagreement, ambiguity, and unresolvedness remain legitimate.

- **Epistemic Standing:** relevance, resonance, symbolic possibility, personal meaning, canon, research evidence, and generated synthesis remain distinguishable.
