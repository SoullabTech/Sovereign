> H1 governs only the explicit Work-context crossing from House into Writer's Studio.
> This protocol tests whether that already-proven mechanism is experienced by a real member
> as continuity of their own Work rather than as navigation between unrelated software surfaces.

## 1 · Human-only question

The machine evidence is already sufficient for routing, authorization, bypass resistance and return-address correctness.

The remaining question cannot be proxied by code:

> **Does the member experience the Work as the same living Work carrying across House → Studio → Write / Develop / Review → Home?**

## 2 · What machines have already established

Do not ask the participant to re-prove these:

- only an admitted member receives the H1 House Work-context doorway;
- a non-admitted member receives plain Writer's Studio;
- a typed valid `work=` claim does not create H1 authority;
- an unauthenticated direct entry receives the sign-in boundary;
- the Studio validates the Work against the authenticated member;
- one-manuscript arrival requires an explicit open gesture;
- Work context survives Write → Develop → Review;
- Return Home returns without carrying manuscript content backward.

Human evidence must therefore concern lived continuity, comprehension, sovereignty and trust — not repeat mechanical acceptance testing.

## 3 · Participant and environment preconditions

Run only when all are true:

1. The member signs into their own ordinary production account.
2. The member already has a real Living Work suitable for the Work-context crossing.
3. No fixture, synthetic Work, or temporary relationship is created to make the witness pass.
4. Production health is green and the H1 cohort switch is open for that member.
5. No facilitator obtains, copies or inspects session credentials.
6. No new telemetry, recording, screen capture or product instrumentation is enabled for the witness.

## 4 · Consent and evidence posture

Before observation, tell the member plainly that:

- they are trying an early House → Writer's Studio continuity experience;
- their existing production Work remains governed by the ordinary product data policy;
- the facilitator may take minimal usability notes about navigation, hesitation and trust;
- no manuscript passage needs to be copied into the research record;
- audio/video recording is off unless separately and explicitly agreed;
- stopping the witness does not affect their account, Work, or cohort access.

The repository record uses a participant-neutral witness label. It does not preserve the member's name,
email, UUID, session token, manuscript title, manuscript excerpt or private reflective content.

## 5 · Participant-facing task

Give only this task before the first attempt:

> **Start from the House. Continue one of the Works that is alive for you into Writer's Studio.  
> Open the writing, move through Write, Develop and Review in whatever way makes sense to you, then return Home.  
> Say what you are noticing as you go if you are comfortable doing that.**

Do not explain H1, the `work=` parameter, cohort logic, controller names, or the intended answer.

Do not coach the first attempt unless safety, data loss or genuine inability to recover requires intervention.

## 6 · Observe, do not interpret for the member

Capture only:

```text
RECOGNITION
Did the member recognize the Work they intended to continue?

FIRST MOVE
What did they do before any coaching?

HESITATION
Where did they pause, reread, or become uncertain?

CONTINUITY
Did Write, Develop and Review feel like places within the same Work?

AUTHORITY
Did anything feel as though MAIA or the system had decided what the Work meant?

RETURN
Did Return Home feel like returning from the same journey, or like leaving an unrelated tool?

TRUST
Did the member worry that entering, exploring or changing modes could lose or alter their Work?

CURIOSITY
What, if anything, did they spontaneously want to do next?
```

Do not convert hesitation into a psychological claim about the participant. It is evidence about the product condition observed.

## 7 · Pre-registered adjudication

### PASS

A PASS requires all of the following:

- the intended Work is recognizable without facilitator interpretation;
- the explicit arrival/open gesture is understandable;
- the member experiences Write, Develop and Review as movements within the same Work;
- no system interpretation is mistaken for member-authored meaning;
- the member can return Home without feeling that Work continuity was lost;
- no authorship, privacy or data-loss concern remains unresolved.

### PASS WITH FRICTION

Use this when the continuity law holds but the member hesitates, misreads copy, or needs a recoverable UI clarification.
Record the friction as a product defect; do not rewrite the witness as a clean PASS.

### NOT ADMITTED / STOP

Stop the witness and close production H1 exposure if any of these appear:

- wrong-member or wrong-Work material;
- cohort authority bypass or unexpected exposure;
- silent manuscript selection where member choice is required;
- unexpected mutation or credible fear that original writing was lost;
- inability to return safely;
- any privacy, consent or authentication boundary failure.

Aesthetic dislike, unfamiliarity, or ordinary usability friction is not by itself a rollback condition.

## 8 · Questions after the walk

Ask only after the uncoached pass:

1. **When you moved from the House into Writer's Studio, did it feel like the same Work continued with you?**
2. **At any point did you wonder whether the system had chosen or interpreted something on your behalf?**
3. **When you moved among Write, Develop and Review, did you still know what Work you were in?**
4. **When you returned Home, did it feel like returning from somewhere in the same larger place?**
5. **Was there any moment you worried your writing could be lost or changed without you?**
6. **What would you naturally want to do next?**

## 9 · Evidence law

A human witness record must name:

- production runtime SHA;
- canonical SHA at the time of observation;
- production health;
- H1 cohort switch state;
- whether the member had an existing Living Work and declared manuscript relationship;
- device/browser class when relevant;
- the uncoached first attempt;
- any facilitator intervention;
- the member's own answers to the six post-walk questions, paraphrased unless an exact quote is necessary and consented.

Do not copy private manuscript content merely to explain a usability finding.

One person's PASS establishes that H1 can be lived coherently by that participant under that production condition.
It does **not** establish universal usability, cohort-wide preference, or flagship acceptance.

## 10 · Standing after protocol ratification

Ratifying this protocol changes no software and does not itself human-admit H1.
