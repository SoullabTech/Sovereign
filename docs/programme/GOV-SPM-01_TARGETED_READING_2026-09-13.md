## 1 · ⭐ R22 vs BASIS — the founder's caution is confirmed

**S5's seven questions, verbatim** — *every durable object must eventually be able to answer:*

```text
Who created me?           — authorship
What generated me?        — mechanism

May I persist?            — persistence permission
May I become collective?  — collective eligibility
May I be restored?        — restoration permission
May I be forgotten?       — deletion guarantee
```

**Verdict:** R22 is a **strong enforcement precedent for governed minting** and is **not** BASIS.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ `LONGITUDINAL_MEMORY_CATEGORY_GRADIENT` — the formation gate already exists as its own doctrine

**Status:** *Working doctrine — not implementation spec*, 2026-05-20. Sibling to `UNPOSSESSED`.

**It answers exactly one question per category:** *should this category form?* — and explicitly **not**
how, when, what the member sees, or how they revoke.

> ⭐ **"This document is the gate before any of those documents are written. Inference-time wiring of
> longitudinal patterns may not proceed until the category each pattern belongs to has been classified
> here."**

**Core invariant:**

> ⭐⭐ **"MAIA may remember in service of continuity, but may not form identity around a member faster
> than the member participates in that formation."**
>
> *Memory the member is authoring or co-authoring is permissible. Memory the system is constructing
> **about** the member, **ahead of** the member, is not.*

⭐ **The discriminator is tempo, not only category.** "Ahead of" is a relation between the system's
formation rate and the member's participation — which no per-claim property can express, because it is
a property of the *relationship over time*.

**Standing consequence for SPM, recorded not acted on:** the gradient asserts a **precondition on
inference-time wiring**. SPM-01 specifies an object whose claims include inferred ones. ⛔ That
dependency is live and belongs in v0.3's conditions, not in this lane's rulings.
