> **May the organism hold — or let another person hold — a representation of a member, or take an
> act concerning a member, that the member cannot see, dispute, or erase?**

## 1 · ⭐⭐ The centre of `SQ-3`, as the sequencing ruling named it

> **Can the organism constitutionally USE a representation concerning a member in a CONSEQUENTIAL
> way if the member has NO STANDING toward that representation?**

⭐ `ACT 4` makes this unavoidable: it ruled that **the authority boundary attaches to USE, not merely
storage** — ⛔ and then stopped before the member's standing toward what is used.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ The chain the ruling must not collapse

```text
HELD ABOUT A MEMBER
  ≠ SHOWN TO THE MEMBER
      ≠ DISPUTABLE BY THE MEMBER
          ≠ REVISABLE BY THE MEMBER
              ≠ ERASABLE BY THE MEMBER
                  ≠ USABLE BY THE ORGANISM
                      ≠ DISCLOSABLE TO ANOTHER HUMAN
```

⭐ **Seven positions, ⛔ not one right described seven ways.** ⚠️ A founder may grant some and
withhold others, and may attach **different conditions and different exceptions** to each.

### 2a · ⚠️ `USABLE BY THE ORGANISM` sits deliberately INSIDE the member-rights chain

⭐ It is **not** a member right — ⭐ it is the axis where `ACT 4`'s **use-not-storage** boundary meets
member standing. ⛔ Its placement is a question, ⛔ not an answer: *does the organism's permission to
USE depend on the member's position in the preceding four, or is it independent of them?*

### 2b · ⭐ The packet's two limbs are now seven positions

The packet recorded **two distinguishable limbs — what may be `HELD`, and what must be `TOLD`** —
and that **a founder may answer them differently.** ⭐ The chain above refines that; ⛔ it does not
supersede it, and ⛔ the two-limb distinction remains available to the ruling.

## 3 · ⭐ The question `ACT 4` left open, stated exactly

```text
ACT 4 §9   created MEMBER-ADOPTED CLAIM as a standing a durable person-level conclusion may carry
ACT 4 §15  ruled member adoption AN ACT DISTINCT FROM SYSTEM INFERENCE
           ⛔ absence of objection is not adoption · ⛔ silence is not confirmation
⛔ NEITHER  governs whether the member must be able to SEE what they would be adopting
```

> ⭐⭐ **What is the constitutional ENTRY CONDITION for `MEMBER-ADOPTED CLAIM`?** ⛔ Without it, that
> standing exists and cannot lawfully be reached.

## 4 · Questions the ruling must hold apart

```text

                      ⚠️ and is holding by the ORGANISM different from holding by ANOTHER HUMAN?

2  THIRD-PARTY        May a practitioner, facilitator or other person hold a representation of a
   HOLDING           member that the member cannot see? ⚠️ under whose authority?

                      on request · never?

4  DISPUTE            May a member contest a representation — ⚠️ and what follows a dispute?
                      ⛔ correction · annotation · suspension of use · nothing — are different.

5  ERASURE            May a member erase? ⚠️ Distinguish ERASING THE REPRESENTATION from
                      ERASING THE FACT THAT IT EXISTED. ⭐ The census's tombstone precedent is
                      evidence that these can diverge.

6  DERIVED VISIBILITY ⚠️ Existence inferable from metadata, counts, ordering, notifications,
                      latency or withdrawal-by-difference. ⛔ Is derived visibility governed by
                      the same rule as direct visibility?

7  INPUT SIDE         ⚠️ A content-based refusal decides whether a member's spoken words become a
                      turn, with no surface to the member. ⛔ Is the INPUT side governed by the
                      same doctrine as the OUTPUT side, or separately?

8  EXCEPTIONS         Are there representations that may lawfully be held unseen?
                      ⚠️ safety · another person's privacy · operational integrity · none.
                      ⛔ If exceptions exist, by what act are they created?
```
