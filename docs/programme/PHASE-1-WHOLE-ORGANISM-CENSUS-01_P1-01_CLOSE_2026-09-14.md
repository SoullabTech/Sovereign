⭐ **The RFI collision has teeth.** Any sentence of the form *"RFI is built"* or *"RFI is not

be made against the word — only against a named object.

**per named object**, and report the collision as evidence. ⛔ A worker that resolves a collision
by choosing one sense has manufactured a ruling.

### 3c · Documents that disclaim their own authority are the corpus's most common shape

Slice 01 found eleven, slice 04 five, slice 05 eight, slice 08 twelve of eighteen. One answers the
question *"what does this map authorize"* with the single word: **Nothing.**

⭐ The inverse is the drift risk, and every slice was asked to rank it: **candidate or draft
documents written in ratified voice.** The worst case found is a document whose header says it is
*recorded, not ratified… rules nothing*, whose body is pure imperative constitutional language, and
which never restates the disclaimer below the header — so a reader entering mid-document reads law.

⚠️ And the reverse failure: **documents self-declaring "Canon" with no recorded ratification act.**
Under the ratification-boundary precedent, asserting canon status is not ratification.

## 5 · What P1-01 changes about P1-02's method

Four constraints the census could not have known before this step.

1. **Census per named object, never per word** (§3a). Domains D (field), H (voice) and B (memory)
   all carry colliding vocabulary.
2. **Domain A has no canonical boundary to measure against** (§4.1). It must report the organism's
   actual convergence points and mark the canonical boundary `UNKNOWN`, ⛔ not adopt the
   authorized-but-unreached target as though it were law.
3. **The checkable / non-checkable split from slice 01 is binding.** Structural constraints with
   named artifacts and literal banned strings may be reported with evidence. The Oath,
   *fluency-is-not-fidelity*, and every litmus test of the form *"the center of knowing must feel
   closer to the user"* may not. ⛔ **A worker must not invent a proxy metric for a constraint the
   corpus states as judgement.**
4. **Eight canon-named code artifacts were recorded as named-but-unverified.** P1-02 may check
   existence; ⛔ it may not treat a document's citation as evidence the artifact exists.
