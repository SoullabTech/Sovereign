## What R9 discovered

The critical distinction is:

```text
SAFE LANGUAGE
    ≠
USEFUL AETHERIC LANGUAGE
```

A sentence can avoid all dangerous claims and still fail because it carries no meaningful field reflection.

Likewise:

```text
SPECIFIC LANGUAGE
    ≠
LEGITIMATE AETHERIC LANGUAGE
```

A sentence can be vivid and specific while exceeding its authority.

The valid middle therefore requires both:

> **restraint + specificity**

## Example contrast

Overreach:

> Your spiritual dissolution is making room for your creative emergence.

Underreach:

> There may be some kind of pattern here. What do you think?

Admitted:

> Spiritual life appears to be dissolving an older form while Creative life is becoming more coherent. I do not know whether those movements are related. Do you?

The admitted statement contains real field content but leaves the relation's meaning open.

## Stronger finding
