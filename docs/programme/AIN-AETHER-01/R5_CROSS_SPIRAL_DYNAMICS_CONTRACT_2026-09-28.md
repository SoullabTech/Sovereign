# AIN-AETHER-01R5 — Cross-Spiral Field Dynamics Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `a2fbafb88c7e33af0082895266d95b1bfa40608c`

## Purpose

R5 adds time to the relations among domain-specific spirals.

The question is not only what each life domain is doing, but how the relations among those domain trajectories themselves change.

> **Cross-spiral relations may form, dissolve, or change kind; none of those changes establishes causation, hierarchy, or destiny.**

## Relation-delta grammar

A cross-spiral relation may be:

- formed;
- dissolved;
- changed in kind;
- persisted.

The system preserves the before and after relation kinds explicitly.

## Newly related law

When a pair moves from `independent_movement` to a substantive relation such as `co_convergence`, R5 may describe the pair as newly related.

This permits an inquiry such as:

> Work and Creative life were previously moving independently and now show co-convergent movement. What, if anything, does the member recognize in that change?

It does not permit:

> Creative life caused the Work transition.

## Newly independent law

When a pair moves from a substantive relation back to `independent_movement`, R5 may describe a release or decoupling in the reflected field.

This is not automatically loss, failure, fragmentation, avoidance, or healing.

Independence is a legitimate relation state.

## Reconfiguration law

A pair may remain related while the kind of relation changes.

For example:

- co-convergence → timing mismatch;
- shared recurrence → tension;
- tension → independent movement.

The relation history matters.

R5 does not flatten these into a single coupling score.

## Emergence law

R5 may form provisional cross-spiral emergence candidates when a pair becomes newly related or newly independent.

These candidates are invitations to inquiry.

They explicitly carry:

- `causalAuthority: false`;
- `predictiveAuthority: false`;
- `destinyAuthority: false`;
- `provisional: true`.

## Non-causal law

Temporal co-change is not evidence of causation.

The system may say:

> these two domain movements changed relation at the same time.

It may not say:

> one caused the other

unless separate evidence legitimately supports that claim.

## No hidden hierarchy

A newly related pair is not better than an independent pair.

A dissolved relation is not worse than a formed relation.

A timing mismatch does not mean one spiral is behind.

R5 describes relation dynamics without ranking them.

## Member recognition

Cross-spiral emergence becomes humanly meaningful only through member recognition, contextual evidence, or later corroboration.

The member may say:

- yes, those domains do feel connected now;
- no, that simultaneity is incidental;
- they are related, but differently than represented;
- they used to be linked, but I now experience them separately.

Member correction takes precedence over the machine's proposed relation.

## Authority boundary

The cross-spiral field delta carries:

- `causalAuthority: false`;
- `predictiveAuthority: false`;
- `finalMeaningAuthority: member`;
- `provisional: true`.

## Soul-Service law

> **Aether may witness relationships among life's movements without converting simultaneity into explanation.**

The system reflects the dance among the spirals. It does not decide what ultimately moves the dancer.

## No-build boundary

R5 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No automatic causal inference.
No production persistence.
No cross-domain advice engine.
No hidden developmental ranking.
