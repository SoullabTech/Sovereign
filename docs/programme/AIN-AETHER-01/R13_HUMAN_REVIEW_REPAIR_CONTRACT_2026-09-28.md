# AIN-AETHER-01R13 — Human-Review Repair Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `f8c6ea19aede44501521651892ea1134500ca33a`

## Purpose

R13 governs how a human semantic adjudication may produce a candidate repaired Aether reflection.

> **Human correction governs the active interpretation; evidence boundaries still govern what can be asserted as supported.**

## Three-layer custody

R13 preserves three immutable historical layers:

1. frozen R11 machine witness;
2. R12 human review record;
3. R13 candidate repair result.

A later layer may supersede the active reflection without rewriting an earlier layer.

## Repair law

A human repair request contains:

- case reference;
- adjudication;
- correction note;
- corrected reflection;
- claim kind;
- spiral references.

The corrected reflection is not activated merely because it was authored by a human.

It must pass the same governed dialogue and semantic-fidelity boundaries used for machine-generated language.
## Dual-gate law

A candidate repair must pass:

1. **dialogue posture** — no identity, destiny, causal, diagnostic, Soul, or empty-language failure;
2. **semantic fidelity** — the corrected reflection must remain grounded in the frozen represented field.

Both are required.

## Human authority and evidence authority

Human judgment may establish that the original reflection was:

- incomplete;
- lifeless;
- badly phrased;
- overinterpreted;
- semantically wrong;
- appropriately refused.

Human judgment therefore governs the need for repair and the lived adequacy of the active interpretation.

But a human correction does not automatically convert unsupported claims into supported ones.

A repair that introduces causation, unsupported relation, destiny, identity, diagnosis, or Soul authority remains inadmissible as a grounded Aether claim.

## Accepted repair

When both gates pass:

- the corrected reflection becomes the active candidate;
- the original machine utterance remains preserved;
- the human adjudication remains preserved;
- final meaning remains member-owned.

## Refused repair

When either gate fails:

- the candidate does not become active;
- the refusal records the exact posture or semantic errors;
- the human review itself is not invalidated;
- a different correction may be proposed later.
## Historical-integrity law

Every repair result carries:

- `machineWitnessMutated: false`;
- `humanReviewRecordMutated: false`.

The system therefore distinguishes:

> changing what is active

from:

> changing what happened.

## Soul-Service law

> **Human participation deepens the reflection without granting either human or machine interpretation the right to possess the whole person.**

The member remains more than every successive interpretation.

## No-build boundary

R13 remains benchmark-only.

No live MAIA route binding.
No production prompt mutation.
No member-data persistence.
No automatic repair promotion.
