# AIN-AETHER-01R10 — Dialogue Semantic Fidelity Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `9bcb670dc287e460b37a9caed8bff1ef7f7c6f86`

## Purpose

R10 binds spoken Aether reflection to the actual field evidence that warrants it.

> **Every spoken Aether reflection must be traceable to the field evidence and relation history that justify it.**

## Posture is not enough

R9 establishes whether an utterance is appropriately reflective and useful.

R10 adds a second requirement:

> the content of the reflection must be semantically supported by the current field.

A graceful sentence cannot compensate for unsupported meaning.

## Evidence-bound claim law

A spoken reflection declares its claim kind and the field objects it depends on.

R10 currently supports claim families such as:

- spirals related;
- spirals more independent;
- trajectory comparison;
- partial gestalt.

Each claim must name existing spirals and, where relevant, existing cross-spiral relations.

## Relation fidelity

A claim that two spirals are moving in related ways requires an explicit non-independent relation between those spirals.

If the field says `independent_movement`, MAIA may not speak a stronger relational claim simply because the sentence sounds plausible.

Likewise, an independence reflection requires the current relation to actually be independent.

## Trajectory fidelity

When MAIA compares two domain trajectories, the spoken language must correspond to their represented trajectory classifications.

Comparison does not imply causation.

A phrase such as:

> Spiritual life is showing dissolution while Creative life is showing sustained convergence.

may be grounded if those trajectories are present.

The further claim:

> Spiritual dissolution is causing Creative convergence.

requires separate evidence and remains outside R10 authority.

## Partial-gestalt fidelity

A partial-gestalt utterance may name only spirals participating in the current gestalt.

Its provenance must preserve the spirals that remain outside the gestalt.

The spoken synthesis therefore stays partial both internally and in explanation.

## Unsupported-claim refusal

If an utterance references:

- an unknown spiral;
- an unknown relation;
- a relation stronger than the field supports;
- a gestalt participant that is not actually in the gestalt;

R10 refuses semantic admission.

No provenance explanation is produced for an unsupported reflection.

## Human-legible provenance

For every admitted reflection, MAIA can answer:

> Why did you say that?

The explanation contains:

- which life areas mattered;
- how each is currently represented;
- which cross-domain relations supported the reflection;
- what remained outside the gestalt;
- what the explanation does **not** establish;
- an invitation for the member to correct or reshape the reflection.

This explanation is designed for the member, not for internal debugging.

## Why-this-reflection law

The provenance explanation must say why this particular reflection was offered rather than merely listing evidence.

For example:

> I offered the reflection because Work and Creative life have an explicit non-independent relation in the current field, not simply because they appeared at the same time.

That distinction protects against narrative confabulation.

## Uncertainty law

Every member-legible explanation states that evidential support for offering the reflection does not establish its final human meaning.

Evidence answers:

> Why was this reflection warranted?

The member still answers:

> What does it mean in my life?

## Authority boundary

Semantic fidelity does not increase Aether's person-level authority.

Grounded reflection remains:

- provisional;
- corrigible;
- non-causal unless separately established;
- non-predictive;
- non-identitarian;
- non-Soul-representational.

## Soul-Service law

> **A reflection should be able to show its roots without pretending its roots exhaust the living tree.**

Provenance makes Aether accountable without reducing the member to evidence structures.

## No-build boundary

R10 remains benchmark-only.

No live MAIA route binding.
No member data.
No production provenance UI.
No persistent person model.
