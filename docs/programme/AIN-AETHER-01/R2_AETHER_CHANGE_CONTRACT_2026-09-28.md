# AIN-AETHER-01R2 — Aether Change Contract

Date: 2026-09-28

## Purpose

R2 defines Aether as a changing relational field rather than a static portrait.

> **Aether change is not only change in what is present; it is change in how what is present belongs to the whole.**

## Two layers of change

R2 distinguishes:

1. **pattern-state change** — a motif appears, disappears, intensifies, dissipates, reconfigures, shifts temporally, or changes in member recognition;
2. **field-geometry change** — relations among motifs are added, removed, strengthened, weakened, or reorganized.

These must not be collapsed into one score.

## Pattern delta grammar

A pattern may be:

- appeared;
- disappeared;
- intensified;
- dissipated;
- reconfigured;
- temporally shifted;
- recognition changed;
- unchanged.

A motif may persist while its role changes substantially.

## Field geometry

R2 represents relations among field patterns through explicit bases:

- shared facet;
- shared quality;
- shared temporal standing;
- contradiction link.

These relations are descriptive of the active reflected field. They are not claims of causal mechanism.

## Reorganization law

A transformation can occur even when the central motif remains.

For example:

```text
before                  after
grief — fear             grief — love
  |                        |  \
  └— isolation             |   creativity
                           └— memory
```

The significant change is not necessarily the disappearance of grief.

It may be the change in what grief is related to, what qualities it carries, and what other motifs organize around it.

## Intensification and dissipation

R2 may call a pattern `intensified` when it gains active facet participation without losing prior facets.

It may call a pattern `dissipated` when facet participation decreases.

These words describe changes in represented field participation. They do not measure psychic energy as a physical quantity.

## Temporal correction law

When the member corrects temporal standing, the active field recomputes and a delta is generated.

The baseline field remains available as the prior reflection.

The correction therefore changes the active trajectory without rewriting history.

## Geometry is not destiny

A relation appearing in the field means the current representation contains a declared basis for relation.

It does not establish:

- causation;
- diagnosis;
- inevitability;
- permanent personality structure;
- spiritual truth.

## Member authority

Every field delta remains provisional.

Final meaning authority remains with the member.

The member may recognize, reject, reshape, or contextualize the apparent movement.

## Soul-Service law

> **Transformation may be reflected without being possessed.**

Aether may show that the field has reorganized.

It does not claim to know the final meaning of that reorganization for Soul.

## No-build boundary

R2 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No production persistence.
No automated interpretation to the member.
No developmental ranking.
No hidden stage assignment.
