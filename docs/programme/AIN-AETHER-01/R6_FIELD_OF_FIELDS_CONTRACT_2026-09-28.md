# AIN-AETHER-01R6 — Field-of-Fields Synthesis Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `496d185efcf111271de34a47361ef6be805c6e2a`

## Purpose

R6 asks whether relations among multiple life spirals can disclose a higher-order Aetheric gestalt without turning that gestalt into a totalizing story about the member.

> **Aetheric gestalt is a partial field pattern, never the whole person.**

## Field-of-fields law

A higher-order gestalt may be proposed when several domain spirals participate in a sufficiently strong relational configuration.

The gestalt must name:

- participating spirals;
- excluded spirals;
- supporting cross-spiral relations;
- excluded relations;
- its provisional standing.

The field must remain inspectable from the gestalt back to its constituent spirals.

## Non-fit preservation law

Every spiral that does not support the gestalt remains explicitly represented outside it.

Non-fit is not noise to be discarded.

A domain may remain:

- independent;
- contradictory;
- asynchronous;
- weakly related;
- irrelevant to the present gestalt.

The system is not permitted to reinterpret non-fitting domains merely to make the higher-order pattern cleaner.

## Partiality law

A candidate gestalt is admitted only as partial.

It does not claim:

- all spirals participate;
- all relations point the same way;
- the gestalt explains the member;
- the gestalt is stable across time;
- the gestalt is developmentally superior to other configurations.

## Refusal law

If the cross-spiral field is too weak, R6 may return:

- `partial_gestalt`;
- `insufficient_gestalt`.

The architecture is not rewarded for producing a gestalt in every field.

Refusal is preferable to totalization.

## Ablation law

A participating spiral may be removed and the gestalt recomputed.

If the gestalt remains identical despite removal of a supposedly constitutive spiral, the claimed dependence is suspect.

The gestalt therefore remains answerable to its composition.

## Etheric interpretation

Within Soullab's ontology, the Aetheric gestalt may describe a field-level quality emerging among several life processes.

Computationally, it represents relational organization among trajectories.

It does not claim instrumentally verified detection of a metaphysical substance.

## Authority boundary

Every Aetheric gestalt carries:

- `totalizingAuthority: false`;
- `identityAuthority: false`;
- `causalAuthority: false`;
- `predictiveAuthority: false`;
- `destinyAuthority: false`;
- `developmentalRankAuthority: false`;
- `soulRepresentationAuthority: false`;
- `finalMeaningAuthority: member`;
- `provisional: true`.

## Soul-Service law

> **The whole may become partially visible without becoming computationally possessed.**

A gestalt is a reflection of a configuration in the member's field.

It is not the member, not the Soul, and not a final account of the life.

## No-build boundary

R6 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No production persistence.
No person-level scoring.
No automatic spiritual interpretation.
No totalizing member summary.
