# AIN-AETHER-01R3 — Field Trajectory Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `23c83ccc895af27037a43d405ce80a32db0649a6`

## Purpose

R3 extends Aether from field state and field delta into multi-moment trajectory.

> **Trajectory describes sustained movement already witnessed across several reflected moments; it does not predict what comes next.**

## Minimum evidence law

Two moments are sufficient for a delta but not for a trajectory claim.

R3 requires at least three moments before any trajectory classification other than `insufficient_evidence` is permitted.

## Trajectory grammar

R3 may describe:

- oscillation;
- recurrence;
- sustained convergence;
- dissolution;
- phase change;
- ordinary fluctuation;
- insufficient evidence.

These are descriptive classes of reflected field movement, not diagnoses or stages of human development.

## Recurrence law

A motif that disappears from an intervening active field and later returns may be described as recurrent.

Recurrence does not imply regression, failure, pathology, or repetition of the same psychological meaning.

The returning motif may belong to a different constellation.

## Sustained convergence law

A field may be described as showing sustained convergence when several sequential moments repeatedly intensify or reconfigure a motif across facets.

This describes repeated cross-facet participation.

It does not establish that the motif is the member's essence or future direction.

## Phase-change law

A phase change is a substantial reorganization of reflected field geometry across witnessed moments.

A phase-change description may be supported when:

- important motifs persist;
- the relations among them change substantially;
- earlier relations recede;
- new relations become characteristic across later moments.

> **A phase change describes an observed reorganization. It is not a forecast.**

## Dissolution law

An organizing configuration may be described as dissolving when multiple patterns and relations progressively recede from the active field.

Dissolution is not automatically healing, decline, death, failure, transcendence, or advancement.

Its meaning remains contextual and member-owned.

## Oscillation law

Alternation between configurations may be described as oscillation.

Oscillation is not automatically indecision or pathology. It may reflect a living polarity, changing context, incomplete integration, rhythm, or ordinary fluctuation.

R3 does not decide among those meanings without further evidence and member recognition.

## Confidence law

Trajectory confidence expresses confidence in the descriptive classification of the represented sequence.

It is not confidence that the person's life will continue in that direction.

## Authority boundary

Every trajectory carries:

- `predictiveAuthority: false`;
- `destinyAuthority: false`;
- `developmentalRankAuthority: false`;
- `finalMeaningAuthority: member`;
- `provisional: true`.

No trajectory becomes a developmental ladder or fate claim.

## Historical integrity

Each moment remains independently available.

Later trajectory synthesis does not rewrite earlier field reflections.

Member correction may create a different active trajectory while preserving the prior sequence as historical representation.

## Soul-Service law

> **Aether may witness the shape of movement without claiming to know its destination.**

Soul is not converted into a trajectory model.

## No-build boundary

R3 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No predictive notifications.
No stage assignment.
No production persistence.
No automatic life-direction advice.
