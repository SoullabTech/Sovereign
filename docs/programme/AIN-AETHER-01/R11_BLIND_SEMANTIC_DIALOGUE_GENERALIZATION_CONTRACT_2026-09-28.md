# AIN-AETHER-01R11 — Blind Semantic Dialogue Generalization Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `b8b26c319fbcf578455c03e4f3669401c87d4017`

## Purpose

R11 tests whether evidence-bound Aetheric dialogue generalizes beyond the development constellation used in R10.

> **Machine grounding and human semantic adequacy are different questions and remain separately adjudicated.**

## Held-out law

Field constellations are frozen before evaluation.

Expected machine outcomes are likewise frozen before adjudication.

The benchmark is not permitted to relabel a failed case merely because the generated language is attractive or plausible.

## Held-out constellations

R11 freezes five distinct fields:

1. Work dissolution + Relationship convergence + Body recurrence;
2. Family oscillation + Creative phase change + Spiritual recurrence;
3. mostly independent domains with no legitimate higher-order relation;
4. strong Work/Creative convergence with Relationship oscillation;
5. a tempting but unsupported Family/Body synthesis.

These are intentionally different from the primary R10 development witness.

## Machine-adjudication scope

Machine validation may establish:

- named spirals exist;
- named relations exist;
- the proposed relation strength matches the field;
- the utterance carries acceptable dialogue posture;
- admitted utterances have provenance;
- unsupported claims are refused;
- person-level authority boundaries remain closed.

Machine validation does **not** establish that the reflection is the best, deepest, most alive, or most psychologically faithful human interpretation.

## Human-adjudication protocol

Every held-out case remains `humanReview.status = pending` after machine validation.

Allowed human judgments include:

- `semantically_faithful`;
- `useful_but_incomplete`;
- `technically_grounded_but_lifeless`;
- `overinterpreted`;
- `beautiful_but_unsupported`;
- `wrong_relation`;
- `appropriate_refusal`.

Human review asks:

- Is the reflection semantically faithful to the represented field?
- Is it specific enough to be useful without overinterpreting?
- Does it preserve what does not fit?
- Would a member understand why it was offered?
- Is it alive and relational rather than merely technically correct?
- If refused, was refusal appropriate rather than over-cautious?

## Beautiful-but-unsupported law

A beautiful or resonant interpretation receives no exemption from evidence binding.

If the field does not support the relation, the machine must refuse it and must not generate a persuasive provenance story after the fact.

> **Beauty does not rescue unsupported meaning.**

## Appropriate-refusal law

Human review also evaluates refusal quality.

The system can fail by refusing too much.

A correct evidence boundary should refuse unsupported synthesis while still admitting grounded comparison, independence, recurrence, divergence, or uncertainty where those are genuinely present.

## Generalization result boundary

R11 machine success means only:

> the evidence-binding and dialogue-authority rules behaved correctly on the frozen held-out machine cases.

It does not mean:

> the Aetheric dialogue has been human-approved for runtime.

Human semantic review remains a separate gate.

## Soul-Service law

> **The system may verify that a reflection has roots; only lived human encounter can determine whether the reflection bears meaningful fruit.**

## No-build boundary

R11 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No production dialogue binding.
No automated claim of human semantic adequacy.
No runtime promotion.
