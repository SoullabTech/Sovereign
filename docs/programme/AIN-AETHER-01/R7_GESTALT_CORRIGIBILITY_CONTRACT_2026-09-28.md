# AIN-AETHER-01R7 — Gestalt Corrigibility Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `619d8f18fe3ca8d637c2246516ab7c9bcef575aa`

## Purpose

R7 gives the member direct authority over the highest current Aether reflection: the field-of-fields gestalt.

> **Aether may propose the gestalt; the member decides whether it is recognized, rejected, narrowed, expanded, or reshaped.**

## Recognition grammar

A gestalt may be:

- unreviewed;
- recognized;
- partly recognized;
- rejected;
- reshaped.

Recognition does not widen the gestalt's epistemic authority.

A recognized gestalt remains provisional, non-causal, non-predictive, non-totalizing, and non-identitarian.

## Rejection law

The member may reject the higher-order gestalt entirely.

When rejected:

- the active gestalt becomes insufficient for interpretation;
- the original proposal remains available as historical representation;
- domain spirals remain intact;
- relation history remains intact;
- disagreement is not reinterpreted as resistance or confirmation.

## Narrowing law

The member may say that one or more participating spirals do not belong in the gestalt.

The active gestalt then recomputes around the remaining participating spirals and their actual relations.

The excluded spiral remains in the source field.

It is removed from the active gestalt, not erased from the member's life history.

## Expansion law

The member may say that a previously excluded spiral belongs in the higher-order pattern.

R7 may add that spiral to the active gestalt and recompute supporting and excluded relations.

Member inclusion changes the active reflective frame; it does not retroactively prove that the machine should have included that spiral originally.

## Historical-integrity law

Every correction preserves two layers:

1. **original machine proposal** — what Aether reflected before member correction;
2. **active corrected gestalt** — the member-shaped reflection now used for interpretation.

The architecture therefore preserves epistemic history without granting the earlier proposal continuing interpretive authority.

## Member note law

Corrections carry the member's own note.

The note is not treated merely as metadata around the machine model. It is part of the authoritative correction act.

## Correction precedence

At the level of member meaning:

> **member correction outranks Aetheric synthesis.**

This does not mean the member's interpretation becomes universal fact.

It means the system does not possess superior authority over what the gestalt means for the member.

## Authority boundary

Correction must never widen the underlying gestalt authority.

Even after member recognition, expansion, or reshaping:

- totalizing authority remains false;
- identity authority remains false;
- Soul-representation authority remains false;
- causal authority remains false;
- predictive authority remains false.

Final meaning authority remains with the member.

## Soul-Service law

> **The member may reshape the reflection because the reflection is in service to the life, not the life in service to the reflection.**

Aether must remain corrigible at its highest layer.

## No-build boundary

R7 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No persistence into a member profile.
No automatic reinterpretation after rejection.
No hidden inference that disagreement means denial.
