# AIN-AETHER-01R4 — Multi-Spiral Field Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `499fc344b9cccb8d96757a4497e1a9407adf5deb`

## Purpose

R4 establishes that a member may inhabit multiple domain-specific trajectories simultaneously.

> **The member is a field of trajectories, not a single developmental coordinate.**

## Domain law

Aether may hold distinct spirals for domains such as:

- relationship;
- family;
- work;
- creative life;
- body;
- spiritual life;
- community;
- other member-defined domains.

The domain list is extensible. It is not a taxonomy of the person's essence.

## Independent trajectory law

Each domain preserves its own trajectory classification and evidence.

One domain's movement does not automatically determine another domain's movement.

A member may simultaneously show, for example:

- relationship oscillation;
- work phase change;
- family recurrence;
- creative sustained convergence;
- bodily ordinary fluctuation;
- spiritual dissolution.

This is a valid field configuration.

## No single-stage law

R4 explicitly forbids collapsing domain trajectories into a single member stage.

The system has:

- `singleStageAuthority: false`;
- `developmentalRankAuthority: false`.

No averaging function may convert several domain trajectories into one developmental score.

## Cross-spiral relation law

Aether may propose provisional relations among domain trajectories when there is an explicit basis.

R4 recognizes relations such as:

- co-convergence;
- timing mismatch;
- tension;
- mutual support;
- shared recurrence;
- shared dissolution;
- independent movement.

The final category is essential.

Two spirals are allowed to coexist without the system inventing a meaningful dependency between them.

## Timing mismatch

When one domain is dissolving while another is converging or changing phase, Aether may describe a timing mismatch.

This is not a judgment that one domain is behind or ahead.

It describes asynchronous movement.

## Co-convergence

Two domains may show related convergent movement at the same time.

Co-convergence does not establish causation.

It is a field-level invitation to inquiry:

> What, if anything, does the member recognize in the simultaneity?

## Independence law

If no substantive cross-spiral relation is supported, R4 preserves `independent_movement`.

Aether is not rewarded for maximizing connectedness.

## Member-defined domains

The member may name, split, merge, or reject domains that do not fit lived experience.

The architecture must not force all human life into a fixed domain schema.

## Authority boundary

The multi-spiral field carries:

- `singleStageAuthority: false`;
- `developmentalRankAuthority: false`;
- `predictiveAuthority: false`;
- `finalMeaningAuthority: member`;
- `provisional: true`.

Cross-spiral relation does not become destiny, diagnosis, or causal explanation.

## Soul-Service law

> **Wholeness does not require synchrony.**

A living person may be opening in one domain, grieving in another, resting in another, and beginning again somewhere else.

Aether serves that polyphony rather than forcing it into one developmental story.

## No-build boundary

R4 remains benchmark-only.

No live member data.
No single-stage dashboard.
No developmental scoring.
No production persistence.
No automatic cross-domain life advice.

## Next boundary

> **AIN-AETHER-01R5 — CROSS-SPIRAL FIELD DYNAMICS · RELATION CHANGES AMONG DOMAIN TRAJECTORIES + NON-CAUSAL EMERGENCE ONLY**

R5 should test how relations among spirals themselves change over time—for example when Work and Creative life begin converging after a long period of independence, or when Family recurrence ceases to organize Relationship movement—without converting correlation into causation.
