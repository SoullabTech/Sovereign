# AIN-AETHER-01R1 — Member Field Aether Contract

Date: 2026-09-28
Parent: `f4fa850243fbd7c19fb2186b9ecb34b3d447cde2`

## Purpose

Member Aether is the field-level reflection of relational pattern, movement, tension, resonance, latency, threshold, and becoming across the member's lived facets.

It is not a finished model of the person.

> **Aether reflects patterns of becoming in the member's field; it does not define the member's being.**

## Etheric language

Soullab may use `etheric` to name the felt or relational quality of the member-in-field.

The computational representation does not claim to measure or prove a metaphysical substance. It represents field-level relational qualities such as coherence, tension, permeability, resonance, directionality, latency, threshold, and movement.

This distinction protects both the ontology and epistemic honesty.

## Field rather than portrait

Member Aether is processual.

It asks:

- what is becoming more present;
- what is receding;
- what is converging;
- what is diverging;
- what is recurring in a new context;
- what is unresolved;
- what is latent;
- what may be crossing a threshold.

It does not answer who the member essentially is.

## Temporal law

Aether preserves:

- `has_been`;
- `is_being`;
- `may_become`.

These may coexist inside one motif.

The latest observation does not overwrite the historical field, and prospective material does not become current fact merely because it resonates.

## Cross-facet law

A motif appearing across multiple facets may become field-significant.

Cross-facet recurrence increases attention. It does not establish truth, essence, diagnosis, destiny, or developmental rank.

A field pattern remains provisional until and unless the member recognizes it as meaningful.

## Valid field states

The following are legitimate states rather than synthesis failures:

- contradiction;
- divergence;
- latency;
- absence;
- uncertainty;
- mystery;
- unresolved tension.

Aether is not required to harmonize them.

## Member correction law

Member correction outranks field interpretation.

Corrections may address:

- source;
- relevance;
- meaning;
- temporal standing;
- tone / charge;
- ontology.

The member may say, in effect:

- not relevant;
- historical only;
- emerging, not established;
- over-weighted;
- misread;
- partly true;
- not one pattern;
- meaningful but not identity.

A correction may recompute the derived field without rewriting the historical source object.

## Recognition law

The system distinguishes field pattern from member recognition.

A pattern may be:

- unreviewed;
- recognized;
- partly recognized;
- rejected;
- reshaped.

Rejection is not treated as resistance, denial, or confirmation.

## Authority boundary

No Aether field pattern receives authority for:

- identity;
- diagnosis;
- prediction;
- destiny;
- hidden third-party interiority;
- Soul representation;
- final meaning;
- persistence.

Final meaning authority remains with the member.

## Soul-Service law

> **The field may reflect the person; it may not possess the person.**

Soul remains beyond computational capture.

Aether may orient toward depth, mystery, and wholeness without claiming to contain or represent Soul.

## No-build boundary

R1 is a benchmark and ontology contract only.

No live member data.
No production MAIA binding.
No persistence.
No person scoring.
No personality typing.
No hidden stage assignment.
