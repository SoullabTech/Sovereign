# BECOMING-02R1 — Reconciliation and founder decision docket

**Standing:** Findings and proposed dispositions, not invented founder rulings.
**Boundary:** Meaning, experience, and contract reconciliation only. No runtime or historical-source mutation.

## D-01 — Two different subjects currently share the word Becoming

The existing `docs/architecture/BECOMING_IMPLEMENTATION_GATE.md` describes a deferred, member-authored continuity/gathering surface. It preserves July 2026 rulings and explicitly labels its gate proposal as noncanonical. It does not describe the prospective imaginal practice ratified in this conversation. It reserves the human concept “Becoming Thread,” not table names, and prohibits speculative implementation. Its proposed G1–G4 gates concern actual member use of carried material.

**Proposed disposition:** Name the new programme's subject “Becoming — Future Self Field,” governed by the current prospective constitution. Preserve the earlier gathering proposal unchanged and separately identify its subject in any later amendment. Do not declare its usage gates satisfied, silently retire its stops, reuse an old thread storage model, or equate the two domains. Any navigation or storage naming decision must explicitly record how they coexist or which proposal is superseded. A founder ruling on this exact distinction precedes build.

**Why this is not redundant bureaucracy:** A future-facing session can begin with no historical corpus. A gathering surface presupposes something intentionally gathered. Treating them as identical would either block the new practice for the wrong reason or bypass a real earlier stop.

## D-02 — Non-impersonation outranks the roleplay exception

The BECOMING-02 conversational draft allowed hypothetical future-voice roleplay when requested. The ratified constitution prohibits impersonation. Labeling impersonation hypothetical does not erase that conflict.

**Proposed repair:** MAIA may offer questions, neutral sentence stems, and explicitly hypothetical third-person possibilities. It does not take the future-self role or compose first-person future-self dialogue. The member supplies both voices. Imported or pasted AI-authored words remain labeled as generated material; member adoption does not erase origin. A request for “tell me what my future self says” receives a brief distinction and an invitation to the member's own imagination, not a blocked or shaming experience.

This applies the stronger existing law immediately; the original draft remains acknowledged rather than rewritten as though it never contained an exception.

## D-03 — Carry is provenance, not prefill

The BECOMING-01 draft contemplated source copies in Journal and derived language entering other facets. The current House crossing contract requires source identity/context beside a blank receiving surface. The member must complete the receiver's own authorship act.

**Proposed repair:** No automatic copy, paraphrase, title, suggested answer, extracted commitment, or prefilled decision. An individually admitted crossing resolves the owned source server-side. Keeping member-authored receiver content and writing the identity-only relation are atomic. Opening a receiver writes neither. Exact return leads to the original object, not a generic room. Existing source-content versions and receiver text remain distinct. Ordinary manual writing is not a system transfer mechanism.

Journal is the proposed first implemented crossing. The eight-facet design is not eight-facet runtime authority. The Divination→Daily Anchor hold is not lifted. Shadow or any unverified receiver must not acquire a working doorway merely because it appears in a design table.

## D-04 — Return is an obligation of the system, not a burden on the person

“Every encounter returns the member to the present” remains verbatim in the constitution. Its operational interpretation must not trap the member in a ritual or fabricate psychological completion.

**Proposed repair:** Return and exit remain available throughout. The member can stop, navigate away, or decline further prompts. Explicit return, interruption, abandonment, connection loss, and unknown completion are distinct system states. The application never records “grounded,” “regulated,” “safe,” or “returned” based only on elapsed time, logout, or a model inference. On resumption it offers present orientation without replaying intense imagery automatically.

## D-05 — Logical objects are not a migration plan

ARRIVAL, HORIZON, ENCOUNTER, DIALOGUE, DISCERNMENT, BRIDGE, and RETURN are meaningful distinctions. They do not authorize seven tables, seven screens, seven mandatory answers, or background persistence of every utterance.

**Proposed repair:** Define a versioned logical aggregate and source provenance before selecting storage. Arrival need not create a database row. Writing privacy, explicit save, optional disclosed draft recovery, audio retention, revision, export, and deletion each need an accepted contract. Reuse existing member identity and consent infrastructure only after a substrate census. Do not create a new consent system or treat Sanctuary material as reusable context.

## D-06 — Imagined content must remain imagined downstream

A member may write “I have a child in this future” or “my partner says…” within an exercise. Those are not autobiographical facts, predictions, or evidence about another person's mind.

**Proposed repair:** Preserve two axes: who authored the text, and what epistemic kind it has. A member-authored imagined event remains an imagined event. Hypothetical scenes cannot enter factual MAIA memory, relationship profiles, health records, or public/shared spaces merely because they are vivid or member-authored. Model-generated reflection remains model-generated. Crossings preserve both distinctions.

## D-07 — Continuity without automated identity authorship

The drafts proposed multi-session synthesis. The older gate and current House law constrain system-authored Recognition. Similarity is not identity and must not silently create threads or memory.

**Proposed repair:** Launch with member-selected prior entries and member-created thread membership. Rich comparison is a separately consented later capability, quoting exact selected sources and preserving dates and uncertainty. No “you are becoming…” classification, trajectory score, readiness inference, or claim of growth. Current member correction outranks a past interpretation.

## D-08 — Invitation without suggestion masquerading as discovery

Examples such as “Is anything here asking to be admired rather than lived?” can be leading or judgmental. Preserving depth does not require planting an interpretation.

**Proposed repair:** Prefer open contrast: “Which parts feel personally meaningful, and which feel expected of you?” Only follow a symbol, figure, sensation, conflict, or interpretation the member actually supplies. Do not introduce a deceased person, wiser authority, diagnosis, destiny, or feared outcome into the scene. Silence and no-image are valid experiences, not new data labels to infer and store.

## Recommended combined ruling

Accept the prospective subject as distinct from the deferred gathering proposal; retain the constitution unchanged; accept D-02 through D-08 as conformance requirements; authorize BECOMING-03 visual/experience-state design only after the identity disposition is recorded. No runtime implementation or old gate waiver follows automatically. This paragraph is a proposed ruling, **not a ruling made by JARVIS**.
