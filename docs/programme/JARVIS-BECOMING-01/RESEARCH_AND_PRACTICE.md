# Becoming — Research and practice provenance plan

**This programme act did not re-run a systematic literature search or validate the quantitative claims in the earlier conversational research overview.** Those claims are not promoted into product evidence. No clinical efficacy assertion is authorized.

## Four distinct kinds of source

**Jungian theory:** prospective function, active imagination, symbolic relationship, compensation, individuation, and the transcendent function can inform the field's questions. They do not establish that a particular imagined future is true or that this product is effective. The psychological “future self” is not simply identical to Jung's Self. Verify primary texts and exact editions before quoting.

**Empirical research:** possible selves, future-self continuity, episodic future thinking, Best Possible Self, and mental contrasting/implementation intentions are different constructs and interventions. A result for one population, exercise, or outcome cannot be transferred to the whole Becoming experience. Establish source, design, comparator, sample, follow-up, effect uncertainty, adverse effects/reporting, and applicability before making a claim. Avoid reproducing previously quoted numerical estimates until verified.

**Kelly's clinical practice:** years of facilitation can contribute sequence, language, discernment, and signs to slow down. A demonstration using invented or voluntarily supplied personal material is appropriate; importing client accounts is not a prerequisite and requires separate consent and safeguards. Preserve Kelly's actual intervention style rather than substituting a generic visualization script. Practice expertise informs design; it is not product efficacy evidence.

**Product hypotheses:** member-led imaginal reflection may support relationship across time; sparse facilitation may preserve authorship; return and optional concrete bridge may connect the experience to present life. These are testable design hypotheses, not outcomes already achieved.

## Evidence review record

For every proposed claim record: claim ID; exact wording; primary source and publication/version date; theoretical or empirical status; population and exclusions; intervention/comparator; measured outcome and follow-up; limits; permitted product wording; reviewer; and current standing. Null or unavailable results remain visible. Sources on safety and accessibility deserve the same care as positive findings.

The review should actively look for limits and contrary findings: when vivid future thought becomes distressing, when desired futures feel alienating, how nonvisual imagination is accommodated, and whether longer-term benefits transfer beyond the studied exercise. Do not diagnose a member from inability to imagine a future.

## Facilitation elicitation

At a separately accepted design act, capture Kelly's demonstration of one ordinary encounter, one no-image encounter, one feared/overwhelming image, and an ending that yields no action. Record actual prompts, pauses, transitions, and places where Kelly chooses not to interpret. Compare those observations with the draft; meaningful differences become findings, not silently absorbed changes.

## Launch learning without psychological surveillance

Proposed formative questions: Was authorship clear? Could the member stop? Was uncertainty welcome? Did a chosen source return correctly? Did the member find anything useful? Collect only explicitly consented, minimal feedback. Do not infer diagnosis, openness, hope, a developmental stage, or wellbeing from imagery, silence, retention, or frequency of visits.

The initial launch is a reflective capability, not a therapeutic efficacy study. A later human-subject research programme requires its own ethical review, consent, design, and adjudication. An internal quality check is not a substitute.
