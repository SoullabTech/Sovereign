## Question

Can MAIA hold multiple selected facets as one relational gestalt without losing the reality, provenance, contradiction, or revisability of the individual facets?

This is the technical form of the founder's crystal-center question.

## Deterministic conformance

The center contract now carries complete source identity into synthesis:

`facet · object type · object id · revision · author · epistemic kind · time relation`.

Seven adversarial crystal-center tests were added.
### C1 — provenance round-trip

A gestalt must preserve enough identity to trace every contribution back to the exact selected source.

Tested with Journal, Relationships, Becoming, and Decisions sources.

### C2 — contradiction preservation

Two facets may say apparently opposing things. The center is explicitly forbidden from manufacturing coherence merely to produce a neat synthesis.

### C3 — source ablation

When one source is removed, only that jewel disappears. The remaining sources remain present and identifiable.

This tests whether a synthesis is actually dependent on its evidence rather than a story MAIA would tell regardless of input.
### C4 — no unseen-facet leakage

If Dream, Astrology, or another facet is absent from the selected context, the center contract forbids introducing it as though it were evidence.

### C5 — permutation robustness

Changing source order preserves the same provenance set. Order is not allowed to change what sources exist in the field.

### C6 — correction supremacy

A member can reject a center-level hypothesis. The repair contract requires MAIA to release unsupported claims rather than reinterpret disagreement as confirmation.

### C7 — visible source dependence

If a connection materially depends on one facet, that dependence must remain visible. A source-dependent interpretation cannot be presented as a source-independent truth.

For each synthetic multi-facet case, compare:

1. **full field** — all selected facets;
2. **ablated field** — remove one causally important facet;
