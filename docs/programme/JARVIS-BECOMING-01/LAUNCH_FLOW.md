## Proposed first-release boundary

A first release can be complete without implementing every possible crossing. Proposed minimum: an accepted Becoming room; present orientation and easy exit; open/question/nonvisual/feared/unknown journeys; member-authored encounter and optional dialogue; explicit save/revisit/delete; member-directed discernment; optional in-place MAIA under its accepted contract; and one real Journal crossing with exact return.

Text must work independently. Voice is included only after a specific pause/listen/stop/retention witness succeeds. An unavailable voice path must not prevent saving or exiting. Existing platform voice architecture is reused, not redesigned as a side effect.

**Not launch dependencies by default:** AI future-self avatars, age progression, voice cloning, automatic longitudinal analysis, inferred identities, a full graph, memory ingestion, autonomous practices, all eight receiving facets, gamification, streaks, clinical outcomes, or public efficacy claims.

Additional edges can be admitted as separately bounded 07-Rn lanes after receiver census and founder approval. A planned receiver is not an available feature. This proposal needs founder scope acceptance; it does not silently remove capabilities from an already ratified launch requirement.

Registered packets are **blocked planning records**: no automatic dispatch, provider spend, production access, or authority to build. The existing legacy registry derives blocking from explicit `blockers`; its `dependencies` alone do not enforce ordering. Every unit therefore records both. When its gate opens, create a bounded successor pinned to the then-current exact candidate and accepted paths; do not simply clear a blocker and reuse stale or broader assumptions.

The legacy packet interface is not relabeled as a fully advanced W0.v2 lifecycle. An empty attempts list means no worker ran. “Blocked” does not mean the product has failed; it means a future act has not been admitted.

## Execution discipline

Recheck actual serving checkout, current canonical, concurrent work, permissions, and disk capacity before any build. Do not operate on the dirty shared checkout. Do not stash, clean, reset, or merge unrelated House work. The documentary branch carries new programme files only.

Each build lane names exact allowed paths, forbidden actions, source snapshot, test population, independent challenge, human stop, rollback, and handoff. One receiver and one consequential act at a time. A failed gate retains its evidence and returns to the precise repair needed.

## Release standing vocabulary

Use DOCUMENTED → CONTRACT ACCEPTED → LOCAL IMPLEMENTED → LOCAL VERIFIED → HUMAN ACCEPTED → MERGED → DEPLOY AUTHORIZED → DEPLOYED → LIVE WITNESSED. These are distinct observations and rulings, not a decorative checklist. No statement of “ready for launch” is allowed before its exact scope and outstanding obligations are named.
