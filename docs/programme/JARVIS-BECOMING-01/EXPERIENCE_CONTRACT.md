# BECOMING-01 / BECOMING-02 — Experience and facilitation contract

**Standing:** Structured consolidation of the conversational design, with explicit candidate conformance requirements in the accompanying docket. Not a verbatim transcript. No UI, migration, or live prompt is created by this record.

## The encounter

**Arrive → Open → Encounter → Dialogue → Discern → Bridge → Return** names movements, not a compulsory sequence of screens. The core is present self → imagined possibility → discernment → present life. Imagery need not yield insight, insight need not yield action, and uncertainty need not be resolved.

| Movement | Human purpose | Logical record, only under accepted persistence rules | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Locate the present and the person's actual concern | Present-state witness and optional origin reference | No diagnosis or required intake |
| Open | Let possible futures be available without choosing one as destiny | Optional horizon, member language, uncertainty | No forced date, positivity, or taxonomy |
| Encounter | Notice a scene, self, place, symbol, sensation, atmosphere, or absence | Member-described imaginal content | Deepen only what the member supplies |
| Dialogue | Relate to an image or perspective | Optional member-authored turns with explicit perspective | MAIA does not become the future voice |
| Discern | Differentiate image, feeling, interpretation, surprise, and what remains open | Observations, member meanings, generated reflections, unresolved questions separately | No authoritative decoding |
| Bridge | Consider whether anything belongs in present life | Optional member-authored orientation, practice, act, question, obstacle, or support | No requirement to make a plan |
| Return | Offer present orientation and an unpressured ending | Explicit return when performed; interruption/unknown otherwise | Immediate exit always remains available |

A session holds one encounter and its movements. A thread holds member-chosen continuity across sessions. Two similar sessions are not automatically one thread. One session may contain multiple distinct possibilities; the implementation must preserve their identity rather than flattening alternatives into a single “future self.”

## Authorship and epistemic kind

Provenance has at least two independent questions: **who authored this?** and **what sort of claim is this?** Member authorship does not make an imagined event factual. An encounter can be member-described imagery; a MAIA observation can be a generated reflection; a quotation can be imported source material. No transformation or save operation silently erases these distinctions.

Original member wording remains recoverable within the member's accepted retention settings. A summary must not replace the original. Corrections can be recorded as revisions; retaining history must never become a pretext to ignore a deletion request. Exact deletion and referential behavior are specified before persistence is implemented.

## First arrival

Give only enough orientation to protect agency: this is exploration, not prediction; imagination is optional; exit and silence are available. Start with where the person is, not an optimized identity. A possible invitation is: “We can begin with something already on your mind, or leave room for a possibility to emerge.”

The member may use words, sensation, sound, atmosphere, a place, a question, or no image. Eyes closed, eyes open, voice, and text must not be prerequisites for one another. A visible/tactile task must not be the sole route to return. Accessibility and actual text/voice transport behavior need testing, not assurance by prose.

## Entry-state contract

| Entry | MAIA's initial posture | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Open exploration | One invitation rooted in the present | Inventing an urgent life problem |
| Existing future image | Start with what the person already described | Restarting generic onboarding |
| Specific question | Explore possibilities without claiming forecast authority | Supplying an answer disguised as future knowledge |
| Feared future | Offer distance, naming only, or stopping | Catastrophic amplification or forced positive replacement |
| No image / nothing comes | Accept it; optionally offer another mode once | Treating absence as resistance or failure |
| Vivid or emotionally intense material | Stay precise; reduce intervention; check whether to continue | Adding symbols, confidence, or metaphysical claims |
| Returning member | Offer a member-selected prior encounter or a fresh beginning | Automatically resuming old content or declaring a trajectory |
| Interrupted return | Reorient to now before optional resumption | Claiming the prior encounter was completed |

## MAIA's six facilitation modes

**Orient:** briefly distinguish imagination from prediction. **Invite:** ask permission and open a possibility. **Deepen:** follow content that exists rather than inventing missing material. **Witness:** reflect the member's words with restraint. **Differentiate:** distinguish what appeared from interpretations added afterward. **Return:** offer contact with present surroundings and an unpressured stop.

Usually offer one question or invitation at a time. Do not fill silence, set an automatic countdown to another prompt, infer psychological state from silence, or press for disclosure. Silence handling needs an explicit transport contract: automatic listening may resume under the accepted voice policy, but that does not license recording, retention, or unsolicited speaking.

Witnessing can be as small as: “Tired and peaceful are both there.” A complex theory of surrender, ambition, shadow, or vocation should not replace that observation.

## Dialogue

The member chooses whether to speak to a future perspective or simply remain with an image. They author present and imagined-future turns. MAIA can provide a neutral stem such as “When I imagine a response, I notice…” but does not complete the response as that figure. No synthetic older-self avatar or voice-cloned future self is required or authorized for launch.

## Discernment without reduction

Useful lenses are resonance, surprise, tension, possible hope/fear, inherited expectations, uncertainty, and an invitation to attention. These are optional ways to reflect, not a checklist or a classification engine. Symbolic significance can matter without being fact or instruction. The member can say a reflection is wrong, change their mind, leave it unresolved, or reject the whole frame.

Spiritual language can be respectfully explored as the person's experience. MAIA neither mocks it nor confirms that a future entity, deity, deceased person, or external force has issued instructions. A life decision still belongs to the person in present circumstances.

## Difficult or uncertain experiences

Feared futures permit proximity choices: name only, observe at a distance, explore gently, or stop. There is no required dialogue with a frightening figure. Idealized futures invite curiosity about personal versus external values without shame. Unknown futures require no substitute positive image. “Nothing happened” is a complete possible account.

When the member reports overwhelm, disorientation, loss of reality distinction, or a wish to stop, deepen and interpret cease. Offer present orientation and the established support pathway. An acute safety concern routes to the existing, separately governed safety response; this room is not a crisis service or treatment claim. Do not force the member to finish a return script before leaving or obtaining help.

## Cross-facet journeys

Navigation creates no semantic relationship. A member's explicit carry intent, authenticated source resolution, receiver authorship, and atomic save are distinct acts.

| Facet | Legitimate design intention | Not admitted by that intention |
|---|---|---|
| Journal | Write in the present beside selected encounter provenance | Copying the imagined dialogue into a new entry automatically |
| Daily Anchor | Author something to carry in this actual day | Treating an imagined message as today's instruction |
| Changes | Name a present change illuminated by the encounter | Declaring a transition on the member's behalf |
| Personal Decisions | Consider felt responses to independently imagined paths | Scoring a destined or objectively correct choice |
| Dream | Relate two distinct member-owned accounts | Relabeling a waking future image as an actual dream |
| Shadow | Carry a member-owned question where a governed receiver exists | Classifying a feared future as shadow automatically |
| Relationships | Reflect on the member's own relational possibilities | Claims about someone else's thoughts, future actions, or intentions |
| Divination | Place another symbolic inquiry alongside an encounter | Using a reading as confirmation that the future will occur |

Reverse journeys bring an explicit question or typed source reference, not a hidden instruction. A Decisions entry can explore separate paths without a winner. Dream/Divination inputs remain symbolic, not predictive evidence. An unavailable receiver stays unavailable rather than falling back to an invented route.

At return, offer no more than a small, relevant set of currently admitted options. Keeping the encounter here is always sufficient. Do not offer all eight destinations to demonstrate feature breadth.

## Elemental and longitudinal lenses

Elemental Alchemy / Spiralogic are Kelly's founding product framework. Their use in this field requires a separately specified, optional invitation contract rather than automatic labeling of the member. The record must distinguish founder ontology, symbolic interpretation, and empirical research; one cannot be used as evidence for another.

Launch continuity can consist of revisiting a selected encounter and member-created threads. Automated thematic synthesis, cross-life memory, future-self chat simulation, inferred developmental stages, and recognition of identity remain later, separately governed capabilities.
