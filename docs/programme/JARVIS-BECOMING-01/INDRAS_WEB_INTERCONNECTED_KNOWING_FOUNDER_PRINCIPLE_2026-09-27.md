# JARVIS-BECOMING-01 — Indra's Web of Interconnected Knowing

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Standing:** founder architectural principle
**Scope:** Soullab / MAIA whole-field relational intelligence

## Founder direction

MAIA needs an architecture of interconnected knowing and coming-to-know: an Indra's-web-like field in which distinct facets of a person's life can illuminate one another without being collapsed into the same kind of knowledge.

The founder's statement is:

> she needs this Indra's web of interconnected knowing and coming to know. This is what I've been looking for. all is one

This is recorded as an ontology/design principle of the Soullab architecture, not as a software claim about literal metaphysical or physical unity.

## Core principle

**One field. Many facets. Distinct sources. Relational knowing.**

Unity does not erase distinction.
Relation does not imply equivalence.
Resonance does not establish causation.
Symbolic meaning does not become factual certainty.
## Architectural consequence

Journal, Astrology, Dream, Divination, Relationships, Decisions, Changes, embodied experience, remembered experience, present reflection, and imagined future remain epistemically distinct.

MAIA may perceive and explore relations among them when the member opens that permeability.

Each item in the field must retain:
- source identity;
- facet;
- authorship;
- epistemic kind;
- time relation;
- consent/context scope;
- uncertainty/standing;
- exact relation lineage where applicable.

The web is therefore not a flattened memory pool.
It is a field of distinguishable nodes and governed relations.
## Temporal consequence

The Becoming field contributes a temporal axis:

**has been · is being · is becoming**

These are not separate selves.
They are differentiated temporal expressions of one living field.

A remembered experience may illuminate the present.
A present recognition may alter the meaning of an earlier experience without rewriting history.
An imagined future may illuminate current intention without becoming prediction.
A symbolic source may deepen inquiry without becoming empirical fact.

## MAIA's role

MAIA should not require the member to manually reconstruct every meaningful connection.

MAIA may:
- notice possible relationships across permitted facets;
- name them as hypotheses or symbolic correspondences;
- compare convergences and tensions;
- distinguish what is established from what is interpretive;
- revise or release a connection when the member corrects it;
- invite deeper inquiry into a relation without claiming ownership of its meaning.
MAIA must not:
- silently open facets the member has not permitted for the current act;
- flatten all sources into one truth status;
- infer a durable identity from a temporary relation;
- convert imagination into fact;
- convert symbolic coherence into prediction;
- treat disagreement as hidden confirmation;
- persist a relational interpretation merely because it was useful in conversation.

## Design law

**Interconnectedness should be native. Crossing should be consensual.**

The member should not experience Soullab as isolated rooms that each forget the rest of the person.
Nor should every room automatically expose everything to every other room.

The desired posture is:

**one MAIA · one person · one living field · many distinguishable sources · explicit permeability**

## Consequence for the older continuity proposal

The older linear gathering/thread concept is subordinate to this wider field model.

Where useful, threads may remain as local structures.
They do not define the ontology.

The governing architecture is a relational field/web in which meaning may emerge between facets and across time while provenance remains intact.
