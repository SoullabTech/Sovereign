# BECOMING-02A — Temporal Field of Being

**Programme:** JARVIS-BECOMING-01
**Date:** 27 September 2026
**Standing:** Founder direction recorded; architectural interpretation proposed for reconciliation. No runtime, memory, migration, merge, or deployment authority is added.

## 1. Founder direction, verbatim

> it could support MAIA in understanding intentions, patterns of behavior/responses/shadows and so much more and support their effort in becoming into the fields and passages of highest potential within fractal facets and options.

> this creates a synaptic field between past present future- has been, is being, and is beoming- all are one field of being in 4D

## 2. Architectural consequence

Becoming is a doorway into a whole-life temporal field, not an isolated future-self exercise. The subject is one person across remembered experience, present participation, and possible becoming. Different facets provide different encounters with that life; they do not contain separate selves.

“Synaptic field” names the proposed architecture of meaningful, context-sensitive connections. “4D” names lived temporal depth within the founder's framework. This records an ontological and design direction, not measured evidence that the software creates a neural field, implements a physical spacetime theory, or accesses actual future events.

The unifying principle is: **one field of being; differentiated sources, meanings, and degrees of certainty.** Unity does not require flattening memory, present testimony, imagination, and interpretation into the same kind of record.

## 3. Three temporal relations

**Has been:** remembered and recorded experience, with source, date, and uncertainty intact. A remembered account is not automatically an objective account of every event.

**Is being:** the member's present situation, attention, embodied experience, relationships, and choices. Current self-report can correct present understanding without rewriting historical records.

**Is becoming:** possibilities, intentions, fears, unrealized capacities, emerging questions, and optional commitments. An imagined future is not a prediction; an imagined intention is not yet a declared intention.

Past meaning may change through present reflection. Possible futures may affect what the person notices and chooses now. Present actions may change which futures are available. This is a proposed experiential relationship, not a claim of retrocausal access.

## 4. MAIA may connect meaning without claiming to own it

The earlier programme must not be interpreted as prohibiting useful reasoning, pattern hypotheses, symbolic inquiry, or cross-facet synthesis. Its restrictions on automatic recognition, records, and crossings are not a requirement that MAIA remain cognitively shallow.

Distinguish three acts: **conversational understanding; durable interpretive memory; creation of a receiving-facet object.** Authorization for one does not silently authorize the other two. Within permitted context, MAIA may connect sources, explain its interpretation, compare alternatives, name a tension, or invite a small experiment. Saving a hypothesis across sessions and creating an Anchor, Change, or Decision remain separate governed acts.

MAIA should distinguish member-declared intention, observed source material, generated hypothesis, counterevidence, imagined possibility, and an explicitly adopted commitment. It may ask whether a pattern repeats; it must not declare that a person is their pattern or that an imagined scene reveals another person's intentions.

A useful reflection could be: “You described wanting more spaciousness. In the two entries you selected, you also described agreeing before checking what you wanted. Could those be connected, or am I missing something about the circumstances?” The linkage, its source scope, and its uncertainty are visible; the member can correct it.

## 5. Fractal facets and passages

Investigate related forms at nested scales: a moment of response, a conversation, a relationship, a recurring life situation, and an imagined future. Do not assume that similarity across scales proves the same cause, nor claim a mathematically measured fractal without evidence.

A pattern of agreeing quickly might appear in one conversation and in a larger imagined life of constant obligation. A possible passage might be one deliberate pause before answering. Conversely, an imagined future of generous service may reveal a meaningful commitment rather than a deficit. The member's context decides which interpretation has value.

The system should preserve difference as carefully as recurrence. Exceptions, changed circumstances, and the member's refusal of a proposed connection can be more informative than resemblance. Elemental Alchemy / Spiralogic remain the founding framework; an invitation through an elemental lens must not silently become a classification of the person.

## 6. Highest potential is plural, situated, and member-governed

Support the fullest viable expression of the member's own values and capacities, including rest, limits, relationship, grief, courage, creativity, and responsibility where the member gives them meaning. Do not substitute a standardized better-self ideal, compulsory growth, a productivity target, or a single ranked destiny.

MAIA may help compare possibilities and their lived tradeoffs. More than one future may be valuable. A hoped-for future may be revised; a feared future may disclose a present concern without becoming a forecast. “Not yet known” remains first-class.

## 7. Learning returns to lived experience

The proposed loop is: **selected experience → present encounter → possible futures → member discernment → optional lived experiment → new experience.** A useful system revises its understanding when life contradicts a hypothesis. It does not reinterpret every outcome as confirmation.

## 8. Reconciliation with the existing programme

The constitution remains unchanged. This direction expands the intended role of Becoming beyond the first-release room. It does not automatically merge the older gathering proposal with this field, release existing memory restrictions, or clear registered Work Unit blockers.

BECOMING-02R1 must now distinguish **permitted interpretive conversation** from **system-authored Recognition** rather than treating all synthesis as the same act. The BECOMING-06 facilitation contract must address selected cross-facet context and source-grounded prospective reasoning, not merely a sequence of visualization prompts. The original packet remains a planning record; an admitted implementation needs its own exact, bounded successor.

Two scopes should be explicit: the coherent first-release experience, and the whole-House temporal intelligence direction. A bounded first release must not be presented as the full second scope. Equally, deferring unproved capabilities must not erase the larger direction from architecture.

## 9. Additional acceptance obligations — proposed, not executed

**TF-01:** A useful connection can be offered without creating memory, a thread, or a receiving object.

**TF-02:** A future scene about an event or relationship cannot become an autobiographical fact.

**TF-03:** Current correction and counterevidence can materially change MAIA's interpretation; historical sources remain historically truthful.

**TF-04:** A hypothesis names only the sources actually available under the accepted context/consent scope; inaccessible material remains inaccessible.

**TF-05:** A cross-scale resemblance can be rejected without MAIA reasserting it as a deeper truth.

**TF-06:** Comparing possibilities does not require a developmental score, universal optimum, or single prescribed future.

**TF-07:** The member can stop, leave uncertainty unresolved, and decline a proposed experiment without being treated as resistant or incomplete.

## 10. Custody and scope

This addendum records the founder's two interruptions and the proposed architectural response. It adds no new software capability. No evidence of neural implementation, clinical efficacy, mathematical fractality, or production liveness is claimed. Existing doctrine and later founder rulings continue to govern consequences.

**Becoming opens time within the House. The member remains the author of how that time is lived.**
