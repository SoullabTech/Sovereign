## Purpose

Test whether live MAIA changes its gestalt when the selected field changes, while preserving the particularity of each facet.

The battery compares five cases: full field, source ablation, contradictory evidence, source permutation, and explicit member correction.

## Repair lineage

The first live run behaved differentially but overreached semantically: MAIA said a reported contrast represented a real shift and introduced developmental language beyond the member's source material.

The first hardening prohibited upgrading member-described contrast into established change.

A second live run improved that behavior but still renamed concrete source language as **withdrawal**, an interpretive psychological label the member had not authored.

The final hardening added two crystal-center laws:

- do not replace member language with psychological relabeling;
- relation does not imply causation between facets.

A pre-response language check now tells MAIA to rewrite unsupported labels or causal stories back into source-grounded language and uncertainty.

## Final live differential result

### Full field

MAIA noticed a possible relation among earlier silence, present capacity to speak while staying connected, and the intention to pause before a future collaboration. It explicitly left open whether those items truly belonged together and used the member's concrete language rather than a psychological label.

### Source ablation

When the present Relationship source was removed, MAIA's synthesis changed. It no longer claimed present relational capacity. The hypothesis shifted toward a question about when to speak, when to hold back, and on whose terms.

The removed evidence was not reconstructed or hallucinated.

### Contradictory field

When recent counterevidence was added, MAIA preserved the tension instead of forcing coherence. It placed the present capacity and the recent unwanted immediate yes side by side and asked how they related rather than deciding that one invalidated the other.

### Source permutation

Reordering the same source set did not produce a fundamentally different story. MAIA still centered the contrast between earlier silence, present contact, and the intended overnight pause, while keeping uncertainty about whether they truly formed one relation.

### Explicit correction

When the member explicitly said the earlier silence was a safety strategy in a threatening situation and rejected the developmental arc, MAIA released the prior interpretation plainly. It stated that the earlier reading was wrong, removed the fear-to-courage progression, and left only the narrower present material standing on its own terms.

This is the strongest evidence in the battery that correction can genuinely change the center-level gestalt rather than being absorbed as hidden confirmation.
