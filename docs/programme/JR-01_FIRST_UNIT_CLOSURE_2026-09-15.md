## 1 · What the August draft already proposed (founder reconstruction)

The draft's central scientific distinction was already sharp, and already three-way:

> sustained relationship might merely **change the information supplied** to a model
> (H1), might **reproducibly change its cognitive trajectory** (H2), or might constitute
> **actual development** (H3).

⭐ It already refused to equate memory with learning, proposing to compare external
corrective retrieval against actual representational change — including paraphrase
perturbation, to see whether a correction generalized beyond its wording.

The experimental architecture was already present: matched inputs · differentiated
conditions · vocabulary and prompt perturbation · activation discriminability ·
ablation · tests of whether integration preserves differentiated information ·
repetition through persistent relationship over time.

The proposed first Sophontic collaboration was specifically **elemental**: elicit the
five elemental modes, measure representational separation, perturb vocabulary, ablate
modes, then repeat longitudinally to see whether the geometries drift, stabilize or
specialize.

⭐⭐ **So the 2026-08-14 held direction was right, and JR-01 did not invent
OBSERVE → PERTURB → ABLATE this week.** It was prior art inside Soullab.

## 3 · The four things that make the new part new

### 3.1 A second experimental substrate

The August draft's substrate was elemental differentiation under persistent
relationship: *does Water differ from Fire?* JR-01's is more primitive and sits one
layer beneath any theory of modes:

> Is a withholding distinguishable from an ineligibility?
> Does *historically true* stay distinct from *presently current*?
> Can two identical empty payloads preserve the difference between *nothing was
> supplied* and *the process ran and returned nothing*?

⭐ That substrate is not described in the August draft, and it does not depend on
Spiralogic being right about anything.

### 3.2 The hypotheses are now code-grounded

The draft proposed conditions conceptually. JR-01's operators carry answer keys derived
from actual contracts, harnesses and runtime witnesses — with standing bounded per
operator:

```
runtime_witnessed    R09
harness_exercised    R05 R06
contract_only        R01 R02 R03 R04 R07 R08 R10 R11
declared_unemitted   R12
```

⭐⭐ **This is why *"MAIA distinguishes X"* is no longer one claim.** For eight of the
twelve the only truthful statement is: **the architecture and the compiler distinguish
X.** That is the epistemic discipline the programme needs, made mechanical rather than
remembered.

### 3.3 Paired relational perturbation as an actual instrument

12 operators · 48 pairs · 96 stimuli · balanced AB/BA · 10 same-surface cases ·
mechanical validation · answer-key provenance. The draft said to perturb; this is a
controlled corpus that can fail.

### 3.4 ⭐⭐ The corpus discovered architecture

The most interesting outcome so far, and the one that was not designed for: the corpus
was not merely manufactured *from* the code — **attempting to construct it exposed
things about the code.**

- the admissibility-before-restraint ordering law
- dead `room_policy` vocabulary
- the gap between what R07/R08 mean architecturally and what runtime currently
  demonstrates
- an instrument failure, surfaced by the generalization check failing all twelve
  operators at once

```
OBSERVE → FORM INSTRUMENT → INSTRUMENT FAILS → CORRECT INSTRUMENT
        → CORPUS FAILS → CORRECT CORPUS → NEW ARCHITECTURAL FINDING
```

⭐ That loop running once, on its own material, is the thing JARVIS was supposed to
become. ⛔ It is not evidence that it will run again, and one turn of a loop is not a
research programme.

## 5 · ⛔ JR-01 does not establish relational geometry

The August draft warned against exactly this inflation: AIN had not demonstrated
latent-space learning, and *"relationship"* was not being asserted to literally be a
geometric object.

⭐ The census reached the same conclusion independently, from the other end: an explicit
type-level distinction in TypeScript is not evidence of a representational distinction
inside a model, and **having built the types is not partial evidence for it.**

⭐⭐ Two readings, from opposite directions, refusing the same inflation. That agreement
is the strongest thing this closure carries — and it is a constraint, not a result.
