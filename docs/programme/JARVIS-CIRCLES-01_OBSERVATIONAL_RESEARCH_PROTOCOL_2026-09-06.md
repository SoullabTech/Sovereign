# Observational Research Protocol — first living Circles

**Status:** PROPOSAL. Not authorized. No cohort may run before the Constitution is ratified and the
boundary verifier passes.
**Premise (founder):** *The remaining serious Circle research is Soullab's own primary research with
living human fields.*

> **What happens when 6, 10, 14, or 18 actual Soullab members inhabit one of these fields for
> several weeks?** Not to find a maximum membership count — to observe whether **recognizability,
> reciprocity, differentiation, repair, field legibility, voluntary silence and sovereignty
> survive.**

---

## 1. What this protocol must not become

- ⛔ Not an engagement study. Volume, frequency, retention and session counts are **not outcomes**
  and are not collected as such.
- ⛔ Not a size-threshold hunt. **Ratify no number** (R11). Size is a covariate, never the object.
- ⛔ Not a scoring instrument. The eight relational capacities are **research dimensions, never
  member scores**, never rendered to members, never aggregated into an index.
- ⛔ Not covert. **Members know they are in an observation cohort and consent to it specifically** —
  observing a sovereignty product without consent would falsify the product.

## 2. Cohort ladder

Per the corrected ladder (Amendments A+B): enlarge **Circle count**, not Circle size.

| Wave | Shape | Question it is designed to answer |
|---|---|---|
| **W0** | 1 Circle, **3–5** members | Does the field cohere at all? Is the threshold legible? *(Also: the smallest formation above the dyad — bears on A-04.)* |
| **W1** | 1 Circle, **6–8** | The literature's most-supported band. Does an inquiry involve everyone? |
| **W2** | 2 Circles, **10–14** each | Recognizability and facilitator load. **Two Circles, not one larger one** — the law under test. |
| **W3** | 3–4 Circles, **14–18** each | Where do capacities begin to strain? Does differentiation appear? |
| **W4** | a **Commons** of the above, one **Constellation** inquiry | Does nesting preserve what the Circles had? |

Each wave is a **stop**. No wave proceeds without a founder read of the prior one.

## 3. Evidence classes

Borrowed from the project's existing field-study discipline. Every observation carries its class;
classes never merge.

| Class | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **E1 · Instrumented** | server-side facts | a member left; a share was revoked; an inquiry closed |
| **E2 · Member-reported** | asked, in the member's own words | *"I didn't want to answer that one"* |
| **E3 · Facilitator-observed** | named, attributed, dated | *"Two people stopped speaking to each other in week 3"* |
| **E4 · Soullab interpretation** | ours, marked as ours | *"That reads as differentiation, not withdrawal"* |

⛔ **E4 never presented as E1/E2/E3.** ⛔ **No E1 metric is used as a proxy for a relational
capacity** — silence is not disengagement, and the protocol must never let a row stand in for a
person's experience.

## 5. MAIA observation — staged, and never both at once

MAIA does not enter a cohort until CIRCLE-09, and then in stages:

```text
STAGE 0   MAIA absent                    the baseline. Do Circles work without her?
STAGE 1   Field Witness, read-only       reflection visible to the facilitator only
STAGE 2   Field Witness, in-field        members see the reflection
STAGE 3   Common-Ground Mediator          invoked only, never default
```

- Every stage transition is a **founder act.**
- ⛔ **Stage 3 never runs concurrently with Stage 2 in the same Circle** — the roles must stay
  separable in the evidence, or drift becomes unmeasurable.
- **Pre-registered falsifier:** if members defer to MAIA's reflection more than to each other,
  Field Witness **fails** and does not proceed. *(This is the AI-mediation caution from R7 made
  testable: minority underrepresentation, order bias, and the psychological-safety reduction found
  in the preprints are the specific effects to look for.)*

## 6. Pre-registration — the protocol's own honesty gate

Before **each wave**, record in advance: what would count as the Circle working · what would count
as it failing · what would make us stop. **Predeclared, per the project's Claim State Authority.**

⛔ **A result that was not predeclared may inform the next wave. It may not be reported as a
finding of this one.** *A gain on the practiced task is never, by itself, evidence of a gain in
life* — the programme's own brain-training rule, applied to ourselves.

## 7. Exit and repair rights — non-negotiable

- Any member may leave any cohort at any time, without explanation, without it being recorded as
  a finding about them.
- Leaving revokes their shares (already implemented).
- **Withdrawing from the research is not withdrawing from the Circle**, and must not be.
- If a rupture occurs that the Circle cannot hold, **the cohort stops.** Members come before data.

## 8. What would falsify the programme's premise

Recorded now, in advance, so it cannot be rationalized later:

1. Members experience a bounded Circle as **constraint rather than safety**.
2. Contribute-before-see in inquiries reads as **compulsion**, not protection.
3. Facilitator load makes Circles **unaffordable to run** at any size worth having.
4. MAIA as Field Witness **reduces** member authority. *(Pre-registered above.)*
5. Members want the thing the comparison set already sells — a feed, discovery, connections — and
   experience the sovereign field as friction with no compensating value.

**Any of these is a real finding and must be reported as one.**
