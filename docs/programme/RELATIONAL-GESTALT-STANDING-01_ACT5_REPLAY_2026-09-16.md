## Finding

Structural standing **changed the conversational move in the desired continuity direction** in this small replay, and did not suppress forward synthesis. It did **not** reliably constrain inference from becoming assertion.

Therefore ACT 5 supports neither `standing solves free synthesis` nor `standing is useless`. It establishes the narrower result: **standing-aware perception and inference admission are separate problems.**
