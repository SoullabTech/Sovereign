## 1. Why T2 exists

T0 defines what teaching must mean.

T1 defines the grammar of a teaching move.

T2 answers the next prerequisite question:

> **Where is MAIA teaching, what kind of source supports the claim, and what epistemic standing may that source carry?**

Without T2, a live teacher could collapse Soullab canon, active research, Library primers, scholarly evidence, public web material, spiritual/philosophical traditions, and MAIA synthesis into one undifferentiated voice.

T2 makes that collapse structurally impossible.

```text

```


## 4. Knowledge source classes

Every source participating in teaching must enter under one explicit class:

### SOULLAB_CANON

Ratified constitutions, canons, governing architecture, and other Soullab material that is canonical **within Soullab**.

Required framing:

> "Soullab canon defines / holds / governs..."

It does not become scientific fact merely because it is canonical internally.

### SOULLAB_RESEARCH

Active or ratified Soullab research.

Research stage is explicit:

```text
PRELIMINARY_DIRECTION
HYPOTHESIS
EXPERIMENTAL_EVIDENCE
RATIFIED_FINDING
```

This is particularly important for Relational Geometry.
The current preliminary Relational Geometry note remains:

```text
SOULLAB_RESEARCH
PRELIMINARY_DIRECTION
ACTIVE_RESEARCH
```

It may be taught as a research direction and conceptual framework.

It may not be narrated as an established scientific finding.

### GOVERNED_LIBRARY

A governed Library item or primer with explicit revision + provenance identity.

Storage in the Library never silently upgrades a source to canon.

A primer remains a governed reference unless another authority establishes more.

### EXTERNAL_SCHOLARLY

Peer-reviewed work, preprints, scholarly books, or institutional reviews.

A scholarly source must carry a citation locator.

It may participate as `SCHOLARLY_EVIDENCE`.

It does not create clinical action authority by itself.

### EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_WEB

Fresh public internet material.

It must carry:

- named source;
- locator/URL;
- retrieval time.
It is labeled `PUBLIC_INFORMATION`, not scholarship.

This class is how future internet access may enter teaching without being mistaken for academic evidence.

### HISTORICAL_TRADITION

Psychological, philosophical, spiritual, contemplative, symbolic, religious, or historical traditions.

Examples include material attributed to Jung, Buber, Taoism, Buddhism, alchemy, archetypal traditions, phenomenology, contemplative traditions, and related lineages.

The system must name the tradition/source.

Traditional standing does not silently become empirical standing.

### MAIA_SYNTHESIS

A cross-source synthesis generated by MAIA.

It must identify at least two basis sources.

It is always:

```text
HEURISTIC_SYNTHESIS
```

It may be useful, creative, illuminating, and testable.

It may not be attributed to any one source.

## 5. Learner material is not knowledge authority

T2 creates a separate context-material axis:
```text
NONE
MEMBER_WORK
COACHING_SCENARIO
PRACTITIONER_CASE_CONTEXT
```

Every one of these enters as:

```text
authority = CONTEXT_ONLY
knowledgeAuthority = false
```

This is load-bearing.

### Writer's Studio

A manuscript can be the object of teaching:

- sentence craft;
- structure;
- pacing;
- voice;
- argument;
- scene;
- character;
- revision;
- editorial reasoning.

But the manuscript does not establish general writing theory merely because MAIA is looking at it.

### Coaching

A coaching scenario can be used to explain or practice a model.

The scenario does not prove the model.

### Practitioner / supervision

A case description can occasion educational comparison of frameworks.

It does not become evidence that a diagnosis, formulation, or treatment claim is true.
T2 grants **no clinical action authority**.

## 6. Claim standings

T2 outputs one explicit claim standing:

```text
CANONICAL_WITHIN_SOULLAB
ACTIVE_RESEARCH
RATIFIED_RESEARCH
GOVERNED_REFERENCE
SCHOLARLY_EVIDENCE
PUBLIC_INFORMATION
TRADITIONAL_ATTRIBUTION
HEURISTIC_SYNTHESIS
```

These standings are intentionally heterogeneous.

They tell the later teaching composer how the claim must be framed.

They are not ranked as a universal ladder.

A spiritual tradition may be exactly the right source for a history-of-ideas question while having no empirical standing.

A peer-reviewed paper may be strong empirical evidence while having no authority to define Soullab's constitution.

## 7. Internet and academic access boundary

T2 does **not** fetch the internet.

It does, however, create the epistemic landing zones needed for future acquisition:

```text
academic/scientific search → EXTERNAL_SCHOLARLY
fresh institutional/news/web research → EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_WEB
historical/philosophical/spiritual source → HISTORICAL_TRADITION
cross-source reasoning → MAIA_SYNTHESIS
```
This means the next research-acquisition gate can give MAIA broad internet reach without giving the internet undifferentiated authority.

Examples:

- explain contracting, inquiry, accountability, values work, or change models;
- use a fictional or member-provided scenario for practice without turning it into diagnosis.
### Practitioner / supervision

Primary teaching tasks may include:

```text
COMPARE_MODELS
EXPLAIN_CONCEPT
TEACH_METHOD
EXPLAIN_RESEARCH
REFLECT_PRACTICE
```

Examples:

- compare Jungian, psychodynamic, IFS, attachment, existential, somatic, systemic, humanistic, ACT/CBT-family, contemplative, or consciousness-oriented frameworks;
- explain philosophical assumptions beneath models;
- distinguish evidence base, tradition, clinical convention, and Soullab synthesis;
- explain consciousness research to a practitioner;
- use case material as context without granting T2 diagnostic or treatment authority.

## 9. Relational Geometry standing

T2 explicitly protects the research status of Relational Geometry.

The current preliminary note may be taught as:

- a Soullab research direction;
- a conceptual proposal that relations may carry information not reducible to objects;
- a bridge among conversational antecedent resolution, typed relations, symbolic relational grammars, Spiralogic configurations, and representational geometry;
- a programme of testable questions.

It may **not** be taught as proof that astrology explains neural cognition or as an established computational mechanism.

The note's own law remains:

> **Metaphor may suggest the experiment. Measurement decides whether anything survives.**
## 10. Falsification suite

T2 is RED if any of the following can occur:

1. Writer's Studio can claim `PRACTITIONER` authority.
2. A manuscript becomes a knowledge source merely because it is present.
3. A practitioner case description becomes clinical evidence.
4. Soullab canon is labeled scientific evidence by substitution.
5. preliminary Relational Geometry research is labeled an established finding.
6. a governed Library primer silently becomes canon.
7. external scholarly evidence lacks an attributable citation.
8. public web material lacks freshness metadata.
9. spiritual/philosophical tradition is silently labeled empirical evidence.
10. MAIA synthesis does not name multiple basis sources.
11. a practitioner teaching envelope acquires clinical-action authority.
12. any T2 output can execute teaching or persist learner state.
13. T2 calls a model, browser, retrieval service, DB, memory, or prompt system.
