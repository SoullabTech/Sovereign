## 1 · ⭐⭐ `INF-7` — the axes may be RELATED, never COLLAPSED

`SYN-2 DERIVED` may derive a relationship **between** participation and authority. ⛔ It may never
**merge** them into a single judgement.

```text
✅ LAWFUL

SYN-1   capability decides member-facing output
SYN-1   no governing source located
SYN-2   decision power exists without located authority


⛔ FORBIDDEN — SYN-2 MAY NOT BECOME

        capability is unauthorized
```

⭐ The lawful `SYN-2` is a **relation between two observations**. The forbidden one is a **verdict
about legitimacy**, and it is outside this census entirely. Where security is implicated it belongs
to **`AUTH-EXPOSURE-01`**.

⛔ This holds at every altitude of synthesis — a graph edge, a caption, a heading, a summary line,
and the founder packet. **A verdict does not become admissible by being phrased as a finding.**

## 2 · ⭐ `NONE LOCATED` and `UNKNOWN` are DIFFERENT, and the difference is load-bearing

```text
NONE LOCATED    the census LOOKED and did not find authority in the permitted evidence
                ⛔ it does NOT mean authority does not exist

UNKNOWN         even that search conclusion cannot be supported
                — the permitted evidence does not establish that a search was conclusive
```

⛔ The two must not be merged in any count, graph, legend or summary. ⛔ `NONE LOCATED` may not be
rendered as absence, and ⛔ `UNKNOWN` may not be upgraded to `NONE LOCATED` to make a column tidier.

*A search that concluded and a search that could not conclude are different epistemic facts, and
the register carries both because the organism produced both.*
