## ⭐⭐⭐ C-00 — THE S-02 ERROR AND ITS MECHANISM (the finding is the mechanism, not the typo)

**The error.** PASS 2 attributed the verbatim clause *"cannot be detected when only examining single brains"* to
**Redcay & Schilbach, Nat. Rev. Neurosci. 2019**. ⛔ **It is not theirs.** Founder located it verbatim in
**Liu & Pelowski, Front. Hum. Neurosci. 2014** (`PMC4012218`), referring to neural mechanisms of information
exchange between interacting brains.

**The mechanism, which is the part that generalizes.** The clause reached PASS 2 through a **search-result
summary**, not through a source. Search summaries **aggregate across several papers and render the aggregate in
quotation-shaped prose**; a clause can then attach to whichever citation sits nearest it in the summary.
⛔ **This is not carelessness that discipline alone fixes — it is a structural property of censusing at search
level.**

⭐ **V-00 is therefore upgraded from an undischarged obligation to a DEMONSTRATED HARM.** PASS 2 argued that an
unread source is a citation, not evidence; PASS 3 is the instance where that produced a false attribution in a
committed record within one pass. **Any RC card whose wording is quotation-shaped and whose source was not opened
must be treated as provisionally misattributed until checked.** ⚠️ PASS 1 and PASS 2 contain other
quotation-shaped material sourced the same way. **It is all suspect on the same grounds**, and re-checking it is
added to the owed list.

### S-02 — RESTATED (supersedes PASS 2 §S-02)

S-02  RESULT SURVIVES · ATTRIBUTION CORRECTED

Liu & Pelowski 2014 (PMC4012218)

## ⭐⭐ C-01 — A-01 NARROWED (supersedes PASS 2 §A-01)

**The overclaim.** PASS 2 wrote: *"The experience that most strongly suggests unitive or relational consciousness
has its best explanation entirely inside one brain."* ⛔ **Too strong.** The cited work establishes **within-brain
mechanisms and correlates sufficient to produce profound alterations of self-boundary**. It does **not** establish
the **exclusion** claim that the phenomenon's *best complete* explanation is entirely intrabrain. Contemporary work
treats contextual and relational variables as potentially relevant to psychedelic experience, though effects on
ego dissolution specifically are **inconsistent** (`PMID 39948726`, founder-supplied).

### A-01 — RESTATED
```text
A-01 — ALTERED-STATES COUNTERPRESSURE

Experiences phenomenologically described as unity,
self-transcendence or dissolved self/world boundaries
can be induced by manipulating processes within a single organism.

5-HT2A agonism and associated changes in large-scale brain
connectivity provide substantial mechanistic accounts of ego
dissolution without requiring a between-agent consciousness.

This does NOT establish that relational context is irrelevant,
nor that all unitive experience is exhaustively intrabrain.

It does establish that phenomenology of unity is not evidence,
by itself, for relational ontology.
```
⭐ **Founder's note recorded as method, not courtesy: *"a stronger falsifier because it claims less and proves
more."*** The narrowed form survives contact with the contextual-variables literature; the PASS 2 form did not.
Supporting mechanism citation: `PMID 36786290` (founder-supplied).

⭐ **The ladder climbs to one rung below the lane's question and stops there.** Every neighbour that could have

⛔ **That convergent stopping is itself the observation.** It is not yet an argument that the step cannot be taken.

## ⭐⭐⭐ R-01 — MANDATORY RC-02 REGISTER ENTRY: THE "RELATIONAL" TERM COLLISION
Tsuchiya & Saigo's proposal (*Neurosci. Conscious.* 2021, `niab034`) characterizes a conscious experience through
its relations **to other conscious experiences**, via category theory / Yoneda. ⛔ **It is not a claim about
consciousness arising in the relation between agents.** Generalized:

```text
RELATIONAL — TERM COLLISION

A  phenomenological relation      experience ↔ experience
B  interpersonal relation         agent ↔ agent            ← Soullab's usage (§13.3 RELATIONAL)
C  ecological / enactive relation  organism ↔ world
D  metaphysical relation           event/entity ↔ event/entity
E  social-constitutive relation    interaction ↔ cognition / self / meaning

⛔ No source may migrate between A–E merely because it uses the word "relational".
```
⭐ **Founder assessment recorded: this register "may turn out to be one of the most important products of the
whole research lane."** It is the RC-FR-03 discipline applied one level up — from *what a finding claims* to
*what a word denotes across literatures.*
