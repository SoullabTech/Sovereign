## FR-01 · WORD WEB — LEXICAL, NOT SEMANTIC

Word Web is a **mechanical writing instrument**. It resolves the census's one
genuinely unspecified destination.

It **may** visualize what is actually present in the writer's text:

- recurring words and phrases
- frequency
- proximity / co-occurrence
- where a term appears
- relationships created by **literal textual usage**

It **may not** infer themes, archetypes, meanings, psychological significance,
or "what the book is really about."

```text
WORD WEB
BINDS: mechanical instrument

text → observable lexical relationships → writer

NOT
text → inferred meaning → writer
```

**Packet C, confirmed.** If a semantic constellation is later wanted, that is a
**different MAIA-derived capability and needs its own authority ruling** — it
may not arrive as an enhancement to this one.

## FR-02 · NOTES / KEEPS / MATERIAL-KIND `note` — THREE JOBS, NOT ONE OBJECT

They overlap only because their names are loose. Their functions are distinct.

```text
KEEP       preserved writing state
NOTE       mutable thinking beside the writing
MATERIAL
  kind=note
           source/reference material brought into the Work
```

**Keep** — a preserved state of the writing. Answers *"I want to preserve this
version/moment of the manuscript."* It belongs to **revision history** and
**must not become the general-purpose notes system.**

**Note** — the writer's **mutable thinking beside the Work**. Answers *"I want
to remember or work with this thought without putting it into the manuscript."*
May belong to a Work and, where useful, a section. A writer-authored working
object, **outside manuscript prose**.

**Material `kind=note`** — a note **brought in as source material**. Belongs in
Materials alongside everything else the writer brings to the Work. Answers
*"This is something I am bringing into the room as material."*

> **Do not collapse them merely because all three may contain text.**

**Notes therefore has a legitimate reason to exist.** The HOLD from the
withdrawn rulings is discharged: the distinguishing inquiry is answered.

## FR-03 · THE MAIA BAND — THREE RELATIONSHIPS WITH POSSIBILITY

Discover, Insights and Suggestions **all survive, and none duplicates another.**

```text
DISCOVER      What might be worth noticing?
INSIGHTS      What reading has emerged?
SUGGESTIONS   What might you choose to do?
```

**Discover** — what becomes newly visible when MAIA looks **across** the Work.
Not a verdict; not an insight with standing. For connections, echoes,
repetitions, tensions, unanswered questions, surprising relationships — things
the writer may not have noticed.
Stance: ***"Something may be here worth looking at."***

**Insights** — already ratified. The **more formed** MAIA-derived observation,
under the standing / provenance discipline already established (WS2-07:
`developmental_readings`, `keep | dismiss | unresolved`, frozen evidence).
Stance: ***"Here is a reading that has emerged."***
Still not truth. Still not authorial authority.

**Suggestions** — **possible next acts** offered to the writer. Not analysis of
what the Work *is*, but optional possibilities for what the writer **might do**:
revisit a section · compare two passages · develop a thread · clarify something
· consider moving or expanding something.
Stance: ***"You could…"*** — never ***"You should…"***

> **Nothing acquires standing merely because MAIA suggested it.**
