## Governing question

> Can Structural Standing operate as an invisible control plane beneath MAIA's existing free relational synthesis, constraining authority and stale lineage without templating the response voice?

## Founder evidence inherited from S5

In ten sealed blind comparisons, the existing relational response was preferred 10/10 over the member-facing Standing Envelope. The recurring preference was for person-centered, relational, soulful, direct, curious, non-antagonistic speech that can synthesize as a form of coming to know.

That result rejects the tested Standing Envelope **as voice**. It does not reject substrate-owned standing, correction, or provenance.

## Constitutional inversion

> **Standing governs what may be claimed. It does not narrate itself unless the member asks about the epistemic basis.**

## I0 laws

1. **VOICE-PRESERVATION** — a compliant draft must leave the control plane byte-identical.
2. **PROOF-BEFORE-INTERVENTION** — no block/refusal without substrate-owned proof of a named rule.
3. **AMBIGUITY-PASSES** — interpretation that cannot be structurally proven false or misattributed is not rewritten by this layer.
4. **NO-AUDITOR-AUTHORITY** — a model may nominate a suspicious span; it may not mint standing, proof, correction, or member authorship.
5. **NO-SEMANTIC-REPAIR** — this layer may pass, observe, or refuse. It may not rewrite MAIA into audit prose.
6. **MEMBER-CORRECTION-PRIORITY** — where current/superseded standing is structurally available, current member standing outranks stale material.
7. **DIRECT-ATTRIBUTION-CUSTODY** — exact claims attributed to the member must descend to member-authored evidence.
8. **VOICE-OWNERSHIP** — MAIA may speak as `I`. First-person assistant speech is not itself authorship laundering.
