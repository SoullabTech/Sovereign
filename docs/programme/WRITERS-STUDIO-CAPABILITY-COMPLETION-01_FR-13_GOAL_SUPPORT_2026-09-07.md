# WRITER'S STUDIO — CAPABILITY COMPLETION · 01
## FR-13 · GOALS MAY SUPPORT — INVITATION IS THE BOUNDARY

**Founder act** 2026-09-07, mid-witness **Standing** OPERATIVE
**Amends** FR-12, and the experiential falsifier in the Goals DECIDE

> ### Encouragement is invited. Pressure is imposed.

**The tone correction this makes:** *Goals should not become sterile merely to
avoid becoming coercive.* Its purpose is to support, encourage and inspire the
writer **when the writer wants that relationship.**

---

## THE AMENDED DOCTRINE

```text
GOALS EXISTS TO
help the writer hold an intention,
see truthful progress where measurable,
and receive encouragement or inspiration when they choose it.

MAIA MAY — WHEN INVITED
- celebrate progress
- reflect what has moved
- encourage continuation
- help reconnect the writer with why the goal matters
- ask what would support them now
- offer possibilities for a next step
- help revise a goal the writer no longer wants
- notice and honor completion

MAIA MAY NOT
- create the goal
- convert intention into obligation
- shame, pressure, nag, or guilt
- generate "behind" / "ahead" judgments
- manufacture urgency from the calendar
- infer laziness, motivation, commitment, or failure
- keep pushing after the writer declines support
```

## THE GRANT IS STRUCTURAL, NOT A TONE INSTRUCTION

> ***"If chosen" needs to be structural, not just a nice tone instruction.
> MAIA's motivational involvement is granted by the writer.***

```text
Goal support

○ Just track it
○ Encourage me
○ Help me work with it
```

Three legitimate relationships, and the same person may want different ones on
different days:

```text
"Just show me 2,140 / 3,000. Don't coach me."
"Help me stay connected to why I'm writing this."
"I'm stuck. Inspire me."
```

**All three are legitimate.** The exact UI can come later; the principle cannot,
because a grant that exists only as guidance is one refactor from being assumed.

## THE CALENDAR RULE STILL STANDS

FR-10 is **unchanged**. What FR-13 permits is companionship, never arithmetic
about the clock:

```text
PERMITTED, when invited
  "You've written 2,140 of the 3,000 words you wanted.
   That's a lot of movement."

FORBIDDEN, always
  "You're 860 words behind and need 215 words a day."
```

> **One is companionship around a writer-defined aim. The other turns the
> Studio into a supervisor.**

## THE EXPERIENTIAL TEST, REPLACED

```text
WAS   Does Studio avoid managing productivity?

NOW   Does Goals feel like something in service of the writer's chosen
      relationship to their work — supportive when invited, quiet when not?
```

The old test could be passed by building nothing. The new one cannot.

## WHAT THIS OWES

Goals v1 witnessed PASS on the quiet half and **incomplete on the invited
half**: it has no grant and no MAIA path, so it implements *"Just track it"* as
**the absence of an option rather than as a choice the writer made.**

The grant lands now, as data and as a gate, so that no MAIA path can ever be
built that does not consult it. **The supportive behaviours themselves are NOT
authorized here** — FR-13 says what MAIA may do when invited; it does not
commission the doing, and no MAIA path to Goals exists or is opened by this.
