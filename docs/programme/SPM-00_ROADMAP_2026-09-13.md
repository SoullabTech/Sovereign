## 2 · ⚠️ Critical-path observation

**Governance sits on the critical path twice** — the consume ruling and D9 — and is the only owner in
the sequence **with no programme behind it.** The census recorded D9's custody as weak; the roadmap
now makes two later steps depend on it.

```text
SPM can complete steps 1 · 3 · 4 · 5 without governance moving at all.
Steps 6 and 7 cannot begin.
```

⛔ **Not a complaint and not a request.** It is the shape of the sequence: the model can be made
intelligible and sovereign while remaining unusable by MAIA, because *what may be done with it* is a
different authority's answer. **A governance step that never arrives does not block SPM; it strands
everything downstream of SPM.**

⛔ **SPM must not resolve this by drifting into the vacuum.** Charter §3.
