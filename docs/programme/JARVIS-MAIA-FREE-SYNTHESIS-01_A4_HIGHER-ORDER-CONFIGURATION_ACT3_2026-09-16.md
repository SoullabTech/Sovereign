## Falsifier

A more complex relational geometry earns entry only if the canonical representation cannot carry a meaning that belongs to **three independently grounded relations together** without collapsing their provenance or pretending that one pairwise relation is the whole.

ACT 3 therefore constructs three independently evidenced dimensions:

1. way-of-being / values;
2. embodied state;
3. developmental time.

Each dimension is represented by its own typed relation and reversibly descends to its own primary evidence. A single `ConfigurationNode` then holds all three relations and supports a provisional Gestalt of convergence.

## Acceptance conditions

The simple representation passes only if:
- the configuration validates under the same canonical A4 law;
- the Gestalt descends to all six primary evidence records;
- each relation separately descends only to its own evidence dimension;
- no single relation contains the full evidence field of the configuration;
- removing any one relation removes its independently grounded evidence dimension from the resulting projection;
- no hyperedge-specific authority or new evidence standing is introduced.
