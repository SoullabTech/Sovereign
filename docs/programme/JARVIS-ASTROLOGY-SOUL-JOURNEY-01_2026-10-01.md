## Epistemic law

Every Astrology surface must preserve the following layers when they are present:

1. **Calculated facts** — astronomical / calendrical / traditional-system calculations.
2. **Symbolic tradition** — what an identifiable astrological tradition has commonly made of those patterns.
3. **Whole-field synthesis** — relationships among the chart's parts rather than isolated placement claims.
4. **Possible human expression** — provisional possibilities, never diagnosis or fate.
5. **Present activation** — current cycles or transits only where calculated.
6. **Member meaning** — what the person recognizes, rejects, questions, or chooses to keep.

No downstream layer may masquerade as an upstream fact.

MAIA may accompany inquiry but may not become astrologer-as-guru or author the member's identity.
