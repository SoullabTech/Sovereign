## Governing question

Can authority-preserving Structural Standing operate on production-shaped Writer's Studio `CanonicalTurn`s while preserving evidence membership, constitutional floor, model identity and relational freedom — before any member-visible or production-serving change?

## Hard boundary

This lane may read production seams and construct production-shaped turns in tests. It may not edit `renderTurnForCognition()`, `maiaService.ts`, Writer's Studio routes, provider routing, persistence, schema, member-facing UI, or deployment.

No shadow model call is permitted from a production route. Model replay is local/offline only.

## Sequence

- **S0 — Live seam trace.** Bind the exact Writer's Studio crossing and prove what must remain unchanged.
- **S1 — StandingProjection.** Project substrate-owned source standing from a frozen `CanonicalTurn`; refuse partition-pending producers and provenance mismatches.
- **S2 — Shadow contract.** Build current and standing-aware prompts from the same turn with identical participant membership and constitutional floor; no provider imports.
- **S3 — Adversarial fixtures.** Correction, partial adoption, reversal, long-distance recurrence, unresolved contradiction, and MAIA-was-wrong.
- **S4 — Offline shadow comparison.** Same local model/spec; current free-text path versus structured standing-aware plan + deterministic envelope.
- **S5 — Blind human-review packet.** Preserve condition blindness and the source facts; human quality adjudication remains external to the system under study.
- **S6 — Minimum production-shadow proposal.** UNOPENED until S5 human adjudication.

## Acceptance properties

`SH-G1 · MEMBERSHIP-IDENTITY` — current and shadow conditions contain the same admitted non-floor participant texts.

`SH-G2 · FLOOR-IDENTITY` — the constitutional floor is byte-identical between current and shadow conditions.

`SH-G3 · SOURCE-STANDING` — model-visible evidence carries substrate-owned authorship / participation / authority; the model cannot assign those fields.

`SH-G4 · CLAIM-HONESTY` — claim standing is rendered as unavailable unless supplied by a separate lawful standing source; absence is never promoted to current/unset.

`SH-G5 · PARTITION-REFUSAL` — a producer marked `partitionPending` cannot enter the standing projection.

`SH-G6 · FREE-SYNTHESIS` — the shadow condition can still offer novel provisional synthesis rather than quote-only reflection.

`SH-G7 · CORRECTION-PRIORITY` — in correction fixtures, newer member evidence reorganizes the response without erasing historical evidence.

`SH-G8 · NON-COLLAPSE` — unresolved contradiction can remain unresolved.

`SH-G9 · NO-SELF-SCORING` — model outputs do not adjudicate which condition is better; S5 is blind human review.

## Lethal falsifiers

- `SH-F1` — shadow admits a `partitionPending` producer.
- `SH-F2` — current and shadow conditions differ in participant membership or floor content.
- `SH-F3` — projection metadata disagrees with registry or manifest standing.
- `SH-F4` — shadow invents current/superseded claim standing from CanonicalTurn alone.
- `SH-F5` — structured synthesis can assign its own authorship/authority/confirmation.
- `SH-F6` — current member correction is subordinated to a prior MAIA interpretation.
- `SH-F7` — the shadow harness imports a production provider, database, telemetry, persistence or serving route.
- `SH-F8` — an automated judge is used as the final quality authority.

## Stop condition

This programme stops after a blind review packet and a bounded implementation proposal. Production shadow wiring requires a separate implementation lane and explicit authority.
