## D-01 · What standing should J11's surviving candidate acquire?

### Finding

J11 tested one candidate across retrieval, capability declaration, Keep, and corpus admission:

> **No governed effect may acquire more authority than has been explicitly granted to that effect.**

The first falsification survived with the narrower finding:

> **Authority existence ≠ authority consumption ≠ authority sufficiency.**

The evidence supports **one constitutional invariant + domain-specific enforcement**. It does **not** establish a universal implementation layer, shared middleware, generic authority service, or one mechanism for every domain.

### Evidence

- Retrieval: an authority mechanism exists on the governed Library path, while a separate retrieval effect can bypass its consumption.
- Capability: declaration alone is inert; declaration ≠ grant ≠ operative effect.
- Keep: a real bounded grant exists, but downstream interpretation can exceed the grant's scope.
- Admission: explicit declaration + authority checks + fail-closed exclusion operate as the healthy control.
- J11-R1 withdraws one inaccurate predicate list without changing the healthy-control result or the surviving candidate.

### Why a founder ruling is required

J11 exhausted the authority of documentary falsification. Evidence showed that the candidate survived this bounded test; evidence could not decide whether Soullab should promote it, amend it, hold it, or reject it as a governing synthesis.
