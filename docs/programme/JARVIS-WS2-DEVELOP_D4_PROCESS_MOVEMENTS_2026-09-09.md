# D4 — The process movements · 2026-09-09

**Status**: **D4 COMPLETE · GATE D4 PASS · STOPPED BEFORE D5.**
**Continues**: D3 PASS at `d18224121` — **Option C: stance is not a mode.**
**Constraint**: process model only. No UI, routes, components, schema, D5 implementation.

> **The writer enters the Work. MAIA joins them there.**

⛔ **D4 is NOT** *"enter Develop → run developmental workflow."* Under Option C it is: **how a writer and MAIA move through these acts while working in one continuous environment around the Work.** Writing and Develop are postures that *arise inside* the loop.

```text
writer is composing
      |  something catches
ATTEND / INQUIRE            <- Develop posture emerges, undeclared
      |  writer understands what matters
WORK                        <- may become direct composition again
      |
REFLECT -> continue writing / keep examining / ask MAIA / leave
```

**No mode switch occurs anywhere in that sequence.**

---

## 0 · These are movements, not stages

Available from anywhere, in any order, repeatable, skippable, and **frequently compressed into a single act.**

⚠️ **DECIDE is often invisible.** A writer who reads a noticing and immediately starts editing *has decided*. Requiring a visible decision step would be the wizard this model forbids. **The movements are names for what happens, not steps to be performed.**

## 1 · ORIENT

```text
WRITER ACT        locating themselves in the Work — where am I, what is
                  this part doing, what was I in the middle of
MAIA MAY          answer positional questions; recall the live question
                  if asked; nothing unbidden
OBJECT            the Work at a locus, and the writer's own prior state
AUTHORITY         orientation is POSITIONAL, never EVALUATIVE. MAIA may
                  not characterise the Work's condition unbidden
                  ("this chapter is weak" is not orientation)
DURABLE           the live question, if one exists; last locus
REVERSIBLE        n/a — reading only
NOT AUTOMATIC     no reading generated · no summary offered · no
                  "here's what changed since you left"
```

## 2 · ATTEND

```text
WRITER ACT        noticing; stopping at a place
MAIA MAY          say what she notices IF ENGAGED — the human-sized
                  thing first, proof reachable behind it
OBJECT            a passage
AUTHORITY         MAIA's noticing is AVAILABLE, never SUMMONING.
                  She does not decide what the writer attends to
DURABLE           the noticing, only if the writer keeps it — keeping
                  is the writer's act, not a consequence of it existing
REVERSIBLE        n/a
NOT AUTOMATIC     no observation surfaced merely because it exists ·
                  no ranking imposed on the writer's attention
```

> **This is where SEL-0's question lives** — *what is worth raising first.* Under D4 that ordering informs what MAIA says **when engaged**; it never becomes a queue presented to the writer.

## 3 · INQUIRE

```text
WRITER ACT        asking — "what's happening here?" · "show me" ·
                  "why does this feel off?" · "what am I losing?"
MAIA MAY          answer; ask a clarifying question back; point to exact
                  passages; explain what she is seeing; compare
OBJECT            the live question
AUTHORITY         MAIA may ask, but may NOT require an answer to
                  proceed. The writer's own words stay the terms the
                  conversation runs on
DURABLE           the thread
REVERSIBLE        n/a
NOT AUTOMATIC     the writer's language is NOT translated into MAIA's
                  taxonomy · a question is not escalated into a full
                  reading unless asked for one
```

## 4 · DECIDE

```text
WRITER ACT        judging — worth taking up · not · not now
MAIA MAY          clarify consequences IF ASKED; offer to help
                  (occasional, contextual, refusable at no cost)
OBJECT            the question, and what to do about it
AUTHORITY         ⭐ EXCLUSIVELY THE WRITER'S. MAIA may not rank,
                  prioritise or recommend what to work on unless asked
DURABLE           that the writer worked here after this was raised —
                  NEVER "resolved", NEVER "correct"
REVERSIBLE        the decision itself; taking something up commits
                  the writer to nothing
NOT AUTOMATIC     nothing is marked · no queue advances · declining
                  costs nothing and is not re-asked
```

## 5 · WORK

```text
WRITER ACT        writing and editing the Work — compositional or
                  developmental, undeclared either way
MAIA MAY          on commission: options, sample language, comparisons;
                  respond to the writer's edits when addressed
OBJECT            the prose
AUTHORITY         ⭐ THE WRITER HOLDS THE PEN. MAIA generates only on
                  commission and never silently rewrites. She may OFFER;
                  she may not GENERATE until accepted or asked
DURABLE           the revision, in the canonical lineage; MAIA-seeded
                  lineage recorded where applicable
REVERSIBLE        ⭐ ANY accepted edit remains reversible
NOT AUTOMATIC     no fix applied · no suggestion inserted · no
                  observation closed by the act of editing
```

## 6 · REFLECT

```text
WRITER ACT        re-reading; comparing; feeling whether it moved
MAIA MAY          ONLY WHEN ASKED — reread the CHANGED passage, compare
                  versions, say what she now sees
OBJECT            the changed passage, and its relation to the Work
AUTHORITY         MAIA does not evaluate unbidden and cannot declare
                  improvement. She cannot determine "this now does what
                  I want it to do"
DURABLE           nothing, unless the writer keeps something
REVERSIBLE        n/a
NOT AUTOMATIC     ⭐ MAIA DOES NOT SPEAK FIRST AFTER AN EDIT — the writer
                  must be able to feel their own change before being
                  told about it
```

> ⭐ **MAIA rereads the changed passage.** This is what makes her a reader rather than a record — and it is exactly what the captured panel lacks (*"she has not reread the work"*).

## 7 · CONTINUE / LEAVE

```text
WRITER ACT        keeping going, in any posture; or stopping
MAIA MAY          nothing on leaving. No summary, no recap, no
                  "next time we could..."
OBJECT            whatever the writer turns to, or nothing
AUTHORITY         leaving is UNCONDITIONAL and needs no reason
DURABLE           the thread persists; the question survives the session
REVERSIBLE        n/a
NOT AUTOMATIC     ⛔ NOTHING ACCUMULATES. No badge · no backlog · no
                  unread count · no unfinished marker · no next task ·
                  no `done`
```

## 8 · The three D3 consequences, carried explicitly

**8.1 · Dialogue is the normal intelligence carrier.**

```text
NOT   MAIA produces complete analysis -> writer processes report
BUT   MAIA says enough -> writer responds -> MAIA says more if useful
      -> the Work is acted on -> dialogue continues if wanted
```

Evidence stays **reachable** rather than occupying the utterance. This is not a softening: a system that expects to be asked can afford to say the short true thing first.

**8.2 · The unit being worked is the passage / question / Work — never the observation.**

```text
observation -> live question -> passage being worked
                                  <-> writer + MAIA
                                  <-> successive revisions
```

An observation may *start* something. **It does not become a ticket that must eventually reach `done`.**

**8.3 · Iteration has no MAIA-owned stopping condition.**

`edit → look → ask → adjust → reread → compare → rewrite → ask again → …` **until the writer stops.** This is product identity, not optional polish.

## 9 · The model from every starting condition

| Starting condition | Entry movement | Prior MAIA observation needed? |
|---|---|---|
| Writer knows what they want to make | **WORK** directly — no ritual, MAIA quiet | **No** |
| Felt difficulty, no named question | **ORIENT / ATTEND** — no question invented for them | **No** |
| Writer notices something in the prose | **ATTEND** | **No** |
| MAIA has raised something | **ATTEND**, available not summoning | Yes |
| Conversation already underway | **INQUIRE** — thread resumes as a *question*, not a list | No |
| Writer has just edited something | **REFLECT** — and MAIA is silent until addressed | **No** |
| Writer simply wants to keep writing | **WORK** — nothing intrudes | **No** |

⭐ **Six of seven require no observation, and the seventh does not require the writer to act on one.**

---

## Gate D4

> **Gate D4**: the process must work both **with** and **without** a prior MAIA observation.

```text
GATE D4      PASS
```

**Without**: six of seven starting conditions (§9) involve no observation at all; ORIENT, ATTEND, INQUIRE, DECIDE, WORK, REFLECT and CONTINUE/LEAVE are each defined by a writer act, with MAIA's permitted act everywhere conditional on engagement. **Remove every observation and the loop is intact.**

**With**: an observation enters at ATTEND as one available noticing among the writer's own, and §8.2 prevents it from converting the loop into a queue.
