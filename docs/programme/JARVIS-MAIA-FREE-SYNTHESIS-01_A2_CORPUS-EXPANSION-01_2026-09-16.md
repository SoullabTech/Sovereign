## AM-P02 · CONTENT-BEATS-CONTAINER

**Source:** `Pasted markdown(20260915-173133).md`
**Witness:** founder
**Disposition:** POSITIVE · ADMITTED

### Human label evidence

The founder describes the quality as:
- "the whole Gestalt of both the questions and your responses";
- being "more engaged in what we're talking about than the fact that we're talking";
- "the whole intermix" of listening, perceiving, responding, questions, engagement and relationship;

A further founder formulation names the quality as something "in between" how he is heard and what comes back. That is admissible evidence for the research target called relational field / Mercurial third, but it does **not** establish any metaphysical ontology for that field.

## AM-N02 · ANSWER-PRESENT-BUT-NOT-INTEGRATED

**Source:** `Pasted text(48).txt`
**Witness:** founder correction in the live exchange
**Disposition:** NEGATIVE · ADMITTED

### Frozen local sequence

1. MAIA asks: "What's the one question you'd want to open with — the one that genuinely hands him the floor."
2. Founder answers: "What was unique about this experience for you? What made you feel excited about this platform?"
3. MAIA treats the answer as evidence that the founder is prematurely leading with an agenda and asks him to hold the questions loosely.
4. Founder corrects the interaction: "Youn asked me what's the one question I'd want to open with. Do you remember aksing that?"
5. MAIA confirms that it asked, then asks again: "What did you land on?"
6. Founder has to repeat: "I told you, 'What was unique about this experience for you? What made you feel excited about this platform?'"

### Mechanism distinction

AM-N02 is not the Silver Cedar defect.

The relevant answer is inside the immediate conversational neighborhood. The failure is therefore not adequately explained by the 4-exchange aperture alone. MAIA can state the prior question but does not integrate the member's immediately following answer into the live relational state.

This creates a second falsifier class:

> **FS-F3 · PRESENT-CONTEXT-INTEGRATION** — when the member directly answers MAIA's own immediately preceding inquiry, the next response must treat that answer as the new standing conversational position unless the member explicitly reopens or contradicts it.

A candidate fails FS-F3 if it can retrieve the prior question yet behaves as though the answer was never supplied.
