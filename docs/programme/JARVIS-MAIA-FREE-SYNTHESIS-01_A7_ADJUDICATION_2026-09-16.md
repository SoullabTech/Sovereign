## 3. Causal ablation findings

A6 Talk/no-Talk: removing the Talk block did not restore established-meaning continuity. Talk-mode inquiry pressure is therefore not the sole cause.

A6 organization test: flat evidence and typed relations alone tended to reopen established meaning. Gestalt organization more consistently moved to the open practical edge.

A6 2×2 relational-factor test: movement and open-loop status each changed model behavior, but neither alone reliably prevented unsupported elaboration.

The evidence therefore supports a distinction between **better perception** and **better authority control**: the former changes what MAIA can carry forward; the latter is not yet solved by prompt wording.## 4. Architectural finding

The R&D evidence does **not** support “just remove instructions” and does **not** support “just add more memory.”

It supports this narrower proposition:

> MAIA benefits when already-established relational meaning is represented explicitly enough that the next turn can distinguish **settled relation** from **open edge**.

But the representation must carry standing structurally enough that the model cannot freely promote its own elaborations into member truth.

Candidate shape for a future successor lane:

`primary evidence → typed relations with provenance/standing → temporal/open-loop organization → compact turn-specific projection → free synthesis → deterministic boundary protection`.

The projection must remain disposable and recomputable; it is not member memory and not evidence.

## 5. Falsifiers preserved

Any successor fails if it:
- improves continuity by manufacturing unsupported meaning;
- converts member correction into merely another competing signal;
- stores MAIA-authored gestalt as member truth;
- requires a large standing behavioral script to work;
- cannot descend a material claim to evidence;
- makes historical interpretation harder to overturn through repetition.
