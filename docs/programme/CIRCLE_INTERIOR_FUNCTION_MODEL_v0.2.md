# CIRCLE INTERIOR FUNCTION MODEL — v0.2

**Date** 2026-09-07 (evening) · ⛔ **DESIGN ONLY. NO IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED.**
**Supersedes** `CIRCLE_INTERIOR_FUNCTION_MODEL_v0.1.md`, retained unchanged.

v0.1 stands in full for **Arrive · Witness · Offer · Respond · Inquire · Remember · Tend**, the verified
census of current surfaces, and the three changes to the existing design. **This revision records only
what the founder rulings changed.** Where the two differ, v0.2 governs.

---

## 1 · OFFER — ratified (FR-22). D-K2 CLOSED.

v0.1 could not say what withdrawal meant for native speech. It can now.

```
OFFER FROM MY FIELD    source exists privately elsewhere → Circle receives a representation
OFFER HERE             authored directly in Circle       → THE CIRCLE OBJECT IS THE SOURCE
```

They are **two species with different revocation semantics**, and the design must not pretend
otherwise.

**Author withdrawal.** Payload leaves. Nothing depending on it → the offering may disappear entirely.
Responses already authored → preserve **only the minimal structural fact** that keeps their authorship
intelligible:

```
[Offering withdrawn]
Maria: "This helped me see something about my own anger."
```

⛔ No original text · summary · embedding · reconstruction · **no hidden preserved payload**. The
author's identity is removed from the visible tombstone absent an independent integrity requirement.

> ⭐ **The author can surrender their meaning. They cannot retroactively surrender Maria's meaning for
> her.**

**Leaving and removal** withdraw the member's live native offerings under the same rule — **boundary
cascade, not facilitator proxy-authorship** (FR-16's distinction, one object out):

> **Ending Circle membership ends eligibility of the departing member's live Circle-native authored
> material to remain in the field. Dependent material authored by others survives independently.**

⚠️ **Honest limit to carry into copy:** in a Circle of six, an unattributed tombstone above attributed
responses leaves the author re-identifiable from context. This is a **stance** — the system does not
name them — **not anonymity.** Never describe it to members as anonymity.

## 2 · RESPOND — ratified (FR-23)

**A response cannot become the parent of another response.** One level, permanently. ⛔ No likes ·
reactions · "helpful" ranking · counts as status · engagement sort. *A response is speech, not a
metric-bearing object.* **D-K7** (whether typed orientations ship at all) stays open.

## 3 · TEND / REPAIR — ratified (FR-24). D-K5 CLOSED.

**Request Attention is a process request, not an accusation object.** It carries `circle_id` ·
`requested_by` · `requested_at` · `state`, and:

> ⛔ **`target_member_id` DOES NOT EXIST.**

*Something in this relationship needs attention* — not *Maria committed infraction X*. The optional
private note to the tender is **ephemeral intake**: not Circle memory, not a member record. ⛔ The
software never declares `Repaired ✅`.

`REPAIR ≠ BOUNDARY/SAFETY ≠ REMOVAL`, never collapsed.

⚠️ **New question this creates → D-M1:** the tender must know whom to contact, so a name lives in the
intake. "Ephemeral" needs a **defined destruction point**, and it cannot be *"when the process closes"*
if closing is indefinite — otherwise the intake is the dossier FR-24 exists to prevent, in a different
table.

## 4 · Unchanged from v0.1, restated as still-governing

Rename `What's alive` → **`Here`** · human-authored orientation above the pulse, *"last movement"* off
the surface · **Circle-native Offer path** · witnessing writes nothing observable · ordinary responses
never gated by contribute-before-see (inquiry only) · `FieldPhase` untouched (CA-14).
