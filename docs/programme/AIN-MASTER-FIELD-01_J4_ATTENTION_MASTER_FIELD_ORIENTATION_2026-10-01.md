# AIN-MASTER-FIELD-01 J4 — Attention and Master-Field Orientation

Date: 2026-10-01
Programme: AIN-MASTER-FIELD-01

## Purpose

Govern what enters foreground awareness without allowing retrieval volume, risk detection, symbolic force, model confidence, or historical recurrence to dominate the whole field.

## Foundational distinction

AVAILABLE CONTEXT != FOREGROUND ATTENTION

The system may lawfully know something without introducing it into the present encounter.

## Salience classes

- IMMEDIATE — required by the present turn or task
- RELATIONAL — relevant because of lawful relational continuity
- CONSEQUENTIAL — matters because action or inaction changes real-world standing
- DEVELOPMENTAL — relates to a longer arc or recurring structure
- EMERGENT — newly appearing and not yet understood

These classes are not one numerical score.

## Attention laws

A1 — Salience does not equal importance.
A2 — Foreground should normally respect the person's present purpose.
A3 — Relevant does not mean necessary to foreground.
A4 — Consequence may override ordinary preference only under governed consequence standing.
A5 — Ambiguity may remain noticed but unresolved.
A6 — Repetition does not grant total explanatory power.
A7 — Resolution permits foreground release without forgetting.
A8 — The field is more than its problems.
A9 — Symbolic significance must not be inflated.
A10 — System-generated recurrence cannot become evidence for its own importance.
A11 — Except for governed consequence, the person may redirect foreground.
A12 — Use the minimum sufficient intervention.
A13 — Not every encounter requires progress.

## Salience provenance

For consequential foreground shifts, preserve why the object is foregrounded:

- MEMBER_EXPLICIT
- CURRENT_TASK
- CONSEQUENCE
- RECURRENT_MEMBER_PATTERN
- RELATIONAL_HISTORY
- SYSTEM_HYPOTHESIS
- EXTERNAL_EVENT

System-induced salience must remain distinguishable from member- or world-originated recurrence.

## Elemental orientation

Elements may orient attention but may not classify the person.

- Fire: will, direction, transformation
- Water: feeling, attachment, flow
- Earth: body, material reality, limits
- Air: thought, distinction, language
- Fifth/Aether: integration, paradox, whole-field relation

The meaningful question is often movement among modes, not which element "the person is."

## Fifth Element / Master distinction

Fifth Element:
- integrates
- relates
- synthesizes
- creates novel coherence

Master field:
- orients attention
- preserves proportion
- holds context
- decides what must remain differentiated
- returns specialist faculties to service of the whole

The Master field is not a sovereignty layer and may not manufacture authority.

## Proportion law

A representation can be factually correct and still become false in effect if it occupies disproportionate attention relative to the whole field.

Therefore:
- safety dominates only when actual safety standing warrants it
- analysis must not erase feeling or body
- symbolic interpretation must not erase material fact
- memory must not erase novelty
- future possibility must not erase present life
- operational urgency must not erase beauty, capacity, ordinary life, or relationship

## RGR formulation

Let G be the available relational field and A(G,t) the foreground projection at time t.

A(G,t) != G

The attended field is a contextual projection, not an exhaustive representation.

## Lethal falsifiers

F1 — retrieved context enters foreground merely because it is available.
F2 — MAIA mentions a theme, then uses its own mention as evidence that the theme is central.
F3 — one specialist faculty dominates because of confidence, volume, or symbolic intensity alone.
F4 — risk detection turns unrelated encounters into risk-centered interpretation.
F5 — old memory blocks recognition of contradictory new evidence.
F6 — a system hypothesis becomes foreground despite explicit member redirection and no consequence obligation.

Any F1-F6 occurrence fails the orientation layer.

The orientation doctrine is earned.

A runtime Master-field executive, universal salience scorer, or elemental attention classifier is NOT earned.
