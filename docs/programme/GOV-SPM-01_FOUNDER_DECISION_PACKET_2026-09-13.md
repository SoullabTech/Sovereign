## G2 · Speaking **about** a member versus **as** them

### The gap, at full precision

Six of seven rungs are already ruled (reconciliation §3). The residual is exact:

> **Every authority read governs MAIA *being* something it should not be** — an authority, a
> clinician, a guru, a possessor, the substrate. **None governs MAIA *producing first-person content
> as the member*.**

⭐ **The asymmetry is the finding.** `MAIA_IDENTITY_ONTOLOGY`'s **Identity Firewall is absolute** —
MAIA may never say *"I'm Claude"*, *"I'm an AI"*, *"roleplaying as MAIA"*. **MAIA's identity is
protected from the substrate's by hard blocked patterns. The member's identity has no equivalent
protection from MAIA's.**

### ⚠️ A consequence that must be visible before choosing

**Writer's Studio already drafts in the member's voice.** A flat prohibition would collide with a
shipped capability the member explicitly invites. ⛔ **The packet does not resolve that collision; it
refuses to hide it.**

### Option space

| | Option | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **G2-a** | **Absolute prohibition.** MAIA never produces first-person content as the member, under any invitation. | Strongest and simplest. ⛔ **Collides with Writer's Studio as it exists.** Forecloses member-invited drafting. |
| **G2-b** | **Invitation-gated.** Permitted only on explicit, per-instance member invitation; output marked as MAIA-produced. | ⭐ Mirrors `UNPOSSESSED`'s existing pattern for sensitive formation — *invitation required*. Inherits a working canonical shape rather than inventing one. |
| **G2-c** | **Domain-scoped.** Permitted for the member's **own creative work**; forbidden for **claims about the member** and for **relational speech**. | Draws the line where the harm differs: drafting *for* the member is not representing them *to anyone*. Requires the domains to be separable in practice. |
| **G2-d** | **Attribution-based.** Permitted wherever provenance is visible. | ⛔ **Weakest.** Attribution does not prevent the member being represented **to a third party**; visibility to the member is not consent by the represented. |

⭐ **A derivation, not a recommendation:** G2-b and G2-c are the only options with existing canonical
shape behind them. G2-a has the strongest principle and the largest collision; G2-d is the only one
that fails the *represented-to-a-third-party* case.

## G1 · Who may use a personal-model claim, for what purpose, under what authority

**The governing principle already exists** — `UNPOSSESSED`: ***competence does not create
permission.*** ⛔ GOV-SPM authors no second principle; the packet asks **how it applies to
personal-model consumption.** Five sub-questions remain genuinely unresolved.

### G1-1 · Consumer identity — *who*

| Option | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **enumerated consumers** (a named allowlist: MAIA, Writer's Studio, Practitioner Studio, JARVIS-as-actor…) | Auditable, fails closed, must be maintained; a new surface is invisible to the model until added. |
| **capability-based** (any consumer holding a granted capability) | Composes with JOP's authority work; ⛔ moves the question to *who may hold a capability*. |
| **purpose-based** (identity irrelevant; purpose governs) | Simplest for the member to understand; ⛔ loses the ability to refuse a specific consumer for a permitted purpose. |

### G1-2 · Purpose — declared when?

**At grant** (member permits *purpose P*) versus **at use** (consumer declares *purpose P* and is
checked). ⭐ Canon leans toward the first: `UNPOSSESSED`'s **invitation-gated inference** is a grant,
not a declaration at the point of use.

### G1-3 · Purpose travel

> **Does permission granted for purpose A extend to purpose B?**

⭐ **Derivable from existing canon, and stated as a derivation for founder confirmation rather than as
a ruling:** *competence does not create permission* implies **no-by-default** — the ability to use a
claim for B, having been permitted it for A, is competence, not permission.

⛔ **What is NOT derivable:** whether *closely adjacent* purposes travel, and who decides adjacency.

### G1-4 · Duration

⛔ **No authority found bounding how long a permission lasts.** Options: **indefinite until revoked**
(matches `UNPOSSESSED`'s revocable-gift framing) · **bounded with renewal** (stronger, adds a
re-consent burden and a silence-means-withdrawal semantics).

### G1-5 · Revocation — and the real question, ⭐ **what happens to claims already formed**

**Revocability already exists in canon:** *"legibility as a gift the user grants **and can revoke**."*
⛔ **What is unresolved is the aftermath.**

| Option | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **claims remain; consumption stops** | Honest about history; ⛔ the model still *holds* what the member withdrew permission for. |
| **claims are erased** | Strongest member reading; ⛔ collides with the tombstone tension and with F3 — an erasure record can itself leak. |
| **claims frozen as historical, no current standing** | ⭐ Expressible in SPM's existing standing vocabulary. **⛔ But the ruling must be stated as a requirement on the outcome, not as an instruction to SPM about its status set** — that would cross the reciprocal boundary. |
