## 0. What this wording must never do (checked against §5 of the companion note)

- **Never imply partnership, endorsement, a paid relationship or a record deal.** "Featured" means shown with credit; nothing more.
- **Never ask for, or imply, any rights in a recording.** A Tier 2 grant is permission to **show a performer's name, words, photo and links** and to **point members to their music where it already lives.** Soullab hosts **no audio** (unknown rights ⇒ no Soullab-hosted media, #1979 §4). *An artist agreeing to be featured is not automatically all rights.*
- **Never make healing, therapeutic or effect claims** about their music — the performer's own description is shown **as theirs, attributed**, never as Soullab's.
- **Never translate a tradition into Soullab's vocabulary.** Lineage-held or sacred music is **not** invited as a Spotlight at all (§5 rule 7); this invitation is **not** to be sent for it without the keepers' participation.
- **Never pressure:** no deadline, no "limited spots," no urgency.

## 1. The line on every Tier 1 card (shown to members)

> *Are you the artist? You're welcome to tell us how you'd like to be shown here — or to ask us to take this down. [Contact]*

Why this wording: it makes the card **answerable to the performer without asking them for anything**; "take this down" is offered **first-class**, not buried.

## 2. The invitation (message from Kelly to a performer) — for review before any use

**Subject:** A small place for your music in Soullab's Listening Room

> Hello [Name],
>
> I'm Kelly Nezat, the founder of Soullab. We're building a Listening Room — a quiet place in our app where members can slow down and listen. I came across **[title]** and it held me. I'd like to share it with our members, with your name and a link back to where you share your music.
>
> **Here's what that looks like today:** a card with your name, a line or two in our own words, and a link to your music on [YouTube / Spotify / your site]. Members who press play hear it **on that platform**, so your plays and your page stay yours. We don't copy, download or host your recording.
>
> **If you'd like more,** you're welcome to tell us how you'd like to be introduced — your own words, a photo, a message to members, a link to where people can find more of your work. That's entirely optional. You can say yes, say not now, or ask us to take the card down, and we'll do it without needing a reason.
>
> I'm not offering a partnership or any kind of endorsement, and there's nothing you need to do. I wanted you to hear about it from us rather than discover it.
>
> With gratitude for the work,
> Kelly
> [Soullab contact]

**Notes on the draft:** it states **what already exists** (the Tier 1 card) before asking anything; it says **"hold me" in the first person** rather than praising the performer's work as a technique or an effect; it names **what we don't do** (copy, download, host); and it ends without a call to action beyond a reply. ⚠️ **"I came across [title] and it held me"** is Kelly's to say truthfully — replace it with her own sentence for each performer; **never send a template line she did not mean.** Whether this message should come from Kelly or from Soullab generally is the founder's call.

## 3. The one-time grant (shown as a short page or reply form; "Yes, feature me")

> **What you're saying yes to**
>
> You're giving Soullab permission to show, in the Listening Room of the Soullab app:
> - your **name** as you'd like it written;
> - a **short introduction** that you write or approve;
> - your **photo** (if you choose to send one);
> - **links** to where your music lives (for example YouTube, Spotify, Bandcamp, your own site);
> - a **message to members** (if you'd like to leave one).
>
> **What this does *not* give us**
> - It gives us **no rights in your recordings or compositions.** We do not copy, download, store or re-host your music. When a member presses play, they hear it from the platform where you share it.
> - It is **not exclusive.** You can share your music anywhere else, and feature with anyone else.
> - It is **not a partnership, endorsement or employment,** and we won't describe it as one.
> - We will **not** say what your music will make a listener feel, and we won't describe it as healing or therapy. If you describe your music that way, we'll show your words **as yours, with your name on them.**
>
> **How you stay in charge**
> - You can **change your introduction, photo or links** at any time, and we'll update them.
> - You can **withdraw at any time, for any reason.** Tell us and we'll remove your card, photo and words **within [N] days**; links to your music on other platforms are yours and aren't ours to remove.
> - If a platform removes your video or turns off embedding, the card **stops showing a player** and links out instead.
>
> **What we keep:** the introduction and photo you gave us, the date you agreed, and a record that you did (so we can honour your choice later). [Retention period.] We do not use any of it to profile members or to rank artists.
>
> ☐ **Yes — feature me with the details above.**   ☐ **Not now.**
>
> [Name as written] · [date] · [contact]

**Notes on the draft:** "what this does **not** give us" is deliberately **longer** than the grant, because the likeliest harm is a performer believing they conferred more or less than they did. It never uses the word "license." **⚠️ Counsel should check:** whether a photo/name permission needs more formal wording in the member countries involved; whether "[N] days" and the retention period are right; whether the performer can bind others in a group (a band, a label, a collaborator) — **a solo performer's yes is not a group's yes**; and whether music published through a **label or distributor** changes who can say yes.

## 4. Replies we should be ready for (draft answers, not a script)

- **"Is there a fee or a payment?"** — ⚠️ **Founder proposal, not yet decided (see §6.1):** no payment either way; as a *thank-you, with no strings*, the performer is welcome as a member when access is open to them, with their own card to introduce themselves. The honest answer **today** is that Soullab is **not** paying and **not** asking payment; **do not promise membership or highlighting beyond what is live when you write.** Whether to offer anything is a founder decision (see §6) and **must not be improvised in a reply.**
- **"Can you take it down?"** — "Yes, and thank you for telling us. We'll remove it within [N] days." No reasons asked, no retention pitch.
- **"Who sees this?"** — "Members of the Soullab app, inside the Listening Room. It isn't a public page. [Confirm if true at the time of sending.]"
- **"Do you track who listens?"** — only what is true of the live system; ⚠️ **not yet defined** — do not answer from this draft.
- **"Can you add my other work?"** — "Yes, send the links you'd like."

## 5. Members' "suggest a performer" form — consent copy (owed in the companion note, §0 item 6)

> **Suggest a performer for the Listening Room**
>
> Is there someone whose music held you? Tell us about them — only what you'd like to share.
>
> - Who is it? ______
> - A link where we can hear them ______
> - Anything you'd like us to know (optional) ______
>
> **What happens next:** this goes **only to Kelly** at Soullab. It is **not shown to other members** and is **not published automatically.** If we feature this performer, it will be after we check the rights and the performer has been invited — never because of how many people suggested them.
>
> ☐ **You may say a member suggested this performer** (unnamed). *(Off unless you choose it.)*
> ☐ **You may use my first name.** *(Off unless you choose it.)*
>
> **What we keep:** what you typed here and the date. We use it for this and nothing else — not to profile you or to shape what MAIA says to you. You can ask us to **delete it any time.** [Retention period.]
>
> **Sanctuary note:** nothing from a Sanctuary session is used here; this form is separate. [Confirm with Sanctuary invariants before building.]

**Notes on the draft:** **default is unnamed**, matching the shelf rule; **no counts or "popular" signals** are promised or implied; the form states **what it does not do** (no profiling, no auto-publish) in the member's own sentence order. ⚠️ A suggestion form is a **new place that collects member-authored text**: whether it falls under the **no-stealth-memory / consent-for-memory** vows and the Sanctuary rules must be **confirmed before it is built** — the wording above only promises what the vows already require.
