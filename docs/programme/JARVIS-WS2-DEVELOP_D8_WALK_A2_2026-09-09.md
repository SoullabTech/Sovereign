## ⭐ Finding A/M2-1 — none of the four is local, and each is non-local *differently*

This is the finding. **The four noticings have four different relationships to place:**

```text
1  DISTRIBUTED PATTERN   n loci, no primary one. The finding is the repetition.
2  RELATION BETWEEN TWO  2 loci, and THE FINDING IS THE RELATION, not either end.
3  RECURRING THREAD      many loci, and the finding is the recurrence itself.
4  BOUNDARY / SEAM       the locus is BETWEEN two places. There is no line
                         at position 9 that the transition happens "at".
```

**Only a genuinely local finding — an awkward sentence, an unclear antecedent — fits the margin natively.**

⭐ **And developmental findings are characteristically non-local. That is what distinguishes developmental reading from line editing.** Structure, arc, continuity, repetition, pacing, coherence — the seven lenses in the live room — are *all* relations across places.

> **The Margin is the natural architecture for line editing and the unnatural one for developmental work** — which is notable, given that developmental capability is the reason this lane exists.

## ⭐⭐ Finding A/M2-2 — the founder's watch item: it *is* an identity problem

The question was whether the architecture can distinguish **three marks** from **one observation represented at three loci.**

**It cannot, and the reason is structural rather than visual:**

```text
In a place-organized architecture, a mark's IDENTITY IS ITS LOCATION.
That is what "place organizes" means.

A finding whose identity spans locations therefore has no representation.
It can only appear as several objects that happen to say related things.
```

**Density was the wrong diagnosis.** The problem is not that marks are numerous:

```text
4 findings  ->  9+ marks
and the writer cannot tell WHICH NINE BELONG TO WHICH FOUR
```

⚠️ **That is not clutter. It is misinformation about how much is wrong with the chapter.** A writer reading nine marks reasonably concludes there are nine problems. There are four. **The architecture has inflated the trouble by a factor of two, and cannot say so.**

⛔ **Classification, per the ruled distinction.** This now reaches further than the tradeoff D7 named:

```text
D7's recorded tradeoff     "everything MAIA notices wants a mark"  (volume)
A/M1 pressure              "some findings have no locus at all"    (scope)
A/M2 finding               "findings have no identity apart from place" (identity)
```

**Whether this is A's structural failure condition is the founder's call.** The argument that it is: the failure condition was *the margin becomes a list*, and a set of nine marks standing for four findings **is already a list — one that misrepresents its own contents.** The argument that it is not: no inventory, count, badge or state has appeared; the marks remain silent and uncounted.

## Finding A/M2-3 — burden: the system cannot count, but the writer can

Under the ruled architecture nothing accumulates: no badge, no count, no state, no `done`, and the writer can keep writing with every mark untouched. **Formally, no obligation is created.**

⚠️ **But the architecture forbids the system from counting. It cannot stop the writer from counting.** Nine marks in a margin are visible arithmetic. **Restraint in the system does not produce restraint in the reader** — and this is the first place in the whole lane where a prohibition on the system fails to deliver the experience the prohibition was written for.
