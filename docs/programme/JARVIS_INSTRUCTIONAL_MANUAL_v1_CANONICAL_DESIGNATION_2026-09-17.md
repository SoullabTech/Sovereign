## 1 · Founder ruling

Founder ruling: **TAKE**.

By that act, `JARVIS_INSTRUCTIONAL_MANUAL_v1.md` is designated the canonical general operating manual for JARVIS-governed work at Soullab.

The artifact is adopted unchanged from the version reviewed on PR #1341. This record does not revise its wording.

## 2 · What canonical means

The manual is the default procedural authority for how JARVIS work is opened, bounded, evidenced, falsified, adjudicated, merged, deployed, witnessed, and handed forward when no more-specific ratified authority governs the act.
Canonical status does **not** convert every explanatory sentence in the manual into independent product doctrine. The manual remains an instructional synthesis whose authority is procedural and operational.

## 3 · Precedence

When authorities differ, use this order:

1. **Explicit current founder ruling.**
2. **Ratified programme-specific law, constitution, charter, or acceptance contract** governing the exact subject.
3. **This canonical JARVIS instructional manual** for general JARVIS procedure.
4. **Lane-specific design or implementation records** that do not carry higher authority.
5. **Historical records, prior syntheses, proposals, hypotheses, and agent recommendations** according to their recorded standing.

A more-specific ratified programme rule therefore outranks a general manual rule without invalidating the manual.

A programme may not cite this manual to evade a stricter founder stop, consent boundary, sovereignty invariant, evidence obligation, or acceptance gate already governing that programme.
## 4 · Effect on new JARVIS work

From this designation forward, a new JARVIS programme or lane should begin from the manual's general grammar unless its governing programme law supplies a more specific sequence.

A new session should not invent its own definitions of programme, flow, lane, act, gate, witness, ruling, PASS, STOP, RETURN, HOLD, or PARK when the manual already defines them.

A new JARVIS flow is still designed around the subject's real decision structure. The manual does not require every programme to use the Governed Knowledge `J0–J10` numbering or any other single stage vocabulary.

The manual's decision rule remains controlling for **new flow vs new lane**: a new lane repairs or advances an existing governing question; a new flow is justified only when the governing question, authority structure, or terminal adjudication is materially different.

## 5 · Effect on existing programmes

Existing programmes are **not reopened, renumbered, or retroactively rewritten** by this designation.

Their ratified charters, founder rulings, accepted contracts, historical witnesses, and recorded stops retain their original standing.

Where an existing programme's historical procedure differs from the manual, the historical record remains true about what governed that act at that time. Future acts in that programme use the precedence rule in §3.
## 6 · What this ruling does not authorize

This designation does not authorize:

- any MAIA runtime change;
- any schema or migration;
- any deployment;
- any production write;
- any corpus ingestion or retrieval repair;
- any change to member consent, memory, identity, Voice, or Sanctuary behavior;
- any merge or deploy in another programme;
- any lifting of an existing founder stop;
- any promotion of a proposal, hypothesis, or candidate law merely because it appears as an example in the manual.

PR #1341 was a documentation act. No production deployment is required for this designation.

## 7 · Supersession and amendment

The manual may evolve, but canonical status belongs to the version actually reviewed and adopted until a later governed act supersedes or amends it.

Future changes should preserve accountable supersession: the prior manual and this designation remain historical evidence rather than being rewritten to imply they always contained later corrections.
