## 2. The amendments, precisely

### A — `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md`

The load-bearing claim — *the discipline operates at the inference layer, not the disclosure
layer* — is **untouched**. What changed is which inferential act it bites on.

> **Holding is possession. Entertaining is not.**

A label the system *keeps* — across the session boundary, in a profile, as retrievable
knowledge — desecrates exactly as the original paragraph says, displayed or not. A
possibility entertained within the live context, carrying no standing and dying with that
context, is not possession.

⛔ **Silence is explicitly preserved as no defence**, so the narrowing cannot be read as
licensing quiet person-models — which is the failure mode this document exists to prevent.

### B — `MAIA_EPISTEMIC_TONE_SPEC_v1.0.md`

The noticing claim is **upheld**. *"This is not a contradiction"* is superseded **only as a
reconciliation claim** — there was a real contradiction, resolved by ruling rather than by
this section's own assertion. Added:

```
notice ≠ standing
notice ≠ persistence
notice ≠ consequential treatment
notice ≠ member knowledge
```

⭐ And the correction that matters most: **the disclosure safeguards were never sufficient
alone.** A pattern may be offered impeccably and still violate RE-009 if it was persisted,
accumulated, carried across the session boundary, or allowed to shape treatment.

### C — `LONGITUDINAL_MEMORY_CATEGORY_GRADIENT.md`

Two changes plus one clarification. The carve-out is bounded by the event-versus-attribution
test. The surfacing clause stands **for assertion** and no longer forbids **introduction as
a corrigible invitation**. "Non-form" is scoped to durable memory formation, so it does not
read as forbidding ephemeral perception.

⭐ *This was the one cell where the three texts genuinely disagreed, and it was resolved by
ruling — ⛔ not by treating this document as controlling, which would have decided Act 4 by
filing accident.* **Retained intact:** *the system records the recognition, not its own
inference.*

## 4. ⚠️ Recorded, not resolved

**(a) Vocabulary divergence at Act 6 — same answer, different words.** A says *"Invitation
required"* and *"user-confirmed"*; C says *"form-with-consent"* and *"participation in the
naming"*; RE-009 says *"member epistemic act."* All three require a member act, so the gate
passes on **answer**. ⛔ **Not unified here** — unifying the vocabulary across three canon
documents is doctrinal drafting beyond reconciling this collision, and *"invitation"* (the
member invites the system to form) and *"recognition"* (the member performs an act on
something offered) may be genuinely different member acts worth keeping distinct. **Founder
question, not Jarvis's.**

**(b) `observation ≠ interpretation` was NOT promoted.** The event-versus-attribution test is
scoped in the placed canon to the RE-009 **persistence boundary** by an explicit scope note.
The general distinction remains unadjudicated in the ACT 1 register. *This was an explicit
ACT 3 prohibition and the scope note is how it is enforced in the text rather than merely
intended.*

**(c) Nothing was verified against the runtime.** ⛔ ACT 3 did not look at code. The
reconciled canon is now a single authority surface, which is the precondition ACT 4 was
waiting on — and the reason ACT 4 could not honestly have run first.
