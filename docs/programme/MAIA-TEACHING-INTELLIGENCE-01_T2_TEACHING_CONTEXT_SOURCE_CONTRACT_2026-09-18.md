## 1. Why T2 exists

T0 defines what teaching must mean.

T1 defines the grammar of a teaching move.

T2 defines **where MAIA is teaching and what epistemic kind of source she is speaking from**.

This is required because the platform must support materially different teaching contexts without collapsing their authority:

- Writer's Studio — writing craft, rhetoric, structure, revision, editorial practice;
- Coaching — coaching models, inquiry, change processes, reflective practice, systems thinking;
- Therapist/practitioner learning — psychology, psychotherapy models, systems, philosophy, spirituality, consciousness;
- General MAIA — Soullab canon, primers, consciousness education;
- Research Lab — Relational Geometry and other hypothesis-bearing research programmes.
## 2. Closed teaching contexts

`tcs-1` recognizes:

```text
general_maia
writers_studio

therapist_practitioner
research_lab
```

Each is paired with its intended audience:

```text
general_maia          → member

therapist_practitioner→ therapist_practitioner

```

This does not create access control or curriculum. It identifies the epistemic/teaching room for a current teaching proposal.

## 4. Source classes

Every already-available source reference is classified as one of:

```text
soullab_canon
soullab_research
governed_library
practitioner_material
external_academic
external_scientific
external_historical_tradition
external_web_general
maia_synthesis
```

These classes do not inherit one another's authority.

A Soullab research note cannot become canon by relevance.

A spiritual tradition cannot become scientific evidence by synthesis.

A current web page cannot become peer-reviewed evidence by citation formatting.

A practitioner-uploaded method remains practitioner-authored material unless separately governed otherwise.

## 5. Epistemic standings

Source class and epistemic standing are bound:

```text
soullab_canon                  → canonical
soullab_research               → research_hypothesis
governed_library               → governed_reference | canonical | research_hypothesis
practitioner_material          → practitioner_authored | governed_reference
external_academic              → peer_reviewed_evidence
external_scientific            → scientific_reference | peer_reviewed_evidence
external_historical_tradition  → historical_or_traditional
external_web_general           → current_web_reference
maia_synthesis                 → maia_synthesis
```
## 6. Claim classes

T2 distinguishes what kind of claim MAIA is preparing to teach:

```text
canonical_statement
research_hypothesis
source_explanation
evidence_summary
tradition_description
comparative_synthesis
```

The contract enforces minimum support:

- `canonical_statement` requires canonical standing;
- `research_hypothesis` requires research-hypothesis standing;
- `evidence_summary` requires academic/scientific standing;
- `tradition_description` requires historical/traditional standing;
- `comparative_synthesis` requires at least two sources.

Research hypotheses, evidence summaries, and tradition descriptions require citations for every participating source.

## 7. Relational Geometry rule

Relational Geometry currently has explicit research-lane material, including the preliminary note.

T2 therefore makes it structurally possible for MAIA to teach:

> "Within Soullab's current Relational Geometry research..."

while refusing to silently present the same material as settled Soullab canon or established science.

Future research promotion may change source standing only through a separate governance act.
## 8. Writer's Studio

Writer's Studio teaching can later draw from:

- governed writing/editing primers;
- Writer's Studio canonical editorial laws;
- rhetoric and narrative theory;
- historical craft traditions;
- external academic research on writing/reading when relevant;
- MAIA synthesis that remains labeled synthesis.

T2 does not alter manuscript custody or Writer's Studio editorial authority.

It does not let teaching write the author's manuscript.

## 9. Coaching

Coaching teaching can later explain models, compare approaches, teach inquiry skills, change theory, systems thinking, reflective practice, and practitioner craft.

Practitioner materials retain practitioner-authored provenance.

Teaching a practitioner's method never authorizes MAIA to impersonate that practitioner or impose the method on a client.

## 10. Therapist/practitioner learning

Therapist/practitioner teaching may later cover psychology, psychotherapy models, depth/Jungian work, attachment, somatic approaches, systems, phenomenology, philosophy, spirituality, consciousness, and comparative models.

The teaching context is educational.

T2 does not authorize clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, client assessment, or claims about a practitioner's client.

Scientific, historical, spiritual, and Soullab sources remain epistemically distinct.

## 12. Falsification targets

T2 is RED if any of the following becomes possible:

1. Writer's Studio context can masquerade as coaching or therapist teaching.
2. Relational Geometry research can support a `canonical_statement` without canonical standing.
3. A general web source can support an `evidence_summary` as peer-reviewed evidence.
4. A historical/spiritual tradition can be relabeled as scientific evidence.
5. A practitioner-uploaded method silently becomes Soullab canon.
6. Citation-required claims proceed without citations.
7. Comparative synthesis proceeds from a single source.
8. Context/source classification creates browsing, retrieval, teaching execution, or learner-state persistence.
9. Unknown learner-state or clinical-assessment fields enter the contract.
