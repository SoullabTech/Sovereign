## 1. The ruling — three layers, three names

Fixed now, before any build, so that *Elemental Alchemy* and Andrea are not encoded into the thing
Soullab intends to keep and offer for every Work.

| Layer | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Generic product capability | **Reading Room** | The platform capability by which a Work is opened to readers in a bounded, consented space: reader access to the Work, presence of other readers, passages, reflection, gathering |
| Generic methodology / configuration layer | **Reading Group Template** | The architecture of an ongoing group relationship to a Work: shape, cadence, passage sequence, prompts, host role, gathering, reflection surface, MAIA participation class, consent and sharing defaults |
| First configured instance | **Elemental Alchemy Reading Group** | One configuration of the template: this Work, this host (Andrea Fagan), this cadence, this language, these prompts, these practices |

```text
READING ROOM                      (platform capability)
│
├── Reading Group Template        (generic configuration architecture)
│   ├── shape            cohort read-through | ongoing club
│   ├── Work binding     any Work — not Elemental Alchemy
│   ├── passage sequence
│   ├── cadence
│   ├── host role        human
│   ├── prompts
│   ├── gathering
│   ├── reflection surface
│   ├── MAIA participation class
│   ├── consent + sharing defaults
│   └── branding slots
│
└── Instance: Elemental Alchemy Reading Group
    ├── Work            Elemental Alchemy (Kelly Nezat)
    ├── Host            Andrea Fagan
    ├── elemental cadence (Fire · Water · Earth · Air · Aether)
    ├── Andrea's facilitation language and prompts
    └── instance-specific practices
```

**Why "Reading Room" and not "Book Club"**: the capability is not tied to books. A Work in the
Studio may be an essay, a course, a dissertation, or a collection — Master Brief §1. A room can
hold any of them; a book club cannot. **Why "Reading Group Template" and not "Reading Group"**:
the same reason `Coaching Journey Template` is not `Coaching Template` — it names an architecture
for an ongoing relationship over time, not a skin or a worksheet set.

Considered and not chosen (all viable): Reading Circle · Reader's Room · Companion Reading ·
Work Room. `Circle` is rejected as a *name* because `lib/circles` is already a live substrate
noun and overloading it would make every future sentence ambiguous — the Reading Room will very
likely be *built on* Circles (§5), which is exactly why it must not be *called* one.

## 2. Two shapes, both in the template — binding when the lane opens

The founder asked for both. They are not the same object and the template must carry both, or the
club will be built as a degraded cohort and feel like one.

```text
COHORT READ-THROUGH        bounded · cadenced · has a first passage and a last one ·
                           completes · membership closes · host carries the sequence

ONGOING CLUB               open-ended · no completion state · membership opens and closes
                           over time · no required position in the Work · people arrive
                           already having read it, or never finishing it
```

⛔ A club is **not** a cohort with the end removed. Completion, progress, position-in-the-Work and
"where the group is" are cohort concepts. A club that tracks whether you have kept up has
converted a relationship into an obligation, which is attachment capture by scheduling
(`MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`). The template holds shape as a first-class field; capabilities
that only make sense for one shape are gated on it, not shown greyed-out to the other.

## 3. Placement — where this lives, and what that costs

The founder's instruction is *"we would want this in the Writer's Studio."* Read against Master
Brief §2 (product grammar, binding), that instruction is honored by splitting authoring from
inhabiting:

```text
WRITER'S STUDIO   the author opens a Reading Room from their Work, configures the
                  template, selects passages, sets the host, sets MAIA participation.
                  This is Stage 15 EXPRESSION — one Work, many expressions, with the
                  developmental history still attached.

MEMBER SURFACE    readers inhabit the room. Reflection, gathering, discussion and
                  presence live where member relational surfaces already live.
```

**Ruling**: the Reading Room is an **expression of a Work, authored from Writer's Studio and
inhabited outside it**. Writer's Studio does not acquire a second community application inside
its Canvas. `Navigator | Easel | Context/MAIA` remains stances of the same Work (Master Brief §2),
not a shell for a social product.

⛔ **This is Stage 15 on the roadmap** (`WRITERS_STUDIO_ROADMAP_STAGE_6_TO_15.md`), and the
roadmap authorizes nothing. Stages 7–14 sit between here and there. **The Elemental Alchemy
Reading Group therefore does not have to wait for Stage 15** — see §6 — but the *generic Reading
Room capability inside Writer's Studio* does. Confusing the two is the drift this ruling exists
to prevent.

## 4. Constitutional constraints — fixed now, binding when the lane opens

### C1 — The group is Developmental Ecology, not a rung
Per `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md` (Invariant 16), a reading group is the *relational
medium* — Personal Field / Relationships / Co-Labs / Practice Fields — not a layer of authority.
Authority moves upward only, through authored experience: a member's **Encounter** with the text →
their own **Reflection** → their own **Recognition**. The group may not manufacture higher-order
meaning about a member. The member may loop freely; the system may not.

### C2 — MAIA does not host, facilitate, or synthesize the group
Andrea is the host. The host role is **human** and is typed as human in the template. MAIA may
hold a member's own reading (their passages, their reflections, their questions) under the
existing consent model. MAIA may **not**: summarize the group into meaning, aggregate members'
reflections into a shared insight, characterize the group's state, or narrate a member's
development to the group. Cross-member synthesis is the exact failure mode Invariant 16 forbids,
and it is also how a reading group becomes a surveillance surface.

### C3 — Reflection is default-private; sharing is a member act
No stealth publication of a reflection to a group. This mirrors the atoms `return_preference`
model and the Daily Anchor `surface_preference` gate (2026-07-03): eligibility to appear in group
view originates in a member act, never in a deploy flag or a host setting.

### C4 — The host cannot read what the member has not shared
A host configuring the room is not thereby granted a view of members' private reading. Host
capability is bounded to what the room publishes, and this is a Co-Lab boundary question, not a
UI question (§5).

### C5 — No progress obligation, no attendance record shown back to the member
A club has no completion state (§2). Even in a cohort, position-in-the-Work is a member's own
orientation aid, not a compliance signal surfaced to the host or the group.

### C6 — Kelly is the author of the Work being read
The founder authoring the Work *and* owning the platform *and* participating in the group is a
concentration of relational power, not a convenience. The template must let the author be absent
from a room opened on their own Work, and the instance must record which role Kelly is in.

## 7. Ownership — Andrea's contribution is not automatically Soullab's

Kelly authored the Work, so the *text* raises no third-party question. **Andrea's facilitation
material does.** Her session structure, prompts, sequencing, and language are hers unless
established otherwise, under the same discipline governing the Larry corpus
(`COACHING-TEMPLATE-EXTRACTION-01_NAMING_RULING_2026-09-04.md` §4).

| Provenance | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Room/membership/consent architecture developed by Soullab | → Reading Room / Reading Group Template |
| Andrea's prompts, facilitation sequence, session language, supplied materials | → stays in the Elemental Alchemy instance unless clearly licensed otherwise |
| Ambiguous | → **classify before generalizing.** Not moved into the template until classified |

Worked trap: generalizing Andrea's five-gathering elemental sequence into a "default cadence" in
the template does not make it Soullab IP. Whether the *cadence architecture* originated in the
platform or in her facilitation practice is answered per item, in the lane.

**Owed before the instance runs with real readers**: an explicit understanding with Andrea about
role, contribution, and what may be generalized. Not a legal instrument at this stage — a written
agreement in plain language, so that §7 is not being decided retroactively later.

## 8. Growth-obligation check (`RECIPROCAL_SOVEREIGNTY_INTENTION_2026-08-04.md`)

This capability increases reach: it puts members in relation to each other around a Work.

- **What uncertainty does this introduce, and how is it preserved?** Whether a shared reflection
  means what the group takes it to mean. Preserved by C2: MAIA never resolves that uncertainty on
  the group's behalf, and no aggregate reading of the group is produced.
- **What provenance and ownership boundaries does this require?** Every visible reflection carries
  its member author and its sharing act (C3). Instance material carries its contributor (§7).
- **What new responsibility does this capability create?** A host now holds relational power over
  readers, and the platform owner is also the author (C6). The template must make the host role
  explicitly bounded rather than implicitly total.
