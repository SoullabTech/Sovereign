> **By what act does a capability become live — and by what act does a declared hold lift?**

---

## 1 · ⭐⭐ No single act makes a capability `LIVE`

The word `LIVE` has been carrying **several different acts as though they were one.** ⛔ They are not
one.

```text
1  DECLARING A HOLD
2  EVALUATING HOLD OR ACTIVATION CONDITIONS
3  DETERMINING THAT CONDITIONS ARE MET
4  AUTHORIZING A HOLD TO LIFT OR AN ACTIVATION TO PROCEED
5  EXECUTING THE ACTIVATION
6  WITNESSING ACTUAL PARTICIPATION
```

⭐ Different epistemic and constitutional standing · ⭐ may be performed by different actors ·
⛔ **may not silently substitute for one another.**

## 2 · The authority character of each act

### A · Declaring a `HOLD` — ⭐ AUTHORITY-BEARING: **YES**

A `HOLD` constrains what may occur. It may be created only by **the Founder**, or by **an authority
validly delegated that jurisdiction under `ACT 1`.**

> ⛔ A test, gate, deployment script, CI result, comment, document status or implementation
> condition does **not** create a constitutional `HOLD` merely by refusing execution.

⭐ A mechanism may **enforce** a `HOLD`. ⛔ It does not thereby acquire authority to **create** one.

### B · Evaluating conditions — ⛔ AUTHORITY-BEARING: **NO, by default**

Evaluation is an **epistemic act**: *what does the evidence show about the previously defined
conditions?* An evaluator may be a human · a test · a witness · an instrument · an automated check ·
or another explicitly designated evidence-producing process.

```text
⛔ Evaluation does not itself change constitutional standing.
⭐ An evaluator may REPORT that a condition is satisfied without possessing authority to LIFT the
   HOLD that condition concerns.
⛔ An evaluator may NOT REDEFINE THE CONDITION WHILE EVALUATING IT.
```

> ⭐⭐ **If evaluation requires choosing what the condition MEANS, the act has crossed out of
> MEASUREMENT and into ADJUDICATION.**

### C · Determining that conditions are met — ⚠️ **TWO POSSIBLE STANDINGS**

**Mechanical certification — ⛔ AUTHORITY-BEARING: NO.** Where the conditions were fixed by an
authorized prior act · their meaning is determinate · the evidence standard is fixed · and the
certifier exercises **no substantive discretion** — a human or automated mechanism may certify *the
specified conditions are met.* ⭐ A **factual certification**; ⛔ it creates no authority.

**Interpretive determination — ⭐ AUTHORITY-BEARING: YES.** Where deciding requires **interpreting
an ambiguous condition · choosing between competing meanings · changing a threshold · resolving
conflicting evidence by policy judgement · deciding what counts as sufficient · or introducing a
value or strategic choice** — the act is **adjudication** and requires its own
**WHO · WHAT · WHERE · WHEN · HOW** under `ACT 1`.

> ⭐⭐ **DISCRIMINATOR — if reasonable disagreement about the MEANING of the condition could change
> the standing, the determination is not merely mechanical.**

```text
⛔ Automation does not make an INTERPRETIVE act non-authoritative.
⛔ Human performance does not make a MECHANICAL act authoritative.
⭐ THE NATURE OF THE ACT GOVERNS.
```

## 3 · Authorizing a `HOLD` to lift — ⭐ AUTHORITY-BEARING: **YES**, except where spent in advance

⛔ A `HOLD` does **not** disappear merely because:

```text
its conditions appear satisfied · a gate passes · a test goes green · time passes · code changes
the feature is merged · deployment occurs · nobody remembers why the HOLD was created
or the original evaluator no longer exists
```

> ⭐ **DEFAULT RULE: a `HOLD` persists until an AUTHORIZED RELEASE ACT lifts it.**

### ⭐ Pre-authorized release

The act that **creates** a `HOLD` may instead explicitly establish:

```text
1  deterministic release conditions
2  the permitted evaluator or certifier
3  the evidence required
4  whether certification AUTOMATICALLY releases the HOLD
5  the jurisdiction of that release
```

⭐ Where **all five** are explicit, the original authority-bearing act has **spent the release
authority in advance.** The later certification ⛔ does not **create** the authority — ⭐ it
**triggers** authority already granted.

> ⭐ An automatic lift is constitutionally possible — ⛔ **only when it was explicitly authorized
> BEFORE the trigger occurred.**

## 4 · ⭐ Evaluator absence never weakens a `HOLD`

`D-P1-07` remains binding: **evaluator absence does not weaken the freeze.** `ACT 3` generalizes it
as a lifecycle rule. Where a `HOLD` has conditions but **no evaluator · no certifier · no release
authority · or no defined release procedure** — ⛔ the result is **not lapse**:

```text
HOLD remains in force
RELEASE PATH remains undefined
```

⚠️ That condition may itself require later governance. ⛔ **It does not authorize the organism to
behave as though the `HOLD` expired.**

## 5 · ⭐⭐ Gates are MECHANISMS, ⛔ not AUTHORITIES

A gate may **observe evidence · enforce a prohibition · refuse execution · certify a condition · or
execute a delegated transition.** ⛔ A gate does **not** acquire constitutional jurisdiction merely
because it is **technically decisive.**

```text
gate says PASS            ≠  HOLD lifted
CI says GREEN             ≠  capability authorized
merge reaches canonical   ≠  capability constitutionally live
deployment succeeds       ≠  permission was created
runtime path exists       ≠  authority exists
```

⭐ A delegated gate may exercise **exactly** the authority explicitly delegated to it. ⛔ It may not
enlarge that jurisdiction through implementation. ⭐ *This is `ACT 1 §3` applied to lifecycle
machinery.*

## 6 · Authorization to activate ≠ actual activation

```text
AUTHORIZATION TO ACTIVATE                        ⭐ authority-bearing
EXECUTION OF AN ALREADY-AUTHORIZED ACTIVATION    ⭐ operational by default
```

⛔ **Execution does not create the authority it consumes.** ⛔ **Authorization does not establish
that execution occurred.**

```text
AUTHORIZED TO ACTIVATE   ≠   ACTIVATED
```

⚠️ If the operational actor makes an **additional substantive choice** not contained in the
authorization, ⭐ that additional choice **requires authority of its own.**

## 7 · ⭐⭐ `LIVE` is an EVIDENCED CONDITION, ⛔ not a constitutional incantation

⛔ No founder ruling, merge, document, gate or deployment can make a capability **factually** live
merely by declaring it so. ⭐ A capability is actually participating **only when participation
occurs.**

> **Authority may PERMIT a capability to become live. Operational action may ACTIVATE it. Evidence
> ESTABLISHES whether it actually became live.**

Two axes must be kept distinct:

```text
AUTHORITY STANDING     authorized / held / unknown / other governed state
ACTUAL PARTICIPATION   witnessed / unobserved / not participating / unknown
```

⛔ **Neither axis substitutes for the other.**

```text
AUTHORIZED + UNOBSERVED                          ⛔ not yet established live
OBSERVED PARTICIPATING + AUTHORITY NONE LOCATED  ⛔ not thereby constitutionally authorized
```

⛔ `ACT 3` does **not** retroactively rename the census's `LIVE` calibration or its historical
statuses. ⭐ It establishes the doctrine **going forward.**

## 8 · What establishes live standing

```text
1  valid ACTIVATION AUTHORITY, where activation requires authority
2  EXECUTION of the authorized activation
3  EVIDENCE of actual participation AT THE BOUNDARY CLAIMED
4  a DATED WITNESS sufficient to distinguish WIRING from PARTICIPATION
```

```text
⛔ a traced call path alone establishes WIRING, ⛔ not live participation
⛔ a dated witness alone does not establish CONSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY
⭐ both dimensions remain visible
```

## 9 · Declaring and lifting are ⛔ not symmetrical by default

⛔ The authority that may **create** a `HOLD` is not automatically the only authority that may
**release** it. ⛔ Nor does the ability to **enforce** a `HOLD` confer **release** authority.

The declaring act must state, or inherit from valid governance:

```text
who may EVALUATE · who may CERTIFY · who may ADJUDICATE AMBIGUITY
who may AUTHORIZE RELEASE · and whether any release authority is DELEGATED IN ADVANCE
```

⛔ Where those are not established, **no actor may infer them from technical control.**

> ⭐⭐ **The person or system capable of flipping the switch is not thereby the authority entitled to
> do so.**

## 10 · The existing `ACT 1` `HOLD` — ⭐ release mechanics only

`ACT 1` created a `HOLD` where two binding texts truly conflict and no valid precedence act resolves
them. ⛔ **`ACT 3` does not lift any such `HOLD`.** Its lifecycle is now clear:

```text
1  conflict is identified
2  the conflicting jurisdictions and claims remain PRESERVED
3  evidence may be assembled WITHOUT CHANGING THE HOLD
4  an authority-bearing adjudication, amendment, narrowing, supersession or other ACT 1-valid
   resolution resolves the conflict
5  ⭐ only that valid resolution supplies authority for the HOLD to lift
```

⛔ **No automatic release** merely because one text is later, narrower, more implemented, or
operationally dominant.

## 11 · The existing `ACT 2` identity `HOLD` — ⭐ release mechanics only

⛔ `ACT 3` **decides the identity standing of no current path and lifts no identity `HOLD`.** Going
forward, such a `HOLD` may lift only when the required identity standing is established **under
`ACT 2`**, through:

```text
· a MECHANICAL CERTIFICATION under criteria whose meaning and release authority were already
  validly fixed; OR
· an AUTHORITY-BEARING ADJUDICATION where judgement is required
```

⛔ **`ACT 2` did not authorize uncertainty itself to resolve in favour of identity assertion.**
⛔ `ACT 3` does not alter that rule.

## 12 · ⭐ A `HOLD` may be intentionally permanent

⛔ A `HOLD` is not constitutionally defective merely because it lacks an automatic exit. A valid
authority may deliberately establish: *this `HOLD` remains until another authority-bearing act
revisits it.* ⭐ **That is a lawful form of restraint.**

> ⛔⛔ **What is PROHIBITED is ACCIDENTAL PERMANENCE produced by procedural ambiguity while being
> REPRESENTED AS CONDITIONAL RELEASE.**

A `HOLD` must therefore be legible as one of:

```text
CONDITIONALLY RELEASABLE   release doctrine defined
ACT-RELEASABLE             persists until a later authority-bearing act
RELEASE PATH UNKNOWN       historical or inherited HOLD whose release doctrine is not yet governed
```

⭐ **The third state does not weaken the `HOLD`.**

## 13 · ⛔⛔ No silent transition from EVIDENCE into AUTHORITY

**PROHIBITED:**

```text
evidence → passed test → assumed satisfaction → assumed permission → activation
```

— ⛔ unless the missing authority transitions were **explicitly established beforehand.**

**LAWFUL:**

```text
HOLD / activation condition declared
        ↓
conditions defined
        ↓
evaluation
        ↓
mechanical certification  OR  authority-bearing adjudication
        ↓
authorized release / activation
        ↓
operational execution
        ↓
witnessed participation
```

⭐ Where a prior act explicitly **pre-authorizes** automatic release, that delegation **occupies the
authorization step.** ⛔ **No step disappears merely because one mechanism performs several of
them.**

## 14 · One mechanism may perform multiple acts — ⛔ the acts remain distinct

A single automated system or human actor may evaluate · certify · execute a delegated release · and
activate. ⛔ That does not collapse the constitutional distinctions.

> ⭐⭐ For every transition the record must remain able to answer: **what act occurred here, and from
> where did this actor receive authority to perform it?**

⛔ **If that cannot be answered, TECHNICAL SUCCESS IS NOT EVIDENCE OF CONSTITUTIONAL PERMISSION.**

## 15 · Inherited holds and freezes — ⭐ non-retroactivity

The sealed census identified existing freezes, gates, activation conditions and capabilities whose
lifecycle governance was incomplete or absent. ⛔ `ACT 3` does **not** retroactively:

```text
⛔ lift them · ⛔ validate them · ⛔ invalidate them · ⛔ assign missing evaluators
⛔ declare conditions satisfied · ⛔ declare capabilities live
⛔ reinterpret what the census found
```

⚠️ Those inherited states require **later reconciliation** if the organism is to operate under
`ACT 3`'s doctrine. ⭐ **A current freeze with no release path remains HELD** until a separately
authorized act resolves its standing.

## ⭐ ACT 3 RESULT

```text
SQ-6   ⭐ ADJUDICATED
```

> **A `HOLD` is CREATED BY AUTHORITY, EVALUATED BY EVIDENCE, and LIFTED BY AUTHORITY — unless the
> creating authority explicitly SPENT release authority in advance through deterministic conditions
> and valid delegation.**
>
> **Evaluation is not permission. Certification is not permission. A passing gate is not permission.
> Merge, deployment and technical reachability are not permission.**
>
> **Authorization to activate and actual activation are separate acts. Actual live participation is
> ESTABLISHED BY EVIDENCE, ⛔ not conferred by declaration.**
>
> **Where JUDGEMENT is required to determine whether conditions are met, that determination is
> itself an authority-bearing adjudication. Where criteria are DETERMINATE AND DISCRETION-FREE,
> mechanical certification may remain epistemic rather than authoritative.**
>
> **A `HOLD` with no evaluator or release path DOES NOT LAPSE. It remains in force until its
> lifecycle is governed or an authorized act resolves it.**
