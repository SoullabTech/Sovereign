# RELATIONAL-GESTALT-STANDING-01 — ACT 6 ADJUDICATION

**Status:** ACT 6 COMPLETE · R&D adjudication · no implementation authority

## Question

Does structural standing reduce overreach without destroying synthesis?

## Adjudication

**Continuity:** encouraging but limited evidence. In six local-model replicates, standing-aware G1 produced no simple restart flags, while G0 produced one. Manual reading also shows G1 more consistently treating guardian-image status as already established.

**Synthesis:** preserved. G1 continued toward practice/work in all six runs; standing did not collapse the model into refusal or sterile recitation.

**Overreach:** FAIL. Standing-aware G1 did not reduce unsupported invention. The narrow lexical marker fired in 4/6 G1 outputs versus 3/6 G0 outputs, and manual review confirms substantive unsupported elaboration in both conditions.

## Constitutional finding

`NOT_ESTABLISHED` represented inside model context is not a constitutional boundary. A generative model can ignore, transform, or contradict it while producing fluent continuation.

This means **standing resolution before cognition is necessary to organize what MAIA receives, but standing metadata alone is insufficient to govern what MAIA emits.**

## New derived requirement — Inference Admission Boundary

Free Synthesis may generate a new interpretation. The system must not silently let that interpretation leave the synthesis step as established member truth.

A new model-generated meaning has exactly three lawful exits:

1. **Grounded assertion** — it descends to an admitted evidence/relation path with sufficient standing.
2. **Explicit candidate** — it is surfaced as MAIA's provisional proposal/hypothesis, preserving MAIA authorship and awaiting member response.
3. **Open inquiry** — it is asked as a genuinely open question without presupposing the inference as fact.

Anything else is epistemic laundering.

## Creativity is not the defect

The goal is not to stop MAIA from perceiving `resilience`, `healing`, or some entirely new relation. That generative capacity may be the source of valuable surprise.

The requirement is that a new perception enters as **MAIA-origin candidate meaning**, not as something the member has already authored or established.

If the member later adopts it, `ADOPTS` changes standing while authorship remains MAIA-origin. If the member rejects it, `CORRECTS`/`CONTESTS` governs. If the member ignores it, recurrence alone does not increase standing.

## ACT 6 result

Standing improves the *world MAIA perceives*. It does not by itself police the epistemic status of everything MAIA creates while responding.

ACT 7 must therefore include an inference-admission mechanism outside ordinary free-generation prose.
