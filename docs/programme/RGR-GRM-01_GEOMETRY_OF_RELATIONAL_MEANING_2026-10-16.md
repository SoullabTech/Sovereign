### Governing proposition

The RGR-00 constitutional law governs:

> **Relation is the object of inquiry; geometry is a candidate language, not a predetermined answer.**

The candidate thesis is intentionally weaker than an ontological definition:

> **For specified tasks, some aspects of relational meaning may be better represented by structures that remain compatible across admissible changes of viewpoint and expression than by isolated semantic-point similarity alone.**

"Meaning is what survives a transformation of perspective" is a **hypothesis to delimit and challenge**, not a theorem or an assertion about the whole of human meaning. Some transformations change meaning; other comparisons need not share a coordinate system; the relevant class of admissible maps must be fixed for each experiment.

### Continuity with RGR-02: ten primitive headings, no hidden eleventh

Keep the accepted typed contextual relational scaffold `(S,R,T,Q,C,P,Γ,B,K,O)` for state, relation, transformation, equivalence/invariant, context, perspective, trajectory, boundary, compatibility and obstruction. Geometry is an **optional enrichment** justified for a defined task. Do not relabel ordinary graph connectivity as a manifold, disagreement as sheaf cohomology, obstruction as psychological resistance, or correlation as identity.

- **Local perspective**: a source-specific partial state or constrained space, not a claim that a person's entire perspective is a formal mathematical stalk.
- **Perspective map**: an explicitly defined translation/restriction with known domain, codomain, direction, failure conditions, and information loss. Not all two-perspective relationships admit a map.
- **Coherence**: a test-specific gluing/compatibility predicate on the declared local records, distinct from agreement, personal truth, endorsement, or trust.
- **Invariant**: a quantity or proposition fixed under the selected admissible transformations, never merely a recurring label.
- **Boundary**: a rule concerning admissible interpretations/transitions, including "do not infer", refusal, privacy, and undecidability. A boundary is not automatically an obstruction.
- **Subspace/Grassmann**: a candidate representation only where ambient features, rank, uncertainty, and transformations are operationalized; `Gr(k,n)` presumes fixed `k,n` and an identified inner-product structure.
- **Flow**: `FLOW_D`, first-class *derived* dynamical object built from existing headings, not primitive #11. Its carrier must be typed. No universal conservation of attention, affect, care or meaning follows.
- **Weather**: candidate contextual modulation; no new coordinate or measured human field is asserted.
- **Elements/Aether**: candidate interpretive/transformational modes, deliberately *excluded* from initial target generation and success labels.

### An explicitly provisional modelling view

For a synthetic episode `E`, we may define local views `V_i(E)`, locally admissible descriptions `L_i`, and declared compatibility maps `ρ_{ij}`. An exact assignment model permits a global description only if an assignment satisfies all constraints after the declared translations. A research question is whether this local-to-global structure improves a held-out relational task. It is **not** yet a faithful sheaf model of human meaning, a proof that human narratives admit global sections, or a physiological model.

### Separation of claims

Mathematical local/global compatibility in a finite toy world is proof only **under that toy world's declared rules**. A behavioral experiment could test relation-sensitive transfer, but it would not establish internal representational reorganization, phenomenological consciousness, therapeutic efficacy, or any universal ontology. Member reports are authoritative as their own accounts; AIN must not turn a model inference into an authored report or independent claim about a third person.
