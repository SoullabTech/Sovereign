# 1 · ⭐⭐ TWO CONSTITUTIONAL QUESTIONS — ⛔ neither may hide inside the other

```text
Q1   MAY MAIA ORIGINATE a relationship between members?

Q2   IF IT MAY — WHAT AUTHORITY AND DUTY PERSIST AFTER THE INTRODUCTION OCCURS?
```

⚠️ **`Q2` is not a detail of `Q1`.** ⭐ A founder may permit origination and rule the after-standing
narrowly, or permit it only where after-standing is bounded, ⛔ or answer `Q1` `NO` and leave `Q2`
unreached. ⛔ **An answer to `Q1` that silently settles `Q2` has decided two questions under one
act's authority.**

# 2 · ⭐⭐ The chain the ruling must not collapse

```text
INTRODUCING
  ≠ RECOMMENDING
      ≠ DISCLOSING
          ≠ MATCHING
              ≠ CONNECTING
                  ≠ CONTINUING TO MEDIATE
                      ≠ GOVERNING THE RELATIONSHIP
```

⭐ **Seven acts, ⛔ not one act described seven ways.** ⚠️ A founder may permit some and refuse others,
and may attach **different authority and different duty** to each.

⚠️ **The chain crosses a boundary partway along.** `RECOMMENDING` and `DISCLOSING` are acts toward
**one member**; `INTRODUCING` and beyond bring **a second person into standing.** ⛔ Where exactly
that crossing lies is **for the ruling**, ⛔ not assumed by the ordering above.

# 3 · Questions the ruling must hold apart

```text
1  ORIGINATION     Is CAUSING a relationship to exist different from ENABLING members who would
                   have found each other anyway?

2  BASIS           On what may an introduction rest? ⚠️ member declaration · organism-derived
                   representation · both · neither. ⭐ ACT 4 §8 already refuses relational authority
                   arising from inference alone — ⛔ but does not say what MAY ground an introduction.

3  CONSENT         From WHOM, and WHEN? ⚠️ before the basis is formed · before the introduction ·
                   from BOTH members · revocably. ⛔ ACT 6 §10: consent cannot create jurisdiction.

4  DISCLOSURE      ⚠️ Must a member know MAIA ORIGINATED the connection — and must each know what
                   was represented of them to the other? ⭐ ACT 5 governs the representation; ⛔ the
                   FACT OF ORIGINATION is not itself a representation about a member.

5  AFTER-STANDING  Does MAIA's standing END at introduction, PERSIST, or CHANGE KIND?  ⭐ This is Q2.

6  ASYMMETRY       ⚠️ May MAIA hold different standing toward the two members — the one it knows
                   well and the one it does not?

7  DISSOLUTION     ⚠️ What happens to MAIA's standing when the relationship ends, or one member
                   withdraws, blocks or objects?

8  NEGATIVE CASE   ⭐ If MAIA may NOT originate, what remains permitted? ⛔ A prohibition that cannot
                   say what it leaves standing is not yet a boundary.
```
