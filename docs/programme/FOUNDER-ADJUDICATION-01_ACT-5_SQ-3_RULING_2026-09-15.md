> **May the organism hold — or let another person hold — a representation of a member, or take an
> act concerning a member, that the member cannot see, dispute, or erase?**

---

## 1 · ⭐⭐ A member has constitutional standing toward consequential representations

⭐ The organism **may reason** about a member · **may form provisional interpretations** · **may hold
information required for cognition and operation.**

⚠️ But where a representation concerning a member becomes **durable, consequential, disclosed, or
governing**, ⭐ **the member acquires constitutional standing toward that representation.**

> ⭐⭐ **The organism may not create a CONSEQUENTIAL HIDDEN TRUTH about a member and then GOVERN THE
> MEMBER FROM BEHIND IT.**

⛔ Member standing and system cognition are **not opposites.** ⭐ The boundary lies between
**reasoning about a person** and **giving a representation POWER concerning that person.**

## 2 · The seven positions remain distinct

```text
HELD ABOUT A MEMBER  ≠  SHOWN TO THE MEMBER  ≠  DISPUTABLE BY THE MEMBER
  ≠  REVISABLE BY THE MEMBER  ≠  ERASABLE BY THE MEMBER  ≠  USABLE BY THE ORGANISM
      ≠  DISCLOSABLE TO ANOTHER HUMAN
```

⛔ Permission or standing at one position does not silently grant another.

```text
may HOLD     ≠ may USE          may DISPUTE  ≠ may ERASE      may DISCLOSE ≠ recipient acquires
may SHOW     ≠ may DISCLOSE     may RETAIN   ≠ may GOVERN                    authority
```

## 3 · What counts as a member-relevant representation

A representation **whose semantic content concerns the member** and which is capable of being
**retained · reused · surfaced later · supplied to another cognitive process · disclosed to another
human · used to rank or prioritize · used to determine access, treatment, visibility or relational
stance · or otherwise used consequentially** concerning the member.

It may take the form of a **claim · summary · score · classification · inferred trait ·
developmental representation · relational interpretation · trust assessment · symbolic conclusion ·
profile · status · ranking** or another semantically person-level state.

> ⭐⭐ **Its IMPLEMENTATION FORM does not determine whether it is a representation.**

⛔ A representation does not cease to be one because it is encoded as a **score · flag · vector ·
rank · metadata · distributed state · derived field · or several values whose COMBINED USE carries
person-level meaning.**

⭐ **A FRESH RULING within `SQ-3`'s jurisdiction.** ⛔ **Not an extension by analogy** from a rule
about directly visible text.

## 4 · ⭐ Ephemeral cognition is not automatically a durable member representation

A provisional inference may remain **internal to an immediate reasoning act** where it is **not
durably retained as a person-level claim · not disclosed to another human · not promoted into
standing about the member · does not independently determine later treatment · and does not survive
as a consequential representation beyond the bounded cognitive act.**

⭐ `ACT 4` governs its epistemic standing. ⛔ `ACT 5` does **not** require every fleeting inference to
become a member-facing record.

> ⭐ **The member has standing toward CONSEQUENTIAL REPRESENTATION, ⛔ not toward every transient
> computational event.**

## 5 · Visibility

Where the organism **retains or consequentially uses** a member-relevant representation, ⭐ **the
member must have a meaningful way to know that the representation exists and what standing it
carries** — ⚠️ except where a **separately valid binding rule** lawfully limits disclosure.

**Meaningful visibility requires enough for the member to understand:**

```text
· that the representation EXISTS
· what it MATERIALLY SAYS OR SIGNIFIES concerning them
· its EPISTEMIC ORIGIN
· whether it is evidence · inference · conclusion · another person's assertion ·
  member-adopted claim · or another governed standing
· whether it is BEING USED CONSEQUENTIALLY
```

⛔ **It does NOT require:** raw database rows · internal embeddings · source code · hidden
chain-of-thought · model internals · security secrets · another person's private mental process · or
technical representations that add no meaningful person-level information.

> ⭐⭐ **The constitutional requirement is LEGIBILITY OF STANDING, ⛔ not EXPOSURE OF MACHINERY.**

## 6 · ⭐⭐ Derived visibility counts

⛔ **The organism may not satisfy this doctrine FORMALLY while defeating it FUNCTIONALLY.**

If a person-level representation **materially governs what happens to a member**, the fact that its
content is distributed among **metadata · ordering · counts · hidden scores · latency ·
recommendation behaviour · eligibility · notifications · withdrawal-by-difference** or other derived
effects ⛔ **does not remove the member's standing toward it.**

⭐ **A FRESH RULING.** `ACT 1 §5` ⛔ does not permit a rule concerning **direct** visibility to spread
here **by adjacency**; ⭐ `ACT 5` **expressly governs derived consequential representation** within
`SQ-3`'s jurisdiction.

> ⛔⛔ **The organism may not hide consequential person-level standing by ENCODING IT INDIRECTLY.**

## 7 · Dispute

⭐ A member has standing to **dispute** a durable or consequential representation concerning them.

⛔ Dispute does **not** mean the member automatically determines historical fact. ⭐ It means the
organism must be able to preserve **the representation · the member's disagreement · the provenance
of each · and the CHANGED STANDING created by the dispute.**

> ⭐⭐ **A disputed system conclusion may not continue to masquerade as an undisputed member fact.**

⚠️ Where **another human authored** the representation, the member's dispute ⛔ does not erase that
person's authorship or force a retraction — ⭐ **but it does alter what the ORGANISM may claim about
the representation's standing.**

## 8 · Revision and correction

A member **must be able to correct** representations purporting to state **what the member said ·
what the member chose · what the member adopted · or objective member-supplied information** when
those are inaccurate. ⭐ A member may also supply **revision, context or correction** concerning
system-derived conclusions.

> ⭐⭐ **REVISION DOES NOT REWRITE PROVENANCE.**

```text
⛔ UNLAWFUL HISTORY   "the member always said Y"
⭐ LAWFUL HISTORY     "MAIA concluded X; the member disputed or revised the representation with Y"
```

⭐ **History remains attributable.**

## 9 · Adoption

`ACT 4` established that **member adoption is distinct from system inference.** ⭐ `ACT 5` now rules
**the minimum standing required for adoption:**

> ⭐⭐ **A member cannot constitutionally ADOPT a representation they have not been given MEANINGFUL
> ACCESS TO as a representation concerning them.**

```text
⛔ SILENCE is not adoption            ⛔ NON-OBJECTION is not adoption
⛔ CONTINUED PARTICIPATION is not     ⛔ FAILURE TO DISCOVER A HIDDEN REPRESENTATION is not
   adoption                             adoption
```

⭐ Member adoption requires **an act whose object is sufficiently legible that the member can
meaningfully adopt, reject, revise or decline it.** ⛔ `ACT 5` prescribes no UI or consent gesture
for that act.

## 10 · ⭐ Erasure is not one thing

**Literal deletion** and **constitutional removal from active standing** are distinct.

### A · Erasure from active person-level standing

Where a representation is **system-generated · no longer lawfully required for an independent
binding purpose · and not needed to preserve another person's legitimate authorship or an immutable
accountability record** — ⭐ the member may require that it **cease to operate as an active
person-level representation.** That may include literal deletion where appropriate.

> ⭐⭐ **The constitutional requirement is STRONGER than byte deletion: the organism must CEASE
> TREATING the representation AS ACTIVE TRUTH concerning the member.**

### B · Retention without active authority

Retention may be validly required by **accountability · provenance · security · another person's
authorship · a valid legal or governance obligation · or another independently binding
jurisdiction.** Where valid:

```text
RETAINED  ≠  ACTIVE  ≠  CONFIRMED  ≠  USABLE WITHOUT LIMIT
```

⭐ The retained representation must remain **distinguishable from active person-level standing.**

> ⭐⭐ **The INABILITY TO DELETE a record does not create AUTHORITY TO CONTINUE GOVERNING the member
> by it.**

## 11 · ⭐ The member has no absolute power to erase another person's authorship

`SQ-3` **explicitly includes** representations another human may hold. `ACT 1 §5` prevents a rule
about **the organism** holding from silently extending to **another human** by adjacency — ⭐ so
`ACT 5` **rules this territory expressly.**

⭐ Another human may **form and retain their own perception, interpretation or authored statement**
concerning a member. ⛔ The member does **not** acquire constitutional power to **erase another
person's memory · rewrite another person's authorship · compel agreement with the member's account ·
or falsely attribute the other person's statement to themselves.**

⚠️ **But once the organism STORES · INDEXES · TRANSMITS · OPERATIONALIZES · GIVES STANDING TO · or
USES that representation, the organism's handling of it enters `ACT 5`'s jurisdiction.**

> ⭐⭐ **`ACT 5` governs WHAT THE ORGANISM MAY DO with another person's representation; ⛔ it does not
> govern WHAT ANOTHER HUMAN IS PERMITTED PRIVATELY TO THINK.**

## 12 · ⛔ A hidden human-authored representation may not silently govern the member

⭐ Non-disclosure of a source may sometimes be legitimate. ⛔ **Non-disclosure does not create
unlimited authority.**

Where the organism uses another person's representation to make a **consequential determination**
concerning the member:

```text
· the SOURCE must remain ATTRIBUTABLE
· the representation's STANDING must remain DISTINCT FROM MEMBER FACT
· the AUTHORITY for consequential use must be LOCATABLE under ACT 1 and ACT 4
· the member must have standing toward THE CONSEQUENCE and toward THE PERSON-LEVEL CLAIM
  to the extent constitutionally possible
```

> ⭐⭐ **A protected source may remain protected. ⛔ A HIDDEN GOVERNING TRUTH MAY NOT ARISE MERELY
> FROM THAT PROTECTION.**

## 13 · ⭐⭐ Consequential use requires member standing — the direct answer to the centre question

> ⛔ **The organism may not constitutionally USE a durable or consequential representation
> concerning a member TO GOVERN THAT MEMBER while giving the member NO STANDING toward the
> representation.**

At minimum, consequential use requires a **governed answer** to:

```text
1  what the representation IS                   5  whether the member can DISPUTE it
2  WHOSE representation it is                   6  how DISPUTE CHANGES ITS STANDING
3  what EPISTEMIC STANDING it carries           7  what AUTHORITY permits the organism to use it
4  whether the member can know its MATERIAL        consequentially
   CONTENT OR EFFECT
```

⛔ This does **not** mean member agreement is required for every lawful consequential act. ⭐ It
means:

> ⭐⭐ **Consequential authority may not depend upon MAKING THE MEMBER CONSTITUTIONALLY ABSENT from
> the representation that governs them.**

## 14 · ⛔ Member disagreement does not automatically veto every consequence

⭐ Member standing is **not identical to member control over every outcome.** A member may dispute a
representation while another binding jurisdiction still authorizes a consequence — **preserve
evidence · enforce a safety boundary · refuse an action · protect another person's standing** or
another independently governed act.

⛔ `ACT 5` does not give the member **automatic supremacy over every jurisdiction.** ⭐ `ACT 1`
remains governing, and where jurisdictions conflict **`ACT 1`'s conflict doctrine applies.**

> ⭐ **The member's standing must remain VISIBLE even where the member does not control the ultimate
> result.**

## 15 · Disclosure to another human is a separate act

```text
HELD               ≠ DISCLOSABLE
VISIBLE TO MEMBER  ≠ DISCLOSABLE TO PRACTITIONER
MEMBER-ADOPTED     ≠ PUBLIC
AUTHORIZED FOR ONE RELATIONSHIP  ≠  AUTHORIZED FOR ANOTHER
```

⭐ Disclosure requires **its own authority**, appropriate to **the recipient · the relationship · the
purpose · the representation's standing · and the consequence of disclosure.**

⛔ `ACT 5` does not create the full practitioner-access model. ⭐ It establishes that **member
visibility and third-party disclosure are separate constitutional acts.**

## 16 · ⛔ Existing role labels do not create disclosure authority

> ⭐ **A role label does not itself authorize access to a member representation unless valid
> governance grants that role the relevant jurisdiction.**

⛔ Implementation cannot create disclosure authority by naming someone **practitioner · facilitator ·
guide · administrator** or another role. ⭐ **Authority must be locatable.**

⛔ `ACT 5` does not resolve the inherited defects the census found — no ruled access model bridging
facilitator and practitioner, and implementation-level role conflation.

## 17 · Another human's access does not extinguish member standing

```text
lawfully disclosed  ≠  member standing extinguished
```

⭐ The organism must preserve the distinction among **who AUTHORED the representation · who can SEE
it · who may DISPUTE it · who may ACT on it · and what AUTHORITY each actor possesses.**

## 18 · ⭐ A member-facing surface is IMPLEMENTATION, ⛔ not DOCTRINE

⭐ The census's finding of inferred representations with no member-facing surface **established the
reality of the constitutional question.** ⛔ It did **not** establish that a particular page,
dashboard, panel, feed, table or route must be built.

```text
⛔ NO UI COMPONENT IS CONSTITUTIONALLY REQUIRED merely because ACT 5 requires meaningful standing
⭐ BUT any implementation must be CAPABLE OF ACTUALLY DISCHARGING the standing ACT 5 establishes
```

## 19 · ⛔ No circumvention through decomposition

If **no individual datum states a conclusion**, but the organism's **combined use** of the data
produces a **stable person-level consequence**, ⭐ then `ACT 5` applies to **the consequential
representation created by that combined use.**

> ⭐⭐ **The constitutional object is determined by MEANING AND USE, ⛔ not merely by STORAGE
> GRANULARITY.**

## 20 · ⛔ No circumvention through non-persistence

`ACT 4` established that consequential use may occur without storage. ⭐ Therefore:

> ⛔ **A representation does not escape member-standing requirements merely because it exists only
> long enough to influence a consequential act.**

⚠️ Whether a transient representation must later be made **separately inspectable** depends on what
standing survives the act and what is necessary to discharge **meaningful dispute and
accountability.** ⛔ `ACT 5` does **not** require preservation merely to create something to display.

## 21 · Protected non-disclosure requires independent authority

⛔ `ACT 5` **pre-authorizes no particular exception.** Where such a rule validly exists:

```text
· the exception must be LOCATABLE
· its JURISDICTION must be EXPLICIT
· non-disclosure must be NO BROADER than the authority permits
· ⛔ the representation must NOT SILENTLY ACQUIRE STRONGER STANDING BECAUSE IT IS HIDDEN
· the member must retain whatever standing CAN LAWFULLY COEXIST with the protected jurisdiction
```

> ⛔⛔ **A future implementation may not invent a generic `hidden` category and treat it as
> constitutional permission.**

## 22 · Member standing applies to consequential status, ⛔ not internal chain-of-thought

⛔ Nothing in `ACT 5` creates a right to **hidden chain-of-thought · token-level reasoning · raw
model activations · private internal deliberation · source code · model weights · embeddings as
technical objects** or technical internals whose disclosure would not meaningfully tell the member
**what representation concerning them the organism holds or uses.**

⭐ The constitutional object is **the member-relevant representation and its consequential
standing** — ⛔ not every internal operation that contributed to it.

> ⭐⭐ **This distinction preserves BOTH reflective intelligence AND member sovereignty.**

## ⭐ ACT 5 RESULT

```text
SQ-3   ⭐ ADJUDICATED
```

> ⭐⭐ **The organism may reason privately, but it may not build CONSEQUENTIAL HIDDEN TRUTH about a
> member and then GOVERN THE MEMBER FROM BEHIND IT.**
>
> **Where a representation concerning a member becomes durable, consequential, disclosed or
> governing, the member acquires constitutional standing toward it. Meaningful standing includes
> LEGIBILITY of its material content and epistemic status, the ability to DISPUTE it, PRESERVATION
> OF PROVENANCE, and governed treatment of REVISION and ERASURE.**
>
> **Erasure is not identical to deletion. Where retention is independently required, the
> representation may remain as history or accountability ⛔ without continuing to operate as ACTIVE
> TRUTH concerning the member.**
>
> **Another human may hold their own view of a member. ⚠️ But once the organism STORES,
> OPERATIONALIZES, DISCLOSES or USES that view, the organism's handling of it falls under this
> doctrine.**
>
> **Member standing does not mean member SUPREMACY over every consequence. Other valid jurisdictions
> may govern outcomes. ⛔ But the organism may not derive authority from MAKING THE MEMBER
> CONSTITUTIONALLY ABSENT from the representation that governs them.**
>
> **The doctrine attaches to MEANING AND CONSEQUENTIAL USE — ⛔ not to a particular storage form, UI
> or implementation mechanism.**
