# RELATIONAL-GEOMETRY-INTEGRATION-01 — R26 / H7a-H7b Ecological Process Validity

**Status:** H7a PILOT FALSIFIER · H7b EQUAL-BUDGET PARTIAL SUPPORT  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Authority:** offline research only

## Question

Do relational representations preserve earlier process structure that is later validated by member-authored conversation better than narrative summary alone?

Five real longitudinal conversation episodes were frozen. Each representation was built only from the earlier cutoff. Later member-authored turns selected which earlier precursor features mattered. A blind decoder saw the representation and feature descriptions but never the later raw outcome.

## H7a pilot

H7a was not adjudicated because representation length was badly confounded: narrative averaged 227.4 words, pairwise 205.6, relation geometry 156.8, and geometry+temporal 113.8. Narrative also exceeded its nominal budget in 4/5 events. The pilot is retained as evidence that representation capacity can dominate ecological recall.

## H7b equal-budget result

All four conditions used compact plain-text notation and were deterministically capped at 120 words.

| Condition | Later-validated feature recall | Perfect events | Mean words | Features / 100 words |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A narrative | 10/15 (66.7%) | 1/5 | 113.4 | 1.76 |
| B pairwise relations | **11/15 (73.3%)** | **2/5** | 67.4 | **3.26** |
| C multidimensional geometry | 9/15 (60.0%) | **2/5** | 81.6 | 2.21 |
| D geometry + temporal | **11/15 (73.3%)** | 1/5 | 85.6 | 2.57 |

## Interpretation

The ecological result does **not** support a monotonic "more geometry is better" architecture. Pairwise relations were the most information-efficient general representation on this small benchmark. Geometry+temporal tied pairwise relations on raw recall but used more words. Multidimensional geometry without temporal structure underperformed both.

The feature pattern suggests a narrower hypothesis: richer temporal geometry may earn itself conditionally when the process is about recurrence, reopened meaning, transformation, or continuity-through-change, while simpler pairwise structure may be preferable for established local relational facts.

This conditional hypothesis is exploratory because it was noticed after H7b. It must be tested on held-out real episodes before it receives standing.

## Law carried forward

> **Representational complexity must earn its cost. Use the least complex geometry that preserves the process-relevant distinctions required by the task.**

No result authorizes live cognition exposure or production integration.
