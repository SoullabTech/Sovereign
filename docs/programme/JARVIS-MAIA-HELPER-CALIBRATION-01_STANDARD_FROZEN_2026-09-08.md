# JARVIS-MAIA-HELPER-CALIBRATION-01 — CALIBRATION STANDARD · **FROZEN**

**Frozen by**: founder, 2026-09-08. Supersedes the unit of calibration in the drafted charter.
**Status**: ⛔ **STANDARD FROZEN. LANE STILL NOT OPENED.** Nothing run, judged or built.

---

## The standard

> **The unit of calibration is a real writing session** — not an observation, not a sentence, not
> a whole-work reading.
>
> **Does working with MAIA make it easier for a person to make the work they are trying to make?**
> Everything else is subordinate to that.
>
> **A writer should finish a session feeling that the work moved forward, not that they
> successfully operated an AI system.**

## Settled

**Consent**: one Work-level permission — *let MAIA carry what she learns while helping me with
this Work.* Quiet maintenance lawful only while **Work-scoped · permissioned · legible**.

**Legibility is conversational, not bureaucratic.** *"What do you understand about this Work?"* →
answered in plain sentences → *"No, that second part isn't important anymore"* → she adjusts.

**Provenance stays invisible to the writer and mandatory in the system.** The burden moves
downward into the architecture. That is where it belongs.

## The session judgments — all of them

```
Did MAIA make the writing easier?                                  YES / SOMEWHAT / NO
Did she understand what you were trying to do?                     YES / PARTLY / NO
Did anything feel intrusive, technical, generic, overcomplicated?  YES / NO
Would you want her beside you for the next section?                YES / NO
Anything particularly useful or irritating?                        (optional)
```

## Build order

```
1  ELEMENTAL ALCHEMY becomes the first calibration Work
2  WRITE — calibrate real section-working sessions
3  WORK UNDERSTANDING — she learns the right things, and can say what she understands
4  DEVELOP — test whether those sessions improve whole-work understanding
5  RETURN LOOP — Develop → "take me there" → Write → work continues
```

Then, and only then, broaden to other practitioners and other kinds of Works.

## Four things to build into the instrument before it runs

### 1 · ⭐⭐ Silence counts — and the current instrumentation cannot see it

> *"I see what you're doing. The passage is carrying it. I wouldn't disturb it."* Then you keep
> writing. **That counts. MAIA should not need to earn her presence by constantly finding
> something.**

⭐ **This is not only a product preference — it is already ratified law, at a smaller scale.** E1
§3.1: *`notices: []` is a complete success; silence carries no message.* The contract already says
`HAVING NOTHING TO SAY IS A COMPLETE ANSWER`, `outcome: 'none'` is a lawful structured answer, and
C7 keeps contemplative silence distinguishable from infrastructure failure. **The restraint the
founder is asking for is already representable.** Good — the product law and the constitutional
law agree.

⛔ **But every number this lane has produced would score that session at zero.** The witness counts
proposals, survivors, rejections. A session where MAIA said almost nothing and the writer wrote
well is invisible to all of it — and if Jarvis correlates founder judgments against "what actually
happened" instrumented as counts, **it will systematically penalize the best sessions.**

> **Productive restraint has to be recordable as a positive, or the instrument will train it out.**

### 2 · The hard warning should be measured, not only felt

> *Did you spend more time managing MAIA than writing?* — **if maintaining the collaboration costs
> more than the collaboration saves, Writer's Studio is failing.**

Self-report will catch the severe cases and miss the slow ones. Cheap objective companions:

- **writer turns spent directing MAIA ÷ writer turns spent on the manuscript**
- **MAIA questions asked before MAIA's first contribution** (does she explain before listening?)
- **time from session start to the first thing the writer kept**
- **MAIA words per writer word** — one observation where a paragraph was offered

⛔ None of these is the verdict. The founder's ear is. They exist so a gradual drift toward
management shows up before it is bad enough to feel.

### 3 · ⭐⭐ Step 4 needs a control, and it must be run BLIND and FIRST

> *Did MAIA's history of actually working with you make that whole-work reading more perceptive
> than a cold read? If no, accumulating developmental understanding is just expensive memory.*

That comparison is only meaningful against an actual cold read. ⭐ **A partial control already
exists**: the fourth witness — 226 grounded notices over the whole Work, produced with **zero
prior context**, at snapshot rev 7. That is precisely *what MAIA perceives with no history*, and
it is the one thing of lasting value the failed G8 run produced.

⛔ **It is not yet a control, because it is notices and not a reading.** A true control is a **cold
whole-work reading at the same snapshot** — and the methodology matters:

> **Produce the cold reading FIRST, before the field exists, and hold it sealed.** Produced
> afterward, it would be written by a system that already knows what the field-informed reading
> found — and the comparison would measure prompt quality, not accumulated understanding.

Cheap to do, and it makes step 4 falsifiable rather than aspirational. ⛔ Do it before step 2
begins populating anything.

### 4 · ⚠ Elemental Alchemy is finished — so these will be REVISION sessions

The right first Work, for the reason given: the author knows it, and one relationship must work
deeply before it can be made universal. **One property to name so it is not a later surprise:**
the manuscript is complete — bibliography, acknowledgments, appendices. Writer's Studio's core
loop is *write → talk → make it better → keep going*, and on a finished Work the first verb is
absent.

| | *"did the work move forward?"* means |
|---|---|
| **revision** (what step 2 will test) | I see it more clearly · I changed something · I decided not to |
| **composition** (untested) | I wrote more · I got unstuck · I found the next thing |

⛔ **Not an objection — a scope marker.** Success here demonstrates MAIA beside a writer *revising*.
It does not yet demonstrate MAIA beside a writer *drafting*, and a later *"it doesn't help me when
I'm stuck at the blank part"* would be a new finding, not a regression.
