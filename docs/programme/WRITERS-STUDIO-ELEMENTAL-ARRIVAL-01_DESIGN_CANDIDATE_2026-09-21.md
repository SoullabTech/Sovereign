# `WRITERS-STUDIO-ELEMENTAL-ARRIVAL-01`
## Elemental Arrival + Intention Custody

**Status** ⭐ **DESIGN CANDIDATE · ⛔ NOT BUILD AUTHORIZATION** (founder, 2026-09-21)
**Sits** before editorial reading · **Scope** ⛔ authorizes no Work-state capability beyond the
currently supported **Draft** and **Completed**
**Related** `WRITERS-STUDIO-CONVERGENCE-01_CHARTER_2026-09-21.md` (L2 · L7 · §9 FACETS-01)

This design discharges the **intention custody** obligation carried open at charter §7.2(1).

---

## I · Purpose — ⛔ what arrival is not

⛔ Not onboarding · ⛔ not profile collection · ⛔ not a questionnaire that classifies the writer.

⭐ It is the **first act of relationship** between the member, the Work, and MAIA, so MAIA may
understand what gives the Work life, why it matters, what form it is taking, what the member hopes
it may do, and what coherence holds it together.

⭐ The member experiences a **thoughtful conversation**. The system may retain a structured
representation underneath. ⛔ The structure is never the experience.

---

## II · The five dimensions

| | Asks | ⛔ Is not |
|---|---|---|
| **FIRE** Inspiration | What is calling this Work into being? | ⛔ topic determination |
| **WATER** Meaning | Why does this matter? What is alive underneath? | ⛔ a polished answer — *"I don't know yet"* is a valid declaration |
| **AIR** Form | What shape is this taking? | ⛔ imposed — MAIA may reflect possibilities; **the member declares** |
| **EARTH** Function | What would you like this Work to do? | ⛔ **a metric by which MAIA later grades the Work** |
| **AETHER** Coherence | What makes this one Work? | ⛔ a request for a thesis statement |

⭐ Meaning may emerge **through** the Work rather than precede it.

---

## III · ⛔ Five form fields are forbidden

The dimensions govern the conversation **underneath**. Arrival opens simply — *"Tell me about the
Work. You don't need to have it figured out."* — and MAIA **follows what the member says.**

⛔ She does not mechanically ask every Elemental question. ⛔ If the member has already spoken to
Fire and Water, she does not repeat those questions **merely to populate fields**.

> ⭐ **The structure serves the encounter. ⛔ The encounter does not serve the structure.**

---

## IV · ⭐⭐ THE CENTRAL CUSTODY RULE

MAIA may **notice · reflect · propose · ask.**
**The member declares.** The system **records the declaration**.

> ⛔ **No inference becomes intention merely because MAIA generated it convincingly.**

Only after **member adoption** may a reflection become part of the Work's declared orientation.
Member responses include: *Yes · Partly · No · Let me say it differently.*

⭐ This is L7 (observe → ask → member declares → record) applied to purpose, and it is what makes
`author-intent` lawful: MAIA holds what she was **told**, ⛔ never what she **inferred**.

---

## V–VII · Intention custody, revision, non-capture

### V · What each declaration preserves

member-approved wording · its Elemental dimension · the Work it belongs to · when declared ·
**whether the member wrote it or adopted wording developed in conversation** · revision lineage ·
whether currently **active · revised · intentionally left open**.

> ⛔ The current declaration may change. **The prior declaration must not silently become current
> again.**

### VI · Intention is revisional, ⛔ not captured

*"That was true when I began, but it isn't what the book is anymore."* ⭐ That is **development of
the Work**, ⛔ not inconsistency.

Both declarations belong to the history. ⭐ Only the later is current **if the member has declared
it so**. ⛔ MAIA must not treat the earlier intention as a hidden truth against which the newer Work
is judged.

### VII · Gestalt non-capture

⛔ No declaration becomes a score · a fixed taxonomy · a claim to the member's *"true intention"* ·
a licence to override material that does not fit · ⛔ no absence may be filled by inference ·
⛔ no contradiction is automatically corrected.

> ⭐ **The Work may exceed what the member can presently say about it. That excess must remain
> possible.**

This satisfies `MAIA_TEMPORAL_RELATIONAL_MEMORY_GESTALT_LAW_2026-09-16.md` — *memory may deepen
orientation; it may never become the fixed lens.*

---

## VIII–IX · How intention returns

**Permitted** (§VIII) — MAIA returns the member's **own declared orientation** to them:

> *"Earlier you said you wanted the reader to encounter the elements through experience before
> explanation. This passage moves into explanation very quickly. I'm wondering whether that matters
> here."* → **What do you think?**

⛔ **Not permitted** — *"This passage fails the purpose of the book."*

> ⭐ **The declaration is a source of inquiry. ⛔ Not a grading rubric.**

**When MAIA asks whether an intention changed** (§IX) — ⛔ never a recurring interruption. Only on:
explicit contradiction · a materially different later declaration · the member saying the Work has
changed · an editorial act substantially dependent on the earlier declaration.
Choices: *Yes, let's revisit · No, both are still true · Not now.*

⭐ **Divergence points both ways** — §VIII's *"I'm wondering whether that matters here"* permits
*the book changed*, and §IX gives that answer a route. The study's both-ways requirement is
**satisfied across VIII + IX**.

---

## X · The Work Compass

⛔ Not a dashboard · ⛔ not a scorecard. A quiet place to return to the Work's **current**
orientation: Inspiration · Meaning · Form · Function · Deeper coherence, each offering **Revisit**
and optionally **History**.

⭐ **Still discovering** is a **first-class state**. ⛔ It is not missing data.

---

## XI · Arrival across the three facets

The Elemental foundation ⛔ does not change across facets. **The relationship changes.**

- **GUIDED** — MAIA carries more of the conversational orientation and helps the writer find words
  for something not yet articulated. She may offer reflections; ⛔ **the member still declares.**
- **LEARNING** — same arrival, plus optional **Why I'm asking**. ⭐ Teaching explains **process**;
  ⛔ it does not prescribe a universal standard of good writing.
- **DIRECT** — the member may enter or revise the Compass directly, and may still choose
  conversation at any point. ⛔ **Direct does not bypass declaration custody.**

---

## XIII–XVI · Surfaces

**XIII · New Work** — *Begin a Work · "You do not need to know exactly what this is yet."*
Primary **Begin with MAIA** · secondary **I already know what I'm making** (a concise version of
the same orientation, ⛔ not a bypass of custody).

**XIV · ⭐ Existing Draft or Completed manuscript — THIS LANE BEGINS HERE.**
*"Before we work on the manuscript, I'd like to understand the Work as you understand it — not only
the pages themselves."* → **Talk with MAIA · Tell MAIA directly · Skip for now.**

> ⭐ **Skipping is always lawful. ⛔ The absence of a Work Compass does not authorize MAIA to invent
> one.**

**XV · Transition** — *"Here is how I'm holding the Work"*, returned in short human form, then
*"Does this still feel like the Work you want me to accompany?"* → *Yes, enter the Work · I want to
change something · Leave some of this open.*

> ⛔ **Nothing becomes canonical through silence.**

**XVI · Inside** — `Elemental Alchemy · Work · Review · Ask MAIA`. The Compass stays quietly
available and ⛔ does not occupy primary visual space.

---

## XII · ⭐ FACET REACHABILITY — RULED

> ⭐ **One observation set. Different default depth. Everything reachable from every facet.**

A facet **may** change how many are initially expanded · how much explanation appears by default ·
whether technical evidence is exposed automatically.

⛔ A facet **may not** make an observation unreachable because the member chose Guided or Learning.
⛔ *"Guided shows six while Direct has access to eighty-seven"* is **impermissible** if the
remaining observations cannot be reached in Guided. Lawful: Guided says *"I'm showing you a few at
a time so this stays workable"* + **See more observations**.

### ⚠️ Implementation constraint this ruling implies — the cut must be non-evaluative

⚠️ *"A few at a time"* requires something to choose **which** few. ⭐ Charter §4 dissolved ranking:
observations are **unranked**, and selection **by consequence is ranking under another name**.

⭐ **The lawful cut is therefore a non-evaluative one, and manuscript order is the natural answer** —
*here are the observations, in the order they occur in your book.* It is lawful by construction, it
preserves *no implicit ranking*, and it is better product: it walks the writer through their book
rather than through a verdict about it.

⛔ *Most severe first · most confident first · most actionable first* are all ranking, and all
require the act charter §4 deferred.

---

## ⭐⭐ XII-b · THE COMPASS LICENSES NOTHING ABOUT THE TEXT

⚠️ **Added here because the design does not state it and the omission is load-bearing.**

Once the Compass exists, it becomes an input MAIA can reason with (§VIII). The hazard is subtle and
severe: **a declaration could appear to discharge a coverage obligation.**

> A member declares *"this book is about grief as initiation."* MAIA later says *"the book coheres
> around grief as initiation."* ⛔ That is a **`whole-work-pattern` claim licensed by a declaration
> rather than by reading** — the non-conclusion discharged by the wrong thing.

⭐ **The repository has already ruled this boundary**, in `lib/manuscript/structure/readScope.ts`:

> *"Materials are out of scope… this reader interprets the Work **AS WRITTEN**, not authorial
> intention reconstructed from auxiliary material. That could become a later, **separately
> consented** capability; it is not this one."*

⭐ The Work Compass **is** auxiliary material. Therefore, binding:

1. ⭐ The Compass may orient **what MAIA attends to**.
2. ⛔ The Compass is **never evidence**, and ⛔ **never discharges coverage**. Whole-work authority
   remains licensed by **recorded coverage of the text**, and by nothing else.
3. ⛔ A Compass declaration may **never** be restated as an observation about the manuscript.
   *"You said the book does X"* ✅ · *"the book does X"* ⛔ — unless the text, read and covered,
   says so.
4. ⛔ Feeding the Compass into the structure reader is the deferred capability `readScope.ts` names,
   and requires its own **separately consented** act. ⛔ Not authorized here.

## XVII · Acceptance test — intention custody

**⛔ The arrival FAILS if:**

1. ⛔ MAIA infers a member intention and stores it without explicit adoption
2. ⛔ An earlier intention silently overrides a later declaration
3. ⛔ An unresolved dimension is treated as missing data to be completed
4. ⛔ MAIA treats the Work Compass as a quality rubric
5. ⛔ MAIA claims to know the member's *"real"* purpose
6. ⛔ A facet changes how much prose MAIA may author
7. ⛔ A facet changes which governed observations are ultimately reachable
8. ⛔ Learning imports an external standard of quality rather than teaching technique
9. ⛔ Changing facets changes the Work Compass or its current declarations
10. ⛔ A member cannot revise an intention while preserving its history
11. ⛔ A member cannot say *I don't know yet*
12. ⛔ A member cannot skip the Elemental arrival and still enter their Work

**Added from the substrate:**

13. ⛔ A Compass declaration discharges a coverage obligation, or is restated as an observation
    about the manuscript (§XII-b)
14. ⛔ The *"few at a time"* cut is evaluative rather than positional (§XII)
15. ⚠️ ⛔ **A never-declared dimension is indistinguishable from a declared-open one.** ⭐ There are
    **three** states, not two — **declared · declared-open (*Still discovering*) · never-declared** —
    and §XIV makes the third ordinary, since skipping is lawful. ⛔ A never-declared Compass must not
    render as a form with blanks inviting completion, which would make #3 fail through visual design
    while passing in data.

> ⭐ **The arrival passes when the member can experience:** *MAIA understands something important
> about what I am trying to bring into being, but she has not defined it for me.*

---

## XVIII · Product law

> ⭐ The Elemental orientation **belongs to the Work**, but its meaning remains **in the member's
> custody**.
>
> ⭐ MAIA may help the Work become **more articulate** without claiming to know **what the Work
> ultimately is**.
>
> ⭐ The Work Compass **remembers what the member has declared without turning declaration into
> destiny**.
