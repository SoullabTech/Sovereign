## 1 · ⭐⭐ The target reading is NOT an Encounter, and this is the finding

The worked example contains, deliberately and correctly:

> *"For much of the book, you teach by moving between story and pattern. Near the end, the pattern
> begins to dominate the story. **A reader may experience that** as the book changing from a
> journey into a reference system."*

> *"Does the final portion still feel like the reader is traveling through the living discovery —
> or does the reader eventually begin **studying the architecture from outside it**?"*

Under the Encounter screen these are **reader-effect claims**, and the second is openly *the
beginning of a case*. The founder names it as such: *"that is a genuinely useful developmental
observation"*, and the IA puts DEVELOPMENT at the funnel's end — *what now seems worth the
writer's attention.*

⭐ **So the seven-choice interface spans two constitutionally different acts.** PT-1 holds:
Encounter precedes intervention and helps the writer recognize the Work **without telling them
what ought to happen to it**. The whole-work reading, as written, crosses into intervention on
purpose, at the point where the writer asked for it.

⛔ **Consequence for calibration: do not calibrate Encounter against this reading.** It would
teach Encounter to do the thing Encounter exists not to do. The reading is the acceptance object
for the **whole-work reading act**; Encounter stays the grounded noticing layer beneath it.

## 2 · ⭐⭐ The current architecture cannot produce this, by ratified law

Nearly every load-bearing sentence in the target is a **cross-window synthesis**:

> *"the book gradually performs the same movement it describes"* · *"encounter → differentiation →
> relationship → integration → map"* · *"near the end, the form itself changes"* ·
> *"the balance changes"*

Encounter has **no synthesis pass**, by explicit ruling (`structuredGenerator.ts`): *window-local
cognition may miss a distant recurrence; missing a possible notice is lawful, inventing an
unconstituted synthesis stage to manufacture cross-window observations is not.* And F-2's repair
made every notice carry a ≤12,400-code-point scope precisely so a window-local cognition **may
never emerge wearing whole-Work authority**.

⛔ **This is not an obstacle to route around. Both laws are correct and both were recently
ratified.** A whole-Work reading needs genuine whole-Work authority — which means it must be a
**separately constituted act that is actually shown the whole Work**, not a synthesis quietly
bolted onto Encounter.

### The fork, stated so it can be ruled

| | shown the **Work** whole | shown the **226 notices** |
|---|---|---|
| scope | genuinely whole-Work — the claim matches the evidence field | whole-Work over *observations*, not text |
| feasibility | 386,031 code points ≈ 100k tokens; within a 1M context in **one call** | small, cheap |
| evidence | anchors bind to the Work directly | ⛔ **interpretation of interpretation** — anchors would point at notices |
| failure mode | expensive; attention over a long field unmeasured | a reading that can drift from the book while sounding grounded |

⭐ **The right-hand column is the tempting one and the dangerous one.** A reading synthesized from
notices inherits every partial grounding (F-1) and every screen false-negative silently, and its
citations would certify *what MAIA said about the book*, not the book.

⛔ Not decided here. What must survive either choice: **the anchor and scope discipline**. A
reading that cannot show the writer where it is looking is an impression about their book.

## 3 · The reading is a better calibration object — with one caveat

**Right, and for the reason given:** *"Yes, that understands my book" · "No, that's missing it" ·
"That makes me want to look again"* are judgments a person can actually make. One artifact, not
226 coded notices, and no machinery to decode.

⚠ **The caveat is attribution.** When the verdict is *"that's missing it"*, the gestalt does not
say which sentence caused it. The 226 had granularity and no usefulness; the reading has
usefulness and no granularity.

⭐ **They are complementary, and the lane should use both:** the **reading** is the acceptance
object — does this understand the book? The **notices** are where language laws get derived —
which sentence shapes earn *"too technical"*, *"too editorial"*, *"too clinical"*. Calibrating on
the reading alone risks laws that cannot be stated; calibrating on notices alone is the trap this
proposal correctly escapes.

## 4 · What the worked example demonstrates that is worth preserving

- **It names the movement of the book, not the categories of the instrument.** *"encounter →
  differentiation → relationship → integration → map"* is a sentence about the manuscript.
- **It refuses the cheap verdict.** On the voice shifts: *"I would not immediately call that
  inconsistency. I would ask what each voice is doing."* Then it says what each voice is doing.
- **It states the book's own governing idea back to the author** — *wholeness is not sameness; it
  is the capacity to hold differentiated forces in living relationship* — and observes that this
  is structural as much as philosophical.
- **It ends by offering, not directing**: *"I can show you where I feel that transition begins."*
  ⭐ That sentence is the whole doctrine in miniature — a developmental question, held open, with
  the evidence available on request rather than pressed on the writer.
