## 4 · ⭐ The four sequencing tests — every question against all four

```text
TEST A · CONSTITUTIONAL UPSTREAMNESS
   Would answering it establish a rule REQUIRED to state or adjudicate another remaining
   question correctly? ⭐ Name which, and precisely what becomes STATABLE afterward.
   ⛔ Do not infer dependency merely because two questions are related.

TEST B · CURRENT DOCTRINAL PRESSURE
   Have ACT 1 or ACT 2 created a LIVE CONSTITUTIONAL STATE requiring this question before later
   adjudication proceeds cleanly? (a HOLD whose release is ungoverned · an authority distinction
   a later question now needs · an undefined act another ruling would depend on)
   ⚠️ A pressure is NOT automatically a priority — ⭐ its EFFECT must be stated.

TEST C · NON-PREJUDICE
   Would taking it next materially PRE-SHAPE another unresolved question? If so, is that
   LEGITIMATE UPSTREAM DOCTRINE or PREMATURE SUBSTANTIVE PREJUDGMENT?
   ⛔⛔ A question must not be sequenced first merely because answering it would make a preferred
   later answer easier.

TEST D · DECISION INDEPENDENCE
   Can it be adjudicated NOW from the sealed evidence plus ACT 1 and ACT 2 without first deciding
   another remaining question? ⛔ If not, it cannot be ACT 3.
```
