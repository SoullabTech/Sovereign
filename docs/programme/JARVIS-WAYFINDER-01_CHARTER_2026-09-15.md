## 0. What this lane is

A navigational instrument, not a dashboard and not a growth programme.

> **Given everything Soullab has built, what is the smallest next move that will produce the most important missing evidence about who MAIA is for and why they stay?**

Wayfinder holds the theory so the founder does not have to. It determines the current gate from evidence, names one objective, and **suppresses the hundred other plausible things a founder could be doing.**

Three proofs are distinguished and never merged:

| Proof | Question | Current standing |
|---|---|---|
| **Product proof** | Does MAIA reliably do what we claim? | Substantial |
| **Relational proof** | Does an ongoing relationship produce meaningful value? | Emerging |
| **Market proof** | Will a definable population seek, pay for and recommend that relationship? | Minimal |

The lane exists to produce the third without degrading the second.

## 1. Form of the instrument (ruling)

**Wayfinder v1 is a document instrument plus a session protocol. It is NOT an application surface.**

Reason: building a GTM dashboard as the first act would be the exact move this instrument exists to refuse — a capability built ahead of the evidence that requires it. It would also be self-refuting: the first thing Wayfinder says is *do not build this week*.

A live surface becomes a legitimate question at Gate 7 and not before, and only if a named member of the cohort is blocked without it.

Operation: the founder opens a Wayfinder session; Jarvis reads this charter and the current gate record, states position, objective, suppression list, and the next single move.

## 2. Constitutional boundary (read before collecting any evidence)

A market instrument that reads members is a different object from one that reads the market. Four repairs are law in this lane.

### 2.1 Evidence Admissibility Law

> **Wayfinder evidence may come only from what a member knowingly gave, or from non-content behaviour. It may never come from the content of a member's conversations with MAIA.**

Admissible:
- what the member **said to a human**, in a conversation they knew was research
- **acts the member took**: signed in unprompted, returned, marked a breakthrough, referred someone, entered a payment method, cancelled
- **non-content telemetry**: session counts, intervals, voluntary return, feature reach
- what the member **chose to publish or share**

Inadmissible, without exception:
- conversational content, transcripts, excerpts, summaries, digests, or inferences drawn from them
- anything from a Sanctuary session, including the fact of what was discussed
- memory atoms, developmental memories, pattern rows, field state, or any MAIA read of a person
- a Soullab-authored interpretation of a member's inner state

Grounding: `MEDIA_FIELD_AND_RELATIONAL_FIELD_BOUNDARIES.md` permitted-flow law; Sanctuary Mode invariants 1–6; Invariant 11 (Declared Significance — significance is the member's to declare).

*If a piece of evidence would be impossible to obtain by asking the member directly, it is inadmissible.*

### 2.2 No per-member developmental scorecard

A company-maintained row reading `Trust ✓ · Continuity ✓ · Recognition ✓` against a named person is a diagnostic read of that person, prohibited by **Invariant 6** (no diagnostic-style labeling) and structurally identical to the status economy the Circles I0 census refused (`contribution_tier`, `min_cognitive_level` — access gated on a measured attribute of a person).

Wayfinder therefore holds:
- **member-authored evidence** — their words, their acts, attributable to them because they gave it
- **cohort aggregates** — counts and proportions across the cohort

It does not hold a Soullab reading of who any member is becoming.

### 2.3 Recognition is not a market target

**Invariant 16** prohibits optimizing for felt recognition. Placing *Recognition* on a gate scorecard creates a commercial incentive to engineer the sensation of being known — the precise prohibited optimization.

So the relational gate (Gate 4) is measured by **Invariant 4's allowed targets**: clarity, usefulness, member-reported growth, and real-world follow-through — plus the member's own unprompted language. Never by an internal estimate of how known someone feels.

### 2.4 Vocabulary separation

`Encounter`, `Reflection`, `Recognition`, `Living Field` are **constitutional layer names** with upward-only authority terminating in the member's authored expression (`CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`). A commercial ladder that borrows those names and terminates in *paid continuity* inverts the direction of authority: it makes revenue the top of a ladder whose top is the member's own authored meaning.

Wayfinder uses its own vocabulary and never the constitutional names:

```
first contact → first value → voluntary return → sustained use
   → continuity valued → applied outside MAIA → transmitted → paid
```

### Gate law

1. **The founder does not decide the gate.** The gate is a finding from evidence against criteria declared below.
2. **Criteria are predeclared and versioned.** They may not be rewritten after an observation in order to make that observation pass. A material change requires a new instrument version and re-observation. (`CLAIM_STATE_AUTHORITY.md` rule 5.)
3. **Three verdicts, not two.** `PASS` · `FAIL` · `INVALID`. Treating INVALID as PASS is how this instrument gets defeated.
4. **A gate can be lost.** If the warrant for a closed gate lapses — the cohort stops returning, the referrals stop — the gate is **downgraded without waiting for a decision to downgrade it**. Downgrade is the instrument working. (Rule 4.)
5. **One gate at a time.** Evidence gathered for a later gate is recorded but does not advance position.
6. **A green gate permits; it does not promote.** Closing a gate makes a public claim *permissible*, under `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`. Publishing it is a separate authorized act.

### INVALID — the verdict that protects this lane

A run is INVALID, not FAIL, when:
- the cohort is too small for the question asked (n below the gate's declared floor)
- members were prompted, reminded, or encouraged by the founder during a return-behaviour window
- members are participating primarily out of relationship to Kelly rather than interest in the problem
- a product failure prevented the member from reaching the thing being measured
- the evidence available is inadmissible under §2.1

**INVALID licenses nothing in either direction.** The gate keeps whatever state it held.

## 4. The gates

### GATE 0 · ORIENTATION

**Question:** What do we actually know, as distinct from what we believe?

**Exit criteria:** every field in the Gate 0 census is either answered with attributable evidence or explicitly marked UNKNOWN; every founder belief about the market is written with its evidence and its status beside it.

**Your job:** answer the census. Nothing else.

**Suppressed:** all building, all recruiting, all positioning work.

**Record:** `JARVIS-WAYFINDER-01_GATE-0_ORIENTATION_2026-09-15.md`

---

### GATE 1 · RIGHT PEOPLE

**Question:** Can we deliberately assemble 5–8 genuinely qualified people who will use MAIA enough that we can learn from their actual behaviour?

*This, not PMF, is the live question.*

**Cohort criteria** — a candidate qualifies only if all hold:

```
✓ already engaged in reflective or developmental work
✓ understands the value of an ongoing process
✓ willing to use AI
✓ able to use MAIA several times across 3–4 weeks
✓ able to articulate what they experience
✓ not participating primarily out of friendship with Kelly
```

Deliberately excluded from the first cohort: AI novelty seekers · casual ChatGPT users · anyone needing heavy onboarding · anyone drawn primarily by features.

**Exit criteria (predeclared):**
- **≥ 5** qualified members have each completed **≥ 3** substantive MAIA encounters
- each qualified against the six criteria **before** invitation, recorded
- **≥ 3** of the five are not close personal friends of the founder

**Floor for any conclusion about return behaviour: n ≥ 5.** Below it, every Gate 3 reading is INVALID.

**Your job:** find three to six more humans. One at a time.
1. Who do you know who fits this profile?
2. Of those, who already trusts you enough to try something unfinished?
3. Invite that person next.

**Suppressed:** new capability · ads · pricing · public funnel · multiple audiences · onboarding optimization · content · partnerships.

---

### GATE 2 · FIRST VALUE

**Question:** Did something meaningful happen — not *did they like it*?

> Almost everyone trying a founder's product will be kind. **Praise is not evidence. Behaviour and unprompted language are.**

**Admissible evidence** — member's own words, recorded verbatim, given knowingly:

```
"I hadn't thought about it that way."
"That's exactly what I'm struggling with."
"It remembered what we talked about."
"This feels different from ChatGPT."
"I want to continue this."
```

plus behaviour: completed a first full conversation · spent meaningful time · explored without instruction · returned after the first session.

**Inadmissible:** anything read from what they actually discussed (§2.1).

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 4 of ≥ 5 qualified members produce at least one unprompted statement of value *and* one behavioural marker.

**Suppressed:** everything in Gate 1's list, plus feature work not unblocking a named member.

---

### GATE 3 · RETURN

**Question:** Do they come back when nobody reminded them?

**The founder may not prompt, remind, nudge, or check in during a return window.** A prompted return makes the reading INVALID.

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 4 of ≥ 5 qualified members return **unprompted** at least twice across a 3–4 week window.

**The "no" is equally valuable — classify it:**

| What they say | What it is |
|---|---|
| "I forgot it existed." | activation problem |
| "I liked it but didn't know what to do with it." | orientation problem |
| "ChatGPT already does what I need." | value / positioning problem |
| "I wanted to talk with MAIA again." | the signal we are looking for |

**Suppressed:** as Gate 2. A classified "no" routes to a finding, not immediately to a build.

---

### GATE 4 · RELATIONAL VALUE

**Question:** Does the relationship itself become valuable over time — beyond AI quality?

This is the unusual Soullab gate and the one where the central hypothesis either becomes real or does not.

**Measured by Invariant 4's allowed targets only:**
- clarity the member reports
- usefulness the member reports
- member-reported growth
- **real-world follow-through** — something changed outside MAIA (Invariant 7, human world priority)
- the member's unprompted language about continuity

**Never measured by:** an internal estimate of trust, felt recognition, attachment, or depth. Those are §2.2 and §2.3 violations.

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 4 members report continuity as load-bearing *in their own words*, and ≥ 2 report a specific real-world action they attribute to the work.

**Suppressed:** pricing · public positioning · scale mechanisms.

---

### GATE 5 · WILLINGNESS TO PAY

**Question:** Would someone act to preserve this?

**Never ask:** "Would you pay $29.99 for this app?" — that produces garbage evidence.

**Ask:** *If your access to MAIA ended next month, what would you do?* Then listen. Later: *would you want to continue this if membership cost money?* Later still, present a real offer.

```
"I would pay."   ≠   entered a payment method
```

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 3 members complete a real payment act against a real offer. Stated intent alone is FAIL-neutral — it is not evidence for this gate.

**Suppressed:** pricing tiers · packaging · funnel · discounting.

---

### GATE 6 · TRANSMISSION

**Question:** Will they tell someone — unprompted?

For something as hard to describe as MAIA, spontaneous transmission is among the strongest signals available.

**Evidence:** recommendation · introduction made · link shared · colleague invited · described MAIA to another person.

**The most valuable artifact of this gate is not the count. It is the words.** How they describe it, verbatim, becomes the market language. Soullab's own copy is not evidence here.

**Exit criteria:** ≥ 3 unprompted transmissions from ≥ 2 distinct members, with the describing language captured verbatim.

---

### GATE 7 · REPEATABILITY

**Question:** Can we deliberately find ten more people like these?

**Exit criteria:** a second cohort of ≥ 10, recruited by a described and repeated method, reproduces Gates 2–6 at comparable proportions.

Only here do partnerships · practitioner communities · events · content · referral programmes · pricing · onboarding optimization · a live Wayfinder surface become worth serious attention. **Not before.**

## 5. The build challenge

When a new capability is proposed, Wayfinder asks one question:

> **What evidence from the current cohort requires it?**
>
> **A.** Multiple members encountered the same limitation.
> **B.** One member encountered a severe blocker.
> **C.** It is necessary to test the current hypothesis.
> **D.** None of these — I think it would improve MAIA.

`A` · `B` · `C` → admissible now.
`D` → **backlog. Continue the current evidence cycle.**

Not because the idea is bad. Because it is not yet the most consequential thing.

`BUILD SOMETHING NEW` is never a default action, and *interesting does not equal consequential.*

Wayfinder is also authorized to say: **there is insufficient evidence to justify changing MAIA; continue observing the current cohort.** Doing nothing is a legitimate output.

## 6. Cycle decisions

At the close of each cycle, exactly one:

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DEEPEN** | hypothesis holding — add more people like these |
| **REFINE** | right population, wrong framing |
| **PIVOT ICP** | value real, stronger pull from another population |
| **REPAIR PRODUCT** | they want the proposition; a failure prevents value |
| **STOP** | evidence does not justify continuing this hypothesis |
| **HOLD** | insufficient evidence; keep observing |
