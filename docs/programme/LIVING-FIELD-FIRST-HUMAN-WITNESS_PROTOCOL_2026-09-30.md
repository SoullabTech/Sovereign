## 1 · Human-only question

The machine evidence can establish authorization, exclusion, rollback, route integrity,
interaction mechanics, and whether MAIA waits for an explicit gesture.

The first human witness asks something different:

> **Can a member inhabit the Living Field as their own living world, discover the early
> instrument without developer explanation, and experience MAIA's entry as an invitation
> rather than as the system taking possession of the field?**

This witness is not a referendum on visual taste. It tests legibility, ownership,
orientation, consent, and lived continuity.

## 2 · What machines establish before a human enters

Do not ask the participant to re-prove these mechanically:

- the ordinary Living Field remains available outside the early cohort;
- the early instrument is visible only when the server admits the authenticated member;
- URL parameters and client-side flags cannot create admission;
- the instrument can be closed for everyone with the rollout switch without removing member material;
- recursive SVG regions are actually interactive;
- progressive labels reveal the immediate semantic neighborhood rather than the entire tree at once;
- dynamic Living Field routes resolve member identity through verified session authority;
- dynamic route params are unwrapped before field operations;
- opening a dimension does not itself create a MAIA encounter;
- MAIA begins only after an explicit member gesture — either **Explore with MAIA →** at the field level or **Enter this dimension with MAIA** inside a dimension.

If any one of those machine boundaries is red on the production candidate, do not begin the human witness.
## 3 · Participant and environment preconditions

Run only when all are true:

1. The member is explicitly named in the Early Field production cohort.
2. They sign into their own ordinary production account.
3. Their existing Living Field is used; no private content is fabricated merely to make the witness pass.
   Readiness is established without reading content: at least one authentic member-owned source already exists in a mounted Living Field / constellation / governed flow source before the witness begins.
4. Production health is green and the deployed SHA is recorded before the encounter.
5. The early-field switch is open and the member's admission endpoint returns `admitted:true`.
6. The ordinary-member exclusion witness has already established that non-cohort members retain the Living Field without the early instrument.
7. No facilitator obtains, copies, prints, or inspects session credentials.
8. No new telemetry, screen recording, audio recording, or product instrumentation is enabled merely for this witness.
9. The explicit-MAIA-entry repair is present in the deployed build.

## 4 · Consent and evidence posture

Before observation, tell the member plainly that:

- they are trying an early way of moving through the Living Field;
- their existing Living Field and authored material remain theirs whether or not they continue;
- some visual and navigational parts are still being tested with a small group;
- they may explore their own field without talking with MAIA;
- MAIA begins only when they make an explicit MAIA gesture: **Explore with MAIA →** at the field level or **Enter this dimension with MAIA** inside a dimension;
- they may stop at any time without explanation and without affecting their account or material;
- the facilitator may take minimal notes about orientation, hesitation, trust, and consent clarity;
- no private reflective passage needs to be copied into the programme record.

The repository record uses a participant-neutral witness label. It must not preserve the
member's name, email, UUID, session token, private field text, or identifying relationship content.

## 5 · Participant-facing task

Give only this task before the first attempt:

> **Start from the House and enter your Living Field. Explore whatever draws your attention.
> Move into one dimension if you want to. If you see a way to enter that dimension with MAIA,
> decide for yourself whether you want to use it. Then return toward the wider field and Home
> in whatever way makes sense to you. Say what you are noticing as you go if you are comfortable.**

Do not explain:

- the cohort gate;
- the field tree;
- Fire / Water / Earth / Air / Aether as a required sequence;
- which node they are expected to choose;
- the intended meaning of any dimension;
- that the explicit MAIA button is the consent test;
- implementation terms such as R1R3, SVG, route, feature flag, or admission endpoint.

The first attempt should remain uncoached unless safety, privacy, data loss, or genuine inability
to recover requires intervention.
## 6 · Observe without interpreting for the member

Capture only the product evidence that can be observed:

```text
ARRIVAL
Did the member understand where they had arrived from the House?

OWNERSHIP
Did the field feel like their own material/world, or like a system report about them?

FIRST ATTENTION
What drew their attention before any coaching?

MOVEMENT
Could they discover how to move from the wider field into a region and deeper dimension?

LABELING
Did progressive labels orient them, overload them, or imply meanings they had not chosen?

HESITATION
Where did they pause, reread, attempt a dead gesture, or lose orientation?

MAIA BOUNDARY
Before the explicit MAIA gesture, did the member believe MAIA was already listening,
reading, interpreting, or participating?

MAIA GESTURE
Was “Enter this dimension with MAIA” understood as a distinct choice?

RELATIONAL SHIFT
If the member chose MAIA entry, did it feel like a clear transition from inhabiting
their own field into a relational encounter?

WIDER
Could the member move back toward the wider field without feeling trapped or losing place?

RETURN
Could they return Home without confusion or duplicate/competing return gestures?

TRUST
Did anything create concern about authorship, privacy, hidden interpretation, or data mutation?

CURIOSITY
What did the member spontaneously want to explore next?
```

Do not convert hesitation into a psychological description of the participant. Hesitation is
evidence about the interface condition encountered.

## 7 · Pre-registered adjudication

### PASS

A PASS requires all of the following:

- the member recognizes the Living Field as a place concerned with their own lived world rather than a system diagnosis;
- the early instrument is discoverable without developer explanation;
- at least one recursive movement is understandable enough to complete without coaching;
- progressive labels help or remain neutral rather than falsely prescribing meaning;
- opening a dimension does not feel like consent to MAIA;
- the MAIA-entry gesture is understood as a distinct voluntary act;
- if MAIA is entered, the relational transition is recognizable;
- the member can widen/return without becoming trapped;
- no unresolved authorship, privacy, consent, or data-loss concern remains.

### PASS WITH FRICTION

Use this when the semantic and consent boundaries hold but the member encounters recoverable
legibility, navigation, wording, contrast, spacing, touch-target, or orientation friction.

Record the friction as a product finding. Do not rewrite it as a clean PASS.

### NOT ADMITTED / STOP

Stop the witness and close Early Field exposure for the cohort if any of these appear:

- wrong-member material or any cross-member leakage;
- a non-cohort member can acquire Early Field admission;
- member material becomes inaccessible because the experimental layer is off;
- opening a dimension starts MAIA without the explicit member gesture;
- the member reasonably believes MAIA has interpreted or acted before choosing her;
- unexpected mutation, loss, or credible fear that member-authored material changed without their act;
- the member cannot safely leave the instrument or return to the ordinary Living Field/Home;
- a failure state misrepresents whether content was sent to MAIA;
- any privacy, consent, authentication, or authorship boundary failure.

Aesthetic dislike, unfamiliarity, or a preference for a different metaphor is not by itself a rollback condition.
## 8 · Questions after the uncoached walk

Ask only after the first attempt:

1. **When you entered the Living Field, what did you think this place was for?**
2. **Did it feel like your field, or like the system was telling you what you are?**
3. **What made you know where you could go next?**
4. **Before you chose “Enter this dimension with MAIA,” what did you think MAIA could already see or do?**
5. **Did that button feel like a real choice to begin a conversation, or like something already underway?**
6. **When you moved back toward the wider field and Home, did you still know where you were?**
7. **Was there any moment you worried that something private had been sent, interpreted, or changed without you choosing it?**
8. **What would you naturally want to explore next?**

Prefer paraphrase in the programme record. Preserve an exact quote only when it is necessary to
understand a finding and the member agrees to that use.

## 9 · Evidence law

A human witness record must name:

- production runtime SHA;
- canonical SHA at the time of observation;
- production health;
- Early Field switch state;
- whether the member was admitted by the server;
- device/browser class when relevant;
- the uncoached first attempt;
- any facilitator intervention;
- whether MAIA was entered;
- whether any MAIA request occurred before explicit entry;
- the member's answers to the post-walk questions, paraphrased by default;
- every friction or defect found and its disposition.

Do not copy private Living Field content merely to explain a usability finding.

One participant's PASS establishes only that this layered experience can be inhabited coherently
by that participant under that production condition. It does not establish universal usability,
cohort preference, therapeutic value, psychological validity, or broad-release readiness.

## 10 · Cohort progression

The small cohort should widen by **evidence**, not elapsed time.

A useful first sequence is:

```text
1 admitted participant
    ↓
review witness + defects
    ↓
2–4 additional admitted participants
    ↓
review common and divergent friction
    ↓
explicit widening ruling
```

Do not silently add participants merely because the first witness passes.

If a Class A stop condition appears, set `EARLY_FIELD_ENABLED=false`, recreate the MAIA service,
and verify that the ordinary Living Field remains intact.

## 12 · Layered Admission test

This protocol is intentionally an instance of the Layered Admission heuristic:

**Base habitat:** the member's ordinary Living Field and authored material.
**Experimental layer:** the early recursive visual instrument.
**Admission authority:** server-side Early Field cohort decision.
**Consent act after admission:** explicit entry into MAIA, if the member chooses it.
**Fail-closed state:** ordinary Living Field without the instrument.
**Rollback:** disable Early Field; member material and ordinary room remain.

If future Living Field work can no longer name those six things cleanly, this rollout pattern must be
reconsidered rather than mechanically preserved.
