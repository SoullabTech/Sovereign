## Proposed canonical corollary

> **GRANT–EFFECT BOUNDARY**
>
> **Authority existence ≠ authority consumption ≠ authority sufficiency.**
>
> A governed effect may exercise only authority it actually consumes from a governed grant sufficient for that effect, and it may not exceed the scope of that grant.

### Why a corollary rather than a separate universal law

The candidate sharpens the existing Representation Authority Law sequence:

`SUBSTITUTION → GRANT → ATTESTATION`

It states what must remain true **after a grant exists**:

1. the governed effect must actually consume that authority;
2. the consumed authority must be sufficient for the particular effect;
3. the effect must remain inside the grant's scope.

This preserves one constitutional home and does not create a second overlapping universal law.
