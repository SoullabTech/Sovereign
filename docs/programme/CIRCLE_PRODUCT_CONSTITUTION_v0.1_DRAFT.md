## CIRCLE-02A · What is a Circle?

- `[PROPOSAL]` **A Circle is a deliberately bounded sovereign relational field in which members can
  meaningfully remain in relationship with one another around a shared interest or inquiry.**
- `[LAW]` *(founder, Amendment B, 2026-09-06)* **Circle is not the name of the container. Circle is
  the name of a particular quality of relational field.**
- `[DOCTRINE]` The field is primary. Content is secondary.
- `[RESEARCH]` A Circle is constituted by **relational capacity, not headcount** (Supplement R1).
- `[PROPOSAL]` A Circle is the smallest field in this ontology that can **survive the departure of
  any one member** — the property Simmel identifies as separating a triad from a dyad.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-01** *(was A-01, survival-property sense)* — is that survival property
  definitional, or merely typical?
- `[LAW]` **FR-02/FR-03** — a Circle is a bounded sovereign relational field **among three or more
  persons**, with **no ratified numerical maximum**. **Circle begins with plurality, not merely
  connection.**

## CIRCLE-02B · What is not a Circle?

- `[LAW]` *(founder)* A **Commons Circle** is not a Circle. The category is **retired** unless
  restored by founder act.
- `[RESEARCH]` A **community**, **network**, or **audience** is not a Circle: shared membership in a
  container is not mutual relationship (Supplement R9 — Reddit and Discourse are excellent Commons
  and poor Circles).
- `[RESEARCH]` A **feed** is not a Circle. Ranking converts collective visibility into popularity.
- `[DOCTRINE]` A Circle is not a chat room. *"More like entering weather than opening a chat room."*
- `[PROPOSAL]` A **Co-Lab** is not a Circle: its object is the work, not the field.
- `[LAW]` **FR-03 — a dyad is a first-class relational field distinct from a Circle.** *Not a
  statement that dyadic relationship is lesser: two-person relational dynamics have a different
  geometry from a plural Circle.* ⛔ No maximum Circle size may be inferred from this.

## CIRCLE-02C · Relational capacities of Circle

`[RESEARCH]` The candidate capacities that constitute Circle-ness (Supplement R1):

```text
recognizability                       reciprocal addressability
meaningful participation              preserved difference
field legibility                      capacity for rupture and repair
voluntary silence / peripheral participation
individual sovereignty within belonging
```

- `[PROPOSAL]` These are **research dimensions**, never member scores.
- `[DOCTRINE]` *"Coherence is felt and reflected, never scored."* Rendering any capacity as a number
  would violate this and produce performance of the capacity instead of the capacity.
- `[PROPOSAL]` ⛔ **No capacity index.** Aggregating the eight into a single score would recreate
  exactly what Principle 7 forbids.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-02** *(was A-05)* — are the capacities **definitional** or **characteristic**?
- `[COHORT]` Which capacities actually degrade first, and at what point?

## CIRCLE-02D · Fields and ontology

`[LAW]` *(founder, Amendment B)* — with the dyad line pending A-04:

| Field | Unit | Constituted by | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PERSONAL** | the person | self / MAIA / inner process | 1 |
| **DYADIC** `[LAW FR-03]` | 2 persons | mutual relationship | 2 |
| **CIRCLE** | persons (**3+**) | bounded sovereign relational field | *empirical — no ratified maximum* |
| **CONSTELLATION** | **Circles** | shared inquiry exceeding one Circle | several Circles |
| **COMMONS** | Circles + persons | shared-interest ecology | dozens → thousands |
| **CO-LAB** | persons in roles | intentional shared work | sized by the work |

- `[LAW]` **Constellation's primary unit is Circles, not individuals.** It is not defined by headcount.
- `[LAW]` **FR-10 — "Circles scale by multiplication, not enlargement."** Ratified with the
  rationale: *enlargement makes the relational conditions of Circle increasingly expensive to
  preserve; multiplication and nesting allow larger collective intelligence without one field
  absorbing unlimited relational complexity.* ⛔ **No headcount attaches.**
- `[RESEARCH]` Externally corroborated by Ostrom's 8th principle (nested enterprises +
  subsidiarity) and by how 100+ person minipublics achieve deliberative quality — as nested
  facilitated small groups, never one enlarged room.
- `[LAW]` **FR-02 — the ontology is ratified. Six fields.** Defined by **unit, purpose, authority
  and membrane** — never by arbitrary headcount.

## CIRCLE-02E · Membership, entry, leaving, removal

- `[DOCTRINE]` Circle entry is **crossing into a field**, not loading a page.
- `[PROPOSAL]` A member sees the **outer membrane** before the interior: purpose, way of
  participating, what stays private, who hosts. ⛔ **No activity counts, no member counts, no
  social proof.**
- `[PROPOSAL]` Entry is an explicit act. Membership never arrives as a side effect of any other
  crossing.
- `[DOCTRINE]` Leaving revokes the leaver's shares (implemented).
- `[LAW]` **FR-05 — removal is a boundary or safety action, never an interpretive judgment.**
  An ordinary member **cannot** remove another. An authorized facilitator **may**, only where an
  explicit boundary or safety condition has been breached. **Grounds must be recorded. Removal must
  cut access and revoke that member's Circle shares exactly as leaving does, and must never alter
  or delete their original source material.** The removed member **must have a route to request
  review by someone other than the person who enacted the removal.**
- `[OPEN]` **CA-10** — the review institution and richer governance. Open beyond VERIFY.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-08** *(was A-11, rejoining sense)* — does a rejoining member recover prior standing?
- `[COHORT]` What does clean leaving feel like from inside the field, for the leaver and the stayers?

## CIRCLE-02F · Consent and membranes

- `[LAW]` **Sanctuary content never crosses any membrane, by any mechanism, including at member
  request during the session.** (`CLAUDE.md`, absolute boundary.)
- `[LAW]` *(founder ruling 2026-07-17)* **Inferred material may not enter a shared field** without
  explicit member ratification and collective eligibility.
- `[DOCTRINE]` **The Membrane Invariant** — permeable by choice, opaque by default, reversible at
  all times.
- `[LAW]` **FR-01 — *Connection may increase reach, but may never lower the consent level of what
  is being connected.*** A more connected field never acquires greater rights to source material
  merely because it is larger or more connected.
- `[LAW]` **FR-01** — crossing is **explicit · representational · destination-specific · revocable
  where revocation is possible · opaque by default. The source remains untouched.**
- `[LAW]` **FR-08.8 — no membrane crossing is a live pointer giving the destination continuing
  access to the source field.**
- `[LAW]` **FR-08** — the never-crosses list is ratified, with correction (§02O).

## CIRCLE-02G · Participation, inquiry, witnessing

**⛔ This section carries the correction that supersedes DISCOVER-v1.**

- `[LAW]` **FR-04 — contribute-before-see belongs to structured inquiry**, where independent
  perception is load-bearing. Independent judgment before deliberation improves collective
  accuracy; observing first inverts the benefit (Supplement R4).
- `[LAW]` **FR-04 — it does not govern ordinary presence in a living Circle.** Reading without posting yields
  meaningful benefit; lurkers and posters differ little across most empowerment outcomes.
  **Peripheral participation is a legitimate relational posture**, not a deficit.

```text
STRUCTURED INQUIRY                      LIVING CIRCLE FIELD
  Feel → Contribute independently         Arrive → Witness / Offer / Respond / Rest
       → Encounter others                       → Participate when and how one chooses
       → Deliberate / witness → Integrate
```

- `[LAW]` **FR-04 — a member may witness without being compelled to contribute. Quiet presence is
  a legitimate mode of participation.**
- `[DOCTRINE]` One inquiry at a time · one response per member per inquiry · responses hidden until
  contributed. *(All three implemented server-side.)*
- `[LAW]` **FR-04** — the existing doctrine wording **returns for explicit amendment later.**
  ⛔ Doctrine is **not** silently rewritten while implementing the verifier.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-03** *(was A-06b)* — may a member **withdraw** an inquiry response? Today: no
  (Gap G-08) — the only irreversible Personal→Circle crossing.
- `[COHORT]` Do quiet members experience the field as inhabitable, or as a room they are failing?

## CIRCLE-02H · Facilitation and authority

- `[RESEARCH]` **Facilitation ranks first** among viability factors — above purpose, interaction
  structure, duration, and (last) size (Falsification §6). Minipublics achieve integrity at 100+
  through facilitation, not through size.
- `[DOCTRINE]` *(open question in the doctrine itself, unanswered since authoring)* What is a
  facilitator **for**? What must a facilitator **never** do? How does a facilitator close an
  inquiry without collapsing multiplicity into a single narrative?
- `[PROPOSAL]` Roles are held **inside** a Circle, not globally — the same person may be a member
  elsewhere. *(Mirrors the ratified Relationship-Workspace position on practitioner roles.)*
- `[LAW]` **FR-05 — facilitator authority is procedural, not interpretive.** May: hold the
  container · orient members · open and close structured inquiries · facilitate participation ·
  invite attention to boundaries · initiate procedural boundary actions.
  **May not:** determine what the Circle means · declare the correct interpretation of a member ·
  define consensus members have not given · **remove someone merely for disagreement, difference or
  discomfort.**
- `[COHORT]` Facilitator load across size — the variable that most plausibly sets a practical
  ceiling.

## CIRCLE-02I · Rupture, repair, moderation, safety

- `[RESEARCH]` Rupture is multi-directional: member↔member · member↔facilitator · member↔Circle ·
  subgroup↔subgroup · subgroup↔Circle · **Circle↔Circle**. Non-parties are affected and must
  metabolize it. As groups grow and subgroups form, subgroup-level conflict increases.
- `[RESEARCH]` **Candidate principle:** *A living Circle must be capable of rupture and repair
  without requiring rupture to become moderation.*
- `[PROPOSAL]` Four responses that must never collapse:

  | RELATIONAL REPAIR | restore or transform relationship |
  |---|---|
  | **BOUNDARY ACTION** | distance, leave, removal, protection |
  | **MODERATION** | enforcement of shared rules |
  | **SAFETY INTERVENTION** | where relational repair is inappropriate or unsafe |

- `[PROPOSAL]` ⛔ **The Constitution must not assume every conflict should be repaired. It must not
  assume every conflict should be moderated.**
- `[PROPOSAL]` Constellations require a repair geometry of their own, because conflict can be
  Circle↔Circle.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-07** *(was A-11, repair sense)* — ratify the four-way distinction and assign
  authority for each. Open beyond VERIFY.
- `[COHORT]` Rupture frequency; what repair actually requires; whether a Circle can hold conflict
  without a moderator.

## CIRCLE-02J · Lifecycle — rest, completion, birth, separation

`[LAW]` *(founder, Supplement R6)*

```text
FORMATION → COALESCENCE → ACTIVE LIFE → MATURATION
        ┌────────────┬────────────┬────────────┬────────────────┐
     CONTINUE       REST       COMPLETE    DIFFERENTIATE → CIRCLE BIRTH

separately:   RUPTURE → REPAIR / TRANSFORMATION / SEPARATION
```

- `[LAW]` **Generative differentiation and rupture-driven schism are different phenomena** and must
  not share a mechanism.
- `[LAW]` **Rest, completion and birth are not engagement failures.**
- `[LAW]` ⛔ **No headcount-triggered fission.** The system may **notice recurring differentiation,
  reflect it, and ask** whether something new is emerging. **The system may not declare that a
  human field has divided.**
- `[LAW]` *(inherited)* This is the ratified **detect → ask → record** pattern, and it follows from
  the **Constitutional Direction of Authority**: authority moves upward through authored
  experience only.
- `[PROPOSAL]` Substrate consequence: a **Circle** cannot close today — only inquiries can
  (Gap G-14). Completion needs a representation before it can be a real terminus.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-04 / CA-05 / CA-06** — ratify the lifecycle; ratify birth-vs-schism as distinct
  mechanisms; assign authority to initiate birth and separation. **Not ratified by the 2026-09-06
  adjudication** — the no-headcount-trigger rule stands from the prior founder act; the lifecycle
  itself awaits ratification.
- `[COHORT]` How long formation takes; what healthy completion feels like; whether differentiation
  is legible before it becomes schism.

## CIRCLE-02K · MAIA authority — Field Witness vs Common-Ground Mediator

- `[RESEARCH]` ⛔ **The Constitution must not claim AI synthesis is inherently corrupting.** The
  Habermas Machine study (5,734 participants) found AI-generated group statements *preferred to
  human-mediated ones*, groups *less divided*, and minority critiques *incorporated*.
- `[RESEARCH]` **But purpose is decisive.** A Circle is often not seeking an endorsable group
  statement — it may be perceiving unresolved difference, accompanying grief, exploring shadow,
  holding incompatible truths, or letting something remain unanswered.
- `[RESEARCH]` Cautions at real confidence: a Nature review on LLMs reshaping collective
  intelligence; and two **preprints** — minority underrepresentation and input-order bias in
  LLM deliberation summaries, and AI-mediated minority support that raised minority participation
  while **reducing psychological safety.**

**A · FIELD WITNESS — default candidate**
- `[PROPOSAL]` Helps the Circle perceive plurality · recurring questions · difference · tension ·
  movement · unresolved material · emergent connections.
- `[DOCTRINE]` Does not summarize, moderate, or direct. *"MAIA must never become the center of
  meaning inside a circle."*
- `[PROPOSAL]` Does not declare consensus · rank · identify winners · score members · decide what
  the Circle means · resolve disagreement.
- `[PROPOSAL]` *"Several different experiences remain present."* — never — *"The group believes…"*
- `[RESEARCH]` Reflecting **plurality** is pro-accuracy; reflecting **majority** is anti-accuracy.

**B · COMMON-GROUND MEDIATOR — invoked candidate**
- `[PROPOSAL]` Available **only on the Circle's explicit request.** Iterative and member-critiqued:
  members critique MAIA's proposed synthesis rather than MAIA announcing what the group believes.
- `[PROPOSAL]` Any synthesis stays **contestable · member-reviewable · revisable · explicit about
  unresolved dissent.**
- `[PROPOSAL]` ⛔ **Field Witness must never drift silently into Common-Ground Mediator.** Silent
  drift is the failure mode; the role boundary is the control, and it must be visible to members.
- `[LAW]` **FR-09 (direction recorded) — Field Witness is default; Common-Ground Mediator is
  explicitly invoked. Any member may request Mediation. ⛔ No individual member and no facilitator
  may silently switch MAIA into speaking for the Circle.** Activation eventually requires a
  **visible Circle-level consent act.**
- `[OPEN]` **CA-13** — the exact collective-assent mechanism.
- ⛔ **FR-09 is NOT a VERIFY blocker** *(docket v2 wrongly marked it one — see ruling record §0)*,
  and **neither MAIA role is built in VERIFY.**
- `[COHORT]` Whether Field Witness increases or decreases member authority — the single most
  important observation in the programme.

## CIRCLE-02L · Discovery and declared interests

- `[LAW]` **FR-06 — discovery may use declared interests only.** Never: MAIA conversation · memory
  atoms · semantic memory · anchors · inferred themes · psychological interpretation · private
  Circle membership · behavioral surveillance.
- `[RESEARCH]` This is the **competitive fault line.** Circle.so's Connect and Mighty Networks'
  AI matching both use behavioural and activity metadata to recommend people. **Declared-interest-
  only discovery is deliberately less capable and is the point.**
- `[LAW]` **FR-06 — interest declarations are contextual**, defaulted to the relevant **Commons**,
  never silently creating a global interest profile. Private Circle membership is never an input.
- `[OPEN]` **CA-11** — explicit reuse of a declaration in another Commons. **Reuse is another
  member act.**
- `[PROPOSAL]` Substrate note: `visibility` and `invite_enabled` are inert columns implying a
  discovery model that does not exist (Gap G-07). **Do not build to the inert columns** — the
  Constitution defines the model, not the leftover schema.

## CIRCLE-02M · Circle ↔ Constellation ↔ Commons

- `[LAW]` A Constellation **receives only what participating Circles intentionally contribute** and
  **does not dissolve Circle boundaries.**
- `[LAW]` **No Circle automatically receives another Circle's material. No Commons automatically
  receives Circle material.**
- `[PROPOSAL]` There is **no direct Circle↔Circle channel.** Circles relate through a Constellation.
- `[PROPOSAL]` A Constellation may offer an inquiry *into* a Circle; the Circle may decline.
- `[RESEARCH]` Mastodon's federation is the closest working analogue: interoperation without
  surrendering local authority (Supplement R9).
- `[LAW]` **FR-07 — no facilitator, majority, system or larger field acquires the right to
  re-release a member's authored material.** Individually authored: **the author** holds release
  authority and chooses the destination representation. Jointly authored: crosses only via a **new
  representation explicitly created or approved for that crossing**, with **every represented
  contributor retaining consent authority over their contribution**; a facilitator may propose or
  facilitate but **may not consent for other members.**
- `[LAW]` ⛔ **FR-07 — until a collective-release mechanism satisfying this rule exists, collective
  Circle material does not cross.**
- `[OPEN]` **CA-12** — that mechanism.

## CIRCLE-02O · Explicit prohibitions — what never crosses

`[LAW]` **FR-08 — ratified with correction.**

> ⚠️ **The draft's original clause 3 was overbroad and was NOT ratified.** It read *"private MAIA
> conversation content into any shared field, by any path, including summary"* — which would have
> prohibited the very thing the Personal → Circle membrane exists for: **a member choosing a piece
> of their own experience and offering a representation of it.** Jarvis's drafting error, corrected
> by founder act.
>
> **Privacy forbids ambient appropriation. It does not forbid a person's sovereign act of sharing
> their own material.**

### ABSOLUTE
1. **Sanctuary content never crosses**, by any mechanism, **including member request within
   Sanctuary.**

### NEVER AUTOMATICALLY OR BY INFERENCE
2. **Inferred material** never enters a shared field without explicit member ratification and
   collective eligibility.
3. **Private MAIA conversation content never crosses automatically** — not by inference, system
   summarization, recommendation, memory mining, or ambient context.
   ✅ **A member outside Sanctuary may explicitly select or author a representation of their own
   MAIA exchange and intentionally Offer to Circle**, under the normal consent and revocation rules.
4. **A Circle's interior** never becomes visible to a Constellation, Commons, other Circle, or
   Co-Lab. **Only an explicitly released representation crosses.**
5. **Membership** never transfers as an implicit consequence of any crossing.
6. **Identity** never crosses a membrane without the member's explicit act, where identity
   disclosure is not already inherent to the destination relationship.
7. **Counts, scores, ranks, streaks, badges, leaderboards and trust levels** do not become Circle
   social-status mechanisms.

### REPRESENTATIONAL INVARIANT
8. **No membrane crossing is a live pointer giving the destination continuing access to the source
   field.**
