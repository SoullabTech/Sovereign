## 3. Which question each vocabulary answers

| Vocabulary | Its **subject** | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| V1 `LIVE/WARNING/PENDING` | a **capability** | does this presently work, and does it carry an unresolved obligation? |
| V2 `Live/Designed/Vision` | a **claim or statement** | what may we say outwardly, and does the evidence warrant it? |
| V3 `built→wired→surfacing→verified` | an **implementation** | how far has this actually travelled toward the member? |
| V4 `Draft→Frozen→Executed→Accepted` | a **governance artifact** | where is this artifact in its own process? |
| V5 `Cat 1…6` | an **artifact kind** | what kind of thing is this, and what standing does its kind confer? |
| V6 `Canon / Recorded-not-ratified / …` | a **document** | does this document govern? |
| V7 `Class A/B/C` | a **change** | who must approve it? |
| V8 freeze | a **protected artifact** | may this be changed, and by what act? |

⭐⭐ **The subject column is the finding of this act.** Each vocabulary is well-defined *for its own
subject*. What no record declares is **which subject a given standing statement is about** — so a
reader encountering a bare term cannot tell which vocabulary is in play.

## 4. Relationship determinations

### 4.1 Genuine conflicts found: **NONE**

⭐ No two vocabularies were found to contradict one another. ⛔ **The governance model is not
incoherent.** Every problem located is one of *addressing*, not of *contradiction* — which is a
materially different, and far better, situation than the census alone suggested.

### 4.2 Homographs across axes — the actual defect

| Term | Axis 1 | Axis 2 | Determination |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LIVE` / `Live` | V1 — a capability works | V2 — a claim is publishable | ⚠️ **Homograph, different subjects.** A capability may be `LIVE` while a story about it is **not** publishable as `Live`, if the story's centre of gravity rests on `Designed` capability. Declared in `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE` §Center of Gravity. |
| `PENDING` | V1 — designed, not implemented | programme usage — awaiting a decision | ⚠️ Overlapping, subjects differ |
| `Draft` | V4 — artifact lifecycle state | V6 — `Status: Draft specification. Not implemented.` | ⚠️ **Homograph.** One is a lifecycle position; the other is a ratification standing |
| `FROZEN` | V4 — `Draft → Frozen` | V8 — corpus tier protection; programme freeze integrity | ⚠️ Overlapping concept, three different subjects |
| `ACCEPTED` | V4 — artifact accepted | programme — founder acceptance of a lane | ⚠️ Overlapping |

⭐ **This is why an agent becomes confidently wrong.** The terms are not ambiguous *within* an axis.
They are ambiguous because the axis is not stated.

### 4.3 Orthogonal — already declared

- **V7 ⊥ V4.** `THREE_AUTHORITY_CHAINS.md`: ⭐⭐⭐ *"Approval authority is not state authority."*
  Already ruled; this act confirms, does not extend.
- **V4 ⊥ V1.** A specification may be `Frozen` while the capability it specifies is `PENDING`.
- **V6 ⊥ V1.** A document may be ratified canon describing a capability that is `PENDING`.

### 4.4 ⚠️ A relationship the four authorized categories do not cover

The act was authorized to determine whether terms are *synonymous, overlapping, orthogonal, or
genuinely conflicting*. Two relationships found fit **none** of the four, and the records declare
them explicitly:

- **V2 is declared DERIVATIVE of V3**, not orthogonal:
  > *"A 'Live' claim must be answerable to a runtime fact. The marketing word is the public face of
  > the engineering ladder; **it may not outrun it.**"*
- **V1's transition conditions are EXPRESSED IN V3**:
  > `PENDING → LIVE` requires *"built, deployed, and verified in production."*
- **V2 is declared ANCHORED to V5**: *"Live — … (Internal anchor: **Cat 6**)"*.

⚠️ **Flagged, not resolved.** Reporting a dependency the records already declare is classification;
adding "derivative" as a fifth relationship *category* would be vocabulary creation. ⛔ This act
reports the three declared dependencies and does **not** introduce the category. Whether the
relationship taxonomy should carry one is a founder question.

## 5. Transition authority — ruled and unruled

### 5.1 ⭐⭐ Transition CONDITION and transition AUTHORITY are different, and are ruled separately

This distinction was not visible in ACT 1 and is the second substantive result of this act.

| Vocabulary | Transition **condition** (*what must be true*) | Transition **authority** (*who may move it*) |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | ✅ **RULED** — a full transition table with required conditions | ⛔ **SILENT** |
| V2 | ✅ **RULED** — evidence against predeclared criteria | ✅ **RULED**, asymmetrically |

`THREE_AUTHORITY_CHAINS.md` — one is an unaddressed question, the other a **documented absence of
authority**. This act does not merge them.

## 6. Does machine legibility require multiple axes rather than one `status`?

### ⭐⭐ Yes — and the repository already contains the proof, by hand

The canon `**Status:**` header is a single field. Its observed values span **four different
questions**:

| Observed `Status:` value | Question it actually answers |
|---|---|
| `Canon` · `Ratified canon — 2026-07-01` · `Recorded, not ratified` · `Draft specification` | document ratification standing (V6) |
| `Non-negotiable architectural constraint` · `Constitutional — enforceable at the Conductor layer` · `Public binding document` | authority class |
| `NOT authorized for build` · `no implementation authority` · `no runtime authority yet` | authorization state |
| `Diagnostic companion to MAIA_CURRENT_STATE_v1.0.md` · `Phase 1 (prompt-layer injection + domain detection)` | content description — **not a status at all** |

⭐ **The collapse the acceptance tests forbid has already occurred, manually, in the most
authoritative directory in the repository.** A parser reading `Status:` cannot recover which
question is being answered, because the field was never scoped to one.

### The finding, stated precisely

A single artifact may simultaneously and without contradiction be:

```
ratified      (V6 — document standing)
Designed      (V2 — claim layer)
PENDING       (V1 — capability health)
Frozen        (V4 — artifact lifecycle)
Cat 2         (V5 — artifact kind)
```

⭐⭐ **Machine legibility therefore requires `subject + axis + value`, not `status`.** The missing
element is not a richer status vocabulary — it is the **declaration of which subject and which axis a
standing statement is about**. Every vocabulary in §2 is already adequate to its own axis.

⛔ **No such structure is designed, named, proposed, or specified here.** This states what a
legibility mechanism would have to respect, not what it should be. Designing it requires a
later founder act.

⭐ **What this act establishes.** The repository does not have four rival status systems. It has
