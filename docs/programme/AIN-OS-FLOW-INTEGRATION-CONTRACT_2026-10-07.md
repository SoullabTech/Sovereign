## 1 · Purpose

This contract defines how a future validated Flow formalism could enter AIN OS without collapsing epistemic standing, personhood, Elemental symbolism, physiology, or member authority.

> **Flow may become a cross-cutting dynamical layer of AIN OS only after it earns formal standing.**

This document does not authorize runtime use.

---

## 2 · Architectural placement

Candidate future stack:

```text
EVIDENCE / EXPERIENCE
        ↓
TYPED RELATIONS
        ↓
FLOW DESCRIPTION
        ↓
STANDING RESOLUTION
        ↓
FIELD / WEATHER MODULATION
        ↓
GESTALT / SYNTHESIS
        ↓
MAIA RELATIONAL RESPONSE
```

Flow does not bypass standing, create authority, or turn symbolic correspondence into physical fact.

## 3 · Candidate Flow Witness contract

A future Flow Witness may ask internally:

```text
CARRIER
What is claimed to move?

SOURCE / ATTRACTOR
From where, toward what?

PATH
Across which relations/states?

DIRECTION
Inward, outward, reciprocal, circulating, diffusing, oscillating, other?

INTENSITY / RATE
How much and how quickly, if evidence supports these distinctions?

RHYTHM / PHASE
Is movement pulsed, sustained, cyclic, interrupted, entrained?

CONDUCTANCE / RESISTANCE
Where is passage easier or more constrained?

BOUNDARY / PERMEABILITY
What membrane or permission condition shapes passage?

TRANSFORMATION
What changes after passage?

COMPLETION / RETURN
Does the process complete, circulate, return, dissipate, or remain open?

WEATHER
What broader field conditions appear to modulate the above?
```

The Witness may propose only what its evidence supports.

## 5 · Elemental operator layer

If separately validated, Elemental operators act on Flow descriptions rather than define the person.

Candidate interface:

```text
ElementalOperator.apply(flow, context) -> transformedFlowHypothesis
```

A future implementation must permit:

- no elemental operator;
- one operator;
- multiple interacting operators;
- ambiguous operator standing;
- member rejection;
- non-elemental alternative explanation.

No person receives a fixed elemental identity from Flow inference.

## 6 · Weather layer

Weather is not a fifth node in a relational graph.

Candidate role:

> a time-varying field condition that changes the ease, direction, coupling, or transition behavior of flows.

A future Weather projection describes conditions, not destiny.

---

## 7 · Temporal memory

A future Flow-aware memory system must preserve path and change.

Example:

```text
t1:
feeling arises
→ attention moves to explanation
→ affective contact attenuates

t2:
feeling arises
→ attention remains somatic
→ expression becomes relational
→ completion occurs
```

The meaningful object is not a fixed trait. It is change in relational trajectory.

Any longitudinal Flow model must retain timestamp/episode scope, evidence source, standing, revisions, member corrections, and historical supersession.

## 8 · Domain projections

### Writer's Studio
Potential carriers: reader attention, narrative tension, image/metaphor, argument, voice, emotional arc.

Possible use: detect where narrative movement stalls; compare revisions by trajectory; preserve authorial intent as an invariant candidate.

### Relationship Room
Potential carriers: disclosure, care, initiative, repair, attention, influence.

Possible use: reciprocity, interruption, asymmetry, return, boundary crossing. No inference creates facts about an absent third party.

### Dream
Potential carriers: image, affect, association, attention.

Possible use: movement among dream figures/images, recurrence, transformation, unresolved return. MAIA may not assign automatic meaning.

### Living Field
Potential carriers: anonymous aggregate themes, participation, attention, collective affect only where lawfully measured.

Possible use: dynamic field visualization, circulation, cluster transitions. No individual surveillance may be inferred from collective weather.

### Embodied Lab
Potential carriers: breath, HRV, EEG features, movement, sound/vibration parameters, first-person report.

Possible use: multimodal temporal alignment, cross-stream coupling, hypothesis generation. Physiological and phenomenological streams retain separate standing.

> **Relations define pathways and constraints. Flow describes typed movement through those relations. Transformation describes what changes. Weather describes changing field conditions of passage. Elemental operators may provide one phenomenological grammar for characteristic transformations of Flow. Standing determines what any such description is allowed to mean.**

This thesis remains provisional until RGR-05 survives challenge.
