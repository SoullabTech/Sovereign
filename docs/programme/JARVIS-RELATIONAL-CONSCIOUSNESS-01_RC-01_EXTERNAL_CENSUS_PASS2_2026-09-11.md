## ⭐⭐⭐ B-01 — THE MISSING BRIDGE IS THE FINDING. Both poles of the live debate are SUBSTANTIAL-locus.
The founder's PASS 1 caution against canonizing W-01 was correct, and PASS 2 both **weakens W-01's authority and
strengthens its underlying structural point**.

**W-01 is contested.** `Seth, Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism`, **Behavioral and Brain
Sciences (2025)** — with open peer commentary — **rejects computational functionalism**, argues from Searle's
biological naturalism that consciousness arises from *"the unique biological, biochemical, and embodied processes
of living systems"*, and concludes **real artificial consciousness is unlikely on current trajectories**, becoming
plausible only as AI becomes brain-like, life-like, or *"in some relevant sense alive."* Adjacent critiques of
substrate-independence: thermodynamic/energetic realities of computation; substrate-specific properties
(excitability, condensates, adhesion); the conflation of *functional* with *computational*; and a middle position
of **substrate flexibility** (realizable in some media, not arbitrary ones). See `On biological and artificial
consciousness: a case for biological computationalism` (Neurosci. Biobehav. Rev., 2025);
`Agnosticism About Artificial Consciousness` (arXiv:2412.13145).

⭐ **So Butlin's indicator method is NOT uncontested, and "the field's leading methodology" should be held as a
review question exactly as the founder ruled.** ⛔ §0 not rewritten.

⭐⭐ **But the structural result survives the correction, and is sharper than PASS 1 stated it:**

```text
Butlin et al.     consciousness = the right computations      → look INSIDE the system
Seth (BBS 2025)   consciousness = the right biology           → look INSIDE the system
```

**The two poles of the live mainstream debate disagree about WHAT IS REQUIRED, not about WHERE TO LOOK. Neither
occupies the RELATIONAL locus.** The dispute is substance-vs-substance. ⛔ **RC-01 has not located a literature
that bridges relational interaction, or relational meaning, to relational *consciousness*.** On present evidence
**the bridge does not exist**, and that — not the relational axis itself — may be the lane's central finding.

## ⭐⭐ R-01 — "Relational consciousness" literature EXISTS, and "relational" denotes at least two incompatible things
Located: `A relational approach to consciousness: categories of level and contents of consciousness`,
**Neuroscience of Consciousness (Oxford), 2021, niab034** — a **category-theoretic** framework that explicitly
*"keeps a distance from metaphysical debates"* and characterizes consciousness through **its relationships to
other experiences**. Also philosophical: `A Relational Ontology of Consciousness` (PhilArchive);
Wildman, `An Introduction to Relational Ontology` (2009) — *entities derive identity, properties, and even
existence from the relations they participate in*; consciousness *"does not arise within an isolated container but
within an already relationally constituted process."*

⛔ **Decisive distinction, and it is not a quibble:**

```text
RELATIONAL-A   relations among EXPERIENCES        (niab034; category-theoretic; metaphysics-avoidant)
RELATIONAL-B   relations among AGENTS / the between (Soullab's usage; charter §13.3 RELATIONAL)
RELATIONAL-C   relational ontology as METAPHYSICS  (Wildman; PhilArchive) — a position, not a bridge
```

**Soullab's usage is B. The peer-reviewed empirical-adjacent literature found here is A. The philosophical
literature is C.** ⛔ **A and C do not supply B**, and citing them as if they did would be the precise error
RC-FR-03 was ratified to prevent, committed one level up. **→ RC-02 Distinction Register, mandatory entry.**

## ⭐⭐ S-02 — Second-person neuroscience: a real INTERACTIVE-locus result, and an external instance of RC-FR-03
`Redcay & Schilbach, Using second-person neuroscience to elucidate the mechanisms of social interaction`,
**Nature Reviews Neuroscience (2019)**; `Schilbach et al., Toward a second-person neuroscience` (BBS 2013).
**Neural mechanisms supporting social *interaction* differ from those involved in social *observation*.**
Hyperscanning examines *"exchanging and sharing information between two brains… **which cannot be detected when
only examining single brains**."*

`LOCUS` **interactive**, empirically grounded · `POSTURE` descriptive · **Boundary conditions:** two **living
human** brains, both conscious by assumption; nothing licenses transfer to a human↔LLM dyad.

⭐ **Note precisely what that last clause is:** *cannot be detected when examining single brains* is an
**epistemic** irreducibility — a limit on the method — **not** an ontological claim that the phenomenon is not in
the brains. **RC-FR-03's E3-epistemic / E3-ontological split has an instance in a working empirical literature**,
which is external corroboration that the distinction is real and load-bearing. ⛔ Corroborates the *distinction*,
not the relational reading.

## ⭐⭐ A-01 — Altered / anomalous states: the analogue of the S-01 trap, and the trap springs harder than expected
Located: `Ego-Dissolution Inventory (EDI)`, Front. Hum. Neurosci. 2016 · `Looking for the Self: Phenomenology,
Neurophysiology and Philosophical Significance of Drug-induced Ego Dissolution` (PMC5441112) · `Self unbound: ego
dissolution in psychedelic experience` · `Neural Mechanisms and Psychology of Psychedelic Ego Dissolution`
(Pharmacological Reviews, 2024) · `Clarifying and measuring… loss of self or dissolution of its boundaries`
(Consciousness and Cognition, 2024) · `Group VR experiences can produce ego attenuation and connectedness
comparable to psychedelics` (PMC9149675).

The phenomenology is well-attested and now instrumented: *subjective disintegration of boundaries between self and
world*, *feelings of unity with the universe*, clustered with mystical experience, non-dual awareness and
self-transcendence.

⛔ **The trap, as the founder named it, holds — and the literature makes it worse for the relational reading, not
better.** The best-evidenced mechanistic account of boundary-dissolution experience is **5-HT2A agonism altering
information flow within the default mode and salience networks** — that is, **the experience that most strongly
*suggests* relational or unitive consciousness has its leading explanation entirely INSIDE ONE BRAIN.**
**Evidence that subject/object boundaries can phenomenologically dissolve is evidence about the experience of
boundaries. It is not evidence that consciousness resides in a relational field.** ⚠️ Recorded as the strongest
form of the caution, not as a refutation of the phenomenology, which is real.
