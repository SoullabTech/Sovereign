## 1 · Why this lane exists

The census (`SPI-CENSUS-01`) recorded **D9 — representation / impersonation boundary** as the
dimension with the **least canonical custody**: no canon document, prompt-level expression only, a
refusal registry containing **no representation refusal**. The precise claim, not the stronger one:

> **Soullab has a strong declared stance against collapsing MAIA into the member, while that stance
> currently has weak canonical custody and little explicit refusal instrumentation.**

⛔ **It does not mean non-impersonation does not exist.** The stance is real; its custody is thin.

**And the roadmap made it load-bearing.** Governance sits on the critical path twice, and was the only
owner in the sequence with no programme behind it. `SPM-00` can complete its object, its substrate
mapping and its member surfaces without this lane moving at all — **and MAIA integration and governed
action cannot begin.** A governance step that never arrives strands everything downstream of SPM.

> ⭐ **The reciprocal boundary, pinned in both lanes:**
>
> **SPM may describe the human model without authorizing its use; governance may authorize uses of
> the model without redefining what the model says is true.**

⭐ **It cuts both ways.** SPM must not decide what may be done with the model; **and this lane must
not, by ruling on use, change what the model asserts is the case.** A permission regime that quietly
edits the record it governs is the mirror image of the failure SPM's charter names. ⛔ If a ruling
here would only work by altering a claim's origin, basis or status, **it is the wrong ruling** — the
question belongs back to SPM.

## 3 · The two questions

### G1 · Consumption

> **Who or what may consume a personal-model claim, for what purpose, and under what authority?**

**Carried in from SPM-01 v0.2 §4.2**, where the column was left deliberately unanswered for every
origin and status. SPM settled only the adjacent, SPM-owned question — *may this claim currently
function as model belief?* — and stopped there.

Two proposals arrived from SPM marked as proposals, ⛔ **not decisions**: that claims which are
`disputed`, `superseded`, or of `unknown provenance` should not be consumable as current belief. They
enter here as input, with no standing.

### G2 · The constitutional boundary

> **What is the constitutional boundary between knowing a person · relating to a person ·
> representing a person · speaking or acting for a person?**

The collapse this exists to forbid, written as it would actually occur:

> *"MAIA knows this about Kelly, therefore MAIA may act or speak as Kelly."*

⛔ **Four permissions, and the sentence works by never pausing between them.** G2's output must make
each pause explicit and refusable.

**Scope note:** `JARVIS-as-actor` is in scope as a **consumer**; `JARVIS-as-method` — the work
discipline building SPM — is **not** (SPM charter §3.1). Same name, two roles.
