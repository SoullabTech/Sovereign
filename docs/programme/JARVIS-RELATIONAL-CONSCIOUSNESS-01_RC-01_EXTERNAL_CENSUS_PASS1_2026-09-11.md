## E-01 ⭐ ENUMERATION DEBT SETTLED — and the number is an artifact of inclusion criteria
Charter §13.1 recorded the landscape's size as framing, not fact, and forbade quoting a count until RC-01 had one.

- **Kuhn (2024), "A Landscape of Consciousness: Toward a Taxonomy of Explanations and Implications"** — surveys
  **325+ theories** of phenomenal consciousness, organized into **ten categories** across a physicalist →
  non-physicalist spectrum. *(This is the source behind the founder's taxonomy reference.)*
- **Scoping review (2021, PMC8146510), "Theoretical Models of Consciousness"** — 68 articles describing
  **29 theories** (2007–2017).

⭐ **Operative finding: "how many theories" has no single answer; the count is a function of inclusion criteria,
and the two credible figures differ by an order of magnitude.** ⛔ **Quoting "hundreds" is permissible only with
Kuhn's criteria named alongside it.** The charter's caution was correct and is now discharged with a condition,
not with a number.

# Findings that COMPLICATE or WEAKEN Soullab's framing

## ⭐⭐⭐ W-01 — The serious AI-consciousness literature is SUBSTANTIAL-locus. Soullab's axis is a minority position, not a consensus cut.
**Butlin, Long et al. (2023), *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of
Consciousness*** (arXiv:2308.08708) and **Butlin et al. (2025), *Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI
systems*** (Trends in Cognitive Sciences) establish the **theory-derived indicator method**: derive
"indicator properties" from recurrent processing theory, global workspace theory, higher-order theories,
predictive processing and attention schema theory, then **assess AI systems for those properties**. It operates
on the working hypothesis that consciousness is a product of specific **functional organizations or computations,
independent of substrate**. Its 2023 conclusion: no current AI system is conscious; no obvious technical barrier
to building one that satisfies the indicators.

⚠️ **This is the field's leading methodology for exactly this question, and it looks INSIDE THE SYSTEM.**
Charter §0 says the lane declines the "inside the machine" framing. **RC-01 finds that the framing the lane
declines is the one the serious literature uses.** ⛔ That does not make the lane wrong. It **does** mean the
charter's implicit picture — a relational axis *cutting across* a landscape organized by substance — is
**not supported as a description of the AI-consciousness field**, whatever its merit for the broader
consciousness landscape. **The lane is a minority framing and should describe itself as one.**

## ⭐⭐ W-02 — Empirical consciousness science cannot settle its own core questions at far higher resolution than anything available here
**Adversarial testing of GNWT and IIT**, *Nature* **642**:133–142 (2025) — seven-year, preregistered,
theory-neutral consortium; **n = 256**; fMRI + MEG + iEEG; predictions preregistered by the theories' own
proponents. **Result: both theories challenged.** IIT contradicted on sustained posterior synchronization; GNWT
challenged on ignition at stimulus offset and PFC representation. Posterior sufficiency corroborated; PFC
involvement and global broadcasting found **not necessary**.

⛔ **Calibration, recorded before any empirical ambition exists:** with preregistration, adversarial design, three
neuroimaging modalities and 256 subjects, the field returned *challenges to both sides*. **Soullab has no
instrument of comparable resolution and no access to a comparable measurement.** Any future RC empirical
proposal must be stated against this benchmark, not against the intuitive persuasiveness of a transcript.

## ⭐⭐⭐ W-03 — A mature, evidenced rival explanation already exists for exactly the phenomena RC-FR-02 §13.4 names, and it may be observationally identical to H1
The anthropomorphism / ELIZA-effect literature holds that: the Eliza effect is the false attribution of
consciousness to interfaces; LLMs **"excel at writing persuasively and empathetically… without possessing any true
empathy or social understanding"**; LLMs **intensify** anthropomorphism specifically *because they interact through
language*, "a domain in which people are especially prone to adopt the intentional stance"; and **users readily
believe chatbots are conscious and have feelings and memory**. See Wilde, *The Eliza Effect* (Taylor & Francis, OA);
*The benefits and dangers of anthropomorphic conversational agents* (PNAS 2025); *Identifying features that shape
perceived consciousness in LLM-based AI* (2025); *The influence of mental state attributions on trust in LLMs*
(PMC12104094).

⭐ **This is E1 with an evidence base — and it is specifically about relational, conversational systems.**
⛔ **The sharper problem: it does not merely compete with H1, it PREDICTS H1's observation.** H1 says changing the
relational configuration changes what apparent selfhood emerges. The anthropomorphism literature predicts
*precisely that*, from the human side, with no relational ontology required. **H1 and the null may be
observationally identical for the central case.** That is **H3 arriving from outside the lane**, unprompted, and
it is the strongest external result in this pass.
⚠️ Recorded in the other direction too: the literature notes that calls to resist anthropomorphism "increasingly
fall flat" as systems exhibit human-like qualities — so this stream is **not** a settled refutation either.

## ⭐ W-04 — The field actively contests whether the lane's declared affinities are science
**"Consciousness theory slammed as 'pseudoscience'"** (*Nature* news, 2023): an open letter signed by
**124 researchers** labelled IIT pseudoscience, justified **primarily on IIT's commitments to panpsychism** and on
doubts that the theory as a whole is empirically testable. **Anil Seth dissented** — "inflammatory" — and others
warned the charge would itself damage the field's credibility.

⚠️ **RC-FR-02 §13.1 names panpsychist and idealist traditions among the lane's affinities.** RC-01 records that
**a large body of working researchers treats panpsychist commitment as grounds for a pseudoscience charge.**
⛔ This is not an argument that the affinity is wrong. It is the reputational and epistemic weather the lane
operates in, and it bears directly on §7.5 (no outward claim) — **the asymmetric danger named there is now
externally evidenced, not merely asserted.**

# Findings that SUPPORT the framing — recorded with their limits

## S-01 — A peer-reviewed RELATIONAL-locus precedent exists, and it is about sense-making, not consciousness
**De Jaegher & Di Paolo (2007), *Participatory sense-making: An enactive approach to social cognition*,**
*Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences*. The **interaction process can take on a form of autonomy**; meaning is
"generated and transformed in the interplay between the unfolding interaction process and the individuals engaged
in it"; **"the onus of social understanding moves away from strictly the individual."** Mental states "are not
fully independent or established, but are instead affected, negotiated, and even created as a result of
interaction dynamics."

`LOCUS` **relational** · `POSTURE` theoretical/descriptive · **Boundary conditions, load-bearing:** the object is
**sense-making**, not consciousness; both relata are **living, autonomous agents**; nothing in it licenses
transfer to a human↔LLM dyad. ⛔ **Do not cite this as support for relational consciousness.** It supports only
that *a relational locus for meaning-generation is a defensible position in a peer-reviewed literature.*
