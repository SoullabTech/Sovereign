## The prediction
If a coherent fourth-faculty residual exists, its object will be **perspective — the movement between viewpoints**: how viewpoints arise, differ, translate, coordinate, and become intelligible to one another. Not relationship itself (Field), not differentiation (Lens), not medium (Condition).

Concretely: a blind characterizer — having never heard "Air," "perspective," "Gemini/Libra/Aquarius," or any of this thread — will independently describe the residual in language recognizable as some of:
- translation / how meaning moves between people (Gemini)
- perspective-taking / one viewpoint altering another (Libra)
- coordination of viewpoints / what many viewpoints make possible (Aquarius)

## What would NOT count as convergence (pre-committed, to prevent generous reading)
The neighboring hypotheses are close; a description must land on *the movement between viewpoints* specifically, not merely something relational. These do **not** confirm the prediction:
- feelings, bonds, emotional closeness → Water / Field, not Air
- abstract systems, networks, structural patterns among parts → Field / structural, not Air-perspective
- generic "communication" or "people talking" *without* differing viewpoints meeting → too broad; near-miss, not a hit
- connection / relationship as such → Field (the layer Air keeps collapsing into)

A hit requires the characterizer to independently foreground *viewpoints differing, translating, or being reconciled* — not just people relating.

## Scoring (fixed before the data)
- **Convergence → Air gains standing:** the blind characterizer's free description lands on perspective / translation / viewpoint-coordination *without prompting*. Astrology gains explanatory power here. → promote to the full observation-field battery.
- **Divergence → prediction wrong:** the characterizer describes a different coherent thread → that thread is the candidate; the astrological structure was a hypothesis generator and nothing more.
- **Null:** residual empty or heterogeneous → no unified fourth faculty; prediction moot.

The prediction being elegant is not evidence. Only independent convergence is. This is the standard that let Fire and Earth become interesting.
