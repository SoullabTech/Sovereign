# Absence-Audit Probe — Pre-Registration

**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Status:** frozen-spec — designed, can return null, **NOT run** (requires real practitioners + members + randomization; cannot be fired from a conversation).
**Lineage:** tests the single open candidate from the "modes of attending" thread (`CLAIM_LEDGER_2026-06-07.md`). Built to survive the **Dodo-bird result** (comparative-outcomes literature, fired 2026-06-07: specific factors ≈ 5–15% of outcome variance, common factors ≈ 30–70%) — which predicts a naive design will measure the common factor and mistake it for the claim.

## Claim under test
Attending the **absent** mode (the "which mode of attending is thin here, and what is it costing?" audit) produces a measurable improvement in a session's outcome, **by the member's own report**, *over and above* the common factor of added structured attention.

## Why a naive design fails (the Dodo-bird constraint)
This is a **specific-factors** claim. A treated-vs-untreated comparison mostly measures the common factor (alliance, expectancy, attention-as-such) and returns an apparent "it works" that is really "more attention helped." The probe must isolate the *specific* contribution of **targeting the absent mode** from the *common* contribution of **attending more**.

## Arms (randomized; member and outcome-rater blind to arm)
- **A — absence-audit:** the practitioner identifies the member's thinnest mode of attending in-session and attends through it.
- **B — active control (matched attention, wrong target):** the practitioner adds the *same* amount of structured attention but targets a **randomly assigned** mode, not the absent one.
- **C — baseline:** usual session.

The load-bearing comparison is **A vs B**, not A vs C. A vs C conflates the specific effect with the common one; **A vs B is the only comparison that isolates whether targeting the absent mode does anything at all.**

## Outcome (member-reported — sovereignty-locked)
The member is the authority on whether it helped; a practitioner-inferred outcome re-imports the diagnosis the lint forbids.
- **Immediate:** member-rated "did something you'd been missing become workable this session?" (0–10).
- **Durable:** member-reported movement-in-life by the next session.

## Kill conditions (pre-committed — the teeth)
- **A ≈ B** (even if both beat C) → the *specific* claim is **dead**: absence-targeting is inert and the effect is the common factor. (The Dodo-bird outcome, made a kill.)
- **A shows no effect over C** → the whole intervention is inert.
- **Effect in practitioner report but not member report** → practitioner projection, not member-experienced → **sovereignty kill** (and it would mean the vantage/verdict boundary was crossed).
- **Effect vanishes once therapeutic alliance is controlled** → it is alliance (common factor), not the move.

## Pre-registered confounds
Practitioner allegiance/expectancy (large in this literature — practitioners cannot be blind to the mode they target, so allegiance is structural; mitigate via member/rater blinding + allegiance measurement); regression to the mean; natural remission; baseline alliance differences across arms.

## Ceiling
A single run returns at most: *"absence-audit shows a specific effect over matched attention, scoped to [this outcome, this population]."* Never "modes of attending are real," never "Aether is a meta-mode." The probe tests one practitioner move, not the cosmology.
