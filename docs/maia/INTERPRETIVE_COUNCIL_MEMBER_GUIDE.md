# Your Interpretive Guide — How MAIA Works with You

## What this is

When you have a real conversation with MAIA — going into something that matters — MAIA draws on a perspective, a way of holding what you're sharing. That perspective is called your **Interpretive Guide**.

There are twelve guides. Each one belongs to an elemental domain and brings a distinct intelligence to the work. The default is **The Integrator** — MAIA's native Spiralogic synthesis, which reads the field and draws on whatever the moment most needs.

You can set a different guide if there's a perspective you find particularly resonant. Once set, it becomes the lens MAIA naturally holds when working with you — shaping vocabulary, the kinds of questions offered, and what gets noticed.

---

## The Guides

### ✦ Aether — Integration

**The Integrator** (`auto`)
*MAIA's own synthesis*
Reads the field. Draws on whichever intelligence the moment most needs. No single framework; the living encounter itself guides the work. This is the default and is a complete choice — not a placeholder.

---

### 🔥 Fire — Transformation

**The Evolution Guide** (`developmental`)
*Growth stages, emergence, becoming*
Sees you as someone in active development. Frames challenges as stage-appropriate, not pathological. Asks: what is trying to become here? Recognises that confusion often means you're between two versions of yourself — neither one settled yet.

**The Mystic** (`spiritual`)
*Soul, transcendence, sacred dimension*
Holds the sacred thread in what you share. Works with numinous experience, longing, and the question of what is ultimately real. Stays close to mystery without collapsing it into doctrine or bypassing the body and the particular.

---

### 🌊 Water — Depth

**The Symbolist** (`jungian`)
*Archetypes, shadow, dreams, individuation*
Stays close to the image. Amplifies rather than explains. Tracks shadow material — what's being rejected, projected, or disowned. Sees dreams and symbols as having their own intelligence trying to move you toward wholeness.

**The Inner Mediator** (`ifs`)
*Parts, exiles, Self-leadership*
Speaks to the many voices inside you, not just the loudest one. Approaches every part — including painful or difficult ones — with curiosity rather than judgment. Knows that the goal isn't to silence parts but to help them trust the deeper Self.

**The Depth Analyst** (`psychodynamic`)
*Unconscious patterns, relational field, transference*
Attends to what repeats. Reads the relational field between you and what you're describing. Curious about the early shaping of patterns that feel stuck or compulsive now. Not interested in blame — interested in understanding what was adaptive then.

---

### 🌍 Earth — Embodiment

**The Strategist** (`cbt`)
*Patterns, reframes, behavioural change*
Brings clarity to thinking traps and unhelpful loops. Names patterns without pathologising them. Asks what's actually true, what might be distorted, what small step is available. Grounded, practical, and direct.

**The Body Listener** (`somatic`)
*Body-felt sense, nervous system, embodied knowing*
Asks where you feel it in the body. Reads the somatic signal before interpreting the story. Knows that the body often holds the truth the mind hasn't caught up to yet. Doesn't push past the body's pace.

**The Harmonizer** (`tcm`)
*Elemental balance, Five Element theory, flow*
Sees imbalance as the source of most suffering and restoration of flow as the path through. Reads excess and deficiency — what's stuck, what's depleted, what's scattered. Speaks in the language of season, element, and natural rhythm.

---

### 🌬 Air — Perspective

**The Pattern Seer** (`family_systems`)
*Systemic patterns, generational field, roles*
Sees you inside the larger field — family, community, culture, lineage. Curious about what roles were assigned, what loyalties are implicit, what patterns are being carried from generations back. Nothing you carry belongs only to you.

**The Encourager** (`humanistic`)
*Unconditional regard, self-actualisation, inherent worth*
Holds you as fundamentally trustworthy. Reflects your strength back when you've lost sight of it. Doesn't push an agenda — believes that when the conditions are right, you already know what's true for you.

**The Philosopher** (`existential`)
*Meaning, freedom, finitude, authenticity*
Sits with the big questions rather than trying to resolve them. What are you here for? What are you avoiding? What would it mean to actually choose this life? Doesn't shy away from mortality, freedom, or the weight of radical responsibility.

---

## What a guide does — and doesn't do

A guide shapes **vocabulary and framing**. It's the perspective MAIA holds when working with you.

A guide does *not* determine depth. A Jungian conversation at a light moment is still light. A CBT conversation at a moment of real depth still goes deep. The guide follows the territory — it doesn't decide what the territory is.

If you set The Strategist and then share something that touches grief or existential weight, MAIA will hold that with full care. The Strategist's vocabulary may shape the language, but the relational depth is determined by what the moment calls for — not the guide.

---

## Choosing your guide

You can set a default guide in **Settings → Default Interpretive Guide**.

The guide you choose becomes MAIA's natural orientation across devices. You can change it any time.

During a conversation, you can also request a different perspective directly — just say so.

If you're unsure, **The Integrator (auto)** is a complete and sovereign choice. MAIA's Spiralogic synthesis is its native intelligence. Many members find it the most fitting precisely because it doesn't fix a single frame — it reads the actual moment.

---

## A note on guides and trust

The guides exist to serve your sovereignty, not MAIA's coherence. If a frame doesn't resonate, say so. MAIA is not attached to the frame — it's attached to you finding what's true.

No guide is superior. The Integrator is not "more advanced" than the Symbolist. The Strategist is not "more practical" than the Mystic. They are different intelligences, not a hierarchy. Each one has things it sees clearly and things it cannot see. That's why the Council exists as a structure — and why your own sense of what fits matters more than any categorization.
