# Soullab Helper Infrastructure Charter

**Vessels for Care, Not Vehicles for Scale**

## Purpose

Soullab's Helper Infrastructure exists to support people who actively **help others** by offering **ethical, non-extractive vessels** for their work.

This offering is designed to **reduce harm**, **support coherence**, and **preserve sovereignty** — for helpers, and for those they serve.

It is not designed to accelerate growth, manufacture authority, or professionalize unfinished inner work.

---

## What This Is

Helper Infrastructure provides **structural support** — not validation, status, or leverage.

It may include:

* Websites, portals, or private applications
* Containers for courses, circles, or guided programs
* Adapted MAIA components for helper-specific contexts

These tools exist to **hold relationships**, not to drive outcomes.

They are **vessels**, not engines.

---

## What This Is Not

Helper Infrastructure does **not** exist to:

* Build personal brands
* Enable scaling strategies or passive income
* Confer legitimacy, credentials, or authority
* Replace relational discernment with technology
* Lock helpers into long-term dependency on Soullab

Soullab does not certify helpers, endorse practices, or guarantee results.

---

## Ethical Orientation

All Helper Infrastructure is guided by the following principles:

1. **Care over scale**
   Infrastructure should reduce distortion, not amplify reach.

2. **Sovereignty over dependency**
   Tools must strengthen the helper's agency — not bind them to Soullab.

3. **Structure without authority**
   Templates provide containment, not legitimacy.

4. **Relationship before system**
   Technology serves the relationship, never the reverse.

5. **Minimal dependency by design**
   All engagements conclude with a **clean handoff** and no structural reliance on Soullab for ongoing operation.

---

## Eligibility & Gatekeeping

Helper Infrastructure is available only to:

* Active Pro members (minimum 3 months)
* Helpers with an existing practice or community
* Those who arrive through their own inner work, not as a shortcut around it

The gate is intentional.
It protects both the helper and the field.

---

## Boundaries & Refusal

Soullab reserves the right to decline any request that:

* Prioritizes growth, scale, or monetization over care
* Seeks authority transfer or implied endorsement
* Resists ethical boundaries or scope clarity
* Attempts to convert infrastructure into a funnel

Refusal is not judgment.
It is part of ethical containment.

---

## Responsibility Statement

Helper Infrastructure confers **responsibility**, not status.

These tools can affect others.
Use requires clarity of role, respect for boundaries, and ongoing discernment.

Soullab's role is to help helpers **not cause harm while helping others**.

---

### This charter exists so the work stays clean —

even when demand increases,
even when money is involved,
even when future versions of Soullab are built by people who were not here at the beginning.
