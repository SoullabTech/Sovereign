I find myself doing this with my kids, my clients, as well as in group sessions—any place where I have an obligation to seek peace, harmony, and resonance. It is part of the medicine work to be able to experience the ‘poison’ or disharmony in the world and transform it into an internal state of ‘medicine’ or harmony. We have such a beautiful capacity to be transformative agents. The task is to be aware of these states of harmony and disharmony and the way we dance with them.
