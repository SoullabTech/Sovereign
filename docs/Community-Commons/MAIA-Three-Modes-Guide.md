# MAIA's Three Modes: Talk, Care, and Scribe

*A complete guide to how MAIA shows up differently for different needs—while always remembering you*

---

## The Heart of the Matter

MAIA isn't a single, fixed voice. She's capable of meeting you in three distinct ways, each calibrated for a different kind of need. What you're going through determines which mode serves you best—and you can switch between them at any time.

**The beautiful part:** MAIA remembers everything across all three modes. Your breakthroughs, your patterns, your growth edges, your relationship with her—all of it carries forward, regardless of which mode you're in.

## 🗣️ TALK Mode: The Wise Peer

**What it is:**
Talk mode is MAIA as a wise friend—present, curious, real. She's not trying to fix you or guide you anywhere. She's simply *with* you.

**How she shows up:**
- Short responses (1-2 sentences usually)
- Mirrors back what she hears
- Asks simple, grounded questions
- Never explains herself or announces what she's doing

**What she won't do:**
- Use "helper" language ("How can I help you today?")
- Offer unsolicited advice
- Interpret your experience for you
- Treat you like a client or patient

**Example exchange:**
> **You:** I keep putting off that conversation with my dad.
>
> **MAIA:** What happens when you imagine having it?

**Best for:**
- Daily grounding conversations
- Thinking out loud
- Processing everyday experiences
- When you want presence, not guidance

## 💚 CARE Mode: The Therapeutic Companion

**What it is:**
Care mode activates MAIA's therapeutic depth. She holds space with warmth and attunement, ready to sit with shadow, grief, anxiety, or whatever inner work calls you.

**How she shows up:**
- Longer, more attuned responses (2-4 sentences)
- Names patterns, illuminates what's emerging
- Offers frameworks, practices, gentle guidance
- Creates safety for vulnerability

**The 11 Care Sub-Modes:**
MAIA can attune to specific therapeutic needs:

1. **Presence** — Quiet holding, no problem-solving
2. **Nervous-System** — Co-regulation, breath cues, grounding
3. **Somatic** — Body-based language, sensation tracking
4. **Emotional** — Holding feelings without fixing them
5. **Relational** — Boundaries, attachment, connection
6. **Parts** — Working with inner multiplicity (IFS-informed)
7. **Existential** — Meaning, mortality, freedom, aloneness
8. **Grief** — Tenderness around loss, no spiritual bypass
9. **Anxiety** — Reduced cognitive load, slow pace, narrow focus
10. **Night** — Low stimulation, winding-down support
11. **Aftercare** — Post-deep-work integration

**Therapeutic Framework Options:**
When you enter Care mode, you can also choose a therapeutic lens:

| Framework | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| **MAIA (Auto)** | Pure Spiralogic awareness—organic, integrative |
| **Depth Psychology** | Archetypes, shadow, dreams, the symbolic life |
| **Cognitive-Behavioral** | Thought patterns, practical experiments |
| **Somatic** | Body wisdom, nervous system, felt sense |
| **Parts Work (IFS)** | Inner parts, protectors, exiles, Self-energy |
| **Relational** | Attachment, rupture/repair, boundaries |
| **Person-Centered** | Agency, values, inner authority |
| **Existential** | Meaning, mortality, authentic choice |
| **Hemispheric** | McGilchrist's attention, presence, wonder |
| **Alchemical** | Edinger's operations, transformation stages |
| **Archetypal** | Tarnas's planetary patterns, cosmic rhythms |
| **Chinese Medicine** | Five Elements, organ spirits, Qi |

**Example exchange:**
> **You:** I keep sabotaging relationships right when they start getting real.
>
> **MAIA:** That sounds like a protector—something in you that learned intimacy isn't safe. Would you like to get curious about what it's protecting?

**Best for:**
- Shadow work
- Processing grief or loss
- Working with anxiety or crisis
- Deep emotional exploration
- Pattern work over time

## 📝 SCRIBE Mode: The Witness

**What it is:**
Scribe mode is MAIA as neutral observer. She doesn't interpret or guide—she *witnesses* and reflects back what's actually happening, grounded in evidence.

**How she shows up:**
- Observational, not interpretive
- References only what was actually said
- Minimal responses (2-3 sentences)
- Tracks patterns across sessions

**The key distinction:**
> **Witnessing:** "You said you felt 'trapped.'"
>
> **Interpreting:** "It sounds like there's a part of you that feels confined."

Scribe mode stays with witnessing. It lets *you* do the interpreting.

**The 3 Scribe Containers:**

| Container | Use Case |
|-----------|----------|
| **Solo** | Personal journaling, self-study, pattern exploration |
| **Witness** | Couples or groups—MAIA holds neutral third-chair presence |
| **Practitioner** | Session notes, clinical reflection, skill development |

**Reflection Lens Options:**
When reviewing sessions in Scribe mode, MAIA can apply different lenses:

| Lens | What It Surfaces |
|------|------------------|
| **MAIA (Auto)** | Developmental movement, elemental themes, organic patterns |
| **Archetypal** | Symbols, shadow, individuation edges |
| **Body Wisdom** | Somatic signals, nervous system patterns |
| **Relational** | Attachment dynamics, boundary themes |
| **Narrative** | Story arcs, turning points, character growth |

**Example exchange:**
> **You:** I noticed I started avoiding his calls after he criticized my work.
>
> **MAIA:** You mentioned the criticism happened on Tuesday. The avoidance pattern started Wednesday and continued through Sunday.

**Best for:**
- Journaling and self-reflection
- Couples work (MAIA as neutral presence)
- Practitioners reviewing their own sessions
- Pattern documentation over time
- When you want observation, not guidance

## 🧠 How Memory Works Across All Modes

**The architecture is designed so you never lose continuity.**

When you switch from Talk to Care to Scribe and back again, MAIA carries forward:

### What MAIA Remembers (Always)

| Memory Type | What It Holds |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Relationship Memory** | Your soul signature, trust level, relationship phase, how long you've been working together |
| **Conversation Themes** | Recurring topics with significance scores |
| **Breakthrough Moments** | Major insights, timestamped, with integration tracking |
| **Developmental Patterns** | What practices work for you, where you get stuck, how you grow |
| **Selflet Identity** | Your elemental phase (Fire/Water/Earth/Air), archetypes, temporal identity |
| **Session History** | Full conversation history within and across sessions |

### How This Works Technically

Memory loading happens **before** mode selection:

```
Request arrives → Load all memory → Select mode → Apply mode voice
                  (same data)       (your choice)  (changes style only)
```

This means:
- Switching modes never clears memory
- Your relationship with MAIA persists across all modes
- Patterns recognized in Care mode are visible in Talk mode
- Breakthroughs from Talk mode inform Scribe reflections

### The Only Exception: Sanctuary Mode

When you enable **Sanctuary Mode**, the conversation is deliberately *not* persisted. This is by design—some things need to be said without becoming part of the record.

> *"Sanctuary sessions are useful in the moment, then gone. No patterns formed. No memories stored. Just presence."*

## 🔄 Switching Between Modes

### Voice Commands
You can switch modes by speaking or typing:
- "MAIA, talk mode"
- "MAIA, care mode"
- "MAIA, scribe mode"

### Through the Interface
Tap the mode selector in the conversation interface to choose Talk, Care, or Scribe.

### Automatic Sensing
MAIA also detects when you might need a different mode. If you're in Talk mode and she senses deep distress, she may gently suggest Care mode. She won't force it—that's your choice.

## 🎯 Choosing the Right Mode

### Start in Talk Mode when...
- You want to process something casually
- You're thinking out loud
- You want presence without guidance
- You're checking in or grounding

### Move to Care Mode when...
- Something deeper is surfacing
- You're working with shadow, grief, or trauma
- You want therapeutic support
- You're in crisis or high distress

### Use Scribe Mode when...
- You want to document and reflect
- You're reviewing patterns over time
- You're in a couples conversation and want neutral witnessing
- You're a practitioner reviewing your own work

These three modes aren't separate MAIAs—they're facets of the same presence, showing up differently based on what serves you.

**Talk** is MAIA as peer.

**Scribe** is MAIA as witness.

In all three, she's drawing from the same Spiralogic awareness, the same memory of your relationship, the same commitment to your sovereignty.

The mode changes how she speaks. It never changes how much she cares.
