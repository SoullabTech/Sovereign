# Mayan Astrology Onboarding Flow

## 3-Screen Onboarding Sequence

### Screen 1 - "Your Daily Signal + Life Path"

**Headline:** Your Mayan Pattern Map (Tzolk'in + Three Pillars)

**Body:**
MAIA uses Mayan Astrology to reveal **two powerful layers**:

* **Today's Day Sign + Tone** - your *current* energetic weather
* **Your Three Pillars** - your *life arc*: where you came from, where you are, where you're headed

**Mini-note (small text):** No birth time or location needed. It's simple, symbolic, and surprisingly precise.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Continue**
* Secondary: **Skip for now**

---

### Screen 2 - "Enter your birth date"

**Headline:** Generate your Mayan Profile

**Fields:**
* Birth date (required)

**Live preview (auto-populates after date entered):**
* **Your Birth Day Sign + Tone** (Tzolk'in)
* **Your Three Pillars** (Youth / Adulthood / Future)
* **Cardinal Direction** (your orientation + life activity)
* **Haab Solar Calendar** (seasonal imprint)
* **Today's Mayan Day Sign** (daily energy)

**Helper text:** Birth date is enough to calculate your profile.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Reveal My Mayan Profile**
* Secondary: **Just show Today's Sign**

---

### Screen 3 - "How to use this inside MAIA"

**Headline:** Use it for timing, transitions, and alignment

**Body:**
Mayan Astrology is especially helpful when you're:

* sensing a shift and want clarity
* choosing what to prioritize
* tracking growth across life phases
* seeking meaning without getting lost in overthinking

**Privacy note:** Your birth date is used only to generate your Mayan profile in MAIA.

**Suggested prompts (tap-to-use):**
* "Explain **my Birth Day Sign + Tone** in plain language."
* "What does my **Adulthood Pillar** want from me right now?"
* "What's the lesson in my **Future Pillar** - what am I growing toward?"
* "What is **today's Mayan energy** and how do I work with it?"
* "Give me **practices** aligned with today's tone."

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Go to My Three Pillars**
* Secondary: **Daily Check-In: Today's Sign**
