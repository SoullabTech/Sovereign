# Chinese Astrology in MAIA

## Overview + User Manual

### What this is

MAIA's Chinese Astrology portal connects you to the **Ba Zi (Four Pillars of Destiny)** and **Da Yun (10-Year Luck Cycles)** systems - revealing:

* **Your Four Pillars** - Year, Month, Day, Hour pillars with Heavenly Stems & Earthly Branches
* **Your Da Yun Timeline** - 10-year life phases showing where you've been, where you are, and where you're headed
* **Your Elemental Balance** - Which of the Five Elements dominate or need cultivation
* **TCM Health Integration** - Organ systems, Five Spirits, and health guidance for each life phase

It's designed for **self-understanding, life timing, and health cultivation** - not fatalistic prediction.

## 3) Understanding the Four Pillars (Ba Zi)

The Four Pillars are calculated from your birth moment using the Chinese calendar:

### Year Pillar
* Represents **ancestors, family karma, early childhood**
* Changes at Chinese New Year (varies Feb 3-5)
* Determines your zodiac animal

### Month Pillar
* Represents **parents, education, youth (0-30)**
* Changes at each solar term (Jie Qi)
* Foundation for Da Yun calculation

### Day Pillar
* Your **true self** - the Day Master
* Most important pillar for personality analysis
* Changes at midnight

### Hour Pillar
* Represents **children, old age, inner desires**
* Reveals hidden aspirations
* Requires accurate birth time

Each pillar combines:
* **Heavenly Stem (Tian Gan)** - 10 stems cycling (Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui)
* **Earthly Branch (Di Zhi)** - 12 branches (zodiac animals)

## 4) Understanding Da Yun (10-Year Luck Cycles)

Da Yun reveals the **major life phases** that shape your destiny. Unlike Western astrology's transits, Da Yun shows predictable 10-year chapters.

### How It's Calculated

1. **Cycle Direction** - Based on gender + year stem polarity
   - Male + Yang year OR Female + Yin year = Forward cycle
   - Male + Yin year OR Female + Yang year = Backward cycle

2. **Start Age** - Distance from birth to next/previous solar term (divided by 3)
   - Typically starts between age 1-10

3. **Luck Pillars** - Progress through the 60 Jia Zi cycle from your Month Pillar

### What Each Period Shows

* **Element theme** - Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water emphasis
* **Zodiac animal** - The animal archetype for that decade
* **Life theme** - "Building Foundation," "Full Radiance," "Precious Wisdom," etc.
* **Health focus** - TCM organ pair and cultivation practices
* **Opportunities & Challenges** - What the decade offers and demands

## 5) TCM Integration (Traditional Chinese Medicine)

Each element governs specific organ systems and spirit aspects:

### Wood (Liver/Gallbladder)
* **Spirit:** Hun - The Ethereal Soul
* **Governs:** Vision, planning, dreams, flexibility
* **Season:** Spring
* **Cultivation:** Gentle movement, green foods, emotional expression

### Fire (Heart/Small Intestine)
* **Spirit:** Shen - The Spirit/Consciousness
* **Governs:** Joy, connection, identity, clarity
* **Season:** Summer
* **Cultivation:** Heart-opening practices, meaningful connection, adequate sleep

### Earth (Spleen/Stomach)
* **Spirit:** Yi - The Intellect
* **Governs:** Thought, focus, nourishment, stability
* **Season:** Late Summer
* **Cultivation:** Regular meals, warm foods, grounding routines

### Metal (Lung/Large Intestine)
* **Spirit:** Po - The Corporeal Soul
* **Governs:** Breath, boundaries, letting go
* **Season:** Autumn
* **Cultivation:** Breathwork, honoring grief, clear boundaries

### Water (Kidney/Bladder)
* **Spirit:** Zhi - The Will
* **Governs:** Wisdom, essence, determination
* **Season:** Winter
* **Cultivation:** Deep rest, facing fears, bone broth, reflection

## 9) Accuracy notes

* Chinese astrology uses the **solar calendar** for pillars (not lunar)
* Month changes at **solar terms (Jie Qi)**, not the 1st of the month
* Hour Pillar requires knowing your birth time + timezone
* Da Yun start age is approximated from solar term distance

## 11) Quick FAQ

### "What's the difference between Year Pillar and Day Master?"

Year Pillar is your public face and ancestral imprint. Day Master (Day Pillar stem) is your true inner self. For personality analysis, Day Master is primary.

### "Why does gender matter for Da Yun?"

It determines whether your luck cycles progress forward or backward through the 60 Jia Zi sequence. This is traditional methodology, not about character differences.

### "Can I use this with Western astrology?"

MAIA supports both systems. Your Western chart shows planetary influences on personality; Chinese astrology shows elemental balance and life timing.

### "What if I don't know my birth time?"

You can still get Year, Month, and Day Pillars plus full Da Yun calculations. Only the Hour Pillar requires birth time.

### "How is this connected to TCM?"

The Five Elements in Chinese astrology directly map to TCM organ systems and spirits. Your elemental balance affects physical health, emotional patterns, and spiritual development.
