# Chinese Astrology Onboarding Flow

## 3-Screen Onboarding Sequence

### Screen 1 - "Your Elemental Blueprint + Life Timing"

**Headline:** Your Chinese Pattern Map (Four Pillars + Da Yun)

**Body:**
MAIA uses Chinese Astrology to reveal **two powerful layers**:

* **Your Four Pillars** - the elemental forces that shape your nature
* **Your Da Yun Timeline** - 10-year cycles showing where you've been, where you are, where you're headed

**Mini-note (small text):** Birth time is optional but improves accuracy. Gender is used only for cycle direction.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Continue**
* Secondary: **Skip for now**

---

### Screen 2 - "Enter your birth details"

**Headline:** Generate your Chinese Astrology Profile

**Fields:**
* Birth date (required)
* Birth time (optional)
* Gender (required)

**Live preview (auto-populates after details entered):**
* **Your Four Pillars** (Year, Month, Day, Hour)
* **Your Day Master** (your true self)
* **Your Elemental Balance** (dominant/deficient elements)
* **Your Current Da Yun** (10-year period)
* **TCM Focus** (organ pair + spirit)

**Helper text:** Gender determines Da Yun cycle direction. Birth time enables Hour Pillar.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Reveal My Chinese Profile**
* Secondary: **Just show Year Sign**

---

### Screen 3 - "How to use this inside MAIA"

**Headline:** Use it for timing, health, and self-understanding

**Body:**
Chinese Astrology is especially helpful when you're:

* making major life decisions
* wondering why certain decades feel different
* seeking health guidance rooted in TCM
* wanting to understand your elemental nature

**Privacy note:** Your birth data is used only to generate your Chinese profile in MAIA.

**Suggested prompts (tap-to-use):**

* "What's the theme of **my current Da Yun period**?"
* "What elements am I **deficient in** and how do I cultivate them?"
* "What **TCM practices** align with my current decade?"
* "How do my **Four Pillars** shape my personality?"

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Go to My Da Yun Timeline**
* Secondary: **View My Four Pillars**
