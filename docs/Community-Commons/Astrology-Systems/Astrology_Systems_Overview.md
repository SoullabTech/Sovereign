# Astrology Systems Overview

## Three Lenses, One Truth

MAIA integrates three distinct astrological systems, each offering a unique perspective on your cosmic blueprint. When multiple systems align on a theme, pay attention - that's signal, not noise.

| System | Origin | Primary Focus | Time Scale |
|--------|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Western** | Greco-Roman | Psychological archetypes, planetary influences | Seasonal (tropical zodiac) |
| **Chinese** | Ancient China | Elemental balance, life cycles, TCM integration | 10-year Da Yun cycles |
| **Mayan** | Mesoamerican | Sacred purpose, galactic alignment | 13-month Tzolkin + life stages |

---

## System Summaries

### Western Astrology
**Core question:** *Who am I, psychologically and archetypally?*

- **Birth Chart:** Sun, Moon, Rising + planetary positions
- **Life Cycles:** Saturn returns (~29y), Chiron return (~50y), Uranus opposition (~42y)
- **Key insight:** Your rising sign is how you meet the world; your moon is your emotional truth

### Chinese Astrology (Ba Zi / Four Pillars)
**Core question:** *What forces shape my life, and when do they shift?*

- **Four Pillars:** Year, Month, Day, Hour - each with Heavenly Stem + Earthly Branch
- **Da Yun:** 10-year luck cycles showing elemental themes per decade
- **TCM Integration:** Each element governs organ pairs, emotions, and spirit aspects
- **Key insight:** Your Day Master (day pillar stem) is your true self; the luck cycles show timing

### Mayan Astrology (Tzolkin)
**Core question:** *What is my sacred purpose in the greater cosmic order?*

- **Day Sign (Nahual):** Your soul essence from the 20 day signs
- **Galactic Tone:** Your creative number (1-13) showing how you manifest
- **Trecena:** 13-day wave cycles for short-term timing
- **Three Pillars:** Past (conception) - Present (birth) - Future (destiny)
- **Key insight:** You chose this incarnation for specific soul work

## Cross-System Patterns

### When Systems Align

Look for resonance across systems. Examples:

| Western | Chinese | Mayan | Theme |
|---------|---------|-------|-------|
| Fire Sun/Mars | Fire element dominant | Serpent or Sun signs | **Leadership, passion, transformation** |
| Strong Saturn | Metal element | Jaguar or Death signs | **Structure, discipline, ancestral work** |
| Moon in Cancer | Water element, Pig/Rat | Water Lily (Muluc) | **Emotional depth, intuition, flow** |
| Uranus prominent | Wood element | Wind (Ik) | **Innovation, change, communication** |

### Element Correspondences

| Western | Chinese | Quality |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Fire | Fire | Passion, action, visibility |
| Earth | Earth | Stability, manifestation, grounding |
| Air | Wood/Metal | Communication, growth, refinement |
| Water | Water | Emotion, intuition, wisdom |

## Integration Prompts

Use these when synthesizing your readings:

1. **Where do all three systems agree?** This is your core essence.

2. **Where do they differ?** This shows the complexity of your being.

3. **What timing aligns?** When Western transits, Chinese Da Yun, and Mayan trecenas all point to similar themes - act.

4. **What's your elemental balance across systems?** Are you fire-dominant everywhere, or is one system showing you a different facet?

## Practical Application

### For Daily Guidance
- Check your **Mayan trecena** for the 13-day theme
- Note **Western transits** for planetary weather
- Remember your **Chinese Da Yun** decade theme as backdrop

### For Major Decisions
- Consult all three systems
- Look for **at least 2/3 alignment** before major moves
- Pay special attention to **challenging aspects** - they're growth opportunities

### For Self-Understanding
- Western: Psychological patterns and shadow work
- Chinese: Life timing and health cultivation
- Mayan: Soul purpose and spiritual direction

## The Synthesis Principle

> "When the stars of three traditions point the same direction, the universe is speaking clearly. When they diverge, you're being shown multiple facets of a complex truth. Both are valuable."

The goal isn't to find "the right" system - it's to use each lens to see aspects of yourself that others miss. Together, they form a more complete picture than any single tradition could offer.
