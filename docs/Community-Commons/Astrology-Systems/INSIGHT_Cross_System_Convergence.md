# Cross-System Convergence (Mini Insight)

**For tooltips, cards, and "How MAIA Thinks" UI elements**

---

## 7-Line Version

> When Western, Chinese, and Mayan astrology independently point to the same theme, pay attention - that's signal, not noise.
>
> Convergence = confirmation. Divergence = complexity.
>
> Before major moves, look for 2-of-3 alignment across systems.
>
> When systems disagree, you're not getting wrong information - you're seeing different facets of a complex truth.

---

## Tooltip Copy

**Cross-System Alignment:** "Multiple ancient systems pointing to the same theme = stronger signal."

**Divergence:** "When systems disagree, you're seeing complexity - Fire in timing, Water in feeling, Earth in purpose."

**2-of-3 Rule:** "Before major decisions, check if at least two systems align on the theme."
