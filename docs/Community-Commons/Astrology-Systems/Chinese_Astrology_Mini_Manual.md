# Chinese Astrology in MAIA

## Your Four Pillars + 10-Year Luck Cycles

Chinese Astrology isn't just "what animal am I?"
In MAIA, it becomes a **life timing system** - revealing your elemental nature, the forces shaping each decade, and practical health guidance rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine.

### What you'll get

**1) Your Four Pillars (Ba Zi)**
Eight characters from Year, Month, Day, and Hour - showing your complete elemental blueprint.

**2) Your Da Yun Timeline**
10-year luck cycles showing:

* **Past** - the decade that shaped you
* **Present** - the forces active now
* **Future** - what's coming next

**3) TCM Health Integration**
Organ pairs, Five Spirits, and cultivation practices for your current life phase.

---

## How it works (simple version)

MAIA uses your birth date and gender to calculate:

* **Four Pillars** - Heavenly Stems + Earthly Branches for each pillar
* **Day Master** - your true self (Day Pillar stem)
* **Elemental Balance** - which elements dominate or need cultivation
* **Da Yun cycles** - your 10-year life phases

Then it shows you:

* your **elemental nature** (what drives you)
* your **current decade theme** (what you're living through)
* your **health focus** (what to cultivate)
