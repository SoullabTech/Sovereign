# Mayan Astrology in MAIA

## Your Galactic Signature + Path of Life

Mayan Astrology isn't just "what animal am I?"
In MAIA, it becomes a **soul map** - revealing the archetype you embody, the patterns that shaped you, and the destiny you're growing into.

### What you'll get

**1) Your Galactic Signature**
A unique combination of **Solar Seal** (archetype) + **Galactic Tone** (expression quality) from the 260-day Tzolk'in calendar.

**2) Your Path of Life**
Three pillars showing your soul's journey:

* **Youth** - what shaped you (birth to ~26)
* **Adulthood** - who you are now (your core signature)
* **Future** - where you're headed (~52 onward)

**3) Today's Energy**
See the current day's galactic signature - useful for timing and daily intention.

## How it works (simple version)

MAIA uses your birth date to calculate:

* **Your Galactic Signature** - Solar Seal + Galactic Tone
* **Your Path of Life** - three archetypal pillars
* **Your Haab Date** - position in the 365-day solar calendar
* **Your Cardinal Direction** - cosmic orientation (East/North/West/South)

Then it shows you:

* your **core identity** (who you are)
* your **past influences** (what shaped you)
* your **future emergence** (where you're evolving)
