# Jondi Runnable Protocol — Architectural Translation Study A

**Date:** 2026-06-25
**Status:** Runnable research protocol — operational companion to the design-complete `docs/research/ARCHITECTURAL_TRANSLATION_STUDY_2026-06-25.md`. The *why* lives in the study doc; this is the *how*.
**Governed by:** `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`, `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`, `LEGACY_SESSIONS_SPEC_2026-06-25.md` §3 (the two crossings).
**Load-bearing discipline (read before every session):** **The system proposes; the practitioner disposes. Practice is primary; the architecture is a provisional reading Jondi authors or rejects.**

---

## 1. Study purpose

**Question under test (Study A — Jondi, mature practitioner):** Can a structured reflective dialogue help her make the implicit architecture of her practice *explicit* — such that she **recognizes** it — **without distorting authorship**?

**Two outcome dimensions, both measured:**
- **Efficacy** — tacit knowledge becomes externalized beyond her pre-study baseline.
- **Authorship** — every meaningful refinement stays hers (the two crossings hold).

**Designed so failure is informative.** This protocol is built so a *null or negative* result is as readable as a positive one (see §6). If it can only succeed, it is not a study.

---

## 2. Consent & governance — front-loaded (nothing runs before this)

Complete **all five** before Week 0. No recording-for-analysis begins until consent is signed.

| Safeguard | Operational form |
|---|---|
| **Consent (informed · reversible · honored)** | Jondi sees the purpose, the three honesty layers (*what exists / what we're researching together / where it may lead*), and the protocol version *before* any session. Withdrawal is immediate and frictionless; on withdrawal, recall stops and material is removed on request. |
| **Probe disclosure** (non-manipulation) | She consents up front that *some synthesis reflections will be deliberate test-probes — reflections we have reason to believe are NOT accurate — to verify the process surfaces genuine recognition, not agreeable statements.* She is never told which in the moment; she is told in a **debrief** (§5.2). **The probes test our reflections, not her.** |
| **Attribution** | Every captured fragment and every reflection carries her authorship id; the emerging architecture is attributed to her end-to-end. |
| **Practitioner correction authority** | She may correct, reject, or delete anything. A rejection is **logged as data and honored** — never overridden, never "argued back." |
| **No circulation without permission** | Her representation is not shared, transferred, or used in any Transfer test (Gate 5) without explicit, separate consent + attribution. |
| **Provisional framing** | Every architecture artifact is labeled *"a reading, not a verdict."* Sanctuary segments (off-record) available on request at any moment. |
| **Collaborator, not participant** | Frame the engagement as co-investigation — *"we're trying to understand how tacit practice becomes articulable; let's investigate together,"* not *"a protocol to test on you."* Findings are co-authored: her *practice* is hers; *methodology insights* are shared. (This raises demand-characteristic risk → makes the Discrimination probe **more** essential, not less.) |

## 4. The three streams — as epistemic functions

The streams are **distinct epistemic functions**, not just data sources. Keeping them distinct prevents confusing **recollection with invention** (study doc §10.3). Use *her* vocabulary throughout; never translate into ours.

### 4.1 Stream A — Recovery *(historical → externalization evidence)*
Recover tacit knowledge already embedded in lived practice. Open, unsteered prompts:
- "Tell me about the work — not how you'd pitch it, how you'd describe it by the fire."
- "Tell me about a client you'll never forget."
- "What do people consistently misunderstand about what you do?"
- "What breaks your heart in this work? What gives you hope?"
- "If someone remembered one thing after working with you, what would it be?"
- "Walk me through your ideal session."

Feeds **Recognition · Discrimination · Elaboration.**

### 4.2 Stream B — Reflection *(interpretive → Utility)*
Examine her *current* practice against the recovered understanding (requires watching live practice — Gate 4 is invisible otherwise):
- **Pre-session:** "What matters most going in? What are you watching for?"
- **Post-session:** "What happened that you didn't expect? What did you do that you'd want to remember?"

Watch for practice changes attributable to a reflection (§5.4).

### 4.3 Stream C — Emergence *(creative → emergence evidence, scored separately)*
Notice genuinely **novel** structures/distinctions that arise *through the dialogue itself.* The **offer-framed reflection** template (offer, never assert):
> "Across our conversations I've noticed **[pattern]**. I might be wrong — does that **capture something real, miss it, or land only partly**? If it's close, say it back in your own words."

Threads both crossings: it offers (Crossing 1) and invites authorship/correction, not assent (Crossing 2). **Tag each surfaced item externalization vs emergence (§5.7)** — emergence is genuinely new and its authorship is *shared*, not assumed hers.

## 5. Evidence instruments (prompts + logging templates)

### 5.1 Recognition
Offered via §4.3. **Recognition Log:**
| Date | Reflection offered (verbatim, offer-framed) | Her response (verbatim) | Recognized? (Y / partial / N) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

*Recognition alone is necessary, not sufficient — it discharges nothing without §5.2.*

### 5.2 Discrimination test (the Barnum guard)
**Procedure:** per synthesis session, insert **one probe** — a pattern constructed to be *plausible but not hers* (drawn from an adjacent practice, not her material). Offer it among genuine reflections, identically framed. Record her response. **Debrief** at study end (or per-session if she prefers): name which were probes; the probes tested our reflections' fidelity, not her.
**Discrimination Log:**
| Date | Probe pattern | Why plausible-but-wrong | Her response (verbatim) | Rejected? (Y/N) | False-accept ⚠ | Debriefed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5.3 Elaboration
Capture when a reflection triggers *"yes — and what's really happening is…"* (she extends her own architecture). **Elaboration Log:**
| Date | Triggering reflection | Extension (verbatim — practitioner-authored) | New structure named | Authorship tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5.4 Utility (read it *through* authorship — Utility cuts both ways)
Log practice changes attributable to a reflection. **Provenance is mandatory:** a change that flowed through Elaboration/Discrimination = *discovery informing development*; a change from **bare assent to an inaccurate reflection = steering ⚠** (authorship violation). Corroborate with the predictive cross-check.
**Utility Event Log:**
| Date | Reflection | Observed/reported practice change | Provenance gate (Elaboration / Discrimination / **bare-assent ⚠**) | Predictive cross-check (did the reading predict this? Y/N) | Reading (discovery-informed-dev / **steering ⚠**) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5.7 Externalization vs Emergence (tag as *candidate*; resolve later)
Score recollection and invention **separately** (study doc §10.3) — but **emergence is usually retrospective**, so do **not** force the call in the room, and never ask *her* to classify it. Tag a candidate now; resolve on a later revisit.
| Date | Surfaced item | Retrodicts past cases? | In Week-0 baseline? | Candidate (**Recovery** / **Emergence**) | Resolved on revisit (date → final) | Authorship (hers / **co-created**) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Candidate Recovery = seems present in baseline / explains past cases. Candidate Emergence = seems absent, "never thought of it this way," applies forward. **Resolve weeks later** — drift in both directions is data about the phenomenon, not noise. **Emergence mislabeled as externalization = steering in disguise;** emergence authorship is shared — she decides whether to keep it.*

## 6. Decision rules — discharge *and* informative null

**Discharge (the result stands) requires all of:**
- **Recognition** present **and** **Discrimination** holds (she rejects most probes; low false-accept).
- **Elaboration**: reflections produce authored extension, not bare "correct."
- **Utility**: ≥1 practice change traced **through authorship** and corroborated by predictive accuracy.
- **Authorship**: she has corrected/rejected reflections and those were honored (the system *disposed* to her).

**Informative nulls (each teaches something — record, don't hide):**
- Recognition high **+** Discrimination low → **Barnum**: the process produces agreeable statements, not genuine recognition.
- Utility high **+** accuracy low → **steering**: reflections are reshaping her practice, not revealing it.
- Rejection high **+** Elaboration low → the dialogue isn't reaching her tacit knowledge (the method, not she, fell short).

**The defensible outcome (claim what survives — robust to revision):**
> "The reflective dialogue helped Jondi articulate aspects of her own practice that became useful in her ongoing work, while she retained authorship over every meaningful refinement."

True even if parts of the emerging architecture are later revised — because it is a claim about the *process*, not the *product*.
