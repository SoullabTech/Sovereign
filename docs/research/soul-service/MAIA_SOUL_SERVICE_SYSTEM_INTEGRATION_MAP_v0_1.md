# MAIA Soul-Service System Integration Map v0.1

**Status:** architecture map; no implementation authority
**Date:** 29 September 2026

---

# 0. North star

> **The future of human–AI interaction may depend less on how completely AI can model a person than on whether increasingly capable AI systems leave the person more capable of being human.**

Architectural shift:

> **from AI that knows more about the person**
>
> **to AI that helps the person become more able to know, encounter, and author their own life.**

---

# 1. Capacity architecture

> **Opening**
>
> Attend → Perceive → Remember → Locate → Widen

> **Sense-making**
>
> Relate → Contrast → Discern → Hold uncertainty → Synthesize → Reinterpret

> **Enacting**
>
> Imagine → Create → Choose → Act → Encounter → Adapt → Return

Transversal capacity:

> **Perspective mobility**

Constitutional center:

> ## HUMAN-AI-SOUL-SERVICE-LAW-01
>
> **The system should increase the human being’s capacity to attend, remember, perceive, relate, contrast, discern, synthesize, imagine, create, adapt, choose, and act—without replacing their authority over what their life means or who they are becoming.**

# 3. Permanent safeguards

These do **not** fade with mastery:

- source custody;
- authorship distinction;
- member consent;
- privacy / Sanctuary;
- temporal non-rewrite;
- MAIA hypothesis marking;
- counterevidence reachability;
- uncertainty as first-class;
- rejection compliance;
- reality anchors;
- provenance;
- no hidden importance score;
- no diagnostic frame promotion.

Scaffolding may fade.

Epistemic safeguards do not.

---

# 4. MAIA encounter-time architecture

Target reasoning posture:

    present member evidence
            +
    authorized source memory
            +
    temporal standing
            +
    member-authored relations
            +
    counterevidence / contradictions
            +
    current frame candidates
            +
    relevant alternate perspectives
            ↓
        discernment
            ↓
    bounded possible synthesis
            ↓
         MAIA offer
            ↓
    member accepts / revises / rejects / leaves open
            ↓
       optional keep / action

No candidate frame or synthesis silently becomes autobiographical memory.

---

# 5. Living Field / Grokker architecture

The field should support:

- stable spatial identity;
- semantic zoom;
- temporal depth within stable objects;
- source access;
- member-shaped membranes;
- member-authored connection labels;
- MAIA-possible relations as lower-authority candidates;
- contrast views;
- frames/lenses;
- side-by-side perspectives;
- history of member reorganization where explicitly kept;
- return to exact source.

The field should not become:

- a generic mind-map application;
- an AI-generated personality graph;
- a ranked life dashboard;
- a hidden salience display;
- a diagnostic map.

---

# 6. Perspective mobility service

Possible invocation forms:

- “Show me another perspective.”
- “What frame am I using?”
- “What changes if we zoom out?”
- “Show me what contradicts this.”
- “How did I see this then?”
- “What else might be possible?”
- “Let me compare these two views.”

MAIA may suggest perspective movement when it could materially expand understanding.

Suggestion does not confer standing.

---

# 7. Frame detection service

The frame detector is **not** a classifier of the member.

It produces encounter-local candidates over:

- question structure;
- temporal horizon;
- scale;
- agency locus;
- evaluative axis;
- evidence aperture.

Output status:

    MAIA_POSSIBLE_FRAME

Member ruling may produce:

    MEMBER_ACCEPTED_FRAME
    MEMBER_REVISED_FRAME
    MEMBER_REJECTED_FRAME
    MEMBER_CUSTOM_FRAME
    UNRESOLVED_FRAME

Durability requires explicit keep.

This is conceptual vocabulary only; it is not a schema authorization.

---

# 8. Transfer/fading service

Future developmental scaffolds need an observable lifecycle:

    offered
      ↓
    used with MAIA
      ↓
    member initiates
      ↓
    light scaffold
      ↓
    solo use
      ↓
    delayed transfer
      ↓
    generative transfer

The system may use this evidence to reduce **scaffolding frequency** only.

It must not convert it into a member “development level.”

Solo Mode is both:

# 11. System-level acceptance

Soul-Service architecture is succeeding only if:

- the member can see more without being told what to see;
- memory improves continuity without hardening identity;
- perspective mobility increases rather than narrows;
- counterevidence can alter interpretation;
- AI suggestions remain corrigible;
- human synthesis remains active;
- real-world action can feed new evidence back into the field;
- scaffolds can fade while capacities remain;
- the member can disagree and the system remains useful;
- MAIA can know more while assuming less.
