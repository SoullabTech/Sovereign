# What this programme has established

The first Soul-Service sequence now has concrete architecture for:

1. **constitutional purpose** — human capacity, not AI indispensability;
2. **frame awareness** — aperture without diagnosis;
3. **perspective mobility** — multiple ways of seeing without steering;
4. **contrast** — counterevidence can change synthesis;
5. **spatiotemporal continuity** — time inside stable identity;
6. **member-shaped relation** — connections have authorship;
7. **Solo Mode** — Soullab remains useful without generated interpretation;
8. **transfer measurement** — assistance is not learning;
9. **planned obsolescence** — scaffolds fade, safeguards remain;
10. **longitudinal falsification** — developmental claims require persistence and generalization.

---

# What remains unproven

This programme does **not** establish that:

- Soul-Service improves human capacities;
- transfer actually occurs;
- stable geography improves memory;
- member-generated mapping outperforms AI-generated mapping;
- scaffold fading is beneficial for all members;
- perspective mobility improves real-world decisions;
- Soullab produces clinical or therapeutic outcomes.

Those remain empirical questions.

---

# Constitutional test preserved

> **If MAIA becomes more capable while the member becomes less capable, the architecture is failing.**

And:

> **If the scaffold disappears and the capacity disappears with it, we provided assistance.**
>
> **If the scaffold disappears and the capacity remains, learning occurred.**
>
> **If the person uses that capacity creatively in contexts the system never taught, development occurred.**
