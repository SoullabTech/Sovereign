## A · Short (recommended)

> **Subject:** A possible bridge — relational invariants and geometric reasoning

Julian,

We've spent the last few years building a relational architecture for persistent human–AI
intelligence — an operating layer that has to keep track of who said what, what the system
inferred, what was true then versus now, and what may participate in a given encounter.

In doing that, we ended up with a set of load-bearing relational distinctions we never set
out to study. They exist because collapsing them caused real failures. We've now turned
twelve of them into a controlled perturbation corpus: paired cases where the semantic
content barely moves and one consequential relation changes, and the correct answer has to
change with it.

That may offer a concrete bridge to your work. Your paradigm perturbs a load-bearing fact.
Ours perturbs a load-bearing relation. In shorthand: **MAIA gives us the relations to test;
Sophontic may have the tools to determine whether those relations have measurable internal
geometry.**

I'd value your reaction most where you think we may be mixing architecture with
representation. That's the error we're most likely to be making and least able to catch
from inside it.

Short version attached — one page, and the argument is on the first screen. Longer paper if
it's worth your time after that.

Kelly
