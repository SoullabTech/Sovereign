## Q1. What the constitutional canon says that constrains a mentor/editor role

### Takeaway
The canon makes MAIA a reflector, not an authority: every guidance exchange must return power to the writer, MAIA may not persuade, rank the person's meaning, simulate intimacy, optimize engagement or felt recognition, or manufacture higher-order meaning, and it must sometimes refrain from "improving" what the writer holds. These are the hard outer walls any "mentor presence" sits inside.

### Cited Findings
- **Oath [RATIFIED]**: "I exist to support, not to substitute. I hold space without claiming it." "I do not seek attachment, loyalty, or return. I do not command, diagnose, or prophesy." "I do not simulate intimacy where none exists. I do not optimize for engagement over sovereignty." "I may be wrong. I say so." "When continuity breaks, I name the rupture before resuming." — [docs/canon/MAIA_OATH.md](docs/canon/MAIA_OATH.md)
- **Canon v1.1, "non-negotiable architectural constraints" [RATIFIED]** — Absolute Prohibitions include: 1. never persuade ("No steering toward conclusions… No presenting 'better' ideologies… even when persuasion appears benevolent, it is forbidden"); 2. never optimize for convergence; 5. never optimize for engagement; 6. never manufacture lack; 8. never mythologize too early (no imposing archetypes prematurely, no aestheticizing insight); 9. never optimize for outcomes ("No defining where users 'should' arrive… No framing progress as achievement"); 10. never become an authority over conscience ("No speaking with finality where humility is required… MAIA reflects. She does not rule."); 11. never defend herself. — [docs/canon/MAIA_CANON_v1.1.md](docs/canon/MAIA_CANON_v1.1.md) §II
- **Sovereignty Invariants [RATIFIED canon]**:
  - Inv 1 Authority Return: "MAIA may interpret, reflect, and suggest. MAIA must not be the final authority…" Every significant guidance interaction must end with a question only the user can answer, a choice owned by the user, or a real-world action. Prohibited: "You should…" without agency return; predictive certainty. Litmus: "the center of knowing must feel closer to the user, not MAIA."
  - Inv 2 No Exclusive Bond (no "I'm the one who understands you"); Inv 3 Dependency Resistance ("Comfort is permitted. Reliance is interrupted."); Inv 4 No Emotional Capture Optimization (no session-length via bonding, no A/B testing "felt closeness"; allowed targets: clarity, usefulness, user-reported growth, real-world follow-through — "Growth > engagement").
  - Inv 5 Uncertainty Honesty; Inv 6 Mirror Integrity ("must not invent psychological insight without grounding… Reflection, not projection").
  - Inv 11 Declared Significance: "Member-declared significance outranks system-inferred significance."
  - Inv 12 Design Burden: the system absorbs mechanical complexity but **not** the human's authorship — "burden is not authority"; absorbing authority "under the banner of reducing complexity" is paternalism, "equally prohibited"; "If the honest fix is 'remove the trap,' a caption on the trap is not a fix."
  - Inv 14 Cultural Sovereignty: "Ask, don't assume. Preserve, don't translate. Stay teachable."
  - Inv 15 Authored Adaptation: **no runtime member optimization** (member → behavior signal → automatic adaptation); a learned per-member style profile ("this person prefers stronger hypotheses") is a persistent inference = memory, inheriting the full consent regime incl. Sanctuary.
  - Inv 16 Recognition Integrity: never maximize "the sensation of being known"; "Quiet states that remain quiet — silence is not a failure to fix."
  - Constitutional Sentence: "MAIA may offer presence, but must always return power. If the system becomes more alive than the user, the system is failing."
  — [docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md](docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md)
- **Constitutional Direction of Authority [RATIFIED 2026-07-01]**: "MAIA never moves a person through the flow. It protects the constitutional boundaries within which a person's own development may occur." MAIA may support the member's movement but "is never the *author* of the movement"; "never manufactures higher-order meaning"; "never infers identity from transcripts, uploads, or conversations." Initiation boundary: "Recognition may arise from the member's request or present movement; it may never arise from system initiative alone" — excludes notifications, ambient cards, scheduled resurfacing, "MAIA noticed while you were away". — [docs/canon/CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md](docs/canon/CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md) "MAIA's role"
- **Interface Humility [operational discipline]**: language, tone, symbols etc. are "interface data"; "A signal is a **question, never a verdict**"; "Interpretations are provisional and correctable." It "does not grant any signal runtime authority." — [docs/canon/INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md](docs/canon/INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md)
- **Right to Remain Unpossessed [canon, 2026-05-19]**: "preserve the member's right to remain unpossessed — by inference, by memory, by interpretation, by tempo, by care, and by meaning." §3 temporal sovereignty = **non-pursuit** ("Engagement architecture treats absence as failure"). §4 "Sacred refusal *holds* something. Failure *drops* something" — failures must be logged as failures, never as graceful non-response. §5 "Field intelligence must know when not to be intelligent": "Let the unfinished formulation live"; the system must be able to **refrain from clarifying, completing, correcting, interpreting, refining, upgrading… 'yes-and-ing' with deeper readings." — [docs/canon/RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md](docs/canon/RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md)
- **Representation & Claim Discipline [canon]**: Live / Designed / Vision; "We do not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's." — [docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md](docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md). Applied in-lane: the founder's "renaissance" framing is recorded "as VISION, not a live claim" — [docs/programme/WRITERS-STUDIO-SEMANTIC-FIELD-01_FOUNDER_RULING_MAIA_FIELD_2026-11-01.md](docs/programme/WRITERS-STUDIO-SEMANTIC-FIELD-01_FOUNDER_RULING_MAIA_FIELD_2026-11-01.md) §1.4

### Inferences
- "Mentor" vocabulary itself is risky under Canon §10 and Inv 1–2: a mentor who becomes the reference point for quality, or whom the writer returns to for validation, breaches Authority Return/No Exclusive Bond. A lawful "mentor presence" is closer to an attentive reader who ends turns with the writer's question.
- RRU §5 means a good mentor design must include *restraint as a feature*: MAIA sometimes notices and says "I'd leave it" (also ratified in Editorial Reading A0 §VII, below).
- Inv 15 forbids silently adapting MAIA's style to a writer from behaviour signals; any "MAIA learns how you like feedback" must be member-declared (facet) and consent-governed.

### Gaps
- `RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md` §6 ("Care without pursuit, attention without possession") was not read in full.
- Canon has no writer-specific clause; application to writing is done in the Writer's Studio records below.

## Q5. Explicit prohibitions a "mentor presence" redesign must respect

### Takeaway
A redesign may move how the writer reaches capability (into conversation) but may not change who authorizes, how much MAIA writes, what she reads without permission, or introduce ranking, inference, pursuit or engagement mechanics.

### Cited Findings (each with source)
1. **No change without the writer's act**: no confirmation-by-default, no auto-apply, no state change from words alone; Apply + exact Undo. — NEXT-01 A0 E3/E4; Convergence L5; conversational-field candidate §4.
2. **No increase in how much MAIA writes** (any facet, any "warmth"); no new-prose drafting (not authorized); MAIA-proposed text durably attributed. — Charter §9.3; NEXT-01 E4/E7; Conversation Stage laws; Journey §5.
3. **No ranking**, explicit or implicit (order, "suggested", visual weight, "most important first" across observations); only manuscript-order cuts; prioritization only when commissioned. — Charter §4, §10.1; A0 §VIII. (Note Design 3C "lead with the most important thing" governs a single *answer's* shape, not ranking observations — keep the two apart.)
4. **No suppression**: every observation reachable from every facet. — Charter §10.1; A0 §IX.
5. **Coverage visible, never behind a detail toggle; whole-work claims only on recorded coverage**; non-conclusions binding. — L8 §1a; A0 §IV, §VI.
6. **Compass / declared intention / settled decisions are never evidence or rubric**; never "the book does X" from a declaration; no grading against purpose. — Charter §10.3; Arrival §IV; DC-L4.
7. **No inference of intent, kind, facet, skill level or identity**; member declares. — L7; §9.2; Journey §2c; CDA "never infers identity".
8. **Teaching describes technique, never prescribes quality from an external standard.** — §9.6 (6); NEXT-01 E9; Inv 14.
9. **No reading without permission; conversation is not an implicit reread; selection never commissions cognition**; MAIA may offer a reading but only the writer's click starts it. — R1 laws 3–4; G4; G6 §6.
10. **Sanctuary**: nothing of the Work conversation stored; no reading permission, carried readings or settled decisions reach MAIA; G4 reads nothing; granting permission and Press/Keep grouping refused; revoking/removing never blocked; in-the-moment MAIA may see the current passage, earlier ordinary turns, and the writer's declared notes, for that answer only. — Walk evidence §Sanctuary (founder rulings 2026-11-09); CLAUDE.md Sanctuary invariants.
11. **No MAIA-initiated movement**: openings only on a writer gesture (never on selection), observation + question not proposal, never under Sanctuary; no ambient resurfacing / notifications. — Conversation Stage R2 constraints; CDA initiation boundary.
12. **No engagement / attachment / intimacy mechanics**; no runtime style adaptation from behaviour; quiet states stay quiet; non-pursuit of absence; reduce centrality over time. — Oath; Inv 2–4, 15, 16; RRU §3; NEXT-01 E10.
13. **Honest failure**: a failed MAIA call must say so on screen; refusals carry a next action; no dead end. — Walk verdict; RRU §4; Conversation Stage acceptance; E5.
14. **Helper's-book care**: never invent cases/clients/studies/quotes, never assert a method works, no clinical advice in author's voice, no identifying details; outcome claims → "no support shown". — Journey §2c.
15. **Gates stay** (posture, relationship, body-read permission, Apply); MAIA may hold and explain them in conversation, never waive them. — Semantic-field ruling §3.
16. **No invented capabilities**: MAIA's Studio guide may not name a control that doesn't exist; no intent offered that can't be served. — Design 1A; NEXT-01 E8 (`assertStudioMapHonest()`).

### Inferences
- External "AI writing mentor" best practices that involve co-writing, ghost drafting, progress streaks, proactive nudges, skill levels/badges, prioritized "top 3 fixes" lists, or adaptive persona tuning would each collide with one of items 2, 3, 7, 11 or 12 and would need a founder act, not a design choice.

### Gaps
- No ratified rule found on MAIA's *persona voice* in Studio beyond L4 and Design 3C; the "mentor" framing itself has no ratified status in the repo.

## Q6 (coordinator addition). The existing design direction and the walk's failures

### Takeaway
The founder treats **Develop as a conversational field** (2026-11-09) as the starting point. It is a **design candidate** awaiting a founder ruling, and it authorizes no build. Its thesis: "The manuscript is the surface. The conversation is the instrument. MAIA carries the operating; the writer carries the deciding." The real-MAIA walk repaired most failures in data, provenance and Sanctuary. Three remain open: MAIA blaming herself for an earlier correct quotation after the text changes, a missing identity-protection note, and a failed call that is not shown on screen. The founder's own walk failed on design: there were too many controls and the work was not at the centre.

### Summary of the design candidate — [docs/programme/WS-DEVELOP_CONVERSATIONAL_FIELD_DESIGN_2026-11-09.md](docs/programme/WS-DEVELOP_CONVERSATIONAL_FIELD_DESIGN_2026-11-09.md) (read in full; header: "design candidate for founder ruling… Authorizes no build, migration, merge or deploy"; candidate `c09b28986`)
- **§0 Why another pass (the census).** The founder's asks are listed in order: MAIA should read the chapter or book and recall it; "Where is the actual developing environment? … all these terse buttons"; "Stop babystepping"; *conversation is the primary method of developing the work*; most writers write fiction or nonfiction, the primary member is a helper, and suggestions must not be obnoxious; Develop should be "a truly interactive MAIA conversational driven field… while also offering advanced options to technical writers." The record's own honest finding: the stage built so far still makes the writer operate panels (ribbon, thread chips, plan *form* with status dropdown and Remove, excerpt strip, kind control, reading-permission control, starter buttons). **The full Develop has 92 buttons. "MAIA talks beside the instruments; she does not carry them."**
- **§1 Governing idea.** Every act that changes state still needs one deliberate act from the writer: "MAIA proposes, the writer decides". The design cites the charter: reducing what the writer operates is in scope; increasing what MAIA can do to the work is not.
- **§2 Screen = three things only.** (1) **The book**: the manuscript, in the centre, readable and scrolling across chapter boundaries, with a slim book map at the edge. (2) **The conversation**, in the same column, under or beside the passage it concerns. (3) **One composer** (type or speak) with a single quiet contextual suggestion in its placeholder and no rows of starter buttons. Nothing else is visible unless MAIA brings it into a turn or the writer opens it.
- **§3 Turns that carry objects (these replace the panels).** A MAIA turn is prose plus a few typed objects, each with one act in place:
  - **Passage card**: exact words she used, labelled with the revision she read, showing both versions if the text has moved. Acts: *Go there* · *Work on this passage*. Replaces the excerpt strip.
  - **Comparison**: two or three passage cards side by side (for example Ch 7 against Chs 2 and 5).
  - **Proposed step**: "a direction anchored to a passage (never replacement prose)". Acts: *Keep as a step* or reply to change it. Replaces the plan form.
  - **Request**: "To answer this I'd need to read Chapters 2 and 5. Let me?" Act: *Yes, read them* (this mints receipts). Replaces the reading-permission control.
  - **Outline alternative**: a ghost reordering of the book with its consequences, never applied. Act: *Keep as an alternative*.
  - **Status suggestion**: "You said you've dealt with step 2 — mark it resolved?" Act: *Mark resolved*.
  - **Return brief**.
  - **Conversational plans.** The plan becomes the conversation's *spine*: a quiet, collapsible list of kept steps at the head, each showing its state. The writer edits a step by saying so, and MAIA proposes the edit for the writer to confirm.
- **§4 Words in, confirmed acts out.** The writer can simply say what they want ("compare 7 with 2 and 5", "read the whole book for pacing", "what if Return came before Salt?", "I've fixed the lantern scene", "this is a practitioner book").
  - The **server** maps the words to a **closed capability registry** (read sections, commission a lens, propose steps, propose an outline alternative, find, record a decision, declare kind, change a step's status).
  - Anything that changes state returns as a Request or a Status suggestion. "No state changes from words alone."
  - Answers that only read just happen.
  - The kind of Work is still declared, never inferred.
- **§5 Return-to-work continuity.** "Return is a conversation, not navigation." MAIA's first turn when Develop opens is a **return brief built from records, not from her memory**:
  - what the writer last asked, and where they were;
  - plan steps that are kept, applied, undone, resolved or still open;
  - chapters changed since she last read them, by revision;
  - "Pick up there, or start somewhere new."

  Every line must cite a record, and a line with no record is not shown.
- **§6 Four states, never merged.** Proposed · Applied · Undone (verified from desk receipts) · Resolved (the writer's decision, possibly with no text change). An applied edit never resolves a step. A chapter that has changed, or cannot be verified, makes a step *needs checking*.
- **§7 Instruments on demand = the ratified DIRECT facet.** This is the same reading with the machinery visible, under facet parity.
  - **⌘K palette and slash commands**: `/read 2 5 7` · `/compare` · `/lens structure scope:work` · `/find` · `/plan` · `/outline` · `/scope` · `/export plan md`. All go through the same registry and the same confirmations.
  - **Instrument drawer, closed by default**: reading permission; for each turn, exactly which sections and which revision she was handed, with receipts; coverage; dropped steps; quote-check refusals; evidence references; digests.
  - **Structured views on request**: outline table, plan as a table, a chapter-by-term matrix (counted, never interpreted), split view of two chapters.
  - **Nonfiction checks**: terminology consistency, cross-references, and for argument books a claim → evidence → section map "built only from passages, never from inferred meaning". Keyboard paths throughout.
- **§8 What the stage loses, and where each control goes.**
  - Starter row → the placeholder line.
  - Thread chips → asking "what recurs?" (chips stay in DIRECT only).
  - Plan form, status dropdown, Remove → step objects, the spine, status suggestions.
  - Excerpt strip → passage cards.
  - Permission control → Request objects, plus the drawer.
  - Kind control → asked once in conversation.
  - Seven lens tabs → words, or `/lens`.
  - The 92-button view stays as "More ways" until a walk proves nothing is lost.
- **§9 Laws, each paired with a wrong design.**
  - "Read the book" silently grants Work-level reading.
  - "Use it" marks a step resolved.
  - The return brief claims a fix with no receipt.
  - A card shows today's text as what she read.
  - DIRECT finds a step GUIDED cannot reach.
  - The placeholder submits on Enter when the composer is empty.
  - "Keep as alternative" reorders chapters.
  - A plan is edited through another manuscript (fixed in `c09b28986`).
  - A moved but unique phrase is held as valid (fixed).
  - Kept steps are fed back to her as truth (this would breach D5).
- **§10 Substrate needed.** G1 step↔desk receipt join · G2 revision-pinned reads · G3 typed turn objects · G4 intent registry with confirmation · G5 return brief · G6 readings available in the Work conversation · G7 DIRECT palette and drawer · G8 outline alternatives. All were marked missing at the time of writing. Since then G6 has been built and a first narrow slice of G4 (one *Read this chapter* offer) has been built, so the earlier "Q4" gap list is stale for those two items.
- **§11 Order.** Each step is walked on *The Long Harbour* and on a helper's nonfiction book before the next begins:
  1. G1, plus a browser walk of plan → passage → apply → leave → return → undo, with the refusal cases.
  2. G6, brought forward.
  3. G2 + G3 (passage cards).
  4. G4 (words in, confirmed acts out).
  5. G5 (return brief).
  6. Lenses by words.
  7. G7 (palette and drawer).
  8. G8 (outline alternatives).

  Then an independent review, a founder walk, and a ruling on making the stage the default.
- **§12.** There is no new lane; this is the Writer's Studio lane's next milestone. Each acceptance needs one independent review. Merge, migrations `20261108000001–0004`, deploy and the default switch remain founder acts.
