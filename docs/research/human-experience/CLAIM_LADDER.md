## The five rungs

```text
INTERNAL HYPOTHESIS
        ↓   a Soullab proposition; may be argued, may not be asserted
RESEARCH-SUPPORTED PRINCIPLE
        ↓   ≥2 independent tier ≤5 sources, paraphrase-checked, boundary conditions named,
            contested register consulted; principle status CANDIDATE → SUPPORTED
MAIA EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE
        ↓   at least one Stage 10–14 experiment on a MAIA surface with an adjudication
REPLICATED / MULTIPLE WITNESS
        ↓   the effect seen again: a second experiment, a second surface, or independent
            human witness under study ethics; not the same members re-asked
PUBLIC SOULLAB CLAIM
            founder act; the claim names its KIND (below) and its rung
```

**Rule:** a claim may occupy no rung higher than its evidence licenses. Ascent is by evidence;
the act of claiming is the founder's. Descent (a rung lost when evidence fails) is recorded,
never quietly dropped. No rung is skipped. Nothing on rungs 1–4 is marketed.

## Claim kinds — every public statement names one

| Kind | What it asserts | What licenses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Empirical** | something measured happened | rung 3 or 4 evidence, with the measurement named |
| **Theoretical** | a model that organizes findings | rung 2, with the findings it organizes cited |
| **Phenomenological** | how an experience is structured for the person | witness evidence (class C confirmed by human observation), never inferred from structure |
| **Ethical** | what Soullab will and will not do | canon; needs no empirical rung, but must not be phrased as empirical |
| **Philosophical** | a position on what relationship, intelligence, meaning are | founder authorship; explicitly marked as position |

The strategic point (founder): when Soullab says something publicly about human–AI
relationship, we know exactly what kind of claim it is. In a field that will be full of
enormous claims, that is the credibility.

ratified by internal adjudication. Ratification of P4′ requires at least one meaningful **external
