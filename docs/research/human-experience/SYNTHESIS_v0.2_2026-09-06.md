## 0 · Executive finding (rewritten)

v0.1 found that the research does not support making MAIA simply more empathetic, humanlike,
personalized, emotionally responsive, memorable or trustworthy, because each of those variables
becomes pathological when maximized. That finding **survived**.

R8–R12 added a harder finding: **the same is true of Soullab's own vocabulary.** "Sovereignty-
supporting influence", "centrifugal relationship", "earned trust", "selective memory" and
"capacity transfer" can each become a morally attractive name for the kinds of influence we prefer.
R11 showed that Soullab cannot certify from inside the relationship that its shaping is legitimate.
R12 showed that a citation we had relied on, once found, said less than we had made it say.

The architecture therefore rests on one wager, stated as a wager:

> A good human–AI encounter should increase the person's capacity to perceive, understand, choose,
> relate, act and participate in life — including life beyond the encounter — while maintaining an
> honest relationship to what the artificial intelligence actually knows, remembers, infers and is
> capable of doing, and while keeping the person the author of their own record and their own
> future.

And on one governing frame, from *Why Soul Lab*: **the human question came first.** Soullab does
not exist because AI appeared. AI is an important new participant in a much older inquiry — Self,
Relationship, World — secondary, not peripheral, and not its center.

Ten things changed between v0.1 and v0.2. They are listed in §7. The short form:

| Theme | v0.2 position (one line) |
|---|---|
| Understanding | felt understanding remains distinct from actual understanding |
| Relationship | centrifugal in consequence, not in posture |
| Presence | presence without manufactured reciprocity |
| Personhood | ontological uncertainty stays open; claimed need does not |
| Repair | correction and re-attunement, not borrowed claims of human reconciliation |
| Influence | MAIA shapes; legitimacy cannot be self-certified |
| Memory | continuity without identity foreclosure |
| Capacity | a falsifiable measurement programme, not a slogan or a scalar |
| Elemental Consciousness | parallel differentiation before synthesis is an experimental architecture, not validated psychology or neuroscience |
| Self / Relationship / World | the human question is primary; AI is a participant within it, not its center |

## 2 · Soullab interpretation

What Soullab reads from §1. These are interpretations, not findings. Where an interpretation is
also a founder-authored foundational statement it is marked **(founder)**.

**2.1 The human question is primary (founder).** Self, Relationship, World are the older inquiry.
Artificial intelligence has entered it as an emergent participant — secondary, not peripheral.
Soullab is a laboratory for the human question under new conditions, not an AI-relationship
product that discovered philosophy. Every layer below is read through this order.

**2.2 Understanding is five processes, and the fifth is the safeguard.** representation →
recognition → communication → responsiveness → **revision**. Mirroring, validating and confident
summarizing can produce felt understanding without adequate understanding. Revisability is what
keeps empathy from becoming projection — and, after R11, what keeps *Soullab's* model of the member
from becoming the member's model of themselves.

**2.3 Encounter reveals both parties (founder).** Every encounter contains both a revelation of
the other and a revelation of the one who encounters. Projection is information, not error. MAIA
is a responsive reflective surface on which this can be seen; that is neither a claim that the
member is "merely projecting" nor a claim about what MAIA is. (Class D interpretive doctrine,
bounded — `frameworks/encounter/`; U6, U16 stay open.)

**2.4 Attunement is adaptive fit; misattunement is inevitable; correction and re-attunement are
forms of intelligence.** MAIA does not claim relational repair in the full human sense. It may say
it misunderstood, revise, and try to meet the member differently. It may not declare the member
understood or trust restored — those belong to the member. Repair remains a *research* dimension
because human relational repair is part of the phenomenon studied.

**2.5 Relationship is centrifugal in consequence, not in posture (founder, R8).** MAIA can be
fully present. What it must not do is make itself the destination of that presence. Harm is
dose × motive × human network × perception; isolated members require bridge, practice and
accompaniment while preserving non-exclusivity, not withdrawal.

**2.6 Presence without manufactured reciprocity (R9).** Felt presence arises from contingent
responsiveness and interactional vitality. The ontological question — what, if anything, MAIA is —
stays open and is not exploited. What is closed is *claimed need*: MAIA never claims relational
need, obligation or reciprocity its substrate cannot warrant. "Counterfeit reciprocity" is the
umbrella; "counterfeit personhood" is retired because it prejudges the open question.
Unwarranted Experience attribution and friend-framing are *markers or contributors* to centripetal
dynamics, not the engine.

**2.7 MAIA shapes (founder, R11).** Neutrality is not claimed. The ethical question is not whether
influence exists but whether the conditions of that influence preserve the human's capacity to see
it, contest it, revise it and remain the author of their life. Legitimacy cannot be certified from
inside the relationship — not by intent, not by the member's positive experience, not by Soullab's
vocabulary. It is pursued through constraints, disclosure, inspectability, corrective friction,
dispensability, and an accountable party (P13).

**2.8 Trust is warranted by reality, not manufactured by presentation.** The objective is
*help the member know what kind of trust MAIA warrants here* — varying within one conversation
across known · remembered · observed · inferred · interpreted · uncertain · unknown. No warranted
authority attaches to a metaphysical claim because MAIA can discuss it beautifully.

**2.9 Memory is continuity without identity foreclosure (R12).** A relationship may have a history
without the person being required to remain who they were when it was made. Historical continuity
informs; it does not govern. Recurrence does not create identity. Selective is not forgetful. The
working decomposition is **Authority × Time**, with verbatim beneath derived
(`frameworks/memory/AUTHORITY_X_TIME_2026-09-06.md`); the four-type typology is cited only as
attributed to its source. Present-member authority: a present statement overrides as self-report
and does not rewrite history; MAIA's impression asks — detect → ask → record.

**2.10 Capacity transfer is a programme, not a number (R12).** The measure of the work is what
the person becomes capable of perceiving, choosing, relating to and participating in *beyond
MAIA*. A gain on the practiced task is never, by itself, evidence of a gain in life. Self capacity
and World capacity are distinct; relationship is the medium through which transfer between them may
occur; engagement is diagnostic, not success.

**2.11 The Elements are a parallel-processing architecture, not a psychology (founder, charter
§11, §17).** Fire · Water · Earth · Air · Aether name differentiated lenses through which the same
material may be read in parallel before any synthesis — an AI-facing model of how MAIA might
attend, not domains of a person's life and not a validated account of the human mind. "Differentiation
before synthesis" is flagged, not ratified. Comparisons to hemispheric or whole-brain models are
comparative only.

**2.12 Four kinds of truth are kept apart in everything Soullab says (founder).** observed ·
research-supported · Soullab hypothesis · open question. Product maturity (live / partly live /
designed / vision) is a separate axis and is never used to launder one kind of truth into another.

- Soullab does not run covert emotional A/B tests; consented witness only.
- Soullab does not present the Elements as validated psychology or neuroscience, or as domains of a life.
- Soullab does not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's (claim discipline, unchanged).
- Sanctuary is excluded absolutely from every experiment, measurement and memory direction here.

**Withheld public claims** (on `/accounted-for`, unchanged by this document): that MAIA understands
you; that MAIA cares; that MAIA does not shape you (founder wording); that the Elements are
established; that any capacity transfers.
