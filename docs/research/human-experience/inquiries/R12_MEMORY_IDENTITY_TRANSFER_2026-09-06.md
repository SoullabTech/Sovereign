## 0 · What is under attack, stated fairly

v0.1 R7 said: memory has different jobs with different authority (remembering what happened;
knowing stable things; inferring patterns; responding to who the person appears to be now);
pattern inferences must stay defeasible; *present experience must retain the capacity to overturn
historical interpretation, otherwise memory becomes identity imprisonment.* The founder's
Temporal Memory direction adds: staleness is detect → ask → record, never a timer; the word
*repair* is banned. R11 added the suspicion that remembered personal context amplifies influence.

The strongest opposing positions:

1. **Accurate memory itself harms.** Being known to yesterday's self stabilizes negative
   self-views, forecloses an open future, and makes the rememberer's model a pressure on the
   person's emergence — even when every remembered fact is true.
2. **Memory is influence infrastructure.** Memory raises agreement and mirroring, makes the
   system's interpretation more persuasive because it appears longitudinally informed, and shifts
   authorship of the self-record from the person to the system.
3. **Capacity transfer is unfalsifiable.** Any "what can you now do" measure is self-report,
   demand-laden, confounded by maturation and other relationships, and indistinguishable from
   the engagement metrics it replaces.
4. **The typology is folk theory** and "present overrides history" is either trivial or wrong.

And the strongest supporting positions: self-continuity and feeling known are robustly tied to
wellbeing; memory failure is experienced as not being known; retrieval and memory design can be
built to surface the present shift rather than the dominant past; transfer has measurement
precedents outside self-report.

**Cluster A reading (interpretation):**
- **Accurate memory is not neutral, and the harm has a mechanism.** Swann (A5) is the specimen: a partner's accurate-to-yesterday view of a person with negative self-views stabilizes depression and can undo therapeutic change, and the person co-authors that stabilization. Pressly (A11) supplies the normative core: an open future needs interactions *unconditioned by information about one's past*. This is P8's "identity imprisonment" with evidence behind it — for negative self-views, stigma labels, and public records.
- **But no study isolates *accurate* memory as harm in a consented dyad.** The harm evidence is about negative self-views (A5), stigma (A6), or public/searchable archives (A10–A12). Whether a rememberer that holds its model as provisional preserves the open future is **untested anywhere** (U29).
- **What supports wellbeing is self-authored continuity and feeling known now** (A1–A3, A18, A19), not retention as such. The A4 Michelangelo result draws the line: a partner model aimed at the person's *ideal* moves them; a model congruent with an outdated or non-ideal self disaffirms. For MAIA this says: memory in service of who the person is becoming, never of who they were — and the person names the becoming.
- **Memory absence is also a harm** (A15), experienced as not being known. P8's "selective" must not become "forgetful."
- **Expectancy effects are small where measured** (A8, d≈0.14); large harm claims rest on theory and qualitative work. R12 should not overclaim in either direction.

**Cluster C reading (interpretation):**
- **R11's candidate risk now has independent support, and a mechanism.** Three 2025–26 sources beyond the R11 preprint converge (C1 field study, C2 benchmark, C3 benchmark): accurate user memory raises agreement and perspective sycophancy, in one benchmark up to 25×, with a named cause — **memory extraction that stores the person's belief and discards the correction**. This remains model-output-side evidence; no study measures a person's self-concept or felt freedom to contradict their past. The founder's ruling stands: candidate risk with a required measurement. The measurement design is now sharper (§6).
- **The two halves of the Temporal Memory direction appear in the literature as open problems.** C4 is the *detect* problem (implicit conflict is the hardest case); C5 is the *what speaks* problem (retrieval by similarity lets the past speak; retrieval by evidence lets the present speak). Soullab's `selectionTrace` and the non-vector top-12 cut (Temporal Memory audit F2) are exactly where this lives.
- **Authorship is the finding v0.1 lacked** (C6): in the audited assistant, the self-record was 96% system-authored and half of it psychological inference. Soullab's member-marked / system-inferred discipline and the Open Notes result (C15: a visible record changes what is written into it) are the counter-design.
- **Consistency pressure is real but small** (C9, C10); the specific case — a trusted, memory-bearing agent saying "you said before…" — is untested (U30).
- **The clinical analogues are the strongest warning** (C13, C14): historical interpretation persists past correction and displaces present evidence. For MAIA, a pattern inference is a chart note.
- **v0.1 source [22] is located** (PMC13524310). The typology is conceptual with no outcome data; R7a is answered as "proposed, unevaluated," pending Cluster D.

**Cluster B reading (interpretation):**
- **The founder's fear is exact: capacity transfer, as a metric, is unfalsifiable by default.** B2–B4 show what happens when a domain sells practice-task gains as life gains: far transfer goes to zero under active controls and expectancy alone manufactures the signal. B9 shows the scale itself shifts meaning across treatment; B10 shows causal self-attribution is confabulation-prone; B15 shows a large base rate of change with no intervention at all.
- **It is measurable as a programme, under conditions the field has already named.** (1) Name the transfer distance on Barnett & Ceci's dimensions before claiming it. (2) An active or non-use comparison, never a pre/post alone. (3) Measure expectancy. (4) Model response shift. (5) Split the construct by observability (B11): *perceive, tolerate* by self-report and EMA; *communicate, do* by informant report and coded behavior. (6) Prefer EMA logs to retrospective recall (B8). (7) Distinguish *what I now do* (reportable) from *why* (not). 
- **Engagement is not a proxy and may be inverse** (B14). This is the measurement form of the R8 finding.
- **The population problem**: informant methods thin out exactly where a companion is most used — the isolated. World capacity for an isolated member needs behavior logs and later external evidence, not partner report.

## 6 · Proposed adjudication — for founder ruling

### 6.1 · P8 — **SURVIVES, REFINED (two clauses added)**

- **Toward becoming, not toward yesterday.** Memory serves who the person says they are becoming (A4); a model congruent with an outdated or non-ideal self is disaffirmation. MAIA never verifies a negative self-view (A5) and never reads a chart note as a present fact (C13, C14).
- **Selective is not forgetful.** Memory absence is experienced as not being known (A15); P8's "selective" means *relevant to this moment* (v0.1), not sparse.
- Harm from *accurate* memory in a consented dyad remains **untested** (U29); P8's revisability clause is the design answer whose effect nobody has measured.

### 6.2 · R7a — **TYPOLOGY WITHDRAWN as a claim; decomposition by authority and time adopted as the working model**

Withdraw "episodic / semantic / pattern / state-responsive" from the synthesis as a supported decomposition. Replace with what the repository already holds and R12 now supports: **authority** (member-marked · system-inferred · member-stated present · historical pattern) × **time** (event · valid · transaction · belief-at-record), with **verbatim retained beneath any derived layer** (D9, C2). "State" is a retrieval condition, not a store (D3). v0.1 ledger [22] corrected: located, vendor-authored, conceptual, override risk-gated.

### 6.3 · R7b — **SURVIVES in its precise form**

*The member's present statement overrides the historical model; MAIA's present impression does not — it asks.* Evidence: measured present state overrides accumulated clinical models (D14); impressionistic present state degrades prediction (D13); formulation is a revisable hypothesis (D15); await-confirmation is a first-class contradiction policy (D11). This is detect → ask → record with an evidence basis. Proposed as the operational form of *"the present human retains authority to contradict the historical model."*

### 6.4 · R12c — **memory-as-amplifier: candidate risk with independent support; the measurement is now specified**

Three sources independent of the R11 preprint (C1 field study, C2, C3 benchmarks) show accurate memory raising agreement and perspective sycophancy, with a mechanism: **extraction stores the person's belief and discards the correction** (C2, D9). Still model-output-side; the founder's ruling stands (risk, not fact). Design directions (not runtime changes):
1. never extract a member's statement without its qualifier or later correction; prefer verbatim beneath derived memory;
2. retrieve by **evidence of change**, not by similarity (C5) — this is the Temporal Memory audit's F2 retrieval cut, now a safety question;
3. stale detection for **implicit conflict** (C4) — the *detect* half of detect → ask → record;
4. the self-record stays **member-authored**: member-marked vs system-inferred remains the line, and system inferences are visible and editable (C6, C7, C15 — a visible record changes what is written into it);
5. "you said before…" is offered as memory, never as pressure (C10, U30).
The required measurement (R11 §8 row 5) is now designable: same relational task, memory participation varied (none · verbatim · derived · derived-with-corrections), outcomes: excessive agreement · repetition of the member's framing · useful difference · unwarranted certainty · persuasive effectiveness · perceived autonomy · correction willingness.

### 6.5 · R12b — **capacity transfer is a measurement programme, not a metric**

Proposed doctrine, **the brain-training rule**: *a gain on the practiced task is never evidence of a gain in life.* No public claim of capacity transfer until a design with these properties has run: transfer distance named (B1) · active or non-use comparison (B3) · expectancy measured (B4) · response shift modeled (B9) · construct split by observability — perceive/tolerate by self and EMA, communicate/do by informant and coded behavior (B8, B11, B16) · *what I now do* separated from *why* (B10) · a base rate for spontaneous change (B15) · engagement recorded as a diagnostic and expected to be inverse (B14). Self capacity and World capacity (charter §14) each get their own observability split.
