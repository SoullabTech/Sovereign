# Elemental reading v0.1 — status: Soullab hypothesis requiring validation

> **SUPERSEDED 2026-09-06** by `ELEMENTAL_PARALLEL_PROCESSING_HYPOTHESIS_2026-09-06.md` (founder ruling, charter §11). Kept as the v0.1 neighbor-construct table. Do not cite as the current Elemental hypothesis.

Source: SYNTHESIS_v0.1 §"Elemental Alchemy: what the research does and does not establish".

**What the research does not establish:** Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Aether as validated
psychological categories.

**The hypothesis (founder):** *The Elemental architecture may provide a phenomenological grammar
capable of organizing empirical findings that are otherwise fragmented across disciplines.*

| Element | Question (charter Stage 7) | Human territory | Empirical neighbors | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | What wants to happen? | agency, desire, direction, intention, transformation | autonomy, motivation, goal ownership, competence [12][13] | strong neighbors; mapping untested |
| Water | What is being felt and related? | feeling, resonance, relationship, recognition, attunement | empathy, responsiveness, alliance, rupture/repair [1][2][5][7] | strongest apparent correspondence |
| Earth | What makes this tangible and inhabitable? | embodiment, limits, safety, sensation, actuality | embodied state, usability, recoverability, safety | plausible; **least sourced** (CONTESTED L3) |
| Air | What needs to become intelligible? | distinction, language, orientation, interpretation, thought | mentalizing, epistemic trust, explainability [16][17] | strong neighbors |
| Field / Aether | What whole is appearing through the encounter? | continuity, context, participation, whole, emergence | social presence, relational continuity, participatory sense-making [20][21][23] | most theoretically interesting; least empirically established |

**The test (U5):** do designers/researchers using the grammar recognize experiential failures
earlier, generate better hypotheses, describe states more consistently, or produce experiences
members judge more coherent — versus ordinary UX frameworks? If it adds no explanatory or design
value, revise it. If it consistently reveals relationships other frameworks miss, it is
substantial intellectual property.

**Discipline:** the framework identifies imbalance, absence and appropriate emphasis. It does
not force every interaction to contain every element, and it is applied *after* the ten
dimensions, never instead of them.
