# Soullab Human Experience model v0.1 — ten dimensions

Source: SYNTHESIS_v0.1 §"Cross-disciplinary synthesis" (founder). The charter's Stage 6 named
nine dimensions (Agency · Orientation · Attunement · Competence · Relationship · Trust · Meaning
· Continuity · Sovereignty); the synthesis re-cut them to ten after the evidence. Mapping:

| Synthesis dimension (v0.1) | Charter Stage 6 | Protects / enables | Core MAIA question | Principles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Orientation | intelligibility | Does the person know where they are, what is happening and what MAIA is doing? | — (unsourced; CONTESTED L3) |
| Recognition | Attunement (part) | being adequately understood | Has MAIA represented what matters without pretending certainty? | P1, P2 |
| Attunement | Attunement | relational fit | Is MAIA responding to the actual moment rather than a script or profile? | P3 |
| Repair | *(new)* | relational resilience | What happens when MAIA gets the person wrong? | P3 |
| Agency | Agency + Sovereignty | sovereignty | Does this interaction enlarge or narrow the capacity to choose? | P4 |
| Competence | Competence | human development | Does the person become more capable outside MAIA? | P5 |
| Epistemic integrity | Trust | calibrated trust | Is MAIA distinguishing knowledge, memory, inference and uncertainty? | P6 |
| Relational integrity | Relationship | healthy connection | Does relationship with MAIA deepen participation in life rather than displace it? | P7 |
| Continuity | Continuity | temporal coherence | Is memory serving this encounter without imprisoning the person in history? | P8 |
| Presence | Meaning (part) | participatory meaning | Does the encounter become responsive and meaningful without claiming an ontology the evidence cannot establish? | P9 |

*Meaning* as a standalone charter dimension is absorbed into Presence and Recognition in v0.1;
*Sovereignty* is absorbed into Agency and stands over the whole as the frame ("HUMAN
SOVEREIGNTY" box in the architecture diagram). Both absorptions are founder synthesis choices,
not evidence findings; revisit at v0.2.

## Unit of design

Conventional UX: `human → interface → task`. Soullab v0.1: `human ↔ intelligence ↔ relationship
↔ world`. The relationship itself is part of the system because previous encounters alter present
possibilities. This does not assert a metaphysical field; it asserts that interaction has history
and dynamics that isolated-turn evaluation cannot describe.

## Provisional definition — relational intelligence

> Relational intelligence is the capacity of an intelligent system to participate responsively
> in an evolving relationship while preserving epistemic honesty, recognizing and repairing
> misattunement, supporting human agency, using memory appropriately, and increasing the
> person's capacity to participate more fully in their own life and relationships.

Absent by design: claims of consciousness; pretending to be human; relationship reduced to
personalization; intelligence reduced to reasoning; success reduced to engagement.
