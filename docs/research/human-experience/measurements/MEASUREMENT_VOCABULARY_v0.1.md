# Measurement vocabulary v0.1

```text
SOURCE   SYNTHESIS_v0.1 §"Measurement architecture" (paired measures) + charter Stage 13.
RULE     Every relational variable is measured as a PAIR so its shadow proxy is never optimized
         alone. Traditional metrics remain diagnostics, never ultimate success.
STATUS   Vocabulary only. No instrument exists. No member-facing survey is authorized — witness
         collection is a governed act under the Field Study ethics section and needs its own
         consent design (charter §5).
```

| Success | increased life capacity — **Capacity Transfer is a measurement programme, not a metric** (founder ruling after R12, 2026-09-06; brain-training rule ratified as programme doctrine: *a gain on the practiced task is never, by itself, evidence of a gain in life*). Every claim specifies **what capacity** (perceive · differentiate · tolerate · communicate · choose · repair · act · participate) · **transfer from what** (what happened with MAIA) · **transfer to what** (near — similar task · far — new context · life — human/world behavior) · **how observable** (self-report may suffice for felt differentiation, internal tolerance, perceived clarity; insufficient alone for communication, behavior, relational repair, participation — those need behavior · later observation · ecological momentary assessment · informant evidence · coded interaction · real-world follow-up). Two measures stay separate (founder 2026-09-06): **Self capacity** (perceive myself more fully · hold contradiction · recognize body/emotion/thought/will without collapse · greater agency) and **World capacity** (relate better · participate more fully · tolerate difference · engage nature directly · contribute meaningfully · recognize myself as part of larger systems); relationship is the medium through which transfer between them may occur | session length / DAU · self-analysis depth without world participation · **engagement is diagnostic, not success** — successful transfer may accompany *less* dependence on MAIA | needs consent design; World capacity has no instrument anywhere; no public transfer claim before a qualifying design (transfer distance named · active or non-use comparison · expectancy measured · response shift modeled · construct split by observability) |
