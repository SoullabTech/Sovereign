   but not the *typology*. Until [22] is located by hand or replaced, the typology in R7 and the
   corresponding half of principle 8 are **Soullab interpretation**, not evidence. Note that the
   typology independently matches Soullab's own Temporal Memory direction
   (`docs/architecture/TEMPORAL_MEMORY_DIRECTION_2026-09-06.md`) — that is a convergence of our
   thinking with our thinking, and must not be counted as external support.

   confidence.** Existence ≠ accuracy. Specific figures quoted in the synthesis (82 samples /
   6,000+ clients in [2]; 140+ papers / 40,000+ participants in [19]; "no significant overall
   relationship" in [6]) are the first things to check against the source text.
3. **Tier profile is healthy**: 21 of 24 scholarly sources at tier 1–3; [11], [14], [21] are
   perspective/ethics/theory and are used as such in the synthesis.
4. **Coverage gaps by domain** (charter Stage 1): domain F phenomenology has one source ([21]);
   domain H contemplative has none; domain B classical HCI (cognitive load, affordances,
   progressive disclosure) has none — the synthesis is a relational-psychology-and-HAI corpus,
   which is the right first cut but not the whole landscape.
