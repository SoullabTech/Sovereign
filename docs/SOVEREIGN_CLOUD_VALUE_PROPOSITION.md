# Sovereign Cloud Value Proposition

> Your device, your memory. Sovereign cloud extends what's possible.

---

## Local Sovereignty (Free)

**Complete experience on your device**

- Full memory, locally stored
- Your data never leaves your machine
- Conversations, journal, insights — always yours
- No account required to begin

---

## Sovereign Cloud (Supported)

**Extension, not permission**

### Personal Extension

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **File uploads** | Upload documents for deeper analysis |
| **Cross-device sync** | Your memory, everywhere you are |
| **Pattern weaving** | MAIA connects threads across months and years |
| **Extended functions** | Unlock additional abilities and features |

### Collective Contribution

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Community intelligence** | Your insights strengthen the whole |
| **Collective wisdom** | Help build something that serves us all |
| **Shared patterns** | Anonymous contribution to pattern libraries |

### Still Sovereign

- Self-hosted infrastructure
- No third parties between you and your data
- No cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- No data mining or monetization
- Complete ownership always

## Why This Model?

> Most technology treats you as a user to extract from. Soullab was built on a different premise: your data is yours, your device is sovereign, and extending your capabilities should be a choice—not a requirement.

**Two paths, both sovereign:**

1. **Local sovereignty** — MAIA runs on your device. Your conversations, journal entries, and memory stay with you. Complete and self-contained.

2. **Sovereign cloud** — When you want more—upload files, sync across devices, let MAIA weave patterns across time, contribute to community and collective intelligence—we've built infrastructure for that. Self-hosted. No third parties. Still yours.

**The key insight:** Supporting Soullab unlocks infrastructure, not access. Your local experience is never diminished.
