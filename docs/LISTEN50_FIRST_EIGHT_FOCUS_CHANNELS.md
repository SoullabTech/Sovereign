## Purpose

LISTEN-50 names and constrains the first eight Focus Field channels without pretending that a title, musical brief, local file, or artist conversation is a license. The package gives artists, producers, rights reviewers, and the source owner one exact specification for what must be created and admitted.

This milestone adds **no recording, media URL, provider integration, entitlement, artist identity, rights holder, payment commitment, or playback authority**. Every channel is `production-brief-only`, has no media fingerprint, and is unplayable.

## Shared musical requirements

Every channel must:

- be human-authored and transparently credited;
- contain no lexical vocals in the initial focus release;
- avoid abrupt starts, startup blasts, jump-scare transitions, advertisements, dialogue, or system-like cues;
- preserve dynamics while controlling unintended loudness jumps;
- use loops only when musically seamless and explicitly authorized;
- use alternate stimulation versions only when the creator explicitly authorizes them;
- contain at least five minutes of genuine source material before it can even be structurally reviewed as a channel;
- avoid medical, neurological, ADHD-treatment, healing-frequency, or productivity claims.

The 48-second synthetic Listening studies are not eligible channel masters.

## Required evidence per channel

A channel cannot proceed to independent admission until the rights package contains current, opaque evidence references for:

1. master ownership and use;
2. composition ownership and publishing use;
3. performer consent;
4. sample and source-recording status;
5. territory;
6. license term;
7. private on-demand playback;
8. timed focus-session use;
9. cross-route Soullab playback;
10. background playback;
11. mobile playback;
12. any editing, looping, sequencing, or alternate-version permission;
13. credits and attribution;
14. withdrawal, expiry, and revocation handling.

The initial package explicitly excludes download, offline Cabin, public broadcast, shared sessions, unbounded remix, stem isolation, AI analysis, model training, and voice imitation. Each requires a later, separately signed authorization.

## Media custody

A structurally reviewable media package must identify:

- one lossless WAV or FLAC source;
- exact SHA-256 and byte length;
- genuine sample rate and bit depth;
- duration;
- source/master version;
- an untouched preservation master;
- creator-approved delivery derivatives, if any;
- loudness, peak, transition, loop-seam, and decode checks;
- the matching rights-package version.

A valid-looking hash or evidence reference is not itself proof. This source model therefore remains unable to return `playable: true`. Even a structurally complete package ends at `INDEPENDENT_MEDIA_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`.

## Channel-specific production direction

### Deep Classical

Long-breath chamber or orchestral writing; stable forward motion; restrained thematic change. Avoid virtuosic display, abrupt cadences, dramatic quotations, and large dynamic shocks.

### Piano Concentration

Human-played solo or lightly prepared piano; spacious phrasing; low repetition fatigue. Avoid showpiece repertoire, hard attacks, mechanical quantization, and memorable hooks that pull language attention.

### Ambient Stillness

Slow harmonic fields, organic resonance, and near-silence. Avoid high-frequency fatigue, obvious short loops, sudden spatial movement, and pseudoscientific frequency claims.

### Cinematic Momentum

Evolving orchestral/textural arcs with measured pulse and lift. Avoid trailer climaxes, dialogue, jump-scare percussion, and rapid cue changes.

### Organic Attention

Acoustic instruments, human timing, breath, warmth, and uncluttered arrangement. Avoid busy solos, lyrical vocals, unconsented cultural borrowing, and over-compressed intimacy.

### Downtempo Flow

Restrained electronic rhythm, warm bass, and slowly changing atmosphere. Avoid club drops, vocal hooks, advertising stingers, and fatiguing sub-bass.

### Nature Current

Ethically captured natural sound integrated with music. Avoid stock clichés, short repeating wildlife calls, unverified field-recording provenance, and nature-as-treatment claims.

### High-Stimulation Focus

Strong controlled pulse, layered percussion, and bounded novelty. Avoid startling breaks, loudness competition, rapid genre switching, and diagnostic or treatment claims.
