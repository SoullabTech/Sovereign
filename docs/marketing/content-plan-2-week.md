# Soullab — 2-Week Content Signal Plan

Goal: Drive recognition → audit requests
Distribution: X, LinkedIn, Substack
Each post is a recognition trigger, not a product announcement.

---

## Week 1

**Day 1**
> Most people don't have a motivation problem.
> They have a structure they haven't seen clearly yet.

**Day 2**
> There's who you are… and how you show up.
>
> When those collapse into one, confusion starts.

**Day 3**
> Burnout isn't always overwork.
> It's often the cost of maintaining the wrong version of yourself.

**Day 4**
> High vision + low structure = constant restarting
> High structure + low vision = slow suffocation
>
> Most people are stuck in one of these.

**Day 5**
> If a system actually understands you, it doesn't motivate you.
> It clarifies you.

---

## Week 2

**Day 1**
> You don't need more discipline.
> You need alignment between identity and action.

**Day 2**
> Most AI systems flatten people into one tone.
> Humans don't work like that.

**Day 3**
> A common pattern:
> internally visionary — externally operational
>
> Creates confusion in both execution and communication.

**Day 4**
> The moment someone feels "this understands me" is when change actually becomes possible.

**Day 5** *(soft CTA)*
> If you could see the structure behind your patterns clearly—
> what would you want it to show you?
>
> → soullab.life/audit

---

## Platform notes

**X / LinkedIn:** Post as standalone text. No images needed. Short. Dense.
**Substack:** Expand Day 3 or Day 4 into a 300–500 word piece with the audit as the closing CTA.

## Field-specific content

**Kelly field** — lead with: identity, patterns, internal tension, shadow, meaning
**Nathan field** — lead with: system coherence, architecture decisions, build logic, operational clarity
