# Writer's Studio — Founder Walk Instrument (Packet 2)

```text
LANE      JARVIS-WRITER-ONBOARDING-PRODUCTIZATION-01
PURPOSE   Kelly walks Writer's Studio AS A WRITER and produces the complete
          "How do I actually use this?" map.
NOT       code · architecture · repair · design review
```

> **The walk is the founder's act.** A source walk cannot produce this. What
> follows is the instrument, pre-filled with what source says each surface will
> do, so the walk records *divergence and comprehension* rather than
> re-discovering mechanics.

## Rules of the walk

1. **First observed outcome is the evidence.** No retries through a failure, no
   URL shortcuts, no patching state mid-walk.
2. **Do not repair during the walk.** Record and continue. (Census discipline,
   inherited.)
3. **Record the runtime SHA before starting.** `docker exec maia-sovereign
   printenv GIT_COMMIT`. Nothing in the walk transfers to a later runtime.
4. **Answer only the seven questions.** Resist designing. The instrument's value
   is that it captures confusion, not solutions.
5. **Note the vocabulary you reach for**, especially where it differs from the
   product's word. That gap is the onboarding.

anyway and record what is actually there — a predicted absence confirmed by a
walk is evidence; a predicted absence assumed is not.

## The seven questions — for every surface

```text
WHERE AM I?
WHAT IS THIS FOR?
WHAT CAN I DO HERE?
WHAT HAS MAIA ACTUALLY DONE?
WHAT SHOULD I DO NEXT?
WHAT HAPPENS IF I LEAVE?
HOW DO I COME BACK?
```

Add one line per surface: **what I had to already know to answer these.** That
line is the missing guidance, stated by the person who needed it.

## Development deserves its own sheet

The mature sequence, and the question at each arrow. **Every arrow may stop** —
record where it stopped for you, and whether stopping felt like a boundary or a
failure.

```text
MAIA observation      → what did she actually notice? in her words or mine?
  ↓
evidence              → could I open what she rested it on? did it match?
  ↓
conversation          → could I talk about it? did it change anything?
  ↓
writer recognition    → did I recognise something? did the product notice?
  ↓
writer decision       → could I record my ruling?          [expect: NO]
  ↓
optional change       → was I offered a change?            [expect: NO]
  ↓
writer adoption       → could I adopt anything?            [expect: NO]
```

Then, once:

- Did the room ever imply MAIA's reading outranked my judgment?
- Did anything read as a score, grade, or verdict?
- Did I want MAIA to just fix it? **If yes, write down exactly when.** That
  moment is where the product is most at risk of becoming the author.
