The former NAS backup path was `pg_dump | gzip`; gzip and 0600 permissions do NOT encrypt writing. Preserve a read-only metadata report first. Do not delete existing backups, rotate production keys, invoke paid providers, or expose actual manuscript text. If a live backup is plaintext, prioritize independently recoverable encrypted replacements and a documented old-copy remediation plan under fresh approval.

**S2 — A real author journey on appropriate protected conventional storage.** Preserve immutable Source and exact sections; one authenticated writer/Work is the only authority, not client identity headers, Living Work title or an old consent. Do not use the R53/R54 fictional-only signer as a production key authority. Keep already protected legacy Works separate and held against silent downgrades.
