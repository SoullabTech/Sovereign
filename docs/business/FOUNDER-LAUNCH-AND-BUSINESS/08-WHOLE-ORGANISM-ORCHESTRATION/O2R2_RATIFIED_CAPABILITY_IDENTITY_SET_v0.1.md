## Journal capture-scope ruling

O2R2 ratifies the semantic rule, not a message-selection algorithm:

> **“Save this” authorizes preservation of the material the member is presently indicating, not an implementation-chosen fixed transcript window.**

Future runtime must resolve a `CaptureScope` before writing.

Candidate sources of scope, in descending authority:
1. explicit member selection/held passage;
2. explicit member reference to a bounded object;
3. a defined current-exchange scope that is transparent and inspectable;
4. if materially ambiguous, ask rather than silently widen.

The current “last five messages” behavior is therefore **existing substrate requiring reconciliation**, not O2R2 canon.

`journal.dream` inherits the same rule, with the additional requirement that content is intentionally being classified as dream material.

## Transit ruling

The split is constitutional because the two acts carry different data authority:

`astrology.transit.current`
- may operate on public/current ephemeris data;
- member-private natal data is not implied.

`astrology.transit.personal`
- requires authorized natal/birth-chart data;
- failure to establish that scope results in truthful non-execution or invitation to provide/setup data;
- cannot silently downgrade to a generic reading while claiming personal interpretation.
## Studio ruling

`studio.choose` is orientation only.

It may:
- explain available Studios;
- present destinations the member may explore;
- send the member to the relevant threshold.

It may not:
- infer professional role;
- grant Studio entitlement;
- choose a Studio based on hidden profiling;
- cross Studio data membranes.

Explicit Studio-open IDs are destination requests.

Runtime access remains governed by identity + entitlement + role + ownership/context.

## Scheduling ruling

The old phrase “schedule a session” hid two different actors.

`booking.practitioner.request`
- member-originating;
- may involve an external booking/calendar effect;
- must name the practitioner/service/context;
- does not create a professional session record merely by requesting time.

`studio.session.create`
- practitioner/professional-context act;
- requires role + Pro Studio/context authority;
- may create professional/session records;
- does not become available from member booking entitlement.

These identities may later split further if evidence shows additional distinct acts.
