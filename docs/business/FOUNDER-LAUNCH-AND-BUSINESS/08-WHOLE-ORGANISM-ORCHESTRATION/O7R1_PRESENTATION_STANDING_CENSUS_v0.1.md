A capability receives `PURPOSE_ONLY` candidate standing only where:

2. there is source language that describes the act's purpose without needing a new product decision;
3. using that purpose does not require choosing availability, route, entitlement, or House placement.

`PURPOSE_ONLY` in O7R1 is still **candidate presentation standing**.

It does not authorize runtime utterance.
