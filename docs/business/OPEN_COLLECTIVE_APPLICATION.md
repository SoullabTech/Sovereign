## Short Description (1 line)

## Long Description

### What is Soullab?

Soullab is a local-first AI system for reflection, journaling, and personal memory. The core product runs entirely on a user's device without payment or account. An optional cloud layer provides sync, backup, and long-horizon pattern analysis for those who choose it.

The product is operated by Soullab LLC (Connecticut). It is not a nonprofit and does not seek to become one.

### What is Soullab Commons?

Soullab Commons is the mission-aligned arm of the project, focused on:

- **Scholarships**: Funded cloud access for members who cannot pay
- **Research**: Studies on ethical memory systems, AI safety in personal contexts, and contemplative technology
- **Stewardship**: Community moderation, safety practices, and ethics review
- **Education**: Open resources on sovereign technology and digital wellbeing

### Why fiscal sponsorship?

We want to accept tax-deductible contributions for mission work without converting the entire product into a nonprofit structure. Fiscal sponsorship lets donors support the commons while the product company operates sustainably on earned revenue.

## Why this matters

Most AI memory products require surrendering data and identity to corporate surveillance systems. Soullab proves a different path is viable: local-first, user-sovereign, funded by community support rather than extraction.

The Commons ensures this model remains accessible to people regardless of ability to pay.

## What happens after you submit

1. OCF reviews (usually 1-2 weeks)
2. If approved, you get a Collective page
3. You can start accepting donations immediately
4. OCF handles tax receipts for donors
5. You request payouts for approved expenses

## OCF fees

- 10% of donations (covers their 501c3 overhead)
- This is standard and transparent
