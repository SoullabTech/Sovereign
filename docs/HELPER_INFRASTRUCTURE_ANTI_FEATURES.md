# Appendix: Anti-Features

**Structural Prohibitions for Helper Infrastructure**

This appendix defines what Soullab's Helper Infrastructure is **not allowed to become**, regardless of demand, convenience, or good intentions.

These prohibitions exist to protect:

* Helper sovereignty
* Relational integrity
* The non-extractive orientation of the work

If an idea conflicts with this list, the answer is **no** — even if the idea seems reasonable.

---

## 1. No Credentialing or Endorsement

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Issue certificates, badges, or titles
* Imply professional legitimacy or readiness
* Act as a prerequisite for helping others
* Be framed as accreditation or training completion

Infrastructure provides **structure**, not authority.

---

## 2. No Growth, Funnel, or Conversion Features

Helper Infrastructure must not include:

* Sales funnels or automated upsells
* Conversion tracking or optimization tools
* Lead magnets, scarcity mechanics, or urgency prompts
* Revenue dashboards tied to helper performance

Care work is not a conversion pipeline.

---

## 3. No Leaderboards, Rankings, or Social Proof Metrics

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Rank helpers by popularity, revenue, or reach
* Display follower counts, testimonials, or "success stories"
* Surface engagement metrics as status indicators

Visibility is not virtue. Popularity is not care.

---

## 4. No Automated Authority Substitution

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Replace relational discernment with automation
* Offer AI-driven advice as a substitute for human judgment
* Automate decision-making that affects others' wellbeing

Technology may **support reflection**, not make choices for people.

---

## 5. No Dependency by Design

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Require ongoing Soullab involvement to function
* Create technical lock-in or opaque systems
* Obscure how tools work or prevent independent operation

All offerings must end in a **clean handoff**.

---

## 6. No Marketplace Dynamics Inside Learning or Care Spaces

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Enable private upselling inside courses or circles
* Blend learning spaces with solicitation
* Encourage helpers to extract value from shared containers

If commerce occurs, it happens **outside** the care vessel.

---

## 7. No Gamification of Helping

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Reward helping with points, levels, or badges
* Incentivize frequency, intensity, or volume of care
* Treat human support as a game mechanic

Helping is not a performance loop.

---

## 8. No Brand Amplification Defaults

Helper Infrastructure must not:

* Center helper identity over the work itself
* Optimize for visibility, recognition, or differentiation
* Encourage personal brand narratives as a primary frame

The work comes first. The helper remains secondary.

---

## Final Rule

If a feature would:

* Increase extraction
* Inflate authority
* Accelerate scale
* Reduce discernment
* Create dependency

…it does not belong in Helper Infrastructure.

No exceptions.
