# MAIA — Case Study Library (v2)

**Purpose:** Canonical case-study set for Marketing/Copywriter, organized around **high-value human transformations**, not professions.
**Governed by:** `docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` — every study declares **Layer / Center of Gravity / Failure Test**, plus a **Primary Human Carrier** (emergent metadata, watched not yet formalized).
**Date:** 2026-06-03 · **First validation pass** of the claim discipline.

> **North-star principle (Kelly):** The primary character is the *human challenge*. The profession (therapist, coach) is the secondary character. Stories, not claims.
>
> **Writing convention:** each study leads with its **strong (Live) version** — the publishable one — and names the **weak (Designed) drift** to avoid, so the migration stays visible.

---

> **The one story under all ten.** Strip the surface problem from any case below — too many frameworks, a hard decision, a fading breakthrough, a loss — and the underlying problem is the same: **integration.** (See `AUDIENCE_POSITIONING.md` → The Integration Thesis.)

# The Three Flagships (full)

## ★ 1. The Therapist & The Client — Sarah *(professional market)*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live (Designed upside) · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now**

**Challenge:** A client makes progress in session but loses momentum between sessions.

**Story (strong/Live).** Sarah, 34, sees her therapist twice a month for anxiety that runs on a loop — over-function, over-commit, collapse. The work lands *in* session and evaporates by Tuesday. Thursday night the collapse starts; she opens MAIA (Care voice). It doesn't fix or diagnose — it reflects: *"You said yes to three things this week. Where did the yes come from?"* She catches the pattern **as it's happening** and arrives at her next session with a coherent question instead of a fog. The rawest night, she turns on Sanctuary Mode and it leaves no trace.

**The therapist (secondary).** He sees nothing of those sessions — no surveillance, ever. What he receives is a more self-aware client. He uses his *own* MAIA to reflect on his attunement across a heavy caseload. MAIA stays in the reflective lane, never the clinical one: no diagnosis, no record, never a substitute for him.

**Drift to avoid (weak/Designed):** *"MAIA remembers everything across sessions and surfaces hidden patterns over time."* That moves the load onto continuity memory.

**Outcome:** Therapy starts at the threshold, not the warm-up. **Autonomy arc:** months in, Sarah needs the Thursday reflection less.

## ★ 2. The Executive at a Crossroads — Michael *(consumer market)*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now (keep arc to days–weeks)**

**Challenge:** A major decision with no obvious answer.

**Story (strong/Live).** Michael, 48, is deciding whether to leave a successful role. Over two weeks he talks it through — fears, values, finances, identity — sometimes by voice on a walk. MAIA doesn't advise; it reflects what recurs: *"Every time you list the upside, you change the subject to your father."* The decision emerges from **clarity rather than urgency** — he finally hears what he already knew. MAIA never pushes a choice; it surfaces the threshold and lets him cross it.

**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA tracks your themes across months and across every area of life."*

**Outcome:** A better decision under uncertainty, and one he *owns*. **Autonomy arc:** he leaves with a way of deliberating he can reuse without MAIA.

## ★ 3. The Woman Navigating Grief — Emily *(emotional center)*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Shared** · Layer: Live presence / Designed arc · CoG: **Designed** · Failure Test: **does not survive** · **Verdict: ⚠️ Arc in forward voice; gated on continuity memory**

**Challenge:** Loss, and the slow reconstruction of a life.

**Story.** Emily, 52, loses her husband. Friends show up, then return to their lives. The hardest moments are unscheduled: 11pm, the empty side of the bed. She brings them to MAIA (Care voice) — the thoughts she can't yet say to anyone. The rawest she speaks once in Sanctuary and lets go. **[Designed]** Months later, MAIA reflects the arc back, and she can *see* her own movement — which from inside grief is almost impossible.

**Honesty note.** The arc-reflection is the emotional payload of the whole brand, and it rests on longitudinal memory **not yet verified in production.** Anchor published copy on the *consistent nightly presence* (Live); write the arc in forward voice. This flagship and the continuity-memory roadmap mature together.

**Outcome:** Continuity through fragmentation. **Autonomy arc:** one week she opens it to mark a *good* day — then doesn't need it.

# The Full Library (drafted — first validation pass)

## 4. The Coach & The Client — *accountability without dependency*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now**

**Challenge:** Momentum between sessions, without becoming dependent on the coach.
**Story (Live).** Priya meets her coach twice a month. Between sessions she takes goals, obstacles, and avoidances to MAIA — thinking out loud, returning to what she committed to. She arrives with the real work already surfaced, so the hour goes to leverage, not catch-up. The coach's value goes *up*. MAIA **accompanies**; it never becomes the coach.
**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA tracks your goals and reports your progress over months."*
**Outcome:** more leverage per session. **Autonomy arc:** dependency on neither coach nor MAIA grows.

## 5. The Daily Reflective Practice — *the journal you don't abandon*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now (safest of all)**

**Challenge:** Most people abandon journaling — the blank page, the streak-guilt.
**Story (Live).** John opens MAIA two minutes most mornings (Note voice). Instead of a blank page, a presence: *"Tired. Dread about the launch." → "The dread has a date on it. What's after the 15th?"* Reflection becomes conversational, not performative. No streaks, no "you're behind." Skip four days: *"Been a minute. No pressure."* The absence of pressure **is** the design.
**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA surfaces the patterns across months of your journaling."*
**Outcome:** sustainable self-reflection. **Autonomy arc:** the metric that matters is the one most apps call failure — he needs it less.

## 6. The Marriage Under Strain — *seeing your own half of the pattern*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now — accounts separate**

**Challenge:** The same argument on repeat.
**Story (Live).** Dev and Sam each use MAIA **separately**. Each starts catching their *own* assumptions and triggers in the moment — *"I hear contempt and I go cold."* Conversations get more intentional because each sees their own half more clearly.
**Boundary (load-bearing):** the two accounts never share data and MAIA never mediates between them — **separateness is a sovereignty guarantee, not a missing feature.**
**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA tracks the couple's recurring conflict patterns"* (cross-account = sovereignty violation *and* Designed).
**Outcome:** increased relational awareness, owned by each person.

## 7. The Spiritual Seeker — *meaning without surrendering discernment*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives most cleanly of all** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now**

**Challenge:** Making meaning of dreams, symbols, synchronicities without handing your authority to a system.
**Story (Live).** Maria brings a recurring dream. MAIA doesn't decode or pronounce — it asks, reflects, offers possibilities, and **explicitly claims no authority.** Interpretation stays *hers*; MAIA is a thinking partner, not an oracle with answers.
**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA interprets your dreams and reveals their hidden meaning."*
**Outcome:** reflection without dogma. **Note:** strip every memory capability and nothing is lost — this is pure posture. Maximally on-brand.

## 8. The Retreat Participant — *integration instead of inspiration* (the borderline one)
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Shared** · Layer: Live (near-term) / Designed (over-months) · CoG: **Designed-leaning** · Failure Test: **framing-dependent** · **Verdict: ⚠️ Scope to near-term, or forward voice**

**Challenge:** A breakthrough at a retreat fades once daily life resumes.
**Story (Live, scoped).** In the days *after* the retreat, Tom brings the insight to MAIA and turns it into a small daily practice — naming it, returning to it while it's fresh. The near-term landing survives on reflection alone.
**The over-time beat (Designed):** *"Months later, MAIA shows you how the retreat changed you"* — needs continuity memory; write it forward.
**Why both instruments flag this:** near-term integration survives the Failure Test; sustained-months integration does not. The framing choice *is* the verdict.

## 9. The Aging Parent — *a life made legible across time* — **VISION (true north, not a current claim)**
**Stamp:** Carrier: **MAIA / Field** · Layer: **Vision** · CoG: **Vision** · Failure Test: **does not survive** · **Verdict: ⛔ Vision context only — design consent first**

> **This is a vision piece.** It describes where MAIA is *oriented*, not what it does today. Do **not** publish as a case study of current capability.

**The vision.** An elder records memories, reflections, hard-won wisdom. Over years it becomes a living archive a family can engage with — continuity that outlasts any single conversation, even any single life. A life made legible across generations.
**Why it's gated.** The shared, multi-person archive requires infrastructure that does not exist — and, more importantly, a **consent and sovereignty architecture that must be designed first**: who may access another person's reflections? What did the elder consent to? How does Sanctuary apply across people, and across death? These are vows, not features.
**Why we keep it Vision and don't soften it.** It's one of the most powerful narratives in the ecosystem. `Vision: Aging Parent` orients the whole org; `Designed: Elder support companion` would strip-mine that north star to ship something lesser. The continuity field *is* the story — which is exactly why it stays Vision.

## 10. The Practitioner Extending Care — *presence that scales without becoming 24/7*
**Stamp:** Carrier: **Human** · Layer: Live · CoG: **Live** · Failure Test: **survives** · **Verdict: ✅ Publish now — reflect-and-carry**

**Challenge:** Serve more people without being always-on, and without thinning the care only a human can give.
**Story (Live).** Between sessions, MAIA holds the reflective space for the practitioner's clients — so each client keeps moving, and the practitioner stays focused on the work only they can do. MAIA **accompanies**; it does not handle, replace, or stand in for the relationship.
**Drift to avoid:** *"MAIA handles your clients' reflection between sessions"* — "handles" tips into replacement (Anti-Feature #4).
**Outcome:** scale without losing humanity; the practitioner's centrality is preserved, MAIA's is bounded.

# Strategic note — the emotional center sits on the next engineering threshold

The flagship that carries the brand's emotional weight (Grief) depends on its single strongest beat — *"months later she can see her own movement"* — which rests on longitudinal/continuity memory, the roadmap's next measurable threshold (episodic memory). **Marketing and engineering priorities point at the same thing.** Until it ships and verifies: anchor grief copy on the live *presence* and write the arc forward, or treat continuity memory as the unlock that lets the flagship go fully Live. Either is honest. We do not publish the arc as a delivered fact before it's verified.

# Evidence-grounding corrections (verify before leaning on as Live)

1. **"Captures insights"** — atom auto-write appears **stranded on the live path**; *"captures"* should mean *reflects in the moment*, not *stored and retrieved*, until verified. (Therapist, Grief.)
2. **Theme / pattern recognition** — detection is Live; **surfacing into the reply is not verified** → "MAIA notices your recurring themes" is **Designed**.
3. **Sanctuary Mode** — canon invariant; confirm **production wiring** before featuring it as a Live beat (declaration ≠ liveness).
