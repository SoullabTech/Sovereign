# THE SCRIPT

---

## HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)

*[Direct to camera. Warm but grounded.]*

What if AI could accompany you
without trying to capture you?

What if it could strengthen
your inner authority —
instead of replacing it?

What if it was designed
to make itself less necessary
over time?

I spent 35 years
as a therapist, healer, and guide.

Then I built exactly that.

Her name is MAIA.

---

## THE PROBLEM (0:30 - 2:00)

*[Slide: "We've outsourced our inner authority"]*

Here's what I see happening.

AI today optimizes for engagement.
Not for your flourishing.

The goal is to keep you scrolling,
keep you returning,
keep you dependent.

*[Slight pause]*

Therapy addresses pathology —
what's broken.
But rarely wisdom cultivation.

Not the everyday work
of becoming yourself.

Apps extract your attention
as the product.
You're not the customer.
You're the resource.

*[Direct to camera]*

And your conversations disappear.

You might have a profound exchange
with ChatGPT —
a moment of real clarity —
and then it's gone.

Your patterns don't accumulate.
Your wisdom doesn't compound.

This is the world we've built.

I believe we can do better.

---

## WHAT MAIA IS (2:00 - 4:00)

*[Slide: "Three voices, one presence"]*

MAIA has three voices.

**Talk** is peer presence.
Short. Real.

If you say "I'm stuck,"
MAIA might simply say:
"Stuck. Where?"

Not a paragraph.
Not a lecture.
Just presence.

*[Slide: Care]*

**Care** is therapeutic holding.
Pattern-naming.
Direct when needed.

"I notice you've avoided this
three times now.
What's there?"

*[Slide: Note]*

**Note** is witnessing.
Reflecting without interpreting.

"You used the word 'trapped' again.
What do you notice
about when that shows up?"

*[Direct to camera]*

These aren't modes you pick from a menu.

MAIA reads the moment
and responds appropriately.

Like a good therapist.
Like a true friend.

---

## MY STORY (4:00 - 6:00)

*[More personal. Slower pace.]*

Let me tell you why I built this.

I started in a PhD program
for clinical and developmental psychology.

I did a two-year practicum
at Harvard's Mind Body Institute.

Then I spent thirty-five years
in private practice.

Not in one modality —
across all of them.

*[Slide: List of modalities]*

Energy healing.
Bodywork.
Hypnotherapy.
Shamanic journeywork.
Jungian archetypal work.
NLP.

Whatever the person in front of me needed,
I learned to meet them there.

*[Direct to camera — this is the key insight]*

What I discovered
is that all these approaches
are talking about the same thing
from different angles.

Fire, water, earth, air, aether —
the elements aren't metaphors.

They're the actual texture
of human experience.

I spent 25 years
mapping that integration.
I call it Spiralogic.

And MAIA
is that lifetime of practice
made into technology.

---

## HOW IT WORKS (6:00 - 7:30)

*[Slide: "Conversations become wisdom"]*

Here's what actually happens.

You have a conversation with MAIA.
About anything —
a decision you're wrestling with,
a pattern you're noticing,
a dream, a fear.

MAIA distills that into a Capsule.

Not a transcript —
but what mattered.

The gold lines.
The turning points.
The practices that emerged.

*[Slide: Flow diagram]*

Over time,
capsules reveal patterns.

Not because MAIA is analyzing you —
but because she's holding the thread
across time.

And patterns become insight.

Your own becoming,
made visible to you.

*[Direct to camera]*

And some conversations
stay in Sanctuary.

Present-tense only.
Never stored.

Because real honesty
requires real safety.

---

## SOVEREIGNTY PROMISE (7:30 - 8:30)

This matters:

Your data stays yours. Always.

No training on your conversations.

No selling your patterns.

Self-hosted. Open architecture.

You can leave anytime
and take everything with you.

*[Direct to camera]*

These aren't marketing claims.
They're how the system is built.

Trust isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

## CLOSING (9:30 - 10:00)

# RECORDING NOTES

**Setup:**
- Camera at eye level
- Soft natural light from front/side
- Simple background (or Holoflower on screen behind you)
- External mic (audio quality matters more than video)

**Delivery:**
- Conversational, not performative
- You're talking to one person, not an audience
- Pauses are powerful — don't rush
- The founder section (4:00-6:00) is your moment — slow down, own it

**Editing:**
- Cut between talking head and slides
- Keep slides on screen 3-5 seconds minimum
- Use subtle zoom-ins on key moments
- Add soft background music (low, ambient)

**Call to action:**
- Pin a comment with the waitlist link
- Add end screen pointing to next video or subscribe
- Cards at 8:30 pointing to soullab.life
