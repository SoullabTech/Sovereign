# MAIA — The Practice Library

**Purpose:** The companion to the case-study library. Case Study = *"that's me."* Practice Library = *"that's what I'd actually do."* Together: *"that's what's been missing."*
**Governed by:** `docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`. **Pairs with:** `CASE_STUDY_LIBRARY.md`, `AUDIENCE_POSITIONING.md`, `CASE_STUDY_FELT_FORM_BRIEF.md`.
**Date:** 2026-06-03.

> **The reframe (Kelly):** The real product isn't MAIA. It's the set of reflective practices MAIA makes easier to sustain. At that point MAIA stops looking like software and starts looking like a way of living. *This is the marketing expression of the Sovereignty Invariant — MAIA decentralizes itself; the practice matters, not the system.*

---

## The one rule (what makes this honest *and* sovereign)

> **Every practice is a verb the member does — supported by MAIA's in-the-moment reflection. Never a capability MAIA performs.**

When that holds, the practice is **Live and sovereign by construction** (the human carries the thread; MAIA is the reflective space). The Library is sorted by that test. The failure mode is a "practice" that's secretly a system capability — especially **synthesis**, which the canon refuses.

---

## Tier 1 — Core Practices (LIVE today · human-carried)

These are real, publishable now. The member acts; MAIA reflects in the moment.

| Practice | What the member does |
|---|---|
| **Morning Reflection** | Opens MAIA ~10 min before the day; names what's present. |
| **Dream Capture** | Brings the dream that won't leave; stays with it instead of letting it evaporate. |
| **Stuck-Point Inquiry** | When stuck with a client, a decision, or a pattern, thinks it through out loud. *(Reframes "Shadow exploration" — a behavior, not a built "Shadow" mode.)* |
| **Decision Inquiry** | Facing a choice, talks through fears / values / consequences until they hear what they already know. |
| **Post-Session Reflection** | (Practitioners) After a session, reflects on what stood out and what to hold. *(Capturing + in-the-moment reflection = Live. Reviewing it before the next session = Tier 2.)* |
| **Difficult-Conversation Review** | After a hard exchange, reflects on their own part. |
| **Reflective Journaling** | Conversational journaling — MAIA asks a question, the member reflects. |
| **Insight Capture** | Names an insight they want to stay with. *(In-the-moment = Live; retrieval later = Tier 2.)* |
| **Listening / Presence Practice** | (Helpers) Reflects on where their attention thinned, where they over-identified. |
| **Vision Review** | (Founders) Returns to the vision; reflects on alignment, not metrics. |
| **Meaning Reflection** | (Grief) Brings the unscheduled hard moment and is met. |
| **Life Review · Story Capture** | (Elders) Tells a story, reflects on a chapter. *(In-the-moment telling = Live.)* |

## Tier 2 — Continuity Practices (DESIGNED · forward voice until verified)

These depend on longitudinal surfacing/retrieval that isn't verified in production yet. Write them in forward voice ("designed to"), or reframe to the human-carried form noted.

| Practice | Why it's Designed | Honest reframe (Live form) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Pattern Review** | requires MAIA to surface what recurred across weeks | *"You re-read your own kept reflections monthly and notice the thread"* — Live **if** kept-entry retrieval is verified; until then, forward voice. |
| **Developmental-Edge Review** | requires longitudinal trajectory | forward voice |
| **Burnout / Depletion Check-in over time** | requires tracking across sessions | forward voice; the *single* check-in ("how depleted am I right now?") is Live |

---

## Anti-feature — NOT a practice MAIA performs

**Cross-Modality Synthesis · Framework Mapping.** MAIA does **not** synthesize or map the member's frameworks — that's interpretive displacement the canon refuses. This is worse than "not built"; it's *designed-not-to.*

> **Honest reframe:** *the member* connects the modalities — "explore how different traditions describe the same territory" — and MAIA reflects and holds the question. Never *"MAIA maps/synthesizes your frameworks."* Human-carried = Live; MAIA-synthesis = forbidden.

## How the two libraries work together

- **Case Study** (felt form): *"That's me — I've had that exact morning."*
- **Practice Library**: *"And here's what I'd actually do on Tuesday."*
- **Result**: *"That's what's been missing."*

The case study earns recognition; the practice makes it concrete; together they let someone see MAIA as a way of living, not a piece of software — without a single claim the system can't honestly carry.
