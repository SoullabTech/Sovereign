## Delivery Notes

**Pacing:**
- Slower than you think. Let phrases land.
- Pauses are powerful. Use them after key statements.
- The slides animate — let them finish before speaking.

**Eye contact:**
- For video: look at the camera, not the slides.
- For live: scan the room, return to individuals.

**Voice:**
- Warm, not performative.
- This is a conversation, not a TED talk.
- You're sharing something real, not selling.

**The founder slide (7):**
- This is your moment. Own it.
- Don't rush your credentials.
- The integration story is the moat — make sure they feel it.

**The close (12):**
- Slow down here.
- "Sovereignty is never paywalled" is the landing.
- Let silence follow before "Thank you."

---

## Video Recording Tips

1. **Lighting:** Face a window or soft light. Avoid overhead shadows.
2. **Background:** Simple. The Holoflower can be subtle on a screen behind you, or just a clean wall.
3. **Framing:** Head and shoulders. Eyes at upper third of frame.
4. **Audio:** External mic if possible. Room echo kills credibility.
5. **Take breaks:** Record in sections. Edit together. You don't need to do it in one take.
