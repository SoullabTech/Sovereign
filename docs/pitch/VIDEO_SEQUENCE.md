# MAIA Video Launch Sequence

## The Philosophy

MAIA spreads through relationship, not advertising.

Each video builds on the trust that precedes it:
- Beta testers already believe — honor that
- Invited members arrive through blessing — welcome them personally
- Public viewers are discovering cold — earn their attention

---

## Recording Order

I'd suggest recording in this order:

1. **Beta tester video first** — Lowest stakes, most forgiving audience. Good warmup.

2. **Public YouTube video second** — This is the polished one. Take your time.

3. **Welcome video last** — By now you'll have recorded twice. This one is short and intimate.

---

## Production Notes

### Beta Tester Video
- Can be recorded on phone
- Natural lighting, simple background
- Authenticity > polish
- Edit cuts are fine

### Welcome Video
- Slightly more polished (people are making a decision)
- Still warm and personal
- Could include subtle Holoflower in background
- 2-3 minutes max — don't oversell

### Public YouTube Video
- Full production value
- Slides / picture-in-picture
- External mic
- Multiple takes, edited together
- Chapters in description

## Metrics That Matter

Not vanity metrics. Relationship metrics.

- **Bead redemption rate** — How many invites are actually used?
- **Blessing completion rate** — Are inviters writing meaningful blessings?
- **Day 7 return rate** — Are invited members coming back?
- **Conversation depth** — Are people going beyond surface chat?

These tell you if the relational growth model is working.
