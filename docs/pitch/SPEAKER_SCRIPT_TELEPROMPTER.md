# DELIVERY NOTES

## Pacing

Slower than feels natural.
Much slower.

The pauses aren't empty —
they're where meaning lands.

Let the slides animate.
Let phrases breathe.
Trust the silence.

## Voice Quality

Warm, not performative.
Grounded, not selling.
Intimate, as if speaking to one person.

You're sharing something real —
not pitching.

## The Founder Slide (7)

This is the heart.

Don't rush your credentials.
Don't minimize them.

The integration story IS the moat.
Make sure they feel it in their bodies.

## The Close (12)

Slow down here.

"Sovereignty is never paywalled"
is the landing.

Let silence follow.

Then: "Thank you."

Nothing more.

# VIDEO RECORDING NOTES

**Lighting:** Natural light from the front.
Avoid overhead shadows.

**Background:** Simple.
A subtle Holoflower on screen behind you,
or just a clean wall.

**Framing:** Head and shoulders.
Eyes at upper third of frame.
Slightly closer than feels comfortable.

**Audio:** External mic if possible.
Room echo destroys credibility.

**Recording:** You can do this in sections.
Take breaks.
Edit together.
You don't need one perfect take.

**Eye Contact:** Look at the camera
as if it were a person you trust.
