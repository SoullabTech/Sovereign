# The Founding Studio Program

*A living professional environment, embodied with you.*

---

> **A Founding Studio is not software that we install for you. It is a professional environment we embody *with* you — one that continues to grow alongside your practice, your understanding, and your life's work.**

---

## What is a Founding Studio?

Most tools help you do tasks. A Founding Studio does something different: it becomes the place where your professional intelligence lives, connects, and continues to develop over time.

Today, a lifetime of work is scattered — across books, talks, notebooks, slides, research, client experience, and memory. A Studio doesn't just store those. It preserves and cultivates the *relationships among them*, so your understanding keeps developing instead of freezing into an archive.

We call the process **embodiment**, and we mean it precisely. It is the same idea running through every layer of what we've built:

- **Technically**, one intelligence (MAIA) becomes contextually embodied in your world rather than answering as a generic assistant.
- **Professionally**, your philosophy and lifetime of work take living form in your Studio.
- **Organizationally**, we learn to faithfully embody *your* world — never to replace it with ours.

It isn't SaaS. It isn't a website. It isn't consulting. It is a professional environment that grows with you.

## Why we're inviting only a handful of founding practitioners

We are not opening accounts. We are establishing a standard.

A Studio only succeeds if it feels authentically *yours* — not like us with your name on it. That fidelity takes depth, and depth doesn't scale by volume. So at this stage we work with a small number of founding practitioners whose work is rich enough to test what the architecture can faithfully hold.

Being a Founding Partner is not a discount tier. It means your practice helps define how this is done for everyone who follows — the embodiment process itself, the workflows, the way a living body of work is cultivated, and the next generation of the platform.

## What the Studio Embodiment process looks like

This is human-led accompaniment, not a setup wizard. Working alongside you, we:

1. **Discover your philosophy** — how you think, what you notice, the questions you return to.
2. **Organize your corpus** — books, talks, research, materials, teaching — brought into one interconnected environment.
3. **Shape your developmental language** — your terms, your distinctions, kept in *your* vocabulary rather than translated into ours.
4. **Configure your MAIA** — so the intelligence in your Studio reflects your practice and your voice.
5. **Cultivate your Body of Work** — so it becomes living and navigable, not a folder of files.
6. **Launch, and keep going** — your Studio opens, and stewardship begins.

Throughout, our discipline is self-effacing: our job is to draw out and embody *your* world, never to impose our own.

## What you'll receive

- **Your Studio** — a coherent professional environment, embodied to your practice.
- **Your MAIA** — configured to your voice, your corpus, and your way of working.
- **Your corpus, organized and interconnected** — searchable and alive, not archived.
- **Your Body of Work as a living environment** — that continues to develop with you.
- **Ongoing stewardship** — a real relationship over years, not a support ticket queue.
- **Sovereignty** — you own your data, your corpus, and your knowledge base. No lock-in. The Studio is yours.

As the platform matures, further capabilities become available to you — turned on when they're genuinely ready and genuinely yours, never bolted on before their time.

## What we ask of you as a Founding Partner

A founding relationship is reciprocal. We ask for:

- **Your time in discovery** — embodiment depends on real access to how you think and work.
- **Your candor** — telling us where it feels true and where it doesn't. That feedback *is* the design.
- **Your patience with a maturing platform** — you are joining early, on purpose.
- **Your partnership in defining the practice** — what you help us learn shapes every Studio that follows.

In return, you are not a customer waiting on a roadmap. You are a partner shaping one.

## Investment

The value here is not a feature set. It is the continuity and stewardship of a professional life — the environment where you think, teach, write, and keep developing your life's work.

- **Founding contribution:** $2,000 to begin the embodiment engagement.
- **Ongoing stewardship:** shaped together as part of the founding relationship — because part of what this first engagement discovers is what stewardship should be.
- **Ownership:** your Studio, corpus, and knowledge base remain yours.

The investment is the consequence of the process, not the point of it. What you are entering is a partnership in embodying your life's work — and the terms exist to serve that, not the other way around.
