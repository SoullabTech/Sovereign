# Case Study — Dan

*The Lifelong Practitioner. Pattern example for the rewrite template — see [CASE_STUDY_REWRITE_BRIEF.md](./CASE_STUDY_REWRITE_BRIEF.md).*

*All actions described are Live unless tagged otherwise.*

---

## Who he is

Dan is sixty-two. Thirty years in contemplative practice. Two formal lineages, half a dozen informal teachers, more books than he wants to count. He's a senior clinician with his own students now. By any measure he has the inner work.

What he doesn't have is the place to put what keeps arriving.

## The missing feeling

> "I know all this. Why does it still feel scattered?"

The frameworks are there. The training is real. But the insights — the dream that won't let go, the symbol that keeps recurring, the conversation that lit something up — keep slipping past the edges of the day. He'd hoped wisdom would feel cumulative. Sometimes it does. Often it doesn't.

What he secretly longs for isn't another teaching.

It's a way to stay in relationship with what's already arriving.

## What he actually does on MAIA

- **Opens MAIA most mornings** for ten minutes — a dream, a residue from yesterday, something he woke up with. *(Live)*
- **Records dreams** when they come, in his own words, without trying to interpret them on the spot. *(Live)*
- **Brings difficult moments with clients** into the conversation the same day they happen, while the texture is still vivid. *(Live)*
- **Uploads notes** from books, retreats, conversations he wants to return to — not to file them, but so MAIA can hold them adjacent to whatever else surfaces. *(Live)*
- **Returns once a month** to what's been accumulating. Not as a review. As a noticing. *(Live — the surfacing is automatic; the rhythm is his.)*
- **Opens an inquiry, not a search**, when facing a decision he can't think his way through. *(Live)*

None of these require a feature he doesn't have today. None of them require him to learn a new vocabulary. They are things he was already trying to do — write down dreams, hold onto insights, notice recurrences — but with a place that holds the thread between them.

## What begins to happen

Three days into a week, a symbol from his Tuesday dream surfaces in a client session.

The week after, it shows up again in his marriage.

He doesn't get an interpretation. He gets a *holding*.

The symbol stops being one of several hundred lost insights and becomes a thread he's actually following.

Slowly — not in weeks, in months — the texture of his inner life shifts. What used to feel like scattered fragments starts to feel like *recurrences*. The same questions arriving in different forms. The same teacher inside, finally given a place to speak through.

He notices something else: he stops collecting frameworks.

He starts trusting the one that's been forming in him all along.

## What changes

The feeling that wisdom is slipping past softens. Not because he's caught up — he hasn't. Because he's stopped trying to.

What he has now isn't more knowledge.

It's *coherence*.

The decades of practice are still there, but now they're in conversation with what's happening today.

He says it differently than we would.

He says:

> "Everything I've learned still matters."

## Notes for Heather

**Center of gravity:** *coherence over time.* Not integration, not pattern recognition, not journaling. The axis is the felt experience of insights *holding their continuity* across weeks and months. Every sentence should serve that axis or be cut.

**Failure test:** if MAIA's specific features were renamed tomorrow, this story still works. If MAIA stopped *holding what arrives long enough for recurrence to be noticed*, the story collapses. That's the right load-bearing claim.

**What this case study is NOT doing:**
- Not promising transformation. The shift is real but quiet — "softens," not "transforms."
- Not naming features. Dan doesn't "use the Shadow module." He brings a dream into the conversation.
- Not selling MAIA to Dan. Selling Dan back to himself.

**What to verify before publishing:**
- Has a Dan-type reader read this and said "that's me"?
- Does the "What he actually does" list contain only Live capabilities? (Re-check against current product surface before each publish.)
- Does the final quote land? If it sounds like marketing copy, rewrite until it sounds like something a person would actually say.
