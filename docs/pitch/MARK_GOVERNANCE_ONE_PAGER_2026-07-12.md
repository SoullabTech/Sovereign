# HOW DECISIONS WORK HERE — One Page for Mark (2026-07-12)

**Status**: Wave 1 artifact, drafted on Kelly's go; pending Kelly's red-line. The governance described below is **ratified canon** (2026-07-11), not company culture — it binds the way policy binds.

## The one rule everything else follows from

> **Derivation is delegated; ratification never leaves the founder.**

Analysis, research, proposals, drafts — anyone can produce them, including you, including AI sessions. But nothing becomes *standing* — no claim, no gate, no commitment, no canon — until Kelly signs it. This is not a bottleneck to work around; it is the honest form of a one-founder constitution, and the platform's audit trail exists because of it.

**What this means for you in practice:**
- **Propose freely.** Your expansiveness is why you're here. Internal vision roams; *outward claims carry stamps*.
- **Never treat a favorable analysis as a green light.** Kelly forwarding your idea with enthusiasm is interest, not ruling. Dispositions are named to be executed — "yes to X" means X, not X-and-adjacent.
- **Bring decisions red-line-ready**: the evidence, the recommendation, the bounded ask — in a form where one word from Kelly resolves it. Ratification bandwidth is the system's scarcest resource; the person who packages decisions well gets more of them ruled.

## How claims work (the short form — the claim card is the long form)

Every capability is **Live** (running, receipt available), **Designed** (specced, deliberately withheld), or **Vision** (named horizon). The ladder never collapses. Before anything leaves your mouth outward: *is it Live/Designed/Vision, and did I say which? Whose story is it? If they ask to see it, does it exist?*

## Transparency vs. exposure — the rule about what you receive

> **Found-and-closed is curriculum; found-and-open is need-to-know.**

You'll notice your reading includes audits that name real defects — that's deliberate; closed findings are how this house teaches. You'll also notice some documents you *don't* get yet: anything describing an open, unremediated issue. Not distrust — surface-area discipline. You receive those documents when their contents are history. Carry the same distinction outward: we are radically transparent about what we've *proven and fixed*, and disciplined about what's *in flight*.

## Cadence

Decisions clear in **sittings** — batched, bounded items, ruled fast when well-packaged. Your proposals join a queue; the queue clears in passes. Building can pause and resume; an unratified pile decays — so a smaller, sharper ask beats a grander, vaguer one, every time.
