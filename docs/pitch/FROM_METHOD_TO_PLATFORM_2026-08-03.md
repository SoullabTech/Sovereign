Three movements. Each one is a different kind of work, and each is worth naming separately, because
they carry different risk and a different claim on the future.

---

## Movement One — Your method becomes explicit

**What it is.** The way you actually work — how you open a relationship, what you offer, what you
notice, what you ask people to carry between sessions — written down clearly enough that software can
hold it without distorting it.

**What changes.** Right now your method lives in you. It travels only where you go, and it stops when
your calendar is full. Making it explicit is the step that makes everything after it possible.

**What it requires.** Two things, and they are different in kind:

> **Practice activation requires a governed articulation of your method, and completion of the
> appropriate rights and custody agreement for materials used within the platform.**

The first is a page or two — what you do, in what order, and why. There is a working precedent and a
template (`docs/practitioner-portal/LORALEE_ONE_PAGER.md`, alongside
`PRACTITIONER_PORTAL_TEMPLATE.md`); **verified 2026-08-03, no Larry equivalent exists yet.**
⚠️ **We cannot write it for you without inventing your method, and inventing your method is precisely
the failure mode this architecture exists to prevent.**

The second is not a document gap — it is a **rights and custody boundary**, and it is unresolved. Who
owns the method as expressed in the platform, what the platform may do with it, and what happens to
it if either party walks away are questions with answers, and they should be written down before the
method is embedded rather than after. A template does not solve this; only an agreement does.

**Status:** ⏳ open, and the fastest thing on this page to unblock.

## Movement Two — The method becomes a place

**What it is.** Now What? — a workspace where the relationship between you and a client actually
lives: what belongs to you, what belongs to them, what is shared, what continues between
conversations.

**What changes.** The method stops depending on your memory and your notes app. A client can return
to their own work without you present. The relationship has a location.

**What it requires.** The trust foundation — identity, relationship structure, privacy boundaries,
authorship — which is largely built and verified, and the Stage One and Two work described in *How We
Build Now What?*, which is not started.

**Status:** ◐ designed, not built.

## Movement Three — The place becomes a platform

**What it is.** AIN OS — the same foundation carrying more than one practice: other practitioners,
authors, organizations, communities.

**What changes.** The architecture that protects one coaching relationship turns out to protect any
relationship where someone is developing and someone else is accompanying them. That is the general
claim, and it is the reason this is built as a platform rather than an app.

**What it requires.** That Movement Two works for one practitioner first. Scaling an unproven
relationship model multiplies whatever is wrong with it.

**Status:** ○ vision.

---

## Why the process runs in this direction

**Most platforms:**

```
Feature → User adopts → Data accumulates → AI learns → More automation
```

**This one:**

```
Human relationship → Meaning and boundaries → Experience design
                   → Platform architecture → Intelligence in service of the human
```

The slower direction is the durable one. Anyone can add a feature; the thing that is hard to copy is
an architecture where the boundaries are structural rather than promised.
