# MAIA — The Practice → Ritual → Autonomy Test (Product Discipline)

> The Sovereignty Invariant is not merely a marketing discipline. It is a **product discipline.**

**What this is:** a single filter for every feature and roadmap decision. The same principle that governs claims, positioning, and stories now governs what we *build.*
**Provenance:** Kelly, 2026-06-03 — emerged from the case-study / practice / ritual architecture.
**Relation to canon:** the operational form of `docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`; sharpens the "Sovereignty Invariant Check" already in `CLAUDE.md` into a chained, decidable test. **Candidate for canon promotion** (steward's call).

---

## The test — run in order, stop at the first failure

1. **Does it support a practice?** — If not, **reject.**
2. **Does the practice strengthen a ritual?** — If not, **question it.**
3. **Does the ritual increase human autonomy?** — If not, **reject.**

A feature must make the human *more capable of carrying their own life.* If it makes them more dependent on the system, it fails — regardless of how impressive or engaging it is.

---

## Worked examples

| Feature | Practice? | Ritual? | Autonomy? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning Reflection** | supports daily attention | becomes the ritual of listening | increases self-authorship | ✅ **Pass** |
| **Decision Inquiry** | person reflects | develops discernment | increases autonomy | ✅ **Pass** |
| **AI-generated life summaries** | requires no practice | weakens attention | increases dependence | ❌ **Fail** |
| **Cross-modality synthesis** | MAIA performs the integration | the human stops integrating | weakens ritual formation | ❌ **Fail** |

## The through-line

Everything now resolves through one question:

> **Does this make the human more capable of carrying their own life?**

That question runs from the very first audience discussion all the way through claims, positioning, stories, practices, rituals, features, and roadmap. One discipline now governs:

- **claims** → `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`
- **positioning** → `AUDIENCE_POSITIONING.md`
- **stories** → `CASE_STUDY_LIBRARY.md` / `CASE_STUDY_FELT_FORM_BRIEF.md`
- **practices** → `PRACTICE_LIBRARY.md`
- **rituals** → `TRANSFORMATION_JOURNEYS.md`
- **features + roadmap** → *this test*

## Why this is the freeze point

The **framework** is finished — not the work. The remaining journeys, and future features, are execution inside a solved structure. The test *is* the structure. What changes from here is the *kind* of work: not "what is the architecture?" but "does this candidate pass the test?"
