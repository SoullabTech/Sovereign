## The one-line read

**The opportunity is larger than we thought; the launch is narrower than we thought.**

---

## The finding that sets the whole sequence

Production evidence (member attribution, 2026-06-06): practitioners **already return** — but to
**member-facing MAIA** (MAIA as their *own* companion), **not** to the practitioner tooling
(Studio / Co-Lab). They have access. They don't cross.

> **The blocker is product-validation of the MAIA→practice bridge — not access, not navigation,
> not feature-completeness.**

This is good news: it's a **bridge problem, not a demand problem.** The deepest layer already
attracts repeated engagement. The question is whether we can convert
**Companion → Practice → Community** into a natural progression.

## The coordinates Heather needs (evidenced vs. bet)

Marketing can only stand on the first row.

| Layer | Status | What it means for copy |
|---|---|---|
| **MAIA as the practitioner's *own* companion** — reflection, preparation, continuity *for them* | **EVIDENCED / LIVE** | Most active part of the system; practitioners return to it. Safe to market today. |
| **MAIA as continuity for their *clients*** — "prepare me for this client," hold the client thread | **DESIGNED / BET** | Built in code, **not yet adopted.** This is the bridge. **Validate in Phase-1 interviews before building a campaign on it.** |
| **Co-Lab as practitioner commons** — communities of practice, consultation, referrals | **VISION / BET** | Founder-dogfooded only. Not a behavior being validated. Do **not** market as built. |

**The precise trap:** the evidenced continuity is the practitioner's *own* thread
(companion-for-self). *"Never lose the thread of a **client** relationship"* is the **bridge-bet**,
not the evidence. Lead with the narrower true claim; earn the wider one.

---

## What to market now / hold / not-yet

- **Market now (evidenced):** MAIA as companion. Continuity, reflection, preparation.
  *"Never lose the thread"* — of your own work. Burden-first narrative: **"You help others. Who helps you?"**
- **Hold as "forming" (bet):** client-continuity / "prepare me for this client" — strong, but frame
  as the near-term promise we're validating, not a shipped feature.
- **Do not market yet (vision):** Co-Lab, community, commons, collaboration, consultation, referrals.
  Frame as *"the first circles are forming — join early,"* never as an existing place.

## The wedge (the smallest irreplaceable thing)

Not *"Professional Home for Practitioners"* — yet.

**"Relationship Continuity for Practitioners."**
Smaller, clearer, explainable — and it expands into the home later.

Moat test — *what does a practitioner lose if they leave?* — answers cleanly only once continuity
is real: *"I lose the thread with my people."* Build toward that answer; don't claim it before it's true.

## Launch sequence (so marketing tracks product reality)

- **Phase 1 — now (validate the bridge):** interview the active returners —
  **Tara, Andrea Fagan, Nathan, Jondi, Rob.**
  Ask: *"What is MAIA helping you do that other tools don't?"* and specifically
  *"When you talk to MAIA, are you ever doing it **about your client work**?"*
  That one answer tells you whether the bridge is **short** (formalize a behavior they already
  improvise) or **long** (create a new behavior). **Not** "which features do you want?"
- **Phase 2 — build the bridge, not more rooms:** prepare-me-for-this-client · summarize what
  matters · what did I commit to · what to remember before this session. Surface what's already built.
- **Phase 3 — community lands (gated):** Co-Lab / circles / referrals / Relationship Memory —
  only after a measurable inversion of today's zero: **N practitioners with ≥1 real client and
  ≥1 prepped session, sustained over several weeks.** Then the commons has somewhere real to land.

## The failure test (apply to every claim before it ships)

*If a practitioner arrives expecting this, does the product deliver it today?*
If no → it's roadmap. Tell it as *"where we're going,"* not as website copy.

---

## The category north star (protected — tell boldly, as the future)

*"The Studio for People Who Help People."* Relational infrastructure for practitioners. This is the
legitimate category and the right story — present **and** future. The discipline is not *"shrink the
vision."* It is *"don't dress the future in present tense."* — *We do not tell tomorrow's story as if
it were today's.*
