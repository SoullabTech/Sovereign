**Descent (Fire → Water):** The inward journey
- Fire 1: The Call — something wants to happen

**Return (Earth → Air):** The outward journey
- Earth 1: Structure — how can this live in the world?
- Earth 2: Building — steady effort, resource gathering
- Earth 3: Manifestation — real in the world now
- Air 1: Sharing — one-to-one articulation
- Air 2: Teaching — codification into framework

This pattern is **fractal** — it operates at every scale: a single conversation, a relationship, a life domain, a life chapter. MAIA serves as the **consciousness GPS** that helps users locate where they are, orient to natural next phases, and navigate with appropriate support.

## The Neuroscience Parallel

This architecture mirrors what we know about human cognition:

**The Corpus Callosum Model**
The brain's two hemispheres process reality in parallel — not sequentially. They maintain separation as a form of higher-order integration. The corpus callosum doesn't merge them; it allows them to communicate while preserving their distinct modes of knowing.

Similarly, MAIA's elemental framework treats Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Aether as **parallel awareness fields** that inform each other without collapsing into a single stream.

**Supporting Research**
- **Iain McGilchrist's "The Master and His Emissary"** — the right hemisphere as the master, the left as the emissary; MAIA embodies this by serving rather than commanding
- **Herrmann's Whole Brain Model** — four quadrants of cognitive preference mapped to elemental domains
- **Jung's Typology of Self** — the individuation process as spiral, not linear; shadow work as the descent phase
