# The Trust Velocity Flywheel

## The Core Insight

MAIA's business model is built on a fundamental truth from 35 years of practice:

> **Trust compounds. Dependency depletes.**

Most AI products optimize for engagement (time in app, return visits, data extraction). This creates dependency, which feels like value but actually erodes trust over time.

MAIA optimizes for sovereignty. This creates trust, which compounds into genuine value.

## Why This Works

### Stage 1: Sanctuary Enables Honesty

People won't speak freely to a system that might:
- Train on their conversations
- Sell their patterns
- Use their vulnerability against them

Sanctuary Mode is the architectural proof that MAIA serves the person, not the data model. This enables the honesty that makes everything else possible.

### Stage 2: Honesty Creates Real Patterns

When people speak freely, real patterns emerge:
- Not performed wellness
- Not what they think they should say
- The actual texture of their inner life

### Stage 3: Real Patterns Feel True

Patterns derived from authentic sharing have a quality that manufactured insights lack. Users recognize themselves. The reflection feels accurate.

### Stage 4: User Correction Makes Patterns Truer

When users can correct or refine what MAIA notices, the patterns become more accurate over time. This is collaborative meaning-making, not algorithmic imposition.

### Stage 5: Truer Patterns = More Value

As patterns become more accurate, they become more useful. Users see their own becoming. The value is undeniable.

### Stage 6: Value Justifies Premium

When something genuinely helps you become yourself, paying for it feels right — not like extraction, but like supporting something that supports you.

### Stage 7: Premium Funds Ethical Development

Revenue from subscribers who value sovereignty funds continued development of sovereignty-respecting features. No need to compromise ethics for growth.

### The Cycle Accelerates

Each turn through the flywheel builds trust. Trust compounds. The flywheel spins faster.

## Metrics That Matter

**Wrong metrics** (engagement trap):
- Daily active users
- Time in app
- Messages per session
- Return rate

**Right metrics** (trust flywheel):
- User-reported accuracy of patterns
- Voluntary subscription conversion (not prompted)
- Referral rate (beads gifted)
- Sanctuary usage (trust indicator)
- Voluntary exits (healthy completion)

The goal is not to maximize engagement. The goal is to maximize sovereignty — which, paradoxically, creates sustainable engagement.
