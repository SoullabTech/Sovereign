## The Three Pitches

### 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> "MAIA is a consciousness companion built on 35 years of psychospiritual practice — Harvard Mind Body Institute, private practice across energy work, Jungian depth psychology, hypnotherapy, shamanic traditions, and more. It's not AI with a therapy prompt. It's a lifetime of integration made into technology. The ethics aren't a feature — they're how I've always practiced. That's the moat."

---

### 2-Minute Founder Narrative

> "I started in a PhD program for clinical and developmental psychology. I did a two-year practicum at Harvard's Mind Body Institute. Then I spent 35 years in private practice — not in one modality, but across all of them. Energy healing. Bodywork. Hypnotherapy. Shamanic journeywork. Jungian archetypal work. NLP. Whatever the client needed, I learned to meet them there.
>
> What I discovered is that all these approaches are talking about the same thing from different angles. Fire, water, earth, air, aether — the elements aren't metaphors. They're the actual texture of human experience. Spiralogic is the map that emerged from holding all of it together for 25 years.
>
> MAIA isn't AI pretending to be a therapist. It's the distillation of everything I've learned about what actually helps people become themselves — made into architecture. The three voices, the capsules, the sanctuary mode, the elemental mapping — none of that came from a product roadmap. It came from 35 years of sitting with people in transformation.
>
> You can copy code. You can't copy a lifetime of practice."

### Member Invitation

> "MAIA doesn't advertise. She spreads through relationship. Every member receives beads — invites to share with people they trust. When you gift a bead, you write a blessing. The person who receives it sees your words before they enter.
>
> This is how conscious community grows. Not viral. Relational. If someone trusted you with a bead, the door is open."
