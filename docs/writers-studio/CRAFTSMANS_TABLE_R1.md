# Writer's Studio — Craftsman's Table R1

Status: CANONICAL PRODUCT CONTRACT  
Phase: JARVIS · Craftsman's Table R1  
Reference case: Elemental Alchemy refinement case study

> The manuscript is the craftsman's table. MAIA is the relational intelligence beside it. Hermes is the invisible continuity that carries understanding into making.

## 1. Purpose

Writer's Studio is not an AI editorial dashboard.

The Studio must let a person move naturally through:

**See → Talk → Make → Refine**

The earlier prototype proved important substrate:
- MAIA can read whole-work and bounded scopes.
- conversation can survive permission boundaries.
- conversation can cross into an exact current manuscript locus.
- exact manuscript text can remain authoritative.
- proposed wording can be represented as separable edits.
- individual edits can remain optional.
- Apply can remain the mutation boundary.

It also proved what must disappear: relationship plumbing, revision-chain management, control grids, and detached editorial rooms.

R1 hides the machinery and puts craft back on the page.

## 2. Canonical experience

### Seeing
MAIA may help understand:
- whole manuscript;
- chapter;
- section;
- passage;
- sentence or phrase;
- structure, arc, theme, continuity, voice, coherence, reader experience, provenance, developmental movement, or another requested lens.

Whole-work observation is orientation, not an automatic edit instruction.

### Talking
Writer and MAIA discover:
- what the writer means;
- what the reader should experience;
- what already works and must be protected;
- what may need strengthening;
- whether the next move is no change, a small change, an example, a structural intervention, or more conversation.

### Hermes crossing
The writer says:

**Work this into the writing →**

That gesture means:
1. preserve the conversation and intention;
2. resolve the strongest lawful current manuscript locus;
3. open the manuscript at that locus;
4. carry what was discovered without requiring restatement;
5. keep MAIA present beside the manuscript;
6. never open a second dashboard or revision room;
7. never manufacture or persist an invisible author turn merely because the writer crossed into Craft.

Hermes has no separate room and does not need a separate persona. It is experienced as continuity. The crossing carries context; it does not pretend the writer said something they did not say.

### Making
The manuscript becomes the working surface. MAIA may demonstrate a bounded craft move directly in the copy using professional editorial notation. Nothing applies automatically.

### Refining
Writer and MAIA may:
- keep;
- reject;
- modify;
- combine;
- ask why;
- ask for another way;
- make an intervention lighter or stronger;
- protect exact language;
- write a new version;
- preview in context;
- apply a writer-owned version;
- undo.

The work stays visible throughout.

## 3. Craftsman's table

The center of Develop Craft is the real manuscript, not a detached textarea and not a comparison dashboard.

Default hierarchy:
1. manuscript;
2. active locus;
3. marks directly on the manuscript;
4. MAIA conversation beside it;
5. contextual tools only when summoned.

Permanent dashboards, card grids, and control matrices are prohibited in Develop Craft.

The surrounding chapter remains visible enough to judge cadence, continuity, and reader experience.

## 4. Markup and Preview

These are two views of the same working state.

### Markup — editor's eye
- original prose stays readable;
- proposed removals and insertions are visible;
- choices are individually addressable;
- annotations attach to exact words.

### Preview — reader's eye
- currently chosen changes are composed into clean prose;
- no editorial marks;
- preview never mutates the canonical manuscript.

## 5. Professional editing conventions

The table should feel credible to a professional editor while remaining understandable to a non-writer.

| Editorial act | Default visual treatment | Plain-language meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Delete | red strike-through or deletion bracket | remove these words |
| Insert | blue inline insertion | add these words |
| Replace | paired red deletion plus blue insertion | use this wording instead |
| Move | subtle move connector and destination cue | move this phrase or sentence |
| Comment | quiet margin note anchored to exact text | MAIA's reason or question |
| Query | small question annotation | something may need clarification |
| Protect / Stet | restore-original marker | keep the author's original |
| Alternative | numbered or lettered inline alternative | another possible way |
| Active locus | quiet bracket or halo | this is where we are working |

Color is never the only signal.

Optional professional proofing depth may expose:
- stet;
- transpose;
- insert;
- delete;
- close up;
- add space;
- paragraph / no paragraph;
- caps / lowercase;
- italic / roman;
- query;
- awkward;
- repetition;
- consistency;
- fact or source check.

These are progressive disclosure, never novice defaults.

## 6. Local edit interaction

Clicking one marked edit opens a compact palette:

**Use this · Keep mine · Another way · Why? · Teach me**

Professional expansion may reveal:
- edit reason;
- style versus meaning classification;
- reader-effect hypothesis;
- provenance or evidence;
- scope.

The palette appears beside the mark and disappears when attention moves away.

## 7. MAIA beside the table

The right field is a conversation, not a second application.

Default:
- MAIA's relevant response;
- one composer: **What do you want the writing to do?**
- optional Ways to work disclosure.

Natural-language requests may invoke any capability:
- Keep more of my sentence.
- This changes my meaning.
- Make the shift more embodied.
- Show me three possibilities.
- Why did you cut that?
- What would a professional copyeditor notice here?
- Read this paragraph in context.
- Look at the whole chapter again.
- Does this solve the arc issue we discussed?
- Compare this with the earlier version.

The writer should not have to translate intention into product commands.

## 8. Suggestion posture and writer-owned synthesis

MAIA's ability to bring wording proactively is governed by the writer's own Studio setting.

### Wait-first
If proactive suggestions are off:
- MAIA discusses, reflects, teaches, and asks;
- wording appears only when the writer explicitly requests it.

### Suggest-first
If proactive suggestions are on:
- MAIA may place a bounded provisional suggestion directly on the table during a visible Craft exchange when useful;
- entering Craft by itself never persists a hidden author prompt to cause that suggestion;
- the suggestion is still a primer, never an answer and never an application.

### Three lawful writer responses
A MAIA suggestion may become raw material in three ways:

1. **Primer** — the writer sees the move and writes something different.
2. **Edit MAIA** — the writer directly changes MAIA's suggested wording.
3. **Hybrid** — the writer combines original language, selected MAIA moves, and new language of their own.

All three produce one **writer-owned working copy**.

From that point forward, MAIA must treat the writer-owned working copy as the current authority. She may help refine it, compare it, or suggest bounded alternatives, but she may not silently restore her earlier proposal.

This is the interaction model established in the Elemental Alchemy case study: MAIA offers material; the writer authors the synthesis; MAIA then helps integrate and refine the writer's actual version.

## 8. Full capability set — available, not permanently displayed

### Understand
- discuss what is happening;
- explain what works;
- name friction;
- identify reader-experience hypotheses;
- teach the craft already present;
- go deeper technically.

### Generate possibilities
- examples;
- ideas;
- two to four genuinely different alternatives;
- smallest useful change first;
- another direction;
- lighter / stronger;
- keep more of mine;
- more embodied, relational, concrete, quiet, clear, or another natural-language intention.

### Edit
- copyedit;
- line edit;
- developmental micro-edit;
- trim repetition;
- strengthen transitions;
- clarify reference;
- repair sentence logic;
- rhythm and cadence;
- diction;
- paragraphing;
- continuity;
- consistency;
- source or provenance note when appropriate.

### Compare
- original versus current working state;
- MAIA option versus writer version;
- earlier revision versus current revision;
- local context;
- chapter context;
- whole-work intention.

### Protect
- settled work;
- quotations and sourced material;
- writer voice;
- intentional ambiguity;
- imagery and metaphor;
- worldview and ontological language;
- rhythm and cadence;
- individual interventions via Stet.

### Apply / Undo
Only a writer-owned chosen state may cross the mutation boundary.

Preview is not Apply.  
Saving an alternative is not Apply.  
MAIA proposing text is not Apply.  
Selecting a mark is not Apply.

## 9. Novice to professional allowance

This is one system, not separate products.

### Guided default
For a non-writer:
- ordinary language;
- one useful next act;
- two to four options maximum;
- smallest useful intervention first;
- visible example;
- brief reason;
- no unexplained jargon;
- MAIA teaches when useful.

Default local palette:
**Use this · Keep mine · Another way · Why?**

### Professional depth
A professional may reveal:
- track-change semantics;
- proofing marks;
- style versus meaning classification;
- line, copy, and developmental rationale;
- scope and revision latitude;
- provenance;
- reader-effect hypothesis;
- continuity links;
- version comparison;
- advanced editorial vocabulary.

### Plain ↔ Expert
Controls explanation density and vocabulary, not intelligence or authority.

### Light ↔ Heavy
Controls intervention latitude:
- Touch;
- Light;
- Moderate;
- Strong;
- Recast.

Default remains conservative. Larger recasts are explicit.

## 10. Whole ↔ part intelligence

The writer can move fluidly across scale without leaving the table.

### Whole manuscript
Examples:
- What is the book doing as a whole?
- Where does the ceremonial arc thin out?
- Where am I repeating this claim?
- Does Chapter 10 earn its place in the larger movement?

Output is orientation plus a small number of evidenced loci, never an automatic whole-book rewrite.

### Chapter
Arc, pacing, reader movement, repetition, transitions, neighboring chapters.

MAIA may surface a few candidate loci. Choosing one brings it to the table.

### Passage
Normal Craft locus.

### Sentence / phrase
Precision work: diction, cadence, syntax, copyediting, micro-continuity.

### Zoom law
**Larger scope tells us where and why; smaller scope is where wording changes become exact.**

No global observation becomes a local edit without evidenced or writer-chosen locus.

## 11. Elemental Alchemy canonical witness

The Chapter 10 case is the reference interaction.

The field recognized that Maya's story made the elemental model lived and relatable while some phase explanation risked becoming systematic or conceptual.

The writer clarified the intention:

> The reader should experience the spiral bodily and relationally, not merely understand it conceptually, without the prose becoming over-written.

MAIA recognized the Fire sequence **Activating, Amplifying, Actualizing** as one place where that tension became concrete.

Correct Studio behavior:

1. Writer chooses **Work this into the writing →**
2. Hermes carries:
   - writer intention;
   - MAIA understanding;
   - what must be protected;
   - relevant chapter context.
3. Table opens at the Fire passage without manufacturing an invisible writer turn.
4. If wording is explicitly requested — or the writer's proactive-suggestion setting permits it during a visible exchange — MAIA makes one small provisional demonstration directly in the manuscript.
5. Writer sees exact red/blue marks.
6. Writer may keep original, accept one change, reject another, ask why, ask to keep cadence, request more embodied but less overwritten, or create a hybrid version.
7. Preview shows selected craft state in clean prose.
8. MAIA can reread that state in chapter or whole-book context.
9. Only the writer's chosen version may be applied.

Must not happen:
- manual hunt for a resolvable passage;
- restating the conversation;
- editorial dashboard;
- isolated revision room;
- capability card grid;
- forced switch between insight mode and editing mode;
- MAIA rewrite replacing writer authorship;
- silent mutation.

## 12. Product laws

1. Manuscript first.
2. MAIA is the single relational voice.
3. Hermes is continuity, not a room or persona the writer manages.
4. A meaningful human gesture advances the relationship.
5. Never require restatement of a meaningful gesture at the next surface.
6. The manuscript is the craftsman's table.
7. Professional capability must not require professional jargon.
8. Capabilities are ambient and contextual, not permanent dashboards.
9. Writer voice, meaning, worldview, rhythm, imagery, and intentional ambiguity are protected by default.
10. Smallest useful change first.
11. Examples are primers, not answers.
12. Reader effects are hypotheses, not facts.
13. Whole-work intelligence orients; exact manuscript evidence authorizes wording work.
14. No proposal is an application.
15. Only a writer-owned chosen version may mutate the manuscript.
16. Undo remains visible after Apply.
17. Settled work can be protected from unsolicited disturbance.
18. Sophisticated substrate must never require the writer to operate the plumbing.

## 15. Definition of done

R1 is complete when the writer can say:

> I know what I am trying to do. MAIA understands it. We are looking at the same words. I can see what she is suggesting, understand why, keep what is mine, try possibilities, and shape the final language with her without ever losing the writing.

That is the Craftsman's Table.
