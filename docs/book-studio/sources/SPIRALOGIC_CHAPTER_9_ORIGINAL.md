## Augmented Reality

Much of our lives and our experiences are governed by insights, information, and habits based on past experiences. We are never fully in a state of no-mindedness, well, at least, no one I know. In other words, each moment of our lives is flavored by our perception which is based on and informed by past experiences.

Although I naturally tend to prefer living spontaneously and in the moment, there are parts of me who know from experience that certain activities lead to certain outcomes. For instance, I love coffee and could drink it all day but there is this part of me that reminds me regularly that drinking it after 4pm will lead to a terrible night sleep. This experiential form of knowing is bolstered by researching the effects of caffeine on the endocrine and nervous system. I also have a teapot with a canister of herbal tea nearby to keep me from making a cup of coffee after 4pm. This is, in a small example, how our lives are augmented. The more I know, the better my decision making and choices will be, most of the time. I still have free will to drink an espresso at 10pm but I am also fated to deal with the results.

When I go into meditations and active imagination, where I imagine meeting with spirit guides, archetypal characters, and going back into memories or projecting into future possibilities, I think of this concept of augmented reality. My experiences in these states of imaginative play are always augmented. For instance, I am monitoring my energy, emotions, senses, and thoughts as I come into contact with different energies and experiences.

While imaginary, they still have the same effect on all elements of my consciousness. My energy rises when I'm in the presence of a wise guide. My emotions soar when I'm having an enchanting experience. My body shifts, twitches, and moves as if it is in the experience. My mind is performing just as if all of it is real, because of an important level, everything we imagine is real, at least to the elements of perception. The more aware I am of the state of these elements the better I am at engaging each moment consciously, lucidly, and effectively.

I watch my son playing virtual reality games and he has no idea what is happening in the room around him. I can be standing inches from him and he isn't aware. He is immersed in a world with just enough physics to make his mind believe it is real. I tried them on and, after about thirty minutes of adjustment, my whole being started to engage these virtual worlds as if they were real. And the wild bit is when I took the headset off my mind had to readjust to the physical world.

We don't live in an objective world, we live in a perceived world. Our experiences are not governed by objective events, objective facts, in an objective reality. Rather, we live our unique realities into the world as if we were moving through a simulator. You can have ten people attending an intimate rehearsal and there will be ten realities happening. None will have the same experience. I find that fascinating.

The beauty of augmented reality is that it can be organized and shared. My experiences in Kathmandu can be shared with you and will give you insight into Kathmandu. It won't be the 'real' Kathmandu but it will affect your experience when you visit. We influence each other. Reality is a joint venture. The more clear we are on the elements of our experience, the more and better we can share with each other.

## Rising with the Spirit of Fire

All great ideas eventually elevate to the fires of inspired vision where they illuminate our spirits, shine into the world, and refine our visions into wise action.

For example, my client and colleague, Lisa, was trying to sort out whether or not to travel to an Emotional Freedom Technique, EFT, energy healing workshop out of state. We started in the first phase of the fire realm as she shared her vision for the experience, how it could help her, who she saw herself to be now and how the workshop would make her better in her private practice. We then moved to the center of the fire and imagined her at the workshop and imaginatively journeyed through the experiences she'd likely have. She felt the excitement of learning something new, the challenges of being around people she had never met before, and the joy of being around those who shared her love for EFT. Then we imagined her coming home with new learning she could apply in her healing practice.

These are the three phases of fire. We started there since we were focused on a future oriented project. Fire ignites our future-oriented intuition around what can be if we have the courage to take the necessary steps to fulfill our destinies. Much of the first phase contains some forms of fantasies, whether fears or unrealistic expectations. The Calcinatio of fire burns away the impurities of what is not true or real about ourselves. Self-doubt, negative self-images, or even grandiose visions of ourselves distort the truth of the pure vision shining within. Still, our spirit within is guided by forces which recognize we aren't perfect carriers of these visions. It knows intuitively that we will burn away what is not real or true about our self-image and our vision as we take the spiritual fire walk to fulfill our will.

Fire visions begin with identifying ourselves with our goals. It's seeing ourselves as the hero of our lives and intuitively experiencing the rewards for our efforts. This is analogous to the first phase of the hero awakening in Joseph Campbell's Monomyth. A fire inside inspires us into action and ignites something within which sees who we are and what we can become in the world. This vision doesn't need to be grand or epic. It can be a vision we have of starting a new exercise routine to improve our health and well-being. The main focus of the first phase of fire (Fire 1) is identifying with our intentions. However, we could stay in this state of identification and never move forward, never leave our couch. As long as we are here, we can maintain fantasies and desires without reality testing them.

The middle phase of fire (Fire 2) is taking the steps needed to see your vision through. For Lisa, her first step was to sign-up for the workshop. While the first phase of fire burns away all of the egoic self-concepts which distort our visions the middle phase of fire asks us to shine our vision out into the world. In school, we are given lessons and then we take tests but in life we are tested by the fires as we learn lessons.

This is the way of the middle fire. It is the gauntlet of action. This is considered the fixed mode in astrology, represented by a circle with a dot in the center, representing us and the world surrounding us. When we dare to take our vision beyond personal identification into expression, we invite the fires of projections and reflections to reveal what is real and working for us.

Lisa said the workshop had its challenges and its teachings. As an introvert, she had a hard time participating in many of the exercises requiring her to demonstrate her learning in front of the class. Even though she felt personally capable of doing the EFT process, her nervousness and fear of being judged by others got in the way of her performing as well as she knew she could. Still, there were moments where fellow students came up to her after her demonstrations to say how much they loved her energy, her style, and her unique way of being with her 'client'. The rewards slowly began to burn away the fears of judgment and being 'wrong'. By the end of the workshop she was resonating with several other students as they shared their experiences with each other.

This is the middle fire realm. When we dare to step out of the vertical axis of our inner selves where our illusions of ourself are safely guarded and tucked away from public scrutiny, we expose our shadows to the world as well as our light. The middle fire process invites us to shine bright. What needs to burn away will. If an artist exhibits their art, they will get all manner of feedback. Some useful, some not. If an actor steps out on stage and performs they are in the field of fire magic where our visions are projected out onto the world and the energy reflects back to us.

If we feel resonance then our performance improves. If we feel discord we are left to manage it. Whether the discord encourages higher levels of performance or causes us to stumble and lose faith in ourselves is the risk we take and the teaching we gain. Performance is an opportunity to shine our light into the world and engage the feedback from the world. If we see it as the next level of growth and an opportunity to shine, the experience is uplifting. All else is an opportunity to release what isn't real, useful, or true about us.

The final phase of fire brings together the upward vectoring energy of our inspired will and the encircling energy of our actions and the reactions of the world around us to form a spiraling movement transforming us. The ultimate goal of fire is to inspire us into actions which will elevate our consciousness, enlighten our minds, and make us wiser beings. This is considered the mutable mode in astrology, where the successful journey through the fire of self-awareness and self-expression leads to self-actualization.

Lisa left the workshop energized about what EFT could bring to her practice (fire) and passionate about why she wanted to offer EFT healing to her clients (water). She also had a more practical sense of how she could use EFT in her practice (earth) as well as a greater understanding of what she would say to her clients to communicate the value and potential of EFT (air). These elements of experience are always present in all of our creative efforts.

Seeing these processes elementally helps to simplify these stages of learning and development so we can become more aware of each stage of our personal process. Lisa has continued to integrate EFT in her practice and has developed her own style of presenting, performing, and expressing the work. It has transformed into her unique approach even as it carries the initial wisdom of EFT process. All of my teachers from different traditions expected me to learn their ways but to practice what I learned in my way. This is the wisdom of fire- illuminate and purify to shine our true nature through all we do.

## The Evolution Imperative of Our Present Era

Once upon a time, our view of who we are individually and collectively was limited by prevailing wisdom, limited information, and cultural biases. Our journey of self-discovery was usually heavily pre-determined by these influences. While these influences still exist, the problem has shifted dramatically. Now, we have so much information from so many very different sources and we are challenged to make sense out of it all and even less know what to do with what we are learning. Our local and cozy relationship with reality is blasted open with exponential growth in new information and insights.

One of the great challenges of our time is how to bring together all of the information from diverse fields of inquiry and distill it into a modern, heuristically useful approach. There are so many wonderful new practices, systems, and approaches to self-development that span time and cultures. Many novel approaches have come and gone. We are now ready to bring them all together as a way to discover the next-level of emerging reality that they are collectively revealing.

We are looking back at our intellectual and cultural heritages with a renewed sense of awe and wonderment. How did they develop? What made them last? What are the unifying elements that exist between all of these enduring systems that make them so effective? The saying that, "there is nothing new under the sun" is nothing new, yet it doesn't solve the problem that we all face: how to effectively apply time-honored wisdom to our own unique, modern lives. A powerful way of remembering the wisdom of our ancestors and redeeming the path ahead is to look at the fundamental elements of our human experience.
