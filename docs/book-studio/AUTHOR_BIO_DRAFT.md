## Version 3 — Long (≈350 words)
*For an "About the Author" page in the back of the book itself — between "Additional Resources" and the final blank pages. Optional but recommended; gives the reader a place to land after the last sentence.*

> Kelly Nezat has spent nearly four decades inside a single question: *what does it actually take to live a phenomenal life?* Not merely a productive one, or even a wise one — but a life in which all the elements of being are in living relationship with each other.
>
> His path began in the academic register. Doctoral studies in clinical and developmental psychology at Suffolk University. A two-year practicum at Harvard Beth Israel's Mind/Body Institute. Years of training in research methodology, with a slow recognition of its limits when it came to what mattered most — the actual moment a person sits across from you in the middle of their unraveling and you have to know what to do.
>
> What followed was thirty years of integrated practice. Energy healing. Bodywork. Hypnotherapy. Shamanic journeywork. Jungian depth psychology. Somatic experiencing. NLP. Meditation guidance. Not as a sequence — as one continuous body of work, learning to meet people wherever they actually were.
>
> Out of that long apprenticeship came **Spiralogic** — an elemental developmental model that traces how fire, water, earth, air, and aether arise in our inner lives, and how staying in relationship with their movement is the practice underneath every contemplative tradition he has studied. *Elemental Alchemy* is the long-form expression of that work.
>
> Kelly is the founder of **Soullab Media** and the architect of **MAIA**, a sovereign consciousness companion that he often describes simply: *it is not technology pretending to be wise. It is a lifetime of practice made into architecture.* MAIA does not interpret. It helps people stay in relationship with what their own lives are already trying to teach them.
>
> Kelly lives in [CITY, STATE] with his wife Andrea and their children Sophie and Augusten. When he is not writing or working on MAIA, he is most often outside with his son — building a fire, walking the woods, watching the dark fall the way it did when he was a boy.
>
> *Elemental Alchemy* is the book he spent twenty-five years looking for and couldn't find. So eventually he wrote it.
