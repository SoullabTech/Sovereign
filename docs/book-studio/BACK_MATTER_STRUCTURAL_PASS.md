## Confirmed defect — the Four Grades set is incomplete

`## The Four Grades of the Elements` (L2440) contains **three of five elements**:

- `### The Four Grades of Water` (L2444)
- `### The Four Grades of Earth` (L2451)
- `### The Four Grades of Air` (L2458)

**Fire and Aether are absent.** All three present sets follow an identical
four-part form (Primal / Inner / Collective / Universal). The Contents promises
"The Four Grades of the Elements" without qualification.

Studio caught this correctly. Remedy is an authorial choice between:
1. **Add** the Four Grades of Fire and of Aether, in the established form; or
2. **Rename/narrow** the section and its Contents entry to what it actually
   contains.

Option 1 is the only one that preserves the book's elemental symmetry, but it
means writing new material after the developmental pass closed — an explicit
authorial decision, not an editorial default.

## Second defect of the same class — not caught by Studio

`## The 12 Facets of the Spiralogic Profile` (L2513) contains **five entries**:

`1. Fire` · `2. Water` · `3. Earth` · `4. Air` · `5. Aether`

The title promises twelve. The Contents repeats the promise (L115). No
twelve-facet enumeration appears anywhere else in the book — the phrase occurs
exactly twice, both times as this title.

This is the **same promise-versus-delivery failure** as the Four Grades, in the
adjacent section, and it is arithmetically explicit rather than inferable. The
same two remedies apply. Note that the twelve-facet model is referenced nowhere
else, so option 2 (rename to what the section is) carries no cross-reference
cost.

**Pattern:** the appendix contains at least two sections whose titles promise a
complete set the content does not deliver. Check the remaining appendix sections
against their own titles before closing this pass.
