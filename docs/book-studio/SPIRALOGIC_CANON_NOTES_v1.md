## Drift watch

Patterns to recognize and resist in future passes (from any source — LLM, collaborator, or self):

1. **Lock-and-relitigate.** A decision is closed, then reframed under new vocabulary in the next pass. Recognize and refuse.
2. **Explanatory bridge sentences.** *"Air is the relational engagement of what has been created"* / *"The arc can be named simply but is lived as recursive movement"* — sentences that *define* what the prose is meant to *enact.* Cut.
3. **Slogan compression.** *"A spiral amplifier of human capacity"* / *"awareness becomes creation and creation becomes shared reality"* — neat, marketable, not yet earned. Hold for after positioning is proven.
4. **Premature canon documents.** Multi-section frameworks with system prompts, state machines, and detection logic — produced before the system has shown what it actually does. Wait for evidence.
5. **Surface noise.** Emoji headers, bolded section frames, decision-tracker tables. Editorial work is not project management.
