# Meet Kelly Nezat

*Draft — Kelly's distilled first-person essay, 2026-06-21 (the "calling / threshold" recollection). **His approval governs.** First person by design: this page is the person speaking; the Amazon bio is the third-person introduction. Credential facts canonical (doctoral studies, no degree). Spiritual experience told as lived testimony, never objective claim (Soullab Principle) — "calling," "priest," "mystery" are all his experience, not assertions the reader must adopt.*

---

I have always experienced myself as a child of mystery.

As a boy, I imagined I might become a priest. I was drawn to prayer, silence, dreams, and the unseen dimensions of life long before I had words for them. As my life unfolded, I realized I wasn't being called into a single religious tradition. I was being called into a lifelong apprenticeship to the mystery that lives within them all.

For many years, I struggled to find where I belonged. I often felt I was walking between worlds—between psychology and spirituality, science and mysticism, reason and intuition, ancient wisdom and emerging futures. I discovered that my home was not on either side, but in the living space between them.

Only much later did Spiralogic give me a language for the life I had already been living. It wasn't an invention as much as a recognition. It revealed that what I had been seeking all along was not another system to believe in, but a way of participating in the relationships between ways of knowing.

Looking back, I no longer see my life as a search for the right tradition.

I see it as a vocation to tend the places where traditions meet, where worlds touch, and where something genuinely new can emerge.

I have come to believe that new worlds do not emerge from choosing one side over another.

They emerge in the living spaces between them.
