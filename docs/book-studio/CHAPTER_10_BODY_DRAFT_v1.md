## Notes on prose choices

- **"A vision arrives."** Opening line is three words to mark the threshold. The reader steps into the arc immediately. No preamble, no explanation of what Spiralogic *is* — the process begins as the sentence begins.
- **"Walks us into the work."** Fire 2 is *journey*, not *exertion*. "Walks" carries pilgrimage register; the work has its own gravity, we are accompanied.
- **"What returns to us through Water is not the vision. It is the essence."** Single negation kept — it earns its place because it corrects a real expectation (we expect to recover what we set out with). Removing it would lose the alchemical signal that *the descent transmutes*.
- **"The elixir asks to be planted."** Bridge sentence — Water 3 to Earth 1. The elixir has agency; we don't decide to plant it, we respond to its asking.
- **"The medicine is now real."** Closes Earth 3 with declarative weight. No metaphor, no qualification.
- ***With.*** Italicized one-word marker for Air, parallel to *If.* / *Why.* / *How.* Set on its own line. The single word performs the relational opening; no explanatory bridge needed. (Earlier draft used "Then we share" — replaced. The italicized *With.* enacts the move; "Then we share" declared it.)
- **"Air 1 — with one other / Air 2 — with many / Air 3 — with the field."** Triplet progression terminates in *the field*, not *the world.* Field sets up Aether without naming it. World would close the arc; field leaves it open.
- **"This is what the elements move through us to make."** Reverses the conventional reading. The elements are not raw material we shape; we are the medium *they* shape through. Spiralogic's deep claim, in one line.
- **"Not metaphor. Process."** Kelly's phrase. Held verbatim.
- **"This is elemental alchemy."** Title returns as the closing line of the passage. The chapter is named by what it just enacted.

## Closed decisions (do not relitigate)

- **Air's spine word: *With.*** Locked. *Then* / *With whom* / *So* — rejected. *What/With* layered slash — rejected. The cognitive function ChatGPT proposed elevating to "What" is already held in (a) the *Know / Intellectualize* faculty pair in the opener and (b) the phrase *"what was made"* in this passage. Recursion, not redundancy. **Do not reopen.**
- **Aether: not in this arc.** Chapter closes on *"This is elemental alchemy."* Aether is held field, not phase — revealed by the chapter that follows, not declared here.
- **Heuristic ≠ system.** *If → Why → How → With* is orientation language. The system is paired verbs + triplet phases + field. Distinct registers; do not collapse.
- **Spelling: *Invision*** (canonical, not Envision). The *In-* prefix carries the inward-turn signature across all four faculty pairs.
