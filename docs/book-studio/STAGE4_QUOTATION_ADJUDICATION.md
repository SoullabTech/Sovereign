## The governing question

> # A quotation must earn the interruption of another voice.

The book is now clear about its lineage. Stage 4 makes sure the remaining outside
voices are there **because the reader needs to hear them, not because the book
needs permission to believe itself.**

## Architecture — function first, not chapter

Chapter-by-chapter would drag the work back into local editing. The live question
is no longer *where is this quotation* but **what job is it doing.**

**Structural classification of all 128 (verified by script, not estimated):**

| Pass | Function | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **4A** | **Section-opening epigraph** — sits directly under a heading | **45** |
| **4B** | **Argument-carrying** — introduced by prose that leans on it | **2 by structure**, more by reading |
| **4C** | **Mid-prose / illustrative** | **81** |

*The structural test finds only 2 formally introduced quotations ("…:" then
quotation). **Argument-carrying is a semantic property, not a typographic one** —
4B's real membership is drawn out of the 81 by reading, not by the parser. Recorded
so the count is not mistaken for the population.*

## Decision-pressure order within each pass

Sorted by how constrained the disposition already is — **not by chapter**:

1. **Misattributed / composite / unsupported** — disposition strongly constrained.
2. **Unverified** — decide whether the thought earns further source work at all.
3. **Paraphrase / mediated / variant** — decide whether accurate restoration improves the book.
4. **Verified** — the hardest question: *does it deserve to remain even though it is true?*
5. **Rights-sensitive** — editorial ruling first, legal feasibility second.

> **Stage 4 must not become "verified = keep."** Provenance no longer decides for
> us. The verified quotations are now the most interesting ones.

## Ruling set

`KEEP` · `CORRECT` · `RESTORE` · `REPLACE` · `INTEGRATE INTO PROSE` · `REMOVE` · `RIGHTS HOLD`

**No new research unless a ruling genuinely depends on an unresolved source lead.**

## Protected — no symmetry repair

**Chapter 3 and Chapter 5 open without an epigraph.** This is settled and is not
a defect to be corrected in 4A. Openings are judged by whether they work.
