# Chapter 10 Opening Page — Pass v1

**Source:** Chapter 10, *Living the Spiralogic Process* — opening page (title + invocation + diagram + faculty stanza).

**Scope:** Opening page only. Body of chapter not in scope for this pass.

**Intent:** Replace the previous opener (which used negation as a rhetorical engine and showed a flat planar spiral) with positive-mode invocation and toroidal geometry. Bring the page into compliance with the Soullab Press editorial canon (text sovereign, geometry as punctuation, "if the reader notices the design, remove something").

---

## What was wrong

1. **Copy used negation as primary rhetorical move.** "Is not a path to follow / but a way of being…" / "You do not move through the elements / the elements move through you" / "Not as a system / but as a rhythm." Three negations in one short page. Each one forces the reader to hold the wrong thing in mind first, then disassemble it. Bollingen-register prose names what is.

2. **Element correspondences were generic.** "Awareness becomes Fire / Feeling becomes Water / Form becomes Earth / Thought becomes Air." These are observational, third-person, and don't carry the Spiralogic faculty system. The actual system speaks in first-person *acts* — what the practitioner **does** at each element.

3. **Diagram showed a flat spiral on a planar ring.** The torus is the structural form of Spiralogic — recursive flow up the central axis, out the top, around the surface, back through the bottom. A planar spiral is a 2D projection of that motion, not the motion itself. The geometry as drawn could not teach what the chapter teaches.

## Locked copy — opening page

> *The Spiralogic Process is a way of attending —*
> *a practice of meeting the elements of awareness*
> *as they move through you,*
> *to remember, alchemize, and create.*
>
> ⸺
>
> *Each moment, a point of entry.*
> *Each breath, a return to center.*
>
> *Fire — I imagine — I invision.*
> *Water — I feel — I internalize.*
> *Earth — I embody — I integrate.*
> *Air — I know — I intellectualize.*
>
> *And through them —*
> *the field remembers itself as you.*
>
> ⸺
>
> *Live it as a rhythm you inhabit.*

### Notes on the stanza

- **Outer faculty / inner faculty** — each element line carries two verbs separated by em-dashes. The first is the outer act (the "I"-act in the world); the second is the recursive turn inward (the "In-" prefix is the structural signature). Together the line *is* a small toroidal motion: outer reach folding into inner return.

- **"Invision"** is canonical, not a typo. *Vision held inwardly.* The four In-verbs (**In**vision / **In**ternalize / **In**tegrate / **In**tellectualize) form the inward-turn signature of the system. Spell-checkers will flag it; do not auto-correct.

- **"Moves as," not "becomes."** Earlier draft had "Awareness becomes Fire." *Becomes* implies transformation toward something other than the self. *Moves as* preserves the alchemical correspondence — the element is the mode of motion of the faculty in you, not its replacement.

## Diagram brief — toroidal cross-section

**Replace** the planar spiral-on-ring diagram. **Match** the line-only, ornamental-restraint register of the first four chapter openers (Journey Begins, Trinity/Flow, Torus of Change, Elements of Wholeness).

### Geometry

- **Cross-section view of a torus.** Side-on. The two opposing curves of the torus surface visible. Central vertical axis explicit (thin line). Reading order: up the axis → out the top → around → in through the bottom → return.
- **One streamline drawn** as a single recursive meridian along the toroidal surface — the path of the flow itself. Not multiple. One line teaches the motion; many lines decorate.
- **Central polyhedron** stays — but sits *on the axis of return*, where in-flow meets out-flow. Not floating on a flat plane.

### Labels

- **Outer ring (cardinal points of the cross-section's widest reach):** the four outer faculties — *I Imagine* (Fire), *I Feel* (Water), *I Embody* (Earth), *I Know* (Air). Element name in small caps; faculty in italic.
- **Inner return (along the axis-fold):** the four inner faculties — *I Invision*, *I Internalize*, *I Integrate*, *I Intellectualize*. Set smaller, italic, lower visual weight. They sit in the inward turn of the geometry.
- **Center:** the polyhedron. No label. The conjunction point speaks for itself.

### Render discipline

- Line-only. No fills, no gradients, no atmospheric infill.
- Ornament weight matches the first four chapter openers exactly.
- If a label can be removed without loss of meaning, remove it.
