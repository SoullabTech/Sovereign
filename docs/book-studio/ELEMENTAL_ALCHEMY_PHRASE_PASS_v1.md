## Canonical principle *(record for the working canon)*

> **Naming establishes the field. Repetition collapses it.**

Extension:
> *The more real the system becomes in the reader's experience, the
> less it needs to be named.*

**Working rule** *(Kelly, applied across the book going forward):*

| Mention | Action |
|---|---|
| First mention in a section | establishes field — keep |
| Second mention nearby | allowed if doing real work — pressure-test |
| Third mention | remove unless essential |

## Per-instance audit

### Instance 1 — Ch2 closer · L479

> *Elemental alchemy connects us with wisdom beyond the reactive,
> fixated mind.*

- **Test:** If removed → "[?] connects us with wisdom…" Sentence loses subject.
- **Function:** Names the practice as Ch2 closes. *(Ch2 is the first
  full chapter the reader has been moving through — first
  substantive body-text use.)*
- **Verdict:** **KEEP.** First substantive use, threshold/closer
  position, naming establishes the field.

---

### Instance 2 — Ch5 Fire · L835

> *…elemental alchemy isn't based on knowledge but on our direct
> experience of our interconnectedness with each other and nature.*

- **Test:** If removed → "isn't based on knowledge…" Sentence needs
  subject. *Possible replacement: "this work isn't based on
  knowledge…"* Possible — but loses specificity.
- **Function:** Distinguishes the system from knowledge-based /
  academic approaches. Definitional positioning at the start of the
  elemental chapters.
- **Verdict:** **KEEP.** Distinguishing function — this is precisely
  the place where naming the system carries weight.

---

### Instance 3 — Ch6 Water closer · L1318  *(distributed cluster member 1/3)*

> *Upon realizing that you are in the water phase in your journey of
> elemental alchemy, it may be useful to answer the direct
> question…*

- **Test:** If removed → *"Upon realizing that you are in the water
  phase in your journey, it may be useful to answer the direct
  question…"* Sentence holds. The reader is deep inside the system
  by Ch6 — *"your journey"* alone carries the meaning.
- **Function:** Pure name-tag. Reader is already inside the frame.
- **Verdict:** **CUT.** Delete *"of elemental alchemy"*. Sentence
  stands.

---

### Instance 4 — Ch7 Earth · L1514  *(distributed cluster member 2/3)*

> *When you find yourself in the earth phase of your journey of
> elemental alchemy, it may be useful to answer the direct
> question…*

- **Test:** Same as Instance 3. *"When you find yourself in the earth
  phase of your journey…"* — sentence holds.
- **Function:** Name-tag. Identical structural twin of L1318.
- **Verdict:** **CUT.** Delete *"of elemental alchemy"*.

---

### Instance 5 — Ch7 Earth · L1560  *(distributed cluster member 3/3)*

> *Of course, our goal in this elemental alchemy journey is to
> develop as much of the elements within our own nature.*

- **Test:** If removed → *"Of course, our goal in this journey is to
  develop as much of the elements within our own nature."* Sentence
  holds.
- **Function:** Name-tag. **Third recurrence** of "your/this
  journey of elemental alchemy" across Part Two — this is the one
  that triggers Kelly's "third mention almost always remove" rule.
- **Verdict:** **CUT.** Replace *"in this elemental alchemy
  journey"* with *"in this journey"*.

---

### Instance 6 — Ch8 Air · L1841

> *As an explorer and practitioner of metaphysical teachings, my
> fascination with elemental alchemy evolved into a devotion to its
> deeper mysteries.*

- **Test:** If removed → *"…my fascination evolved into a devotion to
  its deeper mysteries."* What fascination? Antecedent ambiguous.
- **Function:** Personal testimony — Kelly naming the work she became
  devoted to. Autobiographical anchor.
- **Verdict:** **KEEP.** Testimony naming.

---

### Instance 7 — Ch9 Aether · L1954

> *Aether, in the context of personal and elemental alchemy,
> represents the quintessence or the fifth element beyond the
> traditional four.*

- **Test:** If removed → *"Aether, in the context of personal
  alchemy, represents the quintessence…"* Slightly off — *"personal
  alchemy"* alone reads like Jungian / psychological alchemy rather
  than this specific framework. Better: *"Aether, in the context of
  this work, represents the quintessence…"* Or simpler: *"Aether
  represents the quintessence or the fifth element beyond the
  traditional four."*
- **Function:** Brand-tag in framing clause. Reader is at Ch9 — fully
  inside the system. Doesn't need re-naming.
- **Verdict:** **CUT.** Two replacement options:
  - *(a) plain delete:* "Aether represents the quintessence or the
    fifth element beyond the traditional four."
  - *(b) replace with "this work":* "Aether, in the context of this
    work, represents the quintessence…"
  - **Recommend (a)** — sentence breathes without the framing clause.
