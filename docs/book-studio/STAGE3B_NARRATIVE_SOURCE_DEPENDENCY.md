# Stage 3b — Narrative Source-Dependency Census

**Source of record:** `docs/book-studio/ELEMENTAL_ALCHEMY_FROM_ORIGINAL_FULL.md`
**Opened:** 2026-09-01, immediately after Stage 3 closed.
**Status: 🟡 OPEN — discovery pass.**

## Scope

**Not a paragraph audit.** This pass looks only for passages where the book
**materially depends on an identifiable external source while speaking in its own
words** — retellings, borrowed frameworks, historical accounts, named conceptual
models. Quotations are Stage 2's business and are not re-examined here.

**No developmental rewriting in this stage.** Findings only.

### Three categories that must not be flattened into one

Established by authorial ruling during this pass, and carried into the Author's
Studio requirements:

> **source dependency ≠ lived-experience synthesis ≠ fictional composite.**

- **Source dependency** — the book takes something identifiable from outside
  itself. *This is the only category Stage 3b audits.*
- **Lived-experience synthesis** — the author's own participation, memory,
  practice, or clinical observation. Has no external source to cite. A ceremony
  attended is not a text retold.
- **Fictional composite** — an overtly constructed character. Has no provenance
  question at all.

**A serious writing system needs to tell these apart rather than treating every
non-literal passage as borrowed material.** Most of what this pass examined
turned out to be the second and third categories.

## Record format

`SOURCE → WHAT WAS BORROWED → WHAT THE BOOK ADDS → FIDELITY → MEDIATION →
BIBLIOGRAPHY OBLIGATION → RIGHTS`

## Classifications

`HEALTHY / TRANSPARENT` · `SOURCE CREDIT NEEDED` · `SOURCE BOUNDARY NEEDS
CLARIFICATION` · `FIDELITY CHECK` · `MEDIATION NEEDS NAMING` · `NO MATERIAL
DEPENDENCY`

# FINDINGS

## 1 · The Story of the Four Yogis — L634–678

**`HEALTHY / TRANSPARENT`** — with one boundary line to sharpen.
**This is the canonical specimen. Every other finding is measured against it.**

- **SOURCE** — a teaching story Sadhguru tells.
- **BORROWED** — the story: four yogis of incompatible disciplines, the storm,
  the abandoned temple, the deity appearing, the punchline. Also the **four
  classical yoga paths** (Kriya, Bhakti, Karma, Jnana), which come *with* the
  story as Sadhguru tells it.
- **THE BOOK ADDS** — the retelling in the author's own prose and rhythm; the
  mapping of the four paths onto **Fire, Water, Earth, Air**; the reading of
  **Aether as what appears in the space between them**; the framing use ("I
  return to it whenever someone mistakes their path for *the* path").
- **FIDELITY** — sound. Retold, not reproduced.
- **MEDIATION** — **named in the text itself**: *"a story Sadhguru tells."* This
  is the move the whole census is looking for.
- **BIBLIOGRAPHY** — Sadhguru, *Inner Engineering* and Vasudev, *Mystic's
  Musings* both present. **Flag for Stage 7:** the same author appears under two
  different names — *Sadhguru* and *Jaggi Vasudev*. One heading, cross-referenced.
- **RIGHTS** — none. A retold traditional teaching story in original words.

**The one boundary to sharpen:** *"Each embodies one of the four classical paths
of yoga — and, just beneath that, one of the four elements of consciousness"*
places a **borrowed** mapping and an **authored** mapping inside one sentence.
The yoga paths arrive with Sadhguru's story; the elemental correspondence is the
book's. Minor, and repairable with a clause.

## 2 · The I Ching and the 64 Codons — L687–705

**`SOURCE BOUNDARY NEEDS CLARIFICATION` + `SOURCE CREDIT NEEDED`.**
**The most substantial finding of this pass.**

- **SOURCE** — Terence McKenna (quoted at L697, a Stage 2 census item), and
  behind McKenna, **Martin Schönberger, *The I Ching and the Genetic Code: The
  Hidden Key to Life* (1973)** — the inaugural work proposing the 64-hexagram /
  64-codon correspondence.
- **BORROWED** — **the entire correspondence is borrowed.** It is not an
  observation the book makes; it is a received thesis with a traceable modern
  genealogy.
- **THE BOOK ADDS** — the elemental and developmental reading; the extension into
  "cosmic mandala," dreamtime, and naturalist philosophy.
- **FIDELITY** — the factual claims check out: 64 hexagrams (2⁶) and 64 codons
  (4³) are both correct, and the codon description is accurate.
- **MEDIATION** — **partially named.** McKenna is credited in prose. **Schönberger
  is not named anywhere**, and McKenna is presenting his thesis.
- **THE ACTUAL PROBLEM** — not the McKenna quotation, which is properly framed.
  It is the **five paragraphs after it (L699–705)**, which develop the
  correspondence in the book's own voice, so the reader cannot tell where a
  borrowed thesis ends and the book's synthesis begins. **The source boundary
  closes at the quotation and never reopens.**
- **BIBLIOGRAPHY** — McKenna, *The Archaic Revival* present (**twice — a
  duplicate for Stage 7**). **Schönberger absent.**
- **RIGHTS** — the McKenna quotation is a Stage 2 item. No new exposure here.

**Also flagged:** L705 attaches capital-D **"Dreamtime"** — a specific Aboriginal
Australian cosmology — to a Chinese divination text and a genetics claim, used
generically. Same class as the Black Elk finding. **`UNSUPPORTED CULTURAL
ATTRIBUTION`, carried forward.**

## 3 · The Alchemical Operations — L2180–2184

**`HEALTHY / TRANSPARENT`** — with a small fidelity note.

- **SOURCE** — the ***Book of Lambspring*** and **Edward F. Edinger, *Anatomy of
  the Psyche: Alchemical Symbolism in Psychotherapy*** (1985).
- **BORROWED** — the alchemical operations as psychological process:
  *calcinatio, solutio, coagulatio, sublimatio, coniunctio*.
- **THE BOOK ADDS** — the mapping onto Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Aether.
- **FIDELITY** — sound, with one thing worth saying plainly: **Edinger treats
  seven operations; the book uses five.** *Mortificatio* and *separatio* are
  omitted. That is a legitimate authorial selection driven by the five-element
  structure — but it is a **selection**, and the text currently reads as though
  five is the set. One clause fixes it.
- **MEDIATION** — **named in the text**, including the Jung relationship
  ("one of the alchemical works drawn upon by Carl Jung" — accurate; Jung
  discusses Lambspring in *Psychology and Alchemy* and *Mysterium Coniunctionis*).
  Second-best specimen in the book after the Four Yogis.
- **BIBLIOGRAPHY** — both present. ***The Book of the Lambspring*** **has no
  edition, translator, or date** — Stage 7 repair.
- **RIGHTS** — none. Lambspring is public domain; Edinger is named, not quoted.

## 4 · The Three-Part Self — L1879–1881, L2170

**`MEDIATION NEEDS NAMING`** — the only survivor of the ceremonial sweep.

- **SOURCE** — the tripartite self (**basic / conscious / higher Self**) is
  presented as belonging to "Taoism from China and other shamanic systems" and to
  "shamanic awareness." As a *three-term model in exactly this vocabulary* it is
  far more specific than that: it is the **Huna** formulation transmitted through
  **Max Freedom Long** and carried into modern core-shamanic and energy-healing
  practice — a twentieth-century Western construction, not a Chinese or a
  pan-shamanic one.
- **BORROWED** — the three-term structure and its vocabulary.
- **THE BOOK ADDS** — the aether correspondence and the developmental reading.
- **FIDELITY** — the *model* is used coherently. The **attribution is the
  problem**: a recent Western synthesis is presented as ancient and cross-cultural.
- **MEDIATION** — **unnamed, and this is the finding.**
- **BIBLIOGRAPHY** — nothing covers it. **C. Michael Smith, *Jung and Shamanism in
  Dialogue*** is present (**duplicated — Stage 7**) and is the likeliest actual
  route of transmission; so is **Luata Bray**, named in the Acknowledgements as
  the author's first teacher in the shamanic arts, 33 years ago.
- **RIGHTS** — none.

**Repair direction (not executed):** either name the actual lineage, or narrow the
claim to what can be supported. The Acknowledgements already hold the answer the
body text is missing.

## 5 · Gunas ↔ Astrological Modalities ↔ Sulfur/Salt/Mercury — L541–570

**`SOURCE BOUNDARY NEEDS CLARIFICATION`** — mild.

- **SOURCE** — three separate traditions: the **gunas** (Vedic: sattva, rajas,
  tamas), the **astrological modalities** (cardinal, fixed, mutable), and the
  **tria prima** (Paracelsian: sulfur, salt, mercury).
- **BORROWED** — each of the three systems and its internal definitions.
- **THE BOOK ADDS** — **the three-way correspondence between them, which is the
  book's own construction, not a traditional equivalence.**
- **FIDELITY** — **verified accurate.** All twelve signs are correctly assigned to
  their modalities. The guna characterisations are standard. The
  sattva↔mutable equation is the loosest link, and the text hedges it correctly
  with *"analogous to."*
- **MEDIATION** — traditions named generically; no single source is being followed.
- **BIBLIOGRAPHY** — no obligation for tradition-general material. **Rudhyar,
  *Astrological Timing*** is present but never cited in text — **orphan candidate,
  Stage 7.**
- **RIGHTS** — none.

**The boundary:** the passage reads as though the correspondence is inherited.
It is authored. That is a strength worth claiming, not a debt to disclose.

## 6 · The Trinity: Agent, Agency, Manifestation — L499–511

**`NO MATERIAL DEPENDENCY` — candidate dissolves.**

The terminology is not locatable in any external model. The underlying triadic
shape (a source, a medium, an expression) is a widespread metaphysical
commonplace — the text already attributes it generically ("found in many
spiritual, philosophical, and shamanic traditions"), which is the correct
handling for a commonplace. The one specific claim it makes — *"From the Father
and the Son comes the Holy Spirit"* as Christian theology — **is accurate**
(the filioque). Nothing owed.

## 7 · Maya — L2074–2264

**`NO MATERIAL DEPENDENCY` — candidate dissolves by authorial ruling.**

> **FICTIONAL COMPOSITE / AUTHORIAL CONSTRUCTION** — an overtly fictional
> character created as an amalgamation of multiple client experiences, not a
> representation of any one person and not an external-source dependency.

**Stage 3b does not ask what source Maya comes from.** There is none in the
provenance sense. The book already declares the construction at L2076.

**The narrower check that Stage 3b *may* run — and did run:** do the
**frameworks, teachings, or concepts used to interpret her journey** depend on
outside sources? Two vocabulary items ride along, and neither is a narrative
dependency:

- **"dark night of the soul"** (L2078, L2264) — **John of the Cross**, unnamed.
  Now idiomatic English; naming him is optional, not owed.
- **"inner gold"** (L1620, L1692, L2112, L2122, L2515) — used five times as a
  load-bearing term. **Robert A. Johnson, *Inner Gold*, is in the bibliography
  but never named in the text.** This is a **`SOURCE CREDIT NEEDED`** item in its
  own right, and it **explains a bibliography orphan** — carry both facts to
  Stage 7 together.

## 8 · The ceremonial thread — L389, L457, L920, L1147, L1822

**`NO MATERIAL DEPENDENCY` — dissolves under the lived-experience distinction.**

The teepee circle, the medicine-wheel dream, the peyote prayer meeting, the sweat
lodge, and Maestro Benito are **first-person accounts of the author's own
participation**. They are not retellings of texts and have no external source to
cite. **A ceremony attended is not a text retold.**

Recorded because it is a real result, not an absence of looking: this thread was
the largest candidate set entering the pass and it dissolves almost entirely. The
one framework extracted *from* that practice — the three-part self — is Finding 4.

**One thing carried forward, not as provenance but as apparatus:** the book's
Indigenous and shamanic lineage is named **only in the Acknowledgements** (Luata
Bray; C. Michael Smith), while the ceremonial material sits hundreds of pages
earlier. **Bear Heart, *The Wind Is My Mother*** is in the bibliography and is
quoted at L918 but is never introduced as a teacher-source in the body. Whether
to surface the lineage earlier is an **editorial and ethical question, not a
citation defect** — but it is the same question the Black Elk repair answered
this morning, and it should be decided once rather than site by site.

## Governing principle in force from this point

> **Make the intellectual lineage more truthful so the original thesis can stand
> more fully in its own authority.** *Let the sources be sources. Let the
> synthesis be the author's.*

Ratified 2026-09-01 and recorded in full at the head of
`PUBLICATION_ROADMAP.md`. It governs Stage 4's adjudication of the remaining 128
quotations, and every stage after.

**Read against this pass:** none of the eight findings asks the book to borrow
*more*. Two ask a borrowed thesis to stop being developed as though it were the
book's (Finding 2) and a modern synthesis to stop being presented as ancient
(Finding 4). Both repairs make the lineage **more** truthful and the surrounding
synthesis **more** clearly the author's. That is the shape every remaining repair
should have.
