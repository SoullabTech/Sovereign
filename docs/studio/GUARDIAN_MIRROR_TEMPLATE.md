## The Principle

> **Your parents see your weather, not your words.**

This is the core promise. Parents don't need content. They need signals of safety and stability.

**What you receive:**
- Emotional trend (more settled, same, or more activated)
- Engagement level (checking in regularly or not)
- General themes of growth (confidence, stress regulation, decision independence)
- Any safety concerns, if they arise

**What you don't receive:**
- Transcripts
- Journal entries
- Conversation content
- Private thoughts or disclosures

**Why this works:**
Privacy is what makes the program effective. When teens feel watched, they disengage. When parents feel shut out, they panic. The Guardian Mirror gives you patterns, not content — so your teen has a space that's truly theirs, while you still have visibility into their wellbeing.

**The safety override:**
Privacy holds unless one of three signals appears:
1. Self-harm risk
2. Harm to others
3. Severe functional deterioration

If any of these arise, you will be contacted directly.
