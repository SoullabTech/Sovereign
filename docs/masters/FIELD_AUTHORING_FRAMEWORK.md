# Master Field Authoring Framework

**Version 1.0 — March 2026**
**Status: Design Artifact — Pre-Implementation**

---

## The Shift

A Master's Field is not a profile. It is not a portfolio. It is not a product page.

It is a digital habitat — a place that holds someone accurately enough that the people who need them recognize it as theirs before a single offer is made.

The process of building it is not "setup." It is **field authorship**.

The question is not: *What features do you want?*
The question is: *What must be unmistakably yours?*

---

## What Full Representation Requires

Seven dimensions. All of them are needed. None of them is a form.

### 1. Presence
The felt sense of a master. What people encounter before they understand anything.
- What happens when someone enters their space?
- What quality of attention do they bring?
- Are they warm, fierce, quiet, precise, devotional, playful, scholarly?
- What pace do they keep? What do they slow down for?
- What do people feel in the first 30 seconds?

### 2. Lineage and Authority
Not résumé. Genealogy.
- Who taught them — formally and informally
- What traditions they were initiated into
- What frameworks they've made their own
- What they've had to unlearn
- Who they carry forward
- The lived history that makes their work earned, not learned

### 3. Aesthetic World
This is not decoration. Aesthetic is orientation — how they see, what they value, what they protect.
- Colors and their feel (not just hex codes — what the colors mean)
- Textures, light quality, spatial density
- Photography style: candid or composed, natural or styled, close or wide
- Typography feel: classical, precise, handwritten, modern
- Symbols they love and return to
- Symbols they never want used
- Image references that feel like home
- Sonic atmosphere: silence, birdsong, resonant tone, music
- Natural elements: water, forest, stone, sky, fire

### 4. Relational Style
How they hold people. This shapes MAIA's posture directly.
- Direct or spacious (or both, and when)
- Interpretive or reflective
- Structured or emergent
- Ritualized or practical
- What comes first: body, psyche, spirit, or systems
- How they warm to someone (or don't)
- What they protect in relationship

### 5. Boundaries
What must not be mistaken about them.
- What they do not do
- What they do not want MAIA to say, imply, or approach
- What should never be inferred from their work
- Who this work is for, precisely
- Who this work is not for, honestly
- What tones feel false to them (urgent? vague? cheerful? clinical?)
- What would make them feel misrepresented

### 6. Their World
The ecosystem, not just the individual.
- Events they run or gather at
- Students, collaborators, guest teachers
- Recurring gatherings and their character
- Favorite resources and why they matter
- Signature practices — things only they do
- Community norms: how people are held in their field
- Seasonal rhythms: when their work is most alive, when they retreat

### 7. Offer Architecture
Not products. Pathways.
- How someone enters their world (beginner pathway)
- How a practitioner deepens (intermediate pathway)
- What advanced looks like in their lineage
- Private work vs. group work vs. training vs. community
- What transformation each path is actually for
- What they charge, what they gift, what they won't sell

## The Paradox Layer

Every real master holds something in tension.

Jondi holds precision *and* spaciousness.
A Jungian holds interpretation *and* not-knowing.
A somatic practitioner holds presence *and* non-interference.

The paradox is often the most distinctive thing about them — and the easiest to flatten in digital presentation. The field authoring process should surface what the master is working out, not just what they've resolved. A field that only presents one side of a person feels like a position statement, not a presence.

**Intake question:** *What do you hold together that most people think are opposites?*

## The Five-Phase Field Creation Process

### Phase 1 — Essence Interview
*Conversational, MAIA-led. Not a form.*

MAIA conducts a guided intake conversation across 2–3 sessions. Questions are open, not categorical. Answers are processed into raw material for every subsequent phase.

**Core questions:**
- What happens when someone enters your field?
- What is most often misunderstood about your work?
- What must remain true in any digital expression of you?
- What should people feel in the first 30 seconds?
- What kind of beauty feels like home to you?
- What do you hold together that most people think are opposites?
- What would make you feel misrepresented?
- Who are you not for?
- What have you had to unlearn?
- Who taught you — and what did they actually teach you?
- What is the thing you say that no one else says quite that way?

**Output:** Raw transcript → Essence synthesis → Draft presence copy + MAIA posture seeds

---

### Phase 2 — World Gathering
*Artifact collection. Messy and rich.*

The master contributes raw material — not in any particular order or format.

**What to collect:**
- Portrait photos (3–5, varying context)
- Environmental/field photos (where their work happens)
- Reference images that feel like their aesthetic
- Books, pages, designs they love
- Their own writing samples (emails, session notes, blog posts, anything)
- Phrases they use naturally, phrases they avoid
- Events they've run (brands, imagery, descriptions)
- Testimonials (raw, unedited)
- Teaching materials (syllabi, handouts, frameworks)
- Voice recordings if available
- Symbols, objects, places that matter to them
- Music or sonic references

**Output:** Media library + tagged artifact bank

---

### Phase 3 — Field Profile Synthesis
*MAIA processes all of Phase 1 + Phase 2 into structured form.*

The master doesn't write this. MAIA drafts it. The master reviews and corrects.

**What gets synthesized:**

| Section | What it contains |
|---------|-----------------|
| Essence Profile | Felt sense, pace, presence qualities, the paradox |
| Voice Profile | Natural phrases, avoided language, tonal descriptors, silence patterns |
| Aesthetic Profile | Palette (confirmed), typography feel, imagery style, symbols in/out |
| Relational Profile | How they hold people, what comes first, warmth style |
| Boundary Profile | What they don't do, what MAIA can't say, who it's not for |
| Lineage Profile | Teachers, traditions, initiations, who they carry forward |
| World Profile | Events, community, collaborators, seasonal rhythm |
| Offer Map | Pathways, formats, transformations, what each is for |
| MAIA Posture | Synthesized system prompt block, voice training data |

---

### Phase 4 — Prototype and Reflection
*Show them their field. Ask what's true.*

The master sees:
- Their threshold page (opening line, atmosphere, portals)
- Their presence page (story, lineage, offers)
- A MAIA sample conversation (3–5 exchanges in their field's voice)
- Their palette and typography live on screen
- A short image direction doc (what their visual world should look like)

**Reflection questions:**
- What feels true?
- What feels off?
- What feels too much?
- What is missing?
- What would make you wince if Jondi saw it?
- What would make you want to share it immediately?

**The Correction Protocol:**
Any correction the master makes is logged against the synthesized section that generated it. A correction is not just a fix — it is training data. MAIA learns what was false and adjusts the underlying posture, not just the visible copy.

---

### Phase 5 — Living Refinement
*The field is never finished. It deepens.*

The field evolves as the master's work evolves. The authoring system supports:
- Updating visuals as their aesthetic matures
- Adding voice samples as they produce more writing
- Training MAIA on new session transcripts
- Deepening lineage as new teachers emerge
- Revising boundaries as their scope clarifies
- Expanding the world as new collaborators and events appear

**The Evolution Prompt** (quarterly):
*"Your field said [X]. Is that still true?"*

This is surfaced to the master periodically — not intrusively, but as an invitation. Fields should not go stale. A field frozen in 2026 should not represent a master in 2028.

## The Field Authoring Studio

A dedicated practitioner-facing interface with ten sections. This is not the visitor-facing field — it is the master's working space.

| Section | What they do here |
|---------|------------------|
| **Essence** | Core feeling, pace, presence qualities, paradox statement |
| **Story** | Headline, lead, body paragraphs — editable, with MAIA draft support |
| **Lineage** | Teachers, traditions, initiations, who they carry forward |
| **Visual World** | Palette confirmation, typography feel, symbols in/out, image references |
| **Voice & Language** | Writing samples, natural phrases, avoided phrases, tone descriptors |
| **Boundaries** | What they won't do, MAIA guardrails, who it's not for, false tones |
| **Community** | Events, collaborators, gatherings, seasonal rhythm, community norms |
| **Offers** | Pathways, formats, transformations, pricing philosophy |
| **MAIA Training** | Voice training, framework training, boundary training, correction log |
| **Media Library** | All uploaded images, with tagging and field assignment |

## The Full Schema

```typescript
/**
 * MasterFieldProfile
 *
 * Everything about a master that is not derivable from their offers alone.
 * Lives alongside MasterField — which handles routing, theming, and portal config.
 * This handles identity, atmosphere, values, and the full fabric of who they are.
 */
export type MasterFieldProfile = {
  masterSlug: string; // links to MasterField

  // ── ESSENCE ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  essence: {
    openingFeeling: string;       // "what people feel in the first 30 seconds"
    paceDescription: string;      // "slow and spacious" | "direct and precise"
    presenceQualities: string[];  // ["quiet", "precise", "devoted"]
    paradox: string;              // "I hold precision and spaciousness together"
    whatIsMisunderstood: string;  // the correction they most need made
    whatMustRemainTrue: string;   // the invariant — what cannot be flattened
  };

  // ── LINEAGE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  lineage: {
    teachers: Array<{
      name: string;
      tradition?: string;
      note?: string; // what they actually taught
    }>;
    traditions: string[];
    initiations: string[];        // what formed them, not just what they completed
    frameworks: string[];         // methodologies they've made their own
    livedHistory: string;         // narrative, not résumé
    whoTheyCarryForward: string;  // lineage forward
    whatTheyUnlearned: string;    // what had to be released
  };

  // ── AESTHETIC WORLD ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  aestheticWorld: {
    lightQuality: 'dark' | 'low' | 'dappled' | 'neutral' | 'bright' | 'full';
    textureDescriptions: string[]; // "rough linen", "smooth stone", "dry earth"
    photographyStyle: string;      // "natural light, honest, unposed"
    typographyFeel: string;        // "classical precision" | "quiet modernity"
    symbolsLoved: string[];
    symbolsNeverUse: string[];
    imageReferences: string[];     // URLs, descriptions, or both
    sonicAtmosphere?: string;      // "birdsong", "silence", "resonant bell"
    naturalElements: string[];     // "water", "stone", "forest", "sky"
    spaceDescription: string;      // "I work in..." — the physical/metaphorical
  };

  // ── RELATIONAL STYLE ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  relationalStyle: {
    directness: 'direct' | 'spacious' | 'adaptive';
    approach: 'interpretive' | 'reflective' | 'collaborative';
    structure: 'structured' | 'emergent' | 'responsive';
    ritual: 'ritualized' | 'practical' | 'both';
    primaryEntry: 'body-first' | 'psyche-first' | 'spirit-first' | 'systems-first';
    warmthStyle: string;           // "precise warmth" | "expansive, unhurried"
    whatTheyProtectInRelationship: string;
  };

  // ── BOUNDARIES ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  boundaries: {
    whatTheyDontDo: string[];
    maiaCannotSay: string[];       // exact phrases or approaches
    maiaCannotImply: string[];     // inferences to prevent
    whatShouldNeverBeInferred: string[];
    clientFit: string;             // who this is for, honestly
    clientNotFit: string;          // who this isn't for, honestly
    falseTones: string[];          // "never sound urgent" | "never be vague"
    whatWouldMisrepresentThem: string; // the wince test
  };

  // ── THEIR WORLD ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  world: {
    location: string;              // where they're based, or "works globally"
    languages: string[];
    seasonalRhythm?: string;       // when most alive, when they retreat
    events: Array<{
      name: string;
      description: string;
      rhythm: string;              // "annual", "quarterly", "as-needed"
      role: 'founder' | 'co-founder' | 'contributor' | 'participant';
    }>;
    collaborators: Array<{
      name: string;
      relationship: string;
      fieldSlug?: string;          // if they're also a Master on Soullab
    }>;
    guestTeachers: string[];
    recurringGatherings: string[];
    favoriteResources: Array<{
      title: string;
      author?: string;
      why: string;                 // why it matters to their work
    }>;
    signaturePractices: string[];
    communityNorms: string[];
  };

  // ── OFFER ARCHITECTURE ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  offerArchitecture: {
    pathways: Array<{
      name: string;
      portalSlug: string;          // links to FieldPortal
      forWhom: string;             // "beginners", "practitioners", "those in crisis"
      transformation: string;      // what shift this path is for
      formats: string[];           // "1:1", "group", "self-study", "intensive"
      entryPoint: string;          // how someone finds this pathway
      pricingPhilosophy?: string;  // "sliding scale", "gift economy", "investment"
    }>;
  };

  // ── VOICE PROFILE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  voiceProfile: {
    naturalPhrases: string[];      // phrases they return to
    avoidPhrases: string[];        // language that feels false
    writingSamples: string[];      // raw text (emails, posts, notes)
    toneDescriptors: string[];     // ["precise", "unhurried", "grounded"]
    silencePattern: string;        // when they go quiet, and why
    whatTheyNeverHurry: string;    // what they protect by slowing down
  };

  // ── MAIA POSTURE ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  // Generated from all above sections. Not manually authored.
  maiaPosture: {
    systemPromptBlock: string;     // synthesized, reviewed, corrected
    correctionLog: Array<{
      timestamp: string;
      section: string;             // which section generated the false output
      whatWasFalse: string;
      correction: string;
    }>;
    trainingVersion: number;       // increments with each correction cycle
    lastReviewedAt: string;        // when the master last said "yes, this is me"
  };

  // ── MEDIA LIBRARY ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  media: {
    portraits: Array<{
      url: string;
      context: string;             // "formal", "in session", "outdoors"
      isPrimary: boolean;
    }>;
    fieldImages: Array<{
      url: string;
      use: 'threshold' | 'presence' | 'background' | 'general';
      description?: string;
    }>;
    aestheticReferences: Array<{
      url?: string;
      description: string;
      why: string;
    }>;
  };

  // ── META ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  createdAt: string;
  lastUpdatedAt: string;
  currentPhase: 'essence' | 'gathering' | 'synthesis' | 'prototype' | 'live';
  evolutionPromptDue?: string;     // when to ask "is this still true?"
};
```

## Key Principles

**1. MAIA conducts the intake, not a form.**
Every question that can be asked by conversation should be. The master should feel met, not surveyed.

**2. Correction is training.**
When a master says "that's not me," the correction is logged and feeds back into every layer the false output came from. The field learns from what felt false.

**3. The field must be correctable without starting over.**
Each section is independent. Correcting voice doesn't reset lineage. Updating aesthetics doesn't touch boundaries.

**4. What's generated is a draft. What's approved is the field.**
MAIA synthesizes. The master approves. Nothing goes live in their field without their explicit consent.

**5. Evolution is built in from the start.**
Quarterly evolution prompts. The field is always in process. A master who became something new in year two should not be represented by what they said in year one.

**6. The paradox is the signature.**
The thing a master holds in tension is often more distinctive than everything they've resolved. Surface it. Protect it. Never flatten it.

## Implementation Sequence

What gets built, and in what order:

| Phase | Build | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Now | `MasterFieldProfile` schema | The type system that holds everything |
| Now | Essence Interview questions | The intake, run by MAIA in /fields/[field]/train |
| Next | Media Library (upload, tag, assign) | Photos and references |
| Next | Field Authoring Studio (sections 1–4) | Essence, Story, Lineage, Visual World |
| After | Field Authoring Studio (sections 5–10) | Voice, Boundaries, Community, Offers, MAIA, Media |
| After | Synthesis pipeline (MAIA → profile → posture) | Auto-draft from intake |
| After | Prototype preview (live field from profile) | See it before approving it |
| After | Evolution prompts + correction protocol | Living field |

## The Standard

A field is ready when a person who knows the master reads it and thinks:

*"Yes. That's exactly them."*

Not: *"That's a good representation."*
Not: *"That captures what they do."*

*That's them.*

That is the standard. Everything else is in service of it.
