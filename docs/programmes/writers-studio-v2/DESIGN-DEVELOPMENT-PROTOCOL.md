# WRITER'S STUDIO — DESIGN DEVELOPMENT PROTOCOL

**Status**: authored 2026-08-28, founder-directed. **Controlling before WS2-02 begins.**
**Operates with**: `DESIGN-CONTRACT.md` (FROZEN) and `CAPABILITY-COVENANT.md`.

> `DESIGN-CONTRACT.md` → what it should **look and feel** like.
> `CAPABILITY-COVENANT.md` → what it must **continue to be able to do**.
> **This protocol** → **how we are permitted to get from here to there.**

## GOVERNING RULE

> ## PRESERVE BEHAVIOR. RECOMPOSE PRESENTATION.

Writer's Studio is **not** being rebuilt by discarding the existing organism and recreating the
reference screens. The existing Studio contains functioning behavior, data relationships, editor
capabilities, autosave, materials, versions, MAIA relationships, structural intelligence,
authentication, and other accumulated functionality. Design development preserves those
capabilities while recomposing how they are presented.

---

## 1 · The frozen references govern composition

`DESIGN-CONTRACT.md` is frozen. The canonical writing reference is **`04-writing-field-wide.png`**,
governing the primary **WS-WRITE** composition. **`08`** is secondary — MAIA disposition, material
relationships, supporting-field behavior. **`03 / 06 / 07` are one duplicated reference state, not
three independent directions.**

The references govern composition · hierarchy · spatial relationships · visual rhythm · density ·
emphasis · interaction disposition · overall feel.

**They do not authorize removing existing functionality merely because it is not visible in a
reference image.**

## 2 · Design from the real system

No screenshot replicas containing invented or decorative product behavior. Every implemented
screen arises from real Works, manuscripts, member identity, sections, materials, persistence,
MAIA relationships and state. **No fake screenshot data may become the foundation of production
architecture.**

- Reference screens answer: *how should this experience be composed?*
- The existing system answers: *what must continue to function?*

**Both must survive.**

## 3 · Classify before changing

| | |
|---|---|
| **KEEP** | already appropriate to the new Studio |
| **EXTRACT** | valuable behavior entangled with an old component — separate it so it survives independently |
| **RECOMPOSE** | behavior correct; presentation, hierarchy or placement changes |
| **RETIRE PRESENTATION** | an obsolete visual or interaction treatment may disappear |

> **RETIRE PRESENTATION ≠ RETIRE CAPABILITY**

## 4 · Decompose, do not replace the organism

`Worktable.tsx` decomposes into a shared Studio shell, the WS-WRITE environment, and extracted
capabilities that can appear where appropriate. **Do not build a beautiful replacement component
and abandon the behavioral depth accumulated there.** Preserve or deliberately migrate — with
evidence — its data relationships, editor behavior, autosave, materials access, version/revision
behavior, MAIA integration, structural functionality, authentication and identity custody, and
persistence.

> **The burden of proof is on removal, not preservation.**

## 5 · Additive preservation

Prefer additive integration over destructive simplification. Do not remove functioning
intelligence because the new composition has no obvious place to display it yet.

> **If the capability is valid but its final presentation is unresolved, preserve the capability
> underneath and postpone the presentation decision.**

## 6 · Work remains central

Every review asks first: **is the Work obviously the center of this room?** The Studio must not
center on navigation, dashboards, AI prompts, tools, files, workflow management or configuration.
Those may support the Work; they may not displace it. A returning writer understands immediately:
*This is my Work. This is where I was. This is how I continue.*

## 7 · Preserve creative distances without making a pipeline

The architecture accommodates movement among **Work · Writing · Materials · Shape/Structure ·
MAIA · Expression** — apertures, not compulsory stages. **Do not impose
`IMPORT → OUTLINE → WRITE → EDIT → PUBLISH`** as the governing model. A writer may move from a
sentence to a transcript, into conversation with MAIA, out to chapter structure, and straight back
to the sentence, without fragmentation.

## 8 · MAIA has a distinct posture

> **SYSTEM carries. MAIA proposes. WRITER authorizes. THE RECORD states what, if anything, became
> authoritative.**

MAIA may notice, question, connect, remember, hypothesize, propose. The architecture preserves the
distinctions among **observation · hypothesis · suggestion · recognition · decision · adoption**.
**Repeated retrieval or repeated suggestion must never turn an unresolved hypothesis into
writer-authorized truth.**

## 9 · Provenance persists; salience does not

A remembered item may retain source, authorship, authority status, relationship to the Work, and
whether it was suggested, recognized, adopted or rejected. Its **current relevance is recomputed**.

> **Provenance persists. Authority status persists. Salience does not. Posture is recomputed.**

Important yesterday does not mean important in the writer's present moment.

## 10 · Consent to inspect is not consent to believe

Letting MAIA inspect a transcript, note, manuscript, memory or pattern does not authorize MAIA to
adopt an interpretation on the writer's behalf. Likewise: retrieval is not recognition ·
recognition is not adoption · discussion is not decision · suggestion is not meaning · material is
not manuscript · generated text is not authorship · apparent completion is not publication
authority. **These distinctions belong in architecture, not merely interface copy.**

## 11 · Design reviews use the real experience

**Render evidence** establishes hierarchy, spacing, composition, visual relationships, responsive
behavior, obvious affordances. **Member witness evidence** is required for orientation, continuity,
whether the correct Work appears, whether continuing is natural, whether terminology makes sense,
whether MAIA's place is understandable, whether materials stay distinct, whether creative focus
survives use.

> **Do not treat screenshot approval as experiential acceptance.**

## 12 · First-person witness must remain uncontaminated

During a founder/member walk, do not coach unless help is explicitly requested. Do not silently
repair orientation, explain what a control meant, add route hints, or turn confusion into a design
success by instructing the tester. If external help is required, record **`resolved by external
help`** rather than pretending the interface succeeded. *First-person observation is evidence;
design explanation is different evidence.*

## 13 · Current referent, not historical success

Acceptance is measured against the **actual current implementation**. A prior successful screen,
deployment, branch or walk cannot establish that the present candidate still works. **Always
re-measure at the current referent.** Separate *historical evidence* from *acceptance evidence*.

## 14 · Seven questions for every major design review

1. Is the Work obviously central?
2. Is continuing the writing clear and natural?
3. Can the writer understand where they are and move without losing orientation?
4. Are secondary actions subordinate to the Work?
5. Does the terminology describe lived creative activity rather than software abstractions?
6. Does it remain coherent on mobile and smaller surfaces?
7. Does it preserve writing focus rather than continually pulling attention outward?

**Passing visual comparison alone is insufficient.**

## 15 · The Capability Covenant is a non-regression boundary

Every WS2 unit preserves the architecture's ability to support all twelve covenant capabilities.
A capability does not need its final interface in every unit — **but no unit may architect it
away.**

## 16 · D-012 — new work lands inside the new architecture

Once WS2-03 lands, subsequent development belongs **inside** the new architecture. Do not build
parallel legacy surfaces and plan to reconcile later. **WS2-02 (design system) + WS2-03 (shell)
establish the architectural home; subsequent rooms develop within it.**

## 17 · The standard is not "looks like the screenshot"

> **The frozen reference composition, inhabited by the full living capabilities of Writer's Studio.**

A result fails in **either** direction:

- **Visually faithful but behaviorally diminished → FAIL**
- **Functionally complete but compositionally unlike the frozen contract → FAIL**

Neither preservation of the legacy interface nor reproduction of a concept image. **Convergence.**

## 18 · No silent architectural tradeoffs

If satisfying the visual contract appears to require removing, flattening or materially changing an
existing capability — **stop and surface the conflict.** Do not resolve architectural tradeoffs
through incidental implementation. In particular, never silently collapse:

Work → document · Material → manuscript · MAIA dialogue → manuscript · suggestion → decision ·
memory → generic context · versions → autosave history · structure → outline · expression →
publishing · writer recognition → AI inference.

**Those distinctions are product architecture.**

---

## DESIGN DEVELOPMENT TEST

> **Did we make the intended experience more coherent while preserving the living intelligence,
> behavior, distinctions, and future possibilities already present in Writer's Studio?**

If yes, continue. **If visual refinement required behavioral impoverishment, the design is not
complete.**

## COMBINED GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

The frozen design contract establishes **form**. The Capability Covenant establishes
**possibility**. This protocol establishes **method**.

> Preserve the organism. Recompose the experience. Keep the Work central. Let MAIA participate
> without acquiring authorship. Preserve provenance and meaningful distinctions. Test the real
> experience, not merely the render.
