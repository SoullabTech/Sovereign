# A Few Questions About What's On Your Mind

Thank you for helping with this. There are no right answers, nothing to prepare, and nothing technical here — I'm just trying to understand how people relate to the things currently on their mind.

A few things that help:

- **Be concrete.** A specific thing beats a general category (e.g. "the email to my sister I keep not sending," not "communication").
- A sentence or two per question is plenty; more is welcome.
- Take them one at a time, in order.
- Share only what you're comfortable sharing — skip anything that feels too private.

---

**1.** What did you reopen three times in a week — not urgent, but it wouldn't leave you alone?

**2.** What is alive in your life right now that asks nothing of you today?

**3.** What do you keep mentally carrying because you don't quite trust that it's written down or placed somewhere reliable?

**4.** When did you last feel lost or scattered in your own life — what were you trying to get your bearings on?

**5.** What feels unfinished, but not incomplete?

---

That's all five. Thank you — this is genuinely helpful.
