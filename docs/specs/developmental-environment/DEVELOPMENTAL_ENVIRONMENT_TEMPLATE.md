## The template question

Instead of asking practitioners *"What features do you want?"*, the Studio asks:

> **"What rooms exist in your developmental world?"**

The template is remarkably neutral. It holds many modalities without imposing Spiralogic
or elemental language — the elements remain grammar (per the surfaces spec's Translation
Architecture), and every practitioner names their own world.

## The seven-room grammar (each room = a question the member asks)

| Room | The member's question | Constitutional bound |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Where am I in this journey? | Facts only; self-placed; never inferred (Ruling 1) |
| Living Questions | What am I wrestling with right now? | Member-authored; private by default |
| Insights & Keeps | What do I want to remember? | Member-gesture persistence only |
| Practices | What am I trying to embody? | Member-declared; no compliance tracking |
| Patterns | What themes do I notice over time? | Only when earned substrate exists (Ruling 2); member-pulled |
| Reflections | What would I like MAIA to help me think about? | Enters → asks → reflects (Ruling 3); never ambient |
| Community Field | Who am I journeying with? | ⚠️ inseparable from the unspecced group-shared-material gap; room + material model specified together or not at all |

## The five-stage practitioner sequence (walk-derived, CANDIDATE)

The round-1 persona walks (2026-07-12, `LARRY_WALK_FINDINGS_2026-07-12.md`) surfaced what
may be a universal onboarding sequence for practitioner environments — each stage gated by
the one before it, mirroring the earned-substrate ruling:

```text
Stage 1 — Safe Room       Can the participant trust the room?
                          (Is this private? Who sees this? Is this replacing my coach?)
Stage 2 — Known Holding   Can something meaningful remain — and does everyone in the
                          room KNOW it can? (Can I save this? Will this matter later?)
Stage 3 — Return          Can I continue rather than start over?
Stage 4 — Orientation     Where am I in this journey?   — only after known holding exists
Stage 5 — Ecology         How do these experiences relate over time?
                          — only after sufficient substrate exists (Ruling 2)
```

**Why "Known Holding," not "Holding" (corrected finding, 07-12)**: the round-1 walks showed
the holding substrate already exists in production — the gap is that neither participants
nor MAIA's conversational layer can speak truthfully about it. The stage is passed not when
holding *works* but when it is *legible*: field knowledge, boundary copy, affordance
language, store-boundary governance — not major infrastructure. The room exists, the hands
exist, the shelves exist; the guide must know how to point to them.

Walk evidence so far: Stage 1 already passes in production (P2's privacy answer, unprompted
and exact). Stage 2 is the live gap — the room's holding mechanics exist but the
conversational layer cannot point to them. Stages 4–5 are the held position spec and the
Vision-gated surfaces, in exactly this order. **Status: candidate grammar — round-1 walks
were continuity-prompted, so the stage ORDER is hypothesis until round 2 (continuity-blind
briefs, unprompted-gesture sequence data) confirms it. The architecture beneath it:
Conversation → Retention → Return → Continuity → Developmental Environment — the keep
gesture as foundational infrastructure, pending that confirmation.**

## Two fences the template library must carry from day one

1. **Modality jurisdiction.** Recovery, grief, couples, and depth-psychological templates
   sit closer to clinical terrain than coaching does. The template never changes what MAIA
   is: no diagnosis, no treatment, no therapy claims, no crisis containment beyond the
   standing safety posture — and the practitioner's own scope-of-work governs (work-scope
   invariant). Some templates may require explicit jurisdiction disclosure surfaces
   before they exist at all.
2. **The constitution is not configurable.** A template configures rooms, language,
   rituals — never Rulings 1–3, never the Translation Principle, never memory consent,
   never the off-ramp telos. The Studio's authoring surface must make violating the
   constitution *structurally impossible*, not discouraged (structural refusal of skipped
   judgment).
