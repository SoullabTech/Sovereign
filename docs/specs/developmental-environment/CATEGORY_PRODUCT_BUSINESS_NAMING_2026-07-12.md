## The substitution line (carries beyond naming — into every future claim pass)

> **The practitioner holds the person; the platform holds the material.**

This sentence does more constitutional work than any name will: it is the one-line answer
to the panel question, the trust-copy kernel, and the reason *"continues holding what
matters to people"* is honest where *"continues holding people"* never could be. Whatever
names win, every outward claim gets tested against it. (Candidate for the claim-discipline
canon at the sitting; recorded here because it emerged from the naming work.)

## Two tests every candidate must pass

1. **Failure test (claim discipline)**: if MAIA were switched off tomorrow, is the name
   still true? *Authored developmental environments* survives — the category is real
   independent of the AI. Anything AI-tethered ("AI coaching software") fails, which is
   why Kelly's avoid-list (coaching platform / transformation platform / wellness
   software / client management) is correct — those are all smaller *and* more fragile.
2. **Unification without demotion**: the framing that unifies MAIA · Practitioner Studio ·
   Personal Portal · Now What? · future organizational environments must not quietly
   demote MAIA to a *feature of the platform*. Constitutionally MAIA is the relational
   intelligence **present within** an environment, never the product being configured.
   "Authored developmental environments" preserves this: the environment is the
   container; MAIA is presence.
