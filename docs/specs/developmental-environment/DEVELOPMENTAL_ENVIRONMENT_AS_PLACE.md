## The shift

Once the orienting question becomes **"Where shall I spend time today?"** rather than
"Which feature should I click?", the platform has changed category:

```text
Features   become   Rooms
Usage      becomes  Inhabitation
Sessions   become   Return
Assistant  becomes  Presence within a place
```

People do not log in to use tools. They return to a place that remembers and supports
their unfolding. This may be one of the most distinctive ideas in the platform — it is
not a UI change; it is an ontology of platform.

## The candidate spatial grammar (held, not designed)

```text
Center:      MAIA Presence
Inner ring:  The Living Self      — Questions · Keeps · Practices · Memories
Middle ring: Development          — Position · Themes · Next Steps · Reflections
Outer ring:  Relationships & Field — Coach · Group · Retreat · Programs · Community
```

A member could literally *walk* their developmental environment. The rings also happen to
mirror the constitutional direction of authority — inward material is the member's alone;
authority moves outward only through authored experience.

## The tension this document exists to hold

**"Inhabitation" has a shadow, and the canon already names it: attachment capture.**

A place people *return to* and a place designed to be *dwelt in* are different buildings.
The off-ramp telos is standing law: the room returns people to their lives; the system's
psychological centrality must *decrease* over time. So the Place ontology is only
constitutional if:

- **Return is member-pulled** — the place never calls anyone back (dormancy composes as silence).
- **Inhabitation means the place holds *their* material faithfully** — not that they live there.
- **The outer ring is the point** — the architecture succeeds when time in the place
  produces life outside it. The map's arrows must end beyond its own edge.
- **"Where shall I spend time today?" is the member's question to themselves** — never a
  prompt the system asks, and never a metric the system optimizes.

A residence retains; a *trailhead* orients and releases. This place is a trailhead with rooms.

## What would need a sitting before any design act

1. Whether the ring map becomes interface, metaphor, or neither.
2. The eighth room (Community / Program Field) — inseparable from the unspecced
   group-shared-material gap; room and material model must be specified together.
3. How Place language coexists with "doors" language (doors answer arrival; rings answer
   dwelling — they may be the same geometry at different moments).
4. The capture test: what measurable property distinguishes return from retention, and
   who audits it. (Candidate: returning members' sessions should trend *shorter or
   steadier, never compulsively longer*; no streak mechanics, ever.)
