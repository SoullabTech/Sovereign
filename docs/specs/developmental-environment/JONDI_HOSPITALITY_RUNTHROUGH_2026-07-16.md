# Jondi Run-Throughs — Hospitality Witness Protocol

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Status:** OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL (Kelly-directed: "run throughs with Jondi first")
**Sequence change:** Jondi walks **before** the slice builds and **before** Senja's invitation. Her walk witnesses the current experience, wounds visible — the wounds are part of the evidence.

## What this walk is

The third witness class. Founder witness asks *is this what we intended*; structural witness asks *does it work*; **Jondi asks: would I stay?** Her layer — welcome, belonging, invitation, warmth — is the one neither builder can see (both are too close to the architecture; verdict history proves the pattern).

**Setup facts (verified 2026-07-16):**
- Jondi has a member account (prod, confirmed) — she can walk the interior signed in.
- Production has **zero program-door rows** (`field_programs` empty; demo seeding never ran post-deploy). Her invited-arrival link is either the plain field door, or Kelly says the word and the existing seed script runs in the prod container first.
- The two front-door breaks are LIVE on prod (generic auth card; onboarding exits to `/maia`). She will meet Break 1 if she starts signed out — **deliberately kept in the walk**: her reaction to it is hospitality-layer evidence the fix PR will cite.

## Blind-walk discipline (the instrument's validity depends on this)

- She receives **only what a real client would get**: one Larry-register invitation sentence + the link. No room names, no platform explanation, no constitutional vocabulary, no tour.
- **Do not lead. Do not defend. Do not explain** — if she is fully stuck, the unblocking sentence gets recorded as a failure datum, verbatim.
- Hesitation is evidence: observer notes each one with timestamp + screen + her exact words. Her words are never paraphrased in the record.
- Her conversation content is hers: the record captures *experience observations*, never session material; she chooses what of her kept things gets discussed afterward.

## Walk 1 — Invited arrival (current build, as-is)

1. She opens the link **signed out, on her own phone** (the one condition no witness has used).
2. Break 1 moment: capture her words at the generic sign-in card before helping.
3. She signs in (existing account); observer re-opens the link for her (Break 2's manual recovery — note whether she could have done it herself).
4. Room: she brings something real but light, works it, keeps (or declines) in her own words.
5. Harvest → field → "all doors" → map → she leaves when she feels done. Note where she tries to click, in what order — this is the §G first-reach data.

## Walk 2 — Return (next day, or hours later)

1. Same link, same phone. The only question: **does anything feel like it was waiting for her?** (P2's absence, witnessed at the hospitality layer.)
2. She tries to find what she kept — unassisted. Time it, note the path.

## Question set (asked AFTER each walk, never during)

**Orientation** — Where did you expect to click first? What confused you? What felt inviting? What felt intimidating?
**Belonging** — Did this feel like software or a place? Did you feel welcomed? Did you know why you were here?
**Meaning** — When did you first understand what this was for? Did the map make sense? Did anything feel hidden?
**Return** — Would you come back? Why? What would make you return tomorrow?
**The deep one, last:** Would you stay?

## What her data feeds

- The emotional-journey row (Expected / Safe / Useful / Seen / Returning), scored from a real walk instead of builder inference.
- The flow board's box approvals — Kelly strikes or confirms boxes with her evidence in hand.
- The slice's build order — if her wounds differ from the predicted ones, the slice re-prioritizes before it builds.
- First entries in the lived-use record: real hesitations, bound to moment and screen, member's words verbatim.

## Recording discipline (counsel addition, 2026-07-16 — adopted)

**Record the walks.** Screen recording + her narration + Kelly saying nothing. Hesitations, back-buttons, rereads, dead pauses, and assumptions vanish from memory almost immediately — the recording is the instrument. Her instruction is one sentence: *"Larry invited you here. Please go through the experience naturally and narrate what you're thinking."*

**Question discipline (counsel, adopted): after each stage ask first and mainly — "What do you think is happening?" Never "Did you like this?" Understanding precedes preference; misunderstanding is the signal.** The stage questions below are follow-ups, not openers.

**Pacing: the pauses matter.** Merge → phone walk → *silence* → Jondi → *silence* → disposable identity → *silence* → Senja. Evidence needs room to speak; nothing gets improved inside a silence.

Per-stage witness questions (asked at stage boundaries, never mid-screen):
- **Arrival** — What do you think this is? What do you expect to happen?
- **Registration** — Does "Create my key" make sense? Did anything feel strange? *(the copy was deliberately left unpolished for exactly this question)*
- **Landing** — Do you know where you are? Does this feel like Larry's place?
- **Leaving** — Would you come back? Why?

**Senja's walk (later) gets almost no questions at all** — she is the first genuine cold witness, and what she *assumes* matters more than what she answers.

**Standing caution (adopted):** after these walks, build nothing new — no themes, no community, no advanced maps — until the hesitation evidence has been sat with. The walks will teach more than further architecture.

**Closing three questions (counsel refinement, adopted — asked at the very end, after everything else):**
1. When did you feel most confused?
2. When did you feel most interested or hopeful?
3. **If you returned tomorrow, where would you naturally go first?** *(the relationship question — it reveals what, if anything, formed)*

**Study sequencing (ratified in dialogue, 07-16): TWO walks before the hospitality study.** Kelly's phone walk is evidence too — narrate and record it like Jondi's; once the comparator study is read it cannot be unread, and first-walk evidence has an innocence that cannot be recovered. Order: merge → deploy → Kelly phone walk → Jondi walk → extract hesitations → hospitality study (Circle / Mighty Networks / CoachAccountable / Headspace / Notion) → pattern-vs-hesitation table → only then redesign. **Provenance discipline:** patterns that already match the build arrived by convergence, not derivation — never claim "we designed this because X does it" when the record shows independent arrival through failures, canon, and walks.
