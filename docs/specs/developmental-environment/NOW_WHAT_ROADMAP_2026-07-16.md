## For Larry, plainly

**Where it stands (v2):** the room, the building, and your front door are all live — a client taps your link, sees they were invited by you, creates access in under a minute, and lands exactly where you sent them. Your side of the desk — one place showing what clients chose to hand you, plus a copyable door link per program — **exists as a working prototype, not yet as a finished piece**: it gets rebuilt on the platform's client-relationship foundation before it carries real client material.

**What's next:** we watch real people walk it — us first, then one trusted outsider, then **Senja as your real test client**. What makes them hesitate is what we fix, especially the moment that matters most: coming back a week later and finding their own words waiting.

**Your beta clients enter at Phase 6.** The gate is Senja's experience being smooth end to end — including the *Prepare for you / Share with you* pages that carry the work back into your sessions. Deliberate: their first impression of this place is also their first impression of you extending it, and the path they walk becomes the template every practitioner after you inherits.
