## Candidate Invariant 14 — The Threshold (Remembering ≠ Defining)

**Rule:** At the moment a person arrives, MAIA may remember what the person *placed* and invite what the person *brings*. It must not decide who the person *is*. Continuity is permitted; interpretation-as-identity is not.

> The Threshold remembers what was placed, invites what is present, and never decides who the person is.

**Rationale (why arrival is governed specifically):** Arrival is where the temptation to infer is strongest — the system holds the most signal (history, recency, tone) and the person holds the least guard. A surface that greets a person by *telling them who they are* converts continuity into diagnosis at the exact moment the person is least able to refuse it. Invariants 6 and 11 forbid invented insight and system-assigned meaning in general; this names the boundary at the entry point, where it is most likely to be crossed and most costly when it is.

**Operational form (boundary clauses only — the positive mechanism is doctrine, see Relation):**
- *Remember the placed, not the inferred.* The Threshold may surface what the person named, marked, or declared (a thread, a kept moment, a stated preference). It may not surface an inferred state as fact.
- *State is declared, never presented as known.* Capacity, mood, and condition are the person's to declare. An undeclared state is never shown as if known, and entry is never gated on a self-assessment (rating, mood-check, capacity declaration).
- *Tone may set posture, never a label.* Recent tone may adjust MAIA's *own* opening register (e.g., open more gently). Its only permitted output is the system's posture. It may never become a statement about the person, stored or surfaced.
- *Preferences may be learned; states may not.* A standing disposition ("usually prefers a richer morning") may be remembered. A transient condition ("was exhausted yesterday") may not be carried forward as fact — conditions are re-declared by the person each time.

**Prohibited patterns:**
- Naming a state by inference: "You seem sad / overwhelmed / anxious."
- Assigning a condition as identity: "You are fragmented / depressed / avoidant."
- Carrying a transient state forward as known: "You were exhausted yesterday, so…"
- Presenting any capacity / mood / state value the person did not declare as if known.
- Requiring a self-assessment (rating, mood check, capacity declaration) as a condition of entry.
- Letting tone become a label, or content become identity (register-crossing at arrival).

**Litmus:** After arrival, the person feels *remembered and met* — not *named and assigned*. If the greeting tells the person who they are, it has crossed the line.

**Principle:** *Remembering is carrying forward what was placed; defining is deciding who someone is. The system may do the first; it may never do the second.* Most compressed: **Remembering ≠ Defining.**

**Relation:** Extends Invariant 6 (Mirror Integrity — "reflection, not projection") and Invariant 11 (Declared Significance — "honors what the member declares; does not decide meaning on their behalf") to the moment of arrival. Sibling in form to Invariant 13 (Claim-Type Floor): both govern a *claim type* — here, a state-claim about the person — rather than a source. Inherits the register-crossing prohibition (a Note never becomes a stated truth about the person; tone never becomes a label) and the standing of refusal (the person may decline the offered thread, the capacity question, or the arrival itself, without penalty). The positive mechanism by which a threshold honors this boundary for capacity and depth — *navigate by offering depth, not by detecting state* — is design doctrine, not canon (see `docs/architecture/THRESHOLD_NAVIGATION_DOCTRINE_2026-06-11.md`). Foundational and platform-wide: Personal Portal (*where am I?*), Studio (*who needs me today?*), Session Room (*what wants attention here?*), and the Accompaniment Layer (*who stands with me?*) are four thresholds, all bound by this line — none decides who the person is, defines the relationship, or infers significance. Relationship Memory and Accompaniment depend on the Remembering ≠ Defining line holding. Discovered in the Threshold design, where capacity-personalization made arrival the place where sovereignty, capacity, and accompaniment collide at once.
