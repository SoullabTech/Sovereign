# Personal Field — Run-Sheet Retrospective

**Reusable template.** Fill one after **each** Session 0 (exploratory). Purpose: let *observed practice* shape the eventual PF Session 1 run-sheet — rather than asserting it up front.

---

**Session:** `<participant>` · `session-NNN` · `<date>`
**Facilitator:**

### Questions

1. **Which prompts generated the richest episodes?**
2. **Which prompts fell flat?**
3. **Did the participant naturally speak in verbs or objects?** (early signal for the object-vs-verb axis — record it, do **not** lock the axis)
4. **Were there recurring episode shapes?** (candidate clusters — note, don't decide)
5. **What facilitation moves helped** (or pulled them out of their own frame)?
6. **What should change before the next session?**
7. Anything that surprised us / wasn't anticipated?

---

**Carry-forward:** after N sessions, synthesize these retrospectives → derive the PF Session 1 run-sheet (timing · sequence · facilitation · debrief). Until that synthesis exists, **no run-sheet is asserted**, and **no axis/architecture decision is earned** (that waits on §5/§6 clustering over the coded corpus).
