## What it is

**"Things Worth Keeping"** — a **personal canon**, not a content feed. A living collection of *wisdom that shaped me, words that found me, things I never want to lose, moments that helped me remember who I am.* It is the standing home where the Evening Portal's **Remember** harvest accumulates, alongside curated outside wisdom.

**It is a *meaning field*, not a quote field** (Kelly, 2026-06-14). Quotes are one artifact among many: poems, songs, passages, myths, **member phrases**, relationship moments, personal insights, lines from MAIA, lines from life. The design question is not *"what wisdom should be shown?"* (content) but ***"what belongs in the member's canon?"*** (meaning) — a much richer space, and one that stays on the witnessing side because the canon is the member's, curated by them.

It is "carry forward" (the third of the four verbs) made into a place.

## The drift it must refuse (Drift #1 / #3)

A typical quote feature becomes a **content platform**: endless scroll, inspirational feed, social-style consumption, collections-as-trophies. The Personal Portal is not a content platform. **Structural guards:** no infinite scroll, no feed, no algorithmic "discover more," no counts/streaks, no collection size as a metric. Member-curated; surfaced by resonance; measured by the Leaving Test.

## Version A vs Version B — the Witnessing/Authority axis applied to wisdom

- **Version A (refuse):** generic inspiration — *"The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step."* Pleasant, forgettable, not about you. **Content.**
- **Version B (build):** **witnessed resonance** — a passage brought *beside* something the member already declared meaningful. The quote is not the value; *"something I said mattered enough to be held"* is the value. Cultural creatives want meaning, relevance, accompaniment — not motivation, inspiration, or positivity.

## Layers (all member-curated; substrate = atoms + Keep/Capture + `is_breakthrough`, Live)

1. **Collected Wisdom** — passages the member saves from thinkers/poets/mystics/artists (Jung, Rilke, Oliver, Merton, Baldwin…). Member-added.
2. **My Quotes** — things the member chooses to keep.
3. **My Words** — things the *member* said (*"I keep trying to solve something that wants to be lived."*). Often the most important wisdom in the system; sourced from member-marked moments (never auto-harvested).
4. **Relationship Wisdom** — single lines from meaningful conversations (moments, not transcripts) — the sticky-note keepsakes. Ties to the Relationship Field; **member-marked only; no modeling of the other person** (Drift #4).
5. **Returning Threads** — *the riskiest layer; tightest guard.* A passage the member has returned to. **Structural guard:** a thread "returns" only when the **member re-marks/re-saves it**, or via a **verbatim factual** echo (*"you saved this on Mar 3, Jun 1, Sep 8"*). MAIA must **not** compute thematic recurrence (*"this keeps appearing because you're working on belonging"*) — that is inference (Drift #2) + aggregation (Drift #1). **Member-authored recurrence only.**

## Resonance mode (the companion gesture)

Not algorithmic recommendation. The member asks: *"Is there anything from my Wisdom Field that belongs beside me today?"* MAIA may bring forward a poem / passage / a line from a past conversation / a previously marked moment — **only in relationship to what the member has already declared meaningful.**

Bound by the two Reciprocity guards:
- **Member-initiated** (guard #1): surfaced inside a session the member opened, or on explicit ask — **never pushed as a notification** to re-engage.
- **Member-authored context** (guard #2): *"you saved this 287 days ago"* (fact) — **not** *"during a period of exploring belonging"* unless the member labeled it.

**Allowed:** reflect marked-meaningful, bring related passages/poems/myths/teachings/images, ask thoughtful questions, notice **declared** themes, offer perspective.
**Forbidden:** declare what you're going through, diagnose a stage, tell you what it means, assume significance you haven't expressed.

## Why it fits the Companion tier

Over time it becomes a **personal canon** — witnessed continuity in its most concrete form. It passes the Leaving Test: the goal is not more quotes; it is that *a person's meaning is held, not vanishing.*

## Substrate / build note (claim-disciplined)

- **Live substrate:** atoms + `is_breakthrough` + Keep/Capture — My Quotes, My Words, Soul Encounters, Relationship Wisdom are member-marked atoms with a `wisdom` source/label.
- **New:** a Collected-Wisdom add path (member saves outside passages → a member-owned store); the Wisdom Field surface; Resonance retrieval.
- **Resonance retrieval must be scoped to the member's *own declared items*** — never a model "find an inspiring quote" call over a global corpus (that reintroduces Version A). Retrieval over the member's canon = witnessing; retrieval over the world = content.
- **No new authority:** items are never auto-tagged; the member writes the label.
- **Gate:** the Resonance retrieval path passes the Drift Register (#1 no denominator, #2 no inference, #3 no push) before ship.
