# Book Studio — Founder Walk Field Journal

**For:** the first walk of the Soullab Press Manuscript Room (`/press/manuscript`, live 2026-07-22 @ `427753f1a`).
**Purpose:** capture phenomenological evidence — the authority infrastructure and constitution cannot supply. Deployment proves the room *works*; only the walk proves it *produces recognition*.
**Discipline:** every line below is the founder's to write. This file ships empty on purpose (an empty field and a cursor). Nothing here is pre-filled, suggested, or interpreted.

> **The purpose of the walk is not to prove the room works. The purpose is to discover what actually happens when someone encounters their own words through it.**

A walk that yields nothing is also evidence. Don't optimize, don't rename collections to make them work, don't evaluate features. The question is not "is this good software?" — it is *"does my relationship to my own writing change?"*

**Suggested source:** something with distance — an early Elemental Alchemy draft, old essays, journal fragments. Not the polished current work; the pieces you've half-forgotten. Distance increases the chance of genuine rediscovery.

---

## The four categories (mark each kept line)

- **A — Mechanical success.** It worked. (Necessary, not sufficient.)
- **B — Unexpected keep.** A passage you would not have predicted keeping.
- **C — Inevitable collection.** A name that arrived as "these obviously belong together," not "I should make a category."
- **D — The sentence.** *"I had forgotten that I knew this"* / *"I've been writing about this all along."* — the event.

- **E — Missing-object signal.** "I wish there was a way to…" / "I expected these to…" / "I wanted to gather these differently." **Do not build it. Only record it.** Repeated E-signals across walkers are how the next primitive reveals itself (this is how ingest surfaced).

**Journaling rhythm:** don't overfill during the walk — the act of documenting can interrupt recognition. If a D-event happens, stop and capture it verbatim. Otherwise let yourself wander; write afterward.

Broadened witness statements to listen for (any one crosses the threshold):
- "I forgot I wrote this."
- "These passages have been talking to each other the whole time."
- "This theme is everywhere."
- "I didn't realize this manuscript was already trying to become this book."
