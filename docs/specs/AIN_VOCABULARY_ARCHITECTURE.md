The answer cannot simply be the database. **A practitioner does not own the member's
meaning — but a practitioner does own the language of their offering.**

## 2. Two authorities, running in opposite directions

This is the distinction that makes the stack coherent rather than self-contradictory. *"The
member's layer always wins"* and *"Core defines safety boundaries"* are both true because
they are **different kinds of authority.**

| | Flows | Owns | Overridable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Constraint authority** | **downward** from AIN Core | what may not be done — concepts, interaction verbs, safety boundaries | **never** |
| **Naming authority** | **upward** from the member | what this is *called*, what it *means to me* | always, by the layer below |

> **Core constrains the envelope. The member names what is inside it.** A practitioner layer
> may relabel; it may not re-authorize. Renaming *Practice* to *Flourishing Practice* is
> vocabulary. Renaming it to *Assignment* — thereby importing compliance — is a boundary
> crossing wearing a label's clothing.

This makes the vocabulary layer the operative mechanism for **Sovereignty Invariant 14**
(cultural sovereignty): *are we translating the member's meaning into our vocabulary?* The
stack is how the answer becomes structurally *no* rather than a matter of care.

It also composes with the **Constitutional Direction of Authority**: authority moves upward
through authored experience. A label supplied from above is a container; the meaning placed
inside it is authored from below and is never manufactured downward.

## 3. The layers

### Core (AIN) — the invariant layer

Defines **available concepts · interaction verbs · safety boundaries.**

```
work · practice · explore · keep · connect
```

Core is the only layer that may **forbid**. Its prohibitions are inherited by every
expression and cannot be relabelled away — no vocabulary may introduce compliance,
progress, scoring, ranking or diagnosis into a layer where Core excludes them.

### Practitioner — the expression layer

Defines **tone · domain language · labels.**

| Practitioner | Vocabulary family |
|---|---|
| Larry | leadership · flourishing · executive development |
| A therapist | healing · integration · growth |

Owns the language of their **offering**. Owns nothing about the member's meaning.

### Program / Experience — the specialization layer

May specialize the practitioner's family for a particular container. A retreat may speak
differently from an ongoing engagement inside the same practice.

### Member — the deepest layer

**Always wins for personal meaning.**

```
System:   Current Work
Larry:    Leadership Focus
Member:   "Learning how to stop carrying everything myself."
```

**The member's phrase is the deepest layer.** Where a member has named their own work, that
naming is what renders — no layer above may overwrite it, and no layer above may translate
it into house vocabulary.

## 4. ⛔ UNRULED — what vocabulary attaches to

**Do not bind vocabulary to `practice_fields` yet.** It is tempting because the table exists
and already scopes fields — and that convenience is exactly the risk. Five candidates,
producing materially different futures:

| # | Attached to | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the **practitioner** | one voice across everything they offer; portable between programs |
| 2 | the **practice field** | vocabulary is a property of the practice, shared by any practitioner in it |
| 3 | the **program** | fine-grained; risks every container inventing its own language |
| 4 | the **relationship** | vocabulary emerges between practitioner and member; most alive, least reusable |
| 5 | the **member journey** | vocabulary follows the person across practitioners |

> **Founder instinct, recorded as instinct and NOT as a ruling (2026-08-03):** *practitioner
> owns a vocabulary family · programs may specialize it · members inherit it but can
> personalize it.* That reads as **1 + 3 + member layer**. It remains a ruling to be made.

**Open with it:**

1. **Default vocabulary** when a practitioner supplies none — a neutral universal set, or is
   vocabulary mandatory at field creation?
2. **1:1 binding?** Do the five layers bind exactly to the five verbs, or may a practitioner
   **suppress** a layer their practice does not use?
3. **Member personalization** — is it per-layer naming, or only the free-text naming of their
   own Current Work?
4. What happens to a member's kept material when a practitioner **changes** their vocabulary,
   or when a member leaves one practitioner for another? (Withdrawal ends access, never
   memory — but memory rendered in whose words?)
