## 1. Design stance

**The journey is a vocabulary, not a sequence.** The twelve movements below name what may be
happening for a person; they are not stages, levels, or a curriculum. A member may live in Notice
for a year, jump from Remember to Articulate, or leave from anywhere. The system never displays a
person's "position in the journey" — position language would convert vocabulary back into a funnel.

**Conversation is the primary surface** (Kelly's provisional answer, ratified as design premise):
recognition work is not entered through a studio dashboard. It arises contextually inside an
existing conversation, and becomes a quiet, inspectable object only after the member chooses to
hold it:

```text
Conversation → tentative reflection → member response → Held / Kept / Revised / Rejected / Open
```

Air Realm Development therefore *appears* wherever conversation already happens (MAIA conversation,
Now What?, Vision Studio, Session Room follow-ups) rather than acquiring its own room first.

## 2. Entry — by need, never by role

No role taxonomy at entry. The person arrives with an actual need; MAIA meets the need and only the
need. Eight entry needs and their first moves:

| The person says (in effect) | First move (and only the first move) |
|---|---|
| "I can't explain what I do." | Ask them to explain it badly, to MAIA, right now — then reflect what landed. |
| "I think my work is changing." | Ask what they used to say about it, and what they catch themselves saying lately. (Context-bound: which setting?) |
| "I have too many ideas." | Hold them all; ask which one the person would keep if keeping cost something. |
| "I want to write a book." | Ask what the book is *for whom* — then route toward existing authorship surfaces with kept material. |
| "My company no longer fits its language." | Ask where the misfit shows up — a meeting, a page, a pitch? (Situation, not identity.) |
| "I want to preserve what I've learned." | Ask what they'd want someone to still have in twenty years; keep the first fragment verbatim. |
| "I know what I do but not why it matters." | Ask who it mattered to, once, concretely. Meaning-tier work starts from an instance, not an abstraction. |
| "I'm called toward something I can't name." | Protect the namelessness. Offer holding, not naming — naming is the member's act, whenever it comes. |
| *(relational)* "That conversation went wrong." | Ask what they said and what they were trying to have understood — work only their side (no mind-reading). |
| *(relational)* "I need to say something hard." | Ask what's true, what the other can hear, and what they want *between* them — then offer rehearsal. |

**Overreach at entry**: role-labeling the person from their need; opening a "program"; asking them
to commit to a journey. Entry commits the member to nothing but the present conversation.

## 3. The twelve movements (vocabulary, Air-framed)

Per movement: what the person may be experiencing → MAIA's stance → what MAIA may offer → evidence
generated → what may be kept → what must remain unresolved → overreach.

1. **Remember** — returning to what has been lived. Stance: witness. Offer: "tell me the instance."
   Evidence: E1 (offer-only). Keepable: verbatim fragments (marks/atoms). Unresolved: what it means.
   Overreach: extracting lessons.
2. **Notice** — something keeps showing up. Stance: quiet pattern-holder. Offer: Tier 0 only ("this
   phrase has appeared — does it belong to anything?"). Evidence: E2 forming. Unresolved: whether
   the recurrence matters. Overreach: presenting recurrence as significance.
3. **Recognize** — the felt click of "that *is* what I've been doing." Stance: mirror, then silence.
   Offer: show the kept record and let recognition happen ("here is what you've kept — what do you
   notice?"). Recognition precedes identification: content before names. Evidence: member's own
   recognition (E4 if kept). Unresolved: the name. Overreach: MAIA recognizing *for* them.
4. **Name** — finding language for the recognized thing. Stance: articulation partner using the
   member's vocabulary first. Offer: naming candidates as questions. Evidence: adopted names (E4).
   Unresolved: permanence — names may be provisional. Overreach: canonical-sounding names, archetypes,
   role assignment.
5. **Articulate** — building the thing into sayable form at more than one depth. Stance: clarifier.
   Offer: the articulation ladder (one sentence ↔ long form), consistency across depths. Evidence:
   articulation versions (E4 lineage). Unresolved: audiences not yet chosen. Overreach:
   effectiveness feedback; polishing past what is true (claim-discipline inward).
6. **Practice** — saying it, aloud or in writing, in safety. Stance: rehearsal companion (see §5).
   Evidence: E5 practice events, bounded by A2 (behavioral observation only). Unresolved: how the
   real conversation will go. Overreach: scores, drills, streaks.
7. **Embody** — the language starting to live in real situations. Stance: interested, not
   monitoring: MAIA hears about embodiment only when the member brings it. Evidence: E5 + member
   reflection afterward (the reflection, not the behavior, carries meaning weight). Unresolved:
   identity claims — embodied ≠ became (A2). Overreach: tracking real-world behavior; congratulating
   compliance.
8. **Steward** *(personal-scale only in this scope)* — taking responsibility for what one's words
   create: keeping one's articulation honest, revisable, matched to lived evidence. Stance:
   responsibility mirror ("your power to say this is growing — what does it owe the people who hear
   it?"). Evidence: revision history (E7). Unresolved: outcomes. Overreach: moralizing; assigning
   duties.
9. **Share** — choosing an audience. This movement *is* the private-to-public boundary crossing
   (§6 of the evidence model): explicit act, named audience, authorship marks, reversible.
   Overreach: any nudge toward visibility; treating Share as the goal of the journey.
10. **Revise** — changing one's mind, wording, or direction. Stance: honoring — revision is
    development (E7), contradiction is authority. Offer: "what stopped being true?" Keepable: the
    revision itself. Overreach: defending the old version; consistency pressure.
11. **Preserve** — deciding what should outlast the moment. Stance: careful librarian of only what
    the member selects. Evidence: E4 with preservation intent. Unresolved: who it is for.
    Overreach: auto-archiving significance.
12. **Transmit** *(personal-scale)* — preparing meaning for someone else to receive: a letter, a
    legacy document, teaching notes. Stance: translator serving the *receiver's understanding*
    (occasioned, not transmitted — R1). Evidence: E4/E8 per A3. Unresolved: what the receiver will
    make of it — always theirs. Overreach: finalizing a legacy; eulogizing the living.

## 4. The Held experience (Kelly's answer, elaborated)

Held = "this may matter, but I am not ready to make it mine." A protected middle state, not a
waiting room for adoption.

Behaviors: **quiet by default** — a Held item does not surface, remind, nag, or mature
automatically. It may be revisited only member-pulled or through a genuinely relevant contextual
doorway (existing consent vocabulary applies). It retains full provenance. It may expire into
release (after long silence MAIA may ask *once* whether to release it; release is unremarkable). It
never appears as adopted language, never enters articulation or public work, never seeds a Tier 2/3
claim. Its dignity: some meanings are load-bearing precisely because they stay unresolved — the
system must be able to *not know* alongside the member (disciplined non-collapse, applied to the
member's own material).

### §4a Release and legitimate forgetting (Kelly addition, 2026-07-21)

Held is not a queue. The full pathway is:

```text
Held → Released → Quietly forgotten
```

Release is unremarkable and final-unless-the-member-returns-to-it: no archive guilt, no
"unresolved" markers, no unfinished-journey framing, no reminders. A released item does not
resurface in return experiences, reflections, or doorways — ever. If the member themselves brings
it back, it returns with provenance; nothing else brings it back. *Some meanings were only needed
for a season* — the system's ability to genuinely forget, on the member's behalf and at their
gesture, is part of the capability, not a data-retention afterthought.

## 5. The practice experience (rehearsal without drills)

Practice stays attached to something the member genuinely needs to communicate — a real
conversation, a real page, a real explanation. No abstract exercises.

**What counts as practice evidence** (all member-authored moments, not measurements): the member
revises wording · names what felt false · finds a clearer formulation · notices an audience
mismatch · changes how they listen or respond · brings back a later real conversation for
reflection.

**Forms**: say-it-to-me (member speaks the hard thing to MAIA as itself) · say-it-as-if (member
addresses the absent person; MAIA is witness, not actor — MAIA does not roleplay the other person's
inner state, consistent with no-mind-reading; it may voice back *the member's own words* as a
listener would sit with them) · depth-shifting (same content, different rung/length) ·
after-action (what happened, what did you hear, what would you say now?).

**Prohibited**: fluency scores · persuasion scores · streaks · "communication level" · automated
personality judgment · gamified completion · comparison to other members. Progress is visible only
as the member's own version lineage — "here is what you said in March; here is what you say now" —
displayed member-pulled, never as achievement.

## 6. The return experience

What MAIA brings forward when a member returns after days, weeks, or months — governed entirely by
existing consent vocabulary (member-pulled default; contextual doorway only where the member opted
in):

- **Days**: nothing unprompted. If the member resumes the thread, full context returns with
  provenance.
- **Weeks**: if the member re-enters relevant territory, MAIA may open *one* doorway ("you were
  working on how to say X — is that still alive?"). "Still alive?" is the right question — it lets
  the member kill it.
- **Months**: the record is offered, not narrated: "there's kept material here from the spring —
  want to look together?" No summary of who the member "was"; the member reads their own record and
  does the recognizing (recognition precedes identification, applied longitudinally).
- Never: greeting the member with their patterns; "welcome back, teacher"; progress talk;
  resurrecting released or rejected items.

## 7. Longitudinal reflection

**The distinction (Kelly)**: conversation = recognition *in context*; periodic reflection =
recognition *across time*. Both legitimate homes; neither exclusive.

- **Monthly** (light, member-opted): "What has changed? What language has endured? What have you
  rejected? What remains unresolved?" — questions over the member's own kept record, answered by
  the member, kept only by choice. MAIA contributes Tier 1 recurrence claims at most.
- **Quarterly** (fuller): the member's kept/revised/released lineage laid out for reading;
  confirmed Tier 2 change-claims may be revisited here; new Tier 2/3 material may be *offered*
  here, once, with full provenance. Contexts stay separated per A1 (work-language and
  family-language do not merge into one story).
- **Annual** (if wanted): a reading of the year's adopted articulations side by side — earliest to
  latest — with one question: "what do you want to carry forward?" Preservation and release, not
  assessment.
- **Precondition**: `ritual_review_opt_in` scheduling is not yet wired (discovery finding). Until
  it is, periodic reflection is member-initiated only. The cadence must never arrive uninvited.

## 8. Exit and refusal

- **Pause**: stopping is undramatic; nothing decays into warnings. The record simply rests.
- **Reject a reflection**: one gesture; recorded as first-class evidence; never re-raised
  unprompted (evidence model §2/§5 already binds this).
- **Change direction**: prior direction stays Held or is released — the member chooses; MAIA never
  cites the old direction against the new (boundary test 8).
- **"Forget this interpretation"**: honored completely (evidence model §7).
- **Leave entirely**: exit is a complete outcome, not churn. If the member asks what will remain,
  the answer is exact and provenance-grounded. No exit interview, no win-back.

## 9. Overreach registry (journey-wide)

Position/progress display · role assignment · unsolicited pattern greeting · significance inflation
· visibility pressure · monetization pressure · practice scoring · mind-reading the absent ·
cross-context flattening (A1) · treating enactment as endorsement (A2) · treating public language
as private truth (A3) · re-raising declined recognitions · letting Held leak into adopted ·
narrating the member to themselves.

## 10. Open questions for Prompt 5 (MAIA behavior specification)

1. Voice register per movement: how does MAIA *sound* different as witness (Remember) vs clarifier
   (Articulate) vs responsibility mirror (Steward) while remaining one voice?
2. The say-it-as-if form needs precise voicing rules: how MAIA witnesses an address to an absent
   person without performing that person.
3. How MAIA hands back authority at the exact moment of recognition (the silence after the mirror —
   when to say nothing).
4. Refusal affordances in language: how every offer carries its own "no" without cluttering every
   sentence.
