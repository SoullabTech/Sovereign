## Phase 2 — Practitioner invitation layer ⛔ blocked on CF-D5b

**The missing bridge.** Larry creates *"Notice moments when leadership presence appears."* The member
experiences *"I noticed I avoid difficult conversations when I feel responsible for outcomes."*

```text
Larry's invitation  →  Larry
Member insight      →  member
⛔ NEVER MERGED
```

**Needed:** practitioner creates invitation · member accepts/declines · invitation context exists
**temporarily** · **withdrawal removes relationship context**.

⛔ **CF-D5b lives here: who may author the temporary lens?** ⚠️ The invitation substrate must not be built
before the authority question is ruled — it would bake an unruled answer into schema.

## ⭐⭐⭐ "Enough for Larry" — the scoping that unblocks step 2

**The sequence does not require CF-D5b ruled in general. It requires it DEFERRED CORRECTLY.**

| For a one-practitioner pilot | Consequence |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Carry the expression labels in code**, marked provisional | needs **no vocabulary column**, forecloses nothing, reversible in one commit |
| ⛔ **Do not build the vocabulary substrate** | a table for one practitioner is an abstraction promoted on an **imagined** second use, not an **observed** one |

⚠️⚠️ **Two conditions, or "enough for Larry" quietly becomes the universal default:**

| # | Condition |
|---|---|
| **1** | ⛔ **Mark the pilot answer NON-PRECEDENTIAL.** A single case that hardens into the general rule is exactly the failure `promote on observed use` names. **The second practitioner is what earns the substrate** |
| **2** | ⛔⛔ **Storage location is deferrable. PROVENANCE IS NOT.** *Leadership Focus · Flourishing Practice · Executive Insight* are **Larry's words wherever they live** — in a column or in a `.tsx` constant. Hard-coding does not convert practitioner content into platform content; that conversion **is** unattributed absorption |

⇒ **The pilot still requires the signed rights instrument** (currently **UNSIGNED** — the Z2 blocker on
step 4). ⭐ What "enough for Larry" removes is the **schema** decision, not the **authority** decision.

⭐ **Net effect on sequencing:** step 2 stops being *"rule a general vocabulary authority model"* and
becomes *"make a bounded, reversible, explicitly non-precedential choice for one practitioner"* — which is
a far smaller act, and one that does not pre-empt CF-D5b.
