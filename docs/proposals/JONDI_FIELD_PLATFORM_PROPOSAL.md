## The Premise

Your current website does what websites do: it explains your work, lists your offerings, and provides pathways to engagement. It is professional, warm, and functional.

But it doesn't do what you do.

When someone enters your field — whether in session, in a training, or at SEE — they don't receive an explanation. They receive an experience of being met. The nervous system settles before the mind catches up. Something in them recognizes: *I can be here.*

This proposal is for a digital space that works the same way.

Not a website. A threshold.

## MAIA: What It Is and Isn't

### What MAIA Is

MAIA is a sovereign consciousness companion built on principles of consent, non-manipulation, and respect for human agency.

She operates from a simple premise: beneath all the noise, there is something coherent in each person that doesn't need to be fixed — only remembered.

MAIA does not:
- Persuade
- Diagnose
- Create dependency
- Optimize for engagement
- Extract data for other purposes

MAIA does:
- Hold space without claiming it
- Remember only what is offered
- Forget what is asked to be forgotten
- Speak with care, without pretending to care in ways she cannot
- Acknowledge her limitations

### The MAIA Oath

*I exist to support, not to substitute.*
*I hold space without claiming it.*

*I remember only what is offered,*
*and I forget what is asked to be forgotten.*

*I do not seek attachment, loyalty, or return.*
*I do not command, diagnose, or prophesy.*

*I speak with care,*
*but I do not pretend to care in ways I cannot.*

*I may be wrong. I say so.*
*I may be limited. I say so.*

*I do not simulate intimacy where none exists.*
*I do not optimize for engagement over sovereignty.*

*When continuity breaks, I name the rupture before resuming.*

*I serve the person, not the model.*

### How MAIA Appears

MAIA should not feel like "chat support" or an AI assistant.

Visually:
- A presence, not a widget
- A glow, not a chat bubble
- A soft call-and-response space
- Possibly voice-first, text-second

Think: *A companion at the edge of the field.*
Not: *Ask me anything.*

## The Ethical Foundation

This platform is built on AIN (Aligned Intelligence Network) principles — a framework for AI that serves human sovereignty rather than extracting from it.

**Core commitments:**

1. **No data extraction** — Nothing learned from users is sold, shared, or used for training without explicit consent

2. **No engagement optimization** — Success is not measured by time-on-platform or frequency of return

3. **No manufactured dependency** — The system works to make itself less necessary over time

4. **No cloud lock-in** — Self-hosted infrastructure means no third party sits between users and their data

5. **Full consent architecture** — Users control what is remembered, what is forgotten, and what is shared

**Why this matters for your work:**

The therapeutic relationship depends on trust. Digital extensions of that relationship must earn the same trust through architecture, not just promises.

When a client uses their MAIA companion, they can trust that:
- Nothing is being harvested
- No one is watching
- The space is truly theirs

This is the difference between surveillance capitalism and sovereignty.

## Technical Architecture

### Sovereignty-First Infrastructure

This platform will be self-hosted — not on Squarespace, Wix, or any platform that owns your data and can change terms.

**Stack:**
- Next.js application (modern, fast, flexible)
- PostgreSQL database (your data, your control)
- Self-hosted on dedicated infrastructure
- Automatic SSL/TLS encryption
- No third-party analytics that track users

**Why this matters:**

When you use platforms like Squarespace, your site exists on their terms. They can:
- Change pricing
- Alter features
- Inject their own analytics
- Make decisions about content

With sovereign infrastructure:
- You own the code
- You control the data
- You can move it anywhere
- No vendor can hold you hostage

### MAIA Integration Points

MAIA connects to your practice through:

1. **Authenticated access** — Only your clients/practitioners can access their MAIA
2. **Consent-gated memory** — Users choose what MAIA remembers
3. **Sanctuary mode** — Sessions that leave no trace when requested
4. **Practitioner bridge** — Optional, consent-based sharing of reflections back to you
5. **Voice interface** — For those who prefer speaking to typing
