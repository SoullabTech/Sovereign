# Wireframes for Jondi Whitis Field Platform

## Overview

These wireframes represent the structural and spatial logic of the platform, not final visual design. They communicate:

- Layout and hierarchy
- Spatial relationships
- Content placement
- Navigation patterns
- The "feel" of each space

All wireframes use the dark/warm palette to suggest the aesthetic direction, but final design will involve proper typography selection, photography art direction, and motion design.

---

## Wireframe Index

### 01 - Threshold Landing
`01-threshold-landing.svg`

The entry point. Full viewport, no scroll. Three portals as invitations.

**Key features:**
- Full-screen atmospheric background
- Single tagline, centered
- Three large portal cards
- No menu, no footer, no competing elements
- Warm glow suggesting hearth/presence

**Design principle:** Field Before Function. The visitor is not told what to do — they are drawn toward what matters.

---

### 02 - Walk With Me
`02-walk-with-me.svg`

The client pathway. Split layout with immersive imagery.

**Key features:**
- Full-height image on left (holding, landscape quality)
- Content on right with breathing room
- Minimal text hierarchy
- MAIA companion introduction
- Single primary CTA
- Secondary link for existing clients

**Design principle:** Minimal explanation. Emphasis on safety, companionship, pace.

---

### 03 - Walk With Others
`03-walk-with-others.svg`

The practitioner/mentoring pathway. Mirrored split layout.

**Key features:**
- Full-height image on right
- Content on left
- Two-line tagline
- Minimal offering list (not dense)
- Access point for current practitioners

**Design principle:** Framed as art and presence, not training catalog.

---

### 04 - Hold the Field
`04-hold-the-field.svg`

SEE and collective work. Cinematic top-image layout.

**Key features:**
- Wide cinematic image area (gathering, connection)
- Title overlay on image
- Two content blocks: SEE info + The Commons
- Two CTAs: current gathering + year-round access

**Design principle:** Living commons, not conference page. The field persists beyond the event.

---

### 05 - MAIA Companion
`05-maia-companion.svg`

The MAIA interface for clients/practitioners.

**Key features:**
- Central presence (glow, not chat bubble)
- Conversation space with minimal chrome
- Voice-first input suggestion
- Lab Notes sidebar
- Sanctuary Mode toggle
- Subtle user identification

**Design principle:** This is a field to be with, not a tool to use. Presence, not widget.

---

### 06 - Mobile Threshold
`06-mobile-threshold.svg`

Mobile adaptation of the landing page.

**Key features:**
- Stacked portal layout (not cramped)
- Tagline breaks across two lines
- Large touch targets
- Same atmospheric quality
- Same "no menu" principle

**Design principle:** Mobile-first nervous-system awareness. Large, calm, legible.

## Design System Notes

### Palette
- **Background:** Deep charcoal to midnight (#0d0d14 to #1a1a2e)
- **Accent:** Warm gold/amber (#d4a574)
- **Text primary:** Warm off-white (#e8dcc8)
- **Text secondary:** Muted stone (#a89f8f)
- **UI elements:** Dark with subtle borders

### Typography (Suggested)
- **Headlines:** Serif (Georgia placeholder — final: something elegant, slow)
- **Body:** Same serif, lighter weight
- **UI/Labels:** Clean sans-serif, small, muted

### Spacing
- Generous margins
- Nothing touches edges
- Breathing room between elements
- Content never feels dense

### Motion (To Be Designed)
- Breath-like timing (3-5 second cycles)
- Subtle particle effects
- Gentle fade transitions
- Nothing sudden or attention-grabbing
