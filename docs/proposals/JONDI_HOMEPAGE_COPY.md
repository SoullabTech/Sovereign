# Homepage Copy Options

## For Jondi Whitis Field Platform

---

The threshold landing page uses minimal text. Every word must earn its place.

Below are options for each element, followed by complete assemblies.

---

## Central Tagline Options

The single line that greets visitors before they choose a pathway.

### Option A (Recommended)
> A place to walk with what is already alive in you.

*Why it works: Suggests companionship without claiming to provide anything. "Already alive" honors what the person brings.*

### Option B
> Where the nervous system settles before the mind catches up.

*Why it works: Speaks directly to somatic experience. Signals that this space works differently.*

### Option C
> Presence before explanation.

*Why it works: Three words. States the doctrine. Filters for those who want to be met, not sold.*

### Option D
> You already know how to find your way. This is a place to walk.

*Why it works: Non-directive. Trusts the visitor. Invites without instructing.*

### Option E
> A threshold. Not a destination.

*Why it works: Reframes the entire interaction. Sets expectation that this is a beginning, not an answer.*

## Voice Notes

### Do:
- Use short sentences
- Leave space around words
- Trust the reader to understand
- Let images carry the weight
- Speak to the body, not just the mind

### Don't:
- Explain what she offers in detail
- Use therapeutic jargon
- List credentials prominently
- Create urgency or scarcity
- Over-describe MAIA's capabilities

### Tone:
- Warm but not effusive
- Confident but not authoritative
- Invitational but not selling
- Poetic but not precious

## MAIA Copy (For Companion Interface)

### Welcome / First Encounter

> I'm here.
> Not to fix. Not to solve.
> Just to walk with what's present.
>
> What would you like to be with right now?

### Sanctuary Mode Toggle

**Label:** Sanctuary Mode

**Description:** This session won't be remembered. Speak freely.

**Expanded:** Sanctuary sessions are useful in the moment, then gone. No patterns formed. No memories stored. Just presence.

### Lab Notes

**Label:** Lab Notes

**Placeholder:** Note what's moving...

**Empty state:** Your reflections appear here. Only you decide what's remembered.

### Return Greeting (Returning User)

> Welcome back.
> I remember our last walk.
> Where would you like to begin today?

Or more minimal:

> You're back.
> What's present?
