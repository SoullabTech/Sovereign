## A limit of the instrument, now named

Every one of the 8a harness's thirteen assertions can pass on a page no human can
read. It compares the render against the frozen row; **legibility is not a
property of that comparison.** A page that dumps the row verbatim in source order
scores perfectly.

That is not a defect in the harness — it is the boundary of what a fidelity
witness can establish, and it belongs beside the others this programme has found:

```text
test execution   ≠ type validation
script execution ≠ inclusion in ship program
gate identity    ≠ diagnostic identity
render fidelity  ≠ intelligibility
```

The last one has no machine witness. A person opening the room and saying *"ah,
I see what she thinks the book is"* — or not — is the only instrument for it.

## Why this precedes adoption

The 8b question is whether the founder recognises the Work in her reading. That
judgment is being asked of a screen that requires the thesis to be reconstructed
before it can be judged — and a founder who cannot see the claim cannot fairly
accept or reject it.

thinks the book is"*, not *"what am I looking at"*.
