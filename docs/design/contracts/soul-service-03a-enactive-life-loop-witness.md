## Purpose

03A witnesses the first full movement:

> **source → member-chosen Life Move → expectation → leave Soullab → lived consequence → expectation/consequence contrast → member revision**

The prototype is intentionally synthetic.

It does not monitor actual life.

## Witness question

> **Can Soullab help someone carry a conscious movement into life and then let lived consequence revise the field without turning life into a compliance system or protecting the prior interpretation?**

## Synthetic source

> “I’m worried readers are losing the core idea in this chapter.”

## Member-chosen movement

> “Share the chapter with two readers and ask where they lose connection.”

This is action-shaped rather than conclusion-shaped.

It does not say:

> “Prove the chapter is clear.”

## Historical expectation

Before leaving:

> “I expect both readers to say the core argument is hard to follow.”

This remains historically inspectable after return.

## Surprise aperture

Before leaving:

> “I would be surprised if both readers understood the core idea but pointed to different local problems.”

This makes disconfirmation legitimate before the outcome is known.

## Outside Soullab

The away state explicitly states:

- Soullab is not watching;
- no tracking;
- no compliance check;
- no interpretation while the member is living;
- return is optional.

## Returned consequence

Synthetic member report:

> “Both readers said the core idea was clear. One lost the thread at the chapter transition; the other understood the transition but found the ending abrupt.”

Standing:

> **member-reported lived evidence**

The prototype does not claim this is objective proof of a general law.

## Reality veto

The returned report does not support the earlier broad expectation.

The field therefore exposes:

> expectation ↔ consequence

and states only the bounded evidence relation:

> the broad expectation is not supported by these two reports; two more local points of friction remain.

The member may then choose:

- Keep it
- Weaken it
- Narrow it
- Revise it
- Abandon it
- Leave it unresolved

No revision is selected automatically.

## No outcome ranking

The prototype does not assign:

- success;
- failure;
- growth;
- courage;
- regression;
- avoidance.

It receives consequence before evaluation.

## Non-instrumentalization

The initial state includes:

> **Don’t turn this into a Life Move**

which leads to:

> **This can remain reflection. Nothing has to be turned into an experiment.**

## Acceptance questions

1. Is the Life Move clearly member-chosen?
2. Is expectation preserved separately from consequence?
3. Does the system go quiet while the member is outside?
4. Is return optional?
5. Is the returned consequence labeled by actual standing?
6. Does disconfirming evidence weaken the prior broad expectation?
7. Can the member choose no revision?
8. Can the member abandon the prior frame?
9. Is a member-shaped revision distinct from the lived evidence itself?
10. Does the system avoid success/failure scoring?
11. Does the system avoid turning two observations into a universal rule?
12. Does life materially change the field rather than being absorbed into the prior theory?
