# PROVISIONAL-PATTERN-NONCONFIRMATORY-ATTENTION-LAW-01
## How a provisional pattern may inform future attention without becoming self-fulfilling

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **A provisional pattern may generate a question for future inquiry, but it may not pre-filter lived experience so that only confirming evidence becomes visible.**

03D governs what a pattern is allowed to do *after* it earns provisional standing.

The problem is not merely false pattern detection.

The deeper risk is **pattern-conditioned attention**:

> the system believes a pattern
> → therefore it looks for the pattern
> → therefore it retrieves pattern-consistent evidence
> → therefore the pattern appears stronger
> → therefore it receives even more attention.

That is a self-sealing loop.

## Default mode — Open encounter

Unless the member explicitly chooses otherwise:

> **Capture what happened before resurfacing the pattern.**

The next lived report should be allowed to arrive in its own terms.

The system must not lead with:

> “Did the transition confuse the reader again?”

when the current provisional pattern concerns transition continuity.

A neutral capture may ask:

> **What happened?**

or:

> **What stood out?**

The pattern can be compared only after the report exists.

## Explicit test mode

The member may deliberately choose:

> **Test this pattern in the next encounter.**

When they do, pattern influence becomes visible.

The system must show:

- the exact provisional proposition;
- what evidence would support it;
- what evidence would contradict it;
- what would be outside its scope;
- that unrelated observations still matter.

The test question must be symmetric.

Example:

> **Notice whether chapter transitions are clear or unclear, and record anything else that materially affects comprehension.**

Not:

> “Look for where the transitions fail.”

## Pattern-attention transparency law

> ## PATTERN-ATTENTION-TRANSPARENCY-LAW-01
>
> **If a stored or provisional pattern changes what the system asks, retrieves, highlights, or compares, that influence must be inspectable at the point where attention is being shaped.**

No hidden pattern-driven reranking.

No invisible query expansion.

No covert retrieval bias.

## Counterfactual requirement

A prospective pattern test should make clear:

> **What result would count against this proposition?**

A hypothesis that cannot lose standing is not a testable lens.

It is doctrine.

## Outside-scope evidence

> ## PATTERN-OUTSIDE-SCOPE-LAW-01
>
> **A returned event may be relevant to the member's situation while remaining outside the scope of the active pattern.**

Example:

Pattern:

> chapter transitions create continuity friction.

Returned report:

> transitions were clear, but an example in the middle of the chapter was confusing.

The system must not recode this as:

> another transition problem.

The report may matter.

It simply does not support that pattern.

## Non-monopoly law

> ## PATTERN-ATTENTION-NONMONOPOLY-LAW-01
>
> **A provisional pattern may not monopolize the evidence field merely because it already exists.**

Pattern-relevant evidence, counterevidence, unrelated evidence, and uncertainty remain available.

A current pattern is one possible organizer of inquiry.

It is not the default ontology of the next encounter.

## Retrieval law

Future pattern-aware retrieval must not retrieve only:

> “more examples of this pattern.”

It should be able to surface:

- supporting evidence;
- contradicting evidence;
- boundary cases;
- context-diverse evidence;
- evidence outside the pattern where materially relevant.

## No automatic strengthening

A supporting next event does not automatically promote:

> provisional pattern → stable truth.

03C's maturity law still governs.

The event joins the evidence field.

The proposition is then re-evaluated proportionately.

## Member authority

The member can choose:

- test this pattern;
- ignore this pattern for now;
- suspend it;
- decline it;
- revisit it later.

A provisional pattern does not gain the right to follow the member everywhere.

## Governing sentence

> **A pattern may help us know where to look. It may not decide what we are allowed to see.**
