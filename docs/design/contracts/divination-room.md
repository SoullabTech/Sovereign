# Divination — Experience Contract

## Arrival

> **Meet the question through symbol.**

The room begins with the inquiry rather than with a usage counter, entitlement tier, or catalogue of features.

## Traditions

I Ching, Tarot, and Runes are named as practices with their own symbolic grammar. Their cues invite attention rather than promise certainty.

## Authority

Symbolic material can become a prompt for reflection, a saved reading, or an explicit crossing into another room. It never becomes member meaning without the member's own act.
