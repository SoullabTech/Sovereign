# Community Library — Experience Contract
## Governing activity

The member comes here to **meet material worth reading**, not to receive another generated interpretation surface.

A Library article may offer a framework, teaching, source, lineage, or platform philosophy.

It does not become a claim about the member merely because it appears on the shelf.

## One voice, many sources

The Library may contain many authors, traditions, and forms of knowledge.

Those sources retain their own authorship.

MAIA may later help a member think with a source, but:

> **the Library does not speak; MAIA does not impersonate the source.**

This preserves both source fidelity and the one-MAIA relationship law.
## Current structural act

`One MAIA, Many Fields` is added to **Philosophical Foundations** as a curated member teaching.

The source markdown remains in Community Commons and is baked into the existing allowlisted production manifest.

No new ranking, recommendation, personalization, automatic surfacing, or member inference is introduced.

The existing shelf remains intentionally small; adding an article requires explicit curation rather than automatic publication.
