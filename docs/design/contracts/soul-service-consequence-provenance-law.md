# SOUL-SERVICE-CONSEQUENCE-PROVENANCE-LAW-01

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing epistemic law

> **What happened, what was observed, what was reported, and what it means are separate layers.**

Returned consequence must have explicit provenance.

## 1. Consequence source types

At minimum distinguish:

### MEMBER_REPORT
What the member says happened.

### DIRECT_SOURCE
An exact artifact such as a message, document, recording, or measured result available under authorization.

### THIRD_PARTY_REPORT
What another person reports, where that report is explicitly available.

### SYSTEM_OBSERVATION
A system-observable fact such as an authorized event timestamp or completed workflow.

### UNKNOWN
The system does not know.

## 2. Observation versus interpretation

Example:

> “They did not reply for seven days.”

may be a direct/system fact.

> “They were angry.”

is an interpretation unless independently established.

The system may compare them.

It may not collapse them.

## 3. Temporal association is not cause

> ## CONSEQUENCE-NONCAUSALITY-LAW-01
>
> **An event occurring after a member action does not by itself establish that the action caused the event.**

The system should preserve:

- confounds;
- alternative explanations;
- missing evidence;
- delayed effects;
- unrelated events.

## 4. Intention versus action

The experiment intended and the action actually taken may differ.

Where relevant distinguish:

- intended action;
- reported actual action;
- verified actual action where available.

Do not evaluate an outcome against an action that did not actually occur.

## 5. Outcome completeness

A consequence may still be unfolding.

The system should support:

> **partial outcome**

without forcing closure.

## 6. Outcome valence

Avoid collapsing outcomes into:

- success;
- failure;
- good;
- bad;

unless the member's explicit criterion supports that classification.

Prefer descriptive language.

## 7. Evidence can remain mixed

A returned consequence may:

- support;
- contradict;
- qualify;
- fail to test;
- remain ambiguous.

These are properties of the evidence relative to a question, not properties of the member.

## Acceptance sentence

> **I can see what actually happened without the system smuggling an explanation into the observation.**
