## Governing intention

> **Living Field must feel like being inside the atmosphere of one's life, not looking at an abstract model of it.**

R4B keeps the accepted perspectival topology and widening behavior, but changes the unit of presentation from generic visual nodes toward lived biographical presence.

## What changed

### 1. Member language becomes primary

For Living Field expressions with an admitted excerpt:

- the stored expression is the largest, most readable content;
- the canonical life dimension becomes secondary context;
- factual last-update date becomes tertiary context;
- authorship / privacy / standing remain visible beneath.

This makes source truth feel biographical without generating a new biography.

### 2. Quiet selection becomes explicit and non-ranked

The quiet view uses the existing factual `updatedAt / createdAt` timestamps to order admitted Living Field expressions.

It shows up to four initially.

The UI explicitly states that this ordering is by last update and is **not a judgment of importance**.

If no valid timestamps exist, the view instead states that the subset is partial and non-ranked.

### 3. MAIA candidates remain visibly subordinate

Member-facing authority language is now ordinary and explicit:

- member-authored → **your words**;
- member-confirmed → **you confirmed this**;
- member-shared → **shared with your practitioner**;
- MAIA candidate → **MAIA noticed this — not yet yours**.

A candidate also retains its unconfirmed standing.

### 4. The field is substantially lighter

The Living Field uses a warm parchment / stone atmosphere with restrained amber, muted green, clay, and fine grain.

There are no orbital rings and no dark cosmic field.

The background changes scale subtly on widen, but its color and motion do not encode psychological meaning.

### 5. Living presences are not cards

The R4B Living Field removes bordered node cards.

Each presence sits directly in the atmospheric field with a very soft local veil only for readability.

The canonical category no longer carries the primary visual weight.

## Widening

Quiet view:

- up to four recently updated admitted Living Field expressions;
- explicit non-ranking explanation;
- one humane continuation: **Looking for something else? See more of my life →**

Widened view:

- all admitted Living Field expressions currently returned by the projection;
- same source truth and authority;
- wider atmospheric background;
- explicit statement that order remains last-update order, not importance;
- **Return to a quieter view**.

Widening changes visibility only.

It does not alter source standing or infer importance.

## Scope discipline

Vision Studio and Practice Field keep their previously witnessed R4 field presentation.

R4B does not apply the warm biographical Living Field atmosphere to those perspectives.

The shared component remains shared, but the new humanization grammar is conditional on `focus === 'living'`.

## Epistemic law enforcement

R4B is bound by `LIVING-FIELD-EPISTEMIC-PERCEPTION-LAW-01`.

A new regression suite mechanically checks that:

- the quiet subset discloses its recency basis;
- the UI refuses importance ranking;
- member expression is foregrounded;
- MAIA candidate standing remains subordinate;
- unsupported meaning is explicitly refused;
- the first item is not visually marked as important merely because it is first.
