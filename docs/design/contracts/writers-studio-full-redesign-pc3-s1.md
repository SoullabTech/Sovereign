# Writer’s Studio — canonical Light Shell (PC3-S1) — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

A writer returning to one Work — *The River Between* — and seeing it at a different altitude without leaving it: the chapter they are in stays present at the left, the Work’s pattern or their manuscript page sits at the centre, and MAIA sits beside both, reflecting rather than instructing. The shell is the house those altitudes share.

## Arrival

> **The Work’s name at the top right, the chapter at the left, MAIA at the right: “This passage brings forward your theme of change and transition.”**

Where am I — in *The River Between*, Chapter 6. What is this place for — seeing the Work more clearly. What is here now — the passage I was in, the pattern it belongs to, MAIA’s reflection. Where can I begin — any theme, any chapter, or a thought for MAIA.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| move between altitudes | Home · Write · Develop · Review | four movements of one Work, never product names |
| choose a pattern | “Select one to explore.” | the writer chooses; nothing is pre-ranked |
| turn to MAIA | “Share your thoughts…” | an invitation, not a command box |
| return to the text | “Open in manuscript”, “Back to Manuscript” | every observation leads back to the Work |

## Forbidden here

- Office / productivity chrome, toolbars as the dominant language, generic copilot sidebar (NV-03)
- MAIA falling beneath the Work at desktop widths (the rejected 2026-09-23 preview)
- readiness, quality or “Balanced” as properties of the Work; relevance ranking (VS-16)
- Explore, Publish, Library or global Search in the primary spine until real (VS-17)
- fixture content presented as live (NV-07) — the review route says “Founder review · fixture data” outside the captured frame

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes by construction against the four founder originals named above: one bar, one type pairing, one set of colour roles, the same MAIA voice in every state.

**Distinct room?** Yes — the Develop, manuscript-in-context and Review states keep their originals’ own proportions (framed page vs Work on the ground, 331 px vs 402 px MAIA) while sharing the shell. Whether it feels like Soullab is for the founder to say at PC3-S1 review.
