# Writer's Studio Doorway — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

This is the public threshold into Writer's Studio. A person may arrive because they
carry a manuscript, a lifetime of knowledge, a voice that lives more easily in speech
than prose, or prior experience of editing that made the work technically cleaner but
less theirs. The doorway helps them decide whether this Studio is a trustworthy place
to bring that work.

It is deliberately **not** the Studio itself. Once the person enters, the manuscript
workspace becomes primary.

## Arrival

> **Write what only you can write.**

The first screen answers four questions without requiring Soullab vocabulary:

- **Where am I?** Writer's Studio, a Soullab doorway.
- **What is it for?** Strengthening meaningful writing without replacing its author.
- **Is it for someone like me?** The page names several legitimate entry conditions.
- **Where can I begin?** Enter Writer's Studio, or return as an existing member.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| Primary arrival | **Enter Writer's Studio** | The person is entering a place, not purchasing a feature bundle. |
| Returning member | **Already a member** | Plain continuity language; no acquisition framing. |
| Orientation | **See how it works** | Offers understanding before commitment. |
| Closing gesture | **Begin in Writer's Studio** | Returns attention to the actual work rather than Soullab as an abstract platform. |

## Audience-specific invitation

Campaigns may speak differently to wisdom carriers, existing authors, voice-first
teachers, or writers protecting authorship. Those invitations must lead into the same
underlying Studio. Audience language is an arrival context, never a permanent persona
assigned to the member.

## Ecological disclosure

The larger Soullab ecology is intentionally below the core Writer's Studio proposition.
The person must be able to want the Studio without understanding AIN, MAIA, or the
whole platform. Cross-room discovery later follows the Constellation referral law:
relevant, invited, non-coercive, and easy to decline.

## Forbidden here

- generic "AI writing tool" framing
- promises that AI will author the person's book
- feature-grid SaaS presentation as the primary invitation
- false urgency, countdowns, scarcity, or conversion pressure
- hidden bundling of unrelated Soullab products
- deterministic claims about what kind of writer the visitor is
- treating the founding case study as evidence before its results are actually witnessed

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. The doorway uses Soullab's quiet hierarchy, relational language,
shared identity path, and sovereignty stance. MAIA is present in the product philosophy
without becoming an animated salesperson.

**Distinct room?** Yes. Even with every Soullab label removed, the page is unmistakably
about bringing substantial writing into form while preserving authorship. It does not
read like Astrology, Journal, Relationships, or generic Home.
