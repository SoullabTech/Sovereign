# Writer’s Studio — Free + Pro Orientation

The plan surface answers one practical question without interrupting the Work: **what belongs to every writer, and what deeper rooms are included in Pro?**

Free is not a teaser. It keeps author-owned writing, foundational Develop and Review, foundational MAIA, author-controlled change, essential ownership, privacy and Academy foundations. Pro adds depth, continuity, multiple Works, expanded MAIA, Listen and Publish preparation.

During the invitation-only beta, an authenticated server-side pilot record gives approved writers the currently approved Pro experience at no charge. The plan surface is informative only. It never reads a query toggle or browser storage, never grants admission, and never changes an operation held by a separate security, custody, recovery or release decision.

The plan comparison is deliberately a calm reading page rather than a checkout funnel. It gives Kelly and Andrea a truthful Thursday demonstration of the complete product definition while preserving the difference between **demonstrable product experience** and **commercial or production release**.
