# Writer's Studio Free and Pro — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

A writer wonders what more is possible. This page tells them plainly: Free is the whole Studio on one Work, Pro adds
more Works and the whole-book editorial pass, MAIA is the same MAIA on every plan, and their writing is theirs whatever
they choose. If they want Pro, they ask, and a person answers.

## Arrival

> **Your voice belongs to you.**

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| Understand Free | "The whole Studio, on one Work." | Free is real and complete (ruling 2026-11-02) |
| Understand Pro | "More Works, and the whole-book editorial pass." | Capacity and tools only; nothing that does not exist |
| The taste | "Your first whole-book editorial pass is on us." | One real gift, no timer, nothing taken away (ruling 2026-11-13) |
| MAIA | "the same MAIA" / "Pro adds room and tools, never a different companion." | Sanctuary economy: no one gets a lesser MAIA |
| Ask | "Pro isn't for sale yet. If you'd like it, ask — we answer each request personally. Nothing changes until you hear from us." · "Ask about Pro" | No price for a purchase that cannot be made; a person answers |
| After asking | "Thank you for asking about Pro. We answer each request personally." | Confirms; promises nothing; no follow-up sequence |
| Beta writer | "Pro is included in your beta until …. When it ends, every Work stays yours, and you keep writing on Free." | The #1800 rulings, said plainly |
| In the moment (ProMention) | the refusal message, then "About Pro · Not now" | Mentioned only where Pro was met; "Not now" is remembered |

## Deliberately excluded

- A price, a checkout, or a "proposed" price (no purchase can be made yet).
- Any timer, countdown, trial or expiry pressure.
- "Expanded" or better MAIA as a Pro benefit.
- Listing Publish, Listen or advanced Academy before they exist.
- Pro mentions on arrival, in banners, by email, or raised by MAIA.
- Diminishing authorship, export, privacy or deletion on Free.
