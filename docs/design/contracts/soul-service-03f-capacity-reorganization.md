## Purpose

03F tests whether a pattern can leave current use while the capacities practiced around it remain available to the member without covertly reactivating the pattern.

## Witness pattern

Historical pattern:

> **Some chapter transitions create continuity friction even when the core idea is clear.**

Pattern standing in the core witness:

> **RETIRED_FROM_CURRENT_USE**

Historical evidence remains inspectable.

Prospective influence ends.

## Capacity reorganization

Before retirement:

> pattern-specific scaffold

Examples:

- Check transitions for continuity friction.
- Look for another example of this pattern.

After retirement:

> general optional capacities

Examples:

- Inspect continuity
- Compare versions
- Look for counterevidence
- Change scale
- Leave unresolved

The member chooses whether to invoke them.

## Non-resurrection rule

Using:

> **Inspect continuity**

must not automatically:

- restore the retired pattern;
- search for chapter-transition friction;
- label evidence as support for the old proposition;
- change the pattern's applicability state.

The capacity can be used without reviving the pattern.

## Acceptance questions

1. Can the pattern be retired while history remains inspectable?
2. Do general capacities remain available after retirement?
3. Are those capacities member-initiated rather than automatically triggered?
4. Does invoking a capacity leave the retired pattern retired?
5. Can continuity be inspected without searching for the old proposition?
6. Can counterevidence be used without reconfirmation pressure?
7. Is independent use treated as success rather than disengagement?
8. Can the member choose no tool at all?
9. Does the UI distinguish historical pattern from present capacity?
10. Does the system stop organizing attention around the retired pattern?
