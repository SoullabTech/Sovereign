# Member World — Experience Contract

## Purpose

Member World is the place where a person can regain the whole without collapsing the distinct rooms of their life into one system.

The room answers:

> **What am I holding across my House?**

It does not answer:

> What should matter most?

or:

> What does the system believe about me?
## Member-relative identity

The visible room name is personal:

- Kelly’s World
- Jondi’s World
- Andrea’s World
- or **Your World** when no reliable first name is available.

The route remains /world; member identity never enters the URL.

## House / World distinction

The House is the member's architectural home and the place of return.

World is one room within that ecology.

World may gather pointers to member-owned work and existing House fields, but it does not become the owner of those rooms and does not manufacture relationship between them.

## Founder/internal boundary

The shared World pattern may be available to every authenticated member.
Founder-only material is a separate projection and must never leak into the general member room merely because a founder can see it elsewhere.

Excluded from the member World by default:

- repository programme records;
- canonical JARVIS Work Units;
- deployment / production evidence;
- founder adjudication dockets;
- cross-member context;
- private internal governance state.

## Orientation law

> **Seeing the whole must not become a new authority over the parts.**
