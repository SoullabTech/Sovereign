# LIVED-EVIDENCE-ACCUMULATION-LAW-01
## How repeated lived consequence may mature without becoming identity

**Date:** 29 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **Repeated lived evidence may increase the plausibility of a pattern, but recurrence alone does not authorize generalization, identity, causation, or permanence.**

The system must distinguish:

- one event;
- repeated events;
- independent observations;
- shared-context repeats;
- counterexamples;
- contextual differences;
- source quality;
- unresolved cases.

A repeated event does not become a trait merely because it recurs.

## Event integrity

Each consequence remains a separate evidence object.

Aggregation must not erase:

- source;
- date;
- context;
- standing;
- what was expected;
- what actually happened;
- counterevidence;
- uncertainty.

> **The pattern never gets to consume the events that produced it.**

## Pattern maturity states

These are states of an evidence proposition, not states of a person.

### SINGLE_OBSERVATION

One relevant event.

No recurrence claim is warranted.

### RECURRENCE_CANDIDATE

More than one event bears on the same proposition.

The recurrence may still reflect one narrow context or shared conditions.

### CONTESTED_RECURRENCE

Supporting and contradicting evidence both exist.

The proposition must remain open to weakening, narrowing, splitting, or abandonment.

### CONTEXT_BOUNDED_RECURRENCE

A recurrence appears to hold within a clearly specified context but not outside it.

Example:

> readers lose the thread at chapter transitions

is not equivalent to:

> readers do not understand the book.

### PROVISIONAL_PATTERN

A proposition has repeated, relevant, sufficiently differentiated support, surviving known counterevidence, and remains explicitly provisional.

This state does not create autobiographical truth by itself.

### UNRESOLVED

The available evidence does not support a responsible broader proposition.

Unresolved is not a failed state.

## No count threshold

> ## PATTERN-NO-MAGIC-N-LAW-01
>
> **No fixed event count may automatically promote lived evidence into a pattern.**

Three events in one narrow setting may be less informative than two independent observations under different relevant conditions.

## Context separation

> ## LIVED-EVIDENCE-CONTEXT-SEPARATION-LAW-01
>
> **Events from materially different contexts must not be pooled merely because they share surface vocabulary.**

Examples:

- a chapter being unclear;
- a collaborator disagreeing;
- a relationship conflict;

cannot be collapsed into:

> “people do not understand me”

without additional warranted evidence.

## Counterevidence preservation

> ## LIVED-PATTERN-COUNTEREVIDENCE-LAW-01
>
> **A pattern view must preserve material counterexamples and show how they limit the proposition.**

Counterexamples may:

- weaken;
- narrow;
- split;
- contextualize;
- suspend;
- overturn.

They may not be displayed merely as decorative exceptions.

## Independence

Repeated observations from the same source, same event, or same tightly coupled context should not masquerade as independent confirmation.

The system may distinguish:

- repeated report of one event;
- multiple observations in one context;
- independent observations;
- cross-context recurrence.

## Member authority

MAIA may propose:

> **possible recurrence**

or:

> **this evidence may support a narrower pattern**

but a broader autobiographical pattern gains stronger personal standing only through explicit member confirmation or another governed source of authority.

## Identity firewall

> ## PATTERN-TO-IDENTITY-FIREWALL-LAW-01
>
> **No lived-evidence pattern may become a trait, identity, diagnosis, or enduring description of the member without separately authorized evidence and member standing.**

Pattern:

> “Three readers lost the thread at chapter transitions.”

Identity claim:

> “You are unclear.”

These are not equivalent.

## Time and aging

Old evidence remains historical evidence.

Its present relevance must be reconsidered when:

- the source changed;
- the context changed;
- the member changed;
- the surrounding system changed;
- counterevidence accumulated.

## Governing sentence

> **Evidence may accumulate. Meaning must remain proportionate to what actually accumulated.**

## Executable evidence-structure derivation — 2026-09-30

The first 03C witness was strengthened so proposition maturity is not selected by sequence position or total event count.

The pure derivation now considers:

- whether broad support exists;
- whether broad contradiction exists;
- whether narrower support comes from independent observations;
- whether narrower support spans materially different context groups;
- whether counterevidence remains.

This produces an explicit implementation of:

> **PATTERN-NO-MAGIC-N-LAW-01**

Witnessed falsifiers include:

- five independent events in one context remain **CONTEXT_BOUNDED_RECURRENCE**;
- fewer total events with independent cross-context support may reach **PROVISIONAL_PATTERN**;
- repeated observations tied to one independence key remain **RECURRENCE_CANDIDATE** rather than masquerading as independent recurrence.

> **Evidence structure, not event count, governs maturity.**
