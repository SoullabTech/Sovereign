# Public Discovery Threshold — Experience Contract

## Human activity and the first moment

Someone hears about Writer's Studio, Pro Studio, Astrology Pro, MAIA,
Sound Resonance, Elemental Academy or AIN OS without necessarily knowing
anything else about Soullab. They need to learn what this particular
offering means, whether it is available, and why it might matter to their
life or service. They must be free to leave, return, or choose another
door without penalty or forced cross-product enrollment.

**Arrival:** "Enter through what matters to you."

It is the Soullab environment viewed from its thresholds, not a second
authenticated Home or a privileged navigation layer.

## The gestures

| Gesture | Member-facing language | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Choose one experience | "Discover this doorway" | Open a dedicated, honest explanation without making a privacy-sensitive request |
| Consider other experiences | "Compare the doorways" | Preserve differentiation and voluntary exploration |
| Return to shared Soullab | "Explore Soullab Home" | Respect the canonical shared member arrival point |
| Return with an account | "Sign in" | Use existing trusted authentication rather than an invented beta grant |

## House and Room differentiation

The House is oriented to a person's lived continuity, private Work,
relationships, Journal, and MAIA encounter. This discovery page is public,
static and unfamiliar-user-safe. It cannot read a member identity or
act as evidence of permission to use private rooms.

The seven outward cards express seven **places and audiences**, not a
dashboard of files, clinical cases, saved sources, or inferred member
traits. The architecture does not decide which door the person "should"
take. The existence of one door does not create a membership obligation
in any other.

## What is deliberately forbidden

- Separate studio marketing paths that silently enter private editors
  or chart-processing APIs.
- Unqualified "Live", "Available", "Open source", "Certified" or
  "Clinical" capability claims.
- Forms, payments, contact harvesting, unreviewed signup permissions,
  file imports, prompt/model calls or tracking.
- A new primary CTA overriding public "Enter Soullab → /home".
- Studio previews that request actual confidential manuscripts or
  practitioner information.
- Presenting static beta evidence as deployed product availability.

## Visual check and scope of proof

Full-page WebKit screenshots are stored in the two referenced files.
At desktop, the large quiet invitation precedes a two-column constellation;
at 390px it becomes one readable column. The palette and typography
reference the existing Soullab Home but the page is distinct in its

The local runtime (actual Next.js, loopback only) checked all seven
explicit public pages, unsupported slugs, existing protected redirects,
and anonymous Studio API refusal. It did not make real-account
requests, publish to production, enable search indexing, prove private
storage/backup release, or constitute physical iPhone approval.
