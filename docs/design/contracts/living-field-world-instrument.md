# Living Field — World Instrument — Experience Contract
## What this room is for

A place to meet what is alive in one's world as a field rather than as a list of problems or objects. The member can begin with a question, feeling, change, or possibility; see elemental openings around the whole; move further into a pathway; widen again; or stop and linger. The instrument offers orientation and language for attention. It does not tell the member what the field means.

## Arrival

> **Every journey begins with a doorway.**

The member is invited to name what is stirring, changing, or calling, but may also enter with an open exploration. On entry the whole field appears around the question, “What is calling from the field?”

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| enter | “Step into the field →” | frames entry as movement into lived experience, not creation of a record |
| choose a region | Fire · Water · Earth · Air · Aether | offers distinct ways of attending without assigning a type or score |
| follow inward | child regions such as Creation, Grief, Practice, Reflection, Relation | lets attention become more particular without turning the path into a diagnosis |
| widen | “← Wider” | preserves reversibility and keeps the whole field available |
| pause at a leaf | “This is also a place to linger.” | makes non-progression legitimate; the instrument need not drive completion |
| leave | “Close” | ends the session-local exploration without claiming resolution |

## Forbidden here

- dashboard grid or metric tiles
- scores, levels, progress judgments, or elemental typing of the member
- MAIA-authored conclusions presented as the member's own meaning
- treating a path through the field as a diagnosis, prescription, or completed interpretation
- forcing forward movement when the member may widen, close, or linger
## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. It uses the House's dark field hierarchy, restrained typography and accent, quiet provenance language, and the same basic authority relation: the member's world remains primary and the system stays in service to orientation.

**Distinct room?** Yes. The House presents thresholds and circulation across the whole Soullab world; Writer's Studio presents a manuscript surface; this instrument presents the member's lived world as a navigable field. Its defining gesture is spatial attention — entering, following inward, widening, and lingering — rather than choosing a product object or completing a workflow.
