# PATTERN-CAPACITY-DECOUPLING-LAW-01
## Pattern influence can fade while human capacity remains

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **A human capacity strengthened while working with a pattern must not remain dependent on that pattern's continued authority.**

A pattern is a proposition about a context.

A capacity is an ability of the member.

They must not be conflated.

## Example

Historical provisional pattern:

> **Some chapter transitions create continuity friction even when the core idea is clear.**

Capacities exercised while working with it may include:

- noticing continuity;
- comparing versions;
- seeking counterevidence;
- shifting scale;
- distinguishing source from interpretation;
- holding uncertainty.

If the pattern becomes dormant or retired, the capacities do not become dormant or retired with it.

## Capacity ownership

> ## HUMAN-CAPACITY-OWNERSHIP-LAW-01
>
> **A capacity practiced with Soullab remains the member's capacity. The system must not represent it as belonging to MAIA, a pattern, a prompt sequence, or a product feature.**

The pattern may have scaffolded practice.

The capacity belongs to the human.

## Reorganization

When pattern influence fades, support may reorganize from:

> **pattern-specific prompting**

toward:

> **general, optional, member-initiated capacities**

Example:

Pattern-specific:

> “Check these chapter transitions for the continuity-friction pattern.”

Generalized capacity:

> “Inspect continuity.”

The second must not secretly mean:

> “Find the old pattern again.”

## No ghost scaffold

> ## PATTERN-GHOST-SCAFFOLD-LAW-01
>
> **A dormant or retired pattern must not continue directing attention through renamed prompts, hidden ranking, default highlights, or model-selected examples.**

Retirement is not real if the system still keeps steering the member toward the same proposition.

## Capacity availability

After pattern dormancy / retirement, the member may still choose general capacities such as:

- Compare versions
- Inspect continuity
- Look for counterevidence
- Change scale
- Hold unresolved
- Ask another question

These should be available because they are useful cognitive movements, not because the retired pattern is covertly active.

## De-scaffolding

Pattern-specific scaffolds should become lighter as the member can perform the broader capacity independently.

Desired movement:

> **pattern-guided → capacity-named → member-initiated → independent**

The system may remain available.

Dependency should not be preserved for engagement.

## Historical inspectability

Retiring the pattern does not delete:

- the original proposition;
- supporting evidence;
- counterevidence;
- provenance;
- source version;
- why it once had standing.

Historical truth remains inspectable.

Prospective guidance ends.

## Failure conditions

03F fails if:

- retiring a pattern removes access to the broader learned capacity;
- the old pattern remains the hidden default interpretation;
- general capacity prompts are merely renamed pattern prompts;
- the system keeps selecting evidence to reconfirm the retired proposition;
- the member must keep the pattern active to use a cognitive tool;
- independent capacity is treated as reduced product engagement.

## Governing sentence

> **Patterns may organize learning for a time. Capacities should increasingly belong to the person.**
