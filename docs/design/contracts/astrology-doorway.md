# Astrology Doorway — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

This is the public threshold for someone who is interested in astrology but may
know nothing about Soullab. It should be possible to want this experience because
the astrology itself is compelling.

The doorway does not require prior belief in astrology. It states the epistemic
posture clearly: calculated chart facts are one thing; symbolic interpretation is
another; lived meaning remains the person's.

## Arrival

> **Your chart is not a verdict.**

This line establishes the boundary before making the invitation.

A visitor should understand that the experience offers:

- accurate chart calculation,
- symbolic tradition,
- whole-chart synthesis,
- possible human expression,
- present activation,
- space for the member's own meaning.

Those dimensions may inform one another without being collapsed.

## Audience-specific invitation

Different campaigns may address whole-chart seekers, people wanting non-deterministic
symbolic inquiry, serious learners, or practitioners. These are entry contexts, not
identity labels. They all enter the same Astrology room.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Enter Astrology** | Cross into the actual experience. |
| Orientation | **See the approach** | Understand the epistemic posture before joining. |
| Returning member | **Already a member** | Continue through existing Soullab identity. |
| Closing | **Begin with your chart** | Return attention to the chart rather than the platform. |

## Forbidden here

- horoscope-style certainty presented as personal knowledge
- deterministic future claims
- personality scoring
- implying that a chart proves motives, pathology, destiny, or character
- blurring calculated astronomy with symbolic interpretation
- making MAIA the authority over what a symbol means in the member's life
- using the wider Soullab ecosystem as a product bundle pitch

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. The person retains authority, claims remain bounded, and the
doorway leads into shared Soullab identity rather than a disconnected product.

**Distinct room?** Yes. The visual field, language, and conceptual structure are
unmistakably astrological and would not be confused with Writer's Studio.
