# Ganesha Personal Field — G2-3D Experience Contract

## What this room is for

A member comes here to encounter their own life as they have chosen to name it.
They can see current authored Notes and Attention, earlier Personal Studio
writing retained in its original custody, and people and events they have
personally included. No AI determines which thing is significant, who deserves
attention, or what an experience means. The eventual doorway will be through
Ganesha Life Flow, not through professional administration.

## Arrival

> Your living field.

The member immediately knows this is personal, not a practitioner CRM. The
line beneath it states: "A place for what you have named, chosen, and are
tending. Nothing here is ranked for you." The read-only and incomplete-history
status is truthful: bounded source-specific reads do not prove the absence of
older or quarantined records. No payment prompt or clinical diagnosis appears.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language / current state | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Your living field | A human place, not a dashboard title |
| Revisit Attention | What matters to you | Refers to member curation, not system scoring |
| Return to earlier material | From earlier Personal Studio writing | Preserves personal history without relabeling its owner |
| Notice an authored note | Words you've placed | Authorship stays with the individual |
| Look at time and people | Events you've named / People you're tending | These are member-declared, never inferred |
| Edit, withdraw, cross into Pro | Not present in this preview | R20 deletion, member authoring release and Relationship Space acceptance remain held |

## Evidence-based responsive design

The early static WebKit image showed a contrast failure in the lower mobile
section, despite a successful width-only test. That evidence led to an explicit
light background on the preview root and another screenshot + color check.
The preview uses a single mobile column and a two-column layout when wide.
Labels are legible and neutral; the four questions are available without
a decorative score or a forced interpretation.

## Forbidden here

- Equating historical Personal Studio rows with professional authorization
- Combining two authored Attention orderings into an invented priority ranking
- Inferred contacts, significance, developmental stage or client details
- A false Today based on server-local timezone
- Duplicate or guessed owner references, silent conflict resolution or copying data
- A DELETE button without signed, independently durable deletion custody
- Treating a static fictional screenshot as a shipped or signed-in beta test
- Navigation from House, billing activation, or release without separate authorization
