# Living Field — Grokker R1 Experience Contract

## What this room is for

The Grokker instrument gives a member a spatial way to enter a question, feeling,
change, or possibility and notice how the field opens. R1 is deliberately small:
WORLD, one inward movement, and a faithful return. It adds no durable meaning.

## Arrival

> **Every journey begins with a doorway.**

The member may name what is stirring, changing, or calling, then step into the
five-region elemental WORLD. Leaving the prompt blank remains a valid open
exploration.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| enter | Step into the field | crossing rather than form submission |
| inward | select a region | attention chooses the movement |
| outward | Wider | returns toward context without implying undo |
| leave | Close | ends the local visit without persisting it |

## Forbidden here

- inferred psychological edges presented as fact
- silent persistence, memory write, or relationship learning
- WEB/FLOW/TEXT depth before their own gates
- a dashboard or graph-editor interpretation of the field
- MAIA speaking for the member before the field has been encountered

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. The threshold, quiet hierarchy, provenance restraint,
member agency, and return behavior are continuous with the House and existing
Living Field.

**Distinct room?** Yes. The member is spatially inside a field and can move
inward and wider; that embodied traversal is particular to Living Field.
