# Living Field — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

Living Field is a place for entering what is stirring in a life and following it through a wider landscape of questions, relationships, elemental processes, patterns, and possibilities.

The experience should feel closer to beginning a journey than beginning a task. The field offers places to enter, paths to follow, and room to linger. Meaning develops through the person's encounter with the field.

## Arrival

> **Every journey begins with a doorway.**

> A question, feeling, change, or possibility can be the entrance. Follow what feels alive and see where the field opens.

Primary gesture:

> **Step into the field →**

The first language belongs to the journey itself. System architecture, data models, and AI identity stay backstage.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| enter | Step into the field | feels like crossing a threshold |
| deepen | select a visible region | follows curiosity rather than task sequence |
| free zoom | trackpad / wheel / pinch | approaches or withdraws without committing to a new semantic focus |
| pan | drag open field | changes viewpoint without changing the field's meaning |
| widen | Wider | restores one containing semantic level |
| whole | Whole | restores the full-field orientation in one action |
| path | Path | reveals the route through meaning |
| dwell | This is also a place to linger | legitimizes stillness |
| close | Close | simple exit without drama |
## Room character

The Living Field should feel:

- invitational;
- spacious;
- quietly alive;
- soulful without becoming esoteric decoration;
- exploratory rather than evaluative;
- coherent enough to learn spatially;
- capable of stillness.

Member-facing language prefers positive description of what is present, becoming, opening, gathering, or available.

The interface speaks about the journey, the field, and what is coming into view. It minimizes system-centered explanation.

## Forbidden here

- assessment-form tone;
- profile language;
- AI-forward framing as the main experience;
- card-grid behavior masquerading as a field;
- mandatory next steps;
- global labels applied to the person;
- visual motion without semantic cause;
- gamified discovery;
- decorative cosmic spectacle;
- a dashboard that asks the person to manage their psyche.

## House / Room split

**Same house?** Yes. The room uses the House's restraint, warmth, return orientation, typography hierarchy, and low-noise amber emphasis.

**Distinct room?** Yes. Living Field introduces recursive spatial exploration: regions can be entered, nested meaning becomes visible, and widening returns the traveler toward a larger context.
