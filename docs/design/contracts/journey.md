# Journey — Experience Contract

**Scope is deliberately narrow.** This contract claims exactly one surface,
`app/journey/page.tsx`, because that is the surface actually touched. The
sub-surfaces and components reached from this page are **not** claimed here.
Coverage grows when those surfaces are themselves touched — not by proximity.

**Coverage is not retrospective approval.** Introducing this contract does not
ratify the current Journey page as an approved experiential reference. It
establishes which room this is and what it may not become. The page has never
been walked against an approved reference; that remains owed, and is not
discharged by this file.

## What this room is for

A person orienting inside their own unfolding process. They begin from what is
alive for them right now, find where that sits within a larger field of
development, and decide how they want to move with it. The map is an aid to
movement and orientation — it is material the member thinks with, not a reading
of who they are.

This is what separates Journey from Astrology. Astrology begins from a symbolic
celestial map of the member's chart and offers interpretive lenses. Journey
begins from present lived experience. The two rooms may share visual language;
they do not share a starting point.

## Naming

These three are distinct and must not be collapsed into one another:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Room name** | Journey — the constitutional identifier |
| **Current heading** | "Alchemical Journey" — an experiential expression, not the identifier |
| **Threshold language** | "The Field" — threshold language, *not* the room name |

## Arrival

> **What is alive in you right now?**

The member is met with a question about their present experience before any map
is shown. The map is offered second, as a choice — "Open the field map" — not as
the default frame through which they must arrive.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| Enter reflective practice | "Enter Inner Guide Meditation" | Names the practice plainly; no promise about outcome |
| Reveal the developmental map | "Open the field map" | An action the member takes, not a claim about what they will find |

## Forbidden here

- treating the developmental map as an objective measurement of the person
- system-generated ranking presented as authority over the member
- converting a present-lived inquiry into diagnosis or fixed identity
- MAIA prescribing what stage, element, or developmental movement the member "is"
- presenting the map as more authoritative than the person's own experience

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes — quiet field hierarchy, serif display voice, human-language
gestures, and the same restraint about interpretation that Astrology and Journal
hold. Nothing here reads as a dashboard or an analytics surface.

**Distinct room?** Yes. A member arriving here is asked what is alive in them
now; a member arriving in Astrology is shown a chart. Journey's map is entered
by choice from a present-tense question, which is the boundary between the two
rooms rather than a difference of styling.
