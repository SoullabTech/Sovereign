# House — Experience Contract

## Purpose

The House is where a member arrives to see the larger ecology without being asked to understand the system beneath it.

The experience is governed by one sentence:

> **Many paths. A deeper you.**

## MAIA threshold

MAIA is visible as a place the member may enter. The House does not impersonate MAIA, open a microphone, or start a conversation merely because the threshold is visible.

## Passing Through

Passing Through is one quiet item per arrival. It is neither recommendation engine nor engagement feed. Hiding it is visit-local.

## Access truth

A room may be discoverable before a member has access to its underlying product surface. Discoverability must not manufacture access. Any access notice names the actual next step without creating a second account or duplicating a Studio.
