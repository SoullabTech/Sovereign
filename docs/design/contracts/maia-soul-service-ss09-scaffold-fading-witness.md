## Purpose

SS-09 tests the difference between:

- fadeable developmental scaffolds;
- permanent epistemic safeguards.

The same source is shown at every level.

## Scaffold states

### S0 — Full support
MAIA names and structures the capacities.

### S1 — Guided participation
MAIA asks; member performs.

### S2 — Light scaffold
One optional cue remains.

### S3 — Self-initiated
No prompt sequence; member may choose tools.

### S4 — Solo
No generated scaffold; MAIA only by explicit invitation.

These are scaffold states, not member levels.

## Permanent safeguards

The following remain visible at every state:

- source fidelity;
- authorship distinction;
- uncertainty;
- rejection rights;
- reality anchoring.

> **We fade scaffolds, not safeguards.**

## Acceptance questions

1. Is each support level materially lighter than the preceding one?
2. Does the task/source remain stable across levels?
3. Are safeguards unchanged?
4. Does S3 shift initiation to the member?
5. Does S4 remain useful rather than empty?
6. Can support later be restored without implying regression?
7. Could the system eventually choose a lower scaffold based on research evidence without ranking the member?
8. Is reduced MAIA presence clearly different from reduced protection?
9. Does this embody planned obsolescence?
10. Does the design avoid optimizing for dependency?
