# CHANGES-UX — Integrated Founder-Witness Candidate

## Purpose

This witness asks a different question from the individual implementation acts.

Not:

> Does each control work?

But:

> **Does this now feel like one human encounter with a Change rather than a collection of software capabilities?**

The candidate was walked in the order a member might actually live it.

## Integrated walk

### 1. Return to the Change

The room opens on the member-authored Change.

The Change, not status or I Ching, is the primary material object.

### 2. Notice what happened

The member writes the lived event before classification appears.

The first witness moment was kept successfully as a reflection.

### 3. Consult the I Ching

The member explicitly entered the symbolic practice.

Yarrow Stalks was chosen.

The existing cast route persisted the result and returned the member to the same Change.

The cast became a held symbolic lens rather than a new destination.

### 4. Notice again

A second lived moment was kept without forcing a more specific classification.

The Change therefore accumulated actual temporal material before Pattern was opened.

### 5. See what has been recurring

The temporal field rendered only the member's kept moments and mechanically observable recurrence.

No model ran.

No Change data mutated.

### 6. Ask MAIA what she notices

The member explicitly moved from evidence into hypothesis.

Exactly one context handoff was sent into the stable Living Change conversation.

The handoff carried the evidence already visible in Pattern and asked for one tentative hypothesis, not an answer.

### 7. Make meaning

The member returned from MAIA to the Change and Pattern.

They authored their own meaning in a separate field.

That exact text was persisted as a member reflection.

MAIA's language was not copied into the durable Change.

### 8. Return and reopen MAIA

The member returned to the Change, reopened MAIA, and the room produced:

**0 unsolicited conversation POSTs**

The existing Living Change conversation remained the same stable session.

## Final durable state

At the end of the walk, before cleanup, the temporary Change contained:

- one original Change object;
- one persisted Yarrow I Ching cast;
- three member-authored experiences:
  - first noticed reflection;
  - second noticed moment;
  - member-authored meaning reflection;
- one continuing Living Change MAIA conversation identity.

It did not contain:

- a stored MAIA hypothesis;
- a Council conclusion;
- an auto-generated pattern meaning;
- an automatic status transition;
- a new interpretive-memory object.

## Integrated experiential finding

The architecture now has a clear direction of authority:

```
LIVED CHANGE
    ↓
WHAT HAPPENED
    ↓
SYMBOLIC / RELATIONAL LENSES MAY BE INVITED
    ↓
OBSERVABLE RECURRENCE
    ↓
MAIA MAY OFFER A POSSIBILITY
    ↓
THE MEMBER MAKES MEANING
    ↓
ONLY THE MEMBER'S CHOSEN WORDS RETURN TO THE CHANGE
```

This is the central achievement of the redesign.

The room can become intelligent without becoming authoritative.

## Responsive finding and repair

The integrated witness also caught a real state-composition defect.

During Pattern:

- Pattern already rendered its own **Return to the Change** gesture;
- the right MAIA membrane, because MAIA was closed, also rendered a second return gesture.

The duplicated control represented two competing ownership claims over the same state.

The repair is now:

- normal Change → membrane may offer **Explore with MAIA**;
- MAIA open → membrane may offer **Return to the Change**;
- Pattern open → membrane stays quiet; Pattern owns its return.

This preserves the rule:

> **one foreground gesture per attentional state.**

## What the room is now

The candidate is no longer:

- a bottom sheet;
- an I Ching form;
- a vertical stack of AI features;
- an experience timeline with utilities attached.

It is now one room with reversible depth:

```
Living Change
   ├── Notice
   ├── Consult I Ching
   ├── Explore with MAIA
   └── Widen across time
          ├── Observable recurrence
          ├── Optional MAIA hypothesis
          └── Member meaning
```

Every branch returns to the same Change.

## What still deserves founder eyes

The integrated walk proves behavior and architecture, but visual adjudication should still pay particular attention to:

1. whether the central Change object has enough visual authority;
2. whether the action rail feels invitational rather than tool-like;
3. whether the temporal field feels alive rather than analytical;
4. whether Pattern becomes too intellectually dense;
5. whether MAIA's two-field encounter feels relational rather than split-screen software;
6. whether mobile has enough breathing room after the fields stack;
7. whether any explanatory boundary copy can now become quieter because the architecture itself is carrying the meaning.

Those are matters of experience, not correctness.
