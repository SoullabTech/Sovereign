# Living Constellation Contract v0.1 — CANDIDATE

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Status:** Candidate UX constitution — not ratified
**Evidence base:** repository census at `bfab0c3c7cb02a924fe517a98471d95a895f01e4`
**Scope:** Living Field · Vision Studio · Practice Field · shared visual/relational experience
**Effect:** design constraint only. This document grants no runtime, schema, migration, AI, persistence, sharing, deployment, or production authority.

---

## 0. Why this contract exists

Living Field, Vision Studio, and Practice Field currently appear beside one another but do not yet behave as one intelligible developmental ecology.

A member can enter each room and use its controls without being shown:

- what the room is for;
- what becomes possible through repeated use;
- how it relates to the member's wider life;
- how it relates to the other two rooms;
- what MAIA may propose versus what only the member may author;
- what moves between rooms, and by whose authority.

The result is three useful surfaces that feel more like neighboring utilities than three expressions of a coherent Soullab environment.

This contract defines the shared experience before any redesign begins.

The governing idea is:

> **One member. Distinct constitutional centers. One visible constellation of relationships between them.**

The constellation is a way of seeing and navigating the member's world. It is **not** a new database that absorbs Living Field, Vision Studio, Practice Field, memory, relationships, sessions, or projects into one undifferentiated object.

---

## 1. Constitutional placement: projection, not collapse

Existing canon already distinguishes different centers and different authority layers.

The Living Constellation therefore MUST be implemented conceptually as a **projection/read model over distinct source objects**, not as a new universal persistence substrate.

It may show that two things are in relationship.

It may not make them the same thing.

It may show one object in several rooms.

It may not copy that object into each room and create competing truths.

It may allow a member to bring something into relationship with another domain.

It may not silently promote, reinterpret, or relocate it.

### Governing law

> **Each underlying object keeps its own identity, provenance, authority, lifecycle, and jurisdiction. The Living Constellation renders relationships among those objects without collapsing them.**

This extends the already-ratified Field Object → Project Reference → Placement distinction:

- **source object** — the thing itself;
- **reference/relation** — the member's act that this thing matters in another context;
- **placement** — where and how that relationship is represented on a surface.

Moving a visual node is therefore not automatically a semantic act.

Removing a visual placement is not deletion.

Creating a relationship is a separate member-authored gesture.

---

## 2. The member is the center of orientation, not a graph object to be possessed

The constellation may place **YOU** at its visual center.

That center is a point of orientation, not a claim that the system contains a complete object called the member.

The system possesses representations, records, references, and authored material.

The member remains the living subject from whose perspective these acquire meaning.

### Invariant

> **No representation of the member is equivalent to the member.**

The constellation must never imply that its visible shape is a complete portrait, diagnosis, profile, or model of who the member is.

Absence is allowed.

Contradiction is allowed.

Uncertainty is allowed.

Unrelated things are allowed to remain unrelated.

---

## 3. The three rooms retain distinct meanings

The census shows that these rooms already have stronger and more specific identities than a generic sequence of self-reflection → goals → habits.

The redesign should recover those meanings rather than overwrite them.

### Living Field — the member's life-level field

Primary human question:

> **What is alive, recognized, or asking for attention in my life?**

Living Field belongs to the member.

It is the place where member-authored expressions, recognitions, questions, relationships, states, transitions, sources, and developmental history can become visible without being turned into a fixed profile.

Its existing canonical role remains intact: the current best **member-authored** expression of accumulated recognition, always revisable.

Living Field can include present aliveness, but source material does not become higher-order meaning merely because it is visible nearby.

### Vision Studio — the member's developing body of work

Primary human question:

> **What is my work becoming?**

The current substrate and legacy specification make Vision Studio a developmental environment for practitioners cultivating a body of work over time.

It is not merely a future-visualization tool.

It helps experience, questions, stories, practice, and emerging ideas become more coherent through reflection and recognition.

MAIA may facilitate, reflect, and propose threads.

The member authors what is carried.

Vision Studio should make clearer that the practitioner does not begin empty and does not need to perform finished authority.

### Practice Field — the practitioner's work in relationship

Primary human question:

> **How does my practice meet the people I serve?**

The existing Practice Field is a practitioner-authored relational ecology, not a generic habit tracker or personal experiment log.

It already holds such things as:

- welcome and identity;
- how practitioner and client work together;
- how MAIA participates;
- professional declarations;
- resources;
- current practitioner emphasis;
- bounded MAIA guidance;
- client invitation and formation state.

That meaning should be preserved.

The redesign may make Practice Field feel more alive and developmental, but it must not silently redefine the existing constitutional object into a general-purpose personal-practice system.

---

## 4. The shared developmental movement

The three rooms can feel additive without becoming a linear funnel.

A useful experiential movement is:

> **LIVE → RECOGNIZE → DEVELOP → OFFER → ENCOUNTER → RETURN**

For example:

1. Something happens in life or work.
2. The member reflects and recognizes what matters.
3. A recognition may become part of Living Field.
4. The member may deliberately bring it into Vision Studio as relevant to the work they are developing.
5. Through Vision Studio, a practice principle, question, method, offering, or orientation may mature.
6. The practitioner may deliberately author part of that into Practice Field, where it shapes the relational conditions offered to clients.
7. Real encounters then generate new lived experience.
8. That experience returns to reflection; nothing automatically writes itself upward into identity or doctrine.

### Important

This is a **developmental ecology**, not a required sequence.

The member may move in any order.

The system's authority may not.

The Constitutional Direction of Authority still governs every crossing.

---

## 5. The Living Constellation is the common visual grammar

Every room should include some expression of the same constellation.

The full graph need not always be visible.

The important requirement is continuity: when the member changes rooms, the world should remain recognizably theirs.

The room changes **foreground**, not reality.

### Living Field foregrounds

- life-level recognitions;
- current questions;
- people and relationships;
- body and emotional conditions;
- work/vocation;
- thresholds and transitions;
- things the member has deliberately brought into the field.

### Vision Studio foregrounds

- questions about the work;
- developing ideas;
- projects or bodies of work;
- emerging principles;
- stories and experiences being reflected upon;
- member-authored threads carried from facilitated conversations;
- selected references from the member's wider Field.

### Practice Field foregrounds

- practitioner identity that has been ratified;
- relational agreements;
- methods/resources the practitioner has chosen to offer;
- current practice emphasis;
- how MAIA may participate within bounded authority;
- the client-facing ecology generated from the practitioner's authored field.

---

## 6. Nodes are projections of real objects

The constellation should not invent a second canonical copy of everything it displays.

A visible node should resolve to a real underlying object or an explicitly temporary candidate.

Possible source object families include:

- Personal Living Field expressions;
- Living Field sources;
- member-confirmed Field Note threads;
- Keeps / member memory atoms where constitutionally eligible;
- Projects / Living Works;
- relationships;
- practitioner-authored Practice Field sections;
- member-authored questions;
- practices or commitments where an existing governed object supports them;
- encounters, only when represented at the correct authority layer.

### Node metadata for the projection

A projected node should be able to answer:

- **what is this?**
- **where does it live?**
- **who authored it?**
- **what authority state does it have?**
- **what source does it come from?**
- **when was it first created / last deliberately touched?**
- **may it be shown here?**

The projection may cache display metadata, but source truth remains with the underlying object.

---

## 7. Relationships are explicit objects of meaning

The value of the constellation is not merely that items appear near each other.

The important layer is the relationship between them.

Initial human-readable relation vocabulary may include:

- **connected with**
- **arose from**
- **supports**
- **in tension with**
- **matters to**
- **informs**
- **points toward**
- **being explored through**
- **changed by**
- **responds to**
- **returning with**
- **may connect with** — candidate only

The final vocabulary must be validated against member experience before schema is fixed.

### Relation authority

A durable semantic relationship requires an explicit human act.

Dragging two nodes near each other does not create meaning.

Opening two nodes together does not create meaning.

Repeated co-occurrence does not create meaning.

Model confidence does not create meaning.

### Invariant

> **The system may detect possible relationship; only the member may grant that relationship standing in their Field.**

---

## 8. MAIA-proposed relationships remain candidates

MAIA may eventually propose a possible connection when the member has opened an inquiry or is actively moving through relevant material.

Example:

> “You have returned to simplifying your work several times while talking about exhaustion. I wonder whether these belong together.”

The proposed connection must remain visibly distinct.

Recommended visual grammar:

- **solid relationship** — member-authored or member-confirmed;
- **dotted relationship** — MAIA candidate, not yet accepted;
- **no line** — no standing relationship.

The member must be able to:

- confirm;
- revise the wording or relation;
- leave it open;
- reject it.

A rejected relation must not continue surfacing as though it were a weaker truth.

### Recognition initiation constraint

MAIA-proposed relationships may arise only from a member-triggered inquiry or consented present movement.

No ambient “MAIA noticed something about you” graph growth while the member is absent.

---

## 9. Authorship and standing travel with every projected meaning

At minimum, the constellation must distinguish:

### MEMBER-AUTHORED
The member explicitly created the expression or relationship.

### MEMBER-CONFIRMED
MAIA or another process proposed something and the member accepted it.

### COLLABORATIVELY-ARTICULATED
Language developed in dialogue and explicitly owned by the member.

### MAIA-CANDIDATE
A proposal with zero durable authority until confirmed.

### SOURCE / ENCOUNTER
A lower-authority record of what happened or what was brought, not yet higher-order meaning.

The UI should not flatten these into identical cards.

### Invariant

> **Authority state is part of the content. If the label disappears, the meaning changes.**

---

## 10. Temporal behavior belongs to the source object first

The constellation should feel alive across time, but must not manufacture psychological states from timestamps.

Safe factual temporal signals include:

- created;
- revised;
- last deliberately touched;
- explicitly revisited;
- released;
- returned through a member act.

Terms such as **deepening**, **quiet**, **returning**, or **changing** may be valuable member-facing language, but must not become machine conclusions without a governed source of authority.

A source object's own lifecycle remains authoritative.

The constellation may project that lifecycle.

It may not silently replace it.

### Invariant

> **History may remain visible without being mistaken for the present.**

---

## 11. Contradiction is valid

The constellation may contain simultaneous tensions:

- I want growth.
- I want less responsibility.

- I want intimacy.
- I need distance.

- This work matters deeply.
- I do not know whether I want to keep doing it.

The system must not treat contradiction as dirty data requiring automatic resolution.

A member may choose to relate contradictory nodes explicitly.

MAIA may help explore the tension.

Neither may be collapsed into a synthetic conclusion without the member's authorship.

---

## 12. Graph interaction rules

### Opening a node

Opening a node should reveal:

- its own content;
- provenance;
- developmental history where available;
- existing member-confirmed relationships;
- available actions appropriate to its source domain.

### Moving a node

Visual movement changes **placement only**.

It does not create a semantic relationship.

### Connecting nodes

A deliberate connect gesture creates a candidate relationship statement for the member to name or confirm.

### Removing from the map

Removing a placement hides that representation.

It does not delete the source object.

### Removing a relationship

Deletes or releases the relationship object only.

It does not delete either endpoint.

### Removing the source

Remains governed by the source domain.

The constellation does not acquire deletion authority merely because it can display the source.

---

## 13. Every room must introduce itself before asking for work

Before presenting controls, each room must answer:

1. **What is this place?**
2. **Why might it matter to me?**
3. **What can I do here now?**
4. **How does it connect to the rest of my world?**

Meaning precedes configuration.

### Living Field arrival

**What is alive in your life?**

> Your Living Field is where the parts of life you have chosen to recognize can remain visible, change over time, and come back into relationship with one another. You can explore a question, tend a dimension, revisit something you kept, or simply see what has gathered.

Connection line:

> What you recognize here can be brought into your work elsewhere — but nothing moves without your choice.

### Vision Studio arrival

**What is your work becoming?**

> Vision Studio helps you develop a body of work from experience, questions, stories, learning, and practice. You do not need a finished method or a polished vision. Begin with what is alive, and let coherence emerge over time.

Connection line:

> You can bring parts of your wider Field into this Studio without giving the Studio ownership of them.

### Practice Field arrival

**How does your practice meet the people you serve?**

> Practice Field is where you shape the relational conditions of your work: how you welcome people, how you work together, what you offer, what MAIA may support, and what is presently alive in your practice.

Connection line:

> What becomes mature enough to offer here should remain traceable to what you have actually authored and ratified.

---

## 14. Role-scoped experience

Practice Field is a practitioner surface.

It should not be presented to every member as though professional practice configuration were a universal developmental task.

Where the member has practitioner standing, the three-room ecology can be shown together.

Where they do not, the platform should not imply that they are missing a developmental stage.

The constellation is member-centered.

Its available rooms are role- and context-sensitive.

---

## 15. MAIA's posture changes by room without changing identity

### In Living Field

MAIA primarily witnesses, clarifies, remembers, and helps the member perceive what they have already authored.

### In Vision Studio

MAIA primarily facilitates development of the work: reflecting, questioning, comparing, teaching when invited, and proposing tentative threads.

### In Practice Field

MAIA primarily helps the practitioner articulate and steward relational conditions and practice material, while remaining subordinate to constitutional and professional boundaries.

Across all three:

- MAIA may retrieve;
- MAIA may offer;
- MAIA may compare;
- MAIA may ask;
- MAIA may teach when invited;
- MAIA may propose.

MAIA does not silently author higher-order meaning.

---

## 16. The constellation must be inspectable

Any meaningful node or edge should be able to answer:

> **Why is this here?**

Where applicable, inspection should expose:

- source object;
- source surface;
- authorship;
- member decision;
- relevant evidence or warrant;
- date of authorship;
- revision/release history;
- whether MAIA proposed it;
- whether the member confirmed it;
- whether it is private or shared.

The constellation should make provenance easier to perceive, not hide it behind visual elegance.

---

## 17. The constellation must not become

- a personality graph;
- an AI-generated biography;
- a diagnostic network;
- a quantified-self dashboard;
- a wellness score;
- a productivity funnel;
- a progress tracker;
- a hidden practitioner dossier;
- a graph of everything the model thinks is related;
- a pretext for automatic cross-domain copying;
- a single operational center that absorbs distinct constitutional centers.

The purpose is not:

> “Help MAIA know the member better.”

The purpose is:

> **Help the member perceive, develop, and participate in their own world more fully while preserving the boundaries that keep meaning theirs.**

MAIA becoming better contextualized is a consequence, not the governing aim.

---

## 18. First implementation gate implied by this contract

The next implementation unit after ratification should **not** create graph writes.

It should first create a **read-only Living Constellation projection** over existing source objects.

That projection should prove:

1. the same underlying object can be rendered in more than one room without copying it;
2. source provenance survives projection;
3. authority state survives projection;
4. role/jurisdiction boundaries survive projection;
5. no new semantic relationship is created merely by rendering;
6. the member can understand why each visible thing is present.

Only after that read model is witnessed should the project consider durable cross-object relationships or MAIA-proposed edges.

---

## 19. Open founder decisions

This candidate deliberately leaves several questions open:

1. **Vision Studio scope** — retain its current practitioner/body-of-work identity, broaden it to all members, or provide a role-sensitive variation?
2. **Practice Field naming** — preserve the existing practitioner ecology under this name, or rename the current configuration surface while retaining the constitutional object?
3. **Cross-domain relationship vocabulary** — which verbs feel natural enough to become durable member gestures?
4. **Constellation visibility** — full map by default, small contextual map, or progressive reveal?
5. **Temporal language** — which states should be explicitly member-authored versus safely derived from factual interaction history?

No implementation should settle these silently.

---

## 20. Review test

For every Living Constellation design or implementation change, ask:

> **Does this help the member see a meaningful relationship in their life or work while preserving the identity, provenance, authority, and jurisdiction of everything being related?**

If the relationship becomes clearer but the source boundaries become less clear, the design has failed.

If the graph becomes richer by manufacturing meaning, the design has failed.

If the member can move among Living Field, Vision Studio, and Practice Field and feel that each adds to the same world without any of them swallowing the others, the architecture is beginning to work.
