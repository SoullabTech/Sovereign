# Becoming — House Experience Contract

## Room purpose

Becoming is Soullab’s prospective room.

The member enters from the life they are actually living, lets time open, encounters one or more possible futures, may inhabit them through Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and Aether, and then returns explicitly to the present.

The room does not ask the member to optimize toward an ideal self.

> **The future illuminates the present; the present remains where life is lived.**

## MAIA inside Becoming

MAIA has two distinct postures.

**During the journey**, live guidance is opt-in, one invitation at a time, current-journey-only, and server-forced to sanctuary/ephemeral posture. No ambient historical retrieval belongs in this phase.

**After Return**, the member may explicitly give the whole journey to MAIA for supportive synthesis and continuing conversation inside Becoming. The member should not need to repeat the journey.

MAIA never speaks as Future Self. The member authors both temporal voices.

## Elemental immersion

Earth, Water, Air, Fire, and Aether are modes of perception, not five required form fields and not a scoring system.

The member may enter through whichever element is already alive.

Aether gathers the field; it does not pronounce revelation or objective truth.

Convergence and contradiction are both valid material.

## Across Time

Across Time is secondary to the journey and appears only after the member has kept a journey.

Its temporal grammar remains:

**has been · is being · is becoming**

MAIA may hold explicitly selected material as one relational gestalt while preserving source, authorship, epistemic kind, time relation, uncertainty, and member correction.

The center is a vantage of integration, not another source of facts.

## Governed source ports

House integration admits read-only source ports only where an existing member-scoped House resolver can authenticate and resolve the exact source.

The live port set is:

- Journal
- Dream
- Relationships
- Decisions
- Changes
- Astrology
- Divination
- Reflections
- Ideas

A port requires an explicit member act and exact source identity.

It may return bounded provenance and an exact return route.

It may not persist, auto-discover, infer importance, or silently widen into whole-House retrieval.

Practices is deliberately not advertised as a live source port until a real member-owned source resolver exists.

## House / Room split

The House reveals Becoming as a place.

Becoming owns its own journey grammar, elemental encounter, MAIA postures, local journey store, Return act, and Across Time experience.

The House does not interpret Becoming on the member’s behalf.

Becoming returns to the House without rewriting the House.

## Current integration boundary

This candidate keeps journey storage browser-local.

That is an explicit limitation, not an account-persistence claim.

Account-grade Becoming continuity, voice, and automatic Indra’s Web whole-House retrieval remain outside this act.

The source-port endpoint is read-only and explicit. Technical readability between facets is not permission to retrieve them automatically.

## Acceptance

The room is accepted when the member can move through:

**House → Becoming → Journey → Elements → MAIA → Return → Synthesis → Keep → Across Time → House**

without losing source distinction, present-life orientation, or the ability to disagree with MAIA.
