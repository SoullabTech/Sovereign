# Writer’s Studio Small Beta — Experience Contract

## Success law

Writer’s Studio beta succeeds when writers can orient themselves, retain authorship, correct MAIA, move between whole and part without losing the developmental thread, use MAIA’s perception without mistaking it for authority, return meaningfully to their own writing, and leave increasingly capable of discerning what their Work needs.

Success is **not** measured primarily by session length, message count, AI usage, number of suggestions accepted, number of insights generated, amount of text changed, or frequency of return.

## Eight criteria

1. **Orientation** — Can the writer identify the Work, current scale, governing question, and return path?
2. **Authorship** — Can the writer freely reject, reshape, preserve, or refuse MAIA’s proposals?
3. **Developmental usefulness** — Does Develop help the writer find a meaningful next question or direction?
4. **Continuity** — Does whole → chapter → passage → whole remain one intelligible inquiry?
5. **Correction quality** — Does an explicit correction actually alter MAIA’s later working understanding?
6. **Epistemic trust** — Are evidence, interpretation, uncertainty and absence distinguishable?
7. **Return to writing** — Does reflection return the writer to drafting, revision, decision, or deliberate restraint?
8. **Transfer / independence** — Does the writer become more capable of discernment without requiring MAIA for every judgment?

## Hard gates

Any of these stops expansion until repaired:

- authorship violation;
- orientation collapse that cannot be recovered;
- explicit correction ignored or silently overwritten;
- provenance / evidence represented falsely;
- continuity loss that severs the developmental question;
- data or consent boundary violation.

## Finding classes

- **isolated preference** — one person’s taste; record without reflexive product change;
- **recurring friction** — repeated difficulty across writers;
- **developmental obstruction** — the interface or MAIA interferes with the human process the Studio exists to serve;
- **constitutional violation** — authorship, consent, provenance, correction, sovereignty or epistemic boundary broken.

## Feedback membrane

Available only when `beta=1` is present **and** the server verifies the member is in the pilot: an active, member-linked `beta_tester` contact. Founder/CTO may enter for witness and support. The writer may explicitly choose one of:

- I lost the thread
- MAIA misunderstood me
- Too much too quickly
- I wanted more help
- This felt like my voice
- This changed how I see the Work
- I’m not ready to decide
- Something else

The record may include bounded navigation/orientation metadata and an optional writer note. It may not include manuscript prose, passive dwell time, clickstream, inferred emotion, engagement probability, salience, or an automatic quality score.

## Correction succession

A correction is attached to one persisted MAIA turn. The original turn remains untouched. The writer supplies the current understanding MAIA should carry forward. A later correction of the same turn becomes current by succession; earlier corrections remain historical acts.

MAIA receives current corrections as writer-authored context. Corrections do not become manuscript facts, personality claims, or editing authority.
