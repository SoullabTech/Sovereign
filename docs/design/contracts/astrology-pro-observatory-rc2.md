# Astrology Pro — A living celestial observatory

## What this room is for

A person arrives to encounter the sky as a field of relationships, separating **measured celestial positions**, **symbolic traditions**, and **their own experience**. The chart neither diagnoses their identity nor assigns a developmental level. MAIA can help explore contradictions and possibilities, but the member remains the authority on what is meaningful.

## Arrival and gestures

The arrival identifies the room as an observatory, with the current sky placed near the beginning. A private chart opens only through the member's explicit act.

| Gesture | Human-facing language | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Open chart | Open my chart | Verified own member session, beta admission and current Astrology consent; no client-provided birth record |
| Select a celestial body | Name, position and house | Changes local attention; each member note remains attached to its selected point |
| Explore lenses | Calculated / Explore / My meaning | Distinguishes measurement, inquiry and interpretation |
| Prepare a conversation | Prepare my conversation | Builds a reviewable local draft, but does not send to MAIA |
| Share explicitly | Send to MAIA | Rechecks identity and current consent before selected facts and authored question cross rooms |
| Return | Soullab Astrology | Leaves the preview without claiming permanent interpretation or subscription |

## House and room distinction

What stays common is a human-scale invitation, an editable response rather than AI certainty, quiet typography and a consentful return to MAIA. What is particular is a precise astronomical wheel, not a generalized dashboard or a scorecard of the self.

The current sky is shared, while a natal chart is private. Astrology's symbolic language is held as inquiry, not scientific proof of personal causation. Approximate minor bodies and method limitations remain identified.

## Release boundaries retained

This preview does not authorize other people's charts, practitioner client records, paid plans, automatic memory promotion, gift credits, external email, guaranteed chart exactness or future prediction. Browser evidence is synthetic. Real signed-in and native-device member acceptance, independent security/precision review, and recovery-safe release checks remain separate gates.
