# FACET-FLOW-03 — Astrology + Divination Epistemic Source Separation

## Standing

> **READINESS CONTRACT ONLY · NO NEW ASTROLOGY/DIVINATION → JOURNAL/ANCHOR RUNTIME AUTHORITY**

Dream → Daily Anchor has already established the symbolic-continuity baseline:
the primary source may accompany the member while the receiving room stays blank.
Astrology and Divination require an additional protection because their source objects contain
more than member-authored primary material.

## The five classes that must remain distinguishable

Every symbolic handoff must be able to tell these apart:

1. **Source fact** — what was actually calculated, cast, selected, recorded, or asked.
2. **Symbolic tradition** — inherited meanings attached to a planet, sign, card, hexagram, rune, aspect, cycle, or other symbol.
3. **System synthesis** — a generated combination, interpretation, or relational reading.
4. **Possible human expression** — a hypothesis about how symbolic material might show up in lived experience.
5. **Member meaning** — what the member themselves says this means, evokes, confirms, rejects, or changes.

These classes may coexist on one source page.
They may not become one undifferentiated payload at a crossing seam.

## Receiving law

Journal and Daily Anchor remain member-authored rooms.

A symbolic crossing may therefore carry:

- source identity;
- source type;
- truthful source-fact labels;
- explicitly selected symbolic context, when its epistemic class is visible;
- exact return to the source.

It may not prefill the Journal or Anchor with interpretation, synthesis, advice, or member meaning.
## Readiness matrix

| Source | Durable identity | Exact return | Epistemic separation | Journal/Anchor readiness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dream | yes — canonical owned Dream UUID | yes | primary remembered dream is distinct from later interpretation | **READY / Dream→Anchor live** |
| Divination | yes — saved reading identity (iching:, tarot:, runes:) | yes | typed source packet now separates source fact, symbolic tradition, system synthesis, possible expression, and member meaning | **JOURNAL LIVE / ANCHOR CLOSED** |
| Astrology | member chart identity exists | room navigation exists | no single governed handoff packet yet separates calculated facts from symbolic tradition, synthesis, possible expression, and member meaning | **NOT READY** |

## Divination finding

The existing Saved Reading → Reflection crossing remains valid.

Its law is source custody: the reading stays the reading and Reflection stays the keep.
That does **not** automatically authorize Saved Reading → Journal or Daily Anchor.

For those future crossings, the handoff resolver must expose typed epistemic fields rather than
a single blended excerpt assembled from reading and interpretation text.

Minimum safe packet:

- reading identity and divination system;
- member-authored question, if present;
- cast/drawn symbols as source facts;
- optional interpretation as a separately labeled secondary field;
- no generated guidance in the receiving authorship field;
- exact return URL.
## Astrology finding

Astrology has strong constitutional language but insufficient crossing substrate.

Before Astrology → Journal or Astrology → Daily Anchor can open, the runtime needs a
governed source packet that can identify at least:

- calculated chart or transit fact;
- astrological tradition / symbolic attribution;
- whole-chart or transit synthesis;
- possible lived expression;
- member-authored resonance or meaning, when one exists.

A component-generated synthesis must never masquerade as a calculated chart fact.

“Keep this timing in view today” is only admissible when the member can see precisely
which calculated timing fact is being carried and which language around it is interpretive.

## URL and persistence law

No symbolic prose is placed in the crossing URL.

The durable crossing ledger continues to store identities only.

A receiving surface resolves the source server-side under the authenticated member and
renders epistemic labels before any interpretive context is shown.

## Fail-closed law

If the runtime cannot identify the epistemic class of a symbolic field, that field does not cross.

If a source object cannot be resolved as owned by the authenticated member, nothing crosses.

If calculated/cast facts cannot be separated from generated synthesis, the doorway remains unavailable.
## Forbidden

- treating astrology, divination, or dream symbolism as factual identity claims;
- carrying generated interpretation into Journal as though the member wrote it;
- carrying guidance into Daily Anchor as a recommendation or instruction;
- silently turning symbolic material into MAIA memory;
- promoting “possible expression” to “this is what is happening”;
- stripping provenance so a symbolic statement appears to originate with the member;
- reconstructing source content from a receiving object when the original source still exists.

## Acceptance for FACET-FLOW-03

This act is complete when:

- the five epistemic classes are canonical;
- Dream, Divination, and Astrology have explicit readiness standings;
- existing Divination → Reflection remains recognized without broadening its authority;
- Astrology and Divination → Journal/Anchor remain fail-closed;
- the next implementation act can name one concrete typed source packet rather than “connect symbolism.”
