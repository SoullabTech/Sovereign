# Sound Studio / Listening Field — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

Sound Studio is one living field with several reasons to enter. A person may simply receive music, focus, meditate, rest or prepare; they may investigate a difference, learn through listening, create a work, or enter a deeper professional and research practice. The field does not make those reasons separate products. They remain different relationships to one versioned Sound Work and one member-controlled listening authority.

Personal listening is complete participation. Nothing requires the member to change a setting, explain an experience, use MAIA, publish, collaborate or report an effect.

## Arrival

> **What brings you to sound today?**

The answer is offered through four legible doors:

- **Experience** — listen, focus, meditate, rest, prepare or return;
- **Explore** — compare, investigate and learn through careful listening;
- **Create** — compose a journey and develop a work through return;
- **Professional / Research** — bring musicianship, engineering, cultural stewardship and disciplined inquiry.

The doors progressively disclose depth. They are not four competing applications and do not start sound automatically.

## Persistent player law

The independent player is independent of the current room, not independent of Soullab authority.

- One stable-root provider owns the audio context and experience clock.
- The same context may continue while the member moves through authorized Soullab rooms.
- Pause, Resume, Stop, elapsed time and current passage remain directly legible.
- No route, voice interruption, error, completion or device handoff may automatically restart sound.
- A rights-permitted work may repeat only after the member explicitly enables Loop; Pause preserves that session choice, while Stop clears it.
- Playback grants no microphone, analysis, memory, biometric, rights or research authority.
- The dock must remain usable at phone width without hiding Stop or widening the document.
- Companion Mode may change only Soullab's own field. It cannot read, analyze, pause, mix, or control another music app.
- Independent field volume is available on every device through a full slider, mute/restore, and three transparent starting levels: Under 12%, Soft 22%, and Present 35%.
- A twelve-second fade may end the Soullab field gently; it restores the selected level for a later deliberate start and never replaces immediate Stop.
- MAIA speech retains priority: it pauses the field and never resumes it automatically.

## Companion listening

A member may run Soullab quietly beneath music chosen in another app. This is a relationship between two independently controlled sources, not an in-Soullab integration with the external platform.

- **Under** begins at 12% for a barely foregrounded field.
- **Soft** begins at 22% and is the default when Companion Mode is enabled from a louder setting.
- **Present** begins at 35% when the member wants the field clearly audible.
- **Mute / Restore** silences only Soullab without pausing or losing the prior chosen level.
- **Loop piece** repeats the exact rights-permitted recording only after it reaches its end; **Stop** clears Loop. Open Ground's present edition retains its composed fade-out and fade-in, so the repeat breathes rather than claiming a seamless join.
- **Fade 12s & stop** gives a gentle ending; **Stop** remains immediate and final.
- Tonal, spectral, rhythmic or adaptive shaping is not exposed for a fixed master merely because volume control exists. Those controls require actual stems, transparent method, and creator permission.

The boundary text remains visible in the control panel: other music stays in its original app, and Soullab changes only its own field.
