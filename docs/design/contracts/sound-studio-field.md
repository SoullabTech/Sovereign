# Sound Studio / Listening Field — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

Sound Studio is one living field with several reasons to enter. A person may simply receive music, focus, meditate, rest or prepare; they may investigate a difference, learn through listening, create a work, or enter a deeper professional and research practice. The field does not make those reasons separate products. They remain different relationships to one versioned Sound Work and one member-controlled listening authority.

Personal listening is complete participation. Nothing requires the member to change a setting, explain an experience, use MAIA, publish, collaborate or report an effect.

## Arrival

> **What brings you to sound today?**

The answer is offered through four legible doors:

- **Experience** — listen, focus, meditate, rest, prepare or return;
- **Explore** — compare, investigate and learn through careful listening;
- **Create** — compose a journey and develop a work through return;
- **Professional / Research** — bring musicianship, engineering, cultural stewardship and disciplined inquiry.

The doors progressively disclose depth. They are not four competing applications and do not start sound automatically.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| enter a complete work | `Enter experience` | names an encounter rather than opening a media asset |
| begin by choice | `Begin Open Ground` | the title and member act remain explicit; no autoplay |
| suspend without losing place | `Pause` / `Resume` | ordinary, direct control with no interpretive language |
| end immediately | `Stop` | always visible and final for that playing context |
| understand the arc | `Arrival · Opening · Dwelling · Return` | temporal orientation without predicting the member’s state |
| return from elsewhere | the playing work title / `Open field` | the persistent dock is a doorway back to the same field, not a second player |
| balance beneath another app | `Mix` / `Play alongside another app` | makes the Soullab field independently adjustable without claiming access to the foreground service |
| leave gently | `Fade 12s & stop` | offers a composed exit while immediate `Stop` remains available |
| leave sound complete | `After listening` | reflection is available but never required |
| return to Soullab | `Home` | clear movement back into the House, not a browser-level escape |

## Persistent player law

The independent player is independent of the current room, not independent of Soullab authority.

- One stable-root provider owns the audio context and experience clock.
- The same context may continue while the member moves through authorized Soullab rooms.
- Pause, Resume, Stop, elapsed time and current passage remain directly legible.
- No route, voice interruption, error, completion or device handoff may automatically restart sound.
- Playback grants no microphone, analysis, memory, biometric, rights or research authority.
- The dock must remain usable at phone width without hiding Stop or widening the document.
- Companion Mode may change only Soullab's own field. It cannot read, analyze, pause, mix, or control another music app.
- Independent field volume is available on every device through a full slider, mute/restore, and three transparent starting levels: Under 12%, Soft 22%, and Present 35%.
- A twelve-second fade may end the Soullab field gently; it restores the selected level for a later deliberate start and never replaces immediate Stop.
- MAIA speech retains priority: it pauses the field and never resumes it automatically.

## Responsive embodiment

**Desktop** carries the fullest Studio, series shelf, experience field and later professional authoring depth.

**iPad** is the first immersive Neuropod rehearsal: touch-based, spacious and capable of holding a complete guided or music-only experience without technical clutter.

**iPhone** carries daily continuity: a one-column field, immediate listening, short preparations, persistent controls and a reliable return doorway. It does not pretend to be the full professional workstation.

These are three expressions of the same field. This witness demonstrates responsive parity and within-device route continuity; it does not claim cross-device session transfer.

## Open Ground prototype experience

Open Ground is the first complete experience mounted in the responsive field. It is a six-minute original procedural composition with a 432 Hz A4 reference, no regular beat and four composed passages. The exact private-review AAC listening edition is manifest-bound and played through the one existing persistent HTML audio element; its separately preserved 24-bit/48 kHz FLAC master is not Git-served. This remains private review media, not an admitted member catalogue recording, a therapeutic protocol or a physical Neuropod session.

## Companion listening

A member may run Soullab quietly beneath music chosen in another app. This is a relationship between two independently controlled sources, not an in-Soullab integration with the external platform.

- **Under** begins at 12% for a barely foregrounded field.
- **Soft** begins at 22% and is the default when Companion Mode is enabled from a louder setting.
- **Present** begins at 35% when the member wants the field clearly audible.
- **Mute / Restore** silences only Soullab without pausing or losing the prior chosen level.
- **Fade 12s & stop** gives a gentle ending; **Stop** remains immediate and final.
- Tonal, spectral, rhythmic or adaptive shaping is not exposed for a fixed master merely because volume control exists. Those controls require actual stems, transparent method, and creator permission.

The boundary text remains visible in the control panel: other music stays in its original app, and Soullab changes only its own field.

## Forbidden here

- a capability dashboard as the first experience;
- a second player, second AudioContext authority or separate Sound Studio application;
- autoplay, automatic resume, hidden Stop or route-driven playback reset;
- requiring interpretation, reflection, MAIA, creation or social participation;
- inferring emotion, diagnosis, readiness or neurological state from listening choices;
- decorative waveforms or device claims presented as technical evidence;
- describing viewport emulation as physical iPad/iPhone/Safari or native acceptance;
- implying that a procedural review sketch is a rights-admitted artist master;
- collapsing professional tools into the personal entrance before the member asks for them;
- using engagement time, session count or completion as the primary success measure.

## What success means

- The person felt free to enter, adjust, stop or leave.
- The experience held attention without commandeering it.
- The work could deepen through return.
- Any language or action belonged to the participant.
- Creator authorship remained intact.
- Technical precision supported rather than displaced the encounter.
- Something valuable could continue into life outside Soullab.
- “Nothing happened for me” remained a valid response.

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. The field uses the House’s contained atmosphere, editorial hierarchy, restrained signal, clear Home threshold and member-authored choice. The persistent player stays within the same authenticated root and does not create a separate product shell.

**Distinct room?** Yes. Sound is not decoration here; it is the material through which a person receives, attends, investigates, creates and returns. The experience clock, passage language, work shelf and persistent audible continuity make this recognizably Sound Studio even without its label.
