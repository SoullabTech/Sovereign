# Soul Portrait — private delivery gesture

## What this room is for

A practitioner reviews the finished portrait and knowingly creates an unlisted link for one person, with an optional separately authorized email. If the email service refuses, the link is still available to copy, and the copy plainly describes whether the portrait has already become final.

## Arrival

The bottom of the portrait preview presents **Send this portrait**, the subject's name and the consequence of creating the private link.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Consent before sharing | Confirm this subject authorized this exact recipient and the recipient agreed to receive it | Reaffirmed on every new recipient and retry |
| Finalize | Create private link | The portrait becomes write-once regardless of email success |
| Deliver | Send email | Accepted by the SMTP service is distinct from delivered into an inbox |
| Recover from refusal | Copy link | Owner remains able to share manually after independently checking recipient permission |

## Forbidden here

- emailing an arbitrary third party without fresh subject and recipient attestations
- claiming delivery when the provider refused or timed out
- reactivating a revoked subject's consent by attempting to resend
- presenting an already published portrait as editable
- treating prior screenshots or synthetic source tests as current UI acceptance

## Brand tests

The same House: quiet invitation, explicit consent and an honest return path. A distinct room: the final consented crossing from private author review to one recipient, not a chart calculator or member feed.
