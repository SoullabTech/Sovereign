## Governing journey

> **Encounter → Attend → Discern → Move**

A Living Field presence is not an endpoint and not merely an archive object.

The member may choose whether to:

- stay;
- continue;
- see across time;
- reveal already-authored connections;
- bring the exact presence to MAIA;
- let it rest for now.

No movement is selected or ranked by the system.

## LIVING-FIELD-PRESENCE-TO-MOVEMENT-LAW-01

> **A Living Field presence is not an endpoint. Once a member enters something from their life, the interface should help them discover whether to stay, continue, connect, revisit through time, develop, act, or let it rest—without deciding that movement for them.**

The presence remains the center of gravity.

Movement options are invitations, not prescriptions.

## LIVING-FIELD-NO-DEAD-END-MEMORY-LAW-01

> **Remembering is valuable in itself, but Living Field must not reduce a living history to an archive of things to reread. The member must be able to re-enter life from what is remembered.**

A remembered presence may lead back to an existing source, a real authored connection, an across-time view, or an invited MAIA conversation.

R4C3 creates none of those capabilities; it only prototypes how already-authorized ones could be encountered.

## Atmosphere repair

Founder witness rejected the previous salmon / peach field as too anemic.

R4C3 therefore strengthens atmospheric vitality through:

- richer copper / coral;
- saturated gold;
- deeper teal;
- restrained plum;
- stronger contrast against warm paper;
- slow large-scale atmospheric drift;
- fine grain.

The palette is intentionally more alive and dynamic.

### Atmosphere non-semantic rule

Color and motion may carry vitality, depth, warmth, and continuity.

They may not encode:

- importance;
- psychological state;
- elemental classification;
- developmental stage;
- risk;
- certainty;
- authorship;
- urgency.

The same atmospheric grammar is used across all three prototype journeys.

Reduced-motion environments retain the color field without motion.

## Movement mapping

### Stay

Local presentation only.

No write, save, interpretation, or state transition.

### Continue

Returns to the already-existing source surface where the presence lives.

R4C3 does not create a copy.

### Across time

Shows only source-bearing authored traces with dates / custody.

Temporal sequence is not converted into narrative causation.

### Connections

Shows only member-authored or member-confirmed relationships that already have standing.

Similarity, embeddings, keywords, spatial proximity, and MAIA inference cannot create a connection.

### With MAIA

The exact presence may be brought into an already-authorized MAIA conversation only after explicit member invitation.

MAIA does not acquire authorship or change the presence's standing.

### Let rest

In this prototype, rest means close / disengage for the current visit only.

No persistent suppression, completion state, deletion, or downgrade is created.
