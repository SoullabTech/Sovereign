# Writer’s Studio — Restricted Beta Threshold

## Human purpose

The member meets a clear threshold, not a fake technical failure. Writer’s Studio is not yet open to everyone. No page should hint that this is because of the visitor's writing, personal worth, or a failure of their membership.

## The one useful action

**Return to Soullab** allows the person to leave without a sign-up funnel. Entry eligibility is determined by a separate verified founder-approved cohort record. This page can explain that fact but cannot confer it.

## False impressions to prevent

- The button is not an invitation token, payment tier, queue-jump or hidden approval.
- No countdown, scarcity copy, inferred writing aptitude or sales urgency.
- No claim to have edited, moved, deleted, or evaluated the member’s Work.
- Neither the member’s query string nor a forged identity header may unlock the room.
- Debugging or developer-preview doors cannot be a substitute cohort entrance.

## House / room relationship

**Same House:** Soullab identity, calm visual restraint, truthful language and a path back to the wider Home.

**Distinct room:** This threshold exists only because an intimate, authored writing relationship is being introduced with a small invited group. It does not borrow Astrology or Pro Studio permissions, and it does not pretend to be a fully open writing workspace.

## Evidence limits

Screenshots prove presentation and orientation in two specific browsers. They do not prove a real sign-in link has been delivered to a tester, that production traffic is restricted, or that R20 deletion-safe recovery authority is independently anchored. Those remain separate deployment gates.
