# DREAM-02 — World-Class Dream Room Experience Architecture + State Model

**Programme:** SOULLAB-LIVING-ORIENTATION / DREAM-02  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Scope:** experience architecture and state model only. No runtime build, no schema migration, no route mutation.

## 1. Design thesis

The Dream room is a **living chamber of encounter with the unconscious**.

It is not a dashboard, analyzer, archive-first tool, interpretation report, or sequence of forms.

The room must support the member in staying with a dream long enough for its own life to become perceptible.

The experience should feel:

- intimate;
- quiet;
- symbolically alive;
- psychologically rigorous;
- conversational;
- spacious;
- beautiful without decoration becoming spectacle;
- intelligent without exposing machinery;
- deep without becoming solemn or clinical.

The member should feel that MAIA is **with the dream**, not standing above it.

## 2. Spatial principle

The room is organized around one primary visual fact:

> **The dream remains present while the conversation deepens.**

The dream text must not disappear behind tabs or be replaced by a chat feed.

At desktop width, the room should support a two-field relationship:

- **Dream field** — the remembered dream, stable, readable, primary;
- **Relational field** — MAIA conversation, associations, amplifications, and integration.

These fields may shift in emphasis, but neither should become a detached side panel.

On mobile, they should become one continuous vertical composition with the dream always easy to reopen without losing conversational place.

## 3. Material character

The room should inherit the accepted House language but have its own nocturnal material quality.

Not “purple mystical UI.”
Not stars and particles.
Not occult ornament pasted onto a card layout.

The atmosphere should come from:

- low-luminance depth;
- warm paper / vellum zones for the member's dream;
- soft dark surrounding field;
- restrained metallic or moonlit accents;
- generous margins;
- slow transitions;
- typographic contrast between dream text, member association, MAIA inquiry, and amplification;
- subtle temporal cues that suggest night / dawn without turning the room into a theme park.

The room should feel equally plausible in an old analytic study, a monastery library, or a quiet spacecraft at 4 a.m.

## 4. State model overview

The Dream room has eight experiential states:

1. Arrival
2. Re-member
3. Dream Page
4. Encounter
5. Amplify
6. Dream Series
7. Integrate
8. Return / Crossing

These are **states of relationship**, not wizard steps.

The member may move backward, pause, skip, or leave from any state.

There is no completion percentage.

## 5. State 1 — Arrival

### Purpose

Meet the member without requiring interpretation or administrative choice.

### Primary invitations

- **Record a dream**
- **Return to a dream**
- **Dreams that are still with you** — recent / unfinished / member-kept, not algorithmically ranked “important dreams”
- **Dream series** — secondary, quiet

### Tone

Minimal.

Possible line:

> *What is still with you?*

or:

> *Bring the dream as you remember it.*

No “What wisdom did your dreams bring?”
No assumption that every dream is profound.
No forced title, mood, archetype, or type selection.

### MAIA presence

MAIA is quiet unless invited.

Arrival is not a chat screen.

## 6. State 2 — Re-member

### Purpose

Help the member recover the dream before reflection begins.

### Capture modes

- voice;
- text;
- fragments;
- later: handwriting / image transcription.

### Interface law

The capture field should feel like one page.

No metadata form above it.

Optional prompts may appear only as faint, dismissible supports:

- “What happened first?”
- “What image remains?”
- “Who was there?”
- “What did the place feel like?”
- “What happened just before waking?”

These prompts do not need to be answered.

### Memory behavior

The member may interrupt themselves, revise sequence, add “I’m not sure,” and preserve uncertainty.

Do not clean ambiguity out of the record.

### Completion gesture

**Keep this dream**

Keeping establishes the canonical Dream identity.

## 7. State 3 — Dream Page

### Purpose

Let the dream exist as an object before anyone speaks about it.

### Composition

The dream text is beautifully typeset.

Below or near it:

- date / time;
- optional member-authored place;
- optional audio provenance;
- title only if the member chose one;
- exact source provenance if captured through Journal.

No interpretation appears automatically.

### Quiet actions

- **Explore with MAIA**
- **Add what I remember**
- **Write from this dream**
- **Keep something from this**
- **Dream series**
- **Return to Journal** when applicable

Actions remain visually subordinate to the dream itself.

## 8. State 4 — Encounter

### Purpose

Enter relationship with the dream without leaving the dream behind.

### Conversational form

This is not “chat about the dream” in the generic sense.

MAIA should begin from one precise opening:

- an image;
- a phrase;
- a shift;
- an affect;
- a gap;
- the dream ego;
- the member’s chosen place of attention.

### First-response law

MAIA’s first substantial move should usually do no more than:

1. reflect one exact feature;
2. open one live question.

Example:

> *You keep describing the hallway as “too narrow to turn around in.” I want to stay with that exact quality. What was it like to be there in your body?*

Not:

> *Narrow hallways often symbolize restriction.*

### Conversational pacing

Depth should accumulate through turns.

MAIA should not unload five interpretations in one response.

A world-class exchange may stay with one image for ten minutes.

### Member controls

The member should be able to steer explicitly:

- **Stay with this**
- **Look at another image**
- **Ask what it might mean**
- **Bring in Jung**
- **Bring in alchemy**
- **Look at waking life**
- **Look across other dreams**

These controls may be subtle text affordances, not toolbar buttons.

## 9. State 5 — Amplify

### Purpose

Widen the image without collapsing it.

Amplification becomes available only after:

- the member asks for it;
- or enough personal context has been explored that an amplification can serve the dream.

### Possible lenses

- personal association;
- Jungian;
- mythic;
- alchemical;
- religious / spiritual;
- literary / fairy tale;
- elemental;
- astrological;
- cross-dream series;
- relational;
- cultural / historical.

### Presentation law

Amplifications should not appear as rigid tabs with “the answer” under each.

Prefer a conversational constellation:

> *There are at least three ways we could widen this image...*

Then offer a small number of distinct possibilities.

Each should preserve source and level:

- “In your own associations…”
- “In Jungian terms…”
- “Alchemically…”
- “Across your recent dreams…”

### Polyphony control

MAIA must be able to hold conflicting possibilities without resolving them prematurely.

The member may say:

- “That one resonates.”
- “None of those.”
- “The second one, but not quite.”
- “There’s another meaning.”

That response should shape the next turn.

## 10. State 6 — Dream Series

### Purpose

Make longitudinal symbolic life perceptible.

### Series should not look like analytics

Avoid:

- frequency dashboards;
- archetype percentages;
- trend charts as the primary surface;
- “top symbols”;
- confidence scores.

### Prefer a living constellation

Possible forms:

- chronological cards;
- recurring-image threads;
- places / figures that reappear;
- a visual timeline of exact dream excerpts;
- a constellation of member-confirmed correspondences;
- transformations in relation to an image.

Example:

> **THE HOUSE**
>
> Jul 7 — outside the house  
> Aug 9 — looking through a window  
> Sep 26 — inside the house, in the office

Then:

> *You’ve moved from outside, to looking in, to being inside. Want to stay with that movement?*

### Evidence law

Every surfaced series observation should be traceable to exact dreams.

A machine-proposed similarity may inform MAIA quietly.
If surfaced, it should appear as:

> *These two images may be related. Do they feel related to you?*

Never:

> *Recurring pattern confirmed.*

## 11. State 7 — Integrate

### Purpose

Let the dream enter waking life without converting it into a task.

### Possible integration gestures

- **Carry this image today**
- **Write from this**
- **Keep this recognition**
- **Stay with this question**
- **Continue through active imagination**
- **Return to this dream later**
- **Link this to another dream**
- **Do nothing yet**

### Integration conversation

MAIA may ask:

> *What, if anything, do you want to carry from this dream?*

The phrase “if anything” matters.

### Active imagination

If chosen, active imagination becomes a distinct mode.

The interface should clearly mark:

> **Imaginal continuation**

so the member knows what follows is not part of the original dream and not recovered memory.

The original dream remains visible and unchanged.

## 12. State 8 — Return / Crossing

### Purpose

Allow Dream to remain part of one life rather than a sealed chamber.

Potential crossings:

- Dream → Journal
- Dream → Reflection
- Dream → Daily Anchor
- Dream → Astrology
- Dream → Divination
- Dream → MAIA conversation
- Dream series → Living Field projection

Every crossing carries source identity.

No dream text is placed in a URL.

No target object is silently authored.

## 13. MAIA presence model

MAIA’s Dream presence should have four relational intensities.

### Quiet witness

Used during capture and first reading.

MAIA is visually present but does not speak unless invited.

### Companion

Used during Encounter.

Responses are brief, exact, spacious.

### Amplifier

Used when the member asks for wider symbolic or theoretical context.

MAIA may become more intellectually expansive, but must remain dialogical.

### Integrator

Used when the member asks how the dream relates to waking life or what to carry forward.

MAIA does not turn integration into advice.

These are not user-facing modes or labels.
They are internal interaction disciplines.

## 14. Conversation quality standard

A world-class Dream conversation should demonstrate:

### Fidelity

Use exact language from the dream.

### Patience

Do not hurry to interpretation.

### Depth

Stay with the image long enough for new association to emerge.

### Differentiation

Keep dream fact, personal association, amplification, hypothesis, and waking resonance distinct.

### Intelligence

Offer real Jungian, alchemical, mythic, symbolic, or developmental knowledge when appropriate.

### Soulfulness

Recognize numinosity, mystery, grief, beauty, terror, absurdity, and sacredness without reducing them to technique.

### Humility

Be capable of saying:

> *I don’t know yet.*

### Memory

Remember relevant prior dreams and transformations across a series.

### Non-performance

Do not sound like a “mystical AI.”

No purple prose by default.
No grand claims.
No canned sacredness.

## 15. Example conversation texture

Dreamer:

> *There was a black dog standing at the edge of the woods. I was afraid of it but it didn’t move.*

MAIA:

> *The dog doesn’t chase you. It just stands there at the edge. What was the quality of its stillness?*

Dreamer:

> *Almost like it was waiting.*

MAIA:

> *Waiting feels different from threatening. When you say that, does your fear change at all?*

Dreamer:

> *Yes. It feels sadder now.*

MAIA:

> *Stay with that. A black dog, at the edge of the woods, waiting—and now sadness enters. Does the dog remind you of anyone or anything in your own life before we bring in any larger symbolism?*

Only later, if invited:

> *There are several traditions we could amplify through here. Jungianly, a black animal at a threshold can sometimes constellate instinct, shadow, or a neglected part of psychic life—but I would not want to decide which of those this dog is. Your sense that it is waiting may matter more. Want to explore one of those possibilities?*

That is the target texture.

## 16. Visual hierarchy

### Level 1 — Dream

Largest visual authority.

### Level 2 — Member voice

Associations, corrections, recognitions.

### Level 3 — MAIA inquiry

Distinct but quiet.

### Level 4 — Amplification

Clearly secondary and optional.

### Level 5 — Crossings / actions

Lowest visual authority.

This order protects the dream from becoming background to the interface.

## 17. Progressive disclosure

Nothing should appear before it is useful.

At capture:
- no series;
- no archetypes;
- no lenses;
- no integration actions.

At Dream Page:
- only simple next gestures.

During Encounter:
- conversation first.

During Amplify:
- richer lenses become available.

During Integrate:
- outward crossings become visible.

The room deepens as the relationship deepens.

## 18. Time and return

Dream work often happens across days, months, and years.

The room should make it natural to:

- leave mid-conversation;
- return to the same dream;
- see where the conversation stopped;
- reopen the original dream instantly;
- continue exploration without re-summarizing;
- revisit older dreams when a new one evokes them.

Continuity should feel like relationship, not session recovery.

## 19. Safety and epistemic boundaries

Dream work may touch trauma, grief, psychosis-adjacent material, spiritual experience, sexuality, violence, death, or extraordinary beliefs.

The system should:

- remain grounded in what the member reported;
- avoid validating unverifiable external claims as fact;
- avoid interpreting traumatic material as hidden truth;
- avoid recovered-memory framing;
- avoid deterministic prophetic claims;
- preserve the difference between imaginal exploration and historical fact;
- support the member’s agency in deciding meaning.

Depth does not require certainty.

## 20. Acceptance criteria

DREAM-02 passes only if the proposed experience:

- keeps the dream visually primary;
- allows immediate, low-friction capture;
- supports an hour-long intelligent conversation without becoming chat clutter;
- lets personal association precede amplification;
- supports real Jungian sophistication;
- preserves multiple simultaneous meanings;
- uses computational memory implicitly and intelligently;
- makes dream series longitudinally meaningful;
- allows integration without taskification;
- preserves exact source identity across every crossing;
- avoids dashboard aesthetics;
- avoids fake mystical ornament;
- feels distinctly Soullab rather than like a generic dream app.
