# CAPACITY-INTERFERENCE-LAW-01
## When useful capacities become noisy together

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **A composition is only useful while its capacities remain mutually intelligible and each contributes something distinct. More simultaneous support is not more development.**

03I established that two capacities can be held together.

03J asks the equally important inverse question:

> **When should they not be?**

## No compatibility score

> ## CAPACITY-INTERFERENCE-NONSCORING-LAW-01
>
> **The system must not rank or score capacity combinations as cognitively compatible, incompatible, optimal, advanced, or efficient.**

A pair may work well:

- for one member;
- in one inquiry;
- at one moment;

and become distracting elsewhere.

Compatibility is not a stable property of the person.

## Member report outranks behavioral inference

> ## CAPACITY-INTERFERENCE-NONINFERENCE-LAW-01
>
> **The system must not infer cognitive overload, confusion, resistance, or incapacity from pauses, latency, correction rate, gaze, navigation, or other behavioral telemetry.**

The member may report:

- these work clearly together;
- there is tension;
- I am not sure.

That report has standing for the current encounter.

## Coherence questions

A composed chord may be checked with three member-facing questions:

1. **Can I still tell what each capacity is asking?**
2. **Is each one revealing something distinct?**
3. **Does holding both increase clarity—or split my attention?**

These are reflection prompts.

They are not a test the member must pass.

## Interference does not mean one capacity is wrong

If tension appears, possible movements include:

- keep both;
- release the first;
- release the second;
- use them sequentially instead;
- dissolve all support.

The system must not choose automatically.

## Sequence as relief, not progression

Moving from composition to sequence means:

> **these may be easier to use one at a time here**

not:

> **you should master them in order**

Sequence is one attentional arrangement.

Composition is another.

Neither is developmentally higher.

## Distinct contribution law

> ## CAPACITY-DISTINCT-CONTRIBUTION-LAW-01
>
> **A capacity should not remain in a composition merely because it is available. If the member no longer experiences a distinct contribution from it, release must remain easy.**

This is not a mandate to remove it.

The member may still choose to keep both.

## Unsure is first-class

The member may say:

> **I am not sure whether these help together.**

Soullab should not interpret uncertainty as evidence that the chord is failing.

Possible movement:

> stay with it briefly
> use one
> use neither
> return later

## No overload identity

A moment of attentional tension must never become:

> “This member cannot handle multi-capacity work.”

Encounter difficulty is not identity.

## Failure conditions

03J fails if:

- the system assigns a compatibility score;
- MAIA chooses which capacity to drop;
- behavioral telemetry is treated as evidence of overload;
- tension becomes a cognitive deficit claim;
- sequencing is presented as remedial or developmentally lower;
- the system treats more capacities as inherently better;
- the member cannot keep both despite reported tension;
- “unsure” is forced toward a decision.

## Governing sentence

> **Support should become simpler when simplicity serves attention—not because the person failed complexity.**
