# SOULLAB-FIELD-TOPOLOGY-01R3B — Fieldness + Widening Composition Witness

**Class:** design only
**Frozen R3:** `c9333862ed2f8a68dc08cc2a9b975e081875a23f`
**Runtime authority:** none
**Target:** future presentation replacement for `LivingConstellationPanel`

## Purpose

R3B refines only the visual composition after founder adjudication.

The hierarchy and member-facing wording from R3 are preserved.

The correction is visual:

- less container / card grammar;
- more encompassing field;
- widening as change of spatial scale;
- additional Living Field material emerging through spatial disclosure;
- Vision / Practice remaining locally focused;
- no member-facing product switcher.

## Composition change

R3 used a large bordered panel containing repeated rectangular nodes.

R3B removes the enclosing panel and card surfaces.

Material is represented as points of presence inside subtle concentric horizons:

- a small point marks location;
- label and excerpt remain readable;
- authorship / standing remains attached beneath;
- no line connects one node to another;
- the rings describe field-of-view only, not semantic relation.

The prototype navigation switcher used for R3 review is absent from the member-facing composition.

## Living Field — quiet state

The quiet state shows:

- the Living Field orientation;
- **You are here**;
- three admitted local points;
- **Develop something**;
- **Meet others through your work**;
- **See the wider field** / **Other directions**.

The surrounding rings establish that the visible points sit inside a larger field.

There is no Living Field peer card and no containing dashboard panel.

## Living Field — widened state

Widening changes the field itself.

The horizon expands and additional material becomes visible around the previously visible points:

- Body & Rhythm;
- Family;
- Place;
- Future Self.

There is no `More of the Living Field` box.

The existing points remain in place conceptually while the viewport reveals more of the surrounding field.

The widened state explicitly states:

> The view widens; the field itself changes scale.

This is the governing interaction metaphor for later implementation.

## Vision and Practice

Vision Studio remains foregrounded by:

> **Develop what wants to become more real.**

Practice Field remains foregrounded by:

> **Tend how your work meets other people.**

Each perspective uses the same field grammar:

- local material as points;
- one optional adjacent direction;
- **Widen to Living Field**;
- **Return to where you were**.

The wider horizon is present but quieter than foreground material.

Vision ↔ Practice adjacency remains navigational only and is not drawn as an edge.

## Mobile witness

Absolute desktop placement compressed too tightly in the first R3B mobile render.

The witness caught text collisions before sealing.

Mobile now preserves the field horizon while allowing points to flow vertically in a single readable sequence.

This is intentionally not a return to card UI:

- no bordered node containers;
- no tiled sections;
- no peer-domain cards;
- spatial rings remain behind the sequence;
- provenance remains attached to each point;
- adjacency follows foreground material;
- widen / return remain last.

The hierarchy therefore survives mobile without forcing desktop spatial coordinates onto a narrow viewport.

## R3B standing

The refined composition now expresses:

> **presence → constellation → horizon → possible movement**

rather than:

> panel → cards → secondary cards → buttons

The field remains quiet enough to orient without becoming decorative or implying hidden relationships.

Widening is a perspectival act rather than disclosure of another dashboard section.
