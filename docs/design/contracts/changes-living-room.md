# CHANGES-UX-01 — Living Change Room Experience Architecture + Visual Authority

**Programme:** SOULLAB-LIVING-ORIENTATION / CHANGES-UX-01  
**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** DESIGN + VISUAL AUTHORITY CANDIDATE ONLY · NO LIVE ROUTE MUTATION · NO API / SCHEMA CHANGE

## 1. Core thesis

Changes is the House room for **living through movement over time**.

It is not a change-management dashboard, a divination wrapper, a timeline report, or a collection of AI tools.

The member should feel:

> **I have returned to a passage of my life that is still moving.**

The room keeps the lived Change primary while allowing time, symbolic practice, MAIA, and reflection to deepen the encounter.

## 2. Governing experiential laws

1. **Presence before representation.**
   The member encounters the Change before metadata, type, status, or tools.

2. **One inquiry becoming deeper.**
   Arrival, noticing, consultation, MAIA encounter, pattern, and integration are states of relationship inside one continuous room.

3. **The lived Change has authority.**
   Change type, I Ching, Council, MAIA, and pattern views are secondary lenses. None may visually or semantically replace the member's lived change.

4. **Time is constitutive, not decorative.**
   Changes differs from Dream because its native dimension is movement through time.

5. **Phenomenon first; classification second.**
   The member first says what happened. Reflection / dream / setback / synchronicity / breakthrough labels remain optional secondary distinctions.

6. **Every abstraction returns to experience.**
   Any symbolic interpretation, pattern, or MAIA hypothesis must offer a path back to a lived moment, member-authored words, or an explicit present-tense question.

7. **More intelligence, less apparatus.**
   Existing intelligence should disappear into the room instead of becoming visible machinery.

8. **The room may evoke movement but must not tell the member what their change means.**

## 3. Existing substrate to preserve exactly

CHANGES-UX-01 does not replace the existing Change object or its runtime capabilities.

Preserve:

- studio_changes identity and ownership;
- title / description;
- change type;
- lifecycle status;
- I Ching cast, changing lines, relating hexagram, interpretation;
- Council result;
- MAIA mentor reflection and follow-up intention;
- change experiences;
- relationship / journal / reflection / idea provenance;
- exact return-to-source;
- explicit House crossings;
- member authorship of target meaning;
- existing APIs and persistence.

No schema change is authorized by this act.

## 4. Canonical experiential states

These are not wizard steps and carry no completion percentage.

### A. Arrival
Human question:

> **What is changing?**

For an existing Change:

> **You are here. This is what has been moving.**

Primary content:
- the Change title / member-authored description;
- a quiet sense of where it began;
- one present-tense invitation.

No I Ching prompt dominates arrival.

### B. Name
Purpose: give movement enough form to return to it.

The member may name:
- what is changing;
- what this movement feels like now;
- optional change type only after the member's words exist.

Naming is not classification ceremony.

### C. Living Change
This is the canonical home state for an existing Change.

It holds:
- the member's description;
- where it began;
- what has happened since;
- what feels current now;
- quiet access to deeper lenses.

The Change remains visually central.

### D. Notice
Primary invitation:

> **What happened?**

The member records a lived event before choosing any secondary type.

Optional secondary distinctions:
- reflection;
- field event;
- breakthrough;
- setback;
- dream;
- synchronicity.

### E. Consult
Symbolic consultation is invited, never automatic.

Primary gesture:

> **Consult the I Ching**

Three Coins and Yarrow Stalks are ways of entering the consultation, not administrative radio-button controls.

The cast may illuminate the Change but never become its authority.

### F. Encounter
MAIA enters from inside the Change, not as a detached assistant panel.

MAIA should:
- reflect one precise feature;
- open one live question;
- preserve the difference between member evidence and MAIA hypothesis;
- avoid declaring what the Change “is.”

The Change remains visible or easily reopenable throughout the encounter.

### G. Pattern
The member may widen attention across time.

Pattern can show:
- recurring phrases;
- turning points;
- contradictions;
- dreams;
- setbacks / breakthroughs;
- meaningful temporal spacing;
- member-ratified themes.

It must not produce a score, diagnosis, stage assignment, or deterministic arc.

### H. Integrate
Primary question:

> **What are you carrying now?**

Possible gestures:
- keep an intention;
- write in Journal;
- carry something into Reflection;
- return to a Relationship;
- make a Decision;
- simply leave.

Integration is closure without forced completion.

### I. Return / Crossing
Return is part of the experience, not navigation residue.

The member must be able to see:
- where this Change began;
- what other House place it came from;
- how to return to that exact source when possible.

## 5. Spatial model

Desktop uses a three-part House composition:

1. **Left membrane** — House orientation and room identity.
2. **Living field** — the Change itself, time, and the current state of attention.
3. **Right membrane / MAIA edge** — MAIA available but not dominant.

The center is not a dashboard grid.

The central composition should support:
- one stable Change anchor;
- temporal movement;
- one currently foregrounded action;
- contextual deepening without modal replacement.

Mobile collapses into one vertical field while preserving:
- Change identity near the top;
- one primary gesture at a time;
- easy return to the lived Change from any deep state;
- provenance and source return without sidebars.

## 6. Material and visual character

Changes belongs to the same House as Dream and Journal but must remain distinct.

### Shared with the House
- editorial serif hierarchy;
- restrained brass / gold;
- dark membranes;
- material surfaces;
- abstract MAIA presence;
- generous negative space;
- human verbs instead of software labels;
- subtle ambient motion;
- provenance as lived orientation.

### Distinct to Changes
Changes should feel like an **atmospheric threshold / weather front across time**.

Allowed language:
- horizon-like tonal shifts;
- warm / cool atmospheric movement;
- traces and temporal seams;
- subtle directional gradients;
- luminous pressure zones;
- sparse marks of passage;
- gathering / opening / settling;
- changing density;
- faint connective lines where provenance or time requires them.

Avoid:
- literal mountains;
- paths;
- butterflies;
- phoenixes;
- doors;
- storms as illustration;
- clocks;
- progress bars;
- “transformation” icon clichés;
- mystical stock imagery;
- generic glass cards;
- neon dashboard palettes.

## 7. Living-field behavior by state

### Arrival
Wide, unresolved, low contrast.
The field says: **there is room for this to become visible.**

### Living Change
A stable central region emerges.
The field says: **this movement has continuity.**

### Notice
Attention gathers around the new event.
The field says: **something happened here.**

### Consult
Ambient motion quiets.
Symbolic material gets a contained, ceremonial zone.
The field says: **look with another lens, but keep your footing.**

### Encounter
Two-field polarity appears between lived Change and MAIA.
The field says: **something is being met.**

### Pattern
The field stretches longitudinally.
Temporal traces become legible without becoming a chart.
The field says: **something has been moving over time.**

### Integrate
Visual complexity reduces.
Warmth and negative space increase.
The field says: **you may carry something, or nothing.**

## 8. Information hierarchy

The default order is:

1. member-authored Change;
2. present-tense orientation;
3. lived moments over time;
4. provenance / where this began;
5. invited symbolic or relational lenses;
6. system metadata only where needed.

Status and type should never visually outrank the member's words.

## 9. I Ching placement law

I Ching is a **practice inside Changes**, not the structural owner of Changes.

Forbidden:
- auto-opening the caster when a Change has no hexagram;
- implying an uncased Change is incomplete;
- putting casting controls before the member encounters the Change;
- using hexagram identity as the primary Change header.

Allowed:
- an explicit “Consult the I Ching” gesture;
- a contained consultation state;
- returning from the reading to the same lived Change;
- relating the reading back to specific lived material.

## 10. MAIA placement law

MAIA is one MAIA moving through the House.

In Changes:
- she is available at the edge before invitation;
- she enters only by explicit member gesture;
- she speaks from the Change's actual context;
- she preserves long-encounter continuity;
- she distinguishes evidence, pattern, and hypothesis;
- she never becomes a second Change record or duplicate memory system.

## 11. Provenance law

“Where this began” is not metadata chrome.

It is part of orientation.

A source relationship, journal entry, reflection, or idea should appear as a quiet origin seam and exact return path.

The receiving Change remains member-authored.

## 12. Temporal architecture

Time is represented by **lived moments**, not analytics.

The room may show:
- beginning;
- recent movement;
- turning moments;
- present location;
- open future.

It may not imply:
- inevitable trajectory;
- predicted outcome;
- completion percentage;
- developmental superiority;
- “progress” unless the member has named it that way.

## 13. Accessibility and motion

- full keyboard access;
- reduced-motion removes ambient field movement;
- readable text contrast in all states;
- minimum 44px primary targets on touch;
- no meaning encoded by color alone;
- timeline / temporal field remains understandable linearly;
- mobile preserves source return and Change identity.

## 14. Prototype acceptance questions

The visual prototype passes only if a founder can answer yes to these:

1. Does this feel like a place I return to, not a feature sheet?
2. Is my Change more visually important than the tools around it?
3. Can I feel time without seeing a project-management timeline?
4. Does I Ching feel invited rather than imposed?
5. Does MAIA feel present without taking over the room?
6. Does the room belong beside Dream, Journal, and Relationships without copying them?
7. Can the field suggest movement without defining what the movement means?
8. On mobile, does it still feel like the same room?
