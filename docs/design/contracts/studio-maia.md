# Studio MAIA — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

A practitioner talks with the same MAIA who inhabits the rest of Soullab, but
from within the professional Studio context. MAIA may teach models, craft,
frameworks, and reflective distinctions while practitioner judgment remainswith the human practitioner.

T8B preserves the existing Studio experience and supplies only the hidden,
server-adjudicated room context needed for lawful teaching.

## Forbidden here

- the Studio surface declaration being treated as proof that someone is a practitioner
- MAIA directing treatment or client action
- practitioner learning becoming a durable learner profile
- coaching-craft teaching entering the member's human coaching relationship
- a second Studio-specific MAIA mind

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. It is the same MAIA conversation architecture and the same
constitutional hierarchy of human over system authority.

**Distinct room?** Yes. Studio MAIA is practitioner-facing professional
conversation, not the general member room and not Now What? My Coaching.
