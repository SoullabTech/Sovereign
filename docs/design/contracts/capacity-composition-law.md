# CAPACITY-COMPOSITION-LAW-01
## Temporary cognitive chords without executive capture

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **Two capacities may be held together when the present inquiry benefits from their simultaneous use, but the composition remains temporary, separable, reversible, and member-governed.**

Capacity composition is not:

- a cognitive style;
- a personality profile;
- a best-practice recipe;
- a developmental tier;
- a mandatory workflow;
- a hidden MAIA strategy.

It is a temporary working arrangement.

## Composition versus sequence

### Sequence

> one capacity → inquiry changes → another capacity

The second movement follows from what emerged.

### Composition

> two capacities held concurrently

Both remain active at the same time because their different questions help illuminate the same inquiry.

Neither implies the other.

## Capacity identity preservation

> ## CAPACITY-COMPOSITION-IDENTITY-LAW-01
>
> **A composed capacity must remain identifiable as itself inside the composition.**

Composition must not blur two capacities into an opaque meta-tool.

The member should still be able to see:

- which capacities are active;
- what each foregrounds;
- which one to release;
- whether the combination still helps.

## Member authority

The strongest composition is:

> **member composes the capacities directly.**

MAIA may, after explicit invitation, expose a small bounded set of governed combinations.

MAIA may not:

- rank the pairs;
- choose a “best” composition;
- infer that a member needs a specific combination;
- persist a composition as a cognitive profile.

## No hidden synthesis

A capacity chord does not produce a new interpretation merely by existing.

Example:

> Compare versions + Look for counterevidence

means:

- keep differences across versions visible;
- keep complicating evidence visible.

It does not mean:

> “The revision failed because…”

## Temporary standing

> ## CAPACITY-CHORD-EPHEMERAL-LAW-01
>
> **A capacity chord has encounter-local standing unless the member explicitly authors a reusable practice under separate authority.**

03I does not authorize reusable practices.

## Reshaping

The member may replace one capacity while keeping the other.

Example:

> Compare versions + Counterevidence

may become:

> Compare versions + Change scale

without being labeled:

> upgrade / progression / next step.

## Release

The member may:

- release one capacity and keep the other;
- dissolve the whole composition;
- return to no support.

No-support remains successful orchestration.

## Naming

The member may optionally give a temporary composition their own working name.

Example:

> “Compare without rushing to conclude.”

That label belongs to the member.

The system does not infer it.

## Failure conditions

03I fails if:

- capacities lose their individual identities;
- the composition becomes a fixed workflow;
- MAIA ranks combinations by hidden fit;
- the member cannot release one capacity independently;
- compositions are persisted as cognitive traits;
- more capacities are treated as better;
- the system implies that composition is superior to one capacity or no support.

## Governing sentence

> **Composition should increase freedom of attention, not create a more elaborate form of dependence.**
