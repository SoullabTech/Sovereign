## Purpose

SS-08 operationalizes the distinction between assistance and transfer.

The harness preserves four conditions:

- **A — baseline / unassisted**
- **B — full support**
- **C — light scaffold**
- **D — novel unassisted transfer**

The critical comparison is:

> **D versus A**

B greater than A shows that assistance helps.

D greater than A is evidence consistent with learning / transfer.
