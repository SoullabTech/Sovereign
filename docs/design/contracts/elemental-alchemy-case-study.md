# Elemental Alchemy — Case Study Experience Contract

## What this room is for

A visitor should be able to inspect how Writer's Studio approaches a demanding,
already-published manuscript without being asked to accept a testimonial or a
before/after victory claim on faith.

The case study therefore separates five things that marketing language often
collapses: source, understanding, proposed intervention, author choice, and effect.

## Arrival

> **Case Study 001 — Elemental Alchemy**

The status **Active case study** is visible at the threshold. While the work is
ongoing, the page must not imply completion.

## Evidence law

A future before/after example is admissible here only when it can show:

1. the original passage,
2. the Studio's reading of what is at stake,
3. the proposed intervention,
4. Kelly's actual choice — including rejection or reshaping,
5. the resulting passage in context,
6. what can and cannot reasonably be concluded from that example.

The structure is designed so rejected edits are legitimate evidence. The goal is
not to prove that the AI was right; it is to show whether the editorial relationship
helped the book.

## Forbidden here

- a final 5/5 claim while the refinement remains active
- invented improvement percentages
- selective before/after examples that hide the author's rejection or revision
- presenting AI novelty as evidence of editorial quality
- treating one polished paragraph as proof of whole-book improvement
- describing authorship preservation without showing where the author exercised choice

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. Claims remain modest, sourced, and subordinate to the human
author.

**Distinct room?** Yes. This is not a sales landing page. Its subject is the
inspectable editorial relationship and the evidence it produces.
