## Purpose

SS-03 tests whether the member can move around one stable source through explicit perspectives without losing the source or being steered toward one preferred interpretation.

Prototype grammar:

> **anchor → choose perspective → hold → choose second perspective → compare → notice / leave open → return**

## Perspectives

The prototype includes six apertures:

- Time
- Scale
- Evidence
- Relation
- Agency
- Possibility

These are not six truths.

They are explicit ways of looking.

## Anchor preservation

The source remains physically central and unchanged while perspectives move around it.

Each lens has an explicit line to the same anchor.

No perspective replaces the source.

## Comparison

A selected perspective can be held while the member chooses a second.

The compare state says:

> **Same source. Two apertures.**

Neither is ranked.

The member may name a noticing or leave the comparison unresolved.

## Member synthesis

A member-shaped noticing remains local browser state.

It is not stored or promoted into autobiographical truth.

## Return

Return removes selected perspective state and restores the unchanged source.

## Acceptance questions

1. Does the source remain the center of gravity?
2. Do the six lenses feel like useful movements rather than a conceptual menu?
3. Can two perspectives be held together without implying a winner?
4. Does Evidence naturally invite counterevidence rather than confirmation?
5. Does Relation avoid mind-reading?
6. Does Possibility expand options without prediction?
7. Can the member leave comparison unresolved?
8. Does member-shaped noticing feel like synthesis rather than MAIA interpretation?
9. Does return restore orientation?
10. Does this begin to train perspective mobility rather than merely perform it for the member?
