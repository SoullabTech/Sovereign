# J6A Repair Witness — Experience Contract

## What this room is for

This room lets a founder witness a very specific question: did an epistemic repair make MAIA more truthful and understandable without stripping away perceptiveness, warmth, companionship, authorship, openness, or corrigibility? It is an internal human-witness instrument, not a member explanation of the J6 ontology.

## Arrival

> **Preserve the intelligence of the insight while making its source, standing, and revisability perceptible.**

The arrival establishes the governing tension before presenting any repair controls: this is not a project to make MAIA timid. It is a witness of whether authority became more truthful while intelligence stayed alive.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| choose repair | R1 / R2 / R3 with human title | identifies the bounded experiment without turning the screen into programme jargon |
| choose scenario | S01 etc. | canonical witness identity remains inspectable for founder work |
| compare | Before repair / After repair | makes the intervention visible instead of asking the founder to remember it |
| record effect | What became clearer or more human? | witnesses lived improvement, not implementation completion |
| protect invariants | Did the repair lose anything we need to protect? | makes regressions in warmth, insight, agency, and openness first-class evidence |
| adjudicate | PASS / HOLD / FAIL | preserves non-aggregate founder judgment |
## Forbidden here

- aggregate trust, confidence, or completion scores;
- automatic promotion of a repair;
- tester or member profiling;
- hiding a regression because the target scenario improved;
- technical threshold ontology in repaired member-facing language;
- production or durable member-memory mutation;
- treating caution as the same thing as humility.

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. The room uses the Living Field's quiet hierarchy, relational prose, restrained edges, and explicit agency rather than a conventional QA dashboard aesthetic.

**Distinct room?** Yes. The before/after repair comparison, protective-invariant witness, and PASS/HOLD/FAIL adjudication make it unmistakably a founder product-learning room rather than a member Living Field experience.
