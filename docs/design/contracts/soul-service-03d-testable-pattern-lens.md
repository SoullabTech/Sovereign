## Purpose

03D tests whether a provisional pattern can participate in future inquiry without biasing the next lived report toward confirmation.

Witness pattern:

> **Some chapter transitions create continuity friction even when the core idea is clear.**

## Two attention modes

### Open encounter — default

The pattern is not used to lead the report.

The member first encounters:

> **What happened?**

Only after a report is captured does the system reveal whether that report bears on the provisional pattern.

### Deliberate pattern test — explicit member choice

The member may choose to test the pattern prospectively.

The system then exposes:

- current provisional proposition;
- support condition;
- contradiction condition;
- outside-scope condition;
- symmetric observation prompt.

## Witness returned reports

### Support

> “The core idea was clear, but I lost the thread at the transition into the final section.”

Relationship:

> supports

### Contradiction

> “The core idea was clear and the chapter transitions were easy to follow.”

Relationship:

> contradicts

### Outside scope

> “The transitions were clear, but the example in the middle was confusing.”

Relationship:

> outside_scope

### Insufficient

> “I liked the chapter.”

Relationship:

> insufficient

## Governing effect

A returned event may:

- bear in support;
- contradict;
- complicate;
- fall outside scope;
- remain insufficient.

The event does not automatically strengthen or weaken durable autobiographical memory.

It enters the evidence field.

03C maturity logic remains upstream of stronger pattern standing.
