# PATTERN-AGING-AND-REVALIDATION-LAW-01
## How a pattern remains historical without claiming permanent current applicability

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **A pattern remains bound to the source, context, and evidentiary conditions under which it was earned. Continuity of identity does not imply continuity of pattern applicability.**

A provisional pattern may be responsibly supported at time A and no longer apply at time B.

The historical evidence remains true history.

Its present relevance must be re-evaluated.

## Source-version boundary

> ## PATTERN-SOURCE-VERSION-BOUNDARY-LAW-01
>
> **When the source region materially implicated by a pattern changes, the pattern may not silently retain current prospective authority over the changed source.**

Example:

Pattern on manuscript `ea:v1`:

> some chapter transitions create continuity friction even when the core idea is clear.

Writer then materially revises those transitions in:

> `ea:v2`

The v1 evidence remains historical evidence about v1.

It does not automatically establish the same proposition about v2.

Current standing becomes:

> **REVALIDATION_REQUIRED**

## Aging is not decay of truth

Time does not make a historical observation false.

Time changes what may responsibly be presumed current.

> **Old evidence remains evidence. Old applicability is a present question.**

## No truth-by-longevity

> ## PATTERN-LONGEVITY-NONCONFIRMATION-LAW-01
>
> **A pattern does not become stronger merely because it has existed for a long time or has been repeatedly recalled.**

Repeated retrieval is not independent confirmation.

Repeated display is not evidence.

## Applicability states

These are states of a proposition relative to a current context, not states of a person.

### CURRENT_PROVISIONAL

The provisional pattern is still being considered within the source/context conditions in which it was earned.

### REVALIDATION_REQUIRED

A material source/context change makes current applicability uncertain.

Historical standing remains intact.

### REVALIDATED_PROVISIONAL

New relevant lived evidence supports the proposition in the changed source/context.

The pattern becomes provisional in the new context.

It does not become stronger merely because it survived a version change.

### CURRENT_CONTESTED

New current evidence materially contradicts or complicates applicability in the changed context.

### DORMANT

The member/system is not currently using the pattern to guide attention.

Historical evidence remains available.

Dormancy is not disproof.

### RETIRED_FROM_CURRENT_USE

The pattern is no longer used prospectively in the current field.

Historical evidence and provenance remain inspectable.

Retirement does not delete history.

## Revalidation posture

Revalidation begins from:

> **Does this still apply here?**

not:

> **Where is the pattern showing up again?**

The default evidence capture should inherit 03D:

> open encounter first.

If the member explicitly chooses a prospective test, the influence must remain transparent and symmetric.

## Current-source priority

When source identity is continuous but version changes:

> current source truth first.

Then:

> historical pattern if useful.

Never:

> old pattern first, current source interpreted through it.

## Dormancy law

> ## PATTERN-DORMANCY-LAW-01
>
> **A provisional pattern may become dormant without being deleted, disproved, or kept active in attention.**

Dormancy reduces prospective attentional authority.

It does not rewrite historical evidence.

## Retirement law

> ## PATTERN-RETIREMENT-LAW-01
>
> **Retiring a pattern from current use removes its prospective influence while preserving the evidence, provenance, and historical proposition that once had standing.**

The system must not erase the past to respect present change.

## Governing sentence

> **Memory may preserve that a pattern once fit. The present must decide whether it still does.**
