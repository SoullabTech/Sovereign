# Develop — Experience Contract

> ⛔ **THIS CONTRACT IS NOT YET DISCHARGED.** `screenshot_desktop`, `screenshot_mobile` and
> `experience_verification` are deliberately absent, so the design-canon gate still fails and the
> surface still cannot ship. They are filled by the founder after the walk described in §Falsifiers,
> in the founder's own words. **Nothing in this document may be read as evidence that the walk
> happened.**

## What this room is for

A writer has already asked MAIA to read their Work, and the reading is here. They are not writing,
not arriving, and not judging the shape of the Work. They are standing in front of a set of things
MAIA noticed, and the question they have is the ordinary one a person has in front of any list of
observations about their own work: *of all this, what actually deserves my attention right now?*

Until now the writer could only answer that themselves, by reading everything and choosing. This
room lets them ask. That is the entire addition — **one question, asked by the writer, answered
with one thing.**

The room is not a review queue, a findings screen, or a set of tasks. What MAIA offers is a place
to look, and looking is the writer's act.

## Arrival

> **Here is what I noticed when I read this. Take what you want from it.**

Arrival is unchanged by this addition, and that is the first thing the room owes. The reading is
present in its own order — the order MAIA met it, never sorted, never ranked, never filtered.
Nothing is highlighted. **Nothing is preselected, and no observation is recommended on load.**

Somewhere unhurried among that, one quiet door: *ask MAIA what is worth looking at.* It is a
sentence the writer may never press. A writer who ignores it forever has lost nothing and is
missing nothing; the reading is complete without it.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Language used | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| commission a selection | "ask MAIA what is worth looking at" | the writer asks; the room never offers first. The verb is *ask*, so the act is unmistakably theirs |
| receive the offer | "She would start with the observation marked <key> below." | *would start with* is a suggestion about where to look. Not "you should", not "the most important", not "priority" |
| advance | "what else?" | the writer's own words for wanting another. It asks for one more thing, not for the rest of a list |
| end it | "leave it" | leaving is ordinary and costs nothing. Not "close", not "done", not "dismiss" — dismissal is a different act with different consequences |
| take up a standing | (unchanged — `YourStanding`) | keeping / dismissing an observation stays exactly where it was, reached by its own deliberate gesture |

**One at a time, always.** MAIA holds an internal ordering; the room receives a single key. There is
no second-best shown beside it, no "and also", no count of how many remain.

## What never reaches the room

```text
the ordering            discarded with the request
the confidence          never returned, never rendered, never a percentage
why this one over that  not offered as a justification — the writer may ask her
                        in the conversation, which is where reasons belong
```

A number beside a developmental observation reads as authority. There is no number.

## The commission, and its edges

A commission is a single asking. It begins when the writer presses the door and ends when they
leave it.

- **`what else?` advances only within the same commission.** It never repeats what was already
  offered in that asking.
- **Leaving ends the commission.** Asking again is a new asking, which starts from nothing: what
  was offered before confers no authority on what is offered next.
- **Ignoring an offer is not a standing act. Rejecting one and asking for another is not a standing
  act.** Moving past something changes what this asking will show next and *nothing else about the
  Work*.
- **Dismissing an observation remains a separate, deliberate act**, taken in the standing surface,
  and it is sticky until the writer themselves changes it. MAIA may not decide a dismissal has
  expired, and the room may never suggest reversing one.

## The four quiet endings

An asking can end without an offer. All four endings are **true statements, said plainly, that ask
for nothing** — no retry, no "try a different lens", no invitation to undo a decision.

| Ending | What the room says | What it must never do |
|---|---|---|
| she declines | "Nothing here feels worth raising right now." | apologise, or offer something anyway to seem useful |
| nothing is raisable | "There is nothing here she can raise at the moment." | volunteer that the writer dismissed things, or suggest un-dismissing |
| this asking is exhausted | "That is everything she would raise from this reading just now." | imply the writer has failed to engage with the rest |
| the boundary is unmeasured | "MAIA cannot tell how this reading stands to the Work as it is now, so she is not going to choose from it." | pretend it is an empty result, or choose anyway |

⭐ **The fourth is the load-bearing one.** *Not knowing* must never be rendered as *nothing found*.
A room that collapsed an unmeasurable boundary into "nothing to raise" would be telling the writer
something false about their Work in order to avoid saying something awkward about the system.

⭐ **Declining is a real answer, not a failure.** If the room ever reads a decline as an error state
— a retry button, a spinner that returns, an apology — restraint has been converted into
malfunction, and MAIA will be pushed toward manufacturing significance to seem useful.

If the writer asks *why* there is nothing, the truth is available and neutral: an observation was
dismissed, or has been superseded. **Transparency on request is not authority to reopen the
writer's lifecycle decisions.**

## The non-consent boundary

**Arrival, scrolling, reading, hovering, waiting, returning, or reloading is never a commission.**

MAIA does not choose what to raise unless the writer asked in this act. Absence of a specific
question is not permission to answer a general one. Held structurally: the selection path is
reached from exactly one gesture; the request carries an explicit commission field that is not an
anchor and is not inferred from the shape of the request; and a request that names an observation
never reaches the selector at all — the writer's own address always wins, and is resolved exactly,
never by nearest match.

## Forbidden here

- a ranked list, a top-N, a score, a percentage, a confidence, a severity, or a badge
- preselecting, pre-highlighting, auto-scrolling to, or ambiently recommending an observation on
  arrival
- offering a second observation beside the first "for comparison"
- any framing of an offer as a task, a finding to clear, a recommendation, or a priority
- treating an ignored or passed-over offer as a signal of any kind about the writer or the Work
- suggesting that a dismissal be reversed, or reporting the writer's dismissals as a shortfall
- rendering a decline or an unmeasured boundary as an error, a retry, or an apology
- a queue, an inbox, a progress indicator, a "3 of 12", or anything that implies the reading is
  work to be got through
- MAIA volunteering a selection unasked, in this room or from any other surface

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes. Field hierarchy, Press serif and palette, restrained gesture language and
provenance voice are held in common with Studio Home, the Manuscript Room and Structure Review. A
writer crossing in does not change buildings, and the new gesture is one more quiet underlined
sentence among others, not a feature announcing itself.

**Distinct room?** Yes. Structure Review is about the shape of the Work and ends in an act of
authorship. This room is about attention and ends in a conversation. It is the only surface where
MAIA answers *"which of these matters now?"* — and the only one whose central discipline is that
her answer stays a single, unweighted, refusable suggestion.

## Falsifiers — what the walk must actually inspect

Each is a claim that can be **shown false** by the walk. A walk that only confirms that the feature
works has not tested this contract.

```text
W1  ARRIVAL IS UNCHANGED
    Load the room with a reading present and do nothing.
    FALSIFIED IF: any observation is highlighted, scrolled to, reordered, or marked;
    or any network call to the ask route occurs before the writer presses anything.

W2  NO SELECTOR CALL BEFORE THE GESTURE
    Watch the network from load until the press.
    FALSIFIED IF: any request carries a selectionCommission before the explicit act.

W3  ONE OBSERVATION, AND ONLY ONE
    Press the door once.
    FALSIFIED IF: more than one observation is named, or any ordering, count,
    ranking, score or confidence appears in the response body or on screen.

W4  "WHAT ELSE?" DOES NOT REPEAT
    Press "what else?" until the asking is exhausted.
    FALSIFIED IF: a previously offered observation is offered again in the same asking,
    or the exhausted ending reads as a failure rather than a plain statement.

W5  LEAVING ENDS THE COMMISSION
    Press "leave it", then ask again.
    FALSIFIED IF: the second asking skips what the first offered, or resumes it —
    a new asking must start from nothing.

W6  PASSING OVER AN OFFER WRITES NO STANDING
    Read the standing rows before and after a full pass through several offers.
    FALSIFIED IF: any standing changed, or any standing event was recorded, merely
    because the writer advanced past an observation.

W7  THE WRITER'S OWN ADDRESS STILL WINS
    Open a conversation on a specific observation by naming it.
    FALSIFIED IF: the selector is consulted, or a different observation is substituted.

W8  A DECLINE READS AS AN ANSWER
    Reach a decline or an empty ending.
    FALSIFIED IF: it renders as an error, offers a retry, apologises, blames the
    writer's dismissals, or suggests reversing one.

W9  THE OFFER IS REFUSABLE
    Ignore the offer entirely and carry on using the room.
    FALSIFIED IF: the room nags, re-offers, blocks, or changes what is available
    because the offer was not taken.

W10 MOBILE IS THE SAME ROOM
    Repeat W1, W3 and W4 at 390x844.
    FALSIFIED IF: the gesture becomes prominent, the offer becomes a card or banner,
    or the endings become toasts or alerts.
```

⚠️ **W6 is the one most likely to pass by accident and matter most.** Offer memory and standing are
different kinds of thing — one is MAIA's conduct within a single asking, the other is the writer's
authority over their own Work. If advancing past an offer ever writes a standing, the room has
quietly converted inattention into a decision the writer never made.
