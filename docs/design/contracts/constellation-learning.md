# Founder learning — evidence before conclusions

## Arrival

**Doorway learning**

**What people choose to tell us.**

The report opens on a fixed time window and the actual state of the feedback source. The first paragraph cannot call missing data zero or call feedback proof of improvement.

## Human activity

Kelly needs to understand what people deliberately say about working in a Studio, without having to manage multiple analytics dashboards or read private work. The report keeps evidence units small and their meanings explicit. It does not claim to explain why someone arrived, left, declined an invitation, or returned.

## Gestures

The report is read-only. **Read the latest report** refreshes the same fixed-window view; it does not change a campaign. **Source, boundaries, and what this does not establish** reveals method and provenance. There is no per-member drilldown, cohort filter, date picker, private-note export, or automatic optimization.

## Three distinct observations

- A successful read with no submissions shows zero submissions, not zero usefulness.
- A positive count below five distinct contributors is withheld, not shown as zero.
- An unavailable or malformed source shows unavailable, never an empty funnel.

Campaign attribution, first useful act, return, referral outcome, and contribution require their own evidence. They remain named questions, not invented charts.

## Limits of the witness

The synthetic values were six lost-thread submissions from five contributors, twelve voice-preservation submissions from six contributors, and three misunderstanding submissions from one contributor. The last count was not displayed. These are invented fixtures, not user records. A separate one-contributor fixture containing one hundred submissions remained withheld.

The temporary development preview is removed before commit. Screenshots retain their SYNTHETIC PREVIEW banner so they cannot be mistaken for campaign results. The real founder page is independently server-authorized before any feedback read.

## Kelly's World

The programme record travels through the existing read-only programme projector. This is documentary integration, not a new live Monitor feed or an installed Desktop update. Growth analytics does not inherit authority over the separate Living Constellation member graph.
