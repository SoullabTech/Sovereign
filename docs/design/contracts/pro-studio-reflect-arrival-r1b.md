# Reflect — from one's work inward

## What this room is for

The first act of a professional who is willing to examine their own experience, before attending to anyone else's records. The member arrives with a question rather than a caseload. MAIA is available as a relationship, not a simulated practitioner or a verdict.

## Arrival

> **Your work begins with you.**

This is an *invitation* in the authenticated member environment. The HTML contains no client data, session history, practice profile, team context, or automatic request to a paid Reflect store. The one primary gesture opens the already-existing free MAIA member encounter. The page tells the truth: the dedicated paid Reflect workspace and permissions are not activated.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Human-language label | Bounded effect |
|---|---|---|
| Enter conversation | Begin reflecting with MAIA | Navigate to /maia/encounter, existing free MAIA room; no new grant |
| Return | Return to Soullab | Navigate to /home |

## House and room boundaries

Same House: meaningful reading field, an invitation rather than an AI assertion, restrained material variation, explicit human-authored significance.

Distinct room: neither a marketing paywall nor a clinician dashboard. A professional's growth is the subject, not a managed client object.

## What this evidence does not license

Do not infer that the link can carry an entire Reflect conversation into MAIA, or that a subsequent MAIA utterance is stored as a practitioner-authored teaching. No paid entitlement source, clinical document scope, data migration, billing or recording consent was added or reviewed. This is a candidate pending independent source review and founder visual adjudication.
