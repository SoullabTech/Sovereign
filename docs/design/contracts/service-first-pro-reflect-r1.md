# Reflect — sustain the helper as a whole person

## What this room is for

Professionals and helpers often attend to the lives of others. This room must leave space for their own lives—not as another improvement obligation, but as legitimate care. The existing heading remains **Your work begins with you**, and the new paragraph names **rest, boundaries and the questions you carry**.

The message *Your well-being is not last* is a principle of respect, not a mandatory self-reflection assignment. It must not imply that all visitors are professional clinicians, currently serve others, or need to demonstrate a contribution to deserve support.
