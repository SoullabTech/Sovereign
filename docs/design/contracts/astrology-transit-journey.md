# Astrology — Current Transit Journey

This contract governs the member-facing transit field added under **What is alive now**.

The transit journey is a meeting of two different symbolic acts: the moving sky and the natal chart. The runtime must not collapse them into one undifferentiated interpretation. Transit-specific tradition describes the moving planet, aspect form, and contacted natal function; natal aspect material is reused only as context for how the contacted point is already woven into the birth chart.

## Experience boundary

The field proceeds in an explicit order:

1. calculated contact and timing;
2. why the activation is surfaced;
3. symbolic tradition as a lens;
4. whole-chart context;
5. possible human expression;
6. the member's own meaning;
7. optional wheel focus, Reflection, or explicit MAIA handoff.

No layer may silently promote itself into the layer below it. In particular, symbolic tradition is not astronomy, possible expression is not prediction, and a saved Reflection contains only words the member authored.

## Temporal truth

Applying and separating state follows actual motion, including retrograde motion. Exact dates are searched rather than inferred. A station that prevents perfection is described as a closest approach rather than assigned a fabricated exact date. Open-ended windows are labelled rather than guessed.

## House Wheel and MAIA

Wheel and field navigation are bidirectional only for verified contacts. MAIA receives a transit activation only after the member explicitly invokes the handoff. Nothing is sent merely because the field is opened or explored.

## Standing

This contract witnesses the T3–T6 candidate only. Merge and deployment remain separate admission acts.
