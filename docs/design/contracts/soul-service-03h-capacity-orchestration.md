## Purpose

03H tests whether multiple capacities can be coordinated without making MAIA the member's permanent executive function.

## Synthetic inquiry

> Two reader reports about the same revised passage disagree. One says the transition now works; the other still loses the thread. The author has not decided whether another revision is needed.

No support is active initially.

## Member movements

The member may:

- stay with the inquiry without support;
- choose a capacity directly;
- explicitly ask MAIA to show a few possible ways of working;
- reject all offered capacities;
- choose one;
- later choose a second capacity if the inquiry changes.

## MAIA offer boundary

When invited, MAIA's prototype role is limited to displaying at most three non-ranked capacity options plus:

> **None right now**

The options are governed, not model-ranked.

No hidden fit score exists.

## Sequential use

The member may choose:

> Compare versions

and later, if the comparison reveals a contradiction:

> Look for counterevidence

The second movement is member-initiated.

The UI does not label this:

> Step 2.

## Acceptance questions

1. Is no support active initially?
2. Can the member stay with the inquiry unaided?
3. Can the member choose a capacity directly?
4. Does MAIA appear only after explicit invitation?
5. Are MAIA offers bounded to a small set?
6. Are offers non-ranked?
7. Is none / no support first-class?
8. Can the member reject the whole offer set?
9. Can a second capacity be chosen later without checklist framing?
10. Does the first capacity stop being privileged when the inquiry changes?
11. Is any notion of developmental score absent?
12. Does orchestration feel like support of agency rather than management of cognition?
