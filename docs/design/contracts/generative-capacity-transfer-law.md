# GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-TRANSFER-LAW-01
## Transfer the capacity, not the old frame

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Status:** governing architecture law
**Runtime authority:** none

> **A human capacity may transfer across contexts. The original pattern, evidence, salience, and conclusion do not travel with it by default.**

A capacity such as:

> **Inspect continuity**

may have been practiced in one context.

Its later use in another context must begin from the new context itself.

## Context-boundary law

> ## CAPACITY-TRANSFER-CONTEXT-BOUNDARY-LAW-01
>
> **Transfer carries the form of a capacity, not the content of the source context in which it was first practiced.**

Therefore a transferred capacity must not silently import:

- the original pattern;
- supporting examples;
- counterexamples;
- source version;
- old salience;
- old conclusion;
- old MAIA interpretation.

## Member initiation

> ## GENERATIVE-TRANSFER-INITIATION-LAW-01
>
> **A strong transfer witness begins when the member chooses to apply a capacity in a new context without MAIA first identifying the old pattern there.**

This distinguishes:

- member transfer;
- AI pattern matching.

## Member reshaping

> ## GENERATIVE-CAPACITY-RESHAPING-LAW-01
>
> **A transferred capacity may be renamed, rephrased, recombined, or adapted by the member for the new context without losing its functional lineage.**

Example:

System capacity:

> **Inspect continuity**

Member reshaping:

> **Where does the thread get lost between experience and idea?**

The member's form is not treated as a product taxonomy update.

It is an expression of independent use.

## Generative transfer

The strongest transfer is not repetition.

It is:

> **member selects a useful capacity → applies it in a new domain → reshapes it to fit → uses it without reactivating the original pattern**

## Failure conditions

03G fails if:

- MAIA automatically declares the old pattern is present in the new context;
- historical examples are imported to justify using the capacity;
- the transferred capacity becomes a covert pattern detector;
- the old pattern regains current authority;
- the member cannot adapt the capacity language;
- the system treats its own wording as the canonical form of the human capacity.

## Governing sentence

> **The capacity can travel farther than the pattern that once helped teach it.**
