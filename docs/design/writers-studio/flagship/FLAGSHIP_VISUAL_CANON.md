## §2 · FOUNDER CORRECTIONS — these override the references where they differ

The references are composition authority. ⛔ They are **not** authority for content, capability or
vocabulary. Where a frame shows something below, **the correction governs.**

### 2.1 · `Reader perspective` — ⭐ CORRECTED, NOT REMOVED

⚠️ My read said the tab was out of scope. **That was too strong.** The canon holds both that
`reader-effect` is a permanent non-conclusion **and** that *any later account of possible effect
remains a contestable interpretation.*

⭐ **Founder ruling: keep the capability, rename it.** `Reader (Hypotheses)` → **`Reader
perspective`**, with quiet copy: *Possible reader effects — not predictions.*

| ⛔ Never | ✅ Always possibility-shaped |
|---|---|
| “Readers will…” | “A reader **might** lose the referent here…” |
| “This is confusing.” | “This shift **could** create distance…” |
| “Readers prefer…” | “I **wonder whether** a reader may expect…” |

⛔ It creates no separate developmental-reading truth · ⛔ never turns `reader-effect` into a
conclusion · ✅ textual evidence stays visible and reachable · ⛔ **clicking the view must not
silently commission a new reading.**

### 2.2 · Verdict language — ⛔ BARRED AS FACT

⛔ Out as factual findings: `Stronger transition` · `Potential expansion` · `This is a key passage`
· `Good momentum` · `Rich and coherent` · `Distinctive and authentic` · `This is beautiful` ·
`Especially luminous`.

⭐ **Three-way distinction, frozen:**

```text
PROPERTY OF THE WORK       ⛔ machine verdict
MAIA'S RESPONSE            ✅ contestable relationship
TEXTUAL OBSERVATION        ✅ evidence-grounded
```

| ⛔ Verdict | ✅ Observation | ✅ Stance |
|---|---|---|
| Stronger transition | “The bridge from Chapter 9 into Chapter 10 occurs in one sentence.” | — |
| This is a key passage | “This image appears in four separated sections.” | “I keep returning to this passage.” |
| Especially luminous | — | “I find this passage especially affecting.” |

⛔ **“Key” is out unless “key” is the writer's own declaration.**

### 2.3 · Review tabs are FILTERS, ⛔ never automatic readers — FROZEN

`Structure · Voice · Continuity · Flow · Themes` are ways of viewing **already-admitted** material.

⛔ **Clicking one MUST NOT trigger a new model reading.** Where no reading exists the surface says
so — *“MAIA hasn't read this Work for continuity yet”* — and offers a **separate deliberate
gesture**. ⛔ **No inferred commission from navigation.** (Upholds `A2`: the reader never infers a
lens from its own output.)

### 2.4 · ⭐ PROPOSAL BREADTH IS LOCUS-BOUND AND FACET-INVARIANT — FROZEN

- Hold a **sentence** → MAIA works on **that sentence**.
- Hold a **paragraph** → MAIA may work on **that paragraph**.
- ⛔ She does **not** silently widen to the section.
- ⛔ She does **not** generate more of the member's prose because they chose `Guided`.
- `GUIDED / LEARNING / DIRECT` change **translation and explanation only**.
- ⭐ The **same revision authority and proposal scope** apply in all three.
- `Keep my original` stays first-class · alternatives stay unranked · every candidate still goes
  **Read in context → Apply → Undo**.

⭐ *The risk is not plurality. It is scope creep and increased machine authorship disguised as
support.* This closes the §9.3 broad reading, which was previously **guaranteed by nothing**.

### 2.5 · Map — declared structure vs MAIA interpretation

| Kind | Rendering |
|---|---|
| **Authored structure** — Part I · Chapter 10 · Earth · Living Spiral, where the writer or Work declared them | ⭐ solid, **primary** |
| **MAIA interpretation** — *“MAIA notices a return pattern across these sections”* | ⛔ a clearly **separate overlay or perspective**, ⛔ **never rendered identically** |

⛔ Elemental classification may **not** be inferred from prose where FR-06 forbids it. For Works
without a declared spiral, the visualization **adapts to whatever structure actually exists**.
