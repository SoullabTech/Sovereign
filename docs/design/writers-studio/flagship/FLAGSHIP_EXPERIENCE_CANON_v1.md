# SOULLAB WRITER'S STUDIO
# Flagship Experience Canon v1

Status: DESIGN / EXPERIENCE AUTHORITY  
Purpose: define the felt experience, interaction spine, accessibility law, trust model, and visual acceptance standard for Soullab Writer's Studio.

This canon does not replace repository constitutional law. It translates that law into the member-facing flagship experience.

---

## 1. North Star

> **Writer's Studio should feel like exploring a living world that happens to be your book.**

The Studio is not a document plus tools.
It is a navigable relationship with the Work.

The member should repeatedly experience:

```text
I noticed something.
        ↓
I followed it through my book.
        ↓
I saw a pattern I had not seen before.
        ↓
I returned to the exact passage.
        ↓
MAIA helped me explore it.
        ↓
I tried another possibility.
        ↓
I kept it, changed it, or undid it.
        ↓
I continued deeper into the Work.
```

The reward is discovery, not points.

---

## 2. Adventure Without Gamification

Soullab may use the *pleasure* of exploration without game mechanics that manipulate attention.

### Allowed
- discovery
- maps
- paths
- returning to places
- revealing relationships
- following threads
- trying possibilities
- visible progress through the Work
- invitations to continue

### Not part of the flagship
- points
- XP
- streaks
- badges
- leaderboards
- grades
- quality scores
- completion pressure
- "winning"
- artificial scarcity
- engagement traps

> **Progress is understanding the Work more deeply.**

---

## 3. Audience Law

The same Studio must serve:
- a young writer beginning a first book;
- an experienced author;
- a memoirist who has never studied craft;
- an older writer with decades of lived material;
- a helper, coach, therapist, teacher, or creative practitioner;
- someone who simply knows, "this part isn't working."

The experience must never require professional editorial vocabulary to become useful.

### Plain-language actions beat machinery

Prefer:
- Show me where this appears.
- Take me to the passage.
- Help me understand this.
- What am I circling around?
- Show me another possibility.
- What might I lose if I change it?
- Keep my original.
- Help me see the shape of this chapter.

Avoid requiring:
- lens selection
- pass type
- range mechanics
- developmental-editing terminology
- implementation vocabulary
- expert configuration before exploration

### Accessibility is part of product quality
- rem-based scalable typography
- no important interaction depends on hover
- large, calm interaction targets
- keyboard, touch, and voice paths
- layouts survive 150% text scaling
- mobile surfaces scroll rather than crush data objects
- no information hidden merely because text grows

---

## 4. Product Vocabulary

### Brand language
`Write · Explore · Refine · Become`

This is outer Soullab language.
It is not product navigation.

### Product navigation
`Home · Write · Develop · Review`

Other destinations appear only when real substrate exists.

Desktop and mobile preserve the same semantic names.

Do not silently rename:
- Develop → Explore
- Review → Refine

---

## 5. One Work, Three Movements

### WRITE
Create and revise the Work.

### DEVELOP
See its shape, patterns, relationships, movement, and evidence.

### REVIEW
Revisit what has been seen, inspect findings, follow threads, and decide what to explore next.

These are not three disconnected legacy rooms.
They are movements within one Writer's Studio.

---

## 6. WRITE — Permanent Composition

The manuscript is the permanent content region.

Persistent application chrome may contain:
- Studio identity
- Work identity
- product navigation
- quiet support facet
- Focus

The following are contextual, never permanent competing content columns:
- outline / contents
- MAIA
- Review
- History
- Related
- teaching
- reasoning
- source / material detail

### Legacy failure shapes
A flagship candidate fails if its primary experience materially reproduces:
- `Rebuild preview`
- permanent outline content rail
- permanent MAIA content rail
- permanent three-content-column layout
- old `StudioModeBar` as the primary experience
- dense technical workbench controls as ordinary chrome

---

## 7. Passage → MAIA

A member holds an exact passage.

MAIA appears:
- at that locus;
- contextual;
- dismissible;
- non-resident.

Opening or closing MAIA must not:
- change manuscript width;
- move the selected passage vertically;
- lose scroll position;
- lose Work / chapter / section / range identity;
- silently broaden the commission;
- silently re-read the Work.

On narrow/mobile:
- MAIA may become a sheet or card;
- the manuscript remains the permanent object.

---

## 8. One Conversation Spine

At the same locus the writer may:
- understand;
- discuss;
- disagree;
- teach / explain;
- explore;
- revise.

These are not separate products.

Reasoning and teaching are disclosures within the same relationship.

### Support facets

`Guided · Learning · Direct`

Facet changes may affect:
- translation
- teaching depth
- explanation detail
- visibility of governed machinery

Facet must **not** change:
- observation truth
- evidence
- non-conclusion law
- proposal breadth
- mutation authority
- how much prose MAIA writes
- whether a finding is admitted

---

## 9. Alternatives

Alternatives are peer possibilities.

Required:
- unranked
- all reachable
- named by purpose
- `Keep my original` is first-class

Examples:
- More embodied
- More lyrical
- Clearer and simpler
- Closer to my cadence
- Keep my original

Do not use:
- numbering that implies order
- Best
- Recommended
- Strongest
- severity
- priority
- winner states

### Proposal scope
If the member holds:
- a sentence → proposal stays sentence-bound
- a paragraph → proposal stays paragraph-bound

MAIA may not silently widen scope.

### Required action
Every proposal says:

> **Read in context**

Never Apply from the alternatives list.

---

## 10. Read in Context → Apply → Undo

Before Apply, show:
- exact original locus
- exact candidate
- manuscript context
- changed wording
- original preserved
- conversation reachable

Only this state may offer:
> **Apply this revision**

After Apply:
- exact receipt
- immediate Undo
- History available
- previous wording recoverable
- same Work and authored place preserved

Undo:
- restores previous wording
- retains truthful lineage
- returns to same relationship seam

---

## 11. DEVELOP — Purpose

Develop helps a person see the Work more clearly.

It does not grade the Work.

Allowed:
- structure
- continuity
- recurring images
- themes
- cadence shifts
- textual patterns
- character presence
- member-declared maps
- evidence-linked observations
- coverage

### Prohibited as factual properties of the Work
- strong
- weak
- good
- bad
- authentic
- engaging
- beautiful
- luminous
- key
- important
- priority
- should change

Two lawful alternatives:

1. **Evidence-grounded observation**
   - "The river appears in Chapters 1, 2, 5, 6, 10, and 12."
   - "Direct address becomes more frequent after Chapter 8."

2. **Explicit MAIA stance**
   - "I keep returning to this passage."
   - "I find myself drawn to the directness here."

A stance is MAIA's response, not a property of the Work.

---

## 12. Continuity Map

The Continuity Map is a flagship intelligence object.

Rows may include:
- textual entities
- member-declared patterns / themes
- MAIA observations

Every row must disclose provenance.

Examples:
- `IN YOUR TEXT`
- `YOU NAMED THIS · SEP 14`
- `MAIA NOTICED THIS`

Every cell is an address.

### Governing law

> **The map shows where something appears. It does not, by itself, say what that presence means or what should change.**

Use descriptive frequency:
- Often
- Sometimes
- Once or twice
- Not present

Do not use evaluative scale language such as:
- stronger
- weaker
- better
- more important

---

## 13. Visualization Truth Law

> **A visualization must be as verifiable as its form implies.**

### May look measured
- counts
- presence
- chapter occurrence
- section occurrence
- declared structure
- verified coverage
- word counts
- timestamps

### Interpretive material must not masquerade as measurement

Example:
`Uncertainty → Reflection → Opening → Steadier`

If MAIA inferred those interior states from prose, a conventional line graph falsely suggests measurement.

Interpretations must render as:
- attributed MAIA reading;
- revisable;
- visibly different from measured/countable objects;
- evidence-linked.

---

## 14. Story / Spiral Map Provenance

Two lawful states must render differently.

### The writer authored it
> `You named these.`

### The writer selected a Soullab template
> `You chose the Spiral template.`

Accepting a template is not the same event as naming one's own structure.

### Member-declared map
The member may:
- rename
- reorder
- revise
- remove
- replace

The UI must make this obvious.

Elemental / Spiralogic classification may never silently become authored structure through inference from free prose.

---

## 15. Themes, Voice, Structure, Continuity — Shared Governed Object

Develop views should render from governed components/data rather than hand-authored independent screens.

A conceptual observation contract:

```text
DevelopObservation {
  id
  kind
  label
  description
  evidence[]
  returnTo
  doesNotEstablish[]
  provenance
  coverage
}
```

Construction must refuse an object that:
- lacks evidence;
- lacks return address;
- hides provenance;
- grades the Work;
- makes a cross-Work statement without required coverage.

A whole view must refuse if one required member is inadmissible rather than silently rendering partial truth.

---

## 16. Reader Perspective

`reader-effect` remains a permanent non-conclusion.

Member-facing name:
> **Reader perspective**

Quiet explanation:
> **Possible reader effects — not predictions.**

Allowed:
- "A reader might lose the referent here because..."
- "This turn could create distance..."
- "A reader may expect..."

Not allowed:
- "Readers will be confused."
- "This is engaging."
- "Readers prefer..."
- actual-reader response presented as established fact

---

## 17. REVIEW — Purpose

Review is not a report dashboard.

It is a place to:
- revisit observations;
- follow patterns;
- open exact passages;
- compare places in the Work;
- talk with MAIA;
- add the member's own observations;
- decide the next move.

Every analytical object must lead somewhere.

> **Every analytical view invites a next move.**

---

## 18. Review → Passage Adventure Loop

```text
Review finding
    ↓
Open exact passage
    ↓
Finding travels with the member
    ↓
MAIA is there with that same observation
    ↓
Discuss / Explore / Try possibility / Related
    ↓
Back to Review
```

Required:
- one observation ID
- one canonical wording
- no silent re-reading on arrival
- no duplicate restatement that looks like a second finding
- direct Back to Review trail

The observation travels.
The member does not fall through a trapdoor.

---

## 19. Finding Order

When findings are shown a few at a time, default order follows the Work:

> **book / page order**

Example:
`112 → 118 → 124 → 126`

Any non-explicitly chosen alternative ordering can imply ranking.

Never use:
- Most relevant
- Highest priority
- Most actionable
- Most important

as silent default ordering.

---

## 20. Review Filters Are Not Commissions

Tabs such as:
- Structure
- Voice
- Continuity
- Flow
- Themes

may filter already-admitted material.

Clicking one must not silently trigger a new reading.

If the reading does not exist:
> `MAIA hasn't read this Work for continuity yet.`

Then require a deliberate commission.

---

## 21. Related Passages

A Related Passage requires:
- actual coverage of that location;
- evidence for why it is related;
- exact address.

Do not generate interpretive glosses for chapters MAIA did not read.

When a gloss exists, it remains an observation with evidence and non-conclusion boundaries.

---

## 22. Add Your Own Observation

The member can author directly into the analytical view.

At an exact passage:

> **Add your own observation**

Types:
- Something I noticed
- A question
- A possibility

Optional:
- member-written note
- member-selected relationships / themes

Primary action:
> **Keep with this passage**

The observation:
- belongs to the member;
- retains its exact address;
- becomes visible in Review / maps where lawful;
- remains distinguishable from MAIA observations.

This is not "insight capture machinery."

It is the writer marking their own Work.

---

## 23. Work Changed Since MAIA Read It

Trust requires explicit staleness.

If the Work changes after MAIA read it, show:

> **This chapter has changed since MAIA last read it.**

Include:
- previous reading timestamp
- current version / update timestamp
- what changed, when available
- which current observations rely on earlier material

Offer:
> **Read this chapter again**

and:
> **Not now**

Law:
> **MAIA never silently recommissions herself because the Work changed.**

Previous reading remains inspectable where useful.

---

## 24. Coverage — What MAIA Read

Member-facing trust surface:

> **What MAIA Read**

It should identify:
- actual chapters / sections / passages read
- scope
- timestamp / freshness
- reading identity where relevant
- whether the Work has changed since then

It must not imply external-source verification.

Coverage is top-level trust evidence, not hidden editorial detail.

---

## 25. Capability Honesty

A visible active control must map to real substrate.

If unavailable:
- hide it; or
- show an explicitly unavailable state when that helps the member understand the boundary.

Never render a complete-looking active feature that:
- 404s
- has no substrate
- silently falls back to a different capability

Navigation is capability governed.

Do not include live destinations merely because they look good in a reference image.

---

## 26. Atmosphere

Atmosphere is composition authority, not skin.

The Studio should feel:
- warm
- literary
- spacious
- calm
- inviting
- relational
- premium
- alive without being busy

Environmental imagery may:
- support orientation
- carry Work identity
- make the room feel inhabited

It must not:
- compete with the manuscript
- reduce meaningful working space
- imply interpretation as fact
- become decorative clutter

Focus mode makes atmosphere recede.

---

## 27. Cross-Mode Family

Write, Develop, Review, and Mobile must unmistakably belong to one Soullab Writer's Studio.

Shared:
- typography
- spacing rhythm
- surface treatment
- atmosphere
- interaction grammar
- navigation semantics
- control language
- trust behavior
- return-to-Work behavior

A beautiful Develop screen that looks like unrelated analytics software fails.

---

## 28. Mobile

Mobile is the same product, not squeezed desktop.

Required:
- manuscript primary
- contextual cards / sheets
- semantic navigation parity
- data objects scroll rather than crush
- touch targets meet floor
- findings preserve page/book order
- finding travels to passage
- MAIA travels with same observation
- clear Back to Review

The mobile adventure loop:

```text
Review
→ finding
→ Go to passage
→ exact passage
→ MAIA / same observation
→ next move
→ Back to Review
```

Machine checks may enforce:
- forbidden legacy shapes
- required addresses
- no unsupported verdict language
- coverage requirements
- no direct Apply
- responsive invariants

Machine checks may **not** proxy founder experiential judgment.

Required final founder comparison:
- approved reference
- real rendered candidate
- same viewport

- state identified
- PASS / REVISE

## 30. Visual Gates

### V1 — Write at rest
Manuscript unmistakably primary.

### V2 — Passage + MAIA
No width / vertical / scroll displacement; exact locus retained.

### V3 — Alternatives
Plural, unranked, Keep original, no Apply.

### V4 — Read in context
Candidate seen in Work before mutation.

### V5 — Apply + Undo + History
Receipt and reversibility obvious.

### V6 — Develop
Non-evaluative, evidence-linked, flagship family.

### V7 — Review
Every finding returns to manuscript; no report dead end.

### V8 — Mobile
Same semantics, no squeezed desktop.

### V9 — Legacy-shape falsifier
Old Writer's Studio aesthetic cannot pass.

### V10 — Founder side-by-side
Human visual PASS required.

### V11 — Cross-mode family
One Soullab Studio.

### V12 — Non-professional-writer accessibility
Meaningful next move is obvious without choosing machinery.

### V13 — Visualization truth
Visual form never implies stronger epistemic certainty than the underlying evidence warrants.

### V14 — Member contribution
Member-authored observations visibly remain the member's.

### V15 — Reading freshness
Stale MAIA readings are disclosed before they can be mistaken for current analysis.

---

## 31. Release Rule

A candidate fails release acceptance if it:
- violates behavioral or constitutional law;
- materially diverges from approved flagship composition;
- materially reproduces the legacy Writer's Studio aesthetic;
- hides provenance;
- lacks return addresses;
- presents interpretation as measurement;
- grades the Work;
- strands the writer away from the manuscript;
- silently re-reads or widens authority;
- becomes inaccessible under text scaling or mobile constraints.

> **No production deployment should be authorized until the founder visual gate is PASS.**

The Studio should leave the member with an experience something like:

> My Work is alive.
>
> I can see more of it than I could before.
>
> I know where I am.
>
> I can follow what interests me.
>
> I can always return to my words.
>
> I can try things without losing myself.
>
> MAIA is with me, not ahead of me.
>
> The deeper I go into the Work, the more clearly I encounter what is mine.

**A deeper you. A more human world.**
