# SOULLAB WRITER'S STUDIO
# Beta Task Cards v1

Print or display one card at a time.

---

## CARD 1 — COME BACK TO YOUR WORK

> You haven't opened this book in a while.
>
> Take a look around and do whatever feels natural.

No additional instruction.

---

## CARD 2 — SOMETHING FEELS OFF

> This chapter feels off to you.
>
> You don't know whether it's the shape, the thread, the voice, or something else.
>
> What would you do?

---

## CARD 3 — FOLLOW SOMETHING

> Find something that seems to appear elsewhere in your Work.
>
> Follow it.

---

## CARD 4 — REVIEW

> Find something in Review that interests you.
>
> Follow it into your manuscript.

Then:

> Go back to Review.

---

## CARD 5 — YOUR OWN OBSERVATION

> Mark something in this passage that *you* notice.
>
> Keep it with the passage.

---

## CARD 6 — TRY WITHOUT COMMITTING

> Try another possibility for this passage.
>
> Don't change your original unless you actually want to.

---

## CARD 7 — THE WORK CHANGED

> Look at this chapter and what MAIA is showing you.
>
> Decide what you want to do.

No mention of stale reading.

---

## CARD 8 — WHAT DOES THIS MAP SAY?

> Look at this map.
>
> Tell us what you think it means.

---

## CARD 9 — HELP STYLE

> Try Guided.
>
> Try Learning.
>
> Try Direct.
>
> Tell us what you think changed.

---

## CARD 10 — BIGGER TEXT

> Make the text larger.
>
> Continue working as you normally would.

---

## CARD 11 — PHONE

> On your phone, open Review.
>
> Follow a finding into the passage.
>
> Ask MAIA about it.
>
> Return to Review.

---

## CARD 12 — CURIOSITY

> Is there anything else in your Work you want to follow right now?

Do not offer options.
