# Controlled-Manuscript Beta

For the first usability beta, prefer a Soullab-controlled or otherwise authorized test manuscript.

Read:

> "We're testing Writer's Studio, not you or the quality of your writing. The manuscript in this session is test material provided for the study."

> "We'll take notes about how you use the product and what you say. You may stop at any time or skip any task."

If recording:
> "With your permission, we'd also like to record [screen/audio/video] so we can review the session accurately. You can decline recording where that option is available."

Ask:
- Participation: YES / NO
- Screen recording: YES / NO / N/A
- Audio recording: YES / NO / N/A
- Video recording: YES / NO / N/A

# Participant-Owned Work Beta

Do not use this section until the product team can accurately fill in every bracket below.

Read:

> "You may use your own manuscript, or you may use test material instead."

> "If you use your manuscript, Writer's Studio will [EXACT PROCESSING DESCRIPTION]."

> "Your manuscript will be stored [EXACT STORAGE DESCRIPTION / OR NOT STORED]."

> "MAIA's readings of your manuscript will be retained [EXACT RETENTION DESCRIPTION]."

> "Research/session recordings and facilitator notes will be retained [EXACT RESEARCH RETENTION]."

> "Your manuscript and MAIA's readings will [NOT / IF APPLICABLE] be used for model training."

> "Your manuscript may be processed by [EXACT PROVIDERS/SYSTEMS THAT MUST BE DISCLOSED]."

> "You can request deletion by [EXACT PROCESS]."

> "You may choose test material instead and still participate."

Then obtain the exact required consent for:
- use of participant-owned manuscript;
- system storage/persistence;
- MAIA reading persistence;
- recordings;
- research notes;
- any provider/data processing that requires disclosure/consent.

Never infer consent to product-data persistence from consent to research notes.

---

# Research-team rules

- minimum necessary participant data;
- participant IDs in working materials;
- no unnecessary manuscript excerpts in synthesis;
- redact identifying material from screenshots where practical;
- no new provider routing merely for research convenience;
- no training use without explicit separate authority/consent;
- honor stated deletion/retention behavior;
- if actual data behavior differs from the script, STOP the study and correct the disclosure before continuing.
