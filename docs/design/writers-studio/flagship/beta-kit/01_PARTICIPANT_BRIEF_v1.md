# SOULLAB WRITER'S STUDIO
# Beta Participant Brief v1

Thank you for helping us test Soullab Writer's Studio.

Writer's Studio is being designed to help people explore, understand, develop, and revise their own writing without needing professional editing vocabulary.

You do not need to be a professional writer.

You do not need to know how the Studio works.

In fact, it is more useful to us if you do **not** try to be a "good tester."

## What we want from you

Use the Studio as naturally as you can.

If something is confusing, say so.

If you hesitate, that matters.

If you expect something to happen and something else happens, tell us.

If a word does not make sense, ask what it means.

If you feel unsure about whether MAIA changed something, say that immediately.

## What we are testing

We are testing the product — not your writing.

Nothing about this session is a judgment of:
- your writing ability;
- your manuscript;
- your experience;
- your technical skill.

We want to learn:
- whether the Studio is understandable;
- whether it feels welcoming;
- whether you can always find your way back to your Work;
- whether MAIA feels helpful without taking over;
- whether the experience makes you curious to keep exploring.

## During the session

Please think aloud when you can.

Say things like:
- "I expected this to..."
- "I don't know what this means."
- "I would click this."
- "I'm looking for..."
- "I'm not sure where I am."
- "Oh, that's interesting."
- "I don't trust this yet."

There are no wrong answers.

## Your writing

Use a test manuscript or material you are comfortable using for this session.

If you are using your own Work, only share material you are comfortable including in the test environment.

## The most useful thing you can give us

Your natural reaction.

At the end we will ask one simple question:

> **Is there anything else in your Work you want to follow right now?**

That curiosity — or lack of it — tells us a great deal.

**A deeper you. A more human world.**
