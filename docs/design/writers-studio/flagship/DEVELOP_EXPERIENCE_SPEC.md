## §1 · The three laws that shape everything below

### L-A · ⭐ THE OVERVIEW IS THE ROOM; THE LENSES ARE OPTIONAL DEPTH

**V12** requires that a creative or helper who does not know editorial terminology still understands
the next meaningful act, ⛔ without choosing lenses, passes or ranges.

⭐ Therefore: **every observation is reachable from Overview.** The lens tabs re-present the same
admitted set filtered; they never hold anything Overview does not.

⛔ *"Guided shows 6, Direct shows 87"* is a reachability difference wearing a presentation costume.
⭐ One set · different default depth · everything reachable — §9.7, satisfied by composition rather
than traded against V12.

### L-B · ⛔ A TAB IS A FILTER, NEVER A COMMISSION

⛔ **Clicking a lens MUST NOT trigger a model reading.** Where no reading exists for that lens the
surface says so plainly and offers a **separate deliberate gesture**.

⭐ This upholds `A2` — *the reader never infers a lens from its own output* — at the navigation
boundary, which is where it would otherwise be lost. ⭐ *Navigation is not consent.*

### L-C · ⛔ DESCRIBE, NEVER GRADE

Every entry is an **evidence-grounded observation** or an **explicit MAIA stance**. ⛔ Never a
property of the Work asserted as fact.

```text
PROPERTY OF THE WORK       ⛔ machine verdict
MAIA'S RESPONSE            ✅ contestable relationship
TEXTUAL OBSERVATION        ✅ evidence-grounded
```

## §2 · Lens states — the honest four

Every lens is in exactly one of four states. ⭐ These are **derived from positive facts**, ⛔ never
stored as a status, and ⛔ never inferred from the member opening a tab.

| State | What the member sees | What it offers |
|---|---|---|
| **NOT READ** | *“MAIA hasn't read your Work for continuity yet.”* | ⭐ One deliberate gesture: **“Read for continuity”** — ⛔ the only thing that commissions |
| **PARTIALLY READ** | the observations that exist **+ what remains unread, stated** | *“Read the remaining 2 sections”* |
| **READ** | the observations + coverage | *“What MAIA read”* |
| **READ, NOTHING NOTICED** | ⭐ *“MAIA read all 52 sections for continuity and noticed nothing to bring you.”* | — |

⚠️⚠️ **THE FOURTH STATE IS THE ONE THAT WILL BE BUILT WRONG.** *Read and found nothing* and *never
read* will render identically as an empty list unless they are kept apart by construction.

⭐ Their difference is exactly the one this programme has defended everywhere else: **a completed
reading that surfaced nothing is a RESULT; an absent reading is an ABSENCE.** Collapsing them tells
the member their Work has no continuity issues when in fact nothing has been read — the same defect
class as *never-declared rendered as declared-open*.

⛔ There is therefore **no empty state**. Either a reading exists and said nothing, or it does not
exist and the room says so.

## §3 · The six lenses

⭐ Each names **what it looks at**, ⛔ never a quality it measures. Every entry carries `refs` and a
return.

### 3.1 · STRUCTURE — *How your sections connect and transition*

Observes the **declared** shape: sections, chapters, parts, and the joins between them.

```text
✅  The Part III to Part IV transition runs one sentence.
    The four earlier part transitions each occupy a full section.        §16 §17
✅  Twenty-two sections close with a practice. Eighteen close mid-argument.  §1–§52
⛔  Weak transition.          ⛔  Good structure.          ⛔  Needs work.
```

⚠️ **Where `structure-topology` refs carry no section**, the observation has **no position in the
prose**. ⛔ It is never given an invented one — it sorts after positioned observations and its
return offers *“Show the sections involved”*, ⛔ not a single passage.

### 3.2 · ARC — *The movement across your Work*

⭐⭐ **THE MOST DANGEROUS LENS, AND THE REASON IS §2.5.**

Arc is where **declared structure** and **MAIA interpretation** are most easily confused, because an
arc *looks* structural. The separation is absolute:

| Kind | Source | Rendering |
|---|---|---|
| **Declared arc** | the member named the movements | ⭐ solid, primary, part of the map |
| **MAIA's noticing** | *“MAIA notices a return pattern across §9, §18, §29 and §44”* | ⛔ a **clearly separate overlay or perspective**, ⛔ never rendered identically |

⛔ **MAIA may never name the movements of a Work.** Where none are declared, Arc offers the
undeclared state (§6) and nothing else. ⛔ Elemental classification is never inferred from prose —
FR-06, absolute.

### 3.3 · THEMES — *Ideas and where they develop*

⛔ Not *“key ideas”* — `key` is an importance judgment and is out unless the writer declared it.

```text
✅  The word “spiral” appears 58 times. Forty-one fall in Parts I and II;
    Part IV uses it twice.                                              §3–§18 §41
✅  This image returns in four separated sections, each attached to a
    different element.                                          §10 §18 §29 §44
⛔  Rich and coherent.        ⛔  Key ideas.        ⛔  Underdeveloped theme.
```

### 3.4 · VOICE — *Patterns, shifts and consistency*

⭐⭐ **THE MANUSCRIPT IS THE REFERENCE, ⛔ NEVER AN EXTERNAL STANDARD.** An imported ideal breaches
the `voice` lens and Invariant 14. Every Voice observation is **self-referential** — it compares the
Work to itself.

```text
✅  You address the reader as “you” in 31 of 52 sections. Part IV contains none.  §34–§41
✅  The language moves from explanation into sensory detail at §10.3, and stays there.
✅  I find the directness of this passage striking.        ← lawful STANCE, used sparingly
⛔  Distinctive and authentic.   ⛔  Your voice is strongest here.   ⛔  Inconsistent voice.
```

### 3.5 · CONTINUITY — *Where the thread holds and where it jumps*

```text
✅  “The work” refers to the inner work in §10.2 and to the manuscript in §17.4.
    It is not renamed between them.                                      §10.2 §17.4
✅  Chapter 9 closes on the body; Chapter 10 opens on time.               §9.4 §10.1
⛔  Confusing.        ⛔  Jarring.        ⛔  Breaks the flow.
```

### 3.6 · READER PERSPECTIVE — *Possible reader effects, not predictions*

⭐ The capability is **kept**; `reader-effect` remains a **permanent non-conclusion**. What is
admitted is a **contestable interpretation**, ⛔ never a finding.

⛔ **Every statement is possibility-shaped.** Enforced at the boundary: an entry on this lens that
is not hedged is **refused before it becomes a state the UI could render.**

```text
✅  A reader might lose the referent here, because “the work” changes
    meaning without being renamed.                                        §17.4
✅  This shift could create distance for a reader who arrived through Part I.
✅  I wonder whether a reader may expect the practice to return here.
⛔  Readers will find this confusing.   ⛔  This is confusing.   ⛔  Readers prefer…
```

⛔ Reader perspective creates **no separate developmental-reading truth**, and its textual evidence
stays visible and reachable like every other lens.

## §4 · WHAT MAIA READ — the coverage surface

⭐ **L8: coverage is the ground of trust, ⛔ not technical depth, ⛔ never behind *Show editorial
detail*.** It is a top-level tab **and** an Overview card.

| Shown | Why |
|---|---|
| **how much** — `11 of 13 sections · 85%` | bounds every claim on the page |
| **at what depth** | a title-only read and a body read license different claims |
| **when** — `last read 2m ago` | ⭐ a reading is a reading **at a time**; the Work has moved since |
| **what remains** — *“Read the remaining 2 sections”* | ⭐ the **only** commissioning gesture in the room |

⚠️ **Coverage bounds FROM ABOVE ONLY.** It proves a section was **opened**, ⛔ never that it was
understood, ⛔ never *thoroughness*. The copy says *what MAIA read* — ⛔ it never says *what MAIA
understood*.

⭐ Coverage may discharge `whole-work-pattern`, `across-unread-span` and `outside-coverage`, **and
only on complete body-depth coverage.** ⛔ It never discharges `author-intent` or `reader-effect`.

## §5 · ⭐ RETURN TO PASSAGE — exact behavior

Every observation returns to the exact authored location. **V7.** The behavior is precise, and one
part of it is a real design decision rather than a mechanism:

```text
1  the member clicks the return on an observation
2  the Studio navigates to Write, at the section the observation's earliest ref names
3  the passage scrolls into view and takes the held treatment
4  ⭐ THE OBSERVATION TRAVELS WITH IT — the existing, already-admitted observation is
   presented at the passage
5  ⛔ NO NEW READING IS COMMISSIONED
```

### ⭐⭐ Step 4 is the decision, and step 5 is why it matters

The obvious implementation is: navigate to the section, then let MAIA read the passage so she has
something to say. ⛔ **That is a commission inferred from navigation** — the same defect L-B refuses
at the tab boundary, arriving through the back door.

⭐ **The observation the member clicked is the observation they get.** It was admitted in a frozen
reading; it carries its identity, its refs and its `doesNotEstablish`. Returning to the passage
**presents it**, it does not regenerate it.

⚠️ **A consequence that must be honest, ⛔ not hidden:** the Work may have changed since the reading
was frozen. If the cited text has moved or been revised, the return says so —
*“This observation was made when the passage read differently”* — and offers the frozen evidence.
⛔ It never silently re-anchors to whatever is there now, and ⛔ it never quietly re-reads to
refresh itself.

### Positioning

- refs carrying a section and a code-point range → the exact passage, held
- refs carrying a section only → the section, top of its prose, ⛔ nothing held
- refs carrying no section (`structure-unit`, `structure-units`, `structure-topology`) → ⛔ **no
  single passage exists.** The return offers *“Show the sections involved”* and lists them. ⛔ A
  position is never invented, and ⛔ never coerced to the first section, which would place it at the
  start of the Work.

## §6 · ⭐ THE UNDECLARED-STRUCTURE STATE

Where the member has not declared the shape of their Work:

```text
        You haven't named the movements of this book.

        MAIA can show you the sections in the order you wrote them.
        She will not name the shape for you — that reading is yours to make.

        [ Name the movements ]        [ Keep it open for now ]
```

⭐ **Three states, ⛔ never two.** This is the arrival-experience condition that fails through
visual design while passing in data:

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|
| **DECLARED** | the shape, solid and primary |
| **DECLARED OPEN** — *“still discovering”* | ⭐ a **first-class state**, named as the member named it, ⛔ not missing data |
| **NEVER DECLARED** | the invitation above |

⛔ A never-declared Work rendered as **a form with blanks** fails this through visual design while
passing in data. ⛔ *Keep it open for now* is not the same as *Still discovering* — the first is
deferral, the second is a declaration. Both are lawful; ⛔ they are not interchangeable.

⛔ **Absence of a declaration never authorizes MAIA to invent one**, and ⛔ nothing becomes
canonical through silence. ⛔ A prior declaration must never silently become current again.

⭐ **Skipping is always lawful**, and the room is fully usable undeclared: Structure, Themes, Voice,
Continuity and Reader perspective all work on the sections as written. ⛔ Only **Arc** and the
elemental map depend on a declaration, and both say so rather than degrading silently.
