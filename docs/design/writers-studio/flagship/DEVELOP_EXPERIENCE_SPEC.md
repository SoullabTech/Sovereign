## §0 · ⭐⭐ THE DESIGN QUESTION, ANSWERED

> *How does a person who is not a professional writer open their Work and suddenly see its shape,
> patterns, relationships and possibilities — ⛔ without being graded and ⛔ without needing to
> understand developmental editing?*

⭐ **What makes someone feel graded is being told about their work in a vocabulary they did not ask
for, ordered by a judgment they cannot see. What makes them feel capable is recognising their own
work in what they are shown.**

Three moves follow, and together they are the room:

### 0.1 · Open with RECOGNITION, ⛔ not assessment

⭐ The first thing on the screen is **the shape of their Work** — something *they* made, rendered
whole at a scale they cannot see from inside it. ⛔ Not observations. ⛔ Not a score. ⛔ Not a list
of things to fix.

Second: **what MAIA has read.** Trust precedes everything, and it bounds every claim below it.

Only third: **what she noticed** — in plain language, each returning to their own words.

### 0.2 · ⭐ THE LENSES ARE NOT PRESENTED AS LENSES

A person who is not an editor does not want *Continuity*. They want to know **whether the thread
holds**. So the plain question is the label, and the editorial term is the **secondary** name for
whoever wants it — ⛔ never required to operate the room.

| Real lens | Plain question the member reads | Editorial term, secondary |
|---|---|---|
| `structure` | **How it's put together** | Structure |
| `development` | **Where ideas grow — and where they stop** | Development |
| `arc` | **How it moves** | Arc |
| `continuity` | **Whether the thread holds** | Continuity |
| `coherence` | **Whether it stays consistent with itself** | Coherence |
| `voice` | **How it sounds — compared to itself** | Voice |
| `reader` | **How it might land** | Reader perspective |

⭐ This is **V12 answered by naming**, and it costs the professional nothing: the editorial term is
right there, one line down.

### 0.3 · ⭐⭐ POSSIBILITY LIVES IN THE AFFORDANCE, ⛔ NEVER IN THE SENTENCE

The founder asked for *possibilities*. ⛔ The prescriptive form — *“you may want to expand this”* —
is the grading defect wearing a helpful face: it asserts a consequence MAIA has no commission to
assert.

⭐ **MAIA describes. The button offers.**

```text
⛔  You may want to deepen the section on embodiment.
✅  This transition runs one sentence. The four before it each run a full section.
    [ Work on this →]   [ Show me the others →]
```

⭐ The member reads a fact about their own book and is handed a door. ⛔ Nobody told them their
transition was weak. *That* is the difference between seeing possibilities and being graded, and it
is a composition decision rather than a copy decision.

## §1 · The three laws that shape everything below

### L-A · ⭐ THE OVERVIEW IS THE ROOM; THE LENSES ARE OPTIONAL DEPTH

**V12** requires that a creative or helper who does not know editorial terminology still understands
the next meaningful act, ⛔ without choosing lenses, passes or ranges.

⭐ Therefore: **every observation is reachable from Overview.** The lens tabs re-present the same
admitted set filtered; they never hold anything Overview does not.

⛔ *"Guided shows 6, Direct shows 87"* is a reachability difference wearing a presentation costume.
⭐ One set · different default depth · everything reachable — §9.7, satisfied by composition rather
than traded against V12.

### L-B · ⛔ A TAB IS A FILTER, NEVER A COMMISSION

⛔ **Clicking a lens MUST NOT trigger a model reading.** Where no reading exists for that lens the
surface says so plainly and offers a **separate deliberate gesture**.

⭐ This upholds `A2` — *the reader never infers a lens from its own output* — at the navigation
boundary, which is where it would otherwise be lost. ⭐ *Navigation is not consent.*

### L-C · ⛔ DESCRIBE, NEVER GRADE

Every entry is an **evidence-grounded observation** or an **explicit MAIA stance**. ⛔ Never a
property of the Work asserted as fact.

```text
PROPERTY OF THE WORK       ⛔ machine verdict
MAIA'S RESPONSE            ✅ contestable relationship
TEXTUAL OBSERVATION        ✅ evidence-grounded
```

## §2 · Lens states — the honest four

Every lens is in exactly one of four states. ⭐ These are **derived from positive facts**, ⛔ never
stored as a status, and ⛔ never inferred from the member opening a tab.

| State | What the member sees | What it offers |
|---|---|---|
| **NOT READ** | *“MAIA hasn't read your Work for continuity yet.”* | ⭐ One deliberate gesture: **“Read for continuity”** — ⛔ the only thing that commissions |
| **PARTIALLY READ** | the observations that exist **+ what remains unread, stated** | *“Read the remaining 2 sections”* |
| **READ** | the observations + coverage | *“What MAIA read”* |
| **READ, NOTHING NOTICED** | ⭐ *“MAIA read all 52 sections for continuity and noticed nothing to bring you.”* | — |

⚠️⚠️ **THE FOURTH STATE IS THE ONE THAT WILL BE BUILT WRONG.** *Read and found nothing* and *never
read* will render identically as an empty list unless they are kept apart by construction.

⭐ Their difference is exactly the one this programme has defended everywhere else: **a completed
reading that surfaced nothing is a RESULT; an absent reading is an ABSENCE.** Collapsing them tells
the member their Work has no continuity issues when in fact nothing has been read — the same defect
class as *never-declared rendered as declared-open*.

⛔ There is therefore **no empty state**. Either a reading exists and said nothing, or it does not
exist and the room says so.

## §3 · The SEVEN lenses

⚠️⚠️ **CORRECTION TO THIS SPEC'S OWN FIRST DRAFT.** It named six lenses including **Themes**.
⛔ **`themes` IS NOT A LENS.** The ratified vocabulary in
`lib/manuscript/developmentalReader/contract.ts` is exactly:

```text
structure · development · continuity · arc · voice · coherence · reader
```

⭐ The first draft invented a destination and dropped two real ones — `development` and `coherence`
— which is the same defect class as `Notes · Research · Goals` in the v2 board. ⭐ *A spec is not
exempt from capability honesty because it is a spec.*

Each lens names **what it looks at**, ⛔ never a quality it measures. Every entry carries `refs` and
a return.

⚠️ **One ratified lens question contains grading vocabulary**: `development` asks *“which ideas are
underdeveloped · overexplained…”*. ⭐ That is the **internal question the reader is given**; ⛔ it is
not licence for the member-facing rendering to use those words. The question may grade; **the
output must describe.**


### 3.1 · STRUCTURE — *How your sections connect and transition*

Observes the **declared** shape: sections, chapters, parts, and the joins between them.

```text
✅  The Part III to Part IV transition runs one sentence.
    The four earlier part transitions each occupy a full section.        §16 §17
✅  Twenty-two sections close with a practice. Eighteen close mid-argument.  §1–§52
⛔  Weak transition.          ⛔  Good structure.          ⛔  Needs work.
```

⚠️ **Where `structure-topology` refs carry no section**, the observation has **no position in the
prose**. ⛔ It is never given an invented one — it sorts after positioned observations and its
return offers *“Show the sections involved”*, ⛔ not a single passage.

### 3.2 · ARC — *The movement across your Work*

⭐⭐ **THE MOST DANGEROUS LENS, AND THE REASON IS §2.5.**

Arc is where **declared structure** and **MAIA interpretation** are most easily confused, because an
arc *looks* structural. The separation is absolute:

| Kind | Source | Rendering |
|---|---|---|
| **Declared arc** | the member named the movements | ⭐ solid, primary, part of the map |
| **MAIA's noticing** | *“MAIA notices a return pattern across §9, §18, §29 and §44”* | ⛔ a **clearly separate overlay or perspective**, ⛔ never rendered identically |

⛔ **MAIA may never name the movements of a Work.** Where none are declared, Arc offers the
undeclared state (§6) and nothing else. ⛔ Elemental classification is never inferred from prose —
FR-06, absolute.

### 3.3 · DEVELOPMENT — *Where ideas grow, and where they stop*

⛔ Not *“Themes”*, which has no lens behind it. ⛔ Not *“key ideas”* — `key` is an importance
judgment and is out unless the writer declared it.

```text
✅  The word “spiral” appears 58 times. Forty-one fall in Parts I and II;
    Part IV uses it twice.                                              §3–§18 §41
✅  This image returns in four separated sections, each attached to a
    different element.                                          §10 §18 §29 §44
⛔  Rich and coherent.        ⛔  Key ideas.        ⛔  Underdeveloped theme.
```

### 3.4 · VOICE — *Patterns, shifts and consistency*

⭐⭐ **THE MANUSCRIPT IS THE REFERENCE, ⛔ NEVER AN EXTERNAL STANDARD.** An imported ideal breaches
the `voice` lens and Invariant 14. Every Voice observation is **self-referential** — it compares the
Work to itself.

```text
✅  You address the reader as “you” in 31 of 52 sections. Part IV contains none.  §34–§41
✅  The language moves from explanation into sensory detail at §10.3, and stays there.
✅  I find the directness of this passage striking.        ← lawful STANCE, used sparingly
⛔  Distinctive and authentic.   ⛔  Your voice is strongest here.   ⛔  Inconsistent voice.
```

### 3.5 · COHERENCE — *Whether it stays consistent with itself*

Asks whether a term has changed meaning, and whether a passage contradicts an earlier one.

```text
✅  “The work” means the inner work in §10.2 and the manuscript in §17.4.
    It is not renamed between them.                                      §10.2 §17.4
✅  §4 says the practice is done seated; §31 describes it standing.        §4 §31
⛔  Inconsistent.        ⛔  Contradictory.        ⛔  Sloppy.
```

### 3.6 · CONTINUITY — *Whether the thread holds*

```text
✅  “The work” refers to the inner work in §10.2 and to the manuscript in §17.4.
    It is not renamed between them.                                      §10.2 §17.4
✅  Chapter 9 closes on the body; Chapter 10 opens on time.               §9.4 §10.1
⛔  Confusing.        ⛔  Jarring.        ⛔  Breaks the flow.
```

### 3.7 · READER PERSPECTIVE — *Possible reader effects, not predictions*

⭐ The capability is **kept**; `reader-effect` remains a **permanent non-conclusion**. What is
admitted is a **contestable interpretation**, ⛔ never a finding.

⛔ **Every statement is possibility-shaped.** Enforced at the boundary: an entry on this lens that
is not hedged is **refused before it becomes a state the UI could render.**

```text
✅  A reader might lose the referent here, because “the work” changes
    meaning without being renamed.                                        §17.4
✅  This shift could create distance for a reader who arrived through Part I.
✅  I wonder whether a reader may expect the practice to return here.
⛔  Readers will find this confusing.   ⛔  This is confusing.   ⛔  Readers prefer…
```

⛔ Reader perspective creates **no separate developmental-reading truth**, and its textual evidence
stays visible and reachable like every other lens.

## §4 · WHAT MAIA READ — the coverage surface

⭐ **L8: coverage is the ground of trust, ⛔ not technical depth, ⛔ never behind *Show editorial
detail*.** It is a top-level tab **and** an Overview card.

| Shown | Why |
|---|---|
| **how much** — `11 of 13 sections · 85%` | bounds every claim on the page |
| **at what depth** | a title-only read and a body read license different claims |
| **when** — `last read 2m ago` | ⭐ a reading is a reading **at a time**; the Work has moved since |
| **what remains** — *“Read the remaining 2 sections”* | ⭐ the **only** commissioning gesture in the room |

⚠️ **Coverage bounds FROM ABOVE ONLY.** It proves a section was **opened**, ⛔ never that it was
understood, ⛔ never *thoroughness*. The copy says *what MAIA read* — ⛔ it never says *what MAIA
understood*.

⭐ Coverage may discharge `whole-work-pattern`, `across-unread-span` and `outside-coverage`, **and
only on complete body-depth coverage.** ⛔ It never discharges `author-intent` or `reader-effect`.

## §5 · ⭐ RETURN TO PASSAGE — exact behavior

Every observation returns to the exact authored location. **V7.** The behavior is precise, and one
part of it is a real design decision rather than a mechanism:

```text
1  the member clicks the return on an observation
2  the Studio navigates to Write, at the section the observation's earliest ref names
3  the passage scrolls into view and takes the held treatment
4  ⭐ THE OBSERVATION TRAVELS WITH IT — the existing, already-admitted observation is
   presented at the passage
5  ⛔ NO NEW READING IS COMMISSIONED
```

### ⭐⭐ Step 4 is the decision, and step 5 is why it matters

The obvious implementation is: navigate to the section, then let MAIA read the passage so she has
something to say. ⛔ **That is a commission inferred from navigation** — the same defect L-B refuses
at the tab boundary, arriving through the back door.

⭐ **The observation the member clicked is the observation they get.** It was admitted in a frozen
reading; it carries its identity, its refs and its `doesNotEstablish`. Returning to the passage
**presents it**, it does not regenerate it.

⚠️ **A consequence that must be honest, ⛔ not hidden:** the Work may have changed since the reading
was frozen. If the cited text has moved or been revised, the return says so —
*“This observation was made when the passage read differently”* — and offers the frozen evidence.
⛔ It never silently re-anchors to whatever is there now, and ⛔ it never quietly re-reads to
refresh itself.

### Positioning

- refs carrying a section and a code-point range → the exact passage, held
- refs carrying a section only → the section, top of its prose, ⛔ nothing held
- refs carrying no section (`structure-unit`, `structure-units`, `structure-topology`) → ⛔ **no
  single passage exists.** The return offers *“Show the sections involved”* and lists them. ⛔ A
  position is never invented, and ⛔ never coerced to the first section, which would place it at the
  start of the Work.

## §6 · ⭐ THE UNDECLARED-STRUCTURE STATE

Where the member has not declared the shape of their Work:

```text
        You haven't named the movements of this book.

        MAIA can show you the sections in the order you wrote them.
        She will not name the shape for you — that reading is yours to make.

        [ Name the movements ]        [ Keep it open for now ]
```

⭐ **Three states, ⛔ never two.** This is the arrival-experience condition that fails through
visual design while passing in data:

| State | Rendering |
|---|---|
| **DECLARED** | the shape, solid and primary |
| **DECLARED OPEN** — *“still discovering”* | ⭐ a **first-class state**, named as the member named it, ⛔ not missing data |
| **NEVER DECLARED** | the invitation above |

⛔ A never-declared Work rendered as **a form with blanks** fails this through visual design while
passing in data. ⛔ *Keep it open for now* is not the same as *Still discovering* — the first is
deferral, the second is a declaration. Both are lawful; ⛔ they are not interchangeable.

⛔ **Absence of a declaration never authorizes MAIA to invent one**, and ⛔ nothing becomes
canonical through silence. ⛔ A prior declaration must never silently become current again.

⭐ **Skipping is always lawful**, and the room is fully usable undeclared: Structure, Themes, Voice,
Continuity and Reader perspective all work on the sections as written. ⛔ Only **Arc** and the
elemental map depend on a declaration, and both say so rather than degrading silently.

## §6a · THE MAP — member-declared structure only

⭐ One surface, two sources, ⛔ never rendered the same way.

| Layer | Source | Rendering |
|---|---|---|
| **The shape** | ⭐ member-declared movements / parts / acts | **solid**, primary, the map itself |
| **Elemental or archetypal identity** | ⭐ member-declared **only** | colour on the declared shape |
| **MAIA's noticing** | a frozen reading | ⛔ a **separate, visibly secondary overlay**, toggled off by default |

**Form follows the Work, ⛔ never the house style.** A Work with five declared movements returning
to a centre reads as a **spiral**; a novel with a five-act arc reads as a **curve**; a Work with
ordered sections and no declared movement reads as a **sequence**. ⭐ The visualization adapts to
what was declared; ⛔ it never imposes a shape the Work does not have.

⛔ **No elemental classification is ever inferred from prose** — FR-06, absolute, no exception for a
Work whose subject happens to be elemental.

⚠️⚠️ **SUBSTRATE FINDING — `Declare structure` HAS NOWHERE TO WRITE.** `git grep` finds no
declaration store: no `declared_structure`, no `work_movements`, no persistence of any kind.
`WRITERS-STUDIO-ELEMENTAL-ARRIVAL-01` is a **design candidate** and explicitly carries
⛔ *no Compass persistence or schema*.

⭐ So the undeclared state's invitation is honest **only** once that substrate exists. Until then
the map renders **`Sequence`** — the sections in the order they were written, which is true of every
Work — and ⛔ the `Name the movements` control is **not drawn**. ⛔ Drawing it would be
`assertStudioMapHonest()` broken by a button, which is the same defect as `Notes · Research · Goals`.

## §6b · GUIDED · LEARNING · DIRECT in Develop

⭐ *Three facets. One intelligence. One Work.* ⛔ A facet changes **how much MAIA translates and
explains**. ⛔ It NEVER changes what she noticed, her evidence, her coverage, or what is reachable.

| | GUIDED | LEARNING | DIRECT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lens label | plain question only | plain question, editorial term beside it | editorial term primary |
| Observation | plain language | plain language **+ why this matters in craft terms**, offered | plain language + lens · coverage · refs · rationale |
| Evidence | one gesture away | one gesture away | **shown by default** |
| Map | the shape | the shape + what the pattern is called | + provenance of every layer |

⛔ **THE SAME OBSERVATION SET IN ALL THREE.** ⛔ Different default depth, ⛔ never different
reachability — *“Guided shows 6, Direct shows 87”* is a reachability difference wearing a
presentation costume.

⚠️ ⭐ **The sharpest hazard, named:** GUIDED is where MAIA carries the **most** interface and
translation complexity, and *“MAIA holds more of the process”* is the exact phrase under which
increased machine authorship arrives **sounding like care** — in the population where dependency
risk is highest. ⛔ **A facet changes how much MAIA TRANSLATES; it never changes how much MAIA
WRITES.** Proposal breadth stays **locus-bound and facet-invariant**.

## §9 · ⚠️⚠️ THE HIGH-FIDELITY DEVELOP REFERENCE — *The River Between*

Issued 2026-09-22. Three columns: manuscript left · Develop centre · MAIA right.
⛔ Not archived (same gap as the canon §1).

### 9.1 · ⭐ What it gets right, and it is a lot

- ⭐⭐ **“MAIA reads with you, not ahead of you.”** That is the law of this room in seven words and
  it should be kept verbatim.
- ⭐ **The manuscript is present while developing.** L1 Work-primary made structural: the member
  never leaves their book to look at their book.
- ⭐ **`This Passage` / `Larger Patterns`** expresses scope in plain language — ⛔ no *range*, ⛔ no
  *pass*, ⛔ no *scope*. **V12 answered by composition.**
- ⭐ **Your Story's Arc** as a curve with member-legible stages.
- ⭐ It **feels exciting rather than analytical**, which is the emotional half of the design question.

### 9.2 · ⭐⭐ THE CENTRAL PROBLEM, STATED ONCE

> ⭐ **This screen answers *“what does MAIA think of my book?”* beautifully.
> The design question was *“how does the member see their own Work?”*
> Those are different screens.**

⛔ **The member's own Work is nowhere on it.** No evidence. No section references. No returns. No
coverage. Every element is MAIA's interpretation, rendered as the primary, solid content of the room
— which is precisely the treatment §2.5 reserves for **declared structure** and forbids for
**MAIA interpretation**.

⭐ The fix is **not** to make it colder. It is to **ground every beautiful thing in the member's own
text.** That keeps the warmth and makes it true.

### 9.3 · ⛔ The hero is an `author-intent` claim in the largest type on the screen

> *“A story of change, connection, and what holds us. Your narrative weaves a personal journey with
> a larger world, exploring how inner transformation and outer circumstances shape each other.”*

⛔ This is MAIA telling the member **what their book is about**, as a statement of fact, over a hero
image, unattributed, carrying no evidence. `author-intent` is a **PERMANENT non-conclusion** — no
coverage discharges it, ever.

⭐ It is also the most invasive thing in any reference so far, **and it is beautiful, which is why
it is dangerous.** *The Studio will not choose one for you* and *MAIA will not define the Work for
you* exist to prevent exactly this.

**Three lawful forms, any of which keeps the hero:**

1. ⭐ **The member's own declaration**, in their words, in quotes — *“What you said this book is
   for.”* The most powerful version, and it needs the declaration substrate (§6a).
2. ⭐ **Explicitly MAIA's reading, offered for correction** — *“Here's what I'm hearing in it …
   Does that sound right?”* with **accept · revise · not quite**. ⛔ A reading the member can
   correct is not a definition.
3. **Open with the Arc instead** — their shape, not her summary.

### 9.4 · ⛔ “Key Patterns” — three defects in two words and four cards

- ⛔ **`Key`** is an importance judgment, out unless the writer declared it.
- ⛔ **The four patterns are MAIA's interpretive categories** — *Change and Transition · Connection ·
  Identity and Belonging · Nature as Mirror* — rendered **identically to declared structure**, as the
  solid primary content of the room. §2.5 requires interpretation to be a **visibly secondary
  overlay**.
- ⛔ **Not one carries a section reference or a return.** *“Explore →”* offers a door with no
  address. The member cannot see **where in their book** this pattern is, which is the difference
  between recognising their Work and being told about it.

⭐ Lawful: *“Patterns I noticed”* · each card carrying *“appears in §4, §11, §23”* · each returning
to the passage.

### 9.5 · ⛔ “What's Emerging” is evaluation end to end

| Rendered | Why it is barred |
|---|---|
| *“Your protagonist's inner world is growing **richer**.”* | verdict, stated as a property |
| *“Relationships are opening in new and **interesting** ways.”* | verdict |

### 10.1 · ⭐ Three real improvements over §9

1. **Map it to `development`**, which already asks *which ideas are introduced, abandoned, repeated

### 10.5 · ⛔ The theme cards still have no address

Four cards, four *“Explore this theme →”*, ⛔ **not one section reference**. The chart directly

⭐ **And that is what would make *“Themes are not labels”* true.** A label is a name imposed from

about the object.

### 10.6 · ⚠️ NEW CATEGORY — visual interpretation

## §11 · THE VOICE FRAME — and the defect only this lens can have

### 11.1 · ⭐ The best single line in any reference

> *“Not a style to replicate, but a voice to deepen.”* — MAIA

⭐ That **is** the `voice` lens law, stated for a member: *the manuscript itself is the reference,
never an external standard.* Keep it verbatim.

### 11.2 · ⛔⛔ AND THE CARDS DIRECTLY CONTRADICT IT

**Tone · Perspective · Language · Character Voice** — four named dimensions, each with a definition
written from outside the Work:

> *Tone — the emotional texture that runs through your story.*
> *Perspective — the lens through which your story is experienced.*

⭐ **That is an imported craft taxonomy, which is precisely what the `voice` lens forbids.** Scoring
a Work on four axes brought from elsewhere is an external standard, however gently it is phrased —
and the frame says so itself two inches above.

⭐ **The lawful form describes what this voice actually DOES, in this Work:**

```text
⛔  Tone — the emotional texture that runs through your story.
✅  Your sentences shorten when Clara is certain and lengthen into
    subordinate clauses when she isn’t. Ch 2 and Ch 6 are the clearest cases.   Ch 2  Ch 6
```

⭐ The second needs no taxonomy, carries its evidence, and is about **this** novel. ⛔ The first
would read identically under any manuscript — which is the test for an imported standard.

### 11.3 · Carried, now systematic across Overview · Themes · Voice

⭐ The same three defects recur on every lens frame, so **one fix applies to all of them**:

1. ⛔ **Cards carry no address** while the chart beneath them knows exactly which chapters are
   involved. ⭐ *Make the cards look more like the chart.*
2. ⛔ **“What's Emerging” is a verdict generator** — *“a reflective, grounded tone”* · *“richer
   sensory language”* · *“you're staying with Clara in **meaningful** ways.”* ⭐ The hedge is on the
   section heading and never reaches the items.
3. ⛔ **No coverage anywhere**, on any lens frame.

Plus: *“see how **key** elements shift”* (barred word) · *“Your voice here feels **especially
present**”* (property; ⭐ *“I find your voice especially present here”* is the lawful stance).

## §14 · CONTINUITY — the strongest object yet, and a regression

### 14.1 · ⭐⭐ THE CONTINUITY MAP IS THE BEST THING IN THE PROJECT

A 5 × 12 grid: `Clara · The River · Change · Belonging · Uncertainty` against `Ch 1 … Ch 12`, each
cell shaded **Stronger presence / Moderate / Brief mention**.

⭐ It is the **best answer anyone has produced to the founder's design question**, and it is worth
saying exactly why:

- ⭐ **It is entirely the member's own book.** Not one interpretive category — five things that are
  demonstrably in the text, and where.
- ⭐ **Every cell is an address.** Row × chapter. The *where?* test passes at the granularity of a
  single cell, which nothing else in the project achieves.
- ⭐ **It is non-evaluative by construction.** Presence, ⛔ not quality. There is no way to grade a
  book with a presence grid.
- ⭐⭐ **It reveals something the member genuinely cannot see from inside their manuscript** — that
  *Belonging* is absent until Ch 6, that *The River* thins in the middle — **without telling them
  what it means.** That is *seeing the shape of your Work* with no editorial vocabulary required.

> ⭐ **RECOMMENDATION: promote the Continuity Map to the Overview.** It is doing the job the hero is
> currently attempting, and doing it with the member's own material instead of MAIA's summary.

| *“Even in change, a sense of **hope remains**”* | ⛔ ⭐ this one reads the Work's *meaning*, not its text |

### 14.3 · ⭐⭐ THE PATTERN BEHIND BOTH — corrections are landing PER FRAME, not systemically

⭐ **Each frame is corrected by hand, so each new frame reintroduces what the last one fixed.**

⭐ Also good: **Where This Chapter Lives** (positional, ⛔ not evaluative) · **Nearby Movements**

### 15.2 · ⚠️⚠️ BUT THE EIGHT MOVEMENTS ARE SPIRALOGIC, NOT THIS NOVEL

`I. Home · II. Descent · III. Threshold · IV. Transformation · V. Integration · VI. Belonging ·
VII. Contribution · VIII. Renewal`

⭐ **A member writing a novel about a river did not invent those eight names.** That is the
Soullab developmental arc, applied to their Work and **labelled *member-declared***.

⚠️ **Declaring by ACCEPTING A TEMPLATE is not the same act as NAMING YOUR OWN MOVEMENTS.** Both are
lawful. ⛔ The second must not be presented as the first, and the label currently cannot tell them
apart — which is the *never-declared rendered as declared-open* collapse in a new costume.

⭐ **Two states, visibly different:**

```text
✅  You named these movements.                    →  Home · Descent · Threshold …
✅  You chose the Spiral template, and adapted it →  same shape, provenance shown,
                                                     "these names came from the template"
```

⛔ Otherwise the house framework arrives as the member's own reading of their book, which is exactly
what Invariant 14 and FR-06 exist to prevent. ⭐ For *Elemental Alchemy* the Spiral **is** the Work's
architecture and this is wonderful; ⛔ for a novel it is a frame offered and then attributed back.

### 15.3 · ⭐⭐ NEW DEFECT CLASS — CHARTING AN INFERENCE

Two charts sit in the same room and look identical in form:

| Chart | What it plots | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuity Map** (§14) | ⭐ **presence** — countable, verifiable, checkable cell by cell | ✅ evidence |
| **Emotional Flow** — *Uncertainty → Reflection → Opening → Steadier* | ⛔ **feeling MAIA inferred** from the prose | ⛔ interpretation |

> ⭐⭐ **A chart of an inference is still an inference — and the chart form lends it authority it
> has not earned.**

⛔ The member cannot see the difference. A line graph reads as *measured*; *“the feeling beneath the
events”* is MAIA's reading of their emotional content, plotted as though it were data.

⭐ **Lawful forms, either:** plot something countable that stands in for it and say so
(*“questions per chapter”, “sentences in Clara's interior”*), **or** render it visibly as MAIA's
reading — different treatment, dotted, attributed, and revisable like the map.

⛔ **The general law:** a visualization must be **as verifiable as its form implies.** ⭐ Presence
grids and counts may be charted; inferred interior states may not be charted **in the same visual
language as counts.**
