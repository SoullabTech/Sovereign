  · `3 Primary Characters (in your text)`. ⭐ That is the three-kind provenance model arrived at

- ⭐⭐ **`What MAIA Read` states its SOURCES** — *Chapters 1–12 (100%) · Last updated Sep 22 ·
  **Sources: Your manuscript only***. ⭐ That last line settles the `Sources` ambiguity permanently:
  it names **what she read from**, ⛔ not a claim to have verified anything outside the Work. Keep it
  verbatim.
- ⭐ **The hero is the member's own sentence with an address** — *Chapter 6 · p. 112.* Recognition
  without an author-intent claim.
- ⭐ **Plain frequency legend** — *Often appears · Sometimes appears · Once or twice · Not present.*
  ⭐ Converged with this build's own `often · sometimes · once or twice · not here`, independently.
- ⭐ **Key Passages carry page addresses** and every finding returns.
- ⭐ **“I'll stay with your voice, your intentions, and what's actually on the page.”**
- ⭐ **`Try asking:` is lawful and worth naming as such** — ⭐ **a suggested question the MEMBER asks

## §2 · ⛔ THE ONE SERIOUS DEFECT — a judgment sitting in a row of counts

```text
12 Chapters   5 Core Themes   3 Primary Characters   Balanced Pacing   87% Coverage
2,134 words   (member-declared) (in your text)        Room to deepen    See details
                                                       Ch 6–8
```

⛔ **`Balanced` is a quality verdict about the Work, placed among four countable facts.**
⛔ `Room to deepen Ch 6–8` is prescriptive — it asserts a consequence with no commission.

⭐⭐ **This is the *charting an inference* defect (§15.3) in tile form, and the tile row makes it
worse than a chart does: a judgment placed among counts inherits their authority.** The member reads
five measurements. Four are measurements.

⭐ **Lawful, same tile, same glance:**

```text
⛔  Balanced · Pacing · Room to deepen Ch 6–8
✅  2,134 · words per chapter, median
    Ch 6–8 run 1,100–1,300; the rest run 1,900–2,600.
```

⭐ The second says the thing the first was reaching for, is checkable, and leaves the judgment where
it belongs.

## §3 · ⚠️ Tabs — three have no lens, one is loaded

`Overview · Structure · Pacing · Characters · Themes · Voice · Reader Experience · Readiness`

Against the ratified vocabulary (`structure · development · continuity · arc · voice · coherence ·
reader`): ⛔ `Pacing`, `Characters` and `Readiness` have no lens, and `Continuity` and `Arc` are
missing despite the Continuity Map being the centrepiece of the screen.

⚠️⚠️ **`Readiness` is the one to strike.** ⭐ It is the most evaluative framing in the entire
product: a tab whose only possible content is a judgment about whether the member's Work is **ready**
— which is precisely what *insights, never grades* forbids, and it cannot be rephrased into an
observation because readiness is not a property of text.

⭐ `Pacing` and `Characters` are genuinely useful and lawful as **observations**: *“Ch 6–8 run
1,100–1,300 words; the rest run 1,900–2,600”* and *“Clara appears in 11 of 12 chapters; Mara in 3,
all in Part II.”* ⛔ They need a lens by a governed act, or they map onto `structure` and
`continuity`.

### 5.1 · ⭐⭐ What this build adds that neither reference shows

**A reading is a reading AT A TIME.** `lib/writersStudio/studio/reading.ts`.

> *Read on 18 September. **3 sections have changed since — what's below is true of what MAIA read,
> not of what's there now.***

⭐ Reference A shows *Last updated: Sep 22*, which is the date. ⛔ It does not say **the Work has
moved since**, and that is the quietest untruth available to this product: nothing looks wrong, every
finding still cites a real chapter, and the member cannot know the sentence under discussion no
longer exists.

⛔ Both obvious repairs are refused: ⛔ silently re-anchoring the finding would make MAIA appear to
have read text she never read; ⛔ silently re-reading to refresh is a commission inferred from
navigation. ⭐ **The lawful repair is disclosure** — the finding says its passage changed and offers
**the frozen text it was made against**:

```text
⚠️ This passage has changed since MAIA read it.
   “The current had changed, and she had not noticed when.”
   WHAT MAIA READ
```

⭐ **Superseded readings are kept, ⛔ not deleted** — what MAIA believed before a correction is part
of the record.

**Scope is a claim, not a filter.** A chapter review and a whole-Work review assert different things:
*“Chapter 5 only. Patterns across the whole novel need their own reading.”*

**Tabs filter; ⛔ they never commission.** `TAB-never-commissions` asserts it over the rendered DOM —
36/36. The only commissioning control appears where a reading is **absent or partial**.

**Four lens states, ⛔ never one empty list.** *“MAIA read the whole work for this and found nothing
to bring you”* is a **result**; *“MAIA hasn't read your work for how it moves yet”* is an
**absence**. Rendered separately at `8-review-not-read` and `9-review-nothing-noticed`.
