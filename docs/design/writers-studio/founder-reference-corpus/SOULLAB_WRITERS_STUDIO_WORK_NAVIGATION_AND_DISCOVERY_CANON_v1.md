# SOULLAB WRITER'S STUDIO
# Work Navigation + Discovery Canon v1

Status: FLAGSHIP PRODUCT / INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE AUTHORITY  
Purpose: define how a member moves through their Work without losing place, without creating false rooms, and without turning discovery into a dashboard or menu problem.

This canon is additive to:
- Flagship Experience Canon
- First Arrival + Onboarding
- Intent-First Entry + First Ten Minutes
- Durable Place + Observation Address
- Failure, Recovery + Concurrency
- Visual System Canon
- existing capability-honesty / provenance / coverage law

It does not authorize implementation, new routes, Search, Library, or deployment.

---

# 1. North Star

> **Writer's Studio should feel like exploring a living world that happens to be your book.**

Navigation is therefore not just:
> "How do I get to another screen?"

It is:

> **How do I follow something meaningful through my Work and still know where I am?**

---

# 2. The Three Primary Modes

Primary Writer's Studio navigation is:

```text
Write · Develop · Review
```

These are the three durable modes of relationship to the same Work.

## Write
Be in the manuscript.

## Develop
See the Work from another altitude.

## Review
Return to what has been noticed and decide what to follow next.

Do not add a fourth primary Studio mode without a separate product ruling.

---

# 3. Arrival Is Not a Fourth Room

First Arrival is a state over the Work.

It may resolve into:
- Write;
- current Work root;
- a lawful returning place.

Do not create a generic Home dashboard simply because a nav item exists.

If `Home` has no real product semantics:
> remove it.

---

# 4. Navigation Layers

Writer's Studio has four navigation layers.

## Layer A — Product mode
`Write · Develop · Review`

## Layer B — Work place
Work → part/chapter → section → passage

## Layer C — Discovery object
observation → map cell → related passage → thread → finding

## Layer D — Temporary contextual view
Contents · Related · History · What MAIA Read · evidence · MAIA conversation

These layers must not be collapsed into one giant sidebar.

---

# 5. Work Identity Is the Constant

Every meaningful surface should preserve enough Work identity that the member can answer:

> **What Work am I in?**

At minimum:
- Work title;
- current chapter/section when relevant.

The Work title is not decorative branding.

It is the stable anchor of the Studio.

---

# 6. Place Crumb

A quiet crumb should answer:

> **Where am I?**

Example:

```text
Elemental Alchemy › Chapter 10 › The Living Spiral
```

or:

```text
The River Between › Chapter 6 › The Current Changes
```

The crumb is orientation, not a dense breadcrumb tree.

It may allow navigation to meaningful ancestors.

---

# 7. "Back" Means Return to Relationship, Not Generic Browser History

Back controls should name the relationship they restore.

Prefer:
- Back to Review
- Back to manuscript
- Back to chapter
- Back to Continuity Map

Avoid ambiguous:
- Back
- Close

when the destination matters.

The product should preserve the exact return trail.

---

# 8. Review Trail

A Review finding opens a trail:

```text
Review
→ observation O44
→ exact passage
→ MAIA conversation about O44
→ related place if chosen
→ Back to observation / Review
```

The trail remembers:
- origin mode;
- finding;
- observation id;
- locus;
- related branch if followed.

A finding must never become a trapdoor.

---

# 9. Related Passage Trail

Example:

```text
current passage
→ Related Passages
→ Chapter 3 passage
```

When the member arrives, show why:

> **You followed this here from Chapter 6.**

Then offer:
- Back to Chapter 6
- See the thread
- Talk with MAIA

The relation travels.

The member should not have to remember why they opened this passage.

---

# 10. Discovery Objects Are Portals, Not Destinations

Examples:
- finding;
- continuity cell;
- map node;
- theme occurrence;
- voice observation;
- member note.

Every discovery object should be able to answer:

1. Where does this live?
2. Why am I seeing it?
3. What can I do next?
4. How do I get back?

An analytical object with no route into the Work is incomplete.

---

# 11. Contents

`Contents` is a contextual navigation surface.

It may show:
- parts;
- chapters;
- sections;
- current location.

It is not:
- a permanent outline rail;
- a structural judgment;
- MAIA's inferred hierarchy unless clearly labelled.

Contents should dismiss.

---

# 12. History

`History` is a contextual authored-change surface.

It may show:
- applied revision;
- undo/revert;
- prior wording;
- timestamp;
- actor/provenance.

It is not:
- a primary mode;
- a permanent content column;
- a generic activity feed.

History serves:
> authorship continuity.

---

# 13. Related

`Related` is contextual.

It may show:
- evidence-backed passage relationships;
- member-declared relationships;
- same phrase/entity occurrences;
- observations connected by known evidence.

It must preserve:
- provenance;
- coverage;
- exact address.

Do not invent interpretive glosses for unread material.

---

# 14. What MAIA Read

`What MAIA Read` is a trust / coverage surface.

It answers:
- what Work;
- what chapters/sections/passages;
- when;
- reading identity;
- current/stale status.

It is not:
- a generic Sources room;
- external fact-checking unless such capability separately exists.

This surface should be reachable from any cross-Work observation.

---

# 15. Evidence

Evidence opens contextually from the observation it supports.

The member should see:
- exact quoted/identified locus where lawful;
- historical/current status;
- provenance;
- return.

Evidence should not force the member into a separate research application.

---

# 16. MAIA Conversation

Conversation belongs to:
- the Work;
- the locus;
- the observation or member intent.

It is not an independent destination that loses manuscript context.

The member may dismiss MAIA and continue in the Work.

---

# 17. Discovery Order

Default order follows the Work.

For findings/passages:

> **book order / page order / authored order**

This is the safest non-ranking default.

Other lawful orderings may exist when explicitly chosen by the member, such as:
- newest;
- my observations;
- MAIA observations;
- chapter.

Do not silently order by:
- importance;
- relevance;
- severity;
- actionability;
- confidence.

---

# 18. "Next" Means Next in the Work Unless Explicitly Stated Otherwise

If a finding pager says:

```text
‹ 1 of 4 ›
```

default traversal should be book/page order.

If the product uses a different ordering, label it.

The user should never have to infer whether `next` means:
- next page;
- next ranked result;
- next generated result.

---

# 19. Search — Future Capability

Search is useful but not automatically part of the flagship.

Do not expose `Search` until a real capability exists.

Before adding it, decide which product it is:

## Exact text search
Find literal words/phrases.

## Semantic Work search
Find conceptually related passages.

## Observation search
Find member/MAIA observations.

## Cross-library search
Find across Works.

These are different capabilities with different trust, privacy, and coverage implications.

Do not hide them behind one vague magnifying glass.

---

# 20. Library — Future Capability

Library is outside the three Writer's Studio modes unless separately ruled.

Do not expose it because references contain the word.

If a broader Soullab Library exists later:
- it lives at product-shell level;
- entering a Work returns to the same Studio semantics.

---

# 21. Work Root

The Work title / identity may become the most truthful route to a Work-root surface.

Potential Work-root content:
- current place;
- chapters/sections;
- member-declared purpose;
- recent durable observations;
- lawful resume;
- Work facts.

Do not decide this through nav styling.

The Work-root semantics require a separate implementation specification.

---

# 22. No Dead Ends

Every surface must have at least one meaningful way forward or back.

Examples:

### Finding
- Go to passage
- Discuss
- Explore
- Back to Review

### Map cell
- Open passage
- See thread
- Back to map

### Empty state
- Keep writing
- Choose smaller scope
- Read this chapter

### Failed request
- Try again
- Return to passage
- Keep writing

No screen should end in:
> nothing to do.

---

# 23. No Loss of Place

Opening:
- Contents;
- Related;
- History;
- MAIA;
- evidence;
- coverage

must preserve:
- Work;
- chapter/section;
- selected passage where relevant;
- scroll/locus;
- origin trail.

Closing restores the same place.

---

# 24. Place Changes Must Be Legible

When navigation changes the manuscript locus:

Use one or more:
- subtle highlight;
- focused passage;
- crumb update;
- small arrival message.

Avoid dramatic transitions.

The writer should know:
> **I moved.**

---

# 25. Highlight Is Temporary Orientation

A passage highlight may show:
> this is where the finding lives.

It should not look like:
- error;
- grade;
- permanent semantic classification.

Highlight should fade or become quieter after orientation, while the underlying selection remains available.

---

# 26. Deep Links

Where supported, a deep link to a Work observation/passage should preserve:

- access control;
- Work identity;
- address;
- observation identity;
- current/historical state.

If the recipient lacks permission:
- do not leak Work metadata;
- show lawful access state.

No deep link should silently broaden sharing authority.

---

# 27. URL / Route Semantics

Routes should reflect durable product semantics, not temporary UI accidents.

Prefer:
- stable mode + Work identity;
- query/state for contextual view where appropriate.

Avoid creating a route for every:
- drawer;
- tab;
- temporary MAIA phase;
- animation state.

Exact route design is implementation-owned.

The law is:
> durable concepts deserve durable identity; ephemeral UI does not automatically deserve a route.

---

# 28. Mobile Navigation

Mobile preserves the same conceptual hierarchy.

Primary:
- Write
- Develop
- Review

Context:
- sheets / overlays / inline cards.

Back trails:
- explicit;
- thumb-reachable;
- predictable.

Do not rename modes on mobile.

Do not replace navigation with hidden gestures only.

---

# 29. Keyboard Navigation

Keyboard should support:
- mode navigation;
- opening/closing contextual surfaces;
- moving through findings where lawful;
- returning to manuscript;
- focus restoration.

Do not make keyboard traversal mimic every visual card if a more meaningful semantic order exists.

Focus order follows task meaning.

---

# 30. Voice Navigation

If voice is enabled, member may say things like:

- Go back to my manuscript
- Show me the earlier passage
- Open Review
- Show me where this appears
- Take me back

Voice commands must resolve to the same governed navigation actions.

Voice must not invent capabilities unavailable in UI.

---

# 31. "Adventure" Without Game Map Theater

The Work may feel explorable through:
- threads;
- maps;
- passages;
- relationships;
- return paths.

Do not add:
- quest log;
- XP;
- levels;
- unlocks;
- achievement map;
- progress game board.

The adventure is intellectual/creative discovery.

---

# 32. Orientation at Every Altitude

## Manuscript altitude
Exact passage.

## Chapter altitude
Current chapter / local structure.

## Work altitude
Continuity / declared structure / recurring material.

The product should let the writer move between these without losing identity.

This is one of Soullab's distinctive strengths.

---

# 33. Zooming the Work

Conceptually:

```text
PASSAGE
   ↕
SECTION / CHAPTER
   ↕
WHOLE WORK
```

This is not necessarily literal zoom UI.

It is an information-architecture law:

> **The writer can move from local words to larger pattern and back to exact words.**

Every larger-scale insight must retain a path back down.

---

# 34. Member-Created Paths

Member observations can become navigable paths.

Example:

```text
member question
→ attached passage
→ linked related passage
→ member-declared theme
```

Do not automatically turn every note into a formal structure.

The member chooses what relationships matter.

---

# 35. MAIA-Created Paths

MAIA may suggest:
> **These three passages appear related because the phrase recurs.**

The path retains:
- evidence;
- coverage;
- provenance;
- revisability.

It is not silently promoted to member structure.

---

# 36. Recent / Resume

If durable state exists, a quiet resume surface may show:

- last Work place;
- recent member observation;
- unfinished conversation;
- stale-reading notice.

Do not create an activity feed.

Resume exists to help:
> return to the Work.

---

# 37. Navigation Capability Honesty

Every active navigation control must answer:

- destination exists?
- member has access?
- state can render?
- return path exists?
- capability is implemented?

If not:
- hide;
- disable with truthful explanation where valuable;
- do not render active-looking dead link.

---

# 38. Navigation Failure

If a destination fails:

> **Writer's Studio couldn't open that passage. You're still in Chapter 6.**

Actions:
- Try again
- Back to Review
- Keep writing

Failure preserves current place.

---

# 39. Navigation + Freshness

A historical finding may point to a changed/missing current passage.

When opened:
- show historical evidence;
- disclose resolution state;
- offer current location only when truthfully resolved.

Do not silently jump to "closest" current text.

---

# 40. Navigation + Facets

Guided/Learning/Direct do not alter navigation semantics.

Same finding:
- same Go to passage;
- same Back to Review;
- same Related locations.

Facet changes explanation, not the map of the Work.

---

# 41. Navigation + Accessibility

Required:
- headings/landmarks match visual hierarchy;
- skip/return mechanisms sensible;
- focus restored after drawer/sheet closes;
- map cells keyboard reachable where interactive;
- current location exposed programmatically;
- selected passage not indicated by color only;
- touch targets adequate.

---

# 42. Required Mechanical Gates

## NAV1 — THREE MODE LAW
Primary Studio modes remain Write / Develop / Review.

## NAV2 — NO FALSE DESTINATION
Every live nav item resolves to real capability.

## NAV3 — WORK IDENTITY
Every mode preserves Work identity.

## NAV4 — PLACE IDENTITY
Meaningful navigation preserves exact authored place.

## NAV5 — RETURN TRAIL
Every discovery path has a lawful return.

## NAV6 — BOOK ORDER DEFAULT
Finding traversal defaults to authored order unless explicitly changed.

## NAV7 — CONTEXT NOT ROOM
Contents / Related / History / Coverage / Evidence / MAIA are contextual, not accidental new primary rooms.

## NAV8 — NO LOSS OF PLACE
Opening/closing contextual UI preserves locus.

## NAV9 — RELATED EVIDENCE
Related links have evidence/coverage/provenance.

## NAV10 — HISTORICAL HONESTY
Changed/missing loci are not silently re-anchored.

## NAV11 — MOBILE SEMANTIC PARITY
Mobile preserves the same modes and return semantics.

## NAV12 — CAPABILITY HONESTY
No Home/Search/Library/Export until real.

## NAV13 — DISCOVERY LEADS TO WORK
Every analytical object can reach authored material.

## NAV14 — WORK SCALE LOOP
Local → larger pattern → local path exists.

## NAV15 — ACCESSIBLE ORIENTATION
Current place and return controls are accessible.

---

# 43. Defeat Candidates

### D-N1
`Home` remains active with no destination.

### D-N2
Opening Contents replaces the manuscript and loses scroll.

### D-N3
Related passage opens with no indication why.

### D-N4
Review finding pager uses hidden relevance ranking.

### D-N5
MAIA appears as a fourth primary mode.

### D-N6
History becomes a permanent third content column.

### D-N7
Continuity Map cell has no exact passage destination.

### D-N8
Historical observation jumps to a guessed current passage.

### D-N9
Mobile renames Develop to Explore.

### D-N10
Search icon appears before search semantics exist.

### D-N11
Back returns to generic Write instead of originating Review finding.

### D-N12
What MAIA Read becomes a generic Sources page.

### D-N13
A whole-Work insight has no route to evidence.

### D-N14
Closing MAIA resets the manuscript to top.

### D-N15
A link exposes Work identity to unauthorized recipient.

---

# 44. Founder Navigation Questions

1. Do I know what Work I'm in?
2. Do I know where I am in it?
3. Can I move to the larger shape and back to exact words?
4. When I follow something, do I know why I arrived?
5. Can I always get back?
6. Does the Studio feel explorable without feeling gamified?
7. Are Contents/History/Related helpful without becoming rooms?
8. Does Review lead into the Work rather than away from it?
9. Does Develop reveal relationships I can actually visit?
10. Does mobile preserve the same mental map?
11. Is anything in navigation promising a capability that isn't real?
12. Does the Work remain the subject everywhere?

---

# 45. Product Principle

> **Navigation should make the Work feel larger, not the software feel larger.**

---

# 46. Spirit of Soullab

The member should feel:

> I can enter anywhere.
>
> I know where I am.
>
> I can follow what interests me.
>
> Every pattern leads back to actual words.
>
> I can move from one sentence to the whole Work and back again.
>
> I don't lose my place when MAIA joins me.
>
> I don't fall into tools.
>
> I travel through my own Work.

**A deeper you. A more human world.**
