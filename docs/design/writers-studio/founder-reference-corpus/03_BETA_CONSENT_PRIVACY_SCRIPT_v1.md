# SOULLAB WRITER'S STUDIO
# Beta Consent + Privacy Script v1

Use this as a plain-language session script. Adapt it to applicable legal/privacy requirements before public research.

## Before we begin

> "This is a product research session for Soullab Writer's Studio. We're testing the product, not you or the quality of your writing."

> "You can stop at any time, skip any task, or decline to answer any question."

> "Please use only writing you are comfortable including in this test environment. If you prefer, we can use sample material instead of your own manuscript."

> "We may take notes about how you use the product and what you say during the session. We should not record or retain more personal information than we need for the product research."

If recording audio/video/screens:
> "With your permission, we would also like to record [screen/audio/video] so we can review the session accurately. The recording is for product research and should be handled according to the research retention policy. You can participate without recording if that option is available."

Ask:
> "Are you comfortable continuing?"

If recording:
> "Do you consent to [screen/audio/video] recording?"

Record:
- Session participation consent: YES / NO
- Screen recording consent: YES / NO / N/A
- Audio recording consent: YES / NO / N/A
- Video recording consent: YES / NO / N/A

## Privacy principles for the research team

- collect the minimum participant information needed;
- use participant IDs in working notes;
- do not copy manuscript passages into reports unless needed to document a specific usability finding;
- remove unnecessary identifying information from screenshots;
- do not place sensitive participant content into unrelated tools/services;
- do not use beta manuscripts for model training unless an explicit, separate consent framework authorizes it;
- respect deletion/retention commitments;
- separate product findings from participant identity wherever practical.

## Important

This script is product-research language, not legal advice.

Before external/public beta research, reconcile it with:
- Soullab's actual privacy policy;
- participant terms;
- recording practices;
- retention/deletion policy;
- applicable jurisdictional requirements.
