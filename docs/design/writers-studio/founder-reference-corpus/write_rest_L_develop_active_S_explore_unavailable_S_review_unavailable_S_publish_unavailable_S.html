  1. o1movementCurrent

    The four sections run on a single day's clock, each opening by naming a time: first light in section 1, noon in section 2, after the others leave in section 3, and before evening in section 4. The sequence of time markers is carried in the opening sentences rather than in headings, and it moves forward without repeating a time already passed.

  2. o2positional asymmetryCurrent

    Mara opens the sequence and closes it, and both of her sections turn on a card or cards and on an act of withholding order: in section 1 she declines to arrange the chairs in a circle and leaves the card blank apart from the date; in section 4 she carries the cards to the table but does not sort them. Sections 2 and 3, sitting between them, are given to the group and to Tomas.

    The question the group is described as circling in section 2 — what must be kept, what can change, who names the difference — is not answered anywhere in the four sections.
