## What the process did

### 1. One concern per pass, across the whole Work

Each pass covered a span of the book (Part One: Ch1 + Ch2) for one problem only, and each carried its own test and its own limit:

- **Invitational Voice:** *read it imagining a reader who has been in ceremony for thirty years — does it land as with them or as to them?*
- **Transition:** scope locked at *max 3 full reshapes + 3 light touches*.

**Studio today:** work is passage by passage; no surface runs one concern across many passages.
**Implication:** this is the Commission layer (step 5). A pass is a commission: a concern, a test, a span and a limit, named by the writer or accepted by the writer.

### 2. The author changed the method mid-pass

Transition pass, Kelly (2026-05-04): *reshape the entry point and exit point, not the paragraph — problems are at thresholds, not interiors.*

**Implication:** the writer can redirect how MAIA works within a commission, not only accept or reject its proposals. A method change is a writer act and is recorded as one.

### 3. Diagnosis began with what already worked

The Invitational pass opens with *where the book is already invitational*, then lists flagged passages.

**Implication:** consistent with the relational default (*what I'm noticing → what may help → what do you think?*). A commission's reading names what the Work already does before it names what to change. ⛔ This is presentation, not ranking: flagged passages appear in manuscript order (FACETS-01 reachability ruling).

### 4. The author chose from options, verbatim or reshaped

Most Invitational edits landed as *Kelly's approved option text verbatim*.

**Studio today:** Show edit options and the in-Focus Craftsman's Table (change by change, with *Why?* and *Another way*) already support this. ✅ Keep.

### 5. Settled decisions stayed settled

Chapter 10 carries **Closed decisions (do not relitigate)**, each with its reason. Example: ChatGPT proposed changing Air's spine word from *With* to *What*; rejected because the function is *already held in* the *Know / Intellectualize* pair and in *"what was made"* — *Recursion, not redundancy. Do not reopen.*

**Studio today:** no record of the writer's settled decisions, so MAIA can re-propose what was already refused.
**Implication — the largest gap:** a writer-declared **closed decision** (passage or Work scope, the writer's own wording of what is settled and why). Under intention custody: MAIA may notice or ask; ⛔ only the writer closes or reopens. A closed decision orients what MAIA proposes; ⛔ it is not evidence about the text and discharges no coverage (charter §10.3).

### 6. The reason each line stays was written down

Chapter 10's *Notes on prose choices* records why each kept line stays (*"Not metaphor. Process." — Kelly's phrase. Held verbatim.*), including which line to cut if it ever falls flat.

**Implication:** the revision history keeps the writer's reason alongside the choice, including rejected options. This is already the case-study evidence law (original · reading · proposal · author's choice including rejection · result · limits) applied inside the Studio rather than only in a case study.

### 7. The author's principles were in force before editing

Each AI workspace received the writing constitution first, with a register check (*FIRST determine the register*) and rules such as: *invented experience in Kelly's voice is a forgery, not a draft — ask for one.*

**Implication:** a writer may declare standing principles for a Work, held under the Work Compass custody rules: in the writer's own sentences, revisable, orientation for what MAIA attends to. ⛔ Never a grading rubric, ⛔ never assigned or inferred by MAIA, ⛔ never evidence about the text. The *ask for a lived moment, never invent one* rule is a candidate MAIA-wide behaviour, independent of any one writer.

## What not to carry over

In the Invitational pass, two of ten edits (#4, #9) landed as a *move-shape* Claude applied, with Kelly *to refine if her exact ChatGPT phrasing differs from what landed*. That is MAIA's wording entering the manuscript in place of the writer's — the drift FACETS-01 §9.3 forbids (*a facet never changes how much MAIA writes*).

**Rule for the Studio:** only the writer's own text or the writer's explicit choice of a shown option enters the Work. ⛔ No pending "the author will refine it later" state.

## Two models, one judge

Kelly used ChatGPT and Claude side by side and arbitrated, with the repository as canonical and each workspace a snapshot (*Repo is canonical. Projects are downstream mirrors.*).

**Implication:** the transferable thing is **a second reading the writer judges between**, not a second provider. ⛔ MAIA's runtime uses no other cloud provider (Project Invariants). *Another way* is a small version of this today.
