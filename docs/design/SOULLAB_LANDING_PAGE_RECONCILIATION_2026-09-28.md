# Soullab Landing Page Reconciliation — 2026-09-28

## Purpose

Bring the public Soullab page into alignment with the current platform identity:

- **Soullab** is the member-facing platform.
- **Home** is the canonical platform entrance and orientation field.
- **MAIA** is the relational intelligence and a destination within Soullab.
- **AIN OS** is the sovereign intelligence architecture beneath the platform.

The page should help a first-time visitor understand this hierarchy without requiring internal programme knowledge.

## Primary visitor journey

1. **What is Soullab?**
2. **What is inside it?**
3. **Why is it different?**
4. **Who is MAIA?**
5. **What inquiry drives the work?**
6. **How is the field being extended with real people and practices?**
7. **What commitments govern participation?**
8. **Enter Soullab.**

## Problems found in the previous page

### MAIA carried too much product identity
The page repeatedly positioned MAIA as the thing being entered, the primary experience, and the organizing principle. That conflicts with the new Home architecture.

### Duplicate explanation
A full MAIA narrative section, a product portfolio, an Ask section, the floating Ask widget, and the MAIA section itself repeated overlapping explanations.

### Internal architecture appeared too early
AIN OS matters, but first-time visitors need to understand Soullab before they understand its substrate.

### Recent platform work was buried
Home, cross-facet continuity, Writer's Studio, Teaching Intelligence, governed retrieval/source work, and Relational Geometry were not legible as a coherent frontier.

### CTA intent was mixed
The hero paired member entry with business collaboration. The secondary hero gesture now helps a visitor understand the platform before asking them to contact Soullab.

## Reconciliation

The primary public CTA is **Enter Soullab → /home**.

The hero secondary action is **See what's inside**.

The main scroll no longer mounts the duplicate Narrative, Portfolio, or large Ask sections. The lightweight Ask widget remains available without becoming a second page architecture.

## Current innovation presentation

The page surfaces selected recent work with explicit epistemic/product standing:

- **Living House / Home** — Live
- **Cross-Facet Continuity** — In build
- **Writer's Studio** — In build
- **Teaching Intelligence** — In build
- **Governed Source Fabric** — In build
- **Relational Geometry Reasoning** — Research

The complete capability ledger remains expandable and continues to distinguish:

- Available Today
- Architected for Release
- Research

No research programme should be presented as a deployed capability simply because its architecture is sophisticated or its local witness is promising.

## Landing-page best-practice constraints

- One dominant primary CTA.
- Explain value before internal architecture.
- Show the product before asking for contact.
- Avoid repeating the same proposition in multiple long sections.
- Keep status claims legible.
- Preserve mobile readability and tap targets.
- Use social proof only where the relationship and public claim are appropriate.
- Keep the page scannable while allowing deeper technical/research inspection by disclosure.
- Never make MAIA, AIN OS, or the Holoflower carry explanation that prose should make clear.
