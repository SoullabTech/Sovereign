## 0. ⭐⭐⭐ Method — temporal truth vs present-tense authority

⛔ **A statement is not wrong merely because the constitution later changed.**

| Kind | Test | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Historical statement** | Was it true **when written**, and does it **carry its date**? | ⭐ **Preserve.** ⛔ Never "correct" — it is evidence |
| **Current-status statement** | Does it assert something **in the present tense** that is now false? | ⚠️ **Repair** the present-tense claim |

📌 **Worked pair, same document.** [Track 3 charter](PRACTITIONER_PUBLISHING_TRACK_3_WORKSTREAM_CHARTER_2026-08-06.md):
- L80 — *"**As of 2026-08-06**, none of the four rulings has been made. No ruling document exists in
  the repo"* → ⭐ **dated, and was true.** ⛔ Do not touch.
- L23 — *"Track 2 Rule … ⛔ NOT STARTED — 0 of 4 rulings made"* → ⚠️ **undated present-tense ladder.**
  Now false.

⭐⭐⭐ **The same fact, in two registers, gets opposite dispositions.** That is the whole discipline.

### ⛔ Guard against over-propagation

> member, their identity, history, data, memories, or activity outside the constituted
