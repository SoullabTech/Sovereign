#### ⭐⭐⭐ Q1 — Global lifecycle ≠ lane lifecycle

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The seven tracks describe the complete lifecycle of *any* constitutional domain. ⛔ They are
> not a claim that every lane has reached every track.**

⭐ Each domain occupies **its own position** within that lifecycle. ⛔ A statement made about the
global lifecycle (*"Track 5 proves people can inhabit it"*) is a statement about **what the track is
for** — it ⛔ never promotes a lane to that track.

⚠️ **The failure this distinction prevents:** describing the lifecycle in the present tense and
thereby importing an unearned lane claim. ⭐ Read every unqualified track statement as **global**;
⛔ a lane claim must name its lane and cite its evidence.

##### ⭐⭐⭐ Two orthogonal dimensions

| Dimension | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Program lifecycle (Tracks 1–7)** | describes the **maturation of any constitutional domain** |
| **Domain lane** | describes a **particular subsystem** — Publishing · Writer · Now What? · MAIA · … |

⭐ Every lane occupies a position within the **common** lifecycle, and **each advances
independently**. ⭐⭐ That is the point: *the framework stays stable while the domains move at
different speeds* — so at any moment one lane may sit at Track 2 while another sits at Track 6, with
⛔ **no implication whatsoever** that the second validates the first.

> ⛔⛔ **A lane's position is a measured claim, ⛔ never an illustration.** Any multi-lane picture
> (*"Writer Studio at 5, Publishing at 2, Corpus Callosum at 6"*) is ⭐ **illustrative of the shape
> only** until each position cites its own evidence. ⛔ Do not copy such a list into a register, a
> status report, or a deck — that is precisely how an unearned lane claim enters the record.
>
> 📌 The only position measured and citable in this document is **Practitioner Publishing**, below.

## 2. Track 5 — Populate / Inhabit

The question changes from *"Can we build this correctly?"* to **"Can people actually live inside it?"** ⭐ This is the first phase in which the architecture accumulates **real history**. Nothing is hypothetical anymore.

### 5.1 Seed the domain

The structures exist but are empty: first Works · Programs · Collections · Placements · Offerings · Uptakes.

⛔ The goal is **not volume**. ⭐ It is **truthful examples**.

### 5.2 Real practitioner workflows

Larry actually works. Can he — without inventing workarounds — write notes · publish manuals · send follow-ups · recommend practices · assign work · revise material?

> ⭐⭐⭐ **If practitioners create spreadsheets outside the system, the architecture has not yet become inhabitable.**

### 5.3 First member journeys

⛔ Not analytics. ⭐ **Observation.**

- Do they understand what was shared?
- Does Placement feel like a **relationship** rather than a file transfer?
- Do they understand what belongs to them?
- Do they understand what belongs to Larry?

⭐ The architecture either **disappears into the relationship**, or it keeps drawing attention to itself.

### 5.4 Populate truthfully

⛔ The temptations this track must **refuse**: *"let's auto-create Programs"* · *"let's import everything"* · *"let's classify everyone's PDFs."*

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The system becomes populated through the same authored acts the constitution recognizes.** Every Work. Every Placement. Every Offering. Every Uptake. **Earned.**

#### ⭐⭐⭐ 5.4a The provenance rule (founder, 2026-08-06)

> **Operational adoption may populate only those facts whose originating authored act is known or can
> be truthfully reconstructed. ⛔ Operational convenience is not itself an authored act.**

⭐ This is **§5.4 applied to backfill**, which the temptation list above does not obviously reach: a
migration is not *"auto-create"*, *"import everything"*, or *"classify PDFs"*, yet it can introduce
facts with **no author**.

⚠️ **The question to ask of any populated fact is ⛔ not whether it is constitutional.** It is:

> ⭐ **What is the authored act that makes this true?**

If the answer is *"there wasn't one"*, ⛔ **Track 5 has a genuine concern.** If it is *"the practitioner
explicitly linked the account"* or *"the member accepted the relationship"*, ⭐ the row is ⛔ no longer a
backfill — it is **the record of that act**.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Strengthened 2026-08-09 by Ruling 1 §2.1.** ⭐ What §5.4a raised as a **Track 5 concern** is,
> for this instance, now a **constitutional prohibition**: backfilling a bilateral commitment from a
> unilateral contact record *"where the member's constituting act did not occur"* is forbidden
> outright. ⛔ The §5.4a rule itself needs no change — ⭐ it was correct; the ruling gives it teeth.
> ⚠️ Two distinct gates remain distinct: Ruling 1 governs **whether the commitment exists**; §5.4a
> governs **whether a populated fact has an author**.

📌 **Live instance: `member_id` linkage** (12 of 13 `practitioner_clients` rows). Linking them by
matching email addresses means ⛔ no practitioner declared *"this relationship is now with Member X"*
and ⛔ no member declared *"I am this relationship"* — the system merely **inferred** it. ⭐ That is much
closer to what §5.4 exists to prevent.

⭐ The rule leaves several legitimate implementations open and ⛔ prejudges none: practitioner confirms
identity · member claims invitation · dual confirmation · another already-governed identity ceremony.

⛔ **Unresolved — and to remain a governance question**, ⛔ not one settled accidentally by an
implementation strategy. See
[the Track 4 candidate refinement](TRACK_4_CONSTITUTIONAL_VS_OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE_2026-08-06.md) §6.

##### ⭐⭐⭐ When this gate passes — the condition is evidentiary, ⛔ not temporal

> **Gate 2 passes only when the authored act that grounds the populated fact is itself specified and
> available.**

⛔ **Do not phrase this as *"the population gate cannot pass early."*** ⭐ That reading is temporal and
⛔ wrong: it would block a task whose grounding ceremony **already exists elsewhere in the platform**.
📌 If a governed identity-claim ceremony were already in place, `member_id` linkage could pass **both**
gates ⛔ without waiting for new modeling. ⭐ The gate waits on **evidence of an authored act**, ⛔ not
on a phase.

⭐⭐ **The two gates are different in kind**, which is why they behave differently:

| Gate | Kind | How it is decided |
|---|---|---|
| **Dependency** | ⭐ **negative authorization** — is this *allowed*? | inspect current governance state: ruling exists → proceed · ruling absent → ⛔ stop |
| **Population** | ⭐ **positive provenance** — is the resulting fact *truthful*? | ⛔ cannot be certified until the mechanism that creates the fact exists |

⭐ That some tasks cannot be certified yet is ⛔ **not a weakness of the gate** — it is exactly what
provenance should require.

📌 **Worked contrast.** *Work ratification*: the practitioner performs **Ratify**, an act already in
the governed lifecycle — ⭐ the authored act and the resulting fact **coincide**, so it passes both
gates **today**. *`member_id` linkage*: ⛔ no settled authored ceremony — passes dependency review,
⛔ **pending on provenance**.

### 5.5 Discover missing acts

⭐ Genuinely new constitutional acts emerge here — ⛔ **not** because the model feels incomplete or asymmetrical, but because practitioners **repeatedly attempt something they cannot truthfully express**.

📌 **Precedent:** *Offering* emerged this way. It was ⛔ not invented to make the ontology symmetrical; it was **discovered** because production already contained a distinct human act.

### 5.6 Adoption review

⭐ The findings are **architectural, ⛔ not UX complaints**:

- Where do practitioners consistently hesitate?
- What actions are repeatedly attempted **outside** the system?
- Which constitutional distinctions are **naturally understood**?
- Which require explanation because the UI is not expressing them well?

| Claim | What it is evidence about |

### ⛔⛔ The category error this forecloses

> ⛔ *"Larry has been coaching clients for months, therefore Track 5 is complete."*

**No.** ⭐ Larry has inhabited **the current platform**, ⛔ not **the practitioner publishing architecture**.

⛔ And seeding this domain would populate it **untruthfully** — ⭐ **the refusal stands; its reason has
been amended (2026-08-09).**

| | Reason |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **Superseded** | *"the constitutional container is unsettled"* — ⭐ it is now settled |
| ⭐ **Operative** | Ruling 1 §2.1 **prohibits** *"backfilling bilateral commitments from unilateral contact records where the member's constituting act did not occur"* — reinforced by §5.4 / §5.4a independently |

⭐⭐⭐ **The restriction changed in kind, not degree:** it was *"wait for a ruling"*; it is now
**constitutional prohibition of that route**. ⛔ And note the inverse — per Ruling 1 §5.1, seeding
`relationship_spaces` through a **genuine bilateral constituting act is permitted**, and will
legitimately persist a row. ⛔ Population is not the prohibition; **unilateral** population is.
