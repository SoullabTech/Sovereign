## The three judgments, per row

1. **Accepted?** — accept / reject / amend.
2. **Is its stated authority accurate?** — does the cited section say what the row claims?
3. **Is the claimed dependency actually necessary?** — what breaks if inverted?

⛔ **Not among the three:** the P6→P7 coupling. It is a **separate founder ruling** (plan open
question 9) and **must not be decided to make the plan cleaner or faster.** Until ruled, P7 is
treated as gated. A row-by-row read may surface it; it may not settle it.

**Rejection rule (from the plan):** a rejected row removes its dependents rather than reordering
them past a gate.
