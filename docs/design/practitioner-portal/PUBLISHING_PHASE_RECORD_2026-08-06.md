## 1. The review pipeline

The four instruments are a **sequence**, not four heuristics. Founder-identified 2026-08-06:

| # | Instrument | Question | Failure if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Evidence Scope Rule](../../canon/EVIDENCE_SCOPE_RULE_2026-08-06.md) | what actually exists? | ⭐ you rebuild something that already governs |
| 2 | [Substrate Disposition Test](../../canon/SUBSTRATE_DISPOSITION_TEST_2026-08-06.md) | what should we do with it? | you adapt an incompatible substrate and weaken the requirement to fit |
| 3 | [Fact-Class Test](ACT_GRAMMAR_FACT_CLASS_TEST_2026-08-06.md) §1–5 | does this deserve to exist as a distinct act? | the grammar grows by judgment instead of by test |
| 4 | [Constitutional Gate](ACT_GRAMMAR_FACT_CLASS_TEST_2026-08-06.md) §0 | may the relationship preserve it? | ⭐ a real fact class earns a place it must not have |

⛔ **The order matters.** Steps 1–2 are about the world; 3–4 are about the model. Running 3 before 1
is how `coach_client_shared_items` nearly became a Placement substrate.

### ⭐⭐⭐ One dimension per instrument (founder, 2026-08-06)

⭐ The lane now holds **five** review instruments. ⛔ They are not five versions of one filter — each
governs **exactly one dimension**:

| Instrument | Question |
|---|---|
| [Evidence Scope](../../canon/EVIDENCE_SCOPE_RULE_2026-08-06.md) | Was the evidence measured in the right **scope**? |
| [Substrate Disposition](../../canon/SUBSTRATE_DISPOSITION_TEST_2026-08-06.md) | What **kind of substrate** is this? |
| [Dependency Gate](TRACK_4_CONSTITUTIONAL_VS_OPERATIONAL_CANDIDATE_2026-08-06.md) *(candidate)* | Does this require **unresolved governance**? |
| [Population Gate](TRACK_PROGRESSION_AND_TRACK_5_INHABIT_2026-08-06.md) §5.4a | Does this introduce **truthful facts**? |
| [Rendering Conformance](PRACTITIONER_PUBLISHING_RENDERING_CONFORMANCE_2026-08-06.md) | Can those facts be **stated** truthfully? |

⭐⭐ **The modularity is the property worth protecting**: ⛔ no instrument is a general-purpose
architectural filter. That makes them applicable **independently** and ⛔ far less likely to drift into
contradictory rulings.

⚠️ ⛔ **Modularity ≠ the §1 sequence.** §1's four steps are ordered because each supplies the next one's
premise; these five are **orthogonal dimensions** and the list is ⛔ not a pipeline. Two different
properties of the same instrument set.

📌 **Stopping point (founder, 2026-08-06):** ⭐ the gating framework is ⛔ **not to be refined further**.
The remaining work is **governance rulings, then implementation against them**.
