## 0.5 ⛔ Precondition — Track 6 requires a ratified baseline

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Track 6 cannot begin merely because people are using the software. It begins only when there
> is a ratified constitutional baseline against which drift can be detected.**
> **Otherwise there is nothing to drift *from*.** — founder, 2026-08-06

⭐ **Drift detection is inherently comparative.** That is the whole reason this precondition exists,
and it is structural rather than procedural:

| With a ratified baseline | Without one |
|---|---|
| an observation is **evidence the system moved** | an observation is ⛔ **just another design discussion** |
| the deviation names **which ruling it violates** | the deviation names **nothing** — there is no agreed position to deviate from |
| §3's erosion table is **measurable** | §3's erosion table is ⛔ **rhetorical** |

⚠️ **Consequence.** Usage volume, uptime, tenure, and practitioner satisfaction ⛔ do **not**
constitute entry into Track 6 — not individually, not in aggregate, not over years. ⭐ The only
qualifying condition is a **settled constitution to measure against**.

## 1. Operational governance

Every day introduces situations the design documents never anticipated. The question becomes:

- Does this require a **new ruling**?
- Does an **existing ruling** already govern it?
- Is this simply an **implementation defect**?

⭐ The discipline developed across Tracks 1–4 becomes **operational**:

```
Measure
   ↓
Classify
   ↓
Find governing principle
   ↓
Only then implement
```

⛔ Track 6 does not invent this workflow. It **institutionalizes** it — the same sequence, now run
continuously rather than once per lane.

---

## 2. Constitutional review

Every implementation change asks:

- What ruling **governs** this?
- Does this **violate** an invariant?
- Does this **require a new** invariant?
- Is this actually **changing canon**?

> ⭐⭐⭐ Constitutional review becomes part of engineering. ⛔ Not bureaucracy. **Normal practice.**

---

## 3. Drift detection

⭐ The greatest threat is no longer bugs. It is **slow erosion**.

| Drift | Shape |
|---|---|
| analytics quietly become **surveillance** | measurement outlives its consent |
| **convenience overrides consent** | a shortcut ships because the gate was inconvenient |
| **exceptions accumulate** | each defensible alone, fatal in aggregate |
| UI begins **implying things never declared** | the surface makes claims the constitution never authorized |
| MAIA begins **sounding authoritative** where she should witness | voice drifts from reflection to verdict |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Nothing breaks overnight. The platform simply becomes someone else's product.**

⛔ Drift is not detectable by asking whether anything is broken. It is detectable only by
**re-measuring against the constitution** on a schedule — ⚠️ which is why **§0.5 is a hard
precondition, not a preference**. ⛔ Running this table against an unratified container produces
design opinions, not drift findings.

---

## 4. Canon evolution

⛔ Not every discovery becomes canon. Track 6 establishes **how canon changes**:

```
Observation
   ↓
Repeated observation
   ↓
Candidate
   ↓
Cross-domain confirmation
   ↓
Founder ruling
   ↓
Canon
```

📌 **Precedent: *Acts Create Facts* emerged exactly this way.** ⭐ The sequence is descriptive of a
real maturation that already happened — ⛔ it is not a procedure invented in advance.

⚠️ The two failure modes are symmetric: ⛔ promoting a single observation straight to canon, and
⛔ leaving a cross-domain-confirmed pattern permanently at *candidate* because no one ruled.

## 5. Living evidence

⭐ The publishing lane discovered that **evidence itself needs stewardship**:

- Are production measurements **still true**?
- Has the **substrate changed**?
- Did **deployment invalidate** earlier assumptions?
- Are previous measurements now **stale**?

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Evidence has a lifecycle.** ⛔ It is not merely documentation.

📌 The lane already carries the mechanism: measurements are bound to a SHA
([`…SUBSTRATE_VERIFICATION_2026-08-06.md`](PRACTITIONER_PUBLISHING_SUBSTRATE_VERIFICATION_2026-08-06.md),
SHA `b1399f693`). ⭐ Track 6 makes **re-verification** a standing act rather than a one-time one — a
SHA-bound measurement does not decay silently, it decays **detectably**.

---

## 6. Operational instrumentation

⭐ Instrumentation **changes purpose**. It is no longer *"measure engagement."* It is:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **"Measure whether constitutional promises remain true."**

| Measured | Promise it protects |
|---|---|
| containment still holds | inference does not escape its boundary |
| provenance remains intact | every fact still names its origin |
| rendering remains conformant | the surface says only what was authorized |
| authority snapshots remain immutable | held authority cannot be retroactively rewritten |
| no derived facts enter relational history | ⛔ system inference never becomes member memory |
| no unauthorized crossings occur | scope boundaries hold under real load |

⭐ These are **measurements of truthfulness, not performance**. Performance matters too — but it
**serves** the architecture rather than defining it.

---

## 7. Stewardship reviews

Regular reviews ask:

- Which **rulings are under pressure**?
- Which **concepts are repeatedly misunderstood**?
- Which **workarounds are practitioners inventing**?
- Which **new human acts are emerging**?
- Which **assumptions have now become evidence**?

⛔ The goal is not adding features. ⭐ It is **preserving coherence**.

⚠️ Note the continuity with [Track 5 §5.5](TRACK_PROGRESSION_AND_TRACK_5_INHABIT_2026-08-06.md):
*discovering missing acts* does not stop at adoption. In Track 6 it becomes **periodic**, because a
practice that runs for years keeps producing acts the constitution has not yet recognized.

---

## 8. Success criterion

⛔ Not: *"The platform has 100,000 users."*

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Five years later, the platform still says only what is true, remembers only what was truly
> given, grants only authority that was actually held, and evolves without violating its own
> constitution.**

⭐ Note the criterion is stated **in duration**. Track 6 is the first track that ⛔ cannot be passed
at a moment — only **sustained**.

---

## 9. Boundary with Track 7

| Track | What it is | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **6. Operate** | operating the institution | *Can **today's** living system remain constitutionally true?* |
| **7. Steward** | stewarding the institution **across generations** | *Can **future builders** extend this system without accidentally undoing what made it trustworthy?* |

📌 **The three-question form (founder, 2026-08-06)** — the canonical compression of 5 → 6 → 7:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Track 5** — can people **truthfully inhabit** the model?
> ⭐⭐⭐ **Track 6** — does the **inhabited model remain truthful over time**?
> ⭐⭐⭐ **Track 7** — can **future stewards preserve and evolve that truth without the founders**?

⭐ Note what each successive track removes: Track 5 removes *hypothetical*, Track 6 removes *a
moment*, Track 7 removes *the founders*.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **If Track 5 is where the architecture becomes inhabited, Track 6 is where it becomes durable.**

⭐ The emphasis shifts from **creating correct structures** to **preserving their integrity** as the
system grows, new domains are added, and the inevitable pressure for shortcuts and exceptions begins.
