  may occur **before P5**. **P6b** (real source capture, classification into the composable system,
  ingestion, activation) may **not**. *P5 gates the first real governed material, not every human

- **P8 — narrow authorization.** Authorized: new version `discovered` · prior version remains live ·

## 3. The P6 → P7 ruling — universal

**Ruled independent, under a strict object boundary.**

> **Native authorship is governed by the relationship in which it is authored. Imported authorship
> is governed by the rights instrument under which it crosses into the field.**

Authorship created natively inside the practitioner field derives its legitimacy from the
practitioner's participation in that environment and the platform terms governing that act of
authorship. It does **not** derive from an agreement governing pre-existing intellectual property.

Therefore:
- P7 may open after P2–P5 pass.
- The prior-rights agreement and Attachment A remain **mandatory** for anything brought into the
  environment from outside it.
- P7 before P6 may contain **only** material newly authored by the practitioner inside the platform.
- It may **not** import, quote, summarize, retrieve, or quietly draw from pre-existing practitioner IP.
- It may **not** contain client material, or observations that identify or substantially represent a client.
- The platform **must disclose this boundary plainly at entry**.
- A later agreement may govern imported material **prospectively**; it may **not** silently
  reclassify earlier in-platform authorship.

This is the universal rule, not a Larry exception. **Calendar convenience played no part in the
ruling.**

## 4. Prohibition dispositions (seven)

**Binding today, on prior rulings —** N5 (a trajectory may be shown only through the practitioner's
own record, never converted into stage, score, level, or ranking) · N9 (readiness/completion status
is not authority and may never proxy for it) · N10 (revision proves change — not review, adoption,
permission, or ratification) · N16 (the system may **orient** the empty field, never fill it with
templates, defaults, or a borrowed ontology).

**Accepted through P2 —** N7 (aggregation cannot manufacture a rights grant unavailable at the
individual level) · N8 (popularity, frequency, consensus may not harden into normative authority or

promotion or authorship claims).

**N1–N4, N6, N12–N15, N17:** no grounding defect; none rejected. Two clarifications bind with them —
**N12:** the authority gate is never later "reopened"; eligible composition *begins through* the
gate when the required grants exist, and the boundary remains continuously operative. **N15:** the
specific production misrepresentation has been removed, but the enduring prohibition stands —
engineering may not manufacture Larry's ontology where only Larry's language can supply it.
