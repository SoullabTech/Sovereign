## The claim

The practitioner architecture is a **universal practitioner operating model**, instantiable for a coach, therapist, executive coach, spiritual director, educator, consultant, or mentor. **The developmental architecture stays the same; only the practitioner's vocabulary changes.**

| Universal layer | Executive coach | Flourishing coach | Spiritual director | Leadership consultant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sources | Research, books, client observations | Harvard PP, books, reflections | Scripture, tradition, journals | Case studies, frameworks |
| Wisdom | Distinctions | Flourishing principles | Spiritual discernment | Leadership principles |
| Craft | Coaching skill | Facilitation | Spiritual accompaniment | Organizational intervention |
| Offerings | Executive programs | Courses, workbooks | Retreats | Workshops |
| Relationships | Executives | Individuals & groups | Directees | Leadership teams |

## Seed, not template

Every practitioner starts from the same developmental seed — **Sources · Practice · Creation · Relationships** — and the environment grows into *their* practice. Larry's field should eventually feel unmistakably like Larry's; someone else's should feel completely different. The architecture remains universal; the lived field becomes personal.

Most coaching templates are operational (client / session / notes / homework / next session). This one is developmental:

- **Practice** — how is the practitioner developing? (wisdom · craft · learning · reflection · sources)
- **Creation** — how is the work developing? (books · courses · programs · workbooks · assessments)
- **Relationships** — how are people being served? (clients · groups · organizations · follow-through · continuity)

## Onboarding grammar (new contribution)

Not *"what kind of coach are you?"* (imports a category map). Instead, three seed questions:

1. **"Where does your wisdom currently live?"** — Obsidian · Drive · books · voice notes · journals · coursework · research · PDFs · Notion · paper · **nowhere yet**
2. **"What kinds of offerings do you create?"** — coaching · courses · books · retreats · workshops · podcasts · assessments
3. **"Who do you serve?"** — individuals · couples · teams · organizations · students · communities

Answers create **registered sources and declared shapes** — never ingestion. "Nowhere yet" is a first-class answer (the empty source folder is the correct starting state). Onboarding registers a relationship to each source (owner · location · custody · permission); the original never moves.

### The four stable layers (canonical phrasing)

1. **Sources** — *where my practice is nourished.* Not storage, not ingestion — the map of what informs me. Registered first, connected later only if the practitioner chooses.
2. **Practice** — *how I am becoming.* Not client management — my own development (wisdom · craft · reflection · learning · distinctions · questions · practices).
3. **Creation** — *how my work becomes shareable.* Books, courses, programs, workshops, assessments, retreats, podcasts, articles — **not separate builders; different expressions of one authoring substrate.** ⚠️ Gate note: designing that shared authoring substrate is BLOCKED by the writing-walk gate (#764) — this line records intent, not an architecture authorization.
4. **Relationships** — *how my work enters relationship.* Clients, groups, organizations, communities. Not CRM — living relationships.

### PLATFORM INVARIANT (founder-elevated): Registration ≠ Connection ≠ Integration

```
Register   — "I have this source."
   ↓
Connect    — "AIN may access this source."
   ↓
Integrate  — "I intentionally allow this source to influence my field."
```

**Nothing skips a step.** Each verb is a distinct practitioner act; none may be inferred from the previous one. This protects practitioner sovereignty the way consent-for-memory protects member sovereignty. *(Incorporation into `docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` is a separate ratification act, per the North Star Hierarchy precedent — recorded here as founder-directed, pending formal placement.)*

### SEED INVARIANT (founder-added): Nothing in the seed presumes maturity

A brand-new practitioner must feel equally at home. "Sources: nowhere yet · Offerings: none yet · Clients: two · Practice: just beginning" is honored as completely as twenty books and thirty years. This is a **universality test** for every portal surface: if a surface reads as empty, behind, or incomplete for the beginner, it fails the seed.

### Profession is metadata; development is primary

"Where does your wisdom live?" works for coach, therapist, educator, physician, consultant, author, clergy, executive, facilitator. Profession is never the organizing axis — it is metadata on a developmental field.

### SEED INVARIANT (founder-added): Every layer has the same developmental grammar

| Layer | Begins with | Matures through | Becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sources | Registration | Stewardship | Living source |
| Practice | Experience | Reflection | Wisdom & craft |
| Creation | Idea | Authoring & revision | Offering |
| Relationships | Encounter | Continuity | Trusted relationship |

**None of these layers begin with software. Each begins with an authored human act** — a source is registered, a practitioner reflects, an idea is captured, two people meet. **AIN enters only after the first authored act, preserving continuity without originating the developmental movement.** The invariant is about development, not software: AIN may surface patterns, support reflection, and maintain provenance — the prohibition is only, and exactly, that it never originates the developmental act. This is a deeper invariant than any individual feature; it is the seed-level generalization of the existing law that authority may only move upward through authored experience. *(Founder-designated governance candidate; not ratified — recorded as an AIN axiom candidate in the governance candidates index; incorporation into canon is a separate ratification act.)*

### "Nothing presumes maturity" — the full demand

The invariant means the seed must work equally well for a student beginning Harvard, a coach with fifty clients, an author writing a first book, and a practitioner nearing retirement. Demanding, and exactly what makes the seed genuinely universal.
