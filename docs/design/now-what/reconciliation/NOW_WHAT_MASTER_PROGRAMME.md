## Execution doctrine

1. **Upstream governs downstream.** No IA work settles Larry doctrine. No Figma work settles IA.
   No implementation settles a design question. No prompt silently becomes product doctrine. No
   code manufactures a ruling that does not exist.
2. **Preserve custody.** For every governing decision, identify: authored ruling · implementation
   · current production behavior · conflicts · supersession status. *If implementation exists
   without landed doctrine, say so. If doctrine exists only on an unmerged branch, recover
   custody before treating it as canonical.*
3. **UNKNOWN is not ABSENT** — especially for source material, Larry doctrine, safety
   inheritance, legacy routes, product history, and what a consent gesture meant.
4. **Member-authored fact outranks system salience.** The system may organize explicit
   member-authored facts. It may not silently decide what matters most, what a life means, what
   the "real issue" is, or which interpretation is true.
5. **Research is a lens, not visible structure.** External models inform Layer 3 and design
   constraints unless explicitly promoted. No framework becomes a navigation tab by default.
6. **Human simplicity is load-bearing.** Can this disappear until needed? Can the intelligence
   happen beneath the surface? Does it make the next meaningful move clearer? Does it preserve
   agency? Does it reduce cognitive load? If not, redesign.

## Three-layer product model

- **Layer 1 — Immediate.** As little as possible: one live thread, one orientation, one
  invitation.
- **Layer 2 — Context.** Available when wanted. **Final IA not yet ruled** (NW-D03).
- **Layer 3 — Intelligence.** Mostly invisible: flourishing, transition, coaching frameworks,
  adaptive-change reasoning, memory, pattern detection, safety, provenance, AI reasoning.

**North star**: the surface becomes simpler as the intelligence underneath becomes more
sophisticated. The member should never have to operate the theory.

## Design quality gate

No surface is finished until it passes all eight, and **none passes on aesthetics alone**:
**CLARITY** (do they know where they are?) · **SIMPLICITY** (is anything visible that could
disappear?) · **POWER** (is depth still available?) · **CONTINUITY** (one life-space, not five
mini-apps?) · **AGENCY** (is the member still author?) · **HUMANITY** (does it create room to
think?) · **CRAFT** (would it withstand comparison with world-class software?) · **SOUL** (does
it feel like Now What?, or like generic product design?)

## Build acceptance

Done is not "screens implemented." Done is a member who can: arrive without knowing the system ·
understand where they are · see an explicitly grounded "alive now" context · enter reflection
without dashboard overload · move naturally among the few needed places · work with human
coaching without AI displacement · meet intelligence without operating theory · leave with
clarity, movement, **or legitimate non-action** · return and understand what changed · receive
real human and safety off-ramps · trust that system inference is not presented as their truth ·
have one coherent experience on desktop and mobile · experience simplicity despite deep
capability.
