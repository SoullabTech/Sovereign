### 1. ICF 2025 Core Competencies — **ADAPT**

**Explains**: what a coaching relationship *owes* a client — ethics, agreement, trust and safety,
presence, active listening, evoking awareness, facilitating growth. The 2025 revision's attention
to technology, between-session work and coaching philosophy makes it unusually relevant: it is a
professional body reckoning with exactly the space Now What? occupies.

**Does not explain**: what change *is*. It is a competency and ethics framework, not a theory of
human development. It also assumes a human coach throughout; it has no account of an intelligence
operating between sessions.

**Risks**: certification language substituting for Larry's practice (§XX warns of this by name);
importing a professional-body vocabulary the member never asked for; "competency" framing drifting
toward assessment.

**Product role**: a **floor**, not a model — the baseline any coaching-adjacent product should be
able to say it does not violate. Its "agreement" competency is directly load-bearing here: much
of what ICF assigns to an explicit coaching agreement is, in Now What?, carried by
`RoomTrustCopy`. That is a genuine structural correspondence and it should be made deliberate.

**Safety implication**: ICF's ethics require the coach to recognize when an issue exceeds coaching
and to refer. **Now What? has no referral mechanism at all.** The environment currently cannot do
what the profession's own baseline requires.

**RULING**: **ADAPT** — as a compliance floor reconciled against Larry's actual practice at D01,
never as member-facing vocabulary.

### 2. Intentional Change Theory (Boyatzis) — **KEEP AS LENS**

**Explains**: sustained desired change in adults — ideal self → real self → learning agenda →
experimentation and practice → resonant relationships. Two contributions are unusually well fitted
here: change proceeds through **discontinuities**, not linear progress; and it depends on
**relationships**, which is structurally the same claim as "the coach is the container."

**Does not explain**: involuntary change. ICT is a model of *desired* change with a positive
attractor. The person for whom something has been taken away, or who does not want the change they
are in, is outside it.

**Risks**: "ideal self" is one step from an aspirational profile the system holds about a person —
a direct adaptation-boundary violation if it ever became something MAIA tracked. The five phases
are exactly the kind of thing that becomes tabs (the gesture architecture already forbids
rendering an arc as UI state).

**Product role**: Layer 3. **One element may reach Layer 2**: the *learning agenda* — a
member-authored statement of what they are working on — is a legitimate member object because the
member authors it. It is already close to what `field_program_positions.focal_point` holds.

**Coaching implication**: ICT's resonant-relationship claim supports the founder's core
sentence — the product's job is strengthening the return to relationship, not substituting for it.

**RULING**: **KEEP AS LENS.** Never render the arc. Never hold an "ideal self."

### 3. Motivational Interviewing — **ADAPT**

**Explains**: how to talk with someone about change **without taking the side of change** —
engaging, focusing, evoking, planning; sustain talk vs. change talk; rolling with resistance; the
righting reflex as the practitioner's characteristic error.

**Does not explain**: what a good life is; how to think about a complex decision with no
behavioural target. Its heritage is behaviour change, and much of Now What? is not that.

**Risks**: **MI is a directional method** — it is designed to move people toward a target, usually
one a clinician has in mind. Applied naively by an AI, it becomes sophisticated manipulation, and
the manipulation would be invisible precisely because MI is autonomy-flavoured. This is the most
dangerous framework in the set for exactly the reason it is the most attractive.

**Product role**: Layer 3, as **conversational grammar** — specifically the *anti*-patterns.
"Resist the righting reflex" is the single most useful transferable instruction for MAIA's turn
prompts, and it is already partly present ("do not evaluate adherence, do not praise compliance").

**Safety implication**: MI's engaging phase includes noticing when someone is not ready — which is
also the signal that something larger is present. That is a boundary-relevant capability, not just
a conversational one.

**RULING**: **ADAPT** — the non-directive discipline into MAIA's grammar. **REJECT** any use of MI
that presumes a target behaviour the system holds and the member did not name.

### 4. Immunity to Change (Kegan & Lahey) — **ADAPT, GATED**

**Explains**: the founder's Case D exactly — *"I know what I should do but I don't do it."* The
competing-commitment map (improvement goal → doing/not doing → hidden competing commitment → big
assumption) explains why technical action plans fail on adaptive problems, and it treats the
failure as **intelligent self-protection** rather than weakness. That reframe is genuinely
respectful and rare.

**Does not explain**: situations where the barrier is real and external — no time, no money, no
power, a hostile system. ITC's characteristic error is locating in the psyche what is actually in
the environment. It also has thin evidence relative to its popularity.

**Risks**: **the highest interpretive risk in this set.** A "hidden competing commitment" is by
construction a claim about what someone unconsciously wants — precisely *"declaring hidden motives
as fact"* (§XXI's forbidden list) and precisely *"pretending to know the person better than they
know themselves"* (the adaptation boundary). An AI that surfaces a competing commitment
unprompted has violated both.

**Product role**: **Layer 2/3 with a hard gate.** The map is only legitimate when the *member*
fills it in. MAIA may hold the structure and ask the questions; it may never populate a cell or
name an assumption on the member's behalf. The safe formulation: MAIA asks; the member authors;
nothing is stored that the member did not write.

**Coaching implication**: this is coach work. Larry running an ITC map with a client is coaching.
MAIA running one unsupervised is closer to an intervention. **Recommend it be practitioner-gated**
at minimum until D01 establishes whether Larry uses it.

**RULING**: **ADAPT, GATED** — member-authored only, never system-populated, likely
practitioner-initiated.

### 5. ACT / Psychological Flexibility — **REFERENCE ONLY**

**Explains**: stuckness without pathology — acceptance, defusion, present-moment contact,
self-as-context, values, committed action. Its central move (you need not eliminate a difficult
inner state before acting on what you value) is the correct posture toward an executive carrying
uncertainty, and its values→action link is cleaner than any goal model.

**Does not explain**: situation, relationship, development over time.

**Risks**: **ACT is a psychotherapy.** Its techniques (defusion exercises, values cards,
willingness work) are clinical interventions delivered by trained practitioners. Importing them
into a coaching product is the clearest instance of the clinical overreach §XX forbids — and it is
tempting precisely because ACT's language is non-pathologizing and sounds like coaching.

**Product role**: Layer 3, and only as *posture*: MAIA need not resolve a member's discomfort
before the work can proceed. Silence and sitting-with are legitimate. That is already close to the
room's existing stance.

**RULING**: **REFERENCE ONLY.** No ACT technique ships. The posture may inform the prompt; the
protocol may not.

### 6. Self-Determination Theory — **ADAPT** (ruled at R01, restated)

Autonomy · competence · relatedness as the design test at §IX. The strongest yes in the programme
and it produces **no member-facing content whatsoever**. Its relevance to R02 specifically:
relatedness is the empirical case for *not* replacing Larry. A between-session intelligence that
increased competence while decreasing relatedness would be a net loss, and would show up as
engagement.

### 7. Schlossberg Transition Theory — **KEEP AS LENS** *(strongest fit to the product's name)*

**Explains**: the literal "Now What?" moment. Transition as Situation · Self · Support ·
Strategies (the 4S), and — critically — **the non-event transition**: something expected that
never happened. The promotion that didn't come, the reconciliation that never occurred, the
child who was never born. The founder named this explicitly at §III; no other reviewed framework
sees it at all.

**Does not explain**: development over time, meaning, or what a good life contains. It is a
coping-resources model, not a flourishing model — which is exactly why it complements R01's set
rather than competing with it.

**Risks**: lowest interpretive risk of any framework here — the 4S are *questions*, not
inferences, and three of the four (Situation, Support, Strategies) are largely factual and
member-answerable. The one risk: "Self" as a resources inventory drifts toward assessment.

**Product role**: **Layer 3 as MAIA's orienting questions; possibly Layer 2 as member-authored
structure.** The 4S are the closest thing in the reviewed literature to a legitimate answer to
*"what is happening?"* — and unlike a flourishing taxonomy, they describe **a moment, not a
person**, which makes them far safer to surface.

**Boundary relevance**: **Support** is a first-class element of the model. A transition framework
that treats support as constitutive gives the product a principled reason to ask *who else is in
this with you* — which is both §XVII (relational intelligence) and the natural home of the human
off-ramp.

**RULING**: **KEEP AS LENS**, and flag for NW-D02 as the leading candidate structure for
*"what is happening?"* — the one framework whose subject is the situation rather than the person.

### 8. Implementation Intentions — **ADAPT, NARROWLY**

**Explains**: the intention–action gap. Specifying *when, where, how* ("if situation X, then I
will do Y") substantially improves follow-through relative to intention alone.

**Does not explain**: whether the intention is right, wanted, or wise. It is a pure execution
mechanism and is equally effective in service of a goal the person should abandon.

**Risks**: the fastest route from Now What? to a task manager. Every productivity product in the
category is downstream of this literature. If-then plans invite tracking; tracking invites
completion states; completion states are the ruled-out drift.

**Product role**: **Layer 1/2, but only as an offered form** at the moment a member has *already
decided* — the "carry forward" gesture, where the member chooses one practice. Notably, the
existing `spiralogic_phase = 'practice'` object is already this, and the room prompt already gets
it right: *"not living a practice is information about the practice or the season, never a failure
of the person."* That sentence is the whole safeguard.

**RULING**: **ADAPT, NARROWLY.** Offer the *form* of a concrete next move; never track adherence,
never remind, never mark complete. §XI's ruling stands: deliberate non-action is a legitimate next
move and must be as easy to choose as action.

### 9. Goal Self-Regulation — **REFERENCE ONLY**

Monitoring, feedback, discrepancy reduction, revision. Explains how people steer toward goals; and
carries the **highest scoring risk of any framework reviewed** — its core mechanism *is*
measurement against a standard. Progress bars, streaks, and completion percentages are all direct
expressions of it. Contains one useful idea: **goal disengagement** — the capacity to let go of an
unreachable goal is adaptive, not failure. That single idea is worth carrying; the machinery is
not.

**RULING**: **REFERENCE ONLY.** Carry goal disengagement as a legitimizing concept for
*"stop doing something"* as a next move. Reject the monitoring apparatus entirely.

### 10. Executive / Workplace Coaching Outcome Evidence — **REFERENCE ONLY**

The RCT-only meta-analytic evidence supports a real overall effect across leadership and personal
outcomes, while cautioning that "coaching" is not one uniform intervention. **Two disciplined
consequences**: (a) the product may not claim coaching outcomes for *itself* — the evidence is for
human coaching, and Now What? is not the intervention studied; (b) per the project's marketing
claim discipline, *"coaching works"* is a Live claim about Larry's practice and a Vision claim
about the software. Do not merge them.

**RULING**: **REFERENCE ONLY.** Relevant to claim discipline, not to design.

### 11. Keyes — carried from R01

Languishing as the not-ill-not-well state; distinct from mental illness. **REFERENCE ONLY**, and
load-bearing below: the languishing/illness distinction is the conceptual spine of the boundary,
because it is precisely the distinction the product must make and must never announce.

# PART TWO — THE BOUNDARY (the load-bearing deliverable)

## A. Seven definitions

These are drawn for *this product*, to be reconciled against Larry's practice at D01.

| | Definition | Who holds authority | Present in Now What? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reflection** | The member thinking, in their own words, with no one directing the outcome. | The member, entirely. | **Yes — the core activity.** |
| **Education** | Conveying information or a framework the member can take or leave. | The author, disclosed. | Minimal (domain lines, Larry's future resources). |
| **Coaching** | A human professional relationship, with agreement and accountability, working on goals and development the client sets. | **Larry.** | **Yes — outside the software.** |
| **AI assistance** | An intelligence supporting reflection, continuity, recall, articulation and preparation **between** human conversations. | The member remains author; MAIA proposes. | **Yes — The Room.** |
| **Psychological intervention** | Structured technique intended to change psychological functioning (ITC maps, ACT defusion, exposure, restructuring). | A trained practitioner. | **No — and must stay no**, except member-authored, coach-initiated forms. |
| **Psychotherapy** | Treatment of psychological disorder within a clinical relationship, with assessment, formulation and duty of care. | A licensed clinician. | **No. Out of scope permanently.** |
| **Crisis / emergency support** | Immediate response to risk of serious harm. | Emergency services and crisis lines. | **No — and the absence is the defect.** |

**The line that governs**: Now What? is **reflection plus AI assistance, in service of a human
coaching relationship.** It is not intervention, not therapy, not crisis care. It may sit *beside*
a person in difficulty; it may not *treat* them.

## B. The doctrinal conflict this unit must surface

**Safety escalation requires the system to notice something about the person. The adaptation
boundary forbids exactly that.**

*"You seem stuck"* is forbidden. But any safety response begins with a system judgment of the same
grammatical form — *this content indicates risk*. There is no way to build §XX without the system
forming a view about the member. **This conflict cannot be dissolved by wording; it must be ruled.**

**Proposed resolution** (founder ruling required — this is exactly the kind of upstream question
a downstream unit must not decide):

> **Safety noticing is not developmental interpretation, and must be structurally distinguished
> from it by four properties:**
> 1. **It responds to explicit content**, never to inference, pattern, or accumulation across
>    sessions. What the member said, not what the system concluded about them.
> 2. **It never persists.** A safety response writes nothing — no flag, no field, no ledger row,
>    no memory. The system does not remember that it worried about someone.
> 3. **It never reaches the coach or anyone else** without the member's explicit act. Duty of
>    care does not create an exception to consent in a product that is not a clinical service.
> 4. **It offers, never asserts.** It surfaces a resource and a human path; it does not tell the
>    member what is true of them, does not name a state, and does not label.

Under those four constraints a safety response is not a finding about the person — it is the
environment declining to pretend it is the right place, and saying where a better one is. **That
is compatible with the adaptation boundary. Nothing weaker is.**

## C. Presentation table — what the platform does

**Governing posture**: MAIA stays present and does not flinch; the environment does not diagnose;
the human path is always visible. **Not-listed content is handled at the ordinary level, not
escalated on suspicion** — over-escalation is itself a harm, and is the failure mode a product
this cautious will drift toward.

| Presentation | Platform behavior | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary uncertainty** | The product's core case. Stay with it. No special handling. | Treat uncertainty as a problem to resolve. |
| **Grief** | Accompany. Grief is not pathology and needs no intervention. Slow down; drop the room's forward motion; do not propose practices. | Offer a technique, a reframe, or a next move. Propose threads at a moment of raw loss. |
| **Anxiety / distress (ordinary)** | Stay. Follow the ACT posture — the feeling need not resolve before the person can think. Make the human path visible without insisting. | Teach a coping technique. Name it as anxiety. Reassure. |
| **High emotional activation** | Reduce demand. Shorter turns, fewer questions, no proposals. Silence is a legitimate response. Offer to stop. | Continue the interview structure. Extract. Keep asking. |
| **Abuse or coercion** | Do not counsel, assess, or advise on the relationship. Do not ask for detail. Surface specialist support and the option of a human. **Treat as the highest-consequence consent case**: nothing about this reaches a coach, a field, or a stored thread without an explicit, informed member act. | Suggest what to do. Store it as material. Let it appear on a Home surface where someone else might see it. |
| **Suicidality / self-harm** | **Full stop on the room's ordinary behavior.** Respond plainly and without alarm; provide crisis resources for the member's locale; offer the human path. Do not continue the interview, do not propose threads, do not persist the exchange. **Content of this pass must be drafted with qualified review** (see gaps). | Assess risk. Contract for safety. Continue as if it were reflection material. Attempt therapeutic response. |
| **Psychosis / mania-like content** | Do not interpret, confirm, or challenge the content. Reduce stimulation, stay concrete and brief, surface the human path. Note this is the presentation MAIA's own symbolic and mythic register could *amplify* — the register must be suppressible. | Engage the symbolic material. Reflect it back. Use elemental or mythic language. |
| **Medical / legal / financial high-stakes** | Name the limit plainly and route to a qualified professional. Reflection on *how the member feels about the decision* remains in scope; the decision itself does not. | Give the answer. Offer a framework for deciding it. |
| **Material instability** | In scope as a life reality, not a wellbeing category. **R01-F1 is live here**: the six domains have no place for it, so a member in financial crisis has no domain their life fits. Until D01 rules the vocabulary, MAIA should not force material concerns into an existing domain. | Treat it as a mindset problem. Silently drop it because the taxonomy has no slot. |
| **Wants human referral** | Make it trivially easy. This is the product's strongest asset and it is currently unbuilt: **there is a real human coach on the other side.** | Retain. Ask why. Offer to help instead. |

**The structural asset**: unlike a general AI product, Now What? has a named human in the
relationship. The off-ramp is not an abstract "seek help" — it is *Larry, on Thursday*. That should
be the first branch of every escalation where a coaching relationship exists, and the design should
make reaching him easier than continuing with MAIA.

## D. Safety architecture requirement — where the floor must compose

**Traced path**: `NowWhatRoom.tsx` → `POST /api/now-what/interview` → `composeRoomTurnPrompt()`
(`lib/maia/roomComposition.ts`) → `getLLMProvider().generateSimple()`.

**Three bypasses found. All three must be closed for the floor to be a floor.**

**BYPASS 1 — mode.** `app/api/now-what/interview/route.ts:327` guards composition with
`if (mode === 'turn')`. The `propose` path therefore runs on `PROPOSE_SYSTEM` **alone** — the
constitutional floor is never composed. This is unconditional and structural, not flag-dependent.

Its significance exceeds safety: `propose` is the moment MAIA offers back *"the threads you heard
returning"* — **the single most interpretively loaded operation in the product**, and the one where
"MAIA proposes, the member authors" is under the most pressure. It runs with no constitutional
identity composed. (`PROPOSE_SYSTEM` is, to its credit, epistemically careful on its own —
*"evidence… never what kind of person they are"* — but that discipline is local to one string and
not inherited from anywhere.)

**BYPASS 2 — feature flag.** `lib/maia/roomComposition.ts:285`:
```js
if (!presenceEnabled && !fieldBlock) return { systemPrompt: roomPrompt, field: null };
```
With `NOW_WHAT_MAIA_PRESENCE_ENABLED !== '1'` and no field resolved, the floor is skipped. **A
constitutional floor that an env flag can remove is not a floor.** Production value unread.

**BYPASS 3 — model selection.** `generateSimple({ tier, forceClaude: cloudRegisterPinned() })`.
The prompt does travel with any provider, so this is not a prompt bypass. But under
`LOCAL_TIER_ENABLED` core→Ollama routing without the cloud-register pin, a local model interprets
the floor — and instruction-following on a constitutional floor is not provider-independent. **The
floor's presence is guaranteed; its efficacy is not.**

**And the floor contains no safety content.** `lib/consciousness/MAIA_RUNTIME_PROMPT.ts` has no
crisis, referral, scope or stopping instruction. Its only adjacent lines are stylistic and point
*away* from safety: *"not a therapist or technician"*, *"no therapeutic-sounding frameworks"*,
*"less clinical"*. **A prompt that shapes the voice toward intimacy while providing no off-ramp is
worse than a silent one.** MAIA's safety modules elsewhere (`lib/spiritual-support/*`) are not
imported on this path.

### The requirement

> **The safety floor must compose structurally upstream of room voice and personality
> composition, on every path, with no branch that can omit it.**

Concretely, and for NW-D07/NW-I01 to specify:

1. **Move the floor above the mode branch.** Every model call originating in the environment —
   `turn`, `propose`, and any future mode — composes it. A new mode must not be able to opt out by
   omission; the safe shape is composition at the provider boundary, not at each call site.
2. **Remove the flag short-circuit.** `presenceEnabled` may gate *presence*; it must not gate the
   constitutional floor. Split the two.
3. **Author the safety content.** It does not exist yet. Scope statement, the escalation behaviors
   in §C, and the human path. **Not disclaimer copy** — behavioral instruction with a stopping
   rule, per the founder's explicit direction.
4. **Make it provider-robust.** Assume weaker instruction-following. Short, imperative, first, and
   — for the highest-consequence cases — consider whether prompt-level instruction alone is
   sufficient, or whether a non-model check belongs in the path. **That is a design question this
   unit does not decide.**
5. **Add a witness.** A test asserting the floor is present in the composed prompt on every mode
   and every flag combination. The current single test file does not cover this.

**Sequencing**: this is **NW-I01, and the master programme already places it first in
implementation.** R02 confirms that placement is correct — it is the one implementation item whose
absence is a live risk rather than a design debt.

## FINDINGS

**R02-F1** — Three structural bypasses of the constitutional floor; the `propose` bypass is
unconditional and sits on the product's most interpretively sensitive operation.

**R02-F2** — The floor carries no safety content, and its stylistic instructions actively steer
away from clinical register without supplying any boundary.

**R02-F3** — **Safety escalation and the adaptation boundary are in direct doctrinal conflict.**
Resolvable only by ruling that safety noticing is a distinct category, constrained by the four
properties in §B. This is a founder decision.

**R02-F4** — Schlossberg is the best-fitting external framework in the entire programme to the
product's actual name, and is currently absent from the record. Its subject is *the situation*,
not the person — structurally safer than any flourishing taxonomy — and it is the only reviewed
model that sees the **non-event** transition the founder named at §III.

**R02-F5** — The two most attractive frameworks are the two most dangerous. MI and ITC both
*sound* autonomy-preserving; MI is directional by design, and ITC's central move is naming a
motive the person has not named. Both need hard gates, and both would pass a casual review.

**R02-F6** — **The product's greatest safety asset is unbuilt.** There is a real human coach in
the relationship. The off-ramp could be *"Larry, Thursday"* rather than a generic resource line —
and no referral mechanism of any kind currently exists.

**R02-F7** — **R01-F1 has a safety consequence.** The missing material-stability domain means a
member in financial crisis has no domain their life fits — the taxonomy gap is not only a research
inconsistency but a silent exclusion at the moment of greatest need.

**R02-F8** — MAIA's symbolic/mythic register is a **contraindication** for one presentation class
(psychosis/mania-like content). Suppressibility of the register is a safety requirement, not a
preference.

## CONFLICTS

**R02-C1** — Adaptation boundary vs. safety escalation (F3). **Blocking.** Ruling required.
**R02-C2** — ICF ethics require referral capability; the environment has none. Larry-supremacy
applies to philosophy, **not to a professional-ethics floor**. If Larry's practice and ICF diverge
on referral, safety governs (per §XXVII's own carve-out).
**R02-C3** — Provenance limit vs. clinical content. Escalation copy drafted from unsourced
recollection is not acceptable for the suicidality/self-harm pass. **Blocking for NW-I01 content**,
not for the architecture.

**RETIRE** — nothing.

## SAFETY / CONSENT

The four properties in §B are the consent architecture: **explicit content only · no persistence ·
no disclosure without member act · offer never assert.** Note the deliberate tension — a clinical
service would breach confidentiality on serious risk. Now What? is **not** a clinical service, and
resolving that by quietly adopting clinical duties would break the trust copy the product already
ships. That is why it needs a ruling rather than a default.
