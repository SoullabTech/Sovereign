# NOW WHAT? — GESTURE ARCHITECTURE

**Status**: 2026-08-05, authored under the ratified floor plan (+ same-day
amendments). This is the bridge between architecture and UI — it prevents
rooms from being translated back into boxes. Component mapping happens only
against this document.

**Design premise**: the executive arrives with something important but not
yet formed. The room's job is to help them give shape to what they are
already carrying — in under 30 seconds, without a single decision that isn't
theirs.

---

## The first 30 seconds — returning executive

What the eye meets, in order (each beat settles before the next; nothing
pulses, nothing competes):

1. **Recognition** — *"Welcome back, Sarah."* (their name — a session fact).
2. **Relationship** — *"Your coaching work continues here."* (+ *"Your
   current focus: …"* when they or their coach have stated one, with its
   author shown). They are continuing something that already matters.
3. **The invitation** — *"What are you working through today?"* + one door:
   **Continue with MAIA**. The invitation never adapts; the door label does.
4. **The current thread** — what they are carrying, in their own words,
   directly beneath the door. Recognition of their own material is the
   final proof they are in the right place.

What the hand does: **one gesture — enter the conversation.** Everything
else is available; nothing else is asked.

## The first 30 seconds — first-time executive

1. **Welcome** — plain, unperformed.
2. **Why this exists** — one sentence: their coaching work continues here
   between conversations. (Not a tour, not a philosophy.)
3. **One invitation** — *"What are you working through today?"* + **Begin
   with MAIA**. No inventory, no map, no empty containers explaining what
   will one day fill them. The table simply isn't set yet, and that is a
   valid place to stand.

## The standing answers (every surface, every pass)

- **Always visible**: name/welcome · the relationship line · the invitation
  and its one door · the coach-boundary sentence (persists because it is a
  relationship).
- **Appears contextually** (authored facts only): current focus · the
  carried-forward fact of the last conversation · the table of kept items ·
  the shared-with-coach marker.
- **Can disappear**: everything else. Study and Archive collapse to a single
  quiet door (*"Everything you've carried →"*) — capabilities that emerge
  around meaningful work, never destinations offered in advance.
- **Emerges only when invited**: deeper reflection surfaces, the full
  archive, trust-disclosure detail, withdrawal controls.
- **Changes after a conversation**: if the member kept something at close,
  the table gains it — the room visibly receives their work; the door reads
  *Continue* instead of *Begin*; the carried-forward line updates. The room
  changes only because of an act the member made, and they can see the
  cause.
- **When nothing has happened yet**: the room is complete without content —
  welcome, relationship, invitation, door, boundary sentence. Two quiet
  lines maximum where the table will one day be. Absence is a beginning,
  never an error, and never an essay.

## Gesture grammar

- **One primary gesture per screen.** The arrival floor's is entering the
  conversation. A screen that asks two things asks the member to manage it.
- **Touching a kept item picks it up** — it opens in its own quiet surface
  with the member's existing verbs. It never triggers analysis, and it never
  becomes a "detail view" with metadata chrome.
- **Doors are sentences, not buttons-in-grids.** A door states where it
  goes in the member's words. Two doors never sit side by side competing.
- **Nothing counts, badges, or nudges.** No unread dots, no streaks, no
  "3 items need attention." The room never asks to be looked at.
- **Motion is settling, not attracting** — fade-up on arrival, honored
  `prefers-reduced-motion`, nothing animates after the room is settled.

## What this document forbids (the box-translation failure)

- Rendering Prepare/Apply/Reflect/Return as tabs, steps, or a progress rail
  — the arc is the coaching relationship's rhythm, not UI state.
- Rendering reflections/commitments/questions as three parallel doors — the
  table already carries them, typed and attributed.
- Any element whose justification is "the capability exists."

## Acceptance for any implementation of this document

Screenshot-first (the truth layer), then the walk: a returning executive
understands within 10 seconds — *this is where my coaching work continues* —
and their first act is the work itself, not navigation. A first-time
executive is never shown the system model. If either sees an inventory, the
implementation has failed regardless of what the code looks like.
