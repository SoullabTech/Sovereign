# Now What? Client Home — Vision Reference Disposition (2026-08-05)

**Founder ruling (Kelly, 2026-08-05): the dashboard mockup ("NOW WHAT? — CLIENT HOME PAGE",
sidebar + cards + journey rail + resources) is classified as:**

> **Vision Reference — Not Build Specification.**
> Accepted as v2/v3 experiential inspiration. Not authorized for current implementation.
> The live v1 encounter remains the governing evidence source.

## What this record prevents

A future session finding the mockup (or the accompanying concept text) and treating it as an
implementation instruction. It is not one. The shipped v1 Room (PR #967, prod `8a6aad214`)
carries a deliberately narrower covenant: **do less, claim less, prove the doorway first.**

## What the vision artifact correctly captures (keep as future experience language)

- The felt north star: **"the work is still alive between conversations."**
- A private developmental environment, not a portal.
- Continuity between conversations as the core value.
- Reflection as a place to notice, not complete assignments.
- The member's own emerging wisdom as the artifact.
- Simple arrival: *where am I and what matters now?*
- Its truest line is its smallest: *"This is your space. Your journey. Your growth."*

## The hidden assumptions the mockup imports (the drift to refuse)

The mockup falls into the familiar SaaS gravity:
**dashboard → metrics → progress → recommendations → resources** — exactly what the current
architecture resists. Specific elements that cross ruled lines as drawn:

| Mockup element | Ruling it crosses |
|---|---|
| "Your next step" (computed suggestion) | Slice 0 forbids system-suggested next steps (Duolingo pattern ruled out) |
| "Last conversation: you explored…" (system summary) | Third voice — synthesis over member material |
| Journey rail ●──●──○ with "Where you are now" | No progress-rail; stage position only Larry-placed and labelled |
| Practices status ○ Beginning ● Practicing ○ Integrated | Progress meter / productivity semantics |
| Resources library "from Larry" | No substrate: encrypted lane absent by gate, Larry IP instrument unsigned |
| "Flourish" as a stage name | Larry's framework language without the signed instrument |
| Six-door sidebar to rooms that don't exist | A capability disguised as a room |

## The corrected future model (spirit preserved, rulings intact)

Arrival greeting · *Your work* (member-stated focus) · **Continue your reflection →** ·
the member's own thread, in their own words · *Since we last met* (reflections / questions /
commitments — kept, not recent) · the coaching conversation (participant view + coach-**attributed**
notes) · *Explore with MAIA* (optional companion, never the owner).

What disappears: score · progress rail · inferred next step · AI interpretation · completion state.
What remains: **continuity · memory · authorship · relationship.**

The room should feel like **a journal, a hearth, and a bridge — not a cockpit.**

## Borrowable UI/UX heuristics (founder direction: keep our richness, adopt the craft)

Governing law (pattern study, ruled 08-03): **a pattern is borrowable when it solves
orientation; not when it also carries an ownership model.** The mockup's craft transfers;
the metrics it usually serves do not. Each heuristic below is adoptable *with its strip-rule*:

| Heuristic (adopt) | What it must be stripped of |
|---|---|
| **Arrival orientation triad** — where am I · what happened last · what matters now, as three scannable regions | The third card is the member's own kept commitment *in their words* — never a computed "next step" |
| **Recognition over recall** — typed, headed regions (Decisions · Commitments · Questions · Reflections) instead of one undifferentiated stream | Types come from the member's own tagging gesture (already true in the payload) — never inferred grouping |
| **Visual hierarchy + calm density** — clear regions, generous whitespace, one primary action, premium typographic register | Density of *authored material*, never density of system claims |
| **A journey MAP, not a rail** — named program stages as places, position labelled "placed by your coach" | No filled-dot completion track, no "you completed", no percentage; a map orients, a rail scores |
| **Progressive disclosure** — deep-dive rooms behind doors; home stays calm | Complexity is disclosable, **authorship is not**; doors only to rooms that exist |
| **Continuity framing** — "since we last met" as a region | Contents = kept items (E-2), never an activity feed or recency ranking |
| **Empty states as inhabited quiet** (Q-B) | Never a nag, never "get started" productivity prompts |
| **Aesthetic-usability register** — the mockup's warmth/imagery/premium calm | Pure rendering decision (light vs. current navy = founder aesthetic call); carries no claims |

**Sequencing**: these heuristics say *how to render* whatever the encounter pulls — they do not
themselves authorize a rebuild. Application rides on observed friction, per the freeze.
