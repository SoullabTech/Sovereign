# Practitioner Surface Composition — Larry Toggles the Doors (2026-08-05)

Founder directive: every field/box on the client home becomes practitioner-configurable.
**We build the optimal; the practitioner chooses what is present, and when.**

## Why this is constitutionally clean

The one-house-many-doors ruling (2026-08-03) already draws the line: *doors may differ in
VOCABULARY · SEQUENCE · AVAILABLE ROOMS · first questions — NEVER in AUTHORITY · ownership ·
consent · privacy.* Surface composition is the "available rooms" clause exercised by the
practitioner for their practice. Larry shaping which doors his clients meet is his practice
speaking; it does not touch who owns the material behind the doors.

## What is toggleable — and what is not

**Toggleable (composition):** the presence and order of home doors — living question ·
what-you-are-living · cultivating/Flourishing Field · coaching relationship card ·
story · place-to-think (MAIA) · calendar/upcoming · daily thought. Also each room's
availability as a destination.

**Never toggleable (constitution):**
- The trust strip and consent disclosures — the boundary is not optional furniture.
- The member's access to their own material: the field/archive stays reachable even if its
  home door is off. A practitioner may compose the home; they may not stand between a member
  and what the member authored.
- Bring-forward and withdrawal gestures — consent surfaces belong to the member.
- Anything that would let composition vary *authority* (e.g., toggling MAIA from companion
  to coach, or exposing practitioner visibility the member didn't grant).

## "And when" — composition over time

The temporal half of the directive: doors can open as the engagement deepens (e.g., the
Flourishing Field appears when Larry's program reaches the stage where his method introduces
it). This keys on **practitioner-authored facts** (his program stages, his placement acts) —
a door decision, legitimate under the ruling. It must never key on inferred member state or
activity (the sovereignty rider stands: no ambient cognition decides what a member sees).

## Shape

- **Config substrate:** per-practitioner (optionally per-program, optionally per-stage)
  surface composition — a small member-readable config: `{ door_key: on|off, order }`.
  Defaults = the optimal composition we built. Per-client overrides are held as a QUESTION,
  not assumed: individually-tailored environments are powerful but risk invisible
  differential treatment; needs its own ruling.
- **Client read:** rides the existing home composition call — the home renders only enabled
  doors. Absence stays silent (no "your coach disabled this").
- **Practitioner surface:** a simple composition panel in Larry's studio — the practitioner-
  field lane (already seeded as the next conversation). This is likely its first concrete
  artifact: not an "admin icon" on the member's page, but composition authority in his own
  world.
- **Universalization:** this is the Universal Practitioner Field applied to surfaces — one
  room library (platform), many compositions (each practitioner's world). A therapist, a
  spiritual director, and Larry compose different homes from the same primitives, each in
  their own vocabulary.

## Sequencing

Mockup approval of the constellation → implement rooms with the optimal defaults →
composition config + practitioner panel in the practitioner-field lane. The toggle layer is
additive; nothing built today blocks it.
