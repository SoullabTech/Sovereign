# NOW WHAT? — EXPERIENTIAL FLOOR PLAN

**Status**: ✅ **APPROVED with refinements — Kelly, 2026-08-05.** This is the
first floor plan authorized to move from philosophy toward implementation.
Next phase: **gesture architecture** (`NOW_WHAT_GESTURE_ARCHITECTURE.md`) —
component mapping comes only after that. Room names are **builder
vocabulary**; members never see them.

**Governing sentence**: *Design for "nothing formed yet"; treat formed intent
as the special case, not the default.* Coaching clients often arrive with
felt experience before conceptual clarity — the environment supports
emergence. This is a **relational environment architecture**, not a coaching
app information architecture.

**Who enters**: a coaching client (Larry's, first), between sessions,
arriving from the middle or the after of a working day, carrying something
unformed.

---

## The two rulings (closed 2026-08-05)

1. **Now What? is a PLACE whose primary doorway is conversation** — not a
   resumed conversation. Order: `person → place → conversation → continuity`,
   never `memory → previous session → person`. A memory-led entry privileges
   the system's record over the person's present.
2. **MAIA opens the door; Larry gives the room meaning.** MAIA = presence,
   not authority: conversational companion, continuity bridge, remembering
   only what the person explicitly carried. Larry = relationship, trust,
   human coaching container. The door feels like *"Welcome back. Your work
   is here."* — never *"Welcome to Larry's platform."* The relationship then
   appears quietly: *"Your coaching work with Larry continues here."*

## The House (as ratified)

```
THRESHOLD      Recognition. No choices. "You are welcome here."
    ↓
HEARTH         MAIA conversation. "What is alive for you today?"
  └─ LIVING ROOM (within the Hearth) — the current thread,
     what you have chosen to carry forward
    ↓
STUDY          Reflection, deeper work. Appears when invited.
    ↓
ARCHIVE        What has been carried over time. Never the first encounter.
    ↓
COACH RELATIONSHIP   Persistent quiet presence.
```

**Amendment (Kelly)**: the Living room is **not a separate destination** —
it is a **visible condition of the Hearth**. A person does not choose between
"the conversation room" and "the current-life-thread room"; the thread is
*why the conversation matters*. Hearth = present relationship; Living room =
what is currently alive within that relationship. They belong together.

**Rooms are architecture, not navigation.** No Hearth/Study/Archive tabs.
The member sees: a conversation · their thread · their reflections · their
history · their relationship. **The house is felt, not diagrammed.**

## The rooms

### Threshold
- Recognition, not orientation. "You have arrived," never "here is what you can do."
- **Must answer: "Am I known without being interpreted?"** Known: your name,
  your relationship, what you have chosen to carry. Not known: your
  psychological state, your needs, your readiness.
- Forbidden: choices, menus, explanations, metrics, disclaimers, inventory.

### Hearth
- **Not just conversation — relationship continuity through conversation.**
  A chatbot also has conversation; the Hearth is *"a place where your
  ongoing work can continue."* Conversation is the medium, not the purpose.
- **The door adapts; the invitation does not.** The invitation is always
  *"What is alive for you today?"* Beneath it, one door: first visit
  *Begin with MAIA*, returning *Continue with MAIA*. The system offers
  continuity without imposing it — never *"you should continue this thing."*
- Order within the Hearth: present self → conversation → continuity. The
  carried-forward fact (*"You were carrying this forward"*) comes AFTER the
  invitation, and only from authored acts.
- Forbidden: multiple doors; generated questions from member material.

### Living room (within the Hearth)
- *"What is currently on the table"* — alive ≠ everything that exists.
  **A dining table is not a warehouse shelf.**
- Kept items in the member's words, typed and attributed; shared-with-coach marker.
- Forbidden: rankings, recency framing, counts, completeness, empty-state essays.

### Study
- Deliberate reflection and practice; entered on purpose; emerges when
  invited — never on the arrival floor.

### Archive
- Return and retrieve. **"The past never furnishes the entryway"** — a
  governing principle. Memory is visible because it serves the person, never
  because it is a technical capability. A person does not enter their home
  through the attic.

### Coach relationship
- Persists on the arrival floor **because it represents relationship, not
  capability** — everything else is optional capability; the relationship is
  foundational context.
- **Never a "room" in the member experience.** No "Enter Coach Room" — the
  member sees *"Your work with Larry."* The room exists architecturally;
  the relationship exists experientially.
- Forbidden: coach activity feeds, homework framing, obligations.

## Adaptation boundary (concrete, from the founder)

Allowed — responding to what the person brought forward:
- "You kept this reflection about delegation."
- "You marked this commitment as active."
- "You returned to this conversation."

Forbidden — pretending to know the person better than they know themselves:
- "You seem stuck."
- "You are ready for growth."
- "You probably need this next."

## Ratified principles (checklist for every future pass)

✅ Place before feature · ✅ Relationship before capability · ✅ Authored
facts before inference · ✅ Conversation as medium, not product feature ·
✅ Current thread separated from archive · ✅ Builder rooms invisible to
members · ✅ Doors emerge; they do not compete.

## Amendments (founder, 2026-08-05 — same day; architecture SOUND, do not redesign)

**Positioning**: Now What? is a **between-session executive development
environment** — a triangle, not a dyad: **the coach's developmental framework
· the executive's real work (decisions, pressures, application) · MAIA as
continuity**. Governing sentence: *"Now What? does not replace the coaching
conversation; it extends the developmental arc between conversations."*
The coaching arc the environment serves: **Prepare → Conversation → Apply →
Reflect → Return** — phases of the coaching relationship, never app modules.
The destination is the executive becoming more capable through the coach's
process; the software is the environment and must never become the object of
attention.

**Five ratified refinements**:
1. **Doorway language**: "What is alive for you today?" is practitioner
   vocabulary — replaced with executive-register recognition language:
   *"What are you working through today?"* MAIA may become more poetic
   through relationship; the doorway maximizes recognition.
2. **The threshold names the relationship**: *"Your coaching work with
   [coach] continues here"* appears at arrival, before MAIA. Sequence:
   **relationship → conversation → continuity**. The person is continuing
   something that already matters, not entering an AI environment.
3. **"MAIA opens the door" tightened**: MAIA is **the conversational
   presence within the coaching environment**. The door belongs to the
   coaching relationship; MAIA facilitates crossing the threshold. MAIA is
   never the owner of the environment.
4. **Study/Archive are not places the member goes** — they are
   **capabilities that emerge around meaningful work**. Rule: *if a member
   can click it before needing it, it is probably a capability disguised as
   a room.*
5. **Governing sentence upgraded**: design for **the executive arriving with
   something important but not yet formed**. Executives are not empty — they
   carry decisions, pressures, relationships, uncertainty. The platform's
   job is not to give them content; it is to help them **give shape to what
   they are already carrying**.
