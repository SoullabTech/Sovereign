## Product Purpose

Now What? extends the impact of executive coaching beyond scheduled
conversations. It gives leaders a private environment to reflect on
challenges, capture insights, prepare for important decisions, and
maintain continuity between coaching sessions.

The goal is simple: **make every coaching conversation more valuable by
helping the work continue between conversations.**

## The Executive Experience — five moments in the coaching relationship

### 1. Return: reconnect quickly
When an executive returns, they immediately understand where they left
off, what matters right now, and what they want to explore. Focused and
relevant — not another software system.
**Design goal**: help the executive quickly reconnect with their priorities.

### 2. Reflect: think through real challenges
A place to work through decisions, leadership challenges, opportunities,
difficult conversations, and patterns they want to understand. MAIA
supports reflection and continuity while the executive stays in control
of what matters.
**Design goal**: a high-quality reflection experience that supports better thinking.

### 3. Capture: preserve what matters
During reflection, executives identify insights to carry forward — key
decisions, important realizations, commitments, questions to revisit.
**The executive determines what is meaningful.**
**Design goal**: turn conversations into retained insight and action.

### 4. Apply: support real-world leadership
The value of coaching comes from application: bringing insights into
meetings, decisions, relationships, leadership moments — without the
platform becoming a task-management system.
**Design goal**: connect reflection to real leadership behavior.

### 5. Prepare: improve the next coaching conversation
Before the next session, executive and coach have stronger continuity.
Intentionally shared insights let coaching conversations begin deeper and
more efficiently.
**Design goal**: reduce repetition; increase the value of each engagement.

## Experience Design Principles

1. **Executive first** — designed around the executive's needs, not the
   platform's capabilities. Every feature answers: *does this help a
   leader think better, prepare better, or apply learning better?*
2. **Simple outside, powerful inside** — the executive never manages
   tools, workflows, systems, or AI settings. They focus on their
   leadership work.
3. **Relationship-centered** — the platform supports the coaching
   relationship. It does not replace the coach, the conversation, or the
   executive's judgment. It strengthens the work between sessions.
4. **Executive ownership** — the executive controls what they reflect on,
   what they save, what they share, and what becomes part of their
   ongoing development. The system supports their thinking; it does not
   prescribe it.

## Initial Product Experience — the complete core loop

1. Executive returns. 2. Executive reflects with MAIA. 3. Executive
captures what matters. 4. Executive returns to continue the work.
5. Coach sees what the executive **intentionally** shares.

A stronger coaching relationship without requiring a large platform.

## Design Direction

The first release feels less like a software application and more like a
professional executive workspace: focused · private · intelligent ·
personalized · easy to return to.

**The measure of success**: does the executive feel that their coaching
work continues between sessions?

## Constitutional rider (builder-facing; does not alter the language above)

This register describes value; the ruled constraints still govern what
renders. Three points where the vocabularies must not be collapsed:

1. **"Where they left off"** is positioning language. UI copy still obeys
   E-2: presence is determined by what the member **kept**, never their
   *last* activity, and recency labels remain forbidden on the room.
2. **"Personalized"** means adaptation keyed on **member-authored facts
   and explicit gestures only** — never inferred psychological state
   (Inhabitable Architecture sovereignty rider).
3. **"Shares"** is correct in the evaluation register; in-product copy
   keeps the ruled relational vocabulary (*brought into your coaching* —
   a contribution to the relationship, not document transfer).
