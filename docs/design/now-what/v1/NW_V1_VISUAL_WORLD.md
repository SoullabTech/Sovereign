## ⚠️ A LIMITATION THAT CHANGES SECTION A

**I could not study the reference products.** `helloezra.com` returns `EGRESS_BLOCKED` from this
environment; the same applies to the others. Web *search* works and returns feature descriptions;
it cannot show typography, whitespace, card treatment, mobile composition, or interaction feel.

**So I have not written "5–10 visual principles" per reference.** Doing so would mean inventing
design observations of products I cannot see, and presenting them as study. What follows instead
is (1) one evidenced finding that materially corrects the brief, and (2) the founder's own
synthesis carried forward as *stated direction*, labelled as such.

**To close Section A properly**, someone with access should capture screens of each product's
**member app** — not its marketing site — and this brief can then be completed against evidence.

### The evidenced finding: EZRA's product is not what its marketing site suggests

Search reveals EZRA ships **EZRA Measure™** (assessment integrated into the app), **monthly
reports tracking sessions and employee happiness**, and for EZRAx the **"EZRA Portrait"** — a
psychometric assessment across **nine leadership attributes** that shapes the engagement from the
start. ([our-solutions](https://www.helloezra.com/our-solutions), [why-ezra](https://www.helloezra.com/why-ezra))

**That is the quantified world this programme has ruled out** — closer to CoachHub than the brief
assumes. The calm, editorial, spacious quality is almost certainly **the marketing site**, not the
member experience.

**Disposition**: EZRA stays a **visual-language reference for its public site**, explicitly not a
product model. Borrowing "EZRA's restraint" is legitimate; borrowing its member app is not. The
distinction should be written into the reference pack so no later unit imports the assessment
model with the typography.

**Carried forward as founder direction, not as study** — breathing room · strong typography · one
obvious thing at a time · restrained cards · relationship over metrics · mobile as primary ·
coaching as ongoing relationship, not a course. **What not to borrow**: assessments, portraits,
scores, monthly reports, happiness tracking, nine-attribute models.

**Valence** — borrow conversational simplicity, remembered context, the coach staying present
without becoming the whole product. **Do not borrow proactive continuity.** A coach that meets you
by reading your calendar and priorities is deciding what matters about your life — the adaptation
boundary's *"You probably need this next."* The survivable version sources the same felt
continuity from the member's own act: **MAIA meets you where you left it, not where the system
thinks you should be.**

**BetterUp** — borrow the returning-member home: a quiet field of what is relevant now. **Do not
borrow** progress framing, plans, or dashboard density.

## MOBILE-FIRST V1 INVARIANT (founder ruling, 2026-08-28)

> **Now What? is designed mobile-first for Larry and his clients. Desktop is the expansion of the
> mobile experience, not the source layout squeezed down.**

**Not a responsive requirement to satisfy later — a design invariant that governs every surface
from the first sketch.** For every proposed surface, the **mobile composition is produced first**,
and tablet/desktop derived from it.

Core V1 must remain fully usable on a phone with: one-handed navigation · one dominant action at a
time · **no required multi-column layouts** · no horizontal tables · minimal persistent navigation ·
readable typography without zoom · clear voice/text interaction · **continuity visible without
drilling through menus**.

| Member priority | Larry priority |
|---|---|
| Home · Room · What I'm Carrying · My Story | Today · Client · Session context · Follow-up |

Desktop may show more simultaneous context. It **must not introduce a different mental model.**

> **DESIGN TEST — if the experience is beautiful on desktop but cumbersome on Larry's phone, it
> fails V1.**

### What this rules out immediately, and what it confirms

- **Dashboard grids are now structurally excluded**, not merely discouraged. UX-02 removed the
  five-card grid on composition grounds; mobile-first removes the *possibility* of its return.
- **"What I'm Carrying" is a vertical stream**, which is what it wanted to be anyway — its four
  categories are a sequence in time (matters → considering → chose → happened), and a stream reads
  that natively where a grid does not.
- **The Room already passes.** It is single-column, centred, one composer, one action — the closest
  surface in the product to mobile-native. Its voice affordance stops being a desktop extra and
  becomes **a first-class mobile interaction**.
- **Larry's practitioner view is Today → person → context → session, not tables.** This is the
  sharpest consequence: the existing practitioner substrate exposes client *lists*, and a list on a
  phone is a table waiting to happen. His view must be a sequence of people, not a grid of rows.
- **The 46rem measure stands**, since it was already a single-column reading width — but on phone
  it is the *only* column, so the hierarchy inside it has to carry everything.
- **Continuity in the first viewport** is the hardest constraint. "Where you left things" must be
  above the fold on a phone, which means the greeting, the coach line, and the wordmark cannot all
  precede it. **Something in the current arrival stack has to give.**

## C · PROPOSED V1 INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

**Four member surfaces, and every one already has a route.** No new routes required.

| V1 surface | Route | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **HOME** | `/now-what` | `GET /api/now-what/home` — already returns questions, commitments, shared, sessions |
| **THE ROOM** | `/now-what/room` | unchanged |
| **WHAT I'M CARRYING** | `/now-what/questions` | one table filtered by the member's own tag |

**The single most important fact in this brief**: NW-D00 found Question / Work / Story are
**already three filtered views of one table** (`member_field_note_threads`). *What I'm Carrying*
is not a new object — it is a better **name for a relation** the member already has to material
they already authored.

| V1 category | Existing source |
|---|---|
| **What matters** | kept thread, `spiralogic_phase = 'question'` |
| **What I'm considering** | kept thread, unresolved |
| **What I chose** | kept thread, `spiralogic_phase = 'practice'` |
| **What happened next** | the room's existing `entry=lived` return |

**Three of four exist today. Only "Where you left things" needs anything new** — and that is a
read of the most recent keep, not a subsystem.

## D · MEMBER JOURNEY

```
ARRIVE   →  HOME              "Where you left things"
         →  THE ROOM          "What are you working through?"
         →  KEEP / REVISE / DISCARD        ← the member act
         →  confirmation naming the room it went to   (UX-02)
         →  LEAVE
         →  RETURN → HOME     her words first, "You kept this on Tuesday"
```

**The Living Map is not in V1.** It would reveal a registry, not a life. When it arrives it should
show only the four categories above — established by member acts, never by inference.

## E · LARRY'S COACH WORLD — and it is largely already built

**Single-coach rule**: one practitioner, 10–30 relationships, near-zero administration. Not *how
does a platform manage hundreds of coaches*.

Audit result: `/now-what/practice` (the Practice Workspace — *"the field holder's own room"*)
exists, and the APIs behind the founder's six items exist too.

| Larry needs | Exists |
|---|---|
| People | `/api/practitioner/clients` |
| Today / Upcoming | sessions substrate, already read by My Coaching |
| Client snapshot | Bring-Forward — **only what the member deliberately shared** |
| Session preparation | `lib/practitioner/sessionPrep.ts` |
| After-session note | `phiAccessors/practitionerClientNotes` |
| Invites / access | `/api/now-what/register` + invitation gate |

**Enterprise machinery exists underneath and must stay unused**: teams (19 migrations), cohorts
(10), organizations (5). **Preserved, not surfaced.** No manager dashboards, utilization,
multi-coach permissions, or programme administration in V1.

**The consent invariant Larry's world must not weaken**: `can_be_shown_to_practitioner` defaults
FALSE and moves only by an explicit per-thread member gesture. **Larry's snapshot shows what the
member chose to bring him — never what they wrote privately.** That constraint is what makes this
a coaching relationship rather than surveillance, and it is the one thing in the coach world that
cannot be traded for convenience.

## F · VISUAL DIRECTION

Grounded in tokens that already exist (`PaperRoom` `--nw-*`), not in products I could not see.

**Type** — one serif authority (Iowan Old Style / Palatino) for the member's words and room names;
system sans for structure. **The member's own language is always the largest thing on the screen.**
**Measure** — 46rem, as UX-02 aligned Home to the rooms. **Ground** — warm charcoal gradient with
bronze wash; bronze `#c9a35e` as the single accent. **Surfaces** — hairlines, not cards; UX-02
removed the card grid and it should not return under nicer typography. **MAIA** — present in the
Room, quiet elsewhere; never a docked panel. **Mobile** — primary, not compressed desktop.

## G · FIRST THREE MOCKUP TARGETS — **iPhone, not desktop**

Per the mobile-first invariant, all three are drawn at phone width first. Desktop expansion comes
only after the mobile composition feels right.

1. **Returning Home — iPhone.** The one that proves the thesis: her words first, provenance line
   beneath, one action — **all inside the first viewport.**
2. **The Room — iPhone.** Already closest to right; the target is atmosphere and one-handed reach,
   not restructuring. Voice as a first-class control.
3. **What I'm Carrying — iPhone.** Four categories as a vertical stream, member acts only, no
   inference.

**Not implemented.**
