# NW-V1-COLD-WITNESS-01 — Protocol

**Status**: LOCKED, founder-ratified 2026-08-28. **Design is frozen pending this witness.**
**Subject**: `prototypes/01-returning-home.png` — **that screen only.**

---

## Tester

- has **not** read the architecture or design discussion
- has **not** been coached on Now What?
- ideally resembles the intended member

## Witnesser

- **is not the prototype designer**
- has not read the design rationale, if avoidable
- **does not explain, prompt, reassure, or interpret**
- keeps tone neutral

> *The witnesser must be uncontaminated too, not only the participant.* The three questions are
> good precisely because they contain none of our vocabulary — but the person asking them can leak
> it through tone, emphasis, or a helpful pause.

## Procedure

1. Show **Returning Home only**.
2. Allow **~5 seconds** of silent viewing.
3. *"What do you think this screen is telling you?"*
4. *"What would you do next?"*
5. *"What does this seem to remember about you?"*

**Do not correct or explain between questions.**

## Record verbatim

exact words · meaningful pauses · hesitation · rereading or scanning · first attempted tap ·
questions the participant asks · **anything they overlook**

**Do not convert hesitation into PASS** merely because the eventual answer is correct.

> ⚠️ **Record pauses; do not assign them meaning.** *"Three-second pause before answering"* is
> evidence. *"The person was confused"* is interpretation, and is not permitted unless their
> behaviour or their words support it. The same discipline the product applies to MAIA applies to
> us reading a witness.

## Pass signal

Something substantially like:

> *"It remembers what I was dealing with / decided, and it wants to know what happened."*

**Their terminology is not required.** Ours appearing in their answer is a contamination signal,
not a success.

## Fail / design-evidence signals

- *"This is my dashboard."*
- *"These are my notes."*
- *"I guess I talk to the AI."*
- cannot identify what happened previously
- cannot identify the obvious next action
- **assumes the system inferred something they never supplied**

The last is the most serious: it would mean the interface implies authorship the member never
gave — the failure the whole provenance architecture exists to prevent.

## Standing rule

**Do not repair during the witness.** The first unassisted experience *is* the evidence. A defect
found here is worth more than a screen that passes because someone helped.
