CHAPTER 10

The Living Spiral

Life does not move in straight lines. It circles, deepens, returns, and becomes newly visible from another place.

I. The Living Spiral

The spiral is one of the oldest images for change because it holds a paradox: we return to familiar ground, but we are never standing in exactly the same place.

To live developmentally is to notice that every return carries memory, consequence, and possibility. What once felt like repetition can become recognition when we meet it with enough presence.

We begin to sense that growth is not escape from earlier versions of ourselves, but a widening capacity to remain in relationship with what has been lived.

This is why the living spiral matters. It lets continuity and change belong to the same movement.
