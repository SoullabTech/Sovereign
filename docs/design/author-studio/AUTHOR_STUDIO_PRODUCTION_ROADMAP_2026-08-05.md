## North Star

⛔ Author Studio is not a writing editor. ⭐⭐⭐ **It is a professional creative environment where a
creator's work develops from initial material into finished expression.**

**Central object: the Work** — ⛔ not document, manuscript, draft, or editor.
The **Editing Canvas is one instrument inside the Writer Canvas.**

```
                    AUTHOR STUDIO
                      WORKSPACE
        ---------------------------------
        Capture       Gather        Shape
        Ideas         Sources       Structure
        Notes         Images        Themes
        Quotes        Documents     Concepts
        Journal       Recordings    Outline
                         |
                   Writer Canvas
                         |
                  Editing Canvas
                         |
                      Drafts
                         |
                      Release
```

## Phase 1 — Establish the Studio foundation
**Goal:** the place where a creator's work has a home. ⛔ **Do not begin with writing.**

- **Work object** — the central container. A Work may be a book · article · blog series · teaching ·
  course · body of ideas. Capabilities: create · name · describe intention · associate materials.
- **Work Home** — the first experience. The creator immediately understands ✅ *"This is what I am
  creating"* — ⛔ not *"Here are my files."*
- **Materials layer** — documents · notes · journal entries · images · quotes · recordings ·
  existing manuscripts · fragments.
  ⭐⭐⭐ **Materials are not drafts. They are creative source material.**

## Phase 2 — Build the Writer Canvas
**Goal:** the professional creative environment. It answers ✅ *"How do I develop this work?"* —
⛔ not *"Where do I type?"*

| **Context (left)** | **Creative Field (centre)** | **Intelligence (right)** |
|---|---|---|
| *What am I working on?* | *Where does the work happen?* | *How am I thinking with this?* |
| work identity · source materials · structure · related pieces | writing · composition · development · organization — **where the Editing Canvas eventually appears** | reflection · connections · questions · noticing patterns |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **MAIA remains beside the writing, never inside it.**

## Phase 3 — Integrate the Editing Canvas
**Goal:** professional writing capability. The existing Editing Canvas is **preserved**.
⛔ **Do not redesign it as the product.** Integrate as `Writer Canvas → Write → Editing Canvas`.
**Maintain:** revision history · persistence · concurrency · source protection.

## Phase 4 — Intelligence layer
| ✅ Allowed | ⛔ Not allowed |
|---|---|
| identify connections · surface related material · ask reflective questions · help organize thoughts | write the author's work · replace voice · determine meaning · become the creative source |

## Phase 5 — Refinement and release
Revision · coherence · publishing preparation · export · editions.
The work moves outward: **Blog → Article → Book → Teaching → Publication.**

---

## Production guardrails — every feature must pass

1. **Does this help the creator develop their work?** ⛔ If no — do not build.
2. **Does the creator feel *"I can work here"*?** ⛔ If no — the experience is wrong.
3. **Does this preserve authorship?** ⛔ If MAIA becomes the source — **stop.**

## ⛔ Explicit anti-patterns

Document dashboards · file managers · AI writing assistants · prompt boxes · productivity metrics ·
writing scores · completion tracking · generic chat interfaces.
⛔ **Do not optimize around the existing editor.**

---

## First production milestone

A creator can: **create a Work · bring in source material · organize emerging ideas · enter a
professional writing environment · write using the Editing Canvas · reflect with MAIA · return later
and continue.**

> ⛔ **Success is not** *"Where is the editor?"*
> ✅ **Success is** ⭐⭐⭐ ***"My work lives here."***
