## What this room is for

A writer at length with a single body of text. Layer 3 in the ratified navigation grammar
*(House → Writer's Studio → Manuscript Room → Working Draft)*: the Studio orients across the
life of the work; this room is where one manuscript is worked closely. Its activity is
**composition and revision at length** — the work becoming itself.

## What this room is NOT for

- ⛔ **Not the Press Editor.** Ruled 2026-08-14. Edition-production — footnotes, endnotes,
  citations, bibliography, tables, plates, ISBN, typesetting — belongs to Press Editor.
- ⛔ **Not the Studio environment.** It is one room inside it, never the whole. The defect
  this room's own ruling corrected was the House opening straight onto it, landing the member
  on an upload form with no Studio around it.
- ⛔ Not an information architecture. *"The manuscript tabs are **not** the Studio's
  information architecture — they are local tools inside one room,"* held separate precisely
  so they can change without the environment changing.

## Arrival

> **Your manuscript, open where you left it.**

Where am I — inside one manuscript, not the Studio. What is here — the text, at length. Where
can I begin — wherever the last sitting stopped.

**The way back up must always be visible.** This is a ruled property, not a preference: before
the return link existed, arriving here from the House was a one-way trip into an upload form.
The return link currently reads **"Author Studio."** C1 has ruled **"Writer's Studio"** as the
correct language; that correction remains authorized and blocked until this contract gate closes.

## Gestures

| Gesture | Specified language | Why this wording |
|---|---|---|
| Return to the environment | **← Writer's Studio** | The way up is never hidden |
| Bring in an existing manuscript | **Import into Writer's Studio** | Names the threshold crossed, not the file transfer |
| Write at length | *(no label — the draft is simply open)* | The design says it; the copy must not |
| Keep a version | **Keep a version** | Preservation, not version control |
| Leave safely | *(exit guard — unsaved words are never silently lost)* | Protection, never a prompt to admire |

## MAIA's role and limits

Unchanged from the Canvas and stated here so this room cannot drift: MAIA may reflect on a
passage when invited and surface the member's own prior material. **MAIA may never generate
the manuscript, assert what it means, or decide what belongs in it.** The system offers · the
system preserves · **the creator adopts.**

## House vs Room

**Shared**: token layer, gesture verbs, provenance voice, the always-visible return path.

**Distinct**: this is the **long-form instrument**. The Canvas opens on the work and its
context; this room opens on the *text itself*, in one continuous measure, with autosave and an
exit guard as its defining protections. The Canvas holds the work; this room holds the words.

## What persists, and what does not

*Same three-class discipline as the Writer Canvas contract, per the founder's 2026-08-14
provenance correction.*

**Relationship**: the manuscript's identity as the member named it.

**Authored fact**: the manuscript text · kept revisions and their member-authored notes ·
keeps and collections the member made — **including the words held in autosave**. Copying
authored words into an autosave store does not change their authority class.

**Operational continuity state** (carries no authored meaning, never rendered as such): the
active draft/revision pointer · last-open tab · scroll position · save/checkpoint status
needed to resume.

**Does not persist**: anything inferred about the member.

## Transitions in and out

**In** — from Writer's Studio (Layer 2), by named surface rather than onto whatever tab is
first; from import.

**Out** — back up to Writer's Studio, always. Eventually into Press Editor, and that
transition is governed: **it is not a transfer.** The Working Draft remains intact; an Edition
derives from a declared, immutable revision; later writing is *offered* to the edition, never
synced over it.

## Minimum inhabitable experience

The room is inhabitable when a writer can: open one manuscript and find it where they left it ·
write at length without fighting the surface · keep a version deliberately · leave without
losing words · and see the way back up at all times.

## The two brand tests

**Same house?** Yes — token layer, gesture verbs, provenance voice, and the visible return path.

**Distinct room?** Yes: Journal opens on a question, the Canvas on the member's unfinished
sentence with its context folded at both edges; this room opens on **one long text and almost
nothing else**. It is the only room whose defining protections are autosave and an exit guard,
because it is the only one where a member stays for hours.
