# Writer Canvas — Persona Walk (Step 2 of the Design→Build Sequence)

> **Status**: Step 2 deliverable under
> `WRITER_CANVAS_BEYOND_HOME_OPENING_PROMPT_2026-08-05.md`, testing the
> Step 1 room (`WRITER_CANVAS_ROOM_MAP_2026-08-05.md`). This walk is a
> validation instrument, not a brainstorm. Each persona records: what the
> room gave them, where it strained, and the amendment (if any) the strain
> forces. Amendments are numbered A1–A6 and govern the room map.
>
> **Verdict up front**: the room survives. All five creators could work in
> the three-zone room. No persona required a fourth zone, a separate app,
> or the deleted bottom strip. Six amendments were forced — all of them
> refinements inside the room's shape, none a change to the shape itself.

---

## Persona 1 — The Novelist: "I have a manuscript."

**The walk.** She arrives through the doorway with a 60,000-word
manuscript, mid-book, actively writing. The room imports it… and opens
the **Renewal surface** — original preserved, new expression beside it.

**Strain 1 — wrong arrival.** She is not renewing anything; she is
*continuing*. Renewal framing insults a living manuscript. The room map
conflated two different entry states under "existing manuscript":
a work being **continued** and a dormant work being **renewed**.

> **A1 — Split the "existing manuscript" entry condition.** The doorway's
> second axis ("Where are you starting?") already distinguishes
> *something already in progress* from *an existing manuscript [returning
> to life]*. The Canvas must honor it: **continuing** opens the writing
> surface at the manuscript with Structure and History seeded;
> **renewing** opens the Renewal surface. Import never decides this;
> the member's stated starting condition does.

**Strain 2 — structure is her terrain, not her context.** With 14
chapters, jumping between them via open-drawer → find → close is a
per-minute cost. For long-form work, structure is not occasional context
— it is the daily map.

> **A2 — Drawers are pinnable.** Any drawer can be pinned open as a
> narrow rail beside the field; pinned state persists per work. Default
> remains folded — the blogger never sees what the novelist pins. Calm by
> default, terrain when the work demands it.

**What worked.** Chapters live in Structure (carried in by the import,
not invented by the system). "Keep a version before I cut this chapter"
is a member gesture into History — nothing silently overwritten. The
orientation phrase reads "drafting," from her own acts.

**Answer to the persona question**: yes — the room supports a book
without becoming a book tool, because Structure exists only where
structure exists.

## Persona 2 — The Scholar: "I have a dissertation."

**The walk.** He arrives with a proposal, 120 sources, and an advisor.
Materials takes the sources. He writes in the field, chapters pinned
(A2). Then he needs to **cite** — and the room has no citation gesture.

**Strain 3 — citing happens at the table, not in a drawer.** A reference
manager bolted on as a panel would be warehouse grammar. But citation is
a real need of rigorous work.

> **A5 — Citation is a conditional instrument at the writing surface.**
> Materials may carry citation metadata (author, year, source) when the
> member adds it or the ingest finds it. At the writing surface, a *cite*
> gesture pulls from Materials. The bibliography is a **generated
> expression of the work** — a view produced from citations actually
> used — never a room, never a panel to manage.

**Strain 4 — the advisor is not a co-author.** His advisor must comment
without editing. The room's "presence, not panel" model needs shape:
what an invited person may do differs by relationship.

> *(Absorbed into A4 — see Persona 4: roles ride on the offered/adopted
> grammar. An advisor's comments are offerings that never touch the text;
> a co-author's contributions are offerings the author may adopt.)*

**What worked.** Sources feed the work rather than forming a library
product; advisor feedback arrives attributed; revision cycles are the
History drawer doing its ordinary job; defense prep is just the work
under the development surface.

**Answer**: rigorous academic work can live here — *because* the
scholarly instruments are conditional, not because the room became a
research app.

## Persona 3 — The Blogger / Creator: "I have an idea for a series."

**The walk.** She arrives with an idea for an eight-part series. The
development surface takes the idea; fragments accumulate; the first post
takes form on the writing surface. Then post two begins — and the room
map's language of "the work's *current expression*" strains.

**Strain 5 — one work, many sibling expressions.** A series is one work
(`living_work`) with several expressions (`living_work_expressions` —
the posts). The room assumed one expression on the table.

> **A3 — Expression switching at the head of the room.** The room's
> header names the work and, when the work has more than one expression,
> which expression is on the table — switchable with one light gesture.
> With one expression, no switcher exists. This same mechanism later
> serves *idea → essay → book → course* evolution: a new becoming is a
> new declared expression of the same work, same room.

**What worked.** Lightweightness by absence: no Structure drawer, no
History weight, no collaboration trace — the room is nearly bare and
that bareness is correct. Publishing is a lifecycle act on an expression
(conditional), not a permanent panel.

**Answer**: lightweight creative work feels natural — the blogger's room
is small because her work is small, not because she got a lesser product.

## Persona 4 — The Collaborator: "We are creating this together."

**The walk.** Two co-authors and an editor share one work. Presence at
the room's edge shows who is here — good. Then the editor rewrites a
paragraph, and the question lands: whose text is on the table now?

**Strain 6 — collaboration needs an authorship grammar, not a merge.**
Attribution-after-the-fact is too weak; "approval workflows" as a panel
is warehouse grammar. The platform already ruled the answer elsewhere
(Corpus Callosum grammar): **contributions are *offered*; only the
author *adopts*.**

> **A4 — Offered/adopted is the collaboration grammar.** An invited
> person's contribution arrives on the worktable **as an offering,
> visibly attributed and visually distinct from the work**. The author
> adopts or declines with one gesture; adoption is recorded (who offered,
> who adopted, when) in History. Roles are conditional shapes of the same
> grammar: a **co-author** offers text; an **editor** offers changes; an
> **advisor/reader** offers comments that are never adoptable into the
> text. No approval panel exists — the grammar lives in the field.

**What worked.** Solitary works carry zero collaboration UI. Presence is
people, not management. Contribution history is History doing its job
with attribution.

**Answer**: collaboration preserves authorship **structurally** — the
author's hand is the only hand that can make something part of the work.

## Persona 5 — The Elder / Teacher: "I have decades of notes and teachings."

**The walk.** He arrives with forty years of material — journals, talks,
handouts — and no defined work. The doorway made him a work anyway
("my teachings") and the room opens… onto what, exactly? He needs to
**gather first, discover second, form third.**

**Strain 7 — arrival with material but no formed intention.** Materials
must accept bulk gathering, and the development surface must support
discovery *across* materials before any writing happens.

> **A6 — Gathering is a first-class arrival, always into a work.** Bulk
> ingest lands in Materials of a named work (even loosely named — "my
> teachings"); the development surface can spread materials out and hold
> the member's own groupings and threads. Discovery help comes only
> through the Window, invited: *"what themes run through these?"* is
> MAIA reflecting on the member's own material — allowed; MAIA
> pre-organizing the archive unasked — forbidden. The room stays
> work-centered so gathering never decays into a document manager.

**What worked.** Renewal surface is *his* surface: an old teaching
preserved, a new expression developing beside it. New expressions
(a book, a course) are declared becomings of the same body of work
(A3). The original is never overwritten — the room's deepest promise
matters most to the person with the most past.

**Answer**: accumulated wisdom can become living work — this persona is
the strongest validation of Materials + Renewal + expressions together.

## Walk verdict

| Persona | Room survives? | Amendments forced |
|---|---|---|
| Novelist | Yes | A1 (continue ≠ renew), A2 (pinnable drawers) |
| Scholar | Yes | A5 (citation instrument), feeds A4 (roles) |
| Blogger | Yes | A3 (expression switching) |
| Collaborator | Yes | A4 (offered/adopted grammar) |
| Elder | Yes | A6 (gathering arrival) |

The three-zone shape (Study Wall · Worktable · Window) held for all five.
The deleted bottom strip was missed by no one. No persona pushed the room
toward a separate product; every strain resolved as a **conditional
instrument inside the same room** — which is the design challenge passed:
same studio, different instruments.
