## 1. What is the unit of experience?

⭐⭐⭐ **"The unit of design is a moment."** — Member Experience Design Constitution §165

⛔ Not the manuscript. ⛔ Not the work. ⛔ Not the person. **A moment.**

The Living Work Atlas supplies the unit of *the work's life* — a cycle, not a pipeline:

```
1 Appears → 2 Gathers → 3 Deepens → 4 Waits → 5 Changes → 6 Expresses
                    ↕ Returning — the heartbeat
              (vertical; inherited by EVERY phase)
```

⭐⭐ **Returning is not a phase. It is the heartbeat that every phase inherits.** That is why
`sitting-001` treats return as the whole product rather than a feature, and why the Atlas's cut
lineage moved `objects → movements → time + conditions`: *what exists?* → *what lives?* → *what does
the life require?*

⚠️ **The Waiting phase has no implementation anywhere.** A work that is dormant is, in the Atlas, in
a named living state. In the product it is simply absent from view.

## 2. Where does MAIA stand?

⭐⭐⭐ **Defined entirely by refusal.** `THE_CULTIVATION_METHOD` records the three acts of the field
with their refusals:

> **never writes for you · never edits behind you · never brings anything in unasked**

`R1_EXPERIENTIAL_SPECIFICATION` sharpens the method: *"the useful question is never what features
does it have, but **what does it refuse to do**,"* and names the refusals **"Deliberate refusals —
constitutional, not missing."**

Its comparative anatomy is the clearest statement of MAIA's position in the room:

| Lens | The property admired | The failure mode named |
|---|---|---|
| **Scrivener** | *"The creator moves structure; intelligence never authors it."* | *"Mistaking generated organisation for discovered meaning."* |
| **Obsidian** | *"Connections are declared, never inferred."* — ⭐⭐⭐ **"It has no inference engine."** | asserted relationships |

⭐⭐⭐ **MAIA is not editor, collaborator, librarian, or guide in this room.** The design admires a
tool with **no inference engine** and defines the field by what it will not do. The Writer's Field
is the same boundary as the memory work, in another key.

## 3. What is allowed to move?

- **The creator moves structure.** Intelligence never does.
- **Material moves into the text only by an explicit act.** `INTEGRATE_PRACTICE` names this the
  missing bridge: *"Capture and Gather have value only insofar as material can be **explicitly
  integrated** into that field. 'Integrate' names the missing bridge between preserved material and
  authored expression."*

⭐⭐ This is the **same invariant as the Field Object declaration**, one layer up: the source does not
push; the member brings in. `f24ea189e` ("bring a kept passage into the working draft at the caret")
is that invariant already in code.

---

## 4. What must remain still?

- **Position.** *"Paper's advantage is that nothing moves. Don't move things."*
- **Relationships.** Declared, never inferred.
- **The refusals themselves** — constitutional, not gaps to be filled later.
- ⛔ **Internal vocabulary must not move outward.** `INTEGRATE_PRACTICE`: *"Hearth", "Place",
  "Gesture" remain internal design vocabulary, **never member-facing**.* (Same class as the
  Writer's Studio · Canvas · WriterField ruling of 2026-08-04.)

⭐⭐⭐ *"Nothing moves"* is **not** a UI instruction. It is the preservation of **orientation and
authorship** — the writer's ability to find their own sentence, and their certainty that no one
else moved it.

## 5. The standing tests the corpus already carries

| Test | Source |
|---|---|
| **"Every screen should leave the member closer to their work than when they arrived."** | Design Constitution |
| **"The competency: faithful return, not continuous focus."** | Design Constitution §200 |
| *"…quietly deepens the relationship between a member and their work while asking for as little attention as possible."* | Design Constitution |
| **0 decisions · under 10 seconds · open → first keystroke** | `sitting-001` |
| Copy: *"What is the person trying to do in this moment? Then write only enough words to help them do that. **No explanation. No persuasion. No poetry.**"* → test: *"Clear language that gets out of the way."* | Studio Copy Voice |
| *"Remove before adding."* · *"Design from practice, not software."* · *"The member's attention is sacred."* | Design Constitution |

⚠️ **"Faithful return, not continuous focus"** deserves separate weight. It rules out engagement as
a measure outright: the product is not trying to hold the writer, it is trying to **give them back
their place**. Every retention-shaped instinct is excluded by it.

## 6. The method constraint on how the next slice is chosen

`DESIGN_LENSES` states the working posture, and it constrains *this lane's* next move:

> *"Do not begin by writing code. First understand what the member is trying to accomplish, where
> attention is interrupted, and what can be removed. Design the smallest, calmest experience…
> **Show three alternatives (obvious, simpler, minimal)**, explain the tradeoffs, and only then
> propose implementation… make the Studio feel like a place someone wants to spend years creating,
> not a piece of software they have to learn."*

⭐⭐ **Three alternatives — obvious, simpler, minimal — is a required step the corpus asks for and
this lane has not performed.** A single proposed slice would skip it.
