Paper

Question Why do writers still print the manuscript?
What paper does better
PositionA sentence stays in the same spot forever. Top of the left page, two-thirds down.
Finding againYou remember where it was before you remember what it said.
The marginSpace next to the text that isn't the text. Free, and always there.
Writing in itSame pen, no mode, no menu. Marking is as easy as writing.
Two at onceTwo pages, two hands. Compare without losing either.
Putting it downThe open page is where you were. Nothing to save. Nothing to restore.
How much is leftYou feel the thickness. No progress bar.
It wants nothingThe only thing on the desk that can't interrupt you.
What screens lose
ScrollingThe same paragraph is somewhere different every time. Nothing to remember.
ReflowChange the window and the whole geography moves.
ModesCommenting is a different act, with different controls.
One at a timeComparing means leaving one of the two.
Take
  • The margin as a real place
  • Put things back where they were
  • Mark with no mode change
  • Show how much is left
  • Ask nothing of the writer
Don't imitate
  • Page breaks
  • Paper texture
  • Shadows and covers
  • Fixed page size
  • Page numbers
Finding Paper's advantage is that nothing moves. Don't move things.
The Studio should
  • Put a paragraph back where it was
  • Keep the measure fixed when the window changes
  • Let marking use the same gesture as writing
  • Make the margin useful, not empty
Open questions
  • Comparing two passages
  • Showing how much is left
  • Whether position can survive editing
Don't imitate paper. Understand what it still does better. 2026-07-31
