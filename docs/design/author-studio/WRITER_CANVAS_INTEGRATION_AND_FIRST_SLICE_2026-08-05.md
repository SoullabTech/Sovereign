# Return to the Canvas — Integration Design & the First Slice (Work Continuity Layer, Step 5)

> **Status**: Final design object of the Work Continuity Layer lane,
> following the accepted Work drawer, Materials (+ paper walk M1–M7), and
> Structure (+ paper walk S1–S5) designs.
>
> **RULED (founder, 2026-08-05): the first slice is AUTHORIZED, narrowly.**
> - **Authorized**: the smallest complete Work relationship loop (create →
>   declare becoming → bring one material → see reflected → return). The
>   goal is not to complete the house; **the goal is to prove that the
>   house has a living center.**
> - **Not authorized**: full Materials system · full Structure system ·
>   Collaboration · Learning layer · MAIA intelligence layer · new Canvas
>   zones.
> - **Becoming storage**: test the existing `purpose` field first. The
>   question is *"what does the creator naturally want to say?"*, not
>   *"what metadata does a Work need"* — a creator's one statement is often
>   both why-this-exists and what-it-is-becoming. A separate field may
>   emerge only if creators reveal the split; creating it now would force a
>   conceptual distinction before they do.
> - **Expression declaration confirmed as the load-bearing move**: a
>   relationship action, not an object action — it establishes
>   Work → Expression → current artifact, which is what allows one Work →
>   many forms, forms changing over time, renewal without replacement,
>   expression without identity collapse.
> - **Acceptance walk (ruled)**: novelist · scholar · blogger ·
>   elder/teacher · **world-builder** — asking one question: *did the
>   creator experience a deeper relationship with the Work, or did we
>   merely add another label?* Former → the foundation is real. Latter →
>   the gesture has not yet earned its place.
> - **Two coexisting tests**: universal — *"I can work here"*; world-builder
>   — *"I can live in my worlds here"* (supports exploration before
>   expression; prevents collapsing imagination into documents).
>

## The world-explorer (founder, 2026-08-05 — the deepest form of the success test)

> *"For many, it is about writing. For me it is about exploring worlds I
> think and dream of all day long."*

The Canvas's own founder enters it not to produce text but to **dwell in
and explore worlds**. This is the sixth creator, and the one the design
must never betray: for this creator, the development surface — materials
spread out, threads forming, connections appearing under their hands — is
not preparation for writing; **it is the work itself**. Writing is one
expression a world may take; so is a book, a course, a teaching, a
cosmology.

This deepens the success test one final step:

- v0.1 failure: *"Where do I type?"*
- v0.1 success: *"I can work here."*
- The integrated Canvas: **"I can live in my worlds here."**

Every integration decision below is checked against that sentence.

## The return (what integration means experientially)

Not `open editor → write`, but:

```
Return to Work → Understand context → Choose next creative gesture → Create
```

On return, the room answers four questions **without narrating**:

| Question | Answered by | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| What is this Work? | Work continuity (identity + becoming) | The head of the room, in the member's words |
| What feeds it? | Materials (belongings + sentences) | Materials drawer; anything brought to the table is still there |
| How is it taking shape? | Structure (carried · declared · named threads) | Structure drawer / rail (labeled per S1) |
| Where do I continue? | The Worktable | Open at the last gesture — continuation, not initiation |

**More intelligent without more intrusive — the calm rule of return:**
the room never performs its knowledge. No return summary, no "welcome
back — since you left…", no context screen before the work. The context
is **in the walls** (drawers that now hold true relationships) and the
continuation is **on the table**. The member reaches for context when
they want it; the room simply *is* current. Adaptation keys on authored
facts only, as everywhere.

The integrated Canvas is v0.1's exact room with its organs alive — no new
zones, no layout change, no fourth column. Integration is the drawers
stopping being honest scaffolds and starting to hold declared
relationships.

## The first slice (proposal for ruling)

**The founder's caution, honored**: not all three drawers at once. One
complete lived loop:

```
Create Work
      ↓
Declare what it is becoming
      ↓
Bring material into relationship
      ↓
See that relationship reflected in the Canvas
      ↓
Return and continue
```

### The smallest lived gesture

> *"What is the smallest lived gesture that makes the creator feel:
> 'this Work knows what it is becoming'?"*

**Declaring the expression** — the member, in the Work drawer's Shape
register, saying of the manuscript on the table: *"this is a form of this
Work — it is becoming my book."* One gesture, two effects:

1. The first true relationship exists: the first member-authored write to
   `living_work_expressions` — a declaration, not a file attachment.
2. The room can finally be **honest and whole at its head**: the Work and
   the thing on the table are united by the member's own word, so
   returning becomes *returning to the Work* — not to "the most recent
   manuscript." The v0.1-close display restraint (near, not claimed) ends
   exactly where it should: at the member's declaration.

### Slice contents (proposed)

1. **Identity tending in the Work drawer**: name / rename; state or amend
   the becoming, in the member's words. (Substrate mostly exists:
   `living_works.title`; the becoming's storage — reuse of `purpose` vs.
   its own field — is a slice-ruling question, flagged, not assumed.)
2. **Declare-expression gesture** (Shape register): "this manuscript is a
   form of this Work" + the becoming word. Undeclarable the same way
   (un-belonging deletes nothing). No silent promotion — never set by
   import, arrival, or display.
3. **One belonging gesture, one material type** (Materials drawer):
   *bring this to this Work* with an optional sentence — first type =
   another manuscript/import declared as feeding (M1's arrival-home
   question deferred; platform-native things first). Renders in the
   drawer as designed: sentence first, thing second, home stated.
4. **The reflection of it all**: head of the room united when declared
   (per 2); Shape and Materials registers rendering the declarations;
   return honoring them.

**Explicitly out**: Origins register contents beyond origin-shaped
sentences · citation instrument · gathering-at-volume UI · structure rail
names+jump (next slice candidate, reads existing sections) · work shape ·
collaboration · any MAIA surface change.

### Acceptance (when ruled)

Experiential and visual: the loop walked in the rendered room by the
founder — create, declare, bring, see, leave, return — ending on the one
felt sentence: *"this Work knows what it is becoming."* Technical gates
(tests, typecheck) are preconditions, not acceptance. Then the five
personas again, plus the sixth: does the world-explorer feel their world
held, not their documents managed?
