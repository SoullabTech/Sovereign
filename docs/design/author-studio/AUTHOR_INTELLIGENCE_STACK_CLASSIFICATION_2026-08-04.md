### 3.1 Jurisdictions, not roles (the real contribution)

The proposed intelligences are **a different decomposition of capabilities already identified** — names for responsibilities, not new constitutional decisions. The seven-intelligence model already governs the work at a more fundamental level.

What is new is **constitutional separation of powers**. Not *seven prompts* — **seven authorities with limited jurisdiction**:

- Research may identify unsupported claims. It may never rewrite prose.
- Development may identify structural weakness. It may never fact-check.
- Continuity may identify inconsistencies. It may never recommend stylistic changes.

These are **system guarantees, not prompt instructions** — the distinction that makes it architecture.

**The jurisdiction chain (Kelly, 2026-08-04):**

```
Observation → Classification → Evidence → Question → Suggestion → Adoption
```

Every intelligence operates *somewhere on this chain*. **None may skip directly to Adoption.** This is stronger than parallel silos: one shared chain with bounded jurisdictions, and Adoption reachable only through the author.

**State: Candidate.** Costs nothing to hold; folds into the Witnessed Writing Cycle's typed contracts when that slice is built.

### 3.2 Voice Guardian — open constitutional question

An intelligence whose only output is **rejection**: *does this sound more like the author, or more like AI? If AI — reject.*

> **Does the system contain a persistent model of the author's voice?**

If yes, both generation and refusal **depend on that same constitutional object** — one writes through it, the other filters through it. Refusal-only therefore does **not** automatically escape the Q10 exclusion.

**State: open constitutional question.** Not assumed permitted. ⛔ Do not resolve preemptively; do not build a voice model of any kind on the strength of the refusal framing.

# 6. Constitutional implications

> **The three invariants below are one set, not three notes** (Kelly, 2026-08-04):
> 1. Authority is bounded by jurisdiction.
> 2. Authority composes only through the author, not through the system.
> 3. Knowledge may propagate; authority may not.
>
> *"Those three together prevent an orchestrator from quietly becoming a generalized author."*
> That is what the set is for. Do not adopt one without the others.
>
> **Why removal weakens the remainder** (Kelly, 2026-08-04) — the test for whether the set is
> really inseparable, kept here so a future session cannot adopt a subset in good faith:
> - Without **jurisdiction**, propagation becomes unrestricted delegation.
> - Without **author composition**, jurisdictions can be recombined into a generalized author.
> - Without the **propagation rule**, jurisdictions become isolated silos that cannot benefit
>   from one another's observations.

## 6.1 Jurisdiction is stronger than personality

The new contribution is often described as "roles," but the constitutional object is actually **jurisdiction**.

A role is an implementation choice.
A jurisdiction is a limit on authority.

That distinction matters because implementations may change while jurisdictions remain invariant.

For example:

| Jurisdiction               | May                                                                | May not                                            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Research                   | identify unsupported claims, missing evidence, conflicting sources | rewrite prose, change structure, alter voice       |
| Structure                  | identify ordering, pacing, hierarchy                               | fact-check, rewrite sentences, infer author intent |
| Continuity                 | identify inconsistencies                                           | propose stylistic improvements                     |
| Voice (if ever authorized) | evaluate suggestions against author identity                       | generate replacement prose                         |

The enduring constitutional rule is therefore:

> **Every intelligence possesses positive authority and explicit prohibitions.**

The prohibitions are as important as the permissions.

**State:** Candidate.

---

## 6.2 Authority should compose, not accumulate

A future risk is allowing one intelligence to inherit another's authority.

Example:

Research
→ identifies weak evidence.

Development
→ restructures argument.

Voice
→ rewrites sentence.

If these become sequential permissions inside one chain, the system quietly reconstructs a generalized author.

Instead:

Each intelligence returns independently to the author.
The author integrates them.
The machine never acquires compound authority through orchestration.

This is consistent with the existing constitutional hinge:

> external suggestion → visible evidence → author correction → explicit adoption

The author remains the point where jurisdictions converge.

---

## 6.3 Evidence should cross jurisdictions without transferring authority

Information may travel.
Authority should not.

Example:

Research discovers:

> "This citation appears unsupported."

That observation may be available to Structure.

Structure may conclude:

> "This chapter's argument depends on that unsupported claim."

But Structure still cannot investigate sources.
Research still cannot reorganize chapters.

Knowledge propagates.
Authority does not.

This separation becomes enforceable if each jurisdiction exchanges typed observations rather than executable recommendations.

**State:** Candidate.

Only the author can integrate findings from multiple jurisdictions.

That avoids hidden prioritization logic.

# 8. Voice Guardian — constitutional framing

The unresolved question may be framed more precisely.

The issue is not:

> Does the model generate or reject?

The issue is:

> **Does evaluation require constructing an internal representation of the author's voice?**

If yes, then refusal and generation may differ operationally while remaining constitutionally equivalent.

If no—if evaluation is based only on immediate local comparison without maintaining an author model—then the earlier exclusion may not apply.

That distinction, rather than output direction, is likely the constitutional hinge.

The record is therefore correct to leave this unresolved.

> *These implementation concepts are in service of the Author Studio and Manuscript Room already established by the constitutional rulings.*

**Without that sentence, the implementation language begins to compete with the member language.**
