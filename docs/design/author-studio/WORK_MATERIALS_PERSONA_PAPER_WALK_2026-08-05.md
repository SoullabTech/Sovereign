# Materials & Gathering — Five-Persona Paper Walk

> **Status**: The walk the Materials design gates on
> (`WORK_MATERIALS_GATHERING_DESIGN_2026-08-05.md`), run on paper against
> the accepted design + founder protections, 2026-08-05. One question asked
> at every step, per the ruling: **what did the creator do that made this
> belong?** Sound: selected · named · placed · declared · connected.
> Drift: uploaded · detected · inferred · suggested.
>
> **Verdict up front**: the design survives. Seven findings (M1–M7), none
> breaking the belonging model — each sharpens it. The elder (the ruled
> critical stress test) produced the two deepest: gathering must be
> deliberate placement even at volume (M2), and belonging must be
> re-declarable as works emerge from abundance (M3).

---

## Persona 1 — Novelist (research notes, grandmother's letters as scans)

| Material | What did the creator do? | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Research notes (already on platform) | selected the note, placed it into the work, wrote "orchard research" | ✅ sound |
| Grandmother's letters — scanned images | chose the scans, brought them in, placed them | ✅ sound — with M1 |

**M1 — Some things have no home to keep.** The belonging model says "the
thing keeps its home" — but a scan, a photo, a PDF article has no native
home on the platform. Resolution, not a break: a thing brought from
outside arrives **into the member's own things** (its new home — "brought
in, kept with your things"), and because the member performs that arrival
by hand, arrival and belonging can be one gesture. The rule holds: the
creator chose and placed. What is refused stays refused: no folder sync,
no drive import, no "upload everything" door.

## Persona 2 — Scholar (120 sources, citation needs)

| Material | What did the creator do? | Test |
|---|---|---|
| A source PDF | placed it, its own metadata (author/year) carried along | ✅ sound — carried metadata is part of the thing |
| A BibTeX/reference file | member brought a structured thing; entries arrive as the member's placements | ✅ sound |
| Auto-lookup ("we found this DOI / fetched the abstract") | detected | ⛔ drift — refused |

**M4 — Carried metadata is sound; fetched metadata is drift.** Metadata
the thing brings with it belongs to the thing; metadata the system goes
and gets is the system authoring the material's identity. The (later,
conditional) citation instrument reads carried metadata only.

**M5 — Volume is not only the elder's condition.** 120 sources one at a
time is punitive; gathering-at-volume (the design's gathering section)
applies to the scholar identically. Same rule as M2.

## Persona 3 — Blogger / Creator (links, screenshots, fragments)

| Material | What did the creator do? | Test |
|---|---|---|
| A web link | named it, placed it, wrote why | ✅ sound — the link stays a reference |
| System fetches the page, stores content, summarizes | detected + inferred | ⛔ drift — refused. The URL and the member's sentence are the material |
| A quote | selected the words themselves — quoting IS a creator act | ✅ sound |

**M6 — A quote is a member-made fragment.** Selecting words out of a
larger thing is authorship of the fragment; the quote's home is its
source, its content is the member's selection, its meaning is their
sentence. No auto-excerpting exists ("we pulled key passages" = drift).

## Persona 4 — Collaborator (held — boundary observation only)

Not walked; collaboration has no model yet. One observation recorded for
that future lane: when another hand brings a material to a shared work,
the belonging grammar already anticipates the answer — the material
arrives as an **offering**, and only the author's adoption makes it feed
the work (M7). Offer · preserve · adopt, unchanged. Nothing to design now.

## Persona 5 — Elder / Teacher (the ruled critical stress test: abundance)

The elder arrives with 218 items and no formed work. Walking the failure
mode first: *"great, upload everything"* → warehouse. Then the design's
answer:

| Act | What did the creator do? | Test |
|---|---|---|
| "Bring these 41 journals to *my teachings, gathering*" — one sitting, by hand | deliberate placement at coarse grain: chose WHICH things, named WHERE they belong | ✅ sound — with M2 |
| Sync my drive / watch my folder | uploaded | ⛔ drift — refused, permanently |
| Sentences unwritten on 200 of 218 items | nothing — and that is a correct state | ✅ the design already holds this |
| A year later: "these nine entries feed the book; those twelve feed the course" | re-declared belonging as works emerged | ✅ sound — requires M3 |

**M2 — Gathering is deliberate placement at volume, never ingestion.**
The volume act is itself a selection ("these boxes, this gathering") into
a named — even loosely named — work. The grain is coarse; the hand is
still the member's. The moment a gathering happens without the member
choosing which things and where, it is upload, and it is refused.

**M3 — Belonging is many-to-many and re-declarable.** The elder's journal
entry may feed the gathering, later the book, later the course — each a
separate declaration by the member. This mirrors what
`living_work_expressions` already deliberately allows (an expression may
belong to multiple works). Discovery of what belongs to an *emerging*
work is exactly the founder's question — *"what does the elder naturally
recognize as belonging?"* — and recognition arrives over time, so the
grammar must let belonging be declared late, again, or never.

**The AI boundary, walked with the elder** (where temptation is
strongest): the Window, invited, may say *"you have brought these 218
pieces into this gathering"* (fact) and, on the member's invitation, may
**offer** threads quoting only the member's material — which the member
adopts or ignores. *"Your themes appear to be…"* stated unbidden, or as
assertion rather than offer, fails the recognition boundary. The design's
allowed/invitation/never pattern held at every point of this walk.

|---|---|---|
| M1 | Outside things get a home at arrival (member's things, not the work); arrival+belonging = one member gesture | Addition, rule intact |

| M3 | Belonging is many-to-many, re-declarable, late-declarable | Grammar requirement |
| M4 | Carried metadata sound; fetched metadata drift | AI/provenance guard |
| M5 | Volume gathering serves the scholar too | Scope note |
| M6 | A quote is a member-made fragment | Material type clarified |
| M7 | Collaborator materials will arrive as offerings (held) | Note for future lane |

Every belonging in the walk traced to selected / named / placed /
