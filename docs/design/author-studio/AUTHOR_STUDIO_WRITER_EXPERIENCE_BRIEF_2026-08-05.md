## Founder rulings (2026-08-05 — govern everything below)

1. **Discover = author-owned recognition; system-supported encounter.**
   *"The system may increase the visibility of meaning. It may not claim
   ownership of meaning."* Discover is a studio table where the author lays
   out sketches and begins to see the painting — not "AI insight."
   ✅ surface/group/juxtapose/sequence Source material · ✅ the author
   notices patterns, tensions, echoes · ✅ MAIA may be *invited into
   conversation* about what the author is seeing · ❌ MAIA never announces
   "here is the theme emerging" / "your book is about X" — pattern
   recognition never converts into system authorship. Extended rule: *it
   never writes for you, and **it never decides what your work means for
   you**.*
2. **Release stays latent until the W1–W8 writing loop is revalidated.**
   Home = making the book; Release = **earned destination**. Edition/Release
   objects remain valid; their *visibility* waits for evidence. The home
   says "your book is being made here," never "here are all the things you
   might eventually do." `keepSource()` remains untouched.
3. **Tagline belongs to marketing/invitation, not the working surface.**
   Marketing: *"How a life becomes a book."* Inside the room: quiet —
   *"Your work"* / *"Continue your work."* The author does not need to be
   told they are living an important transformation every time they sit
   down; the room makes the transformation possible.

## 1. Who is the writer when they arrive?

Not a file manager. A person mid-transformation whose material outruns their
plan. Four arrival states, all real:

- **Full hands** — "I have something to add" (a memo, a document, a fragment).
- **Lost thread** — "I have fragments everywhere; I don't know what the book is yet."
- **Momentum** — "I was writing. Let me back in."
- **Completion pressure** — "I need to finish and share this."

**Design default**: the author arriving with *material, but not yet a shape*
— the Author Studio translation of "something important but not yet formed."
Formed intent (momentum) is the happy case, not the design center.

## 2. The five moments (author-state verbs — never phase names)

1. **Continue** — return to the book where I left it. The primary gesture;
   the work is the hearth of this room.
2. **Capture** — bring something in with zero friction. The trust promise
   stated once, plainly: *what you bring in is kept as your Source and never
   altered.*
3. **Discover** — see what is here and what is emerging. ⚠️ Constitutionally
   shaped — see §5.
4. **Write** — enter the draft and make words. (The Writer's Studio, which
   lives INSIDE Author Studio — the ratified two-environment distinction is
   preserved; nothing renamed.)
5. **Release** — bring it into form and share it. ⚠️ Gated — see §5.

Capture/Gather/Shape/Write/Release remain the builder's model. **The phases
exist underneath; they are not the furniture.**

## 3. The smallest complete author loop

> **Bring something in → see it land inside the work → write a passage from
> it → the work visibly grows → return tomorrow and find it all still there.**

That loop — capture, write, return — is what proves *"my life is becoming a
book."* It uses only capabilities that exist and are walkable today.
**Release is deliberately outside the smallest loop**: the Release walk
failed at W8 (member path missing) and must re-walk from W1 before Release
is presented as an available gesture. The current rail's honesty ("not yet
available") is a claim-discipline virtue — the fix is removing the *admin
grammar*, never faking availability.

## 4. How the existing objects support the loop

| Author's moment | Existing object | Surfaces as |
|---|---|---|
| Continue | Living Work / Working Draft | *Your books* → one door: **Continue writing →** |
| Capture | Source (immutable) | **Bring something in** — memo · document · fragment |
| Discover | Gatherings · Passages | **Explore your material** (see §5 shape) |
| Write | Working Draft (Writer's Studio) | the writing surface itself |
| Release | Edition · Release | a quiet *path* line: Draft → Refine → Design → Release |
| (admin) | rename / withdraw | recede into a work's quiet settings — exist when needed, never greet |

The code is closer than it feels: the objects are right; the skeleton is
showing. This is a translation pass, not a rebuild.

## 5. Constitutional flags (must be ruled, not smuggled)

- **Discover and the third voice.** "MAIA helps you see patterns" walks
  straight at the synthesis freeze: cross-material pattern claims are the
  system performing Reflection→Recognition on the author's behalf, and
  Pattern Attunement is explicitly held. The compliant shape: **Discover
  arranges the author's own material for encounter** (unplaced fragments,
  their own marked questions, juxtapositions they can act on) and MAIA is
  available **in conversation about it** — responsive, provenance-grounded,
  never an ambient banner announcing "a theme is emerging." The author does
  the recognizing. Copy accordingly: *"Explore your material — with MAIA in
  conversation, if you want. It never writes for you."*
- **Release gating** (above): no Release gesture on the home until the W1
  re-walk passes.
- **`keepSource()` hold** stands — this brief authorizes no changes to the
  capture write-path.
- **Tagline register**: "How a life becomes a book." is the product promise;
  per the Now What? precedent (*the tagline sells; the interface serves*),
  founder to rule whether it belongs on the member-facing wall or in
  marketing.
