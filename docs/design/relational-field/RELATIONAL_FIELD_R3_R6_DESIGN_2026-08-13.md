# Relational Field — R3–R6 design

**Founder-authored, 2026-08-13.** Design authority for the remaining Relational
Field work. Recorded verbatim in substance because the failure this whole
programme excavated is **design intent living only in conversation**.

⛔ **Not an implementation authorization.** No unit below is started.

---

## The decisive shift

> **The Relational Field should not tell people what their relationships are.
> It should help them encounter what is happening, remember what has happened,
> recognize what is changing, and remain free to determine what it means.**

### MAIA's movement

```
retrieve → attribute → offer → ask → receive correction
```

⛔ **Never:**

```
detect → classify → present as truth
```

That second line is precisely what the contained rupture mechanism did.

---

## 1. Real relational objects — member-created

Person or group name · nature of the relationship · what matters about it ·
current situation *in their own words* · hopes, concerns, boundaries, unresolved
questions · **whether MAIA may remember and reference it later**.

⭐ **This is the load-bearing item.** It solves the missing-declaration problem
directly: *a first-person statement becomes first-person because the member
actually made it — not because a classifier inferred it.*

## 2. A living relationship page

Member-written description · significant moments and conversations · reflections
and journal entries · questions being carried · changes in boundaries or
circumstances · relevant MAIA conversations · corrections to anything previously
understood · **a visible history of what changed and when**.

> A continuity space, not a psychological dossier.

## 3. MAIA enters as witness

> *"You previously described this relationship as feeling distant. Today you are
> speaking about renewed tenderness. Both are in the record."*

> ⚖️ **Correction 2026-08-13.** This exemplar previously closed with *"Does that
> feel like a real change, or are both experiences present?"* — **withdrawn.**
> `RELATIONSHIP_ROOM_CONSTITUTION.md` Article VII forbids any surface asking
> whether a condition has changed. MAIA may present **the record**; the member
> decides what it means and whether to act on it.

Preserves time, contradiction, provenance, and the person's authority — the
opposite of declaring someone "ruptured."

## 4. Elemental relational lens

Differentiated attention, ⛔ never fixed categories. Offered as invitation:
*"Would it help to explore the communication, the feeling beneath it, or the
boundary involved?"* **The taxonomy serves the person.**

| Element | Relational attention |
|---|---|
| Fire | Desire, truth, conflict, purpose, transformation |
| Water | Feeling, attachment, tenderness, grief, resonance |
| Earth | Events, commitments, boundaries, safety, embodied reality |
| Air | Communication, assumptions, interpretation, misunderstanding |
| Aether | The relationship as a whole |

> ⚖️ **Founder ruling 2026-08-13 (D-8).** *"…and what is trying to emerge"* is
> **struck.** It is a Recognition-layer assertion, and
> `docs/canon/CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md` forbids manufacturing
> higher-order meaning. Aether may support **integrative posture** but may never
> manufacture an emergent meaning. ⭐ If the **member** asks that question, MAIA may
> accompany their inquiry **without asserting that an emergence exists.**
> ⛔ Phrasing it as a question does not launder it: asking *"what's trying to
> emerge here?"* has already asserted that something is.

> ⚖️ **Founder ruling 2026-08-13 (D-5) — the selection principle.**
> **What MAIA chooses to retrieve, arrange, foreground, or omit can itself
> communicate an interpretation.** Meaning-bearing presentation **is expression**:
> system-selected ordering, prominence, badges, omission and juxtaposition are
> governed exactly as utterances are. ⚠️ This closes the gap a text-only reading
> leaves open — a structural reading carries no provenance, no quotable words, and
> no handle to correct.
> ⭐ **Not** automatically interpretation: neutral deterministic chronology, and
> organization the **member** selected.

## 5. Member-recognized patterns

⛔ Only **after multiple attributable moments exist**. e.g. *"This concern has
appeared in three conversations."* · *"You often become responsible for restoring
contact."* · *"Your stated boundary and your recent action seem to differ."* ·
*"You corrected my earlier understanding."*

> ⚖️ **Founder ruling 2026-08-13 (D-3) — anti-laundering holds.**
> *"…I will carry the correction forward"* is **struck.** A member-authored
> correction **to a declaration** may persist as a **new declaration event**. A
> correction of **MAIA's observation** does **not** authorize the observation to
> persist: before RF-R6, ⭐ **only the member's resulting words may carry
> forward.** ⛔ A2 §8③ governs — OBSERVED assertions remain in-turn only.
> This resolves the collision JRF-07 identified between A1 §5 and A2 §8③, in
> A2's favour.

⭐ **A pattern joins the field only when the member recognizes, modifies, or
explicitly preserves it.**

## 6. Visible corrigibility

Where it came from · declared or inferred · when last affirmed · whether
corrected · whether MAIA may use it · **Keep / Correct / Release**.

> This is where the field becomes genuinely different from ordinary AI memory.

## 7. Shared relational space — later

> ⚖️ **Founder ruling 2026-08-13 (D-6) — composition, not filtering.**
> ⛔ Shared-space composition **must begin from a separately authorized input
> set.** Filtering a private composition afterward is **too late** — selection
> has already happened, and what MAIA chooses to foreground or omit itself
> communicates the private material it was selected from.
> ⭐ Retrieval consent applies **only to the member's own private field.**
> Sharing requires a **separate, attributable consent event** naming the
> declaration, the recipient or space, the purpose, and its revocability.

Two consented people, **memories and meanings kept distinct**: Kelly's
experience · the other person's experience · shared declarations · MAIA's
explicitly labelled observations.
⛔ **No private interpretation may silently become shared truth.**

## ⭐ The provenance boundary — founder precision, 2026-08-13

> **RF-R3 does not remove containment; it creates the truthful representational
> capacity under which containment can selectively open.**

⚠️ **CORRECTION to an earlier claim in this record.** A prior draft said item 1
lifts the containment *"by construction"*. That overstated it. Precisely:

- Creating a member-owned relationship object makes member declaration
  **representable**.
- ⛔ It does **not** make every statement attached to that object a declaration.
- `DECLARATION_CAPABLE_SOURCES` gains a legitimate member **only when the stored
  value preserves an actual member-authored declaration.**
- ⛔ **Ineligible, permanently:** model inference · observer output · imported
  text · system classification.
- ⭐ **The gate lifts per attributable assertion — never broadly per
  relationship.**

**RF-R3 must define the exact provenance boundary** at which a member-authored
declaration becomes eligible to speak. That definition is a deliverable of the
unit, not an assumption of it.

## Relationship to the rupture containment

`DECLARATION_CAPABLE_SOURCES` is empty because the schema cannot represent member
declaration. RF-R3 creates that capacity — but see the provenance-boundary
precision above: the set gains a member **per attributable assertion**, only where
the stored value preserves an actual member-authored declaration. Containment
**opens selectively**; it is never relaxed.

⛔ Until then the containment stands, and RF-R3…RF-R6 may not inherit or work
around the contained bucket.
