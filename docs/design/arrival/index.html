A · Threshold

Light rising as material. The place is already there; illumination lets the member see that it is a place, and the surface resolves out of the dark rather than appearing.

Motion meaning: attention + transformation · one ambient gesture, form static

B · Living Field

Depth. Three planes of the field hierarchy separate under the pointer, so the member is looking into somewhere. On entry the surface plane advances — being let further in.

Motion meaning: depth + relationship · parallax caused by the member, never ambient

C · Quiet Arrival

Near-stillness. No card, no cinema — composition and typography carry the threshold, and the ruled input reads as print rather than software. One movement in the whole file.

Motion meaning: relationship only · the deliberate test that stillness is a legitimate outcome
