# LIVING-FIELD-GRAMMAR-01
## Transferable Living Field Grammar

**Status:** cross-surface design grammar
**Date:** 29 September 2026

Living Field Grammar is the transferable language through which Living Field Architecture becomes implementable across Soullab surfaces.

It contains:

- semantic tokens;
- experience dials;
- theme inheritance;
- elemental behaviors where explicitly governed;
- motion roles;
- continuity rules;
- semantic zoom;
- presence laws;
- return grammar;
- relational/provenance markings;
- member / MAIA standing;
- retrofit / canon rules;
- accessibility and reduced-motion requirements.

## Core grammar

### Identity
Identity persists through scale unless the member or source truth establishes otherwise.

### Relation
Relation requires standing. Proximity is not relation.

### Time
Time unfolds within stable identity where possible.

### Zoom
Zoom increases semantic resolution, not merely graphic size.

### Motion
Motion reveals change of view, continuity, or member action; it does not manufacture urgency or importance.

### Atmosphere
Atmosphere holds experience; it does not interpret the member.

### Provenance
Authorship and standing remain perceptible at the point of encounter.

### Return
Every deep exploration preserves a truthful path back to the wider field.

### Agency
Member-authored changes outrank model-suggested interpretations.

### Accessibility
Meaning remains available without color, motion, fine pointer control, or hidden hover-only states.
