# GROKKER-LIVING-FIELD-01
## Grokker as Living Field Architecture

**Status:** implementation doctrine
**Date:** 29 September 2026

> **Grokker is a Living Field Architecture implementation for traversing meaning.**

Grokker should not primarily visualize information.

> **It should make meaning traversable.**

Therefore:

- identity persists through scale;
- relationships become perceivable without being fabricated;
- perspective may change without losing the whole;
- motion reveals structure;
- semantic zoom reveals deeper resolution;
- chance remains transparent;
- member-authored geography remains legible;
- MAIA may illuminate the field without becoming its authority;
- every deep exploration retains a truthful path back to the member's wider world.

Grokker is not a generic mind-map tool.

Its purpose is to support perception, relation, synthesis, and return inside a coherent living field.
