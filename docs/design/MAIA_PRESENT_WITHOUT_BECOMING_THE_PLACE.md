# MAIA can be present without becoming the place

**Status: extracted design invariant, 2026-09-06.** Derived from two
independently walked rooms, not from one room generalized. Placed in `docs/design/`;
elevation to `docs/canon/` is a founder act and has not been taken here.

Extracted only after Reflections and Journal each closed a production witness, and
only after the comparison produced a **subtraction** — one property failed to
survive — rather than manufactured convergence. See
`docs/design/contracts/reflections-maia-handoff.md` and
`docs/design/JOURNAL_SPECIMEN_TWO_DERIVATION_2026-09-06.md`.

---

## The invariant

```text
A  The member-owned object remains primary.

C  MAIA enters from that object through a room-owned threshold,
   rather than appearing as an ambient competing destination.

D  Leaving MAIA restores the member to the same place and object.

E  Exit carries no penalty:
   no capture pressure, retention pressure, confirmation tax,
   or implied loss.
```

## The negative finding — recorded with equal weight

```text
NOT INVARIANT
B  Canonical thread continuity.

Reflections:
  continuing canonical conversation
Journal:
  transient one-turn reflection

Therefore continuity, persistence, composer/thread behavior,
and conversation identity are ROOM-SPECIFIC.
```

This is the load-bearing half of the result. Extracted from Reflections alone, a
continuing canonical thread would now sit inside the pattern as though it were
essential — and every future room would inherit a requirement that one of the two
walked rooms actively refuses.

## Geometry is explicitly not part of the pattern

```text
Reflections  → bounded contained sheet
Journal      → subordinate inline reflection

Neither geometry is canonical.
The invariant is relational, not spatial.
```

Both rooms hold A/C/D/E identically while sharing no geometry. Any name or rule
derived from the *container* would have been true of one room and false of the
other. The relationship survived; the shape did not.

## The question a future room asks

Not:

> ~~Should I use the sheet?~~

But:

> **How can MAIA enter this member-owned place without becoming the place?**

Then derive its own answer against A/C/D/E, on the room's own terms.

## Guardrail — the same error has two directions

Do **not** turn Journal's transience into the new generic rule. That repeats the
mistake in the opposite direction: one specimen generalized, differently dressed.

The extracted pattern permits:

```text
persistent  or  transient
sheet       or  inline
conversation or reflection
```

provided A/C/D/E hold. A room that needs continuing conversation may have it. A
room whose activity is served by a single transient reflection may have that. The
invariant constrains the *relationship*, not the mechanism.

## Methodological finding

> **What survived two independently derived rooms is the pattern.
> What differed belongs to the rooms.**

Journal was derived from Journal — its own object, threshold, and law — and not
by importing `MaiaPresence` as a premise. That independence is why the comparison
could produce a subtraction at all. Had specimen two started from the component,
convergence would have been guaranteed and meaningless.

**A third room is not a prerequisite.** The next room to face this question is the
first **consumer and test** of this invariant, not another instrument for
discovering it. If a third room cannot satisfy A/C/D/E on its own terms, that is
evidence against the invariant and is to be recorded as such — not designed around.
