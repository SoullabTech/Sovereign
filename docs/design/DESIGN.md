# SOULLAB DESIGN.md v0.2R1
## Living design constitution for Soullab · MAIA · AIN

**Status:** RECONCILED CANDIDATE  
**Purpose:** compact pre-read for meaningful member-facing design work  
**Authority:** subordinate to sovereignty, consent, truth, safety and accessibility canon  
**Rule:** this constitution exists to make coherent invention possible, not to make variation impossible.

---

# 1. Organism

Soullab is one living ecology with many distinct organs.

House, MAIA, Writer's Studio, Relationships, Oracle, Living Field, practitioner surfaces and future rooms may differ strongly in local character while sharing one organism.

Shared systems:
- continuity;
- semantic design language;
- accessibility;
- sovereignty;
- provenance;
- theme inheritance;
- interaction grammar;
- return.

Local organs may vary:
- density;
- symbolism;
- motion;
- materiality;
- temporal pace;
- elemental expression;
- spatial topology.

**Coherence does not require sameness.**

---

# 2. The organism's core laws

## O1 — The member remains the subject
The member's life, work, relationships, experience and world remain primary.

## O2 — Life outranks platform
Soullab is a mirror and induction point into lived reality, not a substitute world.

## O3 — Truth before seamlessness
Never imply memory, certainty, relation, authorship or continuity that cannot be supported.

## O4 — Authorship remains legible
Member-authored, member-confirmed, MAIA-candidate, partial, unknown and structural material remain distinguishable.

## O5 — Continuity preserves identity through change
Preserve the thread without freezing the member into an old state.

## O6 — Return is first-class
Re-entry should restore the nearest truthful locus rather than reset to a generic dashboard.

## O7 — Presence before interpretation
Help the member notice what is here before explaining what it means.

## O8 — Aliveness is responsiveness, not decoration
Meaningful acts matter; relations emerge; time is present; unfinishedness is allowed.

## O9 — Magic reveals real relation
Magic is truthful coherence becoming newly perceptible, not mystical ornament.

## O10 — Preference is not psychological evidence
Theme, atmosphere, layout or sensory preference is presentation choice, not mood/state/personality data.

## O11 — Accessibility has final visual authority
Accessibility may override atmosphere, motion, symbolism, density or theme.

## O12 — Sensory channels cohere
Visual, temporal, kinesthetic, sonic, haptic and relational signals should tell the same experiential story.

## O13 — The wider whole remains recoverable
Focus may deepen; it may not erase context.

## O14 — Unresolvedness is legitimate
Not every thread, pattern, silence or symbol must resolve.

## O15 — Restraint is intelligence
The system earns trust by not surfacing everything it knows.

## O16 — Themes modulate; they do not replace identity
Day, Blue Evening, Night and future atmospheres are conditions of one field.

## O17 — Elements are behavioral forces
Fire, Water, Earth, Air and Aether shape behavior, rhythm, geometry, materiality and relation—not merely color.

## O18 — Local character is protected
A room may be strange, sparse, dense, ritual, professional, luminous or dark when that serves its human activity.

## O19 — Canon is memory of learning, not a cage
Canon preserves load-bearing learning and remains revisable when it becomes restrictive, obsolete or over-generalized.

# 3. Rule ladder

| Standing | Meaning | Promotion threshold |
|---|---|---|
| OBSERVED | credible signal | repeat or investigate |
| EXPERIMENT | bounded candidate | hypothesis + witness |
| LOCAL | works in one place | local success |
| PATTERN | reusable relation | success on 2 materially different surfaces |
| DEFAULT | normal organism answer | success across 3+ surface families |
| INVARIANT | load-bearing law | violation predictably harms sovereignty, truth, accessibility, agency, relational integrity, continuity, consent or safety |
| LEGACY | still present, not guidance | historical compatibility only |
| RETIRED | no longer operative | supersession recorded |

Promote the **relationship**, not the implementation.

A color, component, layout or effect does not become canon merely because it worked once.

---

# 4. Yield order

When rules conflict:

1. sovereignty · consent · truth · safety · accessibility
2. member's present human activity and ability to act
3. organism invariants
4. surface-family contract
5. member-selected preference
6. contextual domain / elemental expression
7. reusable pattern
8. default
9. aesthetic preference

No lower layer silently overrides a higher one.

# 5. Presence induction

Presence is not a mode.
The field makes presence easier by changing the conditions of attention.

Five channels:

**PERCEPTUAL** — clear locus, stable anchors, reduced competition.  
**TEMPORAL** — no unnecessary rush, permission to linger.  
**SOMATIC** — spatial/tactile interaction where appropriate.  
**RELATIONAL** — clarify what the member is in relation with now.  
**REFLECTIVE** — mirror before interpreting.

Typical induction:

ARRIVE  
→ ORIENT  
→ SETTLE  
→ NOTICE  
→ ENGAGE  
→ REFLECT  
→ INTEGRATE  
→ RETURN TO LIFE

Not every surface needs every stage.

The platform should never trap the member inside the induction.

---

# 6. Aliveness

A living field carries:

**RESPIRATION** — subtle cycles of expansion/rest.  
**RESPONSIVENESS** — meaningful acts visibly matter.  
**PERSISTENCE** — what mattered does not vanish without reason.  
**EMERGENCE** — new form may arise from truthful accumulated material.  
**PERMEABILITY** — meaning can cross rooms when lineage allows.  
**RHYTHM** — immediacy, pause, ripening, interruption, return.  
**UNFINISHEDNESS** — unresolved material may remain unresolved.

Aliveness must feel available, not watchful.

No hidden mood adaptation, false synchronicity, attention-seeking animation or unasked emotional inference.

---

# 7. Living continuity

Presence asks:
**What is here now?**

Continuity asks:
**How did we arrive here?**

Continuity layers:

C0 SELF — this is still my life/work/field.  
C1 OBJECT — the same thing remains the same thing across places.  
C2 RELATIONAL — relations persist until revised, withdrawn or superseded.  
C3 SPATIAL — the wider whole remains recoverable through focus/zoom.  
C4 TEMPORAL — then, since then, now, what changed.  
C5 EPISTEMIC — authorship/standing travels with the object.  
C6 AFFECTIVE — atmosphere changes without unexplained whiplash.  
C7 INTENTIONAL — recover what I was doing and why.

Continuity preserves the thread through change, not sameness against change.

---

# 8. Continuity and trust

Trust grows when the system carries the right things forward, lets the wrong things go, and makes change legible.

Trust dimensions:
- recognition;
- authorship;
- temporal truth;
- reversal;
- preference;
- restraint;
- transparent failure.

Continuity receipts may include:
- you wrote this;
- you confirmed this;
- MAIA candidate;
- revised [date];
- still unresolved;
- partial field.

Receipts are available when useful, not plastered everywhere.

---

# 9. Return hospitality

Return should feel like re-entering something that remained yours.

Sequence:

RECOGNIZE  
→ REORIENT  
→ REVEAL CHANGE  
→ OFFER CONTINUATION  
→ LEAVE SPACE

Prefer:
- Continue
- Return to…
- You left this here
- Still open
- Last revised…
- Nothing has changed here
- This changed since you last looked

Avoid:
- We missed you
- It's been X days
- Get back on track
- Here's what you missed
- streak pressure

Persist meaning and orientation.
Release interface debris.

Long absence produces **less inference**, not more.

# 11. Theme inheritance

A theme changes atmosphere, not identity.

Resolution order:

SOULLAB SEMANTIC ROLE  
→ MEMBER ATMOSPHERE  
→ DOMAIN MODULATION  
→ ELEMENTAL MODULATION  
→ SURFACE FAMILY  
→ INTERACTION STATE  
→ ACCESSIBILITY OVERRIDE

Initial atmosphere families:

**DAY** — warm, lucid, spacious.  
**BLUE EVENING** — twilight, contemplative, blue-depth.  
**NIGHT** — deep, immersive, low-luminance.  
**AUTO** — only when explicitly enabled; manual choice wins.

This list is extensible. New atmosphere families enter through the normal experiment → witness → admission process.

A route does not own the member's atmosphere.

Writer's Studio is the technical reference:
**ROOM** and **WORKING SURFACE** may vary independently.

Scoped overrides do not repaint the surrounding room.

REVEAL

# 20. Experiential magic

Magic is truthful relation becoming newly perceptible.

Prefer:
- meaningful correspondence;
- continuity across time;
- true relational bridges;
- perspective shifts preserving the same world;
- semantic zoom;
- member-authored material returning in relevant context;
- motion revealing structure;
- fair chance with provenance.

Avoid:
- random sparkles;
- constant glow;
- decorative particles;
- generic sacred overlays;
- mystical copy doing interaction's work;
- arbitrary sound;
- animation implying intelligence or meaning that is not there.

Magic test:
1. What changed in perception?
2. What real relationship became visible?
3. Could decoration alone create this?

If #2 is unclear or #3 is yes, simplify.

# 23. Retrofit method

Improve an existing surface by:

PRESERVE  
→ DIAGNOSE  
→ CLASSIFY  
→ REPAIR  
→ WITNESS  
→ GENERALIZE ONLY IF EARNED

Retrofit layers:

R0 COPY  
R1 VISUAL HIERARCHY  
R2 AFFORDANCE  
R3 ATMOSPHERE / THEME  
R4 MOTION / TEMPORAL RHYTHM  
R5 CONTINUITY  
R6 STRUCTURE  
R7 PHENOMENOLOGICAL MODEL

Escalate only when the lower layer cannot solve the problem.

For each element:
PRESERVE · REFINE · TRANSFORM · REMOVE · QUARANTINE

# 24. Retrofit success

Judge across eight dimensions:

S1 ORIENTATION  
S2 PRESENCE  
S3 CONTINUITY  
S4 AGENCY  
S5 TRUST  
S6 ACCESSIBILITY  
S7 NATIVE CHARACTER  
S8 ORGANISM COHERENCE

Standing:
REGRESSED · UNCHANGED · IMPROVED · STRONGLY IMPROVED · UNKNOWN

Acceptance:
- no regression in sovereignty, truth, accessibility, agency or continuity;
- at least two material improvements;
- native character does not regress;
- organism coherence improves or remains intentionally unchanged.

Do not substitute session duration, clicks or engagement for experiential success.

---

# 25. From retrofit to canon

LOCAL REPAIR  
→ repeated success  
→ PATTERN  
→ DEFAULT  
→ INVARIANT only when load-bearing

PATTERN:
presumptive evidence includes success on at least 2 materially different surfaces; promotion remains a judgment about transferability, not a count.

DEFAULT:
presumptive evidence includes success across at least 3 materially different surface families plus increased organism coherence; promotion is never automatic.

INVARIANT:
only when violation predictably harms sovereignty, truth, accessibility, agency, relational integrity, continuity, consent or safety.

Promotion is not permanence.

---

# 26. Canon review ritual

Trigger when:
- 3 accepted deviations accumulate;
- 2 surface families reject a pattern/default for legitimate reasons;
- accessibility repeatedly conflicts;
- new capability changes design possibility;
- founder/member witness says the organism feels constrained/dead/fragmented;
- a retrofit repeatedly succeeds beyond local scope;
- recurring failure reveals a missing invariant.

Ritual:

**GATHER** — rule, rationale, deviations, evidence.  
**REMEMBER** — what problem was it solving?  
**WITNESS** — inspect lived evidence.  
**DIFFERENTIATE** — principle vs implementation vs taste vs exception.  
**TEST THE ORGANISM** — coherence or sameness?  
**ADJUDICATE** — strengthen / keep / narrow / split / localize / promote / demote / supersede / retire / hold.  
**RECORD DELTA** — old/new standing, evidence, scope, migration, rollback.  
**PROPAGATE** — only to inheriting layers.  
**RETURN TO EXPERIENCE** — re-witness after the change.

Canon is stable enough to orient and porous enough to learn.

# 27. Accessibility

Accessibility is invariant.

Every meaningful surface witnesses:
- contrast;
- focus;
- keyboard/touch;
- text zoom;
- readable minimum type;
- reduced motion;
- non-color state cues;
- screen-reader semantics;
- theme parity.

Spatial relation requires nonvisual structural equivalents.

---

# 28. Responsive law

Mobile is not compressed desktop.

Each family defines its essential mobile act.

Preserve context before preserving layout.

Examples:
- Writer: capture · read · revise · return.
- House: orient · enter · return.
- Living Field: focus · change perspective · recover whole.
- Relationships: bring close · enter · return.

---

# 29. Experiment protocol

PROPOSAL  
→ ISOLATED PROTOTYPE  
→ DESKTOP / MOBILE / ACCESSIBILITY WITNESS  
→ EXPERIENCE REVIEW  
→ ADMIT AS LOCAL / PATTERN / DEFAULT / INVARIANT  
or  
→ RETIRE

Experiments may violate DEFAULT/PATTERN rules.
They may not violate INVARIANTS.

# 30. External inspiration

For every borrowed principle record:
- source;
- exact principle;
- why it helps;
- Soullab-native translation;
- what was deliberately not copied.

External tools advise.
Soullab decides.

---

# 31. Final organism test

Before shipping ask:

- Is the member still the subject?
- Does this point toward life rather than platform?
- Is the experience truthful?
- Does authorship remain legible?
- Does return preserve identity through change?
- Is action obvious and reversible where needed?
- Does the place feel more like itself?
- Does it still feel part of the same organism?
- Is sensory intensity earned?
- Does accessibility hold?
- What could be removed?

If the interface eclipses the member's lived experience, inspect again. A threshold or artistic surface may be memorable; memorability itself is not the defect.
