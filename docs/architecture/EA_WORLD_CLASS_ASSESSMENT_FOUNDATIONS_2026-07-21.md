## 1. Purpose

**Organizing doctrine:** Profiles answer *"Who am I?"* Reflections answer *"What is happening, and what might help?"*

The experience exists to help a member move through one arc, repeatedly, over time:

```text
Entrance:  "Help me understand what is happening in my life right now."
Exit:      "I understand my season, what may be asking to grow, and one meaningful next step."
Return:    "I want to come back later and see what changed."
```

**Member benefit:** clarity about the present season; language for what is moving (especially in transition, where accomplished people most lack it — Bridges 1980/2004; Pennebaker's narrative-construction findings); a self-chosen capacity to work with; one member-sized experiment; and, over returns, recognition of their own movement. The felt exit: *"I feel seen. I understand my season better. I have one thing I can try."*

**Practitioner benefit** (later, consent-gated): when a member chooses to share, the practitioner receives the member's own words and chosen focus — never a computed category. Nothing practitioner-visible is system-authored about the person.

**Platform benefit:** the reflection becomes the natural doorway from "arriving with something unformed" into the existing rooms (practices, book, Session Room, Now What?) — an entrance that teaches the platform's actual ontology (accompaniment, not evaluation) on first contact.

**Wisdom Field benefit** (gated, §9): a consent-based pathway by which distilled member recognitions may eventually become process-indexed collective wisdom ("people moving through rebuilding often discover…") — never trait-indexed ("people like you…").

## 2. Non-Purpose

The experience does not claim to measure or determine:

- **Personality** — no types, no dimensions, no scores. (The typing tradition fails its own field's standards: 39–76% of MBTI retakers change type within weeks — Pittenger 2005; no factor analysis recovers the Enneagram's nine types — Hook et al. 2021.)
- **Fixed identity** — the member alone names identity; the system witnesses (E0.1; authorship grammar).
- **Diagnosis or pathology** — nothing clinical, nothing about what is "wrong."
- **Elemental essence** — no dominant element, no elemental balance, no "your elemental nature."
- **Spiritual attainment or developmental rank** — later developmental stages are not happier, better, or higher-status (Cook-Greuter 2013); rank has no empirical warrant and known abuse patterns.
- **Prediction** — nothing about what the member will do or become.

And it refuses the quieter versions: no hidden scoring presented as neutral reflection (title must match mechanism — SAMHSA transparency principle); no repeated states silently compiled into traits (a statistical category error per Molenaar's ergodicity work, not merely a policy); no norms or comparisons to others (there are no norms, and there should be none — movement is measured only against the member's own baseline).

## 3. Construct Map

Constructs the experience may legitimately explore. "Belongs in v1" = the smallest prototype (§11).

| Construct | Definition | Source of data | Misunderstanding risk | v1? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Current state / season** | What is happening now; the member's own name for this period | Self-reported, member's words preserved verbatim | Low — but never system-labeled; "season" has scholarly grounding (Levinson 1978) and better manners than "stage" (timing/weather/return, not altitude) | **Yes** |
| **Developmental tension** | What life seems to be asking; what is ending / emerging / recurring | Self-reported | Medium — must not be reframed as deficiency; endings are grief, not problems (Kegan; Bridges) | **Yes** |

| **Experiment underway / next step** | One member-sized, member-named action | Member-authored; system asks only for the if-then (implementation intentions, d=.65 — Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) | Low | **Yes** |
| **Movement since last reflection** | Change the member recognizes between occasions | Member-recognized, scaffolded by their own record ("the record asks, it never concludes" — PCOMS practice) | Medium — must never aggregate into trait claims | **Yes (return only)** |
| **Recurring themes** | Patterns across occasions | Initially member-noticed; system-noticed only later and only under separate longitudinal consent | High — this is where pattern quietly becomes profile | Later, consent-gated |
| **Barriers** | What obstructs the chosen focus | Self-reported | Medium — deficit framing risk | Later |
| **Life context** | Situation surrounding the season | Self-reported, minimal | Medium — extraction risk; ask for meaning, never narrative detail of difficulty (TIP-57) | Minimal in v1 |
| **Readiness** | Appetite for action vs. rest | Self-reported | Medium — "not ready" must be fully honored (neutral zone is the work, not a failure) | Implicit in v1 |
| **Available capacities** | Strengths presently accessible | Self-reported only | High if inferred | Later |

**Excluded as constructs:** elemental identity, elemental balance, dominance/deficiency, developmental level, personality dimensions — refused in §2.

The old instrument measured (poorly) exactly the constructs this map excludes, and none of the ones it includes. Its scoring engine (pure tally, no tie-breaking — ties silently resolve by code order, favoring Fire; Aether tallied as an ordinary fifth) is not salvageable even on its own terms. **Salvage inventory that does carry forward:** the practices library (25 practices with steps, durations, categories — re-indexable under capacities); the shadow-awareness lines ("Aether integrates; it doesn't bypass" — the strongest writing in the set); the Shadow Work Integration protocol; several reflection prompts; the element→book-chapter mapping (for the teaching pathway, §7).

## 4. Model Options

| Model | Ease of understanding | Member agency | Emotional burden | Interpretive risk | Longitudinal support | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. One-time questionnaire** | High | Low | Low | **High** — form + result = quiz, whatever the copy says | None | This is the gated pattern; also dies at "read result, leave forever" |
| **B. Guided conversation with MAIA** | High | **Highest** | Variable — open conversation about grief can run past safe depth without structure | Medium — conversational drift can slide into interpretation | Weak unless paired with structure | Best accompaniment; hardest to test, highest variance, highest burden |
| **C. Hybrid: structured reflection held in MAIA's presence** | High | High | **Controlled** — structure paces and titrates (trauma-informed); skips are built in | Low — the container is fixed, the meaning is member-authored | Good | The MI evidence's shape: system structures the container; member authors content, naming, verdict (Magill 2014/2019) |
| **D. Longitudinal check-in** | High | High | Low per occasion (≤4–5 items — ORS at 86% sustained utilization vs. 25% for the 45-item OQ) | Low | **This is the point** | Not a separate model — the architecture the others live inside |

**Recommendation: C inside D, staged.** A brief structured reflection (5–7 plain-language questions) held inside MAIA's relational presence — MAIA opens, reflects mid-arc in the member's own words, and offers capacities at the end via elicit-provide-elicit — with the longitudinal return designed in from the first occasion (one scheduled return is part of v1, not a v2 feature). Model B's fuller conversational mode is a natural later doorway ("would you rather just talk this through with MAIA?") once the structured spine is proven. Model A is rejected outright.

This composition satisfies the accompaniment requirement ("someone understands," not "reflection engine") without conversation's testing variance: the *structure* is the safety architecture; the *presence* is MAIA's.

## 5. Question-Design Doctrine

The arc every occasion moves through (inherited, evidence-annotated):

```text
experience → attention → meaning → choice → experiment → recognition
```

**Rules, each with its evidentiary basis:**

1. **Ordinary language; one idea per item** (Haladyna item-writing standards; the platform's own doctrine that elemental language sits behind plain language). ~6th–8th grade reading level. No jargon, no framework vocabulary.
2. **State, not trait.** Every item asks about *now / lately / this season* — never "what are you like." (The gated instrument's 15-for-15 failure on this is the cautionary example.)
3. **Answerable from lived experience** — "what keeps returning?" not "what is your pattern-recognition style?"
4. **Open and evocative; never advice in a question.** "Have you considered…?" is the righting reflex wearing a question mark; it produces sustain talk, which predicts worse outcomes (Miller & Rollnick; Magill).
5. **Temporal questions over trait questions:** what is ending · what is emerging · what keeps returning · what feels unfinished (Bridges; Mezirow). Endings before beginnings — growth is also bereavement (Kegan).
6. **Address the situation, never the identity** — externalization as grammar: "when the doubt shows up…" never "your anxiety" (White & Epston).
7. **Every item skippable + "I'd rather not say" as an honored answer** — never re-asked, never penalized, never inferred from (SAMHSA; skip-is-not-a-signal).
8. **No detail extraction:** ask about present meaning of a difficulty, never for its narrative (TIP-57).
9. **Cultural humility:** the member's vocabulary is preserved verbatim and used back; free-text is never translated into framework categories (Tervalon & Murray-García; Invariant 14; measurement-invariance evidence that "self"/"growth"/"season" don't carry universal meaning).
10. **Useful twice:** each item should make sense to answer again in a month, so the record can later show movement.

**Candidate question intents for v1** (intents, not production copy — drawn from Kelly's clusters): what is happening / most alive right now · what has been difficult *(skippable, meaning-level)* · what keeps returning · what may be ending, what may be emerging · what feels most important now · what would help. The evaluation rubric for every drafted item: *experiential? reversible? increases agency? allows refusal? invites experimentation? useful longitudinally?* — plus the governing test: **does this question help someone understand themselves, or try to tell them who they are?**

## 6. Results Experience

Not a verdict — a reflection back, with authorship visible at every line. The result distinguishes, explicitly and in order:

1. **What the member said** — their words, quoted, primary.
2. **What MAIA noticed** — one or two tentative observations, clearly system-authored, provenance-linked ("you mentioned endings three times"), contestable and deletable (interpretive-transparency standard: explanation without correctability is insufficient).
3. **What remains uncertain** — named, not hidden. Uncertainty honored is trust built; the neutral zone is legitimate ("this fog is the work, not a failure").
4. **What may be worth exploring** — 3–5 capacities offered via the MI grammar (below).
5. **What the member chooses** — or declines; refusal ends the offer cleanly.
6. **What happens next** — the member's experiment, in their words, with an if-then; and the scheduled return.

**The lens-offering grammar (elicit-provide-elicit, verbatim design target):**

```text
ELICIT      "What do you make of what you've written?"
PERMISSION  "There's a way of looking at this I could offer — useful, or would you rather keep your own read?"
PROVIDE     "Some people in seasons like this find courage is what's asking to grow; others find it's grief that needs room. It may not fit."
ELICIT      "What lands? What would you call it?"
RATIFY      — the member's wording becomes the working term; unclaimed offers are discarded.
```

**Member controls, all first-class:** confirm · revise · reject · defer ("not now") · **refuse elemental language entirely** (the experience remains whole through plain-language capacities; refusal is remembered and honored — no quiet re-offering) · view the whole book/library regardless of any chosen focus · choose a different doorway (Session Room, practices, just talk to MAIA).

**Never present:** percentages, scores, bars, types, dominant/secondary anything, strengths/shadow verdicts, assigned paths. (Numbers imply measurement authority the instrument has not earned; scores launder interpretation — psychometric ethics brief, "do nots" 1–5.)

## 7. Elemental Role

Evaluated against the options in the directive:

- **Capacities: YES — as the organizing backend.** Courage, grief-capacity, discernment, boundaries, creativity, grounding, trust, surrender are the member-facing vocabulary; elements organize the capacity/practice library beneath (courage→Fire, grief→Water, grounding→Earth, articulation→Air). MAIA and the content system know the mapping; the member need never encounter it.
- **Teaching pathways: YES — member-pulled.** The book is authored teaching (author→reader, a legitimate relationship the member chooses); the element→chapter mapping carries forward. A member who loves the elemental language can go as deep as they wish — *offered*, per the offered/imposed criteria (one lens among several; member vocabulary preserved; "doesn't fit" valid).
- **Lenses/narrative metaphors: YES, on request** — "Can we look at this through Water?" is always honorable (member-pulled).
- **Phases: LATER** — Spiralogic phase language stays behind its own gates (Spiralogic Profile is S5-gated).
- **Scored: NO. Never.** Not visible, and in v1 not internal either — the title must match the mechanism, and v1's mechanism is honest: there is nothing to score, because nothing is computed about the person.

**Aether:** excluded from the reflection entirely — from capacity lists, from any offered lens set, from anything selectable. Grounds: E0.7 (nonparallel, unresolved ontology), canon ("not worked through doing"), and the freeze on integration surfaces. Aether remains in the **book structure only** (its chapters stay reachable through the teaching pathway) until the E0.7 reconciliation and the coherence-freeze lift — at which point its candidate role is the *integration phase of long-term return* ("how do the elements dance together in your life?" — one of the salvaged prompts), not a sixth capacity.

## 8. Developmental Pathway

```text
current experience → chosen focus → teaching or practice → real-life experiment → return → recognition of change → (eventually) contribution
```

- **Immediately:** the reflection ends with a chosen capacity (or none), one member-named experiment with an if-then, and a scheduled return the member sets (2–4 weeks default; member-adjustable). The chosen capacity unlocks its practice cluster and book pathway — doors, not assignments.
- **After several days:** nothing pushes. If the member opted into return reminders, one gentle, amnesty-toned invitation near the chosen date. No streaks, no chains, no guilt copy; gaps are data about life, not failures. *Celebrate the return, never the chain.*
- **At return (the distinctive capability):** their previous words are shown back — "last time you said…" — and the record asks, it never concludes: *"Reading this now — does anything look different?"* Movement is member-recognized, in their frame, against their own baseline only (PCOMS practice; RCI logic without the arithmetic). The scaffolded look-back matters: insight does not fall out of a chart unaided.
- **Across repeated reflections:** the member's archive accrues as *their* record — entries verbatim, member-deletable, exportable. Any aggregation ("you've mentioned rebuilding in three seasons") is member-initiated, provenance-linked, and revisable. **States expire; traits are never minted.**
- **Cadence envelope** (evidence-set): ≤4–7 items or one short free-write per occasion; weekly ceiling, monthly floor for sustained horizons; member-chosen short intensives allowed but time-boxed.

## 9. Wisdom Field Contribution

**Design (gated — see below).** The field is indexed by **process, never person**: transformation patterns ("people moving through rebuilding often discover…"), never cohorts of types ("people like you…"). This is the structural property that makes the field constitutionally sound by construction: process-indexing requires knowing only what someone is moving through, never what they are.

**Data classes, strictly separated:** private member record (default; only class that exists in v1) → personal longitudinal insight (member's own look-backs) → practitioner-visible (member-shared, explicit, revocable) → anonymized pattern (does not exist until the gates open) → member-authored contribution → collective wisdom statement.

**The only legitimate movement into the field:**

```text
lived episode → reflection → member recognition → member-authored distillation
→ explicit contribution consent (per-contribution, revocable) → de-identified field learning
```

The system never converts private responses into collective claims — not statistically, not "in aggregate," not ever. The member's *distillation* (their sentence, in their words) is the contribution unit; raw answers never leave the private record.

**Gates (standing):** S5 provenance constitution · gold-reflection freeze · offering-act provenance question. Stage-6 contribution is designed-for here and **built never** until those open, in that order. Consent baseline regardless: longitudinal pattern-noticing is separately opt-in from single-session use — *consent to answer once is not consent to be tracked over time.*

## 10. Validation Roadmap

Claims ladder — what may be said at each rung, nothing earlier:

| Rung | Work required | Claims earned |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (now) | — | "A structured reflection. Prompts, not results." Nothing more. |
| 1 | Written construct definitions; expert review (developmental + trauma-informed + cultural) of item bank | "Expert-reviewed" |
| 2 | Cognitive interviews, 2+ rounds, 5–15 people/round (think-alouds: does "season" mean to them what we assume?) | "Comprehension-tested" |
| 3 | Usability + emotional-safety pilot (felt-experience interviews; completion; skip patterns; the §11 criteria) | "Piloted; members report X" (verbatim quotes, no aggregates implied as measures) |
| 4 | If instrument-like elements emerge: item statistics, internal structure, test-retest on anything stable enough to warrant it (n≈100–300 → 300+) | Reliability language, narrowly |
| 5 | Convergent/discriminant evidence; DIF/invariance across subgroups; longitudinal outcome evaluation (does it actually help over months?) | Validity language for specific interpretations |
| 6 | Consequence monitoring (Standards: does use produce the claimed good and no harm — including "do members ever feel typed?") | Public claims of benefit |

Note: because v1 computes nothing about the person, rungs 4–5 apply only if and when the design later introduces anything score-like. The felt-accuracy goal ("that's exactly what I'm experiencing") is pursued through rungs 2–3, which require no psychometrics — only listening.

## 11. Prototype Recommendation

**Smallest credible prototype** (per the ruled spec, confirmed by the burden evidence):

- **5–7 questions**, plain language, per §5 intents; every item skippable; one optional short free-write.
- **3–5 offered capacities** (plain-language; elements backstage only), via the elicit-provide-elicit grammar; refuse/defer first-class.
- **One member-named experiment** + if-then.
- **One scheduled return** (member-set, 2–4 week default) that shows their words back and asks the PCOMS question.
- **No scores (visible or internal) · no percentages · no elemental identity · no Aether · no wisdom-field contribution · no practitioner visibility.**
- Completion burden: under 10 minutes first occasion; under 5 at return.
- Surface: could run as paper/conversation first (founder-guided walkthroughs) before any interactive build — the hypothesis is testable without code.

**Core hypothesis:** *Does the experience help a member understand their present situation more clearly and choose a meaningful next step?*

**Evidence to collect:** felt-exit interviews (target sentence: "that helped me understand my life"); whether a capacity was chosen, revised, refused (refusals are healthy data, not failures); whether an experiment was named in the member's own words; return rate and what returning members recognize; cognitive-interview transcripts on every item; skip patterns.

**What would falsify the concept:** members leave without increased clarity (exit interviews flat); nobody chooses to return; capacities consistently feel imposed rather than recognized; members ask "so what type am I?" anyway (the experience still reads as a quiz — an aesthetics or grammar failure); the questions read as therapy-adjacent intrusion rather than reflection (safety failure); completion collapses (burden failure).

## 12. Decision Gates (for Kelly — nothing proceeds past this section without rulings)

1. **Assessment or guided reflection?** Recommendation: guided reflection (Model C-inside-D). "Assessment" returns only if earned at rung 4+.
2. **One-time or longitudinal?** Recommendation: longitudinal from v1 — the scheduled return is part of the smallest prototype, not an add-on.
3. **Elemental lens introduced when?** Recommendation: never system-initiated in v1. Elements live backstage (capacity indexing) and in the member-pulled book pathway. Revisit after real member evidence.
4. **Member chooses lens before or after system observation?** Recommendation: member's own naming comes first, always; MAIA's noticing is offered after, with permission, per the MI grammar.
5. **Are numerical scores visible?** Recommendation: no — and none internal in v1 either (title matches mechanism).
6. **Is Aether included?** Recommendation: no (book structure only), pending E0.7 reconciliation + freeze lift.
7. **What data is retained?** Recommendation: member's entries verbatim, member-owned (view/export/delete); no derived labels; longitudinal noticing separately opt-in; retention disclosed in plain language before the first sensitive question.
8. **What can contribute to the Wisdom Field?** Recommendation: nothing in v1; the §9 pathway is the design-for; gates are S5 → gold-reflection → offering-act.
9. **Validation standard before launch?** Recommendation: rungs 1–3 (expert review, cognitive interviews, safety/usability pilot) before any general member availability; founder/invited walks may precede that under the existing five-walks evidence practice.

## 13. Experience & Aesthetic Doctrine

**The aesthetics teach the ontology.** The gated screen taught `quiz → score → type` before a word was read (purple/pink gradients, sparkle, element chips, percentage bars). The reflection must teach `reflection → understanding → growth → return` the same way — pre-verbally.

- **Visual foundation:** the canonical Soullab dark navy (already ruled: teal retired, navy canonical) — deep midnight navy, subtle indigo, soft obsidian; gold/pearl accents; muted elemental tones only where a chosen pathway warrants them (ember copper, ocean teal, moss earth, silver-blue — never candy-colored chips). Spacious typography, low visual noise. Register: *MAIA Sanctuary + premium contemplative field* — entering another room in the same house as Session Room and Sanctuary.
- **The Holoflower** (canonical PNG asset) replaces any sparkle/quiz iconography — an ontological symbol (relationship, movement, integration, emergence, wholeness), not branding.
- **Entrance:** Holoflower → *"Reflect on Your Present Season"*-register title → one contemplative sentence → *Begin Reflection*. No element imagery, no item counts, no duration promises, no "discover your…" anywhere. The de-centered quiz cues (15 questions · 5 minutes · balance) do not return.
- **In-flow:** one question at a time; visible skip and pause; progress felt as movement through a reflection, not completion of a form; exits that hold state without guilt.
- **Result:** reads as a page of accompaniment — the member's words given back with care — not a dashboard. Nothing that renders as chart, meter, or badge.
- **Emotional test (the doctrine's acceptance criterion):** *"I feel understood and curious about myself"* — never *"I wonder what type I am."* And beneath it the accompaniment requirement: it must feel like *someone understands*, not like a reflection engine ran.
