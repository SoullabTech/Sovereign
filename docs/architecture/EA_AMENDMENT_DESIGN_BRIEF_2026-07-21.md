## The ambition

Not a rewrite of the assessment. Not a personality test, spiritual typology, elemental quiz, or psychometric profile. **A world-class developmental reflection experience** — something that does not really exist yet — that helps people:

1. Understand what is happening in their lives.
2. Experiment intentionally.
3. Recognize patterns over time.
4. Become wiser.
5. Contribute that wisdom back to humanity.

**The core purpose inversion:** the experience answers *"What would most help this person right now?"* — never *"Who is this person?"*

**What world-class assessments actually do:** increase self-awareness, increase agency, provide language, generate action, feel profoundly accurate, and remain useful months later. They do not primarily classify. The felt test: *"Oh wow… that's exactly what I'm experiencing"* — never *"Interesting. I'm apparently a Fire type."*

## The six-layer architecture

| Layer | Question | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Current experience** | What is happening? | "What feels most alive right now? What has been difficult? What keeps returning? What feels unfinished? What are you longing for?" |
| **2. Developmental tension** | What is life asking? | "What challenge keeps appearing? What capacity are you being asked to grow? What feels necessary but uncomfortable?" |
| **3. Current state** | What season are you in? | rebuilding · grieving · creating · simplifying · becoming courageous · integrating · resting · searching — ordinary language, immediately understandable |
| **4. Potential lenses** | *Only now* might elements appear | Never "You ARE Fire." Instead: "Fire themes may be useful to explore" / "Many people in seasons like yours find courage, action, and creativity especially important" |
| **5. Experiments** | What would you like to try? | "What conversation needs to happen? What practice might help?" — development always moves toward action |
| **6. Recognition** | What are you learning? | "What keeps surprising you? What patterns are emerging?" — possibly MAIA's superpower |

The experience arc: `Witnessing → Understanding → Possibility → Action → Recognition` — never `Questions → Score → Label`.

## What could make it truly world-class

1. **Developmental, not typological** — very few systems do this.
2. **Longitudinal** — most assessments are one-time; MAIA can ask *"What changed since last month?"*
3. **Episodic** — connected to actual life (a divorce, launching a company, caring for a parent, finding purpose).
4. **Wisdom contribution** — almost nobody does this: *"Many people moving through rebuilding discovered…"* / *"Many people entering purpose first experience confusion."*

## A possible experience (eight parts)

1. Tell me about your life right now.
2. What feels most important?
3. What challenge keeps returning?
4. What might life be asking from you?
5. Here are several lenses that may be useful: courage · grounding · grief · creativity · boundaries · meaning. *(Note: plain-language lenses first — elements sit behind them, per the question-design doctrine.)*
6. Which resonate? **Member chooses.**
7. Explore — stories, practices, book chapters, community wisdom.
8. What are you learning? → becomes living wisdom.

## The elements' place

Not identities. Not traits. **Developmental capacities:**

- **Fire** — initiation, courage, action
- **Water** — feeling, grief, relationship
- **Earth** — embodiment, stability, stewardship
- **Air** — meaning, articulation, perspective
- **Aether** — integration, mystery, coherence

(Consistent with the E0 capability inventory and native to Spiralogic. Aether's placement here remains subject to E0.7 — integration function, never a fifth selectable option.)

## The product vision sentence

> **A developmental companion that helps people understand what season they are in, what capacities life may be asking of them, and what experiments may support their becoming.**

And the guiding sentence:

> **MAIA does not tell people who they are. MAIA helps people understand where they are, what may be emerging, and what could help them grow.**

The assessment stops being a quiz. It becomes **an initiation into developmental self-understanding and collective human wisdom** — helping people answer what almost no system helps with: *What is happening to me? What is life asking of me? How do I move well through this season? What have others learned walking a similar path?*

2. **Plain language before elemental language.**

## Kelly's vision refinements (second pass on the brief — recorded, design still dormant)

**Entrance / exit framing:**
- Entrance: *"Help me understand what is happening in my life right now."*
- Exit: *"I understand my season, what may be asking to grow, and one meaningful next step."*

**Five settled non-purposes:** ❌ not identity ("You are Fire") · ❌ not personality ("Here is your type") · ❌ not pathology ("Here is what is wrong") · ❌ not optimization ("Here is how to perform better") · ✅ **development** ("Here is what may be unfolding").

**Layer 4 renamed: "Capacities emerging" (was "potential lenses").** *What may be wanting to grow?* Lenses can feel intellectual; capacities feel developmental. Examples: courage · grief capacity · discernment · trust · boundaries · creativity · surrender. **Elements sit quietly beneath capacities.** The arc becomes: `Experience → Meaning → Season → Emerging Capacity → Experiment → Recognition`.

**The actual world-class differentiator is RETURN, not the elements.** Almost every assessment dies at `take → read result → leave forever`. MAIA becomes `reflect → act → live → return → recognize change → distill wisdom`. The longitudinal witness: *Month 1: "I am rebuilding after my divorce" → Month 2: "I notice I am becoming more courageous" → Month 4: "I keep choosing myself" → Month 8: "I think I understand what this season was teaching me."* Nobody else has this.

**The hidden superpower:** *"What have others learned walking a similar path?"* — never "people like you…" (trait cohort) but **"people moving through rebuilding…" (process cohort)**. Wisdom indexed by transformation, not by type.

**The accompaniment requirement (push harder here):** the brief said "understand what is happening"; add **"feel accompanied while moving through it."** Soullab has always been relational. The experience must not feel like a *reflection engine*; it must feel like *someone understands* — even when the interaction is structured. This is a design requirement, not copy.

**Product vision (current form):**

> MAIA is a developmental companion that helps people understand what season of life they are in, what capacities may be emerging, what experiments may support their growth, and what wisdom may be revealed through living it.

**Prototype success criterion (felt, not metric):** real people consistently leaving saying *"That helped me understand my life."* The prototype delivers `clarity → agency → movement`, nothing else.

## Experience & Aesthetic Doctrine (Kelly — REQUIRED section of the foundations paper)

**The aesthetics themselves teach the ontology.** The current screen teaches `quiz → score → type` (purple/pink gradients, sparkle icon, gamified element chips, "Discover Your Element," BuzzFeed-style hierarchy) — even with fixed language, a personality-quiz look will be read as a personality quiz. The redesign must teach `reflection → understanding → growth → return`.

**Visual direction:** dark navy foundation (deep midnight navy · subtle indigo · soft obsidian) with gold/pearl accents; muted, sophisticated elemental colors used only when needed (ember copper · ocean teal · moss earth · air silver-blue · aether luminous gold-violet — never 🟠🔵🟢); spacious typography, low visual noise. Register: *MAIA Sanctuary + premium executive coaching + sacred contemplative field* — "Apple + Headspace + depth-psychology institute + luxury retreat," never wellness-app.

**The Holoflower replaces the sparkle icon** — it communicates movement, relationship, integration, and Spiralogic ontology where the sparkle communicates "magic personality test."

**Entrance register:** never "Discover Your Element." Instead: *"Reflect on Your Present Season"* / *"Begin Your Reflection"* / *"A Developmental Reflection"* — with contemplative framing copy ("…designed to help you notice what is alive in your life right now, what capacities may be emerging, and what experiments may support your next steps"). Hierarchy: Holoflower → title → brief contemplative copy → Begin Reflection. Elements need not appear at the entrance at all. De-center "15 questions · 5 minutes · discover your balance" (quiz cues); center reflection, understanding, experimentation, return.

**Ten design principles (Kelly, verbatim intent):** (1) Soullab dark-navy language replaces the quiz aesthetic; (2) official Holoflower replaces sparkle; (3) remove all personality-test/gamification/typology visual cues; (4) contemplative, premium, sacred atmosphere; (5) design for witnessing/reflection/possibility/action/return; (6) entering MAIA's field, not taking an assessment; (7) elements only when useful, never as identity markers; (8) full Soullab color system and typography; (9) spaciousness, calm, trust, depth, dignity; (10) emotional response = *"I feel understood and curious"*, never *"I wonder what type I am."*

**Repo alignment notes (recorded for the design phase):** the navy direction matches the platform's already-canonical auth-surface ruling (teal retired, navy canonical); the Holoflower's canonical room asset is the PNG, not an SVG recreation — use the official asset.

**The organizing doctrine (add to foundations paper §1):**

> **Profiles answer: "Who am I?" Reflections answer: "What is happening, and what might help?"**

The product arc is `Mirror → Meaning → Movement → Experiment → Recognition → Return`, never `Assessment → Classification → Recommendation`.

**Scope note:** this doctrine governs the *design phase*. UI redesign **implementation** remains unauthorized along with prototype building, schema changes, and assessment reactivation — all wait behind design and review.
