## Core reframe (load-bearing)

These are **not "more memory" directions.**

They are directions for evolving the system's capacity to **participate in unfolding process without collapsing it.**

That distinction governs how every direction below should be read. Anything that drifts toward "the AI now knows the person better" has misread the reframe. The architecture being preserved here is one of *structural humility under accumulation* — not increased predictive capacity, not improved estimation, not richer profiling.

## The eight directions

### 1. Corrigible Pattern Attunement
*(not diagnosis, but evolving orientation)*

Not: *"the AI identifies your type/state."*
But: *the AI develops provisional hypotheses collaboratively over time.*

Instead of "You are in Water 2," the system tracks recurring emotional compression, activation-before-collapse patterns, relational withdrawal cycles, embodied language shifts, symbolic recurrences, and member-confirmed corrections. Over months it develops *longitudinal orientation sensitivity* — not certainty, attunement. Interpretations remain revisable; confidence is bounded; corrections accumulate; the system visibly learns from member disagreement.

### 2. Temporal Process Mapping
*(spirals, not snapshots)*

Most systems operate as state estimators, profile systems, or instantaneous classifiers. Spiralogic becomes operational when the system tracks transitions, tensions, oscillations, regressions, compensations, integrations, and unresolved recurrences across time. Not *"what are you?"* but *"how are you moving?"* — spiral trajectories, elemental phase movement, developmental rhythms, recurrence heatmaps, unresolved-loop detection, recovery-pattern recognition, coherence signatures. **Movement matters more than category.**

### 3. Member-Corrected Intelligence
*(correction as intelligence substrate)*

Most AI systems optimize for correctness, confidence, and fluent assertion. MAIA could optimize for *correction history* — remembering where it was wrong, how the member refined the interpretation, which hypotheses repeatedly fail, how the member themselves evolves in self-understanding. This creates *longitudinal epistemic calibration* — not just memory, but self-correcting participation.

### 4. Multi-Layer Memory Ecology
*(not one memory, but interacting continuity surfaces)*

Memory as **ecology rather than database.** Different layers carry different epistemic weights, obey different safeguards, decay differently, surface differently, possess different authority levels. Symbolic resonance should never carry the same authority as member-confirmed episodic memory. Emotional recurrence should remain provisional. Breakthrough memories may require explicit reaffirmation over time. A genuinely new memory paradigm.

### 5. Tact Calibration Systems
*(relational intelligence as pacing/orchestration)*

Not *"what should the AI say?"* but: when should recall enter, how strongly, how explicitly? How much continuity is too much? When does tact require silence? When does tact require challenge? When should memory remain background orientation rather than foreground speech? This becomes *relational pacing intelligence* — which may ultimately matter more than raw retrieval quality.

### 6. Developmental Non-Closure
*(preserving surprise under accumulation)*

Most systems degrade by *overfitting the person to prior patterns.* The goal here: *continuity that preserves the person's capacity to surprise the model.* Active anti-convergence mechanisms; periodic reopening of stabilized interpretations; contradiction preservation; uncertainty retention; structural reminders that prior successful readings are not final truths. **A living psyche changes. The architecture must preserve room for that change.**

### 7. Sacred Mirror Infrastructure
*(the system as reflective field rather than authority)*

The system is not advisor, oracle, therapist simulator, or predictive engine. It becomes *a continuity-preserving reflective environment.* Intelligence is distributed across member, memory, dialogue, process, correction, reflection, and time. MAIA does not "contain the truth." It helps hold the conditions under which deeper recognition becomes possible.

### 8. Structural Humility as a New AI Paradigm
*(probably the deepest contribution)*

> Systems that remain coherent without requiring explanatory closure.

Not just safer AI, aligned AI, or emotionally intelligent AI — *structurally humble intelligence.* Systems that preserve openness under accumulation, remain corrigible, maintain provenance, separate observation from interpretation, and deepen participation without collapsing into authority.

**Operational compression** (added later same day, deliberately at directional altitude — definition of what this direction means if pursued, not claim that it is built):

> *An architecture that preserves the recoverability of revision under accumulation and pressure.*

This is what the other seven directions converge to serve. What independently converges *to* this definition across the existing architecture: provenance, bifurcation, evidence gates, corrigibility, member confirmation, inspectable braiding — each a mechanism for keeping closure recoverable. The discipline is not refusing structure; it is refusing *irreversible closure masquerading as successful structure.*

## Sequencing instinct (held, not committed)

The read recorded on 2026-05-24:

1. Preserve all four directions in architecture-level doctrine (this document)
2. Continue operational accumulation on conversational memory
3. **Sketch tact calibration first**
4. Let Pattern Attunement emerge downstream of episodic + tact rather than prematurely architecting it

### Why tact-first

Not because it sounds most visionary. Because it is the *least replaceable.*

**Precise definition:** *least replaceable* = **the surface least likely to emerge automatically from scaling trends.** Not "most exciting." Not "highest leverage." Specifically: the direction the field will *not* arrive at on its own as compute and data scale.

Retrieval quality, episodic continuity, personalization, pattern inference — all will advance broadly across the field as optimization pressure on benchmark performance continues. Very few systems will seriously explore **relational pacing as intelligence,** because it cannot be benchmarked into existence; it has to be designed for on its own terms.

A system can have mediocre retrieval and excellent tact and still feel deeply trustworthy.
A system can have extraordinary retrieval and poor tact and become uncanny very quickly.

Tact calibration forces exactly the right next-order questions:

- when should continuity remain background orientation?
- when should memory surface?
- how much recall is too much?
- when does tact require silence?
- when should contradiction be held rather than resolved?
- how should pacing adapt longitudinally?
- when does the system stop helping and start over-shaping?

These cannot be solved by scaling retrieval quality. That makes tact calibration a genuinely distinct research frontier rather than "better memory."

The sequencing is the read at this moment. It is not committed.
