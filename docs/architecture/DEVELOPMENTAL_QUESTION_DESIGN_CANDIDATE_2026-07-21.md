## Three separate design problems (do not collapse them)

1. **What** questions should MAIA ask?
2. **How** should MAIA ask them?
3. **How does answering them contribute** to personal growth and the collective wisdom field?

## 1. Question principles (from coaching, developmental psychology, narrative therapy, motivational interviewing, adult development)

- **Avoid identity questions early.** "Which element are you?" creates premature closure. "What feels most alive for you right now?" does not. Development runs `experience → reflection → meaning → identity`, never `question → label → identity`. (This is the authorship grammar's developmental arc, restated as question design.)
- **Ask for lived experience first.** "When do you feel most energized?" / "What situations have been asking something from you lately?" — experience is universally understandable; frameworks are not.
- **Ask temporal questions.** Development is movement: *What is ending? What is emerging? What keeps returning? What challenge does life keep presenting?* Temporal questions out-develop trait questions.

## 2. Plain language over concept (the biggest MAIA risk is becoming too conceptual)

People understand: overwhelmed · grieving · excited · stuck · searching · creating · rebuilding.
People do not understand: elemental archetypal differentiation · developmental ontologies · orientation primitives.

**The elemental language sits behind ordinary language.** Example surface:

```text
"What feels most true today?"
□ I need grounding.
□ I need clarity.
□ I feel emotionally overwhelmed.
□ I feel called toward something new.
□ Something deeper is happening that I don't understand.
```

MAIA may internally notice patterns. The person simply feels understood.

> **Constitutional annotation (recorded, not new ruling):** "MAIA internally notices" is legitimate as *context* (E0.1) — but if answers to developmental questions **accumulate** into per-member pattern inference, that is memory formation and must ride the existing consent architecture (atoms model, no stealth memory, "asked, not watched"). Momentary noticing: clean. Silent longitudinal profiling from question answers: requires the same member consent as any other memory. The plain-language menu must not become an unconsented assessment by drip.

## 3. The five functions of a developmental question

- **A. Awareness** — "What keeps asking for your attention?"
- **B. Agency** — "What small choice is available to you?"
- **C. Meaning** — "Why might this matter?"
- **D. Coherence** — "Have you lived through something like this before?"
- **E. Recognition** — "What are you beginning to understand about yourself?"

Recognition may be the most important category — it is the member-authored end of the grammar.

## 4. The wisdom field stores patterns of becoming, not answers

Not: `Burnout = Fire imbalance.` (typology — refused)
But: *"Many people discover that exhaustion can hide grief, over-responsibility, or the need for new meaning."* (lived wisdom)

Field shape: **Episode → Experiments → Insights → Returning Patterns → What Helped.** Richer than typologies, and structurally incapable of typing anyone.

## 5. A possible developmental process (six stages)

```text
1. Witness       What is happening?
2. Meaning       What might life be asking?
3. Experiment    What would you like to try?
4. Recognition   What are you learning?
5. Integration   What now feels true?
6. Contribution  Would you like to offer this insight to the field?
```

Wisdom fields grow through `lived experience → distilled insight → offered gift`.

> **Constitutional annotation (recorded, not new ruling):** Stages 1–5 run inside the existing per-member consent architecture and are broadly consistent with the accompaniment model (witness-first, member-authored meaning, experimentation per E0.6's occasioned/chosen modes). **Stage 6 sits behind standing gates and cannot be designed yet:** member-contributed insight entering a collective field is exactly the territory of (a) the S5 provenance constitution (papers in review), (b) the gold-reflection freeze (frozen until S5 verified), and (c) the unresolved group-keep / offering-act provenance question that also gates Air's collective layer. The stage is recorded as the right *eventual* shape — contribution as a member-initiated gift, never extraction — but it opens only when those three gates open, in that order.

## 6. Evaluation rubric for every MAIA question

Is it experiential? · Is it reversible? · Does it increase agency? · Does it allow refusal? · Does it invite experimentation? · Does it produce insight others may learn from?

## The governing tests

For questions:

```text
Does this question help someone understand themselves,
or does it try to tell them who they are?
```

For the wisdom field:

```text
Does this answer become a label,
or does it become a piece of lived wisdom that may help others?
```

The vision sentence: *not a system that classifies people, but a field that helps people recognize themselves and contributes those recognitions to the collective wisdom of humanity.*
