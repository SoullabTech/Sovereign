# Session Room & Studio — Working Team Paper

**Status: CANDIDATE architecture — provisionally ruled in direction (Kelly, 2026-07-19 evening): production already embodies the distinction; PR #648 (Session Room → "Session Studio" rename) closed unmerged under this ruling. Final closure at the sitting.**

## Core Principle

The platform should never feel like software. It should feel like entering a living developmental environment where people remain in relationship with themselves, others, and what matters most.

MAIA is not the platform. MAIA is the host who knows the house.

## The Two Primary Spaces

### 1. Session Room — Purpose: Accompaniment

The Session Room exists to support human encounters. It is the place where a person is seen, heard, reflected, and remains in relationship across time.

**Guiding question:** *"What is happening here, right now?"*

**Core functions:**
- **Threshold** — arrival experience · consent and agreements · intention setting · selection of session container · entering relationship
- **Presence** — audio/video support · transcript capture · shared attention · real-time witnessing · MAIA quietly present
- **Witnessing** — mark moments · quotes and significant statements · emotional shifts · symbolic moments · important questions · recognition of meaningful experiences
- **Reflection** — Core lens · Spiralogic lens · Mentor lens · session synthesis · developmental understanding
- **Continuity** — previous sessions remembered · living questions carried forward · practices remembered · important themes preserved · longitudinal developmental tracking
- **Carry Forward** — commitments · experiments · practices · questions to continue living into

**Desired feeling:** calm · sacred · relational · simple · unhurried · safe · like sitting with a wise companion.
**Never:** technical · overwhelming · feature-heavy · like project management software · like filling out forms.

### 2. Studio — Purpose: Stewardship

The Studio exists to support practitioners in shaping and holding developmental work over time.

**Guiding question:** *"What is emerging across time?"*

**Core functions:**
- **Relationship Field** — individuals · groups · organizations · communities · developmental ecosystems
- **Observation** — longitudinal patterns · themes · developmental movement · practitioner observations · emerging hypotheses
- **Intelligence** — session synthesis · pattern recognition · developmental trajectories · cross-session insights
- **Creation** — programs · courses · methodologies · books · practices · teaching materials
- **Library** — resources · articles · recordings · PDFs · frameworks · rituals · wisdom collections
- **Practitioner Development** — personal reflections · supervision support · case consultation · method refinement · growth of the practitioner themselves

**Desired feeling:** spacious · creative · thoughtful · generative · organized · alive.
**Never:** corporate CRM software · administrative burden · data entry · a collection of disconnected tools.

## Hospitality Principle

A person should never need to know where features live. MAIA knows the house. The member simply remains in relationship. The practitioner simply follows what is emerging.

## Design Rule (constitutional test, Kelly 2026-07-19)

Before adding any feature, ask —
**Session Room:** *does this deepen accompaniment within a relationship?*
**Studio:** *does this deepen stewardship across relationships?*
If the answer is no, it probably does not belong.

## Architectural Summary

**Session Room** — the sacred encounter.
**Studio** — the stewardship of many sacred encounters across time.
**MAIA** — the host who knows the house and quietly carries continuity between them.

The morning's #648 rename never shipped — it was solving a problem production did not have — and is closed unmerged. Kelly: *"Not because it was wrong in intent. Because it was solving a problem that production apparently did not have."*
2. **The valuable insight inside #648 was never the rename** — it was *there are multiple arrival paths into accompaniment*. Re-homed as `docs/architecture/SESSION_ROOM_THRESHOLD_2026-07-19.md`.
3. **Containment hierarchy** (Kelly): *"Session Room sits inside Studio because accompaniment sits inside stewardship."* `Studio → Relationship → Session Room → Session`.
4. **The synthesis question is constitutional, not accidental.** The same synthesis artifact exists in both spaces read at different levels of abstraction: in the Session Room, authority flows toward the encounter (*what does this mean here?*); in the Studio, authority flows upward (*what patterns emerge across many encounters?*). Directly adjacent to the Constitutional Direction of Authority (Invariant 16): single encounter → relationship pattern → practitioner development, upward only.

6. **Sayability:** the paper's strength is that it names what is. Kelly: *"The architecture is already standing. The paper merely made it visible."*
