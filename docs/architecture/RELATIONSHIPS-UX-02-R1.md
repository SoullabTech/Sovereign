# RELATIONSHIPS-UX-02-R1 — Warm Relational Field Repair

**Status:** Bounded implementation candidate  
**Parent architecture:** `RELATIONSHIPS-UX-02 — Presence & Motion`  
**Scope:** visual/experiential repair only

## Why this repair exists

The production visual witness showed that the UX-02 architecture was structurally coherent but emotionally wrong.

The member encountered:
- a near-black field,
- low-contrast relational text,
- system abstractions before actual people,
- cards that read as records rather than presences,
- MAIA invitations that felt like controls rather than companionship.

The founder witness was explicit: the field felt **alien, impersonal, and too dark**.

This repair treats that as product evidence.

## Governing design laws

### Relational Warmth Law

The member must encounter the felt presence of the relationship before encountering the system's representation of it.

Human names, words, memories, questions, and occasions carry visual priority over abstractions, analytics, classifications, and inferred fields.

### Daylight Depth Law

Depth must not be represented primarily through darkness.

Relationship Space may hold grief, shadow, conflict, mystery, and complexity while remaining warm, legible, spacious, and alive.

### People Before System

Actual relationships lead the landing field.

System-generated holding structures remain available but are visually subordinate and explicitly framed as waiting material rather than people.

### Accessibility Is Hospitality

Readable contrast, clear hierarchy, calm motion, and discoverable invitation are part of relational welcome, not separate polish.

## Four visible repair targets

1. **Landing page warmth + invitation**
   - warm mineral/parchment field
   - visible `Bring someone into view` gesture
   - actual relationships first
   - system holding fields demoted

2. **Relationship cards as human presences**
   - warm surfaces with clear separation
   - readable relational note
   - restrained presence marker
   - no analytics or status chrome

3. **Relationship detail page warmth + readability**
   - warm ambient field across `Now / Story / Field`
   - member occasion treated as meaningful opening material
   - quiet check-in, timeline, and field depth remain readable
   - existing attentional architecture preserved

4. **Contained MAIA as companioning gesture**
   - voice/text invitations become warmer and more human
   - canonical `OracleConversation` remains the only MAIA authority
   - no new voice, memory, chatbot, or relationship runtime

## Explicit non-effects

This repair does **not** change:
- relationship schema,
- relationship APIs,
- relational ontology,
- memory authority,
- voice authority,
- turn-taking,
- MAIA seeding semantics,
- `Now / Story / Field` structure,
- consent,
- retention,
- deployment standing.

## Acceptance

PASS only when the production field feels:

**warm · welcoming · readable · elegant · human · relational · engaging without being busy**

The target member experience is:

> This feels like a place where relationships can actually be held.

Not:

> This is an interesting dark interface.
