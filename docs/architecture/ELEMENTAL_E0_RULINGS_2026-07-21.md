## E0.1 — The three-object grammar is APPROVED (canonical)

```text
Elemental context      What appears relevant in this moment.
Elemental orientation  A lens the member explicitly chooses and may revise or refuse.
Elemental identity     A claim about who the person is.
```

Authority structure: **context may be system-detected · orientation must be member-adopted ·
identity must never be system-imposed.**

MAIA may notice: "This conversation is carrying a strong Fire concern: decision and action."
MAIA may offer: "Would Fire be a useful lens here?"
Only the member may say: "Fire is a recurring signature of my life."

(This ruling ratifies the grammar refined across Kelly's three E0 review passes, including the
fourth term — signature, member-recognized over long evidence — recorded in the E0 doc §6b.1.)

## E0.2 — The Elemental Alchemy assessment crosses the boundary (live constitutional mismatch)

The live assessment's "elemental nature" / strengths / growth-edges language turns a computed
result into a **system-authored identity claim**. Member initiation does not solve the authorship
problem — a person may willingly take a quiz and still receive an imposed conclusion.

**Ruling**: the assessment may eventually become an orientation offering, but its present
identity-authoring grammar is **not constitutionally compatible** with the ratified model.
Legitimate future form: "Your responses made Water especially visible today. Would you like to
explore that lens?" — not "Water is your elemental nature."

No corrective implementation authorized; the surface is recorded as a **live constitutional
mismatch**, not merely a future risk.

## E0.3 — Soulprint requires a formal freeze

A commented-out mount is not governance — it is an accidental technical condition one edit away

**Ruling**: Soulprint's elemental identity presentation is formally classified:

```text
Dormant · Unratified · Must not be re-enabled without ontology and authorship review
```

## E0.4 — Chart aggregation is conditionally legitimate ONLY as context

Chart-derived elemental material remains legitimate only while **all** hold: it describes the
chart or current interpretive context, not the person's essential nature · it does not silently
become persistent member identity · it does not rank a dominant/deficient element as psychological
fact · member-facing elemental language remains offered and revisable · context-derived material
does not automatically enter the Living Profile · the member can decline the elemental frame
entirely.

"This chart contains a concentration of Air symbolism" — may be legitimate.
"You are dominantly Air" — is not.
Any aggregation that persists identity vocabulary or feeds identity claims **fails this ruling**.

## E0.5 — Rename identity-bearing runtime vocabulary (E1 ontology obligation)

`member_spiral_state.dominant_element` may perform contextual orchestration today, but its
vocabulary invites future misuse. No immediate schema migration required; the E1 ontology must
distinguish `active_elemental_context` / `elemental_signal` / `current_elemental_emphasis` /
`member_adopted_orientation` from `dominant_element` / `elemental_identity`.

**Naming is architectural pressure. Context machinery should not wear identity language
indefinitely.**

## E0.6 — The four development modes are APPROVED

```text
Assigned development     System decides what a person should develop.     REFUSED
Chosen development       Member names a capacity to strengthen.           MISSING AND NEEDED
Occasioned development   MAIA creates conditions where development may occur.  PARTIALLY PRESENT
Recognized development   Member notices and adopts evidence of change.    STRONGLY SUPPORTED
```

The likely center of any future elemental architecture: **a member-adopted, revisable, refusable
elemental orientation** — that object may be more foundational than five studios.

## E0.7 — Aether remains unresolved and nonparallel

Aether is **not approved** as a fifth signature studio or equivalent developmental capacity. The
repository holds at least three incompatible meanings (fifth parallel element · integrative field
among elements · transcendent/crown condition) which cannot be treated as interchangeable.

**Provisional direction**: investigate Aether first as *the integrative relation among elemental
capacities and the emergence of whole-pattern coherence* — not as a fifth menu destination.
Because the actual integration machinery is frozen, no Aether product architecture may proceed
before: the ontology is reconciled · the standing coherence freeze is explicitly lifted · evidence
establishes that members experience Aether as a distinct developmental need.
