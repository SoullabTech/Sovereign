# Portal Strategy: Same Room, More Depth
## Business Doctrine for the Personal Portal and Tier Model

**Date:** 2026-06-12
**Status:** Adopted — governs product, marketing, and sales framing for the tier model
**Origin:** Track A pre-build doctrine (Kelly directive)

---

## The core statement

> Same room. More depth.

Every member is in the same place. The platform does not have a "free version" and a "real version." It has one experience with increasing depth of stewardship.

The Explorer is not using a trial. The Companion is not a power user. The Practitioner is not an enterprise customer. These are three kinds of relationship with the work — and the platform serves all three.

---

## This is a journey model, not a billing model

The three tiers are not primarily a revenue structure. They are a description of the stages of relationship a person might have with their own development and with others.

| Level | Stewardship | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer | Self-awareness | With yourself — presence, orientation, beginning |
| Companion | Self-development | With your life over time — continuity, pattern, growth |
| Practitioner | Care for others | With clients and community — support, session, practice |

Each level is not "more features." It is a different kind of stewardship relationship.

Revenue follows that relationship. It does not precede it.

---

## The felt experience at each level

### Explorer

The experience feels complete.

The member can begin a conversation, capture a thought, receive a Daily Anchor, hold continuity at the most basic level, and feel genuinely oriented.

The message is:
> "You belong here."

### Companion

The experience deepens.

Full continuity, patterns over time, Focus Garden, AvoidanceBreaker, gathering strip, longer developmental views. The member begins to work with their life as a thread, not just a session.

The message becomes:
> "Let's work with your life over time."

### Practitioner

The relationship changes again.

Studio workspace, client sessions, session preparation, Co-Lab, practice intelligence. The member is now holding the development of others, not only themselves.

The message becomes:
> "Support others through this work."

---

## The free tier as primary marketing channel

The Explorer experience is not a conversion funnel. It is a demonstration of the philosophy.

A person who finds genuine value in the Explorer tier already understands what MAIA is. When they encounter visible doors to the Companion layer, they are evaluating something they have already experienced — not something they are being asked to trust.

This is a stronger foundation for subscription than any sales copy:
> "I'm already getting value from this. I can see why I'd want the next layer."

Optimizing for Explorer quality is optimizing for Companion conversion — but the conversion is downstream of genuine value, not a goal in itself.

---

## What not to optimize

- Paywalls before relationship
- Feature hiding before comprehension
- Upgrade prompts before the member has had an experience worth upgrading from
- Tier comparisons as conversion pressure

These patterns work against the product's core claim: that this is a sovereignty-first, relationship-oriented platform. A paywall before relationship contradicts that claim before the member has had a chance to experience it.

---

## The visible doors principle (summary)

Explorer members should be able to see the next layer without being pressured toward it. Visible doors name what exists at Companion and Practitioner level, in the language of stewardship rather than features.

> "Patterns become available as your thread grows."
> "Focus Garden helps you work intentionally with recurring themes."
> "Companion unlocks continuity across time."

These are orientation statements. They make the path visible without creating the feeling of being denied access.

See `docs/architecture/VISIBLE_DOORS_2026-06-12.md` for the full implementation rule.

---

## Application to the next 90 days

1. Ship Track A with the Explorer floor genuinely complete
2. Manually promote early members to Companion and Practitioner as the product matures
3. Observe what Companion members use — that is the data for the upgrade story
4. Build billing integration against observed patterns, not feature lists
5. Let the Explorer experience demonstrate the philosophy before the sales conversation begins

The three-month goal is not a subscription rate. It is an Explorer experience good enough that members can see the path ahead.

---

## Relationship to sovereignty doctrine

The tier model is a form of the Sovereignty Invariant: the system supports the person's development without creating dependence. Explorer members should grow toward Companion not because the platform withholds value, but because Companion genuinely represents a deeper layer of stewardship they want to enter.

The same logic applies to Companion → Practitioner. The platform does not recruit practitioners. It serves people whose development leads them toward caring for others — and when that happens, the Practitioner tier is ready.
