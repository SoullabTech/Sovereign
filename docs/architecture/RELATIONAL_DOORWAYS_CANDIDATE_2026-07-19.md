## Governing sentence

> **AIN does not preserve the field. It keeps the doorways through which people may
> choose to re-enter what mattered.**

## What AIN does not do

A field is living, reciprocal, temporal, emergent. It cannot be captured as an object
without becoming something else. Presence cannot be stored. Relationship cannot be
stored. **AIN does not preserve a living relational field. A field can only be
re-entered.**

What can be kept are traces, artifacts, gestures, thresholds — doorways:

- a threshold back toward the field
- the memory that it existed
- the question that opened there
- the invitation to re-enter relationship
- the conditions through which something living might arise again

The pilgrimage analogy: you cannot store the sacred. You can preserve the stories,
relics, thresholds, maps, and practices that allow someone to return. One does not
preserve Jerusalem; one preserves the paths.

The keeper sentence, whose implications reach past this layer (memory, ritual,
therapy, sacred places, developmental work, AI itself):

> **The field cannot be stored. It can only be re-entered.**

## The potential role

AIN may someday keep **member-authored doorways back to what mattered** — including
doorways back toward relationships and shared moments. The claim is deliberately
small: less grandiose than "preserving extraordinary human fields," and far more
original, because it makes a smaller claim while revealing a deeper understanding.

## Relational memory is a new primitive

Relational memory is **not an extension of single-member episodic memory**. Current
memory grammar is `member → experience → remembrance`: every stored object has one
owner, and eligibility originates from a member act (breakthrough marking, anchor
`surface_preference`, `member_pulled` default).

A shared encounter has at least three distinct realities that must not be collapsed:

1. Member A's experience of it
2. Member B's experience of it
3. Anything explicitly held in common

**One person may privately hold their own experience of an encounter without creating
a shared record.** MAIA may remember what Kelly chose to mark about *Kelly's*
experience; that does not create a jointly owned relational record. **A shared
relational artifact requires dyadic (or multi-party) consent** — mutual
authorization, not one member's gesture.

### The deepest primitive is encounter, not relationship

The grammar is phenomenological, not social:

```
Encounter
    ↓
Possible remembrance          (single-member; already exists in substrate)
    ↓
Possible mutual remembrance   (dyadic consent; does not exist; the new primitive)
    ↓
Possible re-entry doorway     (member-pulled surfacing)
```

The focus stays on *something happened here*, never *these people should remain
connected*. The latter is social networking; the former is phenomenological. This is
not only cleaner ontology — it is the existing constitution applied: the
Constitutional Direction of Authority already names Encounter as the ground layer,
and authority may only move upward through authored experience. A shared doorway is
a higher-order artifact that can only be built from an encounter both people
authored. Person↔Person as the base type would let the system skip the encounter
layer — exactly what Invariant 16 forbids.

### Substrate note

This may not require a new store. The Encounter primitive already models a shared
event between members; the genuinely new thing is a *mutual remembrance property* on
top of an encounter, not a parallel memory system. Any eventual cross-member surface
lands in Co-Lab boundary-gate territory (31-check release gate).

**Sequencing consequence (why nothing needs pre-building):** under the coincidence
model, no shared object exists until both members have independently marked — which
means the existing single-member marking substrate (atoms, breakthroughs, anchors)
is already the correct foundation. Coincidence detection can be added later without
retrofitting consent. The five walks and episodic work are literally on the path to
this vision, not a detour from it.

### Open constitutional problems (must be resolved before any spec)

1. **The consent handshake is itself a disclosure.** If MAIA asks B "would you like
   this remembered?" *because A marked it*, A's private gesture has become B's
   notification — MAIA has already acted as messenger before any mutual object
   exists. Doorway-not-matchmaker must govern the handshake, not just the surfacing.
   Candidate resolutions (all unresolved): the ask surfaces only when B
   independently touches the same territory; A carries the invitation personally and
   MAIA holds nothing until both have marked; or independent coincidence of marks
   creates eligibility for a member-pulled mutuality question. The third — the
   **coincidence model** — is favored in sitting, not for efficiency but because it
   preserves mystery: mutuality is discovered, never manufactured. Kelly privately
   marks; months later Michael independently marks; only then does the system
   recognize *something may already exist here*. It implies most shared moments
   never become shared objects — which may be exactly right, because most meaningful
   human encounters remain asymmetric, partial, mysterious, unfinished. The
   architecture should allow that.
2. **Asymmetric mattering must fail invisibly.** Something can matter to A and not to
   B. If B declines and A learns of it, the system has manufactured a wound that
   would not exist without the infrastructure. No shared object forms; A keeps their
   own single-member memory ("this mattered *to me*"); B's decline is never legible
   as decline.
3. **Sanctuary boundary is absolute.** Nothing from a Sanctuary session can become a
   shared relational artifact, including by the other participant's marking.

## Doorway, not matchmaker

MAIA does not decide that a relationship matters now. She preserves a doorway the
person previously chose to keep.

The failure path of every platform: infer affinities → score relationships → prompt
engagement → increase network activity → call it connection. That is how a humane
system becomes an activation engine, and it fails Invariant 16 directly
(system-manufactured relational significance, system-initiated).

**Prohibited under this direction**: inferred affinity scoring, unsolicited matching,
relationship activation, system-authored relational significance. The constitutional
register is:

> "You chose to keep something from your conversation with Sarah.
> Would you like to return to it?"

not:

> "Sarah recently started a project that may resonate."

**Relational recall grammar**: member-originated, mutually authorized where shared,
member-pulled when surfaced.

## The moat

The sovereignty constraints are the product advantage, not a limitation on it.
Anyone can build embeddings, graph databases, recommenders, memory layers. Almost
nobody will willingly constrain themselves enough to build non-interpretive memory,
member-authored meaning, doorway-instead-of-activation, sovereignty-over-optimization.
**These constraints are what prevent AIN from becoming a CRM, LinkedIn, Slack, or
networking activation engine** — and what would make it feel human. Without them,
this direction is an increasingly intelligent networking platform. With them, it
could become something genuinely different.

## Failure Test

> **If the system can create, interpret, activate, or surface a relationship without
> the people involved having authored and authorized that doorway, it has crossed
> the boundary.**

And the survivable epitaph: if MAIA vanished tomorrow, people would not say "it held
my relationships." They might say — in the quietest, most host-like register:

> *"It kept a way back open."*

(Refined in sitting from "it helped me find my way back," which still implied MAIA
actively navigating. The refined form is almost invisible. The person does the
returning.) The closing formulation:

> *MAIA did not hold my relationships. She kept a doorway open, and when I was
> ready, I returned.*

Small enough to be true; large enough to contain the whole vision.

## Naming

**Relational Doorways**, not "Relational Continuity Layer":

- "Layer" implies an architectural commitment this candidate does not have.
- "Continuity" can imply AIN keeps the relationship alive — it does not and cannot.
- "Doorways" accurately preserves agency, impermanence, and mutuality.

("Return" remains a resonant register — the codebase already speaks it via atoms
`return_preference` and the contextual-return default doctrine — but no layer name is
established by this document.)

## Dependencies and sequencing (unchanged)

At least two unresolved dependencies stand before this direction could even be
specified:

1. **Production evidence for individual episodic continuity** (Episodic Phase 2 has
   zero production emissions as of this date).
2. **A constitutional model for shared ownership and consent** (including the
   handshake-disclosure problem above).

The immediate evidence phase remains the **five individual walks** and what one
person remembers or returns to later. **COEE remains a thought-partnership context,
not a product target or pitch** — this framing must never arrive there as a pitch.

The market insight stands, in its defensible form:

> COEE creates conditions in which meaningful human fields arise.
> AIN might someday help people keep consensual doorways through which those
> relationships can be freely re-entered.

## Strategic horizon (held light, not claim)

The sitting surfaced a larger articulation: AIN may ultimately be **infrastructure
for return** — return to oneself, important questions, relationships, communities,
practices, conversations, sacred experiences, unfinished meanings. The codebase has
been converging on this language independently: `return_preference`, contextual
return, porch, doorway, threshold, *"a light on and somewhere for what mattered to
still be waiting."*

Two disciplines on this articulation:

1. **Convergence of language is direction, not evidence of function.** The
   vocabulary converging is worth paying attention to; it does not upgrade any
   claim's Live/Designed/Vision status. Metaphor after measurement — naming must not
   precede it.
2. **This phrase stays Cat 1.** "Infrastructure for return" must not leak into
   public copy, decks, or the COEE relationship until there is production evidence
   that individual return actually happens (the porch test: what one person
   remembers, and returns to, a day later). Beauty must never require exaggeration.
3. **Hold the phrase as direction, not identity.** It may eventually prove too
   broad — return may be central while continuity is broader, relationship deeper,
   or hospitality the truer substrate. Only evidence can determine that.
