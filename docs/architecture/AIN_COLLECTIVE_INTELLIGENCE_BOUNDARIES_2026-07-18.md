# AIN Collective Intelligence Boundaries — 2026-07-18

**Status**: CANON-CANDIDATE. Fourth of the commissioned papers. Consolidates the
ratified rulings (register R5, R6, R12) and the Collective Boundary Model
(`GOLD_REFLECTION_COLLECTIVE_BOUNDARY_MODEL_2026-07-17.md`) with the Provenance
Constitution — restating the collective layer *on top of* the provenance substrate,
which did not exist when the boundary model was first drafted. Where the two
documents differ, this one governs; ratification is Kelly's.

**Founding sentence**: AIN reflects patterns of human flourishing that emerge across
many sovereign lives without exposing, collapsing, or appropriating any individual
life.

**Standing direction (ratified)**: *AIN learns only from what sovereign members
deliberately contribute.*

---

## 1. What changed since the boundary model: collective eligibility is now a provenance question

The original model described a staged pathway with gates. The Provenance Constitution
makes the fifth question — *May I become collective?* — a property every durable
object must answer **at creation, with the answer defaulting to no**. The collective
boundary is therefore no longer a separate policy layer that inspects objects; it is
a field the object either carries truthfully or does not carry at all.

Consequences:

1. **No classification pathway exists.** Nothing becomes collective-eligible because
   an algorithm judged it valuable, anonymous, or safe. Eligibility has exactly one
   mint: a member's deliberate, per-item offering act (Stage 2).
2. **Unattributed historical objects are permanently ineligible** (Provenance
   Constitution §7). An object that cannot prove its posture cannot cross the
   membrane, ever. This retroactively hardens the boundary model's "may never enter"
   list into a mechanical property.
3. **Rejected and unratified construals are ineligible by type.** A MAIA construal
   the member never recognized has `createdBy: maia, generatedBy: inference` and no
   ratifying gesture — the provenance itself refuses collective use without any
   list-checking. `not-true` is terminal for collective purposes (R7).
4. **Sanctuary is doubly excluded**: no durable object exists (S1), and if one ever
   escaped again, its `postureAtCreation` would mark it ineligible — the incident's
   lesson applied forward as defense in depth.

## 2. The pathway, restated on provenance

```text
Stage 0  Private member experience
            — the permanent default home of everything; posture recorded per turn
Stage 1  Member-ratified recognition
            — provenance gains a ratifying gesture (recognized / revised / kept)
Stage 2  Explicit collective offering
            — a SEPARATE member act mints collectiveEligible: true on that item;
              consent is to a described use; silence means no; "no" is ordinary;
              the UI never frames sharing as the generous answer
Stage 3  Thresholded pattern formation
            — patterns form only across ≥N distinct members from Stage-2 objects
              in the contribution store; formation is re-runnable from current
              contributions only (no residual model state that cannot forget)
Stage 4  Collective reflection
            — observations of plurality with provenance ("across many lives…");
              never norms, rankings, advice, or member-facing statistics
Stage 5  No reverse inference — standing invariant
            — nothing at Stage 4 may characterize, address, or target any
              individual, including contributors
```

Withdrawal: per-item, any time; the next formation cycle runs without it; patterns
falling below threshold retire. Deletion of the underlying private object implies
withdrawal — and leaves a deletion manifest the contribution store must obey (R20
applies to the collective store identically).

## 3. What may never enter (unchanged, now mechanically grounded)

Raw transcripts · private memory not individually offered · Sanctuary anything ·
client/session material in practitioner containers (the client's consent to the
practitioner is not consent to the commons; the practitioner cannot consent for the
client) · practitioner notes · unratified AI interpretations · rejected construals ·
sensitive identity/health material (no pathway; standing recommendation: none ever) ·
any cohort where a reasonable participant could infer whose life produced a pattern.

**The reasonable-participant test (ratified, R6) outranks every threshold**: *No
collective pattern may be shown when a reasonable participant could infer whose life
produced it.* Thresholds are necessary, never sufficient.

## 4. Anti-flattening (unchanged in substance)

Collective reflection is not consensus. Formation must not weight by frequency,
popularity, or engagement; rarity recurring across unrelated lives outranks majority
sentiment; tensions are first-class collective objects ("across many lives, success
and belonging pull in opposite directions" is valid; "choose belonging" never is).
No leaderboards of flourishing, no ordering of members, practices, or lives.

- No contribution store, offering gesture, or formation job exists — and none may be
  built before the Provenance Constitution ships, because Stage 2's mint *is* a
  provenance operation.

## 6. Sequence

This paper deliberately sits **last** in the build order it describes:

```text
Relationship → Trust → Sovereignty → Provenance → Reflection → Recognition → Collective Intelligence
```

Collective intelligence is the seventh step, not the second. The platform's
differentiator is not that AIN learns from many lives — everyone claims that. It is
that AIN can prove, object by object, that every life it learned from chose to
teach it.
