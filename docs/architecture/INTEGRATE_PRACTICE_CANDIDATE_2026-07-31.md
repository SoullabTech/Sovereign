# "Integrate" — a candidate practice-level discovery

> **Status: CANDIDATE PRACTICE MODEL — not governing architecture, not ratified.**
> Recorded at Discover/Record level only. It renames nothing, alters no ontology,
> exposes no internal vocabulary, and authorizes no implementation.

## The candidate

> **The creative field is the work being made. Capture and Gather have value only
> insofar as material can be explicitly integrated into that field. "Integrate" names
> the missing bridge between preserved material and authored expression.**

Stronger form of the same claim: **Gather has no intrinsic value.** Its only value is
preserving things until they become part of creation.

### Genuinely new

1. **Integrate as a named act with its own status.** Previously a verb inside one
   section; the candidate proposes it is the **bridge without which the chain breaks**:
   Capture → Gather → **Integrate** → Shape.
2. **A correction to Gather's value claim.** The roadmap says belonging is the key. The
   candidate says belonging is *necessary but not sufficient* — value is realized only
   at integration. This sharpens, and slightly demotes, Gather.
3. **The Living Work Atlas has no integration moment.** Its phases (Appear · Gather ·
   Deepen · Wait · Change · Express) never name material *entering the text*. This is a
   real gap in the atlas, not a restatement.
4. **Practice as an intermediate layer** — `Living Work → Practice → Expressions`
   rather than `Living Work → Expressions`; expressions as *consequences* of practice.
5. **Four irreducible acts** — Notice → Gather → Integrate → Shape, with writing and
   editing as *implementations of Shape*.

### Requires a founder ruling (not assumed here)

- **(a)** Whether Integrate is elevated to a **constitutional act** (item 1/5 above).
- **(b)** Whether **Practice** becomes an intermediate layer (item 4). ⚠️ **This touches
  `LIVING_WORK_ONTOLOGY_RATIFICATION_INSTRUMENT_2026-07-31.md`, which is already seated
  awaiting ratification.** That instrument has **not** been amended and must not be
  edited implicitly — if Practice is adopted, the instrument is re-cut and re-seated as
  an explicit act.
- **(c)** Whether the Atlas is redrawn to include an integration moment.
- **(d)** The member-facing verb (Bring in · Use here · Add to draft).
