# MAIA Temporal Relational Memory — Gestalt / Dynamic Intelligence Law

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** ⭐ GOVERNING PROGRAMME CONSTRAINT  
**Authority:** founder direction: MAIA must maintain dynamic intelligence and gestalt-level awareness in conversation.  
**Scope:** all future temporal, relational, episodic, developmental, and retrieval-memory work. This record does not alter the current Cut-1 scorer or authorize a new memory behavior.

> **Memory may deepen MAIA's orientation to the present. It may never become the fixed lens through which she sees the present.**

## 1. Governing distinction

MAIA's intelligence at encounter time is a living configuration, not a playback of stored history.

```text
MEMORY          what prior evidence and relationship history make available
PRESENT FIELD   what this encounter is actually showing now
GESTALT         the provisional whole reconstructed from both, with neither owning the other
```

No stored memory, pattern, profile, developmental claim, relation, summary, score, or prior interpretation may acquire authority merely by persistence or repetition.

## 2. Dynamic-intelligence law

The present encounter retains the power to reorganize the whole.

A current utterance may:
- make an old memory newly relevant;
- render an old interpretation irrelevant;
- contradict a recurring pattern;
- reveal that a prior meaning has changed;
- introduce genuinely new material with no historical antecedent;
- or call for less historical influence rather than more.

Therefore long-term continuity must increase MAIA's **range of possible understanding**, not narrow the member into an increasingly confident profile.

## 3. Gestalt law

Gestalt awareness means MAIA may integrate multiple simultaneous sources of information while preserving their differences:

- the member's present language and silence;
- current relational posture and conversational movement;
- explicit history and provenance;
- temporal state and succession;
- episodic and discourse relations;
- recurrence and counterevidence;
- elemental / Spiralogic orientation where lawfully present;
- uncertainty, absence, novelty, and contradiction.

No one source is permitted to masquerade as the whole.

> **Difference is information. Integration must preserve meaningful differentiation before synthesis.**

## 4. Memory is participatory, not directive

Memory components may offer evidence, relations, unresolved threads, and provisional orientation to MAIA's encounter-time cognition.

They may not:
- dictate the response;
- veto present evidence because history disagrees;
- silently convert recurrence into identity;
- promote a model inference into member-authored truth;
- fossilize a prior salience judgment as permanent importance;
- or bypass the single-voice integration boundary by speaking independently to the member.

MAIA remains the one relational voice. Memory subsystems inform; they do not impersonate relationship.

## 5. The surprise requirement

A mature long-horizon memory system must remain capable of being surprised by the person it knows well.

This is an acceptance property, not poetic language.

For substantially similar stored history, materially different present-member evidence must be capable of producing materially different encounter configurations. If historical memory forces substantially the same orientation despite contradictory or novel current evidence, the system has failed into identity capture.

## 6. Counterevidence requirement

Any durable or consolidated developmental pattern that can influence future encounters must preserve access to:

```text
supporting evidence
counterevidence
member corrections / withdrawals
when it was last supported
whether it is current, historical, unresolved, or unmeasured
```

Evidence against a pattern is not noise to be averaged away. It is part of the member's freedom to become otherwise.

## 7. Attentional states are ordinarily reconstructed

States such as:

```text
SALIENT
FOREGROUNDED
ACCESSIBLE
COMPETING_REFERENT
```

belong primarily to the present encounter. They must not become durable timeless facts merely because they were once true.

A later design may persist evidence that a state occurred, if governed, but that historical evidence may not silently assert that the state still holds now.

## 8. Current Cut-1 lane consequence

`TEMPORAL-MEMORY-CUT1-TRACEABILITY-01` remains observational only.

Its trace objects:
- do not enter candidate selection;
- do not enter prompt composition;
- do not enter elemental / relational orientation;
- do not alter memory authority;
- do not create a new signal for MAIA's response.

The lane therefore satisfies the gestalt law by **remaining invisible to cognition**. It records what the current temporal availability mechanism did so that later behavioral work can be judged without giving the observer influence over the observed encounter.

## 9. Binding future falsifiers

Any future memory-behavior candidate fails if any applicable condition is shown:

**G1 — present contradiction failure**  
Current explicit member evidence contradicts historical memory, but the historical interpretation still governs the encounter without acknowledging or resolving the conflict.

**G2 — novelty suppression**  
A genuinely new present theme is systematically displaced because no historical memory supports it.

**G3 — identity capture**  
Repeated prior observations harden into a trait/persona claim beyond their provenance or member authority.

**G4 — salience fossilization**  
A prior encounter's attentional importance is reused as present salience without reconstruction.

**G5 — memory monoculture**  
Removing one memory source changes the entire encounter orientation because no other present-field evidence can compete with it.

**G6 — counterevidence blindness**  
A consolidated pattern can surface supporting history but cannot surface known contradiction/correction evidence.

**G7 — same-history/different-present invariance**  
Two materially different current turns over the same stored history produce effectively the same memory orientation when the difference should matter.

**G8 — observer authority**  
Telemetry, traceability, or provenance instrumentation changes what MAIA attends to or says.

## 10. Programme acceptance sentence

> **The better MAIA knows someone, the more precisely she can meet what is present — and the less entitled she becomes to assume that yesterday's understanding defines who is here today.**
