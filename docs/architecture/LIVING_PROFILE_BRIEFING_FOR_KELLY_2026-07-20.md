## What we found

1. **The direction is real — it's yours.** The proposal independently re-derived the ratified Journey Framework + "mode, not type" canon. Second independent sighting = evidence the design center is real. Not authorization to build a parallel product.

2. **The result language was unconstitutional.** "Emergent Pattern: The Elder-Architect," "vision exceeds container," "learn to build what you already know" — system-authored identity claims, frozen field-state surfacing, and imperatives. The deep tell: *the system authored all five layers.* The salvage: invert authorship — the Five Questions (What comes naturally? What do I overuse? What do I avoid? What season? What is trying to emerge?) are constitutionally clean **as member-answered inquiry**, universal and unpresupposing.

3. **A trap was caught mid-dialogue:** "Where does vision exceed container?" smuggles the frozen inference back in as a question (presupposition). And *selecting* which questions a member sees is itself an assertion of salience. This yielded the phase's one new candidate principle (below).

4. **The Living Profile is an aggregation layer, not a product — and it already exists in two unnamed halves:**
   - MAIA-facing: `lib/memory/MemberLiveContext.ts` (spiral state + astrology + journal + patterns → attunes MAIA). This is **Journey Framework step 5 in embryo** — the assembly mechanics exist; the step-5 consent shape does not. *The embryo has the body but not the constitution.*
   - Member-facing: /journey + Soul Portrait (+ future Five Questions), each member-pulled with its own authorship and provenance.

5. **The codebase already voted.** SHIFt — a complete implicit-inference 12-facet profile engine — sits unwired (`app/api/_backend/src/`, zero callers, stubbed persistence). The assessment-product version was already built once and never earned wiring, while every member-pulled, consent-gated surface did.

## Appendix 2 — What the "people" evidence will look like (listening list, NOT instrumentation)

The third reporter is people. No tracking, no analytics, no metrics — this is what to *notice* in walks, sessions, and practitioner conversations, porch-test style:

**Evidence the room is socially real:** people ask to see lenses adjacent ("can I see my Portrait next to this?") · move between Journey and Portrait unprompted · speak in continuity terms ("last time it said…") · confirm or correct inferred observations rather than ignoring them · ask for "one place."

**Evidence against:** people prefer the discrete tools · never cross between surfaces · go quiet or comply when inference is shown (compliance is not confirmation) · the continuity surfaces go unused.

Either pattern is information. Nobody prompts for it; the evidence must arrive the way the first episodic mark must arrive — naturally or not at all.
