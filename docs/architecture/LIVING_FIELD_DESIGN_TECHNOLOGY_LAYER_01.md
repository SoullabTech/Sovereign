# LIVING-FIELD-DESIGN-TECHNOLOGY-LAYER-01
## Design Technology Layer

**Status:** governing architecture layer
**Date:** 29 September 2026
**Runtime authority:** none

Living Field Architecture includes contemporary interaction and rendering technology as a first-class layer.

Technology is not the discipline.

Technology is admitted only when it makes an existing experiential law more fully realizable.

> ## LIVING-FIELD-TECHNOLOGY-LAW-01
>
> **Use the newest technology only when it makes an existing experiential law more fully realizable.**
>
> Technology may intensify continuity, embodiment, presence, relation, accessibility, revelation, or performance.
>
> It may not substitute spectacle for meaning.

It does not ask:

> What technology is fashionable?

## Five admission tests

A design technology should enter only if it materially improves one or more of:

### Continuity
Does it help the same world survive transition?

### Embodiment
Does interaction become more spatial, tactile, or bodily intelligible?

### Revelation
Does it expose real structure, relation, provenance, or temporal depth?

### Presence
Does it reduce abstraction, context loss, or attention fragmentation?

### Performance
Does it make richer experience possible without making the field sluggish or inaccessible?

If none of these improve, the technology is probably novelty.

## Semantic truth hierarchy

The semantic and epistemic substrate remains authoritative.

```text
semantic truth
    ↓
accessible structure
    ↓
spatial / visual representation
    ↓
enhanced rendering / sensory effects
```

Higher-performance layers may render the field.

They do not define:

- identity;
- relation;
- source;
- provenance;
- authorship;
- epistemic standing;
- importance;
- psychological state.

> **GPU renders the field. It never owns the field.**

## Progressive enhancement law

Every enhanced technology must degrade toward a semantically truthful lower layer.

Examples:

- WebGPU → Canvas/SVG → semantic DOM;
- spatial audio → ordinary accessible audio / silence;
- haptic cues → visual/textual confirmation;
- advanced transitions → immediate stable-state transition;
- anchor positioning → ordinary in-flow placement.

No epistemically necessary meaning may exist only in an enhancement layer.

## Accessibility

Technology admission requires consideration of:

- reduced motion;
- keyboard navigation;
- focus order;
- screen-reader semantics;
- color-independent meaning;
- touch and coarse-pointer use;
- cognitive load;
- motion sensitivity;
- optional sound;
- optional haptics;
- device performance constraints.

## Sensory restraint

Sound, motion, haptics, depth, blur, particles, and shader effects must have a role.

Permitted roles include:

- preserving identity across transition;
- confirming member action;
- orienting spatially;
- revealing scale;
- marking a boundary crossing;
- showing reversible reorganization;
- supporting continuity.

They must not imply unsupported:

- urgency;
- psychological activation;
- importance;
- truth;
- risk;
- developmental rank.

## Technology-review question

Before implementation ask:

> **Which existing Living Field law becomes more realizable because this technology exists?**

If the answer is unclear, do not adopt the technology.
