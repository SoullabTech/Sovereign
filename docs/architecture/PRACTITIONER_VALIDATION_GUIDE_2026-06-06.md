## The two rules

1. **Do not show them the Studio.** Don't open the nav, don't list modules, don't demo. If you show 12 modules you'll get politeness, not signal. The whole point is to learn what they'd reach for *unprompted*.
2. **Do not validate features.** "Would you use a caseload tool?" produces yes-to-be-nice. Ask about their **current behavior**, not hypothetical features.

---

## The script (≈20 min)

**1. Anchor in what already works** (don't lead)
> "You've been using MAIA a fair amount lately — what's it actually become useful for?"

Listen for the real job it's doing. That's the beachhead you extend *from*.

**2. The bridge probe** (the core question)
> "What's one thing you currently do *outside* MAIA — for your practice or your clients — that you'd want it to help you hold, remember, organize, or reflect on?"

**3. Concretize the last instance** (turn abstraction into a workflow)
> "Walk me through the last time you did that. Where did it live — a notebook, your head, a doc, an app? What was annoying about it?"

You now have a verb *and* an artifact *and* a current tool you'd be replacing.

**4. The trust boundary** (sovereignty-relevant; reveals the *safe* wedge)
> "Is there practice work you'd want MAIA to hold — and some you wouldn't? Where's the line?"

This protects the design and surfaces which workflow they'd actually trust it with first.

**5. The one-thing counterfactual**
> "If MAIA could do exactly one practice-thing for you next month, what would earn the most trust?"

---

## What to capture (per person)

- **The verb** (hold / remember / organize / reflect / prepare / track)
- **The artifact** (the thing — a client thread, a session prep, a reflection, a decision)
- **Where it lives now** (the tool you'd displace)
- **The trust line** (what they'd *not* want held)

## How to decide the bridge

Lay the three captures side by side. Look for the **intersection** — the workflow ≥2 of them name unprompted. Likely candidates given the data (session-room + decisions already have faint signal): *post-session reflection*, *client continuity / "what did we work on last time"*, *session preparation*, *decision tracking*, *supervision/journaling*. **Pick one.** Build only that, instrument it, and watch whether Tara/Andrea/Nathan cross. That single validated crossing is worth more than finishing all 12 surfaces.

## Anti-goals (things that would waste the conversation)
- Demoing the Studio · pitching modules · asking "would you use X" · promising a roadmap · leading toward a feature you already want to build.
