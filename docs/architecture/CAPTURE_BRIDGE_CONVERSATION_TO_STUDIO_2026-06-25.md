# The Capture Bridge: Conversation → Studio (member-authored promotion)

**Date:** 2026-06-25
**Status:** Architecture. The **member-authored channel** of the promotion pipeline in `PERSISTENCE_GOVERNANCE_ROOM_VS_PERSON_2026-06-25.md` §8. General — governs any conversation → durable-artifact flow (member or practitioner).
**Reads with:** `PRACTICE_SOURCE_AND_TRANSLATION_STUDIO_2026-06-25.md` (the studio + its constitution §8), `PERSISTENCE_GOVERNANCE_ROOM_VS_PERSON_2026-06-25.md`. **Governed by:** `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` (declared > inferred; Sanctuary; deletion authority).

## 0. The separation
**MAIA is where the conversation happens; the Studios are where work lives.** A single conversation can birth many kinds of work — a Vision idea, a Legacy story, a practitioner insight (Flourishing), a research concept. So **MAIA doesn't *own* the artifact — it's the place where it was born.** Mental model:

```
MAIA → Conversation Layer → "Capture this…" → choose destination Studio
```

The capture action is a **bridge**, not a hierarchy.

## 1. Capture = member-authored promotion (the key fit)
This *is* the promotion pipeline (§8.1), with the **member as the promotion authority.** The four promotion questions are all answered **by her** at capture:

| Promotion question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| Referent (room/person, what kind of work) | she chooses the destination |
| Provenance | **member-declared** — the strongest (§8.3) |
| Promotion authority | hers |
| Reader contract | it is her curated artifact |

So capture is the one promotion that needs **zero system inference** — and the **only** legitimate channel for person-state: *"I'm in a season of grief" → Legacy* is member-declared person-state stored **as her declaration**, never as a system claim (§8.3). **The act of saving is itself the declaration.**

## 2. MAIA offers the *choice*, never the *classification*
Offering the destination menu (*Save to Vision / Legacy / Flourishing / Keep / new artifact*) is **hospitality.** MAIA *recommending* a destination (*"this looks like a Vision item"*) is **classifying her work** — the follows-not-leads / declared-over-inferred line. Keep MAIA on the **choice** side. (If suggestion is ever wanted, it must be offer-framed, refusable, and default-off; the safe default is no suggestion. She decides what kind of work it is.)

## 3. Capture must not turn the conversation into filing
Capture-in-the-moment must be **optional and equal to capture-later** (from history). The default is that she simply talks and **nothing needs filing** mid-conversation. Otherwise it violates *"never require organizing before sharing"* (studio §6.1) and the room-receives-she-doesn't-perform posture (studio §8). Effortless = highlight or click *Capture*, choose a Studio, optional short note, continue — **never a required step.** Organization stays the platform's job; expression stays hers.

## 4. Link **and** hold — not pure-link (refinement)
*"Linked, not duplicated"* preserves provenance and avoids redundancy — but **pure-link is fragile against two live invariants**:

- **Sanctuary:** a Sanctuary session is not retained → there is nothing to link back to. Capture must therefore be **explicit-only or disabled in Sanctuary** — never silently link or preserve a session meant to vanish (that would breach the Sanctuary absolute boundary).
- **Deletion authority** (*"nothing disappears without her hand"*): if she later deletes the source conversation, a pure link dangles.

**Resolution:** a capture is a **member-authored artifact that holds the captured fragment** — it stands on its own, because the capture *was* the declaration that made it matter — **plus a provenance link** to the source (live while the source exists; marked *"source removed"* on deletion). She keeps the artifact in the Studio even if she deletes the conversation; provenance is preserved while available. This satisfies both her aims (provenance, low duplication) and both invariants (Sanctuary, deletion).

## 5. Studios are curated collections, not auto-populated repositories
Each Studio holds her artifacts plus a *Conversation Moments* list — provenance links back into the conversation, with surrounding context (e.g. *Vision Studio → Conversation Moments (12) → "June 25 — the future of Soullab…"*). Opening one returns her to that point with context. Over time a Studio is a body of work **she assembled**, not a repository the system filled. (This is *"remembers without possessing"* + *the work is the protagonist*: the Studio holds what she declared, linked to where it was born.)

## 6. Scope note: destinations are context-dependent
The menu depends on who she is, and must not blur the **member-companion vs practitioner-studio** boundary (studio §5):
- **Member:** *Keep* (the existing default-return memory path — not a Studio) · *Legacy* · *Vision*.
- **Practitioner:** additionally *Flourishing* / their own Studios.

Don't offer practitioner destinations to a non-practitioner, and don't let a member-companion capture silently become practitioner-studio material.

## 7. Refinements (2026-06-25)

### 7.1 Offering vs suggesting — the system may learn *workflow*, not *meaning*
Two behaviors both feel like "offering"; only one is governed.
- **Passive offer** (she invokes Capture → neutral menu) and **contextual convenience** (*Vision* pre-selected because she is **in** Vision Studio; *Legacy* defaulted because she has saved there ten times running) are both fine — they adapt to **workflow / room-state** (*"you usually save here"*), the Room side of the referent test.
- **Content suggestion** (*"this conversation looks like a Vision item"*) interprets her **content** → meaning-classification → **forbidden** (follows-not-leads).

The line is exact: *"you usually save captures here"* = room/workflow state; *"this belongs here"* = interpretation. **Guard:** a workflow default is legitimate **only if the destination stays visible and the choice stays conscious.** An invisible auto-route (captures silently landing in Legacy by pattern) hollows out the declaration — and *the act of saving is the declaration* (§1). A default is a pre-selection she sees and can change, never a sort she doesn't notice.

### 7.2 Capture's three layers (the shape of "link and hold")
```
Capture
├── Member-authored fragment   (canonical — the durable object)
├── Optional member note        (her framing)
└── Provenance reference        (link to source; additional context)
```
The fragment is canonical *because the capture was the declaration* — so it stays meaningful even if the source conversation is gone (§4). Provenance enriches; it does not own.

### 7.3 ⚠ The Sanctuary collision (corrects §4) — a steward / canon call
"Author an exception" is elegant **everywhere except Sanctuary**, where it **collides with a hard canonical invariant.** Sanctuary boundary #6: *"Nothing from a Sanctuary session can be saved, extracted, inferred, or converted into long-term memory, **under any circumstances, including by user request during the session**."* Sanctuary's **value is its absoluteness** — she speaks freely precisely because **even she cannot save it later.** An in-session capture-exception converts an absolute guarantee into a defeasible default — a weaker promise that breaks the safety Sanctuary exists to provide.
- **Canon-consistent (safe default):** capture is **unavailable inside Sanctuary.** The "conscious crossing" already exists — it is the choice to *not* be in Sanctuary; if she is preserving, she simply isn't in Sanctuary.
- **Or:** softening Sanctuary to a member-defeasible boundary is a **deliberate canon change** (Kelly's call) — never done implicitly via a capture button.

The deeper sovereignty (absolute safety) outranks the surface sovereignty (let-her-choose-anything).

### 7.4 Studios are collections of *declarations*, not folders
Vision / Legacy / Flourishing are not containers conversations are **sorted** into — they are **authored bodies of meaning built one declaration at a time** (*"this belongs"*). This is *why* §7.1 matters: a Studio is an authored body of meaning **only if every entry was authored-in, not sorted-in.** Content-suggestion would turn a living work back into a repository.

### 7.5 The authorship heuristic (capstone — positive complement to the persistence framework)
> **Whenever durable meaning enters the system, there should be a recognizable human act by which it entered.**

Not every continuity needs a click — **room-state / workflow may accumulate automatically.** But whenever the system holds something as part of the person's **authored life or work**, there must be an **identifiable moment of authorship.** Capture is one; **naming**, **creating a Vision artifact**, **marking a breakthrough** (already live as the member-marked `is_breakthrough` flag), and **declaring a season** are siblings.

This is the positive complement to *"do not compute what cannot be known"* (`PERSISTENCE_GOVERNANCE_ROOM_VS_PERSON_2026-06-25.md` §0):
- **System:** accumulates **room-state** automatically; **refrains** from promoting person-meaning by inference.
- **Member:** promotes **declared** meaning through **acts of authorship.**

*Durable meaning is not something the system discovers about the person; it is something the person gradually entrusts to the system through deliberate acts of authorship.* The system's job is to avoid promoting inferred meaning; the member's privilege is to promote declared meaning — a clean allocation of authority.
