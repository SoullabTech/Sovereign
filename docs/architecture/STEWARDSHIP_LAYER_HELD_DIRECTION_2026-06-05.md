# Stewardship Layer — Held Design Direction

> *The "Jeeves" layer: an orientation steward above memory.*

**Status:** **Cat-1 — Preserved Direction.** Held, not authorized. Not a roadmap item, not an active build. Recorded so it is preserved without inflating into a plan.
**Date:** 2026-06-05
**Precondition:** the **Entrustment Covenant** (`docs/specs/ENTRUSTMENT_COVENANT_PROTOCOL_2026-06-05.md`). This direction does not stand on its own — it is downstream of a reliable covenant.
**Relation:** Sovereignty Invariants 1 (Authority Return), 5 (Uncertainty Honesty), 11 (Declared Significance); the Sacred Mirror Principle; `feedback_witnessing_is_the_irreducible_rung`; `project_interface_humility_guardrail`.

---

## The principle

> **Once the covenant is reliable, the highest-value intelligence above it is not an action agent but an orientation steward.**

It does not replace MAIA. It helps a person understand *where they are* within everything MAIA has already been holding.

---

## The three layers (why this is downstream, not parallel)

**Layer 1 — Memory.** *Can the system hold what matters?* — entrusted memory, durability, retrieval, visibility, honor (the five covenant stages). **Without this, everything above it is performance.**

**Layer 2 — Continuity.** *Can the system preserve the thread?* — not facts, not keeps, but the actual continuity of a person's life across time.

**Layer 3 — Stewardship (this direction).** *Can the system help someone orient within that continuity?*
- "What am I not seeing?"
- "What thread has been active for six months?"
- "What changed since last winter?"
- "How does my new Da Yun relate to the work I've been doing?"
- "What commitments have I made that I'm drifting away from?"

That is not memory. **It is interpretation.** Memory retrieves; the steward contextualizes.

---

## Memory → Meaning, not Agent → Action

Most of the field is building **Agent → Action**: book, buy, schedule, execute. This direction is **Memory → Meaning**: remember, contextualize, orient. A *meaning agent*, not an *action agent* — closer to the center of gravity (preservation of the thread) than any execution layer.

---

## The guardrail (load-bearing)

> **The steward may orient *from* the thread; it may not invent the thread.**

- **Memory** provides receipts.
- **Continuity** provides context.
- **Stewardship** provides interpretation.
- **Stewardship never manufactures evidence.**

This is the stewardship-layer expression of **Invariant 11** (member-declared outranks system-inferred) and **Invariant 5** (uncertainty honesty): the steward orients from what was entrusted and observed, never from what it would like to be true. *Metaphor after measurement, not before.*

### Why this anchors the layer to the covenant
A person will forgive a guide for not knowing everything. They will **not** forgive a guide who confidently orients them around things that were never true. So stewardship is *structurally dependent* on the covenant being reliable — an orientation layer built on memory that might be lying would orient people into a fiction. **This is why the Entrust/Durability work matters so much: it is the foundation that earns the orientation layer.**

---

## The sovereignty binding (the layer's highest risk)

The steward is the **maximum guru-drift surface** in the whole architecture — it interprets a person's own life back to them. It is therefore bound most tightly, not least.

The governing inversion: **better memory must increase the quality of reflection, not the system's authority.** A good steward says *"Here's the pattern I see," "Here's the thread that keeps recurring," "Here's what you've previously said matters"* — then returns the question. A bad steward says *"Here's who you are," "Here's what you should do."* That is the exact line where interpretation becomes substitution. The more context the system holds, the stronger the pull across that line; the sovereignty principle pushes the other way — context compounds into better reflection, never into accumulated authority.

The layer is bound by:

- **Sacred Mirror** — reflect pattern, name possibility, **return agency**. The steward orients, then returns the navigation to the person. Health = the person saying *"I realized…"*, not *"MAIA told me where I am."*
- **Invariant 1 (Authority Return)** — it may offer orientation; it must always return the decision.
- *"If the system becomes more alive than the user, the system is failing."* — an orientation steward that becomes the place a person goes *instead of* knowing their own life has inverted its purpose.

The guardrail has two halves: **epistemic** (orient from the thread, don't invent it) and **sovereign** (orient, then return navigation). Both are required.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not a replacement for MAIA.
- Not an action agent.
- Not authorized, not scheduled, not in build.
- Not a license to synthesize, infer significance, or narrate continuity ahead of verified substrate.

It is the role the "Jeeves" figure occupies in the architecture — and it remains held until the covenant beneath it (Layers 1–2) is reliable enough to orient *from*.
