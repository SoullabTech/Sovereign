# The Studio — Copy Voice

> **Status:** Craft standard (Draft + Record), 2026-07-31. Founder-authored.
> Governs member-facing copy across the Studio. Companion to
> `THE_MEMBERS_WORLD_IS_PRIMARY.md` (candidate), which governs *whose world* the copy
> speaks from; this governs *how it sounds.*

**The voice:** not a brand, not a coach, not an AI — **the kind of note you'd leave on
a workbench.**

## The question, before any copy is written

> **What is the person trying to do in this moment?**
> Then write only enough words to help them do that.
> No explanation. No persuasion. No poetry.

## Principles

1. **One idea per sentence.**
2. **Prefer verbs over adjectives.**
3. **Describe what the person can do, not what the Studio is.**
4. **Never explain the philosophy.** Let the experience carry it.
5. **Delete every sentence that exists to reassure.**
6. **If a sentence sounds inspirational, rewrite it until it sounds useful.**

## Worked rewrites

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Whatever you're working on belongs here. | **Bring your work.** |
| One place to begin. | **Start here.** |
| Name it now, or later. | **Give it a name when you're ready.** |
| The work is here. | **Your work is ready.** |
| Their words are where they left them. | **Pick up where you left off.** |
| A place for thoughts before they become something else. | **Capture ideas as they come.** |

**The strongest rewrite is deletion.** *Pick up where you left off* is unnecessary if
the page already opens exactly where they were. When the design says it, the copy
should not.

## Standing room lines

- **Ideas** — Capture ideas as they come.
- **Journal** — Write to think.
- **Voice** — Record it before it's gone.
- **Research** — Keep what matters together.
- **Keeps** — Save what you want to come back to.
- **Questions** — Keep questions open until they're ready to answer.

## Test

> Clear language that gets out of the way.
