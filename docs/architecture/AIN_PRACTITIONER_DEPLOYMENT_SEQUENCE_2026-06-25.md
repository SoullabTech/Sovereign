## 0. The claim of this document

Every practitioner deployment of AIN runs the **same three stages, in the same order.** The order is not a product preference — it is what the sovereignty architecture *forces*. The three flows in the Legacy Sessions spec (§4.1) are not parallel options; they are a **sequence**, because each stage's governance gates the next.

> **The convergence:** the order the product *wants* (value first, magic last) is the order the governance *requires* (buildable floor first, triadic constitution last). When the desirable order and the permissible order are the same order, you build with confidence.

## 1. The stages

In the practitioner's own voice, the journey is four phases:

1. **"Help me discover what I know."** → Stage 1 (Legacy Sessions)
2. **"Help me practice what I know."** → Stage 2 (Practitioner Intelligence)
3. **"Help me share what I know."** → Stage 3 (Client Companion)
4. **"Help it continue beyond me."** → Stage 4 (Continuation — *the constitutional frontier*, §1.4)

Stages 1–3 are a governed sequence (below). Stage 4 is a frontier that breaks the model's premise and is named, not designed.

### Stage 1 — Legacy Sessions *(discover the architecture)*
**This is the beginning of every deployment, not onboarding.** AIN interviews the practitioner, listens, reflects, and gradually assembles the architecture of their work — in their own language, by their own recognition.

- **Flow:** 1 (practitioner's wisdom captured). **Ladder:** Rung 1–2, reaching 4a.
- **Claim tier:** capture/continuity **Live-adjacent**; reflect-what-named **Designed** (M1); inferred-pattern reflection **Vision** (M2).
- **Gate to Stage 2:** an architecture the practitioner has begun to *author* (not merely assent to).
- **Concierge path (single translation partner):** before M2 machinery exists, the cross-session reflection can be **hand-assembled** and offered — *honest only if labeled as concierge, not automated discovery.* This is a legitimate way to validate Stage 1 with one partner without claiming M2 is Live.

### Stage 2 — Practitioner Intelligence *(make them a better practitioner)*
AIN helps the practitioner directly: preparing for sessions, reflecting afterward, remembering clients, finding resources, organizing ideas. **This is where the practitioner immediately experiences value** — and where the Live lead ("help you become a better coach every month") is actually delivered.

- **Internal seam (important):** Stage 2 has two halves with different governance.
  - **Dyadic-buildable:** prep *for* the practitioner, reflect *with* them, organize *their* ideas, surface resources — Rung 3a/4a, near-term.
  - **Client-memory (Flow 2):** "remembering clients" surfaces a *client's* history to the practitioner → requires the **client informed-consent surface** + `practitioner_cases.privacy_mode` + attribution. **Not** the triadic constitution, but **not** consent-free either.
- **Claim tier:** dyadic **Designed**; client-memory **Designed, gated on client consent.**
- **Gate to Stage 3:** the architecture is discovered, refined, and *validated with the practitioner*; client-memory consent surface is live.

### Stage 3 — Client Companion *(express the philosophy faithfully)*
Only after Stages 1–2 work do clients interact with a companion that expresses the practitioner's philosophy. The companion is not generic — it carries something already discovered, refined, and validated.

- **Flow:** 3 (architecture → clients). **= Legacy Sessions M4.**
- **Claim tier:** **Vision — constitutionally BLOCKED.** Requires the **triadic / multi-party consent constitution** (undrafted) + attribution to the practitioner + `crossing_allowed` at the practitioner→client boundary.
- **This is the headline ("your wisdom carried to every client between sessions") and the most blocked.** It is the destination, not the demo.

### Stage 1.4 — Continuation beyond the author *(the constitutional frontier)*
The practitioner's work becomes larger than their own sessions: their thinking keeps evolving, a community contributes, methods improve, the body of work stays alive rather than frozen. This is the move from **Legacy** (preserving a past) to **living transmission** (a practice that keeps developing while becoming available to others).

- **This is a frontier, not an extension of Stage 3.** Every crossing in `LEGACY_SESSIONS_SPEC` §3 assumes the practitioner is a **live calibration source** — *"the person remains the source of calibration."* Stage 4 contemplates the architecture continuing when the author is **no longer in the loop** (stepped back, community-authored evolution, posthumous). The moment the calibration source can no longer calibrate, the offer→authorship crossing has **no author to receive the offer.**
- **The unanswered questions:** Who calibrates when the author cannot? Whose authorship governs a change the original author never saw? Does community contribution dilute or extend the architecture — and who decides? None of these are answered by the triadic constitution; they need their own.
- **Claim tier:** **Vision — beyond blocked; not yet specifiable.** Name it as a direction. **Do not design it** until Stages 1–3 exist and the live-author crossings are proven. Calling this progression "inevitable" is the risk: it is a cliff dressed as a ramp.

## 2. Why the order is forced (not chosen)

| Stage | Could you skip ahead? | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| **1 → 2** | Build Practitioner Intelligence without capture? | Stage 2's value (prep, reflection, organizing) is *thin* without the architecture Stage 1 discovers — you'd be a generic assistant. |
| **2 → 3** | Launch the Client Companion without Practitioner Intelligence? | The companion would express an *unvalidated* architecture to third parties — and the triadic constitution that would even permit it does not exist. |

You cannot honestly reach Stage 3 before Stage 1, because Stage 3 expresses what Stage 1 discovers. The governance and the product agree.

## 3. The repeatable process this instantiates

Larry is not the first *customer*; he is the first **translation partner.** The deployment sequence is the visible half of a repeatable process — the part that is harder to copy than any feature because it fuses methodology + software architecture + collaborative discovery:

1. **Discover** a practitioner's life's work (Stage 1).
2. **Translate** it into a coherent architecture (Stage 1 → 2).
3. **Configure** the AIN engine to express that architecture (Stage 2 → 3).
4. **Launch** a sovereign platform that grows with both practitioner and clients (Stage 3+).

**The honest edge (epistemic discipline):** one successful partner demonstrates the process is *possible*, not that it is *repeatable*. Repeatability is earned across **independent** partners — a single case cannot discriminate "real repeatable methodology" from "worked once with this person." (Same discipline as the orientation-architecture candidate: induced convergence from one lens is a candidate, not an established invariant.) Larry validates feasibility; the moat is claimed only as independent deployments converge.

## 4. The convergence as a candidate invariant (not yet earned)

The §0 convergence — *the order the product wants and the order the architecture permits are the same order* — is worth naming as a **candidate**, with the same discipline applied to everything else here. It is **not yet an invariant.**

**First, which kind of claim is it?**
- **Discovered law** (it happens to be true that desirable order always equals permissible order), or
- **Declared design discipline** (we *choose* to pursue only products where the orders coincide, and we *design* so they do)?

These have different burdens. A law needs evidence across cases it did not author; a discipline needs only to be honored. Today the honest reading leans **declared discipline**: the *permissible* order is forced (you cannot express what you have not discovered), and we *chose* a product whose *desirable* order matched. That they coincided here is real and good — but partly because we selected for it.

**Falsification condition (what would make it informative):** a case where the order the product wants and the order the architecture permits **diverge**, forcing a choice. If that case can never arise, the claim is vacuous (true by construction). If it can arise and we still find them aligned in a domain we did **not** hand-pick, the claim earns standing.

**The test that earns invariant status:** the principle **predicts the build order in an independent practitioner domain** (not flourishing, not hand-selected) *before* that domain is built — and is right. Until then: a strong candidate, held as a discipline, not asserted as a law. (Cf. `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`: we do not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's — including the story that we have found a law.)

**Sharpening (Kelly, 2026-06-25) — the narrower, falsifiable form.** The deeper claim is not that *every* product aligns with governance, but: **to the extent a practice is *tacit*, faithful expression requires prior discovery** — explicit knowledge can be expressed without discovery; tacit wisdom cannot. This is narrower, falsifiable, and testable across independent practitioners, and it is now carried as the cross-program hypothesis in `docs/research/ARCHITECTURAL_TRANSLATION_STUDY_2026-06-25.md` §6.
