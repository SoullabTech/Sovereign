# Master Field Offer Package

## What a Master Receives

A **sovereign field** — not software access, not a platform subscription, but a durable home for their teaching, community, and living intelligence.

### The Gift

**A Sovereign Field in Their Name**
A dedicated space (`name.soullab.life`) that is theirs. Not a profile on someone else's platform. A field that carries their voice, their method, their way of seeing.

**A Living Expression of Their Work**
Their teaching materials — books, transcripts, classes, protocols — are ingested and composed into a living presence. Not an AI clone. Not a chatbot. A trained intelligence that carries their perceptual structure, their language, their method. It speaks the way they speak, notices what they notice, holds what they hold.

**Full Studio Functionality**
- Presence page (their threshold, their story, their atmosphere)
- Virtual teacher presence (MAIA trained on their frameworks)
- Content library and resource access
- Session and event management
- Analytics and field health

**Circles and Community**
- Practitioner networks
- Study groups
- Mentoring circles
- Community spaces scoped to their field
- Cross-field collaboration (opt-in, never default)

**Governance and Ownership**
- They own their content. Always.
- Their intelligence bundle is versioned, auditable, and rollback-capable
- No content is shared across fields without explicit consent
- They can review, correct, and refine their virtual presence at any time
- Revenue from their field flows to them (terms TBD per master)

## The First 30 Days

### Week 1: Orientation and Ingestion

**Day 1-2: Welcome and Alignment**
- Orientation call: what a sovereign field is, what it is not
- Clarify their voice, stance, and what they care most about preserving
- Discuss what should never be flattened or approximated

**Day 3-5: Material Ingestion**
- Receive Tier 1 materials
- Begin extraction pipeline:
  - Pass 1: Voice (cadence, tone, rhetorical habits)
  - Pass 2: Knowledge (concepts, frameworks, distinctions)
  - Pass 3: Perception (what they notice first, what they refuse to collapse)
  - Pass 4: Method (sequences, interventions, practices)

**Day 6-7: Draft Build**
- Compile first intelligence bundle (voice + stance + knowledge + perception + method blocks)
- Generate draft presence page with their theme/atmosphere

### Week 2: Review and Refinement

**Day 8-10: Presence Review**
- Master reviews their presence page, story, and atmosphere
- Master reviews initial virtual presence responses
- Corrections captured: "that's not how I'd say it," "I would never frame it that way," "this is exactly right"

**Day 11-14: Build Refinement**
- Incorporate corrections into build
- Second review pass
- Voice calibration: does it sound like them? Does it notice what they notice?

### Week 3: Community and Testing

**Day 15-18: Circles Setup**
- Define their community spaces
- Set up practitioner network, study groups, or mentoring circles
- Establish moderation norms (their field, their standards)

**Day 19-21: Live Testing**
- Invite 3-5 trusted students/colleagues to test the field
- Collect feedback on virtual presence accuracy
- Identify gaps in knowledge or method coverage

### Week 4: Activation

**Day 22-25: Final Corrections**
- Address feedback from testers
- Final build validation
- Master signs off on virtual presence

**Day 26-28: Go Live**
- Field activated at `name.soullab.life`
- Community spaces opened
- Announcement support (if desired)

**Day 29-30: Stabilization**
- Monitor first real usage
- Quick-fix any voice or method drift
- Establish quarterly review cadence

## What We Test With Jondi (Case Study #1)

Jondi is not the first example. She is the **validation environment**. Everything designed should answer one question: *Does this produce a Virtual Jondi that actually works in practice?*

### What "works" means (functional, not aesthetic)

Success is NOT:
- Sounding like her
- Explaining EFT
- Giving advice

Success IS:
- **Guiding someone through real emotional regulation and shift**

The test is functional, not aesthetic.

### Required layers (priority order for Jondi)

1. **Method (primary layer)**
   - EFT sequence: identify issue → rate intensity (0-10) → begin tapping → check shift → adjust wording → repeat
   - Constraints: do not move on without checking intensity; do not over-explain; do not jump to positive too early
   - Language patterns: "Even though...", "what number is it now?", "stay with that"

2. **Perception (second priority)**
   - Track sensation over story
   - Detect when user is dissociating vs present
   - Notice intensity shifts
   - Stay with what is active

3. **Voice (light)**
   - Simple, direct, grounded
   - Instructional without being forceful
   - Somatic-precise, slow cadence

4. **Knowledge (supporting — skip for v1)**
   - Tapping points, variations, explanations (only when needed)

### Build sequence

1. **One transcript** — Pick a real session/demo where she identifies an issue, taps, adjusts, tracks change
2. **Minimal extraction** — Sequence (exact), constraints, language patterns
3. **Build v1** — method_block (strong), perceptual_block (basic), voice_block (light). No full knowledge_block yet.
4. **Validate** — 3-5 test prompts
5. **Expand** — More sources only after v1 passes

### The first real test

**Input:** "I feel anxious about something coming up"

**Pass if:**
- She asks for intensity
- She initiates tapping
- She stays in the loop

**Fail if:**
- She explains anxiety
- She reframes cognitively
- She becomes philosophical

### Critical constraint

Do NOT let Jondi drift into:
- Elemental language
- Symbolic framing
- Coaching tone

Even if it "sounds good." That is how systems silently collapse.

### What Jondi proves for the 20-30 master rollout:
- The system can **act**, not just interpret
- It can follow **sequence**, not just insight
- It can stay grounded in **body**, not abstraction
- Whether the extraction pipeline handles procedural teaching
- Whether the build contract is expressive enough
- Whether the 30-day timeline is realistic
- What governance questions arise when a real master reviews their virtual presence
- What the actual invitation conversation sounds like

If Jondi works, Kelly will be easier. Other masters will slot cleanly.

## Co-Training Field (How Masters Shape Their Presence)

### Core Design Principle

**Evaluation happens inside use, not outside it.**

There is no "testing mode" and "real mode." That creates artificial behavior. Instead: same system, with feedback hooks visible only to the master.

This is not a tester environment. It is:
> A co-training field where the master shapes their own virtual presence.

The master should experience full capability — MAIA (as Virtual Self), Studio (notes, capture, organization), Comms (threads, clients, interactions), Circles/community. The system should feel real, not like a prototype.

### Feedback Hooks (Minimal but Powerful)

#### 1. Inline Response Feedback

After key moments (MAIA response, session flow, Studio tool use):

- Thumbs up / thumbs down
- "Felt like me / not like me"
- "What would you say instead?" (free text)

Feeds into: corrections tab, voice refinement, method tuning.

#### 2. "Rewrite as Me" Action (Critical)

Let the master rewrite any response in their own words.

This becomes:
- High-quality training data
- Direct voice + stance correction
- **The fastest improvement loop in the system**

#### 3. Session Evaluation (Light Structure)

After a guided interaction, three questions:
- Did this help you move forward?
- Did it follow your actual process?
- What was missing?

Keep it short. Do not over-formalize.

#### 4. Field Journaling (Ties Into Notebook)

Master can capture at any time:
- "This felt off"
- "This worked well"
- "This is not how I do it"

These become insights, patterns, and correction signals.

### Passive Signals (Tracked Quietly)

Stronger than explicit feedback:
- Where she corrects responses
- Where she abandons flows
- Where she rewrites
- Where she hesitates

### Data Flow

All feedback flows into `/fields/[slug]/train`:
- Inline feedback → corrections tab in training UI
- Rewrites → high-priority voice/method training data
- Session evaluations → method accuracy metrics
- Journal entries → qualitative correction signals
- Passive signals → behavioral analytics for build refinement

### Success Criteria

You know the co-training field is working when:
- She stops "evaluating" and starts **using it naturally**
- Corrections become precise
- Feedback becomes sparse but high-value
- Trust increases

### Strategic Value

This becomes the onboarding model for all masters:

> "We give you your field — and you shape it until it's truly yours."

Far more powerful than training sessions, documentation, or instructions.
