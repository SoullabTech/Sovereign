## Purpose

# I. THE ONE-WAY BARRIER — GOVERNING CONSTRAINT

This is the single most important clause in this mandate.

```
MFR-001  ──────────────────────►  future hybrid synthesis        PERMITTED
MFR-001  ──────╳──────────────►  CMC-001 census findings         FORBIDDEN
CMC-001 findings  ─────────────►  later MFR comparison/synthesis PERMITTED
```

**Frontier output may never reach CMC-001's evidence loop while the census is establishing what MAIA actually does.**

Rationale, stated plainly: CMC-001 §XVIII exists because an uncustodied design target was allowed to become a normative reference for an evidence program. MFR-001 is that same shape at far greater scale — richer, better sourced, more persuasive, and therefore more dangerous to an evidence loop. A census that already knows what good architecture looks like will find it.

Operative rules:

1. No MFR-001 artifact may be cited by a CMC-001 unit, unit return, or ledger.
2. No CMC-001 executor may be given MFR-001 material as orientation, context, or reading.
3. The two programs may not share an executor within a unit.
4. CMC-001 findings **may** flow into MFR-001 comparison and synthesis. The barrier is one-way, not mutual.
5. If an MFR-001 finding appears to bear urgently on a CMC-001 question, that is not grounds to cross the barrier. Record it and wait for the synthesis phase.

Violation is a stop condition: `STOPPED_BARRIER_BREACH`.

The barrier remains absolute until CMC-001 has frozen its observational findings.

# II. THREE EPISTEMIC CLASSES — NEVER MERGE

Frontier material arrives from three distinct origins with three distinct authorities. Conflating them recreates the §XVIII defect internally.

| Class | Origin | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| `LITERATURE` | Published research, verified to exist | Evidence about what has been demonstrated, at its own replication level |
| `FOUNDER_DESIGN` | Founder-authored design intuitions and aspirations | Direction and intent. **Not research findings.** Never cited as evidence |
| `MAIA_PRINCIPLE` | Existing relational design principles already ruled | Binding on evaluation criteria, not on what the literature says |

Every claim in every MFR-001 artifact carries its class, and the class must remain distinguishable all the way through the program to final synthesis.

> **A beautiful idea does not become a research finding because a paper sounds similar to it.**

## Ring-fenced as `FOUNDER_DESIGN`

Explicitly **not** literature, regardless of adjacent published work:

* **Relational memory** — memory belonging to the history of the relationship rather than to either party: shared language, repaired misunderstandings, recurring questions, trust boundaries, ways of communicating that emerged between them.
* **Memory metabolism** — experience → holding → integration → differentiation → consolidation → fading / supersession / transformation.
* **Contextual forgetting** — preserved but no longer influential, as distinct from deleted.
* **Memory reconsolidation** — when a person's relationship to an old event changes, preserve the event and record the changed meaning of it; never rewrite the event.
* **Polyphonic memory** — tolerating meaningful contradiction rather than forcing database consistency. A person can simultaneously hold "my father loved me" and "my father failed me."
* **Field memory** — patterns emerging from relationships among events rather than individual statements, carried with deliberately weak assertion authority.
* **Embodied / multimodal continuity** — voice rhythm, pauses, prosody, writing cadence, images, place, and voluntarily supplied physiological signals, under strict consent and uncertainty boundaries.

These are among the most valuable ideas in the program **and** the ones most likely to be laundered into false citation. Adjacent literature may be cited as *related work*; it may never be cited as *support*.

## Ring-fenced as `MAIA_PRINCIPLE`

> **Depth of synthesis does not confer authority of assertion.**
> **Provenance and visibility are orthogonal.**
> **Merge while structured. Render last.**
> **Not maximum recall, but right relationship to memory — remembering enough that the person is genuinely known, while allowing most memory to remain quietly supportive of what is happening now.**

# III. TWO INDEPENDENT GRADING AXES

Citation validity and capability maturity are **different questions** and may not share a scale. Every candidate carries both.

## Axis A — Evidence quality (per cited work)

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `existence` | `VERIFIED` (resolved against publisher/arXiv API) · `UNVERIFIED` |
| `primary_source` | Direct source read · secondary summary only |
| `date_version` | Publication date and version inspected |
| `venue_status` | `PEER_REVIEWED` · `PREPRINT` · `UNKNOWN` |
| `replication` | `INDEPENDENTLY_CORROBORATED` · `SINGLE_GROUP` · `AUTHORS_CLAIM_ONLY` |
| `artifacts` | Code/data/weights available and inspected · claimed · absent |
| `benchmark_basis` | Which benchmark, which baselines, and whether it measures what the claim asserts |
| `limitations` | As stated by the authors, recorded verbatim in substance |

## Axis B — Capability maturity (per candidate direction)

`DEMONSTRATED` — reproduced results, inspectable artifacts, credible baselines
`PROMISING` — solid single-group results, plausible mechanism, not yet corroborated
`EARLY` — proposed and partially evidenced; mechanism outruns evidence
`SPECULATIVE` — reasoned direction without adequate empirical support, including all `FOUNDER_DESIGN`

**Existence verification makes a work admissible for review. It does not make it trustworthy.** A recent preprint with strong reported numbers is `PREPRINT / AUTHORS_CLAIM_ONLY / PROMISING` at best, regardless of how well its architecture fits MAIA. **Fit is not evidence.**

## The corpus is a seed, not a boundary — hard invariant

**These twenty works establish where the review begins. They do not establish where it ends.**

MFR-001 is charged with finding the cutting edge *and beyond*. It must be free — and is expected — to discover and admit:

* **newer work** published after the seed date, including work the daily watch surfaces;
* **adjacent disciplines** — cognitive science, memory research in psychology, archival and provenance theory, database temporality, distributed-systems lineage, HCI, clinical and therapeutic continuity practice;
* **negative results and failed replications**, which carry the same standing as positive findings and must not be filtered out for being unexciting;
* **implementations and systems** — open-source memory frameworks, production architectures, engineering write-ups — graded on the same two axes as papers;
* **benchmarks and evaluation methods**, including critiques of existing benchmarks;
* **capabilities with no adequate literature at all**, recorded as `SPECULATIVE` rather than omitted.

A frontier area whose survey consists only of seed works has almost certainly not been surveyed. Record what was searched, not merely what was found.

Unit 1 may add or reject works. Rejection is recorded, never silent.

## Epistemic object types — hypothesis under test

The proposed distinction between **event · statement · interpretation · pattern · hypothesis · orientation** is `FOUNDER_DESIGN / SPECULATIVE`, informed by but **not derived from** belief-state memory work.

MFR-001 must be able to conclude that six types are too many, too few, or wrongly cut.

## Memory influence governance — hypothesis under test

The proposed retrieval gate sequence — *relevant? → permitted? → current? → sufficiently warranted? → appropriate for this relationship? → appropriate to surface?* — is `FOUNDER_DESIGN`, motivated by published safety findings that accumulated personalization can bias later intent interpretation. The **motivation** is `LITERATURE`; the **gate design** is not.

Likewise the proposed five-way separation — **remembered ≠ retrievable ≠ influential ≠ speakable ≠ currently true** — is `FOUNDER_DESIGN` and must be testable against, not assumed by, the literature.

# VII. MAIA-SPECIFIC EVALUATION CRITERIA

Benchmark score is **not** the evaluation. Every candidate is scored against:

- Does this help MAIA know the person more **truthfully**?
- Does it **preserve evidence** — does experience survive synthesis?
- Can it recognize **change over time**, distinguishing what was true from what is true?
- Can memory remain **implicit** without losing provenance?
- Can inferred patterns remain **lightly held**?
- Can **relationships have distinct boundaries** — no cross-person leakage by similarity?
- Can the person **inspect, correct, and forget** what is remembered?
- Can MAIA distinguish **experience from interpretation**?
- Does it improve **presence**, rather than simply increase recall?

Existing benchmarks — LongMemEval, LoCoMo, MemoryAgentBench and successors — measure recall, temporal reasoning, updates, abstention and personalization. They are useful and must be used. They do not measure relational continuity. Whether MAIA needs an evaluation suite of her own is a **question for this program**, not a conclusion of it.

# XIII. WHERE THE LANES MEET

Neither lane produces architecture.

**CMC-001 asks what MAIA is.**
**MFR-001 asks what is becoming possible.**

Synthesis is a third act, separately authorized, possible only once:

* CMC-001 has returned and frozen what canonical-live MAIA **actually does**;
* MFR-001 has returned what is **demonstrably possible**, graded on both axes;
* MAIA's relational principles state what **ought** to be true.

Three inputs, none authoritative over the others, meeting at CMC-001 §XXV Phase 5 — which remains **conditional** on the census supporting a unified substrate at all.

## Hard invariant

> **MFR-001 must not make it harder for CMC-001 to conclude that MAIA's existing continuity is better than the frontier, or that no unified substrate is warranted.**

This protects the entire experiment. A frontier review that makes the null result unsayable has stopped being evidence and become advocacy.

The aim is not an intelligence that remembers more about a person. It is one capable of maintaining a differentiated relationship to the past: what happened, what was said, what changed, what may be true, what is inferred, what should remain implicit, what has become obsolete, and what deserves to influence this particular moment.
