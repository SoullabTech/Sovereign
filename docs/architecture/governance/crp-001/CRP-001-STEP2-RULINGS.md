## C1 — **D2 · separate programs**

CRP-001 and MRC-001 are not substitutes.

```text
CRP-001 → HOW repair is proven
MRC-001 → WHAT MAIA continuity work is authorized
```

- **CRP-001 owns the repair method**: crossing vocabulary, evidence schema,
  instrument admissibility, validator, chain registry, proof and disposition
  rules.

  founder-authored continuation exists.

  supply the missing product authorization.

  proof discipline.

D2's single-owner requirement is satisfied by **separating the surfaces**, not
by making two programs compete for one.

**MRC-001 remains HELD / INCOMPLETE — not retired.** Its truncation at §XVIII
still stands, and the missing tail is still the authorization boundary.

## C4 — **G2 · real traffic, metadata and candidate IDs only**

The witness may operate against real production traffic at the **minimum
metadata level necessary to prove crossings**.

**Permitted:** exchange/request IDs; candidate/memory IDs; memory class;
provenance identifiers; ranks and scores; selected/dropped disposition;
assembly location; token counts; timestamps; relationship/scope identifiers
only where already governed and necessary; candidate-bound presence/absence in
the final model request; hashes and fingerprints where useful.

**Not permitted:** generalized inspection of member conversations; raw memory
content; raw final prompt or request content merely for convenience; broad
member profiling; semantic interpretation of member material.

The witness is to be designed so **IDs and provenance survive the pipeline**,
proving

```text
RETRIEVED → SELECTED → ASSEMBLED → FINAL MODEL REQUEST
```

without reading the member's private prose.

**If a later crossing genuinely cannot be proven without content: STOP and
request a narrower G3 authority for that specific purpose. G2 does not
silently expand.**

### Benchmark attachment — same boundary

- **Synthetic personae:** full synthetic content permitted; label `synthetic`.
- **Real member-derived arms:** G2 applies — metadata and IDs only unless
  separately authorized.
- Do **not** export a member's memory set into an offline benchmark merely
  because the benchmark is "research."

> Real member data remains real member data regardless of whether it is being
> observed in production or assembled into an evaluation.

## Attachment A2 — longitudinal minima remain PROVISIONAL

C4 is settled; the numeric evidence-window thresholds are not. They are to be
calibrated from the actual instruments and the first longitudinal proof rather
than ratified in advance. `2 sessions / >1 subject` is **not** asserted to be
epistemically sufficient.

The evidence-window *principle* remains ACCEPTED house law.

## Consequence for the first build

C4 makes the witness cleaner, and the cleanliness is not a side effect —
it is the design constraint. **Architect candidate identity and provenance
through the pipeline rather than solving observability by logging private
content.** The privacy boundary and the better architecture are the same
choice.
