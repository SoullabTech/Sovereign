## Step 4 — chain registry

At minimum three classes:

```text
Canonical cognition chain
  EXISTS → ELIGIBLE → RETRIEVED → SELECTED → ASSEMBLED
  → FINAL MODEL REQUEST → USED → OBSERVABLE IN RESPONSE
  → EXPERIENCED CONTINUITY

Correction chain (mandate §9)
  OLD CLAIM → MEMBER CORRECTION → CORRECTION EVENT
  → OLD CLAIM STATUS → CURRENT REPRESENTATION → FUTURE RETRIEVAL
  → FINAL CONTEXT → FUTURE RESPONSE

Unit-declared substrate chains (schema §2.3)
  A → B → C …
```

Open questions:

1. Is the §9 correction chain frozen as written, or restated in stage
   vocabulary first? Its stage names do not currently match §2.4's style.
2. Do the twelve substrates in mandate §10 each get a registered chain, or do
   they declare per unit?
3. May a unit declare an unregistered chain at all — or must every chain be
   registered in advance? (Registration-in-advance is stricter and makes the
   validator simple; per-unit declaration is flexible and makes derivation
   structural rather than lookup-based.)
4. Do chains cross? A correction that must reach FINAL MODEL REQUEST touches
   both chains, and a crossing spanning two chains has no defined form.

Question 4 is the one likeliest to surface late as a malformed-crossing
rejection during real work.
