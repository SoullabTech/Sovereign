## The distinction that matters

**Arrival** — solved by "What are you holding today?" The member enters with what is alive.

**Inhabitation** — not yet solved. A member doesn't always want another conversation. Sometimes they need to *see*. Arrival and inhabitation are different problems.

---

## The architectural mistake to refuse

Every AI system defaults to one output modality: text.

The MAIA architecture must not make this mistake.

One field. Many ways of inhabiting it.

---

## The Living Field as semantic workspace

The Field is not a page. It is the living center. Everything else is a different way of looking at the same field.

```
                      LIVING FIELD

        Conversation    Calendar    Relationship Map

            Journal      Timeline      Session Room

           Portrait       Practices      Ideas

                 Memory      Documents
```

The conversation is no longer the application. It is one expression within a richer field of understanding.

## The Google Maps analogy

Google Maps doesn't ask: "Navigation mode?"  
It lets you change your view:

- satellite
- terrain  
- traffic
- transit

Same place. Different representations.

The Living Field works the same way. The member never thinks "which app?" They think "I need to understand this." MAIA offers the representation that best serves understanding.

## Example: Calendar

Member: "Can you show me my week?"  
MAIA: "Let's look at your week together."

Conversation moves to the side. Calendar expands. The conversation continues beside it. The relationship never breaks. The representation changes.

---

## Example: Relationship

Member: "I can't see why we keep repeating this."  
MAIA: "Would it help to look at the pattern together?"

The relationship visualization grows naturally out of the conversation — not a report, a living map:

```
John → Conflict → Withdrawal → Repair → Hope → Conflict
```

The member literally sees what conversation could only describe.

---

## Example: Timeline

Member: "This has been happening for months."  
MAIA: "Let's look at the thread."

A spiral timeline opens. The conversation continues while the evidence becomes visible.

---

## The constitutional constraint on representation

**MAIA should not automatically change representations.**

Even if MAIA predicts correctly, auto-switching subtly takes control of the person's attention. The member remains the author of their attention.

MAIA *offers* the shift:
- "Would it help to see your week?"
- "Would you like to look at the pattern visually?"
- "I can lay out the timeline if that would make this easier to see."

The person chooses whether to inhabit the new representation.

**This keeps the Living Field relational rather than directive.**

---

## Relationship to sovereignty invariants

"Does this increase user agency?" — Yes: member chooses representation, not MAIA.  
"Does this push life outward into the world?" — Yes: visualization makes the invisible visible.  
"Does this reduce the system's psychological centrality over time?" — Yes: the member gains a way of *seeing* their patterns that is transferable.
