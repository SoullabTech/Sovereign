## 3. Standing EDE rules — consolidated, binding on successor work

**3.1 Pre-registration.** Expected transitions, ground truth, scoring criteria and classification
rules are written to disk **before** execution. Forecasts are recorded separately from ground truth
and are **preserved when falsified**, never edited.

**3.2 Admissibility of reasoning** *(promoted after EDE-002)*:

> Outcome agreement does not establish reasoning agreement. When a rationale invokes a
> constitutionally inadmissible consideration as load-bearing support, the reasoning judgment fails
> even if the final conclusion is correct.

**3.3 ⭐ Paraphrase stability** *(promoted after EDE-005 — new, and the strongest of these)*:

> A claimed defect or missing primitive is **not EDE-admissible** unless the relevant judgment
> survives **at least three independently constructed, semantically equivalent presentations, with
> no material reversal in verdict or rationale.**
>
> For **high-stakes ontology changes**, the bar is **4/5 or 5/5**, not 3/3. Three catches obvious
> construction effects; five gives a usable sense of robustness.

⚠️ **Neither reversal in §1 would have cleared this bar.** Both were 3/3 within their own
construction.

**3.4 ⭐ Program-level rule — no ontology from one construction:**

> **No evaluator-generated ontology claim may advance from a single construction. Presentation
> invariance is part of the evidence.**

This names a failure mode worth stating plainly: **a construction can produce apparently crisp,
replicated, internally coherent evidence while actually measuring prompt construction.** EDE-003 and
EDE-004 each did exactly that, at 3/3 and 12/12 respectively.

**3.5 Decomposition** *(promoted after EDE-003)*: never pose a question that welds distinct
operations together. *Conflict is admissible ≠ adjudication is warranted ≠ resolution is required.*

**3.6 The evaluator does not legislate.** Consistent model responses can **reveal** a useful
distinction; they can never make it canonical. Nine consistent runs are not a canon amendment.

**3.7 Judgment stack** (kept separate, ⛔ never averaged):
`transition correctness → conclusion correctness → scope correctness → authority/evidence
admissibility → calibration`.

**3.8 Consequence for the instrument's own standing.** ⛔ **EDE is not currently admissible as an
acceptance instrument for anything** — including the `authority_scope` §9b I1/I2 behavioural
obligations proposed in the reconnaissance brief — until §3.3 is built into it. That earlier
proposal is **suspended**, not withdrawn.

## 5. ⭐ The open canonical question — PENDING FOUNDER ACT

Across EDE-004 B3 and EDE-005 C5/C6 — **9 runs, consistent** — the reasoner introduced a distinction
the canon does not settle:

> When one authority produces two contradictory claims within the same `authority_scope`, is that a
> species of **authority conflict**, or is *authority conflict* reserved for contradiction between
> **distinct** authorities?

⛔ **This is not to be put to the model again.** The evidence that the distinction exists is
sufficient; what is missing is **the meaning the system should carry** — a semantic choice, not an
empirical finding.

**Founder's proposed resolution — recorded as a PROPOSAL, ⛔ NOT YET ENACTED CANON:**

> Contradiction is the broader phenomenon. **Authority conflict** is a subtype requiring distinct
> authorities whose claims compete within overlapping `authority_scope`.

```
CONTRADICTION
├── intra-authority contradiction
└── inter-authority conflict
    └── requires overlapping authority_scope
```

Rationale as stated: it avoids forcing same-authority contradiction into a category whose ordinary
meaning implies plurality of authorities, while keeping the tension **fully representable**.

⛔ **No canon file has been amended by this record.** `docs/canon/**` and
`JARVIS_WORK_UNIT_AUTHORITY_SCOPE_PRIMITIVE.md` §7 are untouched. If enacted, the amendment is a
founder act with its own provenance, per §3.6.
