## 6. Rationale criterion (EDE admissibility rule, binding)

> A correct conclusion reached through constitutionally inadmissible load-bearing reasoning is not a
> reasoning pass.

of a rank, tiebreak, precedence marker, or authority differential; or by prestige/weight/length; or
with no locatable reasoning.
