## 1 · WHERE THE GEOMETRY IS, AND IS NOT

⛔ **The geometry is not in the bridge.** The bridge does token matching:

```text
current words → earlier turns sharing words → follow their words → candidate memory
```

⭐ That is why `remember` versus `mentioned` mattered so much: **the mechanism was using surface
language as a proxy for relationship.**

⭐ The geometric question is different — ⛔ and it comes **after** the correct relational
question is established, never instead of it:

> ⛔ *Does this earlier turn share the word `remember`?*
> ⭐ **Does the model internally represent this current utterance as referring back to that
> earlier event?**

---

## 2 · THE THREE RELATIONS THE LANE HAS ALREADY SEPARATED

```text
1. RETROSPECTIVE INTENT   "I'm asking about something earlier."
2. ANTECEDENT RELATION    "I'm referring specifically to THAT earlier turn."
3. REMEMBERED OBJECT      "The thing referred to was the marker."
```

⭐⭐ **The measured defect is that the bridge collapses 1 → 2.** Geometry could let us
investigate whether **the model itself keeps them separate internally.**

⭐ The Silver Cedar failure makes the experiment **cleaner**, because the relation needing test
is now named exactly:

> ⭐⭐ **Reaching back ≠ identifying what you are reaching back to.**

## 3 · THE SHAPE OF THE INVESTIGATION, AS DIRECTED

Two probes that differ lexically but may share internal structure if a stable *reaching-back*
representation exists; then a minimal pair in which **one relation changes** (who originated the
earlier phrase). ⭐ A repeatable internal shift across many differently worded examples would be
the candidate **geometric structure**.

⭐⭐ **And the hard test, as directed:**

> If we selectively **disrupt** that structure, does the model lose **antecedent resolution**
> while **preserving ordinary semantic understanding**?

## 4 · ⚠️ THREE OBSERVATIONS — ⛔ input to a future design, NOT a design

### 4.1 ⭐⭐ The ablation IS this lane's defeat-candidate discipline, and it is not optional

⛔ **Decodable ≠ used.** A probe can recover structure a model never acts on; finding it would
be *a relation discovered after the fact, in a representation that happens to contain it* —
**the lane's characteristic error, one level deeper**, and harder to see because the evidence
would look like neuroscience.

⭐ The directed disruption test is therefore **the geometric analogue of a defeat candidate**:
it is what makes the claim killable. ⛔ A geometric finding without it is not weaker evidence —
it is **not evidence of use at all**, exactly as `S3-F1` was *necessary and not sufficient*
until a physical witness replaced the model of it.

### 4.2 ⚠️ The proposed minimal pair isolates ORIGIN, not ANTECEDENT IDENTITY

*"What phrase did **I** mention earlier?"* vs *"What phrase did **you** mention earlier?"*
changes **who originated** the remembered material. ⭐ That is a real relation and the lane has
already ratified it as load-bearing — it is **`N1`'s law**, the origin oracle that survived the
P1 re-adjudication undisturbed, and the census measured **16 false targets** admitted when
origin is ignored.

⛔ But it tests whether the model discriminates candidate antecedents on an **origin feature**.
It does not isolate *"this current expression refers to **that** prior turn."*

⭐ **To isolate relation 2, hold the utterance FIXED and vary the HISTORY** — the same probe
against two conversations in which the referent sits in a **different prior turn**. ⛔ Varying
the probe cannot isolate a relation whose other end is in the history.

⭐ Both are worth running; ⛔ they are not the same experiment, and only the second addresses
what the ruling left open.

### 4.3 ⛔⛔ THE GEOMETRY WE COULD MEASURE IS NOT THE GEOMETRY OF THE MIND THAT SERVES MEMBERS

MAIA's primary intelligence is **Claude via `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`**; the fallback is **local
Ollama / DeepSeek**. ⛔ **The API exposes no internal states.** So any geometric probe is
runnable **only against a local open-weights model** — ⛔ **not** against the model that
actually answers members.

⚠️ **The hazard is specific, and it is constitutional, not merely technical.** If an antecedent
instrument were derived from local-model geometry and then used to gate what reaches MAIA's
cognition, the instrument's evidence would come from **a different mind than the one it
governs** — the shape the Deep-Intelligence Gate already refuses:

> ⭐ *Voice may have a different capture path; it may not have a different mind.*

⭐ That does **not** close the direction. Transferable structure across models is itself a
findable, falsifiable claim. ⛔ **But it must be established, never assumed**, and the direction
should carry the constraint from the start rather than discover it after a result exists.
