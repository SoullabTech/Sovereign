## The separation

> **MAIA lives the relationship. AIN preserves and governs the relationship.
> JARVIS studies how relational intelligence actually develops.**

⛔ JARVIS should not become another MAIA. MAIA is the participant; JARVIS would be
the scientific steward.

The question it would exist to ask:

> ⛔ Not *what do we believe about relationship?* ⛔ Not *what does the
> architecture claim to do?* But: **which relational hypotheses have survived
> contact with members, perturbation, negative controls, ablation, production
> behaviour, and time?**

## The proposed loop

```
OBSERVE → DIFFERENTIATE → PERTURB → ABLATE → COMPARE → CORRECT → ACCUMULATE
```

**Observe** relational trajectories — not "MAIA remembered X" but what changed
across encounters: retrieval, interpretation, correction, attention, response
structure, uncertainty, member-visible consequence. **Differentiate** mechanisms —
retrieval? relationship state? elemental mode? model capability? prompt wording?
accident? ⛔ refuse to call all of these "relational intelligence." **Perturb** —
change wording preserving meaning; remove spiritual language; change sequence;
introduce correction. **Ablate** — turn off a memory class or mode; if removing a
purported mode produces no measurable difference, it is **behaviourally null**.
**Compare** longitudinally, controlling for retrieved facts. **Correct** — preserve
prior beliefs *and the evidence that overturned them*, so the research is itself
corrigible.

## The evidence staircase

⭐ These are **not four ways of saying "the pattern looks real."** They
progressively test **recognizability → invariance → separability → causal
dependence**:

| | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | stylistic recurrence (Fire/Water read differently) | weak |
| 2 | invariance under vocabulary perturbation | stronger |
| 3 | discriminative predictability above chance | stronger again |
| 4 | **selective impairment under ablation** | causal |
| 5 | generalizes across models and members | discovery |

⭐ This is the same move that closed the traffic witness: 97 flat rupture rows
were refused as proof until there was a live-path control, a real trigger in
production traffic, and a 1:1 pre-deploy calibration.

## Developmental axes

⛔ Better than "memory accuracy" or "user satisfaction," because **each names a
failure that looks like success**:

- greater differentiation **without** fragmentation
- greater continuity **without** fixation
- greater familiarity **without** presumed intimacy
- greater interpretation **without** increased authority
- greater sensitivity **without** surveillance
- greater collective intelligence **without** loss of individuality
- greater learning **without** loss of corrigibility
- ⭐ **continuity without identity foreclosure** *(added 2026-08-14)*

### On the fourth axis

> MAIA should be able to accumulate a remarkably coherent understanding of a
> person **while remaining capable of discovering that its accumulated
> understanding was partial, contextual, outdated, or wrong.** Otherwise
> long-term memory eventually turns familiarity into **ontology**.

⭐⭐ This is the ratified invariant pointed at its hardest case:

> *No representation of the system may acquire more authority simply by being
> copied into a more durable or more convenient store.*

**An accumulated model of a person is probably the most consequential
"convenient store" MAIA will ever possess.**

The measurement discipline that follows: ⛔ not *"which condition produced better
conversation?"* but ***"what changed in the organization of cognition, and what
caused the change?"*** — the difference between product analytics and JARVIS.
⛔ JARVIS would not optimize MAIA toward engagement; it would study how MAIA
becomes relationally more capable **without becoming more presumptuous,
coercive, or epistemically inflated**.

## Possible artefact

A **Relational Intelligence Experimental Corpus**:
`hypothesis → predicted observable → intervention → control → production witness
→ result → confidence → contradictions → next experiment`.

Potential bridge: if external methods can measure internal representational
geometry, JARVIS becomes the layer correlating *"this representational trajectory
changed"* with *"here is the exact relational event, memory condition,
correction, mode, member state, and control condition associated with it."*

> ⭐⭐⭐ **JARVIS automates the work, but not your authority.**
