## The Year Ahead
*A living conversation between your soul and time.*

Your birth chart describes the landscape you carry through your whole life. The transits describe the weather moving across it. Some years ask us to build, some to let go, some to fall in love, some quietly prepare us for a life we cannot yet imagine. This isn't a prediction — it's an invitation to walk with the season consciously.

### 1. A Year of Expanding Horizons
*Traces to: Pluto entering your natal 9th House · Uranus conjunct natal Jupiter*

The world becomes larger than the one you've known. Travel, study, new philosophies, unexpected teachers, questions that reshape how you see yourself. This feels less like *finding answers* than allowing larger questions to appear — and trusting that the appearing is the point. Let the horizon move.

### 2. Trusting Your Own Voice
*Traces to: Uranus square natal Sun · Uranus square natal Mercury · Jupiter crossing natal Sun*

This is an awakening of authorship. This year invites you to trust insights that arrive unexpectedly. New ideas may appear before they make complete sense — give them room. Not every revelation asks for immediate action; some simply ask to be welcomed. Your voice is becoming more deeply your own.

### 3. Home Becomes Sacred
*Traces to: Jupiter entering your natal 4th House · Jupiter crossing natal Sun*

You may discover that home becomes more than a place — it becomes a source of strength. Creating beauty where you live, deepening the relationships closest to you, or simply learning to feel more at home within yourself may become one of this year's quiet gifts.

### 4. The Spiritual Deepening
*Traces to: Neptune entering your natal 11th House · Pluto conjunct natal Neptune · Chiron aspects to your Sun, Midheaven & Neptune*

This is the underlying current — and it is initiation, not confusion. There are seasons when life becomes less certain and more mysterious, not because something has gone wrong, but because something larger is asking to be born. This year may invite you to trust intuition more deeply, to listen beneath appearances, and to let old certainties soften into a wider understanding.
