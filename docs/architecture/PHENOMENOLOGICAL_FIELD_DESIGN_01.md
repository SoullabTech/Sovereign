# PHENOMENOLOGICAL-FIELD-DESIGN-01
## Phenomenological Field Design

**Status:** central method within Living Field Architecture
**Date:** 29 September 2026

Phenomenological Field Design asks what the member actually encounters in experience.

Its questions include:

- What does this place feel like?
- What becomes foreground?
- What recedes?
- What changes with attention?
- What remains continuous?
- What does the body encounter?
- How does time behave?
- What relation becomes perceptible?
- What becomes possible?
- What changes in the member's perception?
- Can the member return without losing orientation?

It treats interface composition, atmosphere, motion, spatial relation, timing, and semantic depth as parts of one lived field.

It does not infer inner truth from appearance.

> **Phenomenology describes the encounter; it does not grant the system authority over the person.**
