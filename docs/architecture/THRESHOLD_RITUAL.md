# The Threshold Ritual — how people enter the work

_Held design direction (not built). Emerged 2026-06-03 from onboarding Heather; the point is **not** onboarding one person — it's discovering a repeatable way people cross into Soullab's work. Companion to `docs/strategy/HEATHER_START_HERE.md`._

> This is a **Cat-1 preserved direction**: a direction held, not yet authorized or built. The "Now" step is done with documents and a human conversation. "Next" and "Later" are deliberate, governed builds — recorded here so the pattern isn't lost, not so it's assumed live.

## Two functions, kept distinct

- **Invitation** is human. It comes from a person (Kelly), directly, and preserves relationship. It is not automated.
- **Orientation** comes from MAIA — what we're building, why it matters, where you might contribute.

Conflating them turns a welcome into onboarding software. Keep them separate.

## The sequence

### Now — handled by people + documents
- The person extends the invitation directly (Kelly → Heather).
- MAIA's authored welcome (`HEATHER_START_HERE.md`) provides orientation.
- The four strategy docs sit beneath it.
- Then: a real conversation. Information transfer is not the goal — locating someone in the work is.

### Next — a reusable "Start Here" inside MAIA
A **reusable threshold**, not a person-specific greeting. Not _"Hello Heather"_ — rather:

> _Welcome. Before we begin, here's what we're building, why it matters, and where you might contribute._

The same structure later welcomes practitioners, collaborators, advisors, therapists, copywriters, founding-cohort members. **Different content, same threshold.** Build it as how _anyone_ enters a Soullab initiative.

### Later — living orientation as a guided conversation
The most interesting form is not a static page but a conversation MAIA holds — e.g.:

- _Tell me about the work you're most passionate about right now._
- _What drew you here?_
- _Which part of this feels most alive?_
- _What feels confusing?_
- _Where do you naturally see yourself contributing?_

This turns onboarding from **information transfer** into **relationship formation** — which is far more Soullab. Likely belongs inside the Living Field.

## The load-bearing boundary (this is what keeps it honest)

Today MAIA cannot speak this to a newcomer from lived knowledge — the welcome was _authored_ in her voice. **That limit is healthy.** It prevents the illusion of relationship. Right now MAIA can offer **orientation**; it cannot honestly claim **familiarity**.

So the future capability to build is **not**:

> ❌ _MAIA remembers Heather._

It is:

> ✅ _MAIA helps Heather locate herself within the field._

Those are very different experiences. The first manufactures intimacy; the second offers a place to stand. Only the second is consistent with the canon — no false intimacy, no attachment capture, sovereignty first (`docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`). Any "Next/Later" build must be **honest and visible** (no stealth seeding of a member's experience) and must locate, never feign memory.

## Why this matters more than onboarding one person

Done well, this isn't an artifact for Heather — it's the **threshold ritual** for everyone who enters the work, and a place where the same discipline that runs the product and the discovery engine (_orientation before familiarity_, like _recognition before positioning_ and _observation before interpretation_) shows up at the very first moment of contact.
