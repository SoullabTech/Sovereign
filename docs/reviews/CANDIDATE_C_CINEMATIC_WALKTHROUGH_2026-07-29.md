## Verbatim proposal (unedited)

**Framing:** not a feature review — an *experiential* review.
Question: *Can someone arrive wanting to write a book and naturally discover the
environment without ever needing to learn the software?*

**Success metric:** not "can they find every feature?" but *"Do they always know what to
do next, while gradually discovering that the environment is richer than they first
realized?"* North Star: **How a life becomes a book.**

### Four complete walks
1. **First Arrival** — never seen AIN. What do they believe this place is within 10
   seconds? Do they understand the destination is their work, not MAIA? Can they begin
   writing immediately? Do they know they can ignore most of the environment today?
   Success: *"I'm in a writing environment,"* not *"I'm learning software."*
2. **Returning Author** — has drafts, notes, conversations, gathered material. Do they
   naturally understand Continue Writing → Gather Ideas → Discover → Refine → Publish
   without instruction?
3. **Power User** — writing daily. Can they discover Evidence Room, version history,
   research, gatherings, relationships, continuity, publishing — without overwhelm?
4. **Publisher Journey** — Design → ISBN → Cover → Editions → Publication → Marketing →
   Sharing. One continuous environment, or five disconnected tools?

### Lenses applied to every screen
- **Relationship** — who am I with? (Author · MAIA · my book · readers · publisher ·
  editor · community). Is this always obvious?
- **Agency** — who is acting? Me, MAIA, or automation? Can members always tell?
- **Orientation** — Where am I? Why am I here? What can I do? What happens next?
- **Progress** — *"I'm closer to finishing my book"* vs *"I clicked another feature."*
- **Discoverability** — no documentation, no training; small discoveries continually
  appear. The Studio should reward curiosity.
- **Animation** — motion communicates **relationship, not decoration**: doors opening,
  rooms revealing, drawers unfolding, manuscripts becoming books, conversations appearing
  *alongside* the work rather than replacing it. Each animation should teach the
  environment.
- **Navigation / progressive disclosure** — week 1 ≈ 20% of the environment; month ≈ 50%;
  months ≈ almost everything. Not by locking features away, but by letting the environment
  reveal itself as the member's work matures.

### Proposed new artifact — Member Journey Storyboard
Not wireframes, not Figma. A film:
`Minute 0 "I have an idea for a book" → Minute 1 I enter → Minute 3 I'm writing →
Minute 8 I discover Gather → Minute 15 I ask MAIA something → Week 2 I discover Refine →
Month 3 I'm preparing publication → Publication day: my book leaves the Studio.`
*"If that storyboard is compelling, the UI will almost always become simpler because every
screen has to justify its place in the story."*

### Per-room output format
Purpose · First impression · Navigation · Motion · Discovery · Decision load ·
Relationship (the work remains central; MAIA is only one resident of the environment).

Intended to be walkable with Jondi, Andrea, Nathan, or a brand-new author against the same
experiential standard rather than implementation details.

# Addendum — second statement (same sitting, 2026-07-29)

Preserved verbatim in substance; unedited framing.

## Product-category claim

Not an authoring tool — **a place where wisdom becomes shareable.** Most writing software
optimizes for *producing text*; the Author's Studio should optimize for helping a person
**discover, shape, and eventually share what only they can contribute.** The Studio is an
environment, not another app; the destination is always the work; MAIA is one resident
among several ways of reaching it, never the gatekeeper of authorship.

Most platforms help people **create content**. Very few help people **cultivate a body of
work.** After ten years inside the Studio a member should not feel they used software —
they should feel they had *a trusted place where every meaningful piece of their work
gradually found its home.*

## Who it is for

Not "authors." People who have lived enough life to know there is something worth giving
away, but don't yet know how to make it receivable: coaches, therapists, educators, clergy,
physicians, researchers, entrepreneurs, artists, parents and grandparents, decades-long
practitioners — anyone with accumulated wisdom but not necessarily a manuscript.

> They rarely think *"I want to write a book."* They think *"There are things I've learned
> that shouldn't disappear with me."*

**Friction for visionary minds** (incl. ADHD traits): the struggle is not a lack of ideas
but notebooks, voice recordings, conversations, sticky notes, workshops, podcasts,
journals, unfinished chapters, photographs, outlines — all pointing at the same body of
work. *The Studio shouldn't ask them to become organized before they begin. It should
gently organize around them.*

## Every room answers one anxiety

| Room | Anxiety it answers |
|---|---|
| Capture | "I'll lose this idea." |
| Gather | "Everything is scattered." |
| Discover | "What's actually emerging?" |
| Structure | "How do these pieces fit together?" |
| Write | "Can I stay with the work today?" |
| Refine | "Is this communicating what I mean?" |
| Publish | "How do I share this?" |

## Discovery as encouragement

You drop in a voice memo, three journal entries, yesterday's chapter. Nothing flashy. Over
the next few minutes small moments appear: a thread quietly connects today's reflection
with something written six months ago; a recurring metaphor is gently surfaced; a chapter
begins to feel heavier because evidence for it has accumulated. *Those moments don't feel
like AI. They feel like remembering yourself.*

## Intention-first navigation

Not *"where is the right tool?"* but *"what do I need today?"* — I need to catch an idea ·
untangle something · keep writing · understand my own themes · prepare this for readers ·
publish. **"The environment chooses the room. Not the member."** Framed as reducing
cognitive overhead while keeping the House available for exploration.

## The sentence

Not *Write your book.* Not *Publish your wisdom.* But:
**"Stay in relationship with your life's work."**
That relationship may produce books, courses, workshops, podcasts, letters, teachings,
communities, conversations, organizations. *The book is often the first expression, not the
destination.*

## Proposed review protocol

Review the Studio **through the life of one person**, not screen by screen. Example: a
62-year-old therapist, thirty years of notes, recordings, workshops and stories, who does
not call herself an author. Walk her from first voice note to publication, asking at every
step: Does this feel welcoming? Does it reduce complexity rather than add it? Does it
preserve her authorship? Does it reveal possibilities without overwhelming her? Does she
feel more connected to her work than to the software?

## Recorded observations on the addendum — evidence, NOT repairs

**C-6 — "The environment chooses the room. Not the member."** This is the single sentence
in the proposal that sits in direct tension with ratified canon. The Constitutional
Direction of Authority holds that **MAIA never moves a person through the layers — it
protects the boundaries within which the person's own development occurs.** Both sides at
equal force: the proposal's reading is *reduced cognitive overhead, House still open for
exploration*; the canonical reading is *system-selected placement is the system authoring
the member's next step.* Discharged only by a founder ruling — no recommendation offered.

**C-7 — "Discovery as encouragement" describes frozen capability.** *A thread quietly
connects today's reflection with something written six months ago · a recurring metaphor is
gently surfaced · a chapter begins to feel heavier because evidence accumulated* — these
are **system-inferred meaning across layers**, which is exactly what the freeze doctrine
holds: member-marked vs system-inferred · no synthesis · provenance-grounded · no
static-UI claims without verified state. Pattern Attunement and cross-layer synthesis
remain **held**. This is not an objection to the experience described; it is a statement
that the described experience is **not currently authorized to exist**, and a review
instrument that evaluates for it would be scoring the environment against unbuilt,
unauthorized behaviour.

**C-8 — The persona walk has no observational substrate.** A 62-year-old therapist who does
not exist cannot answer *"does this feel welcoming?"* Answers produced on her behalf — by
an AI observer or by us — are literary performance wearing the costume of evidence
(confabulation guard). The protocol becomes evidence-bearing only if walked by **an actual
person** in that position. Whether the persona is a *design device* (legitimate, generates
questions) or an *evidence source* (inadmissible) is unruled and must be declared before
use.

**C-9 — Product-category claim is subject to claim discipline.** *"A place where wisdom
becomes shareable," "flagship," "cultivate a body of work"* are outward-facing positioning
claims governed by `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` — Live / Designed / Vision · Center of
Gravity · Failure Test. As stated they are **Vision**. Nothing here authorizes stating them
as **Live**.

**C-10 — Audience expansion changes the field boundary.** Moving the subject from "authors"
to "anyone with accumulated wisdom" widens what the review is reviewing. If C is selected,
the field boundary must be declared before the walk, not discovered during it.

**C-11 — What is unambiguously strong and carries no conflict.** *One room, one anxiety* is
a design test that requires no unbuilt capability and no interpretive authority — a room
either names the anxiety it answers or it does not. *"Stay in relationship with your life's
work"* is compatible with the canon's reduce-psychological-centrality invariant in a way
*"write your book"* is not: it points at the work, not at the system.

# Founder rulings on Candidate C (Kelly, 2026-07-29)

Three rulings, taken per-item. They resolve the standing of C; they do **not** resolve Q1.

**R-C-1 — C is a third candidate instrument, not a replacement for A or B.**
It is not selected by having been written later. Standing holds until Q1 selects an
instrument *or a synthesis of instruments*. (Note: "or a synthesis" widens Q1's option set
beyond A-or-B; that widening is itself part of the ruling.)

**R-C-2 — The 62-year-old therapist is a design device, not an evidence source.**
A fictional persona is valuable for generating design questions and cannot generate
admissible evidence about user experience. **Only an actual participant can.** Discharges
C-8.

**R-C-3 — The environment offers; it does not choose.** Discharges C-6.

> **Superseded (author's original wording, preserved, not deleted):**
> *"The environment chooses the room. Not the member."*
>
> **Operative:**
> *"The environment offers the most relevant room while leaving the member free to enter
> another."* — or more simply — *"The environment makes the next helpful place obvious
> without making it compulsory."*

Kelly's reasoning, recorded because it is the load-bearing part: the original sentence
*"collapses two different ideas."* The design intent was **reduce the cognitive effort of
finding a useful place to work** — which does not require the environment to choose on the
member's behalf. The revision *"separates guidance from placement."*

⭐ **Guidance is consistent with the Constitutional Direction of Authority; compulsory
placement is a different claim.**

⚠️ **This revision is not an accidental merger.** C was revised in response to **ratified
canon**, which governs all candidates equally — not in response to Candidate B. The
preservation rule (do not let one candidate absorb another's constraints before selection)
is untouched. The original sentence is retained above as superseded wording, on the same
discipline that keeps superseded provenance visible rather than rewritten.

# Founder formulations arising from the rulings (Kelly, 2026-07-29)

Recorded because they are more durable than the rulings that produced them. **None is
promoted to canon; none is authorized to govern.**

## ⭐⭐ What a system must know — the architectural consequence of R-C-3

> **"A system that chooses needs a developmental model of the person.
> A system that offers needs a coherent model of the environment.
> Those are fundamentally different architectural commitments."**

This reaches past navigation. It states what each posture *obliges the system to build and
hold*: a choosing system must model the member's developmental position (and therefore
must acquire, store, and act on that model); an offering system must model only what is
reachable and make it legible. The Constitutional Direction of Authority constrains who may
author meaning; this names the **infrastructure each answer requires**.

⛔ **Potentially constitutional, NOT promoted.** Whether this belongs in the Sovereignty
Invariants is a founder question, stated without recommendation. It arrived as a
consequence of a navigation ruling in a review-candidate file — provenance worth keeping
visible if it is ever raised for canon.

## ⭐ Synthesis must be authored, not residual

> **"A synthesis must itself be a ruled instrument, not the residue of unresolved
> differences. Otherwise 'A+B' becomes the default outcome simply because both contained
> good ideas."**

The safeguard on the disposition R-C-1 opened. Union-of-the-good-parts is not a selection;
it is a failure to select, wearing selection's clothes.

## ⭐ "One room, one anxiety" — the sharpened test

> **"When someone enters this room, what is the one uncertainty this room exists to
> resolve?"**

Two failure signatures, both stated by Kelly:
1. **Multiple anxieties compete** → the room's purpose is diffuse.
2. **The answer changes depending on who is describing the room** → *architectural
   ambiguity, not a copy issue.*

Signature 2 is the load-bearing one: it converts a subjective impression into a
detectable condition — **disagreement between independent describers is the evidence**, the
same mechanism that made the five isolated Pass 1 extraction agents productive.

Kelly's read: the test *"seems capable of revealing coherence — or its absence — regardless
of whether Q1 ultimately selects Candidate A, B, C, or an explicitly ruled synthesis."*

⚠️ **Instrument-independence ≠ declaration-independence — recorded as a caution, unruled.**
A lens that survives any instrument still produces **observations**, and observations
require a declaration: which build was observed, what evidence class each claim carries,
who observed and how, and whether felt claims were deferred to human walk. Running the test
before Q1 would generate findings under **no declared instrument** — the exact condition
that made the `soullab-landing` sitting invalidatable-only rather than recalibratable.
⭐ *The test being instrument-independent is a property of the question; it is not a
property of the record the question produces.*

# Closing formulations (Kelly, 2026-07-29) — end of the governance sitting

## ⭐⭐ A lens is a question. An observation is an evidentiary act.

> *"The lens may be instrument-independent. The observation never is."*

Ratifies the caution recorded above. The moment anyone writes **"this room answers anxiety
X,"** they have entered a governed observation. Without a declaration, a named observer, an
evidence class, and confabulation discipline, the observation **does not become false — it
becomes unclassifiable.** That is the precise damage the `soullab-landing` sitting
sustained: not error, but unfileable evidence.

## ⭐⭐ Choose vs. offer is an epistemic and consent question, not a navigation one

Kelly's extension of R-C-3: a system cannot responsibly choose the member's next room
without implicitly claiming three things —

1. it **knows where the member is**,
2. it **knows where they should go**,
3. it is **entitled to use that knowledge for routing**.

Offering makes none of those claims.

> *"Whether or not this becomes a sovereignty invariant, the underlying question is not
> navigation — it's what the system is authorized to know and act upon."*

⛔ Still **not promoted**. The reframing raises its likely home from a navigation ruling to
a consent question, which makes deliberate handling more important, not less.

## ⭐⭐ Selection is reversible. Loss is not.

Two **independent** risks, neither resolving the other:

| | Failure mode | What it argues |
|---|---|---|
| **Constitutional risk** (D1) | candidate → operative **by momentum** | against premature commitment |
| **Operational risk** (D2) | preserved → **accidentally lost** | against leaving work vulnerable indefinitely |

> *"Not every preservation method requires constitutional commitment. A durable archive, an
> immutable snapshot, or another preservation mechanism can exist without declaring the
> contents canonical."*

⛔ **Explicitly does NOT answer D2** — Kelly: *"and I wouldn't let it."* Recorded as an
observation that **durability and governance status are independent properties**, in the
same family as the separations this sitting made between git state, governance state,
evidentiary state, and implementation state.

## ⭐⭐ The sitting's most portable finding

> **"Don't collapse two independent dimensions just because they're often changed
> together."**

Kelly: *"That has been the recurring pattern across almost every successful refinement in
this conversation."* Instances from this sitting alone — git ⊥ governance ⊥ methodological
identity · merged ⊥ verified ⊥ accepted · evidence class ⊥ provenance · preservation ⊥
durability · lens ⊥ observation · guidance ⊥ placement · durability ⊥ canonicity.

## ⭐⭐ Final form of choose vs. offer — two different data models

Kelly's closing refinement (2026-07-29). The distinction names an **architectural
commitment**, not a UX consequence.

| A system that **chooses** must maintain | A system that **offers** must maintain |
|---|---|
| where the member is | the available rooms |
| what they need | their purposes |
| why one path is preferable to another | their relationships, presented intelligibly |

> **"One models the person. The other models the place."**

*"That may be appropriate in some systems"* — but it is a **qualitatively different
commitment**, reaching into **consent, memory, explainability, and the constitutional
direction of authority.** ⛔ Still not promoted to canon; the reframing raises the stakes of
promoting it carelessly.

## ⭐ Rediscovery is not new evidence

Recorded from this sitting's own error: the durability recursion was **already stated in
D2's own text**; re-deriving it read as a fresh finding until checked. *"You didn't discover
a new recursion — you recognized that you had rediscovered one the docket already
contained."* Small, but it keeps the historical record honest. Same family as the earlier
note that this program **independently rediscovered** *representation bound to referent*
while it was already ratified elsewhere: **independent convergence is informative; it is
not additional evidence.**

## ⭐⭐ D1 and D2 have different risk profiles — urgency is not authority

- **D1 can remain open indefinitely without changing its nature.** A static decision queue.
- **D2 changes as time passes** — exposure increases while the governing artifacts stay
  vulnerable. A time-sensitive operational risk.

⛔ **This is not an argument for resolving D2 before D1.** It does not override the
constitutional order. It is an accurate description of two different risk profiles,
preserved so that future discussions do not **conflate urgency with authority.**

## ⭐⭐ Undecided is not Unknown (Kelly, 2026-07-29)

A ninth pair, added by Kelly at the close and belonging to the same taxonomy:

> Continuing to analyze the remaining items *"would risk treating an **Undecided** question
> as though it were still **Unknown**."*

**Unknown** — analysis can still change the answer. **Undecided** — only a ruling can.
Analysis applied to an Undecided question produces the *appearance* of progress while the
decision stays exactly where it was, and can quietly accumulate into an implied
recommendation. ⭐ *"At this point the governance method has done its job. The next movement
belongs to the rulings themselves."*

# Closing note on the record itself (Kelly, 2026-07-29)

## ⭐⭐ The structural signature of the durable formulations

Reading the completed document, the formulations that lasted all share one shape:

1. they **separate two things that are easily conflated**;
2. they **do not decide between them**;
3. they **make future decisions cleaner by preserving the distinction.**

lens ≠ observation · guidance ≠ placement · selection ≠ loss · urgency ≠ authority ·
Unknown ≠ Undecided.

> *"Those aren't product decisions. They're distinctions that make later product decisions
> easier to make honestly."*

## ⭐ Lineage of the load-bearing sentence

Stated explicitly here because the sections above show the steps in sequence without naming
them as a progression. The sentence was **reframed four times and survived each**:

| | Formulation | Register |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | choose vs. offer | interface wording |
| 2 | guidance vs. placement | interaction semantics |
| 3 | what the system is **authorized to know** | consent |
| 4 | **"One models the person. The other models the place."** | architecture |

⭐ *A progression from interface wording to architecture without skipping an intermediate
step.* Recorded so the final sentence is not read as having appeared fully formed — the
same discipline that keeps superseded wording visible beside operative wording.

## ⭐⭐ The diagnostic the sitting actually produced

The sitting began trying to improve a review method. It ended repeatedly asking:

> **"What independent dimensions are we accidentally treating as one?"**

That question produced nearly every durable refinement here, across Git, governance,
evidence, navigation, consent, and durability.

⭐ **It does not tell you which distinction exists.** It tells you *what to look for when a
system feels like it is forcing one value to answer two different questions.*
