## 1. What the founder corrected

The redesign was inheriting a **generic coaching/executive-development** organizing logic. Larry's promise is
**positive psychology + flourishing**. The field must orient around the participant's flourishing journey — not
around problems, tasks, or coaching artifacts.

| | Organizing question |
|---|---|
| ❌ Current | *"What things exist in your coaching system?"* |
| ✅ Corrected | *"How are you growing, flourishing, and bringing your best self into your life and work?"* |

**Zone sequence (founder-authored):**

```
My Flourishing Journey  →  Prepare  →  Practice  →  Discover  →  Connect

WHERE AM I? → WHAT AM I EXPLORING? → WHAT CAN I PRACTICE? → WHAT AM I DISCOVERING? → WHO AM I CONNECTED WITH?
```

Register rule: **"Try this"**, never *"Complete this task"*. Not homework — **preparation for awareness**.

> *"My personal flourishing environment"* — not *"my coach assigned me activities."*

## 2. What this resolves

⭐⭐⭐ **The authority split, stated in the founder's own words:**

> **The practitioner creates invitations and experiences; the member creates meaning from living them.**

This is the same boundary the schema already enforces (experience model §3: a practitioner creates **pathways**;
only the member's gesture creates **inclusion**). The flourishing framing does not weaken it — it names it in
member-facing language. ✅ **Consistent with CF-D1 and with `MY_WORK_FIELD_GOVERNANCE_MODEL_V1`.**

## 3. Three boundaries this direction must not cross

### ⛔⛔ B1 — The example strings are SHAPE, not CONTENT

*"Notice moments when you are operating from your strengths"* · *"Building greater leadership presence"* ·
*"My strength is creating trust"* — these illustrate **register and length**. They are **not** approved copy and
**not** Larry's material.

| Fact | State |
|---|---|
| Larry's primary corpus in this repo | 🔴 **VERIFIED ABSENT** |
| Rights instrument | 🔴 **EXISTS AND UNSIGNED** |

⛔ **Claude may not author flourishing practices, prompts, focus statements, or PERMA-derived copy for this field.**
Doing so is the failure class already named in this lane: **a candidate translation layer carrying source-layer
weight.** Engineering builds the **container**; it never authors the **content**
(`feedback_authority_judgment_needs_a_perceivable_object`).

⚠️ The nine PERMA-adjacent terms (strengths · gratitude · resilience · relationships · purpose · meaning ·
positive emotion · engagement · accomplishment) are a published academic framework, **not automatically Larry's
organizing logic.** Whether Larry's field is organized by them is a **source-layer claim** requiring Larry's
authority over a perceivable object — not an inference we may make on his behalf.

### ⛔ B2 — "Discover" may rename the BAND, never the member's own label

Experience model §2 is unchanged and binding: *decision · commitment · question · reflection* are tags **the
member's gesture wrote** (`spiralogic_phase`), and `app/api/now-what/home/route.ts` groups by them
*"never by inference over their content."*

| Allowed | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| ✅ Naming the **zone** *Discover* instead of *Reflections* | ❌ Re-presenting a member's **reflection** as a *"discovery"*, a *"learning"*, or *"your wisdom"* |

*"These become the participant's own wisdom"* is true as an **outcome**. It is not a licence for the system to
**declare** it. `declared ≠ derived`.

### ⛔ B3 — Renaming zones creates no capability

CF-D2 stands: **only zones with a complete authority chain may render.** The flourishing model has five zones;
so did the superseded five-band proposal. §4 measures how many are alive. **The count did not move.**
