## Purpose

Refine *Elemental Alchemy — The Art of Living a Phenomenal Life* toward a truthful KDP-ready 5/5 state while preserving as much of the original manuscript as possible.

A 5/5 state does not mean that no model can imagine another sentence. It means the unit fulfills its role in the book at the intended level, and further intervention is more likely to diminish than materially improve it.

## Preservation law

The default editorial act is **Keep**.

Preserve:
- Kelly’s original voice and cadence.
- Elemental Alchemy and Spiralogic ontology.
- Sacred, poetic, imaginal, symbolic, and psychologically grounded registers.
- Lived stories, ceremonies, medicine, and relational presence.
- Deliberate spiral return, mystery, and ambiguity.
- As much original wording as possible.

Challenge:
- repetition that is not earning its return;
- reader disorientation or abrupt transitions;
- abstract density and over-explanation;
- weak proportion or stalled narrative movement;
- unsupported quotations, factual claims, permissions, or attribution risk;
- local beauty that does not serve the whole-book arc.

Never:
- wholesale rewrite;
- generic AI voice;
- flatten metaphysics or mystery;
- silently apply changes;
- remove meaningful stories merely to shorten the book;
- overwrite the preserved baseline;
- declare a unit “holds” without Kelly.

## Intervention ladder

1. **Keep** — original unchanged.
2. **Micro** — punctuation, one word, or local repair.
3. **Light** — sentence/paragraph adjustment preserving thought, image, cadence, and voice.
4. **Moderate** — bounded compression, reordering, grounding, or bridge.
5. **Substantial** — exceptional; requires a demonstrated book-level need and explicit author approval.

Every accepted change must answer:
1. What actual problem does this solve?
2. Does it matter enough to disturb the manuscript?
3. Can a smaller intervention solve it?
4. What would be lost?
5. Does it improve the book in context rather than merely smooth the sentence?

## Unit cycle

`Read → understand role → protect strengths → identify consequential issues → exact passage → discuss → smallest sufficient proposal → compare → read in context → author decision → Review → preservation measure → re-read in book → open/refining/holds`

A unit that holds is not routinely reopened. Reopen only for:
- explicit author choice;
- a later accepted change with a real ripple consequence;
- a material defect;
- factual, quotation, permissions, or production risk.

## Model-routing principle

Spend intelligence where judgment can change the fate of the writing.

- deterministic/local tools: exact evidence, structure, hashes, diffs, duplication, lineage, Apply/Undo verification;
- local editorial model: first-pass unit reads and evidence-bound observations;
- stronger independent model: difficult voice/meaning/arc judgments and challenger review;
- council-level reasoning: consequential ambiguity or settlement questions;
- Kelly: final authorship, Apply authority where required by Studio, and “holds” declarations.

Model identity is evidence, not authority.
