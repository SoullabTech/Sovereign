## Preface role

The Preface functions as a threshold and a transfer of epistemic authority. It moves from inherited wisdom into direct experience, then hands authority back to the reader. Its strongest evidence is not explanation but lived encounter.

## Protect

## Issue-by-issue adjudication

### Reflection and Interaction — KEEP

Structure, Reader, Voice, and Overview all noticed the contraction from the long immersive dream into the short Reflection and Interaction section. The convergence is real, but convergence is not proof of defect.

The strongest preservation reading is that the contraction is the act itself: the Preface stops centering Kelly’s singular experience and turns toward the reader. A bridge sentence would risk re-centering the author at the exact moment the text steps back.

**Disposition:** Keep as written.

The opening quotation currently attributed to Rumi sits on the hinge and has separate attribution risk, but that does not establish a prose-transition defect.

**Disposition:** Keep as written, pending quote-source cleanup.

### Micro question A — faculty list repeats at short interval

The dream section names intuition / emotion / senses / mind / soul in elemental form; roughly fifty words later Reflection and Interaction names intuition / emotion / sense / thought / spirit again.

The repetition may be useful transfer from “my experience” to “many of us.” Because the return interval is short, it is the one repetition worth an author glance.

**Disposition:** Author question only. No automatic proposal.

### Micro question B — “heightened awareness / woven” sentence pair

Current passage:

> This moment of heightened awareness showed me the layers of experience woven together. For me, the layers were woven into a single, phenomenal, elemental few moments of heightened awareness.

The repeated “heightened awareness” and “woven” are close enough to notice. The strongest Keep case is substantial: “phenomenal, elemental” echoes the book’s title and the doubling may belong to the ecstatic register.

**Disposition:** Author question only. No automatic proposal.
