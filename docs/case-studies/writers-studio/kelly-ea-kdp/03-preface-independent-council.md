## Preface role

From the sealed Preface evidence alone, the Preface functions most plausibly as a **threshold and transfer of epistemic authority**, not as a thesis statement or summary.

Its movement is:

1. the isolated Zhuangzi epigraph destabilizes ordinary subject/object certainty;
2. Kelly’s dream enacts elemental interconnection as lived experience rather than taxonomy;
3. the prose names the elemental/faculty correspondence as discovery;
4. the reader is invited toward direct encounter;
5. the Call to Adventure hands authority back to the reader and opens Part One.

The council therefore treats **direct experience as the Preface’s central evidentiary standard**. Making the opening substantially more explanatory could weaken its function.

## Protect

The council recommends protecting these features from unnecessary editing:

- the isolation of the Zhuangzi epigraph;
- the dream’s present-tense immediacy and concrete anchors: 3:30 a.m., sleeping son, dew, chimes, wind, crickets;
- the first experiential articulation of Fire / Water / Earth / Air / Aether;
- the uncertainty and mystery of “am I dreaming the elements or are the elements dreaming me?”;
- the Call to Adventure’s transfer of authority to the reader;
- the rhythm created by placing other voices beside vulnerable autobiographical material, unless quotation verification requires another treatment.
