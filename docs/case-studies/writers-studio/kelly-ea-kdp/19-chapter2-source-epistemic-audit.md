## Epistemic-standing audit

### F1 — torus as universal / archetypal natural form

The chapter uses the torus as a central symbolic model and describes it as an archetypal pattern found throughout nature.

**Evidence boundary:** toroidal structures and toroidal components genuinely occur in many physical and biological systems. Scientific literature documents toroidal crystals, toroidal protein assemblies, magnetic/plasma configurations, and toroidal components of cellular flows. That does **not** establish the torus as the single or universal geometry underlying living systems, consciousness, or all nature.

**Preservation-first treatment:** keep the torus as a powerful symbolic/modeling image. Narrow universal scientific claims unless the text explicitly marks them as metaphor or interpretive framework.

### F2 — anatomy “mirrors this toroidal pattern”

The digestive tract does pass through the body and invites a topological analogy to a tube-through-body geometry.

**Issue:** “mirrors the torus” is analogy, not ordinary anatomical classification.

**Preservation-first treatment:** keep as analogy if useful; avoid presenting human anatomy as literally a toroidal biological system without qualification.

### F3 — phi spiral / Fibonacci / golden mean “found everywhere in nature”

**Evidence boundary:** Fibonacci/golden-angle relations genuinely occur in phyllotaxis and some natural patterning. Scientific reviews also document widespread overclaiming, including familiar examples such as the Nautilus/golden-spiral myth.

**Preservation-first treatment:** preserve the spiral metaphor and genuine examples; avoid “everywhere” or universal-design implications unless clearly poetic.

### F4 — “quantum beings” / spooky effects / entanglement

**Issue:** quantum mechanics describes matter and fields at fundamental scales, and entanglement is a real quantum phenomenon. “We are quantum beings” is not a standard scientific inference that supports psychological/spiritual interconnectedness.

**Preservation-first treatment:** this can remain as metaphysical language drawing on quantum imagery if that standing is explicit. Do not use entanglement as empirical proof of spiritual or interpersonal connection.

### F5 — fractal / sacred geometry / toroidal holism in one scientific sentence

The sentence joining “quantum beings,” “fuzzy logic of sacred geometry,” “fractal symmetry,” and “holism of toroidal processes” collapses several unlike epistemic domains.

**Craft consequence:** even before deciding factual truth, the sentence asks one scientific register to authorize multiple symbolic systems at once.

**Preservation-first treatment:** likely candidate for a **Light epistemic clarification**, not deletion of the sacred-geometry material.

### F6 — Tibetan Buddhist sand mandalas

**Standing:** ritual sand mandalas are real Buddhist practices and can symbolize cosmological/ritual wholes; they are deliberately dismantled in many traditions to enact impermanence.

**Preservation-first treatment:** broadly supportable; use tradition-specific language carefully rather than universalizing all mandalas.

### F7 — “All of us are holograms too”

**Issue:** “hologram” is a metaphysical analogy here, not a scientific description of human beings.

**Preservation-first treatment:** preserve only if its analogical standing is clear.

### F8 — Indra’s web/net

**Standing:** Indra’s Net is an important metaphor in Huayan Buddhism for interpenetration/interdependence, with roots in the wider Indra imagery.

**Issue:** the chapter initially says Hindu tradition “likened” the field to Indra’s web, then corrects itself by saying it is a Buddhist metaphor. That self-correction creates avoidable historical wobble.

**Preservation-first treatment:** likely **Micro/Light factual clarification**: identify Indra as the older Indic deity/name-source while locating the jeweled-net metaphor’s philosophical development in Huayan Buddhism.

### F9 — “Indra’s web exists as a vibrational field manifesting all material reality”

**Issue:** this shifts directly from Buddhist metaphor into ontological/scientific assertion.

**Preservation-first treatment:** preserve as Kelly’s metaphysical interpretation if intended, but mark it as interpretation rather than historical Buddhist doctrine or empirical physics.

### F10 — cymatics

**Evidence boundary:** Chladni/Faraday/Jenny-style cymatic phenomena genuinely visualize standing-wave/resonance patterns in physical media. Scientific work describes nodal patterns and standing waves.

**Issue:** cymatics demonstrates vibration shaping patterns under specific physical conditions. It does not by itself establish that frequencies/energies create all physical structure or validate a universal spiritual field.

**Preservation-first treatment:** keep the real phenomenon; separate empirical observation from the metaphysical inference that Kelly draws from it.
