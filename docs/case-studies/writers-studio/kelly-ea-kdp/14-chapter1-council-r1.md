## 1. Chapter 1's apparent role

Chapter 1 is the **threshold**: it converts the Preface's dream-register into lived ground and hands the reader to Chapter 2's movement. It does five jobs:

1. **Invokes** the five elements liturgically (position 9 opening) before explaining anything.
2. **Establishes lineage** — Johan, teacher and medicine man, in the canyon: the author received this medicine.
3. **Transmits it forward** — Augusten at the backyard fire: the medicine is being passed to a son.
4. **Demonstrates it in practice** — the client dialogue on "making it up," the chapter's only scene with dialogue.
5. **Defines the author's authority narrowly** — "storytelling cartographer," and "the real mystery school is your own being."

Compass concurs: role `supported` — "grounds abstract concepts in physical experience while introducing the elemental metaphor."

## 4. Scientific-diction craft test (craft problem only if it survives epistemic separation)

Applied to each audit item: *after* standing is separated, is there still a craft defect?

- **F1 frequencies/cymatics — one craft item survives, and it is deliberately deferred.** Pos. 9 states the same proposition twice in consecutive sentences in slightly different scientific register: "…emphasizing that all of life is rooted in frequencies. All matter, at its base, is the dance of frequencies…". Adjacent restatement is a craft observation independent of truth. **Council recommendation: do not spend a separate literary edit here.** Whatever wording Kelly chooses when she settles F1's epistemic standing will almost certainly dissolve the restatement for free. Sequencing it behind her decision is the smaller act. *(The council is not proposing a change contingent on a scientific question — it is declining to propose one.)*
- **F3 objective-witness — one independent Micro survives, and it is purely mechanical.** Pos. 9: "The more conscious we are of the fluid nature of reality and the fact that we are 'making it up' **allows us** to begin to choose…" — the verb's subject is a subordinate clause; the construction dangles. This is a grammar stumble in the chapter's densest paragraph, not an epistemic question. **Class: Micro.** Target: that sentence. Gain: removes a stumble where the reader can least afford one. Loss: none, if the repair preserves wording and rhythm. The proposition itself: **Keep** — F3's standing is the author's to mark, and the sentence is good.
- **F4 neuroplasticity** (pos. 10 cube; pos. 11 coherence) — **no craft problem survives.** Both sentences are short and clear. The issue is accuracy only. **Source layer.**
- **F5 coherence / "energetically balanced" / "negative energies" / field** — **no craft problem.** This register belongs to the book's ontology. **Keep.**
- **F2 imagination and outcomes** — **no craft problem.** The client dialogue is the chapter's best teaching. **Source layer only, and protect the scene.**

**Extraction artifacts are not craft findings.** The frozen evidence carries interleaved page numbers (16–23), mid-word PDF line breaks ("called / pareidolia", "self-/discovery"), "mosquitos", and "tune-in". These belong to the final KDP production proof. No later pass should read them as prose defects.
