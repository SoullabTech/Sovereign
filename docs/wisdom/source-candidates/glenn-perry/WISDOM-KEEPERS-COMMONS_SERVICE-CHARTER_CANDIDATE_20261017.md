# Wisdom Keepers Commons — A living service of encounter and reciprocity

**Proposed service, not an adopted constitution or shipping product. Founder review required.**

## The invitation

Soullab should not become a platform that *extracts* knowledge from wisdom keepers in order to make MAIA appear wiser. Nor should it become a marketplace where the only meaningful relationship between members and teachers is a paid transaction.

Instead, let Soullab become a place where people can **find the original holders of a teaching, recognize their contribution, encounter their work, and—when all parties choose—enter reciprocal human learning relationships**. Keepers need not join Soullab or yield texts to be honored. Members need not pay, participate, share personal stories, or accept a particular doctrine to belong.

This is an *offering held by a community*, with ethical stewardship and practical costs—not an obligation to produce content or participate for free.

### One-sentence service promise (proposed)

> Soullab helps people encounter living wisdom and the people who steward it, in ways that honor source, consent, difference, human agency, and mutual support.

## Not a new competing Commons

The *Commons* term already has a **ratified** meaning in Soullab's Circle constitution (FR-02): a shared-interest ecology of people and sovereign Circles, distinct from a Circle, a forum, a contributions library, or a Co-Lab. FR-01 says increased connection never creates greater rights to source material. FR-06 says interest-based discovery may use **declared interests only**, not conversations, inferred needs, psychological analysis, or surveillance. FR-07 gives every contributor authority over their own representation; FR-08 absolutely excludes Sanctuary and forbids ambient promotion of private material. Reusing any legacy `community_*` status tiers or `commons_contributions` scoring would import exactly the hierarchy the Circle rulings prohibit.

Therefore **“Wisdom Keepers Commons” is a proposed service within the future FR-02 Interest Commons ecology**, *not* a new Circle type, another forum, an automatic AIN ingestion channel, or a new authoritative container. The existing `/wisdom-keepers/wisdom` room can remain the human-facing threshold for exploring knowledge. This service would add *recognition and real human relationships*, once the appropriate Commons substrate is constituted.

- The Wisdom room: encounter a tradition or source and follow it back to a named keeper.
- An Interest Commons: people optionally declare interest, discover humane opportunities, and choose whether to respond.
- Small Circles: participants voluntarily gather for a bounded inquiry, with explicit consent and no automatic upward release.
- A keeper-led encounter: only if that keeper personally agrees to the format, scope and conditions.
- MAIA: a facilitator of inquiry and navigation when invited; never impersonates a keeper, claims to speak for the tradition, or speaks as a substitute for the person.

None of these changes automatically grants MAIA permission to process or embed copyrighted works.

## Five different ways to participate — without purchasing belonging

**1. Discover.** Read an original author description and follow the author-maintained site, book, public essay or teaching school. A credited, *unaffiliated* source entry needs no false claim of partnership. No referral tracking or inferred member targeting.

**2. Contemplate.** Choose an explicitly attributed symbolic or scholarly perspective for conversation, subject to the corpus and privacy gates. If it is merely original Soullab commentary, say so. If the member does not want the lens, drop it. Chart symbols cannot diagnose a person or determine their life.

**3. Gather.** Members may intentionally declare interest in a theme—for example, the relationship of psychological astrology and depth psychology—and form a small, governed Circle or attend an opt-in conversation. The Commons does not automatically learn about a private conversation.

**4. Meet.** A living wisdom keeper *may* choose to lead an open salon, speak to a small Circle, take part in a question exchange, or be unavailable. A teacher's silence or refusal is complete; there is no entitlement to their time, availability or personal contact.

**5. Support.** A member may choose to buy a book directly, recommend the author's work, contribute a relevant resource, help with an authorized event, volunteer care, write an appreciative note, or donate to a transparently governed community fund. Support never becomes social rank or a prerequisite for respectful access.

Participation in one mode never commits someone to the others.

## The keeper is a person, not a source file

The community may publicly *reference* an unaffiliated writer via verified links, but must not represent them as a Soullab partner, suggest they are available for introductions, or publish contact routes that have not been offered. For a living keeper who chooses to participate:

- Their profile must be reviewed and correctable by them before calling it a **participating keeper**.
- The keeper chooses topics, formats, access, response expectations, visibility, compensation and whether they wish any contact or recording at all.
- Participation is time-bounded and can be paused or withdrawn for future activities; already-shared works and lawful records have separately stated treatment.
- A keeper can disagree with Soullab's framing and request a public correction; the Commons should preserve disagreement rather than hide it.
- No one is obliged to offer personal therapy, counselling, spiritual guidance or individual attention merely because they are listed.
- Traditions may be **collectively** stewarded. Do not assign sole ownership to a single spokesperson or repackage sacred/closed knowledge without the community's own authority.

The architecture distinguishes public reference from an invited participant. A book link is not consent to be recruited, profiled, indexed or contacted automatically.

## The member remains sovereign

- Discoveries and invitations are driven only by interests a person **expressly declares for that Commons** (FR-06); no matching from MAIA memory, astrology data, emotional vulnerability, health records or behavioral history.
- A prospective one-to-one introduction requires **both sides' informed approval** of the particular introduction, its purpose and any contact data exchanged. No default DMs, scraped emails, contact lists or “warm intros” inferred by AI.
- A Circle interior does not become a Commons post, training data, observation report or marketing story. Individually authored representations cross only through the author’s act; collective material needs every represented contributor's consent. Sanctuary content **never crosses**, even when someone asks to release it from Sanctuary.
- The right not to participate and the right to depart must be easy to exercise. No streaks, guilt prompts, absence-triggered pursuit, rank badges, activity scores, behavioral sales funnels or relationship scores.
- If a person makes a financial gift, payment information must not influence the importance MAIA assigns to their voice or the visibility they receive in a Circle.

There is no implied “growth funnel” from a question, to a paid session, to paid membership.

## What reciprocity could mean in practice

The strongest form of reciprocity is **a fair relationship**, not a financial exchange disguised as generosity. A keeper contributes experience and time; members contribute sincere attention, questions, lived counterexamples, care and sometimes money; Soullab contributes the infrastructure, facilitation, fair contracts and safeguarding.

Yet non-transactional must not mean *unpaid labor*. If Soullab invites a teacher into a structured offering, it should propose an appropriate honorarium or other terms that teacher freely accepts—funded by Soullab, transparent pooled support, direct member gifts or some mixture. Fund a **community scholarship/participation pool** if feasible so ability to pay does not determine whether someone can hear a teaching. Do not make “voluntary donation” pressure the condition of meaningful participation.

**Proposed finance commitments, subject to founder/keeper review:**

- No advertising placement, data resale, undisclosed referral commissions, ranking bought through sponsorship, pay-to-be-featured or concealed affiliate tracking.
- Original author purchase/course links go directly to their websites; any future sponsorship must be explicitly named and must not buy interpretive authority.
- Publish the financial rules of an optional pooled fund before accepting gifts: what receives them, fees, what is paid to keepers, who decides, refunds and accounting. Do not state that 100% passes through unless it actually does.
- Agree compensation and intellectual-property boundaries **before** a keeper commits to prepare or teach; avoid retroactive conditions.
- Financial sustainability is legitimate. Soullab can charge transparently for actual platform/facilitation services, but never for the *privilege of access to a person's meaning* and never as an undisclosed tax on a direct author relationship.

The first pilot should be small enough that Soullab can fund real facilitation without relying on speculative referral revenue.

## Human journey: one possible member experience

A member voluntarily opens Wisdom and encounters a section: **“People behind the wisdom.”**

They explore *Glenn Perry — AstroPsychology*, read a short source-attributed explanation that distinguishes symbolic psychology from clinical evidence, and follow a book or Academy link. His entry is explicitly marked **“Independent source reference · not participating in Soullab”** until he consents.

A member who is personally interested can choose **“I’d like to explore this tradition with others.”** This is a destination-specific expression of interest, not a psychological profile or a request to reveal their private MAIA conversations. When the real FR-02 Commons is ready, members can discover consented study opportunities. MAIA may be invited to help them formulate a question; it does not take over a teacher's voice.

If Perry later agrees to a human gathering, that event shows its actual host, format, scope, compensation model, contact and recording terms. No announcement or invite claiming his attendance precedes his explicit agreement. If he declines, members can still read and discuss the publicly available works; the community does not claim his partnership.

At the end, participants may keep personal reflections privately, offer a separately authored thought to a Circle, or simply leave. A collective session is not summarized to the Commons by default. **An encounter can be meaningful even if it leaves no durable data.**

## First bounded pilot, not a launch promise

**Stage 0 — Public reference, now feasible in review.** Use the already-staged Glenn Perry source links and attribution. Label as unaffiliated and unlicensed for source text. No direct member matching, public endorsement claim or active invitation.

**Stage 1 — Founder scholarly review.** The founder reviews `soullab-original-teaching-candidate.md`; verify book editions and precise attributions. This does not authorize live retrieval, but ensures Soullab does not misrepresent the keeper.

**Stage 2 — Invitation, only if founder chooses.** Founder personally contacts the keeper to ask whether any form of relationship is welcome. Receive permission for a *specific* activity if there is an interest, **not a blanket release of all work**. Keep outreach and its timing entirely with the founder; no automatic email or solicitation.

**Stage 3 — A small real encounter.** Only if the keeper affirmatively accepts and the appropriate Circle/Commons release and authorization boundaries exist: one facilitated conversation or intimate study gathering. Clearly identify participation terms, whether the keeper is present, optional patronage/honorarium, and no recording by default. Test accessibility and safety with fictional accounts before the first real participant.

**Stage 4 — A member-authored gift to the Commons.** Where the FR-02 Commons exists and its collective-release mechanism is ratified, someone *may* deliberately offer a new, individually authored reflection with destination-specific consent. This is **not** a transfer of the Circle's interior or the keeper's copyrighted contribution. No automatic AIN ingestion.

**Stage 5 — Learn without surveillance.** Gather voluntary, qualitative feedback from keeper and members about whether they felt represented, welcomed, free to decline, and helped to deepen inquiry. Use aggregate operational accounting only as necessary, without measuring, ranking, or monetizing personal depth.

No stage is implied authorized by completion of the previous one.

## Clear states for any future directory or encounter

| State | Permissible representation | Requires consent? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unendorsed reference** | Verified public bio, attribution, links to original work; label “not participating” | No affiliation implied; respect applicable rights and correction requests |
| **Invited privately** | No public indication of invitation or partnership | Founder-directed outreach only |
| **Participating, scoped** | Agreed description, exact activities, contacts/recording/payment terms | Affirmative keeper consent and review |
| **Paused/withdrawn** | Remove future-participation offers and outbound contact invitations | Keeper can change future standing |
| **Correction/dispute** | Pause disputed statements; preserve only necessary correction/audit record | No invented attribution or endorsement |

**No member-keeper introduction without separate member and keeper approvals.**

## What we must not quietly build

- A “Wisdom Influencer” leaderboard, wisdom score, “top mentors,” badges for engagement or payments.
- An AI substitute speaking *as* Glenn Perry or automatically contacting wisdom keepers.
- A bibliographic page that quietly embeds copyrighted books/essays because they are linked.
- A marketplace where people are shown personal “recommended healers” based on unconsented psychological inference.
- A community product built by bolting this onto the existing `community_*` or `commons_contributions` status economy.
- A request to give private Circle dialogue, Sanctuary content, or group recordings to AIN without individually authored releases and rights.

## Founder decisions still open

The proposed service name **Wisdom Keepers Commons** is working language. Founder should decide whether the public name is simply **Wisdom Keepers**, with “Commons” denoting the already-ratified underlying field. Specific questions for later adjudication: who can invite a keeper; what the minimum consent agreement contains; whether and how Soullab funds teacher honoraria; the first noncommercial encounter format; how community governance will work beyond one founder; and whether an Interest Commons implementation is ready. There is no implied automatic ratification in this file.
