# First Wisdom Circle — Where Symbols Meet Life

**Founder rehearsal kit · no event scheduled · not an invitation to existing Soullab members**

This first gathering is meant to test a **human experience** before creating a member-facing Wisdom Keepers service. It is *not* a Glenn Perry event, a clinical group, a curriculum licensed by Perry, a public Commons, or an opportunity to collect and train on participants' inner lives.

### The one promise

> We can explore what a symbol awakens in us without letting anyone else—not a chart, teacher, group, or AI—decide what our lives mean.

The first participant experience should be generous and complete even when nobody buys anything, discloses a personal history, signs up for another gathering, or posts afterward. The greatest outcome may be that someone hears a question that remains quietly their own.

## Pilot footprint, not a booking

| Attribute | Proposed condition |
| --- | --- |
| Host | Soullab founder or separately authorized **procedural** facilitator, not Glenn Perry |
| Format | **Online first**: a private, host-controlled video gathering for consenting adults; no paid or clinical service |
| Suggested attendance | 6–8 consenting adults, an operational facilitation target rather than a Circle size rule |
| Time | 75 minutes; **online mode chosen as direction**, but provider, date, time zone, access URL and invitations remain **unset** |
| Subject | Human needs in tension: intimacy and autonomy, service and rest, discernment and compassion |
| Main lens | Soullab's original Elemental Alchemy prompts; other traditions as accurately attributed optional metaphors |
| Perry standing | Unaffiliated public source reference, not a guest or approved host |
| Financial terms | Pilot concept assumes no mandatory charge or gift; actual hosting expenses and compensation require founder decision |
| Technology | Phase 1: a **specifically verified private external-video link or equivalent**, not an untested native multiparty MAIA room; see [online-to-hybrid roadmap](./STAGE-G_ONLINE-FIRST_TO-HYBRID_GATHERINGS-CANDIDATE_20261017.md) |
| Recording | OFF. No transcript, AI notetaker, automatic recap or AIN contribution |
| Member data | No natal charts, diagnoses, journals, personal MAIA memory, inferred interests, psychological targeting, or Sanctuary content |
| Output | Members take away their own meaning; the Circle makes **no automatic published artifact** |

An actual pilot cannot proceed until a genuinely scoped membership/invite door, participant notice, and reliable privacy settings are verified on the selected platform. The current Circle/Commons implementation and its cohort access may not yet support this; a mock browser pass is **not** permission to recruit.

## Prepare the online container first

**Founder direction:** Online is the preferred first pilot. Hybrid becomes an additional, separately tested participation configuration later—not a public livestream or automatic recording of local participants. The session remains entirely unscheduled.

For the first online rehearsal, require a genuinely private meeting provider and account with demonstrated host-side access control and recording/transcription settings. Do not claim a third-party provider is sovereign, end-to-end encrypted, free of technical logs or HIPAA-compliant without actual evidence. A video link is not a consent agreement. Use a fictional host, invited participant and denied guest in the test; no real member invitation until the platform passes and the founder separately approves the final send.

**Host procedure:** Have the first host admit consenting members individually, introduce what visible names/cameras/chat mean, check that recording/AI notetaking are off in the selected service, allow camera-off and quiet listening, and know how to pause/close entry or stop the entire room. Explain unavoidable provider/account/technical logs accurately in the final entry notice. If provider capabilities fall short of the promised privacy, stop or select a different venue.

### Original room-and-participant preparation (applies to later hybrid gatherings too)

Before even setting a date:

1. Confirm the founder's authority to host the specific Circle/venue. The Circle interface, API, cohort access, role assignment, and exit behavior must agree. Do **not** add guests to a founder allowlist.
2. Choose a small, quiet place with chairs in a circle or a meeting system with access restricted to approved participants. Avoid an unmoderated public stream or shared public join link.
3. Check in the actual product that **recording, AI transcription, automated summaries, chat logging/replay, analytics and visitor lists** can be controlled according to the announced policy. If not, postpone or choose a different bounded setting.
4. Identify a human co-steward only if useful, and explain their role to participants. A host's professional background does not automatically make this a therapy service.
5. Prepare the public links to Glenn Perry's [website](https://aaperry.com/), [Academy](https://astropsychology.org/) and official [books page](https://aaperry.com/astrology-books/) for anyone who asks. **Do not distribute scanned pages or full text.** The reading is optional; no purchase is needed to participate.
6. Rehearse the six time windows with **fictional** contributions and a refusal scenario. Do not test with someone else's private clinical/client history.
7. Prepare an ordinary contact route for practical accessibility or safety concerns, without creating an unconsented member/keeper directory or exchanging member details.

## What participants are told before entry

Use the separately prepared [participant invitation and agreement](./STAGE-F_FIRST-GATHERING_INVITATION-AND-CONSENT_CANDIDATE_20261017.md), only after completing the release checks. Essential facts must be disclosed ahead of time: the actual host and purpose, group visibility, optional speaking, what is/not recorded, how to leave, handling of urgent concerns, and the absence of Glenn Perry as a participant. Do not invent or promise perfect confidentiality; other humans can remember what they hear, so members should share only what they choose.

A person may decline without explanation. Participation itself never becomes consent for therapy, individual follow-up, recording, publication, research, marketing, or permanent knowledge ingestion.

## Online-to-hybrid transition (not yet part of this pilot)

Once the first private online Circle works as a human encounter, Soullab may explore recurring online study, keeper-hosted salons after specific consent, and only then hybrid meetings with both local and remote members. The hybrid venue will need **a local procedural host plus a remote participant advocate**, accessible two-way audio, consented camera framing, an echo-control rehearsal, remote voice participation parity, a no-recording guarantee consistent with actual provider capabilities, and a demonstrated way to stop or revert to online-only when any of these fail.

A meeting invitation to the first **online** Circle does not authorize broadcasting that person into a later hybrid venue. A Keeper-hosted gathering remains a separate invitation, not a blanket extension of this pilot. Review the [Stage G online-first / hybrid-later design](./STAGE-G_ONLINE-FIRST_TO-HYBRID_GATHERINGS-CANDIDATE_20261017.md) before setting dates or venue permissions.

## The 75-minute human encounter

### 0–10 min — Arrival, orientation and consent

The founder welcomes everyone, says why the Circle exists, names who can hear one another, checks the recording status, and invites the freedom to pass. Briefly explain that this is education/reflection, **not psychotherapy or astrology-as-diagnosis**.

Suggested host words:

> Welcome. This gathering is about how we discover meaning, not about having our lives explained to us. You may speak, listen, write privately, or pass at any time. We will not ask for birth charts, diagnoses, or personal history. We are not recording or summarizing this as an AI resource. What you take away is yours to decide.

Say clearly: no participant should promise to publish, photograph, relay, or quote another participant outside the group. We ask for care and discretion but cannot guarantee every other person's confidentiality. If the meeting technology logs/transcribes contrary to the notice, **stop** rather than quietly proceed.

A check-in can be just a word, a breath, or silence. The facilitator cannot require names beyond what the chosen access/safety method needs.

### 10–20 min — The source and the distinction between symbol and evidence

Present the difference between (a) a person's actual experience, (b) interpretive traditions that offer symbolic vocabularies, and (c) independently verified facts.

Public author credit (original Soullab paraphrase):

> Glenn Perry's AstroPsychology is one example of an approach that brings astrological symbolism into conversation with psychological needs and development. We're acknowledging the author and his work, not claiming he is part of this event or that a horoscope establishes our history, clinical condition, or fate.

This event does **not** teach from Perry's copyrighted chapters or impersonate his instructions. Offer links; interested participants may explore his writing on their own terms afterward.

The host may also name Elemental Alchemy as **Soullab's separate approach**: embodiment, feeling, discernment and intention held within the changing Weather of an encounter. No one-to-one chart-element mapping is asserted.

### 20–35 min — One original inquiry: two needs that can both matter

Offer a **fictional** example: *"I long for closeness, and I also need space."* This is not a covert reference to any real participant.

Facilitator says:

> Think of two needs in your own life that sometimes pull in different directions. If none comes, you may simply imagine this fictional pair. What if neither need is wrong? What does each seem to protect?

Give participants a quiet minute or two. Then guide gently through Soullab's elemental questions, all optional:

- **Earth — the actual:** What concrete situation are we considering, free of diagnosis or interpretation?
- **Water — the felt:** What feeling is present? What would it be like to let that feeling exist without making it a verdict?
- **Air — discernment:** What two needs or meanings might be involved? What do we genuinely know versus assume?
- **Fire — possibility:** What small act of honest choice or creativity could honor both needs?
- **Weather — the field:** What is changing right now? Can the question remain open?

These are original Soullab educational invitations; they do **not** reclassify the Tarot or astrological correspondences as clinical facts. Participants may write privately. Nothing is collected.

### 35–55 min — Voluntary human reflection

Invite sharing *only if someone wants to*. Never call on silent people by name, use private member history to draw them out, assign an astrological type, or move anyone into a breakout where they feel pressured to reveal a story.

Host reflection sequence (choose one at a time):

> What surprised you when both needs were allowed to speak?

> Was one of those needs trying to protect something?

> What changes when you hear another possible interpretation without needing to agree with it?

The group may respond with curiosity, not advice, correction or diagnosis. Members can say "I'd rather just listen" or "That doesn't fit" and be respected immediately.

The facilitator is a procedural host, **not the authority over anyone's meaning**. A group's discomfort with a person's viewpoint is not by itself grounds for removal.

### 55–65 min — Discernment, disagreement and integration

If relevant, invite a different view of the fictional example. Distinguish "this symbol helps me" from "this proves what happened." The facilitator may ask:

> Could two people carry different meanings from the same story and both remain free?

For a psychological astrology claim, the host can acknowledge the tradition, then separate unverified claims from actual experience. No chart predicts a future relationship event. No participant must accept elemental symbolism, depth psychology, or spiritual metaphysics.

If there is unresolved disagreement, allow it. Do not manufacture consensus or declare an energetic field reading as a group fact.

### 65–75 min — Closing without possession

> What, if anything, will you carry privately from this gathering?

One person may name a word. Another may stay silent. Neither response is a success or failure metric.

Thank everyone for their freely chosen presence. Remind them that no recording or group summary is being produced and that they may return to their own work without any obligation to respond or enroll in future sessions. If someone later wants to author their **own** reflection for another destination, that requires a fresh, separate and eligible author-controlled act under FR-07/08. This pilot creates no such mechanism.

Close the meeting intentionally. Do not let a dormant open room become an unattended chat, public transcript or implied follow-up obligation.

## If something difficult emerges

- **Someone is distressed:** Pause, validate their agency, offer room to step away and reconnect privately with an appropriate qualified support person if wanted. Do not require the person to tell their story, or reinterpret distress as a mystical/astrological sign.
- **Someone seeks diagnosis or individualized treatment:** Name the educational boundary and refer them toward an appropriately licensed professional. The founder's clinical qualifications do not silently turn the gathering into psychotherapy.
- **A privacy breach or unsanctioned recording appears:** Stop the relevant interaction, protect participants, follow a separately approved safety/incident process, and do not pretend the event was private.
- **An interpersonal conflict becomes a safety issue:** Apply the existing Circle's procedural boundary and appeals standards. The facilitator cannot remove someone merely for disagreement.
- **A member asks MAIA to join or synthesize the Circle:** Do not enable it; Circle-level AI participation and consent have not been operationally approved for this pilot.
- **Someone requests their contribution go to the Commons or AIN:** This first event has no collective-release mechanism, so refuse automatic transfer. Sanctuary never crosses; collective material cannot be released without every contributor's authorized participation.

## A rehearsal the founder can do alone

Time a shortened 15-minute dry run using the **fictional** needs pair. Speak the opening, run one elemental question, simulate a participant declining to share, simulate a member challenging a chart-based inference, and end with the privacy/exit statement. Check whether the atmosphere feels like a living human gathering rather than an online course, therapy intake or AI demonstration. No pilot participants are required for this rehearsal.

## Afterward: learn without extracting

In a genuine future pilot, optional feedback might ask *only*:

- Did you feel free to participate or remain silent?
- Was the author's work represented accurately and visibly?
- Were privacy and the scope of the gathering clear?
- Did anything about the facilitation feel coercive, extractive or commercially pressuring?
- What would you change if you wanted to return?

Do not ask MAIA to analyze participants or score a person's development. Do not send attendance-based follow-up nudges. Any minimal administrative records, incident reports or payments require a separate declared retention and custody policy before the actual event.
