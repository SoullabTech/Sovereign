# Keeper-Hosted Fields — Direct human engagement without surrendering sovereignty

**Proposed future capability.** This charter does not constitute a new field ontology, a recognized teacher credential, an operating service, partner recruitment, corpus permission, or authorization to contact anyone.

## What an approved Keeper Field would mean

A wisdom keeper who *wishes* to participate could be invited into a Soullab-hosted, consent-governed relational setting. Within carefully defined permissions, that person could introduce their work, host live inquiry, teach, share authorized resources, accompany voluntary groups, and collaborate with other wisdom keepers or members. Members would encounter an actual living source, **not a facsimile generated by MAIA**.

**Approval attaches to a specific person's participation, the particular activity, named audience, and bounded duration—not to every proposition they teach, the truth of their spiritual claims, therapeutic licensure, a transferable title, or unlimited access to Soullab members.**

A verified keeper may be engaged in more than one manner. For example, a public authored essay can be referenced without an agreement, while personally hosted group teaching requires signed participation terms and member entry consent. Neither mode grants rights to the full book corpus.

## This is not a seventh type of field

Soullab already has the six-field FR-02 constitution:

| Engagement form | Existing ontology | Operating distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public attribution, keeper profile and open reading links | Wisdom room / discovery Commons threshold | Direct author links; no Keeper participation claimed |
| A willing keeper and one consenting member | Dyadic | Explicit two-person encounter; no private-data inference or unapproved clinical care |
| Keeper-led group practice, salon, teaching or shared inquiry | Circle | A bounded field with mutual membership, designated procedural facilitator, and consent membrane |
| More than one consented Circle or keeper participating around a topic | Constellation | Participating units remain sovereign Circles; no collective material leaked upward |
| Discovery of traditions, resources, events, and stated interests | FR-02 Interest Commons | Uses only participant-declared interests; a Commons is not a giant open Circle |
| Keeper/member joint research, creative work or course creation | Co-Lab | Purpose-scoped human collaboration with distinct source/IP and publication rights |

A **Keeper-Hosted Field** is a *hosting and participation configuration* over these existing forms, not a license for a Keeper to open any member's Personal field or for Soullab to collapse a Circle's interior into the Commons.

## Three independent approvals before a participating field

**A. Soullab's stewardship approval.** An accountable human reviewer confirms the real person's identity and public professional representation to the extent relevant, any claimed credentials within their actual scope, the proposed use of Soullab's name, conduct/safeguarding expectations, named authority, appropriate jurisdiction and grievance procedure, and a definite expiration/review date. This is **not** scientific or spiritual certification. Founder approval of a pilot is not enough to create unrestricted global moderator or admin powers.

**B. The keeper's scoped acceptance.** The keeper personally agrees to how they are named and described, what resources may be linked/shared, whether they host, cohost or merely visit, topic limits, cohort size/format as an operational choice, accessibility, honorarium or other fair support, moderation, contact boundaries, no-recording default, future revocation, and what happens to their intellectual property. A publicly available email or professional website never substitutes for agreement.

**C. Each member's informed entry.** Participants must choose that field, see the real keeper and the proposed activity, understand whether a conversation is shared with other participants, what may be retained or visible, how to leave, how to report boundary breaches, and any cost if applicable. Entry does not expose private MAIA conversations, journal, chart/birth data, inferred needs, personal medical/therapy history, other Circle membership, or Sanctuary. Participant declarations are contextual to the current Commons and may not silently follow the member elsewhere.

**Operational gate:** Even A+B+C are not sufficient to publish a new service until a real access boundary, role enforcement, visibility checks, recordings/custody controls, exit/revocation, and fictional-account tests have separately passed. During research **every gate remains OFF.**

## Keeper standing is not a score

1. **Unendorsed public reference:** An original author is cited, linked and accurately distinguished from Soullab. The keeper has not joined and should not receive automated contact.
2. **Invitation privately prepared:** The founder may consider outreach, but there is no public indication that participation is pending or likely.
3. **Keeper accepted a defined invitation:** A written or otherwise auditable human agreement covers the specific hosted encounter and a reviewable public description.
4. **Keeper field approved, scoped, and open:** The keeper receives only explicit abilities for the approved field after technical verification and member invitations are ready.
5. **Paused, withdrawn, or disputed:** New gatherings/public invitations stop; access/roles are revoked where applicable; published claims may be corrected. Keepers' original source texts are not deleted merely because a hosted field closes, while Soullab-controlled copies are handled under the agreed custody terms.

These are **operational states**, not reputation levels. Do not order keepers by status, popularity, payments, clinical claims, or implied spiritual achievement.

## A humane Keeper Field, conceptually

The entry should feel more like visiting a thoughtful studio or hosted home than joining a public feed:

- **Welcome:** The keeper's own words about why they come, what they protect, what their teaching is *for*, and explicit boundaries. A nonpartner public-source card must never be presented as if the keeper authored the room.
- **Read and Listen:** Carefully authorized keeper-selected books, articles, talks, podcasts, original commentary, and outbound official links. Each item retains source, rights standing and whether its content is actually hosted or merely referenced.
- **Gather:** Optional small Circle conversations and salons; each has a named host, topic, date, attendance/visibility rules, confidentiality and recording policy. Attendance is never inferred from profile interests.
- **Questions and dialogue:** A keeper may agree to respond to questions in a bounded venue, including how frequently they can reply or that they may never reply. Do not use pressure nudges or promise response times not agreed upon.
- **Practice and reflection:** Members carry a teaching into their own experience privately, optionally bringing a **new author-created reflection** into the field. A reply is not a clinical diagnosis and does not become a Soullab knowledge asset by being posted.
- **Contribute and support:** Human thank-you notes, direct author links, transparent honoraria/gifts, volunteering, or scholarship-support opportunities when governed and available. Participation never depends on buying a relationship.
- **Cross-keeper encounters:** When multiple keepers consent, host carefully facilitated, tradition-respecting dialogues, not debates that collapse differences into a universal MAIA interpretation.
- **Return quietly:** A member can simply leave. Absence is not tracked as failure; no “we missed you” notification.

An entry page may show **what is currently possible**, not mock controls or solicit contacts for unbuilt features.

## Host powers: narrow and revocable

A scoped keeper can: edit their own reviewed public welcome/bio (through a human-review workflow), propose or host approved gatherings, link to their own published work, reply in their own hosted dialogue, maintain agreed topic boundaries, suspend a session, and ask Soullab for a correction or support.

A keeper cannot: browse a member's Personal field, inspect MAIA memory, infer who needs healing, access a chart unless separately and expressly offered for a particular lawful use, inspect other private Circles, harvest email addresses, export a roster for marketing, contact members out of band without consent, re-share recordings, issue mental-health diagnoses via chart symbolism, or grant themselves administrative rights.

**Keeper ≠ facilitator by title.** The ratified Circle law (FR-05) makes facilitation *procedural, not interpretive*. A keeper who teaches may not automatically become an authorized Circle facilitator or remove a participant for disagreement. Circle role assignment/review must obey the existing role and appeals law. A Keeper does not obtain `FOUNDER_MEMBER_IDS`, global `team_admin`, an implicit API grant, or a role by simply being listed in the directory.

## MAIA's role

MAIA is a consented companion and contextual intelligence **in the member's relationship**, not an automatic host, hidden moderator, artificial Perry, or surrogate future/inner-self. If the field invokes her, she may orient participants, distinguish attributed traditions, and help them articulate their own questions; she may never impersonate a keeper or present private reflections to the keeper.

In Circle fields, the original doctrine describes a possible MAIA role as stewarding coherence, but current records also identify the **lack of implemented Circle MAIA participation**. Do not claim that real-time collective witnessing, Circle summaries or mediation is currently available. Any future Circle-level speaking/mediation role needs that Circle's valid collective approval and implementation evidence—not a keeper's unilateral instruction.

## Boundaries for psychological, spiritual and clinical activities

A keeper is allowed to hold spiritual, symbolic, scholarly or contemplative perspectives that some members dispute. Soullab can represent that lineage without endorsing empirical claims. The field must not present astrological interpretation as reliable diagnosis, causally prove trauma or declare a member's destiny. Therapy, psychotherapy, supervision, diagnosis, medical or regulated clinical care require **separate professional/legal authorization, credential verification, jurisdiction, applicable privacy controls and emergency policies**. A Keeper Field invitation alone never constitutes permission to offer therapy.

If a field includes minors or vulnerable groups, age, guardianship, confidentiality, crisis escalation and direct-messaging boundaries need separately ratified safeguards before any access. The pilot assumes an **adult, voluntary, non-clinical educational setting**; it does not authorize exceptions.

## Source rights and financial care

Copyright text-inference rights, teaching/session-recording rights, public source references, and keeper field-hosting rights are four **independent** permissions. Permission to appear in a live salon never grants AI training rights. Permission to cite an article never permits permanent embeddings. Participants author their own contributions, and may choose whether to share separately drafted representations outside the field.

Non-extraction is compatible with fair livelihood: a keeper may agree to a funded honorarium, direct sales of their work, scholarships, voluntary gifts, or a transparently priced course. No purchased recommendation placement, hidden affiliate fee, “unlock the wisdom keeper” contact toll, pressure to donate, participation leaderboard, or platform ownership of the person's teachings.

## A first truthful demonstration: original-study Circle, no keeper claimed

**Working inquiry:** *Where psychological symbols meet lived experience*. This is a founder-hosted educational study format using publicly linked works and carefully attributed original discussion, **not a Glenn Perry-hosted programme**.

An optional pilot design (subject to actual Circle access authority and human invitations) is one 75-minute, nonclinical, adult gathering with an operational facilitation target of roughly 6–8 people; the number is a *session design choice*, not a Circle constitutional maximum. The initial session could explore the difference between symbolic archetypes and first-person experience. No copyrighted readings are redistributed. In any future member-run pilot, private stories stay in the field, recording is OFF, and no group material moves to Commons or AIN.

If and only if the keeper later accepts an invitation, a second, separately approved pilot could introduce them as a guest for one agreed encounter with explicit honorarium, content rights and discussion scope.

### Sample 75-minute educational gathering (fictional run of show)

| Time | Invitation | Safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Arrive; host explains purpose, privacy and the freedom to pass | No promises of therapy, no recording, no compulsory introductions |
| 10–20 | Name the author, lineage and relevant publicly linkable sources | Do not imply Perry participates; no full-text redistribution |
| 20–35 | Explore one **Soullab-authored**, provisional question about competing needs | Not a diagnosis, trauma inference or chart-based fact |
| 35–55 | Quiet reflection and optional first-person voluntary sharing | People can listen; no compelled disclosure or identifying recap |
| 55–65 | Compare different interpretations without forcing agreement | Consent, disagreement and rest are legitimate |
| 65–75 | Close; each participant decides what, if anything, to keep privately | Nothing automatically promoted; exit without follow-up pressure |

The founder, an appropriately designated procedural facilitator or guest may lead only under the current Circle law. A future keeper-led version is a new event agreement, not a retroactive extension of this pilot.

## Additional futures, not current features

- **Living teaching rooms:** approved source files, open readings, properly licensed excerpts, public reflection journals and closed classroom dialogue.
- **Study cohorts:** self-paced preparation coupled with human discussions, certificates of attendance only if truthful, not scored maturity or spiritual progress.
- **Keeper collaborations:** two or more consenting keepers may cohost interdisciplinary conversations without misattributed composite teachings.
- **Cultural stewardship:** lineage/community custodians may require a separate cultural authority process before sharing collective knowledge. A single expert cannot grant rights belonging to a tradition.
- **Ethical residencies and apprenticeships:** longer, supported relationships with clear mutual expectations, safeguarding, grievance pathways and a genuine path to withdraw.
- **Member-to-keeper offerings:** questions, insights, or gratitude chosen by the member for a specific keeper, transmitted only after both sides' contact/visibility consent.

These futures should be prioritized by consent and verified experience, **not** by expected engagement or monetization.
