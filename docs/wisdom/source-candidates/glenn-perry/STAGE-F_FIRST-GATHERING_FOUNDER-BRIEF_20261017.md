# Where Symbols Meet Life

**Soullab Wisdom Circle · First human pilot · Founder review**

### What we're inviting

A small gathering where people explore the relationship between two human needs that seem to pull in different directions—for example, *the need for closeness and the need for space*.

Nobody has to disclose a personal story, accept an interpretation, or agree with a particular tradition. The purpose is simply to make room for genuine perception, relationship and choice.

**The gathering belongs to the people in it.** It is not a marketing funnel, group therapy, astrological diagnosis, AI-generated session or permanent shared-knowledge intake.

### First proposed format

**Online first · 75 minutes · 6–8 consenting adults · Soullab founder as host**

| Minutes | Movement | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Arrive | Welcome, freedom to pass, privacy and boundaries |
| 10–20 | Honor the source | Name intellectual lineages; distinguish symbolism from established fact |
| 20–35 | Two needs, one life | Earth, Water, Air, Fire and Weather as invitations to inquiry |
| 35–55 | Listen | Quiet reflection, sharing only if freely chosen |
| 55–65 | Discern | Allow different meanings, no imposed conclusion |
| 65–75 | Return | Each person keeps what is theirs; no automatic public summary |

Glenn Perry's AstroPsychology is an accurately credited **public reference**, not a guest or partner. Participants can find his works through [his own website](https://aaperry.com/) and [Academy](https://astropsychology.org/); his books do not need to be copied or purchased.

### Our chosen direction

Begin with a **private, host-controlled online meeting** where the human encounter, consent, and technology are genuinely tested. Soullab has meeting-link and individual Session Room substrates but **no accepted proof of ready-to-use private multiperson Wisdom Circles** from this lane. Choose the conferencing provider only after reviewing the real host controls, recordings/transcription setting and external provider data handling; do not invent a Zoom/Meet/LiveKit URL or claim self-hosting is already operational.

After one successful online gathering, continue with opt-in online study Circles and keeper-hosted salons if individual keepers consent. **Hybrid comes later:** local participants and remote participants must be able to hear one another, contribute or remain quiet, and receive equal care. It needs a separate venue, audio/privacy test and distinct event entry consent.

See the [online-first to hybrid roadmap](./STAGE-G_ONLINE-FIRST_TO-HYBRID_GATHERINGS-CANDIDATE_20261017.md). Neither phase has been scheduled or activated.

### The host protects five things

1. **Voluntary presence.** Listening quietly is complete participation.
2. **Meaning sovereignty.** Neither MAIA, facilitator, symbol nor group defines a person's life.
3. **Privacy and honest limits.** No planned recording, transcription, member-data mining or AIN sharing; the actual venue must be verified first, and group confidentiality cannot be guaranteed absolutely.
4. **Author recognition.** Soullab's elemental inquiry and Perry's proposals remain separate and visibly credited.
5. **No transactional pressure.** No required gift, paid introduction, ranking or obligation to return. Actual operating costs and terms will be declared before invitations.

### What is ready now

The [longer facilitator guide](./STAGE-F_FIRST-GATHERING_FACILITATOR-KIT_CANDIDATE_20261017.md) was drafted and checked; the unsent invitation and plain-language entry agreement are prepared; a six-movement rehearsal is available in the **disabled, founder-only preview code**. The test suite passed 138/138 in a disposable environment.

### What has not happened

No event date, room, real participants, outreach, Circle membership, cost or source license has been approved. Nothing has been deployed, booked, sent, recorded or admitted into MAIA's corpus.

### Your next human decision

Choose **which private online meeting provider/account** to test with fictional participants first. Then verify its admission, recording/transcription, privacy and accessibility controls; only afterward choose a date and finalize the invitation. Hybrid venue planning waits for a separate phase. A further explicit decision is required before contacting anybody.

**No need to select all the details at once.** First confirm that *Where Symbols Meet Life* feels like the right opening experience.
