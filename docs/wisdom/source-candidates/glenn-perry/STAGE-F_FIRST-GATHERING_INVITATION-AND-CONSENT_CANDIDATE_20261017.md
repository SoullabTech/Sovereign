**Subject:** An invitation to a small Soullab Wisdom Circle

**Draft**

I'd like to invite you to a small Soullab gathering called **Where Symbols Meet Life**.

Sometimes we find ourselves holding two real needs that appear to conflict: closeness and space, belonging and freedom, responsibility and rest. Rather than trying to solve or diagnose that tension, we'll explore what becomes possible when we listen to it with care.

## 2. The plain-language entry notice — to be given BEFORE joining

**An educational conversation, not therapy.** This is a nonclinical group exploring meaning, feeling and symbolic reflection. It is not medical/mental-health diagnosis, individual psychotherapy, supervision or crisis support. A founder's professional background does not alter the nature of this offered gathering.

**You choose your participation.** You may speak, write privately, listen without speaking, decline a prompt, leave or pause whenever you wish. Your silence will not be treated as failure or concern. No astrology, elemental vocabulary or psychological interpretation needs to fit you.

**What others can perceive.** In a group conversation, the other participants can hear what you choose to say and may remember it. We ask everyone to exercise care, discretion and respect, but Soullab cannot promise perfect confidentiality from other humans. Please decide for yourself what feels appropriate to share.

**Technology and custody.** The proposed format does not include recording, automatic transcription, AI notetaking, MAIA group analysis, marketing reuse, or contribution to AIN. The specific venue/platform must be checked for these functions and any unavoidable technical logs **before** a real event is offered. Do not claim "nothing is ever stored" without such verification. Practical administrative/incident records, if required, need separately stated handling and retention.

**Authorship.** Your words and experiences do not become Soullab's property or MAIA's shared knowledge by being heard. Private MAIA material is never mined for participation. Sanctuary material does not cross into shared fields. Any future author-controlled sharing would be a new decision with a clearly identified destination.

**Source and keeper representation.** Glenn Perry is an **independently referenced author**, not the host or an announced guest. No copyrighted book chapters will be reproduced. Participants may choose to explore his own publications at [aaperry.com](https://aaperry.com/) and the [Academy of AstroPsychology](https://astropsychology.org/), without needing to buy anything to take part.

**Safety and support.** If a question is distressing, you may pass, step away, or tell the procedural host. The group cannot guarantee immediate professional crisis care. Before a real launch, Soullab must name how a participant can request accessibility help, report a breach, and seek review of a procedural safety decision.

**Permission to participate.** Before entering, an actual participant should be shown the current host, date, venue, visibility rules and complete privacy information. Their choice to join should be explicit. An invitation alone is not consent, and consent to attend is not consent to record, publish or contact them outside the event.

## 3. Host welcome wording — first 60–90 seconds

> Thank you for coming. This is a small human gathering, not a lesson in what anyone's life is supposed to mean. The central invitation is to notice how two needs can live inside one person without either having to be wrong.
>
> You're always free to pass. We won't ask for your chart, diagnosis, private MAIA conversations, or any history you don't wish to share. I'm holding the time and boundaries, not interpreting who you are. There is no recording or automatic AI summary in the proposed format, and if our actual technology cannot honor the announced privacy arrangements, we'll stop.
>
> One small request: please care for the stories you hear here. You may take away your own learning, but please don't carry another person's identifiable words outside the room.
>
> Let's begin with something simple. Is there anything that helps you feel able to listen and choose freely today? You can answer with a word, or simply be quiet.
