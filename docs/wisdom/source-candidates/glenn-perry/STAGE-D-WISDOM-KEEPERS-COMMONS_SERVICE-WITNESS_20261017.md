## The service-level shift

The original Perry work began as attributed source curation. The founder's new request advances the question from **“Can MAIA responsibly reference this thinker?”** to **“Can Soullab responsibly help human beings encounter, support and learn with the people and communities stewarding wisdom?”**

Proposed identity: **Wisdom Keepers Commons**, potentially publicly named **Wisdom Keepers**. The word *Commons* here references the already-ratified **FR-02 Interest Commons** in the Circle ecology. This proposal does not create a parallel Commons, Circle, market, school, licensed text corpus or keeper directory. The older forum and contributions-status tables cannot be reused as a shortcut: Circle doctrine disallows status scoring and ambient exposure of private fields.

**Five modes, not a funnel:** discover original work; contemplate on one's own terms; voluntarily gather around an interest; meet a keeper *only by their consent*; and offer support freely without payment-based ranking or retention pressure.

## Architectural fit — checks against established Soullab law

- **FR-01:** connecting people never amplifies access rights to sources.
- **FR-02:** a Commons is a shared-interest ecology of members and Circles; do not collapse it into a large Circle or forum.
- **FR-06:** recommendation and discovery from **member-declared interest only**; never infer a private spiritual/psychological need from MAIA conversation, astrological data, memory or activity.
- **FR-07:** individually authored reflections cross only by their author’s destination-specific act; collectively authored Circle material needs consent from every contributor represented.
- **FR-08:** Sanctuary material NEVER crosses. No Circle interior or private conversational history is mined for public discovery. No status from counts/tiers/badges/ranks.
- **MAIA and AIN:** personal memory is not collective knowledge, and neither MAIA nor AIN acquires authority from a contact link or payment.
- **The right to remain unpossessed:** no nudges based on absence, inferred relationship graph or conversion funnel. A person may withdraw entirely.

## Reciprocity does not demand free labor

A commercial transaction is not the only form of support. Proposed voluntary forms include reading/citing original works, meaningful attendance, a public thank-you, direct purchase at the author’s site, facilitating a mutually desired gathering, or a transparently governed gift fund. Such support cannot buy placement, privilege, “wisdom rank” or entitlement to a keeper's time.

**Importantly, a paid honorarium may be just and necessary.** A living teacher who chooses to prepare and participate in an event should be offered fair, mutually agreed terms; a community should not consume unpaid expertise under the banner of non-extraction. Soullab's real platform and facilitation costs may also be transparent. This charter does not promise every paid Soullab service will become free or establish a fund before governance and accounting exist.
