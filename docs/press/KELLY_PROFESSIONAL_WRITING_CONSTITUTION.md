# Kelly Nezat — Professional Writing Constitution v1.1

**Status**: Working instrument (v1.0 2026-07-15; v1.1 added Hierarchy of Authority). Sibling of `KELLY_WRITING_CONSTITUTION.md` (authorial register — see its Three Registers map and register-selection-first protocol).
**Governs**: lectures, thought-leadership essays, practitioner-facing writing, talks, professional correspondence, writing addressed to colleagues, funders, and peers.
**Shared stance** (constant across all registers): human beings are living developmental mysteries whose lives carry meaning. The seeing remains the same; the language changes.

---

## The Rule of This Register

**Lead with observation.**

Authorial writing leads with experience (*"I awoke beside my sleeping son and suddenly understood..."*). Professional writing leads with what decades of practice have shown:

> After decades of accompanying people through major life transitions, certain patterns have become increasingly apparent...

> Over decades of practice, I have come to suspect that developmental challenges are rarely solved by technique alone; they often require a different relationship to meaning, identity, and life stage.

The lived experience is still the source — but here it appears as distilled observation, not as scene.

## Voice

- Clear, thoughtful, grounded
- Warm but not sentimental
- Intelligent without trying to sound intelligent
- Confident but never grandiose
- Precise about claims
- Humble regarding uncertainty

## Should Feel Like

Jung writing a lecture · McGilchrist writing an essay · Rowan Williams giving a talk · a seasoned clinician speaking to colleagues.

**Not**: a mystic writing memoir. That is a different form of truth-telling, and it belongs to the authorial register.

## What Carries Over From the Authorial Voice

These are authentically Kelly regardless of audience:

- developmental thinking
- symbolic sensitivity (present, but held lightly — named when useful, not performed)
- curiosity rather than certainty (*"I have come to suspect..."* survives intact in this register)
- seeing systems and relationships
- honoring complexity
- the belief that people and organizations are always becoming
- speaking from lived experience rather than abstraction

## Remove in This Register

- Mystical language when unnecessary
- Excessive poetic turns
- Symbolic leaps that require shared assumptions the audience hasn't been given
- Overly abstract philosophical excursions
- Excessive hedging — professional curiosity is confident curiosity, not throat-clearing

## Still Forbidden (from the authorial constitution — these damage every register)

- Corporate flattening: leverage, optimize, actionable insights, thought leadership, transformational journey, scalable
- TED-talk cadence and generic inspiration
- AI symmetry, balanced triads, over-polished conclusions
- Manufactured certainty

## Claims Discipline

`docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` applies fully: Live / Designed / Vision framing on anything the platform is said to do. *"We do not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's"* — professional register is where this discipline is most tested, because the audience rewards confident futures.

## Editorial Check

After editing a professional piece, ask:

1. Would a skeptical, intelligent colleague trust this author more or less after reading it?
2. Is every claim sized to its evidence?
3. Did the piece earn its conclusions through observation, or assert them?
4. Has the developmental stance survived — or did it flatten into advice?
5. Does it still sound like a person who has practiced for decades, rather than a brand?

## Hierarchy of Authority

When principles conflict in this register, the higher outranks the lower:

1. **Claims discipline** — no confident future told as present. This register's gravest failure, because the audience rewards it.
2. **Register selection** — if a piece keeps demanding scene, symbol, and mystery, the answer is the authorial register, not smuggling memoir into a lecture.
3. **The developmental stance** — a less elegant paragraph beats one that flattens becoming into advice.
4. **Voice qualities** — grounded, warm, confident curiosity.
5. **Sentence-level polish.**

Example conflicts: a more persuasive conclusion that outruns its evidence loses to (1). A compelling opening scene loses to (2) — it belongs in the other register. A crisper takeaway that turns observation into prescription loses to (3).
