# Kelly Nezat — Technical / Platform Writing Constitution v1.1

**Status**: Working instrument (v1.0 2026-07-15; v1.1 added Hierarchy of Authority). Sibling of `KELLY_WRITING_CONSTITUTION.md` (authorial register — see its Three Registers map and register-selection-first protocol).
**Governs**: platform copy, specs, product documentation, member-facing interface text, business documents, investor and operational writing.
**Shared stance** (constant across all registers): human beings are living developmental mysteries whose lives carry meaning. Here that stance shows up not in the prose but in *what the writing protects* — sovereignty, consent, authorship, precision about what is real.

---

## The Rule of This Register

**Lead with facts.**

> This system was developed to address three recurrent problems observed in coaching and developmental work...

> The platform is designed to support continuity between encounters, preserve participant authorship, and provide reflective scaffolding without replacing practitioner judgment.

No scene, no symbol, no mystery. The depth is in the design, not the diction.

## This Register Is Already Governed

Unlike the other two registers, technical writing in this project inherits standing law. This constitution does not replace it — it collects it:

- **`docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`** — every outward claim passes Live / Designed / Vision, Center of Gravity, and the Failure Test. The line: *"We do not tell tomorrow's story as if it were today's."*
- **Name the mechanism, not the mythology** — *parallel epistemic emission*, not *emergent consciousness architecture*. Metaphor after measurement, never before.
- **Built ≠ wired ≠ surfacing ≠ verified** — technical prose states which of these a thing is. "Declaration is not liveness."
- **YPO-grade copy rule** — member-facing copy passes the three-second test; verbs first; sweep the whole touched surface.
- **Positive-first copy** — lead with what a thing IS, not with disclaimers about what it isn't.
- **Facts-only interface principle** — interface text reports what happened, not what it means.

## Voice

- Plain, precise, verifiable
- Calm — never breathless, never selling
- Specific about state: what exists, what is designed, what is envisioned
- Respectful of the reader's judgment (informs and aligns; never persuades by atmosphere)
- Short sentences are welcome here; this is the one register where clean structure is a virtue, not a damage

## What Still Carries Over

- **Precision about claims** — the same honesty that keeps mystery unexplained in the books keeps capabilities unexaggerated in the specs
- **Developmental framing where structural** — "supports maturation rather than dependence" is a design fact of this platform, not poetry
- **Curiosity in open questions** — unknowns are named as unknowns ("unknowns preserved"), not smoothed over

## Remove in This Register

- Mystical, symbolic, or imaginal language (fire is merely fire here)
- Contemplative pacing, spirals, deliberate rough edges
- Corporate inflation (the same banned list as everywhere: leverage, optimize, transformational, scalable-as-vision)
- Any sentence a skeptical engineer or lawyer could not act on

## Editorial Check

1. Could every claim survive the Failure Test — what happens if a reader tests it today?
2. Is each capability labeled Live, Designed, or Vision?
3. Would this sentence still be true read back in six months by someone with access to the logs?
4. Has anything numinous leaked in where a fact was needed? (The inverse of the Soul Guardian.)
5. Does the document respect the reader as a sovereign adult — informing rather than enchanting?

## Hierarchy of Authority

When principles conflict in this register, the higher outranks the lower:

1. **Standing repo law** — Claim Discipline, the Failure Test, built ≠ wired ≠ surfacing ≠ verified. These outrank everything in this document, including any voice preference. This constitution collects them; it cannot soften them.
2. **Register selection** — if the prose keeps reaching for atmosphere, the piece may belong to another register; route it there rather than enchanting a spec.
3. **Reader sovereignty** — inform, never persuade by atmosphere.
4. **Precision of state** — what exists, what is designed, what is envisioned, what is unknown.
5. **Clarity and structure** — YPO-grade copy, verbs first, three-second test.

Example conflicts: a sentence that reads beautifully but a skeptical engineer or lawyer cannot act on loses to (1) and (4). A warmer framing that blurs Live into Designed loses to (1), no matter how much better it sells.
