## The intended derivation

1. A 60-second *How MAIA works* welcome for new members.
2. Contextual micro-guides — Keep, Sanctuary, Living Field, sharing, Start Fresh.
3. A searchable Help version the member can ask MAIA conversationally.

## ⭐ The structural problem the derivation must solve

The manual is written in the **normative voice** — *"MAIA should…"*, *"you should eventually be able to…"*.
That voice states **design law**. A member reads it as **present tense**.

The manual is correct as law and would be inflation as product copy. The derivation is therefore not an
editing task; it is a **claim-state pass**, and the derived artifacts are governed surfaces.

## ⭐ Two derived artifacts are downstream of T1-A, not of this record

The Keep micro-guide and the Keep Help topic would each have to tell a member **what act invokes Keep** and
**what exactly gets kept**. That is the open question `T1-A KEEP INVOCATION` was just chartered to answer.

Writing that copy now would settle the contract inside member-facing prose and hand it back upward as
established — the inverted direction of authority. **The guide does not get to decide what the gesture means.**

⛔ Held until T1-A produces the invocation contract.

## What is unblocked

The **60-second welcome** can be authored now, provided its centre of gravity sits on **invitations and member
authority** (§1 begin anywhere · §2 she can stay with you · §14 she does not decide what matters most ·
§16 she may be wrong · §40) rather than on capability. Those survive the Failure Test: strip every Designed and
Vision layer and they still hold, because they describe what the *member* may do.

⛔ The welcome must not include §17/§18-class beats (*ask why something appeared*, *ask what she's using*).
They are Vision and would read as present-tense promises.
