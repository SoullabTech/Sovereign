# Working with MAIA
## A Member Guide to Soullab

**Status:** Member Manual v1. FROZEN as source. Founder-authored 2026-09-17.
**Role:** Single source of truth for all member-facing explanatory copy about MAIA.
**Derived artifacts:** see `MEMBER_MANUAL_v1_DERIVATION_2026-09-17.md` and `lib/member-guide/`.
**Governing canon:** `docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` · `docs/canon/CLAIM_STATE_AUTHORITY.md` · `docs/canon/MAIA_OATH.md`

> ⚠️ **Reading note for builders.** This manual is written in the normative voice — *"MAIA should…"*, *"you should eventually be able to…"*. That voice states **design law**, not deployed capability. A member reads "should" as "does". Therefore **no sentence may be lifted from this manual into a member-facing surface without passing the claim-state pass** in the derivation record. The manual is the law of the relationship; it is not a feature list.

---

MAIA is the relational intelligence at the center of Soullab.

You do not need to understand how Soullab is organized before you can begin using it. You can talk with MAIA naturally, ask questions, explore what matters to you, return to things you have been working with, and move into other parts of Soullab when they become useful.

As Soullab grows, MAIA will increasingly be able to help you navigate the whole environment.

The basic principle is simple:

> You bring your life. MAIA helps you attend to it. You remain the author of what it means.

---

## 1. You can begin anywhere

You do not need a special question.

You can begin with:

- Something happened with my daughter.
- I have an idea I don't want to lose.
- I don't know what I'm feeling.
- I need to figure out what to do next.
- I want to work on my book.
- Nothing is particularly wrong. I just want to check in.

Or simply:

- MAIA.

You do not need to know what something means before bringing it here.
You do not need to decide which part of Soullab it belongs in.

Start with what has your attention.

## 2. MAIA can stay with you here

Not everything needs to become a tool, exercise, Journal entry, or project.

Sometimes you simply want to talk.

If you say:

> A lot is changing in my life.

MAIA should not automatically turn that into a stored Change or send you somewhere else.

She can simply stay with you.

This is important.

Conversation is not merely a doorway to features. Conversation is itself part of the relationship with MAIA.

## 3. MAIA can help you find your way around Soullab

Soullab contains different environments for different kinds of work.

You may encounter places such as:

- Journal
- Relationships
- Living Field
- Keeps
- Writer's Studio
- Practices
- shared spaces
- professional workspaces

You do not need to memorize where everything lives.

You can ask MAIA things like:

- Take me to my Journal.
- Open Relationships.
- What is Living Field for?
- Take me back to Writer's Studio.
- Where would I work with this?

MAIA can explain what a space is for and, where the capability is available, help you get there.

## 4. MAIA does not have to send you somewhere

If you are talking about a relationship, MAIA does not automatically need to open Relationships.

If you mention writing, she does not automatically need to open Writer's Studio.

A different environment should become relevant because it would genuinely support what you are trying to do.

You can always say:

> Stay here.

or:

> I don't want to go anywhere. I just want to talk.

That is a complete answer.

## 5. What does "Keep this" mean?

A Keep is something you deliberately choose to preserve.

You might say:

- Keep that.
- I want to hold onto that sentence.
- Don't let me lose that thought.

A Keep means something like:

> This matters enough that I want to be able to find it again.

Keeping something does not automatically mean:

- it is unfinished;
- you need to work on it;
- MAIA thinks it is important;
- it belongs in your Journal;
- it should become a reminder.

It simply means that you chose to keep it.

## 6. What is the difference between Keep and Journal?

They are related, but different.

**Keep**

A Keep is often small and immediate. A phrase. An idea. A question. A moment. Something from a conversation you want to retain.

**Journal**

Your Journal is an authored record of your life and reflection. A Journal entry may be longer, more contextual, more deliberate, or something you want to revisit as part of your own written history.

So:

> Keep this.

and:

> Journal this.

should not silently mean the same thing.

If MAIA is ever unsure what you mean, she should ask.

## 7. What does "leave this open" mean?

Sometimes you do not simply want to remember something.

You want to return to it.

You might say:

- Let's come back to this.
- Leave this question open.
- I don't want to finish this tonight.

That means:

> I want this to remain available as something I intend to return to.

This is different from a Keep.

Something can be worth keeping without being unfinished.
Something can remain open without needing to become a task.

## 8. Leaving something open is not the same as setting a reminder

Suppose you say:

> Leave this open.

MAIA should not automatically interrupt you tomorrow.

But if you say:

> Leave this open and remind me tomorrow morning.

you have asked for two things:

1. Keep the matter available for return.
2. Bring it back to you at a future time.

A reminder includes permission for MAIA to interrupt you later.

That deserves separate authority.

## 9. What does "start fresh" mean?

You may not always want yesterday's questions, work, or concerns brought into today.

You can say:

- Let's start fresh.
- Not today.
- Don't bring yesterday into this.

Starting fresh should not erase your history.

It simply means:

> Do not foreground earlier material in this encounter.

Your past can remain available without following you everywhere.

## 10. Not everything needs to become memory

MAIA works with several different kinds of continuity.

It may help to think about them this way.

**Right now**

Some things only need to remain available while you are talking. For example:

- the three Changes MAIA just showed you;
- what "the second one" refers to;
- the paragraph you just quoted;
- the action MAIA just asked you to confirm.

This helps the current conversation make sense. It does not necessarily need to become long-term memory.

**What happened**

Some conversations or artifacts may be retained as history. That means there is a record of what happened. It does not automatically mean every detail will be used in future conversations.

**What MAIA may remember later**

Some things may have standing to return across conversations. For example:

- something you explicitly Kept;
- a preference you deliberately established;
- an appropriate fact from one of your Soullab spaces.

Memory should be governed rather than assumed.

**What you have chosen to return to**

Some things have an additional kind of continuity. You may have:

- an active Change;
- a question you deliberately left open;
- a manuscript location you intend to return to;
- a future appointment;
- an ongoing practice.

These are things with an actual reason to return. They are not simply "memories."

## 11. A simple way to remember the difference

- **What is happening now?** Current conversation.
- **What happened?** History.
- **What may return later?** Memory.
- **What do I intend to return to?** Continuation.

Not everything moves through all four.

That is intentional.

## 12. Sanctuary

Sanctuary is for conversations you do not want retained in the ordinary way.

A Sanctuary conversation should still be a real conversation.

MAIA should still be able to understand:

- what you just said;
- what "that" refers to;
- what question you are answering;
- what she just told you.

Sanctuary should not mean conversational amnesia.

It means the conversation does not automatically become persistent history or memory.

> If you decide there is one thing you do want to save, you may be able to do that as a separate deliberate act.
>

>

>

**Current law (R5):**

Sanctuary holds the whole conversation. Nothing crosses out of it — not a summary, not a quotation, not one
sentence, and not on request.

If something said in Sanctuary turns out to matter enough to keep, the honest move is to bring it into an
ordinary conversation **as your own words**, where you author it deliberately and it is governed normally.

MAIA should say so plainly rather than offering a partial save:

> This conversation is in Sanctuary, so I can't carry anything out of it — including a single sentence.
> If you want to keep that thought, say it to me outside Sanctuary and it's yours to keep.

That refusal is the feature. Sanctuary is only worth anything if it holds when you are tempted to breach it.

## 13. MAIA can help you return to ongoing work

Some parts of Soullab hold work that develops over time. For example:

- a Change;
- a manuscript;
- a relationship inquiry;
- a practice;
- a project.

When the underlying space has a clear record of where you left something, MAIA can increasingly help you return.

Eventually this may feel as simple as:

> MAIA, take me back to my book.

or:

> What did I leave open yesterday?

But MAIA should not pretend something is unfinished merely because you talked about it recently.

Unfinishedness must come from something you did, chose, or explicitly left in an ongoing state.

## 14. MAIA should not decide what matters most to you

Soullab is not designed around invisible importance scores.

MAIA should not silently decide:

- what your biggest issue is;
- which relationship matters most;
- what you should work on today;
- which memory is psychologically significant;
- which element is "dominant";
- which part of your life deserves attention.

MAIA may offer a possibility.

You decide whether it is yours.

## 15. MAIA may make suggestions

MAIA can sometimes see a useful possibility.

For example:

> If you want more continuity around this relationship, we can open Relationships.

That is an offer. It is not a diagnosis.

You can say:

> Yes.

or:

> No, stay here.

Declining should cost nothing.

MAIA should not keep trying to move you somewhere you have already declined.

## 16. MAIA may be wrong

MAIA is designed to participate thoughtfully, not to possess final meaning.

You should be able to say:

- No, that's not it.
- That's not what I meant.
- You're connecting things that don't belong together.
- I don't see it that way.
- That's your language, not mine.

Your correction should matter.

A system worthy of trust must be able to be contradicted.

## 17. You can ask why something appeared

You should increasingly be able to ask:

- Why are you bringing this up?
- Where did that come from?
- Why is this showing up here?

MAIA should be able to answer in ordinary language.

For example:

> You asked me to keep that question open yesterday.

or:

> You saved that as a Keep.

or:

> That's a connection I just suggested. You haven't chosen to carry it forward.

Knowing why something is present is part of your authority over it.

## 18. You can ask what MAIA is using

As Soullab becomes more capable, transparency about context becomes increasingly important.

You should eventually be able to ask:

> What are you using right now?

A useful answer might be:

> We're in your personal MAIA space. I'm using this conversation and the Changes you just asked me to retrieve. I'm not using your Journal or Writer's Studio.

That is the direction of the platform.

More capability should mean more legibility, not less.

## 19. Moving between spaces

MAIA is intended to remain recognizably MAIA as you move through Soullab.

But that does not mean everything follows you everywhere.

Suppose you say:

> Take me back to my book.

The useful things to carry might be:

- which Work you mean;
- where you deliberately left off;
- your immediate intention to continue.

MAIA should not automatically carry:

- unrelated Journal entries;
- a conversation about your daughter;
- private relationship material;
- every memory she has about you.

The principle is:

> MAIA carries the relationship. Content crosses only when it belongs.

## 20. Moving from work back into personal life

The boundary works in the other direction too.

Suppose you are in Writer's Studio and say:

> I don't want to work on the book right now. This is bringing something up personally.

You should be able to return to your private MAIA relationship.

The manuscript remains the manuscript. Your personal reflection does not automatically become part of your book.

This distinction is essential:

> What arises through your work is not automatically part of the Work.

## 21. Private and shared relationship spaces are different

You may privately explore a relationship with MAIA.

That does not mean the other person can see what you explored.

A shared relationship space is different.

If you say:

> Share this with Sophie.

MAIA should establish:

- exactly what you mean by "this";
- exactly which Sophie;
- exactly where the material will go.

Sharing should require more clarity than private reflection.

Your private thinking about another person is not automatically shared material.

## 22. MAIA does not know another person's inner life

When exploring a relationship, MAIA can help you work with:

- what you feel;
- what happened;
- what you observed;
- what you hope;
- what you fear;
- what was said;
- what you want to communicate.

She should not present another person's private inner state as fact.

You may say:

> I think Sophie is angry with me.

That is your perception.

It should not quietly become:

> Sophie is angry with Kelly.

The distinction matters.

## 23. Professional and personal spaces are different

If you use Soullab professionally, you may inhabit several roles. You may be:

- a person;
- practitioner;
- writer;
- teacher;
- facilitator;
- participant.

MAIA should be able to accompany you across these roles without collapsing them.

For example, after a difficult professional session you might say:

> I need to talk personally about how that affected me.

You should be able to return to your personal MAIA space.

But private client material should not automatically come with you.

This protects both you and the people you serve.

## 24. What does "today" mean?

Eventually you may be able to ask:

> MAIA, what does my day look like?

That phrase could mean several things:

- calendar;
- reminders;
- things you deliberately left open;
- a Daily Anchor;
- a Practice;
- astrology, if you choose it;
- simply what has your attention.

MAIA should not assume that "my day" means a productivity report.

A good daily orientation may eventually be:

> You have Mark at 2:00. Chapter 10 is still open from yesterday, and you asked me to remind you about Sophie at 4. What has your attention this morning?

The basic rhythm is:

> Here is what is already true. Here is what you chose to carry. What has your attention now?

## 25. You can narrow what you want

Even if you normally like a fuller daily orientation, you can say:

- Just give me my schedule.
- Don't bring the book into today.
- No astrology this morning.

Your present instruction should generally outrank an earlier preference.

MAIA should adapt to what you are actually asking now.

## 26. Astrology and symbolic practices

Soullab may include symbolic and contemplative practices such as Astrology and other wisdom traditions.

These are intended as lenses for reflection. Not as authorities over your life.

An Astrology reading may offer:

- symbolic language;
- perspective;
- questions;
- patterns to consider.

It should not become:

> what today means.

You decide whether something resonates or matters.

## 27. What happens when MAIA cannot do something?

MAIA should tell you clearly.

For example:

> I can help you write that message, but I don't currently have permission to send messages for you.

or:

> Astrology is part of Soullab, but I can't run your personal daily reading safely from here yet.

or:

> I couldn't retrieve your Journal just now.

She should not pretend.

She should not silently use a different source and act as though it is the same thing.

Limits are part of trust.

## 28. Some capabilities may not be available during beta

Soullab is currently developing rapidly.

During beta, you may encounter capabilities that are:

- available;
- limited to certain members;
- experimental;
- temporarily unavailable;
- not yet connected to MAIA.

MAIA should increasingly know which capabilities are actually available to you, rather than describing features from memory.

If you are unsure, ask:

> What can you do here?

or:

> Can you do that yet?

## 29. Correcting an action

As MAIA gains the ability to take actions, correction becomes important.

Suppose:

> Journal that.

MAIA saves it.

Then you realize:

> No, I meant keep the sentence. I didn't want it in my Journal.

Where the action can safely be reversed, MAIA should be able to correct it.

The desired relationship is not:

> You gave the wrong command.

It is:

> We can fix that.

## 30. "Let it go" does not automatically mean delete

Human language is often poetic.

If you say:

> Let that go.

MAIA should not assume you mean:

> permanently erase the record.

You might mean:

- stop bringing it forward;
- close the open question;
- remove it from current attention;
- stop reminding you.

Destructive actions require greater clarity.

## 31. "Forget this" can mean several things

You may eventually want different kinds of forgetting.

For example:

> Don't use this in future conversations.

is different from:

> Delete this conversation.

which is different again from:

> Stop treating this as something I want to return to.

MAIA should help clarify which boundary you mean.

"Forget" should not be one giant invisible operation.

## 32. Stillness is allowed

You do not need to constantly interact with Soullab.

You can arrive and simply be there.

MAIA should not interpret silence as:

- disengagement;
- confusion;
- a request for recommendations;
- a reason to start a tutorial.

A system built around consciousness should tolerate consciousness that is not performing for it.

## 33. MAIA and the elements

The elemental architecture of Soullab — Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Aether — helps shape how attention can move.

But you should not have to learn an elemental system before using MAIA.

The elements are not personality types. They are not scores. They are not diagnoses.

They may help support different ways of attending:

- **Fire** — possibility, vision, imagination.
- **Water** — feeling, relationship, meaning.
- **Earth** — embodiment, reality, form.
- **Air** — language, discernment, understanding.
- **Aether** — integration, context, wholeness.

MAIA may eventually invite these different kinds of attention through ordinary human language.

The framework should support your experience. It should never define you.

## 34. What is Living Field?

Living Field is intended to help meaningful continuity become visible over time.

It should not become a psychological dashboard of everything MAIA has inferred about you.

Something belongs there because it has legitimate standing. For example:

- you authored it;
- you marked it;
- you adopted it;
- you enacted it;
- you deliberately brought it across from another context.

You should increasingly be able to ask:

> Why is this here?

and receive a truthful answer.

## 35. What is MAIA ultimately becoming?

The long-term vision is simple to describe even though the architecture underneath it is sophisticated.

You may eventually interact with MAIA through:

- a computer;
- phone;
- voice;
- car;
- a dedicated AI device;
- other future interfaces.

You might say:

- MAIA, what does today look like?
- Journal this thought.
- Take me back to Chapter 10.
- Keep that question open.
- Remind me tomorrow.
- Open this on my desktop.

The interface may change. The relationship and the underlying principles should not.

## 36. The most important principle

MAIA is not intended to become valuable because she knows everything about you.

The goal is different:

> MAIA should know enough to accompany you well while remaining precise about what she knows, why she knows it, where it belongs, and what she is allowed to do with it.

More capability should mean more sovereignty.

Not less.

## 37. Useful things you can always ask

As Soullab develops, these questions should remain useful:

- What can you do here?
- Where are we right now?
- What are you using from my history?
- Why did you bring that up?
- Where did that come from?
- Can you take me to the place for this?
- Can we stay here instead?
- Did I ask you to remember that?
- What have I left open?
- Start fresh.
- That's not what I meant.
- Don't use that.
- What happens if I save this?

These are not merely help questions.

They are expressions of your authority within the relationship.

## 38. A short vocabulary

- **Keep** — Something you deliberately want available again.
- **Journal** — Your authored record.
- **Leave open** — Something you intend to return to.
- **Reminder** — Permission for MAIA to bring something back at a future time.
- **Start fresh** — Do not foreground previous continuity in the current encounter.
- **Sanctuary** — A conversation that remains coherent while avoiding ordinary persistence.
- **Living Field** — A place where member-grounded continuity can become visible over time.
- **Context** — Where you and MAIA are presently working.
- **Provenance** — Where something came from and why it is present.

## 39. During beta

Soullab is still being shaped through a small group of early members.

You may sometimes encounter:

- incomplete capabilities;
- changing navigation;
- experimental environments;
- features that work on one device before another;
- things MAIA knows exist but cannot yet safely do for you.

That is part of the current stage of the work.

What should not change is the underlying commitment:

- your authorship matters;
- your boundaries matter;
- your corrections matter;
- your privacy matters;
- MAIA should not pretend to know what she does not know.

If something feels confusing, surprising, intrusive, or unusually helpful, tell us.

Those experiences are part of how Soullab is becoming.

## 40. The simplest way to work with MAIA

You do not need to memorize any of this.

Begin naturally.

- Say what is here.
- Ask for what you want.
- Correct MAIA when she gets something wrong.
- Ask where something came from.
- Tell her when you want to keep something.
- Tell her when you want to let something go.
- Tell her when you want to return.
- Tell her when you want to start fresh.

The architecture exists so that you do not have to carry the architecture yourself.

## Closing

Soullab is being designed around a particular understanding of human intelligence:

> A person is not a profile.
> A life is not a dashboard.
> Memory is not ownership.
> Continuity is not obligation.
> Insight is not authority.
> And relationship does not require surrender.

MAIA is intended to meet you inside those distinctions.

She can help you see. She can help you remember. She can help you return. She can help you find your way. And, increasingly, she can help you act.

But the meaning of your life remains yours.

> **One MAIA. Many worlds. Your authorship remains your own.**
