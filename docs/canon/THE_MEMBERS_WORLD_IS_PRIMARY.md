# The Member's World is Primary

> **Status:** **Canon candidate — founder-authored 2026-07-31.** Consistent with
> `RATIFICATION_BOUNDARY_PRECEDENT_2026-07-26.md`, this article becomes **ratified
> canon only through an explicit act of authorization** by the designated authority.
> Writing it here is *preparation* (additive); it changes the governing status of no
> other text and authorizes no implementation until ratified.
>
> **Authored by:** Kelly Nezat (founder) — 2026-07-31
> **Type:** Canon — a constitutional article of *identity and orientation*. It
> governs **the direction of attention** in the interface. It grants no capability.
> **Governs:** every member-facing surface — copy, UI, onboarding, navigation,
> interactions, MAIA's conversation, notifications, empty states, confirmations,
> and error messages. First applied in the Work Studio.

---

## The article

> The Studio speaks from the member's world, never from its own.
>
> The Studio describes what the member comes to do, what they have brought, and
> what they may continue. It does not describe its own capabilities, architecture,
> or internal operations unless honesty, consent, safety, or sovereignty require it.
>
> **The member experiences their work. The platform recedes.**

---

## Why this is constitutional, not a copy guideline

Every interface makes a choice about where attention is directed:

- to the software,
- to the AI,
- to the system's capabilities,
- or to the person's work.

Most design systems ask *how should the interface sound?* This article asks
something more fundamental: **whose world does the interface inhabit?** That is an
identity question, not a style question — which is why it belongs near the
beginning of the canon rather than among interface guidelines.

It completes a triad already established elsewhere in the canon:

- **The creator authors meaning.**
- **The work is the protagonist.**
- **The Studio is the environment.**
- → **The interface inhabits the member's world.**

If the work is the protagonist *in the architecture*, the member's world must be
the protagonist *in the interface.* From this single principle, hundreds of small
design decisions become almost self-evident. A button no longer says "Use AI to
summarize" — it says "Summarize this conversation." A room no longer says
"Research Management" — it says "Research." The interface stops narrating itself
and begins disappearing into the work.

---

## Implications

**The Studio speaks about:** the work · the creator's actions · the creator's
choices · the creator's experience.

**It does not center:** the platform · AI · features · implementation ·
architecture · technical capability.

---

## The Copy Test

Every member-facing sentence should answer one question:

> **Does this describe the member's world, or the Studio's?**
> **If it describes the Studio, rewrite it.**

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| The Studio organizes your ideas. | **Organize your ideas.** |
| The Studio remembers where you left off. | **Pick up where you left off.** |
| The Studio helps connect your research. | **Keep your research together.** |
| The Studio supports multiple creative formats. | **Books, workshops, journals, research, talks, courses, and ideas all belong here.** |

**Companion rule for member-facing writing:** *write like someone inviting a
respected colleague into their studio — not like a product introducing features.*

## Constitutional boundary

This principle **yields only where the Studio must speak truthfully as itself.**
These moments include:

- **Consent** — what will and won't happen (e.g. Sanctuary Mode: "This session
  won't be remembered").
- **Honesty** — what is implemented, held, or unavailable (a *held* room must read
  as held, not built; cf. `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`).
- **Refusal** — when MAIA or the Studio cannot, or will not, do something.
- **Security** — actions affecting privacy, identity, or data.
- **Provenance** — when the source of information matters.

In these moments the Studio speaks plainly about itself, because doing otherwise
would **diminish the member's sovereignty.** The member's world is the protagonist
of the *experience of their work*; the member's *sovereignty* sometimes requires
the system to name itself. The boundary is not an escape hatch from the
principle — it is the seam that keeps the principle from being turned into
concealment.

## The disappearing craft

Every mature craft eventually learns to disappear behind the thing it serves.

- For a violin maker, it is the music.
- For a potter, it is the meal.
- For the Work Studio, it is the creator's work.

The Studio exists to serve the relationship between the creator and the work — not
to become another participant competing for attention. That is the same identity
this canon has established elsewhere, expressed now as a discipline of language and
attention.

## Where this already lives (consolidation, not invention)

This article names, at the level of the *interface*, a posture the canon already
holds at the level of *authority* and *epistemics*:

- **[CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md](./CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md)**
  — meaning may only move upward through authored experience; the system never
  manufactures higher-order meaning. This article is the interface-facing corollary:
  the interface never centers itself over the member's authored work.
- **[INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md](./INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md)** — the system may observe and
  propose; it may not conclude. Here: the interface may serve and recede; it may
  not narrate itself as the protagonist.
- **[RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md](./RIGHT_TO_REMAIN_UNPOSSESSED.md)** — the member
  is never reduced to a machine reading of themselves. Here: the member is never
  made an audience to the machine.
- **[MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md](./MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md)** — governs the
  honesty of outward claims; the "Honesty" clause of the boundary above defers to it.
- **[MAIA_OATH.md](./MAIA_OATH.md)** / **[MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md](./MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md)**
  — the vow to reduce the system's psychological centrality over time. Directing
  attention to the member's work rather than to the platform is that vow made
  concrete in every sentence.
