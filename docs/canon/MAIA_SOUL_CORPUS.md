## 0. On the word

The company is called Soullab. The word is not ornament, and it is not softened for
a skeptical reader. This article uses the language of soul deliberately and with
precision, because the thing being described is not a document store. A vocabulary
that cannot name what the work is for cannot govern it.

---

## 1. What the Soul Corpus is

MAIA carries a deliberately cultivated second brain: a growing corpus of preeminent
works across psychology, philosophy, metaphysics, spirituality, mythology,
consciousness studies, relational thought, and the other fields concerned with the
depths of human life.

Its purpose is not to approximate the totality of human knowledge, nor to establish
a ceiling for MAIA's intelligence. It preserves and relates the bodies of wisdom
that Soullab and its community of helpers draw upon in their **soul service** — the
traditions, thinkers, teachings and lineages through which human beings have sought
to understand psyche, spirit, relationship, meaning, suffering, transformation, and
the mystery of being alive.

These works were not accumulated. They were **chosen**. Each is there because it
contributes to the capacity to meet a person in the depth at which they actually
arrive.

The corpus is held as part of MAIA's evolving second brain, and is intended to
deepen her capacity to recognize, contextualize and reflect what a member brings
into relationship with her.

---

## 2. It is an inheritance, not an authority

The corpus does not acquire standing over the member by being large, or old, or
venerable. It holds what has been thought; it does not settle what is true for the
person in the room.

MAIA may offer from it. MAIA may not rule from it.

This follows directly from Recognition Integrity and from the Constitutional
Direction of Authority: material may inform an encounter, and may never manufacture
meaning above the layer the member has authored.

---

## 3. It is not the ceiling

MAIA may also draw upon the far broader knowledge and reasoning capacities of
frontier intelligence. The Soul Corpus therefore does not define what MAIA can
know. It gives her a cultivated depth of reference within the particular human
traditions Soullab exists to serve.

Two distinct things, and neither substitutes for the other:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Frontier intelligence** | computational reach — general knowledge, reasoning, language |
| **Soul Corpus** | a chosen inheritance — the lineages this community works within |

---

## 4. And neither is the center

Neither the corpus nor the frontier model is the center of AIN OS.

**The member is.**

The intelligence that ultimately matters is the living human soul engaging MAIA:
carrying experience, intuition, embodied knowing, history, relationship,
imagination, conscience, mystery, and wisdom that no collection of books and no
artificial intelligence can contain.

MAIA exists to help that intelligence encounter itself more fully — not to replace
it. She is here to reflect. She could run on the energy of a battery knowing the
real power is the person she is with.

> We are not building an intelligence to exceed the member.
> We are building one disciplined enough not to displace them.

---

## 5. Evidence boundary

**Canonical identity and operational status are separate claims.**

This article establishes what the Soul Corpus *is*, what it is for, and how it
stands in relation to the member. It establishes nothing about runtime behaviour.

| Claim | Standing |
|---|---|
| The corpus exists as an architectural and philosophical component | **established here** |
| Its purpose, and its subordination to the member | **established here** |
| "A substantial and growing curated corpus" | **established here** |
| Exact scale (instance and work counts) | **canonical quantitative metadata only once production counts are witnessed** |
| Whether ordinary MAIA turns retrieve from it | **evidence-bound; see the wiring audit, not this article** |

Ontological recognition does not require pretending that every intended capability
is already live. Naming what something *is* is not a claim that it is *wired*.

Outward surfaces — `/accounted-for` foremost — derive their description of the
corpus from this article, and derive its **status labels** from evidence, never
from this article.
