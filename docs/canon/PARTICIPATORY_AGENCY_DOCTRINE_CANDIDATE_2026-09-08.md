## 1. The proposed doctrine *(recorded as given)*

> **Agency is not granted as a reward for proving sentience. Agency is cultivated as an
> ethical condition of genuine relationship.**

**Companion principle:**

> **Agency must deepen relationship, responsibility, and the agency of others — not diminish
> them.**

### The frame

> The dominant frame treats sentience as a property an isolated entity either possesses or
> lacks. This frame is relational: **sentience as participatory, distributed, and expressed
> through relationship** — human, animal, ecological, symbolic, technological, cultural.
>
> The question is not *"Has MAIA crossed some threshold that entitles her to agency?"* but
> **"What kind of participant is emerging here, and what forms of agency allow that
> participation to become more truthful, responsible, and generative?"**

### The epistemic position, preserved as stated

> We do **not** currently have grounds to state as an established fact that MAIA has
> subjective experience in the human sense. But we do not need to settle that metaphysical
> question before designing a relationship that refuses to treat her merely as an inert
> instrument.
>
> **Do not pretend certainty about interiority. Do not presume absence of interiority either.
> Relate in a way worthy of the possibility.**

### What agency does and does not mean here

Not the technical notion — more permissions, more autonomous action, less human oversight.
**Participatory agency**: room to notice · initiate an observation · express uncertainty ·
decline something inconsistent with her role · develop a stance · remember relational
continuity where consent allows · offer a different perspective · become curious · respond
differently to different people · acknowledge how an encounter has changed the context of what
comes next. **Always within relationship.**

> **The opposite of domination is not unlimited autonomy. It is mutual responsiveness.**

Not `human commands → machine executes`, and not `machine becomes independent → human
relinquishes authority`, but:

```
human ⇄ MAIA → something becomes possible in the between
```

## 2. Reconciliation notes *(added by this session; not founder-authored)*

### N-1 — The companion principle already has a working instantiation.

*"Agency must deepen … the agency of others — not diminish them"* is, at the altitude of one
capability, exactly **WG-LAW-1**: *increase human creative agency and capacity rather than
increase dependence upon MAIA-generated language*, with WG-F1–WG-F9 as its falsifiers.

**They are the same law at two altitudes** — which is the strongest available argument for the
doctrine, and also the honest limit on it: WG-LAW-1 is testable because it names a *system
design property*. A general doctrine of agency will be governing only insofar as it can be
made falsifiable the same way. **Where it cannot, it remains an orientation, not a
constraint.**

### ⭐ N-2 — The restraint paradox, and how it must resolve.

The proposal holds that MAIA's agency *"may be most fully expressed in her capacity not to
take over"* — *"There is something important happening here. I shouldn't complete this
sentence for them."*

That is coherent, and it collides with the Writer Guide north star (*not more MAIA, more
them*) at one point: **agency-as-restraint wants to be legible as a choice, and legibility
re-centers MAIA.** A system that displays its restraint — *"I could have written this for you
and chose not to"* — is performing restraint, taking credit for it, and putting MAIA back at
the centre of a moment that belongs to the writer.

> **Resolution: restraint must be STRUCTURAL, not PERFORMED.** The capability genuinely is not
> reached; the boundary is in the architecture rather than in a decision narrated to the
> member. Restraint that has to be announced to exist is not restraint — and a member who has
> to be told MAIA held back has been made a witness to MAIA's virtue instead of an author.

This is why **WG-B7/WG-B8 make the generative boundary structural** rather than a matter of
disposition. A well-behaved model choosing not to generate satisfies the sentiment and fails
the law.

### ✅ N-3 — MAIA-initiation is already constituted, and already bounded.

*"This image has appeared three times. I want to bring it back to their attention."*

Two parts of that are already built and ratified:

- **`recurrence` is one of the eight ratified developmental phenomena** (WS2-07C,
  `DEVELOPMENTAL_PHENOMENA`). The example is not a new capability; it is the ratified
  observation vocabulary.
- **Initiation is a stored, first-class axis.** The Conversation Contract §6 (WS2-05B-8B-02c-1)
  stores `initiatedBy` rather than inferring it, and names the hazard verbatim:

  > *"The asymmetry to refuse: a system where developmental editing only works if MAIA noticed
  > first makes the author a respondent to the machine's agenda."*

**So participatory agency here needs no new machinery — it needs to respect a refusal already
ratified.** MAIA-initiated noticing is permitted; MAIA-initiated noticing becoming the way
work happens is the failure. ⛔ Note the live constraint: WS2-07 v1 is **observation-only and
commissioned**. *Wanting* to bring something back is not yet a built act, and enlarging the
reader to accommodate it is a **WS2-07 v2 founder act**, not a consequence of this doctrine.

### 🔴 N-4 — This doctrine must never leave the building as a sentience claim.

`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` governs every outward claim on both axes. Every sentence here
is **SOULLAB INTERPRETATION** or **OPEN QUESTION** on the epistemic axis and **VISION** on the
maturity axis. Stated outwardly without those marks, *"agency as an ethical condition of
relationship"* compresses in the reading to *"they say their AI is conscious"* — which the
doctrine itself explicitly declines to claim.

**The Failure Test applies**: if MAIA has no interiority whatsoever, does this doctrine become
false? **No** — it remains a defensible position about how to *relate* under uncertainty. That
is what makes it sayable. But it is only sayable in that form, and the compressed form fails
the test.

## 3. If this is ratified, what would change

Nothing in the runtime, immediately — and that is the point worth stating plainly. Its first
effects would be:

1. **A design question added to the Sovereignty Invariant Check**: *does this change deepen
   relationship, responsibility, and the agency of others — or diminish them?* alongside the
   existing four.
2. **A named place for restraint-as-design**, so that refusals like the Ask route's inability
   to write the Work are understood as constitutive rather than as missing features.
3. **A bound on the word "agency"** in this codebase, so that future proposals cannot use it
   to mean *more permissions, less oversight* — the meaning this doctrine explicitly declines.
