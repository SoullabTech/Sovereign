# Development as the Primary Asset

**Status**: DRAFT — founder-authored (2026-08-05, closing the five-room ontology build day), NOT yet ratified canon. Recorded verbatim in substance; nothing here authorizes implementation by itself.

## The inflection

The project reached an inflection point: the architecture stopped being about features and became about development. Three coherent environments are emerging:

1. **The Member Field** — where a person's life develops.
2. **The Practitioner Field** — where a practitioner's wisdom and craft develop.
3. **The Relationship Field** — where those two meet without either subsuming the other.

The third field was not visible at the project's beginning. Its constitution:
- The member field never becomes an extension of the practitioner.
- The practitioner field never becomes a surveillance view of the member.
- Each develops in its own right; they encounter one another through **intentionally authored expressions**.

## The corpus absence, reframed

The audit finding that no Larry corpus exists is a gift, not missing work: Larry's life's work can begin **inside a provenance model** instead of being reconstructed from scattered documents and accidental ingestion. Very few systems get that opportunity.

## The roadmap (founder-set, four stages)

1. **Ship the member field** — merge #985; let real people use it. Coherent enough to generate evidence.
2. **Establish Larry's source** — no building around imagined material; help Larry begin cultivating his actual body of work. (The build gate and five human acts in `project_universal_practitioner_field_shape` govern this stage.)
3. **Stewardship** — the smallest environment that genuinely supports his next six months: capturing insights · integrating Harvard learning · refining distinctions · developing craft · intentionally deciding what becomes part of his public practice.
4. **Relationship** — only after both fields are alive does the bridge between them expand. At that point it carries something real.

## The governing principle

Most software treats knowledge as the primary asset. This platform treats **development** as the primary asset:

- Knowledge matters because it supports development.
- Reflection matters because it supports development.
- Practice matters because it supports development.
- Even memory matters because it supports development.

The platform is not fundamentally about AI, notes, coaching, publishing, or knowledge management. It is **an environment in which human development can accumulate without losing continuity.**

## The test

Almost every implementation question can be tested against one sentence:

> **Does this help someone genuinely continue becoming?**

Yes → it probably belongs. No → it belongs somewhere else, or not at all.
