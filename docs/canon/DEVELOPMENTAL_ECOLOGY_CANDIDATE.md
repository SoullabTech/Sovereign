# Developmental Ecology

## Status

**Candidate** — stronger than conceptual, not yet ratified (as of 2026-07-08).

- **Evidence:** 4 independent strong convergences (verified — see §8).
- **Scope:** the jurisdictional / stewardship boundary.
- **Known siblings** (outside this family): representational honesty, consent/custody,
  infrastructure sovereignty (see §4).

Observed across multiple independent architectural discussions before being written
down. Held as a candidate under the discipline: *observe freely, canonize
reluctantly.* Promotion requires that future work continue to derive naturally from
it **within its jurisdiction** (§4 Scope). If it does, it earns canon. If it does
not, it remains an instructive candidate rather than becoming premature canon.

This document has a scoped role. It does not attempt to explain every constitutional
family. It explains why one family — the jurisdictional-authority invariants —
coheres. It is a bridge between the Constitution (which states the invariants) and
the Companion (which helps people live them).

---

## 1. Constitutional Horizon

AIN is not a platform for improving people.

AIN cultivates developmental ecologies in which people, relationships, communities,
practices, and bodies of work may develop through participation.

Development belongs to participants, not to the platform.

---

## 2. Ontology

Persons and worlds continually participate in one another. Development is reciprocal
in its **dynamics**:

```text
Person ⇄ World
```

This is an observation about how life unfolds, not a claim the platform is
authorized to act upon symmetrically. Reciprocity in dynamics does **not** license
symmetry in authority (see §4).

---

## 3. Jurisdiction

The platform configures ecologies of participation.

It never configures beings.

```text
Platform
     │
     ▼
Conditions of Participation
     │
     ▼
Person ⇄ World
```

The platform never reaches the world directly, and never reaches into the person.
It shapes only the conditions under which participation occurs.

---

## 4. Authority

**The platform's authority terminates before personhood begins.**

Authority always terminates at the person — not because the person is the whole
story, but because the person is the only being over whom the platform could
otherwise become coercive. That is the asymmetry: reciprocity in dynamics,
asymmetry in authority.

### Scope

This principle governs the **jurisdictional-authority family** of constitutional
invariants — the relational and interpretive constraints. It is the shared root of,
among others:

- the prohibition on assigning meaning (Living Field Mirror — never synthesis)
- the prohibition on inferring worth
- the prohibition on configuring identity
- the prohibition on defining responsibility
- the prohibition on determining contribution
- Recognition Integrity and the Right to Remain Unpossessed
  (Constitutional Direction of Authority, Invariant 16)
- Cultural Sovereignty (Invariant 14)

It does **not** govern custody, locality, or data sovereignty. A principle that
appeared to generate every family would generate none. This one is a family root, not
a universal root.

**Known sibling families** (nearby, but outside this one — do not annex them):

- *Representational honesty* — honest claims about the system's own capability
  (`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`). Governs what the platform says about itself, not
  what it authors about the member.
- *Consent / custody* — Sanctuary Mode's no-retention boundary; what is held, how, why.
- *Infrastructure sovereignty* — self-hosting, provider sovereignty, locality.

Each was tested against P and found to belong elsewhere. Naming them here is what
keeps this candidate from drifting into a theory of everything.

### Generalization (verified 2026-07-08)

The verification pass surfaced one genuinely new finding. The boundary is not merely
*"platforms must not author people."* The Practitioner Onboarding ritual states the
same constraint of a **human** steward — *"development is not mine to author; I steward
conditions."* So the principle is more precisely:

> **Stewards must not author the development of those they accompany.**

That is what makes it portable across MAIA, practitioners, rituals, rooms, and fields
without overreaching into every part of AIN. The platform is one steward among several;
the boundary governs the stewarding relation, not the software specifically.

---

## 5. Operational Discriminator

**Recognition versus interpretation.**

The principle in §4 is a *boundary*. This discriminator is how the boundary is
*located in practice* — without it, "authority terminates before personhood" could
collapse into "the platform must be mute about the person," which would break
reflection itself. Reflecting what the member authored is stewardship of their own
material. Asserting what they did not is crossing the line.

The platform may steward what the member has authored. It may not author the member.

| Member says          | Platform must not say   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| I believe…           | You believe…            |
| I value…             | You value…              |
| I feel responsible…  | You are responsible for…|
| I experience…        | You are…                |
| I seek…              | Your purpose is…        |
| I feel called to serve… | You should serve…    |
| Belonging matters to me… | You need belonging… |
| This community is home… | This is your community…|

The words are often identical. The jurisdiction is completely different.

---

## 6. Method

Development is invited. Never prescribed.

Participation is supported. Never compelled.

Recognition is offered. Never imposed.

---

## 7. Expression

> Development may find outward expression through participation, care, creativity,
> stewardship, relationship, service, or countless other forms authored by the
> person themselves. The platform neither prescribes, ranks, nor measures those
> expressions.

This wording is deliberately **descriptive rather than normative**. Earlier drafts
read "mature development expresses itself *as* contribution," which quietly defined
the fulfilled life and was falsified by the contemplative, the parent of one child,
the elder whose contribution is presence, the artist who never publishes, and the
person living with profound illness. The descriptive form keeps the horizon outward
without defining what a fulfilled life must look like.

## 8. Evidence — why this became a candidate

This candidate is not promoted because it is elegant. It is promoted because it kept
reappearing, independently, across architectural contexts that were not reasoning
about it. Convergence from unrelated directions is the evidence; the following are
the observed reappearances (each traceable to existing canon or working docs):

Verified sources (independent convergence — the constraint, not the vocabulary, is
present and the source would not hold if P were false):

- **Sovereignty Invariants** — Inv 11 (Declared Significance): *"MAIA honors what the
  member declares meaningful. It does not decide meaning on their behalf."* Inv 6
  (Mirror Integrity) and Inv 16 (Recognition Integrity) sit on the same constraint.
  The invariants presuppose P (`docs/canon/MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`).
- **Session Room / Living Encounter** — *"AI interpretations must never overwrite
  source records… authority over meaning stays with the human."* Reasons about
  session architecture and lands on P independently
  (`docs/canon/SESSION_ROOM_LIVING_ENCOUNTER.md`).
- **FIS Field State Primitive** — *"a property of the relational ecology, not a
  property of the person, and not something the system generates"*; explicitly does
  not authorize systems to diagnose or prescribe field state. P-claim-3 verbatim
  (`docs/canon/FIS_FIELD_STATE_PRIMITIVE.md`).
- **Practitioner Onboarding** — *"This person's development is not mine to author. I
  steward conditions; I do not author outcomes."* Applies P to a *human* steward —
  evidence P generalizes beyond the platform
  (`docs/practitioner/ONBOARDING_RITUAL.md`).

Weaker / partial (kept, honestly labeled):

- **ADR-013** (Implicit / Moderate) — configures *regions of attention*, not beings;
  but its central concern is the AINOS↔MAIA boundary, and it would largely still hold
  if P were false. Supports, does not presuppose
  (`docs/adr/013-context-assembly-ainos-maia-boundary.md`).
- **Recognition Integrity** (Implicit / Moderate) — *"we do not optimize for felt
  recognition"* is primarily anti-capture, a **sibling** constraint to P rather than
  P itself (`docs/canon/RECOGNITION_INTEGRITY.md`).

Excluded after verification:

- **Constitutional Direction of Authority** — P is present nearly verbatim, but this
  is the **parent** P derives from (P "completes" its Inv 16). That is near-identity,
  not independent convergence; counting it would be circular. It confirms coherence;
  it adds no independent evidentiary weight.
- **Marketing Claim Discipline** — **fails for P.** Its constraint governs honest
  representation of *the system's own claims* ("we do not tell tomorrow's story as
  today's"), not authorship of *the member's* meaning. It would make complete sense if
  P were false. It belongs to a sibling family (representational honesty), not P's
  jurisdictional-authority family. Removed.

The self-help-vs-developmental-ecology distinction that *surfaced* this document is
deliberately **not** listed as evidence: it is the originating conversation, and
citing it would be circular. Only sources that reasoned about something else, and in
which the constraint is nonetheless latent, count as independent convergence.

Two independent routes reaching the same wall — the ontology side
(*"configures ecologies, never beings"*) and the interface side
(*places-not-features*) — is the strongest available evidence that the wall is real.

**Result:** four independent sources reasoning about *different subjects* (invariants,
session architecture, field-state dimensions, a practitioner ritual) each land on the
same constraint and would not hold if it were false. That is genuine convergence, and
it survives the strict test. Two cited sources were downgraded to partial, and two
were **removed** — one as circular (the parent P derives from), one as belonging to a
sibling family. No contradicting language was surfaced in eight documents; note this
was one adversarial pass, not a proof — the Falsifiers below remain the standing test.

Three questions decided each row — a stricter test than "do the words recur":

1. **Independent implication** — would a careful reader of this source *arrive at* the
   candidate, not merely be able to read it in?
2. **Falsification** — would the source still make sense if the candidate were false?
   If yes, the evidence is weaker; if no, the constraint is genuinely latent.
3. **Compression** — does the candidate explain the source better than the source
   explains the candidate? A real candidate reduces explanatory burden across
   multiple documents.

### Potential falsifiers (conditions for revision)

This candidate names its own conditions for weakening or retraction:

- If a canon document requires the platform to **author meaning** rather than steward
  member-authored meaning, the candidate **fails**.
- If an architectural decision requires **configuring beings** rather than ecologies,
  the candidate **fails**.
- If a field cannot be understood through **encounter as primitive**, the candidate
  **weakens**.
- If a later architectural family consistently **resists compression** by this
  candidate while remaining constitutionally sound, the **scope should be narrowed**
  rather than expanded — the family root shrinks; it does not annex the resistant
  family.

The document is authoritative not because it is well argued, but only insofar as it
faithfully compresses independently observed reality. The Evidence section, not the
principle, is load-bearing.

---

## 9. Summary

- **Ontology** — people and worlds co-create one another.
- **Jurisdiction** — the platform configures ecologies of participation, never beings.
- **Authority** — the person's sovereignty is inviolable; platform authority
  terminates before personhood begins.
- **Method** — development is invited, never directed.
- **Expression** — the meaning and form of participation remain authored by the member.

None of these requires the platform to define what a "good life" is. Together they
explain why AIN is neither a productivity system nor a self-help system: it is a
constitutional environment in which development can occur without the platform
claiming ownership over either the person or the destination of their life.
