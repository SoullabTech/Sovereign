# Soullab Living Orientation System

**Status:** Canon — founder-ratified direction, 2026-09-26.
**Scope:** House, member-facing rooms/facets, MAIA continuity, Living Field projection, and every cross-facet handoff.
**Companion canon:** `THE_HOUSE.md` · `INHABITABLE_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md` · `SACREDNESS_AS_ARCHITECTURAL_ORIENTATION.md` · `FEDERATED_RELATIONAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

> **Soullab helps a person remain in relationship with their own life.**

Soullab is not a collection of wellness tools and not a dashboard of system capabilities.

It is a living orientation environment for one life across time: self, relationships, work, imagination, development, symbolic pattern, embodied practice, sources, choices, changes, community, and the larger field in which the person participates.

The rooms are not separate applications. They are **distinct facets of attention on one life**.

---

## 1. The member is the center of orientation — not a node

The member is the living subject from whose perspective the House has meaning.

The system may possess records, authored material, memories, references, relationships, calculations, and projections. It does **not** possess a complete object called the person.

> **No representation of the member is equivalent to the member.**

The member therefore does not appear as a graph entity that can be completed, scored, modeled exhaustively, or owned by the system.

Soullab may help the member see more of their life. It may never claim that what it sees is the whole person.

---

## 2. The House is the bridge

A useful architectural metaphor is a person on a long voyage.

A vessel may hold navigation, logs, maps, communications, environmental readings, memory, current conditions, what needs tending, where the traveler has been, and where they may be going.

The instruments exist so the traveler can remain oriented. They do not tell the traveler who they are.

That is the role of the House.

> **The House is the bridge from which one life can be oriented without exposing all machinery at once.**

This metaphor is architectural, not aesthetic.

⛔ Soullab must not become a science-fiction cockpit, command center, heads-up display, or dashboard of telemetry.

✅ The approved House may remain warm, architectural, literary, mysterious, elemental, and human while the deeper orientation system becomes increasingly coherent.

---

## 3. Rooms are facets, not silos

Every House place answers an enduring human question.

| Facet | Human question |
|---|---|
| Writing | What wants to take form through me? |
| Relationships | What is alive between me and others? |
| Practices | What helps me return to body, breath, and attention? |
| Community | Where am I participating in what we hold together? |
| Studio | What am I tending in my life and contribution? |
| Decisions | What am I choosing, and what perspective helps me choose? |
| Astrology | What patterns, timing, and symbolic ecology am I participating in? |
| Journal | What have I lived, and what wants words? |
| Reflections | What mattered enough to keep? |
| Ideas | What is beginning to take form? |
| Changes | What is changing, and how am I walking it? |
| Wisdom | What am I learning from teachings, sources, and living knowledge? |
| Library | What have I gathered that I may need again? |
| Divination | What question am I willing to meet through symbolic practice? |
| Living Field | What is the larger configuration of my life right now? |
| Co-lab | What are we making or holding together? |
| Daily Anchor | What do I want to remain connected to today? |

A room that cannot answer its human question clearly is not yet complete.

---

## 4. MAIA is the host and continuity intelligence

MAIA is not another facet competing with Journal, Astrology, Relationships, Writing, or the Field.

MAIA is the relational intelligence able to accompany the member across those facets while preserving:

- authorship;
- provenance;
- explicit scope;
- consent;
- room identity;
- uncertainty;
- the member's right to revise meaning.

> **MAIA may remember what the member has authorized her to carry. She does not absorb the rooms into herself.**

Sometimes MAIA is a destination for conversation.

Sometimes MAIA appears inside a room.

Sometimes MAIA receives only a bounded context packet.

Sometimes MAIA is deliberately absent.

The room decides the threshold.

---

## 5. The Living Field is relationship, not aggregation

The Living Field is the place where relationships among distinct parts of the member's life can become visible.

It is not a universal database and not a complete portrait.

The source objects remain where they belong.

A Journal entry remains a Journal entry.
A person remains a person.
A decision remains a decision.
A manuscript remains a manuscript.
An anchor remains an anchor.

The Field may show relation among them without making them the same thing.

> **Projection is not possession. Relationship is not merger.**

---

## 6. Crossings are as important as rooms

A coherent life cannot be served by beautiful isolated rooms.

The movement between facets is load-bearing.

Every crossing must answer five questions:

1. **What is crossing?** — the exact carrier: question, entry, reflection, source, reading, decision, etc.
2. **Who authorized it?** — explicit gesture, standing consent, read-only projection, or another governed authority.
3. **What remains behind?** — the source object keeps its original identity and custody.
4. **What context is carried?** — only what the receiving facet needs, with provenance.
5. **How does the member return?** — the crossing must not strand the person in another room or erase where they came from.

> **Connection is not permission.**

A technical ability for one subsystem to read another does not itself authorize a member-facing crossing.

---

## 7. Crossing authority

Four authority classes are recognized:

| Authority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `member_explicit` | the member performs a visible gesture that carries something across |
| `member_standing_consent` | the member has explicitly allowed bounded future contextual use |
| `read_only_projection` | the system may show an existing relationship without moving or reauthoring the source |
| `system_automatic` | the system propagates material without an immediate member gesture |

`system_automatic` crossings require explicit constitutional adjudication.

They are never considered acceptable merely because they already exist in code.

---

## 8. Crossing standing

Every crossing is classified as:

- **LIVE** — implemented and aligned with authority/provenance law;
- **PARTIAL** — some substrate exists, but the whole experiential contract is not yet closed;
- **NEEDS ADJUDICATION** — implementation exists but its authority or semantics are unresolved;
- **DESIGN ONLY** — desired architecture with no claim of runtime capability.

This prevents architectural diagrams from silently becoming capability claims.

---

## 9. Cross-cutting lenses are not rooms

Some ways of seeing belong across many facets rather than inside one destination.

### Elemental

> **What mode of participation is present here?**

Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and the Fifth may illuminate differentiated modes of participation. They must not be converted into fixed personality identity.

### Spiralogic

> **What movement, differentiation, integration, or return is visible here?**

Spiralogic may orient developmental movement. It must not become a rigid ladder that assigns a totalizing stage to a person.

### Developmental

> **What trajectory, threshold, or capacity is changing over time?**

Development remains provisional, contextual, and member-correctable. It is not rank.

### Relational

> **What exists between these people, objects, or facets that cannot be reduced to either one?**

Relationship remains a distinct reality rather than a property owned by either side.

### Temporal

> **What changes when this is understood in the actual day, season, cycle, or life phase?**

Time is lived context. Sacred and developmental processes retain temporal sovereignty.

### Symbolic

> **What possibilities become visible through symbol without becoming determinations?**

Astrology, dreams, divination, and other symbolic material may deepen recognition without claiming verdict authority.

## 10. The crossing laws

### Law 1 — Source identity survives the crossing

Carrying a Journal entry into conversation does not turn it into a MAIA statement.

Carrying a Tarot reading into Reflections does not turn it into a generic note.

Carrying a source into Wisdom dialogue does not erase its author or tradition.

### Law 2 — Context never becomes authorship by accident

A prompt, question, quote, or MAIA reflection may accompany a member into another facet as context.

It may not be inserted into the member's authored material as though they wrote it.

### Law 3 — The member can see the crossing

Important cross-facet acts are named in human language:

- `Write from here`
- `Reflect with MAIA`
- `Discuss with MAIA`
- `Save Reading`
- `MAIA may remember this with me`

Hidden propagation is not a substitute for an experiential gesture.

### Law 4 — Return is part of the crossing

A crossing is incomplete if it knows how to leave but not how to return.

### Law 5 — The receiving facet does not erase the source facet

MAIA does not become Journal.
Reflections does not become Divination.
Living Field does not become a universal editor.
The House does not become a dashboard.

## 11. Current examples

### Journal ↔ MAIA

`Reflect with MAIA` carries a kept entry into bounded reflection.

`Write from here` carries MAIA's question back as provenance context while leaving the writing field empty for the member.

### Reflections → MAIA

`Discuss with MAIA` carries the member-kept Reflection while the Reflection remains the member-owned object.

### Daily Anchor → MAIA

Anchors remain private by default. `MAIA may remember this with me` grants bounded standing consent for contextual return.

### Divination → MAIA

`Consult with MAIA` explicitly carries the member's question, chosen symbolic material, and reading into conversation.

### Divination → Reflections

`Save Reading` turns a chosen reading into a kept object only through explicit member action.

### Wisdom → MAIA

`Reflect with MAIA` carries the quote/source with attribution and a return doorway.

### Ideas ↔ MAIA

`Ask MAIA` remains in the Idea thread. Member draft text is saved as the member's block before MAIA responds, and MAIA's response remains a distinct block.

### Journal / Reflections → Changes

A kept Journal entry or Reflection may offer **Name this as a change** / **Name a change**.

The source crosses as authenticated provenance only. The member still authors the Change name, description, and type. The source remains where it was.

### Journal / Reflections → Decisions

A kept Journal entry or Reflection may offer **Consider a decision**.

The receiving Personal Decision shows where the source came from while leaving Decision Title, Context, stakes, state, and time pressure for the member. A durable crossing exists only when the member creates the Decision.

## 12. Crossing debt and first repair

The first crossing audit found that the quick-Journal API automatically created a Reflection capsule for every sufficiently long kept Journal entry.

That violated the newly explicit distinction:

> **Journal is what I wrote. Reflections are what I chose to keep as personal gems.**

The crossing has now been repaired:

- Journal save no longer auto-creates a Reflection capsule;
- a kept Journal entry offers **Keep as a reflection**;
- that gesture is ownership-scoped and idempotent;
- the exact kept entry crosses through the existing governed capsule Keep substrate;
- no LLM re-distillation occurs between the member gesture and the Reflection object;
- historical automatically-created capsules are not silently deleted or rewritten.

The second crossing audit found a separate legacy Journal → episodic-memory bridge. It silently created system-authored episodic rows, including invented title, significance, emotional intensity, and semantic vector metadata.

That bridge is now **retired**.

- Journal save no longer writes `episodic_memories`;
- member-marked episodic moments remain a separate explicit memory act;
- episodic recall remains independently governed by `members.episodic_recall_enabled`;
- the member-facing Memory & Consent surface exposes that recall choice;
- historical system-authored episodic rows are not silently deleted or rewritten.

The governing distinction is now explicit:

> **Writing something in Journal is not consent to convert it into MAIA memory.**

### First durable facet-relation substrate

The first non-MAIA member-explicit cross-facet relations are now live:

- Journal → Changes;
- Reflection → Changes;
- Journal → Personal Decisions;
- Reflection → Personal Decisions.

They are held by `member_facet_crossings`.

That table deliberately stores **relationship identity, not life content**: member id, crossing id, source facet/id, target facet/id, and time. It contains no copied prose, semantic vector, importance score, inferred meaning, or system-authored summary.

The receiving object and its crossing relation are created atomically only after the source resolves as member-owned and the crossing id is admitted by the explicit runtime allowlist.

The receiving room shows the source as provenance while leaving target meaning blank for the member to author.

The target later shows **Where this began** and a **Return to source** doorway.

> **Durable relationship does not require semantic merger.**

This is not a universal graph and grants no automatic Living Field projection authority.

## 13. Product design test

For every room:

> **Does entering this facet give me a deeper, more coherent relationship with this part of my life?**

For every crossing:

> **Can I tell what is moving, why it is moving, and whether I authorized it?**

For the whole House:

> **Can I feel one life being held together without feeling that the system has turned me into a model?**

---

## 14. Failure modes

### Feature silo

A beautiful room that cannot carry meaningful continuity into the rest of the life system.

### Universal graph

Everything becomes a node in one totalizing model and source identity disappears.

### Silent propagation

Material crosses facets because the backend can, not because the member chose it.

### MAIA takeover

Every room becomes chat-first and room-specific human activity disappears.

### Dashboard relapse

The House exposes counts, statuses, metrics, and system categories instead of orientation.

### Metaphor without function

The environment becomes mysterious but the member cannot tell where they are or what they can do.

## 15. Governing integration

The architecture can be summarized as:

```text
                     PERSON
              irreducible living center
                       │
                       ▼
                     HOUSE
              orientation / thresholds
                       │
         ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
         ▼             ▼             ▼
       FACET         FACET         FACET
     Journal       Relations      Writing       …
         │             │             │
         └──── governed crossings ────┘
                       │
                       ▼
                 LIVING FIELD
          relationships / projection
                       │
                       ▼
                     MAIA
       relational continuity across facets
```

MAIA is drawn below only to show accompaniment across the architecture. She is not ontologically beneath the Field and not the final destination.

The person remains the living center.
