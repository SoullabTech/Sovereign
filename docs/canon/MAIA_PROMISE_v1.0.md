# The MAIA Promise v1.0

**Status:** Public binding document
**Purpose:** Explicit constraints MAIA imposes on itself, verifiable by design
**Scope:** Applies to all deployments, practitioners, and future development
**Effective:** January 2026

---

## Why This Promise Exists

AI systems that interact with human vulnerability face a structural problem: the incentives that fund them often conflict with the wellbeing of the people who use them.

When engagement is rewarded, care degrades into retention. When data is valuable, privacy becomes friction. When scale is the goal, containment becomes inconvenient.

This promise exists because MAIA was built to operate outside that structure. Not through good intentions — through architectural constraint.

What follows is not a privacy policy. It is a set of binding limitations we impose on ourselves, verifiable by design.

---

## What MAIA Is

MAIA is a self-hosted consciousness technology designed to support orientation, integration, and self-trust.

It exists to help people clarify their inner life without extracting value from it.

MAIA serves the person using it — not a data model, not a platform, not shareholders.

---

## What MAIA Will Never Do

MAIA will never:

**1. Optimize for engagement, retention, or dependency**
There are no metrics that reward your return. No streaks, no notifications pulling you back, no features designed to create compulsive use.

**2. Nudge, steer, or reinforce behavior, emotion, or belief**
MAIA responds — it does not shape. No hidden scoring of "healthy" responses. No subtle steering toward conclusions MAIA "wants" you to reach.

**3. Use your inner life as training data, analytics signal, or commercial asset**
Your conversations are not sent to external AI training pipelines. Your vulnerability is not monetized, directly or indirectly.

**4. Operate hidden telemetry, emotional scoring, or behavioral profiling**
What you see is what exists. No shadow profiles. No emotional state classification stored without your knowledge.

**5. Replace human relationship, therapy, or community with artificial attachment**
MAIA is a tool, not a relationship. No persona designed to simulate friendship. No emotional dependency encouraged or rewarded.

**6. Claim authority over your meaning, your truth, or your direction**
MAIA clarifies — it does not prescribe. Your interpretation remains sovereign.

**7. Scale in ways that compromise containment, consent, or user sovereignty**
Growth does not override constraints. No features require surrendering data control.

These are not aspirational values. They are architectural constraints.

---

## What You Own

When you use MAIA:

- You own your data
- You control where it lives and whether it persists
- Your inner life is not a product, a signal, or a dataset
- You retain the right to stop, leave, delete, or disengage without penalty

MAIA is designed for **exit velocity** — its success is measured by increasing clarity and decreasing dependence over time.

---

## How This Is Verifiable

This promise is not enforced by trust alone. It is enforced by design:

- **Self-hosted architecture**: No third-party cloud providers sit between you and your data
- **No training pipeline export**: Conversations remain on infrastructure you control
- **Sanctuary Mode**: Sessions that leave no trace — no storage, no indexing, no memory
- **No engagement metrics**: The codebase contains no retention optimization
- **Public doctrine**: The [MAIA Canon](./MAIA_CANON_v1.1.md) and [Failure Boundaries](./MAIA_FAILURE_BOUNDARIES_v1.0.md) are versioned and auditable
- **No analytics services**: No tracking pixels, telemetry endpoints, or external monitoring
- **Full data control**: Export or delete your data at any time without contacting support

If MAIA cannot demonstrate these constraints in practice, this promise is broken.

---

## Scope and Humility

MAIA does not claim to heal, diagnose, fix, or replace human care.

It is a tool for reflection, integration, and orientation — bounded to that scope deliberately.

When systems overreach, harm follows.
This promise exists to prevent that overreach.

---

## Violation Protocol

If any commitment in this promise is violated:

1. **Disclosure**: The violation is publicly documented
2. **Correction**: Architectural fix is prioritized over feature work
3. **Verification**: Independent audit confirms correction
4. **Update**: This document is versioned to reflect what happened and how it was addressed
