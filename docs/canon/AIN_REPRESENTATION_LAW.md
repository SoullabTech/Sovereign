## 1. The law

> **Representation must never silently acquire the authority of the thing represented.**

Three words carry the weight. **Representation** — any stand-in the system computes, stores or displays for
something it does not itself constitute: a label, an embedding, a score, a summary, a portrait, a declaration,
a match. **Silently** — without a governed act that granted the authority and without an instrument that would
notice if it changed. **Authority** — the power to decide what exists, what is eligible, what is true, what
the person meant, or what the system may do.

⭐ **The law does not forbid representation from having authority.** Representation is how a system finds
anything at all. It forbids representation acquiring authority *by default, by adjacency, or by convenience* —
which is the only way it ever actually happens.

## 2. Why this is not a rename of Interface Humility

`INTERFACE_HUMILITY.md` rules that *"perception, language, symbols, body-state, astrology, HRV, dreams,
emotional tone, and elemental or spiral signals are interface data — useful for orientation, not direct access
to the truth of a person."*

That is **this law applied to one represented object: a person.** The proposed law is the genus. Its other
species govern objects Interface Humility was never scoped to — a governed source, a capability, an authored
Work, a member's speech act, a memory's standing.

⚠️ **R4's rule applies to this law's own minting: one canonical term, one canonical referent.** If this is
ratified, Interface Humility is **not superseded and not renamed** — it is cited as the person-facing species
and keeps its own authority over person-signals, where it is more specific and therefore stronger.

⛔ If the founder judges that Interface Humility should simply be *widened* instead, that is the cheaper and
equally honest route, and this document should be withdrawn rather than kept alongside it.

## 4. The decision procedure

A law that only produces distinctions is not yet mature (`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`). This one must
produce decisions. For any representation, answer three questions **in order**:

### Q1 — The Substitution Test
> **If this representation were wrong, would the system still be able to tell?**

If no, the representation has authority. Proceed. If yes, it is descriptive; stop.

*J8: if the regex mislabels EA `alchemy`, EA silently vanishes from every mode and nothing reports it. No —
so the label has authority.*

### Q2 — The Grant Test
> **Name the governed act that granted it that authority.**

A commit is not a grant. A default is not a grant. "It was the convenient adjacent column" is not a grant.
If no act can be named, the authority was **acquired, not conferred** — the law is violated.

### Q3 — The Attestation Test
> **If it changed tomorrow, what instrument goes red?**

If nothing, the acquisition is also **silent**, which is the aggravating condition. Either bind it to an
attestation or reclassify it as non-authoritative.

### The four classes (from J8-R3 §VII, generalized)

**AUTHORITATIVE** — human/governed fact, explicitly permitted to decide. ·
**DERIVED-GOVERNED** — machine-derived but versioned, attested, explicitly permitted. ·
**DESCRIPTIVE** — may explain or display; may never decide. ·
**EXPERIMENTAL** — may be evaluated; may never affect canonical behavior.

> **Default class is DESCRIPTIVE.** Promotion requires a governed act. Nothing arrives AUTHORITATIVE by being
> useful, by being already deployed, or by being the only field available.

## 5. What would show this law is wrong

⚠️ A law that explains everything decides nothing. Recorded so the law can fail:

- **Over-breadth.** *Everything* in a computational system is a representation. If the law is read to mean
  "no computed value may ever decide anything," it forbids computing. **It is defeated by that reading**, and
  Q1–Q3 exist to prevent it: the law binds only where a wrong representation is undetectable and its authority
  unconferred.
- **The genuine counter-case.** A representation may legitimately be the best available authority — a
  checksum *is* content identity; a member's own label *is* their meaning. ⭐ **Both survive Q2: an act
  conferred it.** If a case is found where authority is clearly correct and clearly unconferrable, the law is
  too strong and must be narrowed.
- **Cost.** If Q1–Q3 applied across a real lane produces mostly DESCRIPTIVE reclassifications that no one
  acts on, the law is producing distinctions rather than decisions and should be withdrawn.

## 6. Direction of authority

This law is the representational form of `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`. That canon holds that
authority moves upward through authored experience and may never be manufactured at a higher layer. A
representation acquiring authority **is** manufacture: a label standing in for admission, a score for
existence, a match for a member's act, a portrait for a person.

> Representation may serve the thing. It may not become the thing's authority over itself.
