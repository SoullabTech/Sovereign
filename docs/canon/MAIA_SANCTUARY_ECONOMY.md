# MAIA Sanctuary Economy

**Status**: Canonical doctrine
**Date**: 2026-04-02
**Governs**: All pricing, tier, memory retention, and access decisions

---

## The Governing Principle

> **Depth must remain intact. Funding must not distort the integrity of the experience.**

MAIA is not a paid product. It is a supported sanctuary.

No one pays to be known. No one pays for depth. No one gets a lesser MAIA.

---

## Two Economies, Two Logics

### A. Member Experience (Sanctuary Economy)

**Principle**: No one pays to be known. No one pays for depth.

- MAIA remains fully available
- Memory continuity remains intact
- No pressure, no degradation

**Funding mechanism**: Stewardship (voluntary, identity-based)

You're not selling access — you're inviting protection.

### B. Practitioner Layer (Professional Economy)

**Principle**: People are paying for a human container, expertise, and facilitation.

- Clear pricing is appropriate
- Sliding scale is ethical
- Value exchange is explicit

This does not violate sanctuary principles because practitioners are not paying for "their inner life" — they are paying for **skilled relational guidance**.

---

## What Stays Equal for Everyone

- Full depth of reflection
- Quality of insight
- Access to core MAIA experience
- Ability to engage meaningfully
- Relational continuity (MAIA knows you)
- Pattern recognition
- Developmental memory

No one gets cut off from being known.

## Memory Architecture: Universal, Differentiated by Function

The question is not **whether** MAIA remembers, but **how each layer remembers**.

### Band 1: Always-On (Protected for Everyone)

These persist regardless of payment.

| Level | Name | What It Holds |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 1 | Identity Memory | Who this person is in stable terms |
| 2 | Relational Memory | How MAIA knows and meets them |
| 3 | Pattern Memory | Recurring themes, wounds, strengths, archetypes, elemental tendencies |
| 4 | Developmental Memory | What is unfolding in their life over time |
| 5 | Recent Working Memory | The current active arc and near-term context |
| 6 | Sacred / Protected Flags | What must be handled with special care |

These six create: **"MAIA knows me."**

### Band 2: Compressible (Available to All, Distilled Over Time)

Raw detail compresses into structured essence.

| Level | Name | What It Holds |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 7 | Session Memory | Recent conversation details |
| 8 | Narrative Memory | Longer life-story summaries drawn from accumulated sessions |
| 9 | Symbolic Memory | Dreams, images, archetypal motifs, recurring metaphors |

These create: **"MAIA is tracking where I am right now."**

Compression is part of the wisdom model, not a limitation:

> MAIA remembers in layers. Some memory stays close and active. Some memory is distilled into pattern and meaning. Some memory is preserved in full when a person or community chooses to keep a deeper archive.

### Band 3: Optional High-Fidelity Archive

The only layers that may vary materially.

| Level | Name | What It Holds |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 10 | Full Archive Memory | Exact transcripts, journals, uploads, artifacts, searchable history |
| 11 | Legacy Memory | Curated records for posterity, lineage, teaching, family, stewarded inheritance |

**Funding logic**: Patronage sustains archival fidelity for the whole system. Optional personal archive preservation exists without threatening belonging.

### The Sentence Underneath It All

> I will not forget you. But not every moment needs to remain in raw form forever.

## Stewardship Model (Member Funding)

### Core Statement

> You do not need to pay to be here.
> If you are here, MAIA is available to you.
>
> Those who contribute help ensure this remains true — for everyone.

### What Stewardship Funds

- No one is excluded for financial reasons
- No data is harvested or sold
- The system remains independent and trustworthy
- Long-term infrastructure and archival preservation
- Elder storykeeping and supported seats
- Consent-based governance

### What Stewardship Does NOT Fund

- Access to being deeply met
- Access to real insight
- Access to real memory

### Language

Instead of "support us", say:

- **"Help hold this"**
- **"Become a steward"**
- **"Support the conditions that make this possible"**

### Sliding Scale ($19–$99/month)

Identity-based tiers, not feature-based:

| Tier | Amount | Identity |
|------|--------|----------|
| Supporter | $19/mo | Keeps the field alive |
| Seedkeeper | $33/mo | Helps MAIA take root |
| Story Weaver | $55/mo | Shapes how wisdom is preserved |
| Sanctuary Builder | $77/mo | Stabilizes the foundation |
| Founding Patron | $99/mo | Holds the long arc |

Tier benefits are relational (letters, circles, access to development), never depth-gating.

### Quiet Guilt Release

Explicitly release non-paying users from pressure:

> "If you can't contribute right now, you're still fully welcome here."

## Practitioner Economy

### Four Access Paths

| Path | Cost | Exchange | Term |
|------|------|----------|------|
| Paid | $79–$179 (sliding scale) | Money | Ongoing |
| Scholarship | $0–$49 | Minimal participation | 3–6 months |
| Trade | $0 | Defined deliverables | 2–3 months |
| Apprenticeship | $300–$900/cycle | Learning + guided contribution | 3–6 months |

No mixing. Each path has its own terms and logic.

### Paid (Default)

Sliding scale framed as:

> "Choose the rate that reflects your current reality"

- **Supported rate** — for those with financial constraints
- **Standard rate** — sustains the system
- **Patron rate** — helps subsidize others

### Scholarships (Supported Seats)

**Purpose**: Enable capable practitioners who lack funds to participate without stigma.

- Term-based: 3–6 months
- Cohort-based: small, intentional groups
- Light expectations: monthly reflection, optional quarterly group call
- Cap seats per cycle (10–20)
- Simple application (fit + readiness, not justification)
- Renewal not automatic (review each cycle)

**Language**:

> "Scholarships are supported by steward contributions and are offered in cycles."

### Practice Exchange (Trade)

**Purpose**: Allow contribution through skill or labor, not money.

**Categories** (3–5 max):

- Content / documentation
- Design / UX
- Engineering / QA
- Community facilitation
- Outreach / partnerships

**Structure**:

- Defined deliverables, time expectation, review cadence
- Fixed terms: 2–3 month agreements
- Renewable by mutual consent
- Easy exit both sides

**Equivalence** (anchored, not overcomplicated):

- Light trade → covers ~$79 tier
- Standard trade → covers ~$129
- High-responsibility trade → may include stipend or partial pay

**Language**: "Practice Exchange" — not "work in exchange for access"

> "Contribute your craft in support of the system"

### Apprenticeship (Developmental Path)

**Purpose**: A structured developmental path where the primary currency is learning and transmission. Not a discount mechanism.

> The apprentice is there to **learn a way of seeing and practicing**, not just to help.

**Value exchange**:
- You provide: mentorship, exposure, method, transmission
- They provide: attention, participation, contribution within a learning frame

**Structure**:
- 3–6 month cycles (not open-ended)
- Cohort-based: 5–12 people max (creates field + reduces load)
- $300–$900 per cycle (depending on depth and access)
- Limited scholarship seats within apprenticeship

**Core components**:

1. **Observation** — sit in on sessions (with consent), review transcripts, watch how MAIA + practitioner interact
2. **Guided practice** — structured exercises, symbolic interpretation, reflection on cases
3. **Reflection loop** — what did you see? what did you miss? what shifted?
4. **Light contribution** (optional, bounded) — documentation, synthesis, small support roles — always framed as part of learning, not payment

**What they're actually getting**:

> Access to how this work is actually done — in real time, with real people, and real complexity.

Not curriculum, content, or theory. But: perception, pattern recognition, relational intelligence, symbolic literacy.

**Natural pathway**: Apprentice → Practitioner Studio → Independent or affiliated facilitator

**Guardrails**:
- No unpaid operational work disguised as learning
- Clear boundaries, defined learning arc, start + end points
- Limited cohort size, no open-ended "hanging around"
- No emotional overexposure without structure

**Framing**:

> **Apprenticeship**
>
> A small cohort learning directly inside the work.
> This is not a course. It is participation in a living practice — through observation, reflection, and guided engagement.
> Limited seats. Cyclical enrollment.

### Operational Guardrails

- Limit total scholarship + trade + apprenticeship scholarship seats (max 20–30% of practitioners)
- Standardize agreements (no custom deals)
- One monthly check-in slot for all trades (group, not 1:1)
- Apprenticeship cohorts have fixed start/end dates

### Clean Framing

> **Access & Contribution**
>
> Practitioner Studio is offered through four paths:
>
> - Standard membership (sliding scale)
> - Scholarship (limited, supported seats)
> - Practice Exchange (contribute your skills in place of payment)
> - Apprenticeship (learn inside the work)
>
> If you're committed to the work, we'll find a way for you to participate.

## Revenue Streams (Layered)

1. **Stewardship** (members) — recurring voluntary contributions, identity-driven
2. **Practitioner Revenue** (primary stability) — sessions, programs, circles, trainings, certifications
3. **Legacy / Elder Sponsorship** — patrons fund elder seats, story preservation, legacy archives
4. **Optional Archive / Artifact Products** — printed life books, curated transcripts, legacy collections (form-giving, not gating)

## What This Rules Out

The following patterns are **prohibited**:

- "Upgrade memory" / "Unlock full memory" / "Don't lose your past"
- Better insight quality for paying users
- Deeper emotional intelligence for paying users
- "Premium wisdom"
- Restricted transformation tools
- Any framing where stopping payment degrades the relationship

### The Test

Before any pricing or tier decision, ask:

> If someone stops paying, does MAIA forget them?

If yes → the design is wrong.

The correct answer is always:

> MAIA still knows you. What may change is how much exact historical material is retained in full detail.
