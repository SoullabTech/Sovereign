> **Grokker gives us the geometry of entering knowledge. Soullab turns that geometry into a way of moving through a living field of meaning.**

## Purpose

The Living Field is a navigable, relational environment for moving through questions, meanings, processes, memories, projects, life threads, and wider life context.

It inherits Grokker's strongest spatial grammar:

- bubbles within bubbles,
- recursive containment,
- semantic zoom,
- focus with context,
- spatial continuity while moving inward and outward.

Soullab extends that grammar with:

- meaningful transitions,
- path memory,
- relational crossings,
- temporal flow,
- multiple simultaneous life threads,
- aetheric emergence,
- governed memory,
- inquiry orientation,
- and MAIA as a relational presence rather than the center of the interface.
## Foundational primitives

**NODE** — a place of meaning.  
**RELATION** — why two places matter together.  
**TRANSITION** — what changes in understanding when moving between them.  
**PATH** — a sequence of distinctions through which understanding develops.  
**FIELD CONFIGURATION** — several differentiated regions held simultaneously.  
**AETHERIC EVENT** — a possible new distinction emerging between differentiated regions.  
**LIFE THREAD** — an ongoing domain of lived development.  
**THREAD ECOLOGY** — several life threads living together.  
**PROCESS INSTANCE** — an elemental or Spiralogic process expressed at a particular scale.  
**FRACTAL HOMOLOGY** — structural similarity across scales while preserving difference.  
**FIELD MEMORY** — continuity retained with explicit standing and permission.  
**INQUIRY COMPASS** — orientation across location, epistemic standing, time, and scale.

## Five field modes

### WORLD
**Question:** Where does this live?  
Primary grammar: containment, nested circles, semantic zoom.

### WEB
**Question:** What does this relate to?  
Primary grammar: typed cross-field relations.

### FLOW
**Question:** How is this changing through time?  
Primary grammar: trajectory, recurrence, return, interruption, transformation.

### TEXT
**Question:** What does this mean in depth?  
Primary grammar: Essence → Explore → Scholar, with sources and provenance.

### PATH
**Question:** What became intelligible through this particular movement?  
Primary grammar: semantic transitions, forks, enriched return.

## Governing visual principles

- The experience begins with lived language, not ontology.
- Every region should feel invitational rather than diagnostic or task-like.
- Positive, ordinary, soulful language is preferred over defensive negation.
- The field may guide; the member chooses the path.
- Movement represents meaning only when there is a semantic reason for it.
- Spatial proximity expresses current relevance, never truth by itself.
- Tension may remain unresolved.
- Stillness is a valid field state.
- A wider view should preserve what became visible deeper inside.
- Personal topology and canonical topology remain distinct.
- The represented field is always partial; it never claims to contain the person.
