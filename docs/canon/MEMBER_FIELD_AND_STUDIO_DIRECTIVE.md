# The Member Field and the Studios — Constitutional Directive

**Authored by:** Kelly, 2026-08-02. **Recorded by:** Claude Code, verbatim in intent.
**Status:** **Recorded — awaiting ratification.** Ratification is Kelly's act, not Claude's.
**Scope:** how every implementation touching Field Objects, Studios, Canvas, search, or contextual
offering is to be thought about. This is a **constitutional** directive, not a feature spec: no
implementation is authorized by it, and every implementation must grow from it.

---

## Constitutional principle

**The Writer's Studio does not own the member's ideas. The member's Field owns them.**

Keeps, Ideas, Decisions, Changes, Journal entries, Conversations, Quotes, Research notes, and future
insight objects are all **Field Objects**. They are persistent objects **in the member's life**, not
writing material. **Writing is one possible expression of them.**

## Field Objects

Treat every Field Object as **persistent · searchable · referenceable · reusable · capable of
participating in many projects simultaneously.**

They are **never consumed** by a manuscript. They never become *"used up."* A manuscript **references**
them.

## Writer's Studio

Writer's Studio exists to help a member **think into writing**. It does **not** become a repository of
insights. It should continuously allow the writer to return to the wider Field.

**It is one environment that works with Field Objects. Not their owner.**

## During writing

Assume a writer continually returns to previous thinking — journal entries, notebooks, Keeps, Ideas,
Decisions, Changes, previous conversations, research, quotations, previous manuscripts, source
documents.

**These are not inserted. They are consulted.** Their purpose is to spur memory · deepen reflection ·
expand exploration · reconnect the writer with lived experience · reveal forgotten observations ·
surface previous thinking.

**Writing continues after reflection.**

## Search

Every Field Object must be searchable. **Search is member-directed.**

> *Show journal entries about forgiveness.* · *Find Decisions connected with leadership.* · *Show Keeps
> mentioning embodiment.* · *Find every notebook mentioning fire.*

Search **spans the entire Field while preserving each object's identity.**

## Contextual offering

MAIA may offer Field Objects when context suggests they may help.

**Offering is never insertion. Offering is never clustering. Offering is never interpretation.**

| ✅ Correct | ⛔ Not this |
| --- | --- |
| *"You wrote about something similar last year. Would you like to see it?"* | *"This belongs in this chapter."* |
| | *"These three form a theme."* |

**Recognition belongs to the member.**

## Canvas

The Canvas contains **references** to Field Objects. **Never copies. Never ownership.**

Removing something from the Canvas **never deletes or alters** the Field Object. The same Field Object
may appear in multiple projects, multiple piles, and multiple Studios. **There remains only one
underlying object.**

## Authority

**Only the member may** decide relevance · create meaning · name patterns · declare themes · organize
understanding · decide something belongs in the manuscript.

**MAIA may retrieve. MAIA may offer. MAIA may remember.**
**MAIA does not author the member's understanding.**

## Retrieval goal

The purpose of retrieval is **not information recall**. It is helping the member **re-enter dialogue
with their own life.**

> A journal entry may never become manuscript text. Yet it may transform the chapter. **That is
> success.**

## Design test

> **Does this help the member think more deeply from their own lived Field, or does it merely make
> information easier to retrieve?**
>
> Choose the first.

## The governing invariant

> **The Field is the member's living memory. Every Studio is simply a different way of entering into
> relationship with that Field. Writer's Studio is not a database of writing materials; it is an
> environment where writing grows through dialogue with the member's own Field.**

# Two classes of note — never to be collapsed

## Layer 1 — Field Objects (life-level)

Belong to the member's **life**, not to any one work: Keeps · Ideas · Decisions · Changes · Journal
entries · Conversations · Quotes · Research observations · Dreams · Voice memos · Images.

They can support a book, a course, a keynote, coaching, another future book, or personal reflection.
**They remain independent.**

## Layer 2 — Development Notes (project-level)

Belong to **this manuscript**. Not insights about life — **insights about the work itself**:

> *"Chapter 4 is really about transformation, not suffering."* · *"Need a better transition between
> these two stories."* · *"Move this section after the alchemy chapter."* · *"This argument still feels
> weak."* · *"Research Jung's comments on imagination."* · *"Need a story from Karen here."* · *"This
> chapter wants another voice."* · *"The ending doesn't land yet."*

These live **inside the project. Not in the Field.**

## The ontological split

| | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Field Objects** | **What have I lived?** |
| **Development Notes** | **What is this work becoming?** |

Completely different questions.

---

# The third thing — developmental questions

Sometimes the project itself needs help **thinking**. Not writing. Thinking.

> *"I'm trying to weave together these three themes but I can't yet see the bridge."*
> *"This chapter feels disconnected from the rest of the book."*
> *"I've got six stories that all matter. Which ones are actually carrying the argument?"*

Those are not notes. They are **developmental questions**. **MAIA can participate there much more
actively, because the object is not the member's life — it is the evolving work.**

## Three memory spaces

```
MEMBER FIELD                 Keeps · Ideas · Decisions · Changes · Journals ·
                             Conversations · Research · Quotes · …
        ↓
PROJECT                      Book · Course · Talk · Program
        ↓
PROJECT DEVELOPMENT          Development Notes · Questions · Editorial observations ·
                             Structure notes · Open problems · Research tasks ·
                             Revision intentions
```

**The project continually reaches upward into the Field. The Development Notes continually reach
inward into the manuscript.**

## Where MAIA becomes a developmental partner

Not by writing. By **helping hold the developmental state of the work**:

> *"Three weeks ago you were trying to reconcile the Fire and Water chapters. You've now written both.
> Would it help to revisit that earlier question?"*
>
> *"You've made five notes over the last month about the opening chapter feeling too conceptual. Would
> you like to review them together before revising?"*
>
> *"This research note may answer the structural question you left yourself yesterday."*

MAIA is **not** saying *"here's how to fix your chapter."* It is saying **"here's what your own
developmental process has been wrestling with."**

## The constitutional distinction

> **Field Objects preserve the member's lived consciousness. Development Notes preserve the
> consciousness of the work itself.**

Both persistent. Both searchable. Both contextually retrievable. But **different authority, different
lifetimes, different relationships.**

**The Field survives every project.** A project's Development Notes belong to that project's
evolution — they help the work remember what it has been trying to become, while the Field helps the
author remember what they have lived.

# The re-centering (Kelly, 2026-08-02)

**This is not a rewrite. It is a re-centering.** The work so far has been building toward a Writer's
Studio. The true center is the **Member Field**; the Writer's Studio is **one environment that
participates in it.**

## Keep exactly as is — infrastructure remains correct

WriterField · concurrency · revisions · autosave · identity routing · projects · Canvas verbs ·
placement by reference · pointer-not-copy · Workbench persistence.

**None of this changes.** What changes is the **conceptual center** being built toward.

## The root changes

From `Writer's Studio → { Canvas, WriterField, Keeps, Ideas, … }` to:

```
Member Field  { Keeps · Ideas · Decisions · Changes · Journals · Conversations · Research · … }
      │
      ├──────────────┬──────────────┐
Writer's Studio  Coach Studio   …other Studios
```

**Not a rebuild. Changing the root.**

## The third bucket

Two mental buckets today — Field objects, Manuscript. A third is required:

```
Member Field  →  Project  →  Development Memory
```

Where **the work itself thinks**: *"Need another story here."* · *"The transition doesn't work."* ·
*"Research Jung."* · *"Opening chapter still feels abstract."* · *"Try reversing chapters 3 and 4."*

Not Keeps. Not Journal entries. Not Decisions. **They belong to the evolving project.**

## Contextual retrieval draws from three distinct places

Writing Chapter 5, MAIA may draw on:

| Source | Contents | What it expands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The wider Field** — your life | journals · Keeps · Decisions · Ideas · Conversations · Changes | **the writer** |
| **The project's developmental memory** — the work's own thinking | unresolved questions · editorial notes · research reminders · structural concerns · revision intentions | **the manuscript** |
| **The manuscript itself** | the current text | **the immediate writing** |

Much richer than *"retrieve notes."*

## The phrase to retire

⛔ **Stop saying "writing materials."** These are **living field objects**. Some never become writing.
Some become three books. Some become coaching. Some become life decisions. **The manuscript merely
visits them.**

✅ Checked 2026-08-02: the phrase appears **nowhere** in code or docs, and the member-facing subtitle
is already field-shaped — *"Things you kept. Move them into whatever order makes sense to you."*
Nothing to remove; the discipline is to keep it out.

## The constitutional update, and when it applies

> **The Member Field is the enduring substrate of the platform. Studios do not own insights; they
> enter into relationship with them. Writer's Studio specializes one form of relationship — writing —
> but does not redefine the ontology of the underlying objects. Distinguish three domains: (1) the
> Member Field, containing persistent field objects; (2) the Project, which references those objects;
> and (3) the Project Development Memory, which belongs only to the evolution of that work. Build all
> future phases from this model.**

⏸️ **Applies before Phase 3 expands projects and Canvas** — not after. The existing infrastructure
appears compatible; the conceptual model is corrected **now** so later work grows from the right
foundation rather than being reinterpreted afterward.

# Notes for ratification

Recorded by Claude Code alongside the directive, and **not part of Kelly's text.**

## Where this directive expands MAIA's latitude ⚠️ the one thing to rule on deliberately

Every clause above **constrains** MAIA except one. *"MAIA can participate there much more actively"*
in project development is the single **expansion** in the document — the first place MAIA is
authorized to act on an object rather than only offer and withdraw.

It is well-grounded (the object is the work, not the person), but three things follow that the text
does not settle:

1. **Where the boundary sits inside the work.** *"Which stories carry the argument?"* is a question
   about the manuscript — but answering it shapes what the member comes to believe their book is
   about. The Field/Work distinction does not by itself keep MAIA out of authoring meaning.
2. **Development Notes are member-authored today, but the developmental-partner examples require MAIA
   to notice patterns across them** (*"you've made five notes… about the opening chapter"*). Counting
   and surfacing is retrieval; characterising them as *about the opening chapter feeling too
   conceptual* is a light interpretation. That line needs drawing explicitly.
3. **A Development Note may migrate.** *"Chapter 4 is really about transformation"* can turn out to be
   a life-level realization. Whether migration Layer 2 → Layer 1 exists, and who authorizes it, is
   unaddressed.

## Consistency with existing canon

- **Direction of Authority** (`CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`) — consistent and reinforced:
  recognition stays the member's, and the system protects boundaries rather than moving anyone through
  them. The three memory spaces are a **medium** distinction, not new rungs.
- **Sovereignty Invariant 14 (cultural sovereignty)** — the directive's insistence on preserving each
  object's identity and origin is the same discipline applied to the member's own vocabulary.
- ⚠️ **Terminology collision 1 — "Field":** here it means the member's field of insight objects.
  `Living Field` is an existing substrate (`lib/maia/living-field/*`, and `indexAtomAffinities`
  already runs fire-and-forget on every Keep). **Different senses of one word; disambiguate before
  either borrows the other's machinery.**
- ⚠️⚠️ **Terminology collision 2 — "Development Memory": the name is already taken, by the opposite
  layer.** `developmental_memories` exists and is **member-scoped** — `user_id`, `spiral_cycle`,
  `facet_code`, `significance`, `vector_embedding`, read by `app/api/memory/patterns/*`. It holds the
  **person's** development. Kelly's Layer 3 holds **the work's** development and has no member axis at
  all. Naming the project-level layer *Development Memory* would collide these two directly — which is
  precisely the collapse this directive exists to forbid. **Name Layer 3 something else, or rename the
  existing table; do not let them share a word.**
