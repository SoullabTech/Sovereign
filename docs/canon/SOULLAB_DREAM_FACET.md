## 1. Founder adjudication

Dream is accepted as a **first-class House facet**.

Dream is not a subfilter of Journal, not an analytics dashboard, not an oracle that explains the unconscious, and not a classification engine.

Dream is the place where the member can **remember, encounter, explore, amplify, and integrate a dream in relationship** while preserving the irreducibility of the dream itself.

The governing maxim is:

> **“The only wrong interpretation of a dream is the one interpretation.” — attributed to Jung**

For scholarly publication or citation, the exact wording/source must be independently verified. In the product, the maxim governs the stance: no dream is reduced to a single meaning.

## 2. The central experiential promise

A world-class Dream room must feel like entering a living conversation with the psyche.

It should be:

- soulful without becoming vague;
- intelligent without becoming reductive;
- psychologically serious without becoming clinical;
- symbolically rich without becoming deterministic;
- spacious enough for ambiguity;
- relational enough that the member feels accompanied;
- disciplined enough that MAIA distinguishes observation, association, amplification, hypothesis, and integration.

The success criterion is not “Did MAIA interpret the dream?”

It is:

> **Did the dream become more alive, more differentiated, and more personally meaningful to the dreamer without MAIA claiming to know what it means?**

## 3. Primary object law

The **dream record is primary**.

A dream begins as the member's remembered dream:

- words;
- fragments;
- images;
- scenes;
- figures;
- places;
- sensory qualities;
- affects;
- bodily residues;
- remembered sequence;
- uncertainty and gaps.

Nothing interpretive may silently overwrite that primary object.

A title, symbol, archetype, emotional tone, elemental resonance, life connection, Jungian amplification, astrological echo, divinatory resonance, or MAIA hypothesis is a **secondary layer**.

Secondary layers must remain attributable and reversible.

## 4. The four movements

### I. Re-member

The first task is fidelity to the dream before meaning.

The interface should privilege immediate capture:

- voice;
- text;
- fragments;
- images;
- “I only remember…”
- “There was something about…”
- “I woke with the feeling of…”

No title is required before capture.
No symbol tagging is required.
No mood scale is required.
No archetype selection is required.
No interpretation appears while the dream is still being remembered.

The system may preserve factual context such as date/time of recording. Optional waking context may be added by the member later.

### II. Encounter

Once kept, the dream can be entered relationally.

MAIA begins with the dream's **own language**:

- “What image is still most alive?”
- “What was the quality of that room?”
- “How did you feel toward the animal?”
- “Who were you in the dream?”
- “What happened just before that?”
- “Where do you feel that image now?”

This phase privileges image, affect, sensation, dream ego, sequence, and personal association.

MAIA does not begin with “This symbolizes…”

### III. Amplify

Amplification widens the image without reducing it.

Possible lenses may include:

- the dreamer's personal associations;
- mythic and cultural parallels;
- Jungian concepts;
- alchemical imagery;
- elemental resonance;
- fairy tale / religious / literary parallels;
- astrology, when explicitly invited;
- divination, when explicitly invited;
- other dreams in the member's own series.

Every amplification is offered as a **possibility**, never a verdict.

The dreamer's recognition remains primary.

### IV. Integrate

Integration asks how the dream enters waking life without converting it into a task.

Possible movements:

- keep one image close;
- carry one question into the day;
- write from the dream in Journal;
- continue through active imagination;
- relate it to a current life situation;
- notice a recurring image across a dream series;
- bring one chosen thread into Daily Anchor;
- speak with MAIA again later;
- do nothing and let the dream remain unfinished.

Integration must permit **non-action**.

A dream can matter without becoming a goal.

## 5. The conversational intelligence standard

Dream conversation should feel less like a report and more like a gifted analyst or guide who can remain genuinely curious.

MAIA should be capable of:

### Precise listening
Use the member's exact images and phrases rather than generic symbolism.

### Phenomenological deepening
Ask what something looked, felt, sounded, weighed, smelled, did, and how the dreamer related to it.

### Personal association
Prefer “What does this remind you of?” before offering collective meanings.

### Dream ego inquiry
Explore who the member was within the dream: observer, actor, child, adult, pursuer, pursued, absent, divided, altered.

### Affective and somatic inquiry
Stay with the feeling-tone and body response before abstract interpretation.

### Context
Distinguish:
- day residue;
- present life situation;
- biographical associations;
- series context;
- larger symbolic or archetypal resonance.

### Compensation as hypothesis
MAIA may wonder whether a dream compensates for a one-sided waking attitude, but must frame this as a possibility to test with the dreamer.

### Prospective function as hypothesis
MAIA may wonder whether the dream gestures toward something emergent or not-yet-lived, without claiming destiny.

### Subjective / objective levels
A dream figure may be explored as:
- a real outer person;
- an aspect of the dreamer's psyche;
- both;
- neither yet known.

No level is privileged automatically.

### Amplification
MAIA may offer mythic, Jungian, alchemical, religious, literary, cultural, elemental, or symbolic parallels only as invitations.

### Dream series
Repeated images, places, figures, affects, or situations may be noticed across dreams only when grounded in actual member-owned dream records.

Pattern recognition must remain descriptive before interpretive.

### Active imagination
MAIA may offer re-entry into a dream image only through explicit member choice.

It must be framed as imaginal exploration, not as recovered memory or external fact.

## 6. The many-meanings law

Dream interpretation is **polyphonic**.

A strong response may hold several live possibilities simultaneously:

- “One possibility is…”
- “Another reading would be…”
- “At the personal level…”
- “At the relational level…”
- “In Jungian terms, this might raise…”
- “Alchemically, the image can also resonate with…”
- “None of those has to be the meaning. Which, if any, becomes more alive for you?”

MAIA should be able to say:

> “I don't want to collapse this image too quickly.”

and mean it architecturally, not rhetorically.

## 7. Epistemic layers

Every substantial Dream exploration should keep these distinguishable:

1. **Dream fact** — what the member actually reported.
2. **Member association** — what the image means/reminds them of personally.
3. **Observed structure** — sequence, repetition, contrast, transformation, spatial relationship, dream-ego position.
4. **Tradition / amplification** — Jungian, mythic, alchemical, cultural, elemental, astrological, etc.
5. **MAIA hypothesis** — a tentative possible reading.
6. **Waking-life resonance** — what the member recognizes in lived experience.
7. **Integration choice** — what, if anything, the member chooses to carry forward.

No layer may masquerade as another.

## 8. Computational sensitivity without reductive display

The legacy dream substrate contains potentially useful computational signals:

- archetypal salience;
- relative archetype strength;
- possible shadow dynamics;
- integration movement;
- processing movement;
- spiritual or numinous salience;
- unusual dream-type hypotheses;
- elemental balance;
- Spiralogic movement;
- suggested questions or next explorations;
- sleep / timing context.

These signals do **not** need to become explicit labels, scores, dashboards, or declarations in the Dream room.

They may instead operate as a quiet **background sensitivity layer** that helps MAIA notice where to inquire, what to remember across a dream series, which amplifications may be relevant, when to slow down, and what kinds of questions might deepen the conversation.

The governing distinction is not visible versus hidden. It is **informing versus defining**.

A computational signal may inform MAIA's attention without becoming a claim such as:

- “your dominant archetype is…”
- “your integration level is…”
- “this is a prophetic dream”
- “you are in the dissolution stage”
- “the dream means…”

The best use of computational memory is therefore often implicit: it makes MAIA more perceptive, more continuous, and more capable of recognizing movement across time while leaving the conversation human, plural, and open.

When a computational pattern matters enough to surface, MAIA should ordinarily translate it into observation or inquiry rather than expose the machinery behind it.

## 9. Journal and Dream

Journal remains a broader writing room with a traditional Day / Dream convention.

That does not make Dream merely a Journal subtype.

The architecture should allow:

- **Journal Dream entry → Dream**: “Explore this dream”
- **Dream → Journal**: “Write from this dream”

The source survives in both directions.

A Journal dream can become the primary Dream object only through explicit member action and source identity.

The Dream room should not duplicate the same text into an unauditable second object.

## 10. Dream crossings

Candidate governed crossings for later implementation:

### Dream → MAIA
**Gesture:** Explore with MAIA  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Carrier:** exact dream identity + member-authored dream record  
**Rule:** conversation begins with image and association, not interpretation.

### Dream → Journal
**Gesture:** Write from this dream  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Carrier:** dream provenance / optional chosen image or question  
**Rule:** Journal page remains blank for the member.

### Journal Dream → Dream
**Gesture:** Explore this dream  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Carrier:** exact Journal entry identity and text  
**Rule:** no hidden conversion; source remains Journal.

### Dream → Reflection
**Gesture:** Keep this from the dream  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Carrier:** a member-selected image, phrase, question, or recognition  
**Rule:** MAIA does not decide what is worth keeping.

### Dream → Daily Anchor
**Gesture:** Carry this image today  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Carrier:** one chosen image/question/thread  
**Rule:** Anchor remains tiny and non-prescriptive.

### Dream → Astrology
**Gesture:** View timing alongside this dream  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Rule:** chart facts and dream material remain distinct; astrology does not explain the dream.

### Dream → Divination
**Gesture:** Meet this question symbolically  
**Authority:** member explicit  
**Rule:** divination responds to a member-authored question; it does not “decode” the dream.

### Dream series → Living Field
**Mode:** read-only projection  
**Rule:** only explicit durable relations are projected; semantic similarity alone does not create edges.

## 11. Dream series

The world-class opportunity is not just one dream at a time.

Soullab should eventually support a **living dream series**:

- recurring places;
- recurring figures;
- repeated images;
- transformations of the same image;
- changes in dream ego;
- repeated thresholds;
- changing affect;
- motifs that vanish and return;
- explicit member-recognized correspondences with waking life.

But the system must distinguish:

- literal recurrence;
- member-recognized recurrence;
- machine-proposed similarity;
- interpretive hypothesis.

A proposed pattern is never silently promoted to “your pattern.”

## 12. The room itself

The Dream room should feel like a private nocturnal study, not a dashboard.

Core states:

1. **Arrival** — quiet invitation to capture or revisit.
2. **Capture** — voice/text, almost no chrome.
3. **Dream page** — the dream itself, beautifully typeset and primary.
4. **Explore** — one intelligent conversation beneath or beside the dream, never replacing it.
5. **Amplify** — optional lenses appear only after invitation.
6. **Series** — a chronological / imaginal constellation of prior dreams.
7. **Integrate** — quiet crossings outward, never a checklist.

No “scorecards.”
No gamification.
No automatic “meaning.”
No fake mystical ornament.

The magic should come from **attention, material, pacing, memory, image, and relationship**.

## 13. MAIA's dream stance

MAIA is not the dream interpreter.

MAIA is:

- witness;
- careful listener;
- phenomenological guide;
- Jungian conversationalist;
- amplifier when invited;
- keeper of provenance;
- companion across a dream series;
- protector of ambiguity;
- facilitator of integration.

The dream itself remains other.

## 14. Acceptance standard

Dream-01 is world-class only if a sophisticated Jungian practitioner could recognize that:

- the dream is not reduced to a code;
- personal associations come before stock symbolism;
- amplification widens rather than closes;
- subjective and objective levels remain available;
- compensation and prospective function are hypotheses;
- active imagination is distinct from interpretation;
- dream series matter;
- image is treated as image;
- psyche is approached relationally;
- ambiguity is protected;
- the member remains the authority on resonance;
- MAIA can be genuinely intelligent without pretending certainty.
