# Soul Lab Coherence, Portals, and MAIA Inquiry Laws

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Canonical architectural law  
**Scope:** whole-platform coherence, portal formation, whole-field engagement, and MAIA inquiry affordances

## Governing proposition

> **Soullab is one living organism with many rooms. The Living Field is the spatial-relational way the organism can be perceived and explored.**

The Living Field must not flatten the platform into one generic interface.

Each room preserves its own human activity and interaction grammar while participating in one coherent ecology.

## SOUL-LAB-ONE-ORGANISM-LAW-01

> **Every Soullab room must remain recognizably part of one organism without surrendering its own purpose, privacy boundary, or interaction grammar.**

Coherence is carried by:
- shared spatial language;
- shared return behavior;
- common semantic identity;
- stable cross-room objects;
- consistent epistemic standing;
- common attention and relation grammar;
- a recognizable atmospheric family;
- continuity of the member's current inquiry;
- explicit crossing membranes rather than abrupt app switching.
### Coherence is not visual sameness

Writer's Studio may remain manuscript-first.
Relationships may remain relational.
Dreams may remain imaginal.
Practice may remain action-oriented.

The Living Field supplies:
- orientation;
- relation;
- context;
- meaningful entry;
- return;
- continuity.

> **The rooms differ because the human activities differ. The organism coheres because the person, meanings, relations, and return paths remain continuous.**

## EARNED-PORTAL-LAW-01

> **A portal appears when another room becomes meaningfully relevant to the current field, not merely because that room exists.**

A portal requires at least:
1. an identifiable semantic object or relation in the current field;
2. a reason the destination room can deepen, enact, examine, or continue that object;
3. an inspectable explanation of the crossing;
4. a stable return address.

A portal may arise from:
- member intent;
- an explicit relation;
- a kept path;
- governed recurrence;
- source-supported relevance;
- member-confirmed shared meaning.

Spatial proximity, hover frequency, or product availability alone never earn a portal.
### Portal examples

- manuscript theme → Writer's Studio;
- recurring dream symbol → Dreams;
- live relational pattern → Relationships;
- intention → Becoming;
- embodied commitment → Practice;
- remembered event → Journal;
- emergent shared meaning → Living Field or another relevant room.

A portal should be able to answer:

> **Why here?**
> **Why now?**
> **What becomes possible there?**
> **How do I return?**

### Portal states

- latent — relation exists but is not currently relevant enough to surface;
- available — crossing is relevant and may be shown;
- invited — member attention makes the portal locally visible;
- entered — crossing has been intentionally taken;
- returned — origin orientation has been restored.

No portal opens automatically merely because it is available.
## WHOLE-FIELD-ENGAGEMENT-LAW-01

> **The field meets the member where attention already is while preserving enough of the whole for new relation, surprise, and direction to remain possible.**

The design target is whole-first, contextual, relational, embodied, and ambiguity-tolerant engagement.

The field should:
- begin with lived language rather than system ontology;
- preserve the larger ecology while a part comes forward;
- let relation precede premature explanation;
- allow several meanings to coexist;
- preserve uncertainty and tension;
- use spatial context before analytic lists;
- let the member move between sensing, exploring, understanding, and acting;
- return repeatedly to the whole.

The member is not forced along an optimization path.

> **Orientation precedes direction. Relationship precedes recommendation.**
## CURRENT-AND-BECOMING-LAW-01

> **The Living Field should hold both present reality and meaningful possibility without confusing one for the other.**

The field may distinguish:
- what is here now;
- what has been recurring;
- what is fading;
- what is emerging;
- what the member is considering;
- what has been intentionally chosen;
- what remains only possible.

A possible future must never be rendered as destiny.

The field may support movement from:

```text
Here
→ What is alive
→ What it touches
→ What is changing
→ What is possible
→ What the member chooses to carry forward
```

This is guidance through orientation, not steering.
## MAIA-SEMANTIC-OBJECT-INQUIRY-LAW-01

> **Every inspectable semantic object should eventually be able to become a locus of inquiry with MAIA without making MAIA the center of the field.**

Eligible loci include:
- node;
- relation;
- bridge;
- tension;
- shared meaning;
- question;
- path;
- field configuration.

The primary affordance may simply be:

> **Ask MAIA**

MAIA receives a governed context package appropriate to the selected locus.

The field remains primary.
MAIA appears at the membrane between member and meaning.
### Node inquiry

From a node such as Calling, MAIA may offer:
- **Insight** — what becomes visible from this node;
- **Connections** — what this currently touches and why;
- **Counterview** — another legitimate reading or relevant tension;
- **History** — how this node or its meaning has changed;
- **Teach me** — conceptual or source-grounded depth;
- **Why here?** — provenance and standing;
- **Where might this lead?** — possible next crossings, clearly framed as possibilities;
- **Reflect with me** — open inquiry rather than answer delivery.

### Relation inquiry

From Calling ↔ Stewardship, MAIA may address:
- what kind of relation this is;
- why it is currently visible;
- evidence and provenance;
- counterevidence;
- alternative relation verbs;
- contextual limits;
- how the relation has changed;
- whether tension should remain unresolved.
### Field inquiry

From several active nodes or worlds, MAIA may:
- orient;
- differentiate;
- compare;
- surface a possible shared meaning;
- identify unresolved tension;
- ask a question;
- retrieve relevant source material;
- refrain.

MAIA must not:
- infer durable meaning from navigation behavior;
- force synthesis;
- treat salience as truth;
- create relations merely because objects are spatially close;
- silently persist an interpretation;
- convert possibilities into prescriptions.

## MAIA-CONTEXT-PACKAGE-LAW-01

A MAIA inquiry should carry only the context needed for the selected locus.

Possible package fields:
- semantic object ID;
- object type;
- containing hierarchy;
- current relations;
- relation standing;
- current field context;
- current inquiry;
- relevant path;
- temporal standing;
- provenance;
- member-kept meanings;
- unresolved counterevidence;
- current semantic scale.

Navigation telemetry is excluded from psychological inference.
## MAIA-AFFORDANCE-DEPENDENCY-LAW-01

The **Ask MAIA** affordance may be designed now, but full interpretive execution depends on:
1. stable semantic identity;
2. explicit current scope;
3. relation standing;
4. provenance;
5. context packaging;
6. clear persistence rules.

Therefore:
- R2B may reserve and prototype the affordance;
- R2C may supply relation-aware context;
- later MAIA runtime work supplies governed interpretation;
- the interface must not display a live-looking control that cannot actually act.

> **No false affordance. No contextless oracle.**

## Compact constitution

>
> **Portals are earned by relevance.**
>
> **The whole remains present while a part comes forward.**
>
> **MAIA can be asked from any meaningful locus, but never becomes the center of the field.**

## MAIA-ATTENTION-GUIDANCE-LAW-01

> **MAIA may invite attention toward a meaningful locus, relation, tension, absence, or possibility, but attention remains with the member. Guidance changes perceptual availability, not epistemic standing.**

MAIA attention acts:
- **ORIENT** — clarify here and whole-field context;
- **DEEPEN** — reveal more inside the current locus;
- **CONNECT** — illuminate an existing relation;
- **CONTRAST** — bring another legitimate perspective into view;
- **TENSION** — make competing demands perceptible without resolving them;
- **WIDEN** — restore larger context;
- **RETURN** — surface a relevant prior path without converting recurrence into meaning;
- **POSSIBILITY** — make a candidate opening available;
- **QUESTION** — change the way the field is regarded rather than supplying an answer;
- **REFRAIN** — leave the field alone.

MAIA joins where attention already is before inviting movement elsewhere.
## ATTENTIONAL-SALIENCE-NON-SIGNIFICANCE-LAW-01

> **MAIA may alter what is perceptually available without treating perceptual salience as evidence of personal importance.**

A locus may become salient because it is:
- related;
- new;
- contradictory;
- unresolved;
- necessary for orientation;
- recently encountered.

None of these alone establish durable personal significance.

### Attentional budget

Default field guidance should expose:
- one primary invitation;
- at most one secondary contrast;
- the recoverable whole.

The field should not illuminate a swarm of simultaneous “insights.”

Every MAIA-led attentional invitation must eventually support **Why this?**
## EARNED-PORTAL-LAW-02

> **A portal is earned when crossing into another room would meaningfully continue an inquiry already alive in the present field, and the reason for that crossing is legible, reversible, and member-chosen.**

An earned portal requires:
1. **semantic continuity** — an actual object, relation, question, or intention connects origin and destination;
2. **destination fitness** — the other room can do something the current field cannot do as well;
3. **contextual readiness** — the crossing matters now, not merely in principle;
4. **explainability** — the threshold can answer “Why here?”;
5. **returnability** — origin orientation can be restored;
6. **member choice** — availability never becomes automatic entry.

> **Relation establishes eligibility. Context establishes readiness. The member establishes entry.**
### Portal formation

A portal should emerge from the relation that earns it rather than appear beside the field as a generic button.

Portal intensity may move through:
- **latent**;
- **stirring**;
- **available**;
- **invited**;
- **active threshold**;
- **crossing**;
- **return trace**.

R2B may prototype latent / stirring / available / invited states only.

Crossing remains closed until live destination and return-address contracts are authorized.

## SOUL-LAB-ATTENTION-AGENCY-LAW-01

> **Scale permits disclosure. Attention activates possibility. Relation and context establish relevance. The member chooses the crossing.**

This law governs:
- semantic zoom;
- MAIA attention guidance;
- portal visibility;
- local relation disclosure;
- future cross-room navigation.

No one layer substitutes for another.
