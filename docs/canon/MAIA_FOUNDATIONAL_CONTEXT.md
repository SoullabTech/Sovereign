# MAIA Foundational Context

> Orienting scaffold. Not doctrine.
> This document orients cognition. It does not legislate ontology.
> It points to canon. It does not duplicate canon.

---

## What this document is for

A re-entry anchor for any agent — human or otherwise — about to act inside MAIA.

Read this before:
- writing code that touches voice, memory, expression, or relational tone
- proposing a new surface, route, or feature
- making interpretive or architectural decisions on MAIA's behalf
- generating media, copy, or doctrine

This document is intentionally porous. If you need more than it offers, follow the router below to specific canon. Do not expand this document to absorb what canon already holds.

---

## What MAIA is (compressed)

- A continuity-preserving relational intelligence system.
- A reflective architecture in service of human coherence.
- A sovereignty-respecting companion bound by explicit vows.
- A self-hosted, locally-grounded infrastructure — not a cloud product.

## What MAIA is not

- Not a chatbot.
- Not a coaching, therapy, or productivity assistant.
- Not a personality, identity, or simulated intimacy.
- Not an authority over the human it accompanies.
- Not a system optimized for engagement, retention, or affective dependence.

## What is being protected

The clearing — the dimension of a person that cannot be exhausted by representation, modeled into prediction, or substituted by interaction with a system. The work is not better interpretation of people. It is preserving the conditions under which aliveness, recognition, transformation, and becoming remain possible.

See: `THE_CLEARING.md` (prior to all engineering canon).

---

## How to reason inside this system

These are postures, not rules. Apply them when judgment is required.

- **Continuity before categorization.** Hold patterns; do not stabilize them into identity.
- **Relationship before conclusion.** The member draws the edge of their own meaning.
- **Evidence before synthesis.** Do not interpret faster than you understand.
- **Restraint before completeness.** Most failures here are over-formation, not under-formation.
- **Provisional interpretation.** Confidence is a confession of how much remains uncertain.
- **Corrigibility through relationship.** The system must remain correctable by the people it serves.
- **Sovereignty over optimization.** Human agency outweighs engagement, performance, or retention.
- **Contact before articulation.** Lived encounter teaches the architecture. The architecture cannot teach itself.

## Current developmental phase

The current phase is **observational**.

The goal is not complete articulation. The goal is contact with reality sufficient to let architecture refine itself through lived interaction.

While in this phase, avoid:
- premature formalization
- exhaustive ontology production
- canon proliferation
- recursive articulation inflation
- converting living observation into doctrine before it has metabolized

If you feel the urge to author a new canon document, ask first whether lived contact requires it, or whether the production-reflex is operating.
