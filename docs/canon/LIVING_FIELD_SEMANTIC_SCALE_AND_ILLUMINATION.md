# Living Field Semantic Scale + Illumination Laws

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** Canonical semantic / perceptual law
**Scope:** meaning across scale, semantic conservation, recursive depth, right-side illumination, and what remains visible

## Governing proposition

> **Meaning scales by transformation of representation, not by deletion of reality.**

The Living Field may show less detail at wider scale and more detail at closer scale, but it must not silently erase distinctions, tensions, uncertainty, provenance, or containing context merely to make the field visually simple.

Visual beauty, physics, animation, atmosphere, and dimensionality serve semantic depth. When they conflict, semantic depth governs.

## SEMANTIC-PRIMACY-LAW-01

> **The Living Field is a semantic environment expressed spatially. Meaning outranks representation.**

Priority order:

```text
meaning
→ relation
→ orientation
→ interaction
→ aesthetics
```

This is not a claim that aesthetics are optional. Aesthetics are how the field becomes perceptible, inhabitable, and memorable. They may never make the meaning less honest.
## TWO-AXIS-DEPTH-LAW-01

> **Recursive depth and semantic resolution are coupled but non-identical.**

### Axis A — containing depth

This answers:

> **Where am I inside the ecology?**

Examples:

```text
Living Field
→ Water
→ Relationship
→ Grief
→ Continuing Relation
```

Containing depth is spatial / genealogical.

### Axis B — semantic resolution

This answers:

> **How much of the meaning of this locus is currently resolved?**

Resolution may progress through:

```text
essence
→ constituents
→ neighborhood
→ relations
→ tensions / shared meanings
→ evidence / provenance / history
```

A member may therefore be deeply nested in one world while still seeing only its essence, or remain at a wider containing level while inspecting a relation at high epistemic resolution.

> **More zoom never means more truth. It means more resolution.**
## SEMANTIC-CONSERVATION-LAW-01

> **Every change of scale must preserve the information needed to understand what the current representation stands for and what it leaves unresolved.**

The following are semantic invariants.

### 1. Identity

The current locus retains its identity across scale.

### 2. Containing context / path

The member can recover what contains the current locus. This is current semantic orientation, not developmental lineage.

### 3. Current inquiry

The live question carried by the locus remains available.

### 4. Relation standing

Relevant connections remain represented directly or through an honest condensation.

### 5. Unresolved tension

Conflict, contradiction, qualification, and uncertainty survive condensation.

### 6. Multiplicity

Several meanings may remain several. Wider scale does not manufacture a single interpretation.

### 7. Provenance availability

Full provenance need not remain visually expanded, but its existence and inspectability survive.

### 8. Unresolved remainder

If detail has been condensed, the field must preserve that more remains beneath the representation.

### 9. Return path

The member can widen toward the whole without reconstructing navigation history.

### 10. Temporal standing

When meaning has materially changed through time, the current view must not falsely imply timelessness.
## REPRESENTATION-TRANSFORMATION-LAW-01

Scale transformations should follow:

```text
particulars
→ neighborhoods
→ bundles
→ bridges
→ patterns
→ whole-field gestalt
```

and reverse without semantic invention:

```text
whole-field gestalt
→ patterns
→ bridges
→ bundles
→ neighborhoods
→ particulars
```

The reverse path must unpack the actual constituents from which the wider representation was formed.

Condensation is therefore a reversible projection, not a new interpretation.

## OMISSION-MUST-BE-LEGIBLE-LAW-01

> **Hidden detail must be experienced as latent, not absent.**

When material is below the current level of detail, the field may indicate its existence through:
- child count;
- bundled relation thickness or multiplicity;
- membrane texture / density;
- quiet unresolved markers;
- a visible deeper-entry affordance;
- right-panel wording such as “3 relations beneath this bridge.”

The field must not imply that what is not currently drawn does not exist.
## SCALE-VISIBILITY-LAW-01

### L0 — Whole ecology

Primary question:

> **What kind of whole is here?**

Keep visible:
- elemental / major worlds;
- world identity and brief essence;
- current locus if one exists;
- strongest cross-world bridges as bundles;
- unresolved tension markers;
- broad field pattern;
- one-gesture recovery into any visible world.

Condense:
- individual local edges;
- detailed node labels;
- rationale;
- evidence.

### L1 — Worlds

Primary question:

> **What worlds constitute this whole, and how do they meet?**

Keep visible:
- world identity;
- world essence / inquiry;
- major child regions or child count;
- bridges to other worlds;
- current containing context path;
- unresolved tension crossing the world boundary.

Condense:
- most intra-world detail;
- relation rationale;
- provenance detail.
### L2 — Regional cells

Primary question:

> **What lives inside this world?**

Keep visible:
- child cells;
- their basic identity;
- containing membrane;
- important local relations;
- cross-world relation anchors;
- current inquiry;
- recursive readiness where coherent child material exists.

### L3 — Local neighborhood

Primary question:

> **What surrounds this locus and shapes its meaning?**

Keep visible:
- selected locus;
- immediate neighbors;
- relation kinds;
- parent world;
- relevant external bridge anchors;
- active tension;
- directional context of the wider field.

### L4 — Relational meaning

Primary question:

> **How exactly are these things related?**

Keep visible / inspectable:
- source and target;
- relation type and verb;
- rationale;
- context;
- tension / contradiction / qualification;
- candidate shared meaning where independently supported;
- counterevidence or unresolved qualification when available.

### L5 — Evidence / provenance

Primary question:

> **Why may this meaning stand here at all?**

Keep inspectable:
- provenance;
- authorship;
- standing;
- temporal scope;
- supporting material;
- counterevidence;
- revision history;
- persistence / consent standing where applicable.
## FIGURE-GROUND-SEMANTIC-LAW-01

> **Foreground follows the current semantic locus; background preserves belonging.**

At any depth:
- current locus receives highest legibility;
- immediate context remains clearly available;
- containing context recedes but remains perceivable;
- unrelated detail may quiet substantially;
- unresolved relations may not be dimmed so far that their existence becomes misleading.

Aesthetic glow may follow attention.
Semantic visibility follows relevance and standing.

## RIGHT-SIDE-ILLUMINATION-LAW-01

> **The right panel illuminates the selected semantic object; it does not replace the field or behave like a generic inspector.**

For a selected object it may progressively reveal:
- identity / element / semantic kind;
- essence;
- current inquiry;
- constituents — **In this field**;
- relations;
- tensions / counterforces;
- related fields;
- possible paths;
- developmental / temporal movement;
- evidence / provenance;
- an anchored **Explore with MAIA** action;
- an **Enter field** action when coherent child ecology exists.

The panel must distinguish:
- what is present in the field;
- what is inferred or candidate;
- what is source-supported;
- what remains unknown.
## RIGHT-PANEL-SCALE-LAW-01

The right panel scales with semantic resolution.

### Wide

Show:
- title;
- essence;
- live inquiry;
- constituent count;
- important related fields;
- unresolved tension if present.

### Local

Add:
- constituent list;
- relation neighborhood;
- relation type;
- available deeper fields;
- several legitimate next paths.

### Deep

Add:
- rationale;
- context;
- competing interpretation;
- counterevidence;
- provenance;
- history / revision;
- sources.

The panel may scroll. The field should not be forced to carry prose it cannot hold spatially.

## PATH-PLURALITY-LAW-01

> **The field may guide attention without prescribing one correct route.**

“Possible paths” should show several coherent continuations when several exist.

No visual prominence, ordering, or MAIA suggestion may silently convert a possible path into an authoritative next step.

## BUILD ACCEPTANCE

At every scale ask:

> **Can the member perceive more meaning — not merely more detail?**

And:

> **Can the member still tell what has been condensed, what remains unresolved, and where this meaning belongs in the larger whole?**

If not, the scale transformation fails.
