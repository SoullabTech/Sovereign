# Soul Lab Spatial Navigation Law

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Canonical architectural law

## Governing proposition

> **The Living Field is not merely a visualization inside Soullab. It is a spatial threshold into the whole Soul lab.**

Soullab should eventually be explorable as one coherent living ecology rather than a collection of unrelated applications.

The field may open onto:
- Living Field;
- Relationships;
- Journal;
- Dreams;
- Writer's Studio;
- Vision Studio;
- Practice;
- Becoming;
- other governed rooms as they mature.
## Room integrity

A room is not reduced to a bubble-menu item.

Each room keeps:
- its own human activity;
- its own interaction grammar;
- its own privacy and permission boundary;
- its own depth.

The Living Field provides orientation, relation, entry, return, and continuity between rooms.

> **The field connects rooms without flattening them.**

## Spatial return address

Every governed crossing should eventually be able to retain:
- origin room;
- origin semantic object;
- camera / scale;
- focus;
- relevant path;
- destination room or field;
- return address.

A return must restore meaningful orientation rather than merely route to a URL.
## Earned portals

A platform crossing appears because it is relevant to the current field, not because every product destination must always be visible.

Examples:
- a manuscript theme may reveal Writer's Studio;
- a recurring dream symbol may reveal Dreams;
- a live relational pattern may reveal Relationships;
- an intention may reveal Becoming or Practice.

> **A portal is a meaningful crossing, not navigation chrome disguised as magic.**

## Scale continuity

The same movement grammar should support:
- whole life field;
- life thread;
- platform room;
- project;
- relation;
- local ecology;
- meaning;
- evidence.

Changing scale must not silently change epistemic standing.
## Soul Lab experience law

> **Exploration should feel like entering a living laboratory of relationships, connections, questions, memories, creations, and emerging meaning.**

The experience may evoke discovery and wonder while remaining:
- legible;
- reversible;
- inspectable;
- non-coercive;
- epistemically honest.

Magic comes from responsive depth and meaningful connection, not unexplained system behavior.

## R2A implication

R2A must build a camera/navigation substrate capable of future cross-room continuity.

It does not yet authorize:
- live cross-room portals;
- persistent cross-room memory;
- MAIA interpretation of navigation behavior;
- automatic meaning inference.

Those remain later governed acts.
