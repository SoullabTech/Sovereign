# Living Field Focus + Context Laws

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Canonical perceptual law  
**Scope:** focus, peripheral whole-field context, relation framing, and held tension

## FOCUS-PRESERVES-WHOLE-LAW-01

> **Focus increases local resolution without amputating the surrounding field.**

A focused locus may dominate perception, but the field must preserve:
- its containing world;
- immediate relational neighborhood;
- directional awareness of the wider worlds;
- one-gesture recovery to the whole.

Foreground may intensify.
Background may quiet.
Context may not become absent.
## Focus layers

### Foreground
The selected node, relation, tension, or shared meaning.

### Local context
Immediate neighbors and containing membrane.

### Peripheral context
Other worlds and relevant bridges remain perceptually locatable even when outside the camera frame.

### Whole
The full field remains recoverable without reconstructing navigation history.

> **Deepening changes resolution, not belonging.**
## CONTEXT-ECHO-LAW-01

> **When a meaningful world moves beyond the visible frame, the field may preserve a quiet directional echo of where it remains.**

A context echo:
- is not a navigation menu;
- carries world identity and direction;
- stays visually secondary;
- never changes semantic standing;
- disappears when the world itself is comfortably visible again.

This supports focus-with-context without shrinking the focused field to keep everything literally onscreen.
## RELATION-FOCUS-FRAMING-LAW-01

> **Focusing a relation frames both poles and the space between them.**

Relation focus must not:
- center only one endpoint;
- hide one pole because the other was selected first;
- imply that the midpoint is automatically a synthesis.

The camera should fit:
- source;
- target;
- meaningful between-space;
- enough surrounding context to understand where both belong.
## HELD-TENSION-LAW-01

> **A tension feels held when both poles remain simultaneously present, neither is privileged as the answer, the relation remains intact, and the between-space stays open without forced synthesis.**

Held tension requires:
- equal visual emphasis on both poles;
- simultaneous framing;
- preserved distance;
- visible connection;
- no automatic convergence;
- no compromise node inserted by the system;
- explicit permission for unresolved standing.

The field may hold tension indefinitely.
## TENSION-BETWEEN-SPACE-LAW-01

The between-space of a tension may be perceptible as a containing relational field.

It may show:
- the fact of tension;
- the question generated by the tension;
- provenance and context;
- a candidate shared meaning only when independently supported.

It must not imply:
- balance has been achieved;
- both sides are equally evidentially supported;
- synthesis is inevitable;
- a midpoint is inherently wiser.

> **Held space is not the same as resolved meaning.**
## R2D acceptance

R2D must prove:
1. deep node focus preserves containing-world and whole-field orientation;
2. offscreen worlds remain directionally locatable without becoming navigation chrome;
3. relation focus reframes both endpoints rather than privileging the first selected node;
4. productive tension places both poles simultaneously in view;
5. tension between-space is perceptible but contains no synthetic answer;
6. Whole recovery remains immediate;
7. desktop and touch/mobile retain equivalent orientation.
