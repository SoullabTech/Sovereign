# Living Field Meaning Evolution Laws

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Status:** Canonical epistemic and developmental law  
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-29  
**Scope:** relations, shared meaning, revision, conflict, context, temporal standing, and semantic zoom

## Governing proposition

> **Meaning is not a fixed property attached to an object. It develops through relation, context, perspective, time, experience, and recognition.**

The Living Field therefore preserves how meaning changes rather than storing only a latest interpretation.

## SEMANTIC-ZOOM-DEPENDENCY-LAW-01

> **Semantic zoom may deepen only what the field can keep spatially, relationally, and epistemically coherent.**

Therefore:

- identity precedes hierarchy;
- hierarchy precedes topology;
- topology precedes physics;
- physics precedes attention;
- attention + relation precede level-of-detail;
- level-of-detail precedes semantic zoom;
- semantic zoom precedes recursive depth;
- recursive depth precedes durable field memory;
- durable field memory precedes longitudinal intelligence.

At every scale:

> **the wider whole remains recoverable.**
## SHARED-MEANING-FORMATION-LAW-01

> **Shared meaning may emerge between differentiated things when their relation becomes intelligible, supportable, and recognized. The meaning remains traceable to the constituents and relations from which it emerged.**

Shared meaning forms through a progression such as:

```text
co-presence
→ possible relation
→ articulated relation
→ supported relation
→ recognized relation
→ shared meaning
→ carried-forward meaning
```

A shared meaning object must retain:
- constituents;
- relation or relations;
- context;
- wording;
- standing;
- provenance;
- authorship;
- counterevidence where relevant;
- temporal scope;
- persistence permission.

Shared meaning is contextual. A relation may mean one thing in one life thread and something different elsewhere.

## SHARED-MEANING-PLURALITY-LAW-01

> **Shared meaning may consist in mutually intelligible difference rather than agreement.**

A jointly held field may contain divergent meanings without forcing synthesis.

Example:
- one person experiences an event through freedom;
- another experiences the same event through trust.

The shared field may preserve that difference as the meaningful relation.
## RELATION-TRUST-LAW-01

> **A relation is trustworthy when its type, context, provenance, authorship, standing, scope, and revisability are available to inspection. Trust comes from honest standing, not apparent certainty.**

A trustworthy relation should be able to answer:
- What exactly is connected?
- How are they connected?
- Where does the relation apply?
- Why is the relation present?
- Who proposed, authored, or recognized it?
- What supports it?
- When was it active or recognized?
- Is it local or cross-contextual?
- What counterevidence or exceptions exist?
- Can it be questioned, revised, or removed?

Qualitative standing may include:
- candidate;
- source-supported;
- member-authored;
- member-recognized;
- recurrent;
- integrated.

These describe provenance and standing, not ranked truth scores.

## PROXIMITY-NON-EVIDENCE-LAW-01

> **Spatial nearness may represent an already-governed relation. It may never itself establish the relation.**

Force layout may express relation. Layout position is not evidence for relation.

## ATTENTION-NON-EVIDENCE-LAW-01

> **Attention may change visibility and immediate interaction. It does not by itself change epistemic standing.**

Hover duration, dwell time, repeated glances, zoom frequency, drag behavior, and cursor movement are navigation signals, not psychological evidence.
## PRODUCTIVE-TENSION-LAW-01

> **The field may hold incompatible or competing relations without forcing resolution. Tension is itself meaningful information.**

Relations may:
- resonate;
- support;
- qualify;
- constrain;
- polarize;
- contradict;
- transform;
- contextualize one another.

Not every relation attracts.

Some relations should preserve distance or opposition while remaining connected.

A mature state may be unresolved tension.

The field must resist automatic synthesis when the underlying relation remains unresolved.

## SEMANTIC-CONDENSATION-INTEGRITY-LAW-01

> **Condensation may simplify representation without resolving distinctions that remain unresolved in the underlying field.**

Zooming outward may condense:
- specific edges → bundled relation;
- bundled relations → cross-world bridge;
- detailed tension → named tension pattern.

It may not convert unresolved tension into harmony merely for visual simplicity.
## MEANING-REVISION-LAW-01

> **A new interpretation changes the standing of earlier meaning rather than silently replacing it. Revision remains traceable.**

A meaning lineage may contain:
- earlier interpretation;
- revised interpretation;
- expanded perspective;
- retained meaning;
- integrated meaning;
- unresolved remainder;
- relinquished meaning.

Revision should preserve:
- what changed;
- what prompted the change;
- what remains;
- what was relinquished;
- what is still uncertain.

Earlier interpretations remain part of developmental history unless they are factual errors corrected by evidence.

## MEANING-IS-TEMPORAL-LAW-01

> **Meaning belongs both to what is interpreted and to the evolving life from which the interpretation arises.**

Durable meaning therefore carries temporal standing.

The field should be able to show not only:
> what something means

but:
> **how its meaning has moved.**
## CONTEXTUAL-SALIENCE-LAW-01

> **Context changes which meanings become available and salient without automatically changing their truth standing.**

Context may include:
- mood;
- environment;
- relationship context;
- developmental stage;
- physical state;
- current events;
- new learning;
- cultural context;
- temporal distance;
- life thread.

A meaning being especially present today does not establish that it is deeper, truer, or more representative.

## MEANING-PERSPECTIVE-DIFFERENTIATION-LAW-01

The field distinguishes:
- factual correction;
- interpretive revision;
- perspective expansion;
- emotional revision;
- developmental integration.

These are different processes and must not be collapsed into one generic “updated meaning” state.

## INTERACTION-NON-INFERENCE-LAW-01

> **Navigation behavior may support immediate interaction but does not become psychological evidence by itself.**

No durable claim about preference, importance, personality, diagnosis, or meaning may be inferred solely from interaction telemetry.
## Relation and meaning lineages

Relations themselves may evolve.

Example:

```text
Mother → Control
      ↓ revised by perspective
Mother → Fear-driven protection
      ↓ expanded
Protection ↔ Control
```

A relation lineage should preserve:
- previous relation wording;
- revised relation wording;
- revision type;
- context;
- authorship;
- supporting evidence;
- retained and relinquished meaning.

## Relations and meanings as explorable fields

Recursive depth may open not only nodes but:
- a relation;
- a conflict;
- a question;
- a shared meaning;
- an unresolved polarity.

The **between** may become a world when it has sufficient coherent material.

Example:

```text
Calling ↔ Stewardship
      ↓
Responsible Creation
      ↓
Vision · Limits · Sustainability · Service · Consequence
```

Such a field remains traceable to its constituent relations and may not be fabricated merely to create depth.

## Canonical compact form

> **Soullab does not merely remember what something meant. It can remember how meaning changed.**

> **Conflict may be productive. Difference may remain intelligible without becoming agreement. Meaning may be revised without erasing its history. Context may change salience without becoming truth.**
