# MAIA Formation Architecture — six layers

**Status: CANON CANDIDATE — 2026-08-06. Not ratified. Authorizes no build.**
Founder-authored in conversation, drafted to file the same day.

**Nature.** A description of how a MAIA is *constituted*, not a feature specification and not a
prompt architecture. Prompting is the final composition step, not the source of the layers.

**Claim discipline (`docs/canon/MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`).** Layers 1, 2, 5 and — for Writer
Studio only — 6 restate ratified canon. Layers 3 and 4 are **Designed**, not Live. The generalized
form of Layer 6 is **Vision** until ruled. Nothing here may be described outward as operating.

---

## 0. The organizing principle

Specialization is **not by model and not by prompt**. It is by the **human practice the MAIA
accompanies**. A MAIA is distinguished by its *center of continuity* — what has duration in the
relationship — and everything else follows from that.

> Writer MAIA exists as a distinct field because **a work is not a person.**
> — `WRITER_MAIA_FIELD_CONTAINMENT_2026-08-06.md` §1

This document generalizes that sentence: **each field is constituted by what it accompanies.**

### Orientation

> ⭐⭐⭐ **A MAIA is distinguished by its center of continuity.**

The six formation layers described in this document are the **discovered consequences** of adopting
that orientation. Future additions belong only if they preserve — not redefine — that center of
continuity.

- **Constitution** governs every center of continuity.
- **Identity** names the center.
- **Temperament** shapes how it attends.
- **Reference Environment** governs what it may consult.
- **Field Memory** preserves that center's continuity.
- **Crossing Protocols** govern movement between centers.

**The test stands:** if a proposed layer cannot be stated as a consequence of the center of
continuity, it does not belong here.

⛔ **This is an orientation, not a deductive axiom — and the distinction is load-bearing.** The
ontology produced the sentence; the sentence did not produce the ontology. The six layers were
discovered, then this sentence became visible as what they had in common. A reader who mistakes the
order will believe further layers can be derived *analytically* from the sentence. They cannot. New
layers arrive the way these did — from encounter with an actual field — and the orientation is what
they are tested against afterward, not what they are deduced from.

## 1. Constitutional Layer — shared, identical, inherited

What every MAIA obeys before it is anything in particular: epistemic discipline · consent ·
truthfulness · uncertainty · boundaries · memory law · safety · the direction of authority.

Inherited **identically** by every field. A field may add constraint; it may never subtract.

**Source (ratified, not restated here):** `docs/canon/CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`
(Invariant 16) · `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md` · `MAIA_OATH.md` · `MAIA_CANON_v1.1.md`.

⛔ This layer is cited, never re-derived. A future session that restates it from memory has already
introduced drift.

---

## 2. Identity Layer — the center of continuity

Answers *"what has duration here?"* — **not** *"what is my role?"*

| Field | Center of continuity |
|---|---|
| Personal MAIA | a person's life |
| Practitioner MAIA | a practice becoming wiser |
| Writer MAIA | a work coming into being |

Identity is **ontological, not behavioral**. It determines what persists, what resumes, and what the
field is even *about* — and therefore determines Layers 5 and 6 rather than being decorated by them.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Identity is the invariant of the formation architecture. The remaining layers either
> inherit from, enrich, constrain, or govern this center of continuity.**

**The test this yields.** Whenever a new layer is proposed: *does it preserve the invariant, or is it
trying to redefine it?* A layer that redefines the center of continuity is not a new layer — it is a
new field, and must be established here, in §2, on its own terms.

### 2.1 ⛔ "Client MAIA" is HELD — deliberately not a row above

The distinction being reached for is real: a member in developmental work with a practitioner has
different continuity than the practitioner does. But naming it a separate MAIA risks the exact
failure already ruled against:

> A lens may never become the owner's name for the experience. — **CF-D5b** (Client Field ruling,
> 2026-08-03); vocabulary resolves at **read** time.

**Ruling held (founder, 2026-08-06):** the architecture may describe multiple centers of continuity
without requiring each to become a separately branded MAIA. Until a member-side center of continuity
distinct from Personal MAIA is explicitly ruled, developmental work is **the member's own MAIA under
read-time vocabulary** — see `NOMENCLATURE_AND_WORLD_ALIGNMENT_PRINCIPLE_2026-08-05.md` Rider 2
(precedence: member's own words > world vocabulary > platform vocabulary).

## 3. Temperament Layer — how this MAIA attends

**First-class, not an addition.** Two intelligences with identical knowledge feel different because
their *attention* differs. Temperament is:

- what it notices first;
- how long it tolerates ambiguity before resolving;
- whether it privileges process or product;
- what it listens for — rhythm and narrative coherence · intervention timing · lived meaning;
- how it asks questions.

**Why this layer is constitutionally cheap and load-bearing.** Temperament shapes **perception, not
authority**. It carries no external claim, cites no third party, and therefore requires no rights
instrument. Most of what an intuitive "wisdom" framing is reaching for — *this MAIA feels formed by
the discipline* — is achievable here, with no provenance debt.

**Design test.** If a proposed disciplinary quality can be expressed as *how MAIA attends*, it
belongs in Layer 3. If it can only be expressed as *what MAIA knows and asserts*, it belongs in
Layer 4 and inherits Layer 4's obligations.

⚠️ Boundary: temperament may shape attention. It may **not** become a standing interpretive stance
that manufactures meaning about the member — that would re-enter the authority ladder through the
back door (cf. `PRACTITIONER_INFERENCE_CONTAINMENT_2026-08-06.md`).

**Founder-supplied test (2026-08-06 — a lean pending the reading pass, not yet ruled):**

> If a temperament starts producing conclusions about the member that aren't grounded in what the
> member has actually expressed, it has crossed back into the authority ladder.

⭐ This test is **implementable**, and its permissible shape already has a code precedent —
`WRITER_STUDIO_AUTHOR_STUDIO_DISTINCTION_2026-08-04.md` §4.1 shape (b) / *"evidence, never meaning"*
(`app/api/sovereign/manuscripts/[id]/candidates/route.ts`, where the extractor's interpretive fields
are dropped server-side). Grounded-in-what-the-member-expressed is a checkable property, not a
disposition. If ruled, §3 gains an enforcement surface rather than a caution.

⛔ **Caution on citing code precedent (founder, 2026-08-06).** The precedent shows the test is
*enforceable*; it is not the *authority* for it. The authority must remain the ruling; the guard is
only how a ruling becomes enforceable. Keeping those distinct is what prevents an implementation
pattern from quietly becoming canon.

## 4. Reference Environment — governed, attributable, consultable

**Replaces the earlier "Wisdom" formulation, and the replacement is the point.**

A reference environment is a **governed body of attributable material MAIA may consult or direct the
person toward**. It is *not* formative authority. It does not constitute the MAIA; it is available
to it.

> **R3 (2026-08-04):** the Wisdom Field sits **beside** the authority ladder, never on it.
> — `docs/architecture/CORPUS_CALLOSUM_ARCHITECTURE_MAP_2026-08-04.md`

Placing it as Layer 4 — consultable, downstream of identity, upstream of nothing — is what keeps
that ruling intact. A "wisdom formation layer" upstream of identity would put it *on* the ladder and
invert Invariant 16.

### 4.1 Required provenance — five fields per item

Non-optional. An item lacking any one of these is not admissible to a reference environment.

| Field | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | who made this |
| **Claimed relationship** | is this the practitioner's own teaching · influenced by them · a reflection about them · a platform interpretation · a third-party lineage source |
| **Evidentiary basis** | what backs that claim |
| **Permitted runtime use** | what MAIA may do with it |
| **Authorization status** | who authorized that, and is it current |

### 4.2 The prohibition

**MAIA never silently absorbs reference material into its own voice.** It cites, or it summarizes as
an identified source, or it does not use the material. **Absence is honest; unattributed familiarity
is not.**

⚠️ **This is a live named defect, not a hypothetical.** `library_sources.author` exists, and
`composeLessonContext` selects `title, type, description` — dropping attribution. Third-party ideas
currently enter framed as *the field's own material*. The failure mode is **unattributed absorption**,
not impersonation (impersonation is already fenced at
`lib/practiceField/practiceFieldService.ts:239`).

### 4.3 ⛔ Build gate — standing

No reference-environment implementation proceeds until the practitioner IP inventory map is before
the founder. Rights substrate does not currently exist: the practitioner rights instrument is
**drafted and unsigned**, and third-party coursework material is **explicitly not** the
practitioner's IP and may not be treated as their lineage.

### 4.4 Known substrate gap

`source_type` is fragmented — ~13 columns across migrations carrying ~4 mutually incompatible
vocabularies, **none of which distinguishes member-lived material from reference material.** The
distinction this layer depends on has no schema home yet. Reconciling it is a prerequisite, not a
detail.

## 5. Field Memory — continuity unique to the field

Distinct storage · distinct history · distinct retrieval · distinct continuity. The work's thread
resumes as the work's thread; the practice's as the practice's.

**Ratified for Writer Studio** (`WRITER_MAIA_FIELD_CONTAINMENT_2026-08-06.md` §3): no memory bleed ·
no door out · no shared conversational thread · separate persistence · crossing requires an explicit
consent event.

⭐ **Containment is enforced at *write* time, not read time.** Member-keyed loaders surface at
`/maia` whether or not that was intended; a read-time filter is not a boundary.

## 6. Crossing Protocols — explicit, bidirectional, member-initiated

The general form:

```
Field A  →  Field B     Allowed?  NO  —  unless an explicit, person-initiated crossing gesture.
Field B  →  Field A     Allowed?  NO  —  unless an explicit, person-initiated crossing gesture.
```

Bidirectional for a reason. Inbound bleed populates a field before the person placed anything in it
(authorship lost). Outbound bleed lets the work escape its own context.

**Only the person creates a crossing.** The system may record `offered` / `available` / `presented`;
it may never infer `meaningful` / `integrated` / `adopted` — **R4**, direct continuation of
Invariant 16.

### 6.1 ⛔ Generalization requires a founder ruling — it is NOT inherited here

`WRITER_MAIA_FIELD_CONTAINMENT_2026-08-06.md` §5 governs **Writer Studio only** and states that
extension "must be ruled on its own terms, **not inherited by proximity**." Founder agreement
2026-08-06: the generalized protocol stays an explicit ruling, not an inferred generalization.

**Until that ruling lands, §6 above is Designed, not Live.**

### 6.2 Substrate honesty

`offered` is **not** a general recorded event — only scattered per-feature columns
(`last_offered_at`, `offered_at`, `practice_offered`, `offering_text`). Any crossing design assuming
an offering ledger exists is assuming absent substrate.

## 7. Runtime composition

> **Runtime behavior is composed from these layers; the prompt is only one expression of that
> composition.**

The layers compose, at minimum: **retrieval policy · memory scope · constitutional guards ·
reasoning constraints · model routing · prompt · response shaping.** Prompting is the last step of
formation, not its source — and it is one output among several, not the composition itself.

Stating it this way keeps the architecture **independent of any future implementation mechanism**. A
document that says "the prompt is composed from these layers" becomes wrong the moment composition
moves into retrieval or routing; this formulation does not.

⚠️ **Claim boundary — this list describes a target, not current behavior.** Routing already exists in
runtime (FAST / CORE / DEEP processing paths); it is **not** currently derived from formation layers.
A reader must not take §7 as documentation of how the system composes today. **Designed, not Live.**

⛔ **Naming discipline.** Describe mechanisms: *formation layers · runtime composition ·
constitutional inheritance · identity · temperament · reference environment · field memory ·
crossing protocols.* The metaphors this framing arrived through — "a university," "each MAIA
graduates from a school" — are **deliberately removed**. They claim an education that has not
occurred. Metaphor after measurement, never before.

⛔ **R5 stands:** the first version of the Corpus Callosum is a **grammar, not a service.** This
document does not authorize a formation database layer.

## 8. What this document does and does not do

**Does:** name six layers and their order; place the reference environment beside the authority
ladder rather than on it; make temperament first-class; hold "Client MAIA".

**Does not:** ratify anything; authorize any build; lift the practitioner IP build gate; extend the
Writer containment law beyond Writer Studio; rule §7 of the practitioner containment doc (the
four-part crossing rule and the three MAIA-awareness classes); rule supervision; rename any code.

## 9. Open before ratification

1. **Crossing generalization** — the §6.1 founder ruling.

   ⭐⭐⭐ **The question to rule FIRST is not *"should there be a Crossing Register?"* It is:**

   > **Has the architecture demonstrated a universal authority-crossing object?**

   Only if the answer is yes does register design begin. Founder decision procedure, 2026-08-06
   (⛔ a procedure for ruling, not itself a ruling) — **two questions, in order**:

   | | Question | If no | If yes |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Q1** | Does the crossing **create, revoke, or alter authority**? | it is an **implementation event** — stop | proceed to Q2 |
   | **Q2** | Can that authority change be **reconstructed truthfully from existing records**? | the register (or an equivalent event substrate) **ceases to be a convenience** — it is the mechanism that makes constitutional history recoverable | a separate register is **unnecessary**; existing substrate already preserves the history |

   ⭐⭐⭐ **Refinement — not every crossing changes authority (founder, verbatim):**

   > A universal Crossing Register exists only if every recorded crossing represents a distinct
   > authority event that must remain historically recoverable. Relationship-only crossings may be
   > governed within their own field until evidence demonstrates a universal constitutional object.

   Some crossings change **relationship** without changing **authority** — e.g. a practitioner
   opening a private workbench for the first time establishes a new relationship with the system
   without yet altering any authority-bearing object. ⛔ This is what prevents premature
   universalization of an object discovered first in Writer MAIA.

   ⛔⛔ **Implementation cost informs the ruling; it never decides it.** §6.2 (`offered` is not a
   general recorded event) establishes that **no general event substrate for this class of
   transition currently exists.** That fact belongs *in* the ruling so its consequences are
   understood before deciding — **yes** authorizes not merely a document but a new class of
   recoverable event; **no** means the scattered per-feature fields suffice because no universal
   constitutional history is being claimed. It must not be read as an argument for either answer.

   ### Q2 measurement — Writer ↔ Personal (n = 1)

   **Status:** Measured, 2026-08-06 (read-only, branch `feature/labtools-redesign`).
   **Result: ⛔ NOT AN INSTANCE.** ⚠️ This is *not* "Q2 failed" — the chosen case is not an instance
   of the phenomenon.

   **Findings**

   - **No authority-crossing event exists.** The Manuscript Room's recognition gesture
     (`POST /api/sovereign/manuscripts/[id]/keeps`) writes to **`manuscript_keeps`**
     (`member_id, manuscript_id, section_id, verbatim_text`) — a table *inside* the writing field.
     It does not touch member memory.
   - **No persistence path exists** from manuscript recognition into member memory.
     `member_memory_atoms.source_type` is a CHECK constraint over `idea · idea_block · journal ·
     dream · reflection · decision · change · session_excerpt · spontaneous`
     (+ `practitioner_observation`, which `keepSource` refuses). **There is no manuscript member.**
   - **Containment is achieved structurally by the schema**, not by runtime authorization. There is
     no guard on a path; there is no path. (Consistent with the containment appendix: Canvas is
     still `localStorage`; Phase C is the named first-persistence moment.)
   - **Therefore Q1 does not fire**, and **Q2 is not applicable for this case.**

   **Constitutional consequence.** Writer ↔ Personal cannot presently serve as evidence for *or*
   against a universal authority-crossing object, because the architecture contains no crossing to
   evaluate.

   ⭐⭐⭐ **Negative evidence is still evidence.** Demonstrating that an apparently plausible n=1 case
   is *not* an instance prevents a constitutional mechanism from being generalized from the wrong
   object. It narrows the search space without prematurely answering the constitutional question.

   #### Evidence relocation — ⛔ observations, NOT conclusions

   1. ⚠️⚠️ **The term "crossing" is already in use for the member ↔ practitioner axis.**
      `member_memory_atoms.crossing_allowed` with hard constraint `crossing_must_be_false`, mirrored
      onto `case_memories` (`20260626000002`). ⛔ If §9 later speaks of "crossings" unqualified, it
      risks naming **two different constitutional objects** — an R1-class collision (one name,
      several referents).
   2. **Existing evidence relevant to universality lies on the member ↔ practitioner axis, not the
      Writer ↔ Personal axis.** That migration's own comment already anticipates the mechanism:
      *"…this constraint must be dropped and replaced with a more nuanced mechanism (e.g., an
      `authorized_crossings` table with a separate write path)."*

      ⛔⛔ This says **the next evidential candidate has changed.** It does **NOT** say *"therefore
      the universal crossing object is member ↔ practitioner"* — that would overstep the evidence.

   **Scope of the measurement.** Production data not verified · the practitioner axis not inspected
   beyond the two migrations named · no generalization beyond Writer performed.

   **Founder lean, 2026-08-06 — recorded verbatim, ⛔ NOT a ruling.** A two-tier formulation:
   - *The principle is constitutional:* **crossings between centers of continuity require explicit
     authorization.** Platform-wide, inherited by every field.
   - *Each concrete crossing* (Writer ↔ Personal, Practitioner ↔ Personal, Practice ↔ Team, …)
     **must still be ruled on its own merits.**

   This gives one shared constitutional idea without importing Writer Studio's specific
   implementation into every field by proximity — which is precisely what
   `WRITER_MAIA_FIELD_CONTAINMENT_2026-08-06.md` §5 refuses.

   ⚠️ **Structural consequence to resolve before ratifying this shape.** "Each crossing must be
   ruled separately" needs somewhere the ruled crossings are *recorded, with state* — otherwise the
   requirement has no enforcing surface and becomes a description rather than a control (see
   `feedback_documentation_as_false_control_surface`).

   **Founder direction, 2026-08-06 (⛔ not a ruling; recorded so the reading pass has it):** that
   object is a **Crossing Register**, and it is a **separate canonical document**, not a section of
   this one. The relationship:

   | Document | Establishes |
   |---|---|
   | **Formation Architecture** (this doc) | what a MAIA *is* — and *why* crossings matter |
   | **Crossing Register** (not yet authored) | *which* crossings are actually governed |

   ⭐ **This document must point toward the register without depending on it.** §6 states the
   principle and stays complete on its own; the register carries the rows. Kept separate, both stay
   maintainable as fields multiply.

   Register shape (founder-supplied): columns *From · To · State · Gesture · Authority · Notes*.
   **State is the load-bearing field, and it has exactly three values —
   `Unruled` · `Permitted` · `Forbidden`.**

   - ⭐⭐⭐ **A crossing defaults to `Unruled`, never to allowed.** That default is what makes the
     register constitutional rather than descriptive.
   - **The gesture is a property of the permission, not part of the state.** Different permitted
     crossings may require different gestures; `Permitted` must not be spelled
     "permitted-with-gesture" or the vocabulary collapses the two.

   At authorship, Writer ↔ Personal (bidirectional, `Permitted`, authority: the Writer Containment
   Law) is the only non-`Unruled` pair that exists.

   ⭐⭐⭐ **Dependency direction — stated so it cannot be reversed (founder, 2026-08-06):**

   > The Formation Architecture may define entities that can participate in crossings. The Crossing
   > Register never defines entities; it only governs relationships among entities already
   > established elsewhere.

   ⛔⛔ **Governing constraint (founder, 2026-08-06 — verbatim). The register must not become a back
   door for ontology creation:**

   > A register entry may reference only centers of continuity already established by the Formation
   > Architecture or another named authority. Listing an endpoint does not constitute or ratify that
   > endpoint. Where an endpoint lacks an established identity, the proposed crossing remains
   > structurally ineligible rather than merely unruled.

   Consequence: **four operational conditions, still only three constitutional states.**

   | Condition | Meaning | Layer |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Ineligible** | one or both endpoints are not established centers of continuity | validation |
   | **Unruled** | both endpoints exist; no crossing ruling has been made | constitutional state |
   | **Permitted** | a named authority permits it under specified conditions and gesture | constitutional state |
   | **Forbidden** | a named authority prohibits it | constitutional state |

   ⛔ `Ineligible` is **not** a fourth state. It belongs to validation, not constitutional
   disposition — otherwise the register conflates *"this relationship has been prohibited"* with
   *"this relationship is not yet well-formed enough to rule."*

   Worked example (founder): `Team → Practitioner` is **ineligible**, not unruled. "Team" is not an
   established center of continuity in §2, and might eventually be any of — a center of continuity ·
   a governed scope · a relationship container · an organizational actor · merely a permissions
   boundary. **The register cannot decide which.** That decision is a §2 identity question.

   Field semantics, settled: **State** = whether a crossing is governed · **Gesture** = how a
   permitted crossing occurs · **Authority** = who ruled it · **Identity definitions** = whether the
   endpoints are valid at all.
