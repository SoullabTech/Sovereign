# MAIA Canon — Claim State Authority

**Status:** Canon. Promoted 2026-09-05 (steward ruling). Governs *movement* between claim states.
**Kin:** `MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` (sibling — defines the states), `MAIA_OATH.md`, `MAIA_SOVEREIGNTY_INVARIANTS.md`, `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`.
**Adds no classifier.** Live / Designed / Vision, Center of Gravity, and Failure Test are untouched.

---

## The governing sentence

> **Kelly governs what Soullab chooses to claim. Predeclared criteria and attributable evidence govern the highest state that claim may occupy.**

Two authorities, deliberately separate.

---

## Why this exists

`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` defines the truth condition: **Live** means verified in production, answerable to a runtime fact, and the marketing word may not outrun the engineering state. It defines what the states *mean*.

It does not say **who may move a claim between them, and what constrains that movement.** That is the hole this canon closes. It is a governance rule, not a fourth instrument.

The drift it protects against is specific and quiet: a claim reaching **Live** because someone with authority believed it, authored it, or wanted it — rather than because evidence established it. That drift does not look like dishonesty from the inside. It looks like confidence.

---

## The asymmetry

This is the load-bearing structure, and it is not symmetric:

- **Evidence can license a claim. It cannot compel Soullab to make it.**
- **Kelly may withhold a claim that is demonstrably Live.** Restraint is always available.
- **Kelly may not declare a claim Live when the required evidence does not establish it.** Authority does not manufacture warrant.

Discretion runs downward only. This mirrors `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`: authority may not manufacture higher-order state.

---

## The rules

### 1. Claim authority
The steward decides what Soullab is willing to represent publicly. Whether to speak at all, in what words, on what surface, at what moment — that is representational judgment and it belongs to the steward.

### 2. State authority
`Live / Designed / Vision` is constrained by **evidence against declared criteria** — not by preference, confidence, authorship, urgency, or intent. The state is a finding, not a decision.

### 3. No discretionary promotion
Absence of required evidence cannot be overridden — not by founder, developer, operator, reviewer, or document prose. A sentence asserting a state does not create it. Neither does a merged PR, an approved plan, or a well-written record.

### 4. Downgrade is mandatory when warrant is absent
A claim whose warrant has lapsed, was never established, or has not been re-probed **must be downgraded** — without waiting for a decision to downgrade it. This is the discipline already practiced in the `/accounted-for` source, where un-reprobed runtime claims are downgraded rather than assumed. Downgrade is not a failure event. It is the system working.

### 5. Instrument precedes observation
Criteria are declared **before** the run that tests them. Criteria may not be rewritten after an observation in order to make that observation pass. If the instrument changes materially, **version it and observe again** — the prior run described a different instrument and therefore a different claim.

An instrument whose validity conditions can pass vacuously is worse than no instrument, because it produces a record.

### 6. Evidence provenance travels with movement
Whenever a claim is promoted or downgraded, four things remain recoverable:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | what was measured, bound to a specific commit or artifact |
| **Instrument** | which instrument, at which version |
| **Observation** | the actual result, including its invalidity conditions |
| **State** | the state the movement produced |

A promotion whose provenance is not recoverable is not a promotion. It is an assertion.

---

## The anti-rule

> **Passing evidence does not itself edit public claims.**

Evidence establishes what state is *warranted*. An **authorized representational act** changes the public record. These are two events, and collapsing them collapses the two authorities back into one.

A green witness does not promote a row. It makes promotion permissible.

---

## Verdicts a movement can produce

Three, not two. The third is the one most often lost:

- **PASS** — the evidence establishes the state. Promotion is now permissible (not automatic — see the anti-rule).
- **FAIL** — the evidence establishes that the state is not warranted. Downgrade under rule 4.
- **INVALID** — the instrument did not validly reach its subject. **This licenses nothing in either direction.** It is not a soft PASS and not a FAIL. The subject changed mid-measurement, a guard leaked, the surface was not reached — the run has no finding, and the claim keeps whatever state it already held.

Treating INVALID as PASS is the single most likely way this canon gets defeated in practice.

---

## Relationship to the sibling canon

`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` answers: *what must be true for this statement to remain honest?*

This canon answers: *who may move it, and what constrains the move?*

Neither replaces the other. The instruments classify; this governs. Its maturity test is the same as its sibling's: **it is working when it produces decisions, not distinctions.** Resist growing it.

---

## The line

> **Authority decides whether we speak. Evidence decides what we are entitled to say.**
