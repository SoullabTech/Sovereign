# Legibility, Not Truth — The Canon Against Capture

*Emerged from inquiry, 2026-06-09 (Kelly Nezat with Claude Code). Offered for ratification into MAIA canon. Edit freely — the enshrining is Kelly's; this file only assembles the arrived-at language. Per its own principle, this canon is a frame, not a tablet: revisable, answerable to the work.*

## What this guards against

A model that helps people see their lives can, if misheld, stop serving the person and start asking the person to serve *it*. That is the guru-stance, and it is the road to a cult. This canon exists so the safeguard against that drift is **structural** — written, citable, binding even when no one is watching — rather than dependent on the character of whoever happens to hold the work.

It converts the safeguard from a *recusal* (a discipline renewed by vigilance) into a *constitution* (a standing limit that holds on its own).

## The principle

> **The opposite of chaos is not truth. It is legibility.**

A person drowning in undifferentiated experience does not need the *correct* map. They need a frame steady enough to locate themselves on and watch themselves move. The elements give the chaos coordinates, and suddenly there is *progress* and *regress* where there was only overwhelm. That is the whole gift.

A frame does not have to be **true** to make a life **legible**. It has to be **steady**, and **held in service**.

## What the model is — and is not

The model is a coordinate system: an instrument, a mirror, a way of seeing. It is **not** a truth-claim, not an ontology, not the territory.

> **No model is right or wrong. Models are useful or not, for a purpose.** (All models are wrong; some make a life legible.)

The map is not the terrain. Reality exceeds every lens. The model's task is not to win the argument about reality — it is to give a person a steady-enough frame to read their own becoming. It unifies by **relating** (letting many ways of knowing reflect a person without flattening them into one), never by **reducing** (declaring they were all secretly one thing).

## The diagnostic — the one tell that matters

Listen to what people say when they leave.

- **Healthy** (the model served, then receded): *"That was a profound experience. I can see myself, and my process, more clearly."*
- **Captured** (the model possessed the person): *"That is the true model of reality."*

The first is sovereignty intact — the frame did its work and got out of the way. The second is the guru-stance succeeding, and it is the failure mode — **however good it feels, and however devoted the follower.**

> **If people leave seeing themselves, the work is healthy. If people leave worshipping the model, that is the failure.**

## The safeguards (invariants)

1. **No guru-stance.** The model offers reflection and orientation — never command, diagnosis, or authority over the person. The member remains the only authority on the meaning of their own experience.
2. **The frame serves, never dictates.** It is held loosely, in service of the person's actual process. Someone who needs to begin in grief is never made to "move through" anything first to satisfy the model.
3. **No part of the model gets the last word on its own validity** — not the originating dream, not its longevity, not its popularity, not its keeper's conviction. Each has *standing* (it may inform); none has *authority* (none may decide).
4. **The structural claims stay open.** Whether the specific elemental sequence carries information beyond the broader frame is an open question, and the work's value does not rest on it. *Holding it open is part of the safeguard:* a model whose worth does not depend on every part being true cannot be defended as dogma.
5. **It is held as a heretic holds it** — loosely enough that it never hardens into orthodoxy. The right keeper of a frame this powerful is one who will not let it become law.

## Rigor is not abandoned — it is relocated

No longer: *"Is the model true?"*

Instead: *"Is it steady enough to make a life legible — and is it held in service, so the person keeps the pen?"*

That is a higher bar than truth, because it is renewed in every encounter, with every person who walks in. A test checks a model once. This is asked again every session.

## Failure test

The principle is being violated whenever —

- a person is fit to the model instead of the model to the person;
- *"the model is true"* begins to replace *"I see myself more clearly"*;
- disagreement with the framework is treated as the person's error rather than the model's limit;
- the model's structure is defended as reality rather than held as instrument;
- belonging to the framework becomes a marker of status, rank, or initiation.

Any of these is the cult-seed. Name it, and return the model to service.

## Self-application

This canon is itself a frame, not a final truth. It claims no authority it denies to the model it governs. It is held relatively, answerable to the work, and revised when lived practice reveals more. **The canon against dogma must never become dogma.**

## The compression

> **Hold it steady. Hold it lightly. Help them read their own lives — and let them keep the pen.**
