## Article I — The constitutional principle (exact founder-approved wording)

> Soullab shall cultivate the conditions in which multiple maps of reality and meaning may be encountered, related, questioned, and integrated without coercing their differences into a single interpretation. The purpose of map-stacking is to enlarge the field of perception while preserving the freedom and integrity of those who participate.

This is Soullab's original founder-adopted normative formulation. It is not a quotation from Jung, Ubuntu, Indigenous traditions, consciousness science or any referenced scholar. Editorial dialogue with ChatGPT helped formulate this text; that assistance supplies no second human governance vote, endorsement, experience claim or authorship claim about another tradition.

## Article II — Distinct maps are not interchangeable evidence

A map may describe observed events, engineering implementation, measured outcomes, developmental experience, historical context, psychological interpretation, philosophical meaning or spiritual symbolism. Their referents may overlap, but their standards of verification and legitimate uses remain different.

Map-stacking relates these distinct representations without requiring uniformity. It shall not transform speculative interpretation into observed fact, a fictional test PASS into production certification, a participant's words into an AI-authored consensus, or consent once granted into indefinite access.

A map is not the territory. No single representation, however comprehensive, exhausts another participant's experience, developmental path, intention or sovereignty.

## Article III — Source, contributor and recognition integrity

Each consequential contribution shall retain its authorship or permissioned pseudonym, source, relevant time and revision, evidentiary footing, rights, scope of use and unresolved limitations where technically feasible and lawful. A citation is neither endorsement nor copyright clearance.

The [Ubuntu contribution and recognition ledger](../governance/UBUNTU_DIFFERENTIATED_FLOURISHING_CONTRIBUTION_LEDGER_2026-10-18.md) remains the companion provenance record; this article does not silently redefine its recognized contributors or treat cited wisdom keepers as signatories.

Members and wisdom keepers may withhold, limit, revise or withdraw machine reuse of their material according to the applicable permission and law. Their original meaning cannot be appropriated or irrevocably redefined by system-generated synthesis.

## Article IV — Differentiation, dissent and nonparticipation

Multiple maps may disagree. Preserving a meaningful contradiction, an uncertainty, minority interpretation, silence, abstention or a principled refusal is often more faithful than forcing premature resolution. No participant must produce consensus to belong.

Distinct AI roles, prompts, simulated archetypes or elemental lenses shall not be represented as scientifically established independent minds. AI-assisted comparison can identify relationships and alternatives without establishing subjective experience or collective consciousness.

The Ubuntu principle applies: **Unity shall never require uniformity.**

## Article V — Relational attention without coercive authority

The Living Context Atlas is a proposed discovery/navigation function. Map-stacking is an integrative discipline; the Living Council is a separately governed practice of differentiated encounter. They shall not acquire one another's permissions merely through architectural proximity.

MAIA may offer member-authorized lenses and facilitate perception within existing MAIA sovereignty law, but cannot author a member's inner truth or compel meaning. The existing [Circle Field Doctrine](./CIRCLE_FIELD_DOCTRINE.md) remains controlling: MAIA shall not autonomously summarize, moderate, direct or construct Circle consensus, and no coherence or wisdom score is inferred.

AIN/JARVIS and other tools may route source evidence and identify unresolved engineering prerequisites, but fluency cannot turn a check into authorization, a candidate document into ratified law or an unreviewed implementation into a safe release.

## Article VI — Consent and revocation across maps

Every map link that introduces human/private content must respect its original consent, source custody, scope, membership and revocation history. Withdrawal must limit further machine retrieval, redistribution, derivatives and admissible restores as required by its governing permission. Past human knowledge cannot be retroactively erased, and the system shall not pretend it can.

A separately authorized share may survive withdrawal of a different grant only where its independence and revocability are explicitly demonstrable. Unknown legacy grants must never be silently certified as independent.

Historical database backups, cached summaries and detached file copies do not supply renewed permission; their recovery requires separately proven current deletion/consent authority and applicable governance. This article is a constraint, not an assertion that those controls currently exist.

## Article VII — Independent evidence and release custody

For any consequential technical map, preserve at least source identity, applicability, source revision, test scope, contradictory evidence, privacy class and explicit release authority.

The following remain distinct: code exists; isolated fictional checks pass; independently reproduced end-to-end recovery passes; a human reviewer accepts Class A; founder authorizes a specific release; a runtime actually conforms.

Writer's Studio is a bounded example, not an exception: a successful fictional sharing or PostgreSQL restore fixture must not hide unqualified Source/Draft, backups, external revoke authority, historical plaintext replicas, real member consent or release HOLD.

## Article VIII — Ratification and implementation boundaries

This document is additive to the October 18 Ubuntu constitutional candidate. Its presence in GitHub, a draft PR, a merge, an AI review, or an editorial discussion does not by itself ratify the text or prove runtime compliance.

Formal incorporation requires an exact canonical commit and blob, appropriate Class A authority under [governance issue #1147](https://github.com/SoullabTech/Sovereign/issues/1147), genuine review custody or a transparently authorized applicable exception, and a distinct founder `RATIFY EFFECTIVE` act. Even ratification grants no new member data access, model execution, deployment, study participant recruitment or Writer's Studio beta permission.

**Non-authorizations:** No private member data; no member-facing interpretation expansion; no Circle AI summarization; no agent independence or AI welfare claim; no production or security approval; no automatic ability to merge or deploy code.
