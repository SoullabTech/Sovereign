## The constitutional distinction (prior, standing)

Soullab Press sits third in a three-layer structure:

1. **MAIA Ideas** — where discoveries are captured.
2. **Creative Fields** — where authentic work is developed without any obligation to publish.
3. **Soullab Press** — where an independent editorial process determines what is ready to become a book.

A work must *cross the threshold* into layer 3; nothing is conscripted into publication. This mirrors the platform's Direction of Authority: authority moves upward through authored experience, never manufactured from above.

## The philosophy

Traditional publishing asks: *how do we produce a book?*
Soullab Press asks: **how do we steward a body of work until it naturally becomes a book?**

Publication is the final editorial act, not the beginning. The process below is stewardship, not production.

## The six phases

### Phase 1 — Editorial Foundation
Nothing is written until the foundation is proven.
- Birth data verified · chart verified · sources documented · method identified · editorial intent established · constitutional questions resolved.
- *Verification is instrumented where possible* (e.g. `scripts/dev-fagan-chart-verify.ts` — registry chart vs live ephemeris, 11/11).

### Phase 2 — Editorial Architecture
Before prose, establish the book itself: reader · purpose · structure · movement · chapter architecture · visual language · recurring literary devices · voice. The book becomes a designed organism before sentences accumulate. (Precedent: the doorway method — chapter doorways as movement phases; Aether as the center, not a phase.)

### Phase 3 — Literary Composition
MAIA is an editorial companion, not a writer. For every chapter: What is the recognition? What experience should the reader have? What image carries it? What evidence grounds it? What language is unnecessary?
**The goal is not generating text. It is discovering the inevitable text.**

### Phase 4 — Editorial Refinement
Distinct passes, each with one responsibility: structural · developmental · literary · factual · astrological verification · tone · rhythm · typography awareness. (Precedent: the *Elemental Alchemy* pass files — phrase pass, invitational-voice pass, long-wave reads, proof pass.)

### Phase 5 — Book Design
Cover · interior · plates · typography · page rhythm · opening pages · closing pages · **colophon**. The physical object becomes part of the meaning. The colophon carries provenance into the permanent artifact — what made this text, who reviewed it, what chart it stands on (a printed edition-of-one cannot hover a caveat; honesty must be designed into the object).

### Phase 6 — Publication Stewardship
Only here does publishing happen: ISBN · Library of Congress · metadata · proof copies · editorial approval · printing · distribution.

## The Book Constitution

Before Chapter One exists, the project receives a one-page constitutional document answering:

- Why does this book deserve to exist?
- What transformation is it serving?
- What evidence must remain inviolate?
- What is imaginative interpretation versus verified fact?
- What editorial principles govern every later decision?
- **Under what conditions should this book *not* be published?**

The constitution is the editorial north star: every later decision — structure, language, imagery, design — returns to it rather than relying on taste or momentum. It is the book-scale form of the platform's claim discipline and failure test.
