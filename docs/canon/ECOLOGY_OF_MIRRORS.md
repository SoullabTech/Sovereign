# Ecology of Mirrors

**Status: CANDIDATE (not ratified).** This document names a framing that generalizes existing ratified canon. It is a *lens* over surfaces of mixed maturity — not a claim that every surface described is Live. It may be cited as a working orientation; it may not be cited as evidence that offering, field-animation, or full-arc completion is operational everywhere. Representation never outruns evidence.

Date drafted: 2026-07-02

## The framing

AIN organizes not around features but around **forms of remembrance**. Each surface is a mirror that returns a different horizon of a life to the person living it, and asks one specific question:

> *What can you recognize about your own life when it is returned to you?*

The surfaces together are an *ecology* — a relational medium in which distinct sovereign centers participate — held by one shared discipline: **surface, don't conclude. The member authors meaning.**

The unifying movement is:

**Life → Evidence → Gathering → Mirror → Recognition → Authorship → Offering**

The completed arc — the part that matters constitutionally — is:

**remembrance → recognition → authorship → offering**

It ends in **offering outward**, not in inward self-understanding. The mirror does not exist so the member can gaze at themselves; it exists so a life can move *outward into relationship, work, and world*. A mirror that only returns the self to the self, and never opens toward offering, is incomplete by this canon's own standard.

### The trust layers before Recognition

The passage from Evidence to Mirror is not a single step. It decomposes into three distinct layers of trust, each closing a loophole the previous one leaves open:

**Life → Source provenance → Selection warrant → Inspectability → Recognition → Authorship → Offering**

- **Source provenance** — *where did this come from?* The material is traceable to its origin (a Keep, a journal, an encounter), not fabricated or inferred.
- **Selection warrant** — *why is this here?* When only some material is surfaced, the criterion and the denominator are disclosed (discipline #6). Provenance and warrant answer different epistemic questions: provenance establishes *origin*, warrant establishes *why this item earned its place in the gathering*. Source provenance alone does not answer why *these* items and not others — that is a warrant, not a lineage.
- **Inspectability** — *can I inspect that claim myself?* The member can traverse from the surfaced subset to the full pool and see the selection criterion. A disclosed warrant is still only the system's assertion of why these; inspectability is what lets the member audit that assertion. Disclosure alone does not prove the denominator is real; only inspection does.

**Recognition is gated behind all three.** The member must not be asked to recognize meaning until they can understand what has been brought before them and why. Recognition offered before inspectability is recognition of a curated view whose warrant the member cannot check — which is precisely the quiet authorship this canon refuses. Ordering matters: inspectability precedes recognition, always.

## The discipline that makes this true

This framing is only faithful because every mirror is bound by the same constraints:

1. **Surface, don't conclude.** The system returns lived material faithfully. It does not interpret, diagnose, score, or author significance. Recognition is the member's act, not the system's output.
2. **The member authors meaning.** Authorship of significance stays with the human. The system may draft *from material the member selected*; it never synthesizes meaning the member did not place.
3. **Authority moves upward only.** Encounter → Reflection → Recognition → Living Field → Developmental Ecology. No mirror manufactures higher-order meaning or skips a layer. (See `CONSTITUTIONAL_DIRECTION_OF_AUTHORITY.md`.)
4. **Recognition is not engineered.** Felt recognition is not a target to maximize. Mirrors stay quiet when nothing is placed. (See `RECOGNITION_INTEGRITY.md` / Invariant 16.)
5. **Offering is the member's initiated act.** Sharing outward is a sovereign choice, never a system-pushed surfacing. Consent (the authorship boundary) is distinct from ecology-participation (what the member chooses to share).
6. **A gathering may never hide its denominator.** When a mirror gathers evidence, the *selection* is itself an act with authorial weight. A mirror that surfaces *some* of what it holds must make legible how much it drew from, and on what basis — it may not silently present a curated subset as "what is gathering." Selection without a disclosed warrant is quiet authorship: the system shaping meaning through what it chose to omit, while appearing only to reflect. This is the **selection-warrant test** — the gathering step is faithful only when its denominator is visible: the member can see *drawn from N*, *why these*, and *what a wider view would include*. (Concrete instance: the Living Field refine step surfaces top-scored Keeps by affinity; it must disclose the pool it selected from, not present the subset as the whole of what has gathered.)

   **The denominator must remain inspectable, not merely disclosed.** A count the member cannot open is just another rhetorical number — "129" performing transparency without offering it. The warrant for a gathering is legible only when the member can traverse from the surfaced subset to the full set it was drawn from:

   > **10 of 129 kept passages matched this gathering.**
   > **Why these?** — kept by you · highest affinity to this dimension · created between…
   > **View all 129 →**

   The final line — *view all* — is load-bearing. It turns the denominator from a figure the system performs into an object the member can inspect. The constitutional requirement is implementation-agnostic: **the member must be able to inspect the warrant for the gathering.** Whether the warrant comes tomorrow from affinity, tags, semantic search, or chronology does not matter; the invariant survives the implementation.

   **Inspectability is not reproducibility — do not conflate them.**
   - *Inspectability* — the member can view the broader set and the selection criterion. This is **non-negotiable**; every gathering must be inspectable.
   - *Reproducibility* — given the same criterion and corpus, the member or another observer could arrive at the same gathered set. This is a **stronger, conditional** guarantee: it applies when selection uses deterministic rules (e.g. affinity-score thresholds), and it *should* hold there. But when a gathering incorporates semantic retrieval or learned ranking, full reproducibility may not be practical.

   The line the canon draws: reproducibility is desirable and expected wherever selection is deterministic; inspectability is required everywhere, without exception. A gathering may be non-reproducible and still faithful; a gathering that is non-inspectable is quiet authorship regardless of how it selected.

## What is NOT yet true (guardrail against inflation)

This section is load-bearing. The framing may not be used to assert any of the following:

- **Not every mirror completes the arc.** Offering — the outward turn — is fully wired in three surfaces (Memory, Practice Field, Book Studio — Elemental Alchemy), partial in four (MAIA, Living Field, Vision Studio, Book Studio — Legacy Writing), and absent in Relationship Space. The arc `remembrance → recognition → authorship → offering` is **not uniformly Live across the ecology today.** In particular, the Living-Field-to-legacy pipeline in Book Studio is Designed, not built — a member's gathered life does not yet flow into authored legacy.
- **The ecology is not a unified center.** "Ecology" means *relational medium in which distinct sovereign centers participate* — not a single environment where MAIA orchestrates pattern-synthesis or aggregates individual recognitions into field-level profiles. Aggregation-as-mirror is not authorized by this document.
- **Fields are not merely reflective surfaces.** A Living Field is an ontological entity with a developmental trajectory, memory, and relationships — it is a *participant in its own history*, not only a mirror. This framing describes the mirror *operation*; it does not yet articulate **field-level animation** — how fields develop through participation, how practices mature through embodiment, how relationships evolve independently of individual agency, or how culture emerges laterally. That integration is **Vision**, not Live.
- **Mirroring does not animate fields.** Fields become alive through what members bring and how they relate *to each other*. The system holds the architecture that makes that possible; it does not animate it. "By mirroring better, the system makes fields more alive" is a prohibited inference.
- **Remembrance is fidelity, not curation.** Remembrance means faithful return of what was actually lived (anamnesis), not selection, editing, or aestheticizing of moments designed to feel significant.
- **Relationship-level authorship and outward flow are not yet built.** Relationship Space reflects material back to both members but lacks explicit authorship surfaces and any outward pathway; relationship insight does not yet flow to larger work, shared learning, or world.

## The sentence

The aspirational homepage sentence — *"AIN is a living ecology of mirrors"* — is classified **Vision**, not Live, under the Marketing Claim Discipline. The **mirror** function is Live across all seven surfaces. The word **"living"** asserts field-level animation that is not yet operational. It becomes sayable when: (a) offering completes across the ecology, not just three surfaces; and (b) field-level development (participation, practice maturation, lateral transmission) has a measurable substrate underneath it. Until then the honest form is descriptive, not ontological — e.g. *"AIN is an ecology of mirrors: each surface returns a different horizon of your life so you may recognize and author it."*

## Recorded architectural debt

This canon records a recognized obligation; it does not open an implementation task. The distinction is deliberate: **canon records an obligation that has been recognized; a task represents an intention to satisfy that obligation in implementation.** The debt below is a concrete, bounded gap that need not become a work item until it competes for engineering time. It is stated in implementation-neutral language so it survives whatever retrieval mechanism eventually satisfies it.

> **Living Field gatherings** currently satisfy retrieval but do not yet satisfy the full constitutional disclosure chain.
>
> Missing capabilities:
> - **Source provenance** — *where did this come from?*
> - **Selection warrant** — *why was this selected?*
> - **Inspectability** — *can I verify that explanation?*
>
> Recognition of gathered material remains gated until these are available to the member.
>
> Future retrieval mechanisms (semantic search, learned retrieval, affinity, etc.) inherit the same obligations — **without** requiring reproducibility where reproducibility is not technically meaningful.

These three are genuinely independent responsibilities, each independently testable: a system can have perfect provenance while making an opaque selection, or explain a selection without permitting inspection. They do not collapse into one another.
