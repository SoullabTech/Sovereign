# Soullab Platform Identity Canon
**Ratified:** 2026-09-28  
**Standing:** Founder-directed canon

## Identity hierarchy

- **Soullab** is the member-facing platform and the whole lived environment.
- **AIN OS** is the sovereign intelligence architecture beneath Soullab.
- **MAIA** is Soullab's relational intelligence, encountered throughout the platform.
- **Home** is the member's orienting place inside Soullab.
- **Facets** are distinct places of practice and inquiry: Journal, Dream, Writing, Relationships, Astrology, Divination, Becoming, and successors.

## Governing sentence

> **Soullab is the place. AIN OS is the architecture. MAIA is the relational intelligence that meets you there.**

This hierarchy prevents MAIA from being asked to carry three incompatible meanings at once: company, platform, and conversational presence.

## Entry and route law

The public website may invite a visitor to **Enter Soullab**.

That action enters canonical **`/home`**. It does not deep-link a visitor into the MAIA conversation room.

Bare `/maia` is a compatibility threshold, not the live encounter. It orients old bookmarks and direct visitors back into Soullab Home. The live direct MAIA relationship surface is `/maia/encounter`, entered deliberately from Home or another explicit member gesture.

The experience law is:

> **Enter Soullab → arrive Home → move through facets → deliberately enter `/maia/encounter` when MAIA is wanted → return Home without losing the field.**

Old MAIA runtime substrate may remain where technically useful. Old MAIA/House presentation does not retain product-identity authority.

## Landing-page law

The landing page should answer, in order:

1. **What is this?** — Soullab is a living platform for inner life, relationship, creativity, and becoming.
2. **What can I do here?** — show the member-facing field and its principal facets.
3. **Who or what is MAIA?** — the relational intelligence moving through Soullab.
4. **Why is this different?** — sovereignty, consent, continuity, provenance, non-manipulation, source discipline.
5. **What is live versus emerging?** — maintain explicit production / architected / research distinctions.
6. **Why trust Soullab?** — concrete governance and human-authority commitments, not mystical or technical inflation.
7. **What should I do next?** — one dominant member CTA: **Enter Soullab**.

The page may preserve depth and poetry, but clarity precedes ontology. A first-time visitor should not need to understand AIN OS, Spiralogic, or internal programme language before understanding the product.
