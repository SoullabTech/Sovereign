# Levels of Regard · The Environment Axiom — CANON-CANDIDATE

> **Status: CANON-CANDIDATE — not ratified.** Two refinements Kelly directed locked here
> before they drift (2026-06-23). Both are **constrictive consolidations** of canon that
> already exists (the Attention Doctrine, capability/capacity, Sanctuary, the §1.4
> tend-don't-produce guard) — not new foundations. Per the project's promotion discipline
> they are canon-worthy *in form* (they constrain what the system may do and claim) and
> earn **ratification only by rejecting a real design at real cost** — not by declaration.
> Held as constriction (they *remove* license), never as new primitives to display. They
> are unusually ready precisely because they *organize* existing canon rather than add to
> it.

## I. The levels of regard

| Constitutional | Architectural | Human |
|----------------|---------------|-------|
| **Attention**  | **Hospitality** | **Inhabitation** |

- **Attention** — the governing ethic: *the system owes the person a quality of regard.*
  (= the Attention Doctrine; already canon. This places it, does not add it.)
- **Hospitality** — the architectural expression of that ethic: *the environment is
  prepared without presuming the guest.*
- **Inhabitation** — the human phenomenon that may, or may not, emerge. **Never
  guaranteed or claimed by the system.**

**Arrival** is not a governing concept. It is a **threshold-event** between hospitality
and inhabitation — which is where the several turns of "arrival" naming finally rest:

```
Attention   → the system can practice it
Hospitality → the system can embody it
Arrival     → the person alone can do it
Inhabitation→ the person alone can do it
Relationship→ emerges only through mutual participation
```

### The enforceable core (constriction)

**No level usurps the next — and the system's authority is binary: it terminates at the
Hospitality → Arrival line.**

The transitions are *not* a uniform gradient; they are of different kinds:

- **Attention → Hospitality** — ethic → enactment (both system-side; not an agency handoff)
- **Hospitality → Arrival** — **the decisive system → person boundary**
- **Arrival → Inhabitation** — a deepening *within* the person (event → sustained quality)
- **Inhabitation → Relationship** — solo → mutual

The one that does the constitutional work is **Hospitality → Arrival.** The system's reach
extends exactly to hospitality and **stops.** Below that line it may only *continue*
practicing attention and hospitality — tending conditions — and may never **perform**
arrival, inhabitation, or relationship, nor **claim** them as its achievement. *"We
created an inhabiting experience"* and *"we onboarded them into belonging"* are claims
across a boundary the system cannot cross. The architecture's entire job lives **above**
the line; its discipline is never to act as if it lives below it.

### Where the threshold falls: the first irreversible commitment of attention

The Hospitality → Arrival boundary is **not a fixed point in the sequence** — not "the
route," not "the first sentence." It is wherever the person makes the **first irreversible
commitment of attention**: the first moment after which *leaving is a departure, not a
non-event.* (A person can open a page and leave in two seconds; nothing has begun. The
route is opened; the threshold is not crossed.) Environments place it differently — book:
entry-route → reading; conversation: the first real exchange; Sanctuary: the first act of
entrusted memory — but each has one. The reusable question: **"Where is the first
irreversible commitment of attention?"**

Two precisions hold the sovereignty line:

- **"Irreversible" ≠ un-undoable.** The person can always leave. It means the first
  commitment whose abandonment now *costs* them something — which is how you locate it
  ("where does leaving start to cost?").
- **Offered ≠ crossed.** The architecture sets where it *offers* the threshold (where it
  implicitly says *stay with me*); only the person's report reveals where they *actually*
  committed. The architecture designs the offer; it never claims to know the crossing.
  (Offered = structural/predicted evidence; crossed = observed — §IV.) This is **arrival
  restated**: the crossing is the person's.

Around the threshold, hospitality wears **two faces**, and the sins differ — but both faces
are hospitality under the one attention ethic; only the threshold between them belongs to
the person:

- **Before — invitation** (the drawing face): prepare without presuming. Sin = **grabbing**
  (presuming a commitment not yet given — the funnel).
- **After — holding** (the sustaining face): sustain without betraying. Sin = **betraying**
  (interrupting or exploiting a commitment freely given — the front-matter wall hitting the
  reader who already stayed).

## II. The environment axiom

> **An environment does not produce a human capacity. It protects the conditions under
> which that capacity can be exercised.**
>
> Compressed: **protect conditions rather than produce persons.**

It scales without strain:

- a **reading** environment protects the conditions for reading
- a **conversational** environment, for conversation
- a **remembering** environment, for remembering
- a **learning** environment, for learning
- a **sanctuary**, for sovereignty

This is the **root** of which "tools vs. environments" is downstream: an environment is
*not-a-tool* precisely because it protects conditions rather than produces outcomes. It is
the **compression** of a family already in the work — capability/capacity,
clearing-not-production, the §1.4 tend-don't-produce guard, return-agency-as-unobstruction,
and the Sanctuary invariant — into their common root, now stated as the success criterion
for *what the system should be.*

### Three riders (so the axiom does not drift into its failure modes)

1. **It governs *becoming*, not *crisis*.** Protecting a person in acute danger is itself
   protecting the conditions of their continued agency — not producing a person. The axiom
   **never** licenses non-intervention where a duty of care is owed. (Soullab Principle:
   *discernment includes safety* → a trusted human, not symbolism.)
2. **"Protect conditions" is generous preparation, not pure non-interference.** Hospitality
   is *prepared* **and** *without presuming* — both halves. An environment that only ever
   *refrains* becomes an empty room; withholding the substance to force self-reliance is
   its own paternalism, and crueler. The book must be *there*, well-set; the reception
   moment actively *invites* discernment. Prepare generously; refrain from presuming.
3. **Produce the environment; never the inhabitant.** The system produces a great deal —
   artifacts, environments, conditions, the reading room itself. The line is not "produce
   nothing." It is: never produce the *person's* outcome — their meaning, growth,
   belonging, becoming. Produce the place; never the response to it.

## III. Informational truth and experiential truth

Two truthful orders coexist, and **neither distorts the other.** A book's copyright, ISBNs,
and disclaimer are **bibliographic (informational) truth** — they must exist. The order in
which a reader meets the book is **experiential truth.** Moving the publication apparatus
out of the primary attentional stream is *not hiding information* — it recognizes that the
two orders need not share a sequence. The pattern recurs:

- a profile's factual **completeness** ≠ the order a **relationship** unfolds
- a memory **archive** ≠ the order **remembering** becomes meaningful (the
  garden-not-archive distinction, generalized)
- a portrait's interpretive **depth** ≠ the order someone **encounters** it (the reception
  moment, generalized)

**Rider (the sovereignty guard):** experiential ordering governs *what surfaces first* —
**never what is reachable at all.** The informational order must stay *accessible* (a click
away, never deleted or withheld). The person's right to the complete record — especially
their own data — is absolute; experiential sequencing sets only the default surfacing.
"Experiential truth" must never become a license to make the informational order
unreachable.

## IV. The evidentiary ladder — when is an experiential claim earned?

Each level licenses **exactly one** claim, and no more:

| Evidence | Permitted claim |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Structural** | "The conditions changed." |
| **Predicted** | "We expect this to affect experience." |
| **Observed** | "Readers *reported* a change." |
| **Convergent practice** | "Across readers and contexts, similar shifts recur." |

What **no** level permits: *"The experience was better."* Note the restraint even at
**Observed** — it licenses "readers *reported*," not "the experience changed": a report is
*of* experience, never the experience itself (the first-person-authority boundary).

- **3 → 4 is the jump from *report* to *reliable attribution*.** A single observed report
  cannot isolate whether the *change* (vs. mood, content, expectation) caused it. Only
  repetition *across varied readers, contexts, and works* defeats the confounds — so level
  4 requires **variation, not count.**
- **Level 4 belongs to practice, not design — and to no single project.** A project can
  reach **Observed** on its own; **Convergent practice is earned communally and over time,
  and can never be *declared* by the builder.** (Validity-vs-legitimacy at the evidentiary
  scale: the project establishes validity up to Observed; confidence is earned in the
  world.) This is the finer-grained, experiential form of the Live/Designed/Vision claim
  discipline (`MARKETING_CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md`).

## The through-line

All of this is one thing: **a method for preventing the system from overclaiming.** The
architecture may change conditions, form hypotheses, and learn from reports — but it
**never claims possession of another person's experience.** That restraint is the
practical, evidentiary face of the sovereignty principle: *"never occupy the meaning seat,"*
extended to *never claim the person's experience.* Threshold (the commitment is the
person's), two truths (the experiential order never claims to have produced the meaning),
and the evidentiary ladder (no claim of "better") are three expressions of the one boundary.

## Register

Both refinements are canon-**organizing**, not canon-**adding**: they consolidate the
Attention Doctrine, capability/capacity, Sanctuary, and tend-don't-produce into their
load-bearing shape. Their value is **constriction** — they remove the system's license to
claim or produce the human levels. **Ratification path:** promotion into the Oath/Canon
proper is an explicit, separate act, earned the day either one rejects a real design at
real cost. Until then they are candidates: durable, precise, and not yet authoritative.
