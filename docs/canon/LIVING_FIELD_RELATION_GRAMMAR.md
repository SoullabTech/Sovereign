# Living Field Relation Grammar

**Programme:** JARVIS-VISUAL-FIELD-01  
**Founder authorization:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** Canonical relational law  
**Scope:** relation kinds, visual behavior, trust, productive tension, and shared meaning

## Governing proposition

> **A relation is not merely a line. It has behavior, standing, context, history, and a right to remain unresolved.**

Relations are first-class semantic objects.

They may be inspected, revised, contextualized, contradicted, and eventually become loci of inquiry with MAIA.
## RELATION-KIND-LAW-01

The field may distinguish at minimum:
- **resonance** — two loci amplify or echo one another;
- **support** — one enables, grounds, or sustains another;
- **qualification** — one changes how another should be understood;
- **constraint** — one limits or protects the expression of another;
- **tension** — connected loci exert competing demands;
- **contradiction** — current claims cannot both stand under the same framing;
- **transformation** — one relation changes the meaning or state of another locus;
- **contextual** — a relation holds only under an explicit context.

No relation kind is inherently more important or more true than another.
## RELATION-VISUAL-GRAMMAR-LAW-01

Visual behavior should express relation kind without requiring a legend for basic comprehension.

Guidance:
- resonance → softer / more permeable / lightly pulsing;
- support → calm continuous connection;
- qualification → articulated or segmented connection;
- constraint → structured / measured spacing;
- tension → connection preserved while distance is also preserved;
- contradiction → visible discontinuity or opposing structure without fake resolution;
- transformation → directional change cue;
- contextual → relation carries an explicit context marker at deeper LOD.

Color alone must not carry relation type.

At wide scale, relations may bundle into bridges.
At close scale, relation type differentiates.
## RELATION-TRUST-SURFACE-LAW-01

At sufficient semantic depth, every inspectable relation must expose:
- source and target;
- relation kind;
- relation verb;
- rationale;
- standing;
- context;
- provenance;
- revisability;
- temporal standing when available;
- counterevidence or unresolved qualification when available.

No numeric confidence score is required.

> **Trust comes from inspectable standing, not visual certainty.**

Attention, dwell time, and spatial proximity never raise relation standing.
## PRODUCTIVE-CONFLICT-PHYSICS-LAW-01

> **Conflict may shape geometry without being converted into resolution.**

A tension relation may:
- remain visibly connected;
- preserve greater distance;
- carry a taut visual grammar;
- activate a question;
- remain unresolved indefinitely.

The field must not automatically synthesize competing relations.

A contradiction may remain a contradiction until context, evidence, or member-authored revision changes its standing.
## SHARED-MEANING-INTERSTITIAL-LAW-01

> **A candidate shared meaning belongs first to the relation between loci, not to either locus alone.**

When supported by explicit relation metadata, deeper LOD may reveal an interstitial candidate meaning.

It must:
- remain labeled as candidate unless recognized;
- retain its constituent nodes and relation;
- remain reversible;
- never replace the source loci;
- never gain standing from attention alone.

A candidate shared meaning may later become an explorable field only after recursive-depth requirements are met.
## R2C witness corpus law

R2C uses a controlled prototype relation corpus.

Any new relation introduced solely to prove the grammar must be:
- explicitly marked prototype;
- non-persistent;
- non-member-derived;
- inspectably sourced to the R2C witness.

The prototype may demonstrate productive tension without implying that the relation is a truth about a member.

## Compact constitution

> **Connections can support, resonate, constrain, qualify, transform, or conflict.**
>
> **The field holds the relation honestly before it interprets the relation deeply.**
>
> **Shared meaning may emerge between things without erasing either thing.**
