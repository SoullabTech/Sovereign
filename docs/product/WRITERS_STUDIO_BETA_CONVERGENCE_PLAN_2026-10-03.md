Then run the ordinary member path without developer tools:

6. follow evidence to one exact passage;

8. request an edited version;

> trustworthy editorial companion?

## 8. Small-cohort opening

If the production walk passes:

- activate **5–10 named writers** first;
- keep cohort expansion manual;
- use a direct human support channel;
- review hard-gate incidents immediately;
- do not optimize around engagement metrics.

### Hard-stop incidents

Any of these pauses cohort expansion:

- lost writing;
- silent mutation;
- broken Undo;
- wrong-member / wrong-Work identity;
- false provenance;
- correction ignored or overwritten;
- participant-data / consent breach;
- unrecoverable orientation collapse.

## 9. What beta is allowed to teach us

Beta may determine:

- whether Work/manuscript language is intuitive;
- whether Develop is too dense;
- whether scorecards feel useful or grading;
- whether 5/5 language belongs outside deliberate refinement;
- whether writers understand MAIA's evidence/interpretation boundary;
- whether edits preserve distinctive voice in practice;
- whether latency breaks relational trust;
- whether the writer returns to writing more capable.

These are **beta questions**, not prerequisites to perfect before beta.

## 10. What beta must not be used to excuse

Do not classify these as “learning” if they occur:

- authorship violation;
- silent mutation;
- consent failure;
- provenance fabrication;
- data leakage;
- lost writing;
- broken recovery;
- ignored explicit correction.

Those are defects against the product constitution.
