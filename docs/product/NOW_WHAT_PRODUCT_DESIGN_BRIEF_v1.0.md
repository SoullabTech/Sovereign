## 1. Product Definition

**Now What? is Larry Closs' executive coaching platform that extends the coaching relationship
beyond scheduled conversations.**

It gives CEOs and leaders a private environment to:

- continue their development between coaching sessions
- work through real leadership challenges
- prepare for coaching conversations
- practice new behaviours
- capture insights and commitments
- access Larry's frameworks and programs
- communicate with Larry and facilitators
- see their growth over time

⛔ **It is not:** an AI app · a learning management system · a journal · a CRM · a dashboard · a
document repository.

⭐⭐⭐ **It is the development environment around an executive coaching relationship.**

## 2. The Core Human Loop

⭐⭐⭐ **Every feature must serve this. If a feature does not strengthen this loop, it is secondary.**

```
COACHING SESSION WITH LARRY
            ↓
CEO RETURNS TO DAILY LEADERSHIP LIFE
            ↓
CEO ENCOUNTERS CHALLENGES
            ↓
CEO THINKS / PRACTICES / REFLECTS
            ↓
CEO CAPTURES WHAT MATTERS
            ↓
NEXT COACHING CONVERSATION DEEPENS
            ↓
LEADERSHIP FLOURISHES
```

## 3. The CEO Experience

The CEO must be able to answer immediately —

**"Why am I here?"** → *"This is where my coaching continues between conversations."*

**"What can I do here?"** → five primary activities:

### A. Continue My Coaching — *the central experience*
Conversation with MAIA · preparing thoughts before Larry sessions · reflecting after sessions ·
exploring leadership situations · capturing insights.
**The gesture:** *"Help me think."*

### B. Work on My Leadership — *the CEO's active development*
Leadership challenges · decisions · practices · commitments · goals.
⛔ **Not** *fields · archives · themes.* **Executive language.**

### C. My Programs — *Larry's IP and coaching pathways*
e.g. **Flourishing Leadership** — overview · modules · concepts · exercises · practices ·
resources · conversations.
✅ *"This is the leadership work I am developing through."*
⛔ **Not** *"Here are course units."*

### D. Prepare & Communicate — *the bridge between CEO and coach*
Upcoming sessions · calendar · preparation prompts · questions for Larry · shared reflections ·
messages · facilitator communication.
✅ *"Make my coaching conversations more valuable."*

### E. My Development Journey — *the long arc*
Milestones · insights · practices · changes noticed · commitments completed · personal reflections.
⛔ **Not scores. Not gamification. Not productivity tracking.** ⭐ **A story of development.**

## 4. MAIA's Role

⛔ **MAIA is not the product.**

⭐⭐⭐ **MAIA is the thinking partner between coaching conversations.**

| MAIA helps the CEO | ⛔ MAIA does not |
|---|---|

| reflect | interpret the person |
| organise thoughts | diagnose |
| explore possibilities | decide |
| remember what they chose to carry | replace the relationship |

## 8. Development Rules — for the canon

### Product Loop Closure Law
> **A capability is not a product until it closes a human value loop.**

### Surface Purpose Law
> **User surfaces organize around human intentions, not system capabilities.**

### Executive Legibility Rule
> A CEO should understand, **within ten seconds**:
> 1. Who is this from? 2. Why am I here? 3. What can I do? 4. What happens next?

### No Roadmap Leakage
> Users must never see *coming later · not available · future features.*
> ⭐⭐⭐ **A product reveals capability, not construction status.**
