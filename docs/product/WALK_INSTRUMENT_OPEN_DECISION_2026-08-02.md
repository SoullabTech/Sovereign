## The three conflicts

### 1. Container identity

`docs/product/WRITERS_STUDIO_PHASE_1_WALK_SPECIFICATION.md` has a specific referent:

> *Can the Phase 1 release candidate be accepted after the W8 failure and corrections?*

That is a **release-acceptance instrument**. It judges one assembled candidate, identified
by SHA, against pass/fail criteria.

A Phase 3 walk as sketched would be a different instrument — a **field experiment** asking
whether observed behavior supports Model A or Model B under real use. Different question,
different evidence, different pass semantics. Its proposed dimensions — Arrival ·
Orientation · Agency · Continuity · Relationship · Return path — belong to that other
inquiry. They may inform a future instrument; they **do not belong inside the Phase 1
acceptance container unless explicitly re-scoped**.

⛔ **The Phase 1 container is not to be repurposed.** Repurposing it would break the
one-artifact-one-responsibility rule and would leave Phase 1 — still failed at W8 — with
its container occupied and its own criteria still unauthored.

### 2. Sequence dependency

Canonical ordering, currently recorded:

```text
Phase 1 acceptance walk → evidence → founder acceptance
      → Model A/B reconciliation → Phase 3 authorization
```

A Phase 3 experiment designed to decide Model A vs B would move that decision **ahead of**
Phase 1 acceptance. That may be a legitimate founder decision. ⛔ **It cannot happen by
interpretation.**

### 3. Authorship boundary

Ruled 2026-08-02 (Option 2): criteria are the founder's to author. Drafting them from the
failed-walk record would be **authoring, not reconstructing**. The canonical frame states
`Criteria to be authored by: Kelly`.

**The appropriate Claude action** is therefore: prepare empty structure · surface unresolved
choices · preserve placeholders · **wait**. Not invent criteria.
