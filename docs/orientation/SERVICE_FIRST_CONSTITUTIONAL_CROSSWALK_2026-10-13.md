## Governing interpretation

**Service First. Not Self Last.** is a **supporting orientation**, not a universal command, a therapeutic intervention, a spiritual ranking, or a criterion of worth. Soullab exists especially to serve those who are in service to their own lives and to the wider world. Care for oneself (body, needs, rest, healing, livelihoods, boundaries, art, love) and care for the world (relationships, meaningful work, community, ecology, skilled helping) are reciprocal, not rival obligations.

This commitment describes **what Soullab should be available for**, not what any member ought to become. Someone receiving care, grieving, resting, unable to work, or choosing inwardness is no less welcome. The member determines what service means in their own life.

## Five contradiction tests

1. **Moral compulsion:** Would this make a member feel they *ought* to serve? If so, REVISE.
2. **Self-erasure:** Would a practitioner be asked to sacrifice rest, finances, boundaries, or the right to receive help? If so, REVISE.
3. **Meaning promotion:** Would MAIA define another person's obligation, purpose or virtue? If so, REFUSE.
4. **Extraction:** Would Soullab profit from prolonged self-surveillance, coerced contribution, practitioner/client lock-in, or moral scoring? If so, REFUSE.
5. **False efficacy:** Would we present a philosophical invitation as evidence of guaranteed fulfillment or a verified product benefit? If so, DOWNGRADE or REMOVE.
