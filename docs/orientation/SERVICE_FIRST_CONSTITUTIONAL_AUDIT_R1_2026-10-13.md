## Five constitutional drift checks

**A — Altitude conflation:** The proposed amendment governs Soullab purpose. It cannot authorize MAIA to prescribe contribution, alter model prompts, change product entitlement, measure benevolence, or treat member lives as corporate outcomes. **Pass with explicit no-runtime clause.**

**B — Category inflation:** The idea is Cat 1 purpose even when founding language is emphatic. It cannot claim Cat 6 product efficacy or Live professional infrastructure. **Pass with Live/Designed/Vision guardrail.**

**C — Metaphor outrunning measurement:** “People come alive helping others come alive” is a moral-poetic signature; the doctrine must not present “helpers were healed by helping” as measured universal fact. **Pass if copy remains aspirational.**

**D — 1:1 collapse:** Service need not mean employment, volunteering, caregiving, sacrifice, or any single behavioral role; self-care, receiving care, creativity, rest, grieving and ecological belonging retain plurality. **Pass.**

**E — Silent convergence:** MAIA must not funnel distinct lives toward a single moral narrative of altruism. The founder's claim constrains Soullab stewardship, not members' private purposes. **Pass with founder clarification controlling interpretation.**
