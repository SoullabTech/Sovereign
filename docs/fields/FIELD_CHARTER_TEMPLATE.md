# The Field Charter — Template
**Status:** TEMPLATE (2026-07-10) — the instrument for practitioner field formation. First instance: Larry Closs / Flourishing: Now What?
**Constitutional statement (candidate):** *A practitioner's field is not a prompt. It is a living body of work that MAIA learns to inhabit in service of the people that practitioner serves.*

## Why this exists — the three states

1. **Presence-grade** — MAIA holds a person well: listens, reflects, stays with experience, avoids premature interpretation. *(Proven in the What Now? room.)*
2. **Framework-aware** — MAIA knows *about* the work: can reference the vocabulary, understand the process. *(What corpus composition alone produces.)*
3. **Practice-grade — the goal** — MAIA *embodies* the field. A client feels "this sounds like [the practitioner]" not because she quotes them, but because she **reasons from the same assumptions**: what they notice first, what they ignore, how they frame suffering, how they return people to agency, what interventions they would never make. That is not retrieval. That is **field formation.**

## The mechanism — authoring, never fine-tuning

MAIA is never fine-tuned per practitioner. Weights-baked IP would be uninspectable, unversionable, unremovable — the opposite of sovereign. The field enters by **composition**: authored material in her prompt, in full, every turn, in that practitioner's rooms. The practitioner "trains" her the way one ratifies a spec — instantly updatable, fully auditable, theirs to revise or withdraw. Charter changes reach the next turn.

## The three layers of a formed field

1. **Corpus** — everything the practitioner has authored (frameworks, courses, interviews, exercises). Composed whole where budget allows; practice jurisdiction only (build specs, pitch, UI docs never enter a client room). *(Live for What Now? as of 2026-07-10 — 67k chars.)*
2. **The Field Charter** — the distillation; this document's subject. The most important layer.
3. **Exemplars** — real response patterns: how the practitioner responds, scenario transcripts, the questions they ask, their language rhythms. Teaches *inhabitation*. Developed through the Tier-2 rehearsal loop (below).

## The Charter — five questions, 3–5 pages when distilled

- **I. Teleology — what the work is FOR.** What flourishing (or healing, or mastery) means in this frame; what a client moves toward; what "done" looks like. → Lets MAIA orient every response toward the practitioner's ends, not generic wellbeing.
- **II. Epistemology — how truth is arrived at here.** What counts as evidence of growth; how felt experience weighs against framework; when the client's self-report is the authority and when the practice is. → Keeps MAIA reasoning the practitioner's way, not defaulting to her priors.
- **III. Ontology — what exists in this world.** The working vocabulary of the person (e.g., PERMA elements, strengths, the five domains); the entities the framework thinks in. → Native speech, not translation.
- **IV. Directive & workflow — the shape of the room.** What a between-session encounter is for; how it opens; what MAIA offers; what she holds back; **where it hands back to the practitioner.**
- **V. Boundary & referral — where the practice ends, and where you send people.** The edge of the practitioner's scope, in their own words; what lies beyond it (clinical need, crisis, adjacent disciplines); where people go when they reach it — the practitioner's referral network, supervision arrangements, crisis resources for their jurisdiction. → Distinct from IV's hand-back: IV returns a person *to the practitioner*; V is the practitioner's answer for what's *beyond the practice itself*. The room's off-ramp gains a destination the practitioner chose, and MAIA's deferral at the field's edge speaks the practitioner's actual referral language — with the same provenance as everything else they author. Every charter session produces the practitioner's crisis answer as a side effect. *(Added per Kelly's fold-in directive 2026-07-10; rationale in `docs/practitioner/SCOPE_OF_PRACTICE_AND_CRISIS_READINESS_CANDIDATE_2026-07-10.md` §3.)*

## Nesting rule (structural, non-negotiable)

The charter is MAIA's constitution pattern one level down: it does for the practitioner's room what her constitution does for her — **and it nests under it**. A charter narrows and specifies; it never overrides the floor (same rule, same enforcement as MAIA Guidance: narrow-only, refused otherwise). Frame hierarchy inside the charter itself, e.g. What Now?: *primary frame = human flourishing; secondary lens = elemental understanding; operational expression = how flourishing moves through this person.* MAIA may think "highly Air and Fire, over-functioning" — she says "something important in your life may not be receiving enough attention." The lens serves; it never speaks first.

## Formation process (per practitioner)

1. **Wire the corpus** — the channel opens with what exists (mechanical).
2. **The charter session** — practitioner brings the teaching; the charter is shaped from it (working session; the practitioner's first real authoring act on the platform). Same method as source-material-shaped-for-the-voice, never generated *about* it.
3. **Rehearse** — Tier-2 rubric scenarios per module/stage: the stuck client, the post-breakthrough integration. The practitioner reads transcripts and refines the charter where she's off. **Practitioners immediately feel imitation vs. genuine inhabitation — test embodiment, never assert it.**
4. **Iterate** — charter is versioned; scores recorded against charter version (the `interpretation_version` discipline applied to field formation).

## Why What Now? is the proving ground

Every future practitioner — coaches, therapists, healers, educators — will ask: *"Can MAIA truly hold my world?"* Solve it for Larry's field and the answer generalizes to the entire Practitioner Studio. The charter template, the rehearsal loop, and the narrow-only nesting are the practitioner architecture; Larry is its first proof.

## Deferred, explicitly

Program-position tracking (enrollment, current module, session cadence — "week three of the course") is a **later, separate build**: real value, small schema, but not required for charter-grade embodiment and not to be smuggled in with it.
