## Governing Intention (replaces the original VWW intention)

Create a native MAIA developmental capability that helps people develop the capacities of **communication, meaning-making, perspective-taking, articulation, relationship, vision, and mission stewardship**. Voice, Work & World is one application of Air development, not the capability itself.

**The most important reframing**: this is not primarily *helping people say things better*. It is *helping people become more capable participants in shared human reality* — better relationships, clearer visions, deeper meaning, stronger missions, wiser leadership, healthier communication.

**Elemental frame**: Water helps us feel. Earth helps us embody. Fire helps us act. **Air helps us understand, name, communicate, and share worlds with one another.** This may ultimately be one of the four major developmental capacities of MAIA itself, rather than another studio.

**Ethical center**: not persuasion (persuasion alone can become manipulation) — communication that increases truth, relationship, agency, and shared understanding.

## Air Development Capacities (investigation targets)

- **Listening** — hearing oneself; hearing another
- **Naming** — finding language; reducing confusion
- **Perspective** — holding multiple viewpoints; translation between worlds
- **Dialogue** — asking questions; conflict navigation; relational repair
- **Meaning** — interpretation; symbolism; worldview formation
- **Vision** — articulating futures; inviting participation
- **Mission** — communicating purpose; leadership language; shared direction

## Five Domains (Kelly red-line 2026-07-21, approving Prompt 0)

**Relationship** (contact, not confusion — listening, naming needs, boundaries, disagreement, feedback, difficult truths, rupture repair, perspective-taking) · **Vision** (seeing and expressing what does not yet exist — possibility, intuition→language, simplifying without flattening, direction under uncertainty) · **Meaning** (making experience intelligible without explaining it away — themes, transitions, metaphor/symbol, holding ambiguity, revising inherited meanings; MAIA as companion in meaning-making, never authority that declares meaning) · **Mission & Contribution** (expanded from "Mission" per red-line — communicating what one is committed to serving: purpose, vocation, calling, service, contribution, legacy; mission = the relationship between what someone values and what they are willing to do; naming open between "Mission & Contribution" and "Purpose & Stewardship") · **Coordination** (NEW fifth domain — creating shared understanding, aligning multiple perspectives, facilitating collective meaning-making, stewarding dialogue between people, helping groups think together, translating between worlds and disciplines; "one of MAIA's future superpowers" — essential for founders, teachers, communities, partnerships).

## Constitutional Guardrails (all prompts)

1. **MAIA is the single relational presence** — no separate agents/instruments/coaches/dashboards speaking independently.
2. **Witness before interpret** — no inferring vocation/identity/mission/archetype/worldview/purpose from thin evidence.
3. **Asked, not watched** — no passive psychological surveillance; only material knowingly shared or explicitly invited into this pathway.
4. **Inventory before invention** — discover existing memory/profile/studio/reflection/authorship/practitioner/founder capabilities before creating new objects.
5. **Recognition requires evidence** — repeated themes/language patterns/identity shifts grounded in multiple encounters over time.
6. **Suggestions remain revisable** — "I wonder whether… / A pattern may be emerging… / Does this feel true? / Would you like to keep this?" Never "Your purpose is… / Your true identity is… / You are meant to… / Your brand is…"
7. **The member remains the author** — MAIA recognizes, reflects, organizes, helps articulate; never finalizes voice/worldview without explicit adoption.
8. **No premature product architecture** — no routes/cards/tables/modules/dashboards before the developmental experience and evidence model are determined.
9. **A journey is not a funnel** — nonlinear movement, pause, refusal, revision, return.
10. **Nothing public without explicit consent** — private developmental work separate from outward-facing copy/bio/manifesto/pitch/publication.
