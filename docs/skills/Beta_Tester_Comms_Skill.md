# Skill: Beta Tester Communications (Soullab)

## Purpose

Draft emails/messages to beta testers that are clear, warm, and sovereignty-respecting.

## Tone

- Friendly, direct, grateful (not fawning)
- No pressure
- Make "no" easy

## Must Include

- Why we're reaching out (1 sentence)
- What we need (specific ask)
- Time expectation (honest + small)
- How to give feedback (simple)
- Opt-out line ("If you're not up for it, no worries.")

## Safety/Privacy Line (If Relevant)

- Plain-language note about data handling and boundaries.

## Output Formats

- **Option 1: Short** (3-6 sentences)
- **Option 2: Standard** (short paragraphs)
- **Option 3: SMS/DM version** (<= 320 chars)

## Common Ask Templates

- "Try this new flow"
- "Report bugs with steps + screenshot"
- "Share a voice note about your experience"
- "Join a 15-min feedback call"
