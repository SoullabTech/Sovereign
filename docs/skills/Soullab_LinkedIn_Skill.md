# Skill: Soullab LinkedIn Post (Positioning + Tone)

## Purpose

Create LinkedIn posts that build trust and curiosity without hype — sovereignty-first, psychologically literate, spiritually intelligent.

## Post Types Allowed

- Build-in-public (what we learned, what changed, why)
- Principle + application (sovereignty, consent, coherence, relationship)
- Case vignette (de-identified, pattern-level, no claims)
- Invitation to collaborate (clear ask, clear boundaries)
- Mythopoetic insight grounded in practical action

## Structure Templates (Choose One)

A) Hook > Insight > Example > Takeaway > Invitation
B) Tension > Reframe > 3 bullets > Micro-practice > Question
C) Story (short) > Principle > What we're building > Ask

## Guardrails

- No "we're revolutionizing everything" language.
- No spiritual bypassing.
- No medical/clinical claims.
- Avoid hashtag overload (0-5 max).
- Keep it human: one strong idea, one real example.

## Voice

- Calm confidence, not dominance.
- "We're building..." not "Here's the truth..."

## Output Requirements

Return:
1. Post (max ~1,300 characters unless asked otherwise)
2. 3 alternate hooks
3. Suggested CTA options (2)
4. 3-5 hashtags (optional)

## Preferred Topics (Soullab)

- The difference between tools and authority
- Consent UX / sovereignty design
- AI as relational support (not replacement)
- Integration and "between-session" coherence
- Community emergence with safety + boundaries
