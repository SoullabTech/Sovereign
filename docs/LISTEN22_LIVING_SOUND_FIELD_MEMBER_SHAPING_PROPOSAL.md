# LISTEN-22 — The Living Sound Field: music that members can shape

**2026-10-24 · Soullab Audio & Listening Field · Existing LISTEN-01 #1979**  
**Status:** Founder exploratory vision captured; **design proposal**, not final founder ratification, implementation, artist contract, licensing or production authorization.

## The human promise

> **SOULLAB — For the life you live. For the world we make.**

The founder envisions a living Listening Field where music, contemplative sound, creative atmosphere, cultural knowledge, performance, silence and daily life can interact. Members are not reduced to a streaming profile or recipients of prescribed "healing frequencies." They can **listen, explore, shape, return to, and—in separately consented contexts—share** their sound worlds.

This grows naturally from the **existing** Listening Room, Sound Sanctuary and Between the Tides **A / Organic Current + B / Open Water** founder decision. A and B are not competing products or mandatory exclusive "moods"; they demonstrate two relationships—**warm, human pulse** and **spaciousness, resonance, silence**—that a member can hold together.

The first experience should feel alive and intuitive, not like a digital audio workstation, a medical treatment dashboard, or an AI interpreting a person's emotions. Artist-authored originals remain available exactly as composed, with an optional member-led composition/interaction mode.

## 1. What the member actually does

**Illustrative first journey — existing Listening Room, no new app:**

1. **Arrive:** Member chooses one of the existing invitations **Focus / Create / Drift / Rest**, or simply starts the artist's original performance. No compulsory intention, questionnaire or MAIA analysis.
2. **Listen unchanged first:** An original, rights-authorized work plays with visible authorship, duration, actual delivery quality, volume and Stop. The member can stay in listening mode; all shaping is optional.
3. **Shape the field:** For a work whose creators have explicitly authorized stems/mixing, open a modest *Shape* surface:
   - **Organic Current (A):** pulse, bass, plucked/melodic movement
   - **Open Water (B):** drones, sustaining harmony, environmental air and quiet
   - **Density / stillness:** how much space is allowed between musical events
   - **Duration and ending:** session length, gentle beginning/end; no clicky loops or invented long recording
   - **Optional layers:** only artist-authorized bowls/gongs/nature/field recordings; voice/chant or heritage content **not** presumed available for remix
   - **Artist's mix:** one tap always restores the creator's original; no permanent modification of the master
4. **Carry the music into life:** Authorized background continuity through House, Writer or other accepted rooms, with intentional voice/microphone handoff and no surprise music restart. The accepted AUDIO-PLATFORM-01 safety controls support—not define—the member's music.
5. **Choose whether to keep:** A member can name and save a *private session recipe* (parameter settings and source work IDs, not copied copyrighted audio). Saving/sync awaits independently accepted encryption, deletion/recovery and member isolation; no account memory by stealth.
6. **Optional return/reflection:** “What did you notice?” is an invitation. No diagnosis, prescribed spiritual state, covert behavioral scoring or automatic MAIA recall.

**One continuous sound session owns its authorized stems**; stems inside a mixer do not become independent players fighting each other for focus.

## 2. A longer artistic horizon: discovery, inheritance, living creativity

The founder also imagines sound across **personal, historical, ceremonial, meditative, ritual, communal, world-musical and exploratory** traditions. Treat this as a **curated, artist- and community-led living library**, not an extractive collection of "sacred sounds."

- **Original artist Soundscapes:** actual recordings, compositions, stems and long-form journeys with attribution, artistic context, artist-set remix boundaries, fair splits and revocation.
- **Sound traditions and cultural lineages:** practitioners and custodians decide what may be taught, heard, recorded, excerpted, mixed or performed. Sacred/restricted materials may be **not licensable** and must remain excluded. Respect self-identification; do not produce generated "authentic ceremonial chants" in place of participating communities.
- **Inductive discovery:** the listener follows what they personally notice, resonates with or questions. MAIA may reflect a member's *chosen words* when invited; no automatic interpretation of a tradition or individual.
- **Circles and collaboration:** later, a member may invite a small circle to hear an original work or share an *allowed session recipe*. Invitations are opt-in; reflections, attendance, microphones and recordings are private/consented separately. Group listening/performance rights differ from private listening rights.
- **Practitioner offerings:** sound-led relaxation, attention, meditation or exploratory creative work may be offered with accurate practitioner credentials and clear limits. **No medical efficacy or healing guarantee is inferred** from sound frequencies, tuning, traditions or subjective effects.

**Heritage sources:** WIPO on traditional cultural expressions (music, performances and ceremonies): https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/traditional-cultural-expressions/index ; UNESCO ethics emphasizes free, prior, sustained and informed consent: https://ich.unesco.org/en/ethics-and-ich-00866 . These are guiding references, not substitutes for community-specific legal and cultural permission.

## 3. An artist-level production partnership is possible—not booked

The founder has identified a **potential experienced recording/soundscape collaborator** and sees value in a genuine human co-creation process. **The collaborator's identity, the nature of any preexisting private relationship, private contact details and unconfirmed affiliations must not be recorded in this public issue or approached without founder instructions.**

Possible scope for an *opt-in* collaboration, subject to a separate conversation and professional boundaries:
- Record genuine long-form original instrument/drones, spatial textures, organic pulse and atmospheric material.
- Make **true multitrack source stems** with dynamics, continuity, headroom, mixing permissions and device-appropriate rendering—not call LISTEN-19 dry synth rehearsal guides finished master stems.
- Co-design a safe, artistically meaningful **member-controlled variation envelope** so the piece always remains music, not a tangle of sliders.
- Establish named writer/composer/performer/master ownership, scope of on-demand/interactive/stem/mix/offline/group uses, compensation and attribution; all rights **before** public distribution.
- Compare subjective musical choices and audio-engineering measures under a clearly experimental framework, without presenting speculative frequencies as clinical treatment.

### About 432 Hz and other resonant techniques

Support **musically justified tuning and experimentation**, including optional A4=432 Hz / A4=440 Hz *when the composer authorizes and the source supports it*. Neither tuning is in itself a guaranteed healing frequency or historical universal truth. A published 33-person crossover pilot suggests some physiological differences but cannot establish unique, generalizable treatment effects (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31031095/). Binaural/entrainment evidence remains heterogeneous and limited for broad health claims (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41656644/). Expose technique, intended feeling and known output conditions accurately; keep binaural/isochronic effects optional, comfortable in level, and not compulsory to enjoy the work.

## 4. Product safety and sovereignty

1. **Listening must work without MAIA** and without saving behavioral data.
2. **Creator keeps authorship:** the artist's original performance is unaltered and credited; only approved mixes/layers may be reshaped.
3. **No provider loopholes:** playlist links or Spotify/TIDAL/Apple Music accounts do not authorize copying/ripping/rehosting/AI analysis; provider music stays within its contracts.
4. **Real sound quality:** distinguish pristine preservation master, streamed codec, in-app DSP and actual wired/Bluetooth/OS output. Avoid exaggerated lossless claims. Respect safe levels and transitions.
5. **Private recipes not private recordings:** recipes, journal entries, event participation and sensitive voice data remain separate, member-owned and deletable under accepted R20 safeguards; offline Cabin transfer only when independently accepted.
6. **Cultural permission is first-class metadata:** community restrictions and revocation can override product features; no involuntary modernization of ceremonial material.
7. **No unauthorised wellness claims:** use exploratory, reflective and subjective language. Any medical/clinical effectiveness or physiological/sensing claim requires separate science, consent and review.
8. **No parallel source:** existing LISTEN-01 is the experience owner; AUDIO-PLATFORM-01 #1984 is the shared focus/control dependency. Do not spawn another app, AudioCoordinator, global assistant or isolated product authority.

## 6. Conversation → Sound → Understanding → Composition

**Founder brainstorm expanded October 24, 2026.** Treat these as **experience design examples and specific proposed capabilities**; no source integration, rights authorization, voice cloning, paid recording, feature promise or production permission follows from the examples.

The member can enter the same Audio & Listening Field from three complementary doors, **with the same underlying artist-authorized sound session**:

1. **Describe / converse with MAIA.** The member names a lived feeling, music remembered, an imagined setting, rhythm or ceremonial intention. MAIA asks no more than one useful creative question, offers a sonic direction, and prepares an *original* sound recipe from only authorized sources. It cannot claim to hear, own or sample any private commercial recording. The actual audio engine creates/plays the result when separately authorized.
2. **Shape / play the soundboard.** Start simply with **Pulse & movement (A)**, **Space & resonance (B)**, **Density / silence**. Restore the **artist's original mix** immediately. More specialist controls (timbre, tuning, pan, layers, rhythm structure, attack, decay, transducer/output routing) unfold optionally behind an Audio Engineer view. The simple and expert controls act on **the same underlying sound and session state**; no second renderer/player or separate product.
3. **Listen / discover / learn.** MAIA optionally explains historical contexts, instruments, rhythmic frameworks, melodic or harmonic phenomena, frequency, psychoacoustics, listening ethics, artist credits and changing cultural meanings using attributable sources, uncertainty and cultural stewardship. A member can hear an approved example and experiment with musical qualities without being told what the sound *must mean* psychologically. Learning is optional; a soundtrack plays without it.

**One flow, not three products.** Conversational choices can update the soundboard; moving a control can lead to an optional musical explanation; and a teaching insight can become a member-initiated sonic experiment. No telemetry of intimate music/feelings by default, no covert prompt-to-memory, and no MAIA interpretation without invitation.

### Example A — “A circle of elders expressing gratitude, remembrance and grace”

A member asks MAIA for a field of deep human voices, responsive tones, soft droning foundations and light that evokes companionship and remembrance.

**Responsible immediate response:** “We can create an original, gentle call-and-response soundscape with warm layered tones, breathing space and slow light. Would you prefer wordless voices, instrumental tones or mostly silence?” Any voices must be actual authorized performers, or clearly identified synthetic **abstract voice textures**, never falsely presented as real elders, authentic ceremonies, Indigenous teachings or a participating cultural community. No generated fake sacred language, imitation of named restricted rites, deceptive attribution or automatic live-group recordings. If a community-led authorized ceremonial work exists, its custodians decide if playback, teaching, re-use, mixing and public/group presentation are permitted. The member is free to listen without making or sharing anything.

**Light** means optional low-intensity, slow, accessible visual accompaniment with reduced-motion, no-flashing and audio-only alternatives. Sound/light should not be sold as a clinical therapy or assured healing effect.

### Example B — “MAIA, play Peter Gabriel’s *Sky Blue*”

This means **find authorized listening**, not sample or copy the recording. MAIA can identify an official artist/vendor page or a legally authorized user-provider playback context and open/delegate there. **It cannot silently stream or embed the song inside Soullab** unless direct music rights and provider permissions have actually been granted. The original copyright/recording remains separate from Soullab originals.

**Teaching opportunity:** With verified, properly attributed sources, MAIA may explain the record's artistic context, the relationship of soulful/blues influence to the work, production choices (including openness and restraint), and credited guest musicians. Peter Gabriel's own UP interview discusses deliberate sonic space and his work with the Blind Boys of Alabama; those are **historical statements about the source recording**, not an invitation to reuse protected materials.
- Official album context: https://petergabriel.com/release/up/
- Artist interview: https://petergabriel.com/focus/up-generic-interview/
- Official artist-owned release/listening page: https://petergabriel.bandcamp.com/track/sky-blue

### Example C — “Now create a sound field that naturally follows *Sky Blue*”

MAIA may help translate **high-level, non-exclusive impressions freely described by the member**—spaciousness, warm low resonance, human connection, spacious vocal textures, gradual lift and rest—into a **new work with independently authored melody/harmony/rhythm and only properly licensed stems**.

**Do not** duplicate Peter Gabriel's melody, lyrics, unique riff, distinctive recording, stems or identifying vocal performance; do not ask generative tools to reproduce the original or mimic a specific living artist's signature. The user can choose how the *new, original* field connects emotionally, perhaps beginning in a few seconds of silence and resolving into a low harmonic drone with independently composed voices or instruments. **The automatic transition may not be technically permitted inside a third-party player.** If the provider forbids cross-service playback or crossfade, MAIA offers a manual, visible **“Start my Soullab field after the song”** step without recording or analyzing provider output.

**Music inspiration is a creative invitation, not a license to remix the reference.**

### Example D — “Teach me a rhythm, then let me use it”

MAIA offers an optional short lesson with sourced geographic/history/artist context, a playable *original educational example* and an invitation to vary its pattern through the same soundboard. It identifies living communities and specific permissions when dealing with traditional or ceremonial music, avoids presenting one tradition as universally sacred, and distinguishes historical reports from speculative associations about consciousness. Do not turn a motif controlled by a community or artist into freely remixable stock material without authorization.

**432 Hz** and other tunings can be explored with musicians and described as tuning choices; no universal healing claim or equivalence to historical ritual is inferred. Binaural, isochronic and other physiological techniques require comfort, sound-level, sensory and evidence-bound controls before use. See UNESCO's community-first ethical framework: https://ich.unesco.org/en/ethics-and-ich-00866.

## 7. Proposed initial experience boundary — no runaway feature expansion

**Phase S0 — one expressive, audible A+B session:** maintain LISTEN-18's original artist-approved arrangement untouched; demonstrate **A Pulse**, **B Space**, and **Density/Silence**, plus “Restore original” and an explicit play/pause. Real controls must produce musically meaningful audible changes from **genuinely mix-authorized original materials**. LISTEN-19–21 synthetic guides are private arrangement reference, not already granted commercial remix stems. A static soundboard prototype without a sound change is not acceptance.

**Phase S1 — MAIA conductor with one audible, reversible prompt:** one founder-selected example of describing a sound field, MAIA producing a structured **source-permission-checked** recipe, and the identical S0 session updating transparently; the member can compare before/after and undo. No MAIA audio-text model automatically learns emotional state or listens to provider tracks.

**Phase S2 — optional guide/teacher:** grounded explanations of rhythms, lineages, instrument types, acoustic design, rights and sound-science boundaries, linked to the same live session. The library and soundboard do not grow separate personas; MAIA stays the one optional conversation voice.

**Phase S3 — artist/cultural collaborations, authentic human recordings and circles:** only after actual opt-in partner agreement, culturally appropriate reciprocal compensation/participation and explicit group, recording, mixing, attribution, privacy and territory rights. Participating elders/practitioners must not be fabricated or used as artistic raw material.

**Prototype acceptance:** The founder can begin with no technical instructions, shape an actually heard original musical field and return to the artist original in one action; it feels alive and intentional, not like adjusting meaningless sliders. There is no automatic recording, file sharing, playback of unlicensed music or compulsory interpretation. The first successful demo is **one member journey**, not every future cultural, clinical, social and studio capability.
