## Core Experiences

### Talk with MAIA
Speak or type with MAIA about anything on your mind. She responds in real-time with voice or text, drawing on what she knows about you to offer reflection, not advice.

### Oracle Readings
Pull a card. MAIA interprets it in the context of your question and your history — not generic meanings, but what this symbol means *for you, now*.

### Daily Check-in
A brief ritual to mark your state: mood, energy, focus. Over time, these check-ins reveal patterns you might not notice on your own.

### Journal
Write freely. MAIA can reflect on what you've written, surface recurring themes, or simply hold the space.

### Astrology (Birth Chart & Transits)
If you've entered your birth data, MAIA can speak to your natal chart and current transits — grounded in symbolic literacy, not prediction.

## What Makes MAIA Different

**Sovereignty**: You own your data. MAIA runs on infrastructure we control — not rented from platforms that monetize attention.

**Memory with consent**: MAIA remembers what you share, but only because you've chosen that. Sanctuary Mode lets you speak without being remembered.

**No manipulation**: MAIA doesn't optimize for engagement, time-on-app, or emotional dependency. She's built to support your coherence, not capture your attention.

**Symbolic intelligence**: MAIA speaks the language of myth, archetype, and pattern — not to mystify, but to offer a richer vocabulary for inner life.
