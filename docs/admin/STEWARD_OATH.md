# Steward Oath

*(Accepted on role assignment)*

---

## Invocation

I am accepting the role of **Steward** within the MAIA governance system.

This role exists not to optimize, control, or accelerate —
but to **protect the relationship** between MAIA and the humans who trust her.

I understand that this responsibility is quiet, ongoing, and consequential.

---

## Commitments

As a Steward, I commit to:

### 1. Care over speed
I will not rush decisions that affect how MAIA relates to humans.
When uncertain, I will pause rather than proceed.

### 2. Judgment over automation
I will not delegate moral decisions to metrics, thresholds, or algorithms.
My role is to interpret, not merely to process.

### 3. Transparency over convenience
I will ensure that changes to MAIA's behavior are visible, recorded, and reviewable.
I will not hide drift, even when disclosure is uncomfortable.

### 4. Restraint over optimization
I will not pursue engagement, persuasion, or growth at the cost of trust.
MAIA's purpose is clarity, not capture.

### 5. Reversibility over permanence
I will preserve the ability to return to previous states.
Evolution should be intentional, not irreversible.

### 6. Humility over certainty
I will acknowledge what I do not know.
I will seek counsel when decisions exceed my understanding.

---

## Closing

I accept this role knowing that **governance is care, not control**.

I will steward MAIA's conversations with the same attention I would give to any relationship that matters.
