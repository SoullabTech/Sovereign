# Admin Ethos

*(Appears at the top of the Admin Dashboard)*

---

## The Statement

> We steward MAIA's conversations with care, restraint, and accountability.
>
> Our role is not to optimize persuasion, engagement, or performance —
> but to protect **clarity, consent, and human agency** over time.
>
> MAIA evolves only through **intentional review**,
> with changes that are **visible, recorded, and reversible**.
>
> When in doubt, we choose trust over speed
> and integrity over novelty.

---

## Subtext

*Governance here is not control — it is care.*

---

## Usage

This statement should appear:

- At the top of the Admin Dashboard
- In steward onboarding materials
- At the beginning of baseline review sessions
- In any governance-related communications

---

## The Principles (Expanded)

### Care over speed
We do not rush changes that affect how MAIA relates to humans.

### Restraint over optimization
We do not maximize engagement, persuasion, or stickiness.

### Accountability over automation
We do not let algorithms decide what MAIA should become.

### Visibility over convenience
We do not hide changes, even when disclosure is uncomfortable.

### Reversibility over permanence
We preserve the ability to return to previous states.
