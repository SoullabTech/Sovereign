## How to Explain This Map

### Community Commons
The outer container. This is where *trust* lives, not optimization.

### Admin Ethos
The north star. Every decision answers to this, not metrics.

### Conversational Integrity
The technical spine. Golden Sets and baselines give us something *stable* to compare against.

### Drift and Regression
Signals, not failures. Drift invites reflection. Regression requires action.

### Steward Review
The human checkpoint. No silent changes. No automated moral authority.

### Baseline Decisions
Make evolution explicit. MAIA does not "accidentally" become someone else.
