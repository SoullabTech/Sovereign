# Field Notebook

The empirical memory of the project. Each entry records a real design decision against the
constitutional principle that predicted it, what reality then showed, and where the two diverged.

> **Status (2026-06-18): the methodology is frozen for use.** The apparatus — this notebook,
> [Field Questions](FIELD_QUESTIONS.md), the Corpus Standard schema, and the
> promotion/warrant/confidence ladders — is now enough to carry substantial work. No further
> *anticipatory* refinement: the next change is earned only when a real build cannot be expressed in
> the existing artifacts. The methodology's own value is an **Underdetermined** purpose-claim — the
> evidence it needs now is *use* across six-to-ten genuinely different product decisions, not more
> design.

This is a **field notebook**, not a research notebook. A research notebook begins with a thesis
and collects what supports it. A field notebook begins with reality and asks *what happened* — and
is **obligated to preserve observations that weaken the architecture.** That obligation is what
makes the corpus governance rather than advocacy.

## How an entry works

See [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md). Two independent axes, **both of which can move down**:

- **Promotion** — which layer a finding changed (Operation · Certification · Evidence ·
  Constitution), as a lifecycle: Claimed → Verified → possibly **Refuted/Demoted**.
- **Confidence** — how much constitutional weight it deserves now (L0–L4); may *decrease* on review.

Every entry carries a `Falsifier triggered?` boolean. The entries where it fired are the most
valuable in the corpus — they are where reality corrected the constitution (or the record).

> *A framework that cannot describe what would prove it wrong is not yet ready to explain why it is right.*

## Observations — a third register (added 2026-06-18, earned)

Unexpected findings encountered *while doing the work* — never the object of any pre-registered
question — are recorded in [OBSERVATIONS.md](OBSERVATIONS.md), **not** as notebook entries. Filing a
surprise into a prediction-bearing entry would let it read as though it had been predicted; keeping
the two orthogonal preserves the anti-hindsight discipline. An Observation may **commission** a future
[Field Question](FIELD_QUESTIONS.md), but pre-registering it stays a separate, dated step. This
register was **earned** — O-001 (the consent gate trusting authoritative over apparent state) could
not be expressed in the existing artifacts without a surprise masquerading as a prediction — not
anticipated.

## First reading — the rediscovery test

> *If these four were the only surviving artifacts after the implementation disappeared, could a
> competent team rediscover the constitution?*

**Partially — and the gaps are the finding.**

**Recoverable from these four alone:** the load-bearing *constraints*. A competent team could
reconstruct — consent precedes action (0001, 0004); attention is protected against engagement
(0002); memory restrains formation (0003); authority is granted, revocable, mortal, never seized
(0004); and the meta-discipline that claims must be falsifiable and earned before named (visible
in 0003's status-demotion and 0004's deliberate hold). They would also rediscover that the system
is **corrigible** — 0001 corrected the project's own memory *and* the notebook author's narration;
0003 demoted an over-promoted claim from assumed-live to pending. Note: across the first four, the
*principle*-falsifier never fired — the constitution's principles survived contact. What the
down-machinery caught was at the *status* and *enforcement* layers (a demotion, a duplicate, an
unenforced refusal, a deliberate hold), not the principles themselves.

**Not recoverable:** the *purpose*. All four are refusals or constraints. A team reading only these
would reconstruct a system very good at **refusing harm** — and might never infer what it is *for*:
human coherence, the person preserved as author of their own life, the developmental arc from inner
coherence to shared understanding. The corpus currently over-represents the **refuse** half of the
constitution and under-represents the **cultivate** half.

**Therefore the next entries should be generative, not protective** — cases where a principle led
to *building something that increased agency or coherence* (e.g. Sovereign Memory surfacing,
Relationship Home, Studio-as-soil). A constitution rediscovered from refusals alone reads as a
safety charter; the real one is a developmental charter. Balancing the corpus is the next move —
after a pause to read these four together.

## Schema evolution (the instrument is itself under the instrument)

The entry schema has been revised three times in three sessions, each revision *earned by use* on
the same four cases, not declared in advance:

1. **Promotion split** — Claimed vs Verified (a promotion is a process, not an attribute).
2. **Down-direction** — Refuted/Demoted + a falsifier boolean (the notebook must record principles
   going *down*, not only up).
3. **Two trajectories** — separate *Epistemic Outcome* (what reality did to a claim: Confirmed ·
   Diverged · Falsified · Underdetermined, exactly one **per claim**) from *Confidence delta*
   (up/same/down) and from *Promotion* (what changed in the architecture). Plus the **generative
   self-audit** question, which turns each refusal-entry into a pointer to the aspiration-entry
   that would balance it.

The schema is therefore itself a **hypothesis under observation**, not canon. The axis split
(Epistemic Outcome ⟂ Confidence Delta) was **adopted** after [0003](0003-data-minimization.md)
demonstrated it on a refusal case, then **exercised on a genuinely different kind of case** —
[0005](0005-sovereign-memory.md), the first *generative* (construction) entry. It survived, and the
exercise **taught one new rule**: a generative case's *headline* outcome must track its
**load-bearing claim** (the purpose), not its most-easily-verified claim (the mechanism) — else
every construction headlines "Confirmed" and the corpus re-acquires the inflation bias through the
back door.

**Promoted to Corpus Standard (2026-06-18)** — *to the method, not the constitution*; the schema
studies the architecture, and the two evolve on different clocks. Warranted by four independent
observations (survived refusal cases · survived a generative case · produced a correction the
predecessor could not express · blocked inflation where pressure was strongest), not by preference.
Applied: the headline rule is folded into TEMPLATE; [0001](0001-scheduled-send.md) /
[0002](0002-presence-dots.md) / [0004](0004-authorized-action-vs-standing-authorization.md) are
retrofitted to per-claim outcomes. **Deliberately not built:** the **Claim Index** — a visible
pattern, not yet an artifact; it earns itself after a half-dozen *independent* cases, not by decree.

A *candidate* observation is also held, not promoted: **a mechanism can be Confirmed while its
purpose stays Underdetermined** (0005). It is recorded field data at N=1; its many intuitive
examples are illustrations, not cases. Promoting it now would have it violate itself — treating
plausibility as confirmation. It earns standing only when future generative cases *record* the split.

## Corpus health (an observed property, not a rule)

> **Corpus health depends on both refusal and generative cases. Neither alone is sufficient to
> reconstruct the architecture.**

Not a contributor rule — a property already visible in the corpus. The two case kinds answer
different questions (refusal: *what harm did we avoid?* · generative: *what capacity increased?*),
so the *balance* between them is itself evidence:

- too many refusals → the architecture reconstructs as a **safety system**;
- too many generative stories → it reconstructs as **marketing**.

First datapoint (2026-06-18): five entries, skewing toward constraint (0002/0003/0004 refusals;
0001 a construction filed through its constraints; only 0005 fully generative). By this property the
corpus currently reads closer to a safety system — the rediscovery finding made *measurable*. The
self-audits have already commissioned the rebalancing backlog: co-presence (0002), the calendar
executor (0001 and 0004), and the experiential measurement (0005). *Caveat on the apparent
convergence:* 0001 and 0004 were authored in the same pass by the same agent, so their pointing at
the same build is **one coherent view, not two independent observations intersecting.** It becomes
evidence of the architecture *generating its own experiments* only when the convergence recurs
across **independently authored** entries. If this balance property keeps holding as the corpus
grows, it may itself earn promotion; until then it is a useful observation about the shape of
healthy field evidence.
