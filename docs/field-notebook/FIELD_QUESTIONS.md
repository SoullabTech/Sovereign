# Field Questions — pre-registration for the Field Notebook

Companion to the [Field Notebook](README.md). The notebook records **what happened** (retrospective).
This file records **why we are paying attention** (prospective) — the questions and expectations
filed *before* the observation exists.

## Why a separate, frozen artifact (not just the notebook's prediction fields)

A prediction that sits inside a notebook entry, directly above its own observation, is editable when
the result arrives — it is psychologically trivial to "adjust" the hypothesis to fit what happened
and never notice. A Field Question is filed first, **dated, and frozen.** That freeze is the entire
anti-hindsight property; without it this file would be redundant with the notebook's
`Constitutional Prediction` / `Potential Falsifier` fields.

This is also the precondition for the **discovery-engine** question. Notebook entries 0001–0005 were
all written *retrospectively* — after the builds existed; the notebook has so far only explained in
hindsight. A discovery engine must predict *before*. Calendar is the **first prospectively
pre-registered experiment**; only from here can "the architecture identified the next experiment
before the roadmap" become a checkable claim rather than a post-hoc story.

## The discipline

- File the question **before** the build/observation. Date it.
- Once observation begins, the question is **frozen** — do not edit it.
- The resulting notebook entry **links back** to its Field Question; any gap between the
  pre-registered expectation and the outcome is recorded *in the entry*, never by editing the question.
- Engineering can certify **mechanism** questions (by inspection); only **member-authored field
  cases** can answer **purpose** questions (see the mechanism/purpose candidate law, [0005](0005-sovereign-memory.md)).

## Every Field Question answers

- What are we trying to learn?
- What observation would change our mind?
- What evidence would strengthen the claim? What would weaken it?
- Who can actually observe this?
