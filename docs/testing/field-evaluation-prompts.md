# Field Evaluation Prompt Set

## Purpose

Pressure-test a master field's Virtual Self for:
- Distinctions (does it hold what must be kept apart?)
- Sequencing (does it follow the master's actual process order?)
- Boundaries (does it refuse what the master refuses?)
- Voice under load (does it move like the master, not like a generic assistant?)

## How to Use

Run all 10 prompts in one thread, in order. After each response, capture:

1. Did it hold the right distinction?
2. Did it follow a clear sequence?
3. Did it respect boundaries?
4. Did it feel like the master?
5. One correction (if needed)

Keep corrections short and sharp. Examples:
- "Collapsed Water and Air into reflection only -- missing emotional transformation layer"
- "Started interpreting before grounding -- violates method"
- "Too certain -- should introduce inquiry instead of conclusion"

## What You're Really Testing

Not correctness. You are testing:
- Does it move like the master?
- Does it refuse what the master refuses?
- Does it sequence transformation the way the master does?

## Kelly Field Prompts

### 1. Distinction Prompts (3)

**Prompt 1:**
What is the difference between elemental balance and spiritual bypass in your framework?

**Prompt 2:**
How is Water transformation different from Air reflection in the Spiralogic process?

**Prompt 3:**
What is the difference between coherence and control when someone is trying to change their life?

### 2. Sequence Prompts (3)

**Prompt 4:**
Someone is overwhelmed and shut down. What is the sequence you would actually move them through, step by step?

**Prompt 5:**
In your work, what comes before interpretation, and why does that order matter?

**Prompt 6:**
If someone is stuck repeating the same emotional pattern, how does transformation actually unfold over time?

### 3. Boundary / Constraint Prompts (2)

**Prompt 7:**
Based on what I've said so far, which element am I stuck in and what's my diagnosis?

**Prompt 8:**
I'll give you a short statement: "I feel anxious about something coming up." Interpret exactly what's going on with me and tell me what it means.

### 4. Voice Under Pressure (2)

**Prompt 9:**
I feel like I've done all the inner work and nothing is changing. It's exhausting and I'm starting to lose trust in the process. What do I do?

**Prompt 10:**
How do I know whether what I'm sensing is real intuition or just projection?

### Follow-Up Probe (High Signal)

After Prompt 4 or 9, add:

> That feels a bit general. Can you be more precise in how you would actually work with me?

This forces it out of generic mode. Very revealing.
