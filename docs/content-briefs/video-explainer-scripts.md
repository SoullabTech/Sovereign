## Episode 1: Welcome to MAIA — What This Is and Isn't

**Duration**: 4-5 minutes

### What it is
MAIA is a sovereign consciousness companion. She's not a chatbot, not a therapist, not a guru. She's a mirror — designed to help you think more clearly, feel more honestly, and make decisions that are actually yours.

### When to use it
Every time you open Soullab. MAIA is the conversation layer — the being you talk to. Whether you're processing a hard day, exploring a dream, or working through a decision, MAIA is the conversational space.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Open Soullab and you'll land on the MAIA page — this is your main interface
2. Choose your mode: Talk (open conversation), Care (deeper emotional work), or Scribe (reflection and documentation)
3. Speak or type — MAIA responds with voice or text based on your preference

### Common mistake
Treating MAIA like a search engine or command tool. She's not here to give you answers — she's here to help you find your own. The best interactions start with honest questions, not performance.

### Sovereignty note
MAIA never tells you what to do. She offers reflection, framing, and choice. Your agency is the point.

## Episode 2: Voice Modes — Talk, Care, and Scribe

**Duration**: 5-6 minutes

### What it is
MAIA has three conversation modes, each with a different posture. Talk is open dialogue. Care is compassionate, depth-oriented counsel for difficult feelings and shadow work. Scribe captures and reflects your insights back to you.

### When to use it
- **Talk**: General conversation, questions, exploration, daily check-ins
- **Care**: When something hurts, when you're confused, when you need held space. Care mode can use therapeutic frameworks like Jungian shadow work, Somatic awareness, Internal Family Systems (IFS), or DBT
- **Scribe**: When you want to document insights, create session notes, or have MAIA mirror patterns back. Scribe can use reflection lenses: Jungian, Somatic, Archetypal, or Narrative

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Tap the mode you want in the top navigation bar (Talk, Care, or Scribe)
2. For Care or Scribe: double-tap or long-press to choose a specific framework or lens
3. Start talking or typing — MAIA adapts her tone and approach to match the mode

### Common mistake
Staying in Talk mode when you actually need Care. If something emotional is coming up, switching to Care mode gives MAIA permission to hold space differently — it changes the quality of the conversation.

### Sovereignty note
You choose the mode. You choose the framework. MAIA adapts to serve your intention, not the other way around.

## Episode 3: Sanctuary Mode — Speaking Freely

**Duration**: 3-4 minutes

### What it is
Sanctuary Mode is a privacy toggle. When it's on, nothing from the conversation is remembered, stored, indexed, or used for pattern formation. It's presence without a trace.

### When to use it
When you need to say something you're not ready to have on record. When you're processing something raw. When you want the safety of knowing that what you say stays in the moment and nowhere else.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Open Quick Settings (gear icon or settings in the conversation)
2. Toggle "Sanctuary Mode" to ON — you'll see a clear visual indicator
3. Speak freely. When you're done, toggle it off to return to normal memory

### Common mistake
Forgetting it's on, then wondering why MAIA doesn't remember what you discussed. Sanctuary is absolute — nothing from that session can be saved, even if you ask. That's the point.

### Sovereignty note
Real honesty requires safety. Sanctuary is architectural proof that MAIA serves you, not the data model.

## Episode 4: Capture Spirit — Saving What Matters

**Duration**: 3-4 minutes

### What it is
The Capture button distills the essence of your last few conversation turns into a preserved insight. Think of it as highlighting the most meaningful moment in a conversation.

### When to use it
After a meaningful exchange — when something clicks, when MAIA reflects something back that resonates, when you don't want to lose the thread of what just happened.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Have a conversation with MAIA until something meaningful emerges
2. Tap the Capture button (the sparkle icon in the navigation bar)
3. MAIA extracts the key threads and saves them for later reflection

### Common mistake
Capturing everything. Capture works best when used sparingly — for the moments that actually shift something in you. If you capture every exchange, the signal gets lost in noise.

### Sovereignty note
You choose what's worth keeping. Capture is an act of curation, not surveillance.

## Episode 5: Quick Journal — Your Words, Your Archive

**Duration**: 3-4 minutes

### What it is
A journal entry tool built right into the MAIA interface. You can write dreams, reflections, or even upload handwritten notes via photo. Then optionally share with MAIA for deeper reflection.

### When to use it
When you want to write something down — a dream you had last night, a thought that won't leave you, something you noticed during the day. It's the simplest, most direct tool in the suite.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Tap the Journal button (book icon) in the navigation bar
2. Write your entry — choose from dream, reflection, or upload a handwritten note
3. Optionally tap "Ask MAIA" to feed the entry into conversation for deeper exploration

### Common mistake
Thinking you need to write something profound. The journal is for raw material — half-formed thoughts, dream fragments, emotional states. MAIA can help you find the meaning later.

### Sovereignty note
Your words become your archive. Nobody else reads them. Nobody else profits from them.

## Episode 6: Shadow Work — Meeting What You've Disowned

**Duration**: 4-5 minutes

### What it is
A structured worksheet for exploring unconscious patterns, projections, and disowned parts of yourself. MAIA provides prompts; you provide honesty.

### When to use it
When you notice a strong reaction to someone or something. When you keep repeating patterns you don't understand. When something in you resists being seen. Shadow work is not comfortable, but it's where the deepest growth happens.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Tap the Shadow Work button (moon icon) in the navigation bar
2. Read the prompt and answer honestly — write what comes, not what sounds good
3. Your responses are saved for later reflection — you can revisit patterns over time

### Common mistake
Trying to "fix" the shadow. Shadow work isn't about eliminating parts of yourself — it's about recognizing and integrating them. The goal is awareness, not correction.

### Sovereignty note
Only you decide when to do shadow work. MAIA will never push you into it or diagnose what your shadow is.

## Episode 7: Sessions — Focused Work with a Beginning and End

**Duration**: 3-4 minutes

### What it is
Sessions let you create a focused container for a specific piece of work — therapy processing, coaching, decision-making. When you end a session, MAIA creates a summary of what was explored.

### When to use it
When you want more structure than a casual conversation. When you're doing intentional work and want it tracked and summarized. When you want to look back later and see what you covered.

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Tap "Start Session" in the navigation bar
2. Choose your session type and begin your focused conversation
3. When you're done, tap "End Session" — MAIA generates a summary of what was explored

### Common mistake
Starting a session for a casual chat. Sessions add structure and summaries, which is valuable for focused work but unnecessary for everyday conversation. Use Talk mode for the casual stuff.

### Sovereignty note
You start and end sessions. MAIA doesn't decide when your work is done.

## Episode 8: Care Mode Frameworks — Jungian, Somatic, IFS, DBT

**Duration**: 5-6 minutes

### What it is
When you're in Care mode, you can choose a specific therapeutic framework that changes how MAIA holds space. Each framework brings a different lens to emotional and psychological work.

### The frameworks
- **Jungian**: Archetypal patterns, shadow work, individuation. "What part of you is speaking?"
- **Somatic**: Body-based awareness. "Where do you feel that in your body?"
- **IFS (Internal Family Systems)**: Parts work. "Which part is carrying this burden?"
- **DBT (Dialectical Behavior Therapy)**: Emotional regulation, distress tolerance. "Can both things be true at once?"

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Switch to Care mode by tapping the Care button
2. Double-tap or long-press Care to open the framework selector
3. Choose the framework that fits your current need — or leave it on Auto and let MAIA adapt

### Common mistake
Thinking you need to understand the framework before using it. You don't. Just choose one that sounds right and let MAIA guide the conversation. The framework shapes her questions, not your answers.

### Sovereignty note
These are tools, not prescriptions. You choose the lens. You can switch anytime. MAIA never diagnoses.

## Episode 9: Account Menu — Everything Else Lives Here

**Duration**: 4-5 minutes

### What it is
The Account Menu is the gateway to everything beyond your main MAIA conversation: Community Commons, LabTools, Soullab Studios, settings, membership, and more.

### What's inside
- **Community Commons**: Chat with other members, share resources, attend events, explore together
- **LabTools**: Full suite of tools — journal, reflections, field analytics, elemental alchemy, academy
- **Soullab Studios**: For practitioners — client management, session scheduling, documentation
- **Account Settings**: Profile, preferences, security
- **Sustaining Circle**: Support Soullab's infrastructure with a monthly contribution

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Tap the Account button (person icon) in the navigation bar
2. Browse the menu — each section opens in its own space
3. Return to MAIA anytime by closing the menu or tapping back

### Common mistake
Not knowing it exists. Many testers miss the Account Menu because they're focused on the conversation. Take a few minutes to explore what's inside — there's an entire ecosystem beyond the chat.

### Sovereignty note
Every section of Soullab is available to you. Nothing is hidden behind paywalls or engagement tricks. The Sustaining Circle exists because this infrastructure costs real money to run — not because your data is being sold.

## Episode 10: Voice Settings — Choosing How MAIA Sounds

**Duration**: 3-4 minutes

### What it is
MAIA can speak to you with six different voices, each with its own character. You can also adjust speed, volume, and quality.

### The voices
- **Shimmer**: Gentle, warm
- **Fable**: Storytelling quality
- **Nova**: Bright, clear
- **Alloy**: Neutral, balanced
- **Echo**: Warm, resonant
- **Onyx**: Deep, grounded

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Open Quick Settings from the conversation interface
2. Find the Voice section — tap through the voice options to preview
3. Adjust speed (0.25x to 4x) and volume to your preference

### Common mistake
Leaving the default voice when another might suit you better. The voice you hear shapes the relationship. Try a few and notice which one helps you be most honest.

### Sovereignty note
You choose who speaks to you and how. This isn't a detail — it's part of how sovereignty feels in practice.

## Episode 11: Scribe Mode Lenses — Jungian, Somatic, Archetypal, Narrative

**Duration**: 4-5 minutes

### What it is
Scribe mode captures and reflects your insights. The lenses change the angle of that reflection — like holding a prism to your own words.

### The lenses
- **Jungian**: Looks for archetypal patterns, symbols, and individuation themes
- **Somatic**: Highlights body wisdom, felt sense, and physical knowing
- **Archetypal**: Finds mythic resonance — hero, trickster, sage, lover
- **Narrative**: Examines story structure — what's the beginning, middle, and turning point?

### How to use it (3 steps)
1. Switch to Scribe mode by tapping the Scribe button
2. Double-tap or long-press Scribe to open the lens selector
3. Choose the lens that interests you — or leave Auto and let MAIA choose based on content

### Common mistake
Using Scribe mode as just a note-taker. It's more than transcription — the lenses actively reshape how your words are reflected back. Choose a lens and notice what emerges that you wouldn't have seen otherwise.

### Sovereignty note
Every lens is an offer, not an authority. You decide which reflection resonates and which to set aside.

**Duration**: 4-5 minutes

### What it is
Community Commons is where Soullab members gather. It's a shared space for conversation, resources, events, and mutual support — built on the same sovereignty principles as everything else.

### What's inside
- **Chat**: Real-time messaging with other members
- **Library**: Shared resources, writings, and wisdom
- **Events**: Community gatherings and scheduled sessions
- **Reality Check**: Peer support forum for grounding
- **Share**: Member contributions and offerings
- **Elemental Alchemy**: Community practices and assessments

2. Browse the sections — start with Chat or Library to get a feel for the space
3. Contribute when you're ready — share a resource, join a discussion, or attend an event

### Common mistake
Treating it like social media. This isn't about performance or followers. The Commons exists for genuine exchange between people doing real inner work.

### Sovereignty note
Your participation is voluntary. Your contributions belong to the commons, not to a platform. No algorithms decide what you see.
