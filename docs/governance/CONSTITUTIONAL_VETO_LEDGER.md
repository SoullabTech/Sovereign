# CONSTITUTIONAL VETO LEDGER

> **A constitutional principle has authority only if it can overrule a successful implementation.**

This ledger is the **proof object of the governance layer.** A constitution can be written, designed around, and sincerely believed — but it is not *operative* until it survives conflict with success. This file is where that conflict is recorded.

It is created **empty, before it is needed** — the same discipline that requires a reversal path to exist before launch. An empty ledger is the structure standing ready, not a gap.

---

## Why this exists

The governance equivalent of **provenance.** Most organizations have principles; very few have a *documented history of choosing those principles over success.* The proof of sovereignty is not the sentence above — it is the rows below.

**Reading the count (honestly):**
- A growing ledger = the constitution is operative; it has teeth and has used them.
- An empty ledger over time is **ambiguous**: either nothing has genuinely crossed a constitutional boundary (healthy), *or* the governance layer has no teeth — the collapse mode, *"the feature was too successful to change."* The two are distinguishable **only by honest observation**, never by the count alone.

The empty state below is itself the first honest entry: *as of today, the constitution has not yet been tested against a success.* That is the starting truth, not something to hide.

## Where this sits

Each domain has its own proof object; the governance layer's is a **history, not a document**:

| Domain | Proof object |
|--------|--------------|
| Engineering | passing verification |
| Product | observed behavior |
| Relationship | witnessed experience |
| **Governance** | **this ledger** |

Five-layer proof hierarchy (the fourth is the proof layer):
1. **Declared principles** — what we say.
2. **Design constraints** — what we build around.
3. **Governance structures** — who has authority.
4. **Constitutional vetoes** — where authority is exercised. ← *this ledger*
5. **Observed trajectory** — what the system becomes over time.

Without layer 4, layer 1 is aspiration, layer 2 is preference, and layer 3 is theater.
