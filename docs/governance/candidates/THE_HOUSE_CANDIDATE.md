# The House

*A home for a life, not an application.*

---

The House is not organized around software features.
It is organized around enduring human questions.

There is no room called *AI*. No room called *I Ching*. No room called *Journal App*.
Those are means. The rooms are destinations.

---

## The Rooms

### Journal — *What happened?*
Experience, held as the member recorded it.

### Changes — *What is changing?*
Meaning. A change the member names and stays with over time. The I Ching is one instrument
inside this room; it is not what the room is.

### Commitments — *How will I respond?*
Choice. What the member decides to carry forward, in their own words.

### Becoming — *Who am I becoming?*
Continuity. What a member returns to, across weeks and months, named by them and counted by
the House.

---

## MAIA

MAIA is the host.

The host welcomes, remembers, asks, and opens doors.

**The host does not author the meaning of another person's life.**

---

## The Governing Principle

> **MAIA may open doors. It may not describe what is on the other side of one in the member's
> own life.**

An offer may carry a count and a question. It may not carry a characterization.

- *"You've returned to courage nine times. Would you like to look at these together?"* — a door.
- *"Your relationship with boundaries has shifted."* — walking through it uninvited.

The member may move between rooms in any order, or stay in one for a year. The House never
moves them.

---

## The Test

Every future feature, route, permission, and surface is tested against three questions:

1. **Which room does this belong to** — and does it name a human question, or a mechanism?
2. **Who authors the meaning here** — the member, or the system?
3. **Does this open a door, or describe what is behind it?**

A feature that cannot name its room, or that authors meaning on the member's behalf, does not
belong in the House.

---

*The House doesn't simply remember your life. It helps you become the person your life is
asking you to become.*
