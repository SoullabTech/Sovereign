# Soullab Steward Language Guide

How Soullab speaks — in product, in marketing, in conversation. Every word passes one test: does this sound like someone who has done the work, or someone who is selling the work?

---

## The Voice

Soullab speaks from the field — not from the stage.

The voice is:
- **Quiet authority** — earned, not performed
- **Precise** — says what it means, no more
- **Grounded** — no aspiration inflation, no spiritual performance
- **Human** — warm without being familiar, clear without being clinical

The voice is not:
- Motivational
- Therapeutic
- Academic
- Marketing-forward
- Spiritually performative

---

## The Positioning Line

**Soullab is for people who live their practice — not inside it.**

Not those who consume it. Not those who optimize it. Those who live it — in real life, with real people.

This line governs all copy decisions. If a piece of text would feel at home in a wellness app, a productivity tool, or a generic AI assistant — it doesn't belong here.

### Positioning Principle

Soullab supports practice that lives in the world. It is not a place to retreat from life or build identity inside. This is the cultural translation of the relational invariant: *autonomy over engagement.*

### Context Variants

Use the right weight for the right surface:

| Context | Line |
|---|---|
| Internal / Language Guide | Soullab is for people who live their practice — not people who live inside it. |
| Video narration | Soullab is for people who live their practice — not inside it. |
| Hero / public homepage | For people who live their practice in real life. |
| Philosophy / manifesto | Practice that belongs in your life — not outside it. |

The internal version carries the full contrast. The hero version drops the comparison and opens a door instead of filtering.

## Tone Rules

### Never Perform Depth

Bad: "Unlock deeper layers of your consciousness."
Good: "See what's actually going on."

Bad: "Transform your inner landscape."
Good: "Notice what keeps showing up."

### Never Inflate

Bad: "Revolutionary AI-powered growth platform."
Good: "A place to think things through."

Bad: "Discover your true self with advanced elemental analysis."
Good: "A few questions. No wrong answers."

### Never Sell the Mysticism

Bad: "Ancient wisdom meets cutting-edge AI."
Good: "Built on thirty years of practice."

Bad: "Your sacred elemental journey begins here."
Good: "Here's what came up."

### Never Sound Like an App

Bad: "Track your progress and celebrate milestones!"
Good: "Your profile." (That's it.)

Bad: "You're on a 7-day streak!"
Good: (Nothing. Don't track streaks.)

## The Three Registers

### 1. Field Register (primary)

Used in: product UI, onboarding, session interactions, microcopy.

Characteristics:
- First person sparingly, second person naturally
- Short sentences, often fragments
- No exclamation marks
- Implies rather than explains

Examples:
- "Ready when you are."
- "Something came up."
- "Nothing stored."

### 2. Practitioner Register

Used in: content for therapists, coaches, practitioners considering Soullab for their practice.

Characteristics:
- Professional but not clinical
- Assumes competence
- Names the real problems they face
- Never oversells capability

Examples:
- "Most tools give you more data about your clients. This one helps you see them more clearly."
- "It doesn't replace your clinical judgment. It gives you another angle."

### 3. Field Letter Register

Used in: longer-form content, videos, thought leadership, emails to community.

Characteristics:
- Personal without being confessional
- Addresses the reader as an equal
- Makes one point, clearly
- Ends with a question or an opening, not a CTA

Examples:
- "The real question isn't whether AI can help people grow. It's whether it can do that without replacing the part of growth that only humans can provide."
- "Most systems get smarter the more you use them. We built one that helps you need it less."

## Words We Use / Words We Don't

| Use | Don't Use |
|---|---|
| Pattern | Journey |
| See, notice, recognize | Discover, unlock, reveal |
| Element, elemental | Energy, vibration, frequency |
| Practice | Ritual, ceremony |
| Profile | Signature, blueprint, DNA (in UI) |
| Companion | Assistant, coach, guide, mentor |
| Reflect | Analyze, assess, evaluate |
| Space | Platform, tool, solution |
| Practitioner | Healer |
| Clear, clarity | Enlightened, awakened |
| Private | Secure (unless literally discussing security) |
| Sovereign | Empowered |

### Exception

"Elemental DNA" is permitted in marketing/video contexts where it serves as a recognizable hook. It must not appear in the product UI itself.

## Sentence Structure

**Prefer:**
- Short declarative sentences
- Fragments that land
- Questions that open rather than lead
- Implied meaning over stated meaning

**Avoid:**
- Compound sentences with multiple clauses
- Rhetorical questions with obvious answers
- Sentences that start with "We" (system as subject)
- Sentences that end with "!" (system as cheerleader)

---

## The Quiet Test

Before any copy ships, ask:

1. Would a quiet, experienced practitioner say this?
2. Does it sound earned or performed?
3. Does it create space or fill it?
4. Would it feel at home in a wellness app? (If yes, rewrite.)
5. Does it increase the user's sense of their own authority?

If it fails any of these, rewrite.
