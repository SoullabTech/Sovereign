## 1. Origin — "What is yours?"

Begin with authorship, not with the domains.

**Framework**
- When you think about your body of work, what are the ideas you feel you created or shaped?
- What language do you use that feels distinctly yours?
- What concepts would you recognize immediately as part of your work?
- What should someone understand before they work with you?

**Evolution**
- Where did *Now What?* come from?
- Was there a moment when the framework changed or crystallized?
- What did you believe before this framework existed that you no longer believe?

**Boundaries**
- Are there parts of your work that are yours but should never be surfaced by an AI?
- Are there things you teach only inside a live relationship?
- Are there things that require your presence?

## 3. Lineage — "What shaped your thinking?"

This movement separates Larry's *world* from Larry's *ownership*.

- What thinkers, researchers, books, or teachers have most influenced your work?
- What ideas have you borrowed, adapted, or built upon?
- Are there sources you want MAIA to know are part of your intellectual lineage?

Classify each answer along two independent axes:

| Question | Records |
|---|---|
| "Did this influence you?" | lineage |
| "Do you teach this idea?" | practice reference |
| "Did you create this?" | authored |
| "May MAIA reproduce this?" | permission |

⛔⛔ **A lineage answer never authorizes text.** Larry can license his *selection* — that he studies a source — not the source. The Harvard PSY 1060 slides are Prof. Jason Mitchell's; they may be recorded as lineage and may **never** appear on the authored list or be reproduced. Same for any book, paper, or deck he did not write.

## 4. Practice wisdom — "How do you work?"

The richest layer and the most sensitive.

⛔ Never ask: *"Tell us your client cases."*

- What patterns do you see repeatedly in executives?
- What questions unlock insight?
- What shifts do you hope people experience?
- Where do leaders commonly get stuck?
- What distinctions help someone see something differently?

**If an example arises:**

| Capture | Do not capture |
|---|---|
| the generalized pattern | any identifiable client story |
| the teaching principle | private case history |
| Larry's own framing of it | confidential detail |

Rationale: a client's experience is not Larry's to license. Only Larry teaching *about* his practice is inventoriable here.

## 5. Attachment A completion sequence

Run per item, in this order. An item with any unanswered field stays out.

1. **Identity** — "What is this?" → authored framework · teaching · exercise · story · reference · influence · third-party material
2. **Ownership** — "Who owns this?" → Larry · another author · shared/influenced · unknown
3. **MAIA use** — "What may happen with this?" → `ground` · `offer` · `reference_only` · `private` · `never`
4. **Notes** — "What context does MAIA need so it does not misuse this?"

Unknown ownership → the row is blocked, not deferred. Unknown use → `never`.
