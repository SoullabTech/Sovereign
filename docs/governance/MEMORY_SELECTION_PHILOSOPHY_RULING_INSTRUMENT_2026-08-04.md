## The finding, correctly classified (founder, 2026-08-04)

> **The 8 is not an arbitrary absence of intelligence. It is an ungoverned selection policy.**

Principles are already embedded — consent (WHERE), breakthrough priority, recency ordering,
implicit cost cap. What is absent is not a principle but a **declared relational principle**: the
system has an editorial stance with no constitutional statement for that stance.

**The hidden authority is the ORDER BY, not the LIMIT.** Current de-facto worldview:
`importance + recency = relevance`. Measured effect: 133 stored → 128 consented → 8 injected;
relevance to the present moment never participates; origin-era material is structurally
unreachable once >8 newer atoms exist.

## The ruling question (narrowed)

> **When multiple memories could serve this moment, what should outrank what?**

Not "increase memory." Not "remove the limit."

## Why one ranking may not suffice — the four memory acts (founder)

| The member asks… | The memory act | What it needs ranked first |
|---|---|---|
| *"What did we discuss yesterday?"* | recency | newest |
| *"Do you remember how we began?"* | **origin** | oldest / thread-founding |
| *"What patterns do you see in my growth?"* | developmental synthesis | enduring threads |
| *"What did I commit to?"* | covenant/commitment | marked commitments |

These are **different memory acts**. The current policy answers only the first, always.

## The core fork (founder, refined)

> **Does MAIA serve the person by selecting memories according to a fixed ranking, or by
> recognizing the kind of relationship the moment is asking for?**

A single ordering is not wrong — *it is a philosophy that was never explicitly chosen.*

## Decision space (founder-refined)

- **Option A — Current policy, consciously ratified.** *"We intentionally privilege marked
  significance and recency."* Simple, predictable, auditable; some forms of human continuity remain
  underserved — **not failure if chosen consciously.**
- **Option B — Revised universal hierarchy.** `consent → present relevance → developmental
  significance → explicit importance → recency`. Clearer philosophy, easier implementation;
  **risk: every universal ranking encodes assumptions about human meaning.** Requires relevance to
  enter the query (new capability → Growth-obligation check applies).
- **Option C — Memory-act routing.** Route by the kind of remembering requested (continuity →
  recent · origin → foundational · development → longitudinal · commitment → covenant). Closest to
  how humans remember. **Hardest governance question:**

  > *When MAIA classifies the moment, is she understanding the request or interpreting the person?*

  **The test:** *"Are we discussing our first conversation?"* is explicit — **navigation**. *"This
  seems like a moment of transformation for you"* is interpretive — **meaning-making**, which
  belongs to the member. Only the first class may drive routing silently.

- **Option C′ — Invitation-form routing (founder's resolution of C's hazard).** MAIA does not
  decide the meaning of the moment; she recognizes **patterns of invitation** and offers:

  > ⛔ Not: *"This is a developmental moment, so I will retrieve developmental memories."*
  > ✅ *"This conversation may connect with earlier themes of growth and change. Would it be useful
  > to explore those?"*

  The routing becomes a **consented gesture** — the member confirms the memory act before it
  executes. Same principle as the elemental invitation rule (*mirror, not mold*). The person
  remains the final interpreter.

- **All options:** observability first (already ruled — "why these memories?" becomes answerable
  before any reordering); ⛔ no retrieval-volume increase as a side effect.

## The staged trajectory (founder-articulated, 2026-08-04 — endorsed shape; formal ruling below)

**Stage 1 — End the accidental state.** Ratify the current selection policy *as a known policy,
not an invisible default*. The declaration language matters:

> Not: *"newest memories are the right memories."*
> But: **"This is the current memory participation policy, with known strengths and limitations,
> until a more context-sensitive model is proven."**

*Making the boundary visible is the first act of integrity.* The problem was never that the system
chooses eight — it is that it cannot account for why those eight crossed.

**Stage 2 — Build the observability layer.** Before changing selection, MAIA answers: what sources
participated · what was available · what was selected · what was not · why · what remained unknown.
This is the Corpus Callosum function — not *"I have a better memory"* but **"I know the
relationship between what I could access and what I offered."**

**Stage 3 — Move toward C′ (invitation-based retrieval).** From *"which memory wins?"* to *"what
kind of remembering is being requested?"* The Keep pattern, applied to retrieval:

```
Before invitation acceptance:  MAIA may notice — "there may be earlier material connected to this."
After member acceptance:       MAIA explores that thread.
```

This prevents the system quietly mining a person's history and presenting interpretations as
discoveries. The central safeguard remains **navigation vs meaning-making**:
- ✅ Navigation (member-initiated): *"You asked about our early work — I can look for earlier
  threads related to that."*
- ⚠️ Meaning-making (requires the invitation form): *"This earlier pattern seems significant —
  does it feel meaningful to you?"* The member remains the interpreter.

**Stage 4 — Meaning stays with the member.** Permanent, not a phase.

### The governing paradox

> **MAIA's memory should become more responsive without becoming more presumptive.**
> More continuity, less certainty.
> *Remember enough to accompany. Observe enough to reflect. Know enough to remain humble.*

A system that forgets everything cannot accompany deeply; a system that remembers everything and
assigns meaning to everything becomes intrusive. **The deepest decision is whether memory becomes
a tool for deeper presence or a mechanism for greater authority. The entire Corpus Callosum work
points to the first.**

> **Memory increases MAIA's capacity to accompany; it does not increase her authority to define
