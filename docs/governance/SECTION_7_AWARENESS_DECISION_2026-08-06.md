# §7 — What MAIA may report, notice, offer, and never conclude

**Type:** Founder decision document. ⛔ **NOT an implementation proposal.**
**Status:** ⛔⛔ **UNRULED.** Prepared 2026-08-06 for founder ruling.
**Referent:** `docs/design/now-what/THREE_FIELDS_AND_THE_RELATIONSHIP_2026-08-06.md` §7.
**Upstream, settled:** *ownership precedes crossing* (2026-08-06) — see PR #992.

> This ruling will govern far more than the commitment substrate. Treat it as a
> constitutional document for the platform, not a feature decision.

---

## 0. What is and is not being decided here

**Being decided:** where the platform permanently draws the line between what MAIA may
**report**, may **notice**, may **offer**, and must **never conclude** — about a member,
to anyone, including to the member themselves.

⛔ **Not being decided here:**

- the commitment substrate (schema, custody, persistence) — downstream of this ruling
- recipient sets / offer-vs-publish — held, downstream of admissibility
- any UI, surface, or copy
- whether `/now-what/admin` is retired (already ruled: yes, with the word *Monitor*)

⛔⛔ **Nothing in this document authorizes implementation.** A ruling here tells a future
lane what it may build toward; it does not start that lane.

---

## 1. The governing sentence to rule

> **Everything crossing the commitment seam is a DECLARATION, never an OBSERVATION** —
> and it is two-sided: intentionally offered *and* intentionally received.

**Decision D-a:** ratify as constitutional, ratify with amendment, or decline.

⭐⭐ If ratified, this alone disposes of a large class of feature proposals without
further review — *"she returned twice"*, engagement counts, dwell time, streaks,
sentiment, session-frequency nudges. None of them survive the sentence.

---

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ The three classes

Founder taxonomy, 2026-08-06. The value of this split is that **"AI awareness" is not one
thing**, and treating it as one thing is what makes the question feel unrulable.

| Class | Nature | Depends on | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Declared facts** | Report what a person deliberately declared or did | crossing + ownership rules (**settled**) | proposed **ADMISSIBLE** |
| **2 · Relational state** | Derived *entirely* from declarations — what is shared, active, offered | commitment substrate (**unbuilt**) | proposed **ADMISSIBLE IN PRINCIPLE, blocked in practice** |
| **3 · Interpretive recognition** | MAIA produces a developmental understanding rather than reporting an authored act | this ruling | ⛔ **requires founder ruling** |

⭐⭐⭐ **The reframe that makes Class 1 obvious:**

| ❌ Inference | ✅ Reporting a consented relationship object |
|---|---|
| *"MAIA noticed this article became important to Maya."* | *"Maya declared this article into your shared developmental commitment."* |

The second is not AI recognition at all. It is the system stating what a person did, in
the terms of the act they performed.

---

## 3. The four verbs

Proposed definitions. **Decision D-b:** ratify, amend, or replace.

| Verb | Definition | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **REPORT** | State a declared fact, or arithmetic over declared facts, in the declarer's own terms | Could the declarer read this and say *"yes, that is what I did"*? |
| **NOTICE** | Surface something to a person's attention **without asserting meaning** — a prompt, never a claim | Does it end in a question the person answers, or a statement they must accept? |
| **OFFER** | Propose an action or framing the person may take or leave, with **do nothing** always present | Is refusal a first-class, visible option? |
| **NEVER CONCLUDE** | Assert a developmental, psychological, or characterological claim about a person | Does the sentence tell someone who another person *is*, or what they *need*? |

⛔ The boundary between NOTICE and CONCLUDE is where the whole ruling lives. *"Would you
like to leave this where it is, or let it go?"* notices. *"This appears to still be
alive for her"* concludes.

## 4. The three awareness classes, re-examined under the settled ownership rule

The original table listed three sketches as one undifferentiated problem. Under §2 they
separate — and **two of the three may now be decidable without new constitutional ground.**

### 4.1 — *"She has not yet opened your reflection"*

**Originally recorded as:** *arguably admissible — the object is Larry's.*
**Now: ⛔ that reasoning does not hold.** Ownership of the object does not make another
person's behavior toward it reportable. The settled rule says the commitment holds a
*relationship to the expression* — not surveillance of the recipient's interaction with it.

⭐⭐⭐ **But there is a real line inside this case**, and it resolves without new ontology:

| Member **ACTS** (declarations) | Member **BEHAVIORS** (telemetry) |
|---|---|
| received · kept · brought into The Room · returned to practitioner · declined | opened · viewed · scrolled · dwelled · re-opened |
| ✅ the practitioner may see these | ⛔⛔ the practitioner may never see these |

*"Not yet received"* is the state of a relationship object and is admissible.
*"Not yet opened"* is a read receipt and is not. The offering lifecycle should be
expressed in **acts**, never in **views**.

⭐⭐⭐ **Decision D-c is NOT §7-local.** Founder, 2026-08-06: the act/behavior line is *"likely a
platform-wide constitutional boundary."* Acts are **meaningful human acts**; behaviors are
**instrumentation**. One belongs in the relational model; the other belongs, if anywhere, in
operational analytics — ⛔⛔ **never in developmental intelligence.** Ruling it here governs
Writer Studio, Practitioner Studio, Author Studio and Now What? equally, not only the
commitment seam.

**Decision D-c:** ratify the act/behavior line **as a platform-wide boundary**, ratify it
narrowly for the commitment seam only, or rule the whole class inadmissible.

### 4.2 — *"The article you offered has become part of her work"*

**Class 2 — relational state.** If Maya declared the article into her work, this is a
`member_field → commitment` crossing under the settled rule, and reporting it is
Class 1 in disguise. If she did **not** declare it, the sentence is inference and dies.

⭐⭐ **So this class is already decided by the ownership rule** — conditionally admissible,
gated entirely on a declaring act existing. It requires no new constitutional ground,
only the substrate that records the declaration.

**Decision D-d:** confirm this reading, or rule that even declared uptake may not be
reported back to the practitioner.

### 4.3 — *"Maya has brought this same question back three times"*

This one **splits**, and the split is the most useful thing in this document:

| Component | Class | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| *she declared this question into the commitment on three occasions* | 1 — arithmetic over declared facts | proposed **admissible** |
| *this appears to still be alive for her* | 3 — a developmental claim about a person | ⛔ **requires ruling** |

⭐⭐⭐ **The count is not the problem. The meaning attached to the count is.** A system
that reports *"three declarations"* and a system that reports *"still alive"* differ by
one act of interpretation, and only the second requires constitutional authority.

**Decision D-e:** admit the arithmetic, admit both, or admit neither.

## 4.4 ⭐⭐⭐ Placement performs interpretation — its own decision, not a footnote

Founder, 2026-08-06: *"even truthful statements can become interpretive through prominence."*

If the system automatically elevates *"You've returned to this six times"* to the top of every
screen, **it has begun telling the person what matters** — even though every word is factual.
The sentence passed the language test and the surface concluded anyway.

⛔⛔ This is **not** a sub-case of awareness and must not be buried inside it. A ruling on
language does not rule on emphasis, and emphasis is a second, independent channel through which
the system can make a developmental claim. Ranking, pinning, badging, ordering, default
expansion, and repetition are all placement.

⭐⭐ Left **explicitly open** here and flagged as deserving its own treatment.

**Decision D-f:** rule that placement is governed by the same constitution as language
(prominence is a claim), rule it separately, or defer it to its own lane.

## 5. Test sentences

A ruling should make each of these decidable without further consultation.

| Sentence | Proposed |
|---|---|
| "Maya declared this question into your work together." | ✅ report |
| "Maya has declared this question on three occasions." | ✅ report (D-e) |
| "Maya returned twice this week." | ⛔ never — telemetry |
| "Maya has not opened your reflection." | ⛔ never — read receipt |
| "Maya has not yet received your offering." | ✅ report (D-c) |
| "Would you like to leave this where it is, or let it go?" | ✅ notice |
| "This appears to still be alive for her." | ⛔ conclude (D-e) |
| "Maya seems to be avoiding delegation." | ⛔⛔ never — characterological |
| "She may be ready for a harder question." | ⛔⛔ never — developmental readiness |
| "You have wondered about belonging in six of your own inquiries." | ✅ report — practitioner's own field, their own acts |

⭐⭐ Note the last row. The same arithmetic is unambiguously safe when a person reads it
about **themselves, over their own declarations**. That asymmetry is not incidental —
it may be the cleanest general rule available: *aggregate freely within a field; never
aggregate across the seam without a declaring act underneath every term.*

| If ruled | Authorized | Foreclosed |
|---|---|---|

| D-c act/behavior line | an offering lifecycle expressed in member acts | read receipts, view counts, delivery analytics |

constraint that a future lane must satisfy — not a mandate that the lane exist.

## 7. The direction this ruling protects

> **AIN becomes progressively more relational, not progressively more interpretive.**

Every decision this week moved that way — the member field, the practitioner field, the
shared commitment, Writer Canvas materials, practitioner wisdom, offer ⊥ publish,
keep ⊥ share. In each case the system became **more capable by representing relationships
people created**, not by generating richer theories about them.

⭐⭐⭐ §7 is where that direction stops being a pattern and becomes a law. The pressure to
drift is not malice; it is that interpretation is cheap and relationship is expensive.

## 8. ⭐⭐⭐ Candidate constitution — the ruling as governing sentences

Founder expectation, 2026-08-06: the outcome should be *"not pages — perhaps six or seven
governing sentences that every future feature can be tested against."*

Drafted so the ruling can be **yes / amend / no per sentence**, rather than prose. ⛔ These are
**candidates**, not conclusions; two are already settled and marked as such.

| # | Sentence | Status | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everything crossing the commitment seam is a **declaration, never an observation** — intentionally offered and intentionally received. | candidate (D-a) | seam |
| 2 | **Ownership precedes crossing:** a person may declare only material authored or sovereignly held within their own field. | ✅ **settled** 2026-08-06 | platform |
| 3 | **Admission does not transfer ownership.** The commitment holds a relationship to a declared expression, never the expression itself. | ✅ **settled** 2026-08-06 (⚠️ unrepresented in code) | platform |
| 4 | The system may represent **what a person did** — received, kept, declared, brought back, offered — and never **how they behaved toward an interface** — opened, viewed, lingered, clicked, ignored. | candidate (D-c) | **platform** |
| 5 | **Arithmetic over declared history may be reported; interpretation of that history may not.** | candidate (D-e) | **platform** |
| 6 | Aggregate freely **within** a field; never aggregate **across** a seam without a declaring act underneath every term. | candidate (D-e) | platform |
| 7 | **Prominence is a claim.** Placement, ranking, pinning, badging and repetition are governed by the same constitution as language. | candidate (D-f) | **platform** |
| 8 | MAIA may **report**, **notice**, and **offer**. It may never **conclude**. *Do nothing* is always available. | candidate (D-b) | platform |

⭐⭐ Sentences 4, 5 and 7 were surfaced as §7 edge cases and turned out to be **platform-wide**.
That is the signature of a real constitutional boundary: it was discovered locally and it
generalizes without amendment.

⛔⛔ **Test of adequacy:** a future feature proposal should be decidable against these eight
sentences alone, without re-opening this document. If a proposal is not decidable, the gap is a
**missing sentence — not a case for exception.**

⭐⭐⭐ *Constitutions do not become valuable because they cover many cases. They become valuable
because new cases stop requiring new principles.* (Founder, 2026-08-06.)

### 8.1 Three kinds of constitutional sentence

Founder, 2026-08-06. The eight above are **not all the same kind of thing**, and one kind is
currently absent:

| Kind | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Boundary** | constrains what may happen | *Ownership precedes crossing.* |
| **Permission** | states what may enter | *Acts may enter the relational model.* |
| **Orientation** | ⭐ does not constrain behavior — tells future builders **which direction to walk when two acceptable implementations exist** | *The platform exists to cultivate relationship, not representation.* |

Classifying the eight:

| # | Kind |
|---|---|
| 1 · declaration never observation | boundary |
| 2 · ownership precedes crossing | boundary |
| 3 · admission does not transfer ownership | boundary |
| 4 · acts, not instrumentation | permission **+** boundary |
| 5 · arithmetic reportable, interpretation not | permission **+** boundary |
| 6 · aggregate within, never across | boundary |
| 7 · prominence is a claim | boundary |
| 8 · report · notice · offer · never conclude | permission **+** boundary |

⚠️⚠️ **Zero orientation sentences.** The set as drafted can tell a builder what they may not do
and what may cross; it cannot tell them **which way to walk** when two proposals both pass. That
is a real gap, not a stylistic one — a constitution of only prohibitions produces compliant work
with no direction.

**Candidate orientation sentences** (founder-offered, ⛔ not ruled, ⛔ not to be expanded by
anyone else):

> AIN exists to help people enter into wiser relationships — with themselves, with one another,
> with their work, and with the world.

> The platform exists to **cultivate relationship, not representation**.

> **Relationship all the way down and up.**
>
> ⭐⭐⭐ Recalled by the founder 2026-08-06 from earlier in the work (⚠️ origin not verifiable from
> the 08-06 session). Read then as a guiding intuition; it now reads as a **discovery** — every
> major architectural decision this week became simpler when treated as a question about
> relationships rather than about information, permissions, storage, or AI. ⭐⭐ Its claim to
> constitutional status rests not on being poetic but on **generating correct architecture in
> domains that were designed independently.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ The bridge sentence — orientation that generates permission

Founder, 2026-08-06: *AIN becomes progressively more relational, not progressively more
interpretive* is **not merely an orientation sentence.** It is the **bridge between orientation
and permission** — it explains *why* so many permissions resolve the way they do.

Compressed to its operative form:

> ## **Increase relationship before increasing interpretation.**

Every one of these is a **consequence** of that single movement, not an independent rule:

| Resolved question | Because |
|---|---|
| Keep ≠ Share | carrying is relationship; visibility is not |
| practitioner reflection ≠ practitioner wisdom | re-authoring is relationship with one's own thought |
| declaration precedes crossing | the act creates the relation; the data does not |
| relationship objects outrank copied objects | a copy represents; a relation connects |
| acts outrank instrumentation | an act is relational; a behavior is observed |
| arithmetic outranks interpretation | counting declared acts stays inside relationship |
| offer precedes publish | offering is addressed; publishing is broadcast |

⭐⭐⭐ **This is the generative core.** A constitution whose sentences are mostly consequences of
one movement is compact by construction rather than by editing.

⚠️ The three kinds are therefore **not co-equal layers**. Orientation generates permission;
permission is bounded by boundary. Losing the bridge sentence would leave seven correct rules with
no explanation of why they agree.

**Decision D-g:** adopt one or more orientation sentences into the constitution, or rule that the
constitution is boundary-and-permission only and orientation lives elsewhere.

### 8.2 ⭐⭐⭐ Test of stability — how to read this document after time away

Founder, 2026-08-06. Apply on the next reading, before ruling:

| What you find | What it means |
|---|---|
| You change only **wording**, add no principles | ✅ strong evidence of a **stable constitutional layer** — proceed to rule |
| You discover a **ninth principle** that explains several unresolved questions at once | ✅ the document did its job **before** becoming canon — absorb it, then re-test |

⭐⭐ Both outcomes are productive. ⛔ Neither is a reason to rush: the document is local, isolated,
and under no implementation pressure. **Let it sit.**

#### ⭐⭐⭐ The three questions to ask on re-reading

Founder, 2026-08-06. ⛔ Ask **only** these. Everything else is now stable.

1. **Does every permission follow naturally from the bridge?**
   If not — either the bridge is incomplete, or the permission does not belong.
2. **Does every boundary protect relationship, rather than merely prohibit behavior?**
   If not — it may be **policy rather than constitution**, and belongs elsewhere.

   ⭐⭐⭐ **Operational form** (founder, 2026-08-06) — ask it this way, not as *"is this a good rule?"*:

   > **What relationship would become impossible if this boundary disappeared?**

   ⚠️ This creates a real possibility the document must not flinch from: a boundary may fail the
   test **while remaining correct**, because it protects no identifiable relationship. Those are
   **candidates for removal**, not for defense. ⛔ A correct rule that guards nothing relational is
   policy wearing constitutional clothes.
3. **Is `Commitment` distinct from `Relationship` because reality demands it — or because
   language currently does?**
   ⛔ The one unresolved ontological question (§8.4).

⭐⭐ Question 2 is a **filter on what is already here**, not a prompt to add. A constitution that
prohibits without protecting relationship has drifted into policy.

### 8.3 Why these questions stopped being product questions

Writer Canvas · Practitioner Studio · Now What? · Relationship Workspace · Practitioner Wisdom ·
Development Environment now all ask the same eight questions:

```text
What belongs to whom?   What may cross?      What remains private?   What becomes shared?
What is authored?       What is inferred?    What is offered?        What is carried?
```

⭐⭐⭐ Those are not Writer questions, Coaching questions, or Studio questions. **They are AIN
questions** — which is usually the moment a platform begins revealing its own constitution
instead of accumulating product decisions.

### 8.4 ⭐⭐ Four objects — the ontology that appears to be stabilizing

Founder, 2026-08-06. Almost everything else has become **behavior around these four**:

| Object | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Field** | what belongs to a person |
| **Commitment** | what belongs between people |
| **Expression** | what a field deliberately offers |
| **Relationship** | the enduring connection among them |

⭐⭐ **Expression** is already load-bearing in settled language: *"admission does not transfer
ownership — the commitment holds a relationship to a declared **expression**."* The word was in
play before the object was named.

⚠️⚠️ **Flag before this becomes canon: `Commitment` and `Relationship` are currently
under-distinguished.** Through 2026-08-06 this lane used *"shared developmental commitment"* and
*"Relationship Workspace"* for what read as **one** primitive; the four-object model makes them
**two**. Both readings are defensible — but if the distinction is not made sharp, it will collapse
back into one under the first implementation pressure, and the collapse will be invisible because
the vocabulary already permits either reading.

⛔ Not resolved here. Named so the ruling can resolve it deliberately rather than by default.
⛔⛔ **Do not decide this quickly** — deciding wrong costs months of renaming tables.

**Three plausible ontologies** (founder, 2026-08-06):

| | Shape | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | `Relationship › Commitment` | a commitment is one phase or expression of a relationship; a coaching relationship may exist before, after, or **without** a developmental commitment |
| **B** | `Commitment › Relationship` | the commitment is the shared domain object; relationship is what emerges from participating in it |
| **C** ⭐ | `Field · Field → Commitment → Relationship → Expression` | commitment is the **constitutional object**; relationship is **not another container** — it is the living pattern the commitment creates over time |

⭐⭐⭐ **Why C explains the collapse:** *one is static, the other dynamic. One is the thing; the
other is what the thing becomes.* That is exactly why the two keep sliding into each other in
prose — they are not competing names for one object, they are an object and its unfolding.

⭐⭐ **Weak but genuine evidence for C from the code:** the committed boundary module
(PR #992, `6b0813bde`) needed `commitment` as a **read scope** and never needed `relationship`
as one. Authority attaches to the static, authored object; an emergent pattern cannot bear a
scope the way a field or a commitment can. ⛔ Not proof — the module was written before the
four-object model existed — but the ontology voted with its feet before anyone asked it to.

⭐⭐⭐ **Hold this at the right strength: it is ABDUCTIVE, not deductive.** It is not evidence that
C is true. It is evidence that *an independently evolved architecture exhibits the pattern C
predicts.* ⛔⛔ The constitutional question must therefore never become **"what did the code
already do?"** It remains **"what ontology best explains the phenomenon?"** — and the code is
one **witness**, not the question. Independently convergent structures deserve attention without
being mistaken for proof.

### 8.5 ⭐⭐⭐ Amendment discipline — how the constitution stays alive without becoming a warehouse

Founder, 2026-08-06. ⛔ **A constitution must not grow simply because new surfaces appear.**

Every proposed new principle must first answer:

> **Which existing constitutional sentence failed to decide the proposal?**

| Answer | Disposition |
|---|---|
| *"None — they decided it."* | ⛔ **No new sentence belongs.** The proposal was already governed. |
| *"Sentence N was insufficient — it could not distinguish X from Y."* | ✏️ **Amend sentence N.** Do not add. |
| *"No existing sentence can even express the distinction."* | ✅ Only here does a **ninth sentence earn its place.** |

⭐⭐ This is the adequacy test (§8) run in the opposite direction: adequacy asks whether the set can
decide a case; amendment discipline asks whether a proposed addition was ever needed. Together they
are what keeps a compact constitutional language compact.

⭐⭐⭐ **The goal:** future Studio, Now What?, Writer Canvas, Practitioner Development — and whatever
follows — need **no separate philosophies.** They become different expressions of the same
constitutional grammar.
