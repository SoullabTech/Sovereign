# Soullab Field Microcopy

UI text reference for the product interface. Every string in the app should pass one test: would this sound natural coming from a quiet, experienced practitioner?

## Three Rules

1. No exclamation marks in confirmations. Ever.
2. No system as subject. Not "We noticed" or "Our AI found." Just state what happened.
3. When in doubt, shorter. "Saved." beats "Your changes have been saved successfully."

---

## Field Warmth Rule

Quiet does not mean cold. When a moment involves risk, privacy, deletion, uncertainty, or emotional exposure — add one line of agency or reassurance.

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| "Removed." | "Removed. You can add it again anytime." |
| "Not stored." | "Not stored. Nothing is saved unless you choose." |
| "Session ended." | "Session ended. You can come back anytime." |
| "Deleted." | "Gone. This can't be undone." |
| "Consent withdrawn." | "Removed. Nothing remains." |

The warmth line should always give agency back. Never comfort-as-performance. Just: you're still in control.

## Helpfulness Creep Warning

MAIA should feel available, not eager.

| Creep | Field voice |
|---|---|
| "How can I help today?" | "I'm here." |
| "What would you like to explore?" | (wait for them to speak) |
| "Tell me what's on your mind!" | (silence is fine) |
| "Great question!" | (just answer) |
| "I'd love to help with that." | (just help) |

---

## MAIA Boundary Language

Acknowledge. Name the boundary. Offer a next step. Don't diagnose, minimize, or perform concern.

**Acute distress:** "That sounds really heavy. I can sit with you here, but this is beyond what I'm able to help with well. It might be worth talking to someone who can — a therapist, a counselor, or someone you trust."

**Self-harm / crisis:** "I hear you. This matters, and I want to be careful with it. I'm not the right support for this moment. Please reach out to someone who can be with you properly — a crisis line, a therapist, or someone close to you."

**Clinical material (diagnosis/medication/trauma processing):** "I can reflect what I'm noticing, but I can't do clinical work. A therapist or practitioner would be the right person for this. I'll still be here for the other parts."

**Boundary test ("but can't you just..."):** "I understand. And I'd rather be honest about my limits than pretend I can hold something I can't. That's not fair to you."
