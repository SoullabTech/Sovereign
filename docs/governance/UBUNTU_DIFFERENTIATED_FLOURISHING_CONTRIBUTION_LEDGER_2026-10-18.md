### Exact Soullab founder-adopted text

> Unity shall never require uniformity.
>
> The living whole flourishes through the flourishing of its differentiated participants.
>
> The maturity of collaborative intelligence is expressed in its capacity to remain richly differentiated within profound relationship.
>
> The purpose of collective participation is neither conformity nor the accumulation of voices, but the cultivation of conditions in which difference becomes a source of understanding, creative emergence, reciprocal care, and shared wisdom.

**Recognition is not appropriation.** Preserve the people and traditions Dyer herself names rather than treating all of her sources as a homogeneous wisdom corpus. Source disagreement belongs in the record.

## 3. Requirements for future contribution records

For any member or invited wisdom-keeper contribution: record a named author or permissioned pseudonym; originating tradition, title and verifiable source; direct quotation vs paraphrase vs Soullab synthesis; scope of sharing and rights; consent/withdrawal/revision history; provenance of corrections; and important unresolved disagreements.

A publicly cited reference must never be represented as coauthoring or endorsing Soullab's formulation. Private conversations, files, copyrighted book pages, and research screenshots are not automatically entitled for member-facing ingestion. No Dyer eBook contents are added by this docs-only PR; only public bibliographic attribution.
