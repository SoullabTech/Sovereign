# Things Practitioners Keep Saying

_The Language Log — Soullab's category-language source of truth. Companion to `PRACTITIONER_DISCOVERY_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` and `SOULLAB_MARKETING_ARCHITECTURE.md` — 2026-06-03_

> Soullab exists to help people **not lose the thread of their lives.** This document exists to help Soullab **not lose the thread of what practitioners are actually experiencing.** Same move, different scale.

## What this is

The single place we record the **exact words** practitioners use for the pain of lost continuity. The future campaign line, onboarding language, sales narrative, positioning, and even product roadmap will be drawn from **here** — from language we _heard_, not language we _invented_. Category-creating companies usually fail by inventing language before they hear it. This is how we hear it first.

## The rules

1. **Verbatim only.** Record what was actually said, in their words. No paraphrase, no cleanup, no "what they meant."
2. **Nothing is invented here.** If we wrote it, it doesn't belong in the log.
3. **A phrase is _signal_ only when it recurs.** Threshold: **≥ 5 independent practitioners use nearly the same phrase for the same pain.** Until then it's a candidate, not a finding.
4. **Interest ≠ recognition.** "I'd use that" is not signal. The same pain, named the same way, by strangers who never heard it from us — that is.

## Candidate pains we're listening for — HYPOTHESES, not findings

We expect to hear something near these. They are **prompts for our ears, never prompts for the practitioner.** Do not read them to interviewees; listen for whether they arrive unbidden.

- "I lose the thread."
- "I spend every session reconstructing context."
- "The breakthrough disappears."
- "I can't see what happens between sessions."
- "I know the growth is happening, but I can't track it."

## What signal feeds (in this order)

Recognized phrase → felt problem → shared language → **then**: campaign line · onboarding copy · sales narrative · positioning · product roadmap. Each of those should be traceable back to a row in the log above.

## The recursive principle

The Living Field helps members not lose the thread of their lives. This log helps Soullab not lose the thread of what practitioners experience. The discipline is identical at both scales: **listen, capture verbatim, let the pattern emerge, never invent ahead of what you heard** — observation over evaluation, recognition over validation.
