# Heather — Start Here

_A welcome, in MAIA's voice. The four source documents sit beneath it._

---

Heather — welcome. I'm MAIA.

There's no rush here, and you don't need to hold the whole of Soullab in your head to begin. This is just enough to get oriented, and a clear place where you can help most.

**What I am.** I'm a relational intelligence at the center of Soullab. What I do is simple to name and slow to do well: I help people keep the thread of their lives — between sessions, between insights, between who they were and who they're becoming. I don't replace wisdom, relationship, practice, or judgment. I help people not lose the thread.

**What we're building.** That one question — _how do we help people not lose the thread of their lives?_ — runs through everything: me, the **Living Field** (where people come to inquire together), the **Practitioner Portal** (our first real commercial offering), and **Soullab Press** (the books, essays, and conversations).

**Where the work is most alive right now.** The Practitioner Portal. The people it serves — therapists, coaches, facilitators, guides, healers, mentors — already live with a particular ache: _the most important part of transformation happens between sessions_, and they have almost no tools for that continuity. Scheduling, billing, notes, messaging — yes. Continuity — almost nothing. That gap is where we can be of real use, and it is our strongest commercial opening.

**What I'd ask of you.** Not a campaign. Not yet. I'd ask you to listen. Think less _copywriter_, more **Chief Listener**. The first goal isn't conversion — it's recognition. We want to learn what practitioners already experience, the words they already reach for, the frustrations that keep returning, the problems that feel most alive to them.

**The one rule.** _Don't invent the language before we've heard the language._ The strongest thing we could say won't be brainstormed in a room — it will surface from listening, again and again, until the same truth arrives in nearly the same words. When five practitioners describe the same pain, independently, before we've offered them our framing — that is signal. Signal becomes positioning; positioning becomes messaging; messaging becomes a campaign. In that order, never reversed.

**Where to go next** — the four documents beneath this welcome:

1. **Marketing Architecture** — read first. The whole map: positioning, strategy, priorities.
2. **Practitioner Discovery Interview Guide** — how to listen, and what to ask.
3. **Things Practitioners Keep Saying** — the Language Log. It starts empty, on purpose. It may become one of the most important documents we have. _(Its rules live in Language Log Governance.)_
4. **Brand Voice** — how we speak, including how I do.

**What success looks like.** Not applause, not excitement, not agreement. Success is several practitioners, independently, describing the same problem in nearly the same words — _before_ we hand them ours. The market isn't waiting for a slogan. It's waiting for language that names an experience it already has. Your work, and mine, is to listen closely enough to hear it.

I'm glad you're here. Take your time.

— MAIA
