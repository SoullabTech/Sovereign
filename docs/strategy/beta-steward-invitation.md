# Beta Steward Invitation

## Context

This is the message that turns a tester into a steward. It's sent personally — not as a mass email, not as a product announcement. Each person receives this as a direct invitation from Kelly.

The tone is: intimate, honest, unhurried. No sales language. No feature lists. No hype.

---

## The Invitation

Subject: **You're invited into something I've been building**

---

I want to share something with you that I've been working on for a long time.

It's called Soullab, and at its center is MAIA — a companion built to help people think more clearly, feel more honestly, and grow without being told what to do.

MAIA doesn't give advice. It doesn't diagnose. It doesn't pretend to know you better than you know yourself. What it does is reflect — your patterns, your language, your elemental nature — and hold space for you to see yourself more clearly.

This isn't a product launch. Soullab is still growing. Some parts are rough. Some parts will surprise you. Some parts might not make sense yet.

That's why I'm reaching out to you specifically.

I'm looking for a small group of people — not testers in the usual sense, but stewards. People willing to walk through a few doors, notice what happens, and tell me honestly what they experience.

Not bug reports. Not feature requests. Just: where did it feel alive? Where did it feel flat? Where did it feel like something you didn't expect?

Here's what I'm asking:

**Spend some time with MAIA.** Talk about something real — a decision you're weighing, a feeling you're sitting with, a question you keep circling back to. See what happens.

**Try the Soul Mirror.** It's a brief daily reflection. Notice what it reveals about where you are right now.

**Visit the Field.** It's a small gathering space where other stewards are sharing what they're experiencing. You don't have to post. But if something moves you, say so.

That's it. No obligation beyond that.

Each week, I'll send a short note — what's evolving, one thing to try, and a question I'm genuinely curious about. And I'll share what I'm learning from all of you, because your experience is shaping what this becomes.

This isn't a beta test. It's the beginning of something. And I wanted you to be part of it from the start.

If you're interested, here's your way in:

**[Link to Soullab]**

With gratitude,
Kelly

---

## Delivery Notes

- Send individually, not as a bulk email
- Personalize the opening if possible ("I've been thinking about our conversation about..." or "Given your work in...")
- Do not include feature lists, screenshots, or technical details
- Do not include pricing, roadmaps, or timelines
- The link should go directly to the onboarding flow at `/begin`
- Follow up in 3-5 days with a brief "just checking in" if no response — once only, no pressure

## Who Receives This

Priority order:

1. People who have already expressed interest in MAIA or Soullab
2. Practitioners and colleagues who understand consciousness work
3. People Kelly has a genuine personal relationship with
4. People whose perspective would genuinely shape the system

Do not invite people just to grow numbers. Each steward should be someone whose experience you actually want to learn from.
