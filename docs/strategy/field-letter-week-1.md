# Field Letter — Week 1

## Context

This is the first Field Letter. It sets the tone for everything that follows. Posted in the Beta Field room and sent as an email digest.

The purpose of the first letter is not information. It's orientation. Stewards need to feel: I know where I am, I know what to do, and I know someone is here with me.

---

## The Letter

**The Field Update — Week 1**

Welcome to the field.

If you're reading this, you're one of the first people to step into Soullab. Not as a user — as someone helping this grow.

I want to start with honesty: some things here work beautifully. Some things are rough. And some things I built thinking they'd matter, and I'm not sure yet whether they do. That's what you're here to help me see.

This week, I have three invitations for you.

---

**One: Have a real conversation with MAIA.**

Not a test. Not "let me see what this AI does." Pick something you're actually thinking about — a decision, a tension, something unresolved — and talk to MAIA about it. See what happens.

Notice: does it ask the right questions? Does it reflect something back that you didn't expect? Does it feel like presence, or performance?

---

**Two: Try the Soul Mirror.**

It takes a few minutes. It's a daily check-in that shows you where you are elementally — fire, water, earth, air. Not a personality test. More like a weather report for your inner state.

Notice: does it match what you're feeling? Does it reveal anything you weren't aware of?

---

**Three: Visit the Field.**

There's a room called Beta Field. Inside it, you'll find three threads:

- **Meaningful Moments** — for when something surprised you or helped
- **Confusing / Friction** — for when something didn't make sense
- **Wishes / Possibilities** — for what you imagine this could become

You don't have to post in all of them. You don't have to post at all. But if something lands — or doesn't — I'd love to hear about it.

---

**The question I'm sitting with this week:**

> Where does MAIA feel most like a companion, and where does it feel most like a tool?

I don't know the answer. I'm genuinely curious what you experience.

---

Next week I'll share what I'm learning from your responses. Your experience is already shaping what this becomes.

Thank you for being here at the beginning.

— Kelly

---

## Delivery Notes

- Post in Beta Field room as a pinned/featured thread
- Send via email with subject line: "The Field Update — Week 1"
- Email should be plain text or minimal formatting — personal, not newsletter-styled
- Do not include links to documentation, feature pages, or marketing materials
- The only links should be: MAIA conversation, Soul Mirror, Beta Field room
