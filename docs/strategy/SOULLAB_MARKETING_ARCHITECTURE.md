## The root insight (lead with this, not the technology)

Most AI companies market the technology. Most wellness companies market transformation. Most software companies market features. **We begin somewhere else** — with a problem practitioners and thoughtful people already feel:

> **We lose the thread of our lives.** The thread of our growth. The thread between sessions, between insights, between who we were and who we are becoming.

Everything Soullab builds exists to help people maintain **continuity with the unfolding process of their lives.**

## Three concentric stories

Build the marketing plan around three nested layers — each a different door to the same center.

### Layer One — The Living Field _(public invitation; category + vision)_
Where people discover Soullab. Not a community, forum, or social feed — a place of **shared inquiry**: contributing observations, exploring experiments, engaging living questions, helping cultivate a growing body of wisdom about the human condition.
- **Key message:** _You are not joining a platform. You are entering a living field of inquiry._
- **Emotional outcome:** curiosity · wonder · participation · **belonging without dependency** · meaningful contribution.

### Layer Two — The Practitioner Portal _(first commercial wedge; primary revenue)_
Where the strongest, most expensive market pain lives. Practitioners have tools for scheduling, billing, docs, comms — but almost none for **continuity, integration, developmental tracking, meaning-making, longitudinal growth.** Most sessions begin by reconstructing context; most growth happens between sessions; most breakthroughs vanish because continuity is lost.
- **Position:** a **Continuity Platform for Transformational Practice** — _not_ practice management, CRM, note-taking, or AI coaching.
- **Key message:** _Don't lose the thread._
- **Emotional outcome:** relief · clarity · confidence · depth · stewardship.
- **Campaign themes:** The Space Between Sessions · A Living Record of Becoming · Development Has Memory · The Missing Layer · Don't Lose the Thread.

### Layer Three — MAIA _(the intelligence beneath both — do NOT lead with it)_
The AI category is crowded; the **relational intelligence** category is open.
- **Position:** a relational intelligence that helps people maintain continuity with the evolving process of life.
- **Supports:** reflection · pattern recognition · continuity · perspective-shifting · elemental awareness · integration · development.
- **Does not replace:** wisdom · relationship · practice · discernment · human judgment.

## Strategic sequence

Do **not** lead with "Meet MAIA," "AI for personal growth," or "the future of consciousness." **Lead with practitioner pain → continuity → the Living Field.** Let MAIA emerge naturally as the intelligence supporting those experiences.

## Resource allocation (today)

| Effort | Layer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **70%** | Practitioner Portal | Urgent, expensive, highly-felt problem → revenue. |
| **20%** | Living Field | Establishes category and vision. |
| **10%** | MAIA | More compelling experienced *through* the other two than marketed as another AI product. |

## First revenue campaign — "Don't Lose the Thread"

- **Audience:** coaches, therapists, facilitators, guides, practitioners, educators, mentors.
- **Core insight:** the most important part of transformation happens *between* sessions.
- **Core question:** _What if the developmental process didn't disappear when the session ended?_
- **Promise:** help practitioners maintain continuity with the unfolding process of human growth.
- **Desired outcome:** practitioner demos + founding-practitioner cohort applications.

### The load-bearing line

> **Don't Lose the Thread.**

It speaks at once to practitioners, clients, personal development, continuity, memory, growth, and the deeper Soullab vision — without requiring anyone to understand the whole philosophy first.

## What We Need From Marketing Right Now _(near-term priority — orientation for Heather)_

Not growth. Not scale. Not funnels. Not optimization. Right now we need **discovery and language** — find signal and the simplest true story _before_ building a large campaign. (This is the marketing expression of the project's own discipline: **signal before conversion, recognized-truth before slogan, discovery before scale** — the same rigor as *built ≠ verified*.)

### 1. Category discovery
We don't yet know the simplest language for what we're building. Candidates: _Continuity · Relational Intelligence · Development Has Memory · Don't Lose the Thread · The Living Field._ The job is to discover which one creates **recognition** — not which we like most.

### 2. Practitioner discovery
Before a large campaign, learn which segments resonate most — therapists, coaches, executive coaches, facilitators, healers, educators. **The first goal is signal, not conversion.**

### 3. Message testing
Four candidate stories. Learn which one practitioners _immediately recognize_:
- **A —** The most important part of transformation happens between sessions.
- **B —** Development has memory.
- **C —** Don't lose the thread.
- **D —** A living record of becoming.

### 4. Founding practitioner cohort
Don't launch a product — **launch a cohort.** Not _"buy software"_ but _"help us shape the future of continuity in transformational practice."_ A small founding group (therapists, coaches, guides, facilitators) given access, influence, and direct collaboration — generating stories, language, testimonials, objections, and product learning simultaneously. _This is the same move as the Living Field — co-investigation, not selling — and it can run on the Living Field's existing cohort + observations + living-questions mechanics, so discovery is captured as signal rather than vapor._

### Discovery as one motion

Most companies run market research over here, product development over there, community somewhere else — separate machinery for each. Soullab's architecture collapses them into a single flow:

```text
Living Question → Practitioner Response → Observation Stream
→ Shared Learning → Field Pulse → Marketing Language → Product Direction
```

The same system generates **language, product learning, practitioner insight, testimonials, objections, and category discovery** at once — because the product's own mechanics _are_ the discovery instrument. (See `docs/strategy/PRACTITIONER_DISCOVERY_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.)

### Recognition is not promise

Two different jobs — do not confuse them:

- **Recognition** — _"The most important part of transformation happens between sessions."_ Something practitioners **already know**. This is the door.
- **Promise** — _"Don't Lose the Thread."_ What Soullab offers **in response**. This is the campaign line.

Marketing fails when it starts with the promise. The stronger sequence:

```text
Recognized truth → Felt problem → Shared language → Possible solution
```

Test the **pain-naming** first (recognition); let the **promise-line** win the campaign once the pain is named in the market's own words.

### Success criterion for the first founding round (narrow it hard)

The campaign is an **output** of learning, not its purpose. So judge the first round by signal, not sentiment:

- ❌ "We got positive feedback."
- ❌ "People liked the vision."
- ❌ "We validated the concept."
- ✅ **Five independent practitioners use nearly the same phrase to describe the same pain.**

_e.g._ "I lose the thread." · "I spend every session reconstructing context." · "The breakthrough disappears." · "I can't see what happens between sessions." · "I know the growth is happening, but I can't track it."

The moment that happens, you have **signal** — and Heather's job flips from _inventing messaging_ to **amplifying recognition.** Which is why her role this phase is less copywriter, more **Chief Listener**: the highest-leverage question isn't "what should we say?" but _"what are practitioners already trying to say but don't yet have language for?"_ — closer to ethnography and depth interviewing than to marketing. The first-month deliverable is therefore not a landing page but a living document: **`THINGS_PRACTITIONERS_KEEP_SAYING.md`** (the Language Log) — the source the campaign, onboarding, sales narrative, positioning, and roadmap all draw from. Three instruments make this a system that can **discover its own category language**: the Discovery Guide, the Language Log, and the Living Field.

### The one question for Heather (one month, one thing)
> **What is the simplest _true_ story a practitioner immediately recognizes?**

Not the deepest story, the founder story, or the philosophical story — the one that makes a practitioner stop and say: _"Yes. That's exactly the problem I've been living with."_ Once that sentence is found, the rest of the architecture gets much easier. The suspicion: it's already close to **"The most important part of transformation happens between sessions"** or **"Don't Lose the Thread"** — less marketing slogans than _recognized truths_, which is usually where the strongest campaigns begin.

> **The deepest principle:** The market is not waiting for a slogan. It is waiting for **language that accurately describes an experience it already has.** What we want from the founding cohort is not validation — it is **recognition.** Heather's first contribution may not be writing copy at all; it may be running 10–20 structured practitioner conversations and listening for the recurring phrase that is already true.

## Alignment & guardrails (sovereignty canon)

This architecture is already deeply canon-aligned — **"belonging without dependency,"** MAIA-does-not-replace-judgment, and refusing the crowded "AI" frame all reflect the Sovereignty Invariants. Hold three lines as copy develops:

1. **Continuity claims must map to verified capability.** "A living record of becoming / development has memory / don't lose the thread" is true to the mission — and must stay ahead of nothing. The project's discipline is *built ≠ wired ≠ surfacing ≠ verified*; memory/episodic continuity is the most carefully-bounded claim in the system. Before this fronts a **paid** practitioner audience, name exactly which continuity features are live and verified, and let the copy promise only those. A campaign that out-runs the product becomes the inflation the canon exists to prevent.
2. **The Living Field is now real, not a metaphor.** `/beta-testers` ("The Living Field") ships shared inquiry, experiments, observations, and a measured Field Pulse — Layer One can point at something live, which makes the invitation honest.
3. **No attachment capture.** Every line should increase agency and push life outward; "belonging without dependency" is the test, not engagement.
