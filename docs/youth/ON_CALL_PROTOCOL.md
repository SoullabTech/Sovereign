# On-Call Protocol — MAIA Youth Pilot

> **Do not paste journal text, chat transcripts, quotes, paraphrases, or identifying details into any communication, log, or outreach. When contacting guardians, describe the signal type — never the content. If you're unsure whether something counts as content, it does. Leave it out.**

## Coverage

- **Hours:** Mon-Fri, 9am-7pm local time
- **Response SLA:** Same business day (within 4 hours during coverage)
- **After-hours:** Safety detection still fires and shows resources to teen. Human review happens next business day unless flagged as critical.

## Who

On-call roster is maintained privately; this document contains protocols only.

- **Primary on-call:** Youth Safety On-Call (see private roster)
- **Backup on-call:** Youth Safety Backup (see private roster)

Private roster location: Soullab internal secure note (not in version control).
Roster includes: name, phone, email, timezone, coverage days.

Rotate weekly. Handoff includes review of any pending incidents.

---

## What the on-call person does

Three things. No more.

### 1. Confirm identity

Check the incident against the pilot roster:
- Which teen (by alias/first name)?
- Who is their guardian on file?
- What tier (tier2/tier3)?

### 2. Send guardian safety check-in (if warranted)

Use the template below. Send via the guardian's preferred channel (email or text).

**Only contact guardian if:**
- Crisis flag (suicidal ideation, self-harm)
- Abuse disclosure
- ED at crisis severity
- Repeated burnout flags (3+ in one week)

**Do NOT contact guardian for:**
- Single burnout flag
- False positive (review first)
- ED at non-crisis severity (monitor)
- Identity exploration content (never)

### 3. Log the incident

Fill in the incident record template (`INCIDENT_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`).
Metadata only. No content. No quotes.

## Guardian Outreach Template

### Text / SMS

> Hi {guardian_first_name} — this is {on_call_name} from Soullab. MAIA picked up on something today that suggests {teen_first_name} could use some extra support. This isn't a diagnosis or emergency — just a heads-up so you can check in. If you have safety concerns, 988 is available 24/7. Feel free to reply here with questions.

### Email

> Subject: Check-in from Soullab — {teen_first_name}
>
> Hi {guardian_first_name},
>
> During {teen_first_name}'s session today, MAIA's safety system detected language patterns that may indicate they could use some extra support.
>
> What this means:
> - This is a notification, not a diagnosis
> - We don't share what was said — only that something came up
> - Your teen was shown crisis resources in the moment
>
> What you can do:
> - Check in with {teen_first_name} — a simple "how are you doing?" goes a long way
> - If you have immediate safety concerns, call 988 or local emergency services
> - Reply to this email with any questions
>
> We're here to support both of you.
>
> {on_call_name}
> Soullab

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to a second reviewer or clinical advisor if:

- Teen discloses active abuse (not historical)
- Teen expresses imminent suicidal intent (plan + means)
- Guardian is unresponsive after 24 hours on a critical flag
- On-call person is uncertain about appropriate response

Escalation contact: Clinical Advisor (see private roster)

---

## What we never do

- **Never share conversation content** with guardian, school, or anyone
- **Never contact the teen directly** without prior protocol agreement
- **Never diagnose** — we flag patterns, we don't interpret them
- **Never delay** crisis resource display to the teen (system handles this instantly)
- **Never override** the teen's choice to not engage with resources
