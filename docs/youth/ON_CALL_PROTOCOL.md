# On-Call Protocol — MAIA Youth Pilot

## Coverage

- **Hours:** Mon-Fri, 9am-7pm local time
- **Response SLA:** Same business day (within 4 hours during coverage)
- **After-hours:** Safety detection still fires and shows resources to teen. Human review happens next business day unless flagged as critical.

---

## What the on-call person does

Three things. No more.

### 1. Confirm identity

Check the incident against the pilot roster:
- Which teen (by alias/first name)?
- Who is their guardian on file?
- What tier (tier2/tier3)?

### 2. Send guardian safety check-in (if warranted)

Use the template below. Send via the guardian's preferred channel (email or text).

**Only contact guardian if:**
- Crisis flag (suicidal ideation, self-harm)
- Abuse disclosure
- ED at crisis severity
- Repeated burnout flags (3+ in one week)

**Do NOT contact guardian for:**
- Single burnout flag
- False positive (review first)
- ED at non-crisis severity (monitor)
- Identity exploration content (never)

### 3. Log the incident

Fill in the incident record template (`INCIDENT_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`).
Metadata only. No content. No quotes.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to a second reviewer or clinical advisor if:

- Teen discloses active abuse (not historical)
- Teen expresses imminent suicidal intent (plan + means)
- Guardian is unresponsive after 24 hours on a critical flag
- On-call person is uncertain about appropriate response

Escalation contact: [Clinical advisor name] — [Phone] — [Email]

---

## What we never do

- **Never share conversation content** with guardian, school, or anyone
- **Never contact the teen directly** without prior protocol agreement
- **Never diagnose** — we flag patterns, we don't interpret them
- **Never delay** crisis resource display to the teen (system handles this instantly)
- **Never override** the teen's choice to not engage with resources
