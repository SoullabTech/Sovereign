# Guardian Consent + Teen Assent — MAIA Youth Pilot

## For Parents / Guardians

### What MAIA is

MAIA is a guided reflection and skills-building companion for teens. It helps with grounding, emotional regulation, and perspective-taking. It uses conversation to support your teen in developing self-awareness and inner navigation skills.

### What MAIA is not

- Not therapy. Not a replacement for professional help.
- Not emergency services.
- Not a substitute for a trusted adult, clinician, or crisis support.
- Not a social platform. There is no messaging between users.

### What happens if safety flags fire

MAIA monitors conversations for language patterns that may indicate distress, crisis, disordered eating, or abuse. If a safety flag fires:

1. **Your teen sees crisis resources immediately** — in the conversation, not hidden.
2. **A Soullab team member is notified same-day** during coverage hours (Mon-Fri, 9am-7pm).
3. **You may be contacted** if a serious safety flag occurs. This contact is about safety logistics — not a transcript or summary of what they said.

We will never share the content of your teen's conversations with you. We will only tell you: "Something came up that suggests your teen could use extra support right now."

### Data handling

**What we store:**
- Date of birth (to determine age-appropriate experience)
- Onboarding answers (neurodivergence, support needs — optional, skippable)
- Session metadata (timestamp, duration, whether safety flags fired)
- Conversation content (for the session only; Sanctuary Mode conversations are not retained)

**What we do not do:**
- Sell or share data with third parties
- Use identifiable teen content for model training
- Store content from Sanctuary Mode sessions
- Share conversation content with parents/guardians

**Withdrawal:** You or your teen can stop participation at any time. Notify us and all data will be deleted within 7 days.

### Risks

- Reflection can surface difficult feelings. This is normal and part of growth, but it can be uncomfortable.
- Safety detection may occasionally flag language that isn't actually concerning (false positive), or miss language that is (false negative). No system is perfect.
- Your teen may encounter content or prompts that feel "weird" or don't land right. We actively collect feedback on this.

### Benefits

- Grounding practices after stress
- Self-understanding and emotional vocabulary
- Skills practice (boundaries, identity exploration, burnout recovery)
- A space that is theirs, governed by their choices
