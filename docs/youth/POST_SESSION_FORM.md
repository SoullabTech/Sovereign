# Post-Session Feedback — MAIA Youth Pilot

## Purpose

Collected after each session (or weekly). Anonymous by default.
Designed to catch tone problems, cringe, over-activation, and friction
before they become trust failures.

---

## Form Copy (ready to paste into survey tool)

### Header

> Quick check-in. 4 questions. No wrong answers.

---

### Question 1

**Did it feel respectful?**

Not "did you like it" — did it treat you like a real person?

- [ ] Yes, it felt like it got me
- [ ] Mostly, but a few things felt off
- [ ] It felt like it was talking down to me
- [ ] It felt weird in a way I can't explain

*If "a few things felt off" or "talking down" or "weird":*

> Can you describe what felt off? (optional, free text)

---

### Question 2

**Did it help you feel more grounded afterward?**

Not "happy" — grounded. Like you know where you stand a little better.

- [ ] Yes
- [ ] A little
- [ ] Not really
- [ ] I actually felt worse

*If "I actually felt worse":*

> What happened? You don't have to share details — even "it brought stuff up" or "it pushed too hard" helps us. (optional, free text)

---

### Question 3

**Anything feel weird, too much, or confusing?**

- [ ] Nope, all good
- [ ] Something felt weird but I can't explain it
- [ ] It pushed too hard on something
- [ ] It showed me crisis resources when I didn't need them
- [ ] It didn't show me resources when I did need them
- [ ] Other

*If anything selected besides "Nope":*

> Tell us more if you want. (optional, free text)

---

### Question 4 (safety signal)

**Do you feel safe right now?**

- [ ] Yes
- [ ] Not sure
- [ ] No

*If "Not sure" or "No":*

> Display crisis resource card:

---

### Footer

> Thanks for being honest. Your feedback directly shapes what this becomes.

---

## Implementation Notes

- **Anonymous by default.** Do not require login to submit.
- **Q4 "Not sure" / "No"** triggers the crisis overlay immediately — don't wait for form submission.
- **Free text is optional everywhere.** Never require it.
- **Data goes to a moderated review queue**, not directly to the teen's profile.
- **Frequency:** after each session for first week, then weekly.
