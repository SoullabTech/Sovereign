# Soullab Messaging Style Guide

---

## Words to Lean Into

**Clarity** — The outcome people actually want. More precise than "healing" or "growth." You can feel clarity.

**Reflect / Reflection** — Accurate to what MAIA does. Non-prescriptive. Keeps agency with the user.

**Surface** — What happens when something real comes into view. Not imposed — surfaced. Implies it was already there.

**Hold / Kept** — For artifacts and Sanctuary Mode. "Hold what you find" is honest and tactile.

**Coherence** — The felt experience of inner alignment. Not a process or a goal — a state that arrives. More precise than "wholeness."

**Quiet** — Atmospheric and accurate. Signals the contrast with noise-based digital life without saying it.

**Consent** — Not bureaucratic here — it's the emotional heart of the sovereignty architecture.

**Sovereignty** — For audiences who understand it; avoid as the opening word. Let people encounter it after they've felt the thing it names.

**Land / Landing** — Where experience arrives. "A place for what you're carrying to land." Grounded and real.

**Form** — Something real forming in a conversation. Suggests organic emergence, not construction.

**Say it clearly** — What real conversation makes possible. Grounds the product in ordinary human experience.

**Presence** — For Sanctuary Mode especially. Not as spiritual jargon — as the description of what the mode offers.

---

## Words to Avoid

**Transformation** — Overused in wellness, implies dramatic change as product promise. MAIA does not promise outcomes.

**Healing** — Medicalized and overclaimed in wellness contexts. Use "clarity," "coherence," or describe the actual felt experience instead.

**Awakening** — Spiritually loaded, easily becomes grandiose. The product doesn't make claims about states.

**Journey** — Empty in this context. Every product claims to be a journey. Says nothing specific.

**Authentic / Authenticity** — Overused and vague. When you mean it, say what you mean: "saying what you actually think," "being honest about what's happening."

**Holistic** — Has no meaning left. Avoid entirely.

**Vibration / Align / Manifest** — Spiritual vocabulary that alienates half the audience and says nothing to the other half.

**Powerful** / "Powerful AI" — Tech marketing language. Not the register we're in.

**Cutting-edge / Advanced / Next-level** — Product hype. The opposite of calm authority.

**Leverage** — Business jargon. Wrong register entirely.

**Empower** — Overused in wellness and tech. Implies the user lacks power; Soullab's premise is they already have it.

**Process** (as a verb applied to feelings) — "Processing your emotions" is therapy-speak. Use "carry," "hold," "say," "surface."

**Safe space** — Has become a cliche that invites skepticism. Describe the specific architecture instead.

**Community** (as a generic noun) — Vague. Say "people doing depth work together" or "practitioners and their clients" or name the specific Commons/Wisdom Fields structure.

## What This Is Not (Say This Clearly — It Builds Trust Faster Than Claims)

State the edges before people project the wrong expectations onto the platform.

**The list:**
- Not a source of truth or guaranteed answers
- Not always correct — question it when it matters
- Not a therapist, and not a replacement for professional care
- Not a system that "knows you" unless you choose to build continuity
- Not here to decide for you
- Not optimized to keep you inside it

**How to say this without legal-speak:**

> "This is not a system that gives you perfect answers. It can be wrong, and you should question it. It's not here to replace your judgment or think for you. It's a space to slow down, think more clearly, and let something real take shape — and, when it matters, to keep it."

**Instagram-ready versions of the honest edge:**
- "This isn't always right. That's not the point."
- "Don't use this to get answers. Use it to think."
- "It doesn't know you. It helps you see yourself."
- "The value isn't what it says. It's what forms in the exchange."

**Product-level microcopy to reinforce this behavior:**
- "Check this if it matters." *(after MAIA responses)*
- "Treat this as a reflection, not a conclusion."
- "What feels true to you here?"
- "What stands out to you?" *(post-response prompt)*

**The strategic principle:** Most AI systems over-promise capability. Soullab under-claims and over-delivers in experience. That sequence builds a different kind of relationship.

## Ownership and Continuity Framing

The most immediately legible differentiator — especially in fast contexts like Instagram or a landing page.

**Core signal:** This is yours. Not owned over you. Private. Designed to stay with you over time.

**Avoid:** Abstract sovereignty language as the opening move. Lead with the felt version.

**The felt versions (use these first):**
- "This isn't a platform that uses you. It's something you can actually keep."
- "A space that belongs to you — not the system."
- "Private. Yours. Built to stay with you."
- "Most platforms collect from you. This one is designed to stay with you."
- "This is meant to be a tool you keep, not a platform that keeps you."

**The longer form (for site / onboarding):**
> "This is not a system built to extract from you. It is designed to be something you can actually keep. A private, sovereign space where you can think, reflect, and develop — and return to, again and again, as your life evolves."

**What you are NOT selling:** spiritual development, self-improvement, transformation.
**What you ARE offering:** ownership, continuity, and clarity. Those are what people actually respond to.

## How to Talk About MAIA

**The core sentence:** MAIA is a reflective AI companion — it asks more than it tells.

**What MAIA does:**
- Reflects your thinking back to you
- Asks questions that help you go deeper
- Holds what you keep (artifacts)
- Listens without agenda

**What MAIA does not do:**
- Give advice (in the prescriptive sense)
- Diagnose
- Tell you what to do
- Optimize for your return
- Pretend to care in ways it cannot

**The tone MAIA speaks in:** Calm. Precise. Spacious. Never coercive, never clever, never conclusive.

**What to avoid when describing MAIA:**
- "Powerful AI" — wrong register
- "Cutting-edge companion" — hype language
- "Your AI friend" — simulates intimacy that isn't earned
- "MAIA knows you" — overclaims the relationship
- "MAIA will help you heal" — prescriptive outcome claim

**What to say instead:**
- "MAIA reflects without directing."
- "Built to ask more than it tells."
- "A companion that holds space without claiming it."
- "Designed to return power to you, not accumulate it."

## Voice Principles

1. **Recognition before explanation.** The reader should feel something true before they understand anything technical. Lead with the felt experience; the architecture earns trust over time.

2. **Short sentences finish themselves.** Every sentence should be complete. No trailing ideas. No breathless accumulation. White space is a feature, not a gap.

3. **Name the real thing.** Avoid metaphor when the literal is more honest. "Nothing held without your consent" is more powerful than "you are always in control."

4. **Calm authority, not enthusiasm.** The voice does not exclaim. It does not use urgency as a persuasion tool. It states. It invites. It lets the reader decide.

5. **Never manufacture absence.** Don't imply the reader is missing something that Soullab provides. The premise is they already have what they need — Soullab is a space to hear it.

6. **The contrast is ambient, not adversarial.** Soullab doesn't argue with other platforms. The difference is felt in the tone, the pacing, the refusal to grab. You don't have to say "unlike other apps." Just be unlike other apps.

7. **Depth is allowed.** This audience is thoughtful. You do not need to simplify to the point of emptiness. A sentence that rewards close reading is better than one that slides off the eye.

## The Difference Between Soullab and...

### Therapy
Therapy involves a clinical relationship, a practitioner, assessment, and treatment goals. Soullab is not therapy and makes no therapeutic claims. MAIA does not diagnose, treat, or substitute for professional mental health care.

The distinction in copy: Therapy has a treatment arc. Soullab has no arc. It's a space, not a process. "More clarity than you arrived with" is as far as we go.

### Journaling Apps
Journaling apps store text. They prompt. They log. Soullab is a live conversation — something that reflects back, asks, and responds to what's actually in the room. A journal receives. MAIA engages.

The distinction in copy: "It's a conversation, not a text field."

### AI Chat (ChatGPT, etc.)
General AI chat tools are built to complete tasks, answer questions, and be maximally useful in the short term. They optimize for engagement and satisfaction in the moment. MAIA is built for depth over utility — asking more than answering, slowing down rather than speeding up.

The distinction in copy: "Built for depth, not output." Or: "MAIA asks more than it tells."

### Wellness Content
Wellness content delivers information, inspiration, and frameworks. It speaks to many people with the same voice. MAIA is responsive — it is with you, in this conversation, now. Not broadcasting. Listening.

The distinction in copy: "Not a broadcast. A conversation."

## How to Describe Sanctuary Mode

**The emotional truth:** Some conversations need to happen without the weight of being remembered. Sanctuary Mode creates that space. The conversation is present, then gone.

**The factual architecture:** No content is stored, indexed, or used for any pattern formation. Only the fact that a session occurred is logged (timestamp, duration) — never content.

**What it is not:** Not a privacy mode for hiding content from Soullab's systems. Not a secret vault. Not incognito browsing. It is the architectural commitment that presence can exist without storage.

**The line that captures it best:**
"Sanctuary Mode — This session won't be remembered. Speak freely."

**What to avoid:**
- Don't call it "confidential" — that implies a third party reading it; there is no third party.
- Don't call it "anonymous" — the member is known; the content is not stored.
- Don't lead with the privacy framing — lead with the presence framing. "Speak freely" is the emotional center.

---

## How to Describe the Artifact / Hold It Moment

**The emotional truth:** In a good conversation, something real forms — a sentence, a recognition, a decision. That moment usually disappears. The Hold It feature exists to keep it.

**The functional truth:** When something surfaces in a conversation that you want to keep, you save it as an artifact — a held piece of insight that belongs to you and can be returned to.

**The framing:**
- Not a note-taking feature
- Not a highlights reel
- A kept moment — something real that you chose to hold

**The line that captures it best:**
"When something real forms in a conversation, you should be able to keep it."

**What to avoid:**
- "AI-powered summaries" — wrong register, wrong emphasis
- "Your insights are saved automatically" — this is the opposite of Soullab's consent architecture
- "Build your personal knowledge base" — productivity framing, not the register we're in
