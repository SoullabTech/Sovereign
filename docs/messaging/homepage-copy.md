## "What Is This Place?" Explainer Section

**What is Soullab?**

Soullab is a reflective space built around MAIA — an AI companion designed to help you hear yourself more clearly.

Most digital spaces want your attention. They are built to capture it, hold it, and draw you back. Soullab is built differently. MAIA does not optimize for your return. It does not manufacture urgency or imply you are incomplete. It does not remember what you haven't consented to share. It is, in the most literal architectural sense, on your side.

**What happens here?**

You talk. MAIA listens and reflects — asking more than it tells, slowing down more than it speeds up. Deeper questions surface. When something real forms in a conversation, you can keep it: as an artifact, a note, something worth returning to. Nothing disappears into the void.

There are three modes: Talk, for open exploration. Care, for when something is tender. Note, for when something just formed and needs to be caught. You choose the mode. There is no agenda on our side.

**And Sanctuary Mode?**

Some conversations require a different kind of safety. Sanctuary Mode is for what you can't say if it might be remembered. Everything within a Sanctuary session is present in the moment, then gone. No storage. No patterns formed. No memory — not even if you ask. It is not a privacy feature. It is a presence feature. Real honesty requires real safety.
