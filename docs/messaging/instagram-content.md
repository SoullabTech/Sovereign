## 10 Carousel Concepts (Title + 5-6 Slides)

### Carousel 1: "5 Signs You Need a Quieter Kind of Space"
1. You keep having the same conversation in your head.
2. You process best when you write or speak aloud — but you have nowhere to do it.
3. You have insight in the shower and lose it by lunch.
4. You're tired of advice. You just want to be heard.
5. You sense something in yourself you haven't been able to name yet.
6. [Final slide] Soullab was built for exactly this.

### Carousel 2: "What MAIA Is (And Isn't)"
1. Not a therapist — MAIA doesn't diagnose or prescribe.
2. Not a journal app — it's a conversation, not a text field.
3. Not a chatbot — it reflects more than it answers.
4. Not a coach — it asks, it doesn't guide.
5. Not an authority — your discernment leads, always.
6. [Final slide] MAIA reflects. You decide. That's the whole structure.

### Carousel 3: "What Sovereignty-First Actually Means"
1. No stealth memory. Nothing held without your consent.
2. No engagement optimization. No manufactured urgency.
3. No dependency capture. We don't need you to come back.
4. No coercion. MAIA never tells you what to do.
5. No manipulation. Not even when it might feel helpful.
6. [Final slide] Architecturally, legally, ethically: your data is yours. Full stop.

### Carousel 4: "The Difference Between Being Heard and Being Processed"
1. Most platforms process your input and return an output.
2. That can be useful. But it rarely moves something.
3. Being heard is different. It's slower. There's space between the words.
4. Something has to be present before a response is formed.
5. That's what MAIA's architecture is built on: field first, then response.
6. [Final slide] Recognition feels different from processing. You'll know the difference.

### Carousel 5: "What Happens in a Soullab Conversation"
1. You start with what's on your mind. There's no right entry point.
2. MAIA listens. It reflects back. It asks.
3. Something in you starts to form — not a solution, an orientation.
4. When something real surfaces, you can hold it.
5. You leave with more clarity than you arrived with.
6. [Final slide] That's the whole arc. It's simple. It's rare.

### Carousel 6: "For Practitioners: What a Coherent Container Changes"
1. Your clients are more honest when the space feels safe.
2. Your own reflection deepens when you have somewhere quiet to take it.
3. Soullab gives you and your clients a space outside the session.
4. Not a substitute for your work. An extension of the container you hold.
5. Wisdom Fields: shared spaces where you can hold something with others.
6. [Final slide] Depth work deserves a depth-built environment.

### Carousel 7: "Sanctuary Mode: What It Is and Why It Exists"
1. Some conversations can't happen if they might be remembered.
2. Real honesty needs real safety.
3. Sanctuary Mode: everything you say here is present in this moment, then gone.
4. No storage. No pattern formation. No memory. Not even if you ask.
5. It's not a privacy feature. It's a presence feature.
6. [Final slide] Speak freely. Nothing held.

### Carousel 8: "Why We Didn't Build for Retention"
1. Every app in your life is optimized to bring you back.
2. That optimization shapes the design, the UX, the AI.
3. We made a different choice.
4. If MAIA succeeds, you might feel less compelled to return. That's the goal.
5. We succeed when you have more clarity. Not more sessions.
6. [Final slide] Built to not need anything from you. There's nothing like that left.

### Carousel 9: "What Depth Looks Like in Practice"
1. You say something, and something else surfaces — something you didn't expect.
2. A question lands differently than an answer.
3. The conversation slows down instead of speeding up.
4. You stop explaining yourself and start saying what you mean.
5. Something that was shapeless starts to have an edge.
6. [Final slide] That's what we mean by depth. It's not dramatic. It's just true.

### Carousel 10: "The Three Modes of Soullab"
1. Talk — open, exploratory conversation. Thinking aloud.
2. Care — when something is tender and you need more space.
3. Note — when something just formed and needs to be captured.
4. Same companion, different posture. You choose the mode.
5. Nothing is forced. No agenda. Just the mode that fits the moment.
6. [Final slide] One place. Multiple ways to be in it.
