# Proof 1 — What Now? Reconnection (Experiment Checklist)

**Status:** Not started · **Type:** reversible experiment (evidence-gathering only — no constitutional adoption) · **Date opened:** 2026-07-08

---

> **The purpose of Proof 1 is not to prove Context Assembly. It is to determine whether reconnecting AIN OS allows MAIA's recognizable presence to emerge without sacrificing the identity of the What Now? room.**

Everything below — the code, the routing, the memory retrieval, the field configuration — is in service of one question:

> **Can the person recognize that they are with MAIA?**

If yes, that is an important piece of evidence. If no, the architecture has learned where reality disagrees with the current explanation — which, under the methodology established for this work, is just as valuable.

---

## The two hypotheses are not equivalent

- **Engineering hypothesis:** Context Assembly reconnects What Now? to MAIA.
- **Experiential hypothesis:** the member recognizes MAIA.

Do not let either stand in for the other. **Never use engineering success as a proxy for relational success — nor the reverse.** They are different forms of evidence, and their disagreement is the most informative outcome the experiment can produce.

| Engineering | Encounter | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ works | ✅ "there she is" | Strong evidence for the hypothesis. |
| ✅ works | ❌ "still doesn't feel like MAIA" | The boundary is insufficient, or another missing layer exists. **The most important thing Proof 1 can teach.** |
| ❌ doesn't work | ✅ somehow still feels like MAIA | Investigate — assumptions about the implementation may be wrong. |
| ❌ doesn't work | ❌ doesn't feel like MAIA | Experiment failed; fix the implementation before judging the theory. |

## Guardrails

- This is a **reversible experiment**. It gathers evidence for the ADR-013 four-proof gate (Reconnection / Contextual embodiment / Continuity / Jurisdiction). It does **not** adopt Context Assembly constitutionally.
- **Experiential power is admissible evidence, not a ratification criterion.** The gate stays the four proofs. A felt "there she is" informs whether Proof 1 reconnected anything worth generalizing — it does not, by itself, ratify the boundary.
- ADR-013 remains **Proposed**; Invariant 17 remains **unwritten** in canon; no other surface is migrated by this experiment.
