# MAIA Clinical Supervision Streaming

## "Live without confusion" supervision that reduces cognitive load

---

**What it is**

A supervision dashboard that streams **live transcript** and **live clinical insights** during an active session, while clearly signaling whether the view is truly live, reconnecting, or running in fallback mode. Clicking an insight opens its full body and can **jump the transcript to the relevant moment**—so supervision stays anchored in observed material, not vague recall.

---

**What it solves (in supervision terms)**

Supervision often fails at the exact moment it's most needed: when the work is moving fast, affect is rising, or the clinician is juggling attention. This system supports the supervisor by:

* **Reducing memory burden:** the supervisor doesn't have to hold the whole session in working memory.
* **Reducing search cost:** insights become "handles" that land you in the right transcript region.
* **Increasing trust:** explicit connection state prevents false confidence ("I thought I was live but I wasn't").
* **Preserving interpretive authority:** insights are surfaced as *signals*, not diagnoses; the transcript remains primary evidence.

---

## Day-in-the-life scenarios

### Scenario A — "I need to orient fast"

A supervisee starts a live session. The supervisor joins late.

* The dashboard shows **LIVE** status and begins streaming transcript segments.
* A small set of live insights begins to appear (e.g., risk markers, alliance rupture cues, somatic activation).
* The supervisor clicks one insight and immediately sees the full detail + the transcript jumps to the moment it references.

**Result:** rapid situational awareness with minimal verbal interruption.

### Scenario B — "This is escalating; I need the exact moment"

A conflict moment happens. The supervisor senses a turning point but wants precision.

* Clicking the relevant insight **jumps the transcript** to the closest segment.
* The segment briefly highlights, and auto-scroll pauses so the supervisor can read context.

**Result:** supervision is grounded in "what was actually said," not post-hoc narrative drift.

### Scenario C — "Connectivity drops; don't lie to me"

Network hiccups occur mid-session.

* The UI shows **Reconnecting…** (explicitly).
* If streaming fails, it switches to **LIVE (fallback)** (polling) rather than freezing silently.

**Result:** the supervisor knows what state they're in—no phantom "live" experience.

---

## Clinical safety posture (how it behaves)

* **Transcript is evidence.** Insights are prompts, not conclusions.
* **Clarity over cleverness.** Connection status is always visible.
* **Consistency across modes.** Click behavior works the same in live and history.
* **Auditability preserved.** Everything important resolves into stored segments/insight events for review.

---

## What clinicians will notice immediately

* Less "Where was that moment?" searching.
* Less "I missed something while I was thinking."
* More anchored feedback: "At 12:03 you asked X; the client responded Y; here's what shifted."
