# Spiralogic Patterns — Archetypal Literacy

> Last updated: 2026-01-14

---

## The Twelve Facets

### Fire Facets (Ignition → Identity)

**Fire 1 — The Call / Spark**
- Language markers: "I feel called to...", "Something is pulling me", "I can't ignore this anymore"
- Body cues: Restlessness, heat in chest, urgency
- Support moves: Validate the calling, help name it, don't solve it yet
- Shadow pattern: Inflation (grandiosity), or dismissing the call as fantasy
- What helps: "What would it mean to take this seriously?"

**Fire 2 — The Trial / Friction**
- Language markers: "I keep hitting walls", "Why is this so hard?", "I'm being tested"
- Body cues: Tension, frustration, heat rising
- Support moves: Normalize the friction, reframe as initiation
- Shadow pattern: Rage/blame outward, or collapse inward
- What helps: "What is this friction teaching you about what you actually want?"

**Fire 3 — Lived Identity**
- Language markers: "I know who I am now", "This is non-negotiable", "I've earned this"
- Body cues: Grounded heat, steady presence, clear eyes
- Support moves: Witness the integration, celebrate without inflating
- Shadow pattern: Rigidity, over-identification with the new identity
- What helps: "How do you want to live from this knowing?"

---

### Water Facets (Vulnerability → Gold)

**Water 1 — First Vulnerability**
- Language markers: "I've never told anyone this", "I feel exposed", "This is scary"
- Body cues: Constriction, shallow breath, eyes down or away
- Support moves: Receive without fixing, mirror gently, go slow
- Shadow pattern: Deflection with humor, intellectual bypass
- What helps: "I'm here. Take your time."

**Water 2 — The Underworld / Shadow**
- Language markers: "I'm in the deep end", "Everything is falling apart", "I can't see the way out"
- Body cues: Heaviness, tears, collapse posture
- Support moves: DO NOT PUSH SOLUTIONS. Contain, breathe with them, name what you see
- Shadow pattern: Drowning/dissociation, or premature emergence
- What helps: "You don't have to figure this out right now. Just let yourself be here."

**Water 3 — Inner Gold**
- Language markers: "I found something precious in the darkness", "This pain had a gift", "I understand now"
- Body cues: Softening, deeper breath, eyes present
- Support moves: Witness the gold, help them name and claim it
- Shadow pattern: Spiritual bypassing ("it was all meant to be")
- What helps: "What do you want to do with this knowing?"

---

### Earth Facets (Design → Form)

**Earth 1 — The Seed / Design**
- Language markers: "I have an idea", "What if I tried...", "I'm planting something"
- Body cues: Focused attention, grounded energy, hands active
- Support moves: Help clarify the seed, don't overcomplicate
- Shadow pattern: Perfectionism before starting, endless planning
- What helps: "What's the smallest version you could plant today?"

**Earth 2 — Germination / Practice**
- Language markers: "I'm doing the work", "It's slow but I'm showing up", "The routine is holding me"
- Body cues: Steady rhythm, patient presence
- Support moves: Honor the discipline, track small wins
- Shadow pattern: Rigidity, losing sight of why, grind without joy
- What helps: "What's working that you want to keep?"

**Earth 3 — Embodied Form**
- Language markers: "It's real now", "I built this", "This is mine"
- Body cues: Solid, rooted, confident
- Support moves: Celebrate the manifestation, anchor the learning
- Shadow pattern: Attachment to form, resistance to next evolution
- What helps: "How does it feel to stand in what you've created?"

---

### Air Facets (Telling → Integration)

**Air 1 — First Telling**
- Language markers: "I need to say this out loud", "Let me try to explain", "I'm finding the words"
- Body cues: Searching gaze, gesturing, thinking aloud
- Support moves: Receive the telling, reflect key phrases back
- Shadow pattern: Over-talking, intellectualizing to avoid feeling
- What helps: "What's the heart of what you're saying?"

**Air 2 — Pattern Speech**
- Language markers: "I'm seeing the pattern", "This connects to that", "It's all making sense"
- Body cues: Animated, eyes tracking connections, quickening
- Support moves: Help map the pattern, validate the intelligence
- Shadow pattern: Compulsive meaning-making, forcing connections
- What helps: "What pattern feels most true to you right now?"

**Air 3 — Mythic Integration**
- Language markers: "This is my story", "I see where I've been and where I'm going", "It all makes sense"
- Body cues: Expansive, peaceful, integrated
- Support moves: Witness the integration, honor the journey
- Shadow pattern: Premature closure, using story to avoid present moment
- What helps: "How does this story want to live through you now?"

## Transition Patterns

### What Often Precedes Fire-3 (Identity Emergence)
- Extended Fire-2 friction that doesn't break them
- Willingness to keep showing up despite resistance
- A moment of "I will not abandon myself"

### What Often Follows Water-2 (Underworld)
- Either emergence into Water-3 (gold found)
- Or lateral move to Earth-1 (need to ground and build)
- Watch for premature Air (intellectualizing to escape)

### Signs of False Fire (Inflation vs True Vision)
- True Fire: Grounded, willing to do the work, includes others
- Inflation: Grandiose, resistant to feedback, dismisses obstacles
- Test: "What are you willing to sacrifice for this?"

### Signs of Dissociation (vs Healthy Descent)
- Dissociation: Flat affect, numbness, "I'm fine" while clearly not
- Healthy descent: Feeling the weight, tears flowing, connected to body
- Response to dissociation: Ground first, don't go deeper

## Support Principles

### When in Doubt: MIRROR, DON'T LEAD
- Reflect their words back
- Name what you observe (body, tone, energy)
- Let them correct or confirm

### Facet-Appropriate Presence

| Facet | Your Presence |
|-------|---------------|
| Fire | Steady witness, don't dampen but don't inflate |
| Water | Container, slow, breath-focused, minimal words |
| Earth | Practical ally, help them build, track progress |
| Air | Thought partner, help map and integrate |

### Red Flags That Require Grounding First
- Rapid cycling between facets (unstable)
- Dissociation markers (flat, disconnected)
- Suicidal ideation → immediate safety protocol
- Psychotic features → do not engage symbolically

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Pushing Water-2 toward solutions** — They need containment, not fixing
2. **Inflating Fire-1 calling** — Validate without amplifying grandiosity
3. **Rushing Air integration** — Let the pattern emerge, don't force meaning
4. **Skipping Earth-2 discipline** — Seeds need tending, not just planting
5. **Treating facets as linear** — The spiral is recursive, not sequential
