# Patterns Across Users — Recurring Themes

> Last updated: 2026-01-14

---

## Onboarding Friction

### Pattern
Users receive passkeys but don't complete registration. 92% drop-off rate.

### Contributing Factors
- Passkey concept unfamiliar
- Multiple steps required (passkey → username → password → onboarding)
- Unclear what MAIA offers before committing

### What Helps
- Clear, direct instructions: "Go to soullab.life/begin, enter SOULLAB-[NAME]"
- Explaining what awaits: "MAIA is ready to meet you"
- Removing ambiguity about next steps

---

## MAIA-PAI Confusion

### Pattern
Previous MAIA-PAI users expect old credentials to work.

### Frequency
Common among early adopters and returning users.

### What Helps
- Acknowledge the history: "You used the previous MAIA"
- Explain the fresh start: "MAIA-SOVEREIGN is a new system"
- Reassure: "Your passkey is ready for your new account"

---

## Password vs Passkey Confusion

### Pattern
Users conflate "passkey" (registration code) with "password" (account credential).

### Manifestations
- Entering passkey in password field
- Asking to "reset my passkey" when they mean password
- Confusion about which is needed where

### What Helps
- Clear language: "Your passkey is SOULLAB-[NAME] — use it at soullab.life/begin"
- Distinguish: "After registration, you'll sign in with your username and password"

## Email Deliverability Anxiety

### Pattern
Users worry emails aren't arriving, even when they are.

### Contributing Factors
- Spam folder catches emails
- Expectation of instant delivery
- Previous bad experiences with other services

### What Helps
- Proactive: "Check your spam folder for emails from noreply@soullab.life"
- Set expectations: "The email should arrive within a few minutes"
