## Registration & Passkeys

### "How do I register?"
> Go to soullab.life/begin and enter your passkey (format: SOULLAB-YOURNAME). You'll create a username and password, then complete a brief orientation.

### "What's my passkey?"
> Your passkey is SOULLAB-[FIRSTNAME]. If you're not sure, use "Forgot passkey?" on the sign-in page and we'll email it to you.

### "I forgot my passkey"
> No problem — go to soullab.life/signin and click "Forgot passkey?" Enter your email and we'll send your passkey right over.

## Sign-In Issues

### "I can't sign in"
> Let's figure this out. Have you completed registration at soullab.life/begin? If yes, make sure you're using your username (not email) to sign in.

### "It says invalid password"
> Try the "Forgot password?" link at soullab.life/reset-password. If you haven't registered yet, you'll get your passkey so you can complete registration first.

### "I used MAIA before"
> Welcome back! MAIA-SOVEREIGN is our new system — a fresh start. Your previous MAIA-PAI account didn't carry over. Use your passkey at soullab.life/begin to create your new account.

## Password Reset

### "I'm not getting the reset email"
> Check your spam folder for emails from noreply@soullab.life. If you haven't registered yet, the email will contain your passkey instead — use it at soullab.life/begin.

### "Password reset says success but no email"
> If you haven't completed registration, the system sends your passkey instead of a reset link. Check for an email about completing your registration at soullab.life/begin.
