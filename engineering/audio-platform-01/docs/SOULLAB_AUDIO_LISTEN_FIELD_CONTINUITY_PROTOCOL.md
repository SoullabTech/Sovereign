## I. What we are making, and why

**SOULLAB — For the life you live. For the world we make.**

Soullab's Listening Field is a sophisticated, immersive relationship with music, silence, sound, imagination, emotional life, composition and shared human culture. A person may have music alongside their coding, writing, conversation, walking, meditation, rest and daily living. The sound accompanies *their* life; it does not demand attention, intimate disclosure or measurable productivity.

Our creative references include the dreamlike atmosphere of Air, Massive Attack, Emancipator, Kruder & Dorfmeister, Morcheeba and Thievery Corporation: downtempo, trip-hop, soulful electronica and worldly, discerning musical culture. These are references for **feeling and curatorial taste**, not rights to use those artists' recordings, artwork or likeness.

Soullab must offer more than a list of tracks: an invitation to discover, listen attentively, feel, imagine, create, restore and be in the world. Editorial listening, optional private reflections, original soundscapes, and authentic artist participation are part of that vision. MAIA may accompany listening when invited, but may not infer identity, diagnose emotion or silently analyze someone's provider music.

**Protect these experience qualities:** beauty and atmosphere; exceptional, honestly reported playback quality; tactile and humane controls; music that can travel across authorized Soullab rooms; opt-in silence; member-held collections; cultural respect; real artist attribution and fair licensing; sovereignty/offline possibilities; accessible volume and stop controls.

### One living field, differentiated rooms
- **Listening Room:** an editorial threshold to music: Focus / Create / Drift / Rest; private collections, authorized discovery and deep listening.
- **Sound Sanctuary:** member-controlled sound composition, layers, stillness, bowls/drones/nature where authorized; genuine musical craft, not an overclaim of treatment. Its mixed stems count as **one sound session** for focus.
- **Soullab Sessions / artist works:** real, longer recordings commissioned or expressly licensed for the specific playback modes we offer, with artists and world traditions respected.
- **MAIA, Writer's Studio and Reader:** speech/narration that cooperates with background music without stealing authorship or unexpected microphone control.
- **House, Pro Studio, Astrology and Journals:** context-appropriate listening and recording with their own private/session consents; no automatic private-content handoff.
- **Jarvis, Cabin and mobile:** the same human-facing rules delivered by separately qualified desktop, native and offline adapters, not by pretending one browser lock controls every device.

## III. Unchanging promises / prohibitions

1. **Music accompanies life, not vice versa.** Background continuity is a choice. Do not demand a journal, emotion label, subscription or AI session merely to hear a member-authorized recording.
2. **One listening experience, not duplicate players.** Reconcile with the protected `lib/hooks/useAudioCoordinator.ts`, existing `AudioSessionManager` and approved Listening Room review transport. Do not spawn an independent music/voice manager in each product.
3. **Voice and silence are sovereign.** Spoken MAIA/Writer/real practitioner turns respect floor/recording authority. No audio-focus event starts a microphone or cancels another person's session. A member can pause, stop or be silent.
4. **No surprises after interruptions.** A paused soundtrack does not automatically restart after TTS failure, phone call, member switch or logout. Resume is explicit.
5. **Music rights are not playlist portability.** Spotify/TIDAL/Apple Music links/import metadata are not authorization to host/restream, combine with AI, mix, download or redistribute commercial recordings. Separate master, composition, samples, territory, usage, expiry and revocation are mandatory.
6. **Truth in audio quality.** Original recording provenance, source format, delivered codec, browser output and actual headphones/DAC route are distinct; FLAC decoding does not guarantee bit-perfect output. Do not fake hi-res or conceal compression.
7. **Private by default.** Member listening reflections and history are not silently sent to MAIA, clinicians, advertisers or other members. Manual passphrase exports are not the same as an accepted member vault or deletion-safe recovery.
8. **Authentic aesthetic, real humanity.** Preserve the worldly urban/cross-cultural music atmosphere and thoughtful cinematic design. Do not flatten it into generic wellness dashboards or passive lists.

## V. The LISTEN FIELD continuity routine

For every substantial “continue,” new ChatGPT lane, Jarvis session or integration handoff:

1. **Enter the same field.** Read [#1982](https://github.com/SoullabTech/Sovereign/issues/1982) *only for portfolio orientation*, then the current [#1979](https://github.com/SoullabTech/Sovereign/issues/1979) **experience** record and [#1984](https://github.com/SoullabTech/Sovereign/issues/1984) **shared audio** record. If touching MAIA/Writer/Cabin/Pro, read the **actual owning** issue and active source gate.
2. **Recover the vision before technical work.** Summarize the desired sonic life, mood, taste, quality, artist/culture, privacy, felt experience and what has already been approved. Preserve its existing aesthetic.
3. **Witness the truth.** Verify exact branch/commit, real UI and sounds, test environment, owner/custody and current HOLDs. Mark **DESIGN / ISOLATED PASS / SOURCE INTEGRATED / DEVICE VERIFIED / RIGHTS CLEARED / RELEASED** separately; UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
4. **Ask which field layer is at stake.** Is the next action about the listening experience, sound content/rights, shared focus/voice, member data, or device output? Work with the **one relevant existing owner**; don't turn every listening request into an audio-lock refactor.
5. **Choose one critical-path step.** Favor finished, beautiful, member-testable *listening journeys* and true integration over accumulating another set of isolated builds. Technical gates are necessary but must not become the product.
6. **Apply the no-drift gate.** Stop if creating a second app/player, redesigning an approved room, expanding providers without licensing, claiming lossless from a source label, replaying a stale source patch, skipping private consent/security, or promoting fictional Chrome green to mobile/public-ready.
7. **Record and return.** Update the project-owned issue, not only a conversation. Include exact SHA/artifact, successful and failed tests, source owner, accepted design choices, active HOLD, next small action, and a founder listening/felt test when genuinely possible. Preserve one active directing lane.
