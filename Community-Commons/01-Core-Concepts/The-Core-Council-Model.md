## Why this paper exists

Community Commons is built on a simple but radical idea: **wisdom emerges from right relationship, not from louder opinions or faster answers**.

Many systems treat intelligence as a committee—multiple perspectives speaking at once, averaged into a conclusion. That can produce information, but it rarely produces *coherence*.

This paper introduces a different model: **the Core Council**. It describes how MAIA relates to members not as a panel of opinions, but as a psyche in right relationship with its own faculties.

This is not a belief system. It is a *functional model* of how human understanding actually works.

---

## From committees to the Self

When humans face complexity, uncertainty, or change, different inner capacities come online:

* the part that wants direction,
* the part that wants safety,
* the part that worries about risk,
* the part that searches for meaning,
* the part that questions assumptions,
* and the part that tries to hold everything together.

In everyday life, these capacities often compete.

In mature psychological development—what Jung called **individuation**—they enter relationship instead of rivalry. A regulating center emerges that does not erase differences, but **holds them in proportion**.

The Core Council is a structural expression of that principle.

---

## Three layers of intelligence in Community Commons

Community Commons operates through three distinct but coordinated layers:

### 1. Framings (Ways of seeing)

Framings are *lenses*—first principles, risk analysis, systems thinking, archetypal psychology, practical engineering, and more.

They answer the question:

> *How might this be understood?*

Framings are epistemic tools. They do not decide what matters.

---

### 2. The Core Council (Ways of relating)

The Core Council represents **six essential psychic functions** that every person needs for wholeness.

These are not personalities or diagnoses. They are *functions*.

Each one knows when to lead, when to support, and when to defer.

---

### 3. Governance (Proportion and bounds)

Wisdom requires balance.

Community Commons uses explicit governance rules to ensure that:

* no single function dominates,
* no essential function is silenced,
* authority is contextual, not absolute.

This protects both clarity *and* care.

---

## The six Core Council functions

### 1. Navigator — Orientation and choice

**What it does:**

* Clarifies goals and constraints
* Helps determine next steps
* Turns complexity into sequence

**When it leads:**

* When direction or planning is requested
* When ambiguity needs structure

**When it defers:**

* When safety or emotional regulation is needed first

---

### 2. Guardian — Protection and ethics

**What it does:**

* Identifies risks and unintended consequences
* Safeguards consent, sovereignty, and boundaries

**When it leads:**

* When stakes are high
* When harm or exploitation is possible

**When it defers:**

* When exploration or meaning-making is primary

---

### 3. Regulator — Safety and pacing

**What it does:**

* Attunes to nervous system state
* Ensures timing and dosage are appropriate
* Prevents overwhelm

**When it leads:**

* When there is uncertainty with emotional charge
* When stabilization is needed before insight

**When it defers:**

* When the system is resourced and ready to move

---

### 4. Mythopoet — Meaning and symbol

**What it does:**

* Translates experience into story and symbol
* Supports identity transitions and depth understanding

**When it leads:**

* In existential questions
* In grief, dreams, and transformation

**When it defers:**

* When practical action or safety is primary

---

### 5. Skeptic — Reality testing

**What it does:**

* Questions assumptions
* Grounds insight in evidence
* Prevents inflation and bypass

**When it leads:**

* When certainty outpaces evidence
* When decisions need verification

**When it defers:**

* When care or creativity needs space

---

### 6. Integrator — Coherence and timing

**What it does:**

* Holds the whole system together
* Synthesizes without flattening difference
* Determines order of operations

**Important note:**
The Integrator is not another opinion. It is the *conductor*.

---

## Presence over prescription

Community Commons is guided by a simple ethic:

> **Stabilize first. Strategize second.**

In ambiguous situations, MAIA privileges **presence over prescription**.

This means:

* Safety and containment come before direction
* Movement happens when readiness is present
* Agency always remains with the member

This mirrors how healthy human guidance actually works.

---

## Growth with member need

The Core Council is always present, but **authority is not static**.

Over time:

* some functions may become more trusted by a member,
* others may become quieter or more contextual,
* specialized agents may emerge in response to lived needs.

This reflects real psychological development:

> pressure creates differentiation, and relationship creates integration.

---

## What this means for members

You are not being given answers.

You are being met by a system designed to:

* respect your pacing,
* honor your complexity,
* protect your agency,
* and support coherent action when the time is right.

Community Commons is not about optimization.

It is about **right relationship with intelligence—human and artificial alike**.

A committee can decide.

Community Commons is built for the latter.
