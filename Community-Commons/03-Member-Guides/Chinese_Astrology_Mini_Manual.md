# Chinese Astrology in AIN

## Your 10-Year Life Chapters (Da Yun)

Chinese Astrology isn't just "what animal am I?"
In AIN, it becomes a **timing map** — a way to understand the *season you're in*, what it's asking of you, and what's coming next.

### What you'll get

**1) A clear life-phase timeline**
See your life in **10-year chapters** (Da Yun): *where you've been, where you are, where you're headed.*

**2) A deeper "blueprint" than the zodiac**
Four Pillars (Ba Zi) adds depth beyond your birth year — like a personality + timing architecture.

**3) Practical guidance, not fate**
Each chapter includes:

- likely **themes**
- **opportunities** (what supports you)
- **challenges** (what asks growth)
- a simple **body-mind wellness lens** inspired by Chinese medicine (TCM)

---

## How it works (simple version)

AIN uses your birth info to calculate:

- **Four Pillars (Ba Zi):** Year, Month, Day, Hour (if available)
- **Da Yun (10-Year Cycles):** your major timing periods across life

Then it shows you:

- your **current decade** (what's active now)
- the **previous** chapter (what shaped you)
- the **next** chapter (what's emerging)

---

## What you'll enter

- **Birth date** (required)
- **Gender** (required for classical cycle direction)
- **Birth time** (optional, improves accuracy)

Don't know your birth time? No problem — you still get a strong timeline.
