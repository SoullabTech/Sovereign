# Chinese Astrology in AIN (MAIA)

## Overview + User Manual

### What this is

AIN's Chinese Astrology portal is a reflective system for understanding:

* **Your Zodiac Profile** (Year animal + Five Element tone)
* **Your Four Pillars (Ba Zi / 八字)** — the deeper "destiny blueprint"
* **Your Da Yun (大運)** — the **10-year life cycles** that show *where you've been, where you are, and where you're headed*

It's designed for **self-knowledge, timing, and life-phase clarity** — not prediction as fate, and not medical advice.

## 3) Understanding the Four Pillars (Ba Zi)

Think of Ba Zi as **four columns of symbolic timing** derived from the Chinese calendrical system:

### The Pillars

* **Year Pillar:** ancestry, roots, collective imprint, early climate
* **Month Pillar:** development environment, family/culture shaping forces, "career engine" themes
* **Day Pillar:** *you* (core identity / inner nature)
* **Hour Pillar:** future vector, legacy, later-life unfolding, deeper destiny threads

Each pillar is a **Stem + Branch** pairing.

### Heavenly Stems (10)

A "celestial" cycle expressing **element + Yin/Yang quality**.

### Earthly Branches (12)

A "terrestrial" cycle (often linked with animals) expressing **seasonal, embodied, situational energies**.

Together they form the **60-cycle** (sexagenary cycle) used throughout Chinese astrology.

## 4) What Da Yun is (and why it matters)

### Da Yun = your 10-year life chapters

Da Yun (大運) divides life into **major decades**, each ruled by a distinct **Stem-Branch** combination and elemental tone.

This is the part you were describing:

* **Past decades:** what shaped you
* **Current decade:** what you're working with now
* **Next decade:** what's ripening / coming online

AIN presents Da Yun as a **timeline** so you can see your story as seasons, not random events.

### What each decade includes

For each 10-year period, you'll typically see:

* **Age range** (e.g., 33–42)
* **Stem + Branch** (your decade's signature)
* **Element + Yin/Yang** tone
* **Life theme** (headline meaning of the decade)
* **Opportunities** (what flows more easily)
* **Challenges** (what asks maturity or skill)
* **TCM Organ Focus** (yin/yang organ pair)
* **Spirit Focus (Wu Shen)** — the psycho-spiritual cultivation theme
* A "supportive / neutral / challenging" read based on relationship to your natal pattern

## 5) The TCM lens inside AIN (how to use it)

AIN maps Chinese Astrology's Five Elements into a **practical body-psyche guidance layer**:

### Five Elements → Organ Pairs → Spirit

* **Wood** → Liver / Gallbladder → **Hun** (vision, direction, the "wandering soul")
* **Fire** → Heart / Small Intestine → **Shen** (radiance, identity, connection)
* **Earth** → Spleen / Stomach → **Yi** (integration, nourishment, steadiness)
* **Metal** → Lung / Large Intestine → **Po** (boundaries, grief, letting go)
* **Water** → Kidney / Bladder → **Zhi** (will, essence, deep wisdom)

**How to use this wisely:**
Treat it as a **seasonal focus**, not a diagnosis. It's a way to choose supportive practices, pacing, and attention.

## 10) Quick FAQ

### "Why does gender matter here?"

In classical Ba Zi, the flow direction of Da Yun is derived using **gender + Yin/Yang polarity**. It's not about identity; it's a traditional calculation rule.

### "I don't know my birth time—should I skip Da Yun?"

No. You'll still get a strong decade-cycle map. Birth time just adds an extra layer of specificity.

### "Is this predicting the future?"

It's mapping **life seasons and likely themes**, not certainties. Think: *weather patterns*, not *fixed destiny*.

### "Is the TCM guidance medical advice?"

No. It's a reflective wellness lens. Always consult qualified clinicians for medical care.
