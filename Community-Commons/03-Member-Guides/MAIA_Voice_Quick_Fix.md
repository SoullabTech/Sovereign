# MAIA Voice Not Working? Quick Fix (Member One-Pager)

If MAIA says **"Listening…"** but doesn't hear you, do this:

## Step 1 — Check Permissions (60 seconds)
1) **Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → MAIA → ON**
2) **Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition → MAIA → ON**
3) Close MAIA completely (swipe up), then reopen

## Step 2 — Update MAIA in TestFlight
1) Open **TestFlight**
2) Find **MAIA**
3) Tap **Update** (pull down to refresh if you don't see it)

## Step 3 — Toggle Text Display (optional)
There's a **Show Text / Hide Text** toggle in the upper right.
If it's off, MAIA may be hearing you but you won't see transcript text.

## Step 4 — Try Without Bluetooth
AirPods / car audio can reroute the mic.
Turn Bluetooth off temporarily and test again.

## Step 5 — Last Resort: Reinstall
1) Delete MAIA from your iPhone
2) Reinstall from TestFlight
3) Accept the permission prompts again

## If it's still broken, send support:
- iPhone model + iOS version
- MAIA build number (TestFlight)
- Whether **Microphone** + **Speech Recognition** permissions are ON
- A screenshot/screen recording if possible
