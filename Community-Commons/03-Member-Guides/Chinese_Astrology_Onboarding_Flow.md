# Chinese Astrology Onboarding Flow

## 3-Screen Onboarding Sequence

### Screen 1 — "Your Life Timeline, Not Just a Zodiac"

**Headline:** Your 10-Year Life Chapters (Da Yun)

**Body:**
AIN maps your life into **10-year chapters** so you can understand:

* what shaped you (past chapter)
* what's active now (current chapter)
* what's emerging next (future chapter)

**Mini-note (small text):** This isn't fortune-telling. It's **timing + pattern insight** you can use for better decisions.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Continue**
* Secondary: **Skip for now**

---

### Screen 2 — "Enter birth details"

**Headline:** Generate your Four Pillars + Da Yun

**Fields:**
* Birth date (required)
* Gender (required) — *used for traditional cycle direction*
* Birth time (optional) — *improves hour pillar accuracy*

**Helper text:** Don't know your birth time? You'll still get a strong timeline.

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Reveal My Life Timeline**
* Secondary: **I'll add birth time later**

---

### Screen 3 — "How to use this inside AIN"

**Headline:** Use it when life is shifting

**Body:**
Chinese Astrology is best for:

* transitions, "stuckness," reinvention
* career/relationship cycles
* health + energy patterns (TCM-inspired lens)

**Privacy note:** Your birth data is used only to generate your profile in AIN.

**Suggested prompts (tap-to-use):**
* "Explain my **current 10-year chapter** plainly."
* "What's the **best focus** for this decade?"
* "What's the **growth edge** here?"
* "Give me **practices** aligned with this cycle."

**Buttons:**
* Primary: **Go to My Current Chapter**
* Secondary: **Explore All Cycles**
