# Astrology Systems in AIN (MAIA)

## Three Lenses, One Coherent Read

AIN integrates three distinct astrological traditions — not as separate modules, but as **complementary lenses** that MAIA weaves together for deeper, more actionable insight.

Each system answers different questions. Together, they create a multi-dimensional map of who you are, what you're navigating, and what's emerging.

---

## The Three Systems

### Western Astrology
**What it reveals:** Planetary forces, psychological dynamics, timing of external events

| Component | What It Shows |
|-----------|---------------|
| Birth Chart | Your personality architecture — planets, signs, houses |
| Aspects | How different parts of you interact (harmony, tension, growth edges) |
| Transits | Current planetary weather — what's activating your chart now |
| Saturn Return | Major life restructuring cycles (~29, 58, 87 years) |
| Synastry | Relationship dynamics between two charts |

**Best for:** Understanding psychological patterns, relationship dynamics, and current planetary influences.

**Location:** `/astrology` (main blueprint page)

---

### Chinese Astrology (Ba Zi + Da Yun)
**What it reveals:** Life timing, 10-year chapters, body-mind wellness patterns

| Component | What It Shows |
|-----------|---------------|
| Four Pillars (Ba Zi) | Year, Month, Day, Hour — your destiny blueprint |
| Da Yun | 10-year life chapters with themes, opportunities, challenges |
| Five Elements | Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — elemental balance |
| TCM Lens | Organ systems + Wu Shen (psycho-spiritual cultivation) |

**Best for:** Understanding life phases, timing major decisions, and body-mind seasonal focus.

**Location:** `/astrology/chinese`

---

### Mayan Astrology (Tzolk'in + Path of Life)
**What it reveals:** Archetypal identity, soul evolution, daily timing

| Component | What It Shows |
|-----------|---------------|
| Galactic Signature | Solar Seal (archetype) + Galactic Tone (expression quality) |
| Path of Life | Three pillars: Youth, Adulthood, Future |
| Haab Calendar | 365-day solar position |
| Cardinal Direction | Cosmic orientation (East/North/West/South) |
| Today's Energy | Current day's galactic signature |

**Best for:** Understanding your core archetype, tracking soul evolution, and daily energetic timing.

**Location:** `/astrology/mayan`
