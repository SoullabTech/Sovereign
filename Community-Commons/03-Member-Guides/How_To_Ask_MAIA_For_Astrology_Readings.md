## Choose Your Lens (Or Let MAIA Choose)

### Developmental Lens

**Best for:** Growth, healing, shadow work, relationship patterns, life lessons.

**You'll get:** Maturity tasks, inner conflicts, unconscious patterns, integration steps.

### Mythic Lens

**Best for:** Meaning, soul-story, archetypes, initiation, spiritual narrative.

**You'll get:** The "story" you're living, the archetype(s) active, symbolic practices.

### Timing Lens

**Best for:** "What's happening now?" and "what's coming?"

**You'll get:** Transit/progression themes, activation windows, pacing guidance.

### Integration Lens

**Best for:** Grounding insights into real life.

**You'll get:** Somatic practices, journaling prompts, relationship/career translation.

### Spiralogic Elemental Lens

**Best for:** Your elemental state + alchemical evolution.

**You'll get:** Fire/Water/Earth/Air/Aether balance, facet phase, coherence practices.

## House Systems (Simple Guide)

If you don't know, MAIA will default to a clean, coherent choice.

| House System | Best For |
|--------------|----------|
| **Whole Sign** | Developmental clarity and clean story |
| **Placidus** | Psychological nuance (when birth time is accurate) |
| **Porphyry** | Angle-based storytelling with fewer distortions |
| **Equal House** | Teaching + stable consistency |
| **Koch** | Subjective inner experience (some resonate strongly) |

You can also ask MAIA: **"Run this in Whole Sign + Placidus and compare."**

---

## What MAIA Returns (Default Format)

1. **Core Pattern** — what's structurally true
2. **Current Activation** — what's live right now (if you gave timing data)
3. **Best Next Integration Move** — what to do this week
