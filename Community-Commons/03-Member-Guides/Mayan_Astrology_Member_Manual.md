# Mayan Astrology in AIN (MAIA)

## Overview + User Manual

### What this is

AIN's Mayan Astrology portal connects you to the **Tzolk'in Sacred Calendar** — a 260-day cycle that reveals:

* **Your Galactic Signature** (Solar Seal + Galactic Tone)
* **Your Path of Life** — three pillars showing Youth, Adulthood, and Future archetypes
* **Your Haab Date** — your position in the 365-day solar calendar
* **Today's Energy** — the current day's galactic signature

It's designed for **self-knowledge, archetypal understanding, and timing awareness** — not prediction as fate.

## 3) Understanding the Tzolk'in Calendar

The Tzolk'in is a **260-day sacred calendar** formed by two interlocking cycles:

### 20 Solar Seals (Day Signs)

Each day sign has:
* **Glyph** — the visual symbol
* **Archetype** — the energy persona (e.g., "The Dreamer," "The Warrior")
* **Power / Action / Essence** — core qualities
* **Element** — Fire, Water, Earth, or Air
* **Meaning** — the deeper significance

### 13 Galactic Tones

Each tone has:
* **Number** (1-13)
* **Name** (Magnetic, Lunar, Electric, Self-Existing, etc.)
* **Intention** — the creative purpose
* **Stage** — Initiation, Challenge, Empowerment, or Completion

Together: **13 tones × 20 seals = 260 unique day signatures**

Your **Galactic Signature** combines both: e.g., "Red Magnetic Dragon" (Tone 1 + Seal 1)

## 5) Additional Mayan elements

### Haab Solar Calendar
The 365-day civil calendar:
* **18 months** of 20 days each
* Plus **Wayeb** — 5 "nameless days" of reflection
* Your Haab date shows your position in the solar cycle

### Cardinal Direction
Each day sign aligns with a cosmic direction:
* **East** (Red) — Initiation, new beginnings
* **North** (White) — Refinement, purification
* **West** (Black/Blue) — Transformation, introspection
* **South** (Yellow) — Ripening, manifestation

* Mayan astrology uses the **Dreamspell correlation** (José Argüelles system), which is the most widely used modern interpretation.
* Traditional Mayan day-keepers may use slightly different correlations.
* The Path of Life pillars are archetypal guides, not literal age boundaries.

## 10) Quick FAQ

### "What's the difference between Solar Seal and Galactic Tone?"

The Solar Seal is your day sign archetype (what kind of energy). The Galactic Tone is the quality or intention of that energy (how it expresses).

### "Why are there 260 days in the Tzolk'in?"

260 days corresponds to the human gestation period, making it a calendar of human becoming. It also aligns with astronomical cycles.

### "Is this connected to Spiralogic?"

Yes. Your Mayan element (Fire/Water/Earth/Air) maps to the Spiralogic elemental pathways, creating a bridge between ancient wisdom and conscious evolution work.

### "Can I use this with Western astrology?"

Absolutely. MAIA can help you integrate both systems — your Western chart shows planetary influences, while your Mayan chart shows archetypal timing.
