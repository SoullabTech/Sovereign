# Mayan Astrology in AIN

_A practical guide to your Tzolk'in signature, life-phase pillars, and today's Mayan "cosmic weather."_

## What this is

AIN includes a **Mayan astrology layer** designed for **self-understanding, life timing, and mythic orientation**—without needing birth time or location.

You'll get two kinds of insight:

1. **Today's Mayan Day Sign** (available for everyone, every day)

2. **Your Mayan Birth Profile** (available once you add your birth date)

This system is based on the **Tzolk'in** (260-day sacred calendar), plus additional layers (Three Pillars, cardinal direction, and Haab).

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1 — Add your birth date

Go to **Edit Birth Data** and enter your **birth date**.

- Needed: **Birth date**
- Not needed: birth time, birthplace

### Step 2 — Visit the Mayan Astrology page

Open the **Mayan Astrology** section in the Astrology area (your Mayan page).

### Step 3 — Ask MAIA for it (optional)

In chat, try:

- "What's my Mayan birth sign and what does it mean?"
- "What are my Three Pillars?"

## What you'll see (and how to use it)

### 1) Today's Mayan Day Sign (Daily "Cosmic Weather")

This is the day's energetic signature:
**Tone (1–13) + Day Sign (one of 20 glyphs)**

Use it for:

- Daily intention-setting
- Choosing the _right kind of effort_ (push vs. flow)
- Ritual timing (reflection, cleansing, initiating, completing)

**Member practice:**
Ask: "What does this day ask of me—action, listening, clearing, connecting, completing?"

---

### 2) Your Birth Day Sign (Tzolk'in)

This is your core Mayan imprint:

- **Your Day Sign** = your essential archetypal medicine (what you embody)
- **Your Tone (1–13)** = how your medicine expresses (your "frequency" of becoming)

Use it for:

- Understanding your default strengths and shadows
- Naming your deep gifts (and the way they get distorted under stress)
- Aligning your growth work with your innate pattern

**Member practice:**
Ask: "When I'm at my best, how does this archetype move through me? When I'm stressed, how does it twist?"

---

### 3) Path of Life — The Three Pillars

AIN shows three developmental pillars:

- **Youth Pillar** (birth → ~26): early imprinting, formative lessons
- **Adulthood Pillar** (main life expression): how you live your medicine
- **Future Pillar** (~52+): mature destiny arc, later-life flowering

Use it for:

- Life review and pattern recognition
- Framing transitions (midlife shifts, reinventions, initiations)
- Identifying what you're being asked to mature into

**Member practice:**
Ask: "What did I master in the Youth pillar? What am I integrating in the Adulthood pillar? What is beginning to call from the Future pillar?"

---

### 4) Life Phase Insight (Personalized)

AIN also gives a plain-language summary of what your current phase suggests based on your age and pillar emphasis.

Use it for:

- Normalizing change (you're not "off," you're transitioning)
- Choosing the right inner work (discipline vs. release vs. devotion)
- Giving your story a larger arc (mythic coherence)

---

### 5) Cardinal Direction (with a color + activity)

You'll see a directional orientation (East/North/West/South), often paired with:

- A **color**
- A **core activity** (your soul's "motion")

Use it for:

- Grounding decisions ("what direction am I moving?")
- Locating energetic imbalance (overdoing one mode of being)
- Creating simple ritual: face the direction, set intention, speak the activity

---

### 6) Haab Solar Calendar (Seasonal layer)

The Haab adds a **solar/seasonal** signature (month and meaning).

Use it for:

- Seasonal reflection: "What kind of year is this for me?"
- Longer-cycle context around goals, harvest, rest, repair, renewal

## How MAIA uses Mayan Astrology (important)

MAIA follows this rule:

- In **normal conversation**, MAIA will _not_ push astrology at you. It may hold it quietly as context.
- If you **explicitly ask**, MAIA will speak directly and in detail.
- If you talk about **life phases, transitions, purpose, destiny**, MAIA may gently reference your **Three Pillars** as a helpful mirror.

If you ever want MAIA to _stop_ using it in responses, you can say:

- "Don't use astrology in this conversation."

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see my birth profile**

- Make sure your **birth date** is saved in **Edit Birth Data**.

**My birth time/place is unknown**

- That's fine. **Mayan profile only needs birth date.**

**Something feels "off"**

- Treat the system as a mirror, not a verdict. Ask MAIA:
    - "Offer 2–3 possible ways this could express, and help me choose what's true."

## Glossary (plain language)

- **Tzolk'in**: the 260-day sacred cycle (tone + day sign)
- **Day Sign (20 glyphs)**: your archetypal medicine / core pattern
- **Tone (1–13)**: how the medicine expresses (your developmental frequency)
- **Three Pillars**: youth / adulthood / future developmental signatures
- **Haab**: a solar/seasonal calendar layer (longer rhythm)
