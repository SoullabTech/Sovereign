# Quickstart: Mayan Astrology in AIN

## What it is

AIN includes a Mayan astrology layer (Tzolk'in + supporting cycles) to help you orient your **daily energy**, **core birth signature**, and **life-phase arc**.

## What you need

- Birth **date** (for your full profile)
- Birth time / birthplace **not required**

## Where to start

1. **Add your birth date** in your profile / birth data.
2. Open **Astrology → Mayan**.
3. Check **Today's Mayan Day Sign** (available for everyone).
4. Explore **Your Mayan Birth Profile** once your birth date is saved.

---

## What you'll see (and how to use it)

### A) Today's Mayan Day Sign (Daily "Cosmic Weather")

- Tone (1–13) + Day Sign (one of 20 glyphs)
- Use it to choose the _right kind of effort_ today: initiate, stabilize, refine, release, complete.

### B) Your Birth Day Sign (Tzolk'in)

- Your core archetypal medicine + your tone (how it expresses)
- Use it for self-understanding, shadow work, gifts, and alignment.

### C) Three Pillars (Path of Life)

- **Youth** (birth–~26)
- **Adulthood** (main expression)
- **Future** (~52+)
- Use it to understand life chapters, transitions, and what's maturing in you now.

### D) Cardinal Direction + Color + Activity

Use it to orient your decisions and rebalance when you feel scattered.

### E) Haab Solar Layer (Seasonal context)

Use it for longer-cycle reflection: seasons of growth, harvest, repair, renewal.
