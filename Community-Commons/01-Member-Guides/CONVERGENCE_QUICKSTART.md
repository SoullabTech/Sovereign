## How It Works

MAIA compares three independent traditions:

| System | Origin | What It Reveals |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Western astrology | Mediterranean | Psychological patterns, shadow, timing |
| Chinese astrology | East Asia | Elemental nature, life phases, health |
| Mayan astrology | Mesoamerica | Archetypal purpose, soul direction |

These evolved separately across oceans and millennia. When they agree, you're seeing something real.

## Reading the Signals

### Alignment = Confidence

When 2-3 systems point to the same theme (fire, transformation, consolidation):
- The pattern is strong
- Act with more confidence
- The universe is being clear

### Divergence = Complexity

When systems disagree:
- You're multidimensional, not wrong
- Different facets for different contexts
- Clarify which layer you're asking about

---

## Quick Decision Framework

Before major moves, check:

1. **Do at least 2/3 systems agree?** → Proceed with confidence
2. **Do they diverge?** → Slow down, gather more information
3. **Is one system silent?** → That's space, not a problem
