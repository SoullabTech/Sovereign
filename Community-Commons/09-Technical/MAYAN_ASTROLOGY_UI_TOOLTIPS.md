# UI Tooltips: Mayan Astrology in AIN

_Drop-in tooltip/popover copy for the Mayan astrology interface._

---

## General

**Mayan Astrology (AIN)**
> A sacred calendar lens for timing, life phases, and your core archetypal signature.

**Tzolk'in**
> The 260-day cycle: Tone (1–13) + Day Sign (20 glyphs).

---

## Today's layer

**Today's Mayan Day Sign**
> Today's energetic weather. Use it to choose the right pace: begin, build, refine, release, complete.

**Day Sign (Glyph)**
> The archetype of the day — what kind of medicine is moving.

**Tone (1–13)**
> The day's frequency — how the medicine expresses (initiate, stabilize, manifest, transcend).

---

## Birth profile

**Your Mayan Birth Day Sign**
> Your core medicine — the archetype you're here to embody and mature.

**Your Birth Tone**
> Your signature way of becoming — how your medicine moves through life.

---

## Three Pillars

**Path of Life: Three Pillars**
> Three chapters of development — early imprint, main expression, later flowering.

**Youth Pillar (birth–~26)**
> Foundational imprinting: early lessons, shaping forces, first initiations.

**Adulthood Pillar (main life)**
> Your primary expression: what you're meant to build, embody, and practice.

**Future Pillar (~52+)**
> Mature destiny arc: what ripens later, what begins calling you forward.

**Life Phase Insight**
> A plain-language reflection on the chapter you're in right now.

---

## Direction + seasonal layer

**Cardinal Direction**
> Your orienting current — a compass for decisions, balance, and soul-activity.

**Direction Activity**
> The core motion of your path: what you're meant to practice and express.

**Haab Solar Calendar**
> A seasonal layer — the longer rhythm around your life's growth and renewal.

---

## Gentle guardrails

**Use as a Mirror**
> This is a contemplative lens, not a verdict. Your lived experience is the authority.
