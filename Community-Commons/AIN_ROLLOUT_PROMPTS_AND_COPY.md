## Prompt Starter Kit (Organized by Elemental Themes)

### FIRE — Purpose, Drive & Transformation
*For when energy feels stuck or direction is unclear*

1. What are you ready to burn down and rebuild?
2. Where in your life are you playing it safe when your soul wants boldness?
3. What anger or frustration is pointing you toward something that matters?
4. If you had permission to want what you actually want, what would you ask for?
5. What transformation have you been resisting because it feels too big?

### WATER — Emotion, Intuition & Flow
*For processing feelings and accessing deeper knowing*

1. What emotion keeps visiting you that you haven't fully welcomed?
2. Where are you forcing when you could be flowing?
3. What does your body know that your mind hasn't accepted yet?
4. What grief or loss is still asking for your attention?
5. When you quiet everything else, what does your intuition whisper?

### EARTH — Foundation, Body & Practical Reality
*For grounding and building sustainable structures*

1. What foundation in your life needs repair before you can build higher?
2. How is your body asking to be cared for differently?
3. What practical step have you been avoiding that would change everything?
4. Where do you need more structure? Where do you need less?
5. What resources do you have that you're not fully using?

### AIR — Mind, Communication & Perspective
*For clarity, reframing, and new understanding*

1. What story are you telling yourself that might not be the whole truth?
2. If you could see your situation from 10,000 feet up, what would you notice?
3. What conversation are you avoiding that could unlock something important?
4. What belief served you once but has become a cage?
5. How would someone who loves you describe your current situation?

### AETHER — Soul Pattern, Purpose & Integration
*For connecting to deeper meaning and wholeness*

1. What thread connects the most meaningful moments of your life?
2. If your soul chose this particular life, what might it be learning?
3. What keeps showing up in your life that might be a pattern worth understanding?
4. Where are different parts of you in conflict? What do they each need?
5. What would integration look like for you right now?

## Situational Prompt Categories

### Relationship & Connection
- What pattern keeps appearing in your closest relationships?
- Who do you become around the people you love? Is that who you want to be?
- What boundary do you need to set that you've been avoiding?
- What do you long for in connection that you haven't let yourself ask for?

### Work & Creative Expression
- What work would you do even if no one paid you or praised you?
- Where are you giving your energy that isn't giving energy back?
- What creative impulse have you been suppressing?
- If your work was an expression of your soul, what would need to change?

### Transitions & Uncertainty
- What are you between right now? What's ending, and what hasn't begun?
- What certainty are you clinging to that might need to be released?
- What would trusting the process actually look like for you today?
- What wisdom from a past transition applies to where you are now?

### Shadow & Difficult Emotions
- What part of yourself do you hide from others? What does it need?
- What triggers you most in other people? What might that reveal?
- Where does shame show up in your life? What is it protecting?
- What would self-compassion look like in your hardest moment?
