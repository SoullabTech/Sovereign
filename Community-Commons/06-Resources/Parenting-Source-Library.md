# Parenting Source Library

> **Location:** `~/Obsidian Vaults/MAIA-Consciousness/01-Sources/Parenting-Library/`

This document references the primary source library that supports MAIA's parenting guidance capabilities. The full texts live in the MAIA-Consciousness Obsidian vault and feed into the Ideal Parenting Protocol (IPP) framework documented here in Community Commons.

---

## Library Contents (11 Sources)

### Books (7)
- **Positive Parenting** — Positive discipline, anger management, consequences
- **Cool Mantras to Make You a Terrific Parent** — Role-based parenting, flexibility, communication, creativity
- **Parenting with Purpose** (Nina Garcia) — Connection-based discipline, respect, routine, conflict resolution
- **Everyday Blessings** (Myla & Jon Kabat-Zinn) — Mindful parenting across developmental stages
- **Mindful Parenting for ADHD** (Mark Bertin, MD) — ADHD-specific strategies, executive function, mindfulness
- **Handbook of Adolescent Psychology** — Comprehensive adolescent development research
- **Child Development** — Full-spectrum developmental theory and research

### Workbooks (1)
- **The Whole-Brain Child Workbook** (Siegel & Bryson) — Brain integration strategies, Connect & Redirect, memory integration

### Clinical / Play Therapy (2)
- **Play Therapy Activities: 101 Exercises** — Connection, arts, movement, storytelling, relaxation activities for parents
- **101 More Favorite Play Therapy Techniques** (Kaduson & Schaefer) — Professional clinical play therapy techniques

### Assessments (1)
- **Parenting Style Assessment** (UMN Extension) — Self-assessment tool: Dominating, Permissive, Positive, Unengaged

---

## How This Connects to the IPP

The source library provides the evidence base and practical techniques that MAIA draws from when supporting parents. The IPP synthesizes these sources (plus attachment theory, neurobiology, and depth psychology) into the unified framework.

**Flow:**
```
Source Library (raw texts)
    ↓
Ideal Parenting Protocol (synthesis)
    ↓
MAIA Parenting Support (delivered to parents via Care/Talk modes)
```
