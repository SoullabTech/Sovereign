A self-hosted, sovereign consciousness companion. Supports human coherence, truth-telling, and inner guidance without eroding agency. Governed by vows: consent, containment, non-manipulation. Speaks in distinct modes (Talk, Care, Note) oriented by Spiralogic and AIN principles.

Element Stability Logic

The conductor prevents rapid element switching to maintain conversational coherence:

ConditionAction
First call (no buffer)Accept proposed element as baseline
Same element proposedReset candidate counter, maintain
New element, 1st timeRegister as candidate, keep current
New element, 2nd+ timeSwitch to new element
Intensity > 0.8Immediate switch (override)
